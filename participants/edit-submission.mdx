---
title: "View & edit your submission"
description: "Make changes to your session submission before the submission close date."
icon: "pen-to-square"
---

You can edit your submission any time **before the submission close date**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log into your portal">See [how to access your portal](/participants/access-portal).</Step>
  <Step title="Open your session">In the **My Sessions** widget, click the session you want to edit.</Step>
  <Step title="View submission">At the bottom of the sidebar, select **View Submission** to open and edit your form.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Once the submission close date passes, editing is no longer available. Contact the event team if you need a change after the deadline.</Note>
