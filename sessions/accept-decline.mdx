After a speaker submits a session, it appears in the **Sessions** module. Admins review the submission — along with any evaluator feedback from [Evaluation Plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans) — to approve or deny it.

## The five statuses

| Status | Meaning | What the speaker sees in their portal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ✅ **Accepted** | Officially approved and included in the event; typically shown on the agenda. | Accepted |
| 📥 **Accepted queue** | Recommended or held for acceptance, not yet finalized. | Pending |
| ⏳ **Pending** | Default status for newly submitted sessions; awaiting review. | Pending |
| 📥 **Decline queue** | Being considered for rejection, not yet declined. | Pending |
| ❌ **Declined** | Officially rejected and excluded from the event. | Declined |

<Tip>You can create custom statuses in [Program settings](/sessions/program-settings).</Tip>

<Warning>
Changing a session status does **not** automatically email the submitter or speakers. [Create and send accept/decline emails](/communications/create-send-emails) to notify participants.
</Warning>

Once a session is set to **Accepted**, it becomes visible in the [Agenda](/sessions/agenda) for your event team.

## Review submissions

### Review all submissions

Open the **Sessions** module and adjust your dashboard view to include the session questions from your submission form.

<Note>
The **Sessions** module houses session questions/fields only. To view speaker fields (company, job title, bio), build a dashboard view in the **Contacts → Speakers** module.
</Note>

### Review submissions individually

In the **Sessions** module, select the pencil icon next to the session you want to review.

<Tip>Use [Evaluation Plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans) to invite coworkers to review submissions collaboratively.</Tip>

## Update a session's status

In the **Sessions** module, click the current status of a session, then choose the new status from the dropdown. You can also update statuses in bulk.
