## Agenda settings

Customize your agenda under **Sessions → Settings → Agenda**.

## View your agenda

Open your agenda from **Sessions → Agenda**.

<Note>
By default, only submissions with an **Accepted** status appear in your agenda. Use Agenda Settings to include additional statuses.
</Note>

The agenda supports five layouts. Drag and drop accepted sessions to assign dates, times, or rooms depending on the view:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="List view" icon="list">Default — sessions in a table.</Card>
  <Card title="Day view" icon="calendar-day">Hourly timeline for a single day.</Card>
  <Card title="Week view" icon="calendar-week">Seven-day calendar.</Card>
  <Card title="Month view" icon="calendar">Full-month calendar layout.</Card>
  <Card title="Rooms view" icon="door-open">Sessions organized by room.</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
Tracks assigned to sessions determine their color in day, week, and room views. Track colors don't appear in month view.
</Tip>

In **Rooms view** you can zoom out to see all rooms without scrolling, and click the timeline icon to view rooms on the x-axis. Add and edit rooms in **Session Settings**.

## Conflicts

Conflict detection flags overlapping sessions and double-booked participants (speakers, chairpersons, moderators).

- Navigate to **Sessions → Agenda → Conflicts** to review them.
- Conflicts update on page refresh.
- Sessions with conflicts show a **red dot** in the agenda view — click **Open** to launch the session editor and resolve them.
