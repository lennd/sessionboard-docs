Program Settings is the configuration hub for your event agenda, covering criteria, personas, rooms, tracks, tags, levels, formats, languages, files, roles, and statuses. Setting these up keeps program management efficient from submission review through agenda organization.

## Agenda settings

Customize how your agenda views are organized:

- **Day start / end time** — earliest and latest scheduling times for each event day.
- **Interval** — time increments when creating and resizing sessions.
- **Agenda content** — show session-based items, abstract-based items, or both.
- **Program statuses** — surface sessions beyond just *Accepted* (e.g., *Accept Queue*, *Pending*).
- **Program format & default duration** — preset durations per session format.
- **Room visibility** — control which rooms appear when scheduling.

## Personas

Personas are AI-powered virtual evaluators that reflect a specific role, audience type, or area of expertise — such as a technical expert or a first-time attendee. They provide comparative feedback across diverse viewpoints. (See [AI Evaluations](/evaluations/ai-evaluations).)

To create one, click **Add Persona** and complete:

- **Name** (required)
- **Role** (required)
- **Biography**
- **Feedback style** — Positive, Neutral, Constructive, or Critical
- **Preferences** — up to three **Likes** and three **Dislikes**

<Tip>
Use the **TIERS** framework for effective personas: **T**ask (define the output), **I**nstructions (success criteria), **E**xamples (background + expected output), **R**eferences (docs/links), **S**harpen & iterate.
</Tip>

## Agenda building blocks

| Setting | What it's for | Select type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rooms** | Physical/virtual locations (e.g., "Ballroom A"). Capacity up to 100,000. | Single-select |
| **Tracks** | Broad thematic categories for filtering (e.g., "Industry & Business"). Drive card colors. | Single-select |
| **Tags** | Granular keywords for organizing, filtering, and reporting. | Multi-select |
| **Levels** | Expected attendee expertise (e.g., "Beginner"). | Single-select |
| **Formats** | Session style (e.g., "Panel", "Workshop", "Keynote"). | Single-select |
| **Languages** | Session language. | Single-select |

<Tip>Use **Card Colors** to color the agenda by track, status, room, format, level, or tags.</Tip>

## Files

Sessionboard supports speaker file uploads up to **1.95 GB per file**, with version management and commenting. See [enable & upload files](/sessions/create-a-session).

## Roles

Configure how participant roles display by mapping each to a category (Speaker, Chairperson, Moderator):

- **Category** — determines representation in integrations and embeds.
- **Participants** — count per role.
- **Manage** — when enabled, the role appears under **Event → Contacts**.

Click the ellipsis (**⋯**) to rename, or **Add Role** to create a custom role (choose a category, an icon, and whether to manage it in Contacts).

## Statuses

Create custom session statuses tailored to your workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a status">Click **Add Status**.</Step>
  <Step title="Configure it">
    - **Status name** — visible to portal users
    - **Status category** — every status maps to a category and behaves accordingly (e.g., an *Accepted*-category status syncs to integration partners)
    - **Color** and **Order**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">Click **Create**. Assign the status to sessions via the **Status** field.</Step>
</Steps>
