## Report types

- **Session reports** — combine session and contact data (sessions on the Y-axis).
- **Contact reports** — combine session, contact, and sponsor/exhibitor data (contacts on the Y-axis).
- **Group reports** — combine session, contact, and sponsor/exhibitor data (groups on the Y-axis).
- **Evaluation plan reports** — evaluator details and grades.

## Canned reports

Pre-built reports include *Sessions with Speaker Details*, *Sessions with Evaluation Ratings*, *Speakers with Session Details*, *Chairpersons/Moderators/Submitters with Session Details*, *Sponsors/Exhibitors with Contact and Session Details*, and evaluator/grade reports.

## Create a custom report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a report">In the **Reports** module, click **Add Report** and choose a session, contact, or group report.</Step>
  <Step title="Configure">Set a **Report Name** (required) and description, and choose **Relationships** (e.g., session speakers, evaluation plans) — each becomes a column.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose fields">Pick fields (categorized as Sessions, People, or Groups). Drag to reorder within each category.</Step>
  <Step title="Filters & sorting">
    Filter by field — dropdown (is/is not), checkbox (is/is not checked), number/text (empty, not empty, contains, does not contain), file (empty/not empty). Enable **Must match all filters** to require every condition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & run">Review the setup, then **Run Report** to generate an XLSX or CSV.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>To see additional contacts' email addresses, create a Contact report that includes **Related Additional Contacts**.</Tip>

## Manage reports

Use the gear icon on a report card to **Edit**, **Delete**, or **Duplicate** a report (duplicating saves time when building similar reports).
