Whether a contact has a new email address or prefers to use a different one than what is currently being used, changing a contact's email is simple; however, it is important to keep in mind that changing an email address within their contact profile does not change the contact's portal username/login.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Jan-28-2026-09-50-14-2312-PM.png)

The contact will still be required to use the old email address to log into their portal. If the contact attempts to log in to the event portal using the new email, they will receive the following error message

![Screenshot 2025-04-24 at 10.01.09 AM](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/Screenshot%202025-04-24%20at%2010.01.09%20AM.png)
​

### How to Change the Portal Username

To ensure the contact can access the event portal with the correct email, follow the steps below to update the contact's portal username:
1. Navigate to the Contacts module and locate the contact you want to update.
2. Select the pencil icon to the left of the record to access their profile.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Jan-28-2026-09-52-22-6613-PM.png)

3. Within the Details box > Gear Icon > Change Portal Username

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-17-2026-07-27-02-6475-PM.png)

4. Within the pop-up model, type the new email address within the New Username field and select Update.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-17-2026-07-34-24-8630-PM.png)
