The onboarding checklist is a step-by-step guide for implementation, ensuring that important event settings and modules are configured correctly before launching your program.

## Access the checklist

The checklist is provided as a Google Sheet so your team can track progress without changing the original template.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the checklist link">
    Open the checklist provided by your Sessionboard team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your own copy">
    Click **File** in the top navigation, then select **Make a copy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress as a team">
    Work through each task in your copy, checking items off as you complete them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the checklist includes

The checklist organizes tasks by Sessionboard module and setup phase.

**Event details setup** covers the foundational configuration:

- Event name
- Event slug
- Timezone
- Event dates
- Event logo
- Portal background images
- Record settings

**Module setup guides** cover each area you'll configure:

- Speaker management
- Session submission forms
- Agenda building
- Sponsor management
- Exhibitor management
- Evaluations
- Portal configuration

Each step links to a knowledge base article and a training video.
