---
title: "Can I clone a session?"
description: "Duplicate a session and understand what transfers to the copy."
icon: "clone"
---

Yes. You can duplicate one or more sessions from the Sessions dashboard.

## Clone a session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select sessions">From the Sessions dashboard, check the box next to the session(s) you want to clone.</Step>
  <Step title="Duplicate">In the sub-menu, click **More → Duplicate Sessions**, then confirm **Duplicate**.</Step>
  <Step title="Done">Your session is cloned.</Step>
</Steps>

## What's included

All session information is copied **except**:

- The cloned session's status is set to **Pending**.
- Uploaded files are **not** included.
