The CRM API is the programmable side of Sessionboard CRM. It has its own reference, MCP tools and release notes, apart from the other Sessionboard products.

## Base path

CRM is organization-level, so every operation starts at `/v1/organization/{orgId}/crm`. The same operations are served under `/v1/org/{orgId}/crm`, and under `/v1/crm` with the organization taken from the token. They run on the same regional hosts as the rest of the API: `public-api.sessionboard.com` in the US and `public-api-eu.sessionboard.com` in the EU.

## What you can do

Discovering people in a connected app only reads: it writes nothing in Sessionboard or in the app, and only counts an event's attendees. Review the rows it returns, then send the ones you choose to `POST …/crm/import` with `source` set to the app and `target` set to `people` or `companies`. An event platform's attendees are counted by discovery and brought in with `POST …/crm/apps/{appId}/attendees`, which runs the platform's own attendee sync into People. A person becomes a contact (the record the pipeline and contracts use) when they're tagged `speaker`, linked to a company, or added with `POST …/crm/people/add-to-contacts`; the person and the contact stay two records, linked. `PUT …/crm/apps/{appId}/sync` keeps a source in sync nightly, and answers `403` with `code: sync_disabled` where that isn't turned on.

Only sending, resending and voiding a contract emails anyone: the signer. Confirm the recipient with a person before you call them. The pipeline and contracts are the Sessionboard platform's own, so an organization that moves to the platform keeps them. Editing a template's wording or signer roles is done in Sessionboard's template editor, not through the API.

Request bodies are camelCase (`firstName`, `companyIds`, `updateExisting`, `remoteEventId`, `contactIds`, `stageId`, `templateId`); responses are snake_case. Standard fields only: `GET …/crm/fields` lists them, and custom fields are a Sessionboard platform upgrade.

People and companies are free to store. CRM is free up to 1,000 active contacts (people you emailed, invited, sent a contract or a form, scheduled in a session, or who registered, in the last 365 days, once per email across CRM and Marketing); paid plans above that are yearly. Going over the limit blocks nothing. If a paid plan ends unpaid, writes answer `403` with `code: plan_read_only`, and everything can still be read.

CRM needs the `read:crm` and `write:crm` scopes; see [Authentication](/sessionboard-crm/api/authentication). AI assistants can do the same work with the [CRM MCP tools](/sessionboard-crm/api/mcp-tools). Release notes are in the [CRM changelog](/sessionboard-crm/api/changelog).
