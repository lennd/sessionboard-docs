Release notes for the Marketing API and its MCP tools. Entries are grouped by month, newest first.

## Upcoming

### Webinar takeaways behind one email gate

**Added — takeaways on an event** at `/v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/events/{eventId}/assets` (also `/v1/marketing/events/{eventId}/assets`, organization from the token): `GET` lists them with download counts, `POST .../assets/presign` returns a presigned upload, `POST .../assets` attaches one (`key`, `from_url`, `content_base64` + `filename`, or `link_url`), `PUT` and `DELETE .../assets/{assetId}` change or remove one. Schema `EventAsset`. Reads need `read:marketing`, writes `write:marketing`.

**Added — MCP tools** `list_webinar_assets`, `request_webinar_asset_upload`, `attach_webinar_asset`, `update_webinar_asset` and `remove_webinar_asset`. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools#webinar-takeaways).

**Added — `<sb-portal kind="assets">` and `<sb-data source="assets">` on agent pages,** with one email gate per page view. The gate's sign-up form has placement `asset_gate`, and its sign-ups show with source `asset_gate`. Each download is a `content.downloaded` signal on the person and event.

**Changed — gated videos on agent pages** no longer remember a visitor between page views. One email unlocks every gated video and takeaway on the page until it is reloaded.

<Note>
These arrive with an upcoming Sessionboard release. The date will be added here when they are live.
</Note>

### Recording pages: write the summary, takeaways, sections and FAQ, and attach a transcript you have

**Changed — `PUT /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/content/recordings/{mediaId}`** and the MCP tool `update_recording` take the copy for a recording's agent-site page: `summary`, `keyTakeaways`, `sections` (`[{heading, paragraphs}]`), `faq` (`[{question, answer}]`), `seoTitle` and `metaDescription`. What you write wins over the AI copy; `null` clears a field. `regenerateSummary: true` rewrites the AI summary, takeaways and sections from the transcript. Gallery-route pages read them as `summary_paragraphs`, `key_takeaways`, `sections` and `faq`, and `seo_title` and `meta_description` replace the page's title and description. Descriptions now keep their paragraphs and bullet lines.

**Added — `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/content/recordings/{mediaId}/transcript`** (also `/v1/marketing/content/recordings/{mediaId}/transcript`) and the MCP tool `add_recording_transcript`. Attach a transcript you already have, as speaker, start and text lines, to a recording whose file could not be downloaded or transcribed. The recording becomes ready, and its chapters, page summary and search follow.

Live since November 25, 2026.

### Webinars: choose a library's items, gated recording pages, conversion tracking, an after-webinar plan and what's outstanding

**Added — `GET` and `PUT /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/pages/galleries/{galleryId}/items`** (also `/v1/marketing/pages/galleries/{galleryId}/items`) and the MCP tools `list_gallery_items` and `set_gallery_items`. Hold exactly the recordings you choose (`onlyChosen`), add, remove, order and feature items. An organization-wide library never adds Zoom meeting or call recordings.

**Added — `PUT /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/content/recordings/{mediaId}`** and the MCP tool `update_recording`. Set, move or clear a recording's event, and change its title, description and tags after import.

**Added — `<sb-portal kind="video">` and gallery routes on agent pages.** A gated, captioned player with chapters and a transcript, one page per public library recording at `/webinars/{slug}`, and `VideoObject`, `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` structured data. `get_page_kit` takes an optional `kind`, for example `webinar_recording`.

**Added — `GET` and `PUT /v1/sites/tracking` and `/v1/sites/{siteId}/tracking`** and the MCP tools `get_site_tracking` and `set_site_tracking`. Set Google Tag Manager, GA4, HubSpot, LinkedIn and Meta ids; they go live at once. Pages push `sb_form_submit`, `sb_cta_click`, `sb_video_gate_unlocked`, `sb_video_play` and `sb_video_progress` to the `dataLayer`, and sign-ups are saved with their UTM, ad click id and HubSpot cookie attribution.

**Added — `GET` and `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/events/{eventId}/after-webinar`, `GET` and `POST .../events/after-webinar-defaults`** and the MCP tools `get_after_webinar_plan`, `set_after_webinar_plan`, `get_after_webinar_defaults` and `set_after_webinar_defaults`. A plan set before a webinar runs when it ends. `plan_after_event` and `send_after_event` now also take webinars on any Marketing account.

**Added — `GET /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/outstanding`** (also `/v1/marketing/outstanding`) and the MCP tool `get_outstanding`. Everything outstanding across events, or one, each with the question to ask and the tool calls that handle it.

Live since November 23, 2026.

### Agent pages: your own images, and event pages that move to an agent page

**Added — `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/sites/{siteId}/uploads`** (also `/v1/sites/{siteId}/uploads`) and the MCP tool `request_page_upload`. It copies up to 25 public https images onto Sessionboard's content CDN and returns their addresses, size and pixel dimensions, so a page you write can use them. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and AVIF, up to 15 MB each; SVG is not accepted.

**Added — `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/pages/events/{eventId}/redirect`** (also `/v1/marketing/pages/events/{eventId}/redirect`) and the MCP tool `set_event_page_redirect`. When you rebuild an event's page as an agent page, its `/e/<slug>` address sends visitors there with a permanent redirect. The target must be a published agent page of your organization. `redirect_to: null` takes it off. Needs `write:marketing` and `confirmed: true`.

Live since November 17, 2026.

### Sites: delete one, and archived events' Sites out of the list

**Added — `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/sites/{siteId}/delete`** (also `/v1/sites/{siteId}/delete`) and the MCP tool `delete_site`. It deletes a Site on `confirm: true`. A live Site also needs `unpublish: true`. The Site's address and custom domain are freed at once. It is always a soft delete: **`POST .../sites/{siteId}/restore`** and the MCP tool `restore_site` bring it back with its pages, versions and draft, as a draft, under `<slug>-restored` if its address was taken in the meantime. Needs `write:sites` or the Site's product scope, and membership of the organization.

**Changed — `GET .../sites` and `list_sites`** leave out Sites whose event is archived. Pass `include_archived=true` to see them.

### Marketing: shows and episodes

**Added — shows.** A show you record on a schedule, with numbered episodes, at `/v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/shows` (also `/v1/marketing/shows` with the organization taken from the token): `GET` lists shows with episode counts by status, `POST .../shows/create` creates a draft show, `GET` / `PUT` / `DELETE .../shows/{showId}` reads, changes and archives one, `POST .../shows/{showId}/episodes` and `PUT` / `DELETE .../episodes/{episodeId}` add, change and remove episodes. A show links an existing podcast, newsletter, page and Brand Kit; an episode holds its topic, guests (contact ids), `scheduled_at`, meeting link, recording, status (`idea`, `booked`, `recorded`, `produced`, `published`, `shared`) and `results`. Reads need `read:marketing`, writes `write:marketing`, with a key that is not restricted to particular events.

**Added — `POST .../shows/{showId}/episodes/{episodeId}/run`.** From the episode's recording it drafts a podcast episode, clips and posts, a newsletter issue, a guest kit dry run and the page section, and reports each step (`drafted`, `done`, `skipped`, `waiting`, `failed`). It publishes and sends nothing and is safe to call again.

**Added — MCP tools** `list_shows`, `get_show`, `create_show`, `update_show`, `archive_show`, `create_show_episode`, `update_show_episode`, `remove_show_episode`, `run_show_episode` and `create_podcast`, and the **Run your weekly show** skill. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools#shows).

All of this is additive; nothing existing changes.

These arrive with an upcoming Sessionboard release. The date will be added here when they are live.

### Marketing: edits you can see and undo, and results with a story

**Added — what changed, on every edit.** Edits to an existing event page, Site, lifecycle email proposal, newsletter issue or campaign draft now return `what_changed` (one plain sentence per change in `summary`, and `added` / `changed` with `before` and `after` / `removed` / `moved` as data) and `kept` (where the earlier version is and the call that restores it). Schemas `WhatChanged`, `EditKept`, `EditReceipt`.

**Added — nothing is overwritten without a way back.** Before an API or assistant edit replaces someone else's unpublished Site or event page draft, the draft is kept as a version (`source: checkpoint` in the versions list) and restores with the existing versions restore route. Newsletter issues keep a version before every edit: `GET .../marketing/newsletter/issues/{issueId}/versions` and `POST .../issues/{issueId}/versions/{versionId}/restore` (also under `/v1/marketing/...`). Campaign drafts keep one too (`manage` actions `versions` and `restore_version`). An earlier, unaccepted email proposal stays in the email's history instead of being removed.

**Added — `story` on results.** `GET .../marketing/results` (event, webinar, organization and sponsor reports) and `GET .../marketing/results/outcomes` carry `story`: a headline, three to five findings, drivers, a next move and slide-ready sections with chart specs. Every number cites the field it comes from (`ResultsStory`). The sharing results summary and in-person event results carry it too.

**Added — MCP tools `list_newsletter_issue_versions` and `restore_newsletter_issue_version`;** `update_newsletter_issue` answers with what changed. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools#edits-you-can-see-and-undo).

All of this is additive; existing fields and status codes are unchanged. Publishing, accepting, scheduling and launching still wait for a person's OK.

### Marketing: webinar questions, topic watch time and outcomes

**Added — webinar reporting.** `GET /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/results/questions` lists questions asked in your webinars that are worth following up and whether anyone has followed up in your CRM or AMS since. `GET .../results/topic-watch-time` gives live and on-demand watch time by topic (medians; topics under 5 people left out). `GET .../results/outcomes` gives what happened after people attended: sales pipeline and closed won from accounts that attended, memberships and renewals, or exhibitor and sponsor contracts, depending on your organization's kind. `GET` / `PUT .../results/outcomes/settings` reads and sets how many days after the first webinar a deal or membership still counts (30 to 365, default 180). Every response carries `org_kind` and `labels`, your organization's own words for people and money, and outcomes carry a `method` sentence. All are also served under `/v1/marketing/results/...` with the organization taken from the token, and need `read:marketing` (the settings `PUT` needs `write:marketing`). Additive; nothing existing changes. See the [Marketing API overview](/sessionboard-marketing/api).

**Added — MCP tools `find_questions_to_follow_up`, `get_topic_watch_time` and `get_webinar_outcomes`.** See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).

<Note>
These arrive with an upcoming Sessionboard release. The date will be added here when they are live.
</Note>

### Marketing: AI apps get the product scopes; actions that reach people wait for your OK

**Added — scopes for the self-serve products.** `read:marketing`, `write:marketing`, `read:sites`, `write:sites`, `read:learning` and `write:learning` are now listed in `scopes_supported`, beside `read:brand_kits`, `write:brand_kits`, `read:awards` and `write:awards`. Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini connections get them when they connect, and API tokens for organizations on a Marketing, Learning, Awards or CRM plan can tick them under **Your products**. Every request still checks the organization's product and the person's role. See [Marketing API authentication](/sessionboard-marketing/api/authentication).

**Changed — requests for scopes a client was not registered for.** `GET /oauth/authorize` now leaves out a known scope the client was not registered for and grants the rest, instead of refusing the whole request with `invalid_scope`. Unknown scopes are still refused. Clients registered earlier keep connecting; reconnect to pick up the new scopes.

**Changed — MCP actions that send, publish or spend now wait for a person.** When an AI assistant asks to launch a campaign, send invitations or content kits, publish a page, course or library, approve ads, start captions or assign training, the response is `200` with `status: "needs_approval"`, an `approval_id` and a `url`. Nothing runs until someone on the team approves it in Sessionboard; approving runs the request as that person. Drafts, edits and reads are unchanged. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).

These arrive with an upcoming Sessionboard release. The date will be added here when they are live.

### Marketing: build and edit your event page by API or chat

**Added — the Sites API.** Read your event pages, edit a page's draft (its blocks, menu and theme), see what Publish would change in plain sentences, preview it as a visitor or an invited guest on desktop, tablet or phone, publish, and restore any earlier version into the draft. `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/sites/make` makes a page for an event from the event itself and your Brand Kit, or rebuilds a page you point it at. Every edit lands in the draft, and nothing goes live until you publish with the person's OK; an AI assistant's publish waits for a person to approve it in Sessionboard. The same operations are served under `/v1/sites/...` with the organization taken from the token. They need `read:sites` / `write:sites` or `read:marketing` / `write:marketing`. See the [Marketing API overview](/sessionboard-marketing/api).

**Added — ten MCP tools for your event page,** from `list_sites` and `get_site` to `edit_site`, `publish_site` and `restore_site_version`. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).

### Marketing: how an event is doing

**Added — turnout and pacing for events and webinars.** `GET /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/goals/turnout` returns registrations so far, an estimate of registrations and turnout at the event date (with a range, a confidence label and how it was estimated), pace against the event's goal, the last comparable event, registrations by day before the event, the best time of week to send email and two or three suggestions. `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/goals/turnout/{eventId}/goal` sets or clears the registration and attendance goal. Both are also served under `/v1/marketing/...` with the organization taken from the token. They need the preview scopes `read:marketing` / `write:marketing`. See the [Marketing API overview](/sessionboard-marketing/api).

**Added — MCP tools `get_turnout` and `set_turnout_goal`**, so an assistant can answer "how's it doing?" with the same numbers. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).

### Marketing: sign-ups on your own pages

**Added — sign-up forms.** `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/audience/forms/ensure` adds an email sign-up to an event page, a library or a recording with nothing to fill in (the words come from the event or recording; people confirm their email before they join). With `addToPage: true` it goes on the event page draft; publishing stays yours. Forms can also be listed, created, changed and archived under `/marketing/audience/forms`.

**Added — sign-ups.** `GET /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/audience/subscribers` lists who signed up, where and whether they confirmed, with a CSV export; `POST .../subscribers/create` adds someone who agreed elsewhere and `POST .../subscribers/unsubscribe` stops emailing someone. `GET .../audience/summary` answers "how many people signed up this week?". All are also served under `/v1/marketing/...` with the organization taken from the token, need the preview scopes `read:marketing` / `write:marketing`, and are refused for a key restricted to particular events.

**Added — MCP tools `add_signup_form`, `gate_recording`, `count_signups` and `list_subscribers`.** See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).

### Marketing: video gates, buttons and premieres

**Added — what happens around a recording.** `GET` / `PUT /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/video/items/{mediaId}` reads and changes a recording's gate, button and end card. With nothing set, anyone can watch, the button is "Register for" your next event at the end, and a "Watch next" card follows. `POST .../video/items/{mediaId}/gate` asks for an email before the recording plays or after a set time, using the recording's sign-up form.

**Added — premieres.** `POST /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/video/premieres/ensure` plays a recording at a set time for everyone on the event page, with questions and polls beside it and reminders for visitors who ask; it goes on the page draft and publishing stays yours. Premieres are listed, moved, cancelled or ended under `.../video/premieres`, and `.../premieres/{premiereId}/room`, `/questions` and `/polls` read and manage the room. All are also served under `/v1/marketing/...` with the organization taken from the token, need the preview scopes `read:marketing` / `write:marketing`, and are refused for a key restricted to particular events.

**Added — MCP tools `gate_video`, `get_video_options`, `set_video_options`, `schedule_premiere`, `list_premieres`, `update_premiere`, `get_premiere_room`, `manage_premiere_poll` and `moderate_premiere_question`.** Tools that change what visitors see are marked destructive and ask you first. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).

### Marketing: podcasts, newsletter, journeys, video, website and sponsor links

**Added — podcasts.** `/v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/podcasts` lists, creates, changes and deletes shows; `POST .../podcasts/from-event` turns an event's recordings into a show of draft episodes; `.../podcasts/{showId}/episodes` adds draft episodes and `.../episodes/status` moves them to approved or published; `GET .../podcasts/stats` returns downloads.

**Added — newsletter.** `GET` / `PUT /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/newsletter` reads and changes the newsletter; issues are drafted, read, changed and deleted under `.../newsletter/issues`, sent with `.../issues/{issueId}/schedule` (or held with `.../unschedule`), and `.../issues/{issueId}/results` returns deliveries, opens, clicks, unsubscribes and bounces.

**Added — journeys.** `GET /v1/organization/{orgId}/marketing/journeys` lists the four follow-up journeys; `PUT .../journeys/{journeyId}` changes one or switches it on or off, and `POST .../journeys/{journeyId}/reset` puts its email back to the default.

**Added — video, website tag, consent and sponsor links.** `GET .../marketing/video/media/{mediaId}/playback` and `.../watchers` return a recording's playback details and who watched it. `GET .../marketing/audience/website` and `.../website/visits` return your website tag and the known people who visited; `GET .../audience/people/{personKey}/website` and `.../consent` return one person's visits and consent history (read only). `.../audience/sponsor-shares` lists, creates and revokes private sponsor links to the people who opted in to hear from partners.

Calls that publish, send, switch on emails to real people or share with a sponsor answer `202` with `status: needs_approval` and a link when an assistant makes them; a person approves the request in Sessionboard. All of these are also served under `/v1/marketing/...` with the organization taken from the token, need the preview scopes `read:marketing` / `write:marketing`, and are refused for a key restricted to particular events.

**Added — 21 MCP tools** for the same areas, from `make_podcast_from_event` and `draft_newsletter_issue` to `get_watch_report` and `share_with_sponsor`. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).
