Pathways records how many people registered for each session, how many actually walked in, and how full the room was. You see it per session on the event, rolled up by topic and by speaker, and Sessionboard uses it to flag sessions on your next agenda that are likely to outgrow — or rattle around in — their rooms.

## Why use it

Program committees pick sessions and rooms on instinct, then learn at the door that the keynote overflowed and the breakout was a quarter full. With last year's check-ins in Sessionboard, you can see which topics and speakers actually draw a crowd, move a session into a bigger room before it happens again, and let attendance count toward a speaker's score when you choose who to invite back.

## What the numbers mean

Open **Pathways > Attendees** on the event and select the **By session** tab.

![The By session tab with the stat strip and per-session registered, checked-in, capacity and fill columns](/images/kb/pathways-session-fill-by-session.png)

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Registered** | People enrolled in the session, or the total you imported |
| **Checked in** | People who walked in (a badge scan, door count or check-in), or the total you imported |
| **Capacity** | The capacity from your import, otherwise the session's capacity, otherwise its room's capacity |
| **Fill** | Checked in ÷ capacity. **Overbooked** means more people registered than the room holds |
| **Show rate** | Checked in ÷ registered |

The strip above the table totals the event: how many sessions have data, everyone checked in, the median fill, how many sessions were **At capacity** (95% full or more) and how many were under half full, and the event's show rate. Turn on **Only sessions with data** to hide sessions you have no numbers for, and select a column header to sort by it.

When you have both kinds of data for a session, per-person check-ins win over an imported total, field by field — a session with scanned check-ins and only an enrollment total keeps both.

## Import attendance from a spreadsheet

Use this for badge-scan exports, door counts, or a session report from your registration platform. Your file can be comma-, semicolon- or tab-separated.

![The Import session attendance panel with a file loaded and its columns matched](/images/kb/pathways-import-session-attendance.png)

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the import">
  On the **By session** tab, select **Import attendance**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose what is in the file">
  Pick **A total per session** for one row per session with counts, or **One row per person** for one row per registrant per session. **Download a sample file** shows the columns each one expects.
</Step>
<Step title="Add your file">
  Drop the file on the panel or select **Choose file**.
</Step>
<Step title="Match the columns">
  Sessionboard guesses which column is which from the headers. Check each one under **Match the columns** and change any that are wrong; choose **Not in file** for a column you don't have. The first rows preview below the mapping.
</Step>
<Step title="Import">
  Select **Import rows**. The result lists how many sessions were updated and every row that was skipped, with the reason.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Matching sessions.** The **Session** column can hold the session's id, friendly id, external id, or its exact title. Rows that match no session on the event are skipped and listed.

**A total per session** reads **Registered**, **Checked in** and an optional **Capacity**. A capacity in the file overrides the room's capacity for that session.

**One row per person** needs **Session** and **Email**, plus a **Status**, an **Attended** yes/no column, or both. The email must belong to someone registered for the event. Status words Sessionboard understands:

- Attended: `attended`, `checked in`, `scanned`, `present`, `visited`, `yes`
- Registered: `registered`, `enrolled`, `confirmed`, `accepted`, `booked`, `reserved`, `no-show`
- Cancelled: `cancelled`, `unregistered`, `removed`, `withdrawn`

Re-importing a session replaces its earlier upload, so you can correct a file and import it again. One file can hold up to 50,000 rows.

## See fill by topic and speaker

- **By topic:** **Pathways > Topics** adds **Avg checked in**, **Median fill** and **At capacity** columns once any session has data. Open a topic to see each of its sessions with checked-in, capacity and fill figures.
- **By speaker:** a speaker's Speaker Score card shows a **Room draw** row — how full their sessions were, across every event you have data for.
- **Program report:** **Pathways > Program report** on the event reports total check-ins, median fill and full sessions under **Attendance and ratings**.

## Get room-fit suggestions for your agenda

Once any past event has check-ins, **Program > Agenda** shows a **Room fit** panel on the **List** tab. It forecasts each session's turnout from how its topics and speakers drew before, and from its registrations so far at your usual show rate, then compares that with the room.

![The Room fit panel listing sessions forecast to overflow, each with a suggested larger room and a Move button](/images/kb/pathways-agenda-room-fit.png)

- Sessions forecast over their room's capacity are listed first, with the smallest free room that fits.
- Sessions forecast to fill under about a third of their room get a smaller free room when one exists.
- Select **Move** to move one session, or **Apply … suggested moves** (with the count) to move them all. A move only changes the session's room, and only to a room that is free at that time.
- Sessions with no room capacity and no history to forecast from are counted under the list.

Select **Hide** to collapse the panel.

<Note>
The forecast uses other events' attendance, never the event you are planning. Import last year's check-ins first if this is your first event with Pathways.
</Note>

## Weigh room draw in the Speaker Score

The Speaker Score blends committee reviews, attendee ratings and room draw into one number out of 5. Change how much each counts in **Settings > Speaker Score**.

![The Speaker Score blend editor with four weighted lanes on the left and a live preview of one speaker's score on the right](/images/kb/pathways-speaker-score-blend.png)

1. Under **The blend**, drag a lane's slider or type a percentage. The lanes always add up to 100 — moving one shares the difference across the others. **Reset** puts the default weights back.
2. **Live preview** on the right recalculates as you go: the selected speaker's score with your draft against their saved score, how much each lane contributed, their score per event, and how the scores of all your speakers would shift.
3. Select the speaker's name at the top of the preview to choose who it shows. Search by name or email.
4. Select **Save changes** to apply the blend to every speaker. **Discard** throws the draft away.

**Room draw** scores each of a speaker's sessions by how full the room was (a full room is 5). When a session has no capacity, its check-ins are compared with the event's median session instead. A lane with no data for a speaker is left out of their score rather than counted as zero.

Under **Rules**, **Recent event counts double** weighs the last rated event twice in the all-events score, and **Minimum speaker ratings** sets how many attendee ratings a speaker needs before that lane counts.
