After a speaker submits a session, it appears in **Program → Sessions** in the form's default status — usually **Pending**. Deciding what gets in is a matter of moving each session to the right status, either directly or after an [evaluation plan](/evaluations/evaluation-plans) has scored it.

## The five status categories

Every session sits in one of five standard statuses. The tabs across the top of the Sessions list are these statuses, with a count on each.

![The Sessions list status tabs — All Sessions, Accepted, Accept Queue, Pending, Decline Queue, Declined, Withdrawn, Drafts](/images/kb/sessions-status-tabs.png)

| Status | Meaning | What the speaker sees in the portal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accepted** | Approved and in the program. Appears on the [agenda](/sessions/agenda) and, when synced, in your integrations. | Accepted |
| **Accept Queue** | Recommended or held for acceptance, not yet final. A holding area while decisions or scheduling are worked out. | Pending |
| **Pending** | The default for new submissions. Awaiting review or a decision. | Pending |
| **Decline Queue** | Being considered for rejection, not yet declined. | Pending |
| **Declined** | Rejected and out of the program. | Declined |

The queues exist so a committee can sort as it goes without telling anyone yet. Speakers see only the three outcomes; the queues read as Pending until you move the session on.

Two more tabs hold the sessions that aren't in the workflow: **Withdrawn** (the submitter pulled it) and **Drafts** (started on the form, never submitted — see [draft submissions](/sessions/draft-submissions)).

<Tip>
If your committee thinks in stages like “Shortlisted” or “Needs Revision”, add them as custom statuses under [Program settings → Statuses](/sessions/program-settings#statuses). Each custom status belongs to one of the five categories and behaves like it.
</Tip>

## Review submissions

All at once — the Sessions list is a table of every submission. [Adjust the columns](/reporting/dashboard-views) to include the questions from your submission form, then sort and filter. The Sessions list carries the session's fields; for speaker fields (company, job title, bio) build the same view under **CRM → Speakers**.

One at a time — click the pencil on a row to open the session editor. The **Details** tab has every answer, **Participants** the people, **Files** and **Media** what they uploaded, and **Reviews** the scores from any evaluation plan.

<Tip>
To bring more people into the decision, use [evaluation plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans): reviewers score submissions against your criteria, and the results land on each session's **Reviews** tab and in the [Evaluation Summary](/evaluations/evaluation-summary).
</Tip>

## Change a session's status

In the Sessions list, click the session's current status and pick the new one from the dropdown. The row moves to the matching tab immediately. To move many at once, select them and use [bulk edit](/settings/bulk-edit-fields) on the **Status** field.

A custom role's **Statuses** tab can limit which statuses its members may set — track chairs can send sessions to the Accept Queue while the final **Accepted** and **Declined** calls stay with you. See [limit which statuses a role can set](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members#limit-which-statuses-a-role-can-set).

<Note>
Changing a status does not email anyone by itself. When decisions are final, [send accept and decline emails](/communications/create-send-emails) to the speakers using the built-in **Accept** and **Decline** [email templates](/settings/email-templates) — or build an [automation](/automations/overview) that emails the people on a session when its status changes.
</Note>

Once a session is **Accepted** it appears on the [agenda](/sessions/agenda) for scheduling, and — if the portal's tasks are gated on acceptance — its speakers' post-acceptance tasks open in their portal.

<IfSetting id="enable_speaker_acceptance">
Accepting a session is not the end of the handoff. Each speaker still has to confirm their participation from their portal before the session is settled, so check the confirmation column in [Submissions](app:EventSubmissions) after a decision round.
</IfSetting>
