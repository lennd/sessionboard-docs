After a speaker submits a session, the session appears in the Sessions module. The event admin then reviews the submission, along with any evaluator feedback from the Evaluation Plans, to approve or deny the session.

## Difference Between Each Status

Once sessions have been submitted, admin users can update their status to be either accepted, accepted queue, pending, decline queue, or declined.

<Tip>
👉🏽 [Learn how to create custom statuses](/sessions/session-settings)
</Tip>

- **✅Accepted**

  - The session has been officially approved and will be included in the event.
  - Typically visible on the agenda or schedule.
  - Speakers will see their session as accepted within the event portal.**
    ![Speakers will see their session as accepted within the event portal](/images/kb/3307393c-Screenshot-2025-05-21-at-6.02.32-PM.png)

\**

- **📥Accepted Queue**
  - The session has been recommended or is being held for acceptance, but has not yet been finalized.
  - Often used as a temporary status while final decisions or scheduling logistics are worked out.
  - Allows you to identify sessions that are likely to be accepted but still under review.
  - Speakers will see their session as pending within the event portal
    ![Speakers will see their session as pending within the event portal](/images/kb/36e9b961-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-11.45.28-AM.png)

- **⏳Pending**
  - The default status for newly submitted sessions.
  - Indicates that the session is awaiting review or a decision.
  - Speakers will see their session as pending within the event portal
    ![Speakers will see their session as pending within the event portal](/images/kb/b613972e-Screenshot-2025-05-21-at-6.02.37-PM.png)

- **📥Decline Queue**
  - The session is being considered for rejection, but has not yet been officially marked as declined.
  - Similar to a “holding area” for sessions that may be declined after further review or discussion.
  - Speakers will see their session as pending within the event portal
    ![Speakers will see their session as pending within the event portal](/images/kb/36e9b961-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-11.45.28-AM.png)

- **❌Declined**
  - The session has been officially rejected and will not be included in the event.
  - Speakers will see their session as declined within the event portal.
    ![Speakers will see their session as declined within the event portal](/images/kb/b7d49d9b-Screenshot-2025-05-21-at-6.02.27-PM.png)

[](/sessions/session-settings)

<Tip>
**Note**: Changing a session status does not trigger an email to be sent to the submitter or session speakers. [Learn how to create and send accept and decline emails](/communications/create-send-emails) to your event contacts.
</Tip>

Once a submission status has been changed to "Accepted", the session will be visible within the [Agenda](/sessions/agenda) for the event team.

## How To Review Submissions

### Review All Submissions

To review all submissions holistically can be achieved from the '**Sessions**' module.

![To review all submissions holistically can be achieved from the 'Sessions' module](/images/kb/b0acfac6-image-png-Feb-09-2026-10-19-26-1076-PM.png)

Once you have your submission form available to end users, [adjust your dashboard view](/reporting/dashboard-views) within the '**Sessions'** module to include all of the session questions you included on your submission form.

<Tip>
***TIP****: The '**Sessions**' module is only going to house your session questions/fields. To view any questions/fields for your speakers (i.e. company name, job title, bio), you can create a similar dashboard view within the '**Contacts** \> **Speakers**' module.*

![Once you have your submission form available to end users](/images/kb/cc0a1fd5-image-png-Feb-09-2026-10-19-58-2415-PM.png)
</Tip>

### Review Submissions Individually

Within the **Sessions** module, select the pencil icon next to the session you want to review.

![Within the Sessions module, select the pencil icon next to the session you want to review](/images/kb/815ad137-image-png-Feb-09-2026-10-20-52-6943-PM.png)

<Tip>
***💡 TIP:*** *Use* *[Evaluation Plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans) to invite additional coworkers to review session submissions is a great tool for collaboration and feedback.*
</Tip>

## How To Update The Status Of A Session

Within the Sessions module, select the current status of the session you want to update. Then, select the new status from the drop-down menu.

<Tip>
***💡 TIP:*** *You can even [update session statuses in bulk](/settings/bulk-edit-fields).*
</Tip>

![Within the Sessions module, select the current status of the session you want to update](/images/kb/a50041e6-image-png-Feb-09-2026-10-21-05-5866-PM.png)

<Tip>
***TIP:*** *Once you have updated the status of a session submission form,* *[email the speakers through Sessionboard](/communications/create-send-emails) to notify them of their submission status.*
</Tip>
