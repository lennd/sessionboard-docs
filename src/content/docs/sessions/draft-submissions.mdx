Submitters rarely have everything on hand the first time they open your form. **Save as draft** lets them start, leave, and come back — and every draft is visible to your team from the moment it is saved, so you can see who is part-way through before the deadline lands.

For the submitter's side — saving, resuming and discarding a draft — see [Save a submission as a draft](/participants/save-a-submission-as-a-draft).

## Why use it

Drafts show you the proposals that were started but not finished, so you can nudge the right people before a deadline instead of discovering the gap afterwards. When a submitter resumes a draft, the form takes them to the first step that is still incomplete, so a required field they skipped never turns into a dead-end error at Submit.

## Where drafts appear

A draft is a submission still in the submitter's hands, so it is kept out of your working lists: it does not count toward **All Submissions**, does not go to evaluators, and is not shown on the agenda, in embeds, or through the API. You find drafts in three places.

**The Drafts tab.** Go to **Program → View All** (or **Sessions**) and select **Drafts**. Each row shows the title so far, a **Draft** status, and the **Source** form it is being written on. Because a draft has only the answers the submitter has typed, columns such as format, track and time are often still empty.

![The submissions page on the Drafts tab, listing two draft proposals with a Draft status and their source form](/images/kb/sessions-drafts-tab.png)

**Per form.** On **Program → Forms**, each card counts the submissions and drafts that came through it. Open the form's **⋯** menu and choose **View Drafts** to see the Drafts tab filtered to that form.

![The form card menu with Edit, View Submissions, View Drafts, View Form, Copy Link, Duplicate and Delete](/images/kb/forms-actions-menu.png)

**The Dashboard.** The **Submissions** card on the event Dashboard shows a **Drafts** count with a **View draft session submissions** link that opens the same tab.

## Open a draft

Click the pencil on a draft row to open it in the session editor. You see exactly what the submitter has entered so far — description, participants, files — and can add notes or read it ahead of the deadline. The record stays a draft until the submitter returns and clicks **Submit**; at that point it moves to the status your form assigns new submissions and appears in **All Submissions**.

<Note>
Drafts count toward a form's **Set Submission Limit** when that limit is on, so a submitter with a forgotten draft may be told they have reached the limit. They can finish the draft, or delete it from the form's account step, to free the slot.
</Note>

## Form settings that affect drafts

Open the form (**Program → Forms → ⋯ → Edit**) and go to **Form Settings**.

![The Form Settings step of the form editor showing Deadlines, Submission capacity and the Allow multiple draft submissions switch](/images/kb/form-builder-form-settings.png)

- **Allow multiple draft submissions** — off by default, so each submitter has one in-progress draft per form and the account step offers **Resume draft**. Switch it on and submitters can keep several drafts going; the account step then shows **Start new submission** plus a list of their drafts. The **Effective behavior** box under the switches spells out how the limit and draft settings combine.
- **Close Date** — once set, the form (and the drafts on it) stop accepting changes after that moment, and it unlocks the reminder switch below.

## Send reminder emails

Under **Deadlines**, set a **Close Date** and turn on **Send Reminder Email**. Sessionboard then emails everyone who has a saved draft on the form but has not submitted, **five days** and again **one day** before the close date.

- The two emails use the **Session Form - Five Days Reminder** and **Session Form - One Day Reminder** templates. Edit their wording under **Settings → Email Templates** — see [Email templates](/settings/email-templates). They cannot be deleted.
- Reminders are unavailable while the form has conditional deadlines under **Advanced**, because a single reminder cannot state the right date for every submitter. The switch tells you when that is the case.
- Sent reminders are listed with the rest of the event's mail under **Communications → History** — see [History](/communications/email-sms-history).

<Tip>
Reminders only reach people who saved a draft. To nudge everyone on your list before a call closes, send a campaign from **Communications** instead — see [Create and send emails](/communications/create-send-emails).
</Tip>
