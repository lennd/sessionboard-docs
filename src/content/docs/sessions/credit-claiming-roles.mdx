[Credit Claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming) has its own permissions, so you can give someone the claims and nothing else. Two ready-made roles cover the usual cases: **Credit Claiming Manager** runs credit claiming for the event, and **Credit Claiming Viewer** can look but not change anything.

## Why use it

Accreditation often involves people outside your event team: a CE coordinator who handles claims, or a partner association that needs the audit and the report for its accrediting body. Before these roles, they needed broad access to the event to see a single report. Now they sign in to a Credit Claiming–only view and see exactly what they need.

## The Credit Claiming permissions

In **Event Team → Permissions**, every role has a **Credit Claiming** group with two permissions. The group appears on events that run Credit Claiming.

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Can view claims, attendance, audit and reports** | Open the Credit Claiming page and every tab, the claiming settings and credit types (read-only), and run exports. |
| **Can manage credit claiming (claims, credits per session, settings, credit types)** | Grant and reverse claims, import attendance and credits, change credits per session, save the claiming settings, and edit credit types. |

![Event Team permissions for the Credit Claiming Viewer role, with Can view claims, attendance, audit and reports on and Can manage credit claiming off](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-claiming-roles.png)

Default roles start with:

- **Admin User** and **Admin Lite**: both permissions.
- **Reporting Only Admin**: view only.
- Every other role, including your custom roles: neither, until you turn them on.

## Credit Claiming Manager and Credit Claiming Viewer

Both are default roles in **Event Team → Permissions** on events that run Credit Claiming. They can't be edited; duplicate one to make a variation.

| Role | Holds | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Credit Claiming Manager** | Both Credit Claiming permissions, nothing else in the event | A CE coordinator who sets up credits, imports attendance and fixes claims |
| **Credit Claiming Viewer** | View only, nothing else in the event | An accrediting partner or association that needs claims, attendance, the audit, reports and exports |

Invite the person from **Event Team** and give them one of these roles, as in [Invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members).

Your organization has the same pair under **Organization Settings → Team → Permissions** when it runs Credit Claiming. The organization **Credit Claiming Manager** holds the organization's Credits permissions (view, create, update, delete), and the organization **Credit Claiming Viewer** holds view only. Use them for someone who works across every event's credit.

## What they see

A team member whose only permissions are Credit Claiming ones gets a short sidebar: **Program** holds **Credit Claiming**, **Claiming settings** and **Credit types**, and opening the event lands them on the **Credit Claiming** page.

![The sidebar for a Credit Claiming Viewer, with Program holding Credit Claiming, Claiming settings and Credit types, next to the Credit Claiming page](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-claiming-viewer-nav.png)

A **Credit Claiming Viewer** sees every tab, filter and export, and the claiming settings and credit types read-only. **Grant credit**, **Reverse claim**, the attendance and credit imports, **Apply to sessions** and the save buttons are not there, and the **Claiming** and **Credit types** settings open with the note *View only — your role can see Credit Claiming but not change it*.

<Note>
The activity log isn't available to someone whose Credits access is limited to some [portfolios](/events/portfolios), because it covers every attendee of the event. For them, the **Activity** tab says *Could not load activity*.
</Note>

<Tip>
To hand a partner a live report without giving them a sign-in at all, share the Credit Claiming dashboard instead: **Insights → Share live report** on the Credit Claiming page. See [Insights](/sessions/credit-claiming-review#insights-reports-and-dashboards).
</Tip>
