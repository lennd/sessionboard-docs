A **credit type** is a credential attendees can claim in [Credit Claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming), such as AMA PRA Category 1 Credit, ANCC contact hours or a state board's CE hours. Each type sets its unit, the certificate it issues, and the questions an attendee answers before the claim is saved. Credit types are shared across your organization, so every event uses the same list and the same questions.

Open **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types**, or click **Credit types** at the top of the **Credit Claiming** page.

![The Credit types table with Unit, Accreditor, Questions, Sessions, Certificate and Responses columns and the New credit type button](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-types.png)

## Why use it

Accrediting bodies each ask for something different: one wants an attestation, another wants to know how the attendee will apply what they learned, a third wants its own wording and signature on the certificate. Credit types put those rules on the credential itself. An attendee who claims ANCC hours answers the nursing questions and gets a certificate with your nursing accreditation statement; an attendee who claims only a certificate of attendance answers nothing extra.

## The credit types table

Each row is one credit type:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Credit type** | The name attendees see on the claim site, certificates and transcript. |
| **Unit** | Hours, CEUs, points or credits. |
| **Accreditor** | The accrediting body or jurisdiction, for example ANCC or a state board. |
| **Questions** | How many questions the type asks and whether they are asked on **each claim** or **once**. **Add** when it asks none. |
| **Sessions** | How many sessions offer this type. |
| **Certificate** | **Organization default**, or **Custom template** when the type issues its own. |
| **Responses** | How many attendees have answered the type's questions at this event. Click the number to read the answers. |

Search by name or accreditor. Archived types are hidden until you click **Show archived**.

Common types such as AMA PRA Category 1 Credit, ANCC Contact Hours and ACPE Credit are already there. To award something that isn't listed, click **New credit type**, enter a **Name** and, optionally, the **Accreditor / jurisdiction**, and click **Create**.

Click any row to open its editor. It has four tabs: **Details**, **Certificate**, **Questions** and **Subtypes**.

## Details

![The credit type editor on the Details tab with Name, Unit, Increment and Accreditor / jurisdiction](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-type-details.png)

- **Name**: what attendees see.
- **Unit**: hours, CEUs, points or credits. A standard type's unit is set by its accreditor and can't be changed.
- **Increment**: amounts on sessions must be a multiple of it, for example `0.25`. Claims already made keep their amounts if you change it.
- **Accreditor / jurisdiction**: for example ANCC or a state board.

Click **Save details**.

## Certificate

Every claimed credit issues its own certificate. The **Certificate** tab chooses what it looks like.

![The Certificate tab with the certificate template picker, Signatory name, Signatory title and Accreditation statement](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-type-certificate.png)

- **Certificate template**: **Organization default**, or any certificate template your organization has built under **Program → Collect & Review → Print**. Use a CE certificate for credit and a certificate of attendance for **General Attendance**.
- **Signatory name** and **Signatory title**: printed under a signature line, for example *Director of Education*.
- **Accreditation statement**: the wording your accreditor requires, printed at the foot of every certificate of this type.

Click **Save wording**. Certificates are drawn from the current settings each time they are opened, so attendees who already claimed see the new wording too.

The built-in certificate shows the event's logo, name and dates in its brand colour, the attendee's name, the amount and credit type, the session title, code and date, the certificate number and issue date, and the signatory and statement when you set them.

![A certificate for 0.5 hours of CNE Contact Hours with the certificate number, issue date, session code, signatory and accreditation statement](/images/kb/credit-claiming/certificate.png)

<Tip>
**General Attendance** is how you offer a plain certificate of attendance: a credit type with an amount of `0` on every session. Pick a template for it that doesn't print an amount, since a CE certificate would read "0 hours".
</Tip>

## Questions

Questions are what an accreditor asks the attendee about the credit, such as whether the activity met its objectives, or an attestation that the hours claimed are accurate. The claim site asks them before it saves the claim.

![The Questions tab for CME Credits set to ask on every claim, with three required questions with their answer types, and Add question, Copy to and Copy from at the bottom](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-type-questions.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose when to ask">
    Under **Ask**, choose **Every claim** (attendees answer for every session they claim this credit on) or **Once per attendee** (attendees answer once per event, and later claims of this credit reuse their answers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    Click **Add question** and pick the answer type:

    ![The Add question menu with the answer types Yes / No, Short answer, Paragraph, One choice, Several choices, Rating 1–5 and Acknowledgement](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-type-question-kinds.png)

    | Answer type | What the attendee does |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Yes / No** | Picks Yes or No. |
    | **Short answer** | Types a line of text. |
    | **Paragraph** | Types a longer answer. |
    | **One choice** | Picks one of the choices you add. |
    | **Several choices** | Picks any of the choices you add. |
    | **Rating 1–5** | Picks 1 (low) to 5 (high). |
    | **Acknowledgement** | Ticks a statement, for example *I attest that the credit I am claiming is accurate.* |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and arrange them">
    Type the question, add **Help text** if attendees need guidance, and switch **Required** on or off. Use the arrows (or Alt+↑ / Alt+↓) to reorder, the copy icon to duplicate a question, and the bin to delete it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save questions**. If you close the editor with unsaved changes, it asks before discarding them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To reuse questions, click **Copy to…** and pick the other credit types, then choose **Add to theirs** or **Replace theirs**. **Copy from…** pulls another type's questions into this one. Save your changes first; copying works from saved questions.

Deleting a question that attendees have already answered keeps their answers: it is archived, and its answers stay in **Responses** and in exports.

<Note>
Credit type questions are not Surveys. They belong to the credential, they are answered at claim time, and their answers stay with the claim. Session feedback is a separate [survey](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#session-feedback-survey) linked in the claiming settings, and its results are in **Surveys**.
</Note>

### What attendees see

When an attendee submits a claim for a credit with unanswered questions, the claim site opens **A few questions for your credit**. It lists the questions of every credit type in the claim, says which credit type requires them, and won't save the claim until every required question is answered. For a type asked **Once per attendee**, it tells the attendee they answer those questions once for the event.

![The claim site dialog A few questions for your credit, required by CME Credits, with two Yes / No questions and a paragraph question and Submit and claim](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-questions.png)

### Read the answers

Click the number in the **Responses** column, or the **response** link at the top of the editor, to open **Responses**. It lists every answer attendees gave at this event, with the attendee, the question, the session and the answer. Pick one question to narrow the list. To download the answers, use **Export → Question answers** on the **Audit** tab of the Credit Claiming page ([exports](/sessions/credit-claiming-review#export)).

![Responses for CME Credits listing each attendee, question, session and answer](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-type-responses.png)

## Subtypes

Subtypes split one credit type on sessions and transcripts, for example *ethics* or *live* hours. On the **Subtypes** tab, type a name under **New subtype** and click **Add**. Subtypes then appear next to the credit type on [Credits per session](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#credits-per-session).

## Archive a credit type

Click **Archive** at the bottom of the editor. Archived types are hidden from the session credit picker, and existing claims keep them. Turn on **Show archived** in the table and click **Restore** to bring one back.

<Warning>
Credit types are shared by every event in your organization. Changing a type's questions, certificate or wording changes it for every event that offers it.
</Warning>
