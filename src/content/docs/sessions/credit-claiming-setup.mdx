Setting up [Credit Claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming) takes three pieces: the **credit types** you award, the **credits on each session**, and the **claim site** itself. Each piece has its own place under **Program**, and you can do them in any order. Attendees see nothing until you turn on **Enable credit claiming**.

## Why use it

Your accreditation rules end up in the product rather than in a PDF of instructions. The claim window, the time-slot rule and the required evaluation are enforced on every claim, so the transcript you export holds up without manual clean-up.

## Claiming settings

Open **Program → Configure → Settings** and click **Claiming** in the **Credits** group at the bottom of the settings list. **Credit types** sits under it.

![Program Settings list with the Claiming and Credit types items below Statuses](/images/kb/credit-claiming/program-settings-credits-nav.png)

![The Claiming settings: Enable credit claiming, Claim site link, Claiming opens and closes, Who can claim, One session per time slot, Session list, Session evaluation and Instructions for attendees](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-settings.png)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable credit claiming** | Turns the claim site on. Attendees sign in with their badge number and last name, or their email and last name. |
| **Claim site link** | The public address of the claim site. Copy it with the copy button or open it in a new tab to check what attendees see. It appears once the site exists. |
| **Claiming opens** | When attendees can start claiming, in the event's timezone (named under the field). Leave it blank to open immediately. |
| **Claiming closes** | When claiming stops. Transcripts and certificates stay available after the close, so attendees can download them later. |
| **Who can claim** | **Registered attendees** (registration status confirmed or checked in), **Checked-in attendees only**, or **Anyone who can sign in (no registration check)**. |
| **One session per time slot** | Blocks a claim on a session that overlaps a session the attendee already claimed. On by default. |
| **Session list** | Which sessions each attendee sees: **All sessions**, **Scanned sessions first**, or **Scanned sessions only**. See below. |
| **Session evaluation** | The evaluation attendees answer before each claim. See [The evaluation](#the-evaluation). |
| **Instructions for attendees** | Text shown at the top of the claim site after sign-in, such as "Claim only the sessions you attended in person." |

Click **Save** when you are done.

![Who can claim options: Registered attendees, Checked-in attendees only, and Anyone who can sign in](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-who-can-claim.png)

### Session list

![The Session list options: All sessions, Scanned sessions first, Scanned sessions only](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-session-list.png)

**Session list** uses attendance (door scans, check-ins and imported attendance) to decide what each attendee sees:

- **All sessions** lists every session that offers credit, in time order. Attendees choose what they attended.
- **Scanned sessions first** lists the sessions the attendee has attendance for at the top of each day, marked **Attended**, followed by the rest.
- **Scanned sessions only** lists only sessions the attendee has attendance for. A claim on any other session is refused, so use this when your scan coverage is complete.

Attendance the attendee reported themselves doesn't count for any of these. [Record attendance for credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-attendance) covers the sources that do.

### The evaluation

When no evaluation is linked, claims use a basic built-in form with three agree/disagree questions and a comment box. Click **Create PACE evaluation** to create a PACE-style session evaluation survey for the event and link it in one step. The card then says **Attendees complete this evaluation before each claim** and shows **Open Surveys**, where you edit the questions and read the results.

The evaluation is required only on sessions whose credit has the **Eval** claim gate (see [Credits per session](#credits-per-session)). Each response is saved against the session in **Surveys**, and the transcript records when the attendee completed it.

## Credit types

Open **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types**. Credit types are shared across your organization, so every event uses the same list.

Common types are already there, such as **AMA PRA Category 1 Credit**, **ANCC Contact Hours**, **ACPE Credit** and **ABIM MOC Points**, each with its unit (credits, hours, points) and accrediting body. To award something that isn't listed, such as a certification body's CE hours or a state board, add a custom type. Add subtypes (for example *ethics* or *live*) when one credential has more than one kind.

![Two custom credit types, CPHIMS / CAHIMS CE Hours and General Attendance, each with a Certificate picker](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-types.png)

### Certificates

Every claimed credit issues its own certificate. The **Certificate** picker on each type chooses its template:

- **Org default template** issues your organization's default certificate. Unless your CSM has set a different default for you, that is the built-in **CE / Credit Certificate**, which shows the attendee's name, the credit amount and type, the session title, the claim date, and the event's name and dates.
- Any certificate template your organization has built under **Program → Collect & Review → Print** can be chosen instead. For example, use a CE certificate for your credit types and a certificate of attendance for **General Attendance**.

<Tip>
**General Attendance** is how you offer a plain certificate of attendance: a credit type with an amount of `0` on every session. Pick a certificate template for it that doesn't print an amount, since the CE certificate would read "0 hours".
</Tip>

## Credits per session

Open **Program → After the event → Credit Claiming** and click **Credits per session**. Each credit you attach to a session is a *designation*: one credit type, an optional subtype, an amount, and the claim gates.

![Credits per session: AMA PRA Category 1 Credit, amount 1.5, Eval gate ticked, three sessions selected and Apply to 3 sessions](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credits-per-session.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the credit">
    Pick the **Credit type**, a **Subtype** if it has one, and the **Amount**. The amount uses the type's unit and must be a multiple of its increment (0.25 by default). `0` is allowed, for **General Attendance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the claim gates">
    Tick **Eval** to require the session evaluation before the claim. Tick **Attendance** to refuse a claim unless the attendee has attendance recorded for that session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick sessions and apply">
    Search or **Select all**, tick the sessions, and click **Apply to N sessions**. A session that already has the same credit type and subtype is skipped, so you can apply again safely.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeat for each credential. A session can offer several, for example 1.5 AMA PRA Category 1 credits, 1.5 ANCC contact hours and General Attendance.

### Import credits from a CSV

Click **Import CSV** to add many designations at once. Use four columns in this order:

```csv
session_id,credit_type,amount,subtype
3f1c…,AMA PRA Category 1 Credit,1.5,
3f1c…,ancc_contact_hours,1.5,
Restoring Dunes as Nature-Based Solutions for California Shorelines,General Attendance,0,
```

- `session_id` is the session's id or its exact title.
- `credit_type` and `subtype` match by name, key or id, without regard to case.
- A header row is ignored. The **Eval** and **Attendance** boxes on the page apply to every imported row.
- Rows that already exist are skipped, so re-importing is safe. Rows that can't be matched are listed with the reason, and the valid rows are still saved.

## Share the claim site

Once **Enable credit claiming** is on and saved, copy the **Claim site link** and put it in your post-event email, on your event site, or in your event app. Link attendees to [Claim CE credits and download your transcript](/participants/claim-ce-credits) if they need help.

<Note>
If your event already sends attendees a self-report attendance link, that link sends them to the claim site once claiming is on.
</Note>
