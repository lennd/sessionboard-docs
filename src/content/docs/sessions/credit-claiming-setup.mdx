Setting up [Credit Claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming) takes three pieces: the **credit types** you award, the **credits on each session**, and the **claim site** itself. Each piece has its own place under **Program**, and you can do them in any order. Attendees can't claim until you switch claiming on.

## Why use it

Your accreditation rules end up in the product rather than in a PDF of instructions. The claim window, the time-slot rule, the required survey and each credit type's questions are enforced on every claim, so the transcript you export holds up without manual clean-up.

## Claiming settings

Open **Program → Configure → Settings** and click **Claiming** in the **Credits** group at the bottom of the settings list, or click **Claiming settings** at the top of the **Credit Claiming** page. **Credit types** sits under it.

![Program Settings list with the Claiming and Credit types items below Statuses](/images/kb/credit-claiming/program-settings-credits-nav.png)

The settings are grouped in cards. Change what you need and click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom; it says **All changes saved** when there is nothing left to save.

### Access

![The Access card with Claiming on, the Claim link with Copy link and Open claim site, and Who can claim; below it the Claim window card with Claiming opens and closes and their presets](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-settings.png)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Claiming on / off** | Turns claiming on. Attendees sign in with their badge number and last name, or their email and last name. While it is off, attendees can open the link but can't claim. |
| **Claim link** | The public address of the claim site. **Copy link** copies it; **Open claim site** opens it in a new tab so you can see what attendees see. |
| **Who can claim** | **Registered attendees** (registration status confirmed or checked in), **Checked-in attendees only**, or **Anyone who can sign in (no registration check)**. |

### Claim window

Times are in the event's time zone, which the card names.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Claiming opens** | When attendees can start claiming. Leave it blank to open as soon as claiming is on, or click **Sunday before the event**. |
| **Claiming closes** | When claiming stops. **Until 1 year after** sets it a year after the event; **No end** leaves it open. Transcripts and certificates stay available after it closes. |

### Session list

![The Session list card with the All sessions, Scanned sessions first and Scanned sessions only options](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-session-list.png)

**Sessions to list** uses attendance (door scans, check-ins and imported attendance) to decide what each attendee sees:

- **All sessions** lists every session that offers credit, in time order. Attendees choose what they attended.
- **Scanned sessions first** lists the sessions the attendee has attendance for at the top of each day, marked **Attended**, followed by the rest.
- **Scanned sessions only** lists only sessions the attendee has attendance for. A claim on any other session is refused, so use this when your scan coverage is complete.

Attendance the attendee reported themselves doesn't count for any of these. [Record attendance for credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-attendance) covers the sources that do.

### Rules and attendee instructions

![The Rules card with One session per time slot and the Session feedback survey, and the Attendee instructions card](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-rules-survey.png)

- **One session per time slot** blocks a claim on a session that overlaps a session the attendee already claimed. On by default.
- **Attendee instructions** is text shown at the top of the claim site after sign-in, such as "Claim only the sessions you attended in person." Up to 4,000 characters.

### Session feedback survey

The **Session feedback survey** in the **Rules** card is the survey attendees complete before each claim. When none is linked, claims use a basic built-in form with three agree/disagree questions and a comment box. Click **Create PACE survey** to create a PACE-style session feedback survey for the event and link it in one step. The card then says **Attendees complete this survey before each claim** and shows **Open Surveys**, where you edit its questions and read the results.

The survey is required only on sessions whose credit has the **Survey** claim requirement (see [Credits per session](#credits-per-session)). Each response is saved against the session in **Surveys**, and the transcript records when the attendee completed it.

The survey is about the session. Questions about the credential itself, such as an attestation an accreditor requires, belong on the credit type: see [Questions](/sessions/credit-claiming-credit-types#questions).

## Credit types

Credit types are the credentials attendees claim. They are shared across your organization, and each one sets its unit, the certificate it issues (template, signatory and accreditation statement), and the questions attendees answer before claiming it. Open **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types**. The full guide is [Set up credit types, questions and certificates](/sessions/credit-claiming-credit-types).

![The Credit types table with Unit, Accreditor, Questions, Sessions, Certificate and Responses columns and the New credit type button](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credit-types.png)

## Credits per session

Open **Program → After the event → Credit Claiming** and click **Credits per session**. Each credit you attach to a session is a *designation*: one credit type, an optional subtype, an amount, and its claim requirements.

![Credits per session with CME Credits, amount 0.75, the Survey claim requirement ticked, two sessions selected and Apply to 2 sessions](/images/kb/credit-claiming/credits-per-session.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the credit">
    Pick the **Credit type**, a **Subtype** if it has one, and the **Amount**. The amount uses the type's unit and must be a multiple of its increment (0.25 by default). `0` is allowed, for **General Attendance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the claim requirements">
    Under **Claim requirements**, tick **Survey** to require the session feedback survey before the claim. Tick **Attendance** to refuse a claim unless the attendee has attendance recorded for that session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick sessions and apply">
    Search or **Select all**, tick the sessions, and click **Apply to N sessions**. A session that already has the same credit type and subtype is skipped, so you can apply again safely.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Export** downloads every session's credits as CSV or Excel; see [Export](/sessions/credit-claiming-review#export).

Repeat for each credential. A session can offer several, for example 1.5 AMA PRA Category 1 credits, 1.5 ANCC contact hours and General Attendance.

### Import credits from a CSV

Click **Import CSV** to add many designations at once. Use four columns in this order:

```csv
session_id,credit_type,amount,subtype
3f1c…,AMA PRA Category 1 Credit,1.5,
3f1c…,ancc_contact_hours,1.5,
Restoring Dunes as Nature-Based Solutions for California Shorelines,General Attendance,0,
```

- `session_id` is the session's id or its exact title.
- `credit_type` and `subtype` match by name, key or id, without regard to case.
- A header row is ignored. The **Survey** and **Attendance** claim requirements ticked on the page apply to every imported row, and the result line repeats which ones they took.
- Rows that already exist are skipped, so re-importing is safe. A row whose session, credit type or subtype can't be matched, or that has no amount, is counted in the errors total and listed with its line and the reason (for example *Line 3: unknown session "No Such Session Title"*). The valid rows are still saved.

## Share the claim site

Once claiming is on and saved, copy the claim link (**Copy link** in **Access**, or **Copy claim link** at the top of the **Credit Claiming** page) and put it in your post-event email, on your event site, or in your event app. The claim site carries your event's logo, name, dates and brand colour. Link attendees to [Claim CE credits and download your transcript](/participants/claim-ce-credits) if they need help.

<Note>
If your event already sends attendees a self-report attendance link, that link sends them to the claim site once claiming is on.
</Note>
