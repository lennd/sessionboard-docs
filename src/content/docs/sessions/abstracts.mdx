An **abstract** is a short proposal — a paper, a poster, a case study — that you review before the program takes shape. A **session** is a full proposal with program details: format, length, the slot it needs. Both are records in your program and share the same editor, statuses, evaluation plans, and emails. The difference is what the submission form asks for and where the record lands afterwards, and it is what lets a scientific or research program accept two hundred abstracts first and decide how to group them into sessions later.

## Abstracts and sessions

| | Abstract | Session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Collected by** | A submission form whose **Submission Setup** is set to **Abstracts** | A submission form set to **Sessions**, or **Add Session** by hand |
| **Listed under** | **Program → Abstracts** (and **Program → View All**) | **Program → Sessions** (and **Program → View All**) |
| **Typical content** | Title, abstract text, track, authors | Title, description, format, track, level, speakers, times |
| **After acceptance** | Scheduled on the agenda as is, or grouped into a session as subsessions | Scheduled on the agenda |

Every record opens in the same side panel. An abstract is labelled **Abstract** next to its ID in the panel header, so you always know which kind you are looking at.

## Collect abstracts with a submission form

What a form collects is decided on its first step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Program → Forms**, open the three dots on the form, and click **Edit**. Each event starts with one form, and you can run several — a call for abstracts and a call for sessions, say — each with its own questions, deadline, and notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submission Setup">
    Under **What kind of submissions do you want to collect?**, choose **Abstracts** ("Collect abstract submissions for review before sessions are finalized.") or **Sessions** ("Collect full session proposals with details for your program."). Everything submitted through a form set to **Abstracts** is an abstract.

    ![The Submission Setup step with the Abstracts and Sessions cards and the Participants switch](/images/kb/form-builder-setup.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Participants">
    Leave the **Participants** switch on to include a step that collects author and presenter contact details. Turn it off when you already know the submitters and only want the abstract itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Questions">
    The next step is named **Abstract Information** on an abstracts form. Add the fields a reviewer needs — the abstract text with a word limit, the track, the presentation preference — and leave headshots, full bios, and disclosures for [portal tasks](/portals/portals-101) after acceptance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rest of the builder — welcome screen, participant fields, fees, deadlines, notifications — works the same as any [submission form](/sessions/submission-forms#build-the-form-step-by-step). You can change the choice later by editing the form.

<Tip>
Not sure whether you want abstracts or sessions? If the committee decides *what gets in* before anyone decides *where it goes*, collect abstracts. If every proposal already describes a slot on the agenda, collect sessions. [Which form should I use?](/concepts/which-form-should-i-use#submission-forms) compares submission forms with application, portal, and interest forms.
</Tip>

## The Abstracts list

**Program → Abstracts** lists every abstract in the event, with **Add Abstract** to key one in by hand — for an invited paper, or one that arrived by email. The tabs across the top are the [five status categories](/sessions/accept-decline#the-five-status-categories) with a count on each: **All Abstracts**, **Accepted**, **Accept Queue**, **Pending**, **Decline Queue**, **Declined**, plus **Withdrawn** and **Drafts**.

The list is a table view like the Sessions list. The **Source** column shows which form each abstract came through, and you can add any question from the form as a column, then sort, filter, and save the view — see [dashboard views](/reporting/dashboard-views). The pencil on a row opens the abstract in the side panel, with the same **Details**, **Participants**, **Files**, **Reviews**, and **Connections** tabs a session has.

**Program → View All** shows abstracts and sessions together in one list; **Program → Sessions** shows sessions only.

## Review abstracts

Reviewing an abstract is reviewing a submission:

- **Status.** Click the status on a row to move it, or select several and use **Edit** in the action bar to change **Status** in bulk. Queues let the committee sort as it goes; submitters see **Accept Queue** and **Decline Queue** as Pending until you move the abstract on. See [accept or decline submissions](/sessions/accept-decline#change-a-sessions-status).
- **Scores.** An [evaluation plan](/evaluations/evaluation-plans) can score the abstracts from a form against your criteria, and the results land on each abstract's **Reviews** tab.
- **Email.** Select abstracts and click **Send Emails** to write to their authors from the list, using the built-in **Accept** and **Decline** templates when decisions are final.

## Create subsessions from abstracts

Once abstracts are accepted, the usual next step is to group them: five posters in one poster session, four papers in one symposium. **Create a subsession** does this in one step, keeping each abstract intact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the abstracts">
    In **Program → Abstracts**, tick the abstracts that belong together. The action bar shows how many are selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a subsession">
    Click **More** in the action bar. Under **Abstracts**, choose **Create a subsession** — "Each abstract becomes its own subsession under one parent session". From an open abstract, the same action is under **Actions** in the panel header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the parent">
    Under **Parent session**, either **Create a new parent session** and type its title, or **Add to an existing session** and search for the parent. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The preview lists the subsessions that will be created and shows **Participants shown on the parent session**, which starts from the event's [agenda setting](/sessions/program-settings#agenda) — all participants, the presenting author only, or none. Click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each abstract becomes its own subsession under the parent, taking its title, description, track, format, level, and language. The abstract itself is not consumed: it stays in the Abstracts list with a **Linked** badge, remains editable, and its **Connections** tab points at the subsession it created. A linked abstract cannot be deleted, withdrawn, or duplicated while the link exists — unlink it from **Connections** first. The full rules, including subsession limits and reordering, are in [create a subsession](/sessions/create-a-subsession#create-subsessions-from-abstracts).

To combine several abstracts into a single abstract instead — two groups who submitted the same study, say — choose **Merge into one abstract** in the same menu. The sources stay linked to the merged record and remain editable.

## Where abstracts show

- **Agenda.** [Program settings → Agenda](/sessions/program-settings#agenda) has an **Agenda content** setting: **Sessions (non-abstract program items)**, **Abstracts**, or **Both sessions and abstracts**. Accepted abstracts are scheduled like any other program item when the setting includes them. Subsessions created from abstracts appear under their parent session.
- **Portals.** The portal's sessions page shows a submitter's abstracts on their own **Abstracts** tab, separate from **Sessions**, with the status of each. [Speaker acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) applies to sessions, not to abstracts. A presenter on a subsession sees the parent session and its other subsessions — see [viewing subsessions within the agenda](/sessions/viewing-subsessions-within-the-agenda).
- **Exports.** **Options** at the top of the Abstracts list has **Export .CSV**, **Export .XLSX**, and **Download files bundle...** for the abstracts in the current view, plus **Import Sessions** and **Copy from another event…** to bring abstracts in — see [copy sessions from another event](/sessions/copy-sessions-from-another-event).
