Participant inventory lets you define how many participants each session should carry — a minimum, a maximum, or both — and see at a glance across the Sessions grid which sessions still need speakers. Instead of opening every session to count who is assigned, you read one **Assignment Status** column and prioritize outreach from there.

<Note>
Participant inventory is separate from the **Capacity** field. Capacity is attendee/room headcount and syncs to integrations; the minimum and maximum here are about the people presenting the session.
</Note>

## Turn on participant inventory

The feature is off by default. Enable it once per event:

1. Go to **Program > Program Settings > Agenda**.
2. Find the **Participant Inventory** panel and turn on **Enable participant inventory**.

![The Participant Inventory panel in Program Settings with the Enable participant inventory toggle on](/images/kb/participant-inventory-toggle.png)

Turning it on adds **Min Participants** and **Max Participants** to every session profile and makes two columns — **Assigned Participants** and **Assignment Status** — available on the Sessions grid. Turning it off hides all of it again; saved values are kept.

## Set a minimum and maximum on a session

Open a session and find **Min Participants** and **Max Participants** in the details sidebar, just below **Capacity**.

![The Min Participants and Max Participants fields below Capacity in the session details sidebar](/images/kb/session-min-max-participants.png)

- Both fields are optional. Set only a minimum, only a maximum, or both.
- The minimum cannot exceed the maximum — the save is rejected with an error if it does.
- Clearing a field (or entering 0) removes that bound.
- Duplicating a session copies its minimum and maximum.

## Read assignment status on the Sessions grid

Add the **Assigned Participants** and **Assignment Status** columns from the grid's **Columns** picker. New grid views include them automatically once the feature is on; existing saved views need them added once.

![A session row showing 3 of 2–4 assigned participants and a Meets Minimum status](/images/kb/sessions-grid-assignment-status.png)

**Assigned Participants** shows the current count against the rule — for example `3 of 2–4` with both bounds, `3 of 2+` with only a minimum, or `3 of up to 4` with only a maximum.

**Assignment Status** derives one of three states:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs Participants** | The assigned count is below the session's minimum. |
| **Meets Minimum** | The count is at or above the minimum and below the maximum. |
| **Full** | The count has reached the maximum. |

A session with no minimum or maximum shows a neutral dash — it is never flagged as needing participants.

<Tip>
Filter the grid by **Assignment Status** to isolate every session that still needs speakers, then work the list.
</Tip>

## Who counts as assigned

- Every participant on the session counts, in any role — speakers, moderators, chairpersons, and custom roles.
- A person is counted once, even if they hold two roles on the same session.
- Participants whose status is **Declined** are excluded. Pending, Accepted, and custom statuses all count.

<Tip>
To keep a backup speaker on the session without counting them toward the minimum, give them a [custom participant status](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) mapped to the Declined category.
</Tip>
