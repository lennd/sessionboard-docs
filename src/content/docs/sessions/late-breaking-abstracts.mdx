A late-breaking abstract (LBA) reports results that aren't ready by the main abstract deadline. Authors submit it with the rest of the call, often without **Results** and **Conclusions**, and finish it months later. In Sessionboard you run the whole flow on one submission form: a status your team sets on each abstract decides which deadline applies to it, so you can reopen editing for confirmed LBAs without reopening the form for anyone else.

![Five-stage timeline where regular abstracts lock at the main deadline, converted ones get a completion week, and confirmed LBAs can edit again in July](/images/kb/late-breaking-abstracts-flow.png)

## Why use it

Scientific and medical congresses accept late-breaking abstracts so the newest trial data still makes the program. The hard part comes later: confirmed LBA authors need to get back in to add their results, and every other author must stay locked out. One form with a deadline per status keeps that to a single call, one Abstracts list, one set of review plans and one portal, instead of a second form you have to plan before launch and reconcile afterwards.

## How it works

A submission form has a main **Close Date**. Under **Form Settings → Deadlines → Advanced**, conditional deadlines give a different close date to submissions whose answers match a rule. Each rule names a session question on the form and a value, the first matching rule wins, and a submission that matches no rule uses the main close date.

The question that drives the rules is a **Late-breaking status** dropdown that only your team sets. It sits on the form with the padlock on, so submitters can see it but not change it. After the fast LBA review, your team sets it on each abstract:

| Late-breaking status | Deadline | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *(empty)* | Main **Close Date** | Every regular abstract, and every LBA until it's reviewed |
| `Converted to regular` | About a week after the LBA review | LBAs that didn't qualify and need to add Results and Conclusions |
| `Confirmed LBA` | Your late deadline, for example late July | LBAs that stay late-breaking |

<Tip>
Key the deadlines on the status your team sets, not on the submitter's own **Abstract type** answer. Submitters can change their own answers, so a rule on the type would let anyone switch to the late-breaking type and get the later deadline.
</Tip>

## Set up the form

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the abstract types">
Give the form a dropdown for the abstract type with your late-breaking type as one option, for example `Late-breaking abstract`. Use [question rules](/sessions/submission-forms#use-question-rules) to show the questions each type needs. For late-breaking submissions, keep **Results** and **Conclusions** optional or hide them, so authors can submit without them.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the Late-breaking status question">
Create a dropdown field called `Late-breaking status` with the options `Confirmed LBA` and `Converted to regular`, and add it to the session questions on the form. Click the padlock on its row so it's view-only for submitters, and leave **Required** off. Add a help text such as "Set by the program team after review."
</Step>
<Step title="Add the conditional deadlines">
Open **Form Settings → Deadlines**, set the main **Close Date**, then open **Advanced** and add two rules: `Late-breaking status` is `Converted to regular` with a close date about a week after your LBA review, and `Late-breaking status` is `Confirmed LBA` with your late deadline.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the status as a column">
On the **Abstracts** list, add `Late-breaking status` and the abstract type as columns and save the view. Bulk edit only offers fields that are showing as columns.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Run the review

<Steps>
<Step title="Review LBAs fast">
Create a [review plan](/evaluations/evaluation-plans) for the late-breaking committee and add submissions **By submission filters**, filtered to the late-breaking type. Give reviewers the short window your program allows.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the status in bulk">
When the fast review is done, filter the **Abstracts** list to the late-breaking type, select the ones that qualify, click **Edit**, choose `Late-breaking status` and set it to `Confirmed LBA`. Select the rest and set `Converted to regular`, then change their abstract type to the regular type it becomes. See [Bulk edit fields](/settings/bulk-edit-fields).
</Step>
<Step title="Tell the converted authors">
Select the converted abstracts and use **Send Emails** to tell their authors they have until the converted deadline to add Results and Conclusions. Their portal opens their abstract for editing until that date.
</Step>
<Step title="Include confirmed LBAs in the regular review">
Add the confirmed LBAs to your regular review plan with everything else. They keep their status through the review.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Reopen editing for confirmed LBAs

Nothing changes on the form when the late window opens: confirmed LBAs have had their own deadline since your team set their status. Before it opens, filter the **Abstracts** list to `Confirmed LBA` and use **Send Emails** to tell those authors they can sign in to their portal, open their abstract and add their Results and Conclusions. They can change anything else on the abstract too, and they resubmit when they're done.

While any deadline on the form is still open, the form stays reachable. At the final submit, each submission is checked against the deadline for its own status:

- Confirmed LBA authors can edit and resubmit until the late deadline.
- Authors of regular and converted abstracts whose deadline has passed can't resubmit. The form shows the date their deadline passed, and their abstract stays as it was.
- New submissions can't come in, because a new submitter can't set `Late-breaking status` and the main close date has passed.

Your team can edit any abstract from the **Abstracts** list at any time, whatever its deadline, which covers one-off exceptions.

<Note>
**Send Reminder Email** is unavailable while a form has conditional deadlines, since there's no single close date to count back from. Use **Send Emails** on a filtered list of the abstracts you want to nudge instead.
</Note>

## Other ways to run it

- **A separate late-breaking form.** Give LBAs their own form and reopen only that form later. Decide this before the call opens, because each abstract stays on the form it came through. Abstracts that don't qualify stay on the late-breaking form too, so they would get editing back when it reopens.
- **Your team makes the late edits.** Authors send their Results and Conclusions and your team enters them on the abstract. This works for a handful of LBAs, but authors can't make their other changes themselves.
- **Moving the main close date.** Changing the **Close Date** reopens the form for every submitter, so use it only when everyone should get more time.
