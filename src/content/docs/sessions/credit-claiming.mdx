Credit Claiming gives an event a public **claim site** for continuing education (CE). After the show, attendees sign in with their badge number or email and last name, pick the credentials they hold, claim credit for the sessions they attended, answer the session evaluation, and download a certificate for every credit they claim and a full transcript as PDF or Excel. Your team sets it up and follows it under **Program**.

<Note>
Credit Claiming is an Early Access **Beta** for one event at a time. On the event's **Preview** page, click **Request access** on the **Credit Claiming** card and a Sessionboard CSM turns it on for that event. It is separate from the **Learning** module (Lessons & Credits): you don't need Learning to use Credit Claiming, and turning Credit Claiming on changes nothing in Learning. Credit types are shared, so if your organization has Learning too, both list the same credit types.
</Note>

Credit Claiming gives an event its own continuing-education claim site. It's an Early Access beta that your Customer Success Manager turns on for the event, and it lives under Program, After the event.

Start in Program Settings, under Claiming. Turn on credit claiming, set when claiming opens and closes, and choose who can claim.

One session per time slot stops attendees claiming two overlapping sessions. Session list decides what each attendee sees: here, the sessions they scanned into come first.

Link a PACE evaluation, and attendees answer it before every claim. The results land in Surveys.

Credit types are shared across your organization, and each one issues its own certificate. General Attendance is a zero-credit type for a plain certificate of attendance.

On Credits per session, pick a credit type and amount, tick Eval to require the evaluation, and apply it to many sessions at once. You can import a CSV instead.

Swapcard door scans come in automatically. From any other scanning system, upload a CSV of email or badge, session code and scan time. Running the same file again is safe: existing rows are skipped.

Now the attendee. They open the claim site link and sign in with their badge number, or their email, plus their last name.

They choose the credentials they hold. Sessions they scanned into are marked Attended and listed first.

Claiming a session opens its evaluation. Once it's submitted, they get one certificate for every credit they claimed.

The transcript groups every claim by credit type, with certificate numbers, and downloads as a PDF or an Excel file.

Back in Program, the Audit tab flags claims with no verified attendance, and the Report tab counts claims by credit type.

## Why use it

Accredited meetings owe every attendee a certificate and a transcript, and owe the accrediting body proof that credit went only to people who were in the room and evaluated the session. Credit Claiming puts the claim site on the same sessions, credits and attendance you already manage in Sessionboard. Attendees see the sessions they scanned into first, can't claim two sessions in one time slot, and must answer the evaluation before credit is awarded. You don't need a separate claim vendor, and you don't have to reconcile spreadsheets after the show.

## Where it lives

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Turn the claim site on, set the window and rules | **Program → Configure → Settings → Claiming** |
| Credit types and the certificate each one issues | **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types** |
| Claims, credits per session, attendance, audit and report | **Program → After the event → Credit Claiming** |
| What attendees use | The claim site link, shown under **Claiming** |

![The Program module in the event sidebar with Credit Claiming under After the event](/images/kb/credit-claiming/sidebar-after-the-event.png)

The **Claiming** and **Credit types** settings, and the **Credit Claiming** page, appear only on events with Credit Claiming turned on. Anyone who can view sessions on the event can open the **Credit Claiming** page.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add credit types">
    Under **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types**, check the credentials your meeting awards (AMA PRA Category 1, ANCC contact hours, and the other common types are already there). Add your own, and pick the certificate each one issues. See [Set up credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#credit-types).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put credit on sessions">
    On **Program → After the event → Credit Claiming → Credits per session**, apply a credit type and amount to many sessions at once, or import a CSV. Tick **Eval** to make the evaluation required before a claim. See [Credits per session](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#credits-per-session).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the claim site">
    Under **Program → Configure → Settings → Claiming**, turn on **Enable credit claiming**, set when claiming opens and closes, who can claim, the one-session-per-time-slot rule, which sessions each attendee sees, the evaluation, and instructions. See [Claiming settings](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#claiming-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in attendance">
    Swapcard door scans flow in automatically when **Pull session attendee lists & door scans** is on. Scans from any other system come in as a CSV on the **Attendance** tab. See [Record attendance for credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-attendance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the claim site link">
    Copy the **Claim site link** from **Claiming** and send it to attendees. They sign in, claim, evaluate, and download certificates and a transcript. The steps attendees follow are in [Claim CE credits and download your transcript](/participants/claim-ce-credits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and report">
    Use the **Claims**, **Audit** and **Report** tabs to see who claimed what, flag claims with no verified attendance, and count claims by credit type. See [Review claims and report on credit](/sessions/credit-claiming-review).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can set everything up before the show and leave **Claiming opens** in the future. Attendees who sign in early see when claiming opens, and Sessionboard keeps importing scans in the meantime.
</Tip>
