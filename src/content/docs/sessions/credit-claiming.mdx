Credit Claiming gives an event a public **claim site** for continuing education (CE), in the event's own branding and in English, German, Spanish or French. After the show, attendees sign in with their badge number or email and last name, pick the credentials they hold, claim credit for the sessions they attended, answer the questions each credential asks and the session survey, and download a certificate for every credit they claim and a full transcript as PDF or Excel. Your team sets it up and follows it under **Program**, with filters, an audit, reports, an activity log and exports, and can hand a partner a Credit Claiming–only role.

Credit Claiming gives an event its own continuing-education claim site. It's an Early Access beta that your Customer Success Manager turns on, and it lives under Program, After the event.

The Credit Claiming page has six tabs: Claims, Credits per session, Attendance, Audit, Report and Activity. The buttons at the top open the claiming settings and the credit types, copy the claim link, and open Insights.

Claiming settings are grouped in cards. Under Access, turn claiming on, copy the claim link, and choose who can claim. Under Claim window, set when claiming opens and closes.

Session list decides what each attendee sees. Here, the sessions they scanned into come first. Under Rules, one session per time slot stops anyone claiming two overlapping sessions.

The session feedback survey is what attendees answer before each claim. Create a PACE-style survey in one click, and its answers land in Surveys.

Credit types are shared across your organization. Each one sets its unit and the certificate it issues, with your signatory and accreditation statement.

Each credit type can also ask its own questions, like an attestation your accreditor requires. These aren't a survey. They belong to the credential, they're asked on every claim or once per attendee, and the answers stay with the claim.

On Credits per session, pick a credit type and amount, tick Survey to require the survey, and apply it to many sessions at once. You can import a CSV instead.

Door scans from a connected event app come in on their own. From any other scanning system, upload a CSV of email or badge, session code and scan time. Verified attendance then shows who attended each session, and who hasn't claimed yet.

Now the attendee. The claim site carries your event's logo, name and colours, in English, German, Spanish or French. They sign in with their badge number, or their email, plus their last name.

They choose the credentials they hold. Sessions they scanned into are marked Attended. Claiming one opens its survey, and any questions the credential asks come next.

My certificates has one certificate for every credit they claimed, plus a summary for each credential. The transcript groups every claim by credit type, and downloads as a PDF or an Excel file.

Back in Program, the Audit tab flags claims with no verified attendance, and the Report tab totals credit by type, session and day.

Activity logs every sign-in, claim, download and staff change. And every tab has an Export button that follows the filters on screen.

<Note>
Credit Claiming is an Early Access **Beta**, turned on by a Sessionboard CSM in one of two ways. For a single conference, click **Request access** on the **Credit Claiming** card on that event's **Early Access** page. For every event and field event in your organization, request the **Credit Claiming** card on the organization's **Early Access** page instead. Field events need the organization-wide one; see [Credit claiming for field events](/sessions/credit-claiming-gatherings). It is separate from the **Learning** module (Lessons & Credits): you don't need Learning to use Credit Claiming, and turning Credit Claiming on changes nothing in Learning. Credit types are shared, so if your organization has Learning too, both list the same credit types.
</Note>

## Why use it

Accredited meetings owe every attendee a certificate and a transcript, and owe the accrediting body proof that credit went only to people who were in the room and gave feedback on the session. Credit Claiming puts the claim site on the same sessions, credits and attendance you already manage in Sessionboard. Attendees see the sessions they scanned into first, can't claim two sessions in one time slot, and must answer your accreditor's questions and the session survey before credit is awarded. You don't need a separate claim vendor, and you don't have to reconcile spreadsheets after the show.

## Where it lives

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Turn the claim site on, set the window and rules | **Program → Configure → Settings → Claiming** |
| Credit types, their questions and the certificate each one issues | **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types** |
| Claims, credits per session, attendance, audit, report, activity and exports | **Program → After the event → Credit Claiming** |
| Who can see or manage Credit Claiming | **Event Team → Permissions → Credit Claiming** |
| Every claim-site and staff action | The **Activity** tab, and **History** under **Credit claim** |
| What attendees use | The claim site link, shown under **Claiming** |

![The Program module in the event sidebar with Credit Claiming under After the event](/images/kb/credit-claiming/sidebar-after-the-event.png)

The **Claiming** and **Credit types** settings, and the **Credit Claiming** page, appear only on events with Credit Claiming turned on. Who can open them is set by the **Credit Claiming** permissions: Admin User and Admin Lite can manage it, Reporting Only Admin can view it, and the **Credit Claiming Manager** and **Credit Claiming Viewer** roles give someone Credit Claiming and nothing else. See [Give someone access to Credit Claiming only](/sessions/credit-claiming-roles).

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add credit types">
    Under **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types**, check the credentials your meeting awards (AMA PRA Category 1, ANCC contact hours, and the other common types are already there). Add your own, add the questions each accreditor requires, and set the certificate each one issues, with its signatory and accreditation statement. See [Set up credit types, questions and certificates](/sessions/credit-claiming-credit-types).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put credit on sessions">
    On **Program → After the event → Credit Claiming → Credits per session**, apply a credit type and amount to many sessions at once, or import a CSV. Tick the **Survey** claim requirement to make the session feedback survey required before a claim. See [Credits per session](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#credits-per-session).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the claim site">
    Under **Program → Configure → Settings → Claiming**, switch claiming on, choose who can claim, set when claiming opens and closes, which sessions each attendee sees, the one-session-per-time-slot rule, the session feedback survey, and attendee instructions. See [Claiming settings](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#claiming-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in attendance">
    Swapcard door scans flow in automatically when **Pull session attendee lists & door scans** is on. Scans from any other system come in as a CSV on the **Attendance** tab. See [Record attendance for credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-attendance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the claim site link">
    Click **Copy claim link** on the **Credit Claiming** page and send it to attendees. They sign in, claim, answer the questions, and download certificates and a transcript. The steps attendees follow are in [Claim CE credits and download your transcript](/participants/claim-ce-credits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and report">
    Use the **Claims**, **Audit**, **Report** and **Activity** tabs to see who claimed what, flag claims with no verified attendance, count claims by credit type, session and day, and follow every change. Export any tab, or share a live Credit Claiming dashboard from **Insights**. See [Review claims and report on credit](/sessions/credit-claiming-review).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can set everything up before the show and leave **Claiming opens** in the future. Attendees who sign in early see when claiming opens, and Sessionboard keeps importing scans in the meantime.
</Tip>
