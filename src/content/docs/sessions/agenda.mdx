## Before you schedule

The agenda reads its defaults from **Program → Settings → Agenda**: the day start and end times, the drag interval, which statuses are schedulable, default durations per format, and which rooms show. Set those first — see [Program settings → Agenda](/sessions/program-settings#agenda).

<Note>
By default only **Accepted** sessions appear on the agenda. Add **Pending** or **Accept Queue** under **Program Statuses** in the agenda settings if you want to block out a schedule before decisions are final.
</Note>

## The agenda views

Open **Program → Agenda**. Six tabs across the top look at the same schedule.

![The agenda's List view — one row per session with status, source, format, track, speaker, and start time](/images/kb/agenda-list.png)

| View | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **List** | The table. Same columns, filters, and bulk actions as the Sessions list, plus **Import** and **Add Session**. |
| **Day** | One day as a calendar. Drag to schedule, move, or resize. |
| **Week** | The whole week as a calendar. |
| **Month** | The overview. Cards aren't colored here. |
| **Rooms** | Every room side by side, so you can spot the empty slot in Room A. Switch between **Vertical layout** and **Horizontal layout**, and **Zoom Out** to see every room without scrolling. |
| **Conflicts** | The rules the agenda checks and everything that currently breaks one. |

![The Day view with sessions as colored blocks on the calendar and the Sessions panel on the right](/images/kb/agenda-day.png)

**Go To Date**, **Today**, **Event Start**, and **Event End** move the calendar; the event's dates are marked so you don't schedule outside them.

### The Sessions panel

The panel on the right is your to-do list. **Scheduled** and **Unscheduled** split the sessions that have a time from those that don't, and **Conflicts** lists the ones that break a rule. Drag a session from **Unscheduled** onto the grid to schedule it, drag it again to move it, and drag it into a room in the Rooms view to assign the room. Each drag saves.

![The Rooms view with rooms as columns and sessions placed in their slots](/images/kb/agenda-rooms.png)

<Note>
Dragging a session saves the new time and room, and emails speakers when **Submission change notifications** is on for their portal — see [Notify speakers of submission changes](/portals/submission-change-notifications). Turn it off while you're still shuffling and back on when the schedule is close to final.
</Note>

The icons above the panel switch it to other tools: **Filters & Views** (saved views and active filters), **Card Colors**, **Card Display** (which fields show on a calendar card, up to three extra), **Agenda Settings**, and **Agenda Drafts**.

## Card colors

Open **Card Colors** in the agenda panel to choose what the session cards are colored by. Under **Color By**, pick **Track** (the default), **Status**, **Room**, **Format**, **Level**, or **Tags**. The **Color Key** below lists each value with its color. Your choice is saved in your browser, so it does not change the agenda for the rest of your team.

For every option except Tags, the whole card is filled with the value's color.

### Color by tags

A session can carry several tags, so **Tags** works differently: cards stay white with black text, and each tag appears as a small colored square in the bottom-left corner of the card.

![Four sessions on the day view as white cards, each with a row of small colored tag squares in the bottom-left corner](/images/kb/agenda-cards-color-by-tags.png)

- When a card is too short for its own row of squares, the squares sit at the start of the title line instead.
- When a session has more tags than fit, the last square is replaced by **+N**. Hover over it to see the names of the hidden tags; hover over any square to see its tag name.
- A tag with no color shows as a gray square. Give it one in [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#tags).
- The unscheduled cards in the panel and the card you drag onto the calendar use the same white card and tag squares.

## Conflicts

The **Conflicts** tab is where the agenda tells you what's wrong.

![The Conflicts tab with the two system rules, the Your rules column, and the results table](/images/kb/agenda-conflicts.png)

Two rules are always on and can't be turned off:

- **Room conflict** — two sessions share the same room during the same time slot.
- **Shared contact conflict** — a speaker, chairperson, or moderator is repeated across overlapping sessions.

Under **Your rules** you write your own in plain English — “no two keynotes on the same day”, “keep a sponsor's sessions out of the same slot” — and choose whether each is **Enforced** (blocks the agenda) or a **Warning** (flags only). **Generate recommendations** suggests rules from your program if you'd rather start from a list.

**Results** underneath lists every session that breaks a rule, with the rule it broke. **Open** takes you to the session editor to move it; **Dismiss** hides a conflict you've decided to live with (**Show dismissed (…)** brings it back). Sessions with a conflict carry a red dot on the calendar. Conflicts refresh when you reload the page or click **Refreshed**.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Why is a session missing from the agenda?">

By default the agenda shows only sessions whose status is **Accepted**. A session that is pending, declined, in a custom status, or still a draft does not appear until you accept it or add its status under **Program → Settings → Agenda → Program Statuses**. See [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#agenda).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we build the agenda with pending or custom statuses, not only accepted sessions?">

Yes. **Program Statuses** in the [agenda settings](/sessions/program-settings#agenda) chooses which statuses are schedulable, so you can block out a draft schedule while decisions are still open. Custom statuses are created under [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#statuses); each one belongs to a standard category (pending, accept queue, accepted, decline queue, declined) that drives portals, emails, and integrations.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we filter, color, and customize how the agenda displays?">

Tracks color sessions in the day, week, and rooms views by default; switch to status, room, format, level, or tags under [card colors](#card-colors). Rooms, tracks, tags, formats, levels, and languages all come from [program settings](/sessions/program-settings) and are the filters available on the agenda and in the Sessions table. Columns and saved [table views](/reporting/dashboard-views) control which fields appear in the list, and what speakers and the public see is set separately in the [portal](/portals/portals-101) and the [agenda embed](/sessions/embeds).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we avoid double-booking speakers and rooms?">

**Program → Agenda → Conflicts** lists every overlap: two sessions in one room at the same time, or a speaker, moderator, or chairperson scheduled twice. Sessions with a conflict carry a red dot in the agenda; **Open** takes you to the session editor to move it. See [conflicts](#conflicts).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we schedule many sessions at once — import, bulk edit, or copy from last year?">

[Import](/settings/importing-data) creates or updates sessions with dates, times, and rooms from a spreadsheet, including an **import to update** flow for existing records. [Bulk edit](/settings/bulk-edit-fields) changes one field across many selected sessions, such as moving a track to a new room. [Copy sessions from another event](/sessions/copy-sessions-from-another-event) reuses last year's program, and [clone a session](/faq/clone-a-session) duplicates one. Drag and drop in any agenda view saves the new time and room.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do panels, workshops with parts, and multi-speaker sessions work?">

A session can hold several participants, each with a role: speaker, moderator, chairperson, or a [custom role](/sessions/program-settings#roles). Add them from the session's **Participants** ([create a session](/sessions/create-a-session), [add a moderator and chairperson](/contacts/add-a-moderator-chairperson)). For a block with parts (a symposium with individual talks), create the block as the parent and its parts as [subsessions](/sessions/create-a-subsession); [viewing subsessions within the agenda](/sessions/viewing-subsessions-within-the-agenda) explains how they display; check each app article for how subsessions are handled in a sync (Cvent, for example, has a **Sync Subsessions to Session Programs** setting).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are speakers told when we move their session?">

Yes, when **Submission change notifications** is on for their portal. Dragging a session to a new time or room saves immediately and emails the participants. See [notify speakers of submission changes](/portals/submission-change-notifications).

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
