## How to view your agenda
Your event agenda can be viewed from ***Sessions*** \> ***Agenda***.

<Tip>
**Note:** By default, only submissions with an ***accepted*** status will be accessible from your Agenda. Use the [Agenda Settings](/sessions/session-settings) to include additional session statuses.
</Tip>

![Your event agenda can be viewed from Sessions \> Agenda](/images/kb/c6e1a16a-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-37-28-3493-PM.png)

Your agenda can be viewed in 5 different layouts: [list (default)](#list-default-view), [day](#day-view), [week](#week-view), [month](#month-view), and [room](#rooms-view).

You can drag and drop all accepted sessions to update and assign the date, time, or room, depending on the view you are in.

<Note>
Dragging a session saves the new time and room, and emails speakers when **Submission change notifications** is on for their portal — see [Notify speakers of submission changes](/portals/submission-change-notifications).
</Note>

## Card colors

Open **Colors** in the agenda sidebar to choose what the session cards are colored by. Under **Color By**, pick **Track** (the default), **Status**, **Room**, **Format**, **Level**, or **Tags**. The **Color Key** below lists each value with its color. Your choice is saved in your browser, so it does not change the agenda for the rest of your team.

For every option except Tags, the whole card is filled with the value's color.

### Color by tags

A session can carry several tags, so **Tags** works differently: cards stay white with black text, and each tag appears as a small colored square in the bottom-left corner of the card.

![Four sessions on the day view as white cards, each with a row of small colored tag squares in the bottom-left corner](/images/kb/agenda-cards-color-by-tags.png)

- **Short sessions** — when a card is too short for its own row of squares, the squares sit at the start of the title line instead.
- **Many tags** — when a session has more tags than fit, the last square is replaced by **+N**. Hover over it to see the names of the hidden tags; hover over any square to see its tag name.
- **Tags without a color** show as a gray square. Give a tag a color in [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#tags).
- **Sidebar cards and the drag preview match** — the unscheduled session cards in the sidebar, and the card you drag onto the calendar, use the same white card and tag squares.

## Conflicts
Conflict detection allows you to quickly see if two or more sessions are overlapping, or if a session contact (speaker, chairperson, moderator) is double-booked at the same time.

To view this, navigate to **Sessions** \> **Agenda** \> **Conflicts**. The system will check for any conflicts and display each session here with details.

<Tip>
***NOTE:*** *Conflicts are updated upon page refresh.*
</Tip>

![To view this, navigate to Sessions \> Agenda \> Conflicts](/images/kb/c07b192a-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-42-01-7123-PM.png)

Below, you will find an example of an agenda with no conflicts vs. an agenda with conflicts that have been detected by the system for your review.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Why is a session missing from the agenda?">

By default the agenda shows only sessions whose status is **Accepted**. A session that is pending, declined, in a custom status, or still a draft does not appear until you accept it or add its status under **Sessions > Settings > Agenda** (**Session Statuses**). See [agenda settings](/sessions/session-settings).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we build the agenda with pending or custom statuses, not only accepted sessions?">

Yes. **Session Statuses** in [agenda settings](/sessions/session-settings) chooses which statuses are schedulable, so you can block out a draft schedule while decisions are still open. Custom statuses are created under [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#statuses); each one belongs to a standard category (pending, accept queue, accepted, decline queue, declined) that drives portals, emails, and integrations.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we filter, color, and customize how the agenda displays?">

Tracks color sessions in the day, week, and rooms views by default; switch to status, room, format, level, or tags under [card colors](#card-colors). Rooms, tracks, tags, formats, levels, and languages all come from [program settings](/sessions/program-settings) and are the filters available on the agenda and in the Sessions table. Columns and saved [table views](/reporting/dashboard-views) control which fields appear in the list, and what speakers and the public see is set separately in the [portal](/portals/portals-101) and the [agenda embed](/sessions/embeds).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we avoid double-booking speakers and rooms?">

**Sessions > Agenda > Conflicts** lists every overlap: two sessions in one room at the same time, or a speaker, moderator, or chairperson scheduled twice. Sessions with a conflict carry a red dot in the agenda; **Open** takes you to the session editor to move it. Conflicts refresh when you reload the page. See [conflicts](#conflicts).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we schedule many sessions at once — import, bulk edit, or copy from last year?">

[Import](/settings/importing-data) creates or updates sessions with dates, times, and rooms from a spreadsheet, including an **import to update** flow for existing records. [Bulk edit](/settings/bulk-edit-fields) changes one field across many selected sessions, such as moving a track to a new room. [Copy sessions from another event](/sessions/copy-sessions-from-another-event) reuses last year's program, and [clone a session](/faq/clone-a-session) duplicates one. Drag and drop in any agenda view saves the new time and room.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do panels, workshops with parts, and multi-speaker sessions work?">

A session can hold several participants, each with a role: speaker, moderator, chairperson, or a [custom role](/sessions/program-settings#roles). Add them from the session's **Participants** ([create a session](/sessions/create-a-session), [add a moderator and chairperson](/contacts/add-a-moderator-chairperson)). For a block with parts (a symposium with individual talks), create the block as the parent and its parts as [subsessions](/sessions/create-a-subsession); [viewing subsessions within the agenda](/sessions/viewing-subsessions-within-the-agenda) explains how they display; check each app article for how subsessions are handled in a sync (Cvent, for example, has a **Sync Subsessions to Session Programs** setting).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are speakers told when we move their session?">

Yes, when **Submission change notifications** is on for their portal. Dragging a session to a new time or room saves immediately and emails the participants. See [notify speakers of submission changes](/portals/submission-change-notifications).

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
