Use submissions to build your event schedule. A session can be a submission you have accepted or any other event day scheduled times you want to show to your attendees (i.e., Lunch, Networking Hour, Registration Open).

## Agenda Settings
Customize the settings for building your event agenda by navigating to **Sessions** \> **Settings** \> **Agenda**.

![Customize the settings for building your event agenda by navigating to Sessions \> Settings \> Agenda](/images/kb/1c8f9580-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-36-43-2199-PM.png)

<Tip>
👉🏽 [Learn how to configure Agenda Settings](/sessions/session-settings)
</Tip>

## How to view your agenda
Your event agenda can be viewed from ***Sessions*** \> ***Agenda***.

<Tip>
**Note:** By default, only submissions with an ***accepted*** status will be accessible from your Agenda. Use the [Agenda Settings](/sessions/session-settings) to include additional session statuses.
</Tip>

![Your event agenda can be viewed from Sessions \> Agenda](/images/kb/c6e1a16a-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-37-28-3493-PM.png)

Your agenda can be viewed in 5 different layouts: [list (default)](#list-default-view), [day](#day-view), [week](#week-view), [month](#month-view), and [room](#rooms-view).

You can drag and drop all accepted sessions to update and assign the date, time, or room, depending on the view you are in.

![You can drag and drop all accepted sessions to update and assign the date, time, or room](/images/kb/7c4555ca-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-38-13-8965-PM.png)

## Conflicts
Conflict detection allows you to quickly see if two or more sessions are overlapping, or if a session contact (speaker, chairperson, moderator) is double-booked at the same time.

To view this, navigate to **Sessions** \> **Agenda** \> **Conflicts**. The system will check for any conflicts and display each session here with details.

<Tip>
***NOTE:*** *Conflicts are updated upon page refresh.*
</Tip>

![To view this, navigate to Sessions \> Agenda \> Conflicts](/images/kb/c07b192a-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-42-01-7123-PM.png)

Below, you will find an example of an agenda with no conflicts vs. an agenda with conflicts that have been detected by the system for your review.

**No conflicts**

![Below](/images/kb/532df87b-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-43-11-2743-PM.png)

**Conflicts detected**

By clicking **Open**, the system will open the session editor, allowing you to adjust the conflicts that were detected.

![Conflict3](/images/kb/4d7efa25-Conflict3.png)

Sessions with a conflict will be designated with a red dot in the agenda view.

![Sessions with a conflict will be designated with a red dot in the agenda view](/images/kb/c2eaf8d1-image-png-Feb-23-2026-08-08-08-5119-PM.png)
