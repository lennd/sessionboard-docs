## How to Build a Submission Form
From your **Sessions** module, select the **Forms** button to access the **Session Submission Forms**.

![From your Sessions module, select the Forms button to access the Session Submission Forms](/images/kb/dc06ce4a-image-png-Feb-25-2026-07-59-51-4750-PM.png)

By default, Sessionboard creates an initial submission form that you can customize. Click the ellipsis (⋯) to see additional actions, then select **Edit** to start configuring your form.

![By default, Sessionboard creates an initial submission form that you can customize](/images/kb/5611d521-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-02-28-4646-PM.png)

Edit the four sections of the submission form: Welcome Screen, Session Information, Speaker Information, and Form Settings.

- **Welcome Screen** - Introduction to the event. This is the first screen a user will see when they access your submission form. Event admins can create a custom welcome message with basic text box functionality.

  ![Welcome Screen - Introduction to the event](/images/kb/532b1b52-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-13-42-7361-PM.png)

- **Session Information** - Collect information about the submitted session by adding a page title, page description/instructions, and questions to collect data.

  ***Page Heading*** *is* required and can not be deleted from the form.

  ![Page Heading is required and can not be deleted from the form](/images/kb/09205552-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-14-51-8183-PM.png)

- **Speaker Information** - Collect information for the session's speaker(s) by adding a page title, page description/instructions, as well as questions to collect data.

  ***First Name, Last Name,*** and ***Email*** are required fields that can not be deleted from the form.

    ![First Name, Last Name, and Email are required fields that can not be deleted from the form](/images/kb/a443dbb1-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-31-18-9649-PM.png)

  - Unique Contact Settings
    - **Allow Users to submit new information for existing contacts:** If enabled, users will be able to submit new information for existing contacts. If disabled, existing contacts will need to log in to the portal to submit any additional information you want to collect.
    - **Notify existing contacts that they have been added to a submission:** If enabled, existing contacts will receive an email notification to log in to the portal to view and update their information. If disabled, existing contacts will not receive any notification upon submission.

- **Form Settings** - The following settings can be applied to your form.

### Deadlines

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open Date** | The form won't accept submissions until this date. Leave it empty to open immediately. |
| **Close Date** | Closes the form after this date. New submissions stop, and edits to existing ones are refused. |
| **Send Reminder Email** | Appears once a close date is set. Emails submitters who saved a draft but never completed it, five days and one day before the form closes. |

<Note>
Reminder emails go to **drafts only** — someone who already submitted won't be chased. Edit the wording under **Settings → Email Templates**.

The option is unavailable while conditional deadline rules are set, since there's no single close date to count back from.
</Note>

![The deadline settings, with a close date, reminder email toggle, and the admin notification pickers](/images/kb/7a41d7b4-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-32-15-1932-PM.png)

### Submission capacity

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set Submission Limit** | Limits how many sessions one user may have on this form. This counts saved drafts as well as submitted sessions — at the limit, submitters can resume a draft but can't start a new one. |
| **Allow multiple draft submissions** | Lets one submitter keep more than one draft in progress at a time. |
| **Set Speaker Limit** | The maximum number of speakers on a submission, up to 15. |

<Warning>
The speaker limit is set as high as 15 here, but the public form accepts at most **10** speakers per submission regardless. Setting it higher won't let submitters add an eleventh.
</Warning>

![The submission and speaker limit settings, with the confirmation message editor below them](/images/kb/c25a00d5-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-32-28-1098-PM.png)

#### Primary speaker limits

On the newer forms interface, the form editor's participant limits can also cap how many participants may be marked *Primary*: **Max primary** per role, and **Total max primary** across all roles. These controls appear once [**Enable primary speakers**](/events/event-details) is on for the event, and they can vary by conditional rules. Both the new and older submission portals enforce them — when a limit is reached, submitters can no longer mark additional people as primary; a limit of 0 hides the primary toggle entirely.

### New submissions

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default status for new submissions** | The status every submission created from this form starts in. Choose one of the standard statuses — **Pending** (the default), **Accept Queue**, **Accepted**, **Decline Queue**, or **Declined** — or any custom status from **Program Settings → Statuses**. Each form has its own default, so a lightning-talk form can land submissions straight in Accept Queue while your main call for papers keeps them Pending. |

If you pick a custom status, whether submitters see its custom name or its standard category label follows that status's **Show custom status name to external users** setting. If the custom status is later archived, new submissions from the form fall back to Pending.

### After submission

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmation email message** | The message submitters receive by email after a new submission. A link to the speaker portal is added automatically. |
| **Auto-redirect to speaker portal** | Sends the submitter to their portal 10 seconds after the confirmation page. With it off, they click **Continue to portal** themselves. |
| **Customize the success page message** | The message shown on the confirmation page after submitting, and after payment where fees apply. |
| **Notifications** | Which admins are notified when a new submission arrives, and when an existing one is edited. |

<Note>
On newer (v2) forms some of these move: confirmation and notification emails live on their own **Notifications** step, speaker caps are set per role on the **Participant Information** step, and the settings tab adds **Languages** for multi-language submissions. v2 forms that collect fees also get a **Payments & Fees** step with a base fee, [date & time tiers for early-bird pricing](/awards/awards-pricing-payments-invoices#date--time-tiers-early-bird-pricing), pricing rules, VAT, and promo codes.
</Note>

![The success page message editor at the end of the form settings](/images/kb/576317fb-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-32-38-6915-PM.png)

## Creating, Editing, & Managing Fields

### Creating Fields

<Warning>
**Note**: If your event will use Evaluation Plans, any field names you create will be visible to Evaluators when they review the submitted sessions.
</Warning>

On the **Session Information** and **Speaker Information** pages, event organizers can add system fields or create custom fields to display on the form.

Click the **Add Field** button to view all the fields you can add.

![Click the Add Field button to view all the fields you can add](/images/kb/3eb02470-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-37-29-2067-PM.png)

The **Select Fields** page displays all standard and previously created custom fields. Click them to add it to your form.

![The Select Fields page displays all standard and previously created custom fields](/images/kb/454e5c5e-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-38-34-0257-PM.png)

To add a new field, click the **Create New Field** button and follow the prompts to configure it.

![To add a new field, click the Create New Field button and follow the prompts to configure it](/images/kb/cdf240b4-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-39-09-6851-PM.png)

<Tip>
Two options on the field form decide how far it reaches:

**Field Description** gives your team internal context — what the field is for, or how to fill it in. It is never shown to submitters and exists purely for internal reference.

**Field Level** decides whether the field belongs to one event or to all of them. An **Event Field** is created for and used within a single event. A **Global Field** is created once and reused across every event in the organization, which keeps data consistent and saves setup time.

Field level can't be changed after the field is created, so choose it deliberately.
</Tip>

![The new field form, with the internal field description and the event or global field level choice](/images/kb/8abc562f-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-40-12-7843-PM.png)

### Editing, & Managing Fields

Once fields have been added to your form, select the ellipses within the Actions column to customize questions, use question rules (i.e., conditional logic), edit a field, or remove a field from your form.

![Once fields have been added to your form](/images/kb/d4ec0d27-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-43-20-1682-PM.png)

#### Customize Question
Use **Customize Question** to change how the field appears to submitters.

- **Custom Label** — the name they see.
- **Help Text** — extra guidance under the field.
- **Length Limit** (text fields) — set a **Minimum** and/or **Maximum** length and choose whether to **Count** in **Characters** or **Words**. Submitters see a live counter under the field (for example "45 / 100 words"). An answer outside the limits blocks the step it sits on, so a submitter has to fix it before moving forward rather than discovering it at the end on Submit. The message names the field and the limit — "Answer is too long", with "Abstract must be 100 words or fewer (currently 150)" beneath it — and matches what the server says if the answer reaches it. Leave both blank for no limit.
- **Maximum file size** (file fields) — the size advertised on the form, up to 5 GB. Leave blank to inherit the field default (up to 5 GB). Session **Files** settings do not apply to this form.
- **Accepted file types** (file fields) — tick the formats this question takes, such as PDF only. Leave every box unticked to accept anything. Submitters see the list in the hint under the field, and a file that isn't one of those types is rejected — Sessionboard reads the file's contents, so renaming it doesn't get it through. See [Restrict a file question to certain file types](/faq/what-is-the-maximum-file-size-that-sessionboard-supports#restrict-a-file-question-to-certain-file-types).

On **Headshot** and **Logo**, **Help Text** you write is the only line under the field. Sessionboard does not add a second “Images only (up to 5 GB)” hint beneath it. If **Help Text** is empty, submitters see a type-and-size hint from the field’s accepted types and this **Maximum file size** — for example “Images only (up to 2 MB)”, or “Images only (up to 5 GB)” when size is blank.

Other file fields still show the type-and-size hint even when they also have **Help Text**.

The photo editor still enforces [speaker headshot limitations](/events/event-details) (formats, dimensions, and the event file-size cap). Those limits do not change the hint.

![Use a Custom Label to customize how the field name appears to submitters](/images/kb/efe26ab5-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-46-10-0006-PM.png)

#### Use question rules
Use **question rules** (conditional logic) to create customizable forms that adjust based on the answers that your users provide to various questions. This allows you to use one form to intake multiple different types of sessions.

Question rules can only be used on the following field types:

- Checkbox

- Dropdown

- Number

- Country

<Tip>
When using question rules, keep in mind the following:

1. Create and add all fields to your form, then save it before applying question rules.

2. Open **Use question rules** on either side of the relationship.

   A **conditional question** only appears based on the answer to a previous question. You can set that from either field:

   - Open the later question to set **Show this field when** (for example, Member ID shows when “Are you a member?” is Yes).
   - Open the earlier question — if it is a checkbox, dropdown, number, or country field — to set **This question controls** and choose every later question that should appear for a given answer (for example, Session Format is Workshop shows Duration, Room setup, and Max attendees).

![Q2 (conditional)](/images/kb/d06f9e02-image-png-Feb-25-2026-08-57-10-7159-PM.png)
</Tip>

Question rules only hide or show questions. To write other field values when the submission is saved, use an Assignment [Field Rule](/settings/field-rules).

### Event merge tags in form content

Form content can reference the event by name instead of hard-coding it — useful when you duplicate a form for next year's event or copy it between events.

Use **Insert Merge Tag** in the rich-text toolbar (or type the tag directly) anywhere in the form's written content: the welcome screen, page titles and descriptions, section headers, rich text blocks, and the confirmation message.

| Merge tag | Renders as |
| --- | --- |
| &#123;&#123;event_name&#125;&#125; | The event's name |
| &#123;&#123;event_slug&#125;&#125; | The event's URL slug |

Submitters see the resolved value on the public form. Anything that isn't a recognized tag is left exactly as you typed it.

## Multi-language submissions

On the newer (v2) forms interface, the settings tab has a **Languages** step that lets submitters switch the form into another language.

### How well each language is translated

Sessionboard ships maintained translations of the form's own wording — buttons, navigation, validation messages — for **English**, **French**, **Spanish**, and **German**. Any other language you enable is **machine-translated when the form loads**. It is not professionally localized and nobody reviews it, so treat that wording as a best effort. Anything with no translation available falls back to English.

<Tip>
Where the exact words matter — a legal consent line, an award category, a question whose phrasing was negotiated — write them yourself as a [language variant](/settings/language-translation-variant) instead of leaving them to machine translation. A saved variant always wins.
</Tip>

### Right-to-left languages

Enable a right-to-left language — Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu, Pashto, Kurdish, Divehi, Yiddish — and a submitter who picks it gets the form laid out right to left, not just translated. Text aligns from the right, and the reading order follows the language. Switch back to a left-to-right language and the form returns to normal.

Regional codes resolve to the same direction, so `ar` and `ar-SA` both read right to left. This applies anywhere portal translations are on: the submission form, the portal itself, and follow-up forms.

### Add a translated field to the form

Language variants you have enabled can go on the form itself, next to the default-language field.

In **Add Field**, a field that has variants appears as one collapsed row with a globe icon and a chevron. Click the row to expand it, and the translated versions of that field are listed underneath — click the one you want to add it. A variant you cannot find in the list is usually sitting inside a collapsed parent rather than missing.

Search finds variants as well as parents. Type the translated name and the field surfaces even though its row shows the default-language name, or type a locale code such as `ar` to narrow the list to fields that have an Arabic variant. When your search matches exactly one field through its variant, that row opens expanded, so the entry you searched for is the one you see.

### Replace or unrequire a locked standard field

Some standard fields carry a **Locked** badge, so they cannot be made optional or removed. **Title** is the one organizers hit most often.

The lock lifts as soon as a translated variant of that same field is on the form. Add the Arabic Title beside the English one, and the English Title's **Required** switch becomes editable and **Remove from form** appears. That is the supported way to make Title optional, or to replace the English Title with the translated one. Take the variant back off the form and the field locks again.

Hover the **Locked** badge and it says so, which saves guessing — the explanation appears only when that field actually has a translated variant available to add.

## Additional Form Actions 

From the ellipsis menu (⋯) next to a submission form, event organizers can access additional actions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Customize the form's content, fields, and settings. |
| **View Submissions** | Review all completed submissions for the form. |
| **View Drafts** | See submissions that have been started but not completed. |
| **View Form** | Open the form in a new browser tab. |
| **Copy Link** | Copy the form's public URL to share with submitters. |
| **Duplicate** | Create a copy of the form to reuse or modify for a new purpose. |
| **Delete** | Permanently remove the form from your event. |

<Warning>
Duplicating a form copies the *fields*, not the field definitions. Editing a field on the copy — changing its dropdown values, for example — changes it on every other form that uses the same field.
</Warning>

## Common questions

What event teams ask while building their first call for sessions, with the section or article that answers each.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Should we use a submission form, an application form, or a portal form?">

A **submission form** is for content decisions: it collects the speaker and the session, abstract, or poster they propose, and feeds Submissions, Evaluation, and the agenda. An **application form** is a yes/no about a person or company (scholarships, volunteers, invited-speaker confirmations). A **portal form** collects details from people already in the event. Interest forms source speakers year-round at the organization level. [Which form should I use?](/concepts/which-form-should-i-use) compares all of them.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="One form or several?">

Start with one. **Question rules** ([conditional logic](#use-question-rules)) show different questions by session type or any dropdown answer, so one form can carry general sessions, workshops, and posters. Create a second form when the audiences need different open and close dates, different welcome copy, or a different confirmation flow. Each form has its own dates, submission limit, default status, and notifications, and you can **Duplicate** one from its ellipsis menu. Events on the older forms interface stop at 20 forms.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which fields should be required, and which can we leave optional?">

**First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** are always required on the speaker page and the **Page Heading** on the session page; everything else is your choice per field. Make required only what you need to evaluate the proposal (title, abstract, format, track) and collect the rest, such as headshots, full bios, AV needs, and disclosures, as [portal tasks](/portals/portals-101) after acceptance, when the speaker has a reason to finish them. Use **Customize Question** to set length limits and help text, and [field rules](/settings/field-rules) to show a question only when it applies.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we change the wording, order, logo, and branding submitters see?">

Yes. The **Welcome Screen** carries your introduction; each page has its own title and instructions; **Customize Question** sets a custom label and help text per field; and fields are reordered in the form editor. The event logo from [Event Details](/events/event-details) appears on the form, portal, and emails (see [event images and dimensions](/settings/branding-assets-dimensions)). Event merge tags let the form text reference event dates and names without retyping them ([event merge tags in form content](#event-merge-tags-in-form-content)).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can submitters save a draft and come back, or edit after they submit?">

Drafts are built in: a submitter can leave and return to the form, and [draft submissions](/sessions/draft-submissions) are listed under **View Drafts**. Turn on **Send Reminder Email** with a close date and drafts are chased five days and one day before it closes. After submitting, speakers edit their session from their portal for as long as the form is open and the portal allows it; the **Close Date** refuses edits as well as new submissions. See [can end users submit more than one submission?](/faq/can-end-users-submit-more-than-one-submission) for limits.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to speaker and co-author records — will we get duplicates?">

Every person on a submission becomes a contact, matched on email address. The **Unique Contact Settings** on the speaker page decide whether a submitter may enter new information for an existing contact or whether that contact must log in to update it themselves, and whether existing contacts are notified that they were added. If duplicates still appear, [merge duplicate contacts](/contacts/merge-duplicates). Custom participant roles (co-author, moderator) are defined in [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#roles) and offered on the form.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does the form connect to sessions, the portal, and speakers?">

A completed submission creates the session record (in the form's **Default status for new submissions**) and the speaker contacts, links them, and sends the confirmation email with a link to the speaker portal. From there the session moves through [evaluation](/evaluations/evaluation-plans) and [accept or decline](/sessions/accept-decline); accepted sessions appear on the [agenda](/sessions/agenda) and in the portal. Turning **View Session Submission Form from Portal** on in the portal settings lets speakers reopen their submission.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we share and test the form?">

**Copy Link** from the form's ellipsis menu gives the public URL; **View Form** opens it in a new tab. Submit a test proposal with a personal address before you announce it, then delete the test session and contact. See [how do I share my submission form?](/faq/how-do-i-share-my-submission-form).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who is emailed when a submission arrives?">

The submitter receives the **Confirmation email message** from Form Settings. Admins you list under **Notifications** are emailed when a new submission arrives and when one is edited. Status changes later (accept, decline) are sent through [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails) and [email templates](/settings/email-templates).

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
