## Why use it

Accreditation reporting asks three questions: who claimed what, was each claim backed by attendance, and how much of each credential did you award. Each tab answers one of them from the same claims attendees made, so the numbers you send your accrediting body match the certificates attendees hold. The activity log answers the fourth question, who changed what, when.

## The page header

Under the title, a badge shows whether claiming is **Claiming open**, **Opens soon**, **Claiming closed** or **Claiming off**, with the claim window and the number of claims and claimants. The buttons on the right:

- **Claiming settings** opens the [claiming settings](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#claiming-settings).
- **Credit types** opens the [credit types](/sessions/credit-claiming-credit-types).
- **Copy claim link** copies the claim site's address; **Open claim site** opens it in a new tab.
- **Insights** opens the Credit Claiming dashboard and reports ([below](#insights-reports-and-dashboards)).

## Filter claims

**Claims**, **Attendance**, **Audit** and **Report** share one filter bar, and every count, list and export on the tab follows it.

![The Claims ledger with Export and Grant credit, the filter bar and the first claims](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-ledger.png)

- **Search attendee, email or badge**.
- **Credit type**: one or more credit types.
- **Session…**: session title contains the text you type.
- **Attendance**: the source behind the claim, **Swapcard scan** (or your scan partner), **Imported**, **Check-in**, **Self-reported** or **No attendance record**.
- **Claimed**: a date range, in the event's time zone.
- **Flagged only**: claims the audit flags (no attendance record, overlapping session), with the count next to it.
- **Show reversed** (on **Claims**): include reversed claims and their reversing rows.

**Clear** removes every filter.

## Claims

The **Claims** tab is the ledger for the event.

- The tiles at the top count **Claimants** (attendees with a live claim), **Claims**, **Attendance links** (sessions where attendees can self-report) and **Active destinations** (where claims can be exported).
- **Grant credit** adds a claim for a walk-in or a staff override. Enter the attendee's email and name, pick a session that already has credit attached, and click **Grant credit**. A manual grant skips the claim requirements and appears as **Staff grant**.
- **Claims ledger** lists every claim with the attendee, the session, the amount and credit type, how it was claimed, and the date. A claim made on the claim site shows the attendance behind it, such as **Swapcard scan**, **Imported** or **Check-in**, with *Claimed by attendee* underneath. With no attendance record it reads **Self-reported**, flagged **No attendance record**; a grant reads **Staff grant**. Under the attendee, **Add license or AMS details** stores the license, state or member ids an export to your AMS or a state board needs.

The **More actions** menu on a claim opens **View certificate**, **Copy certificate link**, **Email certificate** or **Reverse claim**. If your organization exports claims to an association management system or a state board, **Export to** and **Export history** are there too.

<Warning>
Claims are never deleted. **Reverse claim** adds a reversing row and removes the claim from the attendee's transcript and from your counts, while the ledger keeps both rows. Turn on **Show reversed** to see them. The attendee can be granted the same credit again. Attendees can remove their own claims on the claim site while claiming is open, which works the same way.
</Warning>

## Credits per session

Attach credit to sessions in bulk, or import a CSV of designations. The tab is covered step by step in [Set up credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#credits-per-session).

## Attendance

The top of the tab imports session scans or check-ins as a CSV ([Record attendance](/sessions/credit-claiming-attendance#import-attendance-from-a-csv)). Below it, **Verified attendance** lists who attended which session, how Sessionboard knows, and whether they claimed it. The tiles count **Attendance records**, **Attendees**, **Claimed** and **Attended, not claimed**, the list attendees to remind before claiming closes.

![Verified attendance with Attendance records, Attendees, Claimed and Attended, not claimed counts above the attendee, session, source, recorded and claimed columns](/images/kb/credit-claiming/attendance-verified.png)

## Audit

The **Audit** tab checks every live claim for the event against recorded attendance. **Claims** and **Claimants** at the left of the filter bar switch between one row per claim and one row per person.

![The Claims audit in Claims mode with the filter bar, the Claims, Claimants, Verified attendance, No attendance record and Overlaps counts, and the By credit type table](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-audit.png)

- The counts show **Claims**, **Claimants**, claims with **Verified attendance**, claims with **No attendance record**, and **Overlaps**.
- **By credit type** totals claims, claimants and the amount awarded per credential. Click a credit type to filter the tab to it.
- In **Claims**, each claim shows its session date and time in the event's time zone, with the zone named (for example *Dec 10 · 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM PST*), its attendance source and its flags:
  - **No attendance record**: the attendee claimed a session they have no scan, import or check-in for.
  - **Overlapping session**: the attendee holds claims on two sessions that overlap in time. The claim site blocks this when **One session per time slot** is on, so overlaps normally come from manual grants or from claims made before the rule was turned on.
- **Claimants** lists everyone who claimed, with the credit they claimed, how many sessions, their last claim and how many claims are verified. Pick a credit type to list everyone who claimed it, the list most accrediting bodies ask for.

![The Claims audit on Claimants, with the same counts and the By credit type table](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-audit-claimants.png)

A flag doesn't reverse anything. Review the flagged claims, follow up with the attendee or import the missing scans, and reverse the claim from the **Claims** tab if it shouldn't stand. Attendance you import later clears the flag.

## Report

The **Report** tab is the summary to send your accrediting body or leadership, for the filters on screen.

![The Report tab with the filter bar, Claims, Claimants, Sessions claimed and per-unit totals, and the By credit type table](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-report.png)

- **Claims**, **Claimants**, **Sessions claimed**, and the amount awarded in each unit (for example *CEUs awarded*), with a totals line across units.
- **By credit type**: for each credential, the number of claims, the number of people who claimed it, and the **Total awarded** in its unit. Pick a credit type to filter the report.
- **Top sessions**: the most-claimed sessions with claims, claimants and verified claims. The filter icon narrows the report to that one session.
- **By day**: claims and people per day, in the event's time zone.

![Top sessions with claims, claimants and verified counts per session, and claims By day](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-report-sessions.png)

## Activity

The **Activity** tab is the log of everything that happened in Credit Claiming for the event, newest first:

- **Attendees**: signing in to the claim site, claiming credit, removing a claim, and downloading a certificate or a transcript.
- **Staff**: granting and reversing credit, importing attendance, adding or removing credit on sessions, changing the claiming settings or a credit type, and exporting.

![The Activity tab filtered to one attendee, showing Downloaded a transcript, Claimed credit and Signed in to the claim site](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-activity.png)

Filter by who (**Everyone**, **Attendees**, **Staff**, **System**), by action, by text and by date. The same entries are in **History** under **Credit claim**, next to the rest of the event's history.

## Export

Every tab has an **Export** button. Pick what to export and **CSV** or **Excel (.xlsx)**; exports use the filters on screen.

![The Export menu on the Audit tab with Claims audit, Claimants and Question answers, each as CSV or Excel](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-export-menu.png)

| Tab | What you can export |
| --- | --- |
| **Claims** | Claims |
| **Credits per session** | Credits per session |
| **Attendance** | Attendance |
| **Audit** | Claims audit, Claimants, Question answers |
| **Report** | By credit type, By session, Claimants |
| **Activity** | Activity |

Exports run in the background: a notice says the export started, the file downloads when it is ready, and it is listed under **History → Exports** to download again later. **Question answers** holds every answer attendees gave to [credit type questions](/sessions/credit-claiming-credit-types#questions).

## Insights: reports and dashboards

The **Insights** menu in the page header ties Credit Claiming into [dashboards](/reporting/dashboards) and [reports](/reporting/report-builder).

![The Insights menu with Create Credit Claiming dashboard, Reports and Share live report](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claiming-insights-menu.png)

- **Create Credit Claiming dashboard** (or **Open Credit Claiming dashboard** once it exists) creates the event's **Credit Claiming** dashboard from its starter: claims and claimants, credit by credit type, claims per day and the most-claimed sessions.
- **Reports** opens the report gallery on the Credits reports: claims by credit type, claimants by credit type, claims by session, claims by day, claims without verified attendance, attendee credit totals, sessions offering each credit type, all claims, and attendance by source.
- **Share live report** shares the Credit Claiming dashboard as a live, read-only link, for an association or accrediting partner who doesn't sign in to Sessionboard.

The Credits widgets (claims, claimants, credit claimed, claims by credit type, claims per day, most-claimed sessions, claims by attendance) are also in the widget catalog, so you can add them to any event dashboard.

<Tip>
For a per-attendee breakdown, export **Claimants** from the **Audit** or **Report** tab. Each attendee's own transcript, with certificate numbers, is on the claim site under **Transcript**.
</Tip>
