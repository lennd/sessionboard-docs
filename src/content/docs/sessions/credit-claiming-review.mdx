**Program → After the event → Credit Claiming** is where you follow [Credit Claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming) once attendees start claiming. It has five tabs: **Claims**, **Credits per session**, **Attendance**, **Audit** and **Report**. **Claiming settings** at the top right opens the [claim site settings](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#claiming-settings).

## Why use it

Accreditation reporting asks three questions: who claimed what, was each claim backed by attendance, and how much of each credential did you award. Each tab answers one of them from the same claims attendees made, so the numbers you send your accrediting body match the certificates attendees hold.

## Claims

The **Claims** tab is the ledger for the event.

- The tiles at the top count the credits attached to sessions, the claims recorded for this event, the sessions with an attendance link, and the export destinations that are active.
- **Grant credit manually** adds a claim for a walk-in or a staff override. Enter the learner's email and name, pick a session that already has credit attached, and click **Grant credit**. A manual grant skips the claim gates and appears as **Staff grant**.
- **Claims ledger** lists every claim: the learner, the session, the amount and credit type, how it was claimed, and the date. A claim an attendee made on the claim site reads as the attendance behind it, the same words as the transcript: **Imported attendance**, **Session scan** or **Check-in**, with *Claimed by attendee* underneath. With no attendance record it reads **Self-reported**; a grant reads **Staff grant**. Turn on **Show reversed** to include reversed claims.

![The Claims ledger with learner, activity, amount, how, claimed date and actions columns](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-ledger.png)

The **More actions** menu on a claim opens its **Certificate**, copies the learner's certificate link (**Copy learner link**), sends it again (**Email certificate**), or **Reverse claim**. If your organization exports claims to an association management system or a state board, **Export to** and **Export history** are there too.

<Warning>
Claims are never deleted. **Reverse claim** adds a reversing entry and removes the claim from the attendee's transcript and from your counts, while the ledger keeps both rows. Turn on **Show reversed** to see them. Attendees can remove their own claims on the claim site while claiming is open, which works the same way.
</Warning>

## Credits per session

Attach credit to sessions in bulk, or import a CSV of designations. The tab is covered step by step in [Set up credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#credits-per-session).

## Attendance

Upload session scans or check-ins as a CSV, with per-row results. See [Record attendance for credit claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming-attendance#import-attendance-from-a-csv).

## Audit

The **Audit** tab checks every live claim for the event against recorded attendance.

![The Audit tab: counts, the By credit type table, and the claims list filtered to two flagged claims marked No verified attendance](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-audit.png)

- The counts show **Claims**, **Claimants**, claims with **Verified attendance**, claims with **No attendance record**, and **Overlaps**.
- **By credit type** totals claims, claimants and the amount awarded per credential.
- The claims list shows each claim's session date and time in the event's time zone, with the zone named (for example *Dec 10 · 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM PST*), as on the claim site and the transcript, whatever time zone your browser is in.
- It shows each claim with its attendance source (for example *imported*, *partner* for Swapcard scans, *checkin*) and its flags:
  - **No verified attendance**: the attendee claimed a session they have no scan, import or check-in for.
  - **Overlapping session**: the attendee holds claims on two sessions that overlap in time. The claim site blocks this when **One session per time slot** is on, so overlaps normally come from manual grants or from claims made before the rule was turned on.
- Turn on **Flagged only** to see just the flagged claims, and click **Export CSV** to download what is listed (claim date, email, name, session, credit type, amount, method, attendance source and flags).

A flag doesn't reverse anything. Review the flagged claims, follow up with the attendee or import the missing scans, and reverse the claim from the **Claims** tab if it shouldn't stand. Attendance you import later clears the flag the next time you open the tab.

## Report

The **Report** tab is the summary to send your accrediting body or leadership.

![The Report tab: Claims, Claimants, Verified attendance and Flagged counts above the By credit type table](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claims-report.png)

- **Claims**, **Claimants**, **Verified attendance** and **Flagged** for the event.
- **By credit type**: for each credential, the number of claims, the number of people who claimed it, and the **Total awarded** in its unit.

<Tip>
For a per-attendee breakdown, export the **Audit** list. Each attendee's own transcript, with certificate numbers, is on the claim site under **Transcript**.
</Tip>
