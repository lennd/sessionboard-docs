A session does not have to be created as a subsession to become one. If the sessions already exist — a poster session that was accepted as its own record, three talks that should sit inside one workshop — you can link them under a parent without rekeying anything. There are two ways in, depending on where you start.

To create a brand-new subsession instead, or to turn a batch of abstracts into subsessions, see [Create a subsession](/sessions/create-a-subsession). To undo a link, see [Reverting a subsession back to a parent session](/sessions/reverting-a-subsession-back-to-a-parent-session).

## From the parent session

Use this when you know the parent and want to pick its subsessions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the parent">
    Go to **Program → Sessions** and click the pencil on the session that will hold the others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connections → Convert Submissions">
    Select the **Connections** tab. In the **Subsessions** panel, click **Convert Submissions**.

    ![The Connections tab of a parent session showing three subsessions and the Convert Submissions, Create Subsession and Sequence times buttons](/images/kb/session-editor-connections.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Submissions">
    The **Convert Submissions to Subsessions** panel lists every session on the event that can be linked, with its ID, format, and start and end. Search by title if the list is long, tick the sessions you want, and click **Confirm**.

    ![The Convert Submissions to Subsessions panel with a search box and a list of sessions, each with a checkbox](/images/kb/subsession-convert-dialog.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert To Subsessions">
    The second step shows the **Primary Session** and the **Submissions to convert**. Click **Convert Submissions** to link them. Sessionboard confirms with **Submissions converted**, and the new subsessions appear in the parent's list numbered after the parent's ID — `SESS-4-1`, `SESS-4-2`, and so on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## From the Sessions list

Use this when you have the sessions in front of you and want to send them to a parent.

1. Go to **Program → Sessions** and select the sessions using the checkbox on each row.
2. In the action bar, open **More** and, under **Sessions**, choose **Assign to Parent Session**.
3. Search for the parent by title or ID and select it. The panel checks each selected session and tells you which **Will be assigned** and which **Will be skipped** — a session is skipped if it already has subsessions of its own, is already a subsession of another parent, or is the parent you chose.
4. Click the **Assign** button — its label shows how many sessions will be assigned.

<Note>
Parent sessions that already contain subsessions, and subsessions themselves, cannot be moved this way. Unlink a subsession first if you need to move it to a different parent.
</Note>

## What happens when a session is converted

- **Dates.** A subsession must start and end inside the parent's time slot. If the session you convert already fits, it keeps its own times; if it does not — or has no times — it takes the parent's start and end. Adjust it afterwards from the subsession's **Details** tab, or use **Sequence times** on the parent to lay all subsessions out back to back (see [Schedule subsessions back to back](/sessions/create-a-subsession#schedule-subsessions-back-to-back)).
- **Speakers roll up.** The converted session's speakers, moderators and chairpersons are added to the parent as well, following **Participants shown on the parent session** in [agenda settings](/sessions/program-settings#agenda), so the parent lists everyone in the room.
- **The ID changes.** The session becomes `PARENT-n` in the parent's list and on the agenda, and keeps that number even if you reorder the subsessions later.
- **Position.** A newly linked subsession lands at the end of the parent's list. Drag it into place on the **Connections** tab — see [Reorder subsessions](/sessions/create-a-subsession#reorder-subsessions).
- **Limits.** A parent can hold up to 500 subsessions, or the lower limit you set under [session settings](/events/event-details). When the parent is at its limit, **Convert Submissions** is disabled and its tooltip says so; from the list, the panel tells you how many slots remain before you assign.

## Where the subsessions show up

On the Sessions list the parent gets an expand arrow, and its subsessions nest underneath it. They also appear on the agenda inside the parent's block, in the agenda embed, and in the speaker portal — see [Viewing subsessions within the agenda](/sessions/viewing-subsessions-within-the-agenda).

![The Sessions list with a parent session expanded to show its three subsessions nested underneath](/images/kb/sessions-list-subsessions.png)

<Tip>
Whether subsessions sync to a connected app depends on the app. Cvent has a **Sync Subsessions to Session Programs** setting; check the article for your app under **Apps**. Subsessions are always available through Sessionboard's public API, including their order.
</Tip>
