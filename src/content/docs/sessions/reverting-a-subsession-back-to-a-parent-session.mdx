Unlinking turns a subsession back into a standalone session. Nothing is deleted: the session keeps its title, description, participants and times, and simply stops being grouped under the parent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the parent session">
    Go to **Program → Sessions** and click the pencil on the parent. On the list, a parent is the row with the expand arrow; its subsessions are nested underneath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connections">
    Select the **Connections** tab. The **Subsessions** panel lists each subsession with its ID and times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlink">
    Click the **⋯** at the right of the subsession and choose **Unlink**.

    ![A subsession row's menu open on the parent's Connections tab, showing Open Subsession, Unlink and Delete](/images/kb/subsession-row-menu.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Unlink Subsession?** dialog names the session and explains that it will become a standalone session again. Click **Unlink**. Sessionboard confirms with **Subsession unlinked — The session is now standalone**.

    ![The Unlink Subsession? confirmation dialog with Cancel and Unlink buttons](/images/kb/subsession-unlink-confirm.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes when you unlink

- **It becomes a top-level session.** The session moves out from under the parent on the Sessions list and gets its own block on the agenda, at the times it already had.
- **Its position is cleared.** The order you set by dragging on the parent's **Connections** tab is scoped to that parent. If you link the session to a parent again later, it lands at the end of that parent's list.
- **Speakers are taken off the parent.** Speakers, moderators and chairpersons who were on the parent only because of this subsession are removed from the parent. Anyone who also appears on another of the parent's subsessions stays.
- **The parent's count updates.** The **Subsessions** heading, the number badge on the agenda block and any connected app that reads the parent all see one fewer subsession.

<Note>
**Unlink** is not **Delete**. The same menu has **Delete**, which moves the subsession to **Deleted** — from where it can be restored — instead of making it standalone. Use **Unlink** when the session should still exist on its own.
</Note>

<Tip>
To move a subsession to a different parent, unlink it here and then link it to the new parent with [Convert Submissions or Assign to Parent Session](/sessions/converting-a-session-to-a-subsession). Sessions that are already subsessions cannot be reassigned directly.
</Tip>
