Gatherings such as chapter meetings, dinners, labs and workshops can award continuing-education (CE) credit too. On a gathering there are no session scans: **the door check-in is the attendance**. A guest who checked in has attended every agenda item, so they can sign in to the claim site afterwards, claim the credit, answer the evaluation, and download a certificate and a transcript. It is the same claim site and transcript conference attendees use; see [Claim CE credits and download your transcript](/participants/claim-ce-credits).

<Note>
Gatherings need the **organization-wide** Credit Claiming beta. On your organization's **Preview** page, click **Request access** on the **Credit Claiming** card, and a Sessionboard CSM turns it on once for the whole organization. After that, Credit Claiming is available on every event and every gathering, and each gathering's team turns claiming on in that gathering's **Settings**. The per-event **Credit Claiming** card is still there for a single conference. Neither one needs or changes the **Learning** module.
</Note>

## Why use it

Chapters, committees and partner meetings often carry CE credit, and they used to need a separate sign-in sheet, a form and a pile of certificates afterwards. Here the check-in your door staff already does is the proof of attendance. Guests get their certificates without any extra steps from you, and the organization sees every gathering's credit in the same transcript and report as the conference.

## Turn on credit for a gathering

Open the gathering, click **Settings**, and scroll to **Continuing education credit** (between **Check-in** and **CRM**). The section appears only when your organization has Credit Claiming. Everything in it saves on its own: it isn't part of the page's **Save** bar.

![The Continuing education credit section turned on, with CPHIMS / CAHIMS CE Hours 1 and General Attendance 0 per agenda item, Require an evaluation, Checked-in guests, the claim window, the linked evaluation and the claim link](/images/kb/credit-claiming/gathering-settings.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Switch **Continuing education credit** to **On**. Guests who are already checked in are counted as attending every agenda item straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the credits offered">
    Under **Credits offered**, click **Add a credit type**, pick the type and enter the amount *per agenda item*. Add as many types as the gathering awards, such as CE hours plus **General Attendance** at `0` for a plain certificate of attendance. Tick **Require an evaluation** if guests must answer the evaluation before claiming. Click **Apply to every agenda item**.

    ![Credits offered with two credit types added and Require an evaluation ticked, before applying](/images/kb/credit-claiming/gathering-settings-credits-draft.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who can claim">
    **Who can claim** starts at **Checked-in guests**, which is what most gatherings want. **Registered guests** lets anyone with a confirmed registration claim; **Anyone with the link** skips the registration check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the claim window">
    **Claiming opens** and **Claiming closes** are filled in for you: from the end of the gathering until 90 days later. Change them and click **Save** if your accrediting body needs a different window. Certificates and the transcript stay available after it closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link an evaluation and add instructions">
    Click **Create PACE evaluation** to create and link a session evaluation; its questions and answers are in **Surveys**. Without one, guests answer a short built-in evaluation. Add **Instructions for guests** if they need to know anything, such as "Claim only the agenda items you attended."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the claim link">
    Copy the **Claim link**, or let your gathering emails carry it (see [Put the claim link in emails](#put-the-claim-link-in-emails)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If the agenda items already carry different credits, **Credits offered** says so, and the button changes to **Replace on every agenda item**. Clicking it sets the same credits on every item and replaces what was there. To keep different credits per item, click **Set per agenda item instead**.
</Warning>

## Different credit on each agenda item

**Credits offered** puts the same credit on every agenda item. When items differ (a 30-minute welcome and a 90-minute workshop, say), use **Credits per agenda item** instead. It is on the gathering's **Program** tab once claiming is on, and on the **CE credit** page under **After**. It works the same way as [Credits per session](/sessions/credit-claiming-setup#credits-per-session) on a conference: pick a credit type, amount and claim gates, tick the agenda items, and apply, or import a CSV.

![The Program tab with the Credits per agenda item button above the agenda](/images/kb/credit-claiming/gathering-program-credits-link.png)

## How attendance works on a gathering

- **Checking a guest in** at the door, or adding a walk-in, records them as attending every agenda item.
- **Turning claiming on** fills this in for every guest already checked in.
- **Adding agenda items later** is covered: a checked-in guest's new items are filled in the next time they sign in to the claim site.
- **Undoing a check-in** removes the attendance that check-in created. Attendance from any other source stays.

Guests whose attendee data your organization isn't allowed to process (processing turned off, or the guest withdrew consent) get no attendance this way. Gatherings have no attendance import; the check-in is the record.

<Note>
With **Who can claim** set to **Checked-in guests**, a guest who never checked in can't sign in to the claim site. They see *Only checked-in attendees can claim credits for this event*. Check them in with your door check-in link (**People → Check-in**) if they were there, and they can claim straight away.
</Note>

## After the gathering

The **After** tab has a **CE credit** step. It shows how many checked-in guests have claimed and how much credit was awarded, who can claim and when the window closes, with **Open** to go to the **CE credit** page. While claiming is off, the step links to **Settings** instead.

![The After tab with the CE credit step showing 3 of 4 checked-in guests claimed, 6 credits, open until Jan 18, 2027](/images/kb/credit-claiming/gathering-after-ce-credit.png)

The **CE credit** page (**After → CE credit → Open**) has the same views as a conference's [Credit Claiming page](/sessions/credit-claiming-review), minus the attendance import: **Claims**, **Credits per agenda item**, **Audit** and **Report**. **Claiming settings** takes you back to the gathering's Settings.

![The CE credit page for a gathering with Claims, Credits per agenda item, Audit and Report tabs](/images/kb/credit-claiming/gathering-credits-view.png)

![The gathering Report tab: claims, claimants, verified attendance and flagged counts, and claims by credit type](/images/kb/credit-claiming/gathering-credits-report.png)

On the **People** tab, every guest who has claimed credit shows a **Credit claimed** badge next to their name, with the number of claims when there is more than one.

![The People tab with a Credit claimed · 2 badge next to a checked-in guest](/images/kb/credit-claiming/gathering-people-credit-badge.png)

## Put the claim link in emails

Gathering emails have a `{{claim_url}}` merge tag. It holds the claim link while credit claiming is on and is empty otherwise. To show a whole line only when claiming is on, wrap it in a section:

```text
{{#claim_url}}Claim your CE credit: {{claim_url}}{{/claim_url}}
```

In the merge-tag picker these are **Claim CE credit link (when Credit Claiming is on)** and **CE credit line (only when Credit Claiming is on)**.
