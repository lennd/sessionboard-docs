Common questions about [Credit Claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming), for the team running the event and for CSMs setting it up.

## Why use it

Most claim-site support tickets come from the same handful of rules. Read them once and you can answer an attendee's email without opening a ticket.

## Certificates and credit

### How do I offer a General Attendance certificate?

Add a credit type named **General Attendance** under **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types**, then attach it to every session with an amount of `0` on **Credits per session**. Attendees who need only proof of attendance select **General Attendance** on the claim site and claim it like any other credential. Point the type at a certificate template that doesn't print an amount: the built-in CE certificate would say "0 hours". Leave the **Survey** claim requirement unticked on General Attendance, and give the type no questions, if attendees shouldn't have to answer anything to get it.

### Does an attendee get one certificate per session or per credit?

One per credit claimed. Claiming 1.5 AMA PRA Category 1 credits and 1.5 ANCC contact hours for the same session issues two certificates, each with its own certificate number (`CE-` followed by eight characters). Each one is linked from the attendee's transcript.

### Can an attendee claim only some of the credits a session offers?

Yes. On the claim site, attendees first select the credentials they want. Each session then shows, and claims, only those credentials. A nurse can claim ANCC contact hours without also claiming AMA credit.

### Can attendees claim part of a session?

No. A claim awards the amount set on the session's credit. Amounts are set per session on **Credits per session**.

## Claiming rules

### What does "One session per time slot" do?

With **One session per time slot** on, an attendee can't claim a session whose time overlaps a session they already claimed. They see: *You already claimed a session in this time slot (session title). Remove that claim first.* To claim the other session, they remove the first claim and claim the second. The rule compares session start and end times, so back-to-back sessions (one ends at 10:30, the next starts at 10:30) don't overlap. Turn it off for formats where people move between rooms, such as poster halls.

### Can attendees change their minds?

Yes, while claiming is open. **Remove** on a claimed session takes the claim off their transcript and summary. Once **Claiming closes** passes, claims can't be added or removed on the claim site, but transcripts and certificates stay available. Your team can still grant or reverse claims from the **Claims** tab.

### When is the session survey required?

When the session's credit has the **Survey** claim requirement. The attendee answers the survey once per claim, and every credential they claim for that session in the same submission shares that response. Credit without the requirement, such as General Attendance, can be claimed without the survey.

### What is the difference between credit type questions and the session survey?

The **session feedback survey** is about the session (did it meet its objectives, was it free of bias). It is set under **Claiming**, asked on credit with the **Survey** requirement, and its results are in **Surveys**. **Credit type questions** are about the credential: what one accreditor asks, such as an attestation. They are set on each credit type, asked whenever that credit is claimed (on every claim, or once per attendee), and their answers stay with the claim, in **Responses** and the **Question answers** export. A claim can ask both. See [Questions](/sessions/credit-claiming-credit-types#questions).

### Can attendees claim in the Attend app?

Yes, for credit without questions. When a credit type asks questions, the app tells the attendee to open the claim site, which asks the questions and saves the claim.

### Who can sign in?

Attendees sign in with their badge number and last name, or their registration email and last name, and **Who can claim** is checked at sign-in. With **Registered attendees**, a cancelled or unconfirmed registration is turned away; with **Checked-in attendees only**, anyone who never checked in sees *Only checked-in attendees can claim credits for this event*. Sign-in itself works before claiming opens and after it closes, so attendees can always reach their transcript.

## Attendance

### What counts as "attended"?

Attendance recorded by someone other than the attendee:

- **Session scan**: Swapcard door scans, with **Pull session attendee lists & door scans** on.
- **Imported attendance**: rows from the attendance CSV import.
- **Check-in**: an Attend app room check-in (room QR code or a monitor scanning the badge).

Attendance an attendee reports about themselves never counts. It doesn't mark a session **Attended**, it doesn't show the session under **Scanned sessions only**, and it doesn't clear the **No attendance record** flag on the **Audit** tab.

### An attendee was in the room but has no scan. What now?

With **All sessions** or **Scanned sessions first**, they can still claim the session; the claim shows **No attendance record** on the **Audit** tab. With **Scanned sessions only**, or when the credit has the **Attendance** claim requirement, they can't claim it until attendance exists. Add a row for them to an attendance CSV and import it, or grant the credit yourself with **Grant credit** on the **Claims** tab.

### What happens to attendance and claims when two people are merged?

Credit Claiming reads the whole merged record. If an attendee's scans were recorded on one record and they sign in to the claim site as the other (for example, a different email), their scanned sessions still show as **Attended**, the **Attendance** claim requirement accepts those scans, and the transcript's **Attendance verified by** detail uses them. Claims stay on the record they were made on, so an attendee should keep signing in the same way (the same badge number, or the same email) to see all of their claims in one transcript.

## Timezones

### Which timezone do the claim site and transcript use?

- The claim site and the on-screen transcript show session times in the **event's timezone**, with the zone next to each time (for example `2:00 PM – 3:00 PM PST`), whatever timezone the attendee's device is in.
- The **Download PDF** and **Download Excel** transcripts show times in the **event's timezone**, with the zone abbreviation (for example `2:00 PM – 3:30 PM PDT`). Check the event's timezone under event settings before attendees download transcripts.
- **Claiming opens** and **Claiming closes** are entered in the event's timezone; the settings say which zone under the two fields.
- Attendance CSV times (`occurred_at`) should include a timezone, such as `Z` or `-07:00`.

## Attendees, exports and access

### Which languages does the claim site support?

English, German, Spanish and French. The claim site opens in the attendee's browser language when it is one of these, and in English otherwise. Attendees can switch with the language menu at the top right, and the claim site remembers their choice on that device.

### How do I export claims for my accrediting body?

Click **Export** on any tab of the Credit Claiming page and choose CSV or Excel. Exports follow the filters on screen, run in the background, and stay under **History → Exports**. See [Export](/sessions/credit-claiming-review#export).

### How do I give a partner association access to the claims only?

Give them the **Credit Claiming Viewer** role on the event: they see claims, attendance, the audit, reports and exports, and nothing else. **Credit Claiming Manager** can also change things. Or share the live Credit Claiming dashboard from **Insights → Share live report**, which needs no sign-in. See [Give someone access to Credit Claiming only](/sessions/credit-claiming-roles).

### Can I see who changed a claim or a setting?

Yes. The **Activity** tab logs attendee sign-ins, claims, removals and downloads, and every staff grant, reversal, import, credit change, settings change and export, with who did it and when. The same entries are in **History** under **Credit claim**.

## Learning and Early Access

### How is Credit Claiming different from the Learning module?

Credit Claiming is an event-level Early Access Beta for the after-the-show claim site. It lives under **Program** and needs nothing else turned on. **Learning** (Lessons & Credits) is a separate organization-level module for lessons, series and year-round CE. They share the same credit types and claims, so a claim made on the claim site also appears in Learning if your organization has it. Turning Credit Claiming on or off never changes Learning.

### How do we get Credit Claiming?

There are two **Credit Claiming** cards. The one on an event's **Early Access** page turns it on for that event only. The one on the organization's **Early Access** page turns it on once for every event and field event in the organization. Click **Request access** on the one you need, and a Sessionboard CSM turns it on. Field events need the organization-wide card; see [Credit claiming for field events](/sessions/credit-claiming-gatherings).

### Does Credit Claiming work on field events?

Yes, with the organization-wide card. On a field event the door check-in counts as attending every agenda item, and you turn claiming on in the field event's **Settings**. See [Credit claiming for field events](/sessions/credit-claiming-gatherings).
