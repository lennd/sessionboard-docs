Common questions about [Credit Claiming](/sessions/credit-claiming), for the team running the event and for CSMs setting it up.

## Why use it

Most claim-site support tickets come from the same handful of rules. Read them once and you can answer an attendee's email without opening a ticket.

## Certificates and credit

### How do I offer a General Attendance certificate?

Add a credit type named **General Attendance** under **Program → Configure → Settings → Credit types**, then attach it to every session with an amount of `0` on **Credits per session**. Attendees who need only proof of attendance select **General Attendance** on the claim site and claim it like any other credential. Point the type at a certificate template that doesn't print an amount: the built-in CE certificate would say "0 hours". Leave **Eval** unticked on General Attendance if attendees shouldn't have to evaluate a session to get it.

### Does an attendee get one certificate per session or per credit?

One per credit claimed. Claiming 1.5 AMA PRA Category 1 credits and 1.5 ANCC contact hours for the same session issues two certificates, each with its own certificate number (`CE-` followed by eight characters). Each one is linked from the attendee's transcript.

### Can an attendee claim only some of the credits a session offers?

Yes. On the claim site, attendees first select the credentials they want. Each session then shows, and claims, only those credentials. A nurse can claim ANCC contact hours without also claiming AMA credit.

### Can attendees claim part of a session?

No. A claim awards the amount set on the session's credit. Amounts are set per session on **Credits per session**.

## Claiming rules

### What does "One session per time slot" do?

With **One session per time slot** on, an attendee can't claim a session whose time overlaps a session they already claimed. They see: *You already claimed a session in this time slot (session title). Remove that claim first.* To claim the other session, they remove the first claim and claim the second. The rule compares session start and end times, so back-to-back sessions (one ends at 10:30, the next starts at 10:30) don't overlap. Turn it off for formats where people move between rooms, such as poster halls.

### Can attendees change their minds?

Yes, while claiming is open. **Remove** on a claimed session takes the claim off their transcript and summary. Once **Claiming closes** passes, claims can't be added or removed on the claim site, but transcripts and certificates stay available. Your team can still grant or reverse claims from the **Claims** tab.

### When is the evaluation required?

When the session's credit has the **Eval** claim gate. The attendee answers the evaluation once per claim, and every credential they claim for that session in the same submission shares that response. Credit without the gate, such as General Attendance, can be claimed without an evaluation.

### Who can sign in?

Anyone with a registration for the event: they enter their badge number and last name, or their registration email and last name. **Who can claim** then decides who may claim: confirmed or checked-in registrations, checked-in only, or anyone who signed in. Sign-in works before claiming opens and after it closes, so attendees can always reach their transcript.

## Attendance

### What counts as "attended"?

Attendance recorded by someone other than the attendee:

- **Session scan**: Swapcard door scans, with **Pull session attendee lists & door scans** on.
- **Imported attendance**: rows from the attendance CSV import.
- **Check-in**: an Attend app room check-in (room QR code or a monitor scanning the badge).

Attendance an attendee reports about themselves never counts. It doesn't mark a session **Attended**, it doesn't show the session under **Scanned sessions only**, and it doesn't clear the **No verified attendance** flag on the **Audit** tab.

### An attendee was in the room but has no scan. What now?

With **All sessions** or **Scanned sessions first**, they can still claim the session; the claim shows **No verified attendance** on the **Audit** tab. With **Scanned sessions only**, or when the credit has the **Attendance** claim gate, they can't claim it until attendance exists. Add a row for them to an attendance CSV and import it, or grant the credit yourself from **Grant credit manually** on the **Claims** tab.

### What happens to attendance and claims when two people are merged?

Credit Claiming reads the whole merged record. If an attendee's scans were recorded on one record and they sign in to the claim site as the other (for example, a different email), their scanned sessions still show as **Attended**, the attendance gate accepts those scans, and the transcript's **Attendance verified by** detail uses them. Claims stay on the record they were made on, so an attendee should keep signing in the same way (the same badge number, or the same email) to see all of their claims in one transcript.

## Timezones

### Which timezone do the claim site and transcript use?

- The claim site and the on-screen transcript show session times in the **attendee's own device timezone**.
- The **Download PDF** and **Download Excel** transcripts show times in the **event's timezone**, with the zone abbreviation (for example `2:00 PM – 3:30 PM PDT`). Check the event's timezone under event settings before attendees download transcripts.
- **Claiming opens** and **Claiming closes** are entered in the timezone of the person editing the settings.
- Attendance CSV times (`occurred_at`) should include a timezone, such as `Z` or `-07:00`.

## Learning and Early Access

### How is Credit Claiming different from the Learning module?

Credit Claiming is an event-level Early Access Beta for the after-the-show claim site. It lives under **Program** and needs nothing else turned on. **Learning** (Lessons & Credits) is a separate organization-level module for lessons, series and year-round CE. They share the same credit types and claims, so a claim made on the claim site also appears in Learning if your organization has it. Turning Credit Claiming on or off never changes Learning.

### How do we get Credit Claiming?

Open the event's **Preview** page and click **Request access** on the **Credit Claiming** card. A Sessionboard CSM turns it on for that event. Each event that needs a claim site is enabled separately.
