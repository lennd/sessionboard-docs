An embed comes in one of five formats: **Embed Styled HTML**, **Embed HTML**, **JSON Feed**, **XML Feed** or **iCal Feed**. The two HTML formats give you five widgets to paste onto a page; the feed formats give an app or database the same data. You can create as many embeds as you need — one per website section, per language page or per audience.

## Why use it

Your agenda already lives on your own website; embeds keep it there and keep it current. Instead of exporting and re-pasting a schedule every time a room or speaker changes, the embed reads straight from Sessionboard, in your site's styling, with any session field — credits, level, track — shown in the pop-up your visitors open.

## Embed Outputs

<Tip>
### Search Behavior
- **Participant details** are always searchable: name, company name, job title, and bio. This applies to every participant role the embed is set to display — speakers, chairpersons, moderators, and any custom role — not only speakers.

- **Schedule Itinerary** & **Sessions**: search matches **session titles** plus the participant details above. It also matches **sub-session titles** and **sub-session participants** (same name, company, job title, and bio fields, same role rule). A match still returns the parent session — schedule embeds show the sub-session nested inline; session and speaker embeds open it from the Sub-sessions tab.

- **Speaker Gallery** & **Speakers**: search matches the participant details above.

- Session detail fields — description, location, format, track, tags, level, and language — are searchable when you enable the matching filter or include the field in the embed. They are not searched when the embed hides them.

- Custom session and contact fields are not searched.

Search never reaches further than the embed shows. A participant role you have turned off, or a field you have not included, is not searchable in that embed.
</Tip>

There are five types of embeds you can create: Schedule Itinerary, Speaker Gallery, Agenda, Sessions, and Speakers.

- **Schedule Itinerary**

  - Search for relevant sessions by keywords (session title or participant details)

  - Use multiple filters to view sessions by format, tag, track, and location.

  - Speakers, chairpersons, and moderators are prominently shown with headshots and biographical information

    ![Schedule Itinerary](/images/kb/adf3004e-Schedule-Itinerary.png)

- **Speaker Gallery** *(speakers are displayed alphabetically by last name)*

  - Showcase your speaker's headshots and relevant information

  - Click on a speaker's image to showcase more speaker information

    ![Speaker Gallery](/images/kb/dee70ecd-Speaker-Gallery.png)

- **Agenda**

  - Displays approved sessions in a grid by location/time

  - Click on a session to view more information, such as times and location

  - Click on a speaker to view more information, such as the title and biography field.
    

    ![Agenda-1](/images/kb/4dd2a865-Agenda-1.png)

- **Session list**

  - Display a list of sessions with speakers and relevant information

  - Click on a speaker to view more information, such as the title and biography fields

  - Use multiple filters to view sessions by format, language, tag, track, and location
    ![Session list](/images/kb/3a707846-Session-list.png)

- **Speakers**

  - Display a list of speakers with their sessions and relevant information

  - Click on a session to view more information, such as times and locations

## How To Create An Embed

Creating an embed does not need any technical knowledge. The builder saves as you go — the status under the embed name reads **Saving…**, then **Saved** — so there is no Save button; click **Back** when you are done.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the embed">
On **CMS → Embeds**, click **Add Embed** and choose **Add Embed** from the menu. **Copy from…** starts from an embed on another event in your organization instead of from scratch.

![The Add Embed menu on the Embeds page with Add Embed and Copy from…](/images/kb/embeds-add-menu.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Type">
Give the embed a **Name**, leave **Enabled** on, and choose the **Format**:

- **Embed Styled HTML** — one line of code that shows an interactive Schedule Itinerary, Agenda, Sessions list, Speakers list or Speaker Gallery, in Sessionboard's styling with your colors.
- **Embed HTML** — the same widgets as plain HTML, for a web developer to style with your own CSS.
- **JSON Feed** and **XML Feed** — structured data for an app or database.
- **iCal Feed** — a calendar link that lists every approved session as a calendar event. Handy for organizers to share with each other.

The format is locked once the embed is saved; create a new embed to use a different one.

![The Type section of a new embed with Name, Enabled and the five format cards](/images/kb/embeds-type.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Style Options (HTML formats only)">
- **Website Color Theme** (Light or Dark), **Primary Color** and **Extra CSS Code** — Embed Styled HTML only. Sessionboard does not validate custom CSS.
- **Date/Time Format** — how session times are written.
- **Day heading format** — how each day is labeled on the Schedule Itinerary day tabs and the Agenda day heading, for example *Tuesday, October 20*, *Tue, Oct 20, 2026*, *20 October 2026* or *10/20/2026*. **Default** keeps the label the embed has always shown. Day and month names follow the embed language; this setting only changes their order and how much of the date appears.
- **Language** — the default language for every widget in this embed: English, German, French or Spanish. See [Embed languages](#embed-languages) for how one embed serves several language pages.
- **Session and Speaker** — *Click session or speaker to open pop-out view*, *Display schedule in browser timezone*, *Show add to calendar button*, *Show session sponsors* and *Search session/speaker by name*. Under **Speaker Embeds**, *Hide session details* keeps sessions off the speaker widgets.
- **Participant Roles** — which roles appear across all widgets: the speaker lists *and* the people on session, agenda and schedule cards. Speakers, Chairpersons and Moderators are on by default, and any custom roles you have created for the event are listed too. Participants are grouped under their role name (for example "Panelists") on cards and pop-out views.

![The Style Options section with theme, primary color, date and day formats, language, extra CSS and the display checkboxes](/images/kb/embeds-style-options.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Filters">
Filters has two different jobs:

- **Attendee filters** — checkboxes for format, track, location, language, level and tag. Each one adds a dropdown to the public Schedule Itinerary and Sessions list so visitors can narrow the schedule. A field does not need to be shown on the session cards for its dropdown to appear.
- **Included sessions** — rules that decide which sessions are in the feed at all: *Status is Accepted*, a specific track, and so on. **Add filter** adds another rule. **Contact Filters** does the same for who appears on the Speakers and Speaker Gallery widgets, using standard or custom contact fields; once you have at least one contact filter you can also switch on **Include contacts without sessions**. The line under the rules — *12 sessions and 15 speakers match the session filters* — updates as you edit them.

![The Filters section: attendee filter checkboxes, an Included sessions rule and the live match count](/images/kb/embeds-filters.png)

<Note>
Some widgets only show sessions that have a start and end time.
</Note>
</Step>
<Step title="Field Options">
Choose what each widget displays. Fields are grouped in three tabs — **Agenda**, **Speaker** and **Session** — and the tabs that can affect the widget currently in the preview are the ones you can edit. Fields marked **Required** are always included; everything else is your choice, and **Select All** ticks the rest of a tab. You can also [rename a field for this embed only](#custom-field-labels).

- **Agenda** — the fields in the pop-up that opens from a session in the calendar grid, from every standard and custom session field. Pick up to nine; the counter (for example "4 of 9 fields selected") shows how many you have, and the rest can't be ticked once you reach nine. JSON and XML feeds aren't limited. Ticking **Starts At** or **Ends At** here also prints the time range on the card in the grid.
- **Speaker** — the fields on each speaker card in the Speakers list and Speaker Gallery.
- **Session** — the fields in the Sessions list; these also drive the Schedule Itinerary.

**Sort Order**, at the bottom of the section, controls ordering:

- **Session speakers (Itinerary, Agenda, Session List)** — **As listed on the session** follows the speaker order on each session, including any reorder after submit, and is the default for new embeds. **Alphabetical by last name** overrides that on session cards and the session pop-up. Existing embeds keep whichever option they were saved with.
- **Speakers (List & Gallery)** — a sort field and direction for the standalone Speakers list and Speaker Gallery only. It does not change names on a session card.
- **Session List** — a sort field and direction for the Sessions list.
- **Schedule Itinerary tie-break** — used when sessions start at the same time; the primary order stays chronological.

![The Field Options section with the Agenda, Speaker and Session tabs and the Sort Order controls](/images/kb/embeds-field-options.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Get the code">
The right-hand panel switches between **Preview** — the widget rendered with your live data, on a desktop or mobile viewport — and **Get Code**. Get Code lists one snippet per widget: Schedule Itinerary, Speaker Gallery, Agenda, Sessions and Speakers, each with **Copy** and **Preview** buttons. Paste one snippet per page; do not put two on the same page. **Snippet language** adds a `language` attribute so a localized page renders in that language. Further down, **Shareable session links** builds a public URL that opens the embed with one or more sessions highlighted.

Copy the snippet and hand it to whoever maintains your website. Feed formats show their URL here instead of a snippet.

![The Get Code panel with the Snippet language picker and the Schedule Itinerary snippet](/images/kb/embeds-get-code.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
New embeds use the speaker order on the session. Existing embeds keep the order you already saved. After you change **Sort Order**, refresh the embed cache so the public page updates.
</Note>

## Custom Field Labels

A field's name comes from your field library, so it reads the same everywhere in Sessionboard. When a public page needs different wording — "Topic Area" instead of "Track", or "Organization" instead of "Company Name" — you can give the field a label that applies to one embed only. The field itself, your forms, and every other embed are untouched.

1. Open the embed and expand **Field Options**.
2. On the **Speaker** or **Session** tab, make sure the field is checked.
3. Click the **...** button at the right of that field's row and choose **Customize label**.
4. Type the wording you want and click **Save**. The custom label appears under the field name in the list, so you can always see which fields you have renamed.

Labels can be up to 255 characters, matching the limit on field names elsewhere. To go back to the field's real name, reopen the dialog and click **Reset to default**. Unchecking a field also removes its custom label.

Because the label belongs to the embed, two embeds on the same event can show the same field under different names — useful when one page is for attendees and another is for press or sponsors.

<Note>
Custom labels are available on **Embed Styled HTML** and **Embed HTML**. JSON and XML embeds keep their original field keys, because those are a data contract that apps and databases read. The **Agenda** tab has no label option either: the calendar grid lays values out by position and never prints a field name.
</Note>

<Tip>
***Note:*** *You may have to 'Refresh the Cache' to see a new label on the live page.*
</Tip>

If the field also has a [translation](/settings/language-translation-variant) saved for a language your event has enabled, visitors viewing that language see the translation rather than the custom label.

## Embed Languages

One embed configuration serves every language — you do not need a separate embed per language.

- **Default language:** set in **Style Options → Language** (English, German, French, or Spanish). All widgets from this embed render their labels, buttons, search, and date formats in that language.
- **Per-page override:** the **Get Code** panel has a *Snippet language* picker. Choosing a language adds a `language` attribute to the embed code (for example `language="de"`), so you can paste a German snippet on your German page and a French snippet on your French page — same embed, no extra setup.
- **Translated content:** if your event uses [language variants](/settings/language-translation-variant), translated session titles, descriptions, and field values appear automatically when the embed renders in that language. That includes the option labels of Track, Format, Level, and Language when you have translated them on the field — the agenda's day tables, the schedule, and session and speaker detail all show the translated name. Anything you have not translated falls back to your default language — content never goes blank.

- **Right-to-left languages:** when an embed renders in a right-to-left language such as Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, or Urdu (for example `language="ar"`), every widget is laid out right to left — text aligns from the right, and columns, arrows, and pagination are mirrored. Switch the language back and the layout returns to left to right.

<Note>
Widget labels and dates translate for any of the four supported languages. Your own content translates only for languages enabled as language variants on the event. Role names you have renamed (for example "Faculty" instead of "Speakers") display exactly as you wrote them in every language; unrenamed standard roles translate with the rest of the widget.
</Note>

## Subsession Details

Sessions with subsessions show them inside the session pop-out view. Visitors can click a subsession to open its own detail view — with its time, location, description, and participants — and click any participant from there to see their profile. The Back button returns one step at a time.

Subsessions are listed in the order set on the parent session's "Connections" tab in Sessionboard, so drag them there to change what visitors see — see [Reordering subsessions](/sessions/create-a-subsession#reorder-subsessions). Subsessions without a set position follow the ordered ones, chronologically by start time.

## Embed Update Frequency

Embeds refresh on their own every 60 minutes. To push a change to the public page sooner, open **CMS → Embeds**, click **More actions** on the embed's card and choose **Refresh Cache**; the page updates within minutes.

![The More actions menu on an embed card with Edit, Get Code, Refresh Cache and Delete](/images/kb/embeds-card-menu.png)

## How To Edit Embeds

Click the card's name, or **More actions → Edit**, to reopen the builder. Every setting except the format can be changed, and changes save as you make them. If you need a different format, create a new embed. **Get Code** on the card jumps straight to the snippets, and the **Enabled** switch in the Type section takes an embed off the air without deleting it — the card shows **Disabled**, and the **All**, **Enabled** and **Disabled** chips above the list filter on that state.

<Tip>
You may have to **Refresh Cache** to see an edit on the live page.
</Tip>
