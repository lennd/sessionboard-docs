## (Parent) Session vs. Subsession Overview

In Sessionboard, a parent session is the main session that appears on your agenda—it acts as the overarching container for related content. A subsession, on the other hand, is a smaller session that takes place within the timeframe or structure of the parent session.Think of a parent session like a full-day workshop, and subsessions as the individual presentations or panels happening within that workshop. Subsessions are grouped under the parent but can have their own titles, speakers, and details.

This structure is helpful when organizing complex events with multiple segments under one larger theme or session block.

When accessing a portal as a subsession speaker, I can see the parent session as well as other associated subsessions.

You can create subsessions from scratch (continue reading below to learn more), create them in bulk from submitted abstracts, or convert existing sessions into subsessions. If needed, you can also revert a subsession to a primary session.

### How To Create A Subsession

<Note>
A parent session can hold up to 500 subsessions. You can set a lower limit for your event — and apply different limits to different kinds of sessions — under Record Settings.
</Note>

1. Navigate to the "Sessions" module.

![How To Create A Subsession](/images/kb/c9e7ff96-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-09-37-9881-PM.png)

2. Select the pencil icon to the left of the session you wish to create a subsession.

   ![Select the pencil icon to the left of the session you wish to create a subsession](/images/kb/2940020f-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-10-13-0906-PM.png)

3. Within the parent session, select the tab "Connections".

   ![Within the parent session, select the tab "Connections"](/images/kb/91fb615d-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-10-46-2776-PM.png)

4. Click on "Create subsession" to the right of the window.

   ![Click on "Create subsession" to the right of the window](/images/kb/60973689-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-11-34-9733-PM.png)

5. Add your subsession details, including name, time, format, description, and speakers
   1. NOTE: The date and time of the submission MUST fall within the date and time of the parent session.
   2. NOTE: Speakers for the parent and subsession are linked. As a result, if you add a new speaker not listed in the original parent session, the speaker will be added not only to the subsession but to the parent session as well.

   ![Add your subsession details, including name, time, format, description, and speakers](/images/kb/11d049c7-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-12-03-2368-PM.png)
6. Once you are done, click the "Create Session" button at the bottom right-hand corner of the pop-up window.

### How To Create Subsessions From Abstracts

If you collect abstracts through a call for papers, you can turn a batch of them into subsessions in one step instead of rekeying each one.

1. Navigate to the "Programs" module and open the "Submissions" view.
2. Select the abstracts you want using the checkbox to the left of each record.
3. In the action bar at the top, open the **Abstracts** menu and choose **Create a subsession**.
4. In the side panel, choose where the subsessions should live:
   1. **Create a new parent session** — enter a title, and Sessionboard creates the parent for you.
   2. **Add to an existing session** — search for and select the parent session.
5. Click Save. Each selected abstract becomes its own subsession under that parent, inheriting the abstract's title, description, track, format, level, and language, and taking the parent's date and time.

A few things to know about this flow:

- **The abstract is not consumed.** Each abstract stays in your Submissions list, marked with a "Linked" badge, and remains fully editable — including its presenters. Open it any time to make corrections; you do not need to unwind anything first.
- **Participants roll up.** Speakers on each abstract are added to the new subsession and to the parent session, matching how subsession speakers behave elsewhere.
- **Linked abstracts cannot be deleted, withdrawn, or duplicated** while the link exists, so the subsession never ends up pointing at a missing record. Remove the link from the abstract's "Connections" tab first if you need to do one of those.
- If your selection would push the parent past its subsession limit, Sessionboard tells you before creating anything — nothing is created partially.

To combine several abstracts into a single abstract instead of creating subsessions, use **Merge into one abstract** in the same menu.

### Reordering subsessions

By default subsessions are listed in the order they were created, with start time as the tie-break. You can set your own order instead.

1. Open the parent session and select the "Connections" tab.
2. In the subsessions list, drag a subsession by the grip handle on its left edge to a new position. The handle appears once the session has more than one subsession.
3. Release it. Sessionboard saves the new order immediately and confirms with "Subsession order saved"; if the save fails, the previous order is restored.

The list can be reordered with the keyboard, too. Tab to the handle, press Space or Enter to pick the subsession up, move it with the arrow keys, and press Space or Enter again to drop it (Escape cancels).

A few things to know:

- **Display IDs do not change.** A subsession keeps its number and its ID whatever position you drag it to, so references in exports and emails stay valid.
- **The agenda and embeds follow the admin order.** The parent's pop-out in the agenda, the Agenda embed, and the session detail view list subsessions in the order you set.
- **New subsessions land at the end.** A subsession created or linked after you reordered has no position yet, so it is listed after the ordered ones, chronologically by start time. Drag it into place to include it. Unlinking a subsession, or moving it to another parent, clears its position.
- **Integration partners receive the order.** Subsessions read through Sessionboard's public API include their position and come back in the same order.

### Frequently Asked Questions
1. Can I email subsession speakers only?
   1. The workflow to email speakers will stay the same. Emails can be sent through the parent session or via the subsession by selecting the square to the left of their name and clicking the "Send Emails" button at the top of the page. From there, you can adjust your recipients by changing the "Who should receive this email?" field to "Select Individual Contacts".
2. Can I add a moderator or chairperson to a subsession?
   1. Moderators and Chairpersons are only associated with parent sessions.
3. Can I add a sponsor or exhibitor to a subsession?
   1. Sponsors and exhibitors are only associated with parent sessions.
4. How are subsessions ordered?
   1. In the order you set by dragging them on the parent session's "Connections" tab — see [Reordering subsessions](#reordering-subsessions). Until you reorder them, subsessions are listed in creation order, with start time as the tie-break.
5. Are subsessions shown within embeds?
   1. Yes, subsessions are visible within embeds.

   ![Frequently Asked Questions](/images/kb/608daa99-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-15-08-2715-PM.png)

6. I am currently using an integration. Do my subsessions sync with the integration partner system?
   1. At this moment, subsession information can not be synced with any of our direct partners. Subsession details will be exposed within Sessionboard's open API.
