A **parent session** is the block on your agenda. A **subsession** is a segment inside it — three papers in one poster session, five talks in a lightning round, the individual presentations within a full-day workshop. Subsessions are grouped under the parent but have their own titles, speakers, times, and details, and a subsession speaker sees the parent and its other subsessions in their portal.

You can create subsessions from scratch, create them in bulk from submitted abstracts, or [convert existing sessions into subsessions](/sessions/converting-a-session-to-a-subsession). A subsession can also be [reverted to a parent session](/sessions/reverting-a-subsession-back-to-a-parent-session).

## Create a subsession

<Note>
A parent session can hold up to 500 subsessions. You can set a lower limit for your event — and apply different limits to different kinds of sessions — under [session settings](/events/event-details).
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the parent session">
    Go to **Program → Sessions** and click the pencil on the session that will be the parent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connections">
    Select the **Connections** tab. The **Subsessions** panel lists any existing subsessions, with **Convert Submissions** and **Create Subsession** at the top.

    ![The Connections tab of a session with the Subsessions panel and its Convert Submissions and Create Subsession buttons](/images/kb/session-editor-connections.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Subsession">
    Click **Create Subsession**. The panel has the same **Details** and **Participants** tabs as a new session — title, status, description, start and end, format, track, and speakers.

    ![The Create Subsession panel with the same details fields as a new session](/images/kb/subsession-create.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Subsession** at the bottom of the panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two rules apply:

- The subsession's date and time must fall **within the parent session's** date and time.
- Speakers on the parent and its subsessions are linked. Add a speaker to a subsession and they are added to the parent too — following **Participants shown on the parent session** in [agenda settings](/sessions/program-settings#agenda) — so the parent always knows who is in the room.

## Create subsessions from abstracts

If you collect abstracts through a call for papers, you can turn a batch of them into subsessions in one step instead of rekeying each one.

1. Go to **Program → Sessions** and select the abstracts using the checkbox on each row.
2. In the action bar, open the **Abstracts** menu and choose **Create a subsession**.
3. In the side panel, choose where the subsessions should live: **Create a new parent session** (enter a title and Sessionboard creates it) or **Add to an existing session** (search and select the parent).
4. Click **Save**. Each selected abstract becomes its own subsession under that parent, inheriting the abstract's title, description, track, format, level, and language, and taking the parent's date and time.

A few things to know about this flow:

- **The abstract is not consumed.** Each abstract stays in your Sessions list, marked with a **Linked** badge, and remains fully editable — including its presenters. Open it any time to make corrections.
- **Participants roll up.** Speakers on each abstract are added to the new subsession. Which of them also appear on the parent follows **Participants shown on the parent session** in the side panel, which starts on the event's [agenda setting](/sessions/program-settings#agenda): all participants, the presenting author only, or none.
- **Linked abstracts cannot be deleted, withdrawn, or duplicated** while the link exists, so the subsession never points at a missing record. Remove the link from the abstract's **Connections** tab first. If you try to delete a linked abstract, the delete fails with a message saying why.
- If your selection would push the parent past its subsession limit, Sessionboard tells you before creating anything — nothing is created partially.

To combine several abstracts into a single abstract instead, use **Merge into one abstract** in the same menu.

## Reorder subsessions

By default subsessions are listed in the order they were created, with start time as the tie-break. To set your own order, open the parent's **Connections** tab and drag a subsession by the grip handle on its left edge (the handle appears once there is more than one subsession). Sessionboard saves the new order immediately and confirms with **Subsession order saved**; if the save fails, the previous order is restored.

The list can be reordered with the keyboard, too: Tab to the handle, press Space or Enter to pick the subsession up, move it with the arrow keys, and press Space or Enter again to drop it (Escape cancels).

- **Display IDs do not change.** A subsession keeps its number and its ID whatever position you drag it to, so references in exports and emails stay valid.
- **The agenda and embeds follow the admin order.** The parent's pop-out in the agenda, the Agenda embed, and the session detail view list subsessions in the order you set.
- **New subsessions land at the end.** A subsession created or linked after you reordered has no position yet, so it is listed after the ordered ones, by start time. Drag it into place to include it. Unlinking a subsession, or moving it to another parent, clears its position.
- **Integration partners receive the order.** Subsessions read through Sessionboard's public API include their position and come back in the same order.

## Schedule subsessions back to back

Instead of setting each subsession's time by hand, you can schedule them in sequence.

1. Open the parent's **Connections** tab and click **Sequence times**.
2. Set **First subsession starts (…)** — shown in the event's time zone —, a **Default duration (minutes)**, and an optional **Gap between subsessions (minutes)**. Under **Duration by session type (minutes)**, give a type its own length; leave it blank to use the default.
3. Reorder the subsessions if needed, then click **Preview** to see each one's start and end time.
4. Click **Apply**.

The parent session's times are not changed. If the last subsession would end after the parent ends, the preview warns you before you apply.

## Common questions

**Can I email subsession speakers only?**
Yes. Select the subsession's speakers with the checkbox on the left and click **Send Emails**, then set **Who should receive this email?** to **Select Individual Contacts**. Emails from the parent session reach everyone on it.

**Can I add a moderator, chairperson, sponsor, or exhibitor to a subsession?**
No — those roles belong to the parent session.

**Are subsessions shown in embeds and the portal?**
Yes. The agenda embed and the session detail view list a parent's subsessions in the admin order, and a subsession speaker sees the parent and its other subsessions in their portal — see [viewing subsessions within the agenda](/sessions/viewing-subsessions-within-the-agenda).

**Do subsessions sync to integrations?**
It depends on the app. Cvent has a **Sync Subsessions to Session Programs** setting; see [Cvent](/integrations/cvent) and the article for your app under **Apps**. Subsessions are always available through Sessionboard's public API, including their order.
