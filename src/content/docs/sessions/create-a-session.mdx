## The sessions list

Open **Program → Sessions**. Every session in the event is one row. The tabs across the top are the statuses — **All Sessions**, **Accepted**, **Accept Queue**, **Pending**, **Decline Queue**, **Declined**, **Withdrawn**, **Drafts** — and the **Source** column tells you whether a session came in through a form or was added by hand.

![The Sessions list with the status tabs across the top and one row per session](/images/kb/sessions-list.png)

## Add a session by hand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add Session">
    Click **Add Session** at the top right of the Sessions list. A panel slides in from the right with two tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Details">
    **Title** and **Status** are the only two you must fill. Then description, **Starts At** and **Ends At**, **Capacity**, **CEU Credits**, **Client ID**, and the program vocabulary — **Format**, **Language**, **Level**, **Track**, **Tags** — and a location. Every dropdown here is populated from [program settings](/sessions/program-settings).

    ![The Add Session panel on the Details tab with title, status, dates, and the program fields](/images/kb/sessions-add-session.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Participants">
    Who's on the session: **Speakers**, **Chairpersons**, **Moderators**, the **Submitter**, **Sponsors**, and **Exhibitors**. **Add** on each block searches your contacts.

    ![The Participants tab of the Add Session panel with a block for each role](/images/kb/sessions-add-session-participants.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Session">
    Click **Create Session**. The new session appears in the list in the status you chose, with **Source** showing it was added by an admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If a speaker, sponsor, or chairperson doesn't come up when you search, they don't exist as a contact yet. Create them under **CRM** first — see [Add & delete a contact](/contacts/add-delete-a-contact) — then come back.
</Note>

## Custom fields

The Add Session panel takes the standard fields. To fill in your custom fields:

1. Create the session.
2. Click the pencil on its row to open the session editor. **Search fields** at the top finds any field on the page.
3. **Custom Fields** sit at the bottom of the **Details** tab. Click a value to edit it in place; each edit saves.

![The session editor open on the Details tab with description, general fields, categorization, and custom fields](/images/kb/session-editor.png)

<Tip>
You can also fill custom fields straight from the list: add the field as a column in your [table view](/reporting/dashboard-views) and click the cell to edit it.
</Tip>
