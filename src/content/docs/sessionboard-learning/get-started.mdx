Sessionboard Learning turns the recordings and meetings you already have into courses, certifications and credit. You review and publish everything; nothing goes live until you say so.

## Sign up

<Steps>
<Step title="Say who learns here">
On **Start Sessionboard Learning**, pick everyone who learns from you: **Members and the public**, **Customers and partners** or **Employees**.
</Step>
<Step title="Say where your courses are today">
Choose **Recordings and videos**, **A webinar series**, **Documents and slides** or **In another LMS**, or **Nothing yet**.
</Step>
<Step title="Name your first course">
Type the first course you want to build, or choose **Not sure yet**.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm your work email">
Enter your work email. We email you a link and a 6-digit code: select **Continue setting up** in the email, or type the code. Either one works once, for 15 minutes. There is no password. Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and use your work account.
</Step>
<Step title="Name your organization">
**What's your organization called?** comes filled in from your email's domain. Change it if it isn't right, then send it.
</Step>
<Step title="Start your trial">
Select **Open my Learning workspace**. You start a 14-day trial of the Growth plan with everything on and no card needed.
</Step>
</Steps>

Already use Sessionboard Marketing? Open **Learning** under **Sessionboard platform** in your sidebar and select **Add Learning to this workspace**. Only an organization admin can add it.


## Sign in

There is no password. Go to **Sign in to Sessionboard Learning**, enter your work email and select **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the email and select **Sign in**, or type the 6-digit code on the page. The link and the code work once, for 15 minutes. If your email opens the link for you, it asks you to select **Continue as** your address first, so the link is still good when you get to it.

Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and pick your work account. The first time you use one with an account that already exists, we email you a 6-digit code to confirm it's you; after that, it signs you straight in. Two-step sign-in still applies when your team uses it.

If you set a password for this account before, it no longer signs you in here: use the emailed link, the code, Google or Microsoft. An account that also works in Sessionboard's platform keeps its password, and an account whose company uses SSO keeps that sign-in.

## Answer the setup questions

Until setup is finished, **Home** walks you through five questions. Each answer is saved as you give it, and nothing is published.

1. **Who learns here?** Members and the public sets your organization up for associations: credit types, transcripts and claims are on. Otherwise it is set up for companies: certifications and assignments come first, and Credits stays off until you need it.
2. **Which credit do you offer?** For example AMA PRA Category 1 Credit™, MOC, ANCC nursing contact hours, ACPE pharmacy, CLE, CPE or CEU. Companies first say whether courses carry credit at all.
3. **Where are your courses and recordings today?**
4. **How should learners sign in?** **A link by email**, **Through our AMS** or **Our single sign-on**. Sign-in through your AMS and single sign-on come with the Association plan (Enterprise for companies); **Talk to an expert** to add them. Until then learners sign in with a link by email, and nothing else changes.
5. **Which busywork should Ask take on?** Transcripts and chapters, draft quiz questions for your review, reminders to people who are behind, and reports in plain language.

Ask never sets credit, never publishes, never grades a final assessment and never signs an attestation. You can reopen these questions from **Getting started** at any time.

## Find your way around

The sidebar has **Home**, **Courses**, **Certifications**, **Learners**, **Signals**, **Credits** (when it is on), **Site**, **Results** and **Apps**. **Home** shows what is waiting for your review, people to nudge, and the month's numbers. The rail on the right has **Ask**, **Help** and **Wish**. To bring in colleagues, select **Invite teammates** in the sidebar; see [Invite your team](/sessionboard-learning/team).

**Switch apps.** The nine-dot button next to the Sessionboard logo lists every Sessionboard app: Platform, Sessions, Awards, Learning and Marketing. The apps your organization has open in one select. The others open a page about the app, where an admin can add it with a 14-day trial and no card, or book a demo of the platform.
