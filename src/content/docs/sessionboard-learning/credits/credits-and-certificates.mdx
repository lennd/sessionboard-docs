Credits lets a course carry continuing-education credit that learners claim when they finish. It is on from the start for associations. Companies turn it on when they need CPE, CLE or nursing hours.

## Turn on Credits

Go to **Credits** and select **Turn on Credits**. Only an organization admin can turn it on.

## What Credits shows

- **Your credit types**: the credit you offer, from your setup answers.
- **Latest claims**: who claimed what, each with a **Certificate** you can print or save as a PDF.
- **Claim sites**: course credit needs no claim site; learners claim at the end of the course.

## Set a course's credit

Credit is set per course, by a person, when you review the draft. See [Make a course from a recording](/sessionboard-learning/courses/make-a-course-from-a-recording#set-the-credit).

## Learners watch the videos first

If a course with credit has video steps, learners must watch a share of each video before they can claim. The share is **80%** unless you change it.

Sessionboard counts the seconds the learner actually played. Skipping ahead to the end doesn't count, and watching the same minute twice counts once. Until the learner has watched enough, the lesson shows **Watch to 80% to claim credit** with how far they are, and **Claim credit** stays off.

To change the share for a course:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the course's credit">
Go to **Courses** and select the course's credit in the **Credit** column.
</Step>
<Step title="Set how much to watch">
Open **More options** and enter a percentage from 50 to 100 under **Learners must watch this much of each video to claim credit**.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Select **Save**. Leave it at 80 to keep the default.
</Step>
</Steps>

Steps that don't play a video, such as reading, quizzes and links, work as before.

## How learners claim credit

<Steps>
<Step title="Finish the course">
The learner completes the last lesson and watches enough of each video.
</Step>
<Step title="Attest">
The finish card shows **Claim** with your attestation text. They tick the box.
</Step>
<Step title="Claim">
They select **Claim credit**. The credit goes on their transcript, and **View certificate** opens the certificate.
</Step>
</Steps>

Learners see their credit under **My progress** on your learning site and can download their transcript as a PDF or Excel file. You can download any learner's transcript from **Learners**.

<Note>
For CE Broker, set up [CE Broker reporting](/sessionboard-learning/credits/report-to-ce-broker) to build the roster in CE Broker's format. To report credit to ACCME PARS or CPE Monitor, download each learner's transcript from **Learners** and submit it to the accreditor yourself.
</Note>
