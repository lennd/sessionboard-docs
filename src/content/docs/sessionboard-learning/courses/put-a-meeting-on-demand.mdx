A past meeting's sessions can become courses: each recorded session becomes a draft, with its faculty, disclosures and credit carried across.

## From a meeting on Sessionboard

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the meeting">
Go to **Courses** and select **Put a meeting on demand**, then choose the meeting.
</Step>
<Step title="Check what comes across">
**What comes across** shows sessions with recordings, faculty disclosures, sessions with credit and attendance records, then each session in a table.
</Step>
<Step title="Make the drafts">
Select **Make the draft courses**. Up to 60 drafts are made per click.
</Step>
<Step title="Review and publish">
Select **Review and publish**. **Before you publish** lists what will publish, what is held as a draft (a missing faculty disclosure) and what is waiting for a recording. Add a recording where needed, then select **Publish**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Credit claimed in the room stays on each learner's transcript. Attendance is never copied into the courses.

## From a registration platform

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Bring in a meeting">
On **Courses**, select **Bring in a meeting from your registration platform**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the platform and event">
Pick the platform under **Where is the meeting?**, connect it if asked, and choose the event.
</Step>
<Step title="Bring it in">
Select **Bring it in**, or **Update it** for a meeting you brought in before. Sessions, faculty and check-ins arrive, and each session becomes a draft course.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Nothing is written back to the registration platform, and recordings never come from it. Add each session's recording when you publish.
</Note>
