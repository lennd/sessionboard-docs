Your Learning Site can offer more than one language. Learners choose theirs at the top of the Site.

## Why use it

Learners read and learn in their own language, and you don't have to rewrite courses by hand.

## Choose languages

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Languages">
On a course, open the **Languages** row, then **Add languages**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the languages">
Courses are written in your language. Learners who pick another language see a translation when the course has one, and the original otherwise. Select **Save languages**.
</Step>
<Step title="Translate and review">
Select **Translate with AI**, or **Translate** for one language. Check each string against the original and edit anything that reads wrong. **Needs a look** filters to what is not translated or out of date.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish">
Learners who choose that language see the translation once you publish. A status reads **Not translated**, **to update**, **Published** or **Draft, not published**.
</Step>
</Steps>

More languages are part of a paid plan; where it is locked the page shows the plan name and **See the plan**.

## Require watching a video

Separately, a course's **Watch to finish** row sets **Require watching before completion**: a learner can mark a video done only after playing a percentage you choose, from 10 to 100. Skipping ahead doesn't count. It is off by default.

## Notes and feedback

In **Learning settings**, turn on **Learner notes** (private notes stamped with the video time, found in My notes) and **Lesson feedback** (a 1 to 5 rating and a comment). Both are on every Learning Site; turning one off keeps what was already written.
