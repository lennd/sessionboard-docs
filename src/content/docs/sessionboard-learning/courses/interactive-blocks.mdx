Interactive blocks sit anywhere in a lesson. Type **/** and pick one from the **Interactive** group. None of them is scored: they are for learners to explore. To check what learners know, use a [question](/sessionboard-learning/courses/questions-and-quizzes).

## Labeled graphic

A picture with numbered markers. Learners select a marker to read what it points at, and see how many they've opened.

![A labeled graphic in the course builder with three numbered markers on a picture and the title and text of each marker below it](/images/suites/learning/learning-labeled-graphic-builder.png)

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the picture">
Drop a picture on the block (JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 15 MB) and describe it under **Description for screen readers**.
</Step>
<Step title="Place the markers">
Select a spot on the picture to put a marker there. You can also select **Add a marker** and type its position in **X** and **Y** (percent of the picture's width and height).
</Step>
<Step title="Write each marker">
Give each marker a **Title** and **What it says**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Markers are buttons learners can reach with the keyboard and a screen reader, and the text opens under the picture, so nothing covers it on a phone.

## Process

Steps learners go through one at a time, with **Back**, **Next** and a dot for each step. Give each step a **Title**, its **Text** and, if you like, a picture. Use **Add a step** and the arrows to add and reorder steps.

![Step 1 of 3 of a process on a Learning Site, with Back and Next buttons](/images/suites/learning/learning-process-site.png)

## Timeline

Dated events from top to bottom. Each event has a **Date** (any text, such as `2019` or `March 2024`), a **Title**, **Text** and an optional picture.

## Sorting activity

Cards learners sort into groups. **Sorting activity** adds a **Sort into groups** question with its own groups and cards: name the groups, write the cards and say which group each belongs to. Learners drag the cards (or use the keyboard), and the answer is checked on Sessionboard's servers, like any question. On a lesson it's a knowledge check; on a quiz it's graded.

<Tip>
Every interactive block works in **Preview** and **Phone**, so you can try it the way learners will.
</Tip>

<Note>
Before you publish, the builder checks each block: a labeled graphic needs a picture and a title on every marker, and a process or timeline needs two or more steps, each with a title.
</Note>
