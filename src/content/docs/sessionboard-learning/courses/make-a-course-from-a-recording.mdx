A course is written from the recording's transcript: lessons, checkpoint questions in each video and a final quiz. You accept, edit or reject every question before anything is published.

## Draft the course

<Steps>
<Step title="Start from a recording">
Go to **Courses** and select **Make a course from a recording or session**. **New course** on Home goes to the same place.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the videos">
In **Create a course from video**, choose up to 10 videos. A video needs a transcript first; one without shows **no transcript yet**.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe the course">
Fill in **Course title** and **Who it's for**. Choose whether to **Award a certification on completion** and how many **Questions in each video** (0 to 3).
</Step>
<Step title="Draft it">
Select **Draft the course**. The draft opens while the assistant reads the transcript.
</Step>
</Steps>

No recordings yet? Select **Upload a recording**, then paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, upload a video or audio file, or bring one in from your webinars (Zoom, Goldcast or ON24).

## Review the questions

Each question shows the moment in the video it comes from and whether it is a **Checkpoint** or part of the **Final quiz**. For each one, select **Accept**, **Edit** or **Reject**.

## Set the credit

If the course carries credit, choose the credit type and use **Less credit** and **More credit**. The draft shows the arithmetic: 60 minutes of content allows 1 credit, in the steps your credit type uses. You set the number; the assistant never does.

<Note>
If Credits is off, the draft says so and offers **Turn it on**. With Credits on but no credit types, add one in **Credits** first.
</Note>

## Publish

Select **Approve and publish**. The button stays off until you have decided every question. Once published, learners see the course on your learning site.

<Tip>
**Courses** lists every course with its status, credit, learners and completions. Select one to see who is taking it or to **Assign this course**.
</Tip>
