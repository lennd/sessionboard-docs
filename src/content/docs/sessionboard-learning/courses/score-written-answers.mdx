**Written answer** and **File upload** questions in a graded quiz are scored by a person. Until someone scores them, the learner's attempt waits and the quiz isn't passed; the rest of the quiz is already marked.

## Find answers waiting

On **Home**, **Ready for your review** shows how many quiz attempts have answers to score. Select **Review** beside it: **Answers to score** sits below **Waiting for your OK**, oldest first.

People who can manage Learning see and score answers. Nobody scores their own.

## Score an attempt

![A learner's written answer given 2 of 3 points with a note, and the resulting score of 91%, which passes](/images/suites/learning/learning-answers-to-score.png)

<Steps>
<Step title="Read the answer">
Each card shows the learner, the quiz, the pass mark and the points already scored automatically. Open **Model answer** to compare with the answer the question's author wrote. A file opens with **Open** and its name.
</Step>
<Step title="Give points">
Enter the points for each answer, or use **None**, **Half** or **Full**. Add a **Note on this answer** if you want the learner to see why.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the result">
Once every answer has points, the card shows the score and whether it **Passes** or is **Not yet** a pass.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
Add an optional **Note to the learner**, then select **Send result**.
</Step>
</Steps>

A pass completes the quiz as any pass does: the course, its credit and any certification follow. The learner gets a message with the result.

## What the learner sees

![A learner's quiz marked as reviewed at 100% and passed, with the reviewer's note and a note beside their written answer](/images/suites/learning/learning-site-reviewed-result.png)

While the answers wait, the quiz tells the learner a person is scoring them. Afterwards it shows the score, whether they passed, your note and each note beside the question it is about. If they didn't pass and have attempts left, they can try again.

<Note>
The answers you score are the ones the learner gave, on the questions as they were when they answered, even if the course has been edited and published again since.
</Note>
