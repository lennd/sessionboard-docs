import { Note, Steps, Step } from '@compat';

Every course is free until you give it a price. To take card payments, connect the payment gateway you already use. The money goes straight to your account.

## Give a course a price

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the price">
Go to **Courses** and select the course's price in the **Price** column. It says **Free** until you set one.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the price">
Type the price. Leave it empty to keep the course free.
</Step>
<Step title="Optional: a member price">
Select **+ Member price** and enter what members pay. Enter 0 to make it free for members.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Select **Save price**. The column now shows, for example, "$49 · members $29".
</Step>
</Steps>

Prices are in US dollars unless you change the currency under **More options** (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP or AUD).

## Who counts as a member

A learner gets the member price when either is true:

- You marked them as a member: in **Learners**, open the learner and tick **Member** under **Member and license**.
- Your association management system (AMS) says they are a current member. Nothing to do.

## Get paid

Connect your payment gateway once, and learners can pay by card. Card details go straight to your gateway; they never touch Sessionboard.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Get paid">
Go to **Site**. Under **Selling**, select **Get paid**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick your gateway">
Choose your payment gateway. Each one says where to find the keys to paste.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste the keys and connect">
Paste the keys and select **Connect**. Sessionboard asks for your two-factor code, because this decides where your money goes.
</Step>
</Steps>

Selling then shows **Card payments: On** and the gateway. To switch gateways later, select **Change gateway**; the old one stops taking new payments.

These gateways are in the list:

| Gateway | What you paste |
|---|---|
| Stripe | Secret key (sk_…) or a restricted key (rk_…) |
| PayPal | Client ID and Secret |
| Authorize.net | API Login ID and Transaction Key |
| Braintree | Merchant ID, Public Key and Private Key |
| Square | Application ID and Access token |
| CyberSource | Merchant ID, Key ID and Shared secret |
| BluePay | Account ID and Secret Key |
| BlueSnap | API username and API password |

Sessionboard works with 100+ payment gateways. If yours isn't listed, select **Request another gateway** and tell us which one.

To try it before real customers pay, tick **Test mode** under **More options** and use your gateway's test keys.

<Note>
Connecting a gateway needs the organization permission **Manage payment gateways** and two-factor authentication. An organization admin has both.
</Note>

## Make a coupon

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Selling">
Go to **Site**. Under **Selling**, select **New coupon**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the discount">
Choose **Percent off** or **Amount off** and enter how much. The code is filled in for you (for example SAVE20); type your own if you like.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Select **Create coupon** and share the code.
</Step>
</Steps>

A coupon works on every course. Under **More options** you can limit it to some courses, set the date it ends, and cap how many times it can be used.

Coupons come off after the member price. A member who uses SAVE20 on a $49 course with a $29 member price pays $23.20. To end a coupon early, select **Stop**.

## What learners see

On your learning site, a priced course shows its price ("$49", or "Members $29" for a member). When a learner opens it, they see what they pay, can enter a code under **Have a code?**, and then:

- **Get this course** when the total is free (a 100% coupon or a free member price);
- **Buy for $X** when you've connected a payment gateway. They pay by card and start straight away.

Until you connect a gateway, a priced course tells learners to use a code or contact you.

Learners you assign a course to, and your own team, never have to buy it.

## Sales, refunds and access

**Site** › **Selling** › **Sales** lists every course a learner got through a price, a member price or a coupon: who, which course, when, what they paid, and any coupon. The total sits above the list.

Refunds happen in your payment gateway. A refunded learner keeps the course unless you take it away: select **Remove access** on their sale. The sale stays on record.

## Ask can do it for you

Ask Ask to set a price ("charge $49 for AI governance essentials, $29 for members"), make a coupon ("make SPEAKERS, 100% off the governance course, five uses"), check whether card payments are on, or remove a buyer's access. You approve each change. Connecting a gateway is always done by you in **Get paid**, because it takes your payment keys.
