Every course is free until you give it a price. To take card payments, connect the payment gateway you already use. The money goes straight to your account.

## Why use it

Sessionboard Learning gives you a branded site where learners sign in, buy courses, work through SCORM or native content, and earn certificates — all in one place. You set the price, connect your payment gateway, and choose which courses require purchase; learners see their progress and certificates in a personal dashboard. Use it to monetize continuing-education content, onboard members, or deliver compliance training without stitching together a separate LMS and storefront.

## Give a course a price

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the price">
Go to **Courses** and select the course's price in the **Price** column. It says **Free** until you set one.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the price">
Type the price. Leave it empty to keep the course free.
</Step>
<Step title="Optional: a member price">
Select **+ Member price** and enter what members pay. Enter 0 to make it free for members.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Select **Save price**. The column now shows, for example, "$49 · members $29".
</Step>
</Steps>

Prices are in US dollars unless you change the currency under **More options** (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP or AUD).

## Who counts as a member

A learner gets the member price when either is true:

- You marked them as a member: in **Learners**, open the learner and tick **Member** under **Member and license**.
- Your association management system (AMS) says they are a current member. Nothing to do.

## Get paid

Connect your payment gateway once, and learners can pay by card. Card details go straight to your gateway; they never touch Sessionboard. Free courses work without a gateway: connect one when you're ready to charge. Once a course has a price and card payments are off, **Getting started** on **Home** shows **Get paid: connect your payment gateway**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Get paid">
Go to **Site**. Under **Selling**, select **Get paid**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick your gateway">
Choose your payment gateway. Each one says where to find the keys to paste.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste the keys and connect">
Paste the keys and select **Connect**. Sessionboard asks for your two-factor code, because this decides where your money goes.
</Step>
</Steps>

With Stripe, Sessionboard checks the key with Stripe before it saves it. Nothing is charged. If you pasted the publishable key (pk_…) or something that isn't a secret key, Get paid says so before you select **Connect**, and a key Stripe refuses is never saved. Test or live comes from the key itself: a test key (sk_test_… or rk_test_…) connects in test mode. When it connects, Sessionboard names the Stripe account the money goes to. A test key is for checking the connection: a gateway in test mode never takes payments on your live Learning Site (see below).

If you haven't turned on two-factor authentication yet, Get paid says so and shows **Turn on two-factor authentication**. Select it and pick a passkey, a code by email or an authenticator app, then follow the one step it shows and keep your recovery codes. Sessionboard then connects your gateway with the keys you already pasted, so you don't type them again.

Selling then shows **Card payments: On** and the gateway. To switch gateways later, select **Change gateway**; the old one stops taking new payments.

To stop taking card payments, select **Turn off card payments** and confirm. Sessionboard asks for your two-factor code again. Priced courses then tell learners to use a code or contact you until you connect a gateway again. Coupons and member prices keep working, and past sales stay on record. If anything else in Sessionboard uses the same gateway, it stops taking payments too.

These gateways are in the list:

| Gateway | What you paste |
|---|---|
| Stripe | Secret key (sk_…) or a restricted key (rk_…) |
| PayPal | Client ID and Secret |
| Authorize.net | API Login ID and Transaction Key |
| Braintree | Merchant ID, Public Key and Private Key |
| Square | Application ID and Access token |
| CyberSource | Merchant ID, Key ID and Shared secret |
| BluePay | Account ID and Secret Key |
| BlueSnap | API username and API password |

Sessionboard works with 100+ payment gateways. If yours isn't listed, select **Request another gateway** and tell us which one.

To try it before real customers pay, use your gateway's test keys. With Stripe, a test key is enough; for other gateways, tick **Test mode** under **More options**. A declined card tells the learner "Your card was declined. Nothing was charged." and they can try another card.

A gateway in test mode never takes payments from your learners on your live Learning Site, because a test card would buy real access. **Selling** says so and offers **Connect a live gateway**. Switch to your gateway's live keys before you start selling.

<Note>
Connecting a gateway needs the organization permission **Manage payment gateways** and two-factor authentication. An organization admin has both.
</Note>

## Make a coupon

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Selling">
Go to **Site**. Under **Selling**, select **New coupon**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the discount">
Choose **Percent off** or **Amount off** and enter how much. The code is filled in for you (for example SAVE20); type your own if you like.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Select **Create coupon** and share the code.
</Step>
</Steps>

A coupon works on every course. Under **More options** you can limit it to some courses, set the date it ends, and cap how many times it can be used.

Coupons come off after the member price. A member who uses SAVE20 on a $49 course with a $29 member price pays $23.20. To end a coupon early, select **Stop**.

## What learners see

On your learning site, a priced course shows its price ("$49", or "Members $29" for a member). When a learner opens it, they see what they pay, can enter a code under **Have a code?**, and then:

- **Get this course** when the total is free (a 100% coupon or a free member price);
- **Buy for $X** when you've connected a payment gateway. They pay by card and start straight away.

Every learner who gets a course through a price, a member price or a coupon gets a receipt by email: the course, what they paid and how (member price, code), the date and an order number.

Until you connect a gateway, a priced course tells learners to use a code or contact you.

Learners you assign a course to, and your own team, never have to buy it.

## Sales, refunds and access

**Site** › **Selling** › **Sales** lists every course a learner got through a price, a member price or a coupon: who, which course, when, what they paid, and any coupon. The total sits above the list.

To refund a sale, select **Refund** on it and confirm. The full amount goes back to the learner's card through your payment gateway, the sale shows **Refunded**, and the learner gets a refund email. They keep the course unless you tick **Also remove access** in the same step.

To take a course away at any time, refunded or not, select **Remove access** on the sale. The sale stays on record.

A sale that charged nothing (a 100% coupon or a free member price) has nothing to refund: use **Remove access**.

<Note>
**Refund** and **Remove access** are for people who manage your plan and billing (the permission behind **Settings** › **Plan and billing**) and organization admins. Others who can edit courses see the sales but not these buttons, and Ask tells them to ask an organization admin.
</Note>

## Ask can do it for you

Ask Ask to set a price ("charge $49 for AI governance essentials, $29 for members"), make a coupon ("make SPEAKERS, 100% off the governance course, five uses"), check whether card payments are on, refund a sale, or remove a buyer's access. You approve each change. Connecting a gateway or turning card payments off is always done by you in **Get paid**, because it decides where your money goes.
