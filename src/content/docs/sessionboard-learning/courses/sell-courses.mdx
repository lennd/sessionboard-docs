Every course is free until you give it a price. You can also set a lower price for members and make coupons that take a percent or an amount off.

## Give a course a price

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the price">
Go to **Courses**. In the course's **Price** column, select **Free**. You can also open the course and select its price there.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the price">
Choose **Paid**, enter the price and pick the currency: USD, CAD, EUR, GBP or AUD.
</Step>
<Step title="Optional: a member price">
Tick **Members pay less** and enter the **Member price**. Enter 0 to make the course free for members.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Select **Save price**. The **Price** column now shows the price and the member price, for example "$49 · members $29".
</Step>
</Steps>

To make a course free again, choose **Free** and save.

## Who counts as a member

A learner gets the member price when either of these is true:

- You marked them as a member. Go to **Learners**, open the learner and tick **Member** under **Member and license**.
- Your association management system (AMS) says they are a current member. The member price then applies with nothing for you to do.

## Make a coupon

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Selling">
Go to **Site**. Under **Selling**, select **New coupon**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the discount">
Choose **Percent off** or **Amount off** and enter how much. The code is filled in for you (for example SAVE20). You can type your own.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the courses">
Leave **All courses** selected, or choose **Only some courses** and tick the ones the code works on.
</Step>
<Step title="Optional: an end date and a limit">
Under **More options**, set the date the code stops working and how many times it can be used in total.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Select **Create coupon**. Share the code with the learners you want to have it.
</Step>
</Steps>

Coupons come off after the member price. A member who uses SAVE20 on a $49 course with a $29 member price pays $23.20.

To end a coupon early, select **Stop** next to it. A stopped code no longer works, and the code is free to use again.

## What learners see

On your learning site, a course with a price shows it on its card ("$49", or "Members $29" for a member). When a learner opens a course they haven't bought, they see:

- the price, and the member price if it applies to them;
- **Have a code?**, where they enter a coupon code;
- what they pay.

When the total is free, for example with a 100% coupon or a free member price, they select **Get this course** and start straight away. The course is then in **My progress**.

Learners you assign a course to, and your own team, never have to buy it.

## Taking card payments

<Note>
**Site** › **Selling** shows whether card payments are on for your workspace. While they are off, learners can't pay online: a priced course tells them to use a code or contact you. Coupons and member prices work either way, down to free.
</Note>

## See your sales

**Site** › **Selling** › **Sales** lists every course a learner got through a price, a member price or a coupon. Each sale shows who it was, the course, the date, what they paid, and whether a member price or a coupon applied. The total sits above the list.

## Ask can do it for you

Ask Ask to set a price ("charge $49 for AI governance essentials, $29 for members") or to make a coupon ("make SPEAKERS, 100% off the governance course, five uses"). You approve the change before it is made.
