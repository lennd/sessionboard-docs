Ask is the box at the top of **Home**, the **Ask** button on the right-hand rail, and the Ask strip on each section. Your chats are saved under **Chats**; start a fresh one with **New chat**.

## What you can ask

| Ask for | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Make a course from a recording" | Lists your recordings; pick one and Ask drafts the course once you confirm. |
| "Put a meeting on demand" | Lists meetings you can turn into courses. |
| "Import a meeting from a registration platform" | Picks the meeting, brings it in and makes the draft courses, asking before each step. |
| "What is waiting for my review?" | Lists drafts and meetings waiting on you. |
| "Who is about to expire?" | Lists certifications expiring soon. |
| "Who is overdue?" | Lists overdue assignments; "Send a reminder" sends once you confirm. |
| "Who's due for recertification this month?" | Lists the people due. |
| "Who owes credit?" | Lists learners who finished but haven't claimed. |
| "How much credit have we issued?" | Totals credit by type. |
| "Draft a Keep learning round" | Drafts a round of course suggestions for your people; each round waits for your OK. |
| "Open my learning site" | Opens your site. |
| "Show me how we are doing" | Summarizes results, with a link to **Results**. |

## Build and edit courses

Ask works in the same draft as the course builder, so an open course shows its changes within a few seconds. Nothing reaches learners until the course is published, and Ask asks you before it publishes.

| Ask for | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "Start a course called Measuring agent ROI" | Makes a draft course with a starter outline. |
| "Build a three-lesson course on…, with a graded quiz" | Writes the modules, lessons and quiz into the draft. |
| "Add a 5-question quiz on this lesson" | Writes the questions from the lesson's text and videos into a new quiz after it. |
| "Turn this recording into a lesson" | Adds a lesson with the recording as a video learners must watch. |
| "Make the pass mark 80% and allow 3 attempts" | Changes the quiz settings. |
| "Give the final quiz a 20-minute time limit" | Sets the time limit. |
| "Draw 3 questions tagged roi in this quiz" | Adds **Questions from the bank**. See [Reuse questions with the question bank](/sessionboard-learning/courses/question-bank). |
| "Add this question to the bank tagged onboarding" | Saves it to the question bank. |
| "Move the holdouts lesson after the baseline" | Reorders the outline. |
| "Show me the quiz as a learner sees it" | Shows the questions without the answers. |
| "Publish the course" | Publishes once you confirm. |

Edit anything Ask wrote in the [course builder](/sessionboard-learning/courses/build-a-course); history keeps every published version.

## What stays with you

- Ask drafts; you approve and publish.
- Credit is never set from a chat.
- Anything that sends or publishes asks you first.

## AI assistants you connect

You can connect an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT from **Apps** > **Manage AI assistants**. It can list your media and courses, draft courses, build and edit them lesson by lesson (questions, quiz settings, the question bank), find learners and assign training. Publishing waits under **Waiting for your OK** until a person approves it, and setting credit is refused.

<Note>
Ask works from a fixed set of actions. If it can't do something yet, it lists what it can do.
</Note>
