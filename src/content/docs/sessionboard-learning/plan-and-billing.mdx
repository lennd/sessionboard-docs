Every plan has every feature; plans differ in how many active learners and admin seats they include. Your workspace starts with a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, no card needed. Open **Plan and billing** from the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Plans

|  | Starter | Growth | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $149 | $449 | $899 |
| Yearly | $1,428 | $4,548 | $8,988 |
| Active learners a month | 300 | 1,500 | 5,000 |
| Admin seats | 2 | 5 | 15 |
| Support | Email and the assistant | Chat 9–6 ET | Priority chat and a setup call |

Every plan includes migration guided by a specialist; Scale adds an hour with one. A learner over your plan still learns: we only tell you.

## Choose a plan

Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly** (yearly saves about 20%), then select **Choose** on a plan and complete checkout. During the trial, the button keeps your plan going once the trial ends.

## Meeting boost

Expecting a crowd after a big meeting? **Add a meeting boost** adds 10,000 learners for one month for $299.

## Card, invoices and cancelling

Select **Manage billing, card and invoices** to update your card, download invoices or cancel. It appears once you have a billing account.

## When the trial ends

From three days before the end of a trial with no card, a banner reminds you to pick a plan. After the trial, everything you made is still there.

## Contracts

Associations with more than 2,500 learners, and companies that need single sign-on, buy Learning on a contract. Select **Talk to an expert** on the billing page.

<Note>
If Learning is on your Sessionboard contract, the billing page says so and there is nothing to pay here.
</Note>
