Every plan has every feature; plans differ in how many active learners, admin seats and video hours they include. Your organization starts with a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, no card needed. Open **Plan and billing** from the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.

The top of the page says what happens next in one line: when the trial ends and what you'll pay then, the date your plan renews, or the date a cancelled plan ends.

Every plan includes migration guided by a specialist (Scale adds an hour with one) and your own domain for your Learning Sites. A learner over your plan still learns: we only tell you.

## Choose a plan

Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly · save ~20%**, then select **Choose** on a plan and complete checkout on Stripe's page. During the trial, choosing a plan keeps the rest of your trial: nothing is charged until it ends, and the line at the top shows the date and the price.

Once you have a plan, it says **Your current plan**, and the others say **Switch to Starter** (or Growth, or Scale). Switching opens Stripe's page, which shows the new price before you confirm.

## Add admin seats and video hours

**Add-ons** lists your admin seats (how many are in use, and how many you've added) and your video hours a month.

1. Select **Add seats** or **Add video hours**.
2. Choose how many. The window shows the price of each and what you'll pay:
   - On a paid plan: about what is charged today for the rest of this billing period, then the monthly (or yearly) amount on your plan's bill.
   - During the trial: nothing until your plan starts.
3. Select **Add**. The new total shows at once.

An extra admin seat is $29 a month ($288 a year on a yearly plan). Extra video is $49 a month for each 100 hours, on monthly plans; on a yearly plan, ask us to add it. If your card is declined, nothing changes: update your card under **Manage billing, card and invoices** and try again.

In **Ask**, "add a seat" or "add video hours" opens the same window with the price.

### When an invite needs a seat

Each person on your team takes an admin seat, invited people included. If an invite needs more seats than your plan has, the invite says how many more can join, and **Add a seat** opens the window with the number you're short of. Once the seats are added, the same invites go out. See [Your team and admin seats](/sessionboard-learning/team).

## Meeting boost

Expecting a crowd after a big meeting? **Add a meeting boost · $299** adds 10,000 learners for one month. When it's on, the page says until when.

## Card, invoices and cancelling

Select **Manage billing, card and invoices** to update your card, download invoices or cancel. It appears once you have a billing account. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for; until then, **Keep Scale** (or your plan's name) undoes the cancellation.

## When the trial ends

From three days before the end of a trial with no card, a banner reminds you to pick a plan. After the trial, everything you made is still there.

## Contracts

Associations with more than 2,500 learners, and companies that need single sign-on, buy Learning on a contract. Select **Talk to an expert** on the billing page.

<Note>
If Learning is on your Sessionboard contract, the billing page says so and there is nothing to pay here. Your Sessionboard contact adds seats and hours.
</Note>
