**Results** shows how learning is going. What leads depends on whether your organization is set up for associations or companies.

- **Completions by month** for the last six months.
- **Credit issued by type** over the last 12 months.
- **Certifications**, with how many expire in the next 90 days.
- **Learning engagement**.

**Home** shows the headline numbers too: credit issued, active learners and transcripts for associations; active learners, people certified and assignments done for companies.

To build your own dashboard, select **Build a dashboard in Claude**. It opens claude.ai with your aggregate numbers only, never individual learners.
