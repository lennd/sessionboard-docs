import { Accordion } from '@compat';

<Accordion title="A video says no transcript yet">
A course is written from the transcript. Transcribe the video first, then draft the course again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't publish a course">
**Approve and publish** stays off until every question is accepted, edited or rejected. The draft tells you how many still need a decision.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Credits is off for this organization">
An organization admin can select **Turn on Credits** on the **Credits** page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A course from a meeting is held as a draft">
Sessions without a faculty disclosure are held back, and sessions without a recording wait for one. Add what is missing, then publish again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I don't see Credits in the sidebar">
Credits only shows once it is on. Organizations set up for companies start with it off.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Learning isn't on in this organization yet">
An organization admin can select **Add Learning to this workspace** to start a 14-day trial.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My sign-in code didn't arrive">
Codes are good for 10 minutes. Check your spam folder, then request a new code.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An invite says my plan's seats are taken">
Each person on your team takes an admin seat, invited people included. Select **Add a seat** under the message: it shows the price for the seats you're short of, and the invites go out once they're added. Or take back an invite you no longer need with the × next to it. See [Invite your team](/sessionboard-learning/team).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A course I published isn't on my learning site">
A course goes on your site's **Course showcase** the first time it's published. With more than one site, pick the sites in **Show on which Site?**; if you chose **Not now**, add it in **Site > Edit site**. Nothing is added to a home page whose **Course showcase** block was removed, or after you took the course off the showcase.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Adding seats or video hours says my card was declined">
Nothing was added or charged. Update your card under **Plan and billing > Manage billing, card and invoices**, then add them again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't add video hours on a yearly plan">
Extra video hours are billed monthly. On a yearly plan, select **Talk to an expert** on **Plan and billing** and we'll add them to your plan.
</Accordion>
