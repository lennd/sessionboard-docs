Your learning site is where learners sign in, browse what you showcase, resume what they started and find their credit and certificates. A new course or certification goes on it by itself when you publish it.

## Create the site

The first time you open **Site**, select **Make my site**: it makes the site with your organization's name and opens it for editing. To choose everything first, select **Set it up myself**:

<Steps>
<Step title="Start it">
Go to **Site** and select **Set it up myself** (or **New site** once you have one).
</Step>
<Step title="Choose what it shows">
Give it a name, then add the certifications, courses and lessons to showcase, a home page heading and an intro.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who can learn">
Under **Who can learn here**, pick **Anyone who signs in**, **Members only** or **Single sign-on only**. Turn on **Learners can start anything showcased** to let people enrol themselves; otherwise they learn what you assign.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Select **Create Site**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each site card shows its address, its members, who can learn there, how many items it showcases and whether people can enrol themselves. Select **Open as a learner** to see it as a learner does.

## Edit the home page

Select **Edit site**. The home page opens in the editor:

- Click any text on the page to change it. Press <kbd>/</kbd> or **+** to add a block, and drag ⋮⋮ to move one. The home page has a **Learning hero** and a **Course showcase**.
- Use **As** to see the page as a **Visitor** (the sign-in page) or a **Learner** (the catalog).
- Your changes are a draft until you select **Publish**.

## New courses and certifications go on your site

When you publish a course or a certification for the first time, it's added to the end of your site's **Course showcase**: the live site and any draft you're editing. You don't need a second step.

- **One site:** a message says which site it went on.
- **Several sites:** **Show on which Site?** opens. The first site is ticked; tick the ones that should list it and select **Show it**, or **Not now**.

Publishing it again after an edit doesn't add it a second time, and something you took off the showcase stays off. If you removed the **Course showcase** block from your home page, nothing is added.

## What learners see

Learners sign in with a link by email, or with your single sign-on if you offer it. They land on **Welcome back**, with **Continue where you left off**, **My learning** and **Explore**. A course is made of pages to watch, read, answer, do or acknowledge. **My progress** holds their credits and transcript, and **My certificates** their certificates.
