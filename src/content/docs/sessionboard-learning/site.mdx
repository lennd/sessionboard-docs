Your learning site is where learners sign in, browse what you showcase, resume what they started and find their credit and certificates.

## Create the site

<Steps>
<Step title="Start it">
Go to **Site** and select **New site** (or **Make your learning site** the first time).
</Step>
<Step title="Choose what it shows">
Give it a name, then add the certifications, courses and lessons to showcase, a home page heading and an intro.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who can learn">
Under **Who can learn here**, pick **Anyone who signs in**, **Members only** or **Single sign-on only**. Turn on **Learners can start anything showcased** to let people enrol themselves; otherwise they learn what you assign.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Select **Create Site**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each site card shows its address, its members, who can learn there and whether people can enrol themselves. Select **Open as a learner** to see it as a learner does.

## Use your own domain

Your site lives at a Sessionboard address until you give it one of your own, such as `learn.yourcompany.com`. Once your own domain is turned on for your workspace, select **Edit** on the site card. The **Domain** list shows the Sessionboard address and every domain your organization has connected, each with its status and the site that uses it, if any. Pick one. A domain serves one site at a time, so picking one another site uses asks you to confirm the move. Pick **Sessionboard address** to go back.

You can pick a domain that is still **Waiting for DNS**: the site keeps its Sessionboard address until the domain is live. To connect a new domain, select **Connect a new domain…**, add the DNS record it shows at your domain provider, then come back and pick it.

## What learners see

Learners sign in with a link by email, or with your single sign-on if you offer it. They land on **Welcome back**, with **Continue where you left off**, **My learning** and **Explore**. Lessons are made of steps to watch, read, answer, do or acknowledge. **My progress** holds their credits and transcript, and **My certificates** their certificates.
