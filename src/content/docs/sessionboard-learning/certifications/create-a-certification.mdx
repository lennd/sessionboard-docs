A certification proves someone finished a set of lessons and courses. It is earned when every required lesson is complete and every quiz is passed.

## Create one

<Steps>
<Step title="Start it">
Go to **Certifications** and select **New certification**.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe it">
Fill in the name, **What it proves** and a badge color. Set **Valid for (months)**, or leave it blank for no expiry.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the requirements">
Add the lessons and courses it requires.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish it">
Set the status to **Published** and select **Create**. Keep it as **Draft** while you work on it, or **Retired** when you stop offering it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Changing the requirements later makes a new version. People who already hold the certification keep theirs.
</Note>

## Follow who holds it

Each certification card shows certified, expiring and overdue counts, with **Assign** and **See holders**. Its page lists what it takes and every person with their state: **Certified**, **In progress**, **Not started** or **Overdue**.

Each certificate has a public **Verify** link, so anyone can check that it is genuine.

## What learners see

When a learner finishes the last requirement, they see **You're certified!** with a verification code. Their certificates are under **My certificates** on your learning site, where they can add one to LinkedIn or print it.
