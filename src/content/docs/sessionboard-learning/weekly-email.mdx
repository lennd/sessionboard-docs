Every Monday morning, in your workspace's time zone, each admin gets **Your week in Sessionboard**: one email about the week before, Monday to Sunday.

## What's in it

- **Needs attention**, at the top when there is something: a trial ending in the next 7 days, a payment that failed, more active contacts than your plan, approvals waiting for you, reviews past their due date, a program or a call for speakers closing this week, a sync that failed.
- **Learning**, with each number against the week before ("up 20% on the week before"):
  - **Enrollments**: people enrolled in a course
  - **Courses completed**
  - **CE credits issued**: credit claimed on your courses
- **Team**: who joined or was removed, role changes, seats used, and the list of owners and admins. Admins get this email and can change your plan, so keep that list up to date.
- **Plan and billing**: your plan, monthly or yearly, trial days left, the renewal date, active contacts against your limit where your plan counts them, and whether a card is on file.

Each number links to the page behind it. If your workspace has other Sessionboard products, each one gets its own section in the same email.

The email is in the language of your Sessionboard account.

## Who gets it

Every admin of the workspace. If you're an admin of two workspaces, you get two emails, one for each. Workspaces are never combined into one email.

In a week when nothing happened, no email is sent, unless something about your plan needs you.

## Turn it off

Select **Turn off this weekly email** at the foot of the email. It stops the email from that workspace only.

To see every workspace you're an admin of with a switch for each, select **Account settings** at the foot of the email and find **Your week in Sessionboard**.

<Note>
Notices about your account, like your trial ending, still arrive by email when you turn this off.
</Note>
