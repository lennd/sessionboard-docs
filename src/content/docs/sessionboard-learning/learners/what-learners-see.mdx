This is the learner's side of your [learning site](/sessionboard-learning/site), so you can answer their questions. To try it yourself, select **Open as a learner** on the **Site** page.

## Sign in

Learners open your site's link and enter their email under **Sign in to** your site's name, then select **Send login link**. The link in the email signs them in; there's no password. If you offer single sign-on, they use that instead.

Who gets in depends on the site's **Who can learn here** setting. On a **Members only** site, someone who isn't a member can sign in but sees a members-only message.

## Home

**Home** greets them and shows how many certifications they hold and how many courses are to do or overdue. Then:

- **Continue where you left off**: the course they were last in, with **Continue**.
- **My learning**: what you assigned them, and what they started.
- **Explore**: everything the site showcases, with a search box. New courses and certifications appear here once you publish them.

## Take a course

A course is a set of pages: things to watch, read, answer, do or acknowledge. Each shows what finishes it, for example **Completes when you reach the end** for a video. When the last page is done, they see **You finished this course** and the next one, if there is one.

### Quizzes

- A quiz shows the pass mark. Below it, they see **You scored 64%. You need 80%.** and can retake it if you allow more than one attempt. A quiz that draws from the question bank asks different questions on a retake.
- **Timed quizzes** open with **Timed quiz: 20 minutes** and the rules: the clock starts at **Start quiz** and keeps running if they leave the page, and when time is up their answers are submitted for them. They can hide the timer with **Hide timer**.
- **Written answers** are scored by a person. Until then the quiz says **A person is scoring your written answers. You'll hear when it's done.** and **You can retake once it's scored.** Once you score it, they see **Reviewed: 85%. You passed.** (or **Not passed yet.**). See [Score written answers](/sessionboard-learning/courses/score-written-answers).

## Credit and certificates

If a course carries credit, its last page says **Finishing this course earns** the credit. Depending on how you set credit up, it's added for them (**Credit earned**) or they select **Claim credit**. Either way it goes on their transcript under **My progress**, and **View certificate** opens the certificate of credit.

Finishing everything a certification requires shows **You're certified!**, with the date it's valid until and a verification code. **Certificates** lists every certificate they hold. Anyone can check a certificate on the site's **Verify a certificate** page with its code.

<Note>
When you update a lesson that a certification depends on, people who hold it see **Refresh due** on that lesson. Redoing the changed parts keeps their certification current.
</Note>
