Assigning puts a certification, course or lesson on someone's list with a due date and tells them.

## Assign

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Assign">
Select **Assign** on **Learners**, or on a certification or course.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick what and who">
Under **What**, choose a certification, course or lesson. Under **Who**, choose **The whole team**, **Specific people** or **Members of a Learning Site**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the terms">
Set **Due in (days)** and whether it is **Required**. You can also announce it in a Slack or Microsoft Teams channel.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
Select **Assign and notify**. People hear by Slack direct message, Teams message or email.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Follow up

**Learners** shows **Learning now** by certification, every assignment with its progress, and the **Most engaged** learners. Select **Remind** on an assignment to nudge everyone who still owes it, at most once every 12 hours.

Find anyone under **Find a learner** to see their courses, certifications with expiry dates, assignments and credit by type. **Transcript PDF** downloads their transcript.

**Home** also lists **People to nudge**: renewals coming up, people who have stalled, and credit someone is owed. Nothing is sent from that list until you choose to.
