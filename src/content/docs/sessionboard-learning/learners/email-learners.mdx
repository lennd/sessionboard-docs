The **Emails** page is where reminders, saved templates and messages to learners live. Learners who unsubscribe or bounce are skipped.

## Why use it

Most people who start a course and stop will come back if asked once. Reminders do that for you, and a saved message reaches exactly the learners you pick.

## Reminders to finish

Someone who starts a course and stops gets an email that takes them back to where they stopped. Turn **Reminders** on or off, and for each one set the days after their last activity, choose the email (**Built-in wording** or your own template) and select **Save reminders**. Each reminder goes once. New activity starts the count again, and each reminder has to come later than the one before, between 1 and 90 days. **Add a reminder** adds another. Reminders to finish keep going on every plan.

## Email learners

From **Learners**, select people (or **Select everyone shown**) and choose **Email** the selected learners. Pick a **Template** or **Write a new message**, then fill in **Subject line** and **Message**. A blank line starts a new paragraph, and `{link}` on its own line becomes a button. Select **Preview** to see it as a learner will, then **Send**. Learners with no email address won't get it. Sending to any learners you pick, and saved templates, are part of a paid plan; the page shows the plan name and **See the plan** where it is locked.

## Templates

**Templates** lists your saved emails, plus the built-in ones. Create **New template**, edit, duplicate or delete your own. The built-in reminders are "Reminder 1: pick up where you left off" and "Reminder 2: still time to finish".

## Who emails come from

Learners see a name and address on every email. By default it is your organization's name, sent by Sessionboard. On a paid plan you can verify your domain once so every Learning email comes from your own address, such as learning@yourorg.org.

## See what a learner was sent

A learner's **Emails sent** lists each email with its kind (**Reminder**, **Message**), status, opens and clicks, and whether it was **Automatic**.
