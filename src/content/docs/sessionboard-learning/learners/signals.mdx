**Signals** shows what your learners did everywhere: courses, credit, certifications, the sessions they attended and the Marketing emails they opened, on one record per person.

## Why use it

The more you connect, the better each person's next lesson fits, and the more you can see about who is engaged. You see what each source turns on before you connect it.

## Read the page

**Where your signals come from** is a map of sources, grouped as **Meetings and Marketing** and **Files**. Each source is counted once for the last 30 days. Select a source to see what it brings. **Refresh signals** updates them; Learning's own signals also arrive as people learn.

Ask can answer "How connected is Learning?" and walk you through what to connect. To connect a source, see [Connect what feeds signals](#connect-what-feeds-signals) below.

## Connect what feeds signals

**Apps** lists sign-in (a link by email, your SSO with SAML or OIDC, SCIM from Okta or Entra ID), your AMS or HRIS, recordings, registration platforms and your CRM. Each row shows whether it is **Available**, **Reads only**, **At launch** or on the Association and Enterprise plans. **Manage AI assistants** shows what Claude, ChatGPT and other connected assistants may do and what they did; publishing always waits for a person.
