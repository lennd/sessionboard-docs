The **Attend** app by Sessionboard puts your event in your pocket: the program, speakers, exhibitors, venue maps and live captions, with your own schedule. This guide is for attendees — your event organizer may send you a link to it.

## Why use it

Everything you need at the event is in one place and works without signal: what is on now, where it is, who is speaking, and the sessions you saved. Updates from the organizers reach you as they happen, and you can ask questions in sessions, read captions in the room and swap details with exhibitors without paper.

## Find your event and sign in

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the link from your organizer">

Tap the event app link in your registration or know-before-you-go email, or scan the QR code on a poster at the venue. It opens the Attend app, or the app store if the app isn't installed yet.

Already have the app? On **Find your event**, search for the event by name, enter the event code your organizer gave you, or tap **Scan a QR code**. If a code matches events in more than one region, pick yours.

</Step>

<Step title="Sign in with your registration email">

Enter the email address you registered with and tap **Email me a link**. You'll get an email with a sign-in link and a 6-digit code — tap the link on this phone, or type the code into the app. Both work for 30 minutes. No password needed.

If your organization uses single sign-on for the event, tap **Sign in with your organization** instead and sign in with your work or member account.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
Only registered attendees can sign in. If the app says it couldn't find your registration, sign in with the exact email you registered with, or check with the organizer. "Sign-in isn't open for this event yet" means the organizer hasn't turned the app on.
</Note>

You stay signed in to each event separately. **Me → Switch event** goes back to your events; **Me → Sign out** signs you out on this phone.

## Find your way around

The tabs at the bottom depend on how the organizer set up the app. You'll usually find:

- **Today** — what is happening now and what is next.
- **Agenda** — the full program, by day, with filters such as track, topic and format.
- **People** — speakers and, if the organizer turned it on, other attendees.
- **Credits** — at events with continuing-education credit, what you've earned and what is left to claim.
- **Map** — the venue maps. **Show on map** on an exhibitor or session takes you there.
- **Me** — your schedule, saves, profile, badge and settings.

The program is saved on your phone, so the agenda, speakers and maps work without signal. The app shows when it last updated.

## Build My schedule

Tap the bookmark on any session to **Save** it — your saved sessions are **My schedule**, under **Me**. You can save speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, topics, posters and products the same way; find them all under **Me → Saved**. Saves made offline are sent when you reconnect.

To put a session in your calendar, use **Add to calendar** on the session and pick **Apple Calendar**, **Google Calendar**, **Outlook.com**, **Outlook (work or school)** or a calendar file. **Subscribe in your calendar** adds all of My schedule and keeps it up to date as you save more.

## Follow live captions

When a session is live, open it and tap **Live captions** to read along as people speak. Tap **Save moment** to keep the caption on screen with the time, and add a note to it later. Your saved moments stay with the session under **Show saved moments**.

## Change the language

**Me → Language** changes the app's language. Captions keep their own language setting.

## Ask questions and take part

Depending on the event, a session may have tabs for **Q&A** (ask a question or upvote others'), **Polls** and **Evaluate** (the session evaluation, near the end of the session). If the event has **Ask**, you can ask the app about sessions, speakers, exhibitors and the venue, and every answer links to what it used.

## Show your badge or scan a code

**Me → My badge** shows your badge QR code for exhibitors who want your details. Above it, the app shows what a scan shares. Your email is only shared if you turn on **Share my email with exhibitors**, and you can turn it off again at any time. **Get a new code** stops your current code from working.

<Tip>
Turn up your screen brightness if a scanner can't read your badge. It works without signal too.
</Tip>

On a trade-show app, the **Scan** tab opens the QR codes on booths, products and session signs.

## Swap contacts with people you meet

If the organizer turned on contact exchange, you can save the people you meet by scanning their badge — no business cards.

<Steps>

<Step title="Scan their badge">

Ask them to open **Me → My badge**. Open **People I met** — it's in the networking card on **Today** and **Me** — and tap **Scan a badge** (or use the **Scan** tab, if the app has one), then point the camera at their QR code. You both save each other's contact, and they get a notification.

</Step>

<Step title="Add a note">

The app fills in **Where you met** from the session you had on in My schedule, or the booth you were at. Tap **Add a note** to remember what you talked about. Notes and tags are private — only you see them.

</Step>

</Steps>

Everyone you've swapped with is in **People I met**, with search and tags, and a count of new people who saved you since you last looked. Open a contact to **Save to contacts** on your phone, **Connect on LinkedIn**, email them if they shared their email, or message them and request a meeting where the event has those. **Export CSV** sends the whole list wherever you like. Scans made without signal are saved on your phone and sent when you reconnect.

**Me → My badge → People you meet** decides what others get when they scan you. Your name, photo, job title and company are always shared. **Share my email** is off until you turn it on, and **Share my LinkedIn profile** is on; turning either off later hides it from everyone who already saved you. Turn off **Let attendees save my contact** to stop swapping altogether.

<Tip>
Don't want to hear from someone you swapped with? Open their contact and **Block** them — they aren't told — or **Remove** them from your list. Removing only takes them off your list; your contact stays in theirs.
</Tip>

## Notifications, profile and the directory

- **Notifications** — the app asks whether to send you notifications a few seconds after you sign in. You'll get at most three a day and none at night, apart from urgent updates. Every message is also in the inbox under the bell on the home screen. Change it any time under **Me → Settings → Notifications**.
- **Profile** — **Me → Edit profile** to update your photo and the details the organizer lets you change. A new email takes effect once you confirm it with a code sent to that address.
- **Attendee directory** — if the event has one, **Show me in the attendee directory** decides whether other attendees can find you under **People**.

## Still having issues?

Contact your event organizer first — they manage registrations and sign-in for the event. For problems with the app itself, email [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with the event name, the email you registered with, your phone model and what you see on screen.
