You have just submitted your session information; however, you may want to make some adjustments to it. No worries! Below, you will find instructions on how to make edits to your submission **before the submission close date**.

1. Log into your portal

2. Within the **My Sessions** widget, click on the session that you would like to make edits to.
   ![Within the My Sessions widget, click on the session that you would like to make edits to](/images/kb/a2dfbcd7-Untitled-design-2-.gif)

3. At the bottom of the sidebar, select the button **View Submission**.

   ![At the bottom of the sidebar, select the button View Submission](/images/kb/b3c112ce-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-12.52.22-PM.png)

   ![At the bottom of the sidebar, select the button View Submission](/images/kb/37d1d1ab-Untitled-design-3-.gif)
