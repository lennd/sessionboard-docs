import { Note, Steps, Step } from '@compat';

To access your portal, use the portal link that's been shared with you. You can find this link in your **session submission confirmation email** (if you're the submitting individual).

<Note>Need the link resent? Contact the event team — they can resend it for you.</Note>

## Log in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal link">Click the link from your confirmation email.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    - **New users** — you'll be prompted to create a password.
    - **Existing users** — enter the password you created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to portal">Select **Continue to portal**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose your portal">Pick the portal you want to work in from the list of portals you have access to.</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **Error message on login?** You're likely using the wrong email address. Contact the event team to confirm the email associated with your account.
- **Forgot your password?** Select **Forgot your password?** at the bottom right of the login box. You'll get a reset email from `no-reply@sessionboard.com`.
