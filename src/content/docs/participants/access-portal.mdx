## How to locate the event portal link & login?

This link can be found in your session submission confirmation email (if you are the submitting individual).

If you need the portal link resent, contact the event team.

![If you need the portal link to be resent to you, please contact the event team for assistance](/images/kb/f7ca207e-Screenshot-2024-05-07-at-2_57_01-PM-1.png)

- **NEW USERS**: You will be prompted to create a password for your account.
  ![NEW USERS: You will be prompted to create a password for your account](/images/kb/d9d3615e-Screenshot-2024-05-07-at-3_10_00-PM-1.png)
- **EXISTING USERS**: Type the password you have created for your account.
  ![EXISTING USERS](/images/kb/276aba3f-Screenshot-2024-05-07-at-3_11_09-PM-1.png)

Once you have provided your email and password, select the "***Continue to portal***" button.

## Sign in with a magic link

Some events let you sign in without a password. After you enter your email, look for **Or, email me a sign-in link** below the password field — or, if the event uses sign-in links only, **Email me a sign-in link** appears as the main button and no password is asked for.

![The portal login form showing the password field with the Or, email me a sign-in link option below it](/images/kb/portal-login/portal-login-mixed-mode.png)

Select it and we'll email you a sign-in link:

![The Check your email confirmation showing where the sign-in link was sent and that it expires in 30 minutes](/images/kb/portal-login/portal-login-link-sent.png)

- The link **expires after 30 minutes**. If it expires, you'll land back on the login page with your email pre-filled — just request a new one.
- If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, or select **Try a different email** if you may have used another address.
- Opening the link signs you straight in to the portal — no password needed.

## Continue to your portal

Next, you will see a list of portals to which you have access. Select the portal you would like to continue within to complete tasks and view resources.

![You will see a list of portals to which you have access](/images/kb/431a762d-Screenshot-2024-05-07-at-3_10_48-PM-1.png)

## Error Message Accessing the Portal

If you receive the below error message, then **you are using the wrong email address** to log into your portal.

Contact the event team to retrieve the email associated with your account.

![Wrong email address error on the portal login page](/images/kb/0bc693c8-Screenshot-2023-03-08-at-4_32_21-PM-1.png)

## Forgot Your Password?

If you **forgot your password and need to reset it**, select "*Forgot your password?*" at the bottom right-hand corner of the login box.

![If you forgot your password and need to reset it](/images/kb/e3a11705-Screenshot-2024-05-07-at-3_11_33-PM-1.png)

You will receive an email from [no-reply@sessionboard.com](mailto:no-reply@sessionboard.com) with instructions to reset your password.
