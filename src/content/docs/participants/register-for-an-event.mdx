This page walks through registering for an event that uses Sessionboard registration. The exact steps depend on the event — a free event may skip payment, and not every event has workshops or add-ons.

## Why use it

Registering online takes a few minutes and gives you everything you need at the door: a confirmation with your QR code, a wallet pass and calendar invite, and a personal attendee portal where you can change your registration later.

## Register

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose your registration">
Open the registration link from the event. If the event has several registrations — for example attendees, groups, presenters or press — choose the one that fits you. A registration that is not open yet says when it opens. Use the language menu to switch languages if the event offers them.

![The registration page listing Attendee registration, Presenter registration with an invitation code and Press registration that opens later](/images/kb/register-choose-registration.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Tell us who you are">
Sign in with your society account to get member pricing, or continue with your email. The form tells you what it found: **Membership found.**, **Your membership has lapsed.** or **No member record for this email.** If your membership has lapsed, or you are not a member, the event may let you join or renew in the same checkout.

Presenter and invitation-only registrations ask for your **invitation code**. It is in your invitation or acceptance email.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick your registration type">
Choose the type that fits you. Member-only types are available once your membership is confirmed. Your price follows your type.

![The Registration type step with Member, Non-member and Trainee and their prices](/images/kb/register-type.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Enter your details">
Fill in your name, organization, name on badge and the event's questions. Buying for a group? Name your colleagues now, or leave the seats to assign later from your portal.

![The Your details step with name, email, organization, job title and country](/images/kb/register-details.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Choose workshops and add-ons">
Pick workshops or sessions if the event offers them. Each shows its price (or **Included**) and seats left; a full workshop offers the waitlist. Two workshops at the same time cannot both be picked. Then add any extras, such as a dinner or a guest pass.

![The Workshops step with a workshop, its time, seats left and price](/images/kb/register-workshops.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Review and pay">
Check your order, tick the event's agreement (for example its cancellation policy and code of conduct), and click **Continue to payment**. Enter a **Promo code (optional)** and click **Apply**. Then choose how to pay — **Pay in full**, **Pay a deposit now**, or **Bill me (invoice with a payment link)** — whichever the event offers. Card details are entered in the payment provider's secure field.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Payment step with the order total, a promo code field and the Bill me (invoice with a payment link) option](/images/kb/register-payment.png)

If your registration needs the organizers' approval, the last button is **Submit for approval** and nothing is charged until you are approved.

## After you register

The confirmation page — and your confirmation email — has:

- your confirmation number and **QR code**, which checks you in at the event,
- **Add to Apple Wallet** / **Add to Google Wallet**,
- **Add to calendar**,
- your receipt, or the balance and due date if you chose an invoice or deposit,
- a link to your [attendee portal](/participants/attendee-portal).

![The You're registered confirmation with the badge QR code, order, balance due, calendar and invoice buttons and What's next](/images/kb/register-confirmation.png)

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **You're registered** | You are confirmed. |
| **Request received** | Your registration is waiting for the organizers' approval. You will hear back by email. |
| **You are on the waitlist** | You will be emailed if a seat opens. |

<Tip>
Keep the confirmation email. Every registration email has a link that signs you in to your attendee portal.
</Tip>

## Still having issues?

Contact the event's registration team, or [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with the event name and the email you registered with.
