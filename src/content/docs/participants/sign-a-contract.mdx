When an event team sends you a speaker, sponsor, or exhibitor agreement through Sessionboard, Docusign emails you a signing link. If the event's portal has **My Contracts** turned on, you can also sign and download agreements from your portal.

## Sign an agreement

1. Log into your portal and click **My Contracts** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Needs Signature**, find the agreement and click **Sign Now**.
3. Docusign opens in a new tab. Review the document, complete any fields, and click **Finish**.
4. Return to your portal. The agreement moves to **All Contracts** with the status **Signed**.

<Note>
The Docusign email works too. Signing from either place updates your portal and the event team at the same time.
</Note>

## Download a signed copy

Under **All Contracts**, click **Download Signed PDF** on any signed agreement to save a copy.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs signature** | Waiting for you to sign. |
| **Signed** | Completed. A signed PDF is available to download. |
| **Declined** | You declined to sign in Docusign. |
| **Voided** | The event team cancelled the agreement. |

## Still having issues?

If **My Contracts** is missing from your sidebar or an agreement you expect is not listed, contact the event team that sent it. They control which agreements are addressed to you and whether the portal shows contracts.
