When an event team sends you a speaker, sponsor, or exhibitor agreement through Sessionboard, you receive an email with a signing link. Depending on how the team sent it, the link opens a Sessionboard signing page or Docusign. If the event's portal has **My Contracts** turned on, you can also see your agreements there.

## Why use it

Signing online takes a few minutes on any device, with no printer or scanner. The signing page works in light or dark mode and with a keyboard alone, and you get the completed PDF by email as soon as everyone has signed.

## Sign from the Sessionboard signing page

1. Open the link in the email. The page shows the agreement as a PDF — use **Open the PDF in a new tab** or **Download PDF** to read it in full.
2. Complete the fields under **Your fields** (initials, dates, or short text), if the agreement has any for you.
3. Under **Your signature**, **Draw** your signature with a mouse, finger or pen, or switch to **Type** and type your full name.
4. Read the **Electronic signature consent** and tick the box to agree.
5. Click **Sign document**. You will see a confirmation, and once everyone has signed you receive the completed PDF by email.

The page follows your device's light or dark setting and works with a keyboard alone: the first Tab stop is **Skip to main content**, and a typed signature can be completed without a mouse.

![The signing panel in dark mode, with Your fields, a typed signature, the ticked consent box, a yellow Sign document button and the Signing progress list](/images/kb/sign-page-dark-mode.png)

If you would rather not sign, click **Decline to sign** and, optionally, tell the sender why. The sender is notified and no further signatures are collected on that version.

<Note>
Some agreements are signed in order. If you open your link before it is your turn, the page tells you who it is waiting on and you are emailed again when it is ready for you. Signing links expire after a set number of days; if yours has expired, the next reminder email carries a fresh one, or ask the event team to resend it.
</Note>

## Sign from your portal

1. Log into your portal and click **My Contracts** in the sidebar. The number beside it is how many agreements are waiting for your signature.
2. Under **Needs Signature**, find the agreement and click **Sign now**.
3. An agreement with the **Sessionboard** badge opens the Sessionboard signing page in the same tab; follow the steps above, and when you click **Sign document** you return to **My Contracts**. An agreement with the **DocuSign** badge opens Docusign in a new tab; review the document, complete any fields, click **Finish**, then return to your portal.
4. The agreement leaves **Needs Signature**. Under **All Contracts** it shows **Signed** once everyone has signed, or **Waiting on others** while a later signer, such as the organizer countersigning, still has to sign.

<Note>
The email link works too. Signing from either place updates your portal and the event team at the same time. If you are the main contact for a sponsor or exhibitor, that company's agreements are listed in your portal as well.
</Note>

## Download a signed copy

Under **All Contracts**, click **Download Signed PDF** on any signed agreement to save a copy. Agreements signed on the Sessionboard signing page are also emailed to every signer once complete.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs signature** | Waiting for you, or for another signer ahead of you, to sign. |
| **Waiting on others** | You have signed; another signer still has to. |
| **Signed** | Completed. A signed PDF is available to download. |
| **Declined** | You or another signer declined to sign. |
| **Voided** | The event team cancelled the agreement. |

## Still having issues?

If **My Contracts** is missing from your sidebar, an agreement you expect is not listed, or your signing link no longer works, contact the event team that sent it. They control which agreements are addressed to you, can resend a link, and decide whether the portal shows contracts.
