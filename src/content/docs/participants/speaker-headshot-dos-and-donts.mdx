---
title: "Speaker headshot do's and don'ts"
description: "Tips for submitting a speaker headshot: face the camera, use a well-lit photo and a neutral background, and avoid uneven lighting or busy backgrounds."
---

Your headshot appears on the event agenda, program site, and any speaker embeds, so it's worth submitting a photo that shows your best side. Follow these simple tips.

![Speaker headshot do's and don'ts: three approved examples and three to avoid](/images/kb/d4a5a314-Headshot-Dos-and-Donts.jpg)

**Do**

- Face the camera and smile naturally.
- Use a well-lit photo.
- Use a neutral background.

**Don't**

- Use uneven lighting.
- Share the spotlight — you should be the only person in the photo.
- Use busy or distracting backgrounds.
