If you exhibit at or sponsor an event that sells booths through Sessionboard, you book on the event's **Exhibit & sponsor** page and then manage everything in your exhibitor portal.

## Why use it

Everything the show team needs from you — signed agreement, payment, staff names, insurance certificate, power order — is in one place with its due date, and you can see what you have bought and what you still owe at any time.

## Book a booth or sponsorship

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose how to take part">
On the event's Exhibit & sponsor page, click **Get started** and choose whether you are exhibiting or are a **Sponsor only** (no booth needed).

![The Exhibit & sponsor page asking whether to exhibit or sponsor only](/images/kb/exhibit-welcome.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Tell us about your company">
Enter your company name and primary contact, and answer the show team's questions. We match you to your existing record, so your history, priority points and staff carry over.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose your booth and package">
Pick a booth on the live floor plan — picking one holds it for you while you finish — and a package. Some events treat your pick as a preference and assign booths themselves.

![The Choose your booth step with the live floor plan showing available and sold booths](/images/kb/exhibit-choose-booth.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Add sponsorships">
Add any sponsorships to the same agreement.
</Step>
<Step title="Review and submit">
Depending on the event, pay in full by card, request an invoice, or **Submit application**. With an application, the show team confirms it and then sends the agreement for signature and the invoice to your portal.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in to your portal

Open the link in your email from the show team. Or open the portal's sign-in page, enter the email your company registered with, and we send you a sign-in link. Only contacts on your company's record can sign in — ask your company's primary contact to add you.

## What is in the portal

| Section | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **My tasks** | Your checklist and key dates. Tick off deadlines and sponsorship deliverables. |
| **Company profile** | Update your listing for the directory and the event app. |
| **Booth & orders** | See your booth on the floor plan, your order and what you still owe. |
| **Sponsorships** | What you sponsor, and add more. |
| **Staff & badges** | Add staff members, see how many staff badges you have left, buy extra badges when your package offers them (**Pay by card** or **Add to my invoice**), and **Copy staff registration link** for colleagues to register themselves. Staff changes close on the date the show team sets. |
| **Exhibitor manual** | Read the rules, move-in and freight sections, and **Read and acknowledge** the ones that ask. |
| **Shop** | **Order extras** such as power, internet, furniture and extra lead licences. Late prices apply after the order-by date. |
| **Invoices** | Deposits, installments and balances. Pay by card and download invoice PDFs. |
| **Contracts** | Review and sign your exhibitor and sponsor agreements. |
| **Leads** | Badge scans from your booth staff, from the first day of the show. |

<Tip>
Your booth staff scan attendee badges in the Attend app or on mobile web. You get each lead's name, title and organization, and their email only if they allow it.
</Tip>

## Still having issues?

Contact the event's exhibits team, or [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with the event name and your company name.
