## Why use it

Your portal is the one place to keep everything an event needs from you up to date, without emailing the organizer: your proposal, files, tasks and the profile attendees see. When you speak at several events, each one can show its own headshot and bio, so one event's requirements never overwrite another's.

### A different headshot or bio for one event

Your headshot and bio on your profile are used by every event you speak at. When
one event needs its own version (for example a black-and-white photo, or a bio
written for that event's theme), open your profile in that event's portal and,
under **Just for** the event, turn on **Use a different photo for this event** or
**Use a different bio for this event**. Only that event uses it: its agenda,
speaker pages and the apps it sends your profile to. Every other event keeps your
own. Turn the switch off to go back to your own headshot or bio.

Organizers can set the same thing on your contact record inside the event
(**Profile for** the event).

Still stuck? Your event organizer is the fastest route — they can see your portal
and reset access. For platform issues, [contact Sessionboard support](/faq/who-can-i-contact-for-additional-assistance).
