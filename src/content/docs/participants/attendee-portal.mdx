Your attendee portal is your personal page for an event you registered for. Open it from the link in any registration email.

## Why use it

Plans change. The portal lets you pay your balance, add a workshop, fix the name on your badge, or hand your place to a colleague yourself — at any hour, without waiting for the registration team to answer an email.

## Open your portal

Click the portal link (**Open your attendee portal**) in your confirmation email or any later registration email. If the event uses a portal Site, you can also **sign in with your email**: use the address you registered with. An address with no registration on the event sees **No registration for this email**. Lost the link? Ask the registration team to resend your confirmation.

### In the event app

If the event has an Attend app, open **Me → My registration** after you sign in. It shows your ticket and registrant type, anything you still owe, a preview of how your badge will print, and the sessions and workshops you picked. **Change my registration** and **Pay** open this portal in your phone's browser, where your card is taken in the payment provider's secure window.

## What is in the portal

![The attendee portal home with My registration, the balance due, My profile, My schedule and Meet people and exhibitors](/images/kb/attendee-portal-home.png)

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See your registration, what's next and anything you need to know before you go. |
| **My schedule** | The sessions and workshops you picked. |
| **Agenda** | Browse the program and **Add to my schedule**. |
| **Networking** | Find attendees who share your interests and the companies in the exhibit hall, and request meetings. |
| **Exhibitors** | Search exhibitors and see the exhibit hall map. |
| **Tickets & receipts** | Your QR code, wallet pass, calendar file, order and receipts. |
| **Edit registration** | Update your details and answers, such as the name on your badge or dietary needs. |

![Tickets & receipts with the badge QR code, order lines, balance, Add to your registration and Manage](/images/kb/attendee-portal-tickets.png)

### Preview your badge

When the event has designed its badge, **Tickets & receipts** shows **Your badge**. Click **Preview my badge** to see how your printed badge will look, with your name, organization and registration type. Something wrong? Fix it under **Edit registration**. The preview is not a badge: its code does not work at check-in, and badges are printed at the event.

## Pay a balance

If you chose an invoice or a deposit, **Tickets & receipts** shows what is still owed. Click **Pay balance** and pay by card. You get a receipt by email.

Have a credit from a cancelled registration? Enter its code under **Have a credit?** and click **Apply**. It pays what it covers.

**Statement (PDF)** lists everything you ordered, paid and were refunded for the event, with what is still owed.

## Payment plans

If you chose a payment plan, **Tickets & receipts** shows each installment, which are paid and when the next one is charged. Installments are charged automatically to the card or bank account you paid with. To use another one, click **Update card**, or **Pay from a bank account instead** when the event accepts bank payments. If a charge did not go through, you see a notice. Update your payment method and the installment is tried again.

## Add workshops or extras

Under **Add to your registration**, add workshops or extras the event still has available, and pay for them the same way. Guest badges are not listed here: a guest is added with their name on the registration form.

## Send your registration to a colleague

If you cannot attend, open **Tickets & receipts → Manage** and click **Send to a colleague**. Enter their first name, last name and email. If the event charges a substitution fee, you pay it by card first. They get their own confirmation, and your registration is closed. In their portal, **Tickets & receipts** shows the ticket and the order number; the order's amounts and receipts stay with the person who paid. After the event's cut-off date, ask the registration team.

## Cancel

Click **Cancel registration**. Before you confirm, the portal shows what the event's cancellation policy gives you back today and any cancellation fee it keeps. If the event offers it, you can take a credit toward a future event instead. Click **Yes, cancel**. When the event does not let registrants cancel a paid registration themselves, the portal says to contact the organizers and offers no **Yes, cancel**; a registration you have not paid for can still be cancelled here.

- If you paid by card or bank account, the refund goes back to it.
- If you paid by check, PO or wire, the registration team sends your refund.
- If you have not paid, any cancellation fee stays owing.

Your receipts stay available in the portal.

<Note>
Some options — sending to a colleague, cancelling, adding extras — appear only when the event allows them.
</Note>
