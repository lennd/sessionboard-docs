Your attendee portal is your personal page for an event you registered for. Open it from the link in any registration email.

## Why use it

Plans change. The portal lets you pay your balance, add a workshop, fix the name on your badge, or hand your place to a colleague yourself — at any hour, without waiting for the registration team to answer an email.

## Open your portal

Click the portal link in your confirmation email or any later registration email. If the event uses a portal Site, you can also **sign in with your email**. Lost the link? Ask the registration team to resend your confirmation.

## What is in the portal

![The attendee portal home with My registration, the balance due, My profile, My schedule and Meet people and exhibitors](/images/kb/attendee-portal-home.png)

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See your registration, what's next and anything you need to know before you go. |
| **My schedule** | The sessions and workshops you picked. |
| **Agenda** | Browse the program and **Add to my schedule**. |
| **Networking** | Find attendees who share your interests and the companies in the exhibit hall, and request meetings. |
| **Exhibitors** | Search exhibitors and see the exhibit hall map. |
| **Tickets & receipts** | Your QR code, wallet pass, calendar file, order and receipts. |
| **Edit registration** | Update your details and answers, such as the name on your badge or dietary needs. |

![Tickets & receipts with the badge QR code, order lines, balance, Add to your registration and Manage](/images/kb/attendee-portal-tickets.png)

## Pay a balance

If you chose an invoice or a deposit, **Tickets & receipts** shows what is still owed. Click **Pay balance** and pay by card. You get a receipt by email.

## Add workshops or extras

Under **Add to your registration**, add workshops or extras the event still has available, and pay for them the same way.

## Send your registration to a colleague

If you cannot attend, open **Tickets & receipts → Manage** and click **Send to a colleague**. Enter their first name, last name and email. They get their own confirmation, and your registration is closed.

## Cancel

Click **Cancel registration** and **Confirm cancellation**. Any refund under the event's policy goes back to your card. Your receipts stay available in the portal.

<Note>
Some options — sending to a colleague, cancelling, adding extras — appear only when the event allows them.
</Note>
