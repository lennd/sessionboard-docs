description: "Log into your portal and use the Files widget to view and download files shared with you by your event team."
audience: ["participant"]
---

1. Log into your portal
2. Click on the button Files at the top of your portal.

   ![View and download portal files](/images/kb/dfc9e0e8-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-12.37.51-PM.png)

3. From the Files widget, select a file you would like to view/download.

![View and download portal files](/images/kb/50d71a42-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-12.37.28-PM.png)
