If your event offers continuing-education (CE) credit through Sessionboard, the organizer sends you a link to the event's **claim site**. Use it to claim credit for the sessions you attended, answer the session evaluations, and download a certificate for each credit plus your full transcript.

## Why use it

Everything for your continuing education at this event is in one place: you claim only what you attended, you get a certificate for every credit the moment you claim it, and you can download your transcript whenever your licensing board or employer asks for it, even after claiming closes.

## Sign in

![The claim site sign-in with Badge number and Email tabs, the Last name field and Continue](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-sign-in.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link from your organizer">
    The page is titled **Claim your credits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sign in">
    Click **Badge number** to sign in with the number printed on your badge, or **Email** to use the email address you registered with (**Registration email**). On a field event, such as a dinner or a workshop, guests have no badges, so the page opens on **Email** and is titled with the event's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your last name">
    Type your **Last name** exactly as it appears on your registration, then click **Continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If sign-in fails, check the badge number or use the email you registered with. If your name on the registration is different (a maiden name, for example), contact the event organizer.

## Choose your credentials

The organizer's instructions appear at the top. Under **1 · Select the credentials you want to claim**, tick each credential you hold, such as AMA PRA Category 1 Credit or ANCC Contact Hours. Only those credentials are claimed in the next step. Each card shows how much of it you have claimed so far.

![Credential cards on the claim site with AMA PRA Category 1 Credit and General Attendance selected](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-credentials.png)

<Tip>
Need only a certificate of attendance? Select **General Attendance**, if your event offers it.
</Tip>

## Claim the sessions you attended

Under **2 · Claim the sessions you attended**, pick a day. Each session shows its time and the credit it offers for the credentials you selected. Sessions marked **Attended** are ones you scanned into or checked in to. Depending on the event, those are listed first, or they are the only sessions listed.

![The claim site session list for Tuesday, with two sessions marked Attended and Claimed with Remove, and other sessions with Claim](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-sessions.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Claim on a session">
    The session opens its evaluation if it needs one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the evaluation">
    Answer the required questions (marked with an asterisk) and add any comments. The line at the bottom lists the credentials you are about to claim.

    ![An open claim with the required Session Evaluation (PACE) questions and Submit claim](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-evaluation.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit claim">
    The session now shows **Claimed**, and a certificate is issued for each credential you claimed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can claim one session per time slot. If you already claimed a session that overlaps the one you are trying to claim, you'll see *You already claimed a session in this time slot*. Click **Remove** on the first session if you claimed the wrong one, then claim the right one. **Remove** works only while claiming is open.

## Check your credit summary

**3 · Your credit summary** totals what you have claimed for each credential. Click **Full transcript & certificates** (or **Transcript** at the top of the page) to open your transcript.

![The credit summary with 3 credits of AMA PRA Category 1 Credit and 0 hours of General Attendance](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-summary.png)

## Download your transcript and certificates

Your transcript groups every claim by credential, with a total for each. Each line shows the session code and title, the date, time and room, the event, how your attendance was verified (for example **Session scan**, **Imported attendance** or **Check-in**), when you completed the evaluation, and a **Certificate** link with its certificate number.

![Your transcript with General Attendance and AMA PRA Category 1 Credit sections and a certificate number on each line](/images/kb/credit-claiming/transcript.png)

- **Print** prints the page.
- **Download PDF** saves the transcript as a PDF.
- **Download Excel** saves it as a spreadsheet with a **Transcript** sheet (one row per claim) and a **Totals** sheet.
- Click a **Certificate** link to open that certificate, then **Download PDF** or **Print**.

![A continuing education certificate for 1.5 AMA PRA Category 1 credits with Download PDF and Print](/images/kb/credit-claiming/certificate.png)

![The downloaded PDF transcript, grouped by credit type with session code, event, date, time, room, attendance, evaluation and certificate number columns](/images/kb/credit-claiming/transcript-pdf.png)

<Note>
Times on the claim site, the on-screen transcript and the PDF and Excel downloads are all in the event's timezone, with the zone shown next to each time (for example `2:00 PM – 3:00 PM PST`). Your transcript and certificates stay available after claiming closes; sign in the same way to download them again.
</Note>
