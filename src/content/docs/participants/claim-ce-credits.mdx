If your event offers continuing-education (CE) credit through Sessionboard, the organizer sends you a link to the event's **claim site**. Use it to claim credit for the sessions you attended, answer any questions your credentials require, and download a certificate for each credit plus your full transcript. It works on a phone as well as a computer.

## Why use it

Everything for your continuing education at this event is in one place: you claim only what you attended, you get a certificate for every credit the moment you claim it, and you can download your certificates and transcript whenever your licensing board or employer asks for them, even after claiming closes.

## The claim site

The claim site carries the event's logo, name, dates and colours. Once you sign in, three tabs sit under the header: **Claim credits**, **My certificates** and **Transcript**. Your name and **Sign out** are at the top right.

![The claim site header with the event name and dates, the signed-in attendee, the English language menu and Sign out, and the Claim credits, My certificates and Transcript tabs](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-site-header.png)

The claim site is in English, Deutsch, Español or Français. It opens in your browser's language when it is one of these; use the language menu at the top right to change it.

## Sign in

![The claim site sign-in under the event header, with Badge number and Email tabs, the Last name field and Continue](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-sign-in.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link from your organizer">
    The page is titled **Claim your credits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sign in">
    Click **Badge number** to sign in with the number printed on your badge, or **Email** to use the email address you registered with (**Registration email**). On a field event, such as a dinner or a workshop, guests have no badges, so the page opens on **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your last name">
    Type your **Last name** exactly as it appears on your registration, then click **Continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If sign-in fails, check the badge number or use the email you registered with. If your name on the registration is different (a maiden name, for example), contact the event organizer. If you are signed out after a while, click **Sign in again**; sign-in links expire for your security.

## Choose your credentials

The organizer's instructions appear at the top of **Claim credits**. Under **1 · Select the credentials you want to claim**, tick each credential you hold, such as AMA PRA Category 1 Credit or ANCC Contact Hours. Only those credentials are claimed in the next step. Each card shows how much of it you have claimed so far.

![Credential cards on the claim site with CNE Contact Hours and General Attendance selected and the hours claimed on each](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-credentials.png)

<Tip>
Need only a certificate of attendance? Select **General Attendance**, if your event offers it.
</Tip>

## Claim the sessions you attended

Under **2 · Claim the sessions you attended**, pick a day. Each session shows its time and the credit it offers for the credentials you selected. Sessions marked **Attended** are ones you scanned into or checked in to. Depending on the event, those are listed first, or they are the only sessions listed.

![The claim site session list for Monday, with sessions marked Attended and Claimed, each with Remove](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-sessions.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Claim on a session">
    If the session asks for feedback, its survey opens below it. Answer the required questions (marked with an asterisk) and add any comments. The line at the bottom lists the credentials you are about to claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit claim">
    If a credential asks its own questions, **A few questions for your credit** opens and says which credential requires them. Answer every required question and click **Submit and claim**. Some credentials ask once for the whole event, so you won't see their questions again on your next claim.

    ![A few questions for your credit, required by CME Credits, with two Yes / No questions, a paragraph question and Submit and claim](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-questions.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is claimed">
    The session now shows **Claimed**, and a certificate is issued for each credential you claimed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can claim one session per time slot. If you already claimed a session that overlaps the one you are trying to claim, you'll see *You already claimed a session in this time slot*. Click **Remove** on the first session if you claimed the wrong one, then claim the right one. **Remove** works only while claiming is open.

<Note>
In the event's Attend app, you can claim credit that asks no questions. When a credential asks questions, the app sends you to the claim site to answer them and claim.
</Note>

## Check your credit summary

**3 · Your credit summary** totals what you have claimed for each credential. **My certificates** and **Transcript** next to it open those tabs.

![Your credit summary with 5.25 hours of CNE Contact Hours and 5.25 hours of General Attendance, and links to My certificates and Transcript](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-summary.png)

## My certificates

**My certificates** lists one certificate for every credit you claimed, grouped by credential, with the session code and title, the date, the certificate number and the amount.

![My certificates: 16 certificates across 2 credit types, with Download all, and the CNE Contact Hours group with Summary certificate, Download all (8), and View and PDF on each certificate](/images/kb/credit-claiming/claim-certificates.png)

- **View** opens a certificate; **PDF** downloads it.
- **Download all** at the top saves every certificate; **Download all** on a credential saves that credential's certificates in one PDF, a page each.
- **Summary certificate** is one certificate per credential with your total and the list of sessions.

A certificate shows your name, the amount and credential, the session title, code and date, the event, the certificate number and issue date, and, when the organizer adds them, a signature and accreditation statement. Open it to **Print** or **Download PDF**.

![A certificate of credit for 0.5 hours of CNE Contact Hours with the certificate number, issue date, session code, event, signatory and accreditation statement, with Print and Download PDF](/images/kb/credit-claiming/certificate.png)

## Download your transcript

**Transcript** groups every claim by credential, with a total for each. Each line shows the session code and title, the date, time and room, the event, the credential and amount, how your attendance was verified (for example **Session scan**, **Imported attendance** or **Check-in**), when you completed the survey, and a **Certificate** link with its certificate number.

![Your transcript with Print, Download Excel and Download PDF, the All credits, General Attendance and CNE Contact Hours filters, and claims with a certificate number and Download this credit](/images/kb/credit-claiming/transcript.png)

- **All credits** or a credential at the top limits the transcript to that credential; the title changes to match, and the downloads follow it.
- **Download this credit** on a line downloads the transcript of that one credit.
- **Print** prints the page.
- **Download PDF** saves the transcript as a PDF.
- **Download Excel** saves it as a spreadsheet with a **Transcript** sheet (one row per claim) and a **Totals** sheet.

<Note>
Times on the claim site, the transcript and the PDF and Excel downloads are all in the event's timezone, with the zone shown next to each time (for example `2:00 PM – 3:00 PM PST`). Your transcript and certificates stay available after claiming closes; sign in the same way to download them again.
</Note>
