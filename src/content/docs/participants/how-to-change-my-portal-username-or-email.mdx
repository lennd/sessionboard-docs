1.  Using the following link log into Sessionboard: https://app.sessionboard.com/login
2.  Once you have logged into your account you will have access to a Sessionboard dashboard listing the event(s) you are associated with. Click the square with your initials in the top right-hand corner of your screen and select "Account Settings". This will take you to your profile.

![Screenshot 2025-01-30 at 10.42.16 AM](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/Screenshot%202025-01-30%20at%2010.42.16%20AM.png)

3.  Navigate to the field "Email Address" and update this field with your new email to be used for future login. ​

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/Knowledge%20Base%20Import/Screenshot%2B2023-08-03%2Bat%2B1_46_19%2BPM-1.png)

4.  Once complete, press the green "SAVE CHANGES" button.

PORTAL ACCESS

If you are a contact who needs to access a portal, select the event name from the dashboard home page. This will take you to your portal if you have access to the event portal.
