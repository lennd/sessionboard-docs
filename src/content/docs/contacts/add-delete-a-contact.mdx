## The Contacts module

**All Contacts** is everyone attached to this event, whatever their role. The entries under it in the sidebar — **Speakers**, **Chairpersons**, **Moderators**, **Submitters**, **Additional Contacts** — are views of the same people filtered by role, not separate lists. Assign a contact to a session as a speaker and they appear under Speakers; nothing is copied.

![The All Contacts table: name, email, assigned portal and task columns, with Add Contact and Options at the top right](/images/kb/contacts-list.png)

Contacts belong to your organization, not to one event. The person who spoke last year is the same record this year, which is why adding a contact starts with a search.

<Note>
Adding a speaker from the Speakers module or from a session creates the contact first, the same way. Once the contact is on a session they show in Speakers as well.
</Note>

## Add a contact

1. In **All Contacts**, click **Add Contact** at the top right.
2. The panel opens on a search, not a form. Type a name or email. Matches are grouped into contacts already **in event** and contacts elsewhere **in organization**.
   - Someone already in the event: click **View contact** to open them — there is nothing to add.
   - Someone in your organization but not this event: tick them and click **Add to Event** at the bottom of the panel. See [Adding existing contacts to a new event](/contacts/adding-existing-contacts-to-a-new-event).
3. Only when nobody comes back, click **Add new contact**.

![The Add Contact panel after a search — one match in the event with a View contact button, a reminder to reuse existing contacts, and the Add new contact button](/images/kb/contacts-add-search.png)

4. Fill in **New Contact Details**. **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** are required; mobile phone, biography and the rest can be completed later, by the contact in their portal or by an import.
5. Click **Create Contact**.

![New Contact Details: required first name, last name and email, then mobile phone and biography](/images/kb/contacts-add-new.png)

<Tip>
Which fields appear in this form, and in what order, is set per event in **Settings > Record Settings > Layouts**. Put the fields your team fills in at creation time at the top and leave the rest for the profile.
</Tip>

## Delete or remove a contact

1. Tick the box to the left of the contact. The bulk bar appears above the table with **Edit**, **Send**, **Assign**, **More** and **Delete**.

   ![The bulk bar for one selected contact — Edit, Send, Assign, More, Delete and Clear](/images/kb/contacts-bulk-bar.png)

2. Click **Delete**. The dialog asks the question that matters:
   - **Remove from this event** takes the contact off this event only. Their record, history and every other event they are part of are untouched, and you can add them back later from the organization.
   - **Remove from all events** deletes the contact from your organization, including every other event. This cannot be undone.

![The Remove 1 contact? dialog with its two choices — remove from this event, or remove from all events](/images/kb/contacts-remove.png)

Removing from this event is the right choice almost every time. Use **Remove from all events** for genuine mistakes — a test record, or a duplicate you would rather delete than [merge](/contacts/merge-duplicates).

<Note>
A contact removed from the event also loses that event's portal access and comes off any sessions they were assigned to. Their sessions stay; only the assignment is cleared.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="It says a contact with this email already exists, but I cannot see them in the event. Why?">

An email address is unique across your whole organization, not just this event. The message means an active contact with that address already exists somewhere in your organization — usually in another event, or removed from this event but not from the organization. Deleted contacts do not block the address.

Instead of creating the person again, click **Add Contact**, type the email, and look under **in organization** — tick the match and click **Add to Event**. To find them outside any event, open **CRM → Contacts** at the organization level and search by email. If two records do exist for the same person under different addresses, [merge them](/contacts/merge-duplicates).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I see my speakers?">

Speakers are contacts with the Speaker role on at least one session, so they are a view of the same records as **All Contacts**. In the event, open **CRM** → **People** → **Speakers**; the same row exists for **Chairpersons**, **Moderators** and **Submitters**. A person appears there once they are on a session in that role — until then they are only in **All Contacts**. See [Create and assign speakers](/speakers/create-assign-speakers) and [why a new contact isn't in the Speakers view](/faq/i-created-a-new-contact-why-do-i-not-see-them-within-the-speakers-module).

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
