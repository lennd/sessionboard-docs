A contact's profile is the one place that knows everything about a person — what you have collected about them, which sessions and events they are part of, what has been sent to them and what they have done in their portal. It opens as a panel over whichever table you were in, so you can move through a list without losing your place.

## Why use it

Organizers who need to honour attendee data-access or deletion requests (e.g. GDPR or CCPA) can now export a person's data, record or clear their consent, or permanently remove their attendee records — all from the Person 360 page, without needing a support ticket. The org-level processing toggle lets privacy-conscious teams pause data processing across all attendees at once.

## Open a profile

In **All Contacts** (or any role view, or Speakers), click **Edit** at the left of the row. Clicking the contact's name in most other places — a session's speaker list, an email's recipients, a pipeline card — opens the same panel.

![The Contact Profile panel — headshot, name and email, the tab row, Portal Tasks, Biography and the Profile fields](/images/kb/contact-profile.png)

The header shows the headshot, name and email, with **Actions** at the top right: **Send Email**, **Send SMS**, **Manage Portal Access**, **View Portal**, **Copy Portal Link**, **Copy URL** (a link to this profile for a teammate) and **Delete**. The expand icon next to it opens the profile as a full page.

![The Actions menu on a contact profile](/images/kb/contact-profile-actions.png)

## The tabs

![The profile tabs: Profile, Notes, Program, CRM, Marketing, Activity, Messages](/images/kb/contact-profile-tabs.png)

The tabs mirror the product's modules, so a contact's record is organized the same way the sidebar is.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Every field on the contact — standard fields, your custom fields and organization attributes — grouped by section, plus **Portal Tasks**, **Additional Contacts** and **Details** (below). Click any value to edit it in place. |
| **Notes** | Internal notes about the person. Notes are never shown to the contact. |
| **Program** | Where this person appears in your programs: **Events**, **Sessions**, **Awards Programs** and **Files** — the files on their record and on the sessions they are part of. |
| **CRM** | Their place in Speaker CRM: **Pipeline** entries, **Contracts**, **Segments** and **Interest Forms** submissions. |
| **Marketing** | **Media** (their assets in the Media Library) and **Advocacy** (their share links and activity). Shown when your organization has Media or the event has Marketing. |
| **Activity** | The unified timeline: emails and SMS sent, portal task completions, field changes, session assignments — everything that happened to this record, newest first. A change made through the Public API shows **via Public API** after the name of who made it. |
| **Messages** | The conversation with this contact in their event portal. Inside an event it is that event's thread; on the organization profile every event's thread is listed. Shown when messaging is enabled for your organization. |

A red dot on **Notes**, **Activity** or **Messages** means there is something unread there.

<Note>
An organization with the [Disclosures](/contacts/disclosures) Preview turned on also has a **Disclosure** tab, between Program and CRM: the contact's disclosure status, open request, relationships on file and submission history.
</Note>

## The Profile tab

**Portal Tasks** at the top is the contact's progress through the tasks their portal assigns — confirm participation, upload a headshot, sign the agreement. You can mark a task complete from here on their behalf.

Below it, the sections follow the layout you set in **Settings > Record Settings > Layouts**: Biography, Profile (job title, company, phone), Address, social links, then your custom fields. A field shows its placeholder — *Add job title...* — until it has a value; click it, type, and it saves when you leave the field — a **Saved successfully** toast confirms it. The name, email and **Biography** are the exception: they wait for the **Save Changes** bar at the bottom of the profile (see [save a field](/faq/how-do-i-save-a-field)).

<Tip>
Changing the **Email** field on a profile does not change the address the contact signs in with. That is the portal username, in the Details card below — see [Email address vs. portal username](/contacts/changing-a-contact-s-email-address).
</Tip>

### Additional Contacts

The assistants and colleagues linked to this contact, with their role. They can be copied on the contact's emails and can open the contact's portal on their behalf. Click **Add** to link one. See [Additional contacts](/contacts/additional-contacts).

![The Additional Contacts card on a profile, listing the contact's assistant with her role, and the Add button](/images/kb/contact-additional-contacts.png)

### Details

The record's system information: its **ID**, the **Portal Username** the contact signs in with, **Last Login At**, the role views they belong to, when an email was last sent to them and how many, created and updated dates, and the **API ID** integrations use.

**Portal options**, next to the username, holds **Manage Portal Access**, and — once the contact has a login — **Change Portal Username** and **Reset Password**.

![The Details card with Portal options open — Manage Portal Access, Change Portal Username, Reset Password](/images/kb/contact-portal-options.png)
