The Contact Profile in Sessionboard is your central hub for managing contact information. It’s divided into four key tabs: **Profile**, **Notes**, **Connections**, **Files**, and **Activity**. Each tab serves a specific purpose and helps streamline event planning and communication.

<Note>
To open a contact's profile, select the pencil icon to the left of the contact record in the Contacts or Speakers module.

![To open a contact's profile](/images/kb/67168a5f-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-17-41-7746-PM.png)
</Note>

## Profile

The **Profile** tab is your quick view of a contact’s involvement in an event.

We suggest expanding the record to see all of the details:
![We suggest expanding the record to see all of the details](/images/kb/a180b45f-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-19-54-1214-PM.png)

What you'll see:

- **Contact fields** — name, bio, photo, role, and contact options such as email and SMS, plus quick access to their portal tasks.
- **Address and social media links**
- **Global attributes and custom fields**
- **Portal tasks** — task progress, such as registration, booking a hotel room, or signing a speaker agreement.
- **Additional contacts** — linked assistants or collaborators.

![An expanded contact profile showing fields, address, and social links](/images/kb/b1ef936b-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-22-16-4785-PM.png)

![The portal tasks and additional contacts sections of a contact profile](/images/kb/dcd29fc1-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-31-22-5645-PM.png)

## Notes

Includes notes (***NOTE**: Notes are not visible to the contact*).

- ***NOTE**: If the user is a Session Submitter ONLY, the session they submitted will not appear within their profile*

![Includes notes (NOTE](/images/kb/1bb633cf-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-29-11-1144-PM.png)

## Connections

Shows which events, sessions & segments this person is involved in.

![Shows which events, sessions & segments this person is involved in](/images/kb/d6c7cead-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-30-09-5120-PM.png)

## Files

The **Files** tab stores all files associated with the contact record and their associated sessions.

![The Files tab stores all files associated with the contact record and their associated sessions](/images/kb/6e0a9b83-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-26-47-1152-PM.png)

## Activity

The **Activity** tab records all communications sent to the contact, along with any changes made to their record.

![The Activity tab records all communications sent to the contact, along with any changes made to their record](/images/kb/a05a1250-image-png-Feb-03-2026-08-27-41-0437-PM.png)
