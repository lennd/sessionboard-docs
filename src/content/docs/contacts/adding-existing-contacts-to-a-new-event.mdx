title: "Adding existing contacts to a new event"
description: "It is not uncommon for a contact to speak at more than one event year after year."
---

It is not uncommon for a contact to speak at more than one event year after year. In Sessionboard, we make it easy for event admins to add existing contacts to new events without having to recreate them.

To add an existing contact to your event, within the Contacts module, select the 'Add Contact' button at the top right-hand corner of your screen.

![Adding existing contacts to a new event in Sessionboard](/images/kb/066966de-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-01-24-6220-PM.png)

![Adding existing contacts to a new event in Sessionboard (2)](/images/kb/62c75b77-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-01-43-6874-PM.png)

Within the pop-up window, search for the contact (either by name or email) you want to add. Select the square box to the right of the contact and click the 'Add To Event' at the bottom of the window.

![Adding existing contacts to a new event in Sessionboard (3)](/images/kb/0c979de2-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-04-02-1799-PM.png)
