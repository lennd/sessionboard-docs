Speakers come back. A contact created for one event belongs to your whole organization, so when the same person is invited to the next event you add the existing record rather than creating a new one — their profile, headshot, bio and history come with them, and you never end up with two of the same person.

## From the event: one or a few contacts

1. In the event, open **CRM > All Contacts** and click **Add Contact**.
2. Search by name or email. Results are split into contacts already **in event** and contacts elsewhere **in organization**.
3. Tick the contacts you want under *in organization* and click **Add to Event** at the bottom of the panel. The button shows how many you have selected.

![The Add Contact panel after a search — matches in the event and in the organization](/images/kb/contacts-add-search.png)

They appear in All Contacts at once, with their organization-level profile — bio, photo, company, social links — already filled in. Event-specific fields (their session, portal tasks, this event's custom fields) start empty, because those belong to the new event.

<Note>
Only create a new contact when the search comes back empty. **Add new contact** creates a second record even if the person already exists under a different email address; if that has happened, [merge the duplicates](/contacts/merge-duplicates).
</Note>

## From the organization: many contacts at once

The organization's **CRM > Contacts** directory lists every contact across all your events. Use it when you are bringing a whole cohort forward — every speaker from last year's program, or everyone in a segment.

1. Open the organization (your organization's name at the top of the sidebar, then **CRM > Contacts**).
2. Filter or search the directory down to the people you want — for example **Events** = last year's event — and tick them, or tick **Select all**.
3. In the bulk bar, click **Add To Event** and pick the event from the list — the footer confirms how many contacts you are adding.

![The directory bulk bar for a selected contact — Communicate, Add To Event, Add To Curated List, Invite To Event, Delete](/images/kb/org-directory-add-to-event.png)

![The Add To Event panel listing upcoming events](/images/kb/org-add-to-event.png)

The same bar offers two ways to email the selection without adding them to anything: **Communicate** opens a blank email, and **Invite To Event** opens the same composer pre-filled with an *Invitation To Apply* — a short note that speaker applications are open, which you edit and send. Neither changes who is in an event.

<Tip>
Segments and curated lists in [Speaker CRM](/speaker-crm/crm) are a good way to keep a "next year" shortlist through the year, then add the whole list to the event in one step when programming starts.
</Tip>
