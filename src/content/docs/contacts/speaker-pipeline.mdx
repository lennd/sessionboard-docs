The speaker pipeline is a board for one event. Each column is a stage, each card is a person you are considering as a speaker, and the stage decides which portal they see. It works like the organization [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline), but it is scoped to a single event and it takes in your call for papers (CFP) as well as people your team finds.

<Note>
The speaker pipeline is available on **Enterprise** events. On a Pro event, **Event Home → All tools** shows it with an upgrade option. Contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) to move an event to Enterprise.
</Note>

## Why use it

If your team scouts speakers by hand and also runs a CFP, everyone who fills in a form looks like a speaker, and everyone gets a portal. The pipeline separates the people you are considering from the people you have confirmed. Your team and your CFP feed one board, you move people forward as decisions are made, and each portal opens only for the stages you choose. Stage replaces a "verified speaker" custom field that you would otherwise rebuild on every event.

## Turn it on for an event

<Steps>
<Step title="Open All tools">
Go to **Event Home** and open the **All tools** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Turn on Speaker pipeline">
Find **Speaker pipeline** in the **Before** phase and turn it on.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the board">
Open **CRM → People → Speaker pipeline** in the event sidebar.
</Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot-slot: speaker-pipeline-all-tools.png. Event Home All tools tab with the Speaker pipeline card, on Enterprise (turn on) and on Pro (upgrade). Alt: "The Speaker pipeline card on the All tools tab of Event Home". */}

When you turn it on, Sessionboard fills the board from what the event already has:

- Speakers with an accepted session go to **Confirmed**.
- People who only submitted to the CFP go to **Applied**.
- Speakers you added by hand who have no session go to **Confirmed**.

Turning it off hides the board and keeps its data. Turning it on again restores it. Both actions are logged in the audit trail.

<Tip>
Before you turn it on for a live event, open each portal's **Selection Criteria** and check the preview. It shows who would match once you add a stage filter, so no one loses their portal by surprise.
</Tip>

## Who gets on the board

Only people with a reason to be considered join the pipeline. Being in the event's contacts does not add someone. A person is in an event's pipeline once.

| Source | What happens |
|---|---|
| CFP submission | The submitter enters at the entry stage you set in **Settings** (default **Applied**). |
| Added by hand | The person enters at the stage you choose when you add them. |
| Import | The person enters at the stage in the column you map during the import. |
| Ask scouting | A suggestion enters at **Sourced** after a teammate approves it. |

Each card shows its source, so you can tell a CFP submitter from a scouted name at a glance. Staff, sponsor representatives and attendees stay out. The option to add every new event contact is off by default.

## Add people and choose a stage

<Steps>
<Step title="Start from the board">
Click **+ Add to pipeline**. You can also add someone from the contact sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the person">
Search the event's contacts by name, email or company. If the person is new, add them. They are added to the event's contacts too.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a stage">
Pick the stage they start in. Add an owner, a score and a short rationale if you want teammates to see why you picked them.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Click **Add**. The person appears as a card, with **Added by hand** as the source.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Move cards

Drag a card to another column, or open the card and choose a stage. Click a card to open the contact sidebar with the person's stage, source, owner, sessions and history.

Each move is saved at once, and it is logged with who made it. Moves can also come from a rule or an automation. See [the audit trail](#the-audit-trail-and-history).

## Settings

Click **Settings** on the board. Changes apply to this event only.

### Stages

Stages come from your organization's **Speaker pipeline template**. A new event starts with these stages:

| Stage | Type |
|---|---|
| Sourced | Open |
| Applied | Open |
| Shortlisted | Open |
| Invited | Open |
| Confirmed | Won |
| Declined | Lost |

An organization admin edits the template under the organization pipeline settings. The **Won** and **Lost** types drive reporting.

### Entry rules

Choose which sources add people, and the stage each one uses: CFP submissions, people added by hand, imports, Ask scouting, and every new event contact (off by default).

### Moves on its own

These rules move people so nobody updates two places:

- **Session accepted → Confirmed.** On by default. A review decision on the person's session moves them to **Confirmed**.
- **All sessions declined → Declined.** Off by default.
- **Keep people added by hand when their session is declined.** On by default. A person your team picked stays **Confirmed** even if a talk is declined.

A move made by a rule shows in the stage history as **by rule**.

## Use stages in a portal's Selection Criteria

Stage is a filter in each portal, next to role, custom fields and session status.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the portal">
Go to **Program → Portals**, open the portal and go to **Selection Criteria**.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the filter">
Click **Add filter**, choose **Speaker pipeline stage**, and tick the stages that should see this portal. For example, a Speaker Portal that matches **Confirmed**, and an Applicant Portal that matches **Applied** and **Shortlisted**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the preview">
The table shows who matches. Save when it looks right.
</Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot-slot: speaker-pipeline-portal-criteria.png. Northwind Health Summit 2027, a portal's Selection Criteria with the Speaker pipeline stage is any of filter and the preview table. Alt: "A portal's Selection Criteria with a Speaker pipeline stage filter". */}

Tasks, forms and files assigned to a portal follow its criteria. When you move a card, the person's portal and tasks change with it. A person who stops matching loses that portal. The work they submitted is kept, and the change is logged. For how portals choose between several matches, see [Portal filters and groups](/portals/portal-filters-and-groups).

## Automations

[Automations](/automations/overview) can react to the pipeline and move people through it.

- **Trigger: Speaker pipeline stage changed.** Choose the stage a person moved from or to.
- **Action: Move to stage.** Move the person to a stage you pick.
- **Filter: Speaker pipeline stage is.** Limit any people automation to certain stages.

For example, when someone moves to **Confirmed**, send a welcome email and assign the speaker tasks. An automation runs once per person per event. A rule or automation cannot trigger itself.

## The audit trail and history

Every add, move, archive, and turning the pipeline on or off is recorded with who did it: a teammate, a rule, an automation, Ask or the system. Automation runs record what they did, linked to the move that started them. Portal matches gained or lost are recorded too.

You can see it in three places:

- **Audit trail** on the board, for this event's pipeline.
- The **Pipeline** tab of a contact, for that person.
- **History** at the organization level, under the **Speaker pipeline** activity type. Filter by event and by actor.

## Where it shows on a contact

On the event contact sidebar, the **Pipeline** tab shows this event's stage as a stepper, where the person came from, the owner, their sessions, which portals they match, and their audit trail. Their stages in other events are listed below it.

On the organization's full contact profile, the **Events** tab gains **Speaker pipeline**, **Came in from** and **Portal** columns, one row per event. Stage is always per event.

The organization Pipeline tab stays separate. It holds your sales and partnership stages.

## Permissions

| What they can do | Permission |
|---|---|
| See the board and stages | View contacts |
| Add people, move cards, change owner | Update contacts |
| Edit stages, entry rules and the portal stage filter | Manage pipeline settings (event admins by default) |
| Edit the organization template | Update organization settings |

Speakers never see stage names. Their portal shows status only. Someone with access to this event only cannot open the organization pipeline.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Does everyone still get a portal?">
Yes. Stage decides which portal a person sees, not whether they get one.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does it replace session status?">
No. **Session accepted → Confirmed** lets review decisions feed the pipeline. Your team can also confirm someone who has no session yet.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can one person be in the pipeline twice?">
No. A person is in an event's pipeline once. They can be in the pipelines of other events at different stages.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens when I turn it off?">
The board is hidden and its data is kept. Portals that filter by stage keep their filter, so review them first.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why is a contact missing from the board?">
Being in the event's contacts does not add someone. Add them with **+ Add to pipeline**, or check the entry rules in **Settings**.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
