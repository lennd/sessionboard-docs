Every session has one **Submitter**: the person who completed the submission form. Often that is the speaker, but not always — an executive assistant submitting for their team, an agency contact entering a sponsor's session, a program committee member adding a talk on a speaker's behalf. The submitter is not a speaker role; it is a pointer to the contact who owns the submission.

Being the submitter matters in the portal. The session appears under the submitter's **My Submissions**, they can edit it while the form allows, and they receive submission-change notifications along with the session's participants. Sessions an admin adds by hand have no submitter until you set one.

## Set or change the submitter

The submitter has to exist as a contact first — find them with **Add Contact** on **CRM > All Contacts**, or create them there ([Add & delete a contact](/contacts/add-delete-a-contact)).

1. Open **Program > Sessions** and click the pencil on the session's row.
2. Open the **Participants** tab and find the **Submitter** block, below the speaker roles.
3. If a submitter is set, click **×** (*Clear submitter*) on their row. Click **Add**, search for the contact and pick them.
4. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the panel.

![The Participants tab of the session editor — the Submitter block holds one contact](/images/kb/session-editor-participants.png)

<Note>
A session has one submitter. Adding a second person who should also be able to see and edit the session in the portal is a participants question — give them a speaker or custom role instead ([add a moderator or chairperson](/contacts/add-a-moderator-chairperson)).
</Note>

## Where else the submitter shows up

- **CRM > Submitters** lists every contact who is the submitter of at least one session in the event — useful for a "thank you for submitting" email that should go to the assistant, not the speaker.
- Session change emails ([submission change notifications](/portals/submission-change-notifications)) go to the submitter as well as the participants.
- The **Withdrawn** and **Drafts** tabs on the Sessions list are the submitter's actions: pulling a submission or saving one without sending it ([draft submissions](/sessions/draft-submissions)).
