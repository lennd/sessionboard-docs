A contact has two addresses that usually match and occasionally drift apart:

- The **Email** field on their profile — where emails and portal invitations are delivered, and what the contact's row shows in All Contacts.
- The **Portal Username** — the address they type to sign in to their portal, and the one shared login they use across every event and organization in Sessionboard.

Editing the **Email** field changes only the first. A contact whose address you update on the profile keeps signing in with the old one, and a login attempt with the new address fails because no account exists for it. When the change is meant to move their login too, do both.

## Change the email on the profile

Open the contact's profile (**Edit** at the left of their row), click the **Email** value in the Profile section, type the new address and click **Save Changes**. A confirmation — *Update this contact's email address?* — reminds you that this is the communication email only; click **Update Email**. Emails from now on go to the new address.

## Change the portal username

1. In the same profile, scroll to the **Details** card at the bottom of the **Profile** tab. **Portal Username** shows the address they currently sign in with — a dash means they have never been given portal access.
2. Click **Portal options** and choose **Change Portal Username**.

   ![The Details card with Portal options open — Manage Portal Access, Change Portal Username, Reset Password](/images/kb/contact-portal-options.png)

3. Enter the address in **New Username** and click **Update**.

   ![The Change Portal Username dialog — the existing username and a New Username field](/images/kb/contact-change-username.png)

The contact signs in with the new address immediately, with the same password. Their sessions, tasks and submissions are unchanged — the login moved, not the record.

<Warning>
A username must be unique across Sessionboard. If the new address already belongs to another login, the dialog says so; in that case the two records are probably the same person and should be [merged](/contacts/merge-duplicates) rather than renamed.
</Warning>

<Note>
**Reset Password**, in the same menu, emails the contact a password-reset link. It is the fastest answer to "I can't get into my portal" once the username is right.
</Note>
