<Warning>
Merging **cannot be undone**. You can merge two or three contacts at a time. Event-level field values, notes and additional contacts come from the primary contact only. Export your contacts first if you want a record of what the duplicates held.
</Warning>

## Who can merge

Duplicate detection and merging are part of **Speaker CRM**, or of the **Unique Contacts** add-on for organizations without the full CRM. Without either, a contact's profile still shows how many potential duplicates were found, with a prompt to contact support to enable Unique Contacts.

## How duplicates are found

Sessionboard compares each contact to the rest of your organization's contacts by **name** and by the part of the **email address before the @**, allowing for small spelling differences. So *Ray Auer* at a hospital address and *Raymond Auer* at a personal address are flagged; two people who share a surname are usually not. Contacts already merged are not suggested again.

## Merge

1. Open the contact's profile — **Edit** at the left of their row in an event's **CRM > All Contacts**, or from the organization's **CRM > Contacts** directory. When Sessionboard has found potential duplicates, a banner at the top reads *We found N potential duplicates of this contact.* Click **Merge Duplicates**. (**Ignore** hides the banner for this visit.)
2. **Select Contacts.** The table lists the potential duplicates with their Contact ID, name, email, portal username, events and last update. Tick the records to merge — two or three in total — and use **Use as primary** on the one that should survive. Search the table if the right record is not listed. Click **Next**.
3. **Compare & Merge.** Each field appears as a row with one column per contact; the primary's values are pre-selected. Click a different value to keep it instead, and use **Show differences** to hide fields where all the records agree. Click **Merge**, then confirm — the dialog reminds you the non-primary contacts will disappear from your events.

A *Contacts merged* message confirms completion, and you land back on the primary contact's profile.

## What the merge keeps

| From every merged contact | From the primary contact only |
|---|---|
| Event memberships — the primary is added to any event only a duplicate was in | Event-level field values (custom fields set per event) |
| Session roles: speaker, moderator, chairperson and custom roles such as co-author | Notes |
| Portal tasks and their completed status, files and follow-up forms | Additional contacts |
| Portal logins — a user who signed in as a duplicate now opens the primary's portal | Field values you did not switch on the compare step |
| Email history: messages sent to a duplicate stay visible in the primary's history | |

The merge also shows up in the primary contact's **Activity** feed.

<Note>
Portal assignment is filter-based, so the primary contact's portal can change after a merge if the merged field values no longer match the portal's filters. Check **Manage Portal Access** on the profile afterwards.
</Note>

## Avoid the duplicate in the first place

Most duplicates come from creating a new contact when one already existed under another address. Search first — see [Adding existing contacts to a new event](/contacts/adding-existing-contacts-to-a-new-event) — and let imports match on email so they update rather than create ([Importing data](/settings/importing-data)).
