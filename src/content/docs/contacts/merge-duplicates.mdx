import { Note, Tip, Warning, Steps, Step } from '@compat';

Merge duplicate contacts to keep your records clean — up to **3 contacts at a time**.

<Warning>
Merging **cannot be undone**. Event-level fields, notes, and additional contacts are inherited from the primary contact. Export your contact data first if you want a backup.
</Warning>

## Merge duplicates
<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a record with duplicates">
    In **Contacts**, select the pencil icon beside the record.
    <Tip>Have Speaker CRM? You can merge from the **Organization Dashboard CRM** too — the process is identical.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the merge">
    The profile shows how many duplicates were identified at the top. Click **Merge Duplicates**.
    <Note>Duplicates are identified by a 70–80% match on the contact's email or name.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select contacts and a primary">
    Check the 2–3 contacts to merge, choose which one remains as **Primary**, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose values to keep">
    On the side-by-side comparison, pick which values to retain for the primary contact, then click **Merge**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You'll see a success message. The retained data stays and duplicate records are removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Merge updates appear on the contacts' **History** page.
- The primary contact's portal assignment may change if merged fields no longer match the portal filters.
- Previously completed tasks from all merged contacts remain visible in the portal.
- The History module still shows all emails previously sent to merged contacts.
