---
title: "Merge duplicate contacts"
description: "Identify and merge up to three duplicate contacts to keep your records clean."
---

import { Note } from '@compat';

Maintaining clean, accurate contact data ensures seamless communication and speaker management across your events. If you’ve identified duplicate contacts in your CRM, you can easily merge them using the built-in merge tool.

<Note>
**❗️IMPORTANT INFORMATION TO KNOW❗️**:

- Up to 3 contacts can be merged at a time.
- Merging of contacts **cannot be undone.**
- Event-level fields, notes, and additional contacts are inherited from the primary contact. We recommend exporting your contact data **before** merging if you wish to keep a backup of historical data.
</Note>

Follow the steps below to merge duplicate contacts:

1.  Navigate to **Contacts** and select the pencil icon beside a record with duplicates
    ![Navigate to Contacts and select the pencil icon beside a record with duplicates](/images/kb/9e245a30-image-png-Mar-20-2026-05-43-43-5578-PM.png)

💡 **Have Speaker CRM?** You can also merge duplicates directly from the **Organization Dashboard CRM** — the process is identical.
2.  A new page will open, displaying the contact's profile with a mention of the number of duplicate contacts identified at the top of the page. Select "**Merge Duplicates**" to begin consolidation.

    *NOTE: Duplicate contacts are identified by a 70-80% match within the contact's email or name.*
    ![A new page will open](/images/kb/5efd5d38-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-15-04-3377-PM.png)
3.  Select the checkboxes next to the contacts you’d like to merge. Within the column "**Primary**," select the contact who will remain as the primary contact. Once complete, select the blue "Next" button in the bottom right-hand corner of your screen.

    ***NOTE**: You must select between 2-3 contacts to merge at a time.*
    ![Select the checkboxes next to the contacts you’d like to merge](/images/kb/997b3acf-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-16-56-1124-PM.png)
4.  A comparison screen will appear, displaying both contact records side by side. Review the list and choose which values you’d like to retain for the primary contact. When you’re ready, click **“Merge”** in the bottom right-hand corner of your screen to complete the process.

    ***NOTE**: Event-level fields, notes, and additional contacts are inherited from the primary contact. We recommend exporting your contact data **prior to** merging if you wish to keep a backup of historical data.*
    ![A comparison screen will appear, displaying both contact records side by side](/images/kb/4f761a9a-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-21-45-6230-PM.png)
5.  You’ll see a confirmation message once the merge is successful. The selected data will be retained, and the duplicate record will be removed from your database.

## Additional Information

- Merging updates will appear on the contacts' history page.
- The primary contact's event portal assignment may change if the filters associated with the assigned portal no longer match the merged fields.
- Previously completed tasks by all merged contacts will be visible in the event portal.
- The History module will continue to show all emails that were previously sent to merged contacts.
