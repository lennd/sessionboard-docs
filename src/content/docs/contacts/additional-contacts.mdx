A keynote speaker's calendar is run by an executive assistant; a sponsor's logo comes from their marketing coordinator. Additional contacts are those people, linked to the primary contact so that they are copied on the emails you send the contact, and can sign in to the contact's portal to see the sessions and finish the tasks on the contact's behalf. They are not contacts of their own — they do not appear in All Contacts, on sessions or in reports.

## Link an additional contact

1. Open the contact's profile (**Edit** at the left of their row in **All Contacts**).
2. Scroll to the **Additional Contacts** card near the bottom of the **Profile** tab and click **Add**.
3. Enter their **First Name**, **Email** and, optionally, **Last Name** and **Role** (for example *Executive Assistant*), then click **Save**.

![The Additional Contacts card on a profile — the linked assistant with her role, and the Add button](/images/kb/contact-additional-contacts.png)

Each contact can have any number of additional contacts, and one person can be the additional contact for several primary contacts.

### By import

An import can create up to three additional contacts per row. The template you generate from **Options > Import > Generate Template** carries three numbered sets of columns — **Addtl. Contact First Name**, **Addtl. Contact Last Name**, **Addtl. Contact Email** and **Addtl. Contact Role** — fill in as many sets as you need. See [Importing data](/settings/importing-data).

## The Additional Contacts view

**CRM > Additional Contacts** in the event sidebar lists every additional contact in the event, one row per link: the additional contact's name, role and email, then the primary contact they belong to, that contact's sessions and portal. Use it to check who is copied on what, or to export the list.

![The Additional Contacts view — additional contact name, role and email alongside the primary contact, their sessions and assigned portal](/images/kb/additional-contacts-module.png)

## Emailing additional contacts

Additional contacts are reached through their primary contact. Select the primary contacts (or their sessions) and click **Send > Send Email** as usual; the composer's **Include additional contacts** setting decides how they are treated:

| Option | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Include additional contacts of recipients (CC)** | The primary contact receives the email and each additional contact receives a copy. |
| **Send only to additional contacts of recipients** | The additional contacts receive the email; the primary contact does not. |
| **Do not include additional contacts** | The primary contact only. |

The same choice is available when recipients come from the Sessions module, and in step 3 of an [email campaign](/communications/email-campaigns). See [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails).

<Note>
Automated emails — task reminders, submission confirmations — go to the primary contact only. When an assistant needs to see something, send it from the module with the CC option.
</Note>

## Portal access

An additional contact signs in to the portal at the same link as the primary contact — **Actions > Copy Portal Link** on the contact's profile, or the portal's link from [the Portals module](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal). They sign in with their own email address; the first time, they are asked to set a password.

Once signed in they choose which contact's portal to open — one additional contact linked to several speakers sees all of them under a single login — and can view sessions, upload files and complete tasks exactly as the primary contact would. Completions are recorded against the primary contact's record.

<Tip>
If an additional contact reports that the portal is empty, check that they are linked to the right contact and that the contact has a portal assigned (the **Assigned Portal** column in All Contacts).
</Tip>
