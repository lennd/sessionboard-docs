Speakers usually arrive through the submission form. Moderators and chairpersons are different: the program team decides who chairs a panel or moderates a track, so an admin adds them. They are ordinary contacts — the same record type as a speaker — attached to a session in a different role, which means they get their own portal tasks, appear in the agenda under their role, and can be emailed as a group from **CRM > Moderators** or **CRM > Chairpersons**.

## 1. Make sure the person is a contact

If they have spoken or chaired for you before, they already exist — find them rather than creating a duplicate. In the event, open **CRM > All Contacts**, click **Add Contact** and search by name or email; tick anyone listed under *in organization* and click **Add to Event**. If the search is empty, click **Add new contact**, fill in **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** (the only required fields) and click **Create Contact**. Details are in [Add & delete a contact](/contacts/add-delete-a-contact).

<Tip>
Programs that use custom roles (see the note below) can skip this step: the **Add** search on those role blocks offers **Create new contact** when the person is not found, which opens the same New Contact form and assigns them to the role in one go.
</Tip>

## 2. Add them to the session

1. Open **Program > Sessions** and click the pencil on the session's row.
2. Open the **Participants** tab. There is a block for each role your program uses — **Speakers**, **Chairpersons**, **Moderators**, the **Submitter**, and **Sponsors** and **Exhibitors** where those are enabled.
3. Click **Add** on the **Chairpersons** or **Moderators** block and pick the contact from the search.
4. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the panel.

![The session editor's Participants tab with a block for each role and Add on each](/images/kb/session-editor-participants.png)

Each participant row has controls for their status on the session (**Pending**, **Accepted**, **Declined**), the eye icon to hide them from public surfaces (**Make private**), and **Remove**. The drag handle reorders people within a block, which sets the order the agenda and speaker pages show them in.

<Note>
The roles available here come from **Program > Program Settings > Roles**. If your program uses custom roles — a *Co-author* or *Presenting Author* — they appear as their own blocks alongside these, and the same steps apply. See [Program settings](/sessions/program-settings#roles).
</Note>
