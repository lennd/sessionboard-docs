Use a **square** speaker headshot at **300 × 300 px**.

## What submitters see

On a submission form, the line under Headshot is yours to write. Add **Help Text** in **Customize Question** and that is the only copy they see. Leave **Help Text** empty and they see a type-and-size hint from the field — “Images only (up to 5 GB)” when **Maximum file size** is blank, or “Images only (up to 2 MB)” if you set 2 MB.

Session **Files** settings do not change that hint. See [Session submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms).

## What the photo editor accepts

Turn on [speaker headshot limitations](/events/event-details) in **Settings → Record Settings** to require formats, an optional file-size cap (10 KB to 5 GB), and max width or height. Larger images are resized to fit; files over the size cap are refused.

<Note>
Sessionboard accepts files up to 5 GB. Some third-party apps apply their own photo limits, including Swoogo (1 MB) and Cvent (2 MB).
</Note>
