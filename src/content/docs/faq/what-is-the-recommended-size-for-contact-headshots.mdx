## What submitters see

On a submission form, the line under Headshot is yours to write. Add **Help Text** in **Customize Question** and that is the only copy they see. Leave **Help Text** empty and they see a type-and-size hint from the field — “Images only (up to 5 GB)” when **Maximum file size** is blank, or “Images only (up to 2 MB)” if you set 2 MB.

Session **Files** settings do not change that hint. See [Session submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms).

## What the photo editor accepts

Turn on [speaker headshot limitations](/events/event-details) in **Settings → Record Settings** to require formats, an optional file-size cap (10 KB to 5 GB), and max width or height. Larger images are resized to fit; files over the size cap are refused.

<Note>
Sessionboard accepts files up to 5 GB. Some third-party apps apply their own photo limits, including Swoogo (1 MB) and Cvent (2 MB).
</Note>

## Resize headshots in bulk

Headshots that are already on file can be brought down to a consistent size without leaving Sessionboard.

1. Open **CRM → All Contacts** (or **Speakers**) and tick the checkbox next to each contact whose headshot you want to resize.
2. In the bar that appears above the table, choose **More → Resize Headshots**.

   ![The More menu on the contacts bulk bar with Resize Headshots](/images/kb/headshots-resize-menu.png)

3. Under **Resize dimensions**, pick **Height x Width** to set both, or **Height** or **Width** alone to scale proportionally. Under **Resize filesize**, optionally enter a target size in KB. Click **Resize**.

   ![The Resize Headshots sheet with dimension and filesize options](/images/kb/headshots-resize-sheet.png)

Headshots smaller than the dimensions you enter are left as they are — only larger images are reduced. The job runs in the background and the new files replace the originals over the next few minutes. The same **More** menu has **Resize Logos** for sponsor and exhibitor logos.

## Automatic compression for new uploads

If you want every headshot submitted from now on to land under a certain size, email [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with your event name and the target size (for example 500 KB). Support turns on headshot compression for the event. From then on, each headshot uploaded through a form, a portal or the contact record is compressed to that target when it is saved; existing headshots are untouched unless you resize them in bulk as above.

To pull the finished files out of Sessionboard, see [Download headshots](/faq/how-to-download-headshots).
