Additional contacts — the assistants, agents and colleagues attached to a speaker's record — are contacts in their own right, so they have their own list.

In the sidebar, expand **CRM** and click **Additional Contacts**. It sits under **People** with **All Contacts**, **Speakers**, **Chairpersons**, **Moderators** and **Submitters**, and works like the other contact views: search, **Columns**, **Sort** and **Filter** all apply, and selecting rows opens the same bulk actions.

To attach an additional contact to a speaker, or to understand what they can see in the portal, read [additional contacts](/contacts/additional-contacts).
