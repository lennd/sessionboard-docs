When a speaker confirms by phone or emails you the answer instead of using the portal, you can close the task for them.

1. Open **CRM → All Contacts** (or **Speakers**) and click the pencil next to the person. Their **Contact Profile** slides in.
2. On the **Profile** tab, expand **Portal Tasks**. The badge shows how many tasks they hold; the bar shows the percentage done, and **All** / **Pending** / **Done** plus a search box narrow the list.
3. Each card shows the task name, its description, a required marker and an **Open** or completed badge. Click **Mark as complete** on the card. Click the same control again to reopen it.

![The Portal Tasks section of a contact profile with Mark as complete on each task card](/images/kb/contact-portal-tasks.png)

The status you set here is what the participant sees in their portal, and what the task columns in the contacts table and your reports read.

Only plain tasks have **Mark as complete**. A **file request** or a **form** is completed by its submission, so those cards have no button — upload the file or record the form on the participant's behalf instead. See [track task completion](/faq/how-to-track-task-completion-in-the-event-portal) for the table-level view.
