Once your session submission form is built, share its public link so people can start submitting. There is no separate publish step — the form accepts submissions as soon as the link is out, unless you set an open date.

## Copy the link

1. Go to **Program → Forms** in your event.
2. Open the **⋯** menu on the form and choose **Copy Link**. The public URL is copied to your clipboard.

![The ellipsis menu on a form — Edit, View Submissions, View Drafts, View Form, Copy Link, Duplicate, Delete](/images/kb/forms-actions-menu.png)

**View Form** in the same menu opens the form in a new tab so you can walk through it as a submitter before you send it. Inside the form builder, **View Form** and **Copy Link** stay in the header on every step.

Put the link wherever your audience is:

- in the call for papers email to your speaker list
- behind a button on your event website
- on your event's Sessionboard [Site](/site/site-builder), where **Settings → Programs / CFP** lists the form as a card on the signed-in home page — no link-pasting needed

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I embed the form on my website?

No. The submission form is hosted by Sessionboard and opens from its link. Link to it from a button on your site rather than embedding it.

### Can I set an open date?

Yes. In the form builder, open **Form Settings** and set **Open Date**. Anyone who follows the link earlier sees *Submissions are not yet open* with the date the form opens. Leave it empty and the form opens as soon as you share the link.

![The Form Settings step with deadlines, submission capacity, and the effective behavior summary](/images/kb/form-builder-form-settings.png)

### Can I set a close date?

Yes. **Close Date** stops new submissions after that date and time and refuses edits to existing ones; visitors see *Submissions closed*. Once a close date is set, **Send Reminder Email** emails people with an unfinished draft five days and one day before the deadline. **Advanced** gives certain answers a different deadline — an extra month for posters, for example. Details are in [Session submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms#form-settings).

### Once I create my form, how do I open it?

Share the link. If the form has no open date, or its open date has passed, it is already accepting submissions.
