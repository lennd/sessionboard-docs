Automated and admin-created emails sent from Sessionboard have a sender email address of "no-reply@sessionboard.com" (If a user requests a new password, the sender of the password reset email is "no-reply@sessionboard.com").

If a portal user is not receiving any emails, work through these checks in order.
1. Checking their spam/junk inbox to ensure the email has not been placed there.
2. Ensure "no-reply@sessionboard.com" is whitelisted so that emails are not blocked.

Lastly, as an event admin, we recommend ensuring the user's contact profile has the correct email address.
