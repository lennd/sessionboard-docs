The badge sits on the contact's headshot at the top of their profile and helps you tell first-timers from people you have worked with before.

## On the contact profile

| Badge | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | The contact record was created within the last 30 days (a rolling window from today). |
| **Returning** | Not new, and the contact is a speaker on an **accepted** session at an event in your organization whose start date has already passed. |
| No badge | Neither applies — for example a contact created 45 days ago who has never spoken at one of your events, or whose only sessions are still pending. |

**New** wins over **Returning**: a long-time speaker whose record was re-created last week shows **New** until the 30 days are up, then flips to **Returning**.

Only accepted, non-draft sessions count, and only the speaker role — being a submitter, moderator, chairperson, sponsor or exhibitor contact at a past event does not make someone **Returning**. Events that have been deleted are ignored.

## In the contacts table

The **New/Returning Speaker** column (add it from **Columns**) answers a slightly different question — has this person spoken for you before?

- Empty — no accepted sessions as a speaker anywhere in your organization.
- **New** — an accepted speaker in exactly one event, or in several events none of which has started yet.
- **Returning** — an accepted speaker in more than one event, at least one of which has already started.

Because the column ignores the record's created date, a speaker accepted for this year's event who spoke last year reads **Returning** in the table even while their profile badge still says **New**.

## Examples

- You imported a speaker yesterday → profile **New**; column empty until a session of theirs is accepted.
- A speaker from last year's event, accepted again this year → profile **Returning** (if their record is older than 30 days); column **Returning**.
- A contact created 45 days ago who has only ever been a submitter → no badge; column empty.

Edits to a record do not change its created date, so they do not affect the badge.
