Every portal decides for itself how tasks, forms and file requests are sequenced on its Tasks page. Open the portal, go to the **Assign Items** step and look for **Portal Tasks order** at the top of the assigned content; the **Ordering method** dropdown has three options.

![The Portal Tasks order card on the Assign Items step with the Ordering method dropdown set to Smart](/images/kb/portal-tasks-order.png)

## Smart (recommended)

The default for every portal. Items are sorted in this hierarchy:

1. **Required** before optional.
2. **Incomplete** (or not yet submitted) before completed.
3. By **type** — tasks, then file requests, then forms.
4. By **due date**, earliest first; items with no due date go last.
5. By **name**, alphabetically.

## Due date

One chronological list across every type — a form due Friday sits above a task due next week. Items without a due date are grouped at the end; ties on the same date fall back to name order. There is nothing to arrange; pick the option and the portal re-sorts from then on.

## Custom

You set a single order and tasks, forms and file requests can be interleaved freely — useful when a form has to come before the task that depends on it.

1. Choose **Custom** in **Ordering method**. The reorder panel opens underneath with two tabs.
2. **My Tasks** holds items assigned directly to the contact or group; **Submission Tasks** holds items scoped to a session, with each session group ordered on its own.
3. Drag items into place in each tab. **Reset** puts them back; the footer button becomes **Save and Next** while the order is unsaved — click it to save.

Things to know in Custom mode:

- Anything you assign to the portal later is appended to the end of the relevant list until you move it.
- Items marked **Dynamic** appear only for some participants — for example a task assigned by filter — so people may see a subset of the list.
- Tasks assigned to a contact directly, outside this portal, are not in the panel but still show in that person's portal.
- Dragging inside the individual **Tasks**, **Forms** and **File Requests** sections is disabled; this panel is the only place to reorder.

Leaving the step with unsaved order changes prompts you to **Save and continue** or discard them. See [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#assign-items) for the rest of the Assign Items step.
