Sessionboard's system status is published at **[status.sessionboard.com](https://status.sessionboard.com)**. It shows whether the application is up right now, any incident that is open, and the updates posted while it is being investigated and restored. Check it first when a page will not load or every save is failing — if there is an incident, it is already being worked on and the status page is where the restoration updates appear.

## Outage, or just you?

An incident on the status page affects everyone. When the status page is green, the problem is somewhere between your browser and Sessionboard, and these resolve most of them:

| Try | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Open [appv2.sessionboard.com](https://appv2.sessionboard.com) in a private or incognito window | Rules out a stale session, a cached page and browser extensions in one step. |
| Switch networks — phone hotspot instead of the venue or office Wi-Fi | Corporate and venue networks sometimes block or throttle the domains Sessionboard loads from. |
| Use a [supported browser](/faq/what-is-the-recommended-browser-for-using-sessionboard), updated | Older browsers fail on newer pages in ways that look like an outage. |
| Reload with the cache cleared (Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R) | A half-loaded page after a deploy comes back whole. |

If a portal user is the one who cannot get in, their symptom is usually a sign-in problem rather than downtime — see [6 common speaker portal issues](/faq/common-speaker-portal-issues-and-how-to-fix-them) and [Unlock a locked account](/faq/unlock-event-users).

<Tip>
On the status page, click **Subscribe to Updates** to be emailed when an incident opens and when it is resolved, so you do not have to keep checking during an event.
</Tip>

## If it is only you

[Contact support](/faq/who-can-i-contact-for-additional-assistance) and include the page link, a screenshot of the error text, the time it happened and your browser — that list is in the support article. The error text matters most: "Something went wrong" on one page is a different problem from a page that never loads at all.

<Note>
Scheduled maintenance is posted on the status page ahead of time, with its start and end, and subscribers are notified of it the same way as an incident.
</Note>
