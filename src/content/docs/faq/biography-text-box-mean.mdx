Rich text boxes — a session description or biography on a submission form, a portal form answer, the Biography field on a contact profile — have a thin status bar along the bottom. The letter at its left edge is the editor's **element path**: the kind of content the cursor is currently inside.

![The Biography editor on a contact profile with the status bar showing “p” and the 199/5000 character count](/images/kb/rich-text-statusbar.png)

## What it means

- **p** — the cursor is in an ordinary paragraph.
- **p » strong** — inside bold text within a paragraph.
- **ul » li** — inside a bulleted list item.

It changes as you move through the text. Nothing needs to be done with it.

## What else is on the bar

- When the field has a character limit, the right side shows the count, for example *199/5000*. The limit is the field's **Maximum Length** ([how to change it](/faq/add-a-field-character-capactiy)) or a form-level **Length Limit**.
- The handle in the corner lets you drag the box taller.

## Can it be removed?

No. The status bar is part of the editor and is shown wherever the field has a limit or can be resized. The “p” is not part of your text: it is not saved with the answer and does not appear on the agenda, the speaker page or anywhere attendees look.
