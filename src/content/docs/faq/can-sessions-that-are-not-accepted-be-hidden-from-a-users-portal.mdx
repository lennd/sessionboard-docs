A speaker who submitted two proposals, one accepted and one declined, has both on their contact profile and both in their portal. There is no switch that limits the portal to accepted sessions, but the portal already keeps the noise down.

## What the portal does with each status

| Status | In the portal |
| --- | --- |
| **Accepted** | Listed under My Submissions with an **Accepted** label, plus its tasks. |
| **Pending**, **Accept Queue**, **Decline Queue** and custom statuses in those categories | Listed with a **Pending** label. The custom status name shows only when that status has **Show custom status name to external users** on in [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#statuses). |
| **Declined** | Folded into a **Show declined (n)** link below the active list, collapsed by default. Tasks tied to a declined session are not shown. |
| **Withdrawn** | Same treatment under **Show withdrawn (n)**. |

The collapsed sections open by default only when the person has nothing active — so a speaker whose only proposal was declined still sees it.

## Keep tasks to accepted speakers

With **Always Show Tasks** off in the portal's Configuration step, tasks appear only to speakers with an accepted session. Anyone with only pending or declined submissions sees no task list. See [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#configuration).

## Does this affect the agenda or your website?

No. The agenda shows only the statuses selected under **Program Settings → Agenda → Program Statuses** (accepted by default), and the Site and embed **Speakers** and **Agenda** pages start from the rule *Status is Accepted*. Declined and pending sessions never reach attendees.

## Removing a session from someone's view entirely

The only ways to take a non-accepted session off a contact's profile and portal are to remove the person from the session, or to delete the session. Deleting does not erase the session permanently; [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) can restore one if you change your mind.

We recommend neither. Keeping the history — what someone proposed, and what happened to it — is what makes next year's speaker sourcing and the New/Returning badges work. A declined proposal left in place costs the speaker one collapsed line in their portal.
