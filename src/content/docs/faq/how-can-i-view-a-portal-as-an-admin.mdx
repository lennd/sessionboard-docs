## From any event page

1. Click **View Portal** at the top right of the screen. The menu offers **View my portal** and **View portal as…**.
2. Choose **View portal as…** and start typing a name. Matching **Contacts** are listed, and **Groups** (sponsors and exhibitors) below them.
3. Click a result. Their portal opens in a new tab.

![The View portal as… dialog with a contact search](/images/kb/view-portal-as.png)

**View portal as…** needs the impersonate-portal permission; team members without it see the entry disabled.

## From the portal editor

Open the portal under **Portals**, and click **Preview as…** in the editor toolbar to pick anyone assigned to that portal. This is the quickest check after changing assigned tasks or fields. See [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#see-it-as-a-speaker).

<Note>
Both routes open a read-only preview. You can open every task, form and file request, but nothing can be submitted or completed from it. To close a task for someone, use their contact profile — see [mark tasks complete for portal users](/faq/mark-tasks-complete-for-portal-users).
</Note>
