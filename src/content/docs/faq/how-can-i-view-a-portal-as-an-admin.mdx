As an admin user, to view what a portal will look like for a speaker, sponsor, or exhibitor, follow the steps below:

1. Create and assign tasks, forms, and file requests to your portal(s). Learn how in [Creating custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals).

2. Click 'View Portal' from the top-right of your admin screen.

   ![View a portal as an admin](/images/kb/33e0d5dd-image-png-Jan-28-2026-09-35-19-1064-PM.png)

3. Select 'View portal as...' and a pop-up window will appear. Here, enter the name or email of the contact (or group) you would like to preview a portal for.
- For each contact or group that matches your search, their assigned portal will be listed.

   ![For each contact or group that matches your search, their assigned portal will be listed](/images/kb/174eec4e-image-png-Jan-28-2026-09-36-23-1190-PM.png)

4. Select the contact you would like to preview their portal as. This will open the portal in a new window.

![Select the contact you would like to preview their portal as](/images/kb/7728c678-image-png-Feb-17-2026-09-46-26-1068-PM.png)

<Note>
This is a preview-only mode. Tasks and assignments can be viewed, but not completed.
</Note>
