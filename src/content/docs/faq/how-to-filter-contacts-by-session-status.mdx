The Contacts module lists everyone attached to your event. Two ways to narrow it to people in a session role, or to people whose sessions have a particular status.

## Use the role pages and their tabs

Under **CRM**, the **Speakers**, **Chairpersons** and **Moderators** pages already filter to contacts holding that role. Each page has tabs across the top that narrow the list further by the status of the sessions the person is on:

- **Session - Accepted**
- **Session - Pending**
- **Session - Declined**

The **Speakers** page also has **Primary Speakers**. Click a tab and the table updates; the count on each tab tells you how many contacts match.

![The Speakers page with the Session - Accepted tab highlighted](/images/kb/contacts-speakers-tabs.png)

## Build a filter on any contacts view

Use this on **All Contacts** when you want to combine a role or session status with other fields, or to save the result as a view.

1. Open the contacts view and click **Filter** in the toolbar.
2. Click **Add Filter**.
3. In the field picker, choose the session field you need. Session fields are prefixed **[Session]**:
   - **[Session] Status - Accepted**, **[Session] Status - Pending** or **[Session] Status - Declined**
   - **[Session] Speaker**, **[Session] Chairperson** or **[Session] Moderator** for the role itself
   - **[Session] Track** and **[Session] Language** for the session's details
4. Leave the condition on **Is checked** to include matching contacts, or switch it to **Is not checked** to exclude them.
5. Click **Apply Changes**.

![The Filter panel with a [Session] Status - Accepted filter set to Is checked](/images/kb/contacts-session-filter.png)

Up to five filters combine with AND, so **[Session] Moderator** is checked plus **[Session] Status - Accepted** is checked gives you the moderators of accepted sessions. To keep the result, use **Save as new view** in the bar above the table.

## Troubleshooting

- **You cannot find the [Session] fields.** The picker is long; scroll past the contact fields to the entries that start with **[Session]**, which sit after **Sessions** near the end of the list.
- **The filter does not change the list.** Confirm the condition reads **Is checked**, then click **Apply Changes** — filters are not applied until you do.
