A contact can only sign in to a portal once they have portal access. There are two ways to give it.

## Automatically, for everyone you add

In **Settings → Record Settings**, turn on **Automatically provision contact portal access**. From then on every contact you create, import or receive through a form gets portal access without another step. See [event details and record settings](/events/event-details).

## By hand, for the contacts you choose

1. Open **CRM → All Contacts** and tick the contacts who need access.
2. In the bar above the table choose **More → Manage Portal Access**.
3. The panel sorts your selection into **People without access** and **People with access**. Click **Give Portal Access** for the first group. Contacts with an invalid email address are listed separately and cannot be granted access until the address is fixed.
4. Once access is granted, **Share Login Details** lets you **Send Email** — with an optional personal message — or **Get Link** to copy the login link and send it yourself.

![Manage Portal Access, listing people with and without access](/images/kb/manage-portal-access.png)

Access is to the event's portals as a whole; which portal a contact lands in is decided by the portal's assignment rules. See [inviting users to the event portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal) and [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101).
