Yes. By default there is no cap on how many proposals one person can submit through a form.

## How a submitter adds another

When someone who has already submitted opens your form link again and signs in with the same email address, the form shows **Your submissions for this form** — every submission and saved draft they have on that form — with a **Start new submission** button. They do not need a separate link.

The confirmation page after a submission offers **Print submission** and **Continue to portal**; it has no built-in "submit another" button. If you want to point people back to the form from there, put the form link in the success page message (**Form Settings → Customize the success page message**).

## Limiting submissions

| Setting | Where | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Set Submission Limit** | Program → Forms → Edit → Form Settings | Caps submissions on that one form. Saved drafts count toward the limit — at the cap the submitter can finish a draft but sees "Submission limit reached" instead of **Start new submission**. |
| **Set submission limit** | Record Settings → General | Caps submissions per person across every form in the event. Used when no form-level limit is set. |

Both are described in [Submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms#form-settings) and [Event details](/events/event-details).

## Watch for duplicate contacts

The submitter is matched by the email they sign in with, so repeat submissions land on one contact. Co-speakers entered on more than one proposal can be duplicated if they are added under different email addresses. Run the [duplicate merge](/contacts/merge-duplicates) after the form closes and confirm every submission has its speakers assigned.
