Moderators and chairpersons can be emailed from two places. Which one you pick decides what the email can say.

| Start from | Best for | Session merge tags |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sessions** | "Here are the details of the panel you are moderating" | Yes — title, date, room, and the roster tags below |
| **Contacts** | "Here is your portal login" or anything not tied to one session | No |

## From the Sessions module

1. Open **Program → Sessions** and tick the sessions whose moderators or chairpersons you want to reach — filter first (by track, date, or status) and use **Select all** to take the whole result.
2. In the bar above the table click **Send Emails**.
3. Under **Who should receive this email?** choose the role: **Moderators**, **Chairpersons**, or any custom role your event has. **All Participants** covers every role at once, and **Select Individual Contacts** lets you hand-pick from the people on those sessions.

![The Who should receive this email? list on a Sessions send — Everyone, All Participants, Speakers, Chairpersons, Moderators, Primary Speakers, Session Submitters, Select Individual Contacts](/images/kb/sessions-send-to-roles.png)

4. Write the message — or click **Template** to load one whose type is *Sessions* — then **Review** and send. Each person gets one email per session they hold that role on, personalized with that session's details. Full walkthrough: [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails).

### Naming them in the message body

Emails sent from the Sessions module can also list a session's moderators or chairpersons inside the message itself, so one email can cover a panel without you typing the names. Open **Merge Tags** and look under **Session Roles**:

![The Session Roles group expanded in the merge tag picker](/images/kb/session-roles-merge-tag-group.png)

| Merge tag | Renders |
| --- | --- |
| `{{{moderators}}}` | `Ada Lovelace, Grace Hopper` |
| `{{{moderators_with_titles}}}` | `Ada Lovelace, Engineer, Analytical Ltd; Grace Hopper` |
| `{{{chairpersons}}}` / `{{{chairpersons_with_titles}}}` | the same, for chairpersons |
| `{{{participants}}}` / `{{{participants_with_titles}}}` | everyone on the session, whatever their role |

Anyone with no job title or company renders as just their name. Every role your event has — including roles you created yourself — gets its own pair of tags named after the role, so a role called **Panel Chairs** gives you `{{{panel_chairs}}}` and `{{{panel_chairs_with_titles}}}`.

### A moderator on several sessions

Someone who moderates more than one session is on more than one panel's email, and that is intended. Tick **Send one email per session** and each session sends a single message with everyone on it addressed together, so a moderator across three panels is on all three, each carrying its own session's details. In an [Email Campaign](/communications/email-campaigns), **Bundle session participants onto one email** does the same with the primary speaker on **To** and the rest on **CC**.

If you would rather send one message covering everything, email them from Contacts instead and insert the **Sessions Summary** merge tag (below).

## From the Contacts module

1. Open **CRM → Contacts** and filter to the people you want — see [Filter contacts by session status](/faq/how-to-filter-contacts-by-session-status).
2. Tick the rows and click **Send → Send Emails**.
3. Compose and send.

<Warning>
Emails sent from Contacts are about the person, not one session, so the single-session merge tags — title, date and time, location, and the roster tags above — are not offered and will not fill in.
</Warning>

What Contacts sends *do* offer is **Sessions Summary** (`{{{sessions_summary}}}`): a table of every session the contact is on as a speaker, moderator, chairperson, or other participant, with each session's ID, status, title, start and end times, and speakers. For a moderator on three panels, that is one email listing all three.

## Point them at the portal

The most reliable place for a moderator to see everything they are on is their portal: **My Submissions** and **Confirmed Participation** list every session with its time, room, and co-participants, and the **Participants** tab on each session shows the rest of the panel. See [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#participation-sections).

To send them there, insert the **Portal Login Link (Hyperlink)** merge tag (or **Portal Login Link (Raw URL)**) rather than pasting a URL — it signs each person in to their own portal. See [Inviting users to the portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal).
