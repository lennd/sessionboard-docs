After the call for papers closes, an accepted speaker often needs to bring in a co-presenter. Three ways to allow it, from most to least self-service.

## Option 1: Let them add participants in the portal

The portal has a built-in control for this.

1. Open **Program → Portals** and edit the portal your speakers use.
2. On the **Configuration** step, under **Settings**, turn on **Manage Related Submissions and Participants**. Speakers can now edit their submissions and the people on them.
3. Turn on **Add Participants to Submissions**. Use **Selectable Participant Roles** to limit which roles they may assign — for example Speaker only, so nobody makes themselves a chairperson.
4. **Save**.

In the portal, the speaker opens their session, goes to its **Participants** tab and clicks **Add Participant**. They enter a first name, last name and email, pick a role, and the new person is created as a contact, attached to the session and given portal login access on the spot. Nothing waits on your team.

**Add Participants to Submissions** needs the Sessions 2.0 feature on the event, which supplies the participant roles the option assigns; the switch is disabled until it is on. Portals that show sessions read-only cannot add participants.

## Option 2: Re-open the submission form

Use this when you also want speakers to revise the rest of their proposal.

1. Open the submission form from **Program → Forms** (under **Collect & Review**) and go to **Form Settings**.
2. Move the **Close Date** into the future. Submissions become editable again until the new date.
3. Make sure **View Submission Form** is on in the portal's **Configuration**, so each speaker has a link from their session back into the form.
4. Add a portal task telling speakers what to do — "Add co-presenters by editing your original submission" — and link the [view and edit your submission](/participants/edit-submission) article for them.

The form's participants step then lets them add speakers the same way they did when they first submitted.

## Option 3: Collect requests with a portal form

Use this if every new speaker should be reviewed before they touch the program.

1. In the **Portals** section of the Program sidebar, open **Forms** (the portal-forms library, not the submission forms above it) and create a **Session** portal form. Add the fields you need: first name, last name, email, and anything else your team wants such as company, bio or headshot. See [create and assign portal forms](/portals/create-assign-forms).
2. Assign the form to the portal on its **Assign Items** step. If only some sessions may add speakers, set **Assign By Filter** on the item.
3. Watch **Submissions** on the form. When a request arrives, create the contact and add them to the session yourself.

<Tip>
Include the session **Title** field on the form so the speaker sees which session the request is for.
</Tip>

Portal users get a simple request form; your team keeps control of who is added.
