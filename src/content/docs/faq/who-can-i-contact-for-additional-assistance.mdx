Support is available weekdays, 7 am – 6 pm ET. You will get a response within 1 business day.

## Why use it

Support answers setup, permission and integration questions, and digs into anything that is not behaving the way you expect. A message that names the event, the page and what you expected to happen lets the first reply solve the problem instead of asking for details.

## Chat

The fastest way to reach us. On any admin page of an organization or event, click the **Help** question-mark icon in the top bar, then **Contact support**. This opens **Ask**, the assistant panel, with the message "I need to contact support." Tell Ask what you need. It answers what it can, and when you ask for a person — or it hits a bug, an account or billing question, or a data fix only our team can make — it hands you off to a live chat with the support team. Ask passes along a summary of your conversation, so you do not have to repeat yourself.

The same Help menu has **Tour the sidebar**, this **Knowledge Base**, **Release Notes**, the **Product Roadmap** and **Submit Feedback**.

<Note>
Looking for the support chat bot that used to open from the Help menu? It has been replaced by Ask. Click **Contact support** and Ask picks up from there, including handing you to a person.
</Note>

## Sign in to the Help Center

When you are signed in to Sessionboard, the Help Center signs you in too: open it from the **Help** menu in the admin app, the speaker portal or Ready Room. Your name appears at the top right. Its menu shows your roles and lets you pick the organization or event the articles are tailored to. Speakers, reviewers and Ready Room operators also get **For you** at the top of the sidebar, with the guides for their role. On a page that still shows **Sign in**, click it to sign in through Sessionboard and come back to the same article. Signing out of Sessionboard signs you out of the Help Center.

## Email

Email [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with any question about your event.

## What to include

Share these in your first message, whether by chat or email, and we can usually help in the first reply.

| Tell us | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Event name | Acme Tech Summit 2026 |
| Link to the page | https://appv2.sessionboard.com/event/123/sessions |
| Screenshot or short video | Image attached or a screen-recording link |
| What is happening, and any error text | "Something went wrong. Please try again." |
| Steps you took | 1. Select contact 2. Send Email |
| What you expected | The email goes out |
| What actually happened | No email went out |
| When it happened | Today, 9:15 am ET |
| Browser and device | Chrome on Mac |

### If it is an integration issue

For a connected app — a registration or event platform, a marketing tool, Zapier or a webhook — add these on top of the list above:

| Tell us | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Which integration | Cvent |
| When it last worked | April 1st, 4:00 PM ET |
| Affected records | speaker@email.com, Session ID 789 |
| Error messages | Screenshot of the sync error in History |

The more boxes you fill, the faster you get back to your event.
