Support is available weekdays, 7 am – 6 pm ET. You will get a response within 24 hours.

## Chat

The fastest way to reach us. On any Sessionboard page, click the blue question mark in the top right of your screen, between **View Portal** and your profile.

![The blue question mark that opens the in-app support messenger](/images/kb/1e0644d2-image-png-Jan-28-2026-07-48-16-9909-PM.png)

## Email

Email [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with any question about your event.

Include these details so the team can help on the first reply:

- Event name
- What you were trying to do, and what happened instead
- A screenshot or screen recording
