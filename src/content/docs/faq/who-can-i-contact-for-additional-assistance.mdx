Hours of Operation: 7 am - 6 pm ET weekdays

Receive a response within 24 hours.

---

#### There are two ways in which you can connect with a support representative from our team:
1.  Chat
2.  Email

### Chat

The easiest way to connect with a support representative is to use our in-app messenger. When accessing a Sessionboard page, click on the blue question mark in the top right-hand corner of your screen between 'View Portal' and your profile.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Jan-28-2026-07-48-16-9909-PM.png)

### Don't feel like chatting? Email us.

Feel free to email our support team at support@sessionboard.com with any questions you may have regarding your event.

#### When contacting support, we recommend including the following information within your communication so that our team can best assist:
-  Event name
-  Description of issue/question
-  Pictures or videos to support the description
