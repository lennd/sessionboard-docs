After **five** wrong passwords in a row, a Sessionboard login is locked. The next sign-in attempt says *The account for … has been locked after too many failed login attempts. Use "Forgot password?" to reset your password and unlock your account.* Resetting the password is what unlocks it — either route below ends the same way.

## As the locked-out person

1. Go to [app.sessionboard.com](https://app.sessionboard.com) and click **Forgot password?** to the right of the Password label. If your organization uses a non-US **Data host region**, pick it first.

   ![The sign-in form with the Forgot password? link beside the Password label](/images/kb/login-forgot-link.png)

2. Enter your email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the email — subject **[Sessionboard] Reset your password** — and choose a new password. The account is unlocked as soon as the reset completes.

Nothing arrives? Check spam, and check the address: the confirmation on screen appears whether or not the email matches an account. If the email is right, you may be signing in to the wrong data host region. Details: [Reset your password](/event-team/reset-password-user-instructions).

## As an admin

1. In the event, open **Event Team** and search for the person by name or email. Speakers, sponsors, and other portal users are listed here too, with a *Portal User* role.
2. Open the row's **⋯** menu. A locked account shows an extra item, **Unlock account** — it only appears when the account is actually locked.
3. Click it. Sessionboard emails the person a password-reset link (the same **[Sessionboard] Reset your password** email) and confirms *The account was unlocked*.

The person sets a new password from that email and signs in. See [Invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members#manage-existing-users) for the rest of the row menu.

<Note>
Portal users who cannot sign in for other reasons — wrong address, no portal access — are covered in [Common speaker portal issues](/faq/common-speaker-portal-issues-and-how-to-fix-them).
</Note>
