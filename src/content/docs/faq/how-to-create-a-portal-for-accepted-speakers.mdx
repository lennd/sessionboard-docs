Two ways to give accepted speakers their own experience. Pick by whether you want accepted speakers in a *separate portal*, or the *same portal* with tasks that only appear once they are accepted.

## Option 1: A portal filtered on session status

The portal's filters can read the status of a contact's sessions directly, so the portal keeps itself up to date as you accept and decline.

1. Open **Program → Portals** and click **Create Portal**. Give it a name, choose the **Contacts** portal type, and continue to **Select Participants**.
2. Click **Add filter** and build two filters:
   - **Speaker** — **Is checked**
   - **[Session] Session Status** — **Is** — **Accepted**
3. Click **Done**, check the table below the filters shows the people you expect, then **Save Filters**.

![The Edit filters dialog with Speaker is checked and Session Status is Accepted](/images/kb/portal-filter-session-status.png)

A contact matches when any session they speak on has the status **Accepted** (the list also offers **Confirmed**, **Pending**, **Declined** and the queue statuses, so the same pattern builds a pending-speakers portal). The moment you accept a session, its speakers move into this portal; nothing has to be flagged by hand. Because everyone belongs to exactly one portal, drag this portal above the general speaker portal on the Portals page so it wins the match — see [order portals by priority](/portals/creating-custom-portals#order-portals-by-priority).

## Option 2: One speaker portal, tasks gated by acceptance

Keep a single portal for everyone with the **Speaker** role, and let the portal hide the work until a session is accepted.

1. Create (or open) the Contacts portal filtered on **Speaker** — **Is checked**. It lists every speaker regardless of session status.
2. On the **Configuration** step, turn **Always Show Tasks** off.

With that switch off, tasks, forms and file requests appear only to speakers whose session is accepted. A speaker whose session is still pending signs in, sees their submission, and gets no homework until the decision is made. Moderators, chairpersons and anyone without an accepted session see an empty task list. See [Configuration in Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#configuration).

## Which one?

| You want | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Different welcome text, resources or branding for accepted speakers | Option 1 |
| Pending speakers to see their submission but not the tasks | Option 2 |
| The simplest setup | Option 2 — one portal, one switch |

If you were flagging accepted speakers with a custom checkbox field to drive a portal filter, replace it with the **[Session] Session Status** filter above; the checkbox had to be maintained by hand every time a session was accepted.
