Open a contact from **CRM → All Contacts** (or a group from **All Exhibitors** / **All Sponsors**) and its profile slides in on the right. Two kinds of field live there.

## Fields that save on their own

Job title, company, phone, address, social links and every custom field save as soon as you leave them: click the value, type, then click anywhere else or press Tab. A **Saved successfully** toast confirms the write. There is nothing else to press.

## Fields that wait for Save Changes

The contact's **name**, **email address**, **star rating** and **Biography** are held until you confirm them. Edit one and a **Cancel** / **Save Changes** bar appears at the bottom of the profile. Nothing is written until you click **Save Changes**; **Cancel**, or closing the profile, discards the edit.

![The Cancel and Save Changes bar at the bottom of a contact profile](/images/kb/contact-save-changes.png)

Saving a new email address shows a reminder that the **Portal Username** is a separate field and does not follow it — see [changing a contact's email address](/contacts/changing-a-contact-s-email-address).
