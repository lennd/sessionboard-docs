---
title: "How do I save a contact field (i.e. biography)?"
description: "After updating a field in a contact's or group's profile, click outside the field and then click Save to store your changes."
---

Once you have updated a field within either a contact's or group's profile, to save the data, you will need to click outside of the field & click Save Changes:
