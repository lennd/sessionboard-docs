The eye icon on a speaker's row controls whether that speaker is shown publicly for that session. Use it to hold back a headline speaker until the announcement, or to keep a stand-in off the website while the line-up settles.

![The Speakers card on a session's Participants tab, hovering the eye icon shows the Make private tooltip](/images/kb/speaker-eye-toggle.png)

## Public or private

| State | Icon | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** (default) | Eye | The speaker appears in [embeds](/sessions/embeds), on your Sessionboard [Site](/site/site-builder) program and speaker pages, and in the generated speaker booklet. The [Open API](/integrations/api-tokens) returns the speaker with `is_public: true`. |
| **Private** | Eye crossed out | The speaker is left out of embeds, Site pages and the booklet. The Open API still returns them on the session, with `is_public: false`, so your own integration can decide. |

The switch is per speaker **per session** — someone can be public on one session and private on another. It changes public visibility only: the speaker still sees the session in their portal, still receives its tasks and emails, and still appears everywhere inside Sessionboard.

## Change a speaker's visibility

1. Open the session (click **Edit** at the left of its row in **Program → Sessions**) and go to the **Participants** tab.
2. On the **Speakers** card, hover the speaker's row and click the eye. The tooltip reads **Make private** or **Make public** depending on the current state.
3. Save the session.

The switch is only on speakers. Moderators, chairpersons and custom roles do not have it — remove someone from the role if they should not be shown.

In the sessions table, a private speaker's name pill carries the crossed-out eye so you can spot hidden speakers without opening each session.
