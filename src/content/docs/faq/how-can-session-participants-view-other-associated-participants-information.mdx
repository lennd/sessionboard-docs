A moderator often needs the speakers' emails before the session; a chairperson wants to read the panel's bios. The portal already shows who is on a session. How much more a participant can see is a per-portal choice.

## What every portal user sees

Open a session from the portal and the **Participants** tab lists everyone attached to it, grouped by role — speakers, moderators, chairpersons, sponsors and exhibitors — with their name and headshot. The tab appears whenever a session has participants; no setting is needed.

![A session in the portal with the Participants tab open, listing speakers and moderators by role](/images/kb/portal-session-participants.png)

Names on this list are not clickable until you turn on the setting below.

## Let them open a participant's details

1. Open **Program → Portals**, click the portal's ellipsis (⋮) and choose **Edit Settings**.
2. In the **Configuration** step, turn on **Manage Related Submissions and Participants**. Participants on the list become clickable and open a details view with **Details** and **Tasks** sub-tabs.
3. Go to **Manage Fields** and open the **Contact Participants** tab (**Group Participants** for sponsor and exhibitor records). **Show/Hide Fields** chooses which fields appear on a co-presenter's record — add **Email**, **Phone**, **Biography** or whatever the role needs, and leave out anything private.
4. Switch **Locked** on for each field you expose. Locked fields are view-only; unlocked fields can be edited by any portal user who opens the record. We recommend locking all of them so people can read, not change, each other's information.
5. **Save**, then check the result with [View portal as…](/faq/how-can-i-view-a-portal-as-an-admin) as one of the participants.

![The Manage Fields step with the field tabs and the Locked column](/images/kb/portal-editor-manage-fields.png)

## What they see once it is on

- The participant's record shows the fields you exposed, in the order you set them.
- If **Email** is one of them, a mail icon appears under the name; clicking it opens a new message to that address.
- The **Tasks** sub-tab lists the participant's tasks read-only. A portal user cannot complete another participant's tasks.

The same setting also lets portal users edit the session's own fields, subject to the locks in the **Submission Fields** tab, and — with **Add Participants to Submissions** on — add new people to the session. See [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#configuration) for the full list of Configuration settings.
