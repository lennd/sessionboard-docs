## Admin event access

Admins have full access to the event, including the ability to:

- Create and view submission forms
- Create portal tasks, files and wiki pages
- Send email communications to contacts and speakers
- Create evaluation plans
- Manage the event agenda
- Create custom reports

## Evaluator event access

An evaluator is a user on your event with one job: complete the evaluation plans assigned to them. They log in to their queue and see the submissions in their rounds, with whatever fields the round's reviewer view exposes. They do not see the sessions module, contacts, emails, settings or reports. A closed plan is view-only for them.

Invite your committee as evaluators rather than admins. If someone needs both — say a track chair who also reviews — give them an admin or custom role as a team member and add them to the plan as an evaluator too. See [invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members) for roles.

## Emailing your evaluators

The evaluation plan sends its own emails: an invitation when you add someone (**Send invite email to new evaluators**), a notice when you open the plan (**Notify evaluators**) and a Monday reminder while **Enable Weekly Reminders** is on. Wording is in [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails).

For anything else — a briefing, a deadline change, a thank-you — use the regular email tools, which send to **contacts**, not to users. An evaluator who was only ever invited as a user is not yet a contact, so:

1. Add your evaluators (and any event team members you want to reach the same way) to **CRM → All Contacts**. [Importing](/settings/importing-data) a spreadsheet is the quickest way for a whole committee.
2. Select them in the contacts table and use **Send**, or build a campaign in Communications. See [create and send emails](/communications/create-send-emails).

Adding someone as a contact does not change their permissions; their access is still set by their user role and the plans they are on.
