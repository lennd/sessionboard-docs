---
title: "I created a new contact. Why do I not see them within the Speakers module?"
description: "Speakers are contacts who are assigned to at least one session as a speaker. By creating a speaker, you have completed step one which is creating them"
---

Speakers are contacts who are assigned to at least one session as a speaker. By creating a speaker, you have completed step one which is creating them as a contact within your event.

For them to appear within the 'Speakers' module, you will need to assign them as a speaker to a session.
