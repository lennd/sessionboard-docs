---
title: "Why a new contact isn't in the Speakers view"
sidebar:
  label: "New contact missing from Speakers"
description: "The Speakers view lists contacts who hold the speaker role on at least one session. A contact you have only just created is not a speaker until you add them to a session."
reviewed: "2026-09-29"
---

**Speakers** under **CRM** is not a separate list you add people to. It is **All Contacts** filtered to people who are attached to at least one session as a speaker — the same way **Chairpersons** and **Moderators** are filtered to those roles.

Creating a contact is step one: they now exist in **All Contacts**. To make them a speaker, open a session and add them as a participant with the **Speaker** role. The moment they are on a session they appear in **Speakers**, and the **Session - Accepted**, **Pending** and **Declined** tabs at the top of that view sort them by the status of the sessions they are on.

A contact who is only a submitter, an additional contact or a portal user with no session stays in **All Contacts** and their own view. See [create a session](/sessions/create-a-session) for adding participants, and [filter contacts by role or session status](/faq/how-to-filter-contacts-by-session-status) for the tabs.
