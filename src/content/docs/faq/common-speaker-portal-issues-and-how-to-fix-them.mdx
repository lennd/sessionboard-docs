## 1. "You do not have access to the portal for this event"

**Symptoms.** A speaker follows the portal link, enters their email, and sees *You do not have access to the portal for this event. If you believe this is an error, please contact the event organizer.* Other speakers on the same portal get in fine.

**Why.** Portal login is checked against the contact's **Portal Username**, not the **Email** field on their profile. They are separate: Email is where you write to them; Portal Username is the login. If the person types an address that is not their Portal Username — or they were never given portal access, so the username is blank — they are turned away. The message also appears when the contact exists in your CRM but has not been added to this event.

**Fix.**

1. Open the contact's profile (**Edit** at the left of their row in **CRM → Contacts**) and scroll to the **Details** card at the bottom of the **Profile** tab. **Portal Username** is the address they must sign in with; a dash means they have no portal access yet.

   ![The Details card with Portal options open — Manage Portal Access, Change Portal Username, Reset Password](/images/kb/contact-portal-options.png)

2. No username? Click **Portal options → Manage Portal Access** and give access. Wrong username? **Portal options → Change Portal Username**. See [Change a contact's email address](/contacts/changing-a-contact-s-email-address).
3. Confirm the contact is on this event: they appear in the event's **Contacts** table, not only in the organization CRM.
4. Send them the login link again — **Copy Portal Link** from the profile's **Actions** menu, or the **Portal Login Link** merge tag in an email — and ask them to sign in with the Portal Username.

## 2. A task is assigned but the person cannot see it

**Symptoms.** You assigned a task; it shows under **Portal Tasks** on their profile; they sign in and the tasks panel says *No tasks found.*

**Why.** Tasks belong to a portal, and a person only sees the tasks of the portal they are assigned to. Portal assignment is decided by each portal's **Portal Filters**, and the first portal in the list whose filters match wins. If the task lives on a different portal, or the person's role has **Portal Access** switched off, nothing shows.

**Fix.**

1. Check which portal they are on: in **Contacts**, click **Columns** and turn on **Assigned Portal**.
2. Open that portal's card menu → **Edit Tasks** and confirm the task is assigned there. If the task is on a different portal, assign it on this one too.

   ![A portal card's menu with Edit Criteria, Edit Tasks, Edit Settings, Edit Appearance, Manage Fields, Duplicate and Delete](/images/kb/portals-card-menu.png)

3. If they are on the wrong portal, open the intended portal's **Edit Criteria** and check the filters against the contact's values — role, session status, group, custom fields. The table under the filters previews who matches.
4. Check the role: **Program settings → Roles** has a **Portal Access** switch per role. Off, and people in that role sign in to *The portal is not enabled for your role*.
5. Look at it as they do: **View Portal → View portal as…** and pick the contact.

The full checklist is in [Why can't a portal user see any tasks assigned to them?](/faq/why-can-t-a-portal-user-see-any-tasks-assigned-to-them)

## 3. A form task will not submit

**Symptoms.** The speaker opens a form task and gets an error, or the submit button does nothing — even after you extended the due date.

**Why.** Due dates and task assignment are set per portal, so extending a date on one portal leaves the same task overdue on another. Required questions the person has not answered also block the submit, and a form left open in a browser tab does not pick up a new due date until it is reloaded.

**Fix.**

1. Open the portal's **Edit Tasks** and confirm the due date saved — on every portal that carries the task, not just one.
2. Ask the person to reload the page (or sign out and back in) so the new due date applies.
3. Check for required questions they have not filled in; the form highlights them.
4. Reproduce with **View portal as…** the affected person.
5. Still stuck? Contact support with the event name, portal name, and the person's email.

## 4. "View portal as" shows an error or a blank portal

**Symptoms.** You choose **View portal as…** for a contact and get *error retrieving portal data*, or the portal loads empty.

**Why.** The contact has no portal login yet (blank Portal Username), or a browser extension is interfering with the page.

**Fix.**

1. Check the contact's **Portal Username** in the **Details** card. If it is a dash, give access with **Portal options → Manage Portal Access**, then try again.
2. Try another browser, or an incognito window without extensions.
3. If the error mentions portal tasks or portal data and the username is set, contact support — it is usually a display issue on the admin side, and the speaker's own portal still loads.

## 5. The portal shows 0 participants

**Symptoms.** Speakers are on sessions, but the portal card shows no participants.

**Why.** Portal participants come from the portal's **Portal Filters**, not from session assignments directly. If the filters do not match your contacts — or the contacts are in the CRM but not on this event — nobody is assigned.

![The Select Participants step with the portal filters and the matching records](/images/kb/portal-editor-criteria.png)

**Fix.**

1. Open the portal → **Edit Criteria** and read the filters. Several filters must **all** match.
2. Compare with the **Contacts** table: filter it the same way and see who comes back.
3. Confirm the contacts are on this event — **Contacts → Add Contact**, search, and **Add to Event** brings an organization contact into the event ([adding existing contacts](/contacts/adding-existing-contacts-to-a-new-event)).
4. For role filters (Speaker, Moderator, Chairperson), confirm the role is set on their session record, not just implied.
5. If the portal is below another whose filters also match those people, the higher portal takes them — reorder on the Portals page ([order portals by priority](/portals/creating-custom-portals#order-portals-by-priority)).

## 6. Portal invitations never arrive

**Symptoms.** You send the portal login email; some people say they never got it.

**Fix.**

1. Check the **Email** field on the contact for typos, and that it is the address they expect mail at. If they also cannot sign in, check **Portal Username** too (section 1).
2. Confirm it was sent: **History → Emails → Sent Emails**, search by name or address, and read the **Status**. Bounced or blocked rows also appear under **Errors**.

   ![The History page on the Emails tab, with Email Campaigns, Sent Emails and Errors sub-tabs](/images/kb/history-emails.png)

3. Ask them to check spam and quarantine for mail from `no-reply@notify.sessionboard.com` (or your [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain) if you use one).
4. Corporate mail filters (Outlook protection, Cisco IronPort, Mimecast and the like) are the usual culprit; their IT team can allowlist `sessionboard.com`.
5. Resend, or send a **Reset Password** from **Portal options** if they have a login but no working email from us.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="A speaker signs in and sees someone else's portal, or the wrong one">

They chose a different account on the way in. Someone who is an [additional contact](/contacts/additional-contacts) for another person, or a contact of a sponsor or exhibitor, can open more than one portal. After signing in they see **Select Account** with **Continue as:**, listing the other people or companies they act for and their own entry, marked **Portal User**.

To change it, they open the menu in the portal header and choose **Switch Portals**, then pick the right entry. A sponsor's contact picks the company name to reach the sponsor portal.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A speaker submitted, but their portal is empty or missing some submissions">

The missing submissions almost always belong to a second contact record under another email address, for example a work and a personal address. The portal shows only the sessions of the contact they signed in as.

1. In **Contacts**, search for the person's name and look for a second record.
2. [Merge the duplicates](/contacts/merge-duplicates), keeping the email they sign in with on the primary record.
3. If there is only one record but they sign in with another address, use **Portal options → Change Portal Username** (section 1).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A speaker's draft submission has disappeared">

Drafts live on the submission form, not in the portal. Ask them to open the same submission form link and sign in with the same email address they used when they started. Their drafts are listed under **Your submissions for this form**, marked **Draft — not submitted**, with **Resume draft**. If it shows *No saved drafts yet*, the draft was saved under a different address or deleted with **Delete draft**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we upload a headshot or complete a profile for a speaker?">

Yes. Open the contact's profile in the admin and upload the headshot there; the portal shows it straight away. If the speaker should be able to do it themselves but cannot:

- In the portal's **Manage Fields** step, check the field is shown and its **Locked** switch is off.
- If it is part of a task they already completed, **Make Completed Tasks View-Only** on that task stops further edits. Edit it for them, or turn the setting off.
- If they act for someone else, they may be in the wrong portal: see the first question above.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
