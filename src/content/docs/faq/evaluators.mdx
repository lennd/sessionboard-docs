At this time, emails can only be sent to users listed within your event’s Contacts module.

![](/images/kb/a4ca70ff-image-png-Feb-02-2026-07-34-14-9887-PM.png)

### Recommendation

To send emails to your team or evaluators, we recommend the following:
1.  Add your event team and/or evaluators to the Contacts module in your event.
  1. Consider importing your contacts to save time!
2.  Once added, you can send them a custom or targeted email using the standard email tools available within Sessionboard.

👉🏽Learn how to create & send emails.

> 💡 Note: Adding team members or evaluators as contacts does not affect their access permissions unless otherwise configured.
