**Stock photo search exists in one place today: the Advocacy Designer.** When you pick a background for a share card, the photo choices include stock photos you can search, next to uploading your own or choosing from your event library. See [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy).

{/* label-ok: Designer */}

## Banners and images on your Site

The [Site builder](/site/site-builder) has no stock photo search. For a hero banner or an **Image** block, use your own file. A hero **Background** can be a brand **Gradient** or a **Countdown** instead of a photo, which needs no image at all.

## Where to get a photo

Use a photo library you have a licence for, download the file, and upload it in the Site builder. Resize large photos first. Add alt text to every image block so screen readers can describe it. See [Describe images](/site/site-builder#describe-images).
