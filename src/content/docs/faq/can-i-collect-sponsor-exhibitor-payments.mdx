Sessionboard takes payments in two places: a **submission fee** on a [submission form](/sessions/submission-forms#payments--fees) and an **entry fee** on an [awards form](/awards/awards-pricing-payments-invoices), both through the payment gateway connected under the organization's **Settings → Payment Settings**. Those invoices appear under **Program → Invoices** and the awards **Invoices** page.

Sponsor and exhibitor packages are not billed through Sessionboard. Booth fees, sponsorship tiers and add-ons are usually contracted and invoiced from your finance system or registration platform, so the group portal is where you direct people to pay and record that they did.

## Point them to your payment page

1. Open **Program → Portals → Tasks** and add a task — "Pay your sponsorship invoice", for example.
2. Set **Task Link** to your payment page. **Enter Task URL** sends everyone to one page; **Use Field** sends each group to a personal link stored on their record, such as an invoice URL you imported.
3. Assign the task to the sponsor or exhibitor portal with a due date and mark it **Required** so it sits at the top of their list.

![The Add Task panel: Task Name, Type, Description and Task Link](/images/kb/portal-task-add.png)

The sponsor marks the task complete once they have paid, and the task column on **CRM → All Sponsors** shows you who has and who has not. See [Assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks).

## Record the payment

- Add a **File Request** to the portal for a remittance or proof of payment, which lands on the group's record for your finance team.
- Track amounts on the group record with **Currency** fields — *Package fee*, *Amount paid* — and a [Formula field](/settings/formula-fields) for the balance, then filter **All Sponsors** on the balance to chase the stragglers.
- The **Tiers** page under Sponsors keeps the package definitions themselves.

If you need a native sponsor invoice in Sessionboard, tell your account team; requests like this shape the roadmap.
