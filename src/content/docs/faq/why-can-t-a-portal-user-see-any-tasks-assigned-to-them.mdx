## 1. Is Portal Access on for their role?

Portal access is controlled per role. Open **Program settings → Roles** and look at the **Portal Access** switch for the role the person holds (Speaker, Moderator, a custom role). Off, and they can sign in but see *The portal is not enabled for your role* instead of their content — and nothing else on this list will help until it is on. See [Roles](/sessions/program-settings#roles).

## 2. Are they signing in with their Portal Username?

A contact has two addresses. **Email**, near the top of the profile, is where you write to them. **Portal Username**, in the **Details** card at the bottom of the **Profile** tab, is what they sign in with. Changing one never changes the other.

1. In **CRM → Contacts**, click **Edit** at the left of their row to open the profile.
2. Scroll to **Details**. **Portal Username** is the address they must use. A dash means they have never been given portal access.

![The Details card with Portal options open — Manage Portal Access, Change Portal Username, Reset Password](/images/kb/contact-portal-options.png)

- No username → **Portal options → Manage Portal Access**, then **Give Portal Access** and **Share Login Details** to send them the login link.
- Wrong username → **Portal options → Change Portal Username** ([how](/contacts/changing-a-contact-s-email-address#change-the-portal-username)).
- Right username, can't get in → **Portal options → Reset Password** emails them a reset link.

If they signed in with a different address, they may have created a brand-new login with no contact behind it — which is exactly what an empty tasks panel looks like. Ask them to sign out and sign in with the Portal Username.

## 3. Are they on the portal that carries the task?

Tasks belong to a portal, and each person is assigned to exactly one portal by that portal's **Portal Filters**. A task assigned on the Speaker portal is invisible to someone the filters put on a different portal.

1. In **Contacts**, click **Columns** and turn on **Assigned Portal** to see which portal each person is on.
2. Open that portal's card menu → **Edit Tasks** and confirm the task is assigned there. If not, assign it on that portal as well.
3. If they should be on a different portal, open the intended portal's **Edit Criteria** and compare its filters with the contact's values. The table under the filters previews who matches; a person who also matches a portal higher in the list lands there instead ([order portals by priority](/portals/creating-custom-portals#order-portals-by-priority)).

![The Select Participants step with the portal filters and the matching records](/images/kb/portal-editor-criteria.png)

Then look at it as they do: **View Portal → View portal as…**, pick the contact, and check the task appears. Ask them to reload their portal.
