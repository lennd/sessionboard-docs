Every person in an event is one contact record. The session's speaker list, the agenda, the Program Site, the portal's Profile page and every export all read from that record, so a wrong name or an old headshot is fixed once, on the contact, and the change shows everywhere the next time the page loads. There is no separate "speaker record" per session to keep in step.

## Which field to edit

| The wrong detail | Where it lives | Fix it as an admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | **First Name** / **Last Name** on the contact | Open the contact from **CRM → All Contacts** (or **Speakers**), click the name in the header, correct it and click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the profile. |
| Email you write to | **Email** on the contact | Same place — edit the field, then **Save Changes**. This is the address that receives emails and SMS. |
| Email they sign in with | **Portal Username**, in the **Details** card | **Portal options → Change Portal Username**. Changing the Email field does not change this, and the other way round — see [Change a contact's email address](/contacts/changing-a-contact-s-email-address). |
| Job title, company, phone, address, social links | Profile fields on the contact | Click the value, type, click away. These save on their own — a **Saved successfully** toast confirms it. |
| Biography | **Biography** on the contact | Edit it on the Profile tab, then **Save Changes**. |
| Headshot | The contact's headshot | Click the headshot in the profile header and choose **Upload New Photo**. Size, resizing and compression rules are in [Headshot size](/faq/what-is-the-recommended-size-for-contact-headshots). |
| Custom fields (dietary, pronouns, AV needs…) | Your custom fields on the contact | Profile tab, in the section the field belongs to. |
| Their role on a session (speaker, moderator, chair) | The session, not the contact | Open the session and change the participant's role — see [Participant roles](/concepts/participant-roles). |
| Session title, abstract, track | The session | Open the session; those fields belong to the submission, not the person. |

<Tip>
If you are correcting many speakers at once — a column of company names, say — export the contacts, fix the column in the spreadsheet and re-import it to update the existing records. See [Import to update existing records](/settings/importing-data#import-to-update-existing-records).
</Tip>

## What the speaker can fix themselves

In their portal, **Profile** shows the same contact fields — bio, headshot, title, company and whatever else you have made visible — and they can edit anything you have not locked. Which fields appear, and which are read-only, is the portal's **Manage Fields** setting ([Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#appearance)). A speaker who says "I can't change my bio" is usually looking at a field you have locked, or at a portal that does not show that field.

Their sign-in email is the one thing they cannot change from the portal. If a speaker has a new address, change their **Portal Username** for them as above, or invite the new address and [merge the two records](/contacts/merge-duplicates) if a second contact was created.

## Why a correction does not seem to show

- **You edited a different contact.** If the same person exists twice — a typo in the email on a second import is the usual cause — the session may be pointing at the other record. Search **All Contacts** for the name; if there are two, [merge them](/contacts/merge-duplicates).
- **The name, email or biography is still waiting for Save Changes.** Those three hold their edit until you click the bar at the bottom of the profile; everything else saves as you leave the field. See [Save a contact or group field](/faq/how-do-i-save-a-field).
- **The page you are looking at was loaded before the edit.** Reload the agenda, the Program Site or the portal.
- **An integration overwrote it.** When an outbound sync is connected, Sessionboard is the source of truth for the mapped fields and pushes its value out on the next run — but a connector that pulls the other way can write the destination's value back. Check the direction in [What syncs where](/integrations/what-syncs#which-way-does-it-flow).

<Note>
Deleting a contact and re-adding them does not fix a detail faster, and it drops their task progress, messages and session assignments with the record. Edit the record instead.
</Note>
