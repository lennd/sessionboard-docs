Member pricing checks each registrant against your member records at checkout. Members get the member type and price, lapsed members can renew while they register, and everyone else pays the non-member price — whatever type they click.

## Why use it

A self-declared "I'm a member" checkbox costs associations real money: anyone can tick it. Member lookup reads the membership status your association management system already sends to Sessionboard, so the discount goes only to people who have earned it, and a lapsed member is offered renewal right when they want the member price.

## Turn on member lookup

Member status comes from an organization user attribute that your association management system fills — for example through single sign-on. Set it up once per event in **Registration → Settings → Member pricing**:

<Steps>
<Step title="Look up members at checkout">
Turn on **Look up members at checkout**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the attribute">
Choose the **Member status attribute** your system fills with membership status.
</Step>
<Step title="Map its values">
Enter **Values that mean member** (comma-separated, for example `Active`) and **Values that mean lapsed**.
</Step>
<Step title="Route each group to a type">
Choose what **Members register as**, **Lapsed register as** and **Non-members register as**.
</Step>
<Step title="Offer join and register (optional)">
Under **Join and register**, pick an add-on that sells membership dues. A non-member can then join and pay the member price in one checkout.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Member pricing panel in Registration settings with the Look up members at checkout switch](/images/kb/registration-settings-member-pricing.png)

Click **Save**. The **Membership** panel on the Registration Overview then shows members, lapsed members, non-members and people who joined while registering.

## Where membership comes from

**Read membership from** chooses the source:

- **Member status attribute (SSO / AMS sync)**: the attribute above. Optionally choose the attributes that hold the **Member id**, **Category** and **Level**.
- **Uploaded member list (CSV)**: your member list, uploaded under **Member list and test lookup**. Use it when your association management system cannot send membership to Sessionboard.
- **iMIS**, **Nimble AMS**, **netFORUM**: listed as **not connected**. A live read from these needs your system's API access, which we set up with you. Until then, use the uploaded member list.

### Upload the member list

Under **Member list and test lookup**, choose a CSV with an `email` column and any of `member_id`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `category`, `level`, `status` and `expires_at`. Click **Preview** to see how many people are new or updated and which rows are skipped and why, then **Import**. The list belongs to your organization, so every event reads the same one.

<Warning>
**Remove people not in this file** replaces the whole organization-wide list, not only your event's members. Anyone missing from the file is removed for **every** event, and their membership lookup and member pricing change at once. Upload the full list — every event's members — when you tick it.
</Warning>

With no `status`, someone on the list is a member until their `expires_at`; a past expiry makes them lapsed.

## Rules: category, level and status

**Rules** decide more than member or non-member. Each rule names a **Category**, **Level** and **Status** (blank means any) and then either sets what they **register as** (and so their price), limits them to a **package** of tickets, or marks that they **cannot register**, with the message they see. The first rule that matches wins. For example: Students → cannot register online ("Students register through their school"); Physician + Fellow → registers as Fellow, Full conference only.

A person a rule refuses cannot check out, and a ticket outside their package is refused at checkout. A registrant type that a rule gives is only for the people the rule matches: someone whose membership maps elsewhere cannot pick it on the form. Your team can still choose it on an order they place for someone, and the order records that override.

### Test an email

**Test an email** shows every step for one person: where their record came from, what it says, member or not, the rule that matched, their registrant type, the tickets they may buy and whether they can register. It reads the saved settings, so save first.

## Join or renew in your system

Put your system's join or renew page in **Join or renew page**. Non-members get a **Join or renew, then come back** link on the form, and lapsed members a **Renew membership** link. We add the way back to the form to the link (or put it where your URL has `{return_url}`). Back on the form, they continue with their email and the lookup runs again, so the member price applies as soon as your system (or your uploaded list) shows them as a member.

## What registrants see

At the start of the form, registrants sign in with their society account or continue with their email. The form then tells them what it found — **Membership found.**, **Your membership has lapsed.** or **No member record for this email.** — and picks their registrant type. Member-only types stay locked unless the lookup confirmed a current member, or the person chose to join.

## Who can choose each type

Every registrant type has **Who can choose this type** (in **Tickets & pricing → Registrant types**):

| Setting | Who can pick it |
| --- | --- |
| **Anyone** | Everyone. |
| **Verified members only** | Only people the lookup confirms as current members. |
| **Lapsed members only** | Only people whose membership has lapsed. |
| **Non-members** | People without a member record. |

Prices are always checked again at checkout. Someone who is not a verified member is charged the non-member price, even if they reached a member type.

<Warning>
A type called "Member" that is set to **Anyone** gives its price to anyone who picks it. The registrant types table flags these with **Anyone can pick this type — set who can choose it**, and the type editor warns you while you edit. Set member types to **Verified members only**.
</Warning>
