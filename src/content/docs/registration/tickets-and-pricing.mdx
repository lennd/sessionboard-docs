**Registration → Tickets & pricing** holds two lists: **registrant types** (who someone is — member, non-member, trainee, presenter) and **tickets** (what they buy). **Registration → Workshops** sells seats in Program sessions.

## Why use it

Registration fees are rarely one number. Members pay less than non-members, early birds less than onsite, and a pre-conference workshop has its own fee and its own room capacity. Setting this up once as types, tiers and seats means checkout always charges the right price for the right person on the right date, and the Overview reports sales per ticket without a spreadsheet.

## Registrant types

A registrant type sets prices, badge ribbons and approval. Click **Add type**, then fill in:

- **Name** — for example Member, Non-member, Student, Presenter.
- **Who can choose this type** — **Anyone**, **Verified members only**, **Lapsed members only** or **Non-members**. See [Member pricing and eligibility](/registration/member-pricing).
- **Colour** — used for the badge ribbon and at the check-in desk.
- **Badge ribbon text** — leave blank to print the type's name.

![The Registrant types table with each type's eligibility and a warning on a Member type anyone can pick](/images/kb/registration-registrant-types.png)

Registrations of a type can also require approval: tick the type under **Settings → Approval**. Archiving a type keeps it on existing registrations.

## Tickets

Click **New ticket**. The **Kind** decides where it appears:

| Kind | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Registration ticket** | The main ticket — full conference, one day, virtual. |
| **Bundle** | A ticket that packages several things at one price. |
| **Session fee** | A paid seat in one Program session. Pick the **Session**; its time, room and faculty come from Program. |
| **Add-on** | Extras sold with a ticket: meals, guest passes, publications. |
| **Donation** | A gift added at checkout. |

Tickets, bundles and session fees are listed under **Tickets**; add-ons and donations under **Add-ons & donations**. Booth packages, sponsorships and exhibitor shop items are tickets too, but you manage them in [Expo](/expo/overview).

### What you can set on a ticket

- **Price** — 0 or blank is free.
- **Capacity** — leave blank for unlimited. Registrations waiting for approval and seats held during checkout count toward it, so a ticket cannot oversell.
- **Max per order**.
- **Date tiers** — early bird, standard, onsite. Each price applies through its end date in the event's time zone, and the base price applies after the last tier. Prices roll automatically.
- **Count tiers** — a price for the first seats sold: "first 100 at 495, the next 100 at 595". Each row covers seats up to its count; after the last row the date tiers and base price apply. The form shows how many seats are left at today's price. A registrant type with its own price is not counted, and a group buying several seats at once pays the tier of its first seat.
- **Price by registrant type** — a different price for each type. Leave a type blank to use the ticket's price.
- **Who can buy** — **Anyone**, **With an access code** (enter the **Access code**) or **Hidden (admin only)**.
- **On sale from** / **Until** — the sales window.
- **GL code** — shown on receipts, exports and the GL split in [Finance reports](/registration/orders-and-invoices#finance-reports).
- **Confirmation email text** — added to the confirmation of everyone who buys it, for example where a workshop meets.
- **Taxable** — whether tax rules apply. See [Tax](/registration/payments#tax).
- **Tax rate** — **Standard**, **Reduced**, **Zero** or **Exempt**: which tax rules apply to this fee. See [Different rates for different fees](/registration/payments#different-rates-for-different-fees).
- **Offered on** — when the event has several forms, pick which forms sell it. Leave all unticked for every form.
- **Fee group** — a label such as `meals` or `workshops`. A promo code can then discount only that group. See [Promo codes](/registration/promo-codes).
- **Sold in exactly** — an order holds exactly this many of the fee, or none. Use it for a table of 10.
- **Only with** — tick other fees: the order (or, for an add-on bought later, the registration) must also have one of them. Use it for a guest dinner that needs the dinner.
- **Guest badge** (add-ons) — each one bought is a named guest with their own registration and badge, priced at the add-on rather than the ticket. Under **Guests register as**, pick the registrant type their badge prints. On the form, the registrant adds each guest's name under the add-on.

### Donations

A **Donation** is a set amount (its price) unless you turn on **Registrant chooses the amount**. Then set a **Minimum** and **Maximum**, **Suggested amounts** shown as buttons (for example `25, 50, 100`), and the **Funds** the registrant can give to, each with its own **GL code**. On the form, the registrant enters an amount and picks a fund; the order line carries the fund and its GL code.

### Staff orders at an earlier price

When you add a registration yourself from **Registrants**, **Use prices as of** prices the order as of a past date — an early-bird rate someone was promised after it ended. The ticket must have been on sale that day. Registrants on the public form always pay today's price.

## Shared capacity

A **pool** caps two or more fees together — tickets that fill the same hall, meals that share a room. Each fee keeps its own capacity too, and a registration must fit both. Under **Shared capacity**, click **Add a pool**, name it, set its **Capacity** and tick the fees in it. The table shows each pool's sold seats against its capacity. When a pool is full, every fee in it shows as sold out on the form, or takes the waitlist on a free form with a waitlist.

## Landing step

Instead of a list of forms, the registration page can ask one question — "Which describes you?" — and send each answer to a form with the registrant type already chosen. Under **Landing step**, click **Set up**, write the **Question**, and for each **Answer** pick the form it **Opens** and, optionally, the registrant type. A type that needs a verified membership still asks for the member check on the form.

## Import or export the fee schedule

**Export fee schedule** downloads every ticket, add-on, bundle, donation and session fee as a CSV, one row per price: a base row, a row per date tier, and a row per registrant-type price. **Import CSV** reads the same columns: `ticket_id, name, kind, registrant_type, tier, through, price, capacity, max_per_order, gl_code, taxable, status`. Prices are amounts like `595.50`.

- A row matches a ticket by `ticket_id`, else by its exact name. A new name creates the ticket from its base row (a row with no tier and no registrant type).
- A ticket with tier rows in the file gets exactly those tiers. `through` is the tier's last day.
- Session fees are created from **Workshops**, not imported.

Click **Start from the current data** to edit what is there, **Dry run** to see each row's result, then **Apply**.

The tickets table shows each ticket's **Price now** (and which tier applies), **Sold** against capacity, sales window and status. Archiving a ticket stops it selling; existing registrations and orders keep it.

![The Tickets table with kind, price now and its date tier, sold against capacity, sales window and status](/images/kb/registration-tickets-table.png)

## Workshops

**Registration → Workshops** sells seats in Program sessions — pre-conference workshops, labs, tours. Time, room and faculty always come from the Program session, so a change in Program shows up here.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a workshop">
Click **Workshop**, pick the **Session**, and set a **Price** (blank or 0 means the seat is included) and a **Capacity** (blank uses the session's own capacity, or unlimited).
</Step>
<Step title="Price it per registrant type">
Under **Price by registrant type**, enter what members or trainees pay instead. Every rate shares one capacity.
</Step>
<Step title="Offer it on a form">
On the form's **Tickets** tab, turn on **Session picks**. Registrants then choose workshops during registration, or add them later from their attendee portal.
</Step>
</Steps>

The table shows seats taken against capacity, **Full** workshops and the **Waitlist** count. Full workshops take a waitlist. Two workshops at the same time are flagged as overlapping, and registrants cannot pick both. Turn on **Every public session** to see sessions you are not selling yet and click **Sell seats** on one.

![The Workshops table with time, price, seats taken against capacity and waitlist per workshop](/images/kb/registration-workshops.png)

**Take out of registration** stops a workshop selling; people who already have a seat keep it.

### Import workshop prices and capacity

**Export prices** downloads every public Program session with its price, capacity, GL code and a `price:<registrant type>` column per type. **Import prices** reads the same file. A row matches a session by `session_id`, else by its exact title.

- A blank **price** keeps the price; `remove` takes the session out of registration; `0` makes it a free workshop.
- A blank **capacity** keeps it; `unlimited` clears it.
- A blank type column keeps that type's price; `-` drops it.

### Picking rules

**Picking rules** on the Workshops page decide what the form lists and what each registrant may pick. Click **Edit rules**:

- **List free sessions** — public Program sessions without a price are offered as free picks. A free session's Program capacity is held at checkout.
- **Overlapping picks** — **Allow, say nothing**, **Allow, with a warning** or **Block: pick one**.
- **Slots** — group sessions the registrant picks among, such as "one Thursday afternoon workshop". **Required** of 1 or more makes the slot required; **At most** limits how many.
- **Each session** — **General** puts it on everyone's registration as included (listed, never picked). **Hidden** keeps it off the form (staff can still assign it). Tick registrant types to say who may pick it, and tickets to show it only with those tickets.

The form shows each workshop's room, track, description and public faculty. Staff adding someone on their behalf, and **Bulk assign**, are not held to these rules.

### Let registrants change paid workshops

Under **Self-service changes**, turn on **Registrants change paid workshops**, and set **Until** to close it on a date. Registrants then see **Change** next to a paid workshop in their attendee portal or manage link. They pick another workshop or drop it, see the difference, and confirm:

- When the new workshop costs more, they pay the difference by card.
- When it costs less, or they drop it, the difference goes back to the card they paid with, through your gateway.
- When the workshop was paid by invoice, check or cash, they are asked to contact you; refund it from the order.

### Move people in bulk

**Bulk assign** adds or removes many people at once. Paste or upload CSV rows of `registration_id,workshop,action` (the workshop is the session id or its exact title; action is `add` or `remove`, add if blank). Click **Dry run** to see which rows would work, then **Apply**. Capacity is enforced unless you tick **Allow over capacity**; overlaps are shown as warnings.
