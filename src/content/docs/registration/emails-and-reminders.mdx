**Registration → Emails & SMS** lists every email registration sends and schedules three follow-ups: finish your registration, balance reminders and know before you go.

## Why use it

The emails after someone clicks Register do most of the work of getting them to the event: the confirmation carries their QR code and calendar invite, an unpaid checkout gets a nudge, an open invoice gets a reminder before it is due, and the week before the event everyone gets what they need at the door. Scheduling them once means nobody on your team has to remember to send them.

## Registration emails

The **Registration emails** table lists each email with when it is sent and whether it is **On**, **On · edited** or **Off**. They are System notifications, so you edit their wording and design in the same email designer as every other event email — click the pencil on a row. See [Email templates](/settings/email-templates).

![The Registration emails table with each email, when it is sent and whether it is on](/images/kb/registration-emails-list.png)

Every confirmation includes the registrant's QR code, a link to their attendee portal and, when they are on in **Settings → Confirmation**, an Apple or Google wallet pass and a calendar file. **Reply-to address** in the same section sets where replies go.

### What else the confirmation can carry

- **Text for what they bought.** In **Tickets & pricing**, give a ticket, add-on or workshop **Confirmation email text** ("Your workshop meets in Room 4 at 8:00 AM"). Everyone who buys it sees that text under the confirmation. Place `{{purchase_text}}` in the email yourself to put it somewhere else.
- **Attachments.** Under **Settings → Confirmation → Attachments sent with every confirmation**, add files such as a venue map or a code of conduct. They go with every confirmation.
- **Wallet and badge tags.** `{{wallet.apple_url}}`, `{{wallet.google_url}}`, `{{badge.preview_url}}`, `{{badge.id}}` and `{{badge.registrant_type}}` can go in any registration email. They are empty when the event has no wallet pass or badge design.

### Registered by someone else

When someone registers colleagues on one order, each colleague gets **Registered by someone else** instead of the plain confirmation: who registered them (`{{purchaser.name}}`), their QR code and their own link to check their details and pick sessions. The person who placed the order gets the confirmation.

### Registration changed

**Registration changed** tells registrants what changed (`{{changes}}`) when they edit their own registration in the attendee portal. Turn it off under **Settings → Confirmation → Email registrants when they change their registration**. When your team edits a registration, the email goes only if they tick **Email the registrant what changed** in the drawer's **Edit**.

## Emails in other languages

Registrants get their emails in the language they registered in, once that translation is reviewed. Set them up under **Registration → Settings → Email translations**: pick a language and an email, write the **Subject** and **Body**, and click **Mark reviewed**. **Draft missing emails with machine translation** fills the emails that have no translation yet as machine drafts — check each one before you mark it reviewed. Merge tags such as `{{registrant.first_name}}` stay as they are. A draft, a machine draft or a missing translation sends the email as written.

Receipts and invoices use the registrant's language for their fixed words (Receipt, Total, Balance) in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian and Dutch.

## Follow-ups

Under **Follow-ups**, turn each step on and set its timing. Steps are checked every hour, and each person gets each step once.

| Step | Goes to | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Finish your registration** | People who started a card checkout and did not finish. Stops once they register. | **Hours after**, for example `1, 24` |
| **Balance reminder** | Registrants with an open invoice, before it is due. Exhibitor invoices are reminded in the exhibitor portal instead. | **Days before due**, for example `14, 3` |
| **Know before you go** | Confirmed registrants, with their QR code, a calendar invite, Add to Apple / Google Wallet links and, when the event has a badge design, their badge preview as a PDF. | **Days before the event** |

Each step shows how many people it would reach. **Send now** runs one pass immediately, even when the step's schedule is off; anyone who already got that step is skipped. Save the schedule before sending.

![The Follow-ups panel with Finish your registration, Balance reminder and Know before you go, their timing and Send now](/images/kb/registration-follow-ups.png)

Click **Save** to keep your changes.

## Reminders

**Reminders** adds as many extra reminders as you need, each on its own schedule. For each one, set **Days before** the event, an optional **Message** shown in the email, and, if it is not for everyone, the tickets or registrant types it goes to. Each reminder goes once to each confirmed registrant it targets, on its day, and stops once the event starts. They use the **Event reminder** email, which you edit like the others. **Send due now** sends every reminder whose day has come to the people who have not had it yet.
