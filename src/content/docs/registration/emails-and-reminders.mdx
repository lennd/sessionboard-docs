**Registration → Emails & SMS** lists every email registration sends, schedules three follow-ups (finish your registration, balance reminders and know before you go) and texts registrants who opted in to SMS.

## Why use it

The emails after someone clicks Register do most of the work of getting them to the event: the confirmation carries their QR code and calendar invite, an unpaid checkout gets a nudge, an open invoice gets a reminder before it is due, and the week before the event everyone gets what they need at the door. Scheduling them once means nobody on your team has to remember to send them.

## Registration emails

The **Registration emails** table lists each email with when it is sent and whether it is **On**, **On · edited** or **Off**. They are System notifications, so you edit their wording and design in the same email designer as every other event email — click the pencil on a row. See [Email templates](/settings/email-templates).

![The Registration emails table with each email, when it is sent and whether it is on](/images/kb/registration-emails-list.png)

Every confirmation includes the registrant's QR code, a link to their attendee portal, the order's receipt as a PDF (an invoice while it is unpaid) and, when they are on in **Settings → Confirmation**, Add to Apple / Google Wallet links and a calendar file. The wallet switch says so when wallet passes are not set up for your account yet; the emails then carry no wallet links. **Reply-to address** in the same section sets where replies go for every registration email and invitation, unless an email's own template sets a Reply-to.

### What else the confirmation can carry

- **Text for what they bought.** In **Tickets & pricing**, give a ticket, add-on or workshop **Confirmation email text** ("Your workshop meets in Room 4 at 8:00 AM"). Everyone who buys it sees that text under the confirmation. Place `{{purchase_text}}` in the email yourself to put it somewhere else.
- **Attachments.** Under **Settings → Confirmation → Attachments sent with every confirmation**, add files such as a venue map or a code of conduct. They go with every confirmation.
- **Wallet and badge tags.** `{{wallet.apple_url}}`, `{{wallet.google_url}}`, `{{badge.preview_url}}`, `{{badge.id}}` and `{{badge.registrant_type}}` can go in any registration email. They are empty when the event has no wallet pass or badge design.

### Registered by someone else

When someone registers colleagues on one order, each colleague gets **Registered by someone else** instead of the plain confirmation: who registered them (`{{purchaser.name}}`), their QR code and their own link to check their details and pick sessions. The person who placed the order gets the confirmation when they hold a seat on it. When they bought only for others (a group order with seats to name later, say), they get **Order confirmation (buyer)** instead: the order number and total, who is registered, how many seats are still open, a link to the attendee portal to name them, and the receipt as a PDF. Colleagues never get the buyer's receipt.

### Canceled and declined

**Registration canceled** goes out when a registrant cancels in the attendee portal and when your team cancels them from the drawer in **Registrants**; untick **Email the registrant that their registration was canceled** in the cancel dialog to skip it. **Registration declined** goes out when your team declines a registration that was waiting for approval, with the reason they gave (`{{decline_reason}}`).

### Registration changed

**Registration changed** tells registrants what changed (`{{changes}}`) when they edit their own registration in the attendee portal. It names only the fields whose value is different, and an edit that changes nothing sends no email. Turn it off under **Settings → Confirmation → Email registrants when they change their registration**. When your team edits a registration, the email goes only if they tick **Email the registrant what changed** in the drawer's **Edit**.

## Emails in other languages

Registrants get their emails in the language they registered in, once that translation is reviewed. Set them up under **Registration → Settings → Email translations**: pick a language and an email, write the **Subject** and **Body**, and click **Mark reviewed**. **Draft missing emails with machine translation** fills the emails that have no translation yet as machine drafts — check each one before you mark it reviewed. Merge tags such as `{{registrant.first_name}}` stay as they are. A draft, a machine draft or a missing translation sends the email as written.

Receipts and invoices use the registrant's language for their fixed words (Receipt, Total, Balance) in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian and Dutch.

## Follow-ups

Under **Follow-ups**, turn each step on and set its timing. Steps are checked every hour, and each person gets each step once.

| Step | Goes to | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Finish your registration** | People who started a card checkout and did not finish. Stops once they register. | **Hours after**, for example `1, 24` |
| **Balance reminder** | Registrants with an open invoice, before it is due. Its **Pay the balance** button opens **Tickets & receipts** in the registrant's attendee portal, signed in, where they see the balance and pay it (`{{pay_url}}`; the change-or-cancel page when the event has no portal). **Resend invoice** sends the same email. Exhibitor invoices are reminded in the exhibitor portal instead. | **Days before due**, for example `14, 3` |
| **Know before you go** | Confirmed registrants, with their QR code, a calendar invite, Add to Apple / Google Wallet links and, when the event has a badge design, their badge preview as a PDF. | **Days before the event** |

Each step shows how many people it would reach. **Send now** runs one pass immediately, even when the step's schedule is off; anyone who already got that step is skipped. Save the schedule before sending.

![The Follow-ups panel with Finish your registration, Balance reminder and Know before you go, their timing and Send now](/images/kb/registration-follow-ups.png)

Click **Save** to keep your changes.

## Reminders

**Reminders** adds as many extra reminders as you need, each on its own schedule. For each one, set **Days before** the event, an optional **Message** shown in the email, and, if it is not for everyone, the tickets or registrant types it goes to. Each reminder goes once to each confirmed registrant it targets, on its day, and stops once the event starts. They use the **Event reminder** email, which you edit like the others. **Send due now** sends every reminder whose day has come to the people who have not had it yet.

To text a reminder as well, tick **Also text it to registrants who opted in to SMS** and, if you like, write a shorter **SMS text**. When the SMS text is empty, the reminder's message is used. The text goes once to each confirmed registrant it targets who opted in, on the same day as the email. See [SMS](#sms).

## SMS

Registrants can opt in to text messages on the registration form. You then text them reminders and onsite notices, such as a room change.

### Turn on the SMS opt-in

<Steps>
<Step title="Turn it on">
In **Registration → Emails & SMS → SMS**, turn on **Offer an SMS opt-in on the registration form**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the consent text">
The form shows the **Consent text** next to the opt-in, so say what you will text about and that registrants can reply STOP. Click **Save consent text** if you change it.
</Step>
</Steps>

The opt-in is on the form's review step. A registrant who ticks it enters their mobile number in international format, with **+** and the country code (for example `+1 303 555 0100`). The form only continues once the number is valid. Each registration keeps the number, the consent text the registrant agreed to and when they agreed; the registrant's drawer in **Registrants** shows all three under **Text messages**. People who do not tick it are never texted.

The panel shows how many registrants opted in, how many of them are checked in and how many opted out.

### Send an onsite notice

<Steps>
<Step title="Write the notice">
Under **Send onsite notice**, write the message (up to 300 characters).
</Step>
<Step title="Pick who gets it">
Click **Checked in** to text registrants who have checked in, or **All opted in** to text every confirmed registrant who opted in. Each button shows how many people it reaches.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Confirm. The texts go out over the next few minutes; the list of notices shows how many were sent.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each notice goes once to each person, even if you click twice. The panel lists the last notices with how many were sent, skipped and failed.

Every text starts with the event name and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out."

### Opt-outs

- **STOP replies.** A registrant who replies STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT is not texted again, for this event or any other. Replying START opts them back in.
- **Do not text.** To stop texting a number for this event only, add it under **Do not text** in international format. Remove it with the bin icon.

<Note>
Texts count toward your **Emails** usage, the same as emails. Each event can send up to 5,000 texts a day. If no SMS provider is set up for your account, the panel says so and nothing is sent until one is.
</Note>
