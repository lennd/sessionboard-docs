**Registration** is the event module where attendees, groups, presenters and press sign up for your event, pay through your own payment gateway, and get a confirmation with a QR code. Everything they tell you on the way lands on their profile, next to their sessions and their CRM record.

<AddOnNote feature="registration">
{' '}Registration is in Early Access as a Beta: choose **Request beta** on the **Registration** tile in **Your toolkit** on the event Dashboard, or open **Preview** in the event sidebar, find the **Registration** card and choose **Request access**. Our team sets it up with you.
</AddOnNote>

## Why use it

Registration keeps the people who attend your event in the same place as the program they came for. Ticket types, member prices, workshop seats and interests are recorded on each person's profile as they register, so Pathways, Marketing and your sponsor reports can use them without an import from a separate registration system. Money goes straight to your organization's own gateway — Sessionboard is not in the flow of funds and charges no per-registrant fee.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
<Step title="Request access">
In the event, open **Preview** at the bottom of the sidebar. On the **Registration** card choose **Request access** and tell us when your registration opens. See [Preview](/get-started/early-access) for how Beta requests work.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect a payment gateway">
Paid tickets need a gateway connected to your organization. Free registration works without one. See [Take payments for registration](/registration/payments).
</Step>
<Step title="Build your tickets and forms">
Add registrant types and tickets in **Registration → Tickets & pricing**, then create a form in **Registration → Forms** and open it. See [Create registration forms](/registration/forms).
</Step>
</Steps>

Once Registration is on, the event sidebar shows three modules: **Registration**, **Onsite** (check-in and badges) and, when your event also has the Expo Beta, **Expo**.

## The Registration pages

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Registrations by status, money in and owed, and sales by ticket, form and registrant type. |
| **Registrants** | Search, open and act on every registration: change status, resend, transfer, cancel. |
| **Forms** | One form per audience — attendees, groups, presenters, press — each with its own window, types and questions. |
| **Tickets & pricing** | Registrant types, ticket types, add-ons and date-tiered prices. |
| **Workshops** | Seats sold for Program sessions. |
| **Orders** | Payments, invoices and refunds. |
| **Sales & invitations** | Reps nominate, managers approve, and each invitation carries a pass from a team quota. |
| **Emails & SMS** | The registration emails and the follow-ups that go out on a schedule. |
| **Promo codes** | Percent, fixed or complimentary codes. |
| **Badges** | Badge designs per registrant type, batch printing and the print log. |
| **Settings** | Payments, member pricing, invoices, tax, approval, confirmation options, branding, languages and groups. |
| **Integrations & API** | Apps, webhooks, Zapier and the Public API. |

## Read the Overview

**Registration → Overview** is the module's home.

![Four stat tiles: Registrations, Revenue, Open invoices and Waiting for approval](/images/kb/registration-overview-stats.png)

When you set an **Attendance goal** (**Settings → Launch**), the Overview shows registrations against it. While the event is in test mode, a banner says so.

- **Registrations** — the total, with how many are confirmed, waitlisted and canceled. Click it to open **Registrants**.
- **Revenue** — paid to your gateway, net of refunds.
- **Open invoices** — pay-later and deposit balances still owed. Click it to see those orders.
- **Waiting for approval** — registrations held for a decision. It opens the Approvals queue when your event has Approvals, otherwise the filtered registrant list.
- **Last 14 days** — new registrations per day.
- **By ticket type** — sold, capacity used and revenue per ticket.
- **By form**, **By registrant type** and **Membership** — where registrations came from, and the member, lapsed, non-member and joined-while-registering mix when member lookup is on.
- **Signals** — how many signals, declared interests and session picks registration has written to people's profiles. **Open attendees** goes to the Attendees view under Pathways.

![The By ticket type table on the Registration Overview with sold, capacity and revenue per ticket](/images/kb/registration-overview-by-ticket-type.png)
