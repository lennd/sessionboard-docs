[Team Lead](/agents/team-lead) can set up an event's registration with you in a conversation. You say how your registration works — who registers, what they pay, until when — and Team Lead builds it in Registration one step at a time. Every change comes back as a card for you to approve first, and everything it builds is a **draft**: nothing opens to registrants until you open it.

## Why use it

A registration setup is six pages of decisions that depend on each other: member prices need the Member attendee type, a paid ticket needs a gateway, the confirmation email needs a sender. Team Lead keeps the order for you, proposes the setup most events start from, and reads each result back so you know it was saved the way you meant.

## Before you start

- The event has **Registration** turned on (Early Access).
- Your event role has the Registration permission **Manage registration settings**. Without it Team Lead can describe the setup but not change it. See [Registration roles and permissions](/registration/roles-and-permissions).
- To choose the payment gateway, you also need the organization permission **Manage payment gateways** and two-factor authentication. Gateways themselves are connected on **Organization settings → Payments**; Team Lead only picks one of them. See [Take payments for registration](/registration/payments#connect-a-gateway).

## Start the walkthrough

Open **Ask** on any page of the event, or the [Agent window](/agents/agent-window), and say what you want, for example:

> Set up registration for this event. Members pay $495 and non-members $695, students $195. Early bird is $100 off until February 28.

Team Lead works through the steps below in this order. At each step it says what is already set up, proposes a typical setup in one sentence, and waits for you to agree or change it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Attendee types">
Who registers: typically **Member**, **Non-member** and **Student**. A Member type is checked against your membership data at checkout, which is what gives members their price. See [Member pricing](/registration/member-pricing).
</Step>
<Step title="Tickets and prices">
What people buy and for how much: a ticket's base price, a price per attendee type (member pricing), and date tiers such as an early-bird price that ends on a date you give. Team Lead never invents a price; if you did not give one, it asks. See [Tickets and pricing](/registration/tickets-and-pricing).
</Step>
<Step title="Payment gateway">
Which of your organization's connected gateways takes the event's card payments. With one gateway Team Lead proposes it; with several it asks which. A gateway marked **(test mode)** takes no real money.
</Step>
<Step title="Email sender">
The name registration emails come from, an optional From address on your verified sending domain, and a Reply-to address. It is set on every registration email — the confirmation, pending, waitlist, reminder and balance-due emails.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirmation email">
The subject, preview text, body and button of the email a registrant gets when their place is confirmed. Merge tags such as `{{registrant.first_name}}` and `{{event.name}}` are filled in when it is sent; the date, place, calendar invite and QR code are added for you. See [Emails and reminders](/registration/emails-and-reminders).
</Step>
<Step title="Registration form">
The form, the tickets it sells, the attendee types it offers, who can open it once it is live, and its questions. It is created as a draft. See [Registration forms](/registration/forms).
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also ask for one step on its own — "add a $95 gala dinner add-on" or "change the confirmation email subject" — and Team Lead does only that step.

## Approve each change

Every change arrives as a **Decision needed** card that shows what will be saved: the ticket's name and base price, the attendee-type prices and date tiers, the sender name, the email subject. On most cards you can edit a value — a name, a price, a capacity — before you approve. **Reject** means nothing was saved; Team Lead asks what you would like instead.

Choosing the payment gateway asks every time, because it decides where the event's money goes.

## When Team Lead cannot do a step

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Choosing the payment gateway needs a fresh two-factor check" | Open **Registration → Settings → Payments** and pick the gateway there (it asks for your code), or confirm your code there and ask Team Lead again within 15 minutes. Team Lead cannot complete the check for you. |
| "Needs the organization permission Manage payment gateways" | Ask an organization admin to choose the gateway, or to give your role the permission. |
| "No payment gateway is connected yet" | Someone with **Manage payment gateways** connects one on **Organization settings → Payments**. Team Lead moves on; free tickets and invoices still work. |
| "A From address must be on your verified domain" | Leave the From address empty to send from the Sessionboard sender with your name, or verify a domain under **Event settings → Domains** first. |
| "This form is already open" | Team Lead only edits draft forms. Change a live form on **Registration → Forms**. |
| "Your account can view registration but not set it up" | An event admin can give your role **Manage registration settings** under **Event team → Permissions → Registration**. |

<Note>
Team Lead acts as you. A step your role cannot do on the Registration pages, it cannot do either.
</Note>

## Go live yourself

When the draft is ready, Team Lead says so and stops. It never opens a form, turns test mode off or sends an email. Run a test registration, then open the form on **Registration → Forms** and go live on **Registration → Settings → Launch**. See [Test, launch and copy registration](/registration/launch).

## Ask how registration is going

Once registration is open, ask Team Lead about it in plain words. It answers from the same numbers as the Registration Overview:

- "How many people registered this week, and how much is still owed on invoices?"
- "Which registration form converts best?" — form views, checkouts started and completed, and the rates between them, per form.
- "How many checkouts were abandoned, and what was in them?" — checkouts still waiting for payment or expired, their value, the last 7 and 30 days, and the tickets they held.
- "Who is on the waitlist for the gala dinner?" — waitlist counts per form and ticket, offers held, expired and taken.
- "Is ada@example.org a member, and is she registered?"
- "How many seats are left in the Thursday workshops?"

<Tip>
The same questions work from Claude or ChatGPT once you connect them to Sessionboard. See [Connect AI assistants to Sessionboard](/integrations/connect-ai-assistants).
</Tip>
