**Registration → Orders** shows what was bought, by whom, what was paid and what is still owed. Booth and sponsorship orders from Expo are here too. The other tabs are:

- **Invoices** — every invoice the event issued.
- **Credits** — credits toward a future event.
- **Refunds to issue** — refunds you owe to people who paid by check, PO or wire.
- **Finance reports** — the failed-transaction log and the reports finance asks for.

## Why use it

Registration money rarely arrives in one card payment. Hospitals pay by purchase order, exhibitors pay a deposit and then the balance, and someone always pays at the desk in cash. Orders keep every payment, invoice and refund against the registration it belongs to, so finance and the registration desk see the same balance.

## Find an order

The **Orders** tab lists orders with their number, purchaser, items, total, balance and status. Search by order number, name or email, or filter by status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Started but not placed. |
| **Unpaid** | A card checkout that has not been paid yet. |
| **Invoice sent** | A pay-later order waiting for payment. |
| **Partially paid** | A deposit or installment is paid; a balance is due. |
| **Paid** | Paid in full. |
| **Partially refunded** / **Refunded** | Some or all of the money went back. |
| **Expired** | An unpaid checkout that timed out; its seats went back on sale. |

![The Orders list with order number, purchaser, items, total, balance and status](/images/kb/registration-orders-list.png)

Click an order to open it. The drawer shows each line (with the price tier that applied), subtotal, discount and promo code, tax, total, what has been paid and the **Balance due** with its due date. It lists the order's registrations — group seats not yet assigned show as **Seat not yet assigned** — and every payment and refund.

![An order drawer with its lines, total, balance due, invoice and the Record a payment, Resend invoice and Cancel order buttons](/images/kb/registration-order-drawer.png)

## Charge a card

On an order with a balance, **Charge a card** opens your gateway's secure card window, for an order taken over the phone or by mail. The card details go to the gateway, never to Sessionboard. The charge goes through your organization's gateway like an online payment.

## Record a payment

For money that did not go through the online checkout, click **Record a payment** on an order with a balance. Enter the **Amount**, the **Method** (**Check**, **Wire / bank transfer**, **Cash**, **Money order**, **Purchase order** or **Other**) and a **Reference** such as the check number or PO number. The payment settles the invoice and shows on the receipt.

To pay an order with a credit, enter its code next to **Apply credit**.

## Payment plans

An order on a [payment plan](/registration/payments#payment-plans) shows a **Payment plan** section with each installment, its due date and whether it is paid. When a scheduled charge is declined, you see how many tries failed and why. **Autopay** turns the scheduled charges off or back on. **Charge the next installment now** charges the card or bank account on file straight away.

## Statements

**Statement for** the purchaser's email opens a PDF listing every order they placed for the event, every payment and refund, and the running balance, with their open invoices. Registrants can download theirs from the attendee portal.

## Send an invoice

**Send invoice** (or **Resend invoice**) emails the purchaser an invoice with a payment link. The invoice number, status and **PDF** show on the order. Invoice terms, numbering, deposits and payment instructions are set in [Registration settings](/registration/payments#invoices-and-deposits).

The **Invoices** tab lists every invoice — **Open**, **Overdue**, **Paid** or **Void** — with the amount, what is still owed, installments and a download button for the PDF. The total outstanding on the invoices shown is at the top. Click an invoice number to open its order.

![The Invoices tab listing open invoices with bill-to, issue and due dates, amount, balance and a PDF button](/images/kb/registration-invoices.png)

## Refund an order

On a paid or partially paid order, open **Refund**. It shows what was paid and refunded so far. Leave **Amount** blank to refund everything left, or enter part of it. Choose **How**:

- **Back to the card / bank account** — through your gateway. An order paid in installments is refunded across its payments, newest first.
- **Check**, **Wire / bank transfer**, **Cash**, **Money order** or **Other** — for a refund you make outside the gateway. It is recorded with its **Reference**, and nothing is sent to the gateway.
- **Credit toward a future event** — the purchaser gets a credit code instead of their money back.

Money paid by check, PO or wire cannot go back to a card. Refund that part another way.

<Warning>
Refunds cannot be undone. A full refund also cancels the order's registrations.
</Warning>

### Cancel under the policy

**Cancel under the policy…** shows what the event's [cancellation policy](/registration/payments#cancellation-policy) gives back today: what was paid, the cancellation fee kept and the refund. You can **Waive the cancellation fee**, **Refund a different amount**, or **Give a credit instead of a refund**. A card refund goes back through your gateway. If the order was paid by check, PO or wire, the refund is added to **Refunds to issue**. On an unpaid order, the cancellation fee stays owing on the invoice.

## Refunds to issue

When a registrant who paid by check, PO or wire cancels, their refund cannot go back through the gateway. It is listed under **Refunds to issue** with the order, the PO number and the amount. Send the refund, enter the check or transfer number, and click **Mark issued**.

## Credits

The **Credits** tab lists credits toward a future event across your organization: the code, the holder's email, the amount and what is left, and when it expires. Credits come from cancellations and moves, or click **Issue a credit** to give one by hand. **Void** cancels one. A credit is spent like a payment on any of your events' orders, by staff with **Apply credit** or by the holder in their attendee portal. Online, only the holder's email can use it.

## Finance reports

**Finance reports** lists, for the event:

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Failed transactions** | Declined and failed card and bank payments, with your gateway's reason, and payments with no answer from the gateway after 15 minutes. |
| **Transactions** | Every payment and refund (card, bank, check, PO, credit) with its GL split. |
| **Reconciliation** | Each gateway payment checked against your gateway. |
| **Accounts receivable** | Open invoices by age: not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days. |
| **Purchase orders** | Orders on a PO and what is still owed. |
| **Code usage** | Each promo code's orders, the discount given and the revenue. |
| **GL summary** | Charges, refunds and net per GL code. |

Each report downloads as **CSV**. Payments are split across GL codes in proportion to the order's lines, using each ticket's **GL code** from [Tickets & pricing](/registration/tickets-and-pricing).

## Cancel an order

**Cancel order** on an unpaid order cancels its registrations and puts the tickets back on sale. Nothing is refunded — refund paid amounts separately.

To cancel one person rather than the whole order, cancel their registration from [Registrants](/registration/registrants#cancel-a-registration).

<Note>
Money goes straight to your organization's own gateway. Sessionboard never holds funds and charges no per-registrant fee.
</Note>
