**Registration → Seating** decides who sits where at a seated function: a gala dinner, an awards banquet or a board lunch. You make one seating plan for each function, add its tables, and seat registrants by dragging them or with auto-seat. Then you print the table tents, place cards, a seating chart and a list for each table.

## Why use it

A seated dinner is usually planned in a spreadsheet that falls out of date when someone cancels or a sponsor buys another table. When the plan works from your registrations, a canceled registrant drops out by themselves, you see the open seats on every table, and the printed tents and place cards match the room.

## Make a seating plan

<Steps>
1. Go to **Registration → Seating** and click **New seating plan**.
2. Name it after the function, for example **Gala dinner**, and add the room if you like.
3. Under **Who can be seated**, leave every box unticked to seat any confirmed registrant. To seat only people who bought the dinner, tick its ticket or add-on, for example a **Gala dinner** add-on.
4. Click **Create plan**.
</Steps>

An event can have several plans, one for each function. Choose the plan you are working on from the list at the top of the page.

## Add tables

<Steps>
1. Click **Add tables**.
2. To add the whole room at once, set **How many** and **Seats**. The tables are numbered from **Start numbering at**, or from the next free number.
3. To add one table, give it a **Number**, a **Name** (for example **Head table**), or both.
4. Optionally add a **Host** and a **Sponsor**. Both are printed on the table tent.
5. Click **Add**.
</Steps>

To change a table, click the pencil on its card. You cannot lower a table's seats below the number of people already sitting there; move some of them first. Deleting a table sends its guests back to **Unassigned**.

## Seat people

The **Unassigned** list on the left shows everyone who can be seated and has no table yet. Each table card shows who sits there and how many seats are taken, for example **8/10**.

- **Drag** a person from **Unassigned** onto a table. Drag them to another table to move them, or back onto **Unassigned** to unseat them.
- **Several at once:** tick people in **Unassigned**, then choose a table under **Seat … at**. Tables without enough open seats cannot be chosen.
- **Unseat** someone with the **✕** next to their name.

A table never goes over its seats. If two people try to fill the last seat at the same time, the second is told the table is full.

### Auto-seat

<Steps>
1. Click **Auto-seat**.
2. Choose how to seat people:
   - **Keep each group order together**: people registered on the same order sit at the same table.
   - **Keep each company together (sponsor table first)**: people from the same company sit together, and a company goes to the table it sponsors first.
   - **Fill tables in name order**: fills tables in turn, by last name.
3. Click **Preview** to see how many will be seated and how many will not fit.
4. Click **Seat** to seat them.
</Steps>

Auto-seat only seats people who are still unassigned. Anyone you already seated stays where they are. When a group is bigger than any table's open seats, it is split over the tables with the most room, and the preview tells you so.

## Who drops out

Only confirmed and checked-in registrants are seated. When a registration is voided, it loses its seat. When a registration is canceled, or no longer holds the plan's ticket or add-on, its seat is freed. It is not shown or printed, and the page tells you how many seats this applies to.

## Print table tents, place cards and lists

Click **Print** and choose:

| Output | What prints |
| --- | --- |
| **Table tents** | One tent per table, on **Letter** or **A4** landscape. Fold the sheet in half along the dashed line. The number, name, sponsor and host face both sides of the table. |
| **Place cards** | One card per seated guest, with their name, organization and table. Choose the card stock first, next to the **Print** button: **Folded place cards, 4 per Letter sheet**, flat cards on **Avery 5392** (4 × 3 in, 6 per sheet), or flat **4 × 3 in** cards one per page. |
| **Seating chart** | Every table and its guests on landscape pages, for the door or the banquet captain. |
| **Per-table lists (PDF)** | One page per table, with numbered seats, names, organizations and registrant types. |
| **Per-table list (CSV)** | One row per seated guest with the table number, table, host, sponsor and seat, for a spreadsheet or your caterer. Tables with nobody seated are listed too. |

<Tip>
Print one table tent on plain paper first, and check that it folds the right way and the text lines up, before you print the whole room on card stock.
</Tip>

Flat place cards use the same card stocks as [Badges](/registration/badges).

## Show registrants their table

When a registrant is seated, their table and seat appear on the **Home** tab of their attendee portal, with the sponsor and host. To keep the seating private until you are ready, switch off **Show each registrant their table in the attendee portal** on the plan.

<Note>
Seating is part of Registration, which is in Early Access. Ask your account manager to turn it on for your event.
</Note>
