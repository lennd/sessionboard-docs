An event can run several registration forms at once. Each form has its own link, opening and closing dates, who may use it, which tickets it sells and what it asks. Registrants who open your registration page choose the form that fits them.

## Why use it

Attendees, presenters and press do not register the same way. Presenters should not pay the public price, press credentials need a check before they are confirmed, and a hospital buying ten seats does not know every name yet. Separate forms let each group register on its own terms without one long form full of conditions — and every form still writes to the same registrants list, orders and reports.

## Create a form

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Forms">
Go to **Registration → Forms** and click **New form**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a starting point">
Each starting point sets sensible defaults you can change later:

![The New form menu listing Attendee registration, Group registration, Presenter registration, Press & media credentials, Exhibitor staff and Custom form](/images/kb/registration-new-form-menu.png)

| Starting point | Starts as |
| --- | --- |
| **Attendee registration** | Anyone can register and pay. |
| **Group registration** | Buy several seats, name people now or later. |
| **Presenter registration** | Opened with an invitation code. |
| **Press & media credentials** | Approval before confirmation. |
| **Exhibitor staff** | Booth staff, drawn from each exhibitor's allotment. |
| **Custom form** | Start blank. |

New forms start as **Draft**, so nobody can register until you open them.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit and save">
The form editor opens. Work through the tabs on the left and click **Save** at the top. **Discard** throws away unsaved changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**New form → Import from another event** copies a form from another of your organization's events, as a draft: its questions, rules, pages, languages and page. Tickets and registrant types are matched by name; anything this event does not have is left off, and you are told which.
</Tip>

The **Forms** list shows each form's status — **Open**, **Upcoming** (open, but its window has not started), **Draft** or **Closed** — with badges for **Invitation code**, **Hidden**, **Approval** and its languages, and how many registrations it has. Use the tabs at the top to filter by status. Under each form's name is its public link. The **⋯** menu on a form has **Open registration** / **Close registration**, **Copy link**, **Duplicate**, **See registrants** and **Archive**. Archiving stops a form taking registrations; existing registrations keep it.

![The Forms list with status tabs and three forms marked Open, Upcoming, Invitation code and Approval](/images/kb/registration-forms-list.png)

## Details

- **Name** and **Link** — the link is the last part of the registration page address.
- **Audience** — Attendees, Groups, Presenters, Press, Exhibitor staff or Custom.
- **Status** — **Draft — not taking registrations**, **Open — within its window** or **Closed**.
- **Window** — **Opens** and **Closes**, in the event's time zone. Leave them blank to rely on the status alone. A press form can open weeks after the attendee form.

### Who can register

**Access** decides who can use the form:

| Access | Who can register |
| --- | --- |
| **Anyone with the link** | Everyone. |
| **With an invitation code** | People who type the **Invitation code**, or open a link that carries it. |
| **Only people on the invite list** | Only email addresses on the event's invite list, such as people invited from [Sales & invitations](/registration/sales-and-invitations). Everyone else is refused at checkout. |
| **Hidden (admins only)** | Nobody on the public page. Staff can still register people on it from **Registrants → Add registration**. |

**Close at this many registrations** closes the form by itself once that many people are registered on it (canceled, declined and waitlisted registrations do not count). The public page then says the form is full. Leave it blank for no limit.

**Require approval** holds every registration on the form in Approvals until someone approves it — for press credentials or trainee verification. See [Approve registrations](/registration/approvals).

![The form editor's Details tab with the section list on the left and the Form, Window and Who can register panels](/images/kb/registration-form-editor-details.png)

### Share

**Share** shows the form's link with **Copy** and share buttons for LinkedIn, X, Facebook and email. When your Site has a custom domain, the link — and every registration link in emails — uses it.

## Tickets

- **Tickets on this form** — tick the tickets this form sells. Leave all unticked to offer every active ticket. Prices and tiers are set in [Tickets & pricing](/registration/tickets-and-pricing).
- **Registrant types** — which types this form registers. Leave all unticked for every type.
- **Add-ons step** — offer add-ons (meals, guest passes, donations) before payment.
- **Session picks** — registrants choose sessions and workshops from the Program. Set **Most sessions a person can pick**, or leave it blank for no limit. Each pick is written to the person's profile.
- **Group seats** (group and exhibitor staff forms) — **Fewest seats**, **Most seats**, and **Name people later**, which lets the buyer assign seats from their portal. Each named person gets their own confirmation.

### Waitlist

- **Waitlist when a ticket is full** — people join the waitlist instead of being turned away. **Move people up automatically** gives a freed place to the next person; off, you offer places from **Registrants**.
- **Hold an offered place for (hours)** — a place that opens is held for that person for this long. They pay from the email (card, through your gateway) or accept a free place; otherwise the place goes to the next person. Leave it blank to confirm free places straight away.
- **Waitlist paid tickets too** — paid tickets can be waitlisted as well. Nobody is charged on the waitlist; they pay when they take a place. Needs a hold time.
- **Waitlist when the form closes** — a closed or full form asks for a name and email instead. **Offer a place** to someone on that list sends them a personal link that lets them register while the hold lasts.

![The form editor's Tickets tab with tickets to offer, registrant types and the Add-ons step and Session picks switches](/images/kb/registration-form-editor-tickets.png)

## Questions

The **Questions** tab uses the same form builder as the rest of Sessionboard, over the fields of the event's Attendees module. Add fields, group them into sections, mark them required, and add a rule so a question shows only after a certain answer. **Attendee fields** opens the field library if you need a new field.

Name and email are always asked.

![The form editor's Questions tab with locked name and email fields and additional attendee fields](/images/kb/registration-form-editor-questions.png)

Questions can be any attendee field type, including **date**, **number**, **email**, **website**, **phone**, **country** and **file upload**. A file upload takes a PDF, an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP) or an Office document up to 10 MB.

### Pages

Under **Pages**, click **Add page** to split the questions over several pages; registrants move through them page by page. Choose each question's page under **Questions by page** — questions without one are on the first page, after name and email. From the second page on, **Show this page only when** skips a page unless an answer, the registrant type or the member status matches. A skipped page's questions are not asked and not required.

### Who answers, and when

**Who answers, and when** lists every question's rule as a sentence, such as "Shown when Dietary needs is Vegan." For each question:

- **Everyone**, **First registrant only** (asked once per order, of the person who registers) or **Staff only** (never on the public form; your team fills it in from the registrant's drawer).
- A rule on the **Registrant type**, **Ticket**, **Member status**, an **Add-on in the order** or a **Group order**. Rules on an earlier answer are set in the builder's rules. A question that depends on an add-on is asked on the add-ons step.

### Import questions from a CSV

**Import questions (CSV)** adds many questions at once. Columns: `label`, `type` (text, textarea, select, multiselect, checkbox, date, number, email, url, phone, file, country), `options` (separated by `|`, or `list:Name` for a choice list), `required` (yes/no), `help`, `section` and `page`. Click **Check** to see what will be added and which rows are skipped and why, then **Add**. Questions this event does not have yet are created as attendee fields; pages named in the CSV are created. Save the form first.

### Choice lists

Lists you reuse — specialties, shirt sizes — are kept under **Registration → Settings → Choice lists**. On the **Questions** tab, **Choice lists** adds a list's choices to one of the event's own choice questions. Choices already on the question stay, so earlier answers keep their meaning.

### Preview as a registrant type

**Preview as** shows, page by page, the questions one registrant type is asked — using the same rules the checkout enforces. It shows saved changes.

Under **Interest questions**, tick the choice questions (topics, specialties) whose answers should become interests on the person's profile. Pathways and Marketing use them to recommend sessions and content.

## Payment

**How people pay** sets the ways to pay on this form. Free tickets skip payment.

- **Card** — paid at checkout in your gateway's secure field.
- **Pay later by invoice** — confirms the registration and emails an invoice with a payment link.
- **Deposit now, balance later** — the deposit percentage and installments come from **Settings → Invoices & deposits**.

Invoice and deposit options need **Allow pay later by invoice** on in [Registration settings](/registration/payments#invoices-and-deposits).

![The How people pay panel with Card, Pay later by invoice and Deposit now, balance later switches](/images/kb/registration-form-editor-payment.png)

## Emails

Every registration gets a confirmation with the QR code, wallet pass and calendar file; registrations waiting for approval get a pending notice. Forms use the event's registration emails — see [Registration emails and reminders](/registration/emails-and-reminders). **Confirmation template** picks a different template for this form; leave it blank for the event default.

## Languages

Tick the languages this form offers — over 30, from Spanish and German to Japanese, Arabic and Hebrew. English is always on. The checkout follows the registrant's language, and they can switch it on the page; their emails and receipt follow it too.

Under **Translations**, pick a language and translate the form name, headline, introduction, page titles, each question, its help text and its choices. Anything left blank falls back to English.

Each language has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Typed by your team, not yet checked. |
| **Machine draft — check it** | Filled by **Draft with machine translation**. |
| **Reviewed — live** | Checked by an admin. Only reviewed languages are offered to registrants. |

**Draft with machine translation** fills the blank texts of the language and leaves what your team wrote. It is a starting point: read every text, fix it, then click **Mark reviewed**. Editing a reviewed language makes it a draft again until it is reviewed. Save the form before drafting.

<Note>
The fixed words of the checkout (buttons, step names) are translated for English, Spanish, French, Arabic, German and Portuguese; other languages show your translated texts with English buttons.
</Note>

![The Languages tab with English and Spanish ticked and translation fields for the form name and each question](/images/kb/registration-form-editor-languages.png)

## Page

**Registration page** sets what people see above the form: **Headline**, **Introduction**, **Header image URL** and an **Accent** color. A preview shows the result. Everything else follows the event's brand, or the look set under **Settings → Form branding**.

![The Registration page panel with headline, introduction, header image, accent color and a preview](/images/kb/registration-form-editor-page.png)

### Tracking

Under **Tracking** on the **Page** tab, add your **Google Analytics 4 measurement ID**, **Google Tag Manager container ID**, **Meta pixel ID** or **LinkedIn Insight Tag partner ID**. The form loads those tags, and a completed registration sends a purchase / conversion event with the order total. Each ID is checked against its format.

## Share the form

The registration page lives on the event's Site. Registrants who open it see every open form as a card and choose one. Links carry options through to the form:

- `?code=` fills an invitation code.
- `?promo=` applies a promo code.
- `?lang=` opens the form in one of its languages.
- `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term` and `utm_content` are saved on the order and shown on it in **Orders**.

The registration page shows the venue with a map link, and the confirmation page has share buttons so registrants can invite colleagues.

</Tip>
