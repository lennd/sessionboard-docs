**Registration → Reports** has the reports a registration team asks for every week, ready to run, and the way into the [Report Builder](/reporting/report-builder) for anything else. Every report runs on your live registrations, downloads as CSV or Excel, and can be changed, saved for your team, shared and emailed on a schedule.

## Why use it

Registration questions rarely wait: how many have paid, who is still on the waitlist, which workshops are full, who has not checked in, what people answered about dietary needs. A ready-made report answers each one in a click, from the same registrations the desk and finance work from, so the numbers agree.

## Run a report

<Steps>
1. Go to **Registration → Reports**.
2. Find the report under its section, or type in **Search reports**.
3. Click the report. It opens with its results, 50 rows to a page.
4. Click **CSV** or **Excel** to download the whole report.
</Steps>

Reports with dates (transactions, failed payments, the GL summary) take a **From** and **To** date.

Downloads are safe to open in a spreadsheet: a cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is written as text, so it never runs as a formula.

## The ready-made reports

| Section | Reports |
| --- | --- |
| **Registrations** | All registrants; registrations by status, by ticket, by registrant type, by form and by day; registrations by source (channel, campaign link and UTM source). |
| **Revenue and payments** | Revenue by ticket and add-on; money in by day; payments by method; orders; orders with a balance due; failed payments. |
| **Refunds and credits** | Refunds (gateway and offline); credits issued toward a future event; transactions with GL. |
| **Invoices and receivables** | Open invoices with days overdue; receivables aging; purchase orders. |
| **Workshops and sessions** | Workshop rosters; workshop seats and waitlists. |
| **Check-in and attendance** | Checked in; not checked in (no-shows); check-ins by day; session attendance; session check-in log. |
| **Badges** | Badges printed; badge prints and reprints. |
| **Promo and access codes** | Code usage (uses, orders, discount given, revenue); registrations by promo code; promo code revenue. |
| **Waitlists** | Event waitlist; workshop waitlists. |
| **Approvals** | Waiting for approval; declined registrations with the reason. |
| **Members** | Members vs non-members; members registered, with their member id. |
| **Answers and demographics** | Every answer to every registration question; answer breakdown (how many gave each answer); registrants by job title; registrants by company. |
| **Exhibitor staff** | Exhibitor and sponsor staff; staff per company. |
| **Seating** | Seating chart: table, seat, host and sponsor. |
| **SMS and notifications** | SMS delivery by kind and status; SMS that did not deliver; staff notification delivery. |
| **Cancellations and transfers** | Cancellations; transfers and substitutions (also moves to another event) staff made, with when and by whom; cancellations and transfers by day. |
| **Finance** | Gateway reconciliation; GL summary. |

The finance reports (transactions, failed payments, reconciliation, receivables aging, purchase orders, promo code revenue and the GL summary) are the same reports as **Registration → Orders → Finance reports**. See [Orders & invoices](/registration/orders-and-invoices#finance-reports).

<Note>
Voided registrations and orders (test and duplicate registrations staff voided) are left out of every report. A registrant whose personal details were removed for privacy still counts, with empty name and email. The transfer reports list the changes staff made; a hand-off a registrant makes in their own attendee portal shows only as the new holder on the registration.
</Note>

## Change a report, or build your own

Any ready-made report is a starting point. Open it and click **Customize, save or schedule**: the Report Builder opens with that report already in it. To start from scratch, click **Build a report**.

In the builder you choose what the report is about, then its columns, filters, grouping and sort order. For registration data, choose one of these:

| Report on | One row per |
| --- | --- |
| **Registrations** | registrant: status, ticket, registrant type, form, source and UTM, check-in, membership, exhibitor staff, badges printed, and their order's total, paid and balance |
| **Registration orders** | order: purchaser, payment method, promo code, PO number, subtotal, discount, tax, total, paid and balance |
| **Registration order lines** | ticket, add-on, workshop fee or donation bought, with quantity and amount |
| **Registration payments** | charge or refund, with method, status and the gateway's reason for a failure |
| **Registration invoices** | invoice, with due date, days overdue and balance |
| **Registration credits** | credit issued from the event |
| **Registration promo codes** | code, with uses, orders, discount given and revenue |
| **Registration session picks** | workshop or session seat, or waitlist place |
| **Registration session check-ins** | scan in or out at a session door |
| **Registration badge prints** | badge print or reprint |
| **Registration answers** | answer to a registration question |
| **Registration seats** | seat at a table |
| **Registration SMS messages** | text sent to a registrant |
| **Registration notifications** | alert sent to staff |
| **Registration changes** | change staff made to a registration: transfer, substitution, move, cancellation, void, merge, approval or decline, with when and by whom |

Then:

- **Save** the report. Saved reports appear under **Saved reports** on **Registration → Reports**, and under **Reports → Custom reports**, for everyone on the event team.
- **Share** a link to it, with an expiry date or a password if you like. A report that hides personal details also hides question answers, which can be personal.
- **Schedule** it: the people you choose get an email on the days and at the time you set, with a link to view the report and download it as CSV or Excel.

See [Build a report](/reporting/report-builder) and [Custom reports](/reporting/custom-reports) for the builder itself.

<Tip>
Group by a column and add a count or a sum to turn a list into a summary. For example, report on **Registration answers**, filter **Question** to your dietary question, group by **Answer** and count.
</Tip>

## Who can see what

Reports follow the event's [Registration permissions](/registration/roles-and-permissions):

- **Can view registrations and orders** is needed for any registration report.
- **Can view finance reports** is also needed for money: revenue, payments, refunds, credits, invoices, balances, and the finance reports. A money column in a custom report needs it too.

A report you cannot run is shown with a lock and the permission it needs. The same rule applies everywhere the report runs. A shared link, a shared dashboard and a scheduled email use the permissions of the person who made them; a dashboard on the event uses the permissions of whoever is looking at it.

The Report Builder is part of **Reports**, so building and saving your own reports also needs access to **Reports** on the event.

<Note>
Registration reports appear only on events with Registration. On other events, Reports looks exactly as before.
</Note>
