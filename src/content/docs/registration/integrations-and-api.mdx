**Registration → Integrations & API** shows where registration data goes and what comes back: installed apps, webhooks, Zapier, API tokens with registration access, and the latest changes other systems made through the Public API.

## Why use it

Registration rarely lives alone. Your CRM wants new registrants, your housing partner wants arrivals, and a badge or lead-retrieval partner wants the attendee list and sends scans back. This page puts every connection in one place, so you can see what has access to registration data and confirm a sync is working.

## Where registrations can go

| Destination | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Apps** | Registration, check-in, badge, scanning and lead-retrieval partners in the **Onsite & Expo** category. Installed apps receive registrations and send check-ins, scans and leads back. |
| **Webhooks** | Post registration, order and check-in events to your own endpoint as they happen. See [Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks). |
| **Zapier** | Send new registrations to a spreadsheet, CRM or chat without code. See [Zapier](/integrations/zapier). |
| **Public API and MCP** | Organization tokens for the Public API and the Sessionboard MCP server. See [API tokens](/integrations/api-tokens). |
| **API log** | Every Public API call and integration push, with status and payload. |
| **Housing** | Send registrants to your housing provider (Passkey, Orchid, onPeak or your own booking page) with their details filled in, and record who booked. See [Hotel booking](/registration/housing). |

## Registrations held in another platform

If registrations for your event are held in another platform, connect it in **Apps**, map it to this event, and turn on its attendee sync in the event's settings for that app. Its registrations then come into Sessionboard as attendees of this event. They are never added to your contacts.

Today **LiveBuzz** and **Visit** have the attendee sync in **Apps**. **Cvent**, **Swoogo**, **Bizzabo**, **Swapcard** and **Expo Platform** do not bring attendees yet: their integrations sync your program only, so they are not listed under **Who counts as registered**. Until they do, [import those registrants from a CSV](/registration/registrants#import-from-a-csv).

**Who counts as registered** decides whose registration opens:

- the event app sign-in;
- the attendee portal;
- Content Gallery items set to **Registrants only**.

| Choice | Who gets in |
| --- | --- |
| **Every confirmed registration, wherever it came from** | Anyone with a confirmed registration, whether it was made in Sessionboard or synced from a connected app. This is the default, and how the event worked before. |
| **Only the sources I choose** | Tick **Sessionboard registrations**, one or more connected apps, or both. For a connected app you can also tick some of its registrant types, for example only Cvent's **Member** registrations. With no types ticked, every type counts. |

People are matched by the email on their registration. In the event app, **Registration ID** sign-in also accepts the other platform's registrant ID, badge number or confirmation number. A canceled registration never counts.

A registration from another platform shows in the attendee portal read-only, with a note to make changes in that platform. It cannot be canceled, sent to a colleague or given add-ons in Sessionboard, because the next sync would undo the change.

<Note>
The panel lists the registration platforms that have no attendee sync yet, such as Maritz RTS, Stova, Grip, Eventbrite, RainFocus and Brella. Registrations held there cannot count until a sync exists. Until then, [import the registrants from a CSV](/registration/registrants#import-from-a-csv).
</Note>

## Your CRM

The **CRM** panel uses your organization's CRM connection (connect HubSpot in **Apps**). Each registration goes to the event's marketing event in the CRM as registered, attended or canceled. Registrants who opted in to marketing also get their ticket, registrant type, registration status, amount and member id on their contact, in the **Sessionboard** property group. The panel shows how many registrations were sent, the last sync and any errors; **Sync now** sends them all again. Turn off **Send this event's registrations to the CRM** to stop it for this event.

<Note>
Registrations are sent to HubSpot. A Salesforce connection is shown as not available on this panel and receives no registrations.
</Note>

## Public API scopes

Grant a token only the scopes a system needs. In **Organization Settings › API Tokens › Create Token**, `read:registrations` is under **Opt-in**: it is never included by default, so tick it for a token that reads registration data. To add `write:registrations`, `read:expo` or `write:expo` to a token, contact Sessionboard support.

| Scope | Gives access to |
| --- | --- |
| `read:registrations` | Forms, ticket types, registrations, orders, payment records, membership, attendees and check-ins, the registration summary, and read-only setup: capacity pools, fee groups, donation funds, workshop rules and the badge print queue |
| `write:registrations` | Create and update registrations and orders from your own systems |
| `read:expo` | Booths and sponsorships |
| `write:expo` | Update booths and sponsorship orders |

![The Public API scopes panel listing read and write scopes for registrations and Expo](/images/kb/registration-integrations-scopes.png)

Registration and Expo endpoints read and write the same records as the admin, and every write is in History. The developer guide is [Registration and Expo in the Public API](https://sessionboard.mintlify.app/guides/registration-and-expo).

## What other systems can read

With `read:registrations`, a connected system can also read:

- **The registration summary**: the numbers on the Overview page (registrations by status and by day, per ticket type, attendee type and form, revenue, orders by status, invoices still owed).
- **Payment records**: card and bank payments made through your payment gateway, refunds, failed attempts with their reason, and money recorded offline. Card numbers, payment tokens and gateway credentials are never shared; a record shows the card brand and last four digits only when your gateway reported them.
- **Membership**: whether an email is a member, lapsed or neither, checked the same way your registration form checks it, and the membership each attendee registered with.
- **Pricing and workshop setup**: shared capacity pools, fee groups and the promo codes limited to them, donation funds with what each has raised, workshop picking rules, and the badge print queue. These are read-only through the API; change them on the Registration pages.

## Ask an AI assistant

The [Sessionboard MCP server](https://sessionboard.mintlify.app/insights/mcp-server) lets Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants answer registration questions from the same data, for example "How many people registered last week?", "Which invoices are overdue?" or "Is this person a member, and are they registered?". Its registration tools only read; they cannot place orders, refund or change settings. The token needs `read:registrations`, and the event must have Registration turned on.

## See what is connected

- **API tokens for this event** — organization tokens with registration or Expo scopes, whether they reach every event or this one, and their scopes. **API docs** opens the developer guide.
- **Webhooks and onsite** — whether webhooks are set up for the event and which events they send, and which onsite jobs a partner runs. **Change** opens [Do it yourself or a partner](/onsite/diy-or-partner).
- **Latest Public API writes** — the last 50 changes other systems made to this event's registration.
