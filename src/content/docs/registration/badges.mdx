**Registration → Badges** is where you design badges, print them in batches ahead of the event, and see every print and reprint. The same designs print at the check-in desk.

## Why use it

Badge printing is where registration data meets the venue. Designing the badge where the registrations live means the name, organization and ribbon on it are always current, and the QR code on every printed badge works at the desk, at session doors and in exhibitors' lead scanners — no second badge system to sync.

## Design a badge

<Steps>
<Step title="Start a design">
Click **New badge**, or pick an existing design under **Designs**. A new badge is saved as a design of its own beside the existing ones, so you can keep one design per registrant type.
</Step>
<Step title="Set it up">
Under **Settings**, give it a **Name**, pick the **Stock**, tick which registrant types it is **Used for** (none ticked means every type without its own design), and optionally make it the **Default design** or add a **Background image URL**.
</Step>
<Step title="Add elements">
Click **Text**, **QR code** or **Ribbon**, then drag elements into place. The preview shows sample names so you can see how a long name fits.
</Step>
<Step title="Style each element">
Select an element to choose what it **Shows** (first name, last name, full name, organization, job title, registrant type, ticket, badge number, or fixed text) and its font size, weight, alignment, color, width and height. A ribbon has its own **Ribbon color**; make one design per registrant type for different colors.
</Step>
<Step title="Show an element only on some badges (optional)">
Under **When it prints**, tick **Only on some badges** and pick a rule: **Registrant type**, **Ticket**, **Organization**, **Job title** or **Badge number**, then **is**, **is not**, **is one of**, **is none of**, **is filled in** or **is empty**. For example, a "Speaker" ribbon that prints only when Registrant type is Speaker. Rules are not case-sensitive, and an element with a rule shows **If** in the preview.
</Step>
<Step title="Check and save">
**PDF preview** opens the badge exactly as the printer gets it. Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Double-sided badges

Turn on **Double-sided** under **Settings** so a badge reads the same whichever way it turns on the lanyard. Under **Back**, choose:

- **Same as the front** — every badge prints its front twice.
- **Its own design** — switch between **Front** and **Back** above the preview to design each side (for example, the Wi-Fi details or a schedule on the back).

On single-badge stocks the PDF has the front then the back of each badge. On sheet stocks each front sheet is followed by its back sheet, with the columns mirrored, so each back lines up behind its front when you print two-sided and flip on the long edge.

### Badge stocks

- **4 × 3 in (badge holder)**
- **4 × 6 in (fold or lanyard)**
- **CR80 card (3.375 × 2.125 in)**
- **Avery 5392 (4 × 3 in, 6 per sheet)**
- **Letter, 2 per page (4 × 6 in)**

The QR code is the same one in the confirmation email and wallet pass, so a printed badge checks people in at the desk, scans them into sessions, and works in the exhibitor lead scanner.

### Names in any language

Names and organizations print in the registrant's own script: Arabic and Hebrew right to left with their letters joined, Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and accented Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters such as Ł, ő or Ж. A name that mixes scripts, such as a Hebrew name with a Latin title, prints in reading order. Text in these scripts is drawn as shapes in the PDF, so you can print it but not select or search it in a PDF viewer.

## Print a batch

Under **Print a batch**, choose a **Registrant type** (or **Everyone**), leave **Only badges not printed yet** ticked for a top-up run, choose the **Order** the badges come off the printer (**Last name**, **First name**, **Organization**, **Registrant type**, **Ticket** or **Registration date**), and click **Print badges**. ![The badge Settings panel with stock and registrant types, and the Print a batch panel](/images/kb/registration-badge-settings-print.png)

Each PDF holds up to 500 badges for confirmed registrants. While **Hold badges for unpaid balances** is on in [Check-in & badges](/onsite/checkin-and-badges), a batch leaves out everyone with a balance due and the confirmation says how many it left out. To print a few people, tick them in [Registrants](/registration/registrants) and click **Print badges** there.

## The print log

![The badge Print log listing who was printed, where and whether it was a reprint](/images/kb/registration-badge-print-log.png)

**Print log** lists admin batches, desk prints and app prints, with reprints marked. Every print is also written to the person's profile, and the Registrants list shows **Printed** with a count.

Printing at the door happens at the check-in desk. See [Run the web check-in desk](/onsite/web-desk).
