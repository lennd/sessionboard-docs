**Registration → Registrants** lists every registration for the event, from every form. Open a person to see what they bought and answered, and to act on their registration.

## Why use it

Registration questions land on your team one at a time: a speaker's colleague is taking their place, someone never got their confirmation, a VIP needs a comp. Registrants puts every person, their status and their order in one list, so each of those is a two-click fix instead of a support ticket to a registration vendor.

## Get notified about registrations

Your team can get an email or a Slack message when registrations change. Set this up in **Registration → Settings → Notifications**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a rule">
Click **Add rule**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the changes">
Under **When**, tick the changes to hear about: **New registration**, **Registration changed**, **Cancelled or declined**, **Voided or merged**, **Added to the waitlist** and **Waiting for approval**.
</Step>
<Step title="Narrow it (optional)">
Tick **Only these tickets**, **Only these registrant types** or **Only these forms**. Leave them empty to cover every registration.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose where it goes">
Add one or more **Email addresses**, separated by commas, and tick any **Slack channels**. Slack channels appear once your organization connects Slack in **Apps**. Each address and channel has its own **Test** button.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose when">
Pick **Delivery**: **Right away**, or **Daily digest** for one summary a day at the hour you pick (in the event's time zone).
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Click **Save notifications**.
</Step>
</Steps>

A registration counts as new once it is confirmed, so an unpaid card checkout is not announced until it is paid. Each change is sent once to each address or channel, even when two rules match it. Messages show the registrant, ticket, registrant type, form and order total, with a link to the registrant. They never include card details. **Recent deliveries**, under the rules, lists the last 25 messages sent and any that failed. A **Test** shows its result next to its button (sent, or why it was not). Test messages are not listed under **Recent deliveries**.

## Find registrants

Search by first, last or full name, email, company or member id, and filter by **Status**, **Form** and **Registrant type**. Each row shows the person's ticket, type, form, status, whether their badge is printed, and when they registered.

![The Registrants list filtered by a search, with ticket, type, form, status, badge and registration date per person](/images/kb/registration-registrants-list.png)

### Registration statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Registered** | Confirmed — they have a confirmation, QR code and portal link. |
| **Checked in** | Checked in onsite. |
| **Pending approval** | Waiting for a decision in Approvals. |
| **Waitlisted** | Waiting for a seat to free up. |
| **Incomplete** | Started a paid checkout and has not finished it. |
| **Canceled** | Canceled by the registrant or your team. |
| **Transferred** | Handed to someone else, who has their own registration. |
| **Declined** | Not approved. |
| **No show** | Registered but did not attend. |
| **Voided** | A test or duplicate your team voided, or merged into another registration. Hidden from the list unless you filter by **Voided**. |

## Open a registrant

Click a name to open the drawer: ticket, registrant type, form, organization, when they registered and checked in, where the registration came from, their answers (an uploaded file is a link that opens it), their **Housing** booking when your [housing provider](/registration/housing) has sent one, whether they registered on a form or were added by staff, and, under **Text messages**, the mobile number they opted in to texts with, the consent text they agreed to and when. From there:

- **Edit** — change their details, answers and ticket. A follow-up question shows only while the answer it depends on applies (for example, a question asked only when **Country** is Argentina appears once Argentina is picked). A ticket picked here keeps the order's price; to charge or refund the difference, use **Change ticket**. A full ticket, or one in a full [shared capacity pool](/registration/tickets-and-pricing#shared-capacity), is refused with "… is sold out." and the registration keeps its ticket. A canceled or declined registration holds no seat, so its ticket can always be changed.
- **Resend confirmation** — sends the confirmation email again.
- **Print badge** — opens their badge as a PDF and logs the print.
- **Transfer** — moves the registration to someone else. Enter the new person's email and name. They keep the same ticket and order and get their own confirmation; the previous holder's profile details are cleared.
- **Cancel** — see below.
- **Void** and **Merge duplicate** — for test and duplicate registrations. See [Void or merge a duplicate](#void-or-merge-a-duplicate).
- **View as registrant** — opens their attendee portal as they see it, read-only. See [View as a registrant](#view-as-a-registrant).
- **Open the order** — goes to the order in [Orders](/registration/orders-and-invoices).
- **Change ticket** — moves them to another ticket. Pick the new one to see the difference first. If it costs more, the difference is added to the order and its invoice; charge it from the order. If it costs less, choose how the difference goes back: to the card, recorded as a check or other refund, or as a credit toward a future event.
- **Substitute (with fee)** — like **Transfer**, with the event's substitution fee. The fee is invoiced as its own order on the registration, or tick **Waive the fee**. Staff can substitute after the cut-off.
- **Move to another event** — moves the registration to another event in your organization with Registration turned on. Enter the **Event ID** and pick the ticket there. It is canceled here, and what it paid becomes a credit that pays the new registration. If it paid more than the new ticket costs, the rest stays a credit for the registrant. If it paid less, the difference is owed on the new order.

![A registrant's drawer with ticket, type, form and actions, and Approve and Decline for a registration waiting for approval](/images/kb/registration-registrant-drawer.png)

Someone on the waitlist shows **Offer a place**. The place is held for them for the form's hold time (or the hours you type) and they get an email to pay or accept it; if they do not in time, it goes to the next person. Until they accept or pay, their attendee portal shows **Place offered**, not Confirmed. For someone on a closed form's waitlist, **Copy their registration link** gives you the personal link the email carries.

A registration waiting for approval shows **Approve** and **Decline**, and **Open in Approvals** when your event uses the Approvals queue. Approving sends the confirmation.

### Cancel a registration

**Cancel** releases the spot and emails the registrant **Registration canceled**; untick **Email the registrant that their registration was canceled** to cancel quietly. A paid order is not refunded automatically. Refund it from **Orders**, or cancel it there with **Cancel under the policy**, which applies the event's [cancellation policy](/registration/payments#cancellation-policy). When a capped ticket frees up, the first person waiting on it is confirmed and emailed.

## Void or merge a duplicate

Use these for test registrations and for the same person registered twice. Both need permission to delete contacts on the event; a reporting-only team member does not see them work.

### Void a registration

**Void** takes a registration out of the event without deleting it. Add a reason if you like (for example, *Test registration*), then click **Void registration**.

A voided registration:

- leaves every count and goal on the Overview, the funnel, form and ticket capacity, session seats, check-in search, badge printing and the Registrants list;
- gets no more emails, and its portal link, event-app sign-in and badge QR code stop working;
- has its queued badges at print stations canceled;
- keeps its orders, payments and History. Filter the list by **Voided** to find it; the drawer shows when and why it was voided.

Orders that only the voided registration is on leave the finance reports, statements and revenue, and stop any payment plan. A group order stays in the reports while anyone else on it is still registered.

<Warning>
Voiding never refunds. If the voided registration paid with a real card, refund the order from **Orders** first. After a void, the message tells you when money is still on its order.
</Warning>

### Merge a duplicate

Open the registration you do **not** want to keep and click **Merge duplicate**. Search for the registration to keep and pick it. **What moves** lists exactly what the merge will move to it:

- its orders and their payments. The registration you keep takes over the order when it has none of its own; otherwise the orders are listed with it. Nothing is charged or refunded, and the payments stay on their orders. Add-ons the duplicate bought count for the registration you keep, so questions that only show for those add-ons stay on its form and in its attendee portal;
- answers to questions the kept registration has not answered (its own answers stay);
- workshop picks and session seats, check-in and session scans;
- staff notes, badge print history, credits issued from it, saved payment methods, and the member id and membership.

Click **Merge and void this one**. The duplicate is voided and points to the registration it merged into; the drawer then opens the registration you kept. History records the merge on both.

## View as a registrant

**View as registrant** opens a person's attendee portal in a new tab, exactly as they see it, so you can check what they were sent, what they owe or which workshops they picked before you answer them.

- It is **read-only**. A banner says whose portal it is and that nothing can be changed, every button is off, and Sessionboard refuses any change made from it.
- Every view is logged in History: who opened it, when and for which registrant.
- The link lasts 30 minutes. Open it again from the drawer for more time.

You need the event permission **Can view portal as other users**, and the event needs a published portal Site. A voided registration has no portal to view.

## Add a registration by hand

For phone-ins, VIPs and comps, click **Add registration**. Enter the person's email, name and organization, pick the **Form**, the **Ticket** and optionally a **Registrant type**, tick any **Add-ons** to sell with the ticket, and choose **Payment**:

- **Complimentary**
- **Send an invoice**
- **Purchase order (invoice)** — enter the **PO number**. The registration is confirmed now, the PO number is printed on the invoice, and you record the payment when it arrives.
- **Card — charge it next** — the order opens in **Orders** so you can click **Charge a card** and enter the card in your gateway's secure window.
- **Paid in cash**
- **Paid by check** — with the **Check number**.
- **Paid by money order** — with the **Money order number**.

Add a **Note on the order** if you like. They get the same confirmation, QR code and portal link as everyone else. Untick **Send the confirmation email** to register them without an email, for example when you will send your own.

## Act on many at once

Tick rows, or tick the header box for everyone on the page. The bar that appears offers **Resend confirmation**, **Print badges** and, under **More**, **Approve**, **Move to waitlist**, **Check in**, **Undo check-in** and **Cancel registrations**. ![The bulk action bar with Resend confirmation, Print badges and the More menu open on Approve, Move to waitlist, Check in, Undo check-in and Cancel registrations](/images/kb/registration-registrants-bulk-actions.png)

The result says how many were updated and how many were skipped because the change did not apply to them. **Check in** skips anyone who is not confirmed, such as people waiting for approval or on the waitlist. **Undo check-in** returns each person to the status they had before they were checked in.

### Decline with a reason

To decline several people at once, tick them and choose **More → Decline with a reason…**. Pick one of your preset reasons or type one. Each person gets the decline email with that reason, and the reason stays on their registration, shown in the drawer. People who are already canceled or checked in are skipped. Set the presets under **Settings → Approval → Decline reasons**. A single **Decline…** in the drawer opens the same dialog for that one person.

## Staff notes

The drawer has **Staff notes**: notes only your team sees, with who wrote each one and when. The registrant never sees them. Delete a note from its trash button; History keeps a record.

## Transactions

The drawer's **Transactions** section shows everything this person was charged, paid and refunded: the order they are on, add-ons bought for them later, each card payment and refund through your gateway, and payments recorded offline (check, cash). It adds them up to a balance and lists each order's lines. A refund on a canceled or reduced order lowers what the order costs, so it is not counted as owed again: a $50 order paid in full with $40 refunded has a balance of $0, the same as in **Orders**. Take payments and refunds on the order in **Orders**.

## Member id

When membership lookup is on, the member id it matched is saved on the registration and shown in the drawer. Staff can change it in **Edit**, and the list search finds people by it.

## Search all events

**Search all events** searches registrations in every event of your organization that uses Registration, and your uploaded member list, by name, email, company, badge number or member id. Click a result to open that person in their event. Team members limited to some portfolios see only their portfolios' events. Team members added to particular events, rather than to the whole organization, search only the events they are on and do not see the member list.

## Import from a CSV

**Import CSV** offers three imports. Each one runs **Preview** first: it tries every row and shows what would be created, updated, skipped and why a row fails, without saving anything. **Import** then does the same for real.

- **Import registrations**: one row per person. Columns: `email` (required), `first_name`, `last_name`, `company`, `job_title`, `ticket` (name), `registrant_type` (name), `member_id`, and any question by its name. Choose the form, a ticket for rows that name none, and the payment: invoice each registrant, already paid by cash or check, or complimentary. People already registered for the event are skipped, or updated from the file if you choose that. Duplicate emails in the file are skipped. Turn off **Send each new registrant their confirmation** to import quietly.
- **Import comps**: complimentary registrations for speakers, VIPs or staff. Add a **Comp reason**, or a `comp_reason` column per row; the reason is kept on each order.
- **Bulk update**: changes existing registrations. Rows find their registration by `registration_id` or `email`. Only the columns in the file change: names, company, job title, ticket, registrant type, member id, answers, and `checked_in` (`yes`, or a date and time, to check them in with that time; `no` to undo). A row that moves someone onto a full ticket or a full shared pool fails with "… is sold out.", and the rest of the file still goes through.

Importing needs permission to change the event's settings.

<Tip>
**Attendee fields & export** opens the Attendees view under Pathways, where you pick columns and export registrants with their answers.
</Tip>
