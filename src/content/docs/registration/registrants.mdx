**Registration → Registrants** lists every registration for the event, from every form. Open a person to see what they bought and answered, and to act on their registration.

## Why use it

Registration questions land on your team one at a time: a speaker's colleague is taking their place, someone never got their confirmation, a VIP needs a comp. Registrants puts every person, their status and their order in one list, so each of those is a two-click fix instead of a support ticket to a registration vendor.

## Find registrants

Search by name, email, company or member id, and filter by **Status**, **Form** and **Registrant type**. Each row shows the person's ticket, type, form, status, whether their badge is printed, and when they registered.

![The Registrants list filtered by a search, with ticket, type, form, status, badge and registration date per person](/images/kb/registration-registrants-list.png)

### Registration statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Registered** | Confirmed — they have a confirmation, QR code and portal link. |
| **Checked in** | Checked in onsite. |
| **Pending approval** | Waiting for a decision in Approvals. |
| **Waitlisted** | Waiting for a seat to free up. |
| **Incomplete** | Started a paid checkout and has not finished it. |
| **Canceled** | Canceled by the registrant or your team. |
| **Transferred** | Handed to someone else, who has their own registration. |
| **Declined** | Not approved. |
| **No show** | Registered but did not attend. |

## Open a registrant

Click a name to open the drawer: ticket, registrant type, form, organization, when they registered and checked in, where the registration came from, and their answers. From there:

- **Edit** — change their details and answers.
- **Resend confirmation** — sends the confirmation email again.
- **Print badge** — opens their badge as a PDF and logs the print.
- **Transfer** — moves the registration to someone else. Enter the new person's email and name. They keep the same ticket and order and get their own confirmation; the previous holder's profile details are cleared.
- **Cancel** — see below.
- **Open the order** — goes to the order in [Orders](/registration/orders-and-invoices).
- **Change ticket** — moves them to another ticket. Pick the new one to see the difference first. If it costs more, the difference is added to the order and its invoice; charge it from the order. If it costs less, choose how the difference goes back: to the card, recorded as a check or other refund, or as a credit toward a future event.
- **Substitute (with fee)** — like **Transfer**, with the event's substitution fee. The fee is invoiced as its own order on the registration, or tick **Waive the fee**. Staff can substitute after the cut-off.
- **Move to another event** — moves the registration to another event in your organization with Registration turned on. Enter the **Event ID** and pick the ticket there. It is canceled here, and what it paid becomes a credit that pays the new registration. If it paid more than the new ticket costs, the rest stays a credit for the registrant. If it paid less, the difference is owed on the new order.

![A registrant's drawer with ticket, type, form and actions, and Approve and Decline for a registration waiting for approval](/images/kb/registration-registrant-drawer.png)

Someone on the waitlist shows **Offer a place**. The place is held for them for the form's hold time (or the hours you type) and they get an email to pay or accept it; if they do not in time, it goes to the next person. For someone on a closed form's waitlist, **Copy their registration link** gives you the personal link the email carries.

A registration waiting for approval shows **Approve** and **Decline**, and **Open in Approvals** when your event uses the Approvals queue. Approving sends the confirmation.

### Cancel a registration

**Cancel** releases the spot. A paid order is not refunded automatically. Refund it from **Orders**, or cancel it there with **Cancel under the policy**, which applies the event's [cancellation policy](/registration/payments#cancellation-policy). When a capped ticket frees up, the first person waiting on it is confirmed and emailed.

## Add a registration by hand

For phone-ins, VIPs and comps, click **Add registration**. Enter the person's email, name and organization, pick the **Form**, the **Ticket** and optionally a **Registrant type**, and choose **Payment**:

- **Complimentary**
- **Send an invoice**
- **Purchase order (invoice)** — enter the **PO number**. The registration is confirmed now, the PO number is printed on the invoice, and you record the payment when it arrives.
- **Card — charge it next** — the order opens in **Orders** so you can click **Charge a card** and enter the card in your gateway's secure window.
- **Paid in cash**
- **Paid by check** — with the **Check number**.
- **Paid by money order** — with the **Money order number**.

Add a **Note on the order** if you like. They get the same confirmation, QR code and portal link as everyone else. Untick **Send the confirmation email** to register them without an email, for example when you will send your own.

![The Add a registration dialog with email, name, organization, form, payment, ticket and registrant type](/images/kb/registration-add-registration-dialog.png)

## Act on many at once

Tick rows, or tick the header box for everyone on the page. The bar that appears offers **Resend confirmation**, **Print badges** and, under **More**, **Approve**, **Move to waitlist**, **Check in**, **Undo check-in** and **Cancel registrations**. ![The bulk action bar with Resend confirmation, Print badges and the More menu open on Approve, Move to waitlist, Check in, Undo check-in and Cancel registrations](/images/kb/registration-registrants-bulk-actions.png)

The result says how many were updated and how many were skipped because the change did not apply to them.

### Decline with a reason

To decline several people at once, tick them and choose **More → Decline with a reason…**. Pick one of your preset reasons or type one. Each person gets the decline email with that reason, and the reason stays on their registration, shown in the drawer. People who are already canceled or checked in are skipped. Set the presets under **Settings → Approval → Decline reasons**. A single **Decline** in the drawer keeps its note as the reason too.

## Staff notes

The drawer has **Staff notes**: notes only your team sees, with who wrote each one and when. The registrant never sees them. Delete a note from its trash button; History keeps a record.

## Transactions

The drawer's **Transactions** section shows everything this person was charged, paid and refunded: the order they are on, add-ons bought for them later, each card payment and refund through your gateway, and payments recorded offline (check, cash). It adds them up to a balance and lists each order's lines. Take payments and refunds on the order in **Orders**.

## Member id

When membership lookup is on, the member id it matched is saved on the registration and shown in the drawer. Staff can change it in **Edit**, and the list search finds people by it.

## Search all events

**Search all events** searches registrations in every event of your organization that uses Registration, and your uploaded member list, by name, email, company, badge number or member id. Click a result to open that person in their event. Team members limited to some portfolios see only their portfolios' events.

## Import from a CSV

**Import CSV** offers three imports. Each one runs **Preview** first: it tries every row and shows what would be created, updated, skipped and why a row fails, without saving anything. **Import** then does the same for real.

- **Import registrations**: one row per person. Columns: `email` (required), `first_name`, `last_name`, `company`, `job_title`, `ticket` (name), `registrant_type` (name), `member_id`, and any question by its name. Choose the form, a ticket for rows that name none, and the payment: invoice each registrant, already paid by cash or check, or complimentary. People already registered for the event are skipped, or updated from the file if you choose that. Duplicate emails in the file are skipped. Turn off **Send each new registrant their confirmation** to import quietly.
- **Import comps**: complimentary registrations for speakers, VIPs or staff. Add a **Comp reason**, or a `comp_reason` column per row; the reason is kept on each order.
- **Bulk update**: changes existing registrations. Rows find their registration by `registration_id` or `email`. Only the columns in the file change: names, company, job title, ticket, registrant type, member id, answers, and `checked_in` (`yes`, or a date and time, to check them in with that time; `no` to undo).

Importing needs permission to change the event's settings.

<Tip>
**Attendee fields & export** opens the Attendees view under Pathways, where you pick columns and export registrants with their answers.
</Tip>
