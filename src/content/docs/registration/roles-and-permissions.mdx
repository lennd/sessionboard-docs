On an event with Registration, **Event Team → Permissions** has a **Registration** group. Its permissions decide what each role can do in Registration, separately from the rest of the event.

## Why use it

Not everyone who works on registration should move money. A desk volunteer edits registrations and prints badges; the finance team refunds and reconciles; one admin owns the forms and prices. Registration permissions let each person do their part without the rest.

## The Registration permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Can view registrations and orders** | Registrants, orders, invoices, statements and the registration ledger, finding people in **Search all events**, and the registration reports on **Registration → Reports** that hold no money. |
| **Can manage registrations** | Edit registrations, add one on behalf of someone, approve and decline, notes, cancel, resend confirmations, assign workshops, print badges, upload attendance. |
| **Can take payments and issue refunds** | Charge a card, refund (to the card, outside the gateway, or as a credit), mark an order paid, issue and void credits, payment plans and charges on a stored card, cash, check, money order and card orders on behalf, the money side of a ticket change or a substitution, moving a registration to another event, and voiding or merging registrations. |
| **Can view finance reports** | **Registration → Orders → Finance reports** (transactions, failed transactions, reconciliation, open invoices, purchase orders, code usage, GL), refunds to issue, and the money reports and money columns on **Registration → Reports** and in custom reports. |
| **Can manage registration settings** | Forms, tickets and pricing, promo codes, registrant types, workshops setup, emails and SMS, sales and invitations, and **Registration → Settings**. |
| **Can manage badges and onsite setup** | Badge designs, onsite settings, print stations and print jobs. |

A button the role cannot use stays on the page, greyed out, and says which permission it needs. The same request made another way (the API, a saved link) is refused.

<Note>
These permissions apply only to events with Registration. On other events nothing changes.
</Note>

## What each role has to start with

When Registration permissions were added, every role kept what it could already do:

- **Reporting Only Admin** can view registrations and read finance reports. **Read-Only User** can view registrations. **Evaluator** and **Portal User** have no Registration permissions.
- Every other event role, default or custom (for example **Admin User**, **Admin Lite** and **Session Manager**), can view, manage, take payments and read finance reports.
- Of those, roles that can update event settings (for example **Admin User**) can also manage registration settings and badges and onsite setup.
- **Registration Finance** has its own set (below).

Turn a permission off for a role in the grid, or duplicate a default role and adjust the copy.

## The Registration Finance role

**Registration Finance** is a default role for events with Registration. It can view registrations and orders, take payments and issue refunds, and read finance reports. It cannot edit registrations, change settings or reach the rest of the event.

<Steps>
<Step title="Invite the person">
In **Event Team**, invite them and choose **Registration Finance** as their role.
</Step>
<Step title="Repeat on each event they work on">
Roles are per event, so add them to every event whose money they handle.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Limit someone to chosen events

A person only works on the events they are on, with the role you give them on each one. To keep a team member to a few events:

- Invite them to those events only. A member who is not an organization member (event-only access) sees nothing else.
- On an event they are on, a role without **Can view registrations and orders** also keeps that event's registrations out of their **Search all events** results.
- With Portfolios, a portfolio-scoped member is limited to the portfolio's events.

## Who can manage payment gateways

Payment gateways are organization-wide. **Organization settings → Team → Permissions** has **Manage payment gateways** under Settings. It is needed to add, edit, test or archive a gateway in **Organization settings → Payments**, and to choose an event's gateway in **Registration → Settings**. Every role that could update organization settings has it to start with.

<Warning>
People with **Manage payment gateways** must use two-factor authentication. When they add, edit, test or archive a gateway, or change an event's gateway, Sessionboard asks for a two-factor code if they have not entered one in the last 15 minutes. Signing in with your organization's single sign-on counts. Turn on two-factor authentication in **Account settings** first.
</Warning>

Every gateway change is recorded in the organization's **History** (who, when, what changed). Gateway credentials are never shown or recorded there.

## Common questions

**Someone sees a greyed-out Refund button.** Their role on this event lacks **Can take payments and issue refunds**. Turn it on for their role, or give them **Registration Finance**.

**An event admin cannot change the event's gateway.** Choosing the gateway needs the organization permission **Manage payment gateways**, which event-only admins do not have. Ask an organization admin to choose it, or to give you an organization role with it.

**I was asked for a code again.** The two-factor check lasts 15 minutes. After that, the next gateway change asks again.
