Registration charges cards through your organization's own payment gateway. **Registration → Settings** picks the gateway and currency and holds the rest of the event's registration options.

## Why use it

Your registration income should land in your own merchant account, under your own name, on your own payout schedule. Registration is pass-through: Sessionboard is not in the flow of funds and charges no per-registrant fee. You keep the gateway relationship you already have, and refunds go back through it too.

## Connect a gateway

Gateways are connected once for the whole organization in **Organization settings → Payments**. See [Supported payment gateways](/awards/awards-list-of-supported-payment-gateways) for the list.

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect it">
In **Organization settings → Payments**, add your gateway and its credentials. You can connect the same gateway in test mode for rehearsals.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick it for the event">
In **Registration → Settings → Payments**, choose the **Gateway** and **Currency**. Leaving the gateway on **None — free registration only** does not by itself turn card payments off: the event then charges through the organization's gateway when it has only one, or the one gateway that takes the event's currency (see [Check payments before you open](#check-payments-before-you-open)). Only with no usable gateway does the event take free registrations alone.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Payments panel in Registration settings with the gateway and currency](/images/kb/registration-settings-payments.png)

Adding, editing, testing or archiving a gateway, and choosing the event's gateway, need the organization permission **Manage payment gateways** and a recent two-factor code. Without the permission the **Gateway** picker is greyed out and says so. See [Who can manage payment gateways](/registration/roles-and-permissions#who-can-manage-payment-gateways).

Gateways connected in test mode show **(test mode)** next to their name. Use one to run test registrations with your gateway's test cards before you open registration, then switch the event to the live gateway.

<Warning>
Without a gateway, paid tickets cannot be sold by card. Forms can still take free registrations and pay-later invoices.
</Warning>

## Check payments before you open

The **Payments readiness** panel under the Payments settings says whether card checkout will work: the gateway the event charges through, its processor (Stripe, Authorize.Net, Braintree and the others in Organization settings all work the same way), test or live mode, and whether it takes the event's currency.

- When the event leaves the gateway on **None**, an organization with one gateway charges through it; with several, the one that takes the event's currency is used. When more than one does, pick one.
- **Currencies this event may use** narrows the gateways' currencies for this event. The event's **Currency** must be on the list, and the gateway must take it, or card checkouts are refused with a message to choose another way to pay.
- **Check the gateway** authorizes 1.00 in the event's currency through the gateway and voids it at once. In test mode, use your processor's test card; no money moves. This is the check that reaches the processor, so it catches account settings a connection test cannot.

## Invoices and deposits

Turn on **Allow pay later by invoice** to let forms offer invoices (each form chooses on its **Payment** tab). Then set:

- **Due after (days)** — the invoice terms.
- **Number prefix** — for invoice numbers.
- **Deposit %** — leave blank for no deposits.
- **Allow installments**.
- **Payment instructions on the invoice** — bank details for wires, where to mail checks.

![The Invoices & deposits panel with due days, number prefix, deposit percentage, installments and payment instructions](/images/kb/registration-settings-invoices.png)

## Tax

Turn on **Charge tax**, choose whether **Prices include tax**, and click **Add tax rule** for each rate: a **Label**, **Percent** or **Fixed**, the **Amount**, the **Countries** it applies to (codes such as `US, CA`), and which **Fees** it taxes. Tickets marked not taxable are skipped.

### Different rates for different fees

Each ticket and add-on has a **Tax rate** in [Tickets & pricing](/registration/tickets-and-pricing): **Standard rate**, **Reduced rate**, **Zero rate** or **Exempt**. A tax rule applies only to fees with the same rate in its **Fees** column. For example, a 19% rule for standard fees and a 7% rule for a book add-on set to reduced. Fees and rules you never change are standard.

### VAT numbers and VAT invoices

- **Ask for the buyer's VAT number** — **Don't ask**, **Optional** or **Required**. The pay step of the form shows the field, and the number must be in its country's format, starting with the country code (for example `DE123456789`).
- **Reverse charge for EU businesses** — a buyer with a valid VAT number from another EU country than yours pays no VAT, and their invoice explains why. Check with your tax adviser whether this applies to what you sell.
- **Seller on invoices** — your **Legal name**, **Address**, **Country** and **VAT / tax number**. Invoices and receipts print these with the buyer's VAT number, the date of supply (the event's start date), each line's VAT rate and the tax per rate.

## Payment plans

Turn on **Offer payment plans** to let registrants split the total into installments. For each plan set the **Name**, the number of **Payments**, the **Days apart**, an optional minimum total (**From total**) and **Paid by (days before)** the event. A plan appears on the pay step only when the order qualifies.

The first installment is charged at checkout. The card or bank account stays on file with your gateway, and the rest are charged automatically on their due dates. If a charge is declined, it is tried again after the **Retry a declined installment after (days)** you set, for example `1, 3, 5`. After the last try, automatic charging stops for that installment and the registrant is emailed to update their payment method. When the plan is paid off, the stored card or bank account is removed from your gateway.

**Accept bank account (ACH / eCheck)** lets registrants pay from a US bank account instead of a card. Your gateway must take bank payments. A bank payment takes a few days to clear, and the registration is confirmed when it does.

## Cancellation policy

The policy decides what a cancellation gives back, both when staff cancel under the policy in [Orders](/registration/orders-and-invoices#cancel-under-the-policy) and when registrants cancel themselves.

- **Cancellation fee** — kept from every refund. On an unpaid order, it stays owing.
- **Add a deadline** — a **Cancel by** date with a **Refund %** and its own **Fee**. The first deadline still ahead applies. Without deadlines, a cancellation gets a full refund minus the fee.
- **After the last deadline** — **No refund**, or **A credit toward a future event only**.
- **Offer a credit toward a future event instead of a refund** — with a **Credit bonus %** (10 gives a credit worth 110% of what was paid) and when it expires.
- **Registrants can cancel in the attendee portal** — they see what they will get back before they confirm. Card refunds go back automatically. Refunds for people who paid by check, PO or wire are added to **Orders → Refunds to issue**. Turned off, registrants who paid are asked to contact you, and those who have not paid can still cancel.
- **Policy wording** — leave blank to show the policy written from these rules.

## Substitutions

- **Registrants can substitute in the attendee portal** — they can send their registration to a colleague.
- **Substitution fee** — paid by card in the portal before the registration moves.
- **Cut-off** — after this day only staff can substitute, from the registrant's drawer.

## Other settings

- **Member pricing** — see [Member pricing and eligibility](/registration/member-pricing).
- **Approval** — the registrant types whose registrations wait in Approvals. See [Approve registrations](/registration/approvals).
- **Confirmation** — **Reply-to address**, **Wallet pass (Apple and Google)** and **Calendar file**.
- **Form branding** — one look for every form: turn off **Use the event's brand** to set a logo, background image, primary color, button text color and font. A form's own **Page** settings still win.
- **Languages** — the languages registrants can switch the checkout to, from over 30. Each form picks its own from these.
- **Groups** — **Fewest seats**, **Most seats** and **Buyers can name people later** for group forms.
- **Launch** — test mode, the attendance goal, **Go live** and copying another event's setup. See [Test, launch and copy registration](/registration/launch).
- **Speakers** — register speakers when they accept a session role. See [Register speakers from Program](/registration/speakers).
- **Email translations** — see [Emails in other languages](/registration/emails-and-reminders#emails-in-other-languages).
- **Choice lists** — reusable lists of choices for choice questions. See [Choice lists](/registration/forms#choice-lists).

Click **Save** at the top. Only what you changed is saved.
