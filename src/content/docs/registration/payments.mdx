Registration charges cards through your organization's own payment gateway. **Registration → Settings** picks the gateway and currency and holds the rest of the event's registration options.

## Why use it

Your registration income should land in your own merchant account, under your own name, on your own payout schedule. Registration is pass-through: Sessionboard is not in the flow of funds and charges no per-registrant fee. You keep the gateway relationship you already have, and refunds go back through it too.

## Connect a gateway

Gateways are connected once for the whole organization in **Organization settings → Payments**. See [Supported payment gateways](/awards/awards-list-of-supported-payment-gateways) for the list.

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect it">
In **Organization settings → Payments**, add your gateway and its credentials. You can connect the same gateway in test mode for rehearsals.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick it for the event">
In **Registration → Settings → Payments**, choose the **Gateway** and **Currency**. **None — free registration only** takes no payments.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Payments panel in Registration settings with the gateway and currency](/images/kb/registration-settings-payments.png)

Gateways connected in test mode show **(test mode)** next to their name. Use one to run test registrations with your gateway's test cards before you open registration, then switch the event to the live gateway.

<Warning>
Without a gateway, paid tickets cannot be sold by card. Forms can still take free registrations and pay-later invoices.
</Warning>

## Invoices and deposits

Turn on **Allow pay later by invoice** to let forms offer invoices (each form chooses on its **Payment** tab). Then set:

- **Due after (days)** — the invoice terms.
- **Number prefix** — for invoice numbers.
- **Deposit %** — leave blank for no deposits.
- **Allow installments**.
- **Payment instructions on the invoice** — bank details for wires, where to mail checks.

![The Invoices & deposits panel with due days, number prefix, deposit percentage, installments and payment instructions](/images/kb/registration-settings-invoices.png)

## Tax

Turn on **Charge tax**, choose whether **Prices include tax**, and click **Add tax rule** for each rate: a **Label**, **Percent** or **Fixed**, the **Amount**, and the **Countries** it applies to (codes such as `US, CA`). Tickets marked not taxable are skipped.

## Other settings

- **Member pricing** — see [Member pricing and eligibility](/registration/member-pricing).
- **Approval** — the registrant types whose registrations wait in Approvals. See [Approve registrations](/registration/approvals).
- **Confirmation** — **Reply-to address**, **Wallet pass (Apple and Google)** and **Calendar file**.
- **Form branding** — one look for every form: turn off **Use the event's brand** to set a logo, background image, primary color, button text color and font. A form's own **Page** settings still win.
- **Languages** — the languages registrants can switch the checkout to. Each form picks its own from these.
- **Groups** — **Fewest seats**, **Most seats** and **Buyers can name people later** for group forms.

Click **Save** at the top. Only what you changed is saved.
