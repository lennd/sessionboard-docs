Some registrations should not be confirmed until a person has looked at them: press credentials, trainee rates, complimentary passes. Those registrations wait as **Pending approval** until your team approves or declines them.

## Why use it

Approval protects discounted and restricted tickets without closing them. A trainee can register at the trainee rate straight away, and a press request comes in through the same form as everyone else — your team checks the credential and confirms it, and nobody gets a badge until then.

## Choose what needs approval

Approval can come from the form or from the registrant type:

- **The whole form** — turn on **Require approval** on the form's **Details** tab. See [Create registration forms](/registration/forms#who-can-register).
- **Some registrant types** — in **Registration → Settings → Approval**, tick the types that need approval, for example Trainee or Press.

A registration that needs approval gets a pending notice instead of a confirmation. It cannot be checked in, and the desk does not print a badge for it.

## Review the queue

When your event has the Approvals Beta, registrations waiting for a decision appear in **Publish → Approvals**:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Approvals">
Go to **Publish → Approvals** and choose **Registrations** in the subject menu. **Waiting on me** lists what needs your decision; **All approvals** lists every registration that went through approval, filterable by **Pending**, **Approved** and **Denied**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open a registration">
Click a row to see the person, registrant type, form and answers.
</Step>
<Step title="Decide">
Click **Approve** or **Deny** — or tick several rows and decide them together. Approving confirms the registration and emails the confirmation; denying declines it.
</Step>
</Steps>

The **Workflows** tab of Approvals has a **Registrations** card describing which forms and types require approval. **Waiting for approval** on the Registration Overview opens the queue directly.

Without the Approvals Beta, approve and decline from [Registrants](/registration/registrants): filter **Status** by **Pending approval**, open a person, and click **Approve** or **Decline** — or tick several and choose **More → Approve**. Decisions made in Registrants close the matching item in Approvals too.

<Note>
Exhibitor and sponsor applications from Expo go through the same queue under **Exhibitor & sponsor applications**. See [Sell booths](/expo/booth-sales).
</Note>
