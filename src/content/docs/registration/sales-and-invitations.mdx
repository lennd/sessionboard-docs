**Registration → Sales & invitations** is for hosted guests: customers your sales team wants in the room, members your membership team is courting, partners' VIPs. Each team gets a quota of passes, reps nominate people, a manager approves, and each nominee gets a personal invitation link that carries their pass.

## Why use it

Comp codes leak. A shared code ends up on a forum, and nobody can say which rep's guests actually came. A pass from Sales & invitations works only through the invitee's own link and only for their email, every team stays inside its quota, and the funnel shows which reps turn invitations into registrations.

## Set up pass quotas

Click **Pass quota** and enter:

- **Team** — for example Corporate sales.
- **Pass** — **Complimentary**, **Percent off** or **Amount off**, with its value.
- **Passes** — how many the team can hand out. Leave blank for no cap.
- **Note**.

The **Pass quotas** table shows each team's passes used, held (approved or invited, not yet registered) and left.

![The Sales & invitations funnel counts and the Pass quotas table with passes used and left per team](/images/kb/registration-sales-funnel-quotas.png)

**Managers approve nominations** decides whether a manager approves each nomination before an invitation can go out. Turn it off to approve nominations as they are made.

## Nominate and invite

<Steps>
<Step title="Nominate">
Click **Nominate**, enter the person's email, name and organization, choose **Pass from** (a team's quota) and say **Why them**.
</Step>
<Step title="Approve">
Under **Nominations**, open **To approve** and click **Approve** or **Decline**.
</Step>
<Step title="Invite">
Click **Invite** on an approved nomination. They get a personal invitation email, and the dialog shows the link so you can copy it. **Resend** sends it again.
</Step>
</Steps>

The pass applies only through that personal link and only for that person's email — the code alone does nothing at checkout. Invited people count as being on the event's invite list, so they can use forms set to **Only people on the invite list**.

## Track results

The figures at the top count **Nominated**, **Approved**, **Invited** (and how many opened their link) and **Registered**. Filter **Nominations** by **To approve**, **Approved**, **Invited**, **Registered** or **Declined**. ![The Nominations table with status tabs To approve, Approved, Invited, Registered and Declined](/images/kb/registration-sales-nominations.png)

**By rep** shows who nominates and whose invitations turn into registrations, with a conversion rate.
