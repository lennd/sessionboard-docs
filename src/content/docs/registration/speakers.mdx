**Registration → Settings → Speakers** registers people when they accept a session role, so speakers do not have to fill in a registration form.

## Why use it

Speakers already said yes in Program. Asking them to register again, on a separate form, is the step they forget — and it leaves your team chasing a list. Registering them when they accept puts them on the registrants list with a complimentary, confirmed registration right away.

## Set it up

- **Register speakers when they accept** — turns it on.
- **Form** — the form their registration is on, usually your presenter form.
- **Ticket (every role)** and **Registrant type** — what they are registered with.
- **By role** — for each session role, **Register** (with its own ticket, or the default) or **Do not register** (for example moderators who already have staff passes).
- **Deadline** — after it, acceptances are no longer registered and the speaker form refuses new registrations. It is in the event's time zone.

Click **Save**. Turning it on does not register people who accepted earlier — click **Register everyone already accepted** for them. For a large program, the first speakers are registered straight away and the rest continue in the background; the counts below update as they finish.

Someone already registered for the event is never registered twice; their registration is marked as a speaker's.

## See who is registered

Below the settings, the counts show how many accepted speakers are registered, pending and not registered. In Program, each accepted participant on a session shows **Registered**, **Registration pending**, **Not registered** or **Not registered · deadline passed** beside their name.

<Note>
Registrations created this way are complimentary and confirmed without approval. Registration emails go out as for any registration.
</Note>
