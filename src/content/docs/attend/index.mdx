**Attend** is the event module for the attendee mobile app. Registrants install one app, find your event, sign in with the email on their registration, and get your program, venue maps, speakers, exhibitors and live captions on their phone — offline too. The **Attend** module in the event sidebar is where you design that app, message attendees, run live Q&A and polls, sell sponsor space, and see how it is used.

<Note>
Attend is an Early Access **Beta**. Turn on **Attend Mobile App** for the event under **Admin Settings → Early Access** (see [Early Access](/get-started/early-access)). Until it is on, the Attend module is not in the event sidebar.
</Note>

## Why use it

Your program already lives in Sessionboard, so the app is never a second copy to keep in sync: a room change or a new speaker reaches every phone on the next sync, and registrants sign in with the registration they already have. Everything attendees do in the app — sessions they save, booths they visit, questions they ask — comes back to you as analytics, sponsor reports and lead lists, instead of disappearing into a separate app vendor.

## Turn on the app for an event

1. In the event, open **Admin Settings → Early Access** and turn on **Attend Mobile App**. The **Attend** module appears in the sidebar.
2. Open **Attend → Mobile App**, choose how attendees sign in, and turn on the event app. See [Design the event app](/attend/mobile-app#sign-in-and-privacy).
3. Pick a template pack and publish the design.
4. Open **Attend → Overview** and work through the setup checklist, then share the event app link or QR poster with registrants. See [Attend Overview](/attend/overview).

Only registrants can sign in: people whose registration for this event is confirmed or checked in.

## What is in the module

The sidebar groups Attend's pages by job. Labels are short to fit the sidebar; each page's own title is in brackets where it differs.

| Section | Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** | App status, the setup checklist, live numbers and the share link and QR poster |
| — | **Mobile App** | The app builder: template pack, pages, theme, tabs, sign-in and privacy |
| — | **Analytics** | Installs, sign-ins, screens, saves, push opens and sponsor impressions |
| Engage | **Notifications** | Push messages to attendees, now or scheduled |
| Engage | **Networking** | Attendee messages, meeting requests, People to meet and reports |
| Engage | **Engagement** (Live engagement) | Q&A moderation, polls and session evaluations |
| Engage | **Suggestions** (Recommendations) | What the app recommends to each attendee, and the interests step |
| Engage | **Ask** | The app's AI assistant |
| Partners | **Sponsor ads** (Sponsor placements) | Sponsor banners and spots in the app |
| Partners | **Showrooms** (Exhibitor showrooms) | Each exhibitor's page: products, deals, contacts |
| Partners | **Leads** (Lead retrieval) | Badge scanning seats for exhibitor and sponsor staff, and lead exports |
| Content | **Posters** (Posters & abstracts) | ePosters and abstracts in the app, from a Poster Gallery |

<Tip>
Send registrants [Use the Attend app](/participants/attend-app) — it walks them through finding the event, signing in, saving sessions and showing their badge.
</Tip>
