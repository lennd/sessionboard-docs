**Attend → Suggestions** (the page is titled **Recommendations**) controls the **Recommended for you** picks in the event app: the sessions, exhibitors and sponsors each attendee is likely to want, each with the reason it was picked.

## Why use it

A big program is hard to navigate, and the session someone would have loved is easy to miss. Recommendations put it in front of them — "Because you saved…", "You picked Oncology" — so attendees fill their schedule faster and visit more booths, while you still decide what gets featured and what never shows up.

## Turn on recommendations

Under **In the app**:

![The In the app card with Recommend to attendees, Ask for interests after first sign-in, and Sessions, Exhibitors and sponsors and Products checked under Recommend](/images/kb/attend-suggestions-in-the-app.png)

- **Recommend to attendees** — picks come from what each attendee chose, saved and attended, with the reason shown on every pick.
- **Ask for interests after first sign-in** — a short, skippable step after sign-in: topics from your program and a few goals (learn something new, meet people, find products and services, earn credits, meet the speakers). Attendees can change their answers later under **Me → My interests**.
- **Recommend** — choose **Sessions** and **Exhibitors and sponsors**.

Then add the **Recommended for you** block to the home screen in [**Mobile App**](/attend/mobile-app#lay-out-the-pages).

<Note>
Topics come from the tags on your program. If your sessions are not tagged yet, the interests step has no topics to offer.
</Note>

## Why something is recommended

Every pick shows one reason, most specific first: something the attendee saved (a session, speaker, track or exhibitor), a topic they picked, topics of sessions they attended, a related topic, **Featured by the organizers**, or **Popular with attendees**. Sessions can also be flagged **Filling fast** or **Full right now**. An attendee with no interests or saves yet gets popular picks until they tell the app more.

## Pin, boost or hide

Under **Pin, boost or hide**, add sessions or exhibitors and sponsors and pick a rule for each:

- **Pin** — comes first for everyone and shows as **Featured**. You can pin up to five of each type.
- **Boost** — ranks higher.
- **Hide** — never recommended.

## Preview as an attendee

**Preview as an attendee** shows what the app recommends to a person right now, and why. Search for an attendee by name or email, or pick **A new attendee (no history)** to see what someone who just signed in gets. Save your changes first to see them in the preview.

<Tip>
Pin your keynote and your title sponsor before the event opens, when nobody has saved anything yet — they are what new attendees see first.
</Tip>
