**Attend → Engagement → Check-in & seats** is where you decide how attendees check in to sessions from the Attend app, how long they must stay for attendance to count, how many seats each session has, and who has reserved one. Check-ins record the session attendance that Lessons & Credits reads when attendees claim continuing-education credit, and a session quiz taken in the app unlocks the claim.

## Why use it

Accredited meetings have to show who was in the room and for how long. With check-in in the app you get that record per session, without paper sign-in sheets or a separate scanning vendor: attendees tap a button or scan the room's QR poster, or a monitor at the door scans their badge, and attendance lands where the credit claim looks for it. Seat reservations and a waitlist keep popular sessions from overfilling, and a quiz in the app means attendees finish the credit requirement before they leave the room.

## Choose how attendees check in

Open **Attend → Engagement** and the **Check-in & seats** tab. The **Session check-in** card holds the rules for the whole event.

![The Session check-in card with all three ways to check in ticked, Check-in opens at 15 minutes, Require check-out on with a 75% minimum time in the room, and unclaimed reservations released after 10 minutes](/images/kb/attend-checkin-settings.png)

Under **Ways to check in**, tick any combination. With none ticked, session check-in is off.

- **Check-in button** — attendees tap **Check in** on the session in the app. The button appears **Check-in opens** minutes before the session starts (15 by default) and goes away when the session ends.
- **Room QR code** — attendees scan the QR poster at the door, with their phone camera or with **Scan the room code** in the app. A room poster checks them in to whichever session is on in that room at that moment. See [Print room QR posters](#print-room-qr-posters).
- **Room monitors scan badges** — people you choose scan attendees' badges at the door with the app. See [Add room monitors](#add-room-monitors).

Click **Save**. Attendees who check in without signal are not turned away: the app keeps the check-in on the phone and sends it when it reconnects, with the time they actually scanned.

<Tip>
A tap on **Check in** is recorded as attendance the attendee reported themselves. A room QR scan or a monitor's badge scan is recorded as a check-in, so when you review claims you can tell a verified scan from a tap. If your accreditor wants proof of presence, use the QR poster or room monitors rather than the button alone.
</Tip>

### Count time in the room for duration-based credit

Turn on **Require check-out** when credit depends on how long someone stayed. Attendees then check out when they leave — **Check out** on the session, the room QR again, or a monitor scanning them out — and the app adds up their minutes in the room within the session's scheduled start and end. Several visits add together; someone still checked in when the session ends has no minutes counted for that last visit until they check out.

**Minimum time in the room** is the share of the session an attendee must be in the room for attendance to count — for example 75%. Attendance is recorded once their minutes reach it. Leave it empty to count any time in the room.

Without **Require check-out**, the first check-in records attendance.

### Stop check-ins when a room is full

**Stop check-ins when a room is full** refuses button and QR check-ins once a session reaches capacity. Attendees with a reserved seat can still check in, and room monitors can always scan people in — the person at the door decides.

A session's capacity is the seat count you set on it (see [Set seats, reservations and a waitlist](#set-seats-reservations-and-a-waitlist)) or, when you set none, the room's capacity. Seats taken counts everyone in the room (with check-out on) or everyone checked in (with it off), plus reservation holders who have not arrived yet.

## Print room QR posters

In the **Sessions** list:

- **Room QR posters** downloads a PDF with one poster per room. Put each on its door for the whole event.
- The QR icon on a session's row downloads a poster for that one session — useful when a session runs somewhere without a room in the program.

Each code only works for its own room or session at this event, so a photo of one poster cannot check someone in to another room.

## Add room monitors

Room monitors are registrations — your staff, volunteers or session chairs — who can scan other attendees in from the app. In the **Room monitors** card, search a registration by name or email and click **Add**. Remove one with the icon beside their name.

Monitors also need **Room monitors scan badges** ticked under **Ways to check in**; you can add them before you tick it.

In the app, a monitor opens the session and taps **Scan attendees in**, chooses **Checking in** or **Checking out**, and scans each attendee's badge: the badge in their app, the check-in QR code from their confirmation email, or the printed badge number when [lead retrieval](/attend/leads) uses printed badges. Monitor scans work at any time and are not refused when the room is full. Scans made offline sync when the phone reconnects.

If a monitor's registration is transferred to someone else, they show **Registration transferred** and the new holder is not a monitor.

## Set seats, reservations and a waitlist

The **Sessions** list shows every published session with who is checked in, its capacity, reservations and quiz. Counts refresh every 30 seconds.

![The Sessions list on the Check-in & seats tab with Room QR posters, Export check-ins and Export reservations buttons, and a session with 40 seats and 0 reserved above sessions with open seating](/images/kb/attend-checkin-sessions.png)

Click **Seats** on a session to set its rules:

![The Seats dialog for a session with Seats set to 40, Require a reservation and Waitlist when full turned on, and an empty Waitlist size](/images/kb/attend-checkin-seat-rules.png)

- **Seats** — the session's own capacity. Leave it empty to use the room's capacity, or for no limit when the room has none.
- **Require a reservation** — attendees reserve a seat in the app before the session starts. Reservations close when it starts.
- **Waitlist when full** — when seats run out, attendees join a waitlist and see their place in it. **Waitlist size** caps it; leave it empty for no limit. Once the waitlist is full too, the app tells attendees the session is full.

When a seat frees up — the attendee releases it, you release it, or a no-show is released — the first person on the waitlist gets it and a push notification. Raising **Seats** moves people up from the waitlist straight away.

### Release no-shows' seats

**Release unclaimed reservations**, in the **Session check-in** card, gives a reserved seat to the waitlist when its holder has not checked in that many minutes after the session starts. Attendees with a reservation see the rule on the session in the app. Leave it empty to keep reserved seats for the whole session.

## Follow a session and export attendance

Click a session in the list to open its page:

- **In the room** (with check-out on) or **Checked in**, **Capacity**, **Seats left** and **Reserved**, with the waitlist count.
- **Checked in** — each attendee, **How** they checked in (**Button**, **Room QR** or **Monitor scan**), **In** and **Out** times, **Minutes** in the room, and **Attendance**: **Recorded** once it counts for credit, **Not yet** while they are short of the minimum time.
- **Reservations** — each reservation's status (**Reserved**, **Waitlist #n**, or how it was released) and whether they checked in. **Release** frees a seat for the next person on the waitlist.

To export, use **Export check-ins** or **Export reservations** — on the **Sessions** list for the whole event, or on a session's page for that session. Both download a CSV you can hand to your accreditor or open in a spreadsheet.

**Attend → Analytics** also shows a **Room check-in** panel with the busiest sessions against their capacity.

## Add a CE quiz to a session

A session's quiz is written once, on the lesson published from that session in **Learning**. The same quiz serves attendees in the Attend app, learners taking the on-demand lesson, and the credit claim — there is no second quiz to keep in sync.

<Note>
Quizzes need **Learning (Lessons & Credits)** for your organization and **Credits** turned on for the event — see [Early Access](/get-started/early-access). The lesson's **Knowledge check**, where you write the quiz, also has to be on for your organization; ask your Sessionboard contact if you do not see it.
</Note>

<Steps>

<Step title="Publish the session as a lesson">

Open the session's profile. In its **Credits** panel, click **Create on-demand lesson**. The lesson is created as a draft with the session's credit and faculty copied over, and opens in Learning. If the session already has a lesson, click **Open lesson** instead.

The lesson does not have to be published to the catalog for the quiz to work in the app.

</Step>

<Step title="Write the quiz in the lesson's Knowledge check">

In the lesson's setup, open **Knowledge check**, add your questions and set the pass threshold, then save the quiz.

</Step>

<Step title="Set the pass mark and attempts on the check-in page">

Back in **Attend → Engagement → Check-in & seats**, the session's **Quiz** column shows the number of questions and the pass mark. Open the session to see its **Quiz** card:

- **Pass mark (%)** — the score that counts as a pass.
- **Attempts allowed** — leave it empty for unlimited attempts. Lowering it does not remove attempts already made.

Click **Save**. The rules apply wherever the quiz is taken — in the app or in the lesson.

</Step>

</Steps>

### How a pass unlocks the credit claim

When a session's credit requires a passing quiz, the attendee's **Credits** tab in the app shows that session as **Quiz needed** with a **Take the quiz** button. They answer in the app — answers are kept on the phone until they submit, which needs a connection — and see their score straight away.

A pass shows **You passed. You can now claim credit for this session.** The pass counts on the claim site too, so the attendee claims as usual and the quiz requirement is already met, whether they passed in the app or in the on-demand lesson. Once they have used every attempt without passing, the app asks them to contact you.

<Note>
The app asks for the quiz only for credit that requires a passing quiz. The session's **Credits** panel sets an evaluation requirement; to make a session's credit require the quiz, ask your Sessionboard contact.
</Note>

Claims that still need an evaluation keep it: the attendee completes the evaluation on the claim site, or in the app when [session evaluations](/attend/engagement#which-survey-each-session-gets) are on.
