**Attend → Mobile App** is the editor for your event's attendee app. You pick a template pack, adjust any page and the home screen, set colors and tabs, and decide how attendees sign in and what they share. The phone in the middle of the editor shows your changes as you make them; attendees see them once you publish.

## Why use it

One click on a template pack gives you a complete, branded app — home screen, agenda, session pages, people, exhibitors and tabs — built for your kind of event, from a medical meeting to a trade show. From there you change only what matters to you, preview it on the phone before anyone else sees it, and publish when you are ready, without an app vendor or a resubmission to the app stores.

## How the editor is laid out

- **Pages and sections** on the left: **Pages** (**Home**, **Agenda**, **Session**, **People**, **Partners**, **Map**, **Me**), **Design** (**Layout**, **Theme**, **Tabs**) and **Access** (**Sign-in & privacy**).
- **The phone** in the middle: the app as it will look. Click a block on the home screen to edit it, tap a tab to switch pages, and switch between **Light** and **Dark** to check both. Until the event has a published program, the phone fills in sample data and says so.
- **The settings panel** on the right for whatever you selected.
- **The header**: whether the app is **App on** or **App off**, the draft status, **Undo** / **Redo**, **Discard changes** and **Publish**.

![The Mobile App editor with Pages, Design and Access on the left, a phone preview of a Scholar home screen in the middle and the template pack picker on the right](/images/kb/attend-mobile-app-editor.png)

## Draft and publish

Design changes save automatically to a **draft** — the status reads **Saving…**, then **Draft saved** and **Unpublished changes**. Attendees keep seeing the published design until you click **Publish**; after that they get the new design on their next sync.

- **Discard changes** deletes the draft and reloads what attendees see now. Undo history is cleared, and this can't be undone.
- If someone changes the app in another tab, the status reads **Changed in another tab** — click **Reload** to pick up their changes before you publish.

The draft covers the layout (pack, pages, home screen, theme, tabs) and venue maps. **Sign-in & privacy** is not drafted: those changes reach attendees as soon as you click **Save**, so turning something off never waits for a publish.

## Choose a template pack

Open **Design → Layout**. A template pack sets every page's layout, the tabs and the home screen in one click.

![Seven template pack cards — Atlas, Scholar, Expo, Mosaic, Keynote, Pulse and Concierge — each with a thumbnail and a one-line description, Scholar selected](/images/kb/attend-mobile-app-template-packs.png)

| Pack | Built for |
| --- | --- |
| **Atlas** | Clean and image-led. Fits most events. The default. |
| **Scholar** | Medical and scientific meetings: a dense program home, browse by facets, codes, credits and disclosures. |
| **Expo** | Exhibitor-first for trade shows and expos, with a **Scan** tab. |
| **Mosaic** | Photo tiles for brands with strong imagery. |
| **Keynote** | Full key art and curated picks for flagship events. |
| **Pulse** | A personal home with rails and a feed. |
| **Concierge** | Text bands for hosted-buyer and small executive events. |

<Warning>
Switching packs replaces your page, home screen and tab changes. Your colors stay.
</Warning>

## Lay out the pages

Each page under **Pages** follows the pack unless you choose another template for it. **Reset to pack** puts a page back.

- **Home** — pick the home layout, then add, reorder and edit its blocks. Blocks show top to bottom; drag to reorder, switch one off to hide it. Blocks include **Event banner**, **Countdown**, **Button grid**, **Quick actions**, **Text**, **Links**, **Happening now**, **My next session**, **Recommended for you**, **Announcements**, **Featured rail**, **Show deals**, **Sponsor banner**, **Sponsor carousel**, **Sponsor strip** and **Ask anything**. A button grid tile can open any page of the app, or the agenda already filtered — "Committee meetings", or one room, for example.
- **Agenda** — how the program is browsed: day tabs, a timeline, browse by facets and more. Browse by facets offers **Day**, **Track**, **Room**, **Topic**, **Session type**, **Learning level**, **Speaker** and **Session code**; pick which ones under the agenda's options.
- **Session** — how a session's page is laid out.
- **People** — how speakers and the attendee directory are listed.
- **Partners** — how sponsors and exhibitors are shown, from logo cards by tier to an exhibitor directory.
- **Map** — the venue maps the app shows. By default the app shows every published [floor map](/expo/floor-plan); **Choose maps** to add an ExpoFP floor plan or any https link (Map Your Show, the venue's site, a PDF), rename maps or change their order.
- **Me** — the attendee's own page: their schedule, saves and profile.

Sponsor banner and carousel blocks are where [sponsor placements](/attend/sponsor-ads) show. The **Recommended for you** block shows [recommendations](/attend/suggestions), and **Ask anything** opens [Ask](/attend/ask).

### The Now block

**Now** puts a short AI summary at the top of Home of what's on now and in the next 90 minutes, under an **AI summary** label, with the attendee's first name in the greeting, an **Ask anything** box and question chips that open [Ask](/attend/ask) with the question already asked. Every attendee sees the same summary; it refreshes every few minutes and counts toward Ask's daily AI budget.

It is set on the **Now** card under the **Home** page's blocks, and is off until you turn on **Show Now on Home**.

- **Auto** writes the summary from the program. **Organizer prompt** adds your guidance, such as "Mention lunch in Hall B at noon."
- With **Use top question topics** on, the chips are your attendees' most asked [topics](/attend/ask#what-attendees-ask-about); turn it off to write your own (up to six, each an emoji, a label and the question it asks). Chips need Ask on.
- The card saves on its own, not with **Publish**.

During early access, our team sets up the Now block with you.

## Set the theme

Open **Design → Theme**:

- **Brand colour** — or **Use the app default**. The app darkens or lightens the color where needed so text stays readable.
- **Corners** — **Square**, **Slight**, **Medium**, **Round** or **Pill**.
- **Spacing** — **Roomy** or **Compact**.
- **Light or dark** — **Follow the phone**, **Light** or **Dark**.

![The Theme panel with a brand colour, Corners set to Medium, Spacing set to Compact and Light or dark set to Follow the phone](/images/kb/attend-mobile-app-theme.png)

## Choose the tabs

Open **Design → Tabs** to pick up to five tabs, in order, from destinations such as **Home**, **My day**, **Agenda**, **People**, **Sponsors**, **Map**, **Ask**, **Credits**, **Search**, **Posters**, **Products**, **Scan** and **Meetings**. **Me** is always there, for the attendee's profile and sign-out. Some packs use a menu instead of a bottom tab bar.

## Sign-in and privacy

Open **Access → Sign-in & privacy**. Changes here are live as soon as you click **Save**.

![The Sign-in & privacy panel with Turn on the event app switched on and the Email link and code and SSO sign-in methods](/images/kb/attend-mobile-app-sign-in.png)

- **Turn on the event app** — while it is off, nobody can sign in to this event in the app, and the event is not listed in the app's event search.
- **Sign-in methods** — choose at least one to turn the app on:
  - **Email link and code** — we email a sign-in link and a 6-digit code to the address on the registration.
  - **SSO** — attendees sign in through one of your organization's OIDC or SAML identity providers (see [SSO](/integrations/sso)), and are let in when that account's email matches a registration. Some associations offer SSO alone.
- **Attendee directory** — let registrants find each other under **People → Attendees**. Choose whether attendees are listed unless they opt out (**List attendees unless they opt out**) or only once they turn on **Show me in the directory**, and what the directory shows. Name is always shown; **Email** and **Phone** are contact details, so show them only if attendees expect to be contacted directly. Speakers, exhibitors and sponsors are always listed. [Networking](/attend/networking) needs the directory on.
- **Attendee profile** — which fields attendees see on their profile in the app and which they can change themselves. A new email only takes effect once the attendee confirms it with a code sent to that address.

Only registrants can sign in — people whose registration for this event is confirmed or checked in. Cancelling a registration ends that person's app session.

## The organization app

At the organization level, **Publish → Mobile App** lists every event's attendee app in one table. With the organization **Mobile App** Early Access beta you can also build one app for your whole organization: its name and branding, which events it lists (featured first), recordings from your Media library and info pages. Tapping an event in it opens that event's own app when it is on, otherwise the event's web page.

<Note>
Attendees choose the app's language themselves, under **Me → Language**.
</Note>
