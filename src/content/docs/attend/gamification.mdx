**Attend → Engagement → Gamification** turns the Attend app into a game: attendees earn points for checking in to sessions, visiting booths, voting in polls and more, see their rank, and chase quests you design. You see a live leaderboard, the numbers each sponsor's booth drew, and a prize list you can export.

<Note>
Gamification is rolling out to Attend events. If you don't see a **Gamification** tab on **Attend → Engagement**, ask your Sessionboard contact to turn it on for your event.
</Note>

## Why use it

Points give attendees a reason to visit the expo floor, stay for sessions and use the app, and they give you something to sell. A **sponsored quest** ("Visit these five booths for 50 bonus points") sends traffic to the booths that paid for it, and the booth scans and completions on this page are the numbers you report back to that sponsor. Nothing extra to install or staff: points come from what attendees already do in the app, plus a QR poster on each booth.

## Turn on points

Open **Attend → Engagement** and the **Gamification** tab.

1. Turn on **Turn on points**. Until you do, the app shows nothing about points and nothing is counted.
2. Choose the **Leaderboard**: **Top 3**, **Top 5**, **Top 10**, or **Private**. With **Private**, attendees see only their own points; you still see the full leaderboard here.
3. Click **Save**.

Points are counted by Sessionboard when the action happens, never sent by the phone, so an attendee can't give themselves points.

<Tip>
The leaderboard in the app only names attendees who appear in the attendee directory. If the directory is off, everyone sees their own score but no one else's name. Turn the directory on in **Attend → Mobile App** (people settings) if you want names on the leaderboard.
</Tip>

## Choose what earns points

**Points per action** lists every action that can earn points, with a suggested value:

| Action | Counts once per |
| --- | --- |
| Checks in to a session | session |
| Exchanges contacts | person met |
| Has their badge scanned at a booth | booth |
| Scans a booth code | booth |
| Votes in a poll | poll |
| Rates a session (the session survey) | session |
| Saves a moment | saved moment |
| Has a meeting accepted (both people) | meeting |
| Asks Ask a question | question |

Set an action to **0** to stop it earning points. **Daily limit** caps how many times a day an action scores for one attendee (days follow the event's time zone); leave it empty for no limit. By default, asking questions is limited to 5 a day, exchanging contacts to 25 and saving moments to 20, so nobody climbs the board by spamming one action.

Repeating an action never scores twice: scanning the same booth again or checking in to the same session again earns nothing. A cancelled meeting loses its points.

## Leave staff and partners off the leaderboard

Under **Who doesn't compete**, tick the people who should not appear on the leaderboard or rank in the prize export:

- **Sponsor and exhibitor staff** (on by default) — anyone on a sponsor's or exhibitor's team.
- A **registrant type**, for example your own staff.
- A **Who's Who group**, for example the Board or Faculty.

They still earn points, and they still see their own score in the app, with a note that they aren't on the leaderboard.

## Create quests

A quest is a set of steps that earns bonus points once every step is done, in any order. Click **Add quest** and fill in:

- **Name**, an optional **Emoji** and **Description** — what attendees see.
- **Bonus points** for completing it.
- **Sponsor** — optional. A quest with a sponsor shows "Sponsored by …" in the app and counts on that sponsor's row below.
- **Steps** — up to five. Each step is an action and how many times, for example **Scans a booth code** × 5. For booth steps, open **Any booth** to limit the step to the booths you tick.

Click **Done**, then **Save**. A quest completes once per attendee; you can edit its steps later, and attendees who already completed it keep it.

<Tip>
Selling a sponsored quest: give the quest the sponsor, and limit its booth step to that sponsor's booth (or their partners' booths). The sponsor's row shows how many attendees completed it.
</Tip>

## Print booth QR posters

**Sponsors and exhibitors** lists every sponsor and exhibitor of the event with:

- **Booth scans** — attendees who scanned the booth's QR poster.
- **Leads scanned** — attendees whose badge the booth scanned with [lead retrieval](/attend/leads).
- **Sponsored quests** — how many attendees completed each quest the sponsor is on.

Click the download icon on a row for that booth's poster, or **All booth posters** for one page per booth. Each poster carries the sponsor's logo and a QR code that only works at this event, so a photo of one booth's poster can't be used at another event.

## Watch the leaderboard and pay out prizes

**Live leaderboard** shows the top 50 attendees with their points and number of awards; it refreshes every 15 seconds. People who don't compete are listed at the end, marked **Not competing**.

**Export for prizes** downloads a CSV of everyone with points: rank, name, email, company, points, awards, quests completed, voided points and whether they compete. Ties share a rank; the attendee who reached the score first is listed first.

## Take back an award

**Recent awards** lists the latest 50 awards. Click **Void** on one that shouldn't count — a scan that happened by mistake, or someone gaming the rules. The points come off the attendee's score and the leaderboard at once. The award stays in the export as voided, so you can explain it if asked. Voiding a step doesn't undo a quest already completed; void the quest award too if it shouldn't stand.

## What attendees see

Once points are on, attendees see:

- **Me → Points** — their points, their rank and how many points they need to reach the top 3, every quest with its progress and sponsor, how to earn points, the leaderboard and their recent points.
- **Booth codes** — scanning a booth's QR poster in the app (the **Scan** tab, or **Scan the room code** on the check-in screen), or with the phone camera, shows "+10 points at Acme" and any quest it completed. Booth codes need a connection.
- A short **+N points** message when their score goes up.
- A **Leaderboard** block on the app's home, if you add it in **Attend → Mobile App**. Without points turned on, the block shows nothing.

See [Use the Attend app](/participants/attend-app#earn-points) for the attendee's view.

## Still having issues?

- **Attendees don't see Points** — check **Turn on points** is on and saved. The app picks it up the next time it is opened.
- **No names on the leaderboard** — turn on the attendee directory; only listed attendees are named.
- **A booth scan says it isn't a booth code for this event** — the poster was printed for another event, or the sponsor was removed from this event. Print a new poster from this page.
