**Attend → Overview** is the event app at a glance: whether attendees can sign in, what is left to set up, how many people are using it right now, and the link and QR poster you hand out.

## Why use it

Before the event it is your launch list — every step that stands between you and "attendees can use this", each with a link straight to the setting. During the event it is the page you leave open: who is signed in, what needs moderating, and whether your welcome push was opened.

## App status

The status strip at the top answers four questions:

![Four status tiles on the Attend overview — App On, App design Unpublished changes, Sign-in Email link with 1 method, and Template pack Expo](/images/kb/attend-overview-status.png)

- **App** — **On** or **Off**. While it is off, attendees can't sign in. Turn it on in [**Mobile App → Sign-in & privacy**](/attend/mobile-app#sign-in-and-privacy).
- **Sign-in** — the sign-in methods attendees can use.
- **Template pack** — the pack the published design uses.
- **App design** — **Published**, **Not published** or **Unpublished changes**, with the date it was last published and a link that opens the [Mobile App editor](/attend/mobile-app).

## Set up the app

The **Set up the app** checklist is computed from your settings, so items tick themselves off as you finish them. Each item links to the page where you do it. When every required item is done, the checklist reads **Ready for attendees**.

![The Set up the app checklist showing 7 of 9 done, with Set up notifications and Test sign-in on your phone still open](/images/kb/attend-overview-checklist.png)

| Item | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| **Choose how attendees sign in** | At least one sign-in method is set. |
| **Publish the app design** | A template pack, colors and home screen are published. |
| **Add venue maps** | A floor map is published or an ExpoFP or venue map is linked. |
| **Review directory and networking** | You have decided who is listed and whether attendees can message and meet. |
| **Set up notifications** | A notification has been sent or scheduled. |
| **Test sign-in on your phone** | Someone has signed in to the app. |
| **Place sponsors** *(optional)* | A sponsor placement is live. |
| **Fill exhibitor showrooms** *(optional)* | At least one exhibitor page has content. |
| **Add posters and abstracts** *(optional)* | Posters & abstracts are on. |

## Share the app with attendees

**Share with attendees** holds the **Event app link** — one link for every phone: it opens the app, or the store if the app isn't installed — and a QR code of the same link.

![The Share with attendees card with a QR code, the event app link, a copy button and Download QR poster (PDF)](/images/kb/attend-overview-share.png)

- **Copy link** to paste it into your confirmation email, event website or know-before-you-go email.
- **Download QR poster (PDF)** for a letter-size poster with your event name and the QR code, ready to print for registration desks and session doors.

<Tip>
Scan the QR code yourself before the event and sign in with a test registration. That ticks off **Test sign-in on your phone**.
</Tip>

## Attendee engagement

**Attendee engagement** at the top of the page answers three questions at a glance:

- **Registered → Active → Engaged.** **Registered** counts live registrations. **Active** counts the ones who signed into the app at least once. **Engaged** counts active attendees who took 5 or more actions (opened a session, speaker or exhibitor page, saved something, asked or voted, checked in, sent a message, requested a meeting, tapped a sponsor) or used the app in 3 or more different hours of the day, in the event's time zone. Signing in on its own doesn't count.
- **Attendee Health**: engaged attendees as a share of active ones.
- **Sponsor Health**: the share of your sponsors and exhibitors that at least one attendee interacted with. An interaction is a view of their page, a save of them or one of their products, a lead scan, a tap on their placement or a meeting request. **See who has none** lists the ones nobody has interacted with yet, so you can help them during the show.

A gauge shows **—** until there is something to divide: no active attendees, or no sponsors and exhibitors on the event.

## Live numbers

**Live** shows what is happening now, refreshed every minute:

- **Signed in today** and how many attendees have signed in so far.
- **Active now** — installs used in the last few minutes.
- **Messages** and **Meetings confirmed**, when [networking](/attend/networking) is on.
- **Push opens**, **Saves** and **Sponsor impressions** with tap-through, over the last 30 days.
- **Open reports** — a link to the moderation queue when attendees have reported someone.
- **Ask questions**, when [Ask](/attend/ask) is on.

Below them, **Daily active, last 14 days** charts active installs and signed-in attendees, with **Top sessions** and **Top exhibitors and sponsors**. **All analytics** opens [Analytics](/attend/analytics).

**Recent activity** lists the newest sign-ins, reports (reason only) and sent notifications.

<Note>
Until someone opens the app, the page shows **No one has opened the app yet** with a shortcut to the share link.
</Note>
