**Attend → Leads** (the page is titled **Lead retrieval**) lets exhibitor and sponsor staff scan attendee badges with the event app. You license seats per team, decide what a scan shares, and export leads for any team.

## Why use it

Exhibitors pay for leads, and today they rent a scanner from a third party that knows nothing about your attendees. Lead retrieval runs in the app staff already have, on their own phones, works without signal on the show floor, and gives you a paid add-on to sell per seat — with every scan counted as a booth visit in the sponsor's report.

## How it works

1. Attendees show **My badge** in the app (**Me → My badge**): a QR code that holds no personal data.
2. Staff on a licensed team open the team's lead list in the app and scan the badge. The first scan claims one of the team's seats for that staff member.
3. The team gets the attendee's name plus the fields you allow, rates the lead hot, warm or cold, adds notes and tags, and answers the team's own questions (up to ten). Scanning the same person again updates the existing lead.
4. Scans made without signal are kept on the phone and sent when it reconnects.

Staff on a team are the registrants whose contact is on that account in **CRM**.

## Settings

Under **Settings** (applies to every team at this event):

- **Lead retrieval** — lets licensed exhibitor and sponsor staff scan attendee badges in the app.
- **Default seats per team** — for teams without their own licence. 0 means only licensed teams can scan.
- **What a scan shares** — the attendee's name is always shared. Add **Job title**, **Company** and **Email (only for attendees who opt in on My badge)**.
- **Printed badges** — also accept badges printed by your registration provider: the **Sessionboard check-in QR (from the confirmation email)**, the **Badge number (from registration sync)** or the **Registration provider's attendee ID**.
- **Consent text on My badge** — what attendees read above their badge QR, in English, German, Spanish and French. Leave a language empty to use the default.
- **Exhibitors see the activity of attendees who scan in (default)** — for attendees who haven't chosen on **My badge**, whether teams that scan them see their activity at this event (see [What a team sees about a lead](#what-a-team-sees-about-a-lead)). Off unless you turn it on.

Changes take effect on the next scan and the next time a team opens its leads.

<Warning>
Badge numbers can be sequential and guessable. Accept them only if your printed badges carry them; scans are limited per staff member.
</Warning>

## Seats per team

**Exhibitors and sponsors** lists every team with its **Access**, **Seats**, **Leads** and **Last scan**:

![The Exhibitors and sponsors table with Access set to Event default or Licensed, seats, lead counts by rating, last scan and a download button per team](/images/kb/attend-leads-teams.png)

- **Access** — **Event default**, **Licensed** or **Off**.
- **Seats** — how many staff can scan. Leave it blank to use the event default.
- Open a team to see who holds its seats. **Release** a seat to free it for someone else; that person's leads stay with the team.

The totals at the top show **Leads**, **Hot leads**, **Teams scanning** and **Seats in use**.

## Live

**Live** shows the show floor right now, refreshed every 30 seconds while the tab is open:

- **Leads last hour**, **Leads today**, **Leads in total**, **Active scanners** (staff who scanned in the last hour) and **Quiet booths**.
- **Teams on the floor**: every sponsor and exhibitor with its leads in the last hour, today and in total, active scanners and last scan. Sort by any column; select a company to open its record.
- **Quiet booths only** shows teams that have lead retrieval on but no scan in the last hour, so floor staff know which booths need help.

Live shows counts only, never attendee names. The counts are the same leads the **Teams** tab and the export show.

## What a team sees about a lead

Besides the lead itself, staff can see what the attendee did at this event under **At this event** on the lead in the app:

- **Sessions attended**, with the team's own sessions tagged **Your session**.
- **Saved your company** and **Saved your products**.
- How often they viewed the company's page.
- **Meetings with your team**.

Teams only see this when the attendee allows it: **Exhibitors who scan me can see the sessions I attended** on **My badge**, or your default under **Settings** for attendees who haven't chosen. It covers this event only and never shows another team's leads or notes. Without it, the app and the export show nothing about the attendee's activity.

## Export leads

**Export all leads** downloads one CSV for every team; **Export** on a team downloads just theirs. The export follows the same sharing rules as the app. It also has **Sessions attended**, **Your sessions attended**, **Saved your company**, **Saved products**, **Profile views** and **Meetings** columns, filled only for attendees who share their activity.

## Attendee privacy

- Teams see an attendee's email only while that attendee shares it — **Share my email with exhibitors** on **My badge**. Turning it off later hides it from leads already taken.
- Teams see an attendee's event activity only while that attendee shares it — **Exhibitors who scan me can see the sessions I attended** on **My badge**. Turning it off later hides it from leads already taken.
- Removing a field under **What a scan shares** hides it from every lead and export at once.
- An attendee can **Get a new code** on **My badge**; the old code stops working, and teams that already scanned them keep their lead.
- Attendees who withdrew consent can't be scanned.

<Tip>
Leads are a paid add-on in most prospectuses: license seats per team here rather than raising the default for everyone.
</Tip>
