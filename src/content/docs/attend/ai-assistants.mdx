Attendees who already use Claude or ChatGPT can connect it to your event and ask it about their own day: "What's on my agenda this afternoon?", "Summarize the keynote's takeaways", "Which sessions on cardiology still count for credit?". The assistant reads the event as that attendee, through the Attend app sign-in, and can't change anything.

<Note>
AI assistants are rolling out to Attend events. If you don't see the **AI assistants** switch in **Attend → Mobile App**, ask your Sessionboard contact to turn it on for your event.
</Note>

## Why use it

Attendees are already asking AI assistants to plan their day. Connecting the assistant to your event means the answers come from your program, your published recaps and the attendee's own schedule, not from guesses. Nothing to install: attendees use the assistant they have.

## What the assistant can read

Only this event, and only what the attendee can already see in the app:

- the public program: sessions, times, rooms, tracks, topics and speakers;
- the attendee's **My schedule**, saved moments and continuing-education credits;
- the session recaps you published, with their transcripts.

It can't change anything, see other attendees, read messages, claim credits, or read anything you put off the record. A recap you haven't published, or a session marked off the record, is never sent.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Attend → Mobile App** and, under **Access**, **Sign-in & privacy**.
2. Turn on **Let attendees use this event in Claude / ChatGPT**.
3. Click **Save**.

The event app must be on too. Attendees then see **Use in Claude / ChatGPT** under **Me** in the app.

## How attendees connect

In the app, under **Me → Use in Claude / ChatGPT**, the attendee:

1. Copies the connector URL and adds it to the assistant as a custom connector (in Claude: **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector**; in ChatGPT: **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Create**).
2. When the assistant opens the Sessionboard sign-in page, taps **Get a connection code** in the app and types the code there. A code works once, for 10 minutes.

The connection lasts until 30 days after the event, or until the attendee disconnects it.

## Disconnect and turn off

- An attendee disconnects one assistant, or all of them, under **Me → Use in Claude / ChatGPT**. The assistant stops reading the event at once.
- Turning the switch off disconnects every attendee's assistant at once. Turning it back on doesn't reconnect them; each attendee connects again with a new code.
- Cancelling or transferring a registration ends that person's connections too.

<Tip>
Every connection, code and disconnect is listed in **History → Attendees** under **Sign-in**, so you can see who connected an assistant and when.
</Tip>
