**Attend → Posters** (the page is titled **Posters & abstracts**) puts your ePosters and abstracts in the event app. Attendees browse, search and filter them, open an abstract and zoom into the poster, save the ones they want to see, and find the poster board on the venue map.

The app takes its posters and settings from one [Poster Gallery](/marketing/poster-gallery), so you set them up once, in the gallery, for both the web and the app.

## Why use it

At a scientific meeting the poster hall is a big part of the program, and attendees want to read abstracts before they walk the boards. Posters in the app work offline, respect the embargo your authors and journals expect, and point straight to the right board — and because the app follows your gallery, there is one set of rules to get right, not two.

## Turn it on

1. Turn on **Show posters & abstracts in the app**.
2. Pick the **Poster gallery**. The app takes its posters and settings from it: title, fields, search and filters, download and sharing, author consent and embargo. Picking it shares it with signed-in registrants, whatever its web access. If the event has one gallery, it is picked for you.
3. Optionally **Also list** poster sessions and abstracts (below).
4. Set how board numbers work, then **Save**.

To list poster sessions and abstracts without poster files, pick **No gallery (abstracts only)**: the app then uses standard settings — search on, track and format filters, downloads allowed — and no embargo.

<Note>
The app must be on for attendees to see posters. Turn it on in [**Mobile App → Sign-in & privacy**](/attend/mobile-app#sign-in-and-privacy).
</Note>

## What the app inherits from the gallery

Under **Inherited from** *gallery name*, the page shows the gallery's settings the app follows. To change any of them, click **Edit gallery settings** and change them in the gallery (**Publish → Posters**); the app picks them up.

- **Access** — registrants signed in to the app always see the gallery, whatever its web access.
- **Embargo** — hidden or titles only, until when. Poster sessions and abstracts in the app follow it too: while it holds, the poster file, abstract text and authors are withheld everywhere the app can show them, including the agenda.
- **Author consent** — when on, only posters with recorded consent are shown.
- **Fields** — which fields show on cards and on the detail screen, plus the disclosure field.
- **Search and filters** — search on or off, filters (track, format, poster session, topics), grouping and default sort.
- **Download and sharing** — download, share, add to calendar, bookmarks and questions.
- **Which posters** — the gallery's content filters narrow which posters it shows.

A gallery that is off still lends its settings, but the app shows no poster files until you turn it on.

## Also list poster sessions and abstracts

An item in the app is one abstract or poster. Besides the gallery's posters, you can list:

- **Poster sessions** — sessions of the formats you pick are poster sessions: their sub-sessions are the boards. A session of that format without sub-sessions is a poster itself.
- **Accepted abstracts not yet placed** — abstract submissions with status Accepted that are not in a poster session yet.

A poster file shows on an item when the gallery has one for it. Drafts, withdrawn work, and abstracts that are pending, declined or in a decision queue are never shown.

## Board numbers and the venue map

**Board number** sets where an item's board label comes from: the **Number inside its poster session**, the **Program number** or **A session field**, with an optional **Prefix** (P12, for example).

Turn on **Boards are booths on the venue map** if you draw each board as a booth numbered like the label on a published floor map; **Find poster board** in the app then opens the map at that board. Otherwise the app points to the poster session's room.

<Tip>
Abstracts are never part of the app's offline program download — each opens on demand — so even a 3,000-abstract meeting syncs quickly. Attendees find **Posters & abstracts** on **Today** and on any home tile or tab you point at it in [**Mobile App**](/attend/mobile-app).
</Tip>
