**Ask** is an AI assistant inside the event app. Attendees ask in their own words — "What's on about heart failure this afternoon?", "Where is booth 214?", "Add the closing keynote to my schedule" — and get an answer that cites the sessions, speakers, exhibitors or map pins it used. **Attend → Ask** turns it on, sets its limits and shows how it is used.

## Why use it

Attendees ask the same questions at the help desk all day. Ask answers them from your own program — on the phone, in their language, at 7 a.m. — and every answer links straight to the session or booth, so attendees can trust it and act on it. The questions it couldn't answer tell you what content people were looking for.

## What Ask answers from

Ask answers only from what the app already shows: published sessions, public speakers, exhibitors and sponsors and their products, posters, venue maps, the text and links on your Home and Info pages, and the attendee's own schedule. Every answer cites the items it used, and each citation opens that item in the app.

- It never sees other attendees, so it can't reveal anyone who isn't in the directory.
- Embargoed abstracts and hidden speakers stay hidden.
- It can add a session to the attendee's **My schedule** when asked.
- It answers in the language the attendee asks in.

## Turn it on

Under **Assistant**:

![The Assistant card with Turn on Ask in the app switched on, Questions per attendee per day, Daily AI budget (USD) and Add a starter question](/images/kb/attend-ask-assistant.png)

1. Turn on **Turn on Ask in the app**. This shows the Ask tab, the **Ask anything** home block and **Ask about this** on sessions, speakers, exhibitors and posters.
2. Set **Questions per attendee per day** (40 by default), counted per event day in the event time zone.
3. Set **Daily AI budget (USD)** (25 by default). Ask pauses for everyone when the last 24 hours of AI cost reach this amount.
4. Optionally write up to six **Starter questions** shown on the Ask tab. Leave them empty to use the built-in ones.
5. Click **Save**.

Then add Ask as a tab or a home block in [**Mobile App → App design**](/attend/mobile-app#choose-the-tabs).

The phone preview in **Mobile App** doesn't ask questions: with Ask on, its Ask tab says so and that attendees use it in the app. Try Ask in the app itself.

<Note>
Ask uses AI, so it needs AI features turned on for your organization. If they are off, turn them on in **Organization settings** before turning on Ask. Ask's usage counts toward your organization's AI credits.
</Note>

## Usage

**Usage (last 7 days)** shows counts and AI spend only — attendees' questions are not shown:

- **Questions today** and **Questions** over seven days, with how many attendees asked.
- **AI cost, last 24 h** against your daily budget, and **AI cost, 7 days**.
- **Answered without a source** — usually "couldn't find that": content attendees looked for that isn't in the app.
- **Added to My schedule** and **Hit a limit**.
- **Most cited** — the sessions, speakers, exhibitors and pages Ask pointed people to most.

<Tip>
Check **Answered without a source** during the event. If attendees keep asking about parking or Wi-Fi, add a Text block to your Info page and Ask will start answering it.
</Tip>
