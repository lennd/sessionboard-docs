**Attend → Showrooms** (the page is titled **Exhibitor showrooms**) builds each sponsor's and exhibitor's page in the event app: what they show, their products, deals for the show and who to talk to. Attendees browse and search them, save the ones they want to visit, and the app plans a walking route between them.

## Why use it

A logo and a booth number don't bring anyone to a stand. A showroom gives each exhibitor a real storefront — products with photos, a show-only discount, the people at the booth — that attendees can find by searching for what they need. Visits and saves land in the sponsor's [analytics](/attend/analytics), which is what exhibitors ask about when they decide whether to book again.

## Who is listed

Every sponsor and exhibitor you add to the event under **CRM** appears here, A–Z. Search by name, booth, category or product. Each row shows the booth, product and photo counts and how many sections are filled in.

The account keeps its own details — name, logo, website, description, phone, address, social links — and the booth comes from your published floor map. Edit those on the account and the floor map; the showroom adds what the app shows beside them.

## Fill in a showroom

Open a sponsor or exhibitor and fill in **Page in the app**:

![A searchable list of four exhibitors with booths and completeness, beside BDS Global Inc.'s Page in the app with Tagline, Video link, Categories and Featured](/images/kb/attend-showrooms-editor.png)

- **Tagline** — one line about what they show.
- **Video link** — an https link.
- **Categories** — type a category and press Enter. Attendees can browse the directory by category.
- **Featured** — listed first in the exhibitor directory and in featured rails.
- **Photos** — https links with captions.
- **Contacts** — name, job title, email and phone.
- **Show deals** — a deal, an optional code, an end date, a link and details. The app hides a deal once it ends.
- **Facts** — short label and value pairs, such as *Founded · 1984* or *Made in · Italy*.

Click **Save**.

<Warning>
Every attendee can see a showroom's **Contacts**. Add only people who agreed to be contacted — these are not the account's CRM contacts.
</Warning>

## Add products

Under **Products**, **Add a product** with a name, category, description and up to eight photos (https links; the first photo is the cover). Mark the best ones **Featured product** and reorder them with the arrows. Attendees can browse, search and save products in the app. Deleting a product removes it from the app the next time attendees sync, and drops saves of it.

## What attendees can do with showrooms

![The Exhibitors page in the Attend app with search, a Saved filter, category chips, A–Z and By booth sorting and Plan my route](/images/kb/attend-app-exhibitor-directory.png)

- Search exhibitors by name, booth, tier, category, tagline, location or product name.
- Save an exhibitor or a product, and keep private notes and photos on their phone.
- Tap **Show on map** to find the booth on the venue map.
- Request a meeting or send a message to the team, when [networking](/attend/networking) is on.
- Open **My route**: the app plans a walk through the saved exhibitors and sponsors they haven't visited yet, level by level and across stairs and elevators.

<Tip>
The **Expo** template pack puts exhibitors first, with an exhibitor directory, a **Products** page and a **Scan** tab. Pick it in [**Mobile App**](/attend/mobile-app#choose-a-template-pack) for a trade show.
</Tip>

<Note>
**Fill exhibitor showrooms** on the [Attend overview](/attend/overview) checklist is optional and ticks off once at least one showroom has content.
</Note>
