**Attend → Engagement** (the page is titled **Live engagement**) is where you switch on what attendees can do inside a session from the app — ask and upvote questions, vote in polls, fill in the session evaluation — and where moderators run them while sessions are on.

## Why use it

No more passing microphones or a separate polling tool per room: every session gets Q&A, polls and its evaluation in the same app attendees already use for the agenda. Moderation keeps the questions on topic, and evaluations answered in the app count as the evaluation for CE credit, so nobody fills one in twice.

## Turn it on

<Tip>
Session check-in, seat reservations and CE quizzes are on the **Check-in & seats** tab of this page — see [Run session check-in, seat reservations and CE quizzes](/attend/checkin-and-seats).

Points, quests, booth QR posters and the leaderboard are on the **Gamification** tab — see [Run points, quests and a leaderboard in the Attend app](/attend/gamification).
</Tip>

Open the **Settings** tab:

- **Q&A** — attendees ask questions in each session and upvote other people's. Choose the **Moderation**:
  - **Approve first** — questions wait for a moderator before anyone else sees them.
  - **Show right away** — questions appear immediately; a moderator can hide them later.
- **Allow anonymous questions** — attendees can hide their name. Staff do not see who asked either.
- **Polls** — speakers and staff run single choice, multiple choice and word cloud polls.
- **Session evaluations** — attendees fill in an evaluation survey in the app near the end of each session.

### Which survey each session gets

Evaluations are [Surveys](/evaluations/surveys). Under **Which survey each session gets**, add a rule per survey and pick the session formats it covers. The first rule that matches a session's format wins; a rule with no formats covers all sessions. With no rules, sessions get no evaluation in the app.

- **Opens before the session ends (minutes)** — the evaluation opens this many minutes before the scheduled end (10 by default).
- **Reminder push** — sends a push when the evaluation opens to attendees who saved the session or took part in it.

<Note>
Sessions with CE credit that require an evaluation always use the claim site's evaluation survey, whatever the rules say. A response in the app counts as the evaluation when the attendee claims credit.
</Note>

## Moderate a session

The **Sessions** tab lists every session with its waiting questions and open polls. Search for one and open it. Each session has three tabs.

### Q&A

Questions are grouped as **Pending** (oldest first), **Live** (most upvoted first), **Answered** and **Hidden**. On each question:

![A session's Q&A tab with Q&A open, the Pending, Live, Answered and Hidden filters, and a pending question with Approve and Hide buttons](/images/kb/attend-engagement-qa-queue.png)

- **Approve** puts a pending question live for everyone.
- **Hide** removes it from attendees; it is kept under **Hidden**.
- **Mark answered** moves it to **Answered**; **Back to live** undoes that.
- **Back to pending** returns it to the queue.

The **Q&A open** switch opens or closes Q&A for that one session. The page refreshes every few seconds and announces new questions waiting for approval.

Attendees see a question only once it is approved (or right away with **Show right away**); the person who asked always sees their own.

### Polls

<Steps>

<Step title="Click New poll">

Write the **Question** and pick the **Type**: **Single choice**, **Multiple choice** or **Word cloud** (attendees answer with a short word or phrase, up to 40 characters). Choice polls take 2–10 options; for multiple choice, set **Most choices per attendee**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose who sees results">

**Results**: **Attendees see results live**, **Attendees see results after it closes** or **Results hidden from attendees**. You always see them.

</Step>

<Step title="Save, then open it in the session">

The poll is saved as a draft. Click **Open** when you are ready for attendees to vote; **Close** ends voting, and **Reopen** starts it again. Speakers on the session can open and close polls from the app too.

</Step>

</Steps>

Each attendee votes once per poll.

### Evaluation

Shows which survey the session uses and how many responses it has. **See results in Surveys** opens the full results.

<Tip>
Speakers see their own session's evaluation results in the app after the session ends, once it has at least three responses — never a single answer that could identify someone.
</Tip>
