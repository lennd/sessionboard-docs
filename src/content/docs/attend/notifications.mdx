**Attend → Notifications** sends push messages to attendees' phones. Every notification also lands in the app's inbox (the bell on the home screen), so attendees who turned notifications off still see it there.

## Why use it

"Doors open in 15 minutes", "the keynote moved to Hall B", "lunch is served on level 2" — the messages that used to go out as emails nobody reads at the venue now reach the phone in their pocket, in their language, and only the people they concern. Opens are tracked, so you know whether it landed.

## Write a notification

<Steps>

<Step title="Click New notification">

The composer opens with a lock-screen **Preview** of the push beside it.

![The New notification composer with English and Spanish tabs, Title and Message fields, When tapped open, Who gets it with a live audience count, Sponsored by, and the Urgent switch with the daily cap and quiet hours](/images/kb/attend-notifications-composer.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Write the message">

Write a **Title** and **Message**. If your event has more than one language, write each one; a language left empty gets the default language's text.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose what a tap opens">

Under **When tapped, open**, pick **The notification (no link)**, **A session**, **A speaker**, **A sponsor or exhibitor**, **A page of the app** (Home, Today, Agenda, Ask, Credits, Me, People, Partners, Map, Info) or **A web address**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose who gets it">

Under **Who gets it**, pick an audience. The composer counts the people in it right now and how many of them have notifications on.

| Audience | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Every registrant of the event. |
| **Registrant types** | Registrants of the types you pick. |
| **Interested in tracks or topics** | Attendees who picked or saved those tracks or topics. |
| **Saved something** | Attendees who saved the sessions, speakers, sponsors, exhibitors, tracks or tags you pick — or any of them. |
| **Sponsor and exhibitor staff** | Team members of the sponsors and exhibitors you pick, or every team. |
| **Speakers** | Registrants who are on the program. |
| **Attended a session** | Attendees checked in to the sessions you pick. |
| **A list of people** | Email addresses you paste, one per line. |

</Step>

<Step title="Credit a sponsor (optional)">

**Sponsored by** credits a sponsor. Sends and opens then show in that sponsor's [analytics](/attend/analytics).

</Step>

<Step title="Test, then send or schedule">

**Send a test to me** delivers it to your own phone and inbox. To get tests, register for the event with your sign-in email and open the app.

Under **Delivery**, choose **Send now** or **Schedule for** a date and time, then click **Send** or **Schedule** and confirm. **Save draft** keeps it for later.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Quiet hours and the daily cap

To keep push useful, attendees get at most three pushes a day, and none overnight in the event's time zone — those wait until morning. The composer shows the exact hours for your event.

<Warning>
**Urgent** skips both the cap and quiet hours. Use it for safety and last-minute logistics only.
</Warning>

## Track and manage notifications

The list shows each notification's **Audience**, **Status** (**Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Sent**, **Canceled**) and **Reach** — recipients, and the share who opened and read it.

- **Unschedule** puts a scheduled notification back to draft.
- Deleting a sent notification removes it from every attendee's inbox. Pushes already on phones stay there.

<Note>
The app asks each attendee for permission to send notifications a few seconds after they sign in, never on first launch. Attendees can turn notifications on or off under **Me → Settings → Notifications**.
</Note>

<Tip>
**Set up notifications** on the [Attend overview](/attend/overview) checklist ticks off once you have sent or scheduled one — a scheduled welcome push the morning the event opens is a good first.
</Tip>
