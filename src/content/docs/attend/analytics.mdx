**Attend → Analytics** shows how attendees use the event app: how many installed it and signed in, which screens, sessions, speakers and partners they opened, what they saved, how many push messages they opened, and how every sponsor placement performed.

## Why use it

It answers the two questions you get asked after every event — "did people use the app?" and "what did sponsors get for their money?" — with numbers from the app itself. The per-sponsor report is the proof-of-value you hand a sponsor at renewal time, and the top sessions and speakers tell your program committee what drew a crowd.

## Pick a date range

Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days** (the default) or **Last 90 days**. Days are counted in the event's time zone.

## The totals

![Eight analytics tiles for the last 30 days — New installs, Attendees signed in, Daily active, App sessions, Push opens, Saves, Sponsor impressions and Sponsor taps](/images/kb/attend-analytics-totals.png)

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **New installs** | Phones that opened the app for the first time, with how many installs were active in the range. Signed-out use counts here too. |
| **Attendees signed in** | Registrants who signed in, and the total number of sign-ins. |
| **Daily active (avg)** | The average number of active installs per day. |
| **App sessions** | Times the app was opened, with total screen views. |
| **Push opens** | Push messages opened, out of how many were sent. |
| **Saves** | Sessions, speakers, exhibitors and other items attendees saved. |
| **Sponsor impressions** and **Sponsor taps** | Sponsor placements seen and tapped, with the tap-through rate. |

**Daily active** charts active installs and signed-in attendees for every day in the range.

## Top lists

**Top screens**, **Top sessions**, **Top speakers** and **Top sponsors & exhibitors** rank what attendees opened most, by views.

## Sponsor placements and sponsor reports

**Sponsor placements** lists every placement in the range — including ones nobody saw — with **Impressions**, **Reach**, **Taps** and **CTR**. **By sponsor** rolls everything up per sponsor: tier, placements, impressions, taps, page views of their page in the app and saves.

Click **Report for** a sponsor to see the same page filtered to that sponsor, titled *Sponsor name — sponsor report*, with impressions and taps by day. Use **Print** to save it as a PDF for the sponsor, or **Export CSV** for the raw numbers.

<Note>
An impression counts once a placement is at least half on screen for a second, and repeat views from the same phone within half an hour count once. Signed-out use is counted by install, never by person.
</Note>

Set up placements in [Sponsor placements](/attend/sponsor-ads).
