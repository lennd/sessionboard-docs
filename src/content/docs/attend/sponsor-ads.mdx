**Attend → Sponsor ads** (the page is titled **Sponsor placements**) puts your sponsors and exhibitors in the event app. Each placement is a partner, a spot in the app, a creative and where a tap goes. Impressions and taps are counted for each, so you can show sponsors what they got.

## Why use it

The app is the screen attendees look at most during the event, which makes it the most valuable sponsor inventory you have. Placements turn it into a line on your sponsor prospectus — sold by slot, scheduled by day, rotated fairly — with a per-sponsor report at the end instead of a guess.

## Where placements show

| Where it shows | What attendees see |
| --- | --- |
| **Splash · Presented by** | "Presented by" with the partner's logo or creative on the event's sign-in screen. |
| **Home · Title sponsor banner** | One banner in the home's **Sponsor banner** block. Highest priority wins; ties rotate by weight. |
| **Home · Banner carousel** | Every live placement in the home's **Sponsor carousel** block, swiped one at a time. |
| **Home · Sponsored tile** | A tile at the end of the home's first button grid. |
| **Agenda · Banner** | A banner at the top of the Agenda. |
| **Exhibitors · Banner** | A banner at the top of the Exhibitors and Partners pages. |
| **Partner lists · Promoted** | The partner first in partner lists, marked "Promoted". |

Every placement shows a visible **Sponsored** or **Promoted** label in the app.

<Note>
Home banner and carousel placements show where the app's home has a **Sponsor banner** or **Sponsor carousel** block. Add them in [**Mobile App**](/attend/mobile-app#lay-out-the-pages).
</Note>

## Add a placement

<Steps>

<Step title="Click New placement">

Pick the **Sponsor or exhibitor**. Placements promote partners already on this event — add sponsors or exhibitors to the event first.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose where it shows and the creative">

Pick **Where it shows**, then upload the **Creative** — recommended 1200 × 375 px (16:5), PNG or JPG, for banners, or 600 × 600 px for the tile. The splash and promoted slots can use the partner's logo instead. Add an optional **Line under the creative**, such as "Visit us at booth 214".

</Step>

<Step title="Choose what a tap opens">

**A tap opens** **The partner's page in the app**, **A web page** (https only), **A session**, or **Nothing (not tappable)**.

</Step>

<Step title="Schedule and rotate">

Set **Starts** and **Ends**, or leave them empty to run all the time. **Priority (0–100)** decides which shows first; among equal priority, **Rotation weight (1–100)** sets the share — weight 3 shows three times as often as weight 1. Turn on **Show in the app** and **Save**.

</Step>

</Steps>

Each placement's **Status** reads **Live**, **Scheduled**, **Ended** or **Off**.

## Track impressions and taps

**Impressions & taps** opens [Analytics](/attend/analytics#sponsor-placements-and-sponsor-reports), where every placement and every sponsor has its numbers and a printable sponsor report. An impression counts once the placement is at least half on screen for a second.

Deleting a placement stops it showing in the app; impressions and taps already counted stay in Analytics.

<Tip>
"Presented by" on a session is not a placement: it comes from the sponsors linked to that session, and its taps are counted in the sponsor's report too. To credit a sponsor on a push message, use **Sponsored by** in [Notifications](/attend/notifications).
</Tip>
