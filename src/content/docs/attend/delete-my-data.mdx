This page is for attendees who use the **Attend by Sessionboard** app on iPhone or Android. It explains how to ask us to delete the data the app keeps about you, and what happens when you do.

## Why use it

You can ask for your app data to be deleted at any time, during or after the event. You don't need the app installed to ask, and the organizer doesn't need to start the request for you.

## Ask for your data to be deleted

Use either of these:

- **Email** [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) from the email address you registered with. Name the event, and say you want your Attend app data deleted.
- **Fill in** the [Data Subject Access Request form](https://www.sessionboard.com/legal/data-subject-access-request). It is linked from our [Privacy Policy](https://www.sessionboard.com/legal/privacy-policy).

What happens next:

1. We confirm who you are. We may ask you to reply from your registered email address.
2. We work with the event's organizer, because your registration belongs to their event.
3. We delete your data and reply to confirm it is done.

Our [Data Subject Rights](https://www.sessionboard.com/legal/data-subject-rights) page says we make the necessary changes within 30 days, and that most requests are handled the same day.

## What is deleted

When we delete your Attend app data, we remove:

- Your saves, including **My schedule**.
- Your Q&A questions and upvotes, your poll votes, and your session survey answers (the **Evaluate** tab).
- Your conversations with **Ask**.
- Your messages and meeting requests.
- Your contact swaps in **People I met**.
- Your saved caption moments, and slide notes that were synced to our servers.
- Your profile photo and the profile changes you made in the app.
- The tokens we use to send you notifications.
- App usage analytics tied to you.

## What the organizer may keep

Some records belong to the organizer, not to the app:

- **Your registration.** It may be kept by the organizer. To have it removed, ask the organizer as well.
- **Credit and certificate records.** At events with continuing-education credit, these may be kept by the organizer for accreditation.

## Clear the app on your phone

You can also remove data from your phone yourself:

- **Me → Sign out** ends your session for that event and clears the event's data stored on the phone.
- **Deleting the app** removes the notes stored on your phone, such as poster notes and slide notes.

<Note>
Signing out and deleting the app only affect your phone. To delete the data on our servers, send a request as described above.
</Note>
