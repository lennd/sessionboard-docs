Contracts has four permissions. You assign them per role at two levels — on **organization roles** in **Organization Settings > Team**, and on **event roles** in **Event Team > Permissions > Contracts** — so the organization keeps control of templates and settings while each event team runs the agreements for its own event.

## Why use it

A speaker agreement is a legal commitment on behalf of your organization, so who can send, change, void, and delete one should be deliberate. Event-level permissions let a session or sponsor manager send and track the agreements for their event without an organization-wide role, and keep them out of other events' contracts.

## The four permissions

| Permission | Allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Can view contracts** | See the Contracts section on records, the signer status and activity, download the signed PDF, and open **CRM > Contracts** (the organization-wide list, scoped to your events when the permission comes from an event role). |
| **Can create contracts (send for e-signature or upload)** | Send with Sessionboard e-sign or via Docusign, upload an already-signed PDF, and connect your own Docusign account. |
| **Can edit contract metadata, assignments, and resend** | Edit a contract's details and event assignments, resend reminders, and replace the PDF of an uploaded contract. |
| **Can delete or void contracts** | Void a contract that is awaiting signature, delete a contract record, and restore a deleted one. |

## Organization roles

Set in **Organization Settings > Team**, an organization role's Contracts permissions apply to every contract in the organization, with or without an event.

| Organization role | View | Create | Edit | Delete or void |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Admin Lite** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Program Manager** | No | No | No | No |
| **Custom roles** | As you set them | | | |

## Event roles

Set in **Event Team > Permissions**, under **Contracts**. An event role's Contracts permissions apply to contracts tied to that event — sent from a pipeline entry, contact, account, or group with that event chosen.

| Event role | View | Create | Edit | Delete or void |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Admin Lite** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Session Manager** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Sponsor Manager** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Exhibitor Manager** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Every other standard role** | No | No | No | No |
| **Custom roles** | As you set them | | | |

<Note>
The Contracts group appears in **Event Team > Permissions** for every event of an organization that has Contracts. Turn the switches on for a custom role, or off for a standard one, like any other permission group.
</Note>

## How the two levels combine

- A team member can act on a contract when **either** their organization role **or** their event role for the contract's event allows the action. An event-only team member with **Can create contracts** on their event role can send an agreement from a pipeline entry in that event; they cannot send one for another event, or one with no event at all.
- **CRM > Contracts** shows every contract in the organization to someone whose view permission comes from an organization role. When it comes from event roles only, the list shows the contracts tied to those events.
- A contract with **no event** — sent at organization scope — is managed by organization roles only.
- **Templates** (**CRM > Contracts > Templates**) are organization assets: viewing them uses the view permission at either level, so an event team member can pick a template when sending, but creating, editing, publishing, archiving, and deleting a template needs the permission on an organization role.
- **Contract settings** — default way to send, reminders, link expiry, countersigner, consent text, auto-advance — and **disconnecting another person's Docusign connection** use the organization's manage-app-connections permission, the same one that connects other integrations. Connecting your **own** Docusign account needs **Can create contracts** at either level.
- The **portal** is separate from team permissions: a participant sees a contract under **My Contracts** when it is addressed to them, when they are a signer on it, or, for an account contract, when they are the account's primary contact. See [Let signers sign from their portal](/speaker-crm/contracts#let-signers-sign-from-their-portal).

<Tip>
Give the people who negotiate agreements **Can create** and **Can edit** on their event role, and keep **Can delete or void** on the organization Admin role, so a sent agreement is only withdrawn by someone who can see every contract.
</Tip>

## Still having issues?

If a team member sees the Contracts tab but every action fails with "not allowed", check their event role under **Event Team > Permissions > Contracts** and their organization role under **Organization Settings > Team** — one of the two needs the permission for the action. If the Contracts group is missing from the event permissions screen, the organization does not have Contracts: it is included with CRM, so contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) or your customer success manager.
