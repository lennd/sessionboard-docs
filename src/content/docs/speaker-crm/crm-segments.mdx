## Create a segment

Filter the directory first — see [Speaker CRM: advanced search](/speaker-crm/crm-advanced-search) — then click **Save Segment** in the toolbar.

Name the segment, check the filter and match counts at the bottom of the panel, and pick the kind of list:

- **Save Dynamic Segment** — the segment stores the filters. Anyone who matches them later joins it automatically, and anyone who stops matching drops out.
- **Save Curated List** — the segment stores the contacts that match right now. It does not change until you add or remove people yourself.

## Open a segment

**CRM > Segments** lists every segment with its contact count. A lightning icon marks a dynamic segment; a list icon marks a curated list. Search by name, or change the order with the sort button (**A - Z (Name)**, **Z - A (Name)**, **Last Updated**, **Newest to Oldest**).

Click a segment to open it in the directory. A banner above the table names the segment and its type; click **Clear segment filter** to return to the full directory.

## Change a dynamic segment's filters

With a dynamic segment open, click **Filter** and adjust the facets. The toolbar's **Save Segment** button then opens a menu: **Save Changes** overwrites the segment's filters, **Save As…** keeps the original and creates a new segment from the new filters, and **Revert Changes** puts the saved filters back.

## Add a contact to a curated list

From the directory:

1. Tick the checkbox at the start of each contact's row.
2. In the bulk action bar, click **Add To Curated List**.
3. Choose the list and click **Add to selected list**.

From a contact's profile, open the **CRM** tab. The **Segments** card lists every segment the contact is in; click **Manage Lists** to tick or untick the curated lists they belong to, then click **Save**. Unticking a list is how you remove someone from it.

## Edit, duplicate or delete a segment

On **CRM > Segments**, open the **⋯** menu on a segment card:

- **Edit** — rename the segment.
- **Duplicate** — copy it, filters or members included.
- **Refresh counts** — recount the contacts in a dynamic segment.
- **Delete** — removes the segment only. Deleting a segment does not delete any contacts.
