Interest Forms collect proposals year-round, before you have decided which event they belong to. An **evaluation plan on the form** lets a committee score those proposals where they land — in your Speaker CRM Pipeline — using the same round-based model as event evaluations. Nothing has to be assigned to an event first.

<Note>
Interest Forms are part of Speaker CRM. Evaluation plans on them need both Speaker CRM and Sessions on your organization, and the person creating the plan needs Interest Forms access.
</Note>

## Why use it

When you run a multi-round evaluation on Interest Form submissions, you often want each round to see only a specific slice of submissions — for example, only those with a particular status. The new **By submission filters** card on the **Assignments** step lets you apply the same filter logic you already use in event evaluations, so reviewers only see the submissions relevant to their round. Separately, if you previously set a **Votes per voter** limit and later decided to remove it, the limit was silently kept because the cleared value was dropped before saving; that is now fixed so clearing the field reliably removes the cap.

## Where to find it

There are two doors into the same plans:

- **Per form:** open **CRM → Interest Forms**, pick a form, and use its **Evaluation** tab. This is where you create the plan for that form.
- **Across forms:** the **Evaluations** tab on **CRM → Interest Forms** (next to Forms, Submissions and Speakers) lists every evaluation plan on every Interest Form in the organization with its status, rounds, progress and deadline. Search by plan or form name and click through to a plan.

![Evaluations tab on Speaker Interest Forms listing a plan with its Interest Form, status, rounds, progress and deadline](/images/kb/interest-forms-evaluations-tab.png)

Each Interest Form carries **one** evaluation plan. If you need a second pass, add a round to the plan rather than a second plan.

## What is the same as event evaluations

If you have built an event evaluation plan, the wizard is familiar — Overview, Rounds, Evaluators, Assignments, and Ballot when a round uses Public voting — and the reviewer side is identical. The parts of [Round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations) that apply unchanged:

- **Rounds** with their own review window, scorecard, anonymization and evaluator pool, run as a Funnel or in Parallel.
- **Scorecards** with rating, text, dropdown and file questions; points or percentage scoring; abstaining with an optional reason.
- Reviewer view configuration — which form fields evaluators see, which appear on the submission card, and whether speaker details are hidden (blind review).
- Assignment logic — All to All, Per Submission, Per Reviewer, Per Submission and Per Reviewer, or Individual Reviewer pairs, with *add*, *replace all* or *replace pending* when you re-run it.
- Summary, exports and reminders — per-round progress and evaluator activity on the plan's Summary, the Individual Grade and Cumulative reports, and due-soon reminders sent in your organization's timezone.
- Public voting rounds, including the random-ballot option described under [Community polling](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#ballot-show-all-sessions-or-a-random-subset), and the [Ballot step](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#step-5-ballot) that puts submissions in front of voters. On an Interest Form the ballot pool is **All submissions in plan scope** or **Individual submissions** — there are no field filters. [Who can vote](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#who-can-vote-eligible-voter-lists) — the eligible-voter list, ticket-holder weighting and **Reset ballots** — works the same way as on an event plan, and the round's results table shows **Weighted avg** and **Distinct voters** with the **All voters / Ticket holders / Community** filter.

## What is different

| | Event evaluation plan | Interest Form evaluation plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What gets scored | Sessions in one event | Submissions to one Interest Form |
| Fields evaluators see | Session fields | The Interest Form's fields |
| Which submissions are in the pool | Session filters, tracks, formats | Submission **status** (Submitted, Pending and Accepted by default; narrow to any set) |
| Evaluate parents or subsessions | Yes | Not applicable — Interest Form submissions have no subsessions |
| Session builds and recommendations | Yes | No — accept or decline in the Pipeline instead |
| Conflict-of-interest declarations | Yes | No |
| Where evaluators work | Your event's portal | Your organization-level Site's portal |
| Public voting link | **Public voting** banner on the round; **Create a Site to share** if the event has none | **Public voting** banner on the round, pointing at the organization Site that lists this Interest Form. If no Site lists the form yet, add the form to a Site first — see [Setting up your Sessionboard site](/awards/setting-up-your-sessionboard-site) |

## Set up a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the plan from the form">
    Open the Interest Form, choose the **Evaluation** tab, and click **Create evaluation plan**. Give it a name, instructions for evaluators, a review deadline, and choose points or percentage scoring. The deadline is set and shown in your organization's timezone, with its abbreviation, on the plan list, the form's Evaluation tab and the evaluator's portal card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the submission pool">
    Pick which submission statuses are in scope. By default the plan scores everything that is Submitted, Pending or Accepted; leave drafts out unless you want evaluators to see unfinished proposals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the rounds">
    Add at least one round with a review window and a scorecard. Choose which form fields evaluators can see and which two or three show on the submission card, and turn on blind review if speaker names should stay hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add evaluators">
    Add evaluators by email. Anyone who is not already a member of your organization is added with the **Evaluator** role, which gives read-only access to Interest Forms and nothing else in your organization. Existing members keep their current role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign and open">
    Run the assignment step for each round, then **Open** the plan. Opening sends the invitation email to the round roster; you can resend it later to only the evaluators who have not received one yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Duplicate a plan to reuse its settings, rounds and scorecards on another Interest Form. Evaluators, assignments and scores are not copied, and the copy starts closed.
</Tip>

## The evaluator experience

Evaluators open the portal of your **organization-level Site** — the same Site your Interest Form is published on — and find their rounds under **My Reviews**, labelled with the form's name. The review workspace lists their assigned submissions with the card fields you chose, and the review form shows the visible Interest Form fields, the speaker details unless the round is blind, and the scorecard. Saving works one submission at a time or in bulk, and Jump To / prev-next move through the assigned list.

If the Site is attached to an event, it shows that event's session evaluations instead. Publish the Interest Form on an organization-level Site so evaluators see the form's rounds.

## After scoring

Scores stay with the submission in the Pipeline. Use the plan's **Summary** for completion and averages, export the Individual Grade or Cumulative report for the committee, and move accepted proposals forward the way you already do — advance them through Pipeline stages or [assign them to an event](/speaker-crm/pipeline) when the program is ready.
