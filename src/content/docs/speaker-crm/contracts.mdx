Contracts lets your team send speaker, sponsor, and exhibitor agreements for e-signature without leaving the record they are working on, across your organization and in every event. You can send with **Sessionboard e-sign** — your own templates, ordered signers, and a hosted signing page, with no external account — or through **Docusign** from each administrator's connected account. Either way, Sessionboard pre-fills the agreement from the contact, account, session, and event data it already holds, tracks who has signed, and stores the signed PDF on the record.

<Note>
Contracts is included with the **CRM** product. If your organization has CRM, it is already on under **CRM > Contracts**; if you do not see it, contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) or your customer success manager about adding CRM.
</Note>

## Why keep contracts in Sessionboard

A speaker agreement signed in a stand-alone e-signature tool is a PDF in someone's inbox. The same agreement sent from Sessionboard is part of the record:

- **One record, no retyping.** The agreement is filled from the contact, account, session, and event you already manage — names, titles, session details, dates, fees, custom properties. Nobody copies a speaker's name into a template, so nobody gets it wrong.
- **One list for every agreement.** **CRM > Contracts** shows every contract in your organization — sent with Sessionboard e-sign, sent through Docusign, uploaded after being signed elsewhere, or imported from your Docusign history — with the same statuses and the same actions.
- **Status without chasing.** Who has signed, who is next, who viewed and did not sign, and when reminders went out are on the row. Signers are reminded automatically on the cadence you set.
- **The signed PDF where the work happens.** The completed document, with its signing certificate, lands on the contact, account, group, or pipeline entry it belongs to, next to the session, the portal tasks, and the emails.
- **The pipeline moves itself.** When every signer has signed, the linked [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline) entry can advance to the stage you choose — a confirmed speaker lands in **Confirmed** without anyone dragging a card.
- **Signers sign where they already are.** Speakers, sponsors, and exhibitors see their pending agreements under **My Contracts** in their portal and sign from there, or from the email — either way the record updates.
- **Who may do what, at both levels.** Contracts permissions are set per role on the organization and on each event, so an event team can run its own agreements while templates and settings stay with the organization. See [Who can manage contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts-permissions).

## Two ways to send

Both paths start from the same **Add Contract** dialog and end with the same tracked contract and signed PDF on the record. Choose per contract, and set the default tab in [Contract settings](#contract-settings).

| | **Sessionboard e-sign** | **Docusign** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you need | Nothing outside Sessionboard | A Docusign eSignature account, connected by each administrator who sends |
| Where the agreement is written | [Contract templates](/speaker-crm/contract-templates) under **CRM > Contracts > Templates**, with `{{variables}}`, conditional clauses, and signer roles | Templates in your Docusign account, with **Text** fields whose data labels match [Sessionboard variables](/speaker-crm/contract-template-variables) |
| Signers | As many roles as the template defines, in order or all at once; roles fill from the record, your countersigner, the sender, or are typed when sending | The template's signer, filled from the contact (or the account's primary contact); change the name and email when sending |
| Where they sign | A Sessionboard signing page branded for the event or organization, with Draw or Type signature, fields, and a consent box | Docusign's signing experience, from the Docusign email or from the portal |
| Pre-fill | Every variable in the document and the email | Every text field whose data label is a variable; review and edit values before sending |
| Status and PDF | Immediate; the PDF is stamped with every signature and a signing certificate | Within moments of Docusign's notification; the completed Docusign PDF is stored |
| Reminders and expiry | Automatic reminders and link expiry from [Contract settings](#contract-settings) | Docusign's reminders; resend the notification from Sessionboard |
| Good for | Speaker agreements, releases, consent forms, sponsor and exhibitor agreements your team controls end to end | Organizations whose legal team already keeps agreements, branding, and audit requirements in Docusign |

## Where contracts live

Each contract belongs to a **contact** or an **account**, and can be tied to an **event**. You can open the **Contracts** section from:

- A contact's profile in **CRM > Contacts** (on the **CRM** tab)
- An account's profile
- A group (sponsor or exhibitor) profile
- A prospect's card in the **Pipeline**

**CRM > Contracts** lists every contract across your organization; its **Templates** tab holds your [Sessionboard e-sign templates](/speaker-crm/contract-templates), and its **Settings** button holds the org-wide e-sign defaults described in [Contract settings](#contract-settings).

![The Contracts tab on a pipeline entry showing one signed contract and one awaiting signature](/images/kb/contracts-tab-pipeline-entry.png)

## Send with Sessionboard e-sign

Open the record, click **Add Contract**, choose the **Send with Sessionboard** tab, pick a published template and the event, check the signers, and click **Send for signature**. Each signer gets an email with a personal signing link to a Sessionboard signing page. The full steps, what the signer sees, and what happens when the last signature lands are in [Send with Sessionboard e-sign](/speaker-crm/send-with-sessionboard-esign).

## Send a contract via Docusign

With a Docusign account connected, choose the **Send via Docusign** tab, pick a Docusign template and the event, review the fields Sessionboard pre-filled, and click **Send via Docusign**. Docusign emails the signer; the status and signed PDF come back to Sessionboard automatically. See [Send via Docusign](/speaker-crm/send-via-docusign) and, to connect an account, [Docusign](/apps/docusign-integration).

## Upload an existing contract

For agreements signed on paper or through another tool, choose the **Upload existing** tab in **Add Contract**:

1. Click **Choose PDF** and pick the signed document.
2. Enter a **Title**, choose the **Event**, and enter the **Signer name** and **Signer email**.
3. Enter the **Signed date**. Optionally add an **Effective date**, **Expiry date**, and **Notes**.
4. Save. The contract appears with the **Uploaded** source and the **Signed** status, and the PDF is available to download.

## Track who has signed

A contract sent with Sessionboard e-sign shows its signer progress on the row — for example **0 of 2 signed · waiting on Jessica Tucker** — next to its status. Click the arrow at the end of the row to expand **Details**:

- **Signers**, in signing order, each with their role and status — **Waiting**, **Invited**, **Viewed**, **Signed**, or **Declined** — plus when they were invited, viewed or signed, the decline reason if they gave one, and how many reminders they have received.
- The **template** name and version the contract was sent from, and whether it signs in order or all at once.
- The **void reason**, when the contract was voided.
- **Recent activity**: created, sent, signer invited, viewed, signed, reminders sent, completed, declined, or voided, each with a timestamp.

![A contract row showing Needs signature, the Sessionboard source badge and "0 of 2 signed · waiting on Jessica Tucker", expanded to list the two signers with their statuses, the template version, and the recent activity](/images/kb/contracts-row-signer-progress-details.png)

## Contract statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs signature** | Sent and waiting on one or more signers. |
| **Signed** | Every signer has signed. The signed PDF is stored on the record. |
| **Declined** | A signer declined — on the Sessionboard signing page or in Docusign. No further signatures are collected. |
| **Voided** | Cancelled before completion. |
| **Draft** | Created but not yet sent. |

Each contract also shows its source: **Sessionboard** for contracts sent with Sessionboard e-sign, **Docusign** for envelopes sent from Sessionboard through Docusign, **Uploaded** for PDFs you added, and **Imported** for envelopes brought in from your Docusign account by Sessionboard.

Status updates are immediate for Sessionboard e-sign and arrive from Docusign automatically within moments. When a contract completes, if [auto-advance](#contract-settings) is set, the linked pipeline entry moves to the chosen stage.

## Manage a contract

Click the **⋮** menu on a contract:

- **Open in Docusign** opens the envelope in your Docusign account (Docusign contracts only).
- **Download PDF** downloads the signed document. Signed contracts also show a download button on the row.
- **Resend reminder** re-sends the signing link to whoever the contract is currently waiting on, or the Docusign notification for a Docusign contract. Sessionboard e-sign also sends [automatic reminders](#contract-settings) on the cadence you set.
- **Void** cancels a contract that is still awaiting signature. Add a **Reason** if you want it recorded — it shows in the contract's [details](#track-who-has-signed) — and confirm **Void contract?** to proceed. Signers who already have a link are told the contract was withdrawn.
- **Delete** removes the contract record from Sessionboard.

<Warning>
Voiding cannot be undone: it invalidates every outstanding signing link, or cancels the envelope in Docusign. Deleting removes the record from Sessionboard but does not change an envelope in Docusign.
</Warning>

## Contract settings

Open **CRM > Contracts** and click **Settings** to set the defaults every Sessionboard e-sign contract follows. Blank fields use the built-in default, which is shown beside each one.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default way to send** | Which tab **Add Contract** opens on: **Send with Sessionboard** or **Send via Docusign**. Both stay available. | Sessionboard |
| **Signing link expiry** | Days a signing link stays valid after it is sent. Expired links are renewed with the next reminder. | 30 days |
| **Automatic reminders** | How often a signer who has not signed is reminded by email. You can also resend by hand from any contract. | Every 3 days |
| **Organization countersigner** | The team member who signs any template role set to **Organization countersigner**. When unset, the administrator who sends the contract signs instead. | Whoever sends |
| **E-signature consent text** | The consent wording shown above the signature on the signing page. Changes apply to contracts sent from then on. | Built-in wording |
| **Auto-advance on contract completion** | The [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline) stage the linked entry moves to when every signer has signed. Shared with the [Docusign panel](/apps/docusign-integration#auto-advance-the-pipeline). | Off |

![The Contract settings panel with Default way to send, Signing link expiry, Automatic reminders and Organization countersigner, each showing its default and current value](/images/kb/contracts-settings-sidebar.png)

Changing these settings uses the organization's manage-app-connections permission — the same one that connects Docusign. Team members without it can open the panel and read the current values.

## Let signers sign from their portal

Signers always receive an email and can sign from it. You can also let them sign from their Sessionboard portal:

1. In your Program Site settings, open **Logged-In Experience**.
2. Under **Portal widgets > Sidebar**, turn on **My Contracts**. It is off by default.

Signed-in participants then see **My Contracts** in their portal sidebar, with **Sign now** on agreements awaiting their signature — Sessionboard e-sign opens the signing page in the same tab, Docusign opens in a new tab — and **Download signed PDF** on completed agreements. A contract is listed for the person it is addressed to, for any signer on it, and, for an account contract, for the account's primary contact. Participants can read their side in [Sign a contract from your portal](/participants/sign-a-contract).

## Permissions

Contracts has four permissions — view, create, edit, and delete or void — that you assign per role at **two levels**: on organization roles in **Organization Settings > Team**, and on event roles in **Event Team > Permissions**. A team member can act on a contract when either their organization role or their event role for that contract's event allows it; templates and Contract settings stay with the organization. The full matrix, the defaults for each standard role, and how the two levels combine are in [Who can manage contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts-permissions).
