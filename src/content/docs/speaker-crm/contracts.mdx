Contracts lets your team send speaker, sponsor, and exhibitor agreements through Docusign without leaving the record they are working on. Sessionboard pre-fills the Docusign template from the contact, account, session, event, and organization data it already holds, shows you every value before it goes out, and then tracks the signature automatically. Agreements signed outside Sessionboard can be uploaded so the record is complete.

<Note>
Contracts is an Early Access feature. Turn it on from **Org → Early Access** or contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com), then [connect Docusign](/apps/docusign-integration). Sending via Docusign requires a Docusign connection; uploading an existing contract does not.
</Note>

## Where contracts live

Each contract belongs to a **contact** or an **account**, and is tied to an **event**. You can open the **Contracts** tab from:

- A contact's profile in **CRM > Contacts**
- An account's profile
- A group (sponsor or exhibitor) profile
- A prospect's card in the **Pipeline**

**CRM > Contracts** lists every contract across your organization.

![The Contracts tab on a pipeline entry showing one signed contract and one awaiting signature](/images/kb/contracts-tab-pipeline-entry.png)

## Send a contract via Docusign

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Contracts tab and click Add Contract">
Open the record, click the **Contracts** tab, then **Add Contract**. Under **Who is this contract with?**, the record is pre-selected; switch between **Contact** and **Account** if you need to.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the Send via Docusign tab">
Pick a **Template** from your connected Docusign account and the **Event** the agreement is for. The **Signer name** and **Signer email** fill from the contact; change them if someone else should sign.
</Step>
<Step title="Write the email (optional)">
Add an **Email subject** and **Email message** for the Docusign notification. Leave them blank to use Docusign's defaults.
</Step>
<Step title="Review and edit fields">
Sessionboard lists every text field in the template and the value it resolved. Each row shows where the value came from, such as **Contact profile** or **Event record**. Rows marked **No source** could not be resolved automatically; type the value yourself. You can edit any value before sending.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Send via Docusign">
Docusign emails the signer. The contract appears on the record as **Needs signature**.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Review & edit fields step of the Add Contract dialog with resolved and unresolved template variables](/images/kb/contracts-add-contract-review-fields.png)

<Tip>
Session variables such as `session.title` resolve when the contact has a session in the selected event. If they show **No source**, type the value in the review step. The full list of variables and how to name your template fields is in [Docusign template variables](/speaker-crm/docusign-template-variables).
</Tip>

## Upload an existing contract

For agreements signed on paper or through another tool, choose the **Upload existing** tab in **Add Contract**:

1. Click **Choose PDF** and pick the signed document.
2. Enter a **Title**, choose the **Event**, and enter the **Signer name** and **Signer email**.
3. Enter the **Signed date**. Optionally add an **Effective date**, **Expiry date**, and **Notes**.
4. Save. The contract appears with the **Uploaded** source and the **Signed** status, and the PDF is available to download.

## Contract statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs signature** | Sent through Docusign and waiting on the signer. |
| **Signed** | Completed. The signed PDF is stored on the record. |
| **Declined** | The signer declined to sign in Docusign. |
| **Voided** | Cancelled before signature. |
| **Draft** | Created but not yet sent. |

Each contract also shows its source: **Docusign** for contracts sent from Sessionboard, **Uploaded** for PDFs you added, and **Imported** for envelopes brought in from your Docusign account by Sessionboard.

Status updates arrive from Docusign automatically. When a signer finishes, the contract flips to **Signed** within moments, and if [auto-advance](/apps/docusign-integration#auto-advance-the-pipeline) is set, the linked pipeline entry moves to the chosen stage.

## Manage a contract

Click the **⋮** menu on a contract:

- **Open in Docusign** opens the envelope in your Docusign account.
- **Download PDF** downloads the signed document. Signed contracts also show a download button on the row.
- **Resend reminder** re-sends the Docusign notification to a signer who has not signed yet.
- **Void** cancels a contract that is still awaiting signature. Confirm **Void contract?** to proceed.
- **Delete** removes the contract record from Sessionboard.

<Warning>
Voiding cancels the envelope in Docusign and cannot be undone. Deleting removes the record from Sessionboard but does not change the envelope in Docusign.
</Warning>

## Let signers sign from their portal

Signers always receive the Docusign email and can sign from it. You can also let them sign from their Sessionboard portal:

1. In your Program Site settings, open **Logged-In Experience**.
2. Under **Portal widgets > Sidebar**, turn on **My Contracts**. It is off by default.

Signed-in participants then see **My Contracts** in their portal sidebar, with **Sign Now** on anything awaiting their signature and **Download Signed PDF** on completed agreements. See [Sign a contract from your portal](/participants/sign-a-contract).

## Permissions

Contracts has its own organization permissions, assigned per role in **Organization Settings > Team**:

| Permission | Allows |
| --- | --- |
| Can view contracts | See the Contracts tab and **CRM > Contracts**. |
| Can create contracts (send via DocuSign or upload) | Send via Docusign and upload existing PDFs. |
| Can edit contract metadata, assignments, and resend | Edit details and resend reminders. |
| Can delete or void contracts | Void and delete. |

Connecting or disconnecting Docusign uses the organization's manage-app-connections permission.
