A contract template is the agreement you send with [Sessionboard e-sign](/speaker-crm/contracts#send-with-sessionboard-e-sign). You write it once under **CRM > Contracts > Templates**, mark where each signer signs, and Sessionboard fills in the names, sessions, fees and clauses from the record every time you send it.

## Why use it

Your legal text stays in one place instead of in a folder of near-identical Word files. Variables remove the retyping — and the typos — that come with a new speaker or sponsor every week, conditional sections keep one template for cases that used to need two, and publishing with versions means a contract that is already out always matches the words it was sent with.

## Create a template

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Templates and click Create template">
Go to **CRM > Contracts** and open the **Templates** tab. Click **Create template**.
</Step>
<Step title="Name it and pick the base module">
Give the template a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description** for your team. Under **Base module**, choose **Contacts (Speakers, People)** for agreements sent to one person — speaker agreements, releases, consent forms — or **Groups (Sponsors, Exhibitors)** for agreements sent to a company. The base module decides which variables are available and which records the template can be sent from, and it is fixed once the template is published.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the Document mode and create">
Leave **Mode** on **Document** and click **Create template**. The editor opens with the template as a **Draft**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Write the document

The editor is a paper-style page. Paste your agreement from Word or Google Docs — headings, lists and tables come across — or click **Import .docx** to replace the document with a Word file. Then use the **Template tools** rail on the right:

- **Variables** — search for a value such as `contact.full_name`, `event.name` or `session.title` and click it to insert `{{contact.full_name}}` at the cursor. Only the namespaces that fit the base module are listed. The full list is in [Contract template variables](/speaker-crm/contract-template-variables).
- **Signer roles** — who signs, in what order. See [Signer roles](#signer-roles).
- **Insert block** — the places a signer fills in: **Signature**, **Initials**, **Date signed**, a **Text field** or a **Checkbox**, each assigned to a role. A block appears in the document as a chip labelled with the role and the label you typed, for example *Speaker · Signature* or *Speaker · T-shirt size*. The label is what the signer sees next to the field, so make it specific.
- **Conditional section** — show a clause only when a variable has a value, or only when it is empty. Select the clause first, pick the variable, and click **Insert section**; the text is wrapped in `{{#if contact.custom.country}} … {{/if}}`. Add an **otherwise** branch to show different text in the other case. Conditions test whether a value is present; they do not compare values.
- **Template settings** — the **Default email subject** and **Default email message** the Send dialog starts from (variables work here too) and how long each **signing link** stays valid, when it should differ from your [organization default](/speaker-crm/contracts#contract-settings).

Click **Save** as you go. The template stays a **Draft** until you publish it, and nothing in a draft reaches the Send dialog.

<Tip>
Use a conditional section for the clauses that only apply sometimes — a Canadian tax clause when `contact.custom.country` is set, a booth clause when `account.custom.booth_number` has a value — so one template covers every case.
</Tip>

## Signer roles

Every template has at least one signer role, and every signature block belongs to one. For each role you set:

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Label** | How the role is shown to your team and to signers, such as *Speaker* or *Organizer*. The **Key** is derived from it and is what blocks refer to. |
| **Who signs** | **The record this contract is for** (the contact, or the group's primary contact), **The group's primary contact**, **Whoever sends the contract**, **Your default countersigner** from [Contract settings](/speaker-crm/contracts#contract-settings), or **Typed in when sending** for a signer the sender names in the Send dialog. |
| **Required** | A required role must have a signature block in the document before the template can be published. |
| **Hint for the sender** | Optional text shown in the Send dialog, such as *Legal / finance contact*. |

Use the arrows to put the roles in signing order, and set **Signing order** to **One at a time, in this order** or **Everyone at once**. A role that is used by blocks in the document cannot be removed or re-keyed until those blocks are removed.

## Preview on a real record

Click **Preview as…**, search for a contact (or a group) and, optionally, an **Event**, then click **Render preview**. Sessionboard renders the agreement as the signer would receive it, with that record's values filled in and conditional sections resolved, and lists every variable it could not resolve. Fix the record, pick the event, or adjust the template until the preview is right.

## Publish and keep it current

Click **Publish**. If something is missing — a required role without a signature block, a block assigned to a role that no longer exists, or an unbalanced conditional section — the editor lists what to fix first. Once published, the template shows **Published v1** and appears in the **Send with Sessionboard** tab of the Add Contract dialog.

Editing a published template changes a working copy, not the published version: the status reads **Published v1 · unpublished changes**, sends keep using v1, and **Publish changes** makes v2 the version new sends use. Contracts already sent keep the version they were sent with. **Revisions** lists every published version and every snapshot taken before a restore; **Restore** puts an older version back into the working copy so you can publish it again.

<Note>
If a teammate saves the same template while you are editing it, the editor tells you and offers **Reload** so you do not overwrite their changes.
</Note>

## Manage templates

From the **Templates** list, each template's **Actions** menu lets you **Edit**, **Duplicate** it as a new draft, **Archive** it to hide it from the Send dialog (publish it again to bring it back), or **Delete** it. Contracts already sent from an archived or deleted template keep their own copy of the agreement.

## Permissions

Templates use the same organization permissions as contracts, assigned per role in **Organization Settings > Team** (see [Permissions](/speaker-crm/contracts#permissions)): **Can view contracts** opens the Templates list and a template in read-only mode; **Can create contracts** creates and duplicates templates; **Can edit contract metadata, assignments, and resend** edits, publishes, archives and restores them; **Can delete or void contracts** deletes them.

## Still having issues?

If a variable stays blank in the preview, check that the record has a value for it and, for `event.*` and `session.*` variables, that you picked an event and the contact has a session in it. If a template is missing from the Send dialog, make sure it is published, not archived, and that its base module matches the record you are sending from. For anything else, contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with the template name and your organization.
