Contacts belong to your organization, and an event sees a contact once they have been added to it. Adding from the directory carries the whole profile across — name, bio, headshot, company and every global field — so nobody retypes details and you do not end up with a second copy of the same person.

## Add contacts to an event

1. Open **CRM > Contacts**. Filter or search until the people you want are on screen — a saved [segment](/speaker-crm/crm-segments) such as *Past keynotes* is the quickest way back to a group you add every year.
2. Tick the checkbox beside each contact. The header checkbox selects everyone on the page.
3. In the bar above the table, click **Add To Event**.

![The directory bulk bar for a selected contact — Communicate, Add To Event, Add To Curated List, Invite To Event, Delete](/images/kb/org-directory-add-to-event.png)

4. The **Add to Event** panel lists your events under **Upcoming** and **Past**; search by name if the list is long. Choose one event and click **Add to selected event**.

![The Add To Event panel listing upcoming events](/images/kb/org-add-to-event.png)

5. The **Confirm** step shows the event and how many contacts you are adding. Click **Proceed**, then **Done** when it reports **Successfully added!**

It can take a few minutes for the contacts to appear in the event's **Contacts**, where they arrive with their profiles filled in and can be managed or removed like any other contact. Back in the directory, the event is listed in each person's **Events** column.

One event at a time: to place the same people in two events, repeat the steps for the second event.

<Tip>
Adding puts someone in the event without telling them. If you want them to submit a proposal, use **Invite To Event** instead, which opens an email with the subject **Invitation To Apply** — see [Speaker CRM: communications](/speaker-crm/crm).
</Tip>
