The Speaker CRM lets you write to people before they belong to any event. You select contacts in the organization directory, compose once, and Sessionboard sends each person their own copy. The composer is the same one your event teams use, so anything you know from [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails) applies here too.

## Email from the directory

1. Open **CRM > Contacts** and tick the checkbox beside each contact you want to write to. Filter first if the list is long — [Speaker CRM: advanced search](/speaker-crm/crm-advanced-search) shows how — or open a segment and select everyone in it.
2. In the bar that appears above the table, click **Communicate**.
3. The **Send Email** panel opens with your selection counted in the header.

![The Send Email panel opened from the organization directory — Settings on the left, Message on the right, with the Details, Preview and Confirmation steps in the header](/images/kb/crm-communicate.png)

The **Settings** column decides who gets the email and how it is addressed:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Who should receive this email?** | Which email address on the contact record to send to. **Contact Email Address** is the default. |
| **Include additional contacts:** | Also send to the other people attached to the contact, such as an assistant. Each of them receives a separate copy and is not copied on the main contact's email. |
| **Replies sent to:** | The address that receives replies. A shared inbox works best; the field holds one address. |
| **Sender Name** | The name recipients see in their inbox. |
| **CC** / **BCC** | Extra addresses to copy on every email sent. |

On the **Message** side, write a **Subject Line** and **Message Body**, or click **Template** to start from one of the organization's templates. **Merge Tags** insert the recipient's own details — first name, company, job title — so a bulk send still reads as one-to-one.

Click **Review** to see the email as the first recipient will, then **Send Now**.

## Invite contacts to an event

**Invite To Event** sits beside **Communicate** in the same bar. It opens the same **Send Email** panel with the subject already set to **Invitation To Apply**, ready for you to point people at an event's call for speakers. Add the link to the form or portal in the body and send as above.

Inviting does not add the contact to the event. If you want them in the event now, use **Add To Event** — see [Speaker CRM: add contacts to an event](/speaker-crm/crm-add-contact-to-events).

## Organization templates

Templates you use from the directory live under the organization's **Settings > Email Templates**, separate from any event's templates. A template created inside an event does not appear in the CRM composer, and an organization template does not appear inside an event.

Click **Add Template** and fill in:

- **Template Name** — the internal name; the list sorts by it.
- **Type** — **Contacts** for directory sends. **Awards** and **Interest Forms** appear when those features are on.
- **Reply To**, **Sender Name**, **CC**, **BCC** — the same envelope settings as the composer, saved with the template.
- **Subject Line** and **Message Body**, with **Merge Tags** available in the editor.

Each row's menu offers **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. The **System** tab lists the emails Sessionboard sends on its own; you cannot edit those here. The editor itself is covered in [Email templates](/settings/email-templates).

<Tip>
A template such as *Speaker invitation* that explains what you are looking for and links to the submission form saves you retyping the same pitch for every event.
</Tip>

## Find what you sent

The organization's **History** page keeps every email sent from the CRM under **Emails**, with three tabs: **Email Campaigns** groups each send with its recipient count and unique opens; **Sent Emails** lists each recipient's copy with its delivery status; **Errors** collects bounces and rejections. Emails Sessionboard generates itself, such as sign-in codes, are not listed.

Search by recipient name or address, click a subject to preview the message, and open a campaign to see the recipient list with a status for each person. The status meanings are in [Email & SMS history](/communications/email-sms-history#email-status-definitions).
