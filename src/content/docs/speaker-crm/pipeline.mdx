Sourcing speakers is a long, many-handed process: someone spots a name, someone researches them, someone reaches out, and months later they are confirmed for a program. The pipeline gives that process one board. Each column is a stage, each card is a prospect, and every card carries its source, score, stage history and notes so anyone on the team can pick it up.

Open it from the organization sidebar: **CRM > Pipeline**.

![The Pipeline board with Identified, Researching, Approved and Contacted columns](/images/kb/pipeline-board.png)

## The board

- **All / Speakers** tabs filter by prospect type.
- **Default view** is the saved-views menu — switch between, create and manage filtered views of the board (by stage, form, score, event).
- **Stages** opens the stage manager (below).
- **Enroll** adds a prospect by hand.

Each card shows the prospect's name and where they came from, a **Submission** pill for cards that arrived through an interest form, the fit **score** when one has been set, the form they came through (*via Speaker Interest Form — 2027 season*), the first line of the rationale, and when the card was last updated. Drag a card to another column to move it, or use the card's **⋯** menu — **Move to stage** and, for submissions, **Assign to event**.

## Stages

The pipeline ships with eight system stages. Each has a **behavior type** that decides how the stage is treated in reporting and in the interest form's submission list; you can rename a system stage, but the type stays.

| Stage | Behavior type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Identified | Open | New — the default landing stage |
| Researching | Open | Being looked into |
| Approved | Open | Cleared to contact |
| Contacted | Open | Outreach sent |
| Interested | Open | Prospect has replied positively |
| Confirmed | Won | Confirmed for a program |
| Future Fit | Nurture | Good, but not for this cycle |
| Declined | Lost | Not moving forward |

Click **Stages** to open **Manage pipeline stages**. Use the up and down arrows to reorder, click a name to rename it, and use **Add custom stage** for a step that is specific to your team — a *Committee review*, say — choosing its behavior type as you add it. Custom stages can be deleted later; any cards still in them move to a stage you pick. Every change saves as you make it and shows on the board at once; **Done** closes the dialog.

![The Manage pipeline stages dialog with system stages, their behavior types and Add custom stage](/images/kb/pipeline-stages.png)

<Note>
For a card that came from an interest form, the stage's behavior type also sets the submission's status on the form: Open and Nurture stages show it as *pending*, Won as *accepted*, Lost as *declined*. See [Interest forms](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms).
</Note>

## How cards get on the board

**From an interest form.** Every submission to a published [interest form](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms) becomes a card in **Identified**, tagged with the form it came through. The submission's title is the card title.

**By hand.** Click **Enroll**. Search your organization's contacts by name or email, choose a **Starting stage** (the first stage, Identified, is pre-selected), and optionally add **Fit & rationale**: a **Score** from 0 to 100 and a one- to three-sentence **Rationale** teammates can read when triaging. Click **Enroll**.

![The Enroll in pipeline dialog — speaker search, starting stage, score and rationale](/images/kb/pipeline-enroll.png)

<Tip>
Score and rationale are what make a board useful six months later. A card that says *Score 82 — strong fit for the clinical operations track; has spoken at two regional meetings* is one anyone can act on; a bare name is not.
</Tip>

## Working a card

Click a card to open its panel.

![A card's detail panel: stage, submission and score pills, rationale, Move to, Assign to event, and the Stage History and Activity tabs](/images/kb/pipeline-card.png)

- **Pills** show the current stage, the prospect type, the score, and the source form.
- **Rationale** appears under the pills.
- **Move to** advances the card to any stage without leaving the panel.
- **Assign to event** (submissions only) promotes the submission into a session on one of your events — you pick the event, then the session's status, track, room and tags — and the event's normal speaker workflow takes over from there. Once assigned, the button becomes **Unassign from *event***.
- **Stage History** lists every stage change with its timestamp.
- **Activity** is the full feed for the card — stage moves, assignments, notes — filterable by who acted: **User**, **Agent**, **System** or **Rule**. Type in **Add a note…** to leave context for teammates; notes do not change the card's stage.
- **Reports** appears for contact and account prospects and holds Scout research reports for them. **Contracts** appears when [Contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts) is enabled.

### What carries over when you assign a submission

The interest form's title and description become the session's title and description. The title must be 255 characters or fewer. Other answers go into the event's session fields: under **Map custom fields**, each answer is matched to a session field, and matches Sessionboard made for you carry an **auto** badge. Pick a different field, or **Do not copy** to leave an answer behind. If no session field fits, create one in the event first. Admin-only form fields are not copied.

## When a card leaves the pipeline

A prospect is "in the pipeline" while their card sits in an Open stage. It leaves when you move it to **Confirmed**, **Future Fit** or **Declined**, or when a submission is assigned to an event. Future Fit is the stage to use generously: it is your bench for next year, and a saved view filtered to it is the first place to look when programming starts again.
