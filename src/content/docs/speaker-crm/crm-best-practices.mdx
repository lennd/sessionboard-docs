## What the Speaker CRM adds

The Speaker CRM is the organization level of Sessionboard. Every contact from every event lives in one directory under **CRM > Contacts**, with the events they took part in, the emails they were sent, and every standard and custom field on their record. From there you filter, save segments, add people to a new event, and run the [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline) and [Interest Forms](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms) that feed it.

Four steps get a new CRM ready to use. Do them in this order — the fields have to exist before the import can map to them, and the import has to land before segments have anything to match.

## 1. Invite your team

Everyone who should work in the organization needs an organization-level invitation; an event-level invitation only opens that event.

Open **Settings > Team**, click **Add**, and choose **Invite User**. Enter the person's **Email**, **First Name** and **Last Name**, then decide how far their access goes:

- **Active User** — off, and the person cannot sign in to the platform or a portal.
- **Organization Access** — on, and the person can open the organization: the directory, segments, Pipeline, reports and settings. Pick the role that answers **What type of user is this?**
- **Events** — the events they can manage. Each one gives them the **Admin User** role on that event; change it on the event's **Event Team** page afterwards if they need less.

![The Invite User panel with Organization Access on, the role cards under What type of user is this?, and the Events picker](/images/kb/org-team-invite.png)

Think about who needs the organization and who only needs an event:

- **Leadership** — dashboards and reports across events, so organization access.
- **Marketing** — segments and campaigns, so organization access.
- **Content and program teams** — usually one or two events each, so event access only.
- **IT** — integrations and single sign-on live under organization **Settings**.

Details and the invitation email: [Inviting organization team members](/speaker-crm/inviting-organization-team-members).

## 2. Create the contact fields you will need

Custom fields hold whatever you track about a person beyond the standard record — previous events, a speaker tier, whether they are internal or external. A field created at the organization level is a **Global Field**: it exists on every event, so the same answer follows the contact from one event to the next.

Open **Settings > Library > Fields**. On the **Contact** tab the standard fields are grouped as **Profile**, **Communication** and **Attribute**; the **Custom** tab holds the ones your team added. Click **Add Field** and complete:

- **Name** — up to 255 characters.
- **Field Type** — dropdown, text, number, date, checkbox, file, and so on.
- **Field Description** — an internal note so the rest of the team uses the field the same way.
- **Field Level** — locked to **Global Field** here; only global fields can be created at the organization level.
- **Contains Personal Data** — masks the field in AI queries and shared reports.

<Tip>
Choose a dropdown over free text wherever the answers repeat — "Previous Event", "Speaker Type (CME / Non-CME)", "Internal / External". A dropdown keeps one spelling per answer, which is what makes filters and segments work later.
</Tip>

More on field types and where fields appear: [Speaker CRM: creating fields](/speaker-crm/creating-crm-fields).

## 3. Import your existing contacts

With the fields in place, bring in the contacts you already have so the CRM starts full instead of empty.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your custom fields to the view">
    On the contacts table, open **Columns** and turn on every custom field you want to fill. The import template is built from the columns in your current view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the template">
    Click **Options > Import**, then **Generate Template**. Match your spreadsheet's headers to the template, or paste your data into it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Options > Import > Upload File** and follow the three steps — header row, column mapping, then validation. Every contact needs a unique email address; a row whose email already exists updates that contact instead of creating a duplicate when **Update record if already exists** is `true`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Before you upload:

- **Remove duplicates** — one row per email address.
- **Standardize formats** — phone numbers with a country code (`+1 (303) 555-0199`), full `https://` URLs, dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Fill required fields** — First Name, Last Name and Email on every row.
- **Map every column** — create the custom field first if your old system has a column with nowhere to go.

After the import, open a few records and check that the values landed in the right fields before you build segments on them.

The complete file rules (row limit, multi-select separators, file and currency columns) are in [Importing data](/settings/importing-data).

## 4. Build your first segments

A segment is a saved subset of the directory. Use it to email one group, to review who spoke last year, or to hand a shortlist to a program committee.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter the directory">
    On **CRM > Contacts**, click **Filter**. The facets are grouped — **Demographics**, **Relevance**, **Company**, **History & Connections**, sessions and your custom fields — and each value shows how many contacts match. Choosing values from different facets narrows the list (AND); choosing several values in one facet widens it (OR). Click **Done**.

    ![The Filter tab of the directory Preferences panel, with facet groups and match counts](/images/kb/crm-directory-filter.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Segment">
    The toolbar now shows the active filter count, **Clear all** and **Save Segment**. Click **Save Segment**, name it, and choose the kind of list:

    - **Save Dynamic Segment** — keeps matching the filters, so new contacts join it automatically.
    - **Save Curated List** — freezes the contacts that match right now; you add and remove people yourself.

    ![The Save Segment panel with the name field, the Dynamic segments and Curated lists explanation, the filter and match counts, and the two save buttons](/images/kb/crm-save-segment.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Segments you have saved are listed under **CRM > Segments**. Managing them — opening, editing, duplicating, adding a contact to a curated list — is covered in [Speaker CRM: segments](/speaker-crm/crm-segments).

Segments that tend to earn their keep:

- **Last year's keynote speakers** (curated) — the people you want to invite back first.
- **Spoke at any event** (dynamic) — an "Events" filter that grows as programs are published.
- **Members who have also spoken** (dynamic) — a membership dropdown plus the Events filter.
