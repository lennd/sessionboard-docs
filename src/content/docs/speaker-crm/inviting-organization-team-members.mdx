Invite people from the organization so one invitation covers every event they need. An invitation sent from inside an event opens that event only.

## Send the invitation

1. Open **Settings > Team**.
2. Click **Add** and choose **Invite User**.
3. Enter the person's **Email**, **First Name** and **Last Name**.
4. Set their access (below) and click **Invite**.

![The Invite User panel with Organization Access on, the role cards under What type of user is this?, and the Events picker](/images/kb/org-team-invite.png)

## What each setting does

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Active User** | Off, and the person cannot sign in to the platform or to a portal. Turn it off later to deactivate someone without deleting them. |
| **Organization Access** | On, and the person can open the organization level — CRM, Pipeline, segments, reports and organization settings. Off, and they only see the events you select. |
| **What type of user is this?** | Appears when Organization Access is on. Pick the organization role, such as **Admin** or **Admin Lite**, or one your team created. |
| **Events** | The events the person can manage. Use **Select…** to pick them, or **Select All**. Each selected event gives them the **Admin User** role on that event. |

<Note>
Someone with Organization Access and no events sees a warning before you invite them. It is allowed, but check it is what you meant — an organization-only user cannot open any event's dashboard.
</Note>

## After you invite

The person receives an email from `no-reply@sessionboard.com` with a link to set their password and sign in. Until they do, their row on **Settings > Team** carries a **Pending** badge, and the row's **⋯** menu has **Resend invite**.

To change what they can do on a single event, open that event's **Event Team** page and pick a different role there. Organization access and role are edited from **Settings > Team**.
