## Why use it

Program teams searching for speakers can now page through large result sets with a consistent control, filter by kind (Sessions vs. Speakers) in a single click, and immediately recognise speakers by their headshot — reducing the time spent cross-referencing profiles. The graph loading overlay prevents accidental double-clicks from queuing up conflicting requests.

## Search by keyword

On **CRM > Contacts**, the **Search people by keywords…** box matches names, email addresses, job titles and company names as you type. Use it for a quick lookup of one person or one organization.

## Filter with facets

Click **Filter** to open the **Preferences** panel on its **Filter** tab. Fields and attributes are grouped so you can scan them:

- **Demographics** — Job Title, Pronouns, Gender, Company Name, Highest Level of Education, Global Region, Country, State, Ethnicity, Languages
- **Relevance** — Audience Type, Target Age Range, Topic / Expertise, Brand, Industry
- **Company** — Annual Revenue, Headcount, Years in Operation, Organization Structure
- **History & Connections** — Events, Speaker Score, Speaker Fee, Availability, Preferred Session Format
- Session fields and your **Custom** contact fields follow

Every value carries a count — **Founder & CEO (13)** means thirteen contacts have that job title. A facet shows its five most common values; click the **Show … more** link for the rest, or type into **Search or type custom value…** to find a value or match on free text.

![The Filter tab of the Preferences panel — Demographics facets with counts beside each value](/images/kb/crm-directory-filter.png)

Click a value to select it. When you are done, click **Done**.

### How the filters combine

- Values chosen **inside one facet** widen the result: Job Title = Founder & CEO **or** Chief Resident.
- Values chosen in **different facets** narrow it: Job Title = Founder & CEO **and** Country = Canada.

The table refreshes with the matching contacts, and the toolbar shows the number of active filters beside **Filter**, a **Clear all** button, and **Save Segment**.

![The directory toolbar after one filter is applied — Filter 1, Clear all and Save Segment, above the filtered rows](/images/kb/crm-directory-filtered.png)

To change the filters, click **Filter** again. To start over, click **Clear all**.

## Save the result as a segment

Click **Save Segment** to keep the filtered list. You choose between a dynamic segment, which keeps matching the filters, and a curated list, which freezes the people who match today. How to pick, and how to manage the segments afterwards: [Speaker CRM: segments](/speaker-crm/crm-segments).
