## Why use it

Good speakers turn up between calls for papers — a card at a booth, an email to a chair, a web form that lands in a spreadsheet nobody owns. An interest form is a standing "speak with us" page that puts every one of them into the CRM pipeline, where you can evaluate them, say what happens next on the success page, and assign them to the right event when it exists — as a session proposal, or as a speaker contact with no empty session created.

## What are Interest Forms?

Interest Forms are a capability inside Speaker CRM that lets you collect speaker and session interest year-round,not just during Call for Papers windows.

Through a customizable Interest Form, you can collect in two modes: Sessions & Speakers (to capture session proposals along with speaker details) or Speakers Only (to capture speaker contact information and profiles without session details).

Submissions are automatically stored in Speaker CRM and surface as entries in the new Speaker CRM: Pipeline, where your team can move prospects through your sourcing workflow.

#### Key benefits
- Continuous pipeline — Collect interest throughout the year, not just during CFP cycles
- Centralized data — All submissions go directly into Speaker CRM. No spreadsheets, no imports, no external tools
- Two collection modes — Choose "Sessions & Speakers" to collect session proposals with speaker info, or "Speakers Only" to collect speaker contacts and profiles without session details
- Multi-event support — Speakers can indicate interest in one or more upcoming events
- Integrated with the Pipeline — Every submission lands in the Pipeline so your team can immediately triage, research, and advance prospects through stages

---

## Interest Forms vs. Submission Forms (SSF)

Interest Forms and Speaker Submission Forms (SSF) serve different purposes:

|  | Interest Forms | Submission Forms (SSF) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level | Organization-level | Event-level |
| Purpose | Collect interest across one or multiple events year-round | Collect session submissions for a specific event |
| Where data lives | Speaker CRM (flows into the Pipeline; can be assigned to events when ready) | Directly inside the event |
| When to use | Year-round pipeline building, early-stage interest capture | Active Call for Papers / session submission period |

## Accessing interest forms
Navigate to your Organization Dashboard > CRM > Interest Forms in the left sidebar. You'll see four tabs:
- Forms — All your Interest Forms with status, submission counts, and speaker counts
- Submissions — All submissions across all forms
- Speakers — All speakers who have submitted interest
- Evaluations — Every [evaluation plan](/speaker-crm/interest-form-evaluation-plans) across your Interest Forms, with status, rounds and progress

The dashboard at the top shows Total Forms, Open Forms, Total Submissions, and Total Speakers.

## Creating an interest form (5-step wizard)
Click **Create Form** in the top right corner. This opens a 5-step setup wizard: Details, Events, Fields, Managers, Notifications.

#### Step 1: Form Details

Configure the basic settings for your form:
- Internal Name — Used to identify the form internally (not visible to submitters)
- Public Title — Displayed to submitters at the top of the form (e.g., "Submit Your Session Proposal")
- Description — Instructions or context shown to submitters
- Opens At / Closes At — Define the submission window. The form will automatically close on the Closes At date
- What are you collecting? — Choose one:
  - Sessions & Speakers — Collect session proposals along with speaker information
    - Max Speakers Per Session — Limit the number of speakers for sessions
  - Speakers Only — Collect speaker interest without session details. There is no session limit to set, and the form's heading on the site reads "Submit your speaker proposal"
- Success page message — What speakers read after they submit, in place of the default "What's next?" list. The confirmation number, PDF download and **Return to Portal** still appear. Leave it empty for the default list

![Step 1 of the wizard, Form Details — internal name, public title, description, opens and closes at, and what you are collecting](/images/kb/interest-form-wizard-details.png)

### Step 2: Events

Select which events speakers can indicate interest in. Submitters will choose from these events when filling out the form.

![Step 2, Events: the upcoming events a speaker can express interest in](/images/kb/interest-form-wizard-events.png)

Only future-dated events can be selected. You cannot create Interest Forms for events that have already passed.

### Step 3: Form Fields

Build your form by configuring what information to collect.

![Step 3, Fields: the Session Information and Speaker Information sections with Add Field on each](/images/kb/interest-form-wizard-fields.png)

The form has two main sections:

#### Session Information (Sessions & Speakers mode only)
- Session Title is required by default
- Click + Add Field or View Session Field Library to add more session fields
- Fields are pulled from your global Session field library — any new fields you add here will also be available across all your programs


#### Speaker Information
- First Name, Last Name, and Email are required by default
- Click + Add Field or View Contact Field Library to add more speaker fields
- Fields are pulled from your global Contact field library

 Tip: Interest Forms use your global field libraries. Any customizations you make will only apply to this form, but adding new fields will add them to your global library for use across all programs.

### Step 4: Managers

Assign co-admins who can help manage this form. Form Managers have full administrative access to manage submissions, configure settings, and oversee the review process for this specific form.

![Step 4, Managers: current managers, existing admins to add, and Invite New User](/images/kb/interest-form-wizard-managers.png)

### Step 5: Notifications

Configure email notifications for submissions. There are two types:
- Submitter Notifications — A Submission Confirmation email sent to the speaker when they submit. Enabled by default. Click Customize to edit the sender name, subject, and body
- Admin Notifications — An email sent to administrators when a new submission is received. Can also be customized

![Step 5, Notifications: submitter confirmation and admin notification toggles with Customize](/images/kb/interest-form-wizard-notifications.png)

## Publishing your form on a site

A form is not reachable until it is on a site — the organization's speaker site or an event site.

1. Go to **Program > Sites** in the left sidebar and open the site.
2. In the builder's settings list, click **Programs / CFP**. The panel lists every form the site could offer: **Selected Programs** at the top, **Available Programs** below.
3. Tick your Interest Form under Available Programs. It moves into Selected Programs, where you can drag to set the order programs appear in on the site, and the canvas preview updates to show it under Open Programs.
4. Click **Save Changes**, and **Publish** if the site uses drafts.

![The site builder with Programs / CFP open — the interest form ticked under Selected Programs and previewed under Open Programs](/images/kb/interest-form-site-programs.png)

Your Interest Form now appears as an Open Program on the site with its submission window and a **Start Submission** button.

## The Speaker Experience

When a speaker visits your Site and logs in, they will see:
1. Open Programs — every published Interest Form appears here with its submission window and a **Start Submission** button, alongside the site's other programs

   ![The site as a signed-in speaker sees it — Open Programs with the interest form and Start Submission](/images/kb/interest-form-speaker-view.png)

2. Submission form — A 2-step flow (Submission → Confirmation):
   - Select Events — The speaker chooses which upcoming events they're interested in
   - Session Information — Session title and any additional fields (if Sessions & Speakers mode)
   - Speaker Information — Name, email, and any additional fields you configured
3. Confirmation — After submitting, the speaker receives an on-screen confirmation, with your success page message if you set one, and an email

Speakers can also track what they sent via **My Submissions** in the portal sidebar.

## Managing submissions
Click on any form in the Interest Forms list to open its dashboard. Tabs across the top — **Dashboard**, **Submissions**, **Evaluation**, **Speakers**, **Audit log** — and, on the dashboard itself:
- Submissions — total received, with a **View All** link
- Speakers — unique speakers who submitted, with a **View All** link
- Setup Checklist — fields, notifications, events and whether the form is on a site yet, ticking itself off as you go

![A form's dashboard: submission and speaker counts, quick actions and the setup checklist](/images/kb/interest-form-dashboard.png)

### Quick Actions
- View Form — Preview the form as a speaker would see it
- Edit Form — Return to the form setup wizard
- Copy Link — Get a direct link to share the form

### The Submissions and Speakers tabs

**Submissions** is one row per proposal: its ID, title, who submitted it, the events they picked, when, and the pipeline stage it is in right now, which you can change from here. Search and filters sit above the table, with **Export** and **Add Submission** for proposals that arrive by phone or in a hallway.

![The Submissions tab of an interest form with stage, events and submitter columns](/images/kb/interest-form-submissions.png)

Select rows and the bulk bar offers **Add to Event**, **Send Email**, **Download Files** and **Delete**.

![The bulk bar for selected submissions](/images/kb/interest-form-submissions-bulk.png)

**Speakers** is the same data by person — name, email, company and the submission they came in on. Everyone here was created as a contact in Speaker CRM the moment they submitted.

![The Speakers tab listing each submitter as a contact](/images/kb/interest-form-speakers.png)

### Download submitted files in bulk

When your form collects file uploads — headshots, abstracts, slide decks — you don't have to open submissions one at a time. Select submissions in the submissions list (per form, or across all forms from the Submissions view) and click **Download Files**. Sessionboard packages every file from the selected submissions into a single zip, organized by submission with each file named after the question it was uploaded to. Merged submissions include their related submissions' files too.

The zip is built in the background: track it and download the finished archive from **History → Exports**. If none of the selected submissions contain files, you'll be told before anything starts.

### Moving data to events

Submissions are stored in Speaker CRM. When you're ready to bring speakers into a specific event, you can seamlessly move submission data to your target events from the Pipeline — linking speakers and session proposals directly into your event programming.

On a **Speakers Only** form there is no session to map: pick the event and click **Assign**, which shows how many submissions you selected. The speakers and the submitter are added to the event as contacts, no session is created, and the submission shows as assigned to that event. Assigning it to the same event again tells you it is already assigned.

On a **Sessions & Speakers** form, the Format, Track, Level and Language answers carry over to the new session when the event has an option with the same name; an answer the event has no match for is left blank and the session is still created.

## How submissions flow into the Pipeline

Every submission to an Interest Form is automatically created as an entry in your Speaker CRM: Pipeline, a kanban-style workflow that lets your team move prospects through stages such as Identified, Researching, Approved, Contacted, Interested, Confirmed, Future Fit, and Declined.

From the Pipeline you can:
- Move prospects between stages with drag-and-drop
- Open a card to review the original submission, stage history, activity, and scouting reports
- Manually enroll additional speakers that did not come through a form
- Assign prospects to a specific event when they're ready to be programmed

For full details on managing the Pipeline, see [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline).

## Key Things to Know
- **Multiple submissions:** Speakers can submit multiple proposals to the same form
- **Auto-created contacts:** Submitted speakers are automatically added as contacts in Speaker CRM
- **Form closing:** Forms close automatically based on the Closes At date you set in Step 1
- **Global field libraries:** Pipeline Interest Forms use your global Session and Contact field libraries — new fields added in one form are available across all programs
- **Future events only:** You can only select future-dated events when configuring which events speakers can choose from
- **Sites required:** To make the form accessible to speakers, it must be selected under **Programs / CFP** in a site
- **Auto-enrolled in the Pipeline:** Every new submission appears in the Pipeline (Identified stage by default) so your team can start working it immediately
