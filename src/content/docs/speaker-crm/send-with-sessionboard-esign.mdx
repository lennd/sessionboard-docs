## Why use it

Your legal text lives in one [contract template](/speaker-crm/contract-templates) instead of a folder of near-identical files, the agreement is filled from the record so nobody retypes a name or a session title, and the signature comes back to the same record it left from. Reminders go out on their own, the signed copy is on the speaker, sponsor, or exhibitor the moment the last person signs, and the [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline) entry can move to **Confirmed** without a hand on it.

## Before you start

- **A published template.** Write it under **CRM > Contracts > Templates** and click **Publish**; drafts and archived templates do not appear in the Send dialog. See [Write a contract template](/speaker-crm/contract-templates).
- **A record to send from.** A contact (speakers, people) for a Contacts template, or an account or group (sponsors, exhibitors) for a Groups template. The template's base module decides which records it can be sent from.
- **Permission to create contracts** on your organization role or on your event role for the event the agreement is for. See [Who can manage contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts-permissions).

## Send the agreement

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Contracts section and click Add Contract">
Open the record — a contact's **CRM** tab, an account or group profile, or a pipeline entry — find **Contracts**, then click **Add Contract**. Under **Who is this contract with?**, the record is pre-selected; switch between **Contact** and **Account** if you need to.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the Send with Sessionboard tab">
The dialog opens on the tab set as your organization's [default way to send](/speaker-crm/contracts#contract-settings). Pick a **Template**. Only [published templates](/speaker-crm/contract-templates#publish-and-keep-it-current) for this kind of record are listed — Contacts templates on a contact, Groups templates on an account or group.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the event">
Choose the **Event** the agreement is for to use that event's branding on the emails and signing page and to fill event and session variables such as `event.name` and `session.title`. Leave it at **Organization scope** only for agreements that are not tied to one event — a team member whose Contracts permissions come from an event role must pick the event.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the signers">
**Signers** lists every role in the template in signing order. Rows marked **From record** are filled from the contact or account and from your [organization countersigner](/speaker-crm/contracts#contract-settings); rows marked **Entered when sending** need a **Name** and **Email** from you. The sender's own role, when the template has one, is filled from your account. You cannot send until every required signer has a name and an email.
</Step>
<Step title="Write the email (optional)">
The **Email subject** and **Email message** start from the template's defaults and can use the same variables as the document. Each signer receives this message with their personal signing link.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Send for signature">
The contract appears on the record as **Needs signature** with the **Sessionboard** source. In a template that signs **in order**, only the first signer is emailed; each following signer gets their link when the previous one finishes. In a template that signs **all at once**, everyone is emailed immediately.
</Step>
</Steps>

![Send with Sessionboard tab with a published Speaker Agreement template selected, Organization scope, an ordered signer list with one signer from the record and one entered when sending, and the email subject and message](/images/kb/contracts-add-contract-send-with-sessionboard.png)

<Tip>
Variables in the template and the email — `contact.full_name`, `event.name`, `session.title`, `account.custom.po_number` and so on — are the same ids as [Contract template variables](/speaker-crm/contract-template-variables). A variable with no value on the record is left blank in the sent document, so fill in the contact or account profile — and pick the event, for event and session variables — before you send. **Preview as…** on the template shows exactly what a given record will produce.
</Tip>

### Sponsor and exhibitor agreements

For an account or group, the role set to **The record this contract is for** or **The group's primary contact** is filled from the account's primary contact for the event you picked, or its organization-wide primary contact when the event has none. If the account has more than one primary contact, the row is left for you to choose; if it has none, enter the signer's name and email, or add a primary contact to the account first.

## What the signer sees

Each signer's email opens a signing page branded for the event or organization. It shows the agreement as a PDF with its document fingerprint, the fields for that signer's role (initials, dates, text), a **Draw** or **Type** signature pad, and the [e-signature consent text](/speaker-crm/contracts#contract-settings) they must agree to. The signer clicks **Sign document**, or **Decline to sign** with an optional reason. The page works on any device, in light or dark mode, and with a keyboard alone.

If the template signs in order and a signer opens their link early, the page tells them who it is waiting on, and they are emailed again when it is their turn. Participants can read their side in [Sign a contract from your portal](/participants/sign-a-contract).

## When the last signer finishes

Sessionboard stamps every signature into the PDF, appends a signing certificate — who signed, when, from where, and the document fingerprint — stores the result on the record as **Signed**, and emails each signer a copy. If [auto-advance](/speaker-crm/contracts#contract-settings) is set, the linked pipeline entry moves to the chosen stage.

## Reminders, expiry, and changes

- **Automatic reminders** go to whoever the contract is waiting on, on the cadence in [Contract settings](/speaker-crm/contracts#contract-settings) (every 3 days by default). **Resend reminder** in the contract's **⋮** menu sends one now.
- **Signing links expire** after the number of days in Contract settings (30 by default), or after the template's own expiry when it sets one. An expired link is renewed with the next reminder.
- **Void** the contract from its **⋮** menu to withdraw it while signatures are outstanding; every open link stops working and signers who already have one are told. A sent contract keeps the template version it was sent with — [publishing changes](/speaker-crm/contract-templates#publish-and-keep-it-current) to the template affects new sends only.

## Track it

The row shows the progress — **0 of 2 signed · waiting on Jessica Tucker** — and expands to each signer's status, the template version, and the activity. Details are in [Track who has signed](/speaker-crm/contracts#track-who-has-signed).

## Still having issues?

If a required signer row is empty, the record is missing a name or email — fix the contact or account, or type the signer in the dialog. If the template is missing from the list, make sure it is published, not archived, and that its base module matches the record you are sending from. For anything else, email [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with the record name, the template, and a screenshot of the **Signers** list.
