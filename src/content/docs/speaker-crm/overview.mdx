Every event in Sessionboard belongs to an organization, and every contact you create in an event is an organization contact. Speaker CRM is the set of tools that works at that level — across events rather than inside one. It answers the questions a single event cannot: who has spoken for us before and how did it go, who applied last year and was not placed, which companies keep sending us speakers, and who should we be talking to for next year.

## Getting to the organization

Click your organization's name at the top of the left sidebar (or the switcher beside it) to leave an event and land on the organization **Dashboard**. The sidebar changes: instead of one event's Program, CRM and Marketing you see the organization's.

![The organization dashboard — totals, upcoming events, engagement flow, top companies and speaker source](/images/kb/org-dashboard.png)

## Dashboard

The tiles at the top count **Events**, **Contacts**, **Accepted Speakers** and **Returning Speakers** across the organization, with **Upcoming Events** listed below. The charts read from your contacts and sessions:

- **Speaker Engagement Flow** — all contacts, how many were emailed, how many accepted, how many spoke at more than one event.
- **Top Companies** — companies with the most contacts, so you can see where your speaker bench is concentrated. Click a company to open that list of contacts.
- **Speaker Source** — how speakers arrived: manual entry or import, submission form, or an application.
- **Contacts by Region** — where contacts are, from the City, State, Zip and Country fields.
- **Areas of Focus** — the session **Tags** your contacts have spoken on. Click a tag to open the matching contacts.

**Add Dashboard** creates a custom dashboard alongside the built-in **Overview** — see [Report builder](/reporting/report-builder).

## What lives where

### Program

- **Events**, **Awards**, **Webinars** — every program in the organization, past and upcoming. **Manage** opens one; **Delete** removes it permanently along with its forms, sessions and event-specific data.
- **Briefs**, [**Portfolios**](/events/portfolios), **Requests**, **Sites** — organization-level program tools, including the organization site that hosts your [interest forms](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms).

### CRM

- **Contacts** — every contact in the organization (speakers, moderators, chairpersons, submitters, sponsor and exhibitor contacts), whether created in an event or here. Columns, sort, filter and saved views work as they do on an event's contact list; the bulk bar adds **Add To Event**, **Add To Curated List**, **Communicate** and **Invite To Event**. **Exhibitors** and **Sponsors** are the organization's accounts.
- **Pipeline** — the kanban board where prospects move from *Identified* to *Confirmed*. See [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline).
- **Interest Forms** — standing "speak with us" forms that feed the pipeline all year. See [Interest forms](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms).
- **Segments** — dynamic segments and curated lists of contacts. See [Segments](/speaker-crm/crm-segments).
- **Scout**, **Contracts**, **Portal Tasks** — prospect research, [speaker contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts), and the portal tasks assigned across events.

![The organization Contacts directory with name, email, job title, company, last email and events](/images/kb/org-directory.png)

### Marketing, Learning, Reports, History

**Marketing** holds the organization's [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy) and [Dispatch](/marketing/dispatch) programs and media tools; **Learning** appears when Lessons & Credits is enabled. **Reports** are organization-wide, and **History** is the log of every email your team sent from the organization or any of its events — campaigns and individual sends, with delivery status. System-generated emails are not listed.

### Settings

- **Organization Details** — name, slug, logo and banner, public info.
- **Team & Permissions** — who has access to the organization and to which events. See [Inviting organization team members](/speaker-crm/inviting-organization-team-members).
- **Library** — **Files**, **Fields**, **Session Settings** and **Tags** shared across events. Organization fields are how you keep the same custom field on a contact from one event to the next; see [Creating CRM fields](/speaker-crm/creating-crm-fields).
- **Email Templates** and **Email Themes** — templates for emails sent from the organization, and the branding applied to them. See [Speaker CRM: communications](/speaker-crm/crm).
- **Integrations**, **API Tokens**, **Connected Apps**, **SSO** — see [Integrations](/integrations/overview) and the [developer documentation](https://sessionboard.stoplight.io/docs/sessionboard).
- **Pipeline** — stage configuration for the Speaker CRM pipeline.

<Note>
Contacts, fields and files exist at the organization level for every Sessionboard customer, which is why last year's speaker is already there when you add them to this year's event. Speaker CRM adds the working surfaces on top: the dashboard charts, Pipeline, Interest Forms, Segments, Contracts and duplicate merging.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What does “Speaker CRM: Not Enabled” mean?">
Your organization doesn't have Speaker CRM. The amber **Speaker CRM** pill on an event's Contacts page shows this when you hover over it. Contacts, fields and files still work at both the event and organization level. To ask about adding Speaker CRM, click **Learn more** in the pill and send the form, or contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we merge duplicate contacts without Speaker CRM?">
Yes, with Unique Contacts, which your Customer Success Manager turns on. Without Speaker CRM or Unique Contacts, a contact's profile can still show how many likely duplicates were found, but merging stays locked. See [Merge duplicate contacts](/contacts/merge-duplicates).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
