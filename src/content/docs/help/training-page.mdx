The **Training** page is built to onboard each new member of your team at their own pace. It gives you a checklist of the steps your organization or event needs, short videos for each one, and a running count of how far along you are. Everyone on the team can open it; what it shows depends on your permission role.

## Where to find it

Click **Training** in the left navigation. You get one at the organization level (team access, the Speaker CRM, templates and brand, which carry into every event) and one inside each event (that event's setup). Click **Browse all videos** at the top of the page for the full video library.

## What is on the page

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Your onboarding** | Shows your progress as a ring. When your role has steps, it also shows a second ring for the whole event or organization. Inside an event it asks whether the event runs a Call for Papers. |
| **Start here · Platform overview** | The key videos for getting started. After you finish each one, click **Mark as viewed**; it then reads **Viewed**. |
| **Your setup path** | A checklist grouped into modules. Open a module to see its steps, each with a **Learn how** link and a **Watch the chapter** video. Steps can be marked complete, or show **Detected** when Sessionboard found the work already done in your setup. |
| **Explore by product** | The most useful features in each product. |
| **Ask** | A note pointing you to Ask, which answers product questions inside Sessionboard. |

## Follow your setup path

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Training">

Click **Training** in the left navigation, at the organization or in the event you are setting up.

</Step>

<Step title="Tell it about your event (events only)">

Under **Your onboarding**, answer "Does this event run a Call for Papers?": **Yes, we collect submissions** or **No, we import our data**. The setup path changes to match. Events with a Call for Papers collect submissions through forms and review them; events without one import sessions and speakers directly, and submission forms and reviews are hidden from the path.

</Step>

<Step title="Work through the modules">

Open a module under **Your setup path**. Click **Learn how** to read the guide for a step, or **Watch the chapter** to play its video. Tick a step to mark it complete. Progress updates in the ring at the top.

</Step>

<Step title="Mark the key videos">

Under **Start here · Platform overview**, watch each video and click **Mark as viewed**.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
Not everyone can set the Call for Papers answer. If your role cannot, you see the current value with a note that an admin set it for the whole team, or that it was suggested from the event's setup and an admin can confirm it.
</Note>

## When some steps are not yours

Your path only counts steps your role can do. If your role has none, the page says none of the setup steps are yours to do, because an admin configures the event and you work inside it. The **Start here** videos are still the fastest way to learn your way around. Steps your work depends on but your role cannot do appear under **Handled by an admin**, each marked **Waiting** or **Done**, with the name of someone to ask.

## FAQ

**Why did a step tick itself?** It shows **Detected** when Sessionboard found the work already done in your setup, so there is nothing to tick.

**Does marking something complete affect my teammates?** Your own ring shows your path. The second ring shows the whole event or organization, so an admin can see the full picture.

**I cannot find an answer here.** Email support@sessionboard.com.
