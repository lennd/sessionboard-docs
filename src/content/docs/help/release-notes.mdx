## September 26, 2026

- **Site builder: drafts, blocks, and customizable program pages** — edits save to a draft and go live when you click Publish, with undo, redo, and a check for broken blocks before publishing. Landing and custom pages are built from 21 block types, including images, video, form links, buttons, and columns. Public Speakers and Agenda pages can use the List, Gallery, Agenda grid, Session list, or Schedule layout with the same fields, filters, and display options as an embed, or import them from one. [Site builder](/site/site-builder)
- **A wave of Early Access features is now on for everyone** — AI Event Agents, Email Campaigns, Automations, Print Agendas, and Notifications & Messaging graduated from Early Access and are on by default for every organization, with no toggle needed. [Early Access](/get-started/early-access)
- **New Navigation for every organization** — the grouped sidebar is now the standard navigation. Organizations with an event starting within 30 days keep the old sidebar until that event wraps. A short tour points out where things moved the first time you see it. [Find your way around the sidebar](/get-started/sidebar)
- **Marketing, Advocacy, and Live Transcribe on every event** — the Marketing module now appears for every event with its Overview, [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy), and [Live Transcribe](/marketing/live-transcribe). The rest of the module remains an Early Access beta, and the Overview shows only what your organization has.
- **Notes and Messages are now separate on records** — contact, session, and group records all carry a team-only **Notes** tab with the same editor, and **Messages** is reserved for conversations delivered to the people on the record. Internal notes that used to live inside session message threads moved to the session's Notes tab. @mention a teammate in a note and they are notified. [Notifications and messaging](/communications/notifications#messages-and-notes-on-records)
- **Portal messaging is off by default** — new portals start with **Enable Messaging** off, so opening a reply channel to portal users is a deliberate choice per portal. Portals that were already using messaging keep it on. [Create custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals#let-portal-users-message-your-team)
- **Poster Gallery has a full guide** — the Early Access beta now ships with complete documentation: building a gallery, access modes, embargo and consent, and the visitor experience. Request access per event from Early Access. [Poster galleries](/marketing/poster-gallery)
- **Studio retired in favour of Agents** — the old Remix, persona AI Evaluators, and the paged AI Agenda Builder are replaced by the Editor, Reviewer, and Scheduler agents in the **Agents** tab, which every event now has. Existing persona evaluation plans and their scores remain readable. [Agents overview](/agents/overview)

## September 25, 2026

- **LiveBuzz theaters from any session field** — **LiveBuzz theaters come from** picks what becomes a theater: the room (default) or any dropdown session field such as Format, Track, or Tags, matched by name and created when missing. Topics can come from the same fields. [LiveBuzz](/apps/livebuzz-integration#connect-livebuzz)
- **LiveBuzz syncs only the sessions and speakers you choose** — **Only sync sessions where** and **Only sync speakers where** add rules on any dropdown field; records that stop matching are unpublished like an un-accept, and skipped ones say which rule they missed. [LiveBuzz](/apps/livebuzz-integration#what-syncs)
- **A stopped LiveBuzz sync reads Cancelled** — cancelling an in-progress sync from Sync History lands the row as **Cancelled** with a "Sync cancelled" header, not as a Warning. Selecting people and clicking **Sync to LiveBuzz** is documented as an admin override. [LiveBuzz](/apps/livebuzz-integration#stop-a-sync-that-is-taking-too-long)
- **Maritz RTS pulls large windows in chunks** — a first backfill of a big show, or a long gap between runs, is pulled automatically in chunks of at most 9,000 registrants instead of failing at Maritz's cap. Reg State stores Maritz's raw status code. [Maritz RTS](/apps/maritz-rts-integration#run-a-sync)
- **Text colour on Sessionboard Sites** — Primary, Accent, and Text Color under Logged-In Experience now apply to the landing page, sign-in cards, and portal, including the evaluator site. Text Color is applied exactly as entered, so check it against your gradient. [Setting up your Sessionboard Site](/awards/setting-up-your-sessionboard-site#branding-settings)
- **Up next in Ready Room** — the session view has an **Up next** panel with a **Next session** button, the ordered queue for the room and the day, and a search to jump to any session, so a producer never returns to the schedule between sessions. A running transcription has to be stopped first. [Ready Room](/marketing/ready-room#what-the-crew-sees)
- **Limit a session manager by Source** — a session filter on a team member's role can now use **Source**, so they see only sessions that came in through a given submission form, or were added manually. [Invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members)
- **Can promote sessions permission** — promoting submissions to the next round of a funnel evaluation plan needs the new **Can promote sessions** permission under Evaluation Plans. Admins have it; grant it to a custom role to let session managers promote, within their session filter. [Invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members#custom-roles)
- **Participant inventory is enabled by support** — the **Participant Inventory** panel in Agenda settings appears once Sessionboard support has enabled the feature for your event. [Track participant inventory on sessions](/sessions/participant-inventory#turn-on-participant-inventory)
- **Visit checkbox questions map by answer count** — a Visit checkbox with several answers maps to a Sessionboard multi-select dropdown, and a single-answer checkbox maps to a Sessionboard checkbox. The consent picker lists contact forms only. [Visit](/apps/visit-integration#field-mappings)
- **Automation recipients combine** — picking several **Send to** choices on a send-email step emails everyone in any selected group, once each. [Automations](/automations/overview#send-email)
- **Bundled campaign counts match the send** — the recipient count on a one-email-per-session campaign leaves out bounced and unsubscribed contacts, the same as the send does. [Email campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns#one-email-per-session)
- **Reorder subsessions by drag and drop** — drag subsessions into your own order on the parent session's Connections tab, with keyboard support. The agenda, embeds, and the public API follow the order; display IDs do not change. [Create a subsession](/sessions/create-a-subsession#reordering-subsessions)
- **Default status for new submissions** — each submission form, application form, and awards program chooses the status new entries start in: a standard status or a custom one for session forms, and Submitted, Under review, Accepted, or Rejected for awards. The status toggle is now **Show custom status name to external users**. [Submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms#new-submissions)
- **Participant inventory per role** — set a **Min / Max** for each participant role on a session, read them together in the **Role Inventory** grid column, and filter on an **Assignment Status: Role** column to find every session still missing a moderator. [Track participant inventory on sessions](/sessions/participant-inventory#set-limits-per-role)
- **Archive fields instead of deleting them** — **Archive** hides a field from forms, pickers, and column pickers for the organization or a single event while keeping stored values read-only; restore it from the **Hidden Fields** tab. System fields can be archived but not deleted, and the five core fields can be renamed. [Create and delete custom fields](/faq/how-to-create-and-delete-custom-fields#archive-a-field)
- **Export Advocacy assets** — select campaigns and choose **Export assets** to gather every card's asset URL and share link as a table or CSV, or download all rendered cards as a ZIP organised by campaign and card type, delivered by email when it is built. [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy#export-assets)
- **Generate a report from the Evaluators tab** — **Options → Generate report** on an evaluation plan's Evaluators tab creates a Custom Report with the tab's columns plus average scores per round, per evaluator question, and across rounds, ready to share, schedule, and export. [Custom reports](/reporting/custom-reports#generate-a-report-from-an-evaluation-plan)
- **Generic speaker tags on transcripts** — tag a voice as Audience Member, MC / Emcee, Staff Member, Unknown Speaker, or a type your organization adds, with a label to tell several apart, without creating a contact. [Media library](/marketing/media-library#tag-speakers)
- **Edit transcript lines** — correct any line of a transcript in place; recaps, exports, search, and the attendee hub use the corrected text, the original is kept behind an **Edited** chip with history, and re-transcribing warns before discarding manual edits. [Media library](/marketing/media-library#edit-a-transcript-line)

## September 24, 2026

- **The speaker portal holds the file limit** — once a session is at its file limit, another brand-new file is refused, including when an event admin is using the portal. A new version of a file already on the session is still allowed. The Files tab in the event admin app is not held to that limit. [Upload files](/participants/upload-files)
- **Automation emails can go to one session role** — a session automation's **Send to** is the same role list as Send on the Sessions list. Pick submitters, all participants, speakers, primary speakers, chairs, moderators, or a custom role, and combine more than one. Automations saved before this change keep their previous recipients until you change **Send to**. [Automations](/automations/overview#send-email)
- **Visit consent can be a contact-only form** — the consent picker includes a form or task assigned to one contact, not only ones assigned to a portal. A Visit checkbox can also map to a Sessionboard multi-select, and the values sync both ways. [Visit](/apps/visit-integration)
- **System notification emails show as Sent** — emails such as a review assignment are recorded in History as **Sent** once they leave Sessionboard, including the from-address, instead of staying on **No delivery data**. Older messages that were never recorded this way still show **No delivery data**. [History](/communications/email-sms-history#email-status-definitions)

## September 21, 2026

- **Field changes show up in History** — renaming a field, changing its type, or adding, renaming or removing a dropdown option is now recorded in **History → Audit** under the **Field** type, with who did it and when. Deleting a field is recorded too, and the entry keeps the deleted field's name so the history still reads properly afterwards. Changes made through the public API appear alongside the rest. [History](/communications/email-sms-history#changes-to-your-setup-not-just-your-records)
- **Membership, permission and API token changes are recorded** — inviting someone to the organization, changing their permission role, removing their access, editing a permission profile, and creating or revoking an API token all now leave an entry in History. Token values are never recorded. [History](/communications/email-sms-history#changes-to-your-setup-not-just-your-records)
- **History filters are grouped** — the **Type** and **Action** filters separate records from configuration and from access and security, so the list stays scannable as more of the product is covered. Both narrow the CSV export as well as the screen. [History](/communications/email-sms-history#narrowing-the-log)

## September 19, 2026

- **Exports name their timezone** — every date and time in a downloaded file now carries the zone it was written in (`2026-04-01 18:30 PDT`, or an explicit offset where the zone has no abbreviation, or `UTC` where no timezone is set). Exports render in the event or organization timezone while grids render in your browser's, and neither used to say so. [Exporting data](/reporting/exporting-data#dates-and-times-in-an-export)
- **Restrict a file question to certain file types** — **Customize Question > Accepted file types** limits a file upload question to PDF, Word, Image, or any other combination you tick, on session submission forms and awards entries alike. Sessionboard reads the file's contents rather than trusting its name, so renaming a JPEG to `.pdf` no longer gets it past a PDF-only question. [Restrict a file question to certain file types](/faq/what-is-the-maximum-file-size-that-sessionboard-supports#restrict-a-file-question-to-certain-file-types)
- **Awards notifications send from your organization's address** — leaving a notification's **From** field blank now means "use the organization system email" instead of a Sessionboard address that failed your domain check, so a notification saves without being hand-edited first. [Awards notifications & email templates](/awards/awards-notifications-email-templates#who-the-emails-come-from)
- **Portal audiences by source and session status** — a contacts portal can now be filtered by **[Session] Source** (which submission or application form brought someone in, plus Manual) and by **[Session] Session Status**, including custom statuses. [Create custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals#filter-by-how-a-contact-arrived-or-by-session-status)
- **Portal due dates are enforced** — once a task, form, or file request passes its due date, portal users can no longer mark it complete. Set an **Extended Due Date** to allow completion afterwards; already-complete items can still be reopened. [Create & assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks)
- **Copied participants join the destination event** — copying sessions with **Copy participants** on now adds those contacts to the event you copied into, so they appear in Contacts and on the session's Participants tab instead of being invisible to every event-scoped view. [Copy sessions from another event](/sessions/copy-sessions-from-another-event)

## September 18, 2026

- **Rename a field in one embed** — give a session or speaker field different wording on a public page ("Topic Area" instead of "Track") without renaming the field anywhere else. Click the ellipses button beside a selected field in the embed's field step and choose **Customize label**. Two embeds on the same event can show the same field under different names. [Custom field labels](/sessions/embeds#custom-field-labels)
- **Clearing a suppression lifts it everywhere** — the suppression list now covers every blocked address your event is responsible for, including ones blocked by another event or by a system email, and clearing a block releases every block held against that person instead of only the one raised here. When the upstream provider refuses part of the clear, the confirmation names which list is still holding the address. [Suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list)
- **Sending-domain health, rechecked on a schedule** — organization **Settings → Admin** now shows whether your custom sending domain is still authenticated, when it was last checked, and which DNS records are failing. A domain that silently stops validating raises an alert instead of being discovered mid-campaign. [Add-on: custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain#check-your-domains-authentication-status)
- **Delivery history for automations** — a new **Delivery history** tab in Automations lists every record an automation enrolled and whether an email actually went out, and keeps the record after the automation is deleted. [Automations](/automations/overview#delivery-history)
- **"Don't personalize" really means it** — a campaign test send with no record picked now leaves merge tags literal, instead of resolving them against a record Sessionboard guessed for you. [Email campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns)
- **System notification emails appear in Sent Emails** — emails such as **New review assigned** are now recorded in **History → Sent Emails**. [History](/communications/email-sms-history)
- **Help text on portal fields** — write guidance under any field in a portal's **Manage Fields**, per portal, and portal users see it right where they fill the field in. [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#add-help-text-to-a-field)
- **Match session imports on your own ID** — a session import row can be matched on **Client Session ID** as well as Session Friendly ID, and mapping an identifier onto the **Update session if already exists** switch now fails the row instead of quietly creating a duplicate. The award and interest import template's notes row is written in your admin language. [Importing data](/settings/importing-data)
- **Drop a standard speaker field from Grip** — an event can now remove Mobile Phone, Headshot, or Biography from what it sends to Grip, so personal mobile numbers need not leave Sessionboard. [Grip](/apps/grip-integration#drop-a-standard-speaker-field-for-one-event)
- **Visit registration emails in the registrant's language** — map a Sessionboard contact field to **Language (Visit translation ID)** and Visit sends its confirmation in that language. The Sync Results modal now also leads with the run's real status and the reason it failed. [Visit](/apps/visit-integration)
- **Reviewer recommendations and session builds in single-round plans** — both settings are reachable in a plan with one round, not only in multi-round plans. [Round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations)

## September 17, 2026

- **A guide to the sidebar** — a new article maps the whole sidebar: what lives in each module at the organization and event level, how to pin the pages you use daily, and where fields, tags and personas moved to. Sessionboard switches every organization to the grouped sidebar on September 25, 2026; organizations with an event starting within 30 days are switched after that event. [Find your way around the sidebar](/get-started/sidebar)
- **Recent runs on the Coordinator dashboard** — the Coordinator's Dashboard now lists its last ten runs: what each one drafted, how it ended, and how many eligible speakers it did not reach. A finished run used to leave no trace in the product at all. [Check Coordinator run history](/agents/coordinator-run-history)
- **Submission forms read right to left** — pick Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, or another right-to-left language on a multi-language form and the form is laid out right to left, not only translated. [Right-to-left languages](/sessions/submission-forms#right-to-left-languages)
- **Find translated fields in Add Field** — search the form builder's **Add Field** list by a field's translated name or by locale code, and the row containing your match opens expanded. The **Locked** badge on a standard field now explains on hover that adding a translated variant of it unlocks the row. [Add a translated field to the form](/sessions/submission-forms#add-a-translated-field-to-the-form)
- **Portal times name the event timezone** — a custom **Date & Time** field in a portal used to render in UTC with nothing on screen naming the zone, so a participant could read a time hours away from the one you entered. It now shows the event timezone with its abbreviation, the way session start and end times already did, and daylight saving is applied per value. [Dates and times in the portal](/portals/portals-101#dates-and-times-in-the-portal)
- **File request submissions can notify the event team** — when a contact or group submits a file for a file request, you can get a bell notification and an email instead of watching the File Requests tab. **Notifications & Messaging** must be on for the organization, and **File Request Submitted** starts off — turn in-app and/or email on under **Notifications > Preferences**. [Collect documents/file requests](/portals/collect-documents#get-notified-when-a-file-is-submitted)
- **Session file uploads notify once, and only for participants** — uploading files on a session's **Files** tab used to send admins two notifications per upload, both worded as if a revision had been submitted, including when an admin did the uploading. It now sends one, only when a participant uploads, and says whether the file was uploaded or replaced. [Enable upload & download of content](/sessions/enable-upload-download-content)

- **Reconcile payments against your payment gateway** — a new **Reconciliation** tab on Awards and event invoices puts each invoice next to your gateway's own record of the charge: both statuses side by side, the processor's charge ID, a link to its hosted receipt, and a result saying whether the two agree. **Needs attention** narrows the table to the payments that disagree, and the organization-wide Awards export adds the charges your gateway processed that have no invoice at all. [Awards pricing, payments & invoices](/awards/awards-pricing-payments-invoices#reconciling-payments-with-your-gateway)
- **Tree fields open the next level in one click** — picking a value that has sub-values now reveals the level below it immediately, instead of taking a second click to expand. A new **only allow selecting the last level** field setting makes the upper tiers navigation-only, so every stored answer is a specific end value, and exports with several tree answers now join them with a pipe — the same separator an import file uses, so an export can be edited and re-imported. [Custom field type reference](/concepts/custom-field-type-reference#the-tree-field-multi-level-choices)
- **Reviewer recommendations on evaluations** — evaluators can mark each submission **Accept**, **Decline**, or **Alternate** alongside their scores; Alternate proposes new values for fields you choose (Format, Track, …) plus a note. Counts roll up in a Recommendations column on the plan's Submissions tab. [Round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#reviewer-recommendations)
- **Reviewer session builds** — let evaluators propose combining their assigned abstracts into full sessions, within a per-reviewer quota you set per session type. Proposals land on a new **Builds** tab where you approve — creating the real session with the abstracts as subsessions — or decline with a reason. [Reviewer session builds](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#reviewer-session-builds)
- **Sort by score and saved views for evaluators** — evaluators can sort their queue by the score they've given and save named filter + sort combinations per round, with a starred default that applies automatically. [Evaluate sessions](/evaluations/how-to-evaluate-sessions#sorting-and-saved-views)
- **Suppression list for email campaigns** — see every address your event can no longer email, why each one was blocked, and clear a block once the cause is fixed. The list separates addresses that genuinely **Bounced** from those caught by a **Send failure**, where an entire message was rejected before sending — usually an unverified sending domain — so nobody received it and nothing bounced. Campaign run history now names which of the two skipped a recipient. [Suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list)
- **Maritz RTS connector** — pull registrant profiles from Maritz Real-Time Services onto existing event contacts: job title, company, bio, and registration state stay current automatically on a 10-minute schedule. Inbound only — registrants match by Registrant ID then email, unmatched registrants are skipped and logged, and contacts are never auto-created. [Maritz RTS](/apps/maritz-rts-integration)
- **Embeds in your visitors' language** — every embed widget can now render in English, German, French, or Spanish: labels, buttons, search, and dates translate, and session titles, descriptions, and field values follow your event's [language variants](/settings/language-translation-variant) with automatic fallback for anything untranslated. Set a default in Style Options, or paste a per-language snippet on each localized page — one embed serves them all. [Embed languages](/sessions/embeds#embed-languages)
- **Choose which participant roles embeds show** — a new Participant Roles setting in the embed builder controls which roles appear across all widgets, including any custom roles you've created. Participants are grouped under their role name on session cards and pop-out views. [Agenda & speaker embeds](/sessions/embeds#how-to-create-an-embed)
- **Subsession details in embeds** — visitors can click a subsession inside a session's pop-out view to open its own detail view with its time, location, description, and participants. [Subsession details](/sessions/embeds#subsession-details)

- **Copy a site's configuration** — the Add Site dialog can start a new site from an existing one, copying settings, branding, and pages while you pick a fresh URL; the Clone Event options gain a matching **Sites** checkbox. [Setting up your site](/awards/setting-up-your-sessionboard-site#creating-a-new-site) · [Clone an event](/faq/clone-an-event)

Ideal for an instant "we received your submission" email. [Automations](/automations/overview)
