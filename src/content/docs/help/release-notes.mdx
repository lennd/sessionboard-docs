## October 6, 2026

- **Sites: Rooms layout for the Agenda page** — the Agenda page's **Layout** gains **Rooms**: one column per room, sessions stacked by time, a tab per day, the room's capacity under its name when you have set one, and an **Unassigned** column for sessions without a room. Rooms follow the order from **Program → Settings → Locations**. Like the Agenda grid, it needs the Location field on. [Layouts](/site/site-builder#layouts)
- **Sites: Countdown block** — a new block under **Marketing** counts down to one exact moment and prints the date in your event's time zone. Pick **Tiles**, **One line** or **Badge**, add a label, hide the seconds, set what it says once the moment passes, and add a button. It starts on your event's start date; **Use event start** puts it back. [Countdown](/site/site-builder#countdown)
- **Sites: your own domain (limited release)** — a Site can be served from a domain you own, such as `events.example.org`, with pages at the root and your domain alone in the address bar, search results and share cards. Connecting is handled with your Customer Success Manager for now: you add a CNAME and a TXT record, and the Site goes live on the new address once the certificate is issued. Marketing-layout pages are indexable with canonical links, schema.org `Event` data and a `sitemap.xml` on your domain. [Your own domain](/site/site-builder#your-own-domain)
- **v1 / v2 badges on forms and evaluation plans** — every submission form and evaluation plan now shows a **v1** or **v2** badge, with a **Version** filter and a count of what is still on v1, so you can see what to convert before an event's 60-day lock applies. Your Customer Success Manager can unlock v1 editing for an event when converting is not an option; the list then says so. [Forms still on the older builder](/applications/building-your-submission-form#forms-still-on-the-older-builder) · [Evaluation plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans#where-evaluation-lives)
- **Cloned events come out on v2** — cloning converts v1 submission forms and v1 evaluation plans on the copy (the originals are untouched) and turns on the Embeds 2.0 builder; v1 plans that use personas are copied as-is and keep their **v1** badge. [Clone an event](/faq/clone-an-event#what-gets-copied)
- **Welcome survey: revisit a section after submitting** — opening a section link of a submitted welcome survey now shows that section read-only, with **Back to summary**, instead of landing on the summary every time.

## October 5, 2026

- **Public voting: the ballot shows your plan name and instructions** — the heading on a public voting ballot is now the evaluation plan's name (the awards program's name on an awards round) and the text under it is the round's instructions, formatting included, in place of the generic *Cast your vote* copy. Works on event, Interest Form and awards rounds. [Public voting on the Program Site](/site/program-site#public-voting)
- **Who can vote on Interest Form and awards rounds** — the **Ballot** and **Who can vote** groups from event evaluation plans now appear on Interest Form rounds and on awards rounds set to Public voting: random ballots with a per-voter size and **Reset ballots**, an eligible-voter list you upload with **Ticket holder** / **Community member** groups, and ×2 / ×1 weighting. Round results on both add **Weighted avg** and **Distinct voters** with an **All voters / Ticket holders / Community** filter. **Votes per voter** on an Interest Form round is now enforced on the ballot and shown to voters as their budget. [Interest Form evaluation plans](/speaker-crm/interest-form-evaluation-plans#what-is-the-same-as-event-evaluations) · [Awards: who evaluates this round](/awards/managing-submissions-reviewers-rounds#who-evaluates-this-round)

## October 2, 2026

- **Public voting: see what you have voted on, undo a vote, step through the ballot** — submissions a voter has scored carry a **Voted** badge, the ballot counts them (*3 of 12 voted*) and filters to **Voted** or **Not voted**; inside a submission, **Previous** and **Next** move through the ballot and **Back to all submissions** returns to the list, as evaluators do in their queue. A voted submission shows its saved score, and **Undo vote** takes it back while the round is open, returning the vote to a **Votes per voter** budget. Works on event, Interest Form and awards rounds. [What voters see once they have voted](/site/program-site#what-voters-see-once-they-have-voted)
- **Sites: favicon and social share image** — **Branding** on a Site gains a **Favicon**, shown in the browser tab on every page of the Site including vote ballots, and a **Social share image**, the card shown when a link to the Site or a vote link is pasted into Slack, LinkedIn, Teams or iMessage. Both offer **Upload** or **Copy from…** the organization's image library, and both are carried over when a Site is copied. Left empty, the favicon falls back to the logo and the share card to the portal background image or logo. [Site URL & branding](/awards/setting-up-your-sessionboard-site#site-url--branding)
- **Email subsession speakers from a session selection** — the Send Email composer in **Sessions** gains **Sessions to include**: keep the default **Selected sessions only**, add every selected session's subsessions with **Selected sessions and their subsessions**, or send to **Subsessions only**. It works with **Select all N matching this filter**, so one send reaches every subsession presenter in a program, each with their own subsession's merge tags. [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails#fill-in-the-envelope)
- **Upload slots: ask speakers for specific files** — **Program → Settings → Files** gains a **File Requests** card with a **Use upload slots** switch. On, you define a list of slots — Poster, Presentation, Handout — each with its own due date and extended deadline, accepted file types, file count, **Required** flag, session filters (**Format is Poster**, say) and the roles who may upload; drag to reorder, archive and restore. Turning it on converts your current due date, file limit and file types into the first slot, so nothing changes for speakers until you add more. New and cloned events start with slots on; existing events keep their single file request until you flip the switch. The Poster Gallery page flags events still on the single request, since a gallery collects from a Poster slot. [Upload slots](/sessions/enable-upload-download-content#upload-slots) · [Where posters come from](/marketing/poster-gallery#where-posters-come-from)
- **Public voting on Interest Form evaluation plans reaches voters** — the **Public voting** banner on an Interest Form round now links to the organization Site that lists the form (it previously offered to create an event Site), voters following that link land on their ballot, and the round's results table loads. Eligible-voter lists and ticket-holder weighting remain event-only, so Interest Form rounds no longer show a **Who can vote** group. [Interest Form evaluation plans](/speaker-crm/interest-form-evaluation-plans#what-is-different)
- **Voting on an Interest Form ballot** — Format, Level and other catalog dropdowns on a submission now show their labels to voters instead of internal ids; a saved score stays on the scorecard after **Submit score**; and a score chosen before signing in is submitted automatically once the voter is back from the sign-in link, provided the scorecard still asks the same questions. The sign-in email a voter receives says *Sign in to cast your vote* rather than talking about submissions, and the sign-in and welcome cards fall back to readable text when a Site's saved text colour cannot be read on its primary colour.

## October 1, 2026

- **Participants in the order they were submitted** — a new **Participant display order** on the Roles page (limited release — the lock beside **Add** says whether it is on for your event) shows every participant on a session in one list, in the order the submitter listed them, with role badges; roles themselves drag to reorder, and reports sort by the same **Display order**. Submissions already in your event keep the order they were entered in on the form. [Role order and participant display order](/sessions/program-settings#role-order-and-participant-display-order)
- **Role Attributes** — define Text, Textarea and Dropdown attributes per participant role on a new **Attributes** tab of the Roles page (Enterprise, alongside Speaker Affiliations), fill them in on each session's Participants tab, and have submission forms collect them next to affiliations with the **Collect role attributes** switch; each attribute is a sortable column on participant contacts in the Report Builder. [Attributes](/sessions/program-settings#attributes)
- **CMS module in the organization sidebar** — **Sites** and **Content Galleries** moved out of **Program → Build** into their own **CMS** module, in the same slot as the event CMS (Program, CRM, Marketing, CMS, Learning). Its Overview explains the four publishing surfaces and which ones are built per event. [The sidebar](/get-started/sidebar#cms)
- **Notification settings from the bell** — the settings icon in the top-right of the notifications menu opens Preferences for the organization or event you are in. [Notifications](/communications/notifications#the-notification-bell)
- **Ballot step for Public voting rounds** — the evaluation plan wizard gains a **Ballot** step whenever a round uses Public voting. Add submissions to the ballot from the whole plan scope, by submission filters, or one by one, and remove any that have not been voted on yet; a plan whose rounds are all Public voting goes straight from Rounds to Ballot. Random ballots are no longer dealt from an empty round, and voters whose random subset is short are dealt submissions added later, even after voting has started. **Reset ballots** gives way to a dealt/votes notice once a vote exists. Works on event plans and Interest Form plans. [Ballot step](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#step-5-ballot)
- **Title slides inside speakers' presentations, and a check for stale ones** — after a Slides run, download any single session's deck or attach the decks to sessions without generating again. **Program → Files** is now **Presentations & files**, with filters by file type, session status, submission status and title-slide state, and an **Add title slide to presentations…** action that writes a *(with title slide)* copy of each selected PDF or PowerPoint with the branded title slide at the start, end or both — originals untouched. Every generated slide and merged copy is marked **Out of date** when the session or template changes afterwards, with the reasons and a one-click **Regenerate**. [Presentations & files](/marketing/slides#presentations--files) · [Keep title slides up to date](/marketing/slides#keep-title-slides-up-to-date)
- **Design title slides in Sessionboard** — Slides now starts from six branded designs built from your brand kit and session roles, with a live preview on your real sessions. Choose which participant groups appear and in what order (moderators first, say), how many fit, and which details print under each name; export editable PowerPoint slides or Letter and A4 cover pages, with an optional end slide. [Slides](/marketing/slides)
- **The unread dot on list rows explains itself** — the red dot beside a session, contact, sponsor, exhibitor or person in a list marks unread messages, notes or activity, not an error. Hover it to see what is waiting and click it to open the record on the tab that has the unread item. [The unread dot on a row](/communications/notifications#the-unread-dot-on-a-row)
- **Download a blank copy of any form** — **Download Form** on submission, application, portal, intake, interest and awards forms saves the form as a PDF or Word document, with every question, section, description and choice laid out in order, including conditional questions, each noted with when it shows. [Download a blank copy of a form](/concepts/download-a-blank-form)

[Export recipients with their magic links](/marketing/advocacy#export-recipients-with-their-magic-links)

[Reviewer session builds](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#reviewer-session-builds)
- **Visit: map dropdown options to Visit answers** — a **Map values** step on each dropdown or multi-select mapping row pairs every Sessionboard option with the Visit answer it registers as, auto-suggests matches even when Visit's answer names carry a bracketed prefix, and sends the answer code on sync; unmapped values are skipped and listed in that person's sync result. [Map values](/apps/visit-integration#map-values-send-the-visit-answer-not-the-option-text)

[Which field maps to which question](/apps/visit-integration#which-sessionboard-field-can-map-to-which-visit-question)
- **Automations fire on every source of change** — a field-change automation now runs whether the value was changed by an admin, a portal user, a form, an import, the Public API, or an integration writing back (for example the Visit registration ID). [Automations](/automations/overview#when)
- **Automations: Update language value** — a new action translates or copies a session or person field into another language enabled on the event when the record changes, filling only empty values or replacing them. [Update language value](/automations/overview#update-language-value)
- **Fill a language for many sessions at once** — select sessions on the Sessions list or Submissions view, then choose **More** > **Update language values** to translate, or copy as-is, one text field from one enabled language into another for up to 5,000 sessions. It can fill only empty values or replace existing ones, runs in the background with progress, and doesn't trigger field-change automations. [Fill a language for many sessions at once](/settings/language-translation-variant#fill-a-language-for-many-sessions-at-once)
- **See which language a submission came in** — every form submission records the language it was filled in. The session sidebar shows it next to the form name, and the Public API returns it with `expand=submission_source`. [Which language a submission came in](/settings/language-translation-variant#which-language-a-submission-came-in)
- **Dashboards hold up to 20 widgets** — the per-dashboard widget limit is raised from 10 to 20, and the limit is enforced everywhere a widget is added, including the AI dashboard builder. [Report builder](/reporting/report-builder#manage-a-saved-report)
