Community is where you tell us what to build next and watch it happen. You can vote for the ideas that matter to your team, suggest new ones, follow what is planned and in progress, and read what just shipped.

It replaces our old feedback board at feedback.sessionboard.com. Every idea, vote, comment and product update from that board moved into Community, so your earlier votes still count.

## Open Community

Click **Community** in the sidebar footer, either in your organization or inside any event. It opens on **Ideas**.

The **Help** menu in the header goes straight to a tab:

| Help menu entry | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Release Notes** | What's New |
| **Product Roadmap** | Roadmap |
| **Submit Feedback** | Ideas |

## Ideas

**Ideas** lists every suggestion from Sessionboard customers, with its vote count and status. Sort by **Top**, **Trending** or **Newest**, filter by status (**Open**, **Under Review**, **Planned**, **In Progress**, **Shipped**), or narrow to your own organization's ideas: **We submitted**, **We voted**, or **My org is on it** for anything your organization submitted, voted for or commented on.

- **Vote** for an idea to tell us you want it. Votes are how we weigh what to build, so vote for everything your team would use, not just your top pick.
- **Open an idea** to read the details and comments, add your own comment, and see whether we have posted an official response.
- Click **Submit Idea** when nothing on the list covers it. Describe the problem you are trying to solve, not only the button you want. That makes it easier for other customers to find and vote for it.

<Tip>
Use **Search ideas…** before you post. If someone already suggested it, voting for that idea carries more weight than a second copy.
</Tip>

## Roadmap

**Roadmap** shows the ideas we have committed to, grouped by how far along they are. When an idea you voted for moves forward, it moves here too.

## What's New

**What's New** is the product update feed, newest first. A red dot on the tab means there are updates you have not read yet. The same updates are listed with links to each guide in the Help Center [release notes](/help/release-notes).

## Updates on ideas you voted for

When we change the status of an idea you voted for, post an official response on it, or ship it in a product update, we can notify you in Sessionboard and by email. Clicking the notification opens the idea, or What's New for a product update.

<Note>
Notifications go to the Sessionboard account that cast the vote. A vote that came over from the old feedback board under an email address that has never signed in to Sessionboard stays counted, but does not receive notifications.
</Note>
