It replaces our old feedback board at feedback.sessionboard.com. Every idea, vote, comment and product update from that board moved into Community, so your earlier votes still count.

## Why use it

Community is where you ask for the changes your team needs and follow them through to release. When a workaround keeps costing your team time, vote for an idea someone already raised or submit your own, then watch it move across **Roadmap** and into **What's New** when it ships. If you voted for an idea, you get a notification when its status changes.

## Open Community

Click **Community** in the sidebar footer, either in your organization or inside any event. It opens on **Ideas**.

The **Help** menu in the header goes straight to a tab:

| Help menu entry | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Release Notes** | What's New |
| **Product Roadmap** | Roadmap |
| **Submit Feedback** | Ideas |

## Ideas

**Ideas** lists every suggestion from Sessionboard customers, with its vote count and status. It opens on **Open** ideas. Sort by **Top**, **Trending** or **Newest**, filter by status (**Open**, **Under Review**, **Planned**, **In Progress**, **Shipped**, or **All statuses**), or narrow to your own organization's ideas: **We submitted**, **We voted**, or **My org is on it** for anything your organization submitted, voted for or commented on. The counts beside each category follow whatever filters are active, so the number in the sidebar always matches the list.

![The Ideas tab: categories with counts on the left, Top / Trending / Newest, Submit Idea, the organization and status chips, and idea cards with their vote counts](/images/kb/community-ideas-board.png)

- **Vote** for an idea to tell us you want it. Votes are how we weigh what to build, so vote for everything your team would use, not just your top pick.
- **Open an idea** to read the details and comments, add your own comment, and see whether we have posted an official response.
- Click **Submit Idea** when nothing on the list covers it. Describe the problem you are trying to solve, not only the button you want. That makes it easier for other customers to find and vote for it.

<Tip>
Use **Search ideas…** before you post. If someone already suggested it, voting for that idea carries more weight than a second copy.
</Tip>

## Roadmap

**Roadmap** shows what is on the way and what just landed, in three columns:

- **Beta**: features our team turns on with you. Click **Request access** to ask.
- **Preview**: features you can turn on yourself from [Early Access](/get-started/early-access). Click **Turn it on**.
- **Released**: the newest features that are live for everyone, each linking to its release note and listing the Community ideas it delivered.

![The Roadmap tab with its Beta, Preview and Released columns](/images/kb/community-roadmap-board.png)

When an idea you voted for ships, it appears under the release that delivered it, and the idea itself shows **Shipped in** with a link to that release note.

## What's New

**What's New** is the product update feed, grouped by the day each change went live (**Today**, **Yesterday**, then the date). A **New since your last visit** line marks where you left off.

To see updates for one part of the product, pick it in the **Module** menu above the feed, such as **Program** or **Marketing**. The menu lists only modules that have updates; **All modules** shows everything again.

![The What's New feed with the Module menu set to Marketing, showing a dated group and one Marketing update card](/images/kb/whats-new-module-filter.png)

![The Community item in the sidebar with a red dot to its right, above Early Access](/images/kb/community-sidebar-unread-dot.png)

A red dot next to **Community** in the sidebar means something has gone live since you last looked. While the dot is showing, clicking **Community** opens straight onto **What's New**; once you have read it, the dot clears and **Community** opens on **Ideas** again.

Each card shows what changed, the area of the product it belongs to, and two links: **Read the release note** opens the entry in the Help Center [release notes](/help/release-notes), and **How to use it** opens the guide for that feature. When a change comes with screenshots or a video, a thumbnail sits on the right of the card. Click it to open them full size and step through with the arrows.

![A What's New card with a Fixed badge, the Marketing module, the change description and a screenshot thumbnail labelled 1 screenshot](/images/kb/community-whats-new-card-thumbnails.png)

![The screenshot viewer opened from a What's New card, showing the image with its caption underneath](/images/kb/community-whats-new-media-lightbox.png)

## Release notes by email

If you would rather not check **What's New**, have the release notes come to you. The email is a short digest sent on the days something new goes live: one line per change, each with a link to its release note. Nothing is sent on days with no changes, there is no marketing in it, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

### Turn it on in Sessionboard

At the top of **What's New**, switch on **Release notes by email**. The digest goes to the email address on your Sessionboard account.

![The Release notes by email card at the top of What's New, with its description and the switch set to Off](/images/kb/community-whats-new-email-card.png)

The same switch is in your account settings, under **Community notifications**, so you can turn it off from either place. It is one setting for your account, not per organization.

![The Release notes by email switch in Account settings, below Community notifications](/images/kb/account-settings-release-notes-email.png)

### Subscribe from the Help Center

Anyone can subscribe without signing in, for example a colleague who does not have a Sessionboard login yet. Use the **Get release notes by email** form on the [release notes](/help/release-notes) page or the Help Center home page, or click **Get updates** in the Help Center header, next to **Contact support**.

![The Get updates button in the Help Center header, to the left of Contact support](/images/kb/help-center-get-updates-button.png)

![The Get release notes by email dialog, with a Work email field and a Subscribe button](/images/kb/help-center-get-updates-dialog.png)

Enter a work email and click **Subscribe**. We send one email to confirm it is you; the digest only starts after you click the link in it. If you turned the switch on inside Sessionboard, you are already on the list and do not need to subscribe again here.

<Note>
The confirmation link is valid for a limited time. If it has expired, subscribe again and a new one is sent. To stop the emails, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any digest, or switch **Release notes by email** off in Sessionboard.
</Note>

## Updates on ideas you voted for

When we change the status of an idea you voted for, post an official response on it, or ship it in a product update, we can notify you in Sessionboard and by email. Clicking the notification opens the idea, or What's New for a product update.

To turn these off, open your account settings and find **Community notifications**. Switch off **In-product** or **Email** for **Idea you voted for was updated** or **Product update for an idea you voted for**. The **Manage notification preferences** link at the bottom of every Community email opens the same card. These choices belong to your account, so they apply in every organization you belong to.

![The Community notifications card in Account settings, with an In-product and an Email switch per notification type](/images/kb/account-settings-community-notifications.png)

<Note>
Notifications go to the Sessionboard account that cast the vote. A vote that came over from the old feedback board under an email address that has never signed in to Sessionboard stays counted, but does not receive notifications.
</Note>
