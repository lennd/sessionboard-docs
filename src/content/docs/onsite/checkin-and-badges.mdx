**Onsite → Check-in & badges** is the onsite dashboard: live check-ins and badge prints, the desk and door links your staff open on their phones, and the badge settings for the desk. The Onsite module comes with Registration.

## Why use it

Onsite check-in usually means renting scanners and laptops from a vendor and exporting your attendee list the night before. With desk links, any phone or tablet your staff already carry becomes a check-in desk that prints badges, takes walk-up payments and keeps working when the venue Wi-Fi drops — and every check-in lands back on the person's record as it happens.

## Watch the numbers

![The Onsite stat tiles: Checked in, Badges printed, Walk-ups and Check-in run by](/images/kb/onsite-checkin-stats.png)

- **Checked in** — and the share of everyone expected (confirmed and checked-in registrants). Refreshes every 30 seconds.
- **Badges printed** — desk, app and batch prints.
- **Walk-ups** — people who registered and paid at the desk.
- **Check-in run by** — **Your staff**, or the partner you chose in [Do it yourself or a partner](/onsite/diy-or-partner).

## Create desk and door links

Each link opens the check-in desk in any phone or tablet browser, with nothing to install. The same link opens in the Attend app's staff mode.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a link">
Under **Desk and door links**, choose **At the door**, give the link a label (for example Door A) and click **New link**.
</Step>
<Step title="Limit it to sessions (optional)">
To scan people into a room, choose the sessions the door covers. Leave it as **Whole event** for the event entrance.
</Step>
<Step title="Share the link and PIN">
The link, a QR code for it and its PIN show once, when you create it. Copy them and share the PIN separately from the link.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Desk and door links panel with an At the door link, New PIN and revoke buttons, and the New link controls](/images/kb/onsite-desk-door-links.png)

**Host view** links show who is in the room, live, by organization type, with no check-in buttons — for a host or a sponsor at a dinner.

To lock phones out, revoke the link with its delete button: every phone that used it stops working. **New PIN** revokes a link and makes a new one with a new PIN in one step.

<Warning>
Anyone with a link and its PIN can check people in. Share the PIN only with your staff, and revoke the link after the event.
</Warning>

## At the desk

Desk staff search or scan the QR code or wallet pass, check people in and print their badge. See [Run the web check-in desk](/onsite/web-desk).

- Reprints are logged and marked as reprints.
- Walk-ups register and pay at the desk through your gateway, or as cash, check or comp.
- Check-ins work offline and sync when the signal returns; printing a badge needs a connection.

Turn on **Hold badges for unpaid balances** to keep badges from printing for anyone who still owes money. Sessionboard refuses the print and the reprint everywhere — the web desk, the Attend app, print stations and batch prints — until the balance is paid, so the desk takes payment first.

![The At the desk panel with the Hold badges for unpaid balances switch](/images/kb/onsite-at-the-desk.png)

## Print stations

A print station is a named badge printer at the venue, such as **Hall A desk**. Desk staff check people in on any phone and send the badge to a station; the laptop or tablet beside that printer prints the badges in the order they arrive.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the stations">
Under **Print stations**, click **Add a station** and give it a name. Choose its stock (or **The design's stock**) and a design (or **Each person's type design**). If the printer feeds a little off, nudge everything right or down (negative moves left or up), in inches, up to 1. Click **Save**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open Station mode beside each printer">
On the device next to the printer, open a desk link, unlock it with the PIN, then choose **Station mode** and pick which station it is. It prints each badge sent to it, in order. **Pause** holds it until you click **Start printing**; badges sent meanwhile wait in the queue. **That one failed** marks the last badge failed, even after it printed, so it shows under **Queues**.
</Step>
<Step title="Send badges from the desk">
At the desk, choose the station under **Print to**. In the Attend app, choose it under **Printer**.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Queues** under the stations list badges that are waiting or failed. **Send again** puts a failed badge back in its queue; **Cancel** takes it out. A badge that sits half-printed for 5 minutes goes back in the queue on its own.

## Self check-in kiosks

Tablets running the Attend app in kiosk mode let attendees check themselves in and print their own badge. A kiosk can send its badges to a print station, or print a station's queue itself. See [Set up self check-in kiosks](/onsite/kiosk).

## Upload attendance

To mark people attended from another list — a door system's export, a scanner log or a sign-in sheet — use **Attendance upload**:

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose a CSV">
Each row needs one column that names the person: **email**, **registration id**, **badge** (the printed badge number) or the other system's ID (for example **external id** or **Cvent id**). Add a time column (**checked in at**, **arrival** or **time**) to keep when they arrived; without one, the upload time is used. A time without a time zone is read in the event's time zone; a time that names one is kept as it is.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the file">
**Check the file** changes nothing and tells you how many would be checked in, how many were not found, and how many are canceled, pending or waitlisted. Rows whose arrival time is in the future are counted separately so you can fix them.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply">
**Apply** checks them in. People already checked in keep their first arrival unless you tick **Replace the arrival time of people already checked in**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Uploaded check-ins count in **Checked in**, show on each registration, and reach webhooks and integrations like a check-in at the door.

## Badges printed by other systems

If attendees carry badges printed by another registration system, add its format under **Badge QR formats from other systems** so the desk and session doors read them. For each format, give it a name, a pattern and what its code holds: **Email address**, **Badge number**, **Registration ID from the other system** or **Sessionboard registration ID**. The part of the pattern in brackets is what is looked up — for example `^CVT-(\d{6})$` reads the six digits after `CVT-`. Tick **Any case** to ignore upper and lower case.

Use **Try a code** to scan or paste a sample badge and see whose registration it finds. Sessionboard's own badges and QR codes always scan, and **Try a code** says when a code is one of ours and whose it is; formats are tried after them. The web check-in desk and session door scans apply the same formats. The Attend app reads the same formats offline.

<Note>
Patterns are limited to keep scanning fast: up to 10 formats, under 200 characters each. These aren't supported:

- back-references, like `\1`
- lookaheads and lookbehinds, like `(?=…)`
- repeated groups, like `(a+)+`
- repeated alternatives, like `(a|b)+`; use a character class such as `[ab]+` instead

A format saved before these rules that breaks one shows **Turned off**, and its badges don't scan until you fix the pattern or remove it. Sessionboard never changes a pattern for you.
</Note>
