The web check-in desk is what a desk link opens on a staff phone, tablet or laptop. It needs no app and no account — just the link and its PIN from [Check-in & badges](/onsite/checkin-and-badges).

## Why use it

The desk has to be fast and has to keep working when the venue Wi-Fi does not. Staff find a person by typing three letters, tap once to check them in, and the badge prints on the printer next to them. Without a signal, check-ins are saved on the device and sync on their own.

## Unlock the desk

Open the link and enter the PIN, then **Unlock**. The device stays unlocked until the day is over. Open the link once with a connection; after that it keeps working offline.

If the page says **This link is not active**, the link was revoked or replaced — ask the organizer for a new one.

## Check someone in

<Steps>
<Step title="Find them">
Type three letters of a name or company in **Search by name or company**, or tap the scan button and point the camera at the QR code from their email or wallet pass. Scanning needs a browser that can read codes from the camera.
</Step>
<Step title="Check in">
Tap **Check in**. Tap the undo button on the row to undo a mistaken check-in.
</Step>
<Step title="Print the badge">
With **Print badge on check-in** on, the badge prints as soon as they are checked in. Otherwise, or for a reprint, tap the print button on the row. The row shows **Badge printed** or **Printed ×2**, and reprints are logged.
</Step>
</Steps>

Rows show each person's registrant type and anything the desk needs to know before printing — for example what they still owe.

### People who cannot be checked in yet

A registration waiting for approval shows **Not approved yet**, and a waitlisted one **Waitlisted**. Checking them in is refused with the reason — **Pending approval — not confirmed yet** — and no badge prints. Approve them first from [Approvals](/registration/approvals).

### Balances and held badges

When **Hold badges for unpaid balances** is on, a registrant who still owes money shows **badge held**. Printing their badge says it is held and asks you to take payment first.

## Add a walk-in

Tap **Walk-in** (or **Add a walk-in** when a search finds no one) and enter their **Name**, **Work email** and, optionally, **Company**. The email matches them to the CRM later and keeps them from being added twice.

When the event sells tickets at the desk, a walk-up also picks a **Ticket** and a **Payment**:

- **Card** — through your gateway's secure card window. Needs a signal.
- **Cash**, **Check** or **Comp** — recorded on the order.

Then tap **Add and check in** (or **Record … and check in** for a paid ticket). Selling a ticket needs a signal; a walk-in on a free event does not. On a form that requires approval, a paid walk-up waits for approval.

## Working offline

With no signal, check-ins, undos, scans and walk-ins are saved on the device. A bar shows how many are waiting; they sync when the device reconnects, or tap **Sync now**. Printing a badge and selling a ticket need a connection.

## The host view

A host link opens a read-only view: how many are checked in, not yet arrived and walk-ins, and lists of **Who made it** and who has not. It has no check-in buttons.
