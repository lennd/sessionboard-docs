The web check-in desk is what a desk link opens on a staff phone, tablet or laptop. It needs no app and no account — just the link and its PIN from [Check-in & badges](/onsite/checkin-and-badges).

## Why use it

The desk has to be fast and has to keep working when the venue Wi-Fi does not. Staff find a person by typing three letters, tap once to check them in, and the badge prints on the printer next to them. Without a signal, check-ins are saved on the device and sync on their own.

## Unlock the desk

Open the link and enter the PIN, then **Unlock**. The device stays unlocked until the day is over. Open the link once with a connection; after that it keeps working offline.

![The check-in desk asking for the PIN to unlock the phone](/images/kb/onsite-desk-pin.png)

If the page says **This link is not active**, the link was revoked or replaced — ask the organizer for a new one.

## Check someone in

<Steps>
<Step title="Find them">
Type three letters of a name or company in **Search by name or company**, or tap the scan button and point the camera at the QR code from their email or wallet pass. A USB or Bluetooth scanner can scan straight into the search box: the emailed QR code, a printed badge and, on a Registration event, badges from other systems that match the event's [Badge QR formats](/onsite/checkin-and-badges#badges-printed-by-other-systems) list the person, ready to check in. On a Registration event the scanner also reads printed badges and badges from other systems that match the event's [Badge QR formats](/onsite/checkin-and-badges#badges-printed-by-other-systems); a code that matches no one says **That code is not for this event**. Scanning needs a browser that can read codes from the camera.
</Step>
<Step title="Check in">
Tap **Check in**. Tap the undo button on the row to undo a mistaken check-in.
</Step>
<Step title="Print the badge">
With **Print badge on check-in** on, the badge prints as soon as they are checked in. Otherwise, or for a reprint, tap the print button on the row. The row shows **Badge printed** or **Printed ×2**, and reprints are logged.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The check-in desk on a phone with search results, balances owed, pending approvals, a Check in button and a printed badge](/images/kb/onsite-desk-search.png)

Rows show each person's registrant type and anything the desk needs to know before printing — for example what they still owe.

### People who cannot be checked in yet

A registration waiting for approval shows **Not approved yet**, and a waitlisted one **Waitlisted**. Checking them in is refused with the reason — **Pending approval — not confirmed yet** — and no badge prints. Approve them first from [Approvals](/registration/approvals).

### Balances and held badges

When **Hold badges for unpaid balances** is on, a registrant who still owes money shows **badge held**. Their badge does not print or reprint — not here, not at a print station and not in the Attend app — until the balance is paid. The row shows **Badge held until paid** in place of **Print badge**. Take payment first.

### Print to a station

When the event has [print stations](/onsite/checkin-and-badges#print-stations), choose one under **Print to** above the search. Badges then go to that station's queue instead of this device's printer, and the desk says **…'s badge is in the Hall A queue**. Choose **This device's printer** to print here again. The choice is remembered on this device.

### Station mode

On the laptop or tablet beside a station's printer, open the desk link, unlock it, and choose **Station mode**. Pick which station this is under **This is**. The device takes the oldest badge in the queue, opens it for printing, and moves on to the next. **Pause** stops it until you click **Start printing**, even if the page reloads; a badge sent meanwhile waits in the queue. **That one failed** marks the last badge failed, also after it printed (for up to 30 minutes), so it shows in Onsite › Queues with **Send again**.

## Add a walk-in

Tap **Walk-in** (or **Add a walk-in** when a search finds no one) and enter their **Name**, **Work email** and, optionally, **Company**. The email matches them to the CRM later and keeps them from being added twice.

When the event sells tickets at the desk, a walk-up also picks a **Ticket** and a **Payment**. The tickets are the ones ticked on the registration form; a form with none ticked offers every active ticket.

- **Card** — through your gateway's secure card window. Needs a signal. The amount is priced for the walk-up's form, registrant type and membership, exactly as it will be charged; if the price changes before the card is taken, nothing is charged and you start again.
- **Cash**, **Check** or **Comp** — recorded on the order.

![The Walk-in sheet with name, work email, company, a ticket and the Card, Cash, Check and Comp payment choices](/images/kb/onsite-desk-walk-in.png)

Then tap **Add and check in** (or **Record … and check in** for a paid ticket). Selling a ticket needs a signal; a walk-in on a free event does not. On a form that requires approval, a paid walk-up waits for approval.

## Working offline

With no signal, check-ins, undos, scans and walk-ins are saved on the device. A bar shows how many are waiting; they sync when the device reconnects, or tap **Sync now**. Printing a badge and selling a ticket need a connection.

## The host view

A host link opens a read-only view: how many are checked in, not yet arrived and walk-ins, and lists of **Who made it** and who has not. It has no check-in buttons.
