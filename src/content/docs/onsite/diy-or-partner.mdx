**Onsite → Partners** (the page is titled **Do it yourself or a partner**) lets you decide, job by job, who runs each part of onsite: your own staff with Sessionboard's tools, or a partner you connect in **Apps**.

## Why use it

Every event's onsite setup is different. You might run check-in yourselves on staff phones but keep the badge-printing company you have used for years, or hand lead retrieval to the exhibit-hall partner. Choosing per job means you never pay for a tool you do not need — and whichever you choose, check-ins, scans, leads and walk-up payments come back to Sessionboard.

## Choose per job

For each job, pick **Do it ourselves** or **Use a partner**.

| Job | Doing it yourselves |
| --- | --- |
| **Check-in desk** | Scan QR codes and wallet passes in the Attend app or any phone browser; look up by name or email, mark paid, register and take payment from walk-ups; works offline. |
| **Badge printing** | Print at the desk to your own thermal or laser printers, or batch-print PDFs ahead. Every print and reprint is logged. |
| **Session scanning & attendance** | Door scanning in the Attend app or mobile web; scans post attendance and credit to the person's record. |
| **Exhibitor lead retrieval** | Booth staff scan badges in the Attend app or mobile web — no devices to rent — with qualifying questions and notes. |
| **Housing** | Link registrants to your hotel block from the confirmation page and attendee portal. |
| **General service contractor** | Collect service orders, deadlines and documents in the exhibitor portal shop and manual. |

**Set it up** under a job opens the page where you configure your own tools.

## Use a partner

Choose **Use a partner**, then pick a connected partner from the list. Partners are the apps in the **Onsite & Expo** category of **Apps**; **Browse Onsite & Expo apps** opens it. A partner that is not connected yet is marked, with a link to connect it.

The partner works from your registrants, registrant types and badge fields, and check-ins, scans, leads and walk-up payments come back to Sessionboard.

<Note>
Partners are listed in Apps, not recommended. Any agency you work with can also get an Event Team role scoped to their job.
</Note>
