The Attend app has a staff mode for the registration desk and session doors. It runs the same desk and door links as the [web check-in desk](/onsite/web-desk), with the phone's camera for scanning and AirPrint for badges. **Onsite → Staff tools** has the app store links and the roles staff can have.

## Why use it

A native app scans faster in a busy lobby, prints straight to the printer at the desk, and holds up on a show floor with no signal. Staff mode needs no separate login: the check-in link and PIN you already hand out are the credential.

## Open staff mode

<Steps>
<Step title="Install the app">
Staff install the event's Attend app from the App Store or Google Play — the links are on **Onsite → Staff tools**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the check-in link">
Open the desk or door link on the phone (tap it, or scan its QR code) so it opens in the app, or paste the link into the app.
</Step>
<Step title="Unlock">
Enter the PIN. The link decides the role: a desk link opens the registration desk, a door link limited to sessions opens door scanning.
</Step>
</Steps>

## At the desk

- **Search** by name, or **scan** a QR code or wallet pass with the camera. You can also type a code.
- **Check in**, and undo a mistaken check-in.
- **Walk-in** — add someone who is not on the list.
- **Print badge** — prints the badge to an AirPrint or network badge printer. Every print is logged; cancelling the print dialog logs nothing.
- A registration waiting for approval shows **Pending approval** and cannot be checked in.

## At the door

A door link limited to sessions scans people into those sessions for attendance and credit. Scanning someone twice says they are already scanned, and a registration waiting for approval is refused with the reason. A session scan also checks the person in to the event.

## Offline

Searches use the list already on the phone, and check-ins, scans and walk-ins made without a signal are queued and sent when the phone reconnects.

## Roles

![The Who can use it panel with Check-in staff, Door scanners, Booth staff and Agency staff](/images/kb/onsite-staff-tools-roles.png)

**Onsite → Staff tools** describes who can use what:

- **Check-in staff** — search, scan, check in, print and reprint badges, register and take payment from walk-ups. No refunds.
- **Door scanners** — scan into sessions and workshops for attendance and credit.
- **Booth staff** — lead scanning for their own company. See [Lead retrieval](/attend/leads).
- **Agency staff** — the same roles for any partner you invite, scoped to their job.

<Tip>
Any iPhone, Android phone or tablet works. Badge printing pairs with common thermal and laser printers over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth — or skip printing and hand out pre-printed PDF badges.
</Tip>
