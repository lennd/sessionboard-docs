A self check-in kiosk is an iPad or Android tablet running the Attend app in kiosk mode, locked to one event. Attendees check themselves in and print their own badge, so the staff desk is free for walk-ups and questions. Set kiosks up under **Onsite → Check-in & badges → Self check-in kiosks**.

## Why use it

Most attendees arrive with their ticket ready. A row of kiosks checks them in and prints their badge, and the people who need help go to the staff desk.

## What attendees do

<Steps>
<Step title="Find their registration">
The kiosk offers the ways you allow. Attendees can scan the badge on their Apple or Google Wallet pass, or under **My badge** in the event app. They can type their email and then the 6-digit code the kiosk emails them. Or they can type their last name and their confirmation number. The last name matches without accents or extra spaces, so *Muller* finds *Müller*; hyphens and apostrophes still count. A kiosk never searches by name and never shows a list of who is registered.

The QR code in the confirmation email works at the staff desk, but not at a kiosk. That code carries the registration's ID, which other attendees can see, so anyone could make a copy. The wallet pass and app badge carry a private code that only that person has.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm it's them">
The kiosk shows their first name and last initial, and their company.
</Step>
<Step title="Check in and print">
One tap checks them in and prints their badge, or sends it to a print station.
</Step>
</Steps>

The kiosk sends people to the staff desk when it can't help them: someone who isn't registered, a registration waiting for approval, and a registration with a balance due while **Hold badges for unpaid balances** is on. **Kiosks take no payments.** After a minute with nobody using it, the kiosk goes back to its welcome screen.

## Add a kiosk

You need the Registration permission **Manage badges and onsite setup**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add it">
Under **Self check-in kiosks**, click **Add a kiosk** and name it, for example **Lobby kiosk 1**. Add one kiosk for each tablet.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose where its badges print">
Choose **Its own printer**, **Send badges to a print station**, or **Check in only (no badge)**.
- For its own printer, pick the model and the badge stock. For a Zebra printer, or a Brother TD-4 set to ZPL2 emulation, also enter the printer's IP address. The port is usually 9100. Pick the printer's resolution too.
- A kiosk with its own printer can also print a [print station](/onsite/checkin-and-badges#print-stations)'s queue: choose the station under **Also print a station's queue**.
</Step>
<Step title="Activate the tablet">
After you create the kiosk, its code (like `ABCD-EFGH`), a QR code and a link appear. They are shown once. On the tablet, open the Attend app, go to **Staff tools → Kiosk mode**, and type the code or scan the QR. On an iPad that prints through AirPrint, pick the printer when the app asks. The tablet then stays in kiosk mode.
</Step>
</Steps>

**New link and code** replaces a lost code. Tablets that are already running keep working. **Revoke** stops the kiosk after you confirm in a dialog, because the link and code stop working for good: the tablet leaves kiosk mode within a minute. Each kiosk shows when it was last seen.

## Kiosk settings

These settings apply to every kiosk at the event. Running kiosks pick up a change within a minute, without being activated again.

- **Reprints at a kiosk** — how many badges the kiosk prints for one person. The first badge always prints. Badges printed at the desk count too.
  - **No reprints**: only the first badge.
  - **One reprint**: the first badge and one more.
  - **Unlimited reprints**.
  - **Reprints need the staff PIN**: a staff member types the PIN at the kiosk to allow each reprint.
  Sessionboard enforces this when it prints, not the tablet. If a print fails, **Try printing again** prints the same badge again, and it doesn't count as a reprint.
- **How attendees find their registration** — scanning their wallet pass or event-app badge, an emailed code, or last name plus confirmation number. Keep at least one on. The email code works only for a registered address and expires after 10 minutes. The kiosk shows the same "check your email" screen for any address, so nobody can use it to find out who is registered.
- **Staff PIN** — 4 to 6 digits. Staff press and hold the event name on the kiosk, then type the PIN to leave kiosk mode. The same PIN allows a reprint when reprints need it.
- **Print the badge at check-in** — turn this off to have kiosks only check people in.
- **Welcome line** — one optional line under the welcome title.

## Supported printers

Kiosks print in two ways:

| How | Printers |
|---|---|
| ZPL over the network (TCP port 9100) — the tablet sends the badge straight to the printer | Zebra ZD421, ZD621, ZD411, ZT231 and ZT411; other ZPL II printers; Brother TD-4420DN, TD-4520DN and TD-4550DNWB set to ZPL2 emulation |
| The tablet's own print system (AirPrint on iPad, the Android print service with the maker's plug-in) | Brother QL-820NWB and QL-1110NWB; any AirPrint or Android print-service printer |

<Note>
None of these models has been tested with Sessionboard kiosks on real hardware yet. They are supported because they accept these print formats. Print a test badge on your printer and stock before doors open.
</Note>

On thermal ZPL printers, badges print in black only. Your design's text, QR code and ribbons print; images and colours don't. On Android, printing through the print service shows the system print screen each time, so a ZPL printer works better on an Android kiosk.

<Tip>
Put the kiosk tablet and its printer on the same Wi-Fi network, and give the printer a fixed IP address so the kiosk always finds it.
</Tip>

<Note>
If **Staff tools** in the Attend app has no **Kiosk mode**, update the app from the App Store or Google Play.
</Note>
