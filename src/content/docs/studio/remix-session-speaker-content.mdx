Remix lets you select any group of sessions or speakers, set a tone and any custom guidance, and have AI rewrite their content. Nothing changes until you review each suggestion and save it, so you can run Remix across a whole track without risking your published copy.

<Note>
Prefer to watch? See [Remix content (video)](/videos/video-ai-content-remix).
</Note>

## Before you start

The more Remix knows about your event, the better its suggestions. Fill in these fields under **Settings → Event Details**:

- **Event Type**
- **Event Website URL**
- **Event Location**
- **Theme**

These give the AI the audience and context it needs, so titles and descriptions come back sounding like your event rather than generic conference copy.

![Event Details fields that give Remix context about your event](/images/kb/f014c09b-image-png-May-25-2026-07-10-15-8355-PM.png)

## Open remix
Go to **Studio** in the left sidebar and click **Remix Content**.

![Studio with the Remix Content tool](/images/kb/0f72ea30-image-png-Feb-17-2026-08-39-16-8963-PM.png)

<Note>
If your event has AI agents enabled, the sidebar shows **Agents** instead of **Studio**, and the Editor agent handles this work in place of Remix.
</Note>

## Remix your content

The steps below use sessions as the example. Remixing speaker content follows the same flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Choose what to optimize:
    - **Session Titles** — clarity and engagement
    - **Session Descriptions** — clarity and audience appeal
    - **Tags** — suggested from session content
    - **Tracks** — suggested categorization

    Then set your parameters:
    - **Tone** — pick from the dropdown (Professional, Friendly, Academic, and others)
    - **Additional Guidance** — optional free-text instructions, for example "Make titles more engaging for a marketing audience"

    ![Configure step with content types, tone, and additional guidance](/images/kb/c5da06f4-image-png-Feb-25-2026-01-38-04-2935-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select content">
    Filter by session attributes, or browse and pick specific sessions to target.

    ![Selecting which sessions to remix](/images/kb/47766944-image-png-May-25-2026-07-03-59-6375-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    The left panel lists everything you selected. Open each one to compare:
    - the original title and description
    - the AI-optimized version
    - a summary of what changed, such as "Shortened for clarity"

    Click **Regenerate** for a different version, or **Save** to keep it. When you have worked through the list, click **Finish Review**.

    ![Reviewing original and optimized content side by side](/images/kb/36bb9767-image-png-May-25-2026-07-05-20-9999-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Summary">
    The summary lists everything you reviewed and saved. Your remixed content is live and ready for your portal, agenda, and event materials.

    ![Summary of remixed sessions](/images/kb/96aeb727-image-png-May-25-2026-07-06-08-6866-PM.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Remix only writes what you save. If you close the review before saving, nothing changes.
</Tip>
