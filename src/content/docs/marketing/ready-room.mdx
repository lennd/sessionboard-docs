Ready Room is the AV crew's view of your event. It shows the schedule by room, the speaker files for each session, and the transcription controls — all through a magic link, so nobody needs a Sessionboard account or onboarding on show day.

<Note>
Ready Room is part of the Marketing module, which is currently Early Access.
</Note>

## Give your crew access

There are two kinds of link, and the difference matters.

| | Named invite | Direct magic link |
|---|---|---|
| Lasts | 30 days | 30 minutes |
| Created from | **Transcribe → Invites** | Emailed on request |
| Best for | Crew you know in advance | Someone who needs in right now |

Both links are reusable until they expire, so a crew member can close the tab and come back.

<Warning>
A direct magic link expires in **30 minutes**. Don't email them the week before — the link will be dead by show day. Use named invites for anything planned in advance.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create named invites">
    Go to **Marketing → Transcribe → Invites** and create one per crew member or per room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    Share it however you already communicate with the crew. On first open they enter their email, which is what attributes activity to a person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They open Ready Room">
    The link goes straight to the event's Ready Room. No password, no account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Crew can also request a link themselves, but only if their email is already known to the event — from a previous link, an existing Ready Room user record, or a role with Ready Room permission. This stops the request form becoming a way in for anyone.

## What the crew sees

- **Schedule** — sessions by room and day. Open one for its detail view.
- **Files** — the speaker materials attached to each session, downloadable directly.
- **Transcriptions** — the transcript for a session, live or completed.
- **Mission control** — for operators with an admin login, when enabled.

A session's detail view puts the AI summary, participants, and presentation files on the left, with the transcription controls and live transcript on the right.

![A session detail view in Ready Room, with transcription controls and the live transcript on the right](/images/kb/d2fd9490-image-png-Jun-16-2026-04-11-38-7380-PM.png)

From here they start and stop transcription with the same controls described in [Live Transcribe](/marketing/live-transcribe). The panel tells the crew when transcription has not been switched on for the event, and which Event Settings page turns it on.

The **Transcriptions** tab collects every captured session for the event in one list.

## Multi-room events

Two optional settings under **Transcribe → Settings** change how larger events run.

**Room Console** gives each room its own page, meant to sit open on the room's computer. It listens for remote start and stop commands.

**Mission Control** gives one operator a view of every room at once, with remote start and stop plus an operator chat. It needs an admin login rather than a magic link, and it needs Room Console enabled to be able to control rooms — turning it on by itself will prompt you about that.

<Tip>
For a single-track event, skip both. They're worth the setup at three or more concurrent rooms, where walking between rooms to press start stops being practical.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the AV team need Sessionboard accounts?">
    No. Magic-link access is the whole point — no accounts, no onboarding, no licences.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I revoke access?">
    Named invites expire after 30 days. For immediate removal, remove the invite so the link stops resolving.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can admins use Ready Room?">
    Yes, your normal admin login works, which is useful for previewing what the crew will see.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The crew says their link doesn't work.">
    Check which kind it was. Direct magic links last 30 minutes; named invites last 30 days. Issue a named invite if they need standing access.

    Links can be opened more than once until they expire, so "I already clicked it" is not the cause — and sending someone a fresh link no longer invalidates the ones in their earlier emails.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
