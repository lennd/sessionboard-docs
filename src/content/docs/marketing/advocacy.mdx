## Why use it

Your participants, sponsors and exhibitors reach audiences your own channels can't, and so does your own team. Advocacy gives each of them a ready-made card and post on the networks they actually use, including LINE, Instagram and WeChat for audiences in Asia and on mobile. Every click is traced back to the person who shared and the channel they shared on, so you can see which advocates fill the room and reward the ones who do.

## Before you start

- A **register link** — the URL your shares point at. Sessionboard prefills your event website if it's set under **Settings → Event Details**.
- Participants, sponsors, exhibitors or attendees on the event, with email addresses if you plan to notify them. With **I'll share a link or embed** you don't need anyone on the campaign at all.
- Your event branding, so the generated share cards carry your logo and colors.

## Who can share

You pick who shares when you create the campaign. You can't change it afterwards.

| Who | Who it is | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Participants** | Everyone with a participant role on the event's sessions: speakers, chairs, moderators and any custom role you created, such as Panelists. The Recipients step lists the roles your event uses. | Nothing extra |
| **Groups** | Sponsor and exhibitor companies, sharing that they're exhibiting or sponsoring | Nothing extra to email links, export them or use the open link — each company's contacts on the event get the company's card (see [Advocacy for exhibitors and sponsors](#advocacy-for-exhibitors-and-sponsors)). Exhibitor Pro or Sponsor Pro only to target **By portal** or to show the campaign inside an exhibitor or sponsor portal. |
| **Attendees** | People registered for the event, sharing that they're going | Attendees from a registration platform connected in **Apps** with attendee sync on, or a list you [import](#bring-in-your-attendees) — or none at all with **I'll share a link or embed**. Only confirmed or checked-in attendees who have not opted out of marketing are included. |
| **Your team** | Your own staff, posting to their own LinkedIn from posts you schedule | Dispatch on your organization. See [Dispatch: team campaigns](/marketing/dispatch). |

Participants, sponsors, exhibitors and attendees are your **event community**. Your own staff are **your team**.

## The Campaigns list

Go to **Marketing → Advocacy**. The **Campaigns** tab lists every campaign on the event, event community and team campaigns together: **Name**, **Shared by**, **Status**, **Sharers**, **Shares**, **Clicks** and **Updated**. Click a name to open the campaign.

![The Campaigns list with the All, Your team and Event community filter, the search box and status filter, and two event community and two team campaigns with their Shared by chips](/images/kb/advocacy-campaigns-list.png)

- **All**, **Your team** and **Event community** narrow the list to one kind of campaign. Each shows how many campaigns it holds.
- **Search campaigns…** and the status filter (**Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Ended or completed**) work across both kinds.
- The **⋯** menu on a row has **Edit**, **Duplicate** (team campaigns), **Pause sharing** or **Resume sharing**, and **Delete…**.
- Tick campaigns to act on several at once: **Export assets** (event community campaigns) or **Delete**.

Team campaigns appear when your organization has Dispatch. At the organization level, **Marketing → Advocacy** has a **Your team** section with every team campaign in the organization and **New team campaign**.

### Start a campaign

Click **New campaign**. **Who's sharing it?** asks one question:

- **Your event community** (the default) opens Quick Launch, described below.
- **Your team** opens the team campaign builder. See [Dispatch: team campaigns](/marketing/dispatch).

![The New campaign dialog — Your event community, selected by default, and Your team, each with who shares and what comes next](/images/kb/advocacy-new-campaign-dialog.png)

## Launch a campaign
Choose **Your event community** and click **Continue**. This opens Quick Launch — five steps, with a live preview on the right that shows exactly what one of your advocates will see. Use **Previewing as** at the top to switch which person the preview follows. The longer campaign editor is for changing a campaign that already exists.

## Share a link or embed instead of inviting people

Choose **I'll share a link or embed** on the Recipients step when you'd rather hand out one link yourself — in a newsletter, a participant packet, a Slack message — or put the card on your website. Nobody is invited and no email goes out. Anyone who opens the link or the embed gets their own card and their own tracked link.

![I'll share a link or embed on an Attendees campaign — the campaign link with Copy link and QR code, the QR code with Download QR code, and the note that people who haven't registered can make a card](/images/kb/advocacy-attendee-link-or-embed-qr.png)

What each audience gets:

- **Attendees** — the campaign link, with **Copy link** and a **QR code** you can download. Anyone with the link can make a card, including people who haven't registered yet; they're added to the event's attendees when they make it.
- **Groups** — the campaign link and QR code. Every exhibitor and sponsor on the event can sign up through it, not only companies you picked. Underneath, **Let companies that aren't on the event yet sign up** decides what happens when someone types a company you don't have; see [Let new companies sign up](#let-new-companies-sign-up).
- **Participants** — participants share through the embed on your page. After you launch, get the embed code from the campaign's **Share** tab. Every participant on the event can use it.

![I'll share a link or embed on a Participants campaign — One open link you send yourself, and the note that participants share through the embed on the Share tab](/images/kb/advocacy-quick-recipients-link-or-embed.png)

The Share console still simulates the advocate's page with a sample person or company, and the campaign launches with nobody assigned.

![The Share console note for a campaign with nobody assigned — the preview uses a sample participant from this event](/images/kb/advocacy-share-console-nobody-assigned.png)

## Bring in your attendees

<Tip>
Running an Attendees campaign? [Advocacy for attendees](/marketing/advocacy-attendees) walks through every way to reach attendees — open link, registration emails, confirmation-page embeds, email from Sessionboard and CSV export — and which one fits your event.
</Tip>

An Attendees campaign reads the event's attendee list. Fill it any of these ways:

- **Sync from your registration platform.** Connect Cvent, Swoogo, Swapcard, Visit, LiveBuzz, Bizzabo or Expo Platform in **Apps** and turn on its attendee sync.
- **Import a list.** On the event's **Attendees** page, click **Import attendees** (also offered on the campaign's audience step when nobody is eligible yet). It is the same importer as [Importing data](/settings/importing-data#attendees): **Email** is required, and **Registrant ID**, **Registration Status**, **Marketing Opt-in** and **Headshot** are optional columns. Tick **These attendees agreed to receive marketing from us** for rows whose opt-in cell is blank.
- **Let attendees sign themselves up** with the campaign's [open link](#open-link).

Each attendee's card shows their first and last name, company and job title, and the headshot they add on their share page — or, until they add one, the **Headshot** from your import.

## Reach your attendees

Open an Attendees campaign and select the **Share** tab. Everything you hand to attendees is there, ready to copy.

### Open link

Turn on **Open link — anyone with the link can make their own card** to get one link for the whole campaign. Anyone who opens it enters their name, email, company and job title, ticks the consent box and gets their own card straight away — no attendee list needed. Put it in a newsletter, on the event website or on your registration confirmation page.

![The Open link card on a campaign's Share tab — the switch turned on and the open link URL with a Copy button](/images/kb/advocacy-attendee-share-open-link.png)

![The open link's signup page — event name and dates, first name, last name, email, company and job title fields, the consent checkbox and Make my card](/images/kb/advocacy-open-link-join-page.png)

Someone whose email is already on your attendee list is never shown a card on the page: they see **Check your inbox**, and if they can share, Sessionboard emails them their own personal link. An attendee on your list who is cancelled, waitlisted or opted out of marketing gets no card and no email. The signup page opens in the event's language, else the visitor's browser language (English, French, German, Spanish or Arabic).

### Personal links in your registration emails

Put each attendee's personal link in your registration platform's confirmation email (or on its confirmation page). The link opens that attendee's share page with their card ready to share — no login. On the **Share** tab, pick your registration platform to get the link with your merge fields marked.

![Personal links in your registration emails — the Registration platform picker set to Cvent and the personal link template with placeholders for the registrant id and email](/images/kb/advocacy-attendee-share-personal-links.png)

The link has this shape:

```
https://<share link host>/r/a/<campaign id>/<platform>/<registrant id>?e=<registrant email>
```

- **Share link host** — copy it from any share link the campaign shows, so the link points at your region.
- **Campaign id** — the part of the campaign page's address after `/campaigns/`.
- **Platform** — `attend`, `cvent`, `swoogo`, `swapcard`, `visit`, `livebuzz`, `bizzabo`, `expo_platform` or `csv`: the platform the attendee registered on. Use `attend` for registrations taken in Sessionboard Attend, and `csv` for an imported list — the registrant id is then the row's **Registrant ID**, or its email if the row had none.
- **Registrant id** and **registrant email** — your platform's merge fields for the registrant's id and email address.

Add `?e=` with the registrant's email (URL-encoded) for every platform except Attend (an Attend registration id is a random uuid, so it is accepted on its own). For Cvent, always send it as well: a Cvent id is only accepted on its own when it is a random (version 4) uuid. If the id, email or campaign don't match an eligible attendee on an active Attendees campaign, the link shows a **This link isn't available** page. A malformed link (a mistyped id, for example) shows the same page.

What each platform needs:

| Registration platform | `<platform>` | Registrant id | `?e=` email | Registered after the last sync |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sessionboard registration | `attend` | Registration id | Not needed | Always on the event |
| Cvent | `cvent` | Cvent attendee id | Send it | Looked up in Cvent on click |
| Swoogo | `swoogo` | Registrant id | Required | Looked up in Swoogo on click |
| Visit | `visit` | Visitor id | Required | Looked up in Visit on click |
| LiveBuzz | `livebuzz` | Customer id | Required | Looked up in LiveBuzz on click |
| Swapcard | `swapcard` | Person id | Required | Sync first |
| Bizzabo | `bizzabo` | Registrant id | Required | Sync first |
| Expo Platform | `expo_platform` | Registrant id | Required | Sync first |
| Uploaded list | `csv` | Registrant ID column, or email | Required | Import first |

### Open link

Turn on **Open link — anyone at a company on this campaign can get its card** for one link for the whole campaign. Put it in your exhibitor newsletter, the exhibitor manual or on the exhibitor services page. Anyone who opens it enters their name, email and company and gets the company's card straight away — useful for the booth staff and marketing contacts the organizer never added to Sessionboard.

![The Open link card on a Groups campaign's Share tab — the switch turned on and the open link URL ending in /r/g/…/join with a Copy button](/images/kb/advocacy-group-share-open-link.png)

![The exhibitor signup page — "Exhibiting or sponsoring at 2025 Texas Covered Conference + Expo?", first name, last name, email, a required Company field, the consent checkbox and the Make my card button](/images/kb/advocacy-group-join-page.png)

The rules that keep it safe:

- **Which companies can sign up.** The page suggests the companies on the campaign — on an I'll share a link or embed campaign, every exhibitor and sponsor on the event. A company that isn't there is refused, unless you let new companies sign up (below).
- **A first-time email becomes a contact of that company on the event**, so they appear under the company's record from then on, and they get the card at once.
- **An email already in your CRM is never shown a card on the page.** The visitor sees **Check your inbox**, so nobody can open a colleague's share page by typing their address. If that person is already a contact of the company, they're emailed their own personal link. If they aren't yet, they're emailed a link to confirm it was them first. They're added to the company only when they open it, and then their card opens. Until then, nothing changes, so typing someone's address can't put them on a company's contact list. The confirmation link works for 3 days; after that, the page asks them to enter their details again for a new one.

### Let new companies sign up

**Let companies that aren't on the event yet sign up** is on the Recipients step (with I'll share a link or embed) and under the open link switch on the **Share** tab. It is on for new campaigns and off for campaigns created before it existed.

![Let companies that aren't on the event yet sign up, switched on, with Add new companies as set to Let them choose (Exhibitor or Sponsor)](/images/kb/advocacy-group-link-or-embed-new-companies.png)

A company with the same name already in your organization is reused rather than duplicated. **Add new companies as** decides the type: **Let them choose (Exhibitor or Sponsor)**, **Exhibitor** or **Sponsor**. The number of companies that can be added this way in an hour is limited, to stop misuse.

## Emails your advocates get

Every email Advocacy sends — Notify, reminders, team campaign posts and nudges, reward unlocks, Email a colleague, and the links people ask for — is built the same way, so it reads as coming from someone they know:

- **From a person.** The sender name is the person behind it followed by "via Sessionboard": you for Notify, the campaign owner for reminders and rewards, the teammate who set up a team campaign, the advocate for Email a colleague. **Reply-To** is that person's address, so a reply reaches a colleague, not a no-reply mailbox. Links people ask for themselves come from "*your organization* via Sessionboard" and reply to the campaign owner. The address itself stays on Sessionboard's sending domain, or your organization's verified sender.
- **A calm, specific subject.** For example "Your share card for *your event* is ready", "Josh asked you to share a post about *your event*" or "Reminder from Josh: share a post about *your event*". A subject you typed yourself is kept.
- **Your branding.** The logo and color from your brand kit, or the event or organization logo, with a neutral header when there is none.
- **One button**, such as **Open your share card**, **Open your share kit** or **See your results**, and under it "If the button doesn't work, use this link:" with a plain link that names the host.
- **A footer that explains itself**: why the person got the email (for example, that you added them to the campaign), "Questions? Reply to this email to reach *name*.", "Sent by Sessionboard on behalf of *your organization*.", and **Why am I getting this?** and **Unsubscribe** links. Every email also has a plain-text version.
- **The event's language**, including Arabic, right to left.

The Notify dialog's preview shows the same button, link and footer note.

### Unsubscribe

**Unsubscribe** opens a page that explains who sent the email and why, and changes nothing until the person chooses **Stop emails about this campaign** or **Stop all Advocacy emails from *your organization***. Mail apps that offer one-click unsubscribe do the same for the campaign. Sessionboard keeps only a fingerprint of the address, not the address itself.

After that, Notify, reminders, team campaign posts, nudges and reward emails skip the person; Notify lists them as skipped, and a team campaign shows them as skipped for each post. Rewards they earn stay earned. Emails a person asks for themselves — their own share link or a confirmation — and setup instructions a colleague sends have no unsubscribe link; they say "If you weren't expecting this, you can ignore it." instead.

## Change who is on a live campaign

Open the campaign, click **Edit campaign** and go to **Recipients**. In **Select** mode, set the filters (for example, session status **Accepted**), then choose **When applying**:

- **Add to existing** (default) — adds the people who match. Nobody is removed.
- **Replace not yet engaged** — also removes people who don't match and haven't shared, downloaded their card or opened their share page yet. People who have engaged stay.
- **Replace all** — also removes everyone who doesn't match, even if they have engaged.

![The When applying choice under a campaign's recipient filter — Add to existing, Replace not yet engaged (selected) and Replace all — with the Apply filter button](/images/kb/advocacy-recipients-when-applying.png)

People who already match are never touched. Before a **Replace** is applied, a confirmation shows exactly how many people will be removed; **Cancel** changes nothing. Removed people are not notified. Their share page link stops working and the campaign leaves their portal, but their past shares and clicks stay in your reports. Assigning them again later restores their access.

Moving a campaign from I'll share a link or embed to **Select** limits its open link to the people and companies you pick.

## How the links work

Every recipient gets their own short link: a Sessionboard address ending in `/r/` and a short code. The host depends on the region your organization's data is hosted in, so links from an EU-hosted organization start differently from US ones. Copy a link from the campaign rather than typing it. When someone clicks it, Sessionboard records the click, applies your UTM parameters and promo code, and forwards them to your destination — optionally through your branded landing page first. A link opened inside the WeChat, LINE, Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn app reaches your destination with that app as its `utm_medium` when your campaign's medium is a channel name such as `linkedin`, so your site's analytics credit the right network; a medium you typed yourself, such as `social`, is always kept.

Each channel on the share page hands out its own copy of the advocate's link, marked with the channel (`?ch=linkedin`, `?ch=qr` and so on), so a click is credited to the channel it came from as well as to the advocate. The forward to your destination adds a click id (`sb_click`) that the Sessionboard website tag uses for [conversion tracking](/marketing/advocacy-conversion-tracking).

Because each link is per person, shares and clicks attribute back to the individual advocate rather than to the campaign as a whole.

## How each channel shares

Channels are chosen per campaign under **Share channels**, in Quick Launch or **Edit campaign**. A new campaign starts with LinkedIn only. Campaigns created before LINE, Instagram and WeChat were added keep the channels they had, so those channels are off until you turn them on. WeChat is never on by default: turn it on for each campaign that should offer it. Advocates only see buttons for the channels you turn on.

Every channel shares the advocate's tracked link, and each share shows up under its own name in **Channel mix**, **Results by channel** and the **Activity** feed.

| Channel | What the advocate's button does |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Asks how to post: **Post with your photo (recommended)** posts the card as an image with the message, after the advocate connects LinkedIn once and reviews the post; **Just share the link** opens LinkedIn's share box. See [Share your event card](/participants/share-your-event-card#post-to-linkedin). |
| **LINE** | **Share on LINE** copies the full post, then opens LINE's share page in a new tab: the LINE app on a phone, LINE's web picker on a computer. LINE only takes the link, so the advocate pastes the copied message into the chat if they want it. The chat preview shows the campaign's share card. A post can be up to 5,000 characters. |
| **Instagram** | Instagram has no web share link, so **Share to Instagram** hands over the card itself. It always copies the caption with the tracked link first. On a phone or tablet it opens the device's share sheet with the card, and the advocate picks Instagram (Feed, Story or a message). On a computer, or if the phone's browser refuses the share sheet, the card downloads and a message offers **Open Instagram**. A caption can be up to 2,200 characters. |
| **WeChat** | WeChat has no web share link either, so **Share to WeChat** opens a dialog instead of posting. See [Share to WeChat](#share-to-wechat). |
| **Email** | With Email on, advocates also get **Email a colleague**: up to five addresses and a note. Sessionboard emails each colleague the advocate's card and link. Addresses aren't kept or added to any list, and the same address is invited at most once a week per campaign. |

On a phone that has a share menu, advocates also get **Share…**, which opens the phone's own share menu with the card attached where the phone allows it. It is reported as **Phone share menu**.

A few details for Instagram:

- Links in Instagram captions aren't clickable. The download message reminds advocates to add a link sticker to a Story or put the link in their bio.
- On an animated campaign, the animation renders first. On a phone, a message with a **Share now** button opens the share sheet when it's ready. On a computer, it downloads as soon as it's ready.
- If an advocate closes the share sheet without posting, no share is recorded.

### Share to WeChat

WeChat offers no way for a website to post on someone's behalf, so advocates share to WeChat from WeChat itself. **Share to WeChat** opens a dialog with everything they need, on a computer or a phone:

- **A QR code of their tracked link.** On a computer, they scan it with WeChat on their phone, which opens the link inside WeChat. From there they tap **···** and choose **Send to Chat** or **Share to Moments**.
- **Download card** — the share card as an image, at most 1080 px wide, to post in a chat or on Moments. Moments crops the feed preview to a square, so keep the important part of the card near the center.
- **Caption** — the post with the tracked link, shown in full. **Copy caption** copies it to paste beside the card, and **Copy link** copies just the link.

Already on a phone? The dialog suggests copying the link and sending it to yourself in WeChat's **File Transfer** chat, then sharing it from there.

The dialog appears for card, text and animated shares. For a text post it has the QR code and caption without a download, and for an animated card the download button is **Download animation (MP4)**. Opening the dialog counts as one WeChat share in **Channel mix** and the **Activity** feed. Captions in Simplified or Traditional Chinese are copied exactly as written.

A WeChat share shows WeChat's own link preview, not the campaign's share card, which is why the dialog offers the card as an image to post alongside the link.

Separately, each advocate signs into their share page with a **magic link** — the link in the notify email, or one you copy from their row on the **Recipients** tab. A person can hold several magic links at once: copying or exporting a new one doesn't stop the one you emailed from working. Each lasts 90 days, and every one of a person's links stops working when you remove them from the campaign or end the campaign.

A share page belongs to the person it was sent to. If you open someone else's share page while signed in as yourself, it tells you so instead of loading; to see what they see, use **Preview as…** on the campaign page. When you view a contact's portal as them, their Advocacy card shows a note to the same effect in place of **Share now**.

## Export recipients with their magic links

To send the invitations from your own email tool, open the campaign's **Recipients** tab and click **Export**. Tick people first to export just them; with nobody ticked, it exports everyone on the campaign. Confirm, and you get a CSV with one row per recipient: **Name**, **Email**, **Company**, **Recipient type**, **Status**, **Notified at**, **Opens**, **Shares**, **Clicks**, **Tracked share link**, **Magic link** and **Magic link expires**.

On an Attendees campaign, **Notified at** shows when each attendee was emailed their link from Sessionboard.

Each row's magic link signs that person straight into their share page, so treat the file like a list of passwords. On a Groups campaign, each company expands to one row per contact on the event, with a **Company** column and a link each (see [Advocacy for exhibitors and sponsors](#advocacy-for-exhibitors-and-sponsors)); a company with no contacts gets one row with no link. Exporting needs the same contacts or accounts export permission as **Export assets**.

## Export assets

Hand your advocates' rendered cards and tracked links to your marketing team without copying them one at a time. Tick the campaigns you want in the campaign list and choose **Export assets**, or open one campaign's **Campaign actions** menu and choose **Export assets** there. Then pick an export format:

![The Export assets dialog for one campaign — Download ZIP and Gather share links, with Cancel and Start ZIP export](/images/kb/advocacy-export-assets.png)

- **Gather share links** returns a table with one row per card — **Campaign**, **Card type**, **Subject**, **Asset URL**, and **Share link**. Use **Copy all links** to put them on your clipboard or **Download CSV** to keep the table. Asset URLs expire after 7 days, so re-run the export when you need fresh ones.
- **Download ZIP** builds every rendered card into a ZIP, organised into a folder per campaign with a folder per card type inside (`/<Campaign>/<Card Type>/`, for example **Participant Cards** and **Participant Session Cards**). It runs in the background: Sessionboard notifies you in-app and by email when it is ready, with a download link that lasts 7 days.

An Attendees campaign exports one row per attendee advocate, named from the attendee list, in an **Attendee Cards** folder. Attendees who withdrew consent are left out. Export assets works on event community campaigns.

Draft campaigns are left out unless you tick **Include drafts**. A card is rendered the first time its advocate opens or posts it, so cards nobody has opened yet are listed as **Not rendered yet** in the links table and in a `MISSING.txt` file inside the ZIP rather than generated.

You need the contacts or accounts export permission on the event to export assets. The permission is checked again when the ZIP is built, so if you are removed from the event or lose the permission before the job runs, the export shows as **Failed** in the history with the reason instead of producing a file.

## Read the analytics

Open the **Analytics** tab for the whole event, or a single campaign for its own numbers. The event's Analytics and Activity tabs include your team campaigns, and their rows open the team campaign.

- **Advocates** — how many assigned people have activated, shown as activated over assigned
- **Shares** — total share actions across channels
- **Clicks** — total clicks, with unique visitors counted separately by consent cookie
- **Advocacy funnel** — Assigned, Opened, Shared, Clicked
- **Channel mix**, **Top advocates**, **Geography**, and a per-campaign breakdown

A campaign's own Analytics tab adds **Top advocates** for every advocate (searchable, sortable, filterable by activity and top channel, with CSV export) and **Results by channel**, and with conversion tracking set up, a **Conversions** section. See [Advocacy rewards and results](/marketing/advocacy-rewards#results-by-channel-and-advocate) and [Track conversions from Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy-conversion-tracking#read-the-conversions-section).

Under the **Channel mix** chart, **Clicks opened in the app** counts the clicks on advocates' links that opened inside an app's built-in browser: WeChat, LINE, Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn. It is the only way to see clicks from WeChat, where shares happen through a QR code or a downloaded card rather than a share button. A link opened in the phone's own browser isn't counted there, because nothing in the visit says where it came from. The list appears once at least one such click is recorded.

The **Activity** tab is the raw feed, filterable by Opens, Shares, and Clicks.

## Across events

**Advocacy** at the organization level rolls every event's campaigns into one read-only view. Campaigns are always created inside an event, so use the org view for reporting rather than setup. Its **Your team** section lists your team campaigns across the organization.

The page reads like a single event's Analytics tab, summed across events: a line counting how many campaigns are active across how many events, then **At a glance** (advocates activated of assigned, shares, unique destination clicks, each with a 30-day sparkline), the **Advocacy funnel** from assigned to clicked with the drop-off between stages, **Channel mix**, **Top advocates** across every event sorted by clicks then shares (with **Export CSV**), **Geography**, and **By campaign** — every campaign tagged with its event, with assigned, shared, clicked, converted and status columns.

## Languages

Share pages, share cards, the suggested post, the open-link signup page, the embeds and the emails Sessionboard sends to advocates follow the event's language. Advocates can also download their own card in three sizes; see [Share your event card](/participants/share-your-event-card#download-your-card).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can advocates choose their card's background?">
    Yes, when you offer choices. Add photos or styles under **Background choices for advocates** in the campaign's **Designer**, and each advocate gets a **Background** row on their share page with your designed background plus the extras. Their pick is saved, and it's the background on the card they post or download.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I copy or export a magic link, does the emailed one stop working?">
    No. A recipient can have several working magic links, so the emailed link, a copied link and an exported link all sign them in until each one expires after 90 days. Removing the person from the campaign, or ending the campaign, turns all of them off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do my advocates show as assigned but never activated?">
    Assigned means they're on the campaign; activated means they've opened their share page. If nobody has activated, check that you sent the notify email and that recipients have valid email addresses.
  </Accordion>
