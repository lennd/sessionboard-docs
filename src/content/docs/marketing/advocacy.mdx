## Why use it

Your speakers, sponsors and exhibitors reach audiences your own channels can't. Advocacy gives each of them a ready-made card and post on the networks they actually use, including LINE and Instagram for audiences in Asia and on mobile. Every click is traced back to the person who shared, so you can see which advocates fill the room.

## Before you start

- A **register link** — the URL your shares point at. Sessionboard prefills your event website if it's set under **Settings → Event Details**.
- Speakers, sponsors, or exhibitors on the event, with email addresses if you plan to notify them.
- Your event branding, so the generated share cards carry your logo and colors.

## Who can share

You pick the audience when you build the campaign.

| Audience | Who it is | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Speakers** | Your presenters sharing that they're speaking | Nothing extra |
| **Groups** | Sponsor and exhibitor companies | Exhibitor Pro or Sponsor Pro |

## Launch a campaign
Go to **Marketing → Advocacy** and click **New campaign**. This opens Quick Launch — five steps, with a live preview on the right that shows exactly what one of your advocates will see. Use **Previewing as** at the top to switch which person the preview follows. The longer campaign editor is for changing a campaign that already exists.

## Manage a live campaign

Open a campaign from the Advocacy list. The header has **Preview as…** (see the console as any advocate), **Edit campaign**, and a **Campaign actions** menu with **Notify recipients**, **Export assets**, **Pause**, **End campaign** and **Delete campaign**. Below it are the campaign's numbers and the **Recipients** and **Activity** tabs.

![The Campaign actions menu on a campaign — Notify recipients, Export assets, Pause, End campaign, Delete campaign](/images/kb/advocacy-campaign-actions.png)

## How the links work

Every recipient gets their own short link in the form `https://sessionboard.com/r/` plus an eight-character code. When someone clicks it, Sessionboard records the click, applies your UTM parameters and promo code, and forwards them to your destination — optionally through your branded landing page first.

Because each link is per person, shares and clicks attribute back to the individual advocate rather than to the campaign as a whole.

## How each channel shares

Channels are chosen per campaign under **Share channels**, in Quick Launch or **Edit campaign**. A new campaign starts with LinkedIn only. Campaigns created before LINE and Instagram were added keep the channels they had, so LINE and Instagram are off until you turn them on. Advocates only see buttons for the channels you turn on.

Every channel shares the advocate's tracked link, and each share shows up under its own name in **Channel mix** and the **Activity** feed.

| Channel | What the advocate's button does |
|---|---|
| **LINE** | **Share on LINE** copies the full post, then opens LINE's share page in a new tab: the LINE app on a phone, LINE's web picker on a computer. LINE only takes the link, so the advocate pastes the copied message into the chat if they want it. The chat preview shows the campaign's share card. A post can be up to 5,000 characters. |
| **Instagram** | Instagram has no web share link, so **Share to Instagram** hands over the card itself. It always copies the caption with the tracked link first. On a phone or tablet it opens the device's share sheet with the card, and the advocate picks Instagram (Feed, Story or a message). On a computer, or if the phone's browser refuses the share sheet, the card downloads and a message offers **Open Instagram**. A caption can be up to 2,200 characters. |

A few details for Instagram:

- Links in Instagram captions aren't clickable. The download message reminds advocates to add a link sticker to a Story or put the link in their bio.
- On a video campaign, the video renders first. On a phone, a **Your video is ready** message appears with a **Share now** button that opens the share sheet. On a computer, the video downloads as soon as it's ready.
- If an advocate closes the share sheet without posting, no share is recorded.

Separately, each advocate signs into their Creator console with a **magic link** — the link in the notify email, or one you copy from their row on the **Recipients** tab. A person can hold several magic links at once: copying or exporting a new one doesn't stop the one you emailed from working. Each lasts 90 days, and every one of a person's links stops working when you remove them from the campaign or end the campaign.

A share page belongs to the person it was sent to. If you open someone else's share page while signed in as yourself, it tells you so instead of loading; to see what they see, use **Preview as…** on the campaign page. When you view a contact's portal as them, their Advocacy card shows a note to the same effect in place of **Share now**.

## Export recipients with their magic links

To send the invitations from your own email tool, open the campaign's **Recipients** tab and click **Export**. Tick people first to export just them; with nobody ticked, it exports everyone on the campaign. Confirm, and you get a CSV with one row per recipient: **Name**, **Email**, **Recipient type**, **Status**, **Notified at**, **Opens**, **Shares**, **Clicks**, **Tracked share link**, **Magic link** and **Magic link expires**.

Each row's magic link signs that person straight into their console, so treat the file like a list of passwords. Groups get no magic link, because a link signs in one person. Exporting needs the same contacts or accounts export permission as **Export assets**.

## Export assets

Hand your advocates' rendered cards and tracked links to your marketing team without copying them one at a time. Tick the campaigns you want in the campaign list and choose **Export assets**, or open one campaign's **Campaign actions** menu and choose **Export assets** there. Then pick an export format:

![The Export assets dialog for one campaign — Download ZIP and Gather share links, with Cancel and Start ZIP export](/images/kb/advocacy-export-assets.png)

- **Gather share links** returns a table with one row per card — **Campaign**, **Card type**, **Subject**, **Asset URL**, and **Share link**. Use **Copy all links** to put them on your clipboard or **Download CSV** to keep the table. Asset URLs expire after 7 days, so re-run the export when you need fresh ones.
- **Download ZIP** builds every rendered card into a ZIP, organised into a folder per campaign with a folder per card type inside (`/<Campaign>/<Card Type>/`). It runs in the background: Sessionboard notifies you in-app and by email when it is ready, with a download link that lasts 7 days.

Draft campaigns are left out unless you tick **Include drafts**. A card is rendered the first time its advocate opens or posts it, so cards nobody has opened yet are listed as **Not rendered yet** in the links table and in a `MISSING.txt` file inside the ZIP rather than generated.

You need the contacts or accounts export permission on the event to export assets. The permission is checked again when the ZIP is built, so if you are removed from the event or lose the permission before the job runs, the export shows as **Failed** in the history with the reason instead of producing a file.

## Read the analytics

Open the **Analytics** tab for the whole event, or a single campaign for its own numbers.

- **Advocates** — how many assigned people have activated, shown as activated over assigned
- **Shares** — total share actions across channels
- **Clicks** — total clicks, with unique visitors counted separately by consent cookie
- **Advocacy funnel** — Assigned, Opened, Shared, Clicked
- **Channel mix**, **Top advocates**, **Geography**, and a per-campaign breakdown

The **Activity** tab is the raw feed, filterable by Opens, Shares, and Clicks.

## Across events

**Advocacy** at the organization level rolls every event's campaigns into one read-only view. Campaigns are always created inside an event, so use the org view for reporting rather than setup.

The page reads like a single event's Analytics tab, summed across events: a line counting how many campaigns are active across how many events, then **At a glance** (advocates activated of assigned, shares, unique destination clicks, each with a 30-day sparkline), the **Advocacy funnel** from assigned to clicked with the drop-off between stages, **Channel mix**, **Top advocates** across every event sorted by clicks then shares (with **Export CSV**), **Geography**, and **By campaign** — every campaign tagged with its event, with assigned, shared, clicked, converted and status columns.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do advocates need a Sessionboard login?">
    No. They get a magic link to the Creator console, which needs no account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which channels can they share to?">
    LinkedIn, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, LINE, Instagram, email, copy link, QR code and the download kit. LinkedIn is the only channel on by default for a new campaign; turn on the others per campaign under **Share channels**. See [How each channel shares](#how-each-channel-shares) for what the LINE and Instagram buttons do.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a campaign after launching it?">
    Yes. Open the campaign and click **Edit campaign** to walk through audience, basics, recipients, templates and copy, landing page, and launch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no date on some session share cards?">
    A session that has not been scheduled yet has no date or time to show, so its share card omits the date line rather than printing a placeholder. The line appears automatically once the session is scheduled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My background image or logo isn't showing on the share card">
    Cards on photo styles show your uploaded background image edge to edge, with your event logo over it. If a campaign created earlier showed a dark overlay hiding the background, or the logo area was blank, that was a rendering bug that has since been fixed — affected cards now display correctly on their own, with no need to edit or relaunch the campaign. If a card still looks wrong, check that the event's logo and background are set under your event branding, then contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can advocates choose their card's background?">
    Yes, when you offer choices. Add photos or styles under **Background choices for advocates** in the campaign's **Designer**, and each advocate gets a **Background** row in their Creator console with your designed background plus the extras. Their pick is saved, and it's the background on the card they post or download.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I copy or export a magic link, does the emailed one stop working?">
    No. A recipient can have several working magic links, so the emailed link, a copied link and an exported link all sign them in until each one expires after 90 days. Removing the person from the campaign, or ending the campaign, turns all of them off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do my advocates show as assigned but never activated?">
    Assigned means they're on the campaign; activated means they've opened their Creator console. If nobody has activated, check that you sent the notify email and that recipients have valid email addresses.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
