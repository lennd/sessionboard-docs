The Quotes library holds the sharp, complete lines from your recordings — the ones worth putting on a card or opening a post with. AI pulls candidates from a transcript, you keep the good ones, and each can become a LinkedIn post or quote card in a click.

<Note>
Quotes are part of the Marketing module, which is currently Early Access.
</Note>

## Pull quotes from a recording

Quotes are extracted from a recording rather than added directly in the library, so start in Media.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a recording with a finished transcript">
    Go to **Marketing → Media** and open an item whose transcript has finished processing. Quote extraction reads the transcript, so Sessionboard tells you where a recording stands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find quotes">
    Click **Edit & clip** to open the recording in the editor, then **Find quotes** at the top of the **Chapters & clips** panel. AI pulls around a dozen candidate lines, favouring complete thoughts over fragments, and a message tells you how many were added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review them">
    Open **Media → Quotes**. The header counts the quotes and reminds you to delete any that aren't worth keeping. Keep, edit, or delete each one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work with a quote

Each card shows the line, who said it, a link to the recording at the moment it was said, the chapter of the recording it came from, and the clips that cover the same moment. It offers:

- **Copy** — the text, for pasting anywhere
- **Edit** — the **Edit quote** dialog: fix the wording in **Quote**, correct **Speaker** (who said it), and adjust the comma-separated **Topics**
- **Create** — turn it into a **LinkedIn post** or a **Quote card**: a new Create project named after the quote opens, with the line and its speaker as the starting text
- **Delete**

<Tip>
Trim quotes when you edit them. Transcripts capture speech, and spoken sentences usually need tightening before they read well on a card.
</Tip>

The **Create** action is available on event-scoped quotes. At the organization level the library is for browsing and searching across every event.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a quote by hand?">
    Not from the library itself — there's no Add button. Quotes come from running **Find quotes** on a recording, and you can then edit the text freely.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Find quotes greyed out or erroring?">
    The recording has no finished transcript yet. Wait for transcription to complete, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many quotes come back at once?">
    Around twelve per run. You can run it again on the same recording.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do quotes carry speaker attribution?">
    Yes, taken from transcript diarization. Correct the speaker tagging on the transcript first and the quotes come out attributed properly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
