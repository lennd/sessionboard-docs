Your attendees register somewhere else. Your exhibitors buy booths somewhere else. Advocacy still works, because it needs only two things from any system: a way to know who the person is, so they get their own card, and a place to put a link, so they find it. This page walks through both for the systems our customers use most. The campaign builder itself is covered in [Advocacy campaigns](/marketing/advocacy); the attendee toolkit in [Advocacy for attendees](/marketing/advocacy-attendees).

## Why use it

A share from someone who is going, exhibiting or sponsoring reads as a recommendation. The best moment to ask is the one your other system already owns: the registration confirmation, the booth confirmation, the exhibitor manual. Putting the card there costs one paste, and every click and registration it drives is credited to the person who shared.

## Pick your path

Start from what the people are and where they live.

| Who | Where they live | Path |
|---|---|---|
| Attendees | A registration platform with a Sessionboard app and attendee sync (Cvent, Swoogo, Swapcard, Bizzabo, Visit, LiveBuzz, ExpoPlatform) | [Connected registration platform](#attendees-on-a-connected-registration-platform) |
| Attendees | Any other registration system (Map Your Show registration, A2Z/Personify, Eventbrite, RainFocus, your own site), or a platform whose confirmation emails can't carry merge tags | [Any other registration system](#attendees-on-any-other-registration-system) |
| Exhibitors and sponsors | An exhibitor or floor-plan system (Map Your Show, A2Z, ExpoPlatform, Cvent Exhibitor Management, Swapcard Exhibitor Center) or a spreadsheet | [Exhibitors and sponsors](#exhibitors-and-sponsors-from-an-exhibitor-system) |
| Anyone, before the list exists | Nowhere yet | Turn on the campaign's **Open link** and put it in your newsletter and on your website. No list needed. |

<Tip>
You can start today with the open link on every campaign, add the personal links or embeds to your confirmation flow when the platform allows it, and send one **Email attendees** or **Email exhibitors** from Sessionboard a few weeks out to catch everyone who came in before you launched.
</Tip>

## Attendees on a connected registration platform

Cvent, Swoogo, Swapcard, Bizzabo, Visit, LiveBuzz and ExpoPlatform each have a Sessionboard app with an attendee sync. Once it is on, registrants arrive in Sessionboard on their own and every one of them has a personal link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on attendee sync">
    In **Apps**, open your registration platform, map the event and turn on its attendee sync. The connection's access check must show attendees as readable (for Cvent, the API client needs the `event/attendees:read` scope). Confirm registrants are appearing on the event's **Attendees** page before you go further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build an Attendees campaign">
    **Marketing → Advocacy → New campaign → Attendees**. Pick a card style, set the register link and activate. Turn the **Open link** on as well; it costs nothing and catches anyone the list misses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the personal link template for your platform">
    On the campaign's **Share** tab, under **Personal links in your registration emails**, pick your platform. The template shows the link with two placeholders: `{registrant id}` and `{email}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into the confirmation email">
    Open the confirmation email in your registration platform's email editor and paste the link, then replace the two placeholders with the platform's own personalization tags for the registrant's unique id and email address. Every platform names these differently: Cvent calls them data tags, most others call them merge tags or variables. Use the registrant's **id**, not their confirmation number, and the **email** they registered with. Add the email even where the table in [Advocacy campaigns](/marketing/advocacy#personal-links-in-your-registration-emails) says it is optional; it never hurts and makes the link work for every registrant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the card to the confirmation page, if the page allows scripts">
    Under **Embed on your confirmation page**, copy **Their personal card** and paste it where your platform lets you add HTML with a script to the thank-you page, replacing the same two placeholders with the same tags. If the page accepts HTML but not scripts, link to the personal link or the open link instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register yourself and click">
    Make a test registration, open the confirmation email and click. You should land in your own Creator console with your card ready. If you see **This link isn't available**, check the platform picked on the Share tab, the two tags, and that your test registration shows on the Attendees page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For Cvent, Swoogo, Visit and LiveBuzz, someone who registers after the last sync is looked up on the platform the moment they click, so the link works from the first confirmation email. For Swapcard, Bizzabo and ExpoPlatform the registrant must have synced first; run the sync continuously or on a schedule tight enough for your confirmation timing.

## Attendees on any other registration system

Map Your Show registration, A2Z/Personify, Eventbrite, RainFocus, an in-house registration site, or any platform that has no Sessionboard attendee sync: the registrants come in as a file, and the links still work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export the registrants">
    From your registration system, export confirmed registrants with at least **email**, **first name**, **last name**, **company**, **job title** and the system's **registrant or badge id**. Include a marketing opt-in column if the system has one, and a headshot URL if it does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import them into Sessionboard">
    On the event's **Attendees** page click **Import attendees**, or use **Import an attendee list** on the campaign's audience step. Map the system's id to **Registrant ID**, its status to **Registration Status** and its opt-in to **Marketing Opt-in**. Details column by column: [Importing data → Attendees](/settings/importing-data#attendees).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Uploaded attendee list on the Share tab">
    Under **Personal links in your registration emails**, set the **Registration platform** to **Uploaded attendee list**. The template becomes `…/csv/{registrant id}?e={email}`. The `{registrant id}` is the **Registrant ID** you imported; if your file had no id column, it is the email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the links where registrants will see them">
    If your registration system's confirmation emails can carry its own merge tags for the registrant's id and email, paste the template there as in the connected-platform steps above. If they can't, use any of these instead, all from the Share tab:

    - **Email attendees** from Sessionboard: each imported attendee gets their personal link, wrapped in your message. Run it again after each re-import and choose **Only new**.
    - **Export personal links (CSV)**: a mail merge in your own email tool, using the **Magic link** column.
    - **Open link**: one URL that works for everyone. Put it in the confirmation email as a plain link, in your newsletter and on the event website.
    - **Signup form embed**: the open-link form on your confirmation page, prefilled from whatever name, email, company and title fields the page knows. See [Advocacy for attendees](/marketing/advocacy-attendees#embed-the-card-on-your-confirmation-page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-import on a schedule">
    Registrations keep coming. Re-import the export weekly, then daily in the last stretch before the event, with **When a row already exists** set to create new and update existing. **All attendees** campaigns pick the new people up automatically; a hand-picked campaign needs them added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A registrant who clicks a personal link before their row is imported sees **This link isn't available**. If your import cadence can't keep up with your confirmation timing, lead with the open link in the confirmation email and use personal links in your later emails.
</Note>

## Exhibitors and sponsors from an exhibitor system

Exhibitor systems such as Map Your Show, A2Z, ExpoPlatform, Cvent Exhibitor Management and Swapcard Exhibitor Center own the booth sale and the booth contacts. Sessionboard does not need a connection to any of them for Advocacy: a Groups campaign works from the exhibitor and sponsor companies on your event and the contacts listed under each company. Nobody logs into a portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export companies and booth contacts">
    From your exhibitor system, export the exhibitor list with each company's booth contacts: company name, contact first and last name, email and job title, plus booth number and sponsorship tier if you want them on the record. In Map Your Show this is an Excel or CSV export from the exhibitor or reports area; ask your account manager for the export that includes the contacts, since the default list often carries only the person who bought the booth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the companies and contacts in Sessionboard">
    Add them as exhibitor groups with contacts, or sponsor groups with tiers: [Create exhibitor groups & contacts](/sponsors-exhibitors/adding-exhibitor-groups-contacts), [Create sponsor groups & contacts](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsors). A spreadsheet [import](/settings/importing-data) creates or updates many at once. Every contact you list under a company will get that company's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a Groups campaign">
    **Marketing → Advocacy → New campaign → Groups**. Choose **All** companies, **Select** by type or tier, or **Manual**. The card reads "We're exhibiting at *your event*" for exhibitors and "We're proud to sponsor *your event*" for sponsors, with the company's logo slot. Activate it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand out the links from the Share tab">
    - **Email exhibitors their links**: Sessionboard emails every contact of every company their own link to the company's console. Run **Only new** after each export-and-import round.
    - **Export personal links (CSV)**: one row per person with **Company**, **Email** and **Magic link**, for the exhibitor newsletter you already send from Map Your Show, your marketing tool or your exhibitor services team's inbox.
    - **Open link**: one URL for the whole campaign. Put it in the exhibitor manual, the exhibitor services page, the booth confirmation email and the exhibitor dashboard message in your exhibitor system. Anyone at a company on the campaign enters their name, email and company and gets the company's card; booth staff you never added to Sessionboard included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell exhibitors what to do with it">
    One line in the exhibitor manual is enough: "Add your logo, pick a background and post the card with your tracked link. Every click back to the show is credited to your company." Sponsors get the same with their tier on the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Groups campaigns, emailing links, the export and the open link work on every plan. Exhibitor Pro or Sponsor Pro is needed only to target a campaign **By portal** or to show it inside an exhibitor or sponsor portal. Details and the safety rules of the exhibitor open link: [Advocacy for exhibitors and sponsors](/marketing/advocacy#advocacy-for-exhibitors-and-sponsors).
</Note>

## System by system

| System | Who | How they get into Sessionboard | Best link | Where to put it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cvent | Attendees | App with attendee sync; looked up on click | Personal link (`cvent`) + card embed | Confirmation email, Attendee Hub confirmation page |
| Swoogo | Attendees | App with attendee sync; looked up on click | Personal link (`swoogo`) + card embed | Confirmation email, confirmation page |
| Swapcard | Attendees | App with attendee sync; sync first | Personal link (`swapcard`), open link | Confirmation email, event app announcement |
| Bizzabo | Attendees | App with attendee sync; sync first | Personal link (`bizzabo`) + card embed | Confirmation email, confirmation page |
| Visit, LiveBuzz | Attendees | App with attendee sync; looked up on click | Personal link (`visit` / `livebuzz`) | Confirmation email |
| ExpoPlatform | Attendees | App with attendee sync; sync first | Personal link (`expo_platform`) | Confirmation email |
| Map Your Show | Attendees | CSV export → **Import attendees** | Personal link (`csv`) where merge tags exist, otherwise open link | Confirmation email, event website |
| Map Your Show, A2Z, ExpoPlatform, Cvent Exhibitor Management | Exhibitors, sponsors | Export → exhibitor / sponsor groups and contacts | **Email exhibitors**, CSV mail merge, exhibitor open link | Exhibitor manual, exhibitor newsletter, booth confirmation, exhibitor dashboard |
| Eventbrite, RainFocus, A2Z/Personify, your own site | Attendees | CSV export → **Import attendees** | Personal link (`csv`) or open link | Confirmation email, event website |
| Grip | Attendees | No attendee sync yet | Open link, or CSV import + `csv` links | Event app, confirmation email |
| No system yet | Anyone | Nothing | Open link | Newsletter, website, social |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My registration system has no merge tag for the registrant id, only the email">
    Import your registrants without a **Registrant ID** column. The registrant id then is the email address, so the personal link becomes `…/csv/{email}?e={email}` with the platform's email tag in both places. URL-encode the address if your platform does not do it for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can one campaign cover attendees and exhibitors?">
    No. Attendees are an **Attendees** campaign and companies are a **Groups** campaign; each has its own card, Share tab and analytics. Run both from the same event.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do exhibitors need the Exhibitor portal?">
    No. Their links open the company's Creator console directly, signed in, with no portal login. The portal is a separate product; when you have it, the campaign also appears on the company's Portal Home.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do clicks and registrations show up?">
    On the campaign's **Analytics** tab, per advocate. A click goes through your branded landing page to your register link with the advocate recorded as the source. Registrations completed on your registration platform's own pages are credited through the `sb_ref` value on the destination link; your Customer Success Manager has the setup steps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone's link shows This link isn't available">
    They are not on the event yet (not synced or imported), the **Registration platform** on the Share tab doesn't match how they came in, a tag was pasted wrong, or they are cancelled, waitlisted or opted out. Fix the cause and the same link works; or send them the open link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
