An Attendees campaign gives everyone registered for your event their own branded "I'm attending" card and a personal tracked link. They post it in one tap, and every click and registration it drives is credited to them. This guide covers every way to get the card into attendees' hands and which one fits your situation. The campaign builder itself is described in [Advocacy campaigns](/marketing/advocacy).

## Why use it

Your attendees know the people you want next year: their colleagues, their peers, their followers on LinkedIn. A post from someone who is going reads as a recommendation, not an ad. Attendee Advocacy turns registration into that post — the moment someone registers, they can share a card with their name and company on it, and you can see which attendees brought others with them.

## Pick your approach

Most events use two or three of these together. Start from what you already have.

| Your situation | Use | Attendee list needed? |
|---|---|---|
| You want buzz before registration opens, or you have no list in Sessionboard | [Open link](#share-one-open-link) in your newsletter, on your website or on social | No |
| Attendees register on Cvent, Swoogo, Swapcard, Bizzabo or another platform that sends a confirmation email | [Personal links in your registration emails](#put-personal-links-in-your-registration-emails) | Yes — synced or imported |
| Your registration confirmation page accepts HTML and scripts | [Embed on your confirmation page](#embed-the-card-on-your-confirmation-page) | Personal card: yes. Signup form: no |
| You have a spreadsheet export of registrants | [Import the list](#bring-in-your-attendee-list), then [email them from Sessionboard](#email-attendees-from-sessionboard) | Yes — imported |
| You send email from your own marketing tool | [Export personal links](#export-personal-links-for-your-own-email-tool) and mail merge | Yes |
| Your registration platform is connected in **Apps** | Turn on attendee sync, then [email them from Sessionboard](#email-attendees-from-sessionboard) or use personal links | Yes — synced |

<Tip>
A common combination: turn on the open link so the campaign works from day one, put personal links in the confirmation email for everyone who registers, and send one email from Sessionboard a few weeks out to the people who registered before you launched.
</Tip>

## Set up the campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new campaign">
    Go to **Marketing → Advocacy** and click **New campaign**. Under **Who's sharing**, choose **Attendees**. The tile shows how many attendees can share — for example *120 eligible of 150 attendees*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get attendees in, or turn on the open link">
    With nobody eligible yet, the step offers two ways forward: **Import an attendee list** right there, or switch on **Open link — anyone with the link can make their own card**. You can do both.

    ![The Attendees audience on Quick Launch's Build step — no attendees yet, with Import an attendee list and the Open link switch turned on](/images/kb/advocacy-attendees-build-empty.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the card and the post">
    Pick **Image** or **Video**, a card style and your share message, exactly as for a speaker campaign. The card fills itself in for each attendee from their name, company, job title and headshot. Leave the share message empty and attendees start from a short ready-made post saying they're attending, with their tracked link underneath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipients">
    **All attendees** follows the list live: anyone who registers, is confirmed or checks in later joins the campaign automatically. **Manual** lets you hand-pick. With the open link on you can continue with nobody on the list — attendees join through the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Set the register link and landing page, then click **Activate campaign**. With the open link on, the success screen shows the link ready to copy and a shortcut to the campaign's **Share** tab.

    ![Share your open link on the Quick Launch success screen — the open link URL, Copy open link and a link to the Share tab](/images/kb/advocacy-attendees-launched.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything you hand to attendees afterwards lives on the campaign's **Share** tab.

## Bring in your attendee list

Personal links, the personal card embed and Sessionboard's own email all need attendees on the event. There are three ways to get them there:

- **Sync from your registration platform.** Connect Cvent, Swoogo, Swapcard, Visit, LiveBuzz, Bizzabo or Expo Platform in **Apps** and turn on its attendee sync. New registrations arrive on their own.
- **Import a spreadsheet.** Click **Import attendees** on the event's **Attendees** page, or **Import an attendee list** on the campaign's audience step. It is the same importer as [Importing data](/settings/importing-data#attendees): map your columns, preview, import. **Email** is required; **Registrant ID**, **Registration Status**, **Marketing Opt-in** and **Headshot** are optional.
- **Let attendees add themselves** through the [open link](#share-one-open-link).

![The attendee importer's Map columns step — each spreadsheet column matched to an attendee field, with the row count and the marketing opt-in checkbox](/images/kb/attendees-import-map-columns.png)

### Who can share

Only attendees who are **confirmed** or **checked in** and have **not opted out of marketing** are included. Cancelled and waitlisted registrations never get a card. On import, tick **These attendees agreed to receive marketing from us** to treat rows with a blank opt-in cell as opted in; a row that says no stays out.

## Share one open link

One link for the whole campaign, no list needed. On the **Share** tab, turn on **Open link** and click **Copy open link**.

![The Open link card on a campaign's Share tab — the switch turned on and the open link URL with a Copy button](/images/kb/advocacy-attendee-share-open-link.png)

Anyone who opens it sees your event's name and dates, enters their first name, last name, email, company and job title, ticks the consent box and clicks **Make my card**. Their Creator console opens straight away with their card ready to post. To come back to it later, they open the link again and enter the same email: Sessionboard emails them their personal link.

![The open link's signup page — event name and dates, first name, last name, email, company and job title, the consent checkbox and Make my card](/images/kb/advocacy-open-link-join-page.png)

Good places for it:

- your event newsletter and "registration is open" emails
- a button on the event website's registration or "Why attend" page
- your own social posts — "Going? Make your card"
- the registration confirmation page, as a link when the page can't take an embed

**Someone already on your list** who opens the open link isn't handed a second card: they see **Check your inbox**, and Sessionboard emails them their existing personal link — so their shares stay credited to one person. An attendee who is cancelled, waitlisted or opted out gets no card and no email. The page follows the visitor's browser language (English, French, German or Spanish).

Turning the open link off makes the link stop working; turning it back on restores the same link.

## Put personal links in your registration emails

Every attendee on your list has a personal link that opens their own Creator console, signed in, with their card ready. Adding it to the confirmation email your registration platform already sends is the highest-reach option: every registrant sees it at the moment they are most excited.

On the **Share** tab, under **Personal links in your registration emails**, pick your **Registration platform**. The **Personal link template** shows the link with your platform's merge fields marked — paste it into the confirmation email (or confirmation page) and replace the placeholders with the platform's registrant id and email merge tags.

![Personal links in your registration emails — the Registration platform picker set to Cvent and the personal link template with placeholders for the registrant id and email](/images/kb/advocacy-attendee-share-personal-links.png)

The attendee must already be on the event in Sessionboard when they click — synced from that platform or imported with the same registrant id. The full link format, and which platforms need the email on the link, is in [Personal links in your registration emails](/marketing/advocacy#personal-links-in-your-registration-emails).

<Tip>
Send yourself a test registration and click the link in the confirmation email before you announce anything. If it shows **This link isn't available**, check that the registrant is on the event in Sessionboard and that the merge tags match your platform's registrant id and email.
</Tip>

## Embed the card on your confirmation page

If your registration platform's confirmation page accepts HTML with a script, the card can appear on the page itself. Under **Embed on your confirmation page**, copy one of two embeds:

- **Their personal card** — the attendee's own card with share buttons, keyed by your platform's registrant id and email merge fields. Use it when attendees are synced or imported.
- **Signup form (open link)** — the open-link form, right on the page, prefilled with the registrant's name, email, company and job title from your merge fields. Use it when they aren't on your list in Sessionboard. It needs the open link on.

![Embed on your confirmation page — the personal card embed code and the signup form embed code, each with a Copy button](/images/kb/advocacy-attendee-share-embeds.png)

The embed shows in the language of the page it sits on (its `lang`), or set the `language` attribute; English, French, German and Spanish are supported. Remove any prefill attribute your platform doesn't have a merge field for.

## Email attendees from Sessionboard

When attendees are on your list, Sessionboard can email each of them their personal link. Under **Email attendees their links**, click **Email attendees** (or **Campaign actions → Notify recipients**).

![The Notify dialog for an Attendees campaign — subject and message on the left, a preview with the Open my share card button on the right](/images/kb/advocacy-attendees-notify.png)

You write the subject and message; the preview on the right shows what arrives. Each email ends with an **Open my share card** button and the link to paste, written in the event's language. Click **Send me a test** first to see it in your own inbox.

Run it again after more people register: once anyone has been emailed, the dialog asks whether to send to **Everyone** or **Only new** — the people never successfully emailed for this campaign.

## Export personal links for your own email tool

Under **Export personal links (CSV)**, click **Export CSV**. The file has one row per attendee: name, email, status, their opens, shares and clicks so far, their tracked share link, and the **Magic link** — the personal link that opens their console — with the date it expires. Use the **Magic link** column in a mail merge from your own email platform, and export again for fresh links once they expire.

<Warning>
Each link signs its attendee straight into their Creator console. Treat the file like a password list and don't share it outside your team.
</Warning>

## What attendees see

Whichever way they arrive, attendees land in their **Creator console** — no account, no password.

![An attendee's Creator console — Ready to share, their card with name, company and job title, the headshot slot, and the share message with their tracked link](/images/kb/advocacy-attendees-creator-console.png)

1. **Their card** shows their first and last name, company and job title on your event's design. Until they add a headshot, it uses the **Headshot** from your import or an empty photo slot.
2. **Make it yours** lets them change their photo, fix their name, company and title, and pick a background if you offered choices. Edits show in the preview and render into the post.
3. **The share message** is your default text with their personal tracked link near the top, where feeds don't cut it off. They can edit it.
4. **Post** — they connect LinkedIn and publish in one tap, or use any other channel you turned on for the campaign (X, Facebook, WhatsApp, LINE, Instagram, email, copy link, download).

Their share and click counts sit at the top of the page, so they can see the reach they're driving.

## Track results

Open the campaign:

- **Recipients** lists every attendee in the campaign with whether they opened their console, how many times they shared, the clicks their link drove and their last activity. Attendees who signed up through the open link appear here as soon as they join.
- **Activity** is the live feed of opens, shares and clicks.
- **Analytics** shows the funnel from assigned to opened, shared and clicked, the channel mix, and **Top advocates** — the attendees who drove the most clicks. See [Read the analytics](/marketing/advocacy#read-the-analytics).

A click on an attendee's link goes through your branded landing page to your register link, with the attendee recorded as the source. Registrations that happen on your registration platform's own pages are credited through the `sb_ref` value on the destination link — your Customer Success Manager has the setup steps.

## Privacy and consent

- The open link's form records the attendee's consent before it makes a card.
- Imported and synced attendees are only included when they haven't opted out of marketing.
- Personal links and exported links are sign-in links: anyone holding one can open that attendee's console.
- Click tracking counts clicks on an attendee's link. It doesn't collect personal data about the people who click.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I turn on the Attendees audience?">
    There is nothing to turn on. In **Marketing → Advocacy → New campaign**, choose **Attendees** under **Who's sharing**. If the tile shows no eligible attendees, import a list or turn on the open link on the same step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an attendee list before I launch?">
    No. With the open link on, a campaign can launch with nobody on it, and attendees join through the link. Personal links, the personal card embed and **Email attendees** need attendees on the event.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What information is on an attendee's card?">
    Their first and last name, company and job title, and their headshot — the one they add in the Creator console, or the **Headshot** column from your import until they do. The rest of the card is your event's design: logo, colors, background and card style.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone sign up twice through the open link?">
    No. An email that's already on the campaign gets **Check your inbox** and their existing personal link by email, so one attendee always has one card and one link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if an attendee cancels?">
    A cancelled or waitlisted attendee drops out of **All attendees**, and their personal link stops working.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which languages are supported?">
    The open-link page follows the visitor's browser language, the embeds follow the language of the page they sit on, and the emails Sessionboard sends follow the event's language. English, French, German and Spanish are supported, and anything else falls back to English.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
