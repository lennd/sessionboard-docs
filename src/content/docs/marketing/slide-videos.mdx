## Why use it

Most sessions are recorded as audio, or as a room camera nobody wants to watch. The deck is what people came to see, and the speaker already uploaded it. A slide video pairs the two without anyone sitting in an editor, so every talk with a deck can go to your content gallery, your learning platform or a feed the same day.

## What it needs

- **A recording linked to its session.** The slides come from the session's files.
- **A deck in the session's files:** a PDF or a PowerPoint file. When a session has several decks, the newest is used; you can pick another on the slide video page. Posters are not used.
- **A finished transcript.** The slides are timed by matching what is said to what is on each slide.

When all three are in place, the slide video is made automatically as soon as the transcript is ready. Nobody has to start it. A slide video that already exists is never replaced automatically, so your edits are safe.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the recording">
    Go to **Marketing → Media** on the event and open the recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its slide video">
    Click **Slide video**. On an audio recording it is in the **Actions** list.

## Choose a look

Under **Look**, pick a **Layout**:

| Layout | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Framed** | The slide framed on a dark background, with captions and the speaker's name below it. |
| **Full frame** | The slide fills the frame and the captions sit over it. |
| **Speaker sidebar** | The slide on the left; the speaker's photo, name and title, and the session title on the right. |
| **Spotlight** | Your brand color behind the slide, with your logo and the session title across the top. |

![The Look card: the four layouts, the Captions and Speaker name card switches, the speaker's name and the line under it, and Trim from 0:00 to 2:54](/images/kb/slide-video-look.png)

The same card also turns **Captions** and the **Speaker name card** on or off, sets the name and the line under it, and sets **Trim** to cut the start or end of the recording.

The speaker sidebar uses the session speaker named on the card, or the first speaker on the session. Their photo and title come from their contact record. When a speaker has no photo, their initials are shown instead.

## Add an opening and a closing

Under **Opening and closing**, switch on an **Opening card**, a **Closing card**, or both, and choose a design and how many seconds it stays on screen (2 to 10).

![The Opening and closing card with the Opening card on, the Classic band design and 4 seconds, and the Closing card off](/images/kb/slide-video-opening-closing.png)

- The **opening card** is a title slide for this session: the event, the session title and its speakers, laid out to fit however many speakers there are and however long their names are.
- The **closing card** is the same design's end slide.

The designs are the event's title slides from [Slides](/marketing/slides): the templates you saved for the event, listed under **Your title slides**, then the six designs built from your brand kit, listed under **From your brand kit**. Change a template in Slides and the next version of the video uses the change.

After you save and re-render, the card shows a preview of the opening or closing used in the current version.

<Note>
If the template you picked is deleted, the card says so. Pick another design, or the next version leaves that card out.
</Note>

Click **Edit with chat** and describe the change in plain words, for example:

- "Use the speaker sidebar layout and bring slide 4 in at 1:00"
- "Bring slide 4 in when the speaker starts on the timeline"
- "Open with our summit title slide for 4 seconds"
- "Trim the first 10 seconds"

The answer lists the changes it proposes, such as **Slide 4 → 1:00** or **Layout: Speaker sidebar**. A time you give is used exactly; "when the speaker starts on…" finds that moment in the transcript. Nothing changes until you click **Apply changes**, which saves and re-renders. If you ask for a design the event doesn't have, it tells you which ones it can use.

## Start over

**Start over from the deck** reads the slides again, matches them to the talk again and resets the layout, captions, trim, slide times and opening and closing cards. Use it after the speaker uploads a new version of the deck. It asks first, because your edits are not kept. When the session has more than one deck, pick it beside the button.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The slides change too early or too late">
    Timing comes from matching the transcript to the words on each slide, so slides that are mostly pictures, or a speaker who goes off script, can land a little off. Move the slide's start time by hand, or ask in chat to bring it in "when the speaker starts on" a topic.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A PowerPoint deck didn't work">
    PowerPoint files are converted to PDF first. If the conversion fails, the page says so: export the deck as a PDF, upload it to the session's files and click **Start over from the deck**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The speaker's photo or the logo is missing">
    The photo comes from the speaker's contact record, and the logo from your brand kit. Images on a site that doesn't answer, or that isn't a public `https://` address, are left out: the speaker's initials are shown, or the video has no logo.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There's no opening or closing card option">
    Opening and closing cards come from the event's title slides, so they are only offered for recordings that belong to an event.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There's no Open in Clips button">
    **Open in Clips** shows only where Clips is turned on for your account. **Download MP4** is always there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
