<Note>
Create is on for every event under **Marketing → Source & produce → Create**. You can also open it from the sessions list: select sessions, then **Create** and pick what to make — [Slides](/marketing/slides) first, then the Create formats below.

## What you can make

| Format | What it produces |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn post** | A hook, body, and CTA grounded in what your speakers said |
| **Blog post** | An article of roughly 600 words, cited |
| **White paper** | A long-form, source-grounded thought-leadership piece |
| **Carousel** | A branded multi-slide deck for LinkedIn or X |
| **Video** | A clipped, branded video built from your recordings |

## Draft something

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Go to **Marketing → Create**. Describe what you want in the prompt box — for example, "A LinkedIn post on what our fintech speakers said about regulation" — and click **Get started**. You can also pick a format directly, start from a template, or open one of the suggestions built from your recent content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your sources">
    The **Sources** rail on the left is what grounds the draft. Set a topic, narrow to an event, and pick specific sessions or media. The panel tells you how many sources the draft is drawing from.

    Under **Settings**, choose a language — English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, or Arabic — and set the promotional versus thought-leadership balance with the CTA slider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Click **Draft from sources**. If you arrived from a suggestion that already has sources attached, the first draft generates on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit">
    Edit directly in the document. Select text and use **AI** to improve writing, shorten, lengthen, fix grammar, make it punchier, change tone, or translate. **Copilot** opens a writing panel that takes an instruction for the whole document, such as "Tighten the intro and end with a question" — it proposes the rewrite there and only touches the document when you apply it. Changes save as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    The menu beside **Save** holds **Save as a copy**, **Post to LinkedIn** (opens LinkedIn with your post ready to paste), **Download PDF**, **Download Word (.doc)**, **Copy as Markdown** and **Copy text**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Post to LinkedIn** copies your text and opens the LinkedIn composer — it doesn't publish for you and can't schedule. Paste and post from there.
</Warning>

<Tip>
If you change sources after drafting, Create tells you the draft is out of date. **Redraft** rebuilds it from the new sources, so export anything you want to keep first.
</Tip>

## Templates

Six starting points cover the usual jobs: recap your latest event, highlight reel, speaker spotlight, key takeaways carousel, thought-leadership brief, and post-event thank-you. Above them, **Suggested for you** offers starting points built from the recordings already in your media library — a clip, a post or a blog from a specific session.

Inside an event there's also a **Templates** link for carousel *design* templates, which control how slides look rather than what they say.

## Where drafts go

Everything you write stays in its Create project, listed under **Recent projects** on the Create home; the menu on a project card opens, renames or deletes it. Written formats also appear in [Posts](/marketing/posts), which is a library view of the same projects. Video projects live with your [clips](/marketing/clips) instead.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it make things up?">
    Drafts are grounded in the sources you select and cite them. Fewer sources means thinner grounding — if a draft feels generic, add sessions or recordings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use it without any recordings?">
    Yes, a topic alone will produce a draft. It just won't be grounded in your content, which is most of the value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a recording greyed out in Sources?">
    It has no finished transcript. Only transcribed media can ground a draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it use our brand voice?">
    Yes, when a [brand kit](/marketing/brand-kits) with a baked voice applies to the event.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
