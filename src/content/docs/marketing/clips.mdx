Clips turn a long recording into short videos built for social. AI proposes the moments worth cutting, you adjust the boundaries, and export in the aspect ratio and caption style you need.

<Note>
Clips are part of the Marketing module, which is currently Early Access.
</Note>

## Create a clip
Clips are cut from a recording, so start in Media.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a recording with a finished transcript">
    Go to **Marketing → Media** and open the item. Clip suggestions need a completed transcript.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click **Edit & clip**. The recording opens in the Create video editor: the transcript on the left, the player in the middle, and the **Chapters & clips** panel on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate suggestions">
    Under **Suggested clips**, click **Generate** (or **Regenerate** to redo them). AI proposes around six moments, each with a title, the quote it is built around and a one-line reason. **Copilot** opens the clip copilot in the Team Lead panel if you would rather describe the clips you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and adjust">
    **Preview** plays the suggested range; **Trim** opens start and end controls so you can move the boundaries. To cut a video by hand instead, use **Edit by transcript** on the left: **Remove** the lines you don't want (or **Remove fillers** for the ums and ahs), then **Export edited video** stitches what is left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Pick the aspect ratio above the list — **Original**, **Vertical · 9:16** or **Square · 1:1** — and the captions: **No captions**, **Clean**, **Bold**, **Boxed**, or one of the **Animated** styles (colored, scaling, background). Then click **Export** on the clip. Rendering runs in the background and the card shows **Rendering…** until it's done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Finished clips appear under **Exported clips** at the bottom of the panel, on the recording's **AI artifacts** tab, and in **Media → Clips**.

<Tip>
Use 9:16 with Bold or Karaoke captions for LinkedIn and other feeds. Most people watch with the sound off, so burned-in captions do the work.
</Tip>

## The clips library

**Media → Clips** lists your exported clips as cards with their length and title. The tabs filter by origin — **All**, **AI**, **Yours**, **Imported** — and **Search clips…** finds one by title. A clip appears here once its export has finished; a suggestion that has never been exported only exists in the editor's **Suggested clips** list. Opening a clip shows its own page with the player and the same **Edit & clip** and **More actions** as a recording; to adjust the cut and re-export, go back to the source recording's editor.

<Tip>
Search by title is the quickest way back to a particular clip. Every clip you export is filed under **Yours**, whether you cut it by hand or started from an AI suggestion.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many suggestions do I get?">
    Around six per run, and you can generate again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I generate clips on this recording?">
    It has no finished transcript. Suggestions are chosen from transcript content, so transcription has to complete first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my own captions or branding?">
    Caption style is chosen at export. Logo watermarking and clip styling come from your [brand kit](/marketing/brand-kits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does export take?">
    It renders in the background — minutes for a short clip. The card shows **Rendering…** until it's ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my clip rejected or marked failed?">
    A clip's time range must fall inside the recording — a range past the end of the source is rejected with "Clip range is outside the source duration" before anything renders. A render that would produce an empty file fails with a reason on the clip card instead of appearing ready with a broken download.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
