Advocacy already shows who shared and who clicked. Conversion tracking shows what happened next: who landed on your page, who clicked your call to action (CTA) and who completed your goal, credited to the advocate and the channel they shared on. Everything is reported inside the campaign's **Analytics** tab in Sessionboard; nothing is sent to Google Analytics or another tool.

## Why use it

Clicks show that people were interested; conversions show what advocacy was worth. With one tag on your site, every registration, purchase or booking that follows an advocate's link is credited to that advocate and the channel they shared on, so you can prove the return on your campaign and reward the people who actually filled the room.

## What counts as a conversion

A **conversion** is the goal you want people to reach after they click an advocate's link, within the attribution window: registering, buying a ticket, booking a meeting. It is counted when:

- someone completes a registration or a sign-up on a Sessionboard page (registration forms, Sites, event and webinar pages);
- someone reaches a page you marked as a conversion page, such as your thank-you page; or
- your page calls `sessionboard.track('conversion', …)`.

Each order or registration number counts once, so a reloaded thank-you page never counts twice.

A **CTA click** is a click on a button or link you care about, such as **Register** or **Buy tickets**. A **landing** is a click that reached a page with the website tag (or a Sessionboard page).

## How it works

Every share link adds a click id (`sb_click`) to your page's address, next to the UTM parameters. The id names the campaign, the advocate and the channel. The Sessionboard website tag on your site keeps the id on your domain for the attribution window and reports the landing, CTA clicks and conversions against it. Sessionboard pages do the same without any code.

## Sessionboard pages need nothing

When a campaign's links go to a Sessionboard registration form, Site, or event or webinar page, landings, clicks on **Register** and completed registrations or sign-ups are recorded automatically. The campaign's **Conversion tracking** card says **Nothing to install**.

## Set up conversion tracking on your own website

Open the campaign and expand **Conversion tracking** (it is also on the **Share page** step of Quick Launch).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the website tag">
    Copy the **Website tag** and paste it into the `<head>` of the page your links point to and of your thank-you page. With a tag manager, add it as a **Custom HTML** tag that fires on all pages. It is one tag for all of Sessionboard: it loads in the background and never slows down or breaks the page. Your organization has one tag; if Marketing's website tag is already on your site, it is the same line and you do not need a second one.

    ![The website tag with its Copy button and Send to a developer](/images/kb/advocacy-website-tag-snippet.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Sessionboard what to count">
    Under **CTA buttons and links**, name each CTA and match it by **CSS selector** (for example `.btn-register`) or by **Link address contains** (for example `/register`). Under **Conversion pages**, name the goal and enter part of the thank-you page's address. Set the **Attribution window (days)**: a CTA click or conversion counts if it happens within that many days of clicking the shared link (1 to 90, default 30). Click **Save tracking settings**.

    These settings belong to your organization's website tag, so they apply to every campaign that links to the same site.

    ![CTA buttons and links, conversion pages and the attribution window](/images/kb/advocacy-website-tag-rules.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the installation">
    See [Check installation](#check-installation).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Record CTAs and conversions from your own code

When a conversion is not a page (a purchase confirmed in a modal, a booking widget), call this after it succeeds:

```js
sessionboard.track('conversion', {
  name: 'Registration',  // shown in your analytics
  value: 499,            // optional
  currency: 'USD',       // optional, three letters
  id: 'ORDER-123'        // optional: your order or registration number, counted once
});
```

For a button: `sessionboard.track('cta', { name: 'Register' })`.

The tag loads asynchronously. If your page may call `sessionboard.track` before it has loaded, add the one-line queue snippet shown under **Record CTAs and conversions from your own code** before the tag; the calls are replayed when it loads.

A conversion named **Registration** also counts as a referred registration for [Advocacy rewards](/marketing/advocacy) and the **Registrations** column.

## Check installation

Under **3. Check installation**, enter the page your share links go to and click **Check installation**. Open the test link: it is your page with `?sb_test=` added. The card shows, live:

- **Pass** — the tag loaded on the page, with how many elements each CTA matched and any CTA clicks or conversions the page sent while the test was open. Click your CTA on the test page to see it arrive.
- **Fail** — the reason: the tag didn't report (it is not on the page, the tag manager isn't published, or an ad blocker stopped it), or a tag from a different Sessionboard account is on the page.

A CTA that is not found on the page is listed so you can check its selector. Test visits never count in your results and set no cookie. The test link works for 30 minutes.

![A passed installation check, with both CTAs found on the page and a click on Register recorded](/images/kb/advocacy-check-installation-pass.png)

## Send to a developer

If someone else edits your website, click **Send to a developer** and enter up to five addresses and an optional note. Sessionboard emails the snippet, the steps and your CTA and conversion setup, with a link to a setup page that shows whether the tag is working yet. Replies come to you, and the people you send it to are not added to your contacts.

![Send setup instructions for the Sessionboard website tag](/images/kb/advocacy-send-to-developer.png)

The same **Send setup instructions to someone technical** action is on every technical setup: custom domains for Sites and pages (Settings → Domains) and email sending domains (SPF and DKIM records). Each email links to a read-only setup page with the records or code, copy buttons and the live status. Setup pages work for 30 days.

![The setup page a developer opens, with the status, the steps and the website tag with a Copy button](/images/kb/setup-instructions-page.png)

## Read the Conversions section

On the campaign's **Analytics** tab, **Conversions** shows:

- **Conversions**, **Conversion rate** (conversions ÷ clicks) and **Value** (per currency). **What counts as a conversion?** explains how they are counted.

  ![Conversions, conversion rate and value](/images/kb/advocacy-conversions-stats.png)

- **Funnel** — shares → clicks → landings → CTA clicks → conversions, with the rate of each step. Clicks are people, not page loads; bots are left out.

  ![The funnel from shares to conversions](/images/kb/advocacy-conversions-funnel.png)

- **Top advocates by conversions** — every advocate's clicks, landings, CTA clicks, conversions, rate and value. Sort any column and **Export CSV**.

  ![Top advocates by conversions](/images/kb/advocacy-conversions-by-advocate.png)

- **By channel**, **CTA clicks by name** and **Conversions by name**.

Until anything has been tracked, the section says **No conversions tracked yet** with a **Set up conversion tracking** button that opens the setup card.

## Privacy

- The tag stores only the share click's id on your domain (a first-party `sb_click` cookie), never a name or email address. It reads no form fields.
- It follows your consent banner: `window.sbConsent`, OneTrust, Cookiebot and Google Consent Mode, and Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track mean no. For organizations hosted in the EU, and for visitors in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, nothing is stored or sent until the visitor consents. A withdrawal deletes the stored id.
- Without a click id from a share link, the tag records nothing for Advocacy.
- An order id that looks like an email address is dropped.

  <Accordion title="My registrations happen on another platform (Cvent, Swoogo, Eventbrite…)">
    Add the website tag to the registration platform's pages through its custom header or tag manager field, and mark its confirmation page as a conversion page (or call `sessionboard.track('conversion', …)` there).   <Accordion title="Why is a conversion missing?">
    It happened after the attribution window, the visitor declined consent, the page didn't have the tag, or the visitor didn't arrive from a share link (no click id). Run **Check installation** on the conversion page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the attribution window later?">
    Yes. The window applies to events received after the change; conversions already counted stay counted.
