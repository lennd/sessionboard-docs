A team campaign is for your own staff: the **Your team** side of [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy). You package event content into a schedule of posts — LinkedIn posts, quote cards, clips — pick a goal, and send it to sales, membership or marketing through Slack, Microsoft Teams and email. Each teammate posts to their own LinkedIn from a personal [share kit](/marketing/dispatch-share-kit), and in tracked mode you get a leaderboard instead of a guess about who actually posted. Team campaigns sit beside your event community's campaigns in **Marketing → Advocacy → Campaigns**.

<Note>
Team campaigns are part of [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy), which every organization has: choose **Your team** when you start a campaign. There is nothing to request or switch on. Any organization can connect Slack and Microsoft Teams to deliver them, and email works without either.
</Note>

Advocacy's two kinds of campaign point in opposite directions. Event community campaigns activate people outside your organization — participants, sponsors, exhibitors, attendees. Team campaigns equip people inside it.

## Why use it

Sending colleagues a clip and "please share" in Slack gets a few reposts and no idea who did what. A team campaign gives every teammate the same short brief, a schedule that sends itself, copy they can make their own and a one-tap post, and records each post against their name, so you can see who has shared and nudge only the people who haven't.

## Before you start

- **Slack** or **Microsoft Teams** connected, if you want to deliver to channels or people there. Every organization can connect both; the recipient picker links to **Connect Slack** when it is not connected yet. Email alone works without either. For Teams, your Teams admin adds the Sessionboard app in Microsoft Teams once; people who added the app, and channels of teams it was added to, can then be picked.
- Team members in your organization, so the recipient picker can find them.
- A **destination link** if you're running in tracked mode.

## Create a team campaign

Go to **Marketing → Advocacy**, click **New campaign**, choose **Your team** under **Who's sharing it?** and click **Continue**. To create one for the whole organization rather than one event, use **New team campaign** in the **Your team** section of the organization's **Marketing → Advocacy** page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="What's the goal?">
    Pick an outcome: **Drive registrations**, **Boost attendance**, **Grow reach & authority**, **Maximize on-demand**, **Drive pipeline**, or **Custom**. This sets the default call to action and which metric is treated as primary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Who's it for?">
    Choose the audience — **Sales**, **Membership**, **Marketing**, or **Custom** — and write the call to action. In tracked mode, add the destination link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Why we're sharing this">
    Write a line or two about what the posts are for and how to post them. It is shown at the top of every teammate's [share kit](/marketing/dispatch-share-kit) and quoted in the email they get.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What goes in">
    Name the campaign and choose the asset types to include: **LinkedIn post**, **Quote card**, **Clip**, **Audiogram**, **Carousel**, **Blog snippet**, or **Talking points**. At least one is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The posts">
    Turn on **Generate a posting schedule** to lay out posts at T-21, T-14, T-7 and T-1 days before the event. Edit the copy and asset for each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How it reaches them">
    Choose **Deliver to share (tracked)** or **Deliver to use (untracked)**, then add Slack channels, Slack people, **Microsoft Teams channels**, **Microsoft Teams people** and email recipients. You can also generate a public link, which reports in aggregate only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create team campaign**, or **Send test** first to send it to yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The **Source** dropdown on the "What goes in" step points you at the material to draw from — this event's content, a recording, a Content Plan, or the Quotes Library. Write the copy and attach the assets for each post in the post editor, which is what the campaign sends.
</Tip>

## How posts go out

Once the campaign is **Scheduled** or **Active**, each post goes out on its own when it's due:

- A post with a date goes out on that date.
- A **T-N** post goes out N days before the event's first day, at 9:00 in the event's time zone.
- A **Day N** post is counted from the day the first post went out.

Each post is sent once. A post that is more than a day late — for example, because you launched the campaign after its date — is not sent on its own; send it with **Send now**.

Each teammate gets a direct message in Slack or Microsoft Teams, when they're there, **and** an email when you have their address. Channels get a post that tells people to look for their own share kit. See [Post from your team share kit](/marketing/dispatch-share-kit).

## Send and follow up

Open the campaign from the Campaigns list to see its schedule timeline.

- **Send now** delivers the next pending post right away. If that post's asset isn't set up yet, the dialog says **Finish "*post*" before sending** and offers **Edit the post** instead; nothing is sent.
- Each scheduled row shows how many teammates have shared it (for example *1/1 shared*, then **All shared**), and lets you nudge people who haven't shared yet, mark the post sent, or edit its copy.
- **Send test** delivers to just you, which is worth doing before the first real send.
- **My share kit** opens your own kit when you're on the recipient list.

## Pause and resume

Click **Pause sharing** on the campaign (or in the **⋯** menu on the Campaigns list). The campaign shows **Paused**: scheduled posts wait, and **Send now** still works. **Resume sharing** returns it to **Active** if something has been sent already, otherwise to **Scheduled**, and due posts go out on the next run.

![A paused team campaign — the Paused status, Resume sharing in the header, and the banner Paused. Scheduled posts won't go out until you resume. Send now still works.](/images/kb/dispatch-paused-banner.png)

## Track performance

Tracked campaigns show **Clicks**, **Shares** and **Conversions**, with the metric matching your chosen goal highlighted and progress shown as "*N* to go" until you hit the target. **Top performers** ranks your team by shares.

Team campaigns also count in the event's **Advocacy → Analytics** and **Activity** tabs, beside your event community campaigns; their rows open the team campaign.

Untracked campaigns show no metrics at all — that's the trade-off for a simpler kit. You can switch a deliver-only campaign to tracked later.

## Duplicate or delete

- **Duplicate** copies the campaign, its posts and its **Why we're sharing this**, ready for another event.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm; a campaign that has been sent also asks you to type `DELETE`, and your team stops getting its posts. The toast offers **Undo**. See [Delete a campaign](/marketing/advocacy#delete-a-campaign).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did the old team campaigns page go?">
    Team campaigns are in **Marketing → Advocacy → Campaigns**, filtered with **Your team**. Old links to the separate team campaigns page open that list; at the organization level, they open the **Your team** section of the Advocacy page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do recipients need a Sessionboard account?">
    No. The Slack or Teams message or the email opens their personal share kit, and they post from there. See [Post from your team share kit](/marketing/dispatch-share-kit).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between tracked and deliver-only?">
    Tracked gives every recipient a personal link, which produces click and share attribution plus the leaderboard. Deliver-only just hands over the content with no measurement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Slack or Teams channels?">
    Slack isn't connected, or the Sessionboard app hasn't been added in Microsoft Teams. The picker says **Slack isn't connected** with a **Connect Slack** link, or **Microsoft Teams isn't connected** / **Add the app to a team first**. Email delivery still works in the meantime.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A scheduled post didn't go out">
    Check that the campaign isn't **Paused**, that the post's asset is set up, and that the post isn't more than a day late. Late posts wait for **Send now**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
