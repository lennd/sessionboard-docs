<Note>
Dispatch needs its own Early Access flag on top of the Marketing module. If you see the **Dispatches are in Early Access** card, click **Request access**.
</Note>

Advocacy and Dispatch look similar but point in opposite directions. Advocacy activates people outside your organization — speakers, sponsors, exhibitors. Dispatch equips people inside it.

## Before you start

- **Slack connected**, if you want to deliver to channels or people there. Email alone works without it.
- Team members in your organization, so the recipient picker can find them.
- A **destination link** if you're running in tracked mode.

## Build a dispatch
Go to **Marketing → Dispatch** and click **New Dispatch**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="What's the goal?">
    Pick an outcome: **Drive registrations**, **Boost attendance**, **Grow reach & authority**, **Maximize on-demand**, **Drive pipeline**, or **Custom**. This sets the default call to action and which metric is treated as primary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Who's it for?">
    Choose the audience — **Sales**, **Membership**, **Marketing**, or **Custom** — and write the call to action. In tracked mode, add the destination link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What goes in">
    Name the dispatch and choose the asset types to include: **LinkedIn post**, **Quote card**, **Clip**, **Audiogram**, **Carousel**, **Blog snippet**, or **Talking points**. At least one is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The posts">
    Optionally turn on a posting schedule, which lays out posts at T-21, T-14, T-7, and T-1 days. Edit the copy and asset for each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How it reaches them">
    Choose **Deliver to share (tracked)** or **Deliver to use (untracked)**, then add Slack channels, Slack people, and email recipients. You can also generate a public link, which reports in aggregate only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create dispatch**, or **Send test** first to send it to yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The **Source** dropdown on the "What goes in" step points you at the material to draw from — this event's content, a recording, a Content Plan, or the Quotes Library. Write the copy and attach the assets for each post in the post editor, which is what the dispatch sends.
</Tip>

## Send and follow up
Open a dispatch to see its schedule timeline.

- **Send now** or **Send to team** delivers the next pending item over Slack and email.
- Each scheduled row lets you nudge people who haven't shared yet, mark an item sent, or edit its copy.
- **Send test** delivers to just you, which is worth doing before the first real send.

## Track performance
Tracked dispatches show **Clicks**, **Shares**, and **Conversions**, with the metric matching your chosen goal highlighted and progress shown as "*N* to go" until you hit the target.

**Top performers** ranks your team by shares.

Untracked dispatches show no metrics at all — that's the trade-off for a simpler kit. You can switch a deliver-only dispatch to tracked later.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do recipients need a Sessionboard account?">
    No. They receive the kit in Slack or email and share from there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between tracked and deliver-only?">
    Tracked gives every recipient a personal link, which produces click and share attribution plus the leaderboard. Deliver-only just hands over the content with no measurement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reuse a dispatch for another event?">
    Yes. Open it and choose **Duplicate**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Slack channels?">
    Slack isn't connected. The picker shows **Slack isn't connected** with a **Connect Slack** link. Email delivery still works in the meantime.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
