Image and Animated campaigns give every advocate a personalized card. The **Media** format hands them your own content instead: a session recording, a clip, an audiogram, a slide video or a quote card from your Media library. Everyone gets the same items, each with their own tracked link, so shares, clicks and registrations are credited exactly as for a card.

<Gallery />

## Why use it

Your best promotion is often something you already made: the keynote's sharpest minute, a quote card from a speaker, a slide video of last year's session. The Media format puts those in your speakers', exhibitors' and attendees' hands, posted natively as a video or an image rather than as a bare link, and still tells you who brought whom.

## What you can share

| Type | From |
|---|---|
| **Videos** | Recordings in your Media library, or a video you upload |
| **Clips**, **Audiograms**, **Slide videos** | Clips you made in Media |
| **Quote cards** | Your Quotes library (posted as a square image) |

An item can be shared once its file is ready and it isn't archived. A campaign holds up to 50 items.

## Start a Media campaign in Quick Launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the Media format">
    In **Marketing → Advocacy → New campaign → Your event community**, choose who's sharing, then pick **Media · From your library** under **Format**. The Designer and card styles step aside: Media isn't personalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose media to share">
    Click **Choose from Media, or upload**. **Choose media to share** lists your library: search by title, quote or speaker, filter by **Type**, switch **Library** between **This event** and **All your media**, sort by **Newest**, **Oldest**, **Longest** or **Title A–Z**, and **Export** the list as CSV. Each item shows where it is **Shared in** already, and warns when it is over a channel's video limits. Tick as many as you like and click **Use N items**.

    ![Choose media to share — search, All types, This event, Newest and Export, Upload a video, and one picked video](/images/kb/advocacy-media-picker.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or upload a video">
    **Upload a video** takes an MP4 or MOV up to 500 MB and 3 minutes long; a longer or larger file is refused with the reason before it uploads. Square, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 all work. The video goes into your Media library, gets its transcript and captions as usual, and is picked as soon as it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the items">
    **Media to share** lists what you picked, with **Add or change media** to go back to the picker. Use the up and down arrows to set the order advocates see, and the cross to remove an item (it stays in your Media library). The preview on the right shows **What every advocate gets**.

    ![The Media format selected, with Media to share listing one picked video, its move and remove buttons, and the upload limits](/images/kb/advocacy-media-rail.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish as usual">
    Recipients, Share console, Landing and Launch work as for any campaign. A Media campaign can't be activated with nothing picked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share with advocates from Media, Clips or Quotes

You can also start from the content. In **Media**, **Clips** (audiograms and slide videos included) or **Quotes**, at the event or organization level, tick the items and click **Share with advocates**:

1. **Where should they go?** — **New campaign** creates a Media campaign with these items picked and opens Quick Launch at Recipients, so you confirm who shares and launch. **Add to a campaign** adds them to a Media campaign already on the event; advocates in it see the new items right away.
2. **Who shares them?** — **Participants**, **Groups** or **Attendees**.
3. At the organization level, **Pick the event this is for**.
4. Click **Create campaign**, or **Add N items** to add them to the campaign you picked.

![The Share with advocates sheet — Where should they go? with New campaign selected and Add to a campaign, and Who shares them? with Participants, Groups and Attendees](/images/kb/advocacy-media-share-with-advocates.png)

Each item in the library shows **Shared in** with the campaigns it is part of.

## What advocates see

On their share page, advocates get **Pick something to share**: a gallery of the campaign's items to play or preview. They pick one, edit **Your post** (their link is already in it), and share:

- **Post the video to LinkedIn** posts the file natively as a LinkedIn video (up to 200 MB and 10 minutes); a quote card posts as an image (**Post the card to LinkedIn**). Anything else posts the text with their link. The first time, **Connect LinkedIn to post it** connects their account; **LinkedIn link** shares just the link instead. LinkedIn must be one of the campaign's share channels.
- **Share…** on a phone opens the share sheet with the file.
- **Download** saves the file and copies the caption with their link, ready to paste wherever they post it.
- **Copy link** and the campaign's other channels share the link. When someone shares an item, its link shows that item's poster as the preview image.

![An advocate's media gallery — the video with its player, Your post with the tracked link, and Download, Copy link and LinkedIn link](/images/kb/advocacy-media-advocate-gallery.png)

Buttons follow the channels you turned on under **Share channels**. An item over a channel's video limit (for example X takes 2 minutes 20 seconds, WhatsApp 16 MB) shows a warning to share the link there instead.

Results land in the campaign's Analytics like any other share, with each share recorded against the item.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the media personalized for each advocate?">
    No. Everyone shares the same files. What's personal is the link in their post, so every click and registration is credited to them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch an existing card campaign to Media?">
    Pick the format while you build the campaign. Switching back to Image or Animated in Quick Launch restores your card settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see all my media in the picker?">
    **This event** shows the event's media; switch **Library** to **All your media**. If your role only covers the event, the picker says to ask an organization admin for the rest.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
