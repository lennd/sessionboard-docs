A poster gallery turns the posters already uploaded to your sessions into a public, searchable page you can hand out as one link. Visitors browse by track or format, open a poster in the browser — PDF, image, video, and audio all render inline — and see it attributed to its session and authors.

<Note>
Poster Gallery is an Early Access **Beta**: request access per event from [Early Access](/get-started/early-access) and our team will switch it on for you. Once enabled, the gallery admin lives under **CMS → Poster Gallery**.
</Note>

## Where posters come from

A gallery does not hold its own uploads. It collects session content of type **Poster** from the sessions you point it at, so the workflow your speakers already know — uploading to their session, usually through the portal — is what fills the gallery. Only the latest version of each poster shows, and draft or withdrawn sessions are excluded automatically.

No poster file yet for some sessions? The **Poster Builder** can draft one from the session's abstract — see below.

## Create a gallery

<Steps>

<Step title="Open CMS → Poster Gallery">

The page counts how many posters are available in this event. Click **New Gallery** to create one and open the builder.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the content">

On the **Content** tab, pick the **Session formats** and **Tracks** that feed the gallery — leave everything unchecked to include all of them. **Matching posters** previews exactly what a visitor will see, and **Poster coverage** splits the selected sessions into **Uploaded** and **Missing** so you can chase the gaps from one list.

</Step>

<Step title="Brand it">

On **Appearance**, set the **Public title** and **Description**, pick a **Theme** and **Accent color**, and add a **Logo** and **Banner** — you can reuse the event's own assets. A **Gallery sponsor** slot (label, logo, link) is there if a sponsor paid for the wall.

</Step>

<Step title="Decide what visitors can do">

**Behavior** holds the visitor-facing toggles — **Search**, **Allow downloads**, **Share actions**, **Bookmarks**, **Add to calendar**, and **Ask the author** — plus how the gallery is grouped (**Track**, **Session**, or none), the **Default sort**, and which **Filter chips** visitors get.

</Step>

<Step title="Set access, then go Live">

On **Access & Compliance**, choose who can view (see below), then flip the **Live** switch in the header. **Copy public link** gives you the URL to publish; **Preview** shows the gallery as a visitor before you share it.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
The header's **Share & print** menu has a **Download QR** for the whole gallery and **QR labels for poster boards** — one printable label per poster, so the physical poster hall links back to the online one.
</Tip>

## Who can view it

| Access mode | Who gets in |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone with the link. |
| **Share link only** | The public URL is off; only the share links you create work. Each has a name, an optional password, and can be deactivated on its own. |
| **Gated — verified email** | Visitors verify their email first; optionally restrict to **Allowed email domains**. |

## Embargo and author consent

Both live on **Access & Compliance**:

- **Embargo until** holds posters back until a date and time in the event's timezone. **Teaser** mode shows each poster's title with a countdown while the file itself stays locked; **Hide** shows nothing until the embargo lifts.
- **Require author consent** publishes only posters whose author accepted your **Consent statement** — presented as a required checkbox when they upload a poster in the portal. The Content tab's coverage warns how many selected sessions still lack consent.

## The visitor experience

Visitors search across posters, authors, and topics, filter by track and format, sort by title, first author, program number, or session time, and keep a browser-local **My list** of bookmarks. Opening a poster shows the file inline with its session, authors, an AI summary and author narration where you added them, a citation, a per-poster link and QR code — and, if enabled, downloads, calendar, and an **Ask the author a question** box. Questions are collected for moderation on the gallery's **Analytics** tab; they are not emailed to the author automatically.

**Analytics** also tracks opens, dwell time, and the searches that found nothing — the fastest read on which topics your audience wanted and could not find.

## Draft posters from abstracts

For sessions with no poster file, the Content tab's **Create drafts from abstracts** (or **Draft with AI**) opens the **Poster Builder**: a block-based editor (sections, key findings, charts, figures, references) with a title, a two- or three-column **Layout**, and an accent color. **Publish to gallery** renders it to PDF and adds it to the same content pool as uploaded posters — **Republish** after edits.
