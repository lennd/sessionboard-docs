It isn't a separate distribution tool bolted on the side. It runs on the same speaker, session, and event data you already manage in Sessionboard, which is why a clip knows who said it and a share link knows which speaker it belongs to.

<Note>
Every event gets **Marketing** in the sidebar with the overview, [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy), [Live Transcribe](/marketing/live-transcribe), [Media](/marketing/media-library), [Create](/marketing/create), and [Slides](/marketing/slides). The rest of the module — voices, stories, clips, quotes, posts, and brand kits — is an Early Access beta; the overview page shows only what your organization has. See [Early Access](/get-started/early-access).
</Note>

## The event overview

**Marketing > Overview** is the module home for one event. Its sidebar groups the pages by what they do: **Promote** (Advocacy, and with the Marketing beta, Dispatch and Plan), **Source & produce** (Transcribe, Media, Create, Slides, plus Voices, Posts, Quotes, Clips and Brand with the beta). A page that is turned off for your organization is left out of the sidebar and the overview alike, so what you see here is what you have.

The page itself has four parts, top to bottom:

- **Quick actions** — one button per job: **Capture a session**, **Upload media**, **Create content**, **Plan a month**, **Send a dispatch**, **Launch advocacy**. Each opens the page where that work happens; hovering one shows a short card explaining what it is for. **API reference** and **Live Transcribe settings** sit above them.
- **Content Journey** — live counts for this event: **Sessions captured** (sessions with a transcript), **Media items** (the library), then **Shares**, **Clicks** and **Conversions** from Advocacy. It is the one-glance answer to "is anything moving?".
- **Review queue** — everything AI generated for this event that still needs a decision. Recaps and reports that are generating, failed or in draft are listed with their status; **Approve** moves a draft on, **Publish** makes an approved recap live for attendees, and **All recaps** opens the full list. Beside them are the event's newest Create drafts and clips. The queue only appears when there is something in it.
- **The surface map** — a card for every page in the module, grouped **Source & produce** and **Distribute**, with a one-line description of what each does. A card opens the same page as its sidebar entry.

Training videos for the Marketing module sit at the foot of the page.

## The organization overview

**Marketing > Overview** at the organization level is the cross-event picture. It repeats the loop — **Source & produce**, then **Distribute**, and the line that every result feeds the next cycle — and gives four totals across all your events: **Media items**, **Hours of content**, **Speakers attributed** (speakers with content attached to them in the library) and **Creators activated** (speakers who have published from their Creator console).

The cards below are the organization-wide pages. **Live Transcribe** is marked **Per event** because capture only happens inside an event; **Advocacy** opens the organization roll-up of every event's campaigns (see [Across events](/marketing/advocacy#across-events)). The other pages — Media, Create, Posts, Quotes, Clips, Brand, Plan and Dispatch — appear once your organization has the Marketing beta and the organization-wide Media library, each behind its own switch.

## How it fits together

The three phases of an event each feed the next.

**Before** — speakers submit bios and materials through their portal, and your AV team gets everything they need in the [Live Transcribe Console](/marketing/live-transcribe-console) without needing accounts.

**During** — [Live Transcribe](/marketing/live-transcribe) captures each session from the soundboard and produces a speaker-attributed transcript, an AI summary, and extracted themes within minutes of the session ending.

**After** — the [Media library](/marketing/media-library) holds everything, searchable by topic, speaker, or keyword. [Create](/marketing/create) drafts content from it, [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy) puts it in your speakers' hands with tracked links, and last year's content promotes next year's event.

<Tip>
Set up your [brand kit](/marketing/brand-kits) before your first event with Marketing enabled. Voice and visual rules apply at generation time, so a kit added later won't retroactively improve drafts you've already made.
</Tip>

## Where content is attributed

Every transcript, quote, clip, and share links back to the speaker who delivered it. Over time that builds an institutional record of who spoke on what, how it performed, and who your most effective advocates are — searchable across your whole event history rather than trapped in one event's folder.

- Engagement tracking: who shared what and how it performed
- Subject-matter expert discovery by topic across your speaker network
- Cross-event content attribution per speaker

## Common situations

**"Our event ended three weeks ago and we still haven't published anything."** Transcripts and summaries are ready minutes after each session. Clips and quotes come out of the library rather than an edit queue, and Advocacy campaigns launch in days.

**"On event day the AV team asks for speaker files ten minutes before each session."** The Live Transcribe Console gives them assets, schedules, and transcription controls through a magic link — no accounts, no onboarding.

**"We run four events a year and the content ends up in a Dropbox folder nobody revisits."** The Media library centralizes everything, searchable by topic and speaker, so past content promotes future events.

**"We want speakers to share but we never know whether they did."** Advocacy gives each speaker a personal tracked link, so shares and clicks attribute to the individual rather than disappearing.

## Integrations

Marketing is the content layer, not a replacement for your marketing stack.

| Integration | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini | MCP / AI | Query Sessionboard data from your AI tool of choice, no manual exports |
| Goldcast, Zoom, and other webinar platforms | Video ingest | Import recordings and transcripts into the Media library |
| Cvent | Event management | Event data connected and continuously enriched |
| Gleanin & Snöball | Distribution | For organizations with existing contracts; Advocacy replaces these long term |

The MCP integration is read-only and token-scoped, with PII obfuscated by default and a 90-day audit log.
