A reward gives advocates something to share for: VIP lounge access for anyone who brings three people, or a ticket for the top ten. Advocates see their progress on their share page, Sessionboard emails them when they unlock a reward, and you see who earned what. The same counting drives a campaign's results by channel and by advocate.

<Gallery />

## Why use it

Most advocates share once and stop. A goal they can see — "2 more to unlock it" — brings them back, and a contest with a public leaderboard turns your most connected participants and exhibitors into a friendly competition. Because rewards count only what each advocate's own link brings, with self-referrals, duplicates and bots left out, you can promise a prize without worrying about gaming.

## Add a reward

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Rewards card">
    Open the campaign. The **Rewards** card sits under **Reminders**; it is also on the **Landing page** step when you edit the campaign. Click **Add reward**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the type">
    - **Milestone** — everyone whose link reaches the number gets it. Example: bring 3 people, get VIP lounge access.
    - **Contest** — the top sharers when it ends win it. Example: the top 10 by registrations win a ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Name the **Reward** and add **Details (optional)**. Advocates see both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how it's counted">
    **Counted by** is **Registrations their link brought** or **Clicks on their link**. For a milestone, set the **Number to reach** (1 to 10,000). For a contest, set the **Winners** (1 to 100) and when it **Ends**, and choose whether to **Show a leaderboard to advocates** (first names only, on each advocate's share page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the unlock email">
    **Email advocates when they unlock it** is on by default. It is sent once, from the event, with a link to their results. Edit the **Email subject** and **Email message**; you can use `{first_name}`, `{reward}`, `{event}`, `{count}` and `{rank}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save reward**. The reward appears on the card with its type, its number and how many people earned it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the pencil to edit a reward and the bin to remove it. Removing asks first: advocates stop seeing the reward, and who already earned it stays on record.

## What counts

Only what an advocate's own link brings counts:

- **Clicks** count distinct visitors, and clicks from bots are left out.
- **Registrations** are registrations from people who clicked the advocate's link: on Sessionboard registration, or on any page with the [website tag](/marketing/advocacy-conversion-tracking) recording a conversion named **Registration**.
- **Self-referrals don't count.** A registration from the advocate themselves is left out.
- **Duplicate people don't count.** Each person counts once, for whoever brought them first.

An advocate earns each reward once. Rewards are checked after each referred registration, whenever the earners list or a share page opens, and every hour. A contest is settled once, when it ends.

## Who earned rewards

Under the rewards, **Who earned rewards** lists each advocate who earned one, with the **Reward**, their **Email**, the **Count** or **Rank**, when they **Earned** it and whether they were **Emailed**. Search advocates or rewards, filter by reward or emailed status, sort any column, and export the list as CSV.

## What advocates see

On their share page, advocates get **Your results**: "Your link brought N people", their clicks and registrations, a progress bar for each milestone ("2 more to unlock it", then **Unlocked**), and for a contest the end date, their position ("You are #4") and, when you turned it on, the first-name leaderboard. See [Share your event card](/participants/share-your-event-card#see-your-results).

## Results by channel and advocate

A campaign's **Analytics** tab has two tables that use the same counting as rewards: bot clicks, self-referrals and duplicate people are left out.

- **Results by channel** — for each channel advocates shared on: **Shares**, **Advocates**, **Reach** (people who opened the link), **Clicks**, **Registrations** and **Conversion**. Each channel hands out its own tagged link, so a click is credited to the channel it came from. Channels include **Phone share menu**, **Email a colleague**, **Copied link**, **QR code** and **Download**; clicks on an untagged link are **Not tagged**.
- **Top advocates** — every advocate on the campaign, with what their own link brought: **Shares**, **Clicks**, **Registrations**, **Top channel**, **Rewards** and **Last shared**. Filter by **Activity** (**Brought registrations**, **Got clicks**, **Shared**, **Not shared yet**) or by top channel, sort any column, and export CSV.

For landings, CTA clicks and conversions on your own website, see [Track conversions from Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy-conversion-tracking).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a reward after advocates have started earning it?">
    Yes. Edit it from the Rewards card. People who already earned it keep it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the leaderboard show full names?">
    No. Advocates see first names only, and only when you turned on **Show a leaderboard to advocates** for that contest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone registered after clicking an advocate's link but it didn't count">
    Check that the registration happened on Sessionboard registration or on a page with the website tag, that the person isn't the advocate, and that nobody else's link brought them first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
