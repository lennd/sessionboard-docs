When you share clips with speakers, sponsors or your team, each clip gets a public page of its own. The page plays the clip, names the speakers, prints what was said, and gives visitors a button back to your event. You decide what the button does and whether search engines can find the page.

<Note>
Clip pages are part of the Marketing module, which is currently Early Access.
</Note>

## Why use it

A clip posted to a feed is seen once and scrolls away. A clip page gives that clip a home: a link that still works next month, a transcript people and search engines can read, and a button that sends the viewer to your event website. You also see what each clip did, so you know which moments to make more of.

## What is on a clip page

- **Your event's look.** The header uses the event's [brand kit](/marketing/brand-kits): logo, header colour and button colour.
- **The player.** Play, seek, mute, captions and full screen all work with a keyboard. Captions come from the recording's transcript.
- **The speakers.** Names, with each speaker's photo when they have one.
- **Share buttons.** LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Email and **Copy link**.
- **Your button and links.** One button, **Visit site** by default, and up to six links under it.
- **Powered by Sessionboard.** A small link at the bottom of the page.
- **The transcript.** Only the part of the recording the clip covers. Each speaker's turn has a time you can select to jump the player there.

A clip has a page once it has been sent to at least one person with **Share** on the Clips page. A clip you hide from speakers has no page, and its link shows "This clip is not available".

## Set the button, links and download

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Clip page settings">
    Go to **Media → History → Clip sharing** and select **Clip page settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where the settings apply">
    **This event** changes one event. **Default for every event** changes every event in your organization that has no setting of its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the button">
    Enter a **Label** and where it **Goes to**. Leave both empty to keep **Visit site**, which opens the event website. If the event has no website and you set no address, the page shows no button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add links">
    Select **Add a link** for each extra link, such as the agenda or registration. Each needs a label and a full web address starting with `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what visitors can do">
    Turn on **Let visitors download the clip** to add a Download button. Turn off **Let search engines find these clip pages** to keep the event's clip pages out of search results (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. Pages update within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You do not have to set anything. With no settings, a clip page has the **Visit site** button to the event website, no download, and can be found by search engines.

## Let search engines find your clips

Clip pages are built to be read by search engines and AI assistants: the title, speakers and transcript are in the page itself, with a description for link previews and video details in the format search engines ask for.

Search is on unless you turn it off. To keep an event's clip pages out of search, turn off **Let search engines find these clip pages** in **Clip page settings** for that event. To keep every event out unless you say otherwise, turn it off under **Default for every event**.

<Warning>
Because search is on by default, a shared clip's title, the speakers' names and the transcript can appear in public search results. If your speakers have not agreed to their sessions being published, turn search off for the event before you share its clips.
</Warning>

To keep one clip out of search while the rest of the event is indexed, open the clip from the **Clips** page and, under **Sharing**, turn off **Search engines can find this clip**. The page still works for anyone who has the link.

Search engines index pages on their own schedule. A page can take days to appear after a clip is shared, and days to drop out after you turn search off.

## See what each clip did

Open a clip from the **Clips** page. The **Sharing** panel shows, for that clip:

- **Views** of the clip, in people's galleries and on its page.
- **Shares**, with the count for each channel (LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Email and others).
- **Links copied** and **Downloads**.
- **Button clicks**, with clicks on the links under the button counted separately.
- **Visits from shared links**: people who arrived through a link a speaker, sponsor or teammate posted.

To compare clips, go to **Media → History → Clip sharing** and choose **Top clips**. The table adds a **Button clicks** column and lists the channels each clip was shared to. **Export CSV** downloads the same numbers.

<Tip>
When a speaker posts their own link to a clip, the page credits the views, shares and button clicks that follow to that speaker. You see them under **By person**.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can see a clip page?">
    Anyone with the link. No sign-in is needed. The page exists only for clips you have shared and not hidden.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I take a clip page down?">
    Hide the clip. On the **Clips** page, select the clip, open **More actions** and choose **Hide from speakers**. The page stops working, and it is never offered to search engines again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the page if I remove a person from the share?">
    The page stays up, because the clip is still shared with others. The removed person's link no longer credits them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no transcript on my clip page?">
    The page prints the transcript once the recording's transcript is ready. A clip cut from a recording with no transcript shows the player, speakers and buttons only, and has no captions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a speaker have initials instead of a photo?">
    The speaker has no photo on their profile, or the photo was not approved. Add or approve the photo and the page picks it up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own domain for clip pages?">
    Not yet. Clip pages are served from Sessionboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
