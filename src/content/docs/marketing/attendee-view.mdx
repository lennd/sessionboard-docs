The attendee view is a public page for your event's content. Attendees browse the schedule, follow live captions, watch replays, and read the [recaps](/marketing/recaps) you publish — all from one link, with no login.

Configure it under **Marketing → Transcribe → Attendee View**.

<Note>
The attendee view is part of the Marketing module, which is Early Access, and needs **Transcribe** enabled for the event. It is a different thing from the **Attend** product, which handles registration and check-in.
</Note>

## Publish it

The page is private until you turn it on. Flip **Publish attendee view** and anyone with the link can reach it; turn it off and the page returns an unavailable message.

<Warning>
Publishing makes the transcripts and AI summaries of **every captured session** in the event publicly readable, not just the ones you highlight. Read [what becomes public](#what-becomes-public) before you switch it on.
</Warning>

Recaps and reports are the exception: those are published individually from [Recaps](/marketing/recaps), so the band only shows what you have explicitly approved and published. Session podcasts work the same way.

## What attendees see

The landing page opens on your event branding — logo, background image, and dates — with a searchable session browser underneath, split into **Past**, **Live**, and **Upcoming**. Attendees can switch between grid, day, and room views. The top navigation carries **Sessions**, **Speakers**, and **Media**.

Two bands appear when there's something to put in them:

- **Live now** — sessions currently being captured, with rolling recaps across every room.
- **Recaps & reports** — the recaps you have published.

Opening a session depends on where it sits in the schedule:

| Session state | What opens |
|---|---|
| Live | Live stage — real-time captions, insights, reactions, and comments |
| Past | Replay — recording audio with synced captions |
| Upcoming | Session detail — schedule, room, description, and speakers |

A session's own page carries whatever exists for it: speakers, sponsors, the AI recap, highlights, the full transcript, attachments, and a podcast recap if you published one. Attendees can download the summary as a PDF or a LinkedIn-ready image.

There's also an **Ask this event** assistant that answers questions grounded in the event's transcripts.

The **Speakers** directory lists everyone marked public. A speaker set to non-public is left out of it.

## Share it

Everything you need is under **Share & embed** in the settings.

- **Link** — the public URL. **Copy** it, or **Open** it to check the page.
- **QR code** — point a phone at it. Good for signage, badges, and slides.
- **Embed code** — an iframe you can drop into your own event website.

Two more surfaces are worth knowing about:

- **Venue signage screen** — a separate URL built for a monitor in the venue, rotating through live stages with a join QR code.
- **Live audio listen-in** — rebroadcasts room audio to attendees on the page.

<Warning>
Turn on live audio listen-in only with speaker and venue consent. It rebroadcasts what is said in the room to anyone holding the link.
</Warning>

## What becomes public

Once published, anyone with the link can read:

- Every session in the event, with schedule, room, and description
- Full transcripts and AI summaries for every captured session
- Session attachments and recordings
- Public speakers, with their photo, title, company, and social links
- Attendee comments and reactions left on live and replay pages
- Recaps, reports, and podcasts **you have published**

The link is unguessable rather than secret — treat it as public the moment you share it.

To keep a session out, control it at capture time rather than after: mark the session **Do Not Record** or **Licensed Content — No Transcription** so there's no transcript to expose. Speakers can also set their own consent preferences in the Speaker Portal. To keep a speaker out of the directory, set them to non-public.

<Tip>
Publish after your first day rather than before your first session. You get to see exactly what attendees will see, with real content in it, before the link goes anywhere.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do attendees need an account?">
    No. The page is public to anyone with the link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own domain?">
    The page is served from your Sessionboard app URL with the event's identifier in the path. Share it with a QR code or a link from your event site, or embed it in a page on your own domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I unpublish?">
    The page and everything behind it stop responding immediately. Republishing restores it with the same link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I show only some sessions?">
    Publishing covers the whole event. Shape what appears by controlling what gets captured — sessions marked Do Not Record have no transcript or summary to show.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does branding follow my brand kit?">
    The page uses the event's logo, background image, and highlight colour. Brand kits apply to recap and report exports.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can attendees download the transcript?">
    Transcripts are readable on the session page and as synced captions on replays. Downloads are offered for the AI summary as a PDF or image, and for published recaps and reports.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
