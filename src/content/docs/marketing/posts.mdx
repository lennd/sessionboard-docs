Posts is the library of written work you've drafted in Create. Filter by format, search across everything, and click any card to reopen it for editing.

<Note>
Posts is part of the Marketing module, which is currently Early Access.
</Note>

## What's in it

Everything drafted in [Create](/marketing/create) except video projects, which live with your [clips](/marketing/clips). Filter by **LinkedIn**, **Blog**, **White paper**, or **Carousel**, or search by name.

Inside an event you see that event's posts. At the organization level you see everything across every event.

## What it doesn't do

Posts is a directory, not a workflow. There's no approve step, no scheduling, and no publishing — clicking a card takes you back into Create, where the editing and export tools live.

<Note>
Sessionboard doesn't publish to LinkedIn on your behalf from anywhere. Create's **Post to LinkedIn** copies your text and opens the composer; distribution through speakers runs via [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a post missing?">
    Video projects are excluded by design — look under Clips. Otherwise check whether you're in an event view, which filters to that event.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I schedule a post from here?">
    No. Schedule the cadence in [Content plan](/marketing/content-plan) and distribute through [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete one?">
    Delete the project from Create.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
