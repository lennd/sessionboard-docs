Recaps turns what [Live Transcribe](/marketing/live-transcribe) captured into finished deliverables: one report for the whole event, a debrief for each day, and a recap for every session. Each one is written from the real transcripts, editable section by section, and reaches attendees only after you approve and publish it.

Find them under **Marketing → Transcribe → Recaps**.

<Note>
Recaps is part of the Marketing module, which is Early Access, and needs **Transcribe** enabled for the event.
</Note>

## The three kinds

The page splits into two zones — **Event recaps** at the top, **Session recaps** in a table below.

| | What it covers | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| **Event report** | The whole event | Executive summary, Key takeaways, Topics unpacked, Looking ahead, LinkedIn caption |
| **Day debrief** | One event day | The same five sections, scoped to that day |
| **Session recap** | A single session | Session summary, Key points, LinkedIn caption |

There is a fourth type, **Session podcast**, an audio recap generated per session. It has its own column in the session table and its own approve and publish step.

## Generate a recap

A session recap is built from that session's AI summary, so the session needs to have been captured with **AI summaries** turned on. The event report and day debriefs need at least one captured session to draw from — the **Generate** button on the event report card stays inactive until then.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick what to generate">
    Click **Generate** on the event report card, on a day card, or on a row in the session table. Select several session rows first to generate them together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it">
    Event reports and day debriefs are queued and show a **Generating** badge while they run — usually a minute or two. Session recaps are built from the summary that already exists, so they appear straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Everything lands as a **Draft**. Click **Open** to read it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the filter chips — **All**, **Captured**, **Needs review**, **Published** — to work through a long session list. **Needs review** is the queue that matters most.

<Tip>
Turn on **Auto-generate recaps** in **Marketing → Transcribe → Settings** and a session recap is drafted whenever a capture stops, with the day debrief drafted the following morning. Drafts only — nothing publishes on its own, and event reports are always generated by hand.
</Tip>

If generation fails, the card shows **Failed** with the reason and a **Retry** button.

## Edit a recap

Opening a recap gives you one card per section. Click **Edit text** to switch a section to a plain text box, make your changes, and click **Save**. There's no autosave, and each save is kept as a version.

Three ways to change the writing:

- Edit it yourself — type directly into any section.
- Ask the copilot — the recap copilot in the Team Lead sidebar takes instructions like "make the summary punchier and cut it to two paragraphs" and proposes a rewrite you can apply or discard.
- **Regenerate** — rebuild from the transcript with an optional instruction. You get a diff to review, and nothing changes until you click **Apply**.

Every section has a character budget, and you'll see a warning when you go over it. **History** lists every version with who changed it and why. Restoring an older version adds it as a new version rather than rewinding, so nothing is lost.

## Approve and publish

Recaps move through **Draft → Approved → Published**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Click **Approve** on a draft you're happy with. Select multiple session recaps in the table to approve them in one go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. The recap goes live on your [attendee view](/marketing/attendee-view) under **Recaps & reports**, and gets its own shareable page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Republish after edits">
    Editing something already published shows an **Unpublished changes** badge. Attendees keep seeing the published version until you click **Publish changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Unpublish** takes a recap back to Approved and hides it from attendees. **Archive** removes it from the working list.

<Warning>
Approve and Publish are unavailable while a recap has unsaved edits. Save first.
</Warning>

Publishing also harvests notable lines into your [Quotes library](/marketing/quotes).

## Export and share

| Export | Event report & day debrief | Session recap |
|---|---|---|
| **PDF** | Yes | Yes |
| **Print PDF** | Yes | — |
| **LinkedIn carousel** | Yes | — |
| **Share card** (1200×630 image) | Yes | Yes |

Exports are generated from the version you're looking at, so a published recap exports what attendees see. A recap that is generating or failed has nothing to export.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a session showing 'Not captured'?">
    That session has no AI summary to build from. Either it wasn't transcribed, or AI summaries were off when it ran. Capture it, or turn on post-session recovery in **Transcribe → Settings** to re-transcribe from the recording.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I generate recaps for a whole event at once?">
    Yes. Select the session rows and use the bulk **Generate**, **Approve**, and **Publish** actions. Each bulk action handles up to 500 sessions at a time. Sessions that already have a recap are skipped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does anything publish automatically?">
    No. Auto-generated recaps are drafts. Publishing is always a deliberate click.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who on my team can work on recaps?">
    Anyone with access to the event.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the sections in a recap?">
    The sections are fixed per recap type, and you edit the content within them. To reshape the writing itself, use Regenerate with an instruction, or the copilot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do attendees see?">
    Only recaps you have published, on the attendee view. Each one also has its own page you can link to directly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
