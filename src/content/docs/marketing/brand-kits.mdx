A brand kit holds your logos, colors, fonts, writing voice and posting rules in one place. Set it up once and every AI draft, event page, email, carousel and clip picks it up, so generated content looks and sounds like you rather than like generic conference copy. Brand kits live under **Marketing → Brand**, for the organization and for each event.

The quickest way to fill a kit is to let Claude or ChatGPT do it: copy one prompt from the kit, paste it into your assistant, and watch each section fill in on the page.

## Why use it

Every email, event page, share card and clip Sessionboard makes for you reads the kit. A complete kit means your content is on-brand from the first draft, without a designer checking each one. Claude or ChatGPT can fill a kit from your website in a few minutes, and you watch every change land.

## What's in a kit

Each part of a kit is a named field, and it is the same field everything else reads:

- **Logos** — **Main logo** (for light backgrounds), **Light logo** (white or reversed, for dark backgrounds) and **Icon** (a square mark), plus optional **Background images** for share cards
- **Colors** — **Primary** for buttons and headers, **Accent** for highlights, and **Background** and **Text**. An empty color uses a neutral default
- **Fonts** — a **Heading font** and a **Body font**, by family name. Google Fonts names work everywhere
- **Voice** — a **Tagline**, how you sound, tone words, the words you use, things to do and to avoid, and example lines
- **Posting rules** — hashtags, accounts to tag, a call to action and default link, emoji use, reading level and UTM source; under **More rules**, a closing line or disclaimer and words never to use
- **Video clips** — caption style, whether clips show your logo, and which sizes to make

The top of the kit shows how many of the eight essentials are set: main logo, primary and accent colors, heading and body fonts, tagline, how you sound, and tone. Everything saves as you go, and the line under the kit name says when it was last saved and by whom.

Kits live at the organization level. Events choose which kit to use.

## Create a kit
Go to **Marketing → Brand**, click **New kit** and give it a name — "Main" or "Awards 2026", for example. Your first kit becomes the organization default automatically.

## Fill a kit with Claude or ChatGPT

Your assistant reads your website and fills in the kit through the Sessionboard connector, one section at a time. You can keep the kit open and watch it happen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Sessionboard to your assistant">
    Do this once. Follow [Connect Claude](/integrations/connect-claude) or [Connect ChatGPT](/integrations/connect-chatgpt), and sign in with your Sessionboard login when asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the prompt">
    Open the kit and click **Fill with Claude or ChatGPT**. Check the **Website** — it is filled in if you have already imported from your site — then click **Copy prompt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your assistant">
    Click **Open Claude** or **Open ChatGPT**, start a chat with the Sessionboard connector turned on, and paste the prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it fill in">
    Keep the kit open. Each section updates as the assistant writes it, the section it just changed is outlined for a moment, and the line under the kit name reads **Updated just now by Claude** (or ChatGPT). When it finishes, the assistant lists what it set and anything it could not find.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The assistant only uses what your brand publishes. It never invents a logo, so upload one yourself if it could not find it. It also leaves fields you have already filled alone unless one is clearly wrong, and tells you first.
</Note>

<Tip>
Connected Sessionboard to Claude or ChatGPT before November 2026? If the assistant says it is not allowed to edit brand kits, disconnect the Sessionboard connector and connect it again once. API keys need the **Fill in Brand kits** access, and a key limited to particular events cannot change a kit, because a kit is shared by every event.
</Tip>

## Fill a kit yourself

- **Import from website** — paste your site's address and click **Import**. Sessionboard reads the colors and fonts from the page and keeps the site as a source for your voice.
- **Learn from website** — under **Voice**, once a website is saved, this writes how you sound, tone, vocabulary, do, avoid and example lines from it. It takes a moment, and you can edit every field afterwards.
- **Upload** each logo in its slot, and set colors with the picker or a hex value such as `#1A5ADB`.

<Tip>
The **Preview** shows a share card and a social post with the kit applied. Check it before rolling the kit out — colors that work on a website often need adjusting for a feed.
</Tip>

## Apply a kit to an event
Go to **Marketing → Brand** inside the event and choose the kit. Events without an explicit kit inherit the organization default, and the card says **Used for this event** (org default) so you know which is in effect.

**Kit actions** on the card opens **Edit kit** and **Set as org default**; an event with its own kit can return to **Use org default**. **New kit** here creates a kit in the organization library and assigns it to this event in one step. Inside a kit, **Where it's used** lists its events; **Add event** assigns it to another one.

## What uses a kit

- **Create** — voice and posting rules are added to every draft
- **Field event pages and emails** — colors, fonts, logos and tagline, through the `{{brand.*}}` tokens
- **Carousels** — colors, fonts, and logos
- **Clips** — caption style, logo, sizes
- **Advocacy** — share card templates

Deleting a kit isn't destructive to those surfaces; they fall back to the default.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Learn from website disabled?">
    It needs a website first. Use **Import from website** at the top of the kit and the button becomes available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I write the voice by hand instead?">
    Yes. Every voice field is editable whether or not Sessionboard has learned it from your website.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The assistant says it can't edit brand kits">
    Disconnect the Sessionboard connector in Claude or ChatGPT and connect it again. Connections made before the assistant could edit brand kits do not include that access until they reconnect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Fill with Claude or ChatGPT">
    It appears once your organization is on the release that includes it. You can still use **Import from website** and fill the kit by hand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can different events have different kits?">
    Yes — that's the point of naming them. Assign per event; anything unassigned uses the org default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are these the same as print document branding?">
    No. [Print agendas](/marketing/print-agendas) use a separate branding setup under **Settings → Branding**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
