## What's in a kit

- **Brand voice** — a summary, tone words, signature vocabulary, things to do, things to avoid, and example passages
- **Visual kit** — colors, fonts, logos, and backgrounds
- **Output rules** — default hashtags, link and UTM source, handles to tag, a default call to action, emoji usage, reading level, boilerplate or disclaimer text, words to avoid, and clip settings for caption style, logo watermark, and aspect ratios

Kits live at the organization level. Events choose which kit to use.

## Create a kit
Go to **Marketing → Brand** and click **New kit**, then give it a name — "Main" or "Awards 2026", for example.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import your visuals">
    Under **Visual kit**, click **Import from website**, paste your site's address and click **Pull** — Sessionboard lifts colors and fonts from the page. Add logos and backgrounds by upload, by pasting a link, from your cloud drive, or by copying one you've used on another kit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a voice source">
    Under **Brand voice**, add a URL that represents how you write — an About page or a strong blog post works well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bake the voice">
    Click **Bake voice**. Sessionboard reads the source and fills in summary, tone, vocabulary, do, avoid, and examples. It takes a moment, and you can edit every field afterwards. Once a voice exists the button reads **Re-bake**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set output rules">
    Add hashtags, a default link and UTM source, handles to tag, a call to action, and any words to avoid. Choose how freely it uses emoji and a reading level, and add boilerplate if every post needs a standard close. Set the clip caption style, logo watermark and aspect ratios if you produce video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your first kit becomes the organization default automatically. Everything auto-saves.

<Tip>
The **Live preview** shows how a clip share card and a LinkedIn post look under the kit. Check it before rolling the kit out — colors that work on a website often need adjusting for a feed.
</Tip>

## Apply a kit to an event
Go to **Marketing → Brand** inside the event and choose the kit. Events without an explicit kit inherit the organization default, and the card says **Used for this event** (org default) so you know which is in effect.

**Kit actions** on the card opens **Edit kit** and **Set as org default**; an event with its own kit can return to **Use org default**. **New kit** here creates a kit in the organization library and assigns it to this event in one step.

## What uses a kit

- **Create** — voice and output rules are prepended to every draft
- **Carousels** — colors, fonts, and logos
- **Clips** — caption style, watermark, aspect ratios
- **Advocacy** — share card templates

Deleting a kit isn't destructive to those surfaces; they fall back to the default.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Bake voice disabled?">
    You need at least one URL source first. Add one under Brand voice and the button becomes available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I write the voice by hand instead?">
    Yes. Every field is editable whether or not you've baked one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can different events have different kits?">
    Yes — that's the point of naming them. Assign per event; anything unassigned uses the org default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are these the same as print document branding?">
    No. [Print agendas](/marketing/print-agendas) use a separate branding setup under **Settings → Branding**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
