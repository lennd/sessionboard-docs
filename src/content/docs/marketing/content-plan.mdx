## Before you start

You need either a **transcribed session** to build from, or a topic you can describe in a sentence. Plans built on a real session are noticeably better, because the generator is grounded in what was actually said rather than working from the topic alone.

## The plan list

**Marketing > Plan** opens on your plans. Each card shows the plan's name, its status (**active** once it has items), its duration, the number of items, the channels it uses and the date it starts from. Click a card to open the plan. **New plan** starts another, and the guide card at the top of the page (**Show guide**) summarizes what Plan does and links on to Advocacy.

## Build a plan
Go to **Marketing > Plan** and click **New plan**. The **New content plan** dialog asks for four things.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Source">
    Pick a captured session, or describe a topic instead. One or the other is required. The session picker only appears when the event has at least one transcribed session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goal">
    Choose **Grow LinkedIn**, **Drive demos**, **Promote an event**, **Thought leadership**, or **Nurture audience**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Duration">
    14, 30, 60, or 90 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Channels">
    Toggle the channels to include — Email, Clip, Blog, Lead magnet, Quote card. LinkedIn is always on and can't be turned off; it's the anchor the cadence is built around.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the plan">
    Click **Build the plan**. Generation takes a moment, then the calendar appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work the plan

The plan opens on its items, dated and in order. Each row carries the date, the title, a channel badge (LinkedIn, Email, Clip, Blog, Lead magnet or Quote card), a status pill, the hook the post opens with, and a line on why that item is in the plan. Switch between **List** and **Calendar** views with the toggle in the header.

- **Create** on any item opens a Create project drafted from that item. Once an item has a draft the button reads **Open draft** and takes you back to it.
- **Generate the month** drafts every undrafted item in one pass.
- The status dropdown moves an item through **planned**, **approved**, **scheduled**, **published**, or **skipped**, and holds **Remove from plan**.
- **Add item** inserts something the generator didn't think of: pick the channel, a date and a title. Click a title to edit the title and date inline.

<Warning>
**Replan** replaces every item in the plan. Anything you've hand-edited is lost, so draft or export work you want to keep first.
</Warning>

## Publish to advocacy
On event plans, **Publish** appears on an item once it has a draft. It turns the item into an Advocacy campaign link and marks it **In Advocacy**.

<Warning>
Publish creates the campaign link only. It does not assign recipients, send notify emails, or post anything to LinkedIn. Finish the campaign in [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy) — assign an audience and notify them — or nothing will actually be shared.
</Warning>

Publish is unavailable on organization-level plans, since Advocacy campaigns are always scoped to an event. Build the plan inside the event if you intend to distribute it.

## Track progress
The progress strip shows **Drafted**, **Scheduled**, and **Live in Advocacy**. Clicks and conversions aren't reported here — once at least one item is live, the strip gains a **View clicks & conversions in Advocacy** link to the event's Advocacy analytics.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I have no transcribed sessions?">
    Describe a topic instead. You'll still get a dated cadence, just built from the topic rather than grounded in a transcript.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the dates?">
    Yes, edit an item's date inline. Replanning regenerates all dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Plan post to LinkedIn for me?">
    No. Plan schedules and drafts; distribution runs through Advocacy, where the advocate posts from their Creator console.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
