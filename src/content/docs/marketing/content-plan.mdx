Plan takes a single captured session — or just a topic — and lays out weeks of content around it. You get a dated calendar of posts anchored on LinkedIn, each one ready to draft in Create and, on event plans, publish into Advocacy.

<Note>
Plan is part of the Marketing module, which is currently Early Access.
</Note>

## Before you start

You need either a **transcribed session** to build from, or a topic you can describe in a sentence. Plans built on a real session are noticeably better, because the generator is grounded in what was actually said rather than working from the topic alone.

## Build a plan
Go to **Marketing → Plan** and click **New plan**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Source">
    Pick a captured session, or describe a topic instead. One or the other is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goal">
    Choose **Grow LinkedIn**, **Drive demos**, **Promote an event**, **Thought leadership**, or **Nurture audience**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Duration">
    14, 30, 60, or 90 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Channels">
    Toggle the channels to include — Email, Clip, Blog, Lead magnet, Quote card. LinkedIn is always on and can't be turned off; it's the anchor the cadence is built around.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the plan">
    Click **Build the plan**. Generation takes a moment, then the calendar appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work the plan

Switch between **List** and **Calendar** views.

- **Create** on any item opens a Create project drafted from that item.
- **Generate the month** drafts every undrafted item in one pass.
- The status dropdown moves an item through planned, approved, scheduled, published, or skipped.
- **Add item** inserts something the generator didn't think of. Titles and dates are editable inline.

<Warning>
**Replan** replaces every item in the plan. Anything you've hand-edited is lost, so draft or export work you want to keep first.
</Warning>

## Publish to advocacy
On event plans, **Publish** turns an item into an Advocacy campaign link and marks it **In Advocacy**.

<Warning>
Publish creates the campaign link only. It does not assign recipients, send notify emails, or post anything to LinkedIn. Finish the campaign in [Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy) — assign an audience and notify them — or nothing will actually be shared.
</Warning>

Publish is unavailable on organization-level plans, since Advocacy campaigns are always scoped to an event. Build the plan inside the event if you intend to distribute it.

## Track progress
The progress strip shows **Drafted**, **Scheduled**, and **Live in Advocacy**. Clicks and conversions aren't reported here — once items are live, the plan links through to Advocacy analytics for that.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I have no transcribed sessions?">
    Describe a topic instead. You'll still get a dated cadence, just built from the topic rather than grounded in a transcript.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the dates?">
    Yes, edit an item's date inline. Replanning regenerates all dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Plan post to LinkedIn for me?">
    No. Plan schedules and drafts; distribution runs through Advocacy, where the advocate posts from their Creator console.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
