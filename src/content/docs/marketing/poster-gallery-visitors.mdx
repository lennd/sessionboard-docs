Everything on the [gallery builder](/marketing/poster-gallery) decides what a visitor can do. This page walks the hall from their side, so you can tell an author or a sponsor what to expect — and check your own settings against it.

Open it yourself any time with **Preview** in the builder header; it shows the gallery exactly as a visitor sees it, whether or not the gallery is Live.

![A published poster hall: the branded header above a grid of poster cards, each with its program number, title, author and track](/images/kb/poster-hall-grid.png)

## Why use it

Most of the questions a poster gallery generates come from people who are not admins: an author asking where their narration plays, a sponsor asking what their logo sits next to, a chair asking whether visitors can download the file. Reading the hall once, from the visitor's seat, answers all three and tells you which switch to change.

## The hall

The header carries your logo, the **Public title**, the poster count and the description.

![The public poster hall header with the gallery title, the event name, the poster count and the description](/images/kb/poster-hall-header.png)

Under it sits the toolbar: search, a chip per track, the sort options, a light/dark switch and **My list**.

![The poster hall toolbar with All tracks and five track chips, four sort options, a Dark theme switch and My list](/images/kb/poster-hall-toolbar.png)

- **Search** covers titles, authors, sessions and program numbers. Searches that find nothing are counted for you on the gallery's Analytics tab.
- **Sort** offers title, first author, program number and session time.
- The **filter chips** are the ones you chose under **Filter chips shown to visitors**.
- **Dark** is the visitor's own choice; your **Theme** setting only decides where they start.

Each poster is a card with its program number, title, authors, track and format — and, when **Attendee voting** is on, a vote control with the running count.

![A poster card showing the program number 36, the poster thumbnail, the title, the author, a track chip, a format chip and a vote control reading 1](/images/kb/poster-hall-card.png)

Searching narrows the wall in place.

![The poster hall filtered by a search for "readmissions", showing the two matching posters](/images/kb/poster-hall-search.png)

## Opening a poster

Clicking a card opens the poster over the wall: the file renders inline — PDF, image, video and audio all play in the browser — with zoom and **Full screen**.

![A poster open over the gallery, with the poster rendered inline, zoom controls, Full screen, and a meta rail on the right](/images/kb/poster-hall-detail.png)

The rail beside it carries the title, the authors and the facts about the session.

![The poster's meta rail showing PRESENTED with the session date and time, TRACK and FORMAT](/images/kb/poster-hall-detail-meta.png)

### Author narration

When the author has recorded or uploaded a narration, **Author narration** plays it next to the poster.

![The Author narration player in the poster's meta rail](/images/kb/poster-hall-narration.png)

<Note>
On a narrow window the meta rail collapses below the poster, so the narration player moves with it.
</Note>

### What else they can do

![The poster's action list: Copy link to this poster, QR code, Copy citation, Add session to calendar, Download poster and Ask the author a question](/images/kb/poster-hall-detail-actions.png)

| Action | Appears when |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy link to this poster** and **QR code** | **Share actions** is on |
| **Copy citation** | **Share actions** is on |
| **Add session to calendar** | **Add to calendar** is on and the poster session has a scheduled time |
| **Download poster** | **Allow downloads** is on |
| **Ask the author a question** | **Ask the author** is on |

## Questions

**Ask the author a question** collects a name, an email and the question.

![The Ask the author a question form on an open poster](/images/kb/poster-hall-ask-author.png)

Questions land on the gallery's **Analytics** tab under **Questions for authors**. Nothing is sent to the author automatically — you decide what to forward, and to whom.

## Voting

With **Attendee voting** on, every card and every open poster carries the vote control. A visitor votes once per poster; voting again takes the vote back.

A public hall has no sign-in, so a vote is tied to the visitor's browser. Clearing site data, or opening the hall in a private window, lets the same person vote again. On a **Gated** gallery the vote is tied to the verified email instead, which is as close to one-person-one-vote as an open hall gets. Either way, read the ranking in Analytics as a popularity signal rather than a ballot — if you are awarding a prize on the result, say so in your own rules.

**Show vote counts** decides whether visitors see the tally. Turn it off and the hall shows no numbers while **Most voted posters** still ranks them for you.

## Bookmarks and My list

With **Bookmarks** on, a visitor can save posters to **My list** and come back to them.

![The poster hall showing only the posters saved to My list](/images/kb/poster-hall-my-list.png)

The list lives in that visitor's own browser. It does not follow them to another device, and you never see it.

## QR codes

Two kinds, both from the builder header's **Share & print** menu:

- **Download QR** is one code for the whole gallery — for signage, the programme, a slide at the close of the session.
- **QR labels for poster boards** prints one label per poster, so somebody standing at a board can open that exact poster, read the abstract and hear the narration on their phone.

Each poster also carries its own **QR code** in the share actions, for an author who wants one for a slide or a business card.
