Title slides are the slides shown on stage before and between sessions. Instead of building one per session by hand, design a title slide once in Sessionboard — start from a branded design, choose which participants appear and in what order — and generate a slide for every session from the data you already have. The output is a normal, editable .pptx (or a paper-size page for PDF covers), so text stays editable on site. You can also stitch the title slide into the presentations speakers upload, and Sessionboard tells you when a generated slide has fallen behind the session it was made from.

A holding slide is the title slide on the screen in each room before a session starts. Title, time, the speakers and their headshots. Building one per session by hand is a day of work for someone on the production team, and it goes stale the moment a speaker changes. Slides builds them for you from the session data you already have. Three tabs. Templates, Generate, Jobs.

A template is your own PowerPoint file. This event has one. The card shows the file, when it was updated, and how many speakers a slide can hold. Preview, Generate, and under More actions, Edit, Duplicate and Delete. Deleting a template keeps any slides already generated, so you can tidy up without losing output.

Here's how a template knows where things go. You type tokens into the text boxes of your slide. Session dot title. Session dot time range. Speaker one dot name. Keep your own fonts and colours, and Sessionboard swaps the token for the real value. The Token reference panel lists every token for this event, including your custom session fields, with a copy button on each. For headshots, put the token in a picture's alt text and the picture is replaced.

Preview fills the template with a real session, exactly as the generated slide will look. Pick any session from the list and check the title fits, the time reads right, the names line up. Download the pptx if you want to check it in PowerPoint. This is where you catch a layout problem before you generate two hundred slides.

Edit covers the name, the date format, the time format, and one switch that matters. Visible to speakers. Turn it on and each speaker can download their own generated slide from the speaker portal. To change the slide design itself, upload a new file as a new template.

Two ways to add a template. Upload template takes a pptx, up to fifty megabytes, and lists every slide with the tokens it found and how many speakers it fits. A token it doesn't recognise gets flagged, so a typo is caught here, not on stage. No template yet? Start from Brand kit builds a simple sixteen by nine deck from your brand colours and logo. Download it, restyle it in PowerPoint, and upload your version.

Generate is a three step wizard. Choose the template. Then which sessions. All of them, a selection you pick here, or the filter you had open on the sessions list, because you can also start this from the sessions list with Create, then Slides.

Output is the part worth slowing down on. One slide per session, where every presenter shares the slide, or one slide per presenter, so a panel of four gives you four. Then one combined deck in agenda order, which the AV team usually wants, or one file per session, zipped. Attach to each session saves the slide into the session's files as a Holding Slide. The summary line reads back the whole choice before you commit.

Every run lands on Jobs. Big runs finish in the background, you get a notification and an email with the link, and this table shows the status. Download the deck, open the per session results to see what was generated or skipped, and Regenerate re-runs the job with the same settings after you fix a speaker or a time.

When Attach is on, the slide sits on each session's Files tab, labelled Holding Slide. It shows in the Live Transcribe Console and in the public API. Regenerating replaces the previous slide rather than adding a second, and the file keeps its id, so anything downstream keeps working.

That's Slides. One template, every room covered, and when the agenda shifts you regenerate instead of rebuilding. Next in Source and produce is Posts, where the content you make in Create is collected for review.

<Note>
Slides is on for every event. Find it under **Marketing → Source & produce → Slides**, or start from the sessions list with **Create → Slides**.
</Note>

## Before you start

- Sessions with participants, headshots and times filled in. Empty fields are left off the slide rather than shown as blank labels.
- Your event's **Brand kit** (logo, colours, fonts). Every starter design is built from it, so set the brand kit first and the slides match out of the box.

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a starter design">On the **Templates** tab, click **New template**. Six starter designs are drawn with your brand kit and the session roles on your event — Classic band, Split panel, Full-bleed brand, Minimal light, Dark stage and Clinical. Click **Use this design**, or **Start blank** for the default layout with just your colours and logo.</Step>
  <Step title="Fine-tune in the designer">Settings sit on the left, the live preview on the right. Every change re-renders the preview; nothing is locked by the starter you chose.</Step>
  <Step title="Preview with a real session">Use **Preview with session** to render the design with one of your own sessions, exactly as the generated slide will look. Switch between **Title slide** and **End slide**, and between **Slide (16:9)**, **Letter (portrait)** and **A4 (portrait)**.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Name the template, decide whether it is **Visible to speakers**, and click **Create template**.</Step>
</Steps>

### Design

- **Layout** — Band, Split, Full bleed, Minimal or Stage.
- **Colours** — background, primary, accent, text and muted text. **Reset to brand** restores your brand kit colours.
- **Heading and body fonts**, the **logo** and its placement, and the **background** (solid colour, gradient or a photo with an adjustable overlay so text stays readable).

### Session info

Switch on the session facts to show — event name, session title, date, time, room, track and session type — plus up to three custom session fields, and choose the date and time formats.

### Participants

This is where you control who appears on the slide.

- **Maximum participants on the slide** — 1 to 10. People beyond the limit are handled by the overflow rule below.
- **Role groups** — one group per session role on your event (Moderators, Chairpersons, Speakers, Authors…). Switch a group off to leave those people off the slide. **Top to bottom is the on-slide order**, so move Moderators above Speakers to show them first. Each group can have a heading above it, its label inline with each name, or no label.
- **Rows under each person** — the name is always first; add up to two more from job title, company, role or any contact field.
- **Headshots** — on or off, and circle, rounded-square or square.
- **When more people than fit** — **Shrink to fit** makes every headshot smaller so everyone fits, or **Show "+N more"** shows the first participants and a chip with the count.

![The Participants settings with a Maximum participants slider set to 10 and the ordered Role groups list — Moderators, Chairpersons, Speakers, Authors — each with up and down arrows, a visibility switch and a label style](/images/kb/slides-designer-participants.png)

### End slide

Turn on **Add an end slide** to close each generated deck with a headline, optional body text (a thank-you, hashtag, or where to find recordings) and your logo. You can also switch it on or off per run when generating.

## Formats

| Format | Size | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Slide (16:9)** | 10 × 5.625 in | PowerPoint decks and the screen in the room |
| **Letter (portrait)** | 8.5 × 11 in | A cover page for US-letter PDFs, such as the front of a session handout |
| **A4 (portrait)** | 8.27 × 11.69 in | A cover page for A4 PDFs |

The same design re-flows for each format, so one template covers the screen and the handout.

## Generate slides

From the **Generate** tab, or from the sessions list (select sessions → **Create → Slides**):

1. Choose the template.
2. Choose the sessions: **All sessions**, **Selected sessions**, or the **Current sessions filter** you had applied on the sessions list (up to 2,000 sessions per run).
3. Choose **One slide per session** or **One slide per presenter**.
4. Choose **One combined deck** (a single file in agenda order, handy for the AV team) or **One file per session** (separate files, zipped).
5. Choose the **Format** and whether to **Include end slide**.
6. Turn on **Attach to each session** to save each session's slide as a session file.

![Generate wizard Output step with Granularity, Output, Format, Include end slide and Attach to each session options and a Generate slides button](/images/kb/slides-generate-output.png)

Large runs finish in the background. You get an in-app notification and an email with the download link, and the **Jobs** tab shows progress, per-session results and a **Regenerate** action that re-runs a job with the same settings after you fix a speaker or a time.

## After a run: download per session, attach, add to presentations

Every run keeps one deck per session, so you do not have to decide everything up front. On the **Jobs** tab, open a finished run's per-session results:

- **Download deck** / **Download zip** — the combined output you chose.
- **Download per session** — pick one session and download just its deck.
- **Attach to sessions** — save the decks from this run as session files, for all sessions in the run or a selection, without generating again. Attaching replaces the session's previous title slide rather than adding a second.
- **Add to presentations…** — stitch the title slide into the presentations already uploaded to those sessions (see [Add a title slide to uploaded presentations](#add-a-title-slide-to-uploaded-presentations)).

![Per-session results panel for a finished run with Download deck, Download per session, Attach to sessions and Add to presentations actions above the attached, generated and failed counts and the session list](/images/kb/slides-job-panel.png)

## Edit, duplicate and delete templates

Open a template card's **More actions** to **Edit** (reopens the designer), **Duplicate** or **Delete**. Deleting a template keeps any slides already generated and attached to sessions.

## Attached slides

Attached slides appear on each session's **Files** tab labelled **Title slide**, in the Live Transcribe Console, and in the public API (`GET /v1/sessions/:id/files?type=holding_slide`). Speakers see them in the portal's session **Files** tab, labelled **Title slide** and download-only, when the template's **Visible to speakers** switch was on at the time the slide was generated. Flip the switch and regenerate to change what speakers see.

Regenerating or re-attaching replaces the previous title slide for that session instead of adding a duplicate. The file keeps the same id, so integrations reading session files keep working.

## Presentations & files

**Program → Files** is now **Presentations & files**: every file attached to a session in the event, with filters built for the run-up to show day.

- **Type** — presentation, abstract, poster, handout, title slide or CFP submission PDF.
- **Session status** — the statuses on your event, core and custom, exactly as they appear on the sessions list.
- **Submission status** — draft, submitted, pending review, accepted or declined, from the session's latest submission.
- **Title slide** — **Attached**, **Missing** (sessions whose files have no title slide yet), **Out of date** or **Merged copy**.

Each card or row shows the file type, the session it belongs to and a title-slide badge. Select files to act on them together: **Generate title slides** opens the Generate wizard scoped to the selected sessions, and **Add title slide to presentations…** stitches the title slide into the selected presentations.

## Add a title slide to uploaded presentations

When speakers upload their own decks, you can put the branded title slide on the front (and the end slide on the back) without opening each file.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the presentations">In **Presentations & files**, select the presentations (PDF or PowerPoint) and click **Add title slide to presentations…**. From the **Jobs** tab, **Add to presentations…** on a finished run picks the presentations of that run's sessions.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the template and position">Pick the template, then **Start** (title slide before the first page), **Start + end** (title slide first, end slide last) or **End**. The slide is sized to the file — 16:9 for a PowerPoint deck, Letter or A4 to match a portrait PDF.</Step>
  <Step title="Add">Each presentation gets a **new copy** named *\<file\> (with title slide)* next to the original; the original is never changed. Leave **Replace existing merged copies** on so re-running refreshes the same copy instead of adding another.</Step>
</Steps>

![Add title slide to presentations dialog with the template picker, Start, Start + end and End position cards, the Replace existing merged copies switch and the Add button](/images/kb/slides-merge-dialog.png)

Merged copies are ordinary session files: they show in the session's **Files** tab with a **Merged copy** badge, in the speaker portal when the file is visible, and in the public API. Only PDF and PowerPoint files up to 200 MB can be merged; anything else is reported as skipped with the reason.

## Keep title slides up to date

Sessionboard remembers what each title slide and merged copy was built from — the title, date, time, room, participants and their details, the custom fields on the slide, and the template design. When any of those change afterwards, the file shows **Out of date** with the reasons (for example *Title changed*, *Participants changed*, *Template changed*) in the session's **Files** tab and in Presentations & files.

- Filter Presentations & files by **Title slide → Out of date** to see everything that needs a refresh before the event.
- Click **Regenerate** on an out-of-date badge to rebuild that session's title slide — and its merged copies — with the same template, format and position as before. The files keep their ids.
- To refresh many at once, select the sessions' files and use **Generate title slides** with **Attach to each session** on, or **Add title slide to presentations…** with **Replace existing merged copies** on.

<Tip>
Run through the **Out of date** filter the day before show day, after the last speaker changes land. It turns "did anyone update the slides?" into a one-click check.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit the generated slides in PowerPoint?">
    Yes. Every slide is built from native text boxes, shapes and pictures, so titles, names and colours stay editable in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a long title shrink?">
    Sessionboard sizes the title to fit its box so it never spills over the participants. The preview shows the same size the generated slide uses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A session has more people than the maximum">
    The overflow rule in the Participants panel decides: **Shrink to fit** fits everyone with smaller headshots, **Show "+N more"** shows the first participants plus a count. Groups are applied in order first, so with Moderators above Speakers the moderators always make the cut.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is one participant missing?">
    Their session role is in a group that is switched off, or they fall past the maximum. Check the Role groups list and the maximum in the Participants panel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a title slide change the speaker's file?">
    No. The merge always writes a new *(with title slide)* copy next to the original. Delete the copy if you do not want it; the original is untouched either way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A file says Out of date but nothing important changed">
    Any change to what is printed on the slide counts — including a room swap, a speaker's job title, or an edit to the template. Hover the badge to see the reasons; if they do not matter for this session, you can leave it as is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can use Slides?">
    Anyone with access to the event's session files. Creating templates, generating slides, attaching and merging use the same permissions as adding session files.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
