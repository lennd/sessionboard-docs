Title slides are the slides shown on stage before and between sessions. Instead of building one per session by hand, design a title slide once in Sessionboard — start from a branded design, choose which participants appear and in what order — and generate a slide for every session from the data you already have. The output is a normal, editable .pptx (or a paper-size page for PDF covers), so text stays editable on site. You can also stitch the title slide into the presentations speakers upload, and Sessionboard tells you when a generated slide has fallen behind the session it was made from.

<Note>
Slides is on for every event, in the US and EU regions alike; there is nothing to request or turn on. Find it under **Marketing → Source & produce → Slides**, or start from the sessions list with **Create → Slides**.
</Note>

## Why use it

One branded design becomes a correct, editable title slide for every room, built from the session data you already keep — participants in the order you choose, headshots, times and tracks — and attached to each session the moment the run finishes. When a speaker changes, Sessionboard flags the slide as out of date so you regenerate the handful that moved instead of rebuilding the deck, and the exports put every room's opener in one **Title slides** folder for the production team.

## Before you start

- Sessions with participants, headshots and times filled in. Empty fields are left off the slide rather than shown as blank labels.
- Your event's **Brand kit** (logo, colours, fonts). Every starter design is built from it, so set the brand kit first and the slides match out of the box.

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a starter design">On the **Templates** tab, click **New template**. Six starter designs are drawn with your brand kit and the session roles on your event — Classic band, Split panel, Full-bleed brand, Minimal light, Dark stage and Clinical. Your brand kit's heading font wins, so Minimal light and Clinical use a serif title only when the brand kit sets no heading font. When the event has more than one role, every role group starts with a heading, Speakers included. Click **Use this design**, or **Start blank** for the default layout with just your colours and logo.</Step>
  <Step title="Fine-tune in the designer">Settings sit on the left, the live preview on the right. Every change re-renders the preview; nothing is locked by the starter you chose.</Step>
  <Step title="Preview with a real session">Use **Preview with session** to render the design with one of your own sessions, exactly as the generated slide will look. Switch between **Title slide** and **End slide**, and between **Slide (16:9)**, **Letter (portrait)** and **A4 (portrait)**.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Name the template, decide whether it is **Visible to speakers**, and click **Create template**. A template needs a name; a blank one shows *Name is required*.</Step>
</Steps>

### Design

- **Layout** — Band, Split, Full bleed, Minimal or Stage.
- **Colours** — background, primary, accent, text and muted text. **Reset to brand** restores your brand kit colours.
- **Heading and body fonts**, the **logo** and its placement (any of the four corners, on every layout), and the **background** (solid colour, gradient or a photo with an adjustable overlay so text stays readable).

### Session info

Switch on the session facts to show — event name, session title, date, time, room, track and session type — plus up to three custom session fields, and choose the date and time formats.

### Participants

This is where you control who appears on the slide.

- **Maximum participants on the slide** — 1 to 10. People beyond the limit are handled by the overflow rule below.
- **Role groups** — one group per session role on your event (Moderators, Chairpersons, Speakers, Authors…). Switch a group off to leave those people off the slide. **Top to bottom is the on-slide order**, so move Moderators above Speakers to show them first. Each group can have a heading above it, its label inline with each name, or no label. A group with no label that follows a headed group gets a thin divider, so its people never read as part of the group above.
- **Rows under each person** — the name is always first; add up to two more from job title, company, role or any contact field. Job title and company come from the contact's own **Job Title** and **Company Name** fields.
- **Headshots** — on or off, and circle, rounded-square or square.
- **When more people than fit** — **Shrink to fit** makes every headshot smaller so everyone fits, or **Show "+N more"** shows the first participants and a chip with the count of everyone left out. With a maximum of 10 and 11 people, the slide shows 10 people and **+1 more**. **Shrink to fit** ignores the 1–10 maximum and shows everyone up to 18, on up to three rows (three columns in portrait formats); only past 18 does a **+N more** chip appear. The panel's summary reads **Up to 18** while Shrink to fit is selected.

### End slide

Turn on **Add an end slide** to close each generated deck with a headline, optional body text (a thank-you, hashtag, or where to find recordings) and your logo. You can also switch it on or off per run when generating.

## Formats

| Format | Size | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Slide (16:9)** | 10 × 5.625 in | PowerPoint decks and the screen in the room |
| **Letter (portrait)** | 8.5 × 11 in | A cover page for US-letter PDFs, such as the front of a session handout |
| **A4 (portrait)** | 8.27 × 11.69 in | A cover page for A4 PDFs |

The same design re-flows for each format, so one template covers the screen and the handout.

## Generate slides

From the **Generate** tab, or from the sessions list (select sessions → **Create → Slides**):

1. Choose the template.
2. Choose the sessions: **All sessions**, **Selected sessions**, or the **Current sessions filter** you had applied on the sessions list (up to 2,000 sessions per run). **All sessions** and the filter leave out drafts (unsubmitted submissions), so the count matches the sessions list; abstracts and subsessions are included.
3. Choose **One slide per session** or **One slide per presenter**.
4. Choose **One combined deck** (a single file in agenda order, handy for the AV team) or **One file per session** (separate files, zipped).
5. Choose the **Format** and whether to **Include end slide**.
6. **Attach to each session** is on by default, so each session's slide is saved as a session file as soon as the run finishes — no second step. Turn it off for a download-only run.

![Generate wizard Output step with Granularity, Output, Format, Include end slide and Attach to each session options and a Generate slides button](/images/kb/slides-generate-output.png)

Large runs finish in the background. You get an in-app notification and an email with the download link, and the **Jobs** tab shows progress, per-session results and a **Regenerate** action that re-runs a job with the same settings after you fix a speaker or a time. The list shows the newest 50 runs; **Load more** reaches older ones.

## After a run: download per session, attach, add to presentations

Every run keeps one deck per session, so you do not have to decide everything up front. On the **Jobs** tab, open a finished run's per-session results:

- **Download deck** / **Download zip** — the combined output you chose.
- **Download per session** — pick one session and download just its deck.
- **Attach to sessions** — save the decks from this run as session files, for all sessions in the run or a selection, without generating again. Attaching replaces the session's previous title slide rather than adding a second.
- **Add to presentations…** — stitch the title slide into the presentations already uploaded to those sessions (see [Add a title slide to uploaded presentations](#add-a-title-slide-to-uploaded-presentations)).

## Edit, duplicate and delete templates

Open a template card's **More actions** to **Edit** (reopens the designer), **Duplicate** or **Delete**. Deleting a template keeps any slides already generated and attached to sessions.

## Attached slides

Attached slides appear on each session's **Files** tab labelled **Title slide**, in the Live Transcribe Console, and in the public API (`GET /v1/sessions/:id/files?type=holding_slide`). Speakers see them in the portal's session **Files** tab, labelled **Title slide** and download-only, when the template's **Visible to speakers** switch was on at the time the slide was generated. Flip the switch and regenerate to change what speakers see. Organizers always see title slides on the session's **Files** tab, whichever way the switch is set.

Title slides and `-with-title-slide` merged copies never use up a speaker's upload slots. A session limited to one file still lets the speaker upload their own deck.

Regenerating or re-attaching makes the new slide the current version of that session's title slide instead of adding a duplicate. The previous slide stays in the file's version history, and the file keeps the same id, so integrations reading session files keep working.

When you export session files — **Download Files** from the sessions list, or a download bundle — each session's title slide sits in its own **Title slides** folder next to the session's uploads, so production finds every room's opener in one place. Only the current slide is exported; superseded versions stay in the file's history.

## Presentations & files

**Program → Files** is now **Presentations & files**: every file attached to a session in the event, with filters built for the run-up to show day.

- **Type** — presentation, abstract, poster, handout, title slide or CFP submission PDF.
- **Session status** — the statuses on your event, core and custom, exactly as they appear on the sessions list.
- **Submission status** — draft, submitted, pending review, accepted or declined, from the session's latest organization-portal submission. Sessions with no organization submission get a status from the session itself: **Draft** for an unsubmitted draft, **Submitted** for a session that came in through a call for papers.
- **Title slide** — **Attached**, **Missing** (sessions whose files have no title slide yet), **Out of date** or **Merged copy**.

Each card or row shows the file type, the session it belongs to and a title-slide badge. Select files to act on them together: **Generate title slides** opens the Generate wizard scoped to the selected sessions, and **Add title slide to presentations…** stitches the title slide into the selected presentations.

## Add a title slide to uploaded presentations

When speakers upload their own decks, you can put the branded title slide on the front (and the end slide on the back) without opening each file.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the presentations">In **Presentations & files**, select the presentations (PDF or PowerPoint) and click **Add title slide to presentations…**. From the **Jobs** tab, **Add to presentations…** on a finished run picks the presentations of that run's sessions — only original uploads, never the title slides or earlier merged copies — and defaults to that run's template.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the template and position">Pick the template, then **Start** (title slide before the first page), **Start + end** (title slide first, end slide last) or **End**. The slide is laid out for the file's own shape — 16:9 or 4:3 to match a PowerPoint deck or landscape PDF, Letter or A4 to match a portrait PDF — so it is never stretched.</Step>
  <Step title="Add">Each presentation gets a **new copy** named `<file>-with-title-slide.<ext>` next to the original; the original is never changed. Leave **Replace existing merged copies** on so re-running refreshes the same copy instead of adding another.</Step>
</Steps>

![Add title slide to presentations dialog with the template picker, Start, Start + end and End position cards, the Replace existing merged copies switch and the Add button](/images/kb/slides-merge-dialog.png)

Merged copies are ordinary session files: they show in the session's **Files** tab with a **Merged copy** badge, in the speaker portal when the file is visible, and in the public API. Only PDF and PowerPoint files up to 200 MB can be merged; anything else is reported as skipped with the reason. Title slides and earlier merged copies are skipped as sources, and a password-protected PDF is skipped with *This PDF is password-protected. Remove the password and upload it again.* A damaged file that cannot be opened is skipped too, with *This file could not be read*; re-export it from PowerPoint or your PDF tool and upload it again.

## Keep title slides up to date

Sessionboard remembers what each title slide and merged copy was built from — the title, date, time, room, participants and their details, the custom fields on the slide, and the template design. When any of those change afterwards, the file shows **Out of date** with the reasons (for example *Title changed*, *Participants changed*, *Template changed*) in the session's **Files** tab and in Presentations & files.

- Filter Presentations & files by **Title slide → Out of date** to see everything that needs a refresh before the event.
- Click **Regenerate** on an out-of-date badge to rebuild that session's title slide — and its merged copies — with the same template, format and position as before. The files keep their ids. A file that fails is retried; if it still fails, the message names it so you can regenerate just that one.
- If the template a slide was made from has been deleted, the badge says so instead of offering **Regenerate**. Generate the session again from another template with **Attach to each session** on.
- To refresh many at once, select the sessions' files and use **Generate title slides** with **Attach to each session** on, or **Add title slide to presentations…** with **Replace existing merged copies** on.

<Tip>
Run through the **Out of date** filter the day before show day, after the last speaker changes land. It turns "did anyone update the slides?" into a one-click check.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit the generated slides in PowerPoint?">
    Yes. Every slide is built from native text boxes, shapes and pictures, so titles, names and colours stay editable in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a long title shrink?">
    Sessionboard sizes the title to fit its box so it never spills over the participants. The preview shows the same size the generated slide uses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A session has more people than the maximum">
    The overflow rule in the Participants panel decides: **Shrink to fit** fits everyone with smaller headshots, **Show "+N more"** shows the first participants plus a count. Groups are applied in order first, so with Moderators above Speakers the moderators always make the cut.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is one participant missing?">
    Their session role is in a group that is switched off, or they fall past the maximum. Check the Role groups list and the maximum in the Participants panel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a title slide change the speaker's file?">
    No. The merge always writes a new `-with-title-slide` copy next to the original. Delete the copy if you do not want it; the original is untouched either way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A file says Out of date but nothing important changed">
    Any change to what is printed on the slide counts — including a room swap, a speaker's job title, or an edit to the template. Hover the badge to see the reasons; if they do not matter for this session, you can leave it as is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A participant shows initials instead of their photo">
    Sessionboard draws the initials disc when a headshot link is broken or the picture is hosted outside Sessionboard, in the preview and in every export alike. Fix the contact's headshot and regenerate. Initials use the first letter of each name part and skip quotes and punctuation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the text white on my brand colour?">
    Text on a brand-coloured band or panel is set to white or near-black, whichever reads better, so the event name, date and title stay legible on a projector.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can use Slides?">
    Anyone with access to the event's session files. Creating templates, generating slides, attaching and merging use the same permissions as adding session files.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
