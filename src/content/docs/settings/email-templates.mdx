## Create email templates
To create an email template, navigate to Settings > Email Templates within your event dashboard and click the "Add Template" button at the top right-hand corner of your screen.

There is no limit to how many templates you can create. Templates will be ordered alphabetically (A-Z) by the template name.

![Create Email Templates](/images/kb/a426ca11-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-27-38-3571-PM.png)

<Note>
Sessionboard provides a standard "Accept", "Decline", One Day Reminder", and "Five Days Reminder" email templates that can be customized for your event use.
</Note>

These templates can not be deleted.

![These templates can not be deleted](/images/kb/61242d98-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-28-05-2439-PM.png)

Within the pop-up window, customize your email to include:

❗IMPORTANT: If you change a template module after you begin typing the message body, the current merge tags will not be valid. Please ensure to reapply your merge tags as they differ per module.

![Within the pop-up window, customize your email to include](/images/kb/a907b9fe-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-28-37-6402-PM.png)

| Template Name: Internal Name   Type: This determines the merge fields available to use as well as where the template can be used within the platform.   Groups: Exhibitor and Sponsor Modules   Contacts: Contacts, Speakers, Chairpersons, Moderators, and Session Submitters Modules   Sessions: Sessions Module     Reply To: The email address that should receive replies to the email sent.   We recommend using a shared inbox or an event-specific email address. This field only supports one email address.     Send From (only available if you have purchased the use of a custom email domain): Specify the email address you would like your email to be sent from.  CC: Send a copy of your email to up to five visible email addresses that you want to include on the thread.  BCC: Send a copy of your email to up to five hidden email addresses. ## Edit, delete, and duplicate templates
To manage the actions for an email template, select the three dots to the right of the template name.

![Edit, Delete, and Duplicate Templates](/images/kb/4752b898-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-30-14-2556-PM.png)
