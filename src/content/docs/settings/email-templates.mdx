An email template is a message you write once and pull into any send. Templates carry a subject, a body, a reply-to address and merge tags — placeholders that become each recipient's own name, session title or portal link when the email goes out. Every event starts with four built-in templates, and you can add as many of your own as you like.

## Where templates live

Templates belong to the event. In the event sidebar, open **Settings** (under **Configure**) and choose **Email Templates**. Your organization has an **Email Templates** page too, under the organization's **Settings**, with the same editor. Templates there are for sends from the organization directory — the [Speaker CRM](/speaker-crm/crm) — and are kept separate from event templates: an event's composer lists only that event's templates, and the CRM composer lists only the organization's.

![The Email Templates page, with the All Templates, Lifecycle, Custom and System tabs and the Add Template button](/images/kb/email-templates-list.png)

The page has four tabs:

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **All Templates** | Every template you can send, built-in and custom. |
| **Lifecycle** | The four built-in templates: **Accept Sessions**, **Decline Sessions**, **Session Form - One Day Reminder** and **Session Form - Five Days Reminder**. You can edit and duplicate them; they cannot be deleted. |
| **Custom** | The templates your team created. |
| **System** | The emails Sessionboard sends on its own — invitations, sign-in codes, portal digests, submission confirmations. Each row shows the channel, when it goes out and what triggers it. You do not write these; see [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails). |

Columns show each template's **Name**, **Subject**, **Type** and **Category**, and the list is alphabetical by name — a naming habit such as *Speaker – …* keeps related templates together.

## Create email templates

Click **Add Template**. The editor opens as a panel on the right.

![The Add Template panel with Template Name, Type, Reply To, Sender Name, CC, BCC, Subject and Message Body](/images/kb/email-template-add.png)

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Template Name** | Internal only — recipients never see it. |
| **Type** | **Contacts**, **Exhibitors/Sponsors** or **Sessions**. The type decides which merge tags you can use and where the template appears: Contacts templates show up when you email people from Contacts, Speakers or any contact-based module; Exhibitors/Sponsors templates when you email companies; Sessions templates when you email the people on a session, which is where Accept and Decline live. |
| **Reply To** | The address that receives replies. It takes one address — use a shared inbox or an event address rather than someone's personal one. |
| **Sender Name** | The name shown in the inbox. Leave it blank and Sessionboard uses your event name (or your organization name for an organization template). |
| **Send From** | Only shown when your event has a [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain). The address the email is sent from; it must be on your authorized domain. |
| **CC** / **BCC** | Up to five addresses each, separated by commas. Invalid addresses are ignored. |
| **Subject** | The subject line as it appears in the inbox. |
| **Message Body** | The email itself. Click **Merge Tags** to personalize it. |
| **Send one email per session** | Sessions type only. Everyone on a session is addressed together in one email instead of each person receiving their own copy; personalization uses the primary speaker. |

<Warning>
Pick the **Type** before you write. Merge tags differ by type, so if you change the type after placing tags in the body, those tags no longer resolve and you have to insert them again.
</Warning>

### Merge tags

Click **Merge Tags** above the message body, search, and click a tag to drop it at your cursor. Each tag is replaced with that recipient's own value when the email is sent — **First Name**, **Session Title**, the **Portal Login Link** as a hyperlink or a raw URL — so one template produces a personal email for every person.

![The Merge Tags panel open beside the message body, filtered to portal](/images/kb/email-template-merge-tags.png)

Sessions templates add tags for the session itself — title, date and time, room, length — and a **Session Roles** group that names the speakers, moderators and chairpersons. The full list is in the [merge tags reference](/communications/email-campaigns#merge-tags-reference).

### Send a test

**Send test**, at the bottom of the panel, emails the template to an address you enter. Choose a record under **Personalize as** to see the merge tags filled in for a real person, or leave it as *Don't personalize*. A test is a one-off preview; it does not create a send record in [History](/communications/email-sms-history).

Click **Add Template** to save. It appears in the list and in the **Template** picker the next time you [send an email](/communications/create-send-emails).

## Edit, duplicate and delete templates

Click **⋯** at the right of a template's row for **Edit**, **Duplicate** and — on custom templates only — **Delete**. Duplicating is the quickest way to make a variant of a built-in template, for example an Accept email for one track.

![The row menu with Edit, Duplicate and Delete](/images/kb/email-template-menu.png)

## Who the emails come from

Templates have no from address of their own. Every email Sessionboard sends for you — template or not — goes out from `no-reply@notify.sessionboard.com` unless your event has a [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain), in which case the **Send From** field appears on templates and in the send composer and you choose an address on your domain. The **Sender Name** is what recipients see either way.

<Tip>
Templates hold the words; the send picks the people. Filter a module, select records, and choose the template in the composer — [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails) walks through it. For sends you repeat or larger audiences, [Email Campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns) use the same templates and merge tags.
</Tip>
