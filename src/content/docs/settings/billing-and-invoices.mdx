Open **Settings**, then **Billing** in your organization to see what you owe, which reminders went out, and who to call. The page has three cards: **Invoices**, **Billing notices** and **Your Account Team**.

<Note>
**Billing** only appears for people whose role can view billing details. If you cannot see it, ask an organization admin to change your role. Your contract, plan and usage against limits are managed by Sessionboard and are not shown to customers on this page.
</Note>

## Invoices

The top line shows how many invoices are open and the total outstanding. When nothing is due it reads **Nothing outstanding.**

Each row shows:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Invoice** | The invoice number |
| **For** | What the invoice covers |
| **Amount** | The invoice total |
| **Open** | What is still unpaid on it |
| **Due** | The due date |
| **Status** | Whether it is paid, open or overdue |

An unpaid invoice has a **Pay** button that opens the payment page for that invoice in a new tab. If a reminder has been emailed for an invoice, the row says **Reminder sent** with the date.

If the card says invoices could not be loaded, reload the page. If they still do not load, email support@sessionboard.com.

## Billing notices

This is the record of every invoice reminder we emailed to your team, so you can answer "did finance get it?" without asking us.

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | When the reminder went out |
| **Invoice** | Which invoice it was about |
| **Sent to** | Who received it |
| **Sent by** | Who at Sessionboard sent it |
| **Open at the time** | How much of the invoice was unpaid when it went out |
| **Delivery** | Sent, Delivered, Opened, Delayed, Bounced, Not delivered, Marked as spam or Unsubscribed |

If a reminder shows **Bounced**, **Not delivered** or **Marked as spam**, the person did not get it. Ask us to change who receives billing reminders, or ask the person to check their spam folder and allow mail from Sessionboard. Until nothing has been sent, the card reads **No invoice reminders have been sent.**

## Your Account Team

Your Customer Success and billing contacts, with a link to book a meeting where they have shared one. If no one is assigned yet, the card gives support@sessionboard.com.

## Common questions

**Can I change who gets invoice reminders?** Yes. Ask your account team or email support@sessionboard.com with the addresses to include or leave out.

**Can I download a past invoice?** Use **Pay** to open the invoice page for that invoice. Anything you cannot find there, your account team can send.

**Where do I see my plan limits?** Per-product plans are described in the guides for [Marketing](/sessionboard-marketing/), [Learning](/sessionboard-learning/) and [Awards](/sessionboard-awards/). Your account team can confirm your contract.
