<Note>
**Settings → Portals** appears once the customizations feature is enabled on your event. Contact support@sessionboard.com to turn it on. Which sign-in methods a portal accepts is a separate page, **Settings → Portal Login** — see below.
</Note>

Adjust your portal branding — logo, background, colors, fonts — to match your organization and event. Open **Settings → Portals** in the event; the page has two tabs, **Portal Login** for the sign-in screen and **Portal Appearance** for the portal itself.

<Warning>
The settings apply to **every** portal in this event. A [brand kit](/marketing/brand-kits) applied to the event fills them in for you; adjust here to override.
</Warning>

---

## Why use it

The portal is the part of Sessionboard your speakers actually see, so it should look like your event: your colours, fonts and logo on the sign-in screen and inside. A portal that matches the event website gets fewer "is this legitimate?" emails and more completed tasks.

## Portal Login (the sign-in screen)

![The Portal Login tab of Portal Customization with logo and background images with their alt text, button and background colors, and the Sign-in heading section with Heading and Sub copy](/images/kb/portal-login-customization.png)

On the **Portal Login** tab you can set:

- **Logo Image** and alt text
- **Background Image** and alt text (recommended 1500 w × 400 h)
- **Button Color** and **Button Text Color**
- **Background Color**
- **Welcome Message** font, size and color
- **Login Message** — the rich-text note under the welcome message, for example who to contact for help
- **Advanced CSS** and **Advanced Scripts**

The preview on the right updates as you change values. Select **Save** when you are done.

### Sign-in heading

The **Sign-in heading** section on the same tab sets the words above the sign-in options, for example *Log in or create an account* when your submitters sign in with your member account:

![The Sign-in heading section of Portal Login with a Heading and Sub copy filled in](/images/kb/portal-login-sign-in-heading.png)

- **Heading** (up to 200 characters) replaces **Get started** on the **Sign in** step of your submission forms, and **Welcome to** followed by the portal name on the portal login page.
- **Sub copy** (up to 500 characters) appears under the heading on both.

Both are plain text and optional; leave them blank to keep the default heading. They apply to every portal and every submission form in the event. Once someone has entered their email, the usual **Create account** or **Log in** heading takes over.

## Portal Appearance (inside the portal)

![The Portal Appearance tab — Image Settings with logo and background, the full-width switch and position, then Style Settings with accent color and system font](/images/kb/portal-appearance.png)

On the **Portal Appearance** tab you can set:

- **Logo Image** and alt text (recommended 100 w × 100 h)
- **Background Image** and alt text (recommended 1920 w × 200 h), whether to **Make background image full width**, and the **Background image position**
- **Accent Color** — buttons, links and highlights throughout the portal
- **System Font**
- **Advanced CSS** and **Advanced Scripts**

## Portal Login methods

**Settings → Portal Login** (a separate page, always available) chooses how speakers and other portal users sign in. At least one method must stay enabled. **Username & password** lets portal users sign in (and sign up, when enabled) with their email address and a password; **Magic link** emails them a sign-in link instead — no password needed, and links expire after 30 minutes. Organizations with [single sign-on](/integrations/sso) configure it on the same page.

![The Portal Login settings page — Username & password on, Magic link switch](/images/kb/portal-login-methods.png)
