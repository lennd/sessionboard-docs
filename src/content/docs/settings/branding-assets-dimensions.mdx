Every event has two images of its own, set under **Settings → Event Details → Image Settings**. Sizing them to the recommendations below keeps them sharp and uncropped everywhere they appear.

![The Image Settings section on Event Details — a square Logo Image recommended at 300 by 300 and a wide Background Image recommended at 3000 by 1000, each with an Upload new button](/images/kb/event-details-images.png)

| Image | Recommended size | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Logo Image** | 300 × 300 px (square) | The event's tile on your team's dashboard, the emails Sessionboard sends for the event, and the default logo wherever the portal or a form has none of its own. |
| **Background Image** | 3000 × 1000 px (3:1) | The default header background for the portal, portal tasks and intake forms when no portal-specific image is set. |

<Note>
Images that do not match these proportions are scaled to fit: a logo is shown whole inside a square, while a background is cropped to fill its wide banner. Keep text out of background images — a cropped headline is the most common complaint.
</Note>

## Why use it

Organizers using a medium-blue brand colour no longer have to worry that button labels are hard to read; the platform automatically picks the text colour that meets accessibility standards without any manual adjustment.

## Add or replace an image

1. Open **Settings → Event Details** and scroll to **Image Settings**.
2. Click **Upload new** beside the image you want to change and choose **Upload** to pick a file from your computer, or **Copy from…** to reuse an image already in your organization's library.
3. Click **Save** at the bottom of the page.

To remove an image, use the small **×** on its thumbnail, then save.

## Portal and submission-form images are separate

The portal and each submission form can carry their own logo and background — with their own recommended sizes — under **Settings → Portals** and **Settings → Submission Forms**. Set those when a portal or form needs branding that differs from the event's. Otherwise the Event Details images are used.

- [Portal settings](/settings/portal-settings) — the Portal Login and Portal Appearance images
- [Submission form settings](/settings/submission-form-settings) — the Appearance tab

<Tip>
Your organization also has a logo and background image, under organization **Settings → Details** (recommended 300 × 300 and 1500 × 500). Some system emails, such as portal sign-in links, use the organization logo when the event has none.
</Tip>
