Entries on the Speaker CRM pipeline show a colored score badge. **Pipeline** in **Organization Settings** controls where the colors change, so the badge means the same thing to everyone on your team. The setting applies to the whole organization.

## Where to find it

Open **Organization Settings**, then **Pipeline**. The tab appears only for people whose role includes **Can update organization settings** (under **Organization Settings** in the role's permissions).

## How the colors work

| Badge | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Green (high priority) | At or above the green threshold |
| Blue (mid priority) | At or above the blue threshold and below green |
| Gray (low priority) | Below the blue threshold |

The defaults are 75 for green and 50 for blue.

## Change the thresholds

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Pipeline">

Go to **Organization Settings > Pipeline**. Under **Score thresholds** you see two number fields.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the new values">

Set **Green threshold (high priority)** and **Blue threshold (mid priority)**. Both must be whole numbers from 0 to 100, and blue must be lower than green. A message appears below the fields if a value is not allowed, and saving is disabled until it is fixed.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the preview">

The **Preview** row shows sample scores (15, 35, 55, 70 and 85) in the colors your values produce.

</Step>

<Step title="Save">

Click **Save changes**. You see "Pipeline score thresholds saved." and the pipeline cards and entry drawer use the new colors.

</Step>

</Steps>

To go back to the defaults, click **Reset to defaults (75 / 50)** and then **Save changes**. Reset only fills the fields; nothing changes until you save.

## FAQ

**Does this change anyone's score?** No. It only changes the color a score is shown in.

**Why can't I see the Pipeline tab?** Your role does not include **Can update organization settings**. Ask an organization admin.

**Can I set thresholds for one event?** No. The thresholds are organization-wide.
