<Note>
**Settings → Submission Forms** appears once the customizations feature is enabled on your event. Contact support@sessionboard.com to turn it on.
</Note>

Adjust the look of your session submission forms — background, button colors, typography — to match the branding of your organization and event. Open **Settings → Submission Forms** in the event.

![Submission Form Customization in Event Settings — the Appearance tab with logo, background image and button color controls](/images/kb/submission-form-appearance.png)

<Warning>
These settings apply to **every** submission form in the event. A [brand kit](/marketing/brand-kits) applied to the event fills them in for you; adjust here to override.
</Warning>

---

## Appearance

Within **Appearance**, you can set:

- **Logo Image** and its alt text
- **Background Image** and its alt text, whether to **Make background image full width**, and the **Background image position**
- **Background Color**
- **Primary Button** color and text color
- **Secondary Button** color and text color
- **Advanced CSS** and **Advanced Scripts** for anything the controls do not cover — use these with care, they load on every form

Select **Save** when you are done.

## Typography

![The Typography tab — System Font, then font size and color for the form title, section headers, section descriptions and question labels](/images/kb/submission-form-typography.png)

Within **Typography**, you can set:

- **System Font**
- **Form Title** size and color
- **Section Header** size and color
- **Section Description** size and color
- **Question Labels** size and color
