## The three sign-in methods

- **Username & password** — portal users sign in (and sign up, when portal signup is enabled) with their email address and a password. This is the default.
- **Magic link** — portal users receive a sign-in link by email and sign in without a password. Links expire after 30 minutes.
- **Single sign-on (SSO)** — portal users sign in through your organization's identity providers. This section appears only when SSO is enabled for your organization; contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) to set it up.

At least one working sign-in method must stay enabled. The switch for the last enabled method is locked until you turn another method on, and SSO only counts as a working method once at least one identity provider is connected to your organization and enabled for the event.

<Note>
Provider selections under SSO are kept when the SSO switch is turned off, so turning SSO back on restores the same providers.
</Note>

## How the login page changes

- **Password and magic link both on** — the password field stays the primary sign-in, and anyone with an email also sees **Or, email me a sign-in link**, including people who have never signed in and have no password.
- **Magic link only** — the login page shows no password fields anywhere. **Email me a sign-in link** becomes the primary action, and password sign-in is rejected by the server, not just hidden.
- **Magic link only, with portal signup enabled** — someone whose email isn't recognized is asked for their first and last name, then emailed a sign-in link. Their account is created only after they open the link, which proves they own the email address.

## Magic link behavior

- Links expire **30 minutes** after they are requested. An expired link returns the person to the login page with their email pre-filled so they can request a new one.
- A link can be opened more than once within those 30 minutes. Corporate email scanners often open links before the recipient does, so a single-use link would stop working before the person ever saw it.
- Requesting a link for an email address that has no access to the event shows the same "check your email" confirmation but sends nothing — the login page never reveals which email addresses are registered.
- One email address can request **five links every 15 minutes** on a single event. Past that the page still shows the same "check your email" confirmation and no further email goes out, for the same reason as above — a different message would tell an attacker which addresses are real. If a participant says the link never arrived and they have been clicking **Email me a sign-in link** repeatedly, have them wait out the 15 minutes rather than keep requesting; any link they already received still works until it expires.
- Turning **Magic link** off takes effect immediately: links that were already sent stop working, even if they have not expired.

<Tip>
Share [Access your portal](/participants/access-portal) with your participants — it walks them through both the password and magic-link sign-in flows.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Turning **Username & password** off blocks password sign-in for every portal user on this event, including people who already have a password. Make sure magic link or SSO is ready before you switch it off.
</Warning>
