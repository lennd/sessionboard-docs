After a webinar ends, Sessionboard reads the provider's post-webinar reports and records each thing an attendee did, such as answering a poll or asking a question, on that attendee. You see it on the webinar's **People** tab under **Attendance & engagement**: totals, a timeline of the webinar, and every interaction per person, each matched to what was being said at that moment when the recording is in Media.

## Why use it

The provider's reports tell you that 40 people answered a poll. Engagement in Sessionboard tells you who they were, what they asked, which part of the talk they reacted to, and what they are interested in. That is what makes the follow-up personal, and what Pathways uses to learn which topics each person cares about.

## What each provider reports

| | Zoom | Goldcast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attendance, minutes, join and leave times | Yes | Yes |
| Poll answers | Yes | No |
| Q&A questions | Yes | No |
| Chat | No | No |
| Reactions | No | No |
| CTA clicks and demo requests | No | Yes |
| Ratings | No | Yes |
| Written feedback | No | Yes |

When a provider does not report something, the card says so, for example **Chat isn't available from Zoom.**, rather than showing an empty count.

## Read the report

- **Totals**: **Registered**, **Attended**, **No-shows**, **Avg. minutes**, then **Poll answers**, **Questions**, **Chat** and **Reactions**. Goldcast webinars also show **CTA clicks**, **Ratings** (with the average) and **Feedback**.
- **Interactions over the webinar**: a timeline in two-minute steps, colored by type. Hover a bar to see the time range and the chapter of the recording it falls in.
- **The people table**: expand a row to see that person's interactions in order, with the time into the webinar, their answer, and the chapter and the line of the transcript that was playing.

Interactions are matched to registrants by email. Panelists and anyone who used an address that did not register are counted in the provider totals but are not listed.

<Note>
Engagement arrives with the first sync after the end time. The moment and chapter for each interaction fill in once the recording is transcribed and chaptered in Media, which can take a few hours. See [Follow up after a webinar](/webinars/after).
</Note>

## How engagement feeds Pathways

Every registration, attendance and interaction is stored as an activity on the person, tagged with topics from your taxonomy:

- Registering and attending are tagged with the webinar's topics: its session's topics, then topics that match its title and description, then topics of the recording.
- Each poll answer, question, reaction or rating is tagged with the topics of what was said at that moment, or what the person wrote, falling back to the webinar's topics.
- Not showing up is not counted as interest.

[Pathways](/pathways/overview) weighs these the way you would expect: attending counts more than registering, and asking a question counts more than reacting. They feed session picks, likely audience, Topics and, when HubSpot is connected, the topics and engagement score Sessionboard writes to each contact.
