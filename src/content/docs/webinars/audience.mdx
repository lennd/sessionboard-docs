A webinar's **People** tab holds everyone who registered, a **Pre-webinar survey** that asks them what they want to learn, and **Attendance & engagement**, which fills in after the webinar ends with who came, how long they stayed and what they did.

## Why use it

Registrants tell you what they came for when you ask before the webinar, and the provider tells you what they did during it. Keeping both on each person means the host can answer the questions people actually have, and the follow-up can send each attendee the part of the recording they care about, instead of one generic "thanks for joining".

## Send a pre-webinar survey

The pre-webinar survey is a short [Pathways](/pathways/overview) pre-event quiz for this webinar. You do not need Pathways turned on to use it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create it">
On the webinar's **People** tab, find **Pre-webinar survey** and click **Create survey**. The questions are written from the webinar's topic, and you see them before anything is sent.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the questions">
Open **Questions** to read them. The survey asks:

- Which topics they want covered (up to three), from the webinar's topics and your organization's taxonomy.
- Their goals for the webinar (up to two).
- How much they already know.
- **What is one question you want answered?** as an open answer (up to 500 characters).
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
Click **Send to registrants**. Choose who gets it: **Everyone**, **Not yet sent**, **Attended**, **No-shows**, **Answered the survey** or **Haven't answered**, or pick people one by one. Click **Continue**, check the list, then **Send**.

Each registrant gets their own link by email (**Help shape** followed by the webinar's name). Registrants without an email, who already answered or who were already sent the survey are skipped. To share the survey another way, click **Copy link**.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Pre-webinar survey card with Copy link, Send to registrants, the Sent, Responses and Questions counts and the per-registrant answers](/images/kb/webinars-survey-card.png)

The card shows how many were **Sent**, the **Responses** (and the share of those sent), and a row per **Registrant** with when they **Responded**, their **Interests**, and their **Goals and answers**.

Answers become interests and goals on each person's profile. They show up in the engagement report, shape each attendee's [recap](/webinars/after#personalised-follow-up), and count toward topic interest on the [Plan board](/webinars/plan#the-plan-board).

<Tip>
The invitation is a webinar email you can edit, alongside the confirmation, reminder and recording emails, in the **Emails** section of the webinar's **Settings**.
</Tip>

## See who attended

After the webinar ends and the next sync runs, **Attendance & engagement** on the **People** tab fills in from the provider's reports. Until then it says how many people registered.

![Registered, Attended, No-shows and Avg. minutes on the Attendance and engagement card](/images/kb/webinars-engagement-card.png)

- **Registered**, **Attended** (with the share of registrants), **No-shows** and **Avg. minutes**.
- A table of every registrant with **Attended** or **No-show**, **Minutes**, **Joined – left**, their **Interests** from the survey and their **Interactions**. Filter it to **Everyone**, **Attended** or **No-shows**.

What people did during the webinar, such as poll answers and questions, is on the same card. See [Read webinar engagement](/webinars/engagement).

People who joined the webinar without registering (with an email address, and not the host or a panelist) are added as checked-in registrants, so their attendance and engagement count too.

If attendance never arrives, check the provider card on **Overview**. When the provider stopped accepting the saved sign-in, the card says so and offers **Reconnect**.
