Two tools help you decide what a webinar is about and who should be on it. **Recommendations** start from a topic you already have and look across all your events for speakers, content and sponsors on the same subject. The **Plan board** works the other way round: it suggests webinars you could run next, each with speakers from your own contacts and the funnel outcome it serves.

## Why use it

Most organizations already know the people who could fill their next webinar. They spoke at last year's conference, sponsored a related track, or recorded a session on the same topic. Recommendations put that history in front of you while you write the webinar, and the Plan board turns what your audience is asking for into a balanced list of ideas, so the calendar does not depend on whoever happens to volunteer.

## Recommendations for a webinar

Recommendations appear in two places:

- On **New webinar**, under **Topic**: write the title and description, then click **Get recommendations**.
- On an existing webinar, under **Program → Recommendations**, built from the webinar's title and description. Click **Refresh** after you change them.

![The Recommendations tab with matched topics and recommended speakers, each with Add, Invite and Copy invite](/images/kb/webinars-recommendations.png)

You get three lists, each ranked by how closely it matches:

- **Recommended speakers**: contacts who spoke on the topic at your events or are tagged with it, with the sessions and media that show why. Click **Add** to put them on the webinar, or **Invite** / **Copy invite** to reach out. Speakers already on this webinar show **On this webinar**.
- **Related content across your events**: sessions, recordings and clips on the same subject, from the last five years.
- **Sponsors & exhibitors to approach**: accounts that sponsored or exhibited at matching sessions, or at the events that ran the most sessions on the topic, with their primary contact. Click **Draft outreach** to write a first message.

With AI on for your organization, you also get a **Suggested draft** of the title, description and agenda. Click **Use this draft** on New webinar, or **Use as description** on an existing webinar.

Recommendations read your whole organization's contacts. If you see **Ask an org admin for Contacts access**, your role does not include Contacts.

## The Plan board

Open **Program → Webinars** and click the **Plan** tab. You can also click **See more ideas** on **Ideas for your next webinar**.

![The Plan tab with the Focus box, Generate more, Fresh board, the Stage and Format filters and the Funnel coverage row](/images/kb/webinars-plan-board.png)

The board shows six ideas. Each idea card has:

- A **stage** (where it sits in the funnel: Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Retention, Expansion or Advocacy) and a **format**, such as Expert interview, Panel, Customer story, Fireside chat, AMA / office hours, How-to workshop or Industry outlook.
- A title, a hook and the angle.
- **Goal**, **Metric** and **CTA**: the outcome the webinar is for and how you would measure it.
- **Why now**: the evidence, such as rising interest in the topic from surveys and past webinars, a topic your audience asks about that you have little content on, or a past webinar that did well.
- **Speakers** from your contacts, with the reason for each, and **Builds on**: sessions, media or webinars it can draw from.
- A suggested month and length.

Ideas use what your organization already has: your taxonomy topics, survey answers and webinar engagement, your past webinars and the speakers tagged with each topic. Nobody's personal answers are shown, only counts. People who spoke on a webinar in the last four months are suggested last.

### Shape the board

- Type a **Focus**, such as "more customer stories about AI", and click **Generate more**.
- Filter by **Stage** and **Format**.
- **Funnel coverage** counts the ideas per stage. Click a stage with no ideas to add two.
- **More like this** on a card adds three similar ideas. The **X** removes one.
- **Fresh board** starts again.

<Note>
Ideas aren't saved. Every visit draws a fresh board, so draft the ones you like.
</Note>

### Turn an idea into a webinar

Click **Draft this webinar** on a card (or **Draft** on the strip). **New webinar** opens with the title, description and stage filled in and the speakers added, marked **From your Plan board**. A speaker with no email in your contacts shows **(no email — invite by hand)**.
