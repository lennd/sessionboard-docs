Post a webinar as a LinkedIn Event on your company page. Sessionboard fills in the title, time, description, speakers, cover image and your registration link. Then it keeps the LinkedIn Event up to date until the webinar starts, and people who register through LinkedIn land in the webinar like everyone else.

<Note>
If **LinkedIn Events** is not in **Apps** for your organization yet, contact support.
</Note>

## Why use it

LinkedIn is where your speakers' and members' professional networks already are. A LinkedIn Event shows up on your company page, can be shared and promoted, and shows who is going. With Sessionboard you don't copy details across by hand, and every registration still lands on one attendee list with its join link, reminders and Pathways history.

## Before you start

You connect LinkedIn Events once for your organization:

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect">
In **Apps → Event channels → LinkedIn Events**, click **Connect** and sign in with a LinkedIn account that is an admin or content admin of your company page.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the page">
Choose the company page your events are posted from.
</Step>
<Step title="Allow posting as the page">
Approve the second LinkedIn prompt, which lets Sessionboard post as that page. A LinkedIn Event is only visible once the page has posted it.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Post this webinar as a LinkedIn Event card with a Connect LinkedIn Events button](/images/kb/webinars-linkedin-events-connect.png)

Until this is done, the webinar's **Overview** shows **Connect LinkedIn Events** or **Finish LinkedIn setup**, and the **Webinars** page shows a small **Promote webinars on LinkedIn** card. You can dismiss the card.

## Create the LinkedIn Event

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the webinar">
On **Program → Webinars**, open the webinar. You can also do this right after publishing, from the **Published** dialog, or from the **Pages** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Create LinkedIn Event">
The **Post this webinar as a LinkedIn Event** card sits under the provider card on the **Overview**. Click **Create LinkedIn Event**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check what will be posted">
The dialog shows exactly what goes to LinkedIn: the cover image, title, time, **Speakers**, **Register link**, **Description** and the **Page post**.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Click **Create LinkedIn Event**. For a draft webinar, the button says **Create when published**. The LinkedIn Event is then posted when you publish.
</Step>
</Steps>

Within a minute, a **LinkedIn Events** card appears under **Channels** on the Overview. It has **Open on LinkedIn**, the post's impressions and clicks, and how many people registered through LinkedIn.

### What gets posted

- **Title, start and end time**, as an online event.
- **Description**: the webinar's description, then its speakers with their job titles and companies. Only speakers shown publicly on the run of show are listed.
- **Cover image**: the webinar's background image. Without one, LinkedIn uses its default image.
- **Register button and link**: your registration page. The link is tagged `utm_source=linkedin`, `utm_medium=event` and `utm_campaign=webinar-<id>`, so sign-ups are attributed to LinkedIn.
- **Page post**: a short post from your page announcing the event, with the time and speakers.

## How people register

By default, **Register** on LinkedIn opens your registration page. People register there as usual. They get the confirmation email with their join link, and they show **LinkedIn** as their source under **People**.

If your LinkedIn connection includes LinkedIn's registration form, the dialog also offers **LinkedIn form**. People then register on LinkedIn without leaving it. Sessionboard brings them in within 15 minutes as confirmed attendees, unless the webinar has **Approve registrants** turned on. It also registers them with Zoom or Goldcast and sends their join link. Their answers and LinkedIn's consent choices come with them.

<Tip>
You choose the registration method when you create the LinkedIn Event. LinkedIn does not let it change afterwards.
</Tip>

## Get speakers to invite their connections

LinkedIn lets anyone who clicks **Attend** on an event invite their 1st-degree connections to it. No tool can send those invitations for them, so Sessionboard points your speakers and attendees at the event instead. While the LinkedIn Event is live, it shows up wherever they share the webinar:

- **Share kit and Advocacy share page.** An **Invite your connections on LinkedIn** card has an **Open the LinkedIn Event** button. It asks them to click **Attend** and then use **Invite** on LinkedIn. Speakers see it on their [Advocacy](/webinars/advocacy#when-the-webinar-is-a-linkedin-event) share page, and teammates see it in their [Dispatch share kit](/marketing/dispatch-share-kit).
- **Dispatch emails.** The email under **Share it now** adds a line that links the LinkedIn Event.

Their posts still carry their own tracked registration link, so sign-ups are credited to them and to the webinar. The LinkedIn Event is an extra step, not a replacement for the link. The card and the email line go away once the webinar ends, or if you delete the LinkedIn Event.

## Keeping it in sync

- **Edits here go to LinkedIn.** Changes to the title, time or description are pushed when you save. Speakers you add under **Program** reach LinkedIn within the hour.
- **Changes made on LinkedIn** to the name or time show under **Needs you** on the Overview, with a button to keep Sessionboard's version and one to take LinkedIn's.
- **After the start time, LinkedIn locks the event.** Later edits stay in Sessionboard.
- To take the listing down, click **Delete LinkedIn event** under **Settings → Channels**. This deletes the LinkedIn post and the event from your company page.

## What LinkedIn does not allow

- **The LinkedIn "Attend" list can't be read.** LinkedIn does not share who clicked **Attend**. Registrations come back through your registration page or through LinkedIn's registration form.
- **Some choices are permanent.** The organizer page, online versus in person, and the registration form are fixed when the event is created. Changing them means deleting and recreating the LinkedIn Event, which loses its attendee list and the post's engagement.
- **No edits after the start time.**
