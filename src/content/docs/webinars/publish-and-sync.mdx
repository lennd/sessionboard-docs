You create a webinar in Sessionboard as a draft, then publish it to Zoom or Goldcast. Publishing creates the webinar in the provider, adds your speakers as panelists and opens registration. From then on Sessionboard and the provider stay in sync, and when someone changes the title, start time or duration in the provider you choose which version wins.

## Why use it

Your team edits the webinar in one place, and the provider follows. Registrants sign up on your own page with your branding and questions, get their join link in your confirmation email, and land on the same contact record as everything else they do with you. When a host moves the start time in Zoom, you see it and decide, instead of finding out from confused registrants.

## Draft a webinar

<Steps>
<Step title="Start it">
On **Program → Webinars**, click **New webinar**. You can also start from an idea on the [Plan board](/webinars/plan#the-plan-board) with **Draft this webinar**.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe the topic">
Under **Topic**, say what it is about and for whom, and add a **Description** (shown on the registration page). Click **Get recommendations** to see speakers, related content and sponsors on the same subject from across your events. See [Recommendations](/webinars/plan#recommendations-for-a-webinar).
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the basics">
Give it a **Title**, when it **Starts** and its **Time zone**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the webinar options">
Set the **Duration**, then pick the **Provider**: **Zoom**, **Goldcast** or **Decide later**. Under **Registration**, choose **On our page and form** or **On the provider's page**. Under **In the webinar**, turn on what the provider should set up: **Q&A**, **Polls**, **Practice session**, **Cloud recording**, **Waiting room**, **Approve registrants**. **Pathways · enroll everyone who registers** adds each registrant to Pathways.

![The Duration, Provider, Registration and In the webinar options on the New webinar form](/images/kb/webinars-new-webinar-options.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Click **Create webinar**. It opens on its **Overview** as a draft. Add speakers under **Program** before you publish so they become panelists.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Publish to Zoom or Goldcast

On the webinar's **Overview**, the provider card shows the state of the webinar in the provider. Click **Publish** on the provider card. (The **Publish** button at the top of the webinar publishes its registration page.)

Publishing runs five steps and reports each one:

1. **Create in Zoom** (or Goldcast): the webinar is created, or updated if it already exists.
2. **Add speakers as panelists**: the session's speakers become panelists or speakers in the provider.
3. **Registration on our page**, or **Registration on Zoom's page** when you chose the provider's page.
4. **Join link in our confirmation email**, or the provider's own emails.
5. **Advocacy cards**: the share cards for attendees and speakers. See [Advocacy for a webinar](/webinars/advocacy).

When it finishes, the dialog says **Published**, and registration is open. If a step needs a retry it says **Published with problems**: click **Try again**. Publishing again is safe, because finished steps are skipped. Click **Publish** again any time you change the webinar here.

### How registration works

When registration is **On our page and form**, every confirmed registrant is registered in the provider in the background, and their personal join link goes into your confirmation email. A provider problem is recorded on the registrant and shown on the provider card. It never blocks the registration itself.

A Goldcast event takes registrants through its own registration form, so the Goldcast event needs a registration form for registrants from Sessionboard to reach it.

## Sync now

Sessionboard syncs every published webinar every 15 minutes, and Zoom also tells Sessionboard about changes as they happen. Registrants are kept up to date both ways. After the end time, the sync reads attendance, engagement and the recording.

To sync right away, click **Sync now** on the provider card. It shows **Syncing…** until the sync finishes, then **Synced**, or **Sync with Zoom failed** with the provider's message.

![The Zoom provider card showing In sync, the registrant, panelist and recording counts, and the Open in Zoom and Sync now buttons](/images/kb/webinars-provider-card.png)

The provider card shows:

- **Registrants**: how many there are, and with registration on your page, how many have reached the provider.
- **Panelists** and **Recording**.
- The state: **Not published yet**, **First sync pending**, **In sync** with the time of the last sync, **Sync problem**, **Needs you**, or **Sign-in expired — Reconnect**. When the provider refused something, the card shows **Zoom said:** and the message.

## Resolve changes made in the provider

When someone changes the **Title**, **Start time** or **Duration** in Zoom or Goldcast, the webinar shows the change under the provider card as **Changed in Zoom**, with what changed and what your registrants currently have. Pick one:

- **Keep … and tell registrants**: Sessionboard takes the provider's value.
- **Put it back to …**: Sessionboard pushes its own value back to the provider.

Until you choose, the webinar shows **Needs you** on the Webinars page.
