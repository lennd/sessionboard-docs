[Advocacy](/marketing/advocacy) gives the people around your webinar a branded card and a personal link to share it. For a webinar it is set up for you when you publish: attendees get a share card in their confirmation email, and each speaker and co-presenter gets their own share link, listed on the webinar's **After** tab for you to pass on.

## Why use it

Speakers and registrants are the best promoters a webinar has, but asking each of them to write a post rarely happens. With Advocacy on, every confirmation email carries a ready-made card, every speaker has a link that counts their shares, and you can see who is bringing people in, without building a campaign by hand for each webinar.

## How it is set up

When you [publish a webinar](/webinars/publish-and-sync#publish-to-zoom-or-goldcast), the last step, **Advocacy cards**, creates two Advocacy campaigns for it:

- **Attendees**: every confirmation email carries the registrant's personal share link and card. See [Advocacy for attendees](/marketing/advocacy-attendees).
- **Speakers and co-presenters**: one share link per person, with speaker-worded share copy and three cards (Speaker, Speaker and session, Session).

Creating the campaigns needs the Advocacy create or update permission on the webinar. If your role does not have it, the step shows as still to do and publishing carries on. Someone with the permission can turn it on later from the **After** tab.

## Share cards before the webinar

Every webinar gets six share cards to post before it starts, made in your event's brand. Each card shows the webinar's title, its date and start time in the webinar's time zone, the speakers' names, and the registration link with a QR code. Three designs show the speakers' faces (**Speaker spotlight**, **Speaker lineup**, **Portrait**) and three do not (**Bold headline**, **Save the date**, **Ticket**). Use the faceless designs when your speakers have no photo or you want a type-only look.

![The Share cards section on a webinar's Overview tab, with six cards, a Download button and an In Advocacy switch on each, and Regenerate and Send to the team at the top](/images/kb/webinars-share-cards.png)

The cards are on the webinar's **Overview** tab, under **Share cards**. They are made the first time you open the tab. Then:

- **In Advocacy**: turn this on for the cards your speakers, co-presenters and attendees should see. They appear on their share page next to the other cards, ready to post to LinkedIn with their own tracked link, until the webinar starts. Three cards are on to begin with.
- **Download** a card to post it yourself or use it in an email.
- **Regenerate** after you change the title, date, speakers or brand. The tab tells you when the cards are out of date. Cards already in Advocacy or a Dispatch update to the new version.
- **Send to the team** creates a [Dispatch](/marketing/dispatch-share-kit) with the cards, one LinkedIn post per card on a schedule that ends before the webinar. You review it before it is sent.

Only speakers shown publicly on the run of show appear on the cards. A speaker without a photo gets their initials on the face designs; add a photo under **Program** and click **Regenerate**.

<Note>
Once the webinar starts, the cards leave your speakers' and attendees' share pages, and a new Dispatch uses the recording's clips instead. See [Follow up after a webinar](/webinars/after#share-with-your-team).
</Note>

## Speakers and co-presenters

Speakers are the people with a speaker, chair or moderator role on the webinar's run of show, plus the host and panelists from Zoom or Goldcast. The list keeps itself current: after every sync, a co-presenter added in the provider or under **Program** gets a link, and someone who is no longer on the webinar has their link turned off. A link someone already posted keeps working.

![The Advocacy card on a webinar's After tab with sharing on and a speaker's Copy link and Share card buttons](/images/kb/webinars-after-advocacy.png)

On the webinar's **After** tab, **Advocacy** shows:

- Whether sharing is **On** or **Paused**, and how many attendees have shared so far.
- **Speakers**, with each person's link and how many times they shared. Click **Copy link** to pass it on, or **Share card** to open the card they will see.
- **No link yet** for a speaker added since the last sync, and **Removed in Advocacy** for someone you took off the campaign in Advocacy.

If Advocacy is not on for the webinar yet, click **Turn on**. When some speakers are missing a link, click **Give speakers links**.

<Note>
Zoom and Goldcast send panelists their own joining links, so Sessionboard does not email speakers their share link. Send it to them yourself with **Copy link**, or from the campaign in Advocacy.
</Note>

## Track shares

Shares, clicks and registrations from each link are tracked in Advocacy like any other campaign. See [Read the analytics](/marketing/advocacy#read-the-analytics).
