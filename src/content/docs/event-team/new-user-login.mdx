Once an admin has [added you to an event team](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members) — as an admin, evaluator, or any other role — you sign in to Sessionboard to work on that event. What happens first depends on whether you already have a Sessionboard account.

## New to Sessionboard

You receive an email with the subject **[Sessionboard] Join the &lt;event name> Team**. It comes from the person who invited you, shown as *&lt;their name> (via Sessionboard)*, and it contains a **Join your event team** button plus the same link written out underneath in case the button does not work in your mail client.

The link opens an **Almost there...** page where you confirm your first and last name, create a password and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Passwords need at least 8 characters, including a capital letter, a number and a special character. Click **Continue**, then sign in with your email address and the password you just created.

<Note>
The join link is personal to you. If you were expecting one and it has not arrived, check your spam folder, then ask the admin who invited you to open **Event Team**, find your row and choose **Resend invite** (or **Copy invite URL** to send it to you another way). Both options are available until you have signed in for the first time.
</Note>

## Already have a Sessionboard account

If you already use Sessionboard — for another event or another organization — you receive **&lt;inviter's name> has invited you to join Sessionboard** instead. It tells you which event you were added to and includes a **View my events** button and a reminder of the email address your account is under. There is no new password to create: sign in as usual and the event appears on your dashboard.

## Signing in

Go to [app.sessionboard.com](https://app.sessionboard.com) and enter your email address and password. **Keep me signed in** keeps you logged in on that browser. Organizations that use single sign-on can choose **Sign in with SSO** instead, and if you have set up a passkey, **Sign in with a passkey** is also available. If your organization's data is hosted in Europe, switch **Data host region** at the bottom of the form before signing in.

![The Sessionboard sign-in form — Email address, Password with a Forgot password? link, Keep me signed in, Sign in, Sign in with SSO and Sign in with a passkey](/images/kb/login-forgot-link.png)

After signing in you land on your dashboard, which lists every event you have access to. Click an event name to open it.

<Tip>
Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in form — see [Reset password (user instructions)](/event-team/reset-password-user-instructions).
</Tip>
