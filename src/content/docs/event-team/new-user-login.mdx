title: "New User Login Instructions"
description: "Share this article with any event team members who need assistance logging into Sessionboard."

Once you've been set up as an event team member (i.e. admin or evaluator), you'll be able to log into Sessionboard to work on your events.

An email will be sent to you from no-reply@sessionboard.com stating that someone from your team has invited you to join Sessionboard. You can access Sessionboard by clicking the button "View My Events" or "Join your event team".

Sample Invite EmailSubject Line: &lt;User Name> has invited you to join Sessionboard
