import { Note, Tip } from '@compat';

Forgot your password? You can reset it yourself from the sign-in page — no admin needed.

1. Go to [app.sessionboard.com](https://app.sessionboard.com) and click **Forgot password?**, to the right of the Password label. (The old `/forgot` address still works and brings you to the same place.)

   ![The sign-in form with the Forgot password? link beside the Password label](/images/kb/login-forgot-link.png)

2. The card switches to **Reset Password**. Enter the email address your Sessionboard account is under and click **Send Reset Link**.

   ![The Reset Password form — Email address and a Send Reset Link button, with Back to login above it](/images/kb/forgot-password.png)

3. The page confirms: *If you entered a valid email address, we've sent you an email with a link to reset your password.* Open the email — subject **[Sessionboard] Reset your password** — and click **Reset your password**. Choose a new password and sign in with it.

<Note>
If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder; it is sent from no-reply@sessionboard.com. The confirmation appears whether or not the address matches an account, so also double-check you used the email your invitation was sent to.
</Note>

<Tip>
Admins: a team member who is locked out after five wrong passwords shows an **Unlock account** option in their row menu on the **Event Team** page — see [Invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members#manage-existing-users).
</Tip>
