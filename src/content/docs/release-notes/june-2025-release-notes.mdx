Release Date: June 3, 2025

Welcome to this month’s product updates! Below is a summary of new features and enhancements released during this cycle. These updates are designed to improve your overall experience and streamline your workflows within Sessionboard.

---

### New Features
1. Session Audit Log: Track all changes made to a session with the new Audit Log, now accessible directly from each session page. NOTE: All changes to a session made on or after June 4th will appear in the session audit log.

![Screenshot 2025-06-03 at 4.12.21 PM](/images/kb/4247322b-Screenshot-2025-06-03-at-4.12.21-PM.png)

2. Import Headshots: Teams can now import headshots into Sessionboard by adding a public-facing image URL when creating or updating a contact. This makes it easier to bulk upload contact photos.

![Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 11.55.08 AM](/images/kb/3535e407-Screenshot-2025-06-06-at-11.55.08-AM.png)

![Screenshot 2025-06-06 at 11.54.22 AM](/images/kb/6d28887c-Screenshot-2025-06-06-at-11.54.22-AM.png)

### Enhancements
1. CRM Dashboard Widgets:

![Screenshot 2025-07-16 at 11.09.19 AM](/images/kb/981a936f-Screenshot-2025-07-16-at-11.09.19-AM.png)

1. Speaker Engagement Flow: Visualizes the speaker journey from initial outreach to acceptance, including how many were emailed, accepted, or linked to multiple events.
  2. Top Companies: Lists companies with the highest number of associated contacts, helping you spot organizations with strong speaker participation.
  3. Speaker Source: Breaks down how speaker records were added—via submission form, application, or manual entry/importing.
  4. Areas of Focus: Categorizes contacts based on topics or areas of expertise, helping you analyze trends and speaker alignment (references the standard session fields 'Track' & 'Tags').
  5. Contacts by Region: Conveniently see where your contacts are located around the globe.
1.
  1. Top Companies: Displays a list of companies with the highest number of associated contacts (references the standard field 'Company Name').
1. [History Module] Audit Log: The newly designed audit log page provides a detailed, table-based view of actions, such as create, update, and delete, along with the exact date and time they occurred.
