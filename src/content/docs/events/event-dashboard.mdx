## Why use it

The Dashboard gives you one place to see every tool included in your plan, which ones are in use, and which you have not set up yet, with one button to start each. Live counts on the tiles show real activity, and the Needs you list ranks what is off across the event so you fix the biggest problems first. Switch to the Timeline view for a before, during and after checklist of what to focus on next.

## What is at the top

![The date and countdown, the greeting, the Today's briefing pill, the Ask box and the Today tab at the top of the event dashboard](/images/kb/event-dashboard-hero.png)

The band across the top shows today's date, a countdown to the event start (or **Event in progress** / **Event ended** once it has begun), and a greeting.

Beside it are Ask's shortcuts:

- **Today's briefing** — plays Ask's daily voice summary of the event. The pill reads **Preparing today's briefing…** while it is being generated, and sits muted until one exists.
- **Need attention** — a count of items Ask thinks you should look at. It appears only when there is something on the list; click it to open the list.
- **Ask about your event…** — a search-style box. Type a question and Ask opens with the answer, grounded in this event's data.
- **Top picks** — a short row of the agent suggestions most relevant right now, shown when Ask has some.

Ask is on for every event. See [Agents overview](/agents/overview).

## Tabs

The **Today** tab is the dashboard described on this page. Next to it you may see:

- **Custom dashboards** you or a teammate built — from a template, an AI prompt or widget by widget. These need Reports permissions; see [Custom dashboards](/reporting/dashboards). **Add Dashboard** on the right starts a new one.
- **Saved briefings** — briefings you asked Ask to keep, one tab each.
- **Agent View** — appears while Ask is showing you a view it built in answer to a question.

Selecting a tab keeps it in the page URL, so a link to a saved dashboard opens on that dashboard.

## Training

Under the tabs, a **Training** band keeps the learning material for this event in view:

![The Training band — an Enabled on your event carousel of feature cards, a Your learning journey progress ring with a Continue button, and a Don't miss a release panel with the latest release note and a Changelog link](/images/kb/event-dashboard-training.png)

- **Enabled on your event** — a carousel of the modules this event uses, each with **Explore more** links into the matching Help Center articles.
- **Your learning journey** — how far you are through the video chapters and setup steps, with **Continue** picking up where you left off. Each chapter plays inside the guide it was scripted from — start with [What is Sessionboard?](/get-started/overview#training-00-platform-overview).
- **Don't miss a release** — the newest release note, with a **Changelog** link to the rest.

**Minimize** shrinks the band to a single pill; open it again from the same spot.

## Your toolkit

The first card on the Today tab lists every tool your event comes with, whether or not you have set it up yet.

![Two rows of tool tiles in the Your toolkit card, each with an icon, a state chip such as In use or Ready to start, a count or a one-line description, an Included chip and an Open link or a set-up button, with the Tools / Timeline toggle top right](/images/kb/event-dashboard-your-toolkit.png)

- Tiles sit in two rows and scroll sideways. A fade and a round arrow on the right mean there is more; click the arrow or scroll to see the rest.
- Tools you already use show **In use** with a live count: submissions, confirmed speakers, sessions, campaigns, transcripts, clips, galleries.
- Tools included in your plan that you have not touched show **Ready to start**, an **Included** chip and one button that takes you to the set-up screen, such as **Launch a campaign** for Advocacy or **Set up recording** for Transcriptions.
- Tools outside your plan or still in Early Access are shown too, with a **Preview** or **Beta** chip, so you can see the whole product. The button on those tiles acts through the Early Access program: **Join Early Access** if your organization has not enrolled, **Turn on preview** for a Preview feature, or **Request beta** for a Beta. After a request the tile reads **Requested**. If you cannot manage Early Access, the tile links to the program page instead. See [Early Access](/get-started/early-access).
- **N of M included tools in use** in the header counts the tools you can open today.
- The last tile, **Don't see what you want?**, opens the Ideas board in Community so you can tell us what to build next.

**Tools** and **Timeline** at the top right switch between the grid and a three-column view, **Before**, **Event days** and **After**, that places each tool in the phase of the event where it matters. The current phase is highlighted with **You are here**, one tile is marked **Next for you**, and each column shows how many of its tools are in use. Your choice is remembered on this browser.

## Needs you

**Needs you** ranks what is off across the event, with the count on the left and an **Open** button that takes you to the screen where you fix it: overdue portal assignments, scheduled sessions with time conflicts or no room, accepted speakers missing a bio or headshot, reviewer assignments past their deadline, and so on. **See all in Ask** opens the full list.

![Ranked rows in the Needs you card, each with a count badge, a one-line description, the section it belongs to and an Open button](/images/kb/event-dashboard-needs-you-kpis.png)

## Headline numbers

Under Needs you, the tiles summarize the program at a glance:

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Submissions** | Session submissions received through your submission forms, the total of every form's count in **Your forms**, with the **Accepted**, **Pending**, **Declined** and **Withdrawn** split under it |
| **Accepted speakers** | People on accepted sessions, and how many accepted sessions they are on |
| **Registered** | Registered attendees, shown when the event has registration data |
| **Sponsors** / **Exhibitors** | Groups in each module, shown when the event uses them |

**Pending** includes sessions that are pending, in the accept queue, or in the decline queue. Click any figure to open the list behind it, filtered to that status.

<Note>
**Submissions** and **Accepted** can disagree. Sessions your team added by hand, or imported, count toward their status but were never submitted through a form, so they do not appear in **Submissions**.
</Note>

## Submission Pacing

![This year's cumulative submissions drawn as a line in the Submission Pacing card, with a Compare to picker to overlay a prior event at the same distance from event start](/images/kb/event-dashboard-submission-pacing.png)

Submission Pacing plots cumulative submissions in the run-up to the event start. Choose **Compare to** and pick a prior event to overlay its curve at the same distance from event start, so "we have 140 at T-60 days; last year we had 120 at T-60" is one glance. Switch between **Days before event** and **Calendar date** for the x-axis.

## Recent submissions

The most recent submissions, with source form, title, status, topics and when they came in. **View all** opens the full submissions list.

## Sections

Below that, the dashboard is split into four sections that mirror the modules, one tab each: **Submission Forms**, **Participants**, **Evaluations** and **Agenda**. Pick a tab to switch sections; the choice is kept in the page URL.

- **Submission Forms** — your forms and their submission and draft counts.
- **Participants** — participant counts by role, portal assignment status, and submission status. Role rows can add up to more than the unique participant count when one person holds more than one role.
- **Evaluations** — progress for Evaluation 2.0 plans: reviewer assignments complete, evaluators active, a score snapshot, and the most active plan.
- **Agenda** — agenda health (scheduled vs. unscheduled accepted sessions), distribution by room, track, tag and format, sessions by day, and what is **Next up**. **Include submissions** sets which statuses count as on the agenda; the default follows your agenda settings.

![The Participants section — a Participants by role ring with Speakers, Chairpersons and Moderators, a Submission Status bar, and Contact assignments with pending, overdue and total counts](/images/kb/event-dashboard-participants.png)

![The Agenda section — Agenda health with all accepted sessions on the agenda, Agenda distribution by track and by format, and Sessions by day](/images/kb/event-dashboard-agenda.png)

<Note>
Sections only show data for modules the event uses. An event without Evaluation 2.0 plans, for example, shows an empty Evaluations section with a link to create one.
</Note>

## Needs attention

When something is off, a **Needs attention** callout inside the relevant section lists it with a link to fix it — for example:

- Accepted speakers still missing a bio or headshot
- Speakers who have not responded to an acceptance request
- Session submissions awaiting a decision
- Overdue portal assignments
- Reviewer assignments past their deadline, or review completion below 50%
- Accepted sessions with no time slot, scheduled sessions with no room, time conflicts, or sessions outside the event dates

Each line's action button (**Manage acceptance**, **Schedule now**, **Assign rooms**, **Nudge reviewers**, and so on) takes you to the screen where you resolve it.

## Inside Submission Forms

The first section is the one most teams live in during the call for papers.

![Your forms cards in the Submission Forms section with their Open or Closed state, submission and draft counts, and View and Manage links](/images/kb/event-dashboard-submission-forms.png)

### Your forms

Each submission form in the event gets a card with its **Open**, **Closed**, or **Upcoming** state, submission and draft counts, and **View** / **Manage** links. The form marked **Primary form** is the one the dashboard treats as your main call for papers. Use **Manage forms** to go to **Program > Forms**.

Not sure which kind of form you need? See [Which form should I use?](/concepts/which-form-should-i-use).

<Tip>
The dashboard is the fastest way to brief someone new on the event. Open it, read **Your toolkit**, **Needs you** and the headline numbers, then ask **"walk me through this event"** for the narrative version.
</Tip>
