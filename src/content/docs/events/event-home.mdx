Home is the page an event opens on. Links to an event, from your events list, from search and from our emails, land here. It starts with one question, **What do you want to happen?**, and shows what the event is already using and what to pick up next. The [Event Dashboard](/events/event-dashboard) is the row directly under it in the sidebar.

## Why use it

Home lets you start from the result you want instead of from a menu. Say it in your own words and Ask does the looking, or pick the next tool from a short list chosen for where your event is. When you want the whole picture, **All tools** lists everything the event can use, described by the job it does.

## Ask for what you want

Type in the box under **What do you want to happen?** and send it, or choose one of the suggestions under it. There are up to four, and they change with the event and with you:

- Anything that needs your attention comes first.
- If submissions are waiting, you are offered the exact count to decide on.
- The rest fit the stage the event is at: setting up, in progress, happening now or ended.
- You are only offered things your role can act on. Someone who cannot see submissions is not asked about them.

Asking opens the conversation full screen:

- The answer appears in the middle. Keep going in the box at the bottom.
- Your earlier conversations for this event are listed on the left. Select one to read it again, or choose **New conversation**.
- Choose **Dock** to carry on in the Ask panel beside the page you were on. Anything you have typed comes with you.
- Close it with the **Esc** key or your browser's Back button. You return to Home where you left it.

Ask is one conversation shown two ways. The **Ask** button in the top bar opens it as a panel beside any page, and **Expand** in the panel's header gives it the full page. The panel does not open by itself when you enter an event. See [Ask](/agents/overview) for what it can do.

## What Home shows

| Part | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Dates and countdown | The event dates and how far out it is, above the event name. |
| Four numbers | Submissions, submissions awaiting a decision, sessions accepted, and participants on accepted sessions. A participant is any contact on an accepted session in any role, counted once however many sessions they are on. Select a number to open the list behind it. |
| **In use on this event** | Each tool the event is already using, named by the job it does, with a live count. Select a card to open it. |
| **Next for this point in the event** | Tools your plan already covers that fit the stage the event is at. **See what it does** explains the tool before you commit to it. **Set it up** takes you to its first screen. |
| **From Sessionboard** | Upcoming webinars, new posts from our blog and product updates. Each opens in a new tab. |

A brand-new event has nothing in use yet. Its first suggestion is **Get feedback from your community and peers**: create a review plan so community members and peers can score each submission.

## See what a tool does, then set it up

**See what it does** opens a short description of the tool: when you would use it, where it lives in the event, and whether your plan covers it. From there you can choose **Ask how it would work here** to have Ask explain it for this event, or **Set it up**.

**Set it up** opens the tool on a first screen made for starting, not an empty list:

| Tool | What the first screen offers |
| --- | --- |
| Surveys | Three templates with their questions written: session feedback, session and speaker feedback, and speaker feedback. Each creates a draft you can change. You can also start blank. |
| Submissions | The ways to start your call for content. |
| Agenda | The ways to start building the schedule. |

The first time you open one of these while it is empty, Ask opens beside the page with a request typed in for you. Nothing is sent until you send it. This happens once for each tool.

Other tools open on their usual page.

### If an action is greyed out

Home follows your permissions. When your role cannot open or set up a tool, its button is still shown but cannot be selected, and pointing at it says **You do not have permission to perform this action**. The same applies to the Early Access actions. An organization admin can change your role. See [Permissions](/event-team/permissions-matrix).

## All tools

**Home** and **All tools** are the two tabs at the top of the page. **All tools** lists everything you can do with the event, grouped by job.

Each card leads with the job and its outcome. Under them is the name of the feature that does it and, where it belongs to one, the package it is part of.

Filter the list with the buttons above it:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every tool. |
| **In use** | Tools the event is using now. |
| **Ready to add** | Tools your plan includes that you have not set up. Choose **Set it up** to start. |
| **Ask us** | Tools that are in Early Access or outside your plan. The card says how to get it. |

<Note>
**All tools** replaces the **Your toolkit** card that used to sit on the Event Dashboard. The tools and their live counts are the same.
</Note>

## Who sees Home

Everyone who can open the event's dashboard sees Home. A member whose only role is reviewing lands on their reviews, as before.

The organization has a Home too. It leads with the same question and box, asked across all your events. See [The sidebar](/get-started/sidebar).

<Tip>
Not sure where something lives? Ask for the result, for example "remind speakers who have not sent a headshot". You do not need to know which page does it.
</Tip>
