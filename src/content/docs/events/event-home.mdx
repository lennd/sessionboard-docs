Home is the page an event opens on. It starts with one question, **What do you want to happen?**, and shows what the event is already using and what to pick up next. The [Event Dashboard](/events/event-dashboard) is the row directly under it in the sidebar.

## Why use it

Home lets you start from the result you want instead of from a menu. Say it in your own words and Ask does the looking, or pick the next tool from a short list chosen for where your event is. When you want the whole picture, **All tools** lists everything the event can use, described by the job it does.

## Ask for what you want

Type in the box under **What do you want to happen?** and send it, or choose one of the three suggestions. The suggestions change with the event: a new event is offered **Create a review plan for this event**, an event in progress is offered questions such as **Which submissions still need a decision?**

Asking opens the conversation full screen:

- The answer appears in the middle. Keep going in the box at the bottom.
- Your earlier conversations for this event are listed on the left. Select one to read it again, or choose **New conversation**.
- Close it with the **X**, the **Esc** key or your browser's Back button. You return to Home where you left it.

Ask no longer opens by itself as a side panel when you enter an event. The **Ask** button in the top bar still opens it on any page. See [Ask](/agents/overview) for what it can do.

## What Home shows

| Part | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Dates and countdown | The event dates and how far out it is, above the event name. |
| Four numbers | Submissions, submissions awaiting a decision, sessions accepted, and speakers on accepted sessions. |
| **In use on this event** | Each tool the event is already using, named by the job it does, with a live count. Select a row to open it. |
| **Next for this point in the event** | Two tools your plan already covers that fit the stage the event is at. **Add** takes you to the set-up page. |

A brand-new event has nothing in use yet. Its first suggestion is **Get feedback from your community and peers**: create a review plan so community members and peers can score each submission.

## All tools

**Home** and **All tools** are the two tabs at the top of the page. **All tools** lists everything you can do with the event, grouped by job.

Each card leads with the job and its outcome. Under them is the name of the feature that does it and, where it belongs to one, the package it is part of.

Filter the list with the buttons above it:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every tool. |
| **In use** | Tools the event is using now. |
| **Ready to add** | Tools your plan includes that you have not set up. Choose **Add** to start. |
| **Ask us** | Tools that are in Early Access or outside your plan. The card says how to get it. |

<Note>
**All tools** replaces the **Your toolkit** card that used to sit on the Event Dashboard. The tools and their live counts are the same.
</Note>

## Who sees Home

Everyone who can open the event's dashboard sees Home. A member whose only role is reviewing lands on their reviews, as before.

The organization has a Home too. It leads with the same question and box, asked across all your events. See [The sidebar](/get-started/sidebar).

<Tip>
Not sure where something lives? Ask for the result, for example "remind speakers who have not sent a headshot". You do not need to know which page does it.
</Tip>
