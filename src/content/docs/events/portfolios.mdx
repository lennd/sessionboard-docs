A portfolio is a named group of events with its own team. Use portfolios when one organization runs programs that different people look after — a clinical education series and a set of community forums, a regional split, an annual flagship and the workshops that feed it. Each portfolio rolls up the events, speakers and sessions inside it, carries a team that can be scoped to see only those events, and hands off to the Report Builder for cross-event reporting.

Portfolios is a Beta feature in [Early Access](/get-started/early-access). Once it is on for your organization, **Portfolios** appears in the organization sidebar under **Program**.

## What a portfolio holds

| | What goes in | How it gets there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Events** | Whole events. An event belongs to at most one portfolio. | **Add events** on the portfolio, or the **Portfolio** field in the event's **Settings > Details**. |
| **Content** | Contacts, accounts, awards programs and media from the organization level. | **Add to portfolio** in the bulk bar of the Contacts, Accounts, Awards and Media lists. |
| **Team** | People and role groups who can see or manage the portfolio. | **Add member** and **Assign role group** on the **Team** tab. |

The counts on each portfolio card — events, team, speakers, sessions and tagged items — come from its events and tagged content. Speakers and sessions are counted across the events in the portfolio, so a card is also the quickest read on how big a program is.

## Create a portfolio

<Steps>
<Step>
In the organization sidebar, open **Program > Portfolios** and click **Add Portfolio**.
</Step>
<Step>
Give it a **Name** and, optionally, a **Description** that says what belongs in it. The description shows on the card and at the top of the portfolio page.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a **Color** and, if you want one, upload a **Logo** (500 × 500 recommended). The color marks the portfolio wherever it appears — the card, the portfolio filter on the Events and Contacts lists, and the **Add to portfolio** menu.
</Step>
<Step>
Set an **Order** to control where the card sits in the list, then click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Everything on the form can be changed later from the portfolio's **Settings** tab or the **Edit** button in its header. Deleting a portfolio removes the grouping and its team assignments; the events, contacts and programs inside it are untouched.

<Tip>
The first time you open Portfolios with none created, Sessionboard offers three starting points — a regional series, a medical track program and a team partition. They only pre-fill a name, description and color; the portfolio is yours to shape.
</Tip>

## Add events

Open a portfolio and use the **Events** tab:

- **Add events** opens a picker of the organization's events. Search by name, tick the ones you want and click **Add**. Events already in this portfolio are marked so you cannot add them twice.
- The remove control on an event row takes it out of the portfolio. The event itself is not affected.

An event can only be in one portfolio at a time. Adding an event that already belongs to another portfolio moves it.

You can also assign from the event's side. In the event, open **Settings > Details** and choose a portfolio in the **Portfolio** field, or pick **No portfolio** to take it out. Either route ends in the same place.

The organization's **Events** list has a portfolio filter next to the search box. Pick a portfolio to see only its events, and each event row links to the portfolio it belongs to.

## Tag content

The **Content** tab shows how many contacts, accounts, awards programs and media items are tagged into the portfolio, with a link to each list filtered to those items.

Tagging happens from the lists themselves. In the organization's **Contacts** directory, tick the people you want, then choose a portfolio from **Add to portfolio** in the bulk bar. The same control sits in the bulk bar of the Accounts, Awards and Media lists. Sessionboard confirms how many were added and how many were already tagged. Tagging is additive — a contact can sit in several portfolios.

The **Contacts** directory has the same portfolio filter as the Events list, so a portfolio's team can work from just their people.

<Note>
Content tagging is what portfolio-scoped access uses to decide which contacts, accounts, programs and media a scoped user can see. If a scoped team member cannot find a contact, check that the contact is tagged into one of their portfolios.
</Note>

## Give a portfolio a team

The **Team** tab lists who has access beyond organization Admins, who see every portfolio.

- **Add member** adds a person from your organization's team as a **Manager** or a **Member**. Managers can manage the portfolio's content; members can view it.
- **Assign role group** attaches one of your organization's roles, so everyone in that role gets the portfolio at the level you choose. New people added to the role inherit it.

Adding someone to a portfolio does not by itself restrict them. What it does is decide what a *portfolio-scoped* role can see.

### Portfolio-scoped access

Organization permissions include a scoping row per module — in **Settings > Team > Permissions**, open a role and look for **Can only view/manage events in assigned portfolios** under Events, **Can only view/manage contacts in assigned portfolios** under People, and the matching rows under Awards, Interest Forms, Sites, Accounts and Files. Turn one on, and people in that role only see the records in that module that belong to their portfolios — the events assigned to them, the contacts tagged into them, and so on. Their portfolios are the union of what they were added to directly and what their role group was assigned.

A scoped user with no portfolios sees nothing in that module, so assign the team before you scope the role. Without the scoping option, a role's access is organization-wide as usual. Roles are covered in [Inviting organization team members](/speaker-crm/inviting-organization-team-members).

<Warning>
Scoping is per module. A role scoped on Events but not on Contacts sees only its portfolio's events and every contact in the organization. Set the scoping option on each module you want to partition.
</Warning>

## Report across a portfolio

The **Analytics** tab opens the Report Builder with the portfolio examples ready — sessions by event and status breakdown by event across the events in the group. Pick one to open it with its columns, filters and sort in place, then reshape it. See [Report Builder](/reporting/report-builder).

The **Overview** tab is the day-to-day view: a stat strip for events, speakers, sessions and team, and the next events in the portfolio with a **View All** link into the Events tab.

## Permissions

| To | You need |
| --- | --- |
| Open **Portfolios** and a portfolio page | **Can view portfolios** |
| Create one | **Can add portfolios** |
| Edit, add events, tag content, manage the team | **Can update portfolios** |
| Delete one | **Can delete portfolios** |

Organization Admins have all four. The fifth row in the Portfolios group, **Can only manage portfolios where added as manager**, limits a role to the portfolios where the person is a Manager.
