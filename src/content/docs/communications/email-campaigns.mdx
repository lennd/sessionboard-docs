A campaign is a send to a segment rather than a selection. Where [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails) starts from rows you ticked in a table, a campaign starts from a definition — *speakers on accepted sessions who have never logged in to their portal* — and can be scheduled, repeated on a cadence, and told to skip anyone it already reached. Both use the same [templates](/settings/email-templates), merge tags and [themes](/settings/email-themes).

## Accessing email campaigns

In the event sidebar, click **Send** (under **Collect & Review**). The page lists every campaign for the event; **New Campaign** at the top right starts the wizard.

![The Email Campaigns list with status tabs, the Settings menu and the New Campaign button](/images/kb/email-campaigns-list.png)

Tabs organize campaigns by status:
- **All:** every campaign regardless of status
- **Drafts:** campaigns in progress, not yet sent or scheduled
- **Scheduled:** campaigns queued for a future send
- **Sending:** campaigns currently in delivery
- **Sent:** completed campaigns
- **Paused:** campaigns that have been temporarily stopped
- **Cancelled:** campaigns that have been cancelled

Two further statuses show on the campaign card but have no tab of their own: **Partially sent**, when a send finished with some recipients failing, and **Failed**, when the send could not complete at all. Look for those under **All**.

Filter by Campaign name, Status, Type, Send date, or Creator.

The **Settings** menu at the top right leads to the pages campaigns depend on:
- **Email Templates:** the reusable messages — see [email templates](/settings/email-templates)
- **Email Themes:** the header, footer, colours and fonts wrapped around every send — see [email themes](/settings/email-themes)
- **History:** every email, per recipient, with its delivery status — see [History](/communications/email-sms-history)
- **Analytics:** open, click and bounce figures across campaigns
- **Unsubscribes:** contacts who opted out
- **Suppression List:** addresses blocked because mail to them failed, and where you clear a block — see [suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list)

## Creating a campaign

Click **New Campaign**. The wizard has five steps — **Audience**, **Basic info**, **Recipients**, **Preview**, **Schedule** — and saves a draft as you go.

### Step 1: Audience

Who are you reaching? Everything downstream — merge tags, recipient filters, the preview — follows from this.

![Step 1, Audience: the People, Companies and Sessions cards](/images/kb/email-campaign-audience.png)

| Audience | Who | How it sends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **People** | Speakers, chairs, moderators, attendees, your team — any contact. | One email per person. |
| **Companies** | Exhibitors and sponsors. | One email per company. |
| **Sessions** | Pick sessions, then email their participants with that session's title, time and track merged in. | One email per session-participant pair — a speaker on three sessions gets three emails, each about its own session. |

<Warning>
Merge tags differ by audience. If you switch audience after writing the body, the wizard lists the tags that will stop resolving; remove and re-insert them from the picker for the new audience.
</Warning>

### Step 2: Basic info

An **Internal name** for the campaign (recipients never see it), then the message: **Subject line** and **Email body**. **Start from a template** pulls in one of your saved [email templates](/settings/email-templates); **Write with AI** drafts the body for you to edit, and **Suggest subjects** proposes subject lines once there is body copy to work from. Under **Sender & reply**, set **Sender name**, **Reply-to**, **CC** and **BCC** — leave them blank to use the event defaults. **Send from** appears here only when your event has a [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain). The merge-tag sidebar on the right inserts a tag at the cursor.

![Step 2, Basic info: subject, body with merge tags, sender and reply-to](/images/kb/email-campaign-basic-info.png)

Use merge tags to personalize the message. The [merge tags reference](#merge-tags-reference) below lists them.

### Step 3: Recipients

Define who receives the campaign. The panel on the right shows the number of **matching recipients**, the **Filters applied**, and a **Sample** of matching names and emails so you can spot-check — all three update as you add filters.

![Step 3, Recipients: quick filters on the left, the live count and sample on the right](/images/kb/email-campaign-recipients.png)

Three ways to narrow the list, and they stack:

- **Describe who to email** — type it in plain English ("speakers who haven't confirmed yet") and click **Suggest**; the wizard translates it into filters you can then adjust.
- **Quick filters** — the common slices:
  - **Role in event** — Speakers, Chairpersons, Moderators, Submitters
  - **Submission status** — Accepted, Pending, Declined, Withdrawn, Waitlisted
  - **Participant acceptance** — whether the person has confirmed their participation: Accepted, Declined, Pending, Not Accepted
  - **Added to a submission on/after** — only people added as a submission participant on or after a date. Useful for "welcome" sends that should only reach people who joined since your last campaign.
  - **Portal activity** — Any, Logged in or Never logged in, for chasing people who have not opened their portal
- **Contact fields** and **Session fields** — filter by any field on the contact (job title, company, country, your custom fields), or narrow to contacts who participate in at least one session matching a session filter.

When you add more than one field filter, choose how they combine: **All filters (AND)** requires a recipient to satisfy every filter, while **Any filter (OR)** includes anyone who satisfies at least one. The recipient count and the filter summary update to reflect the logic you pick, so "Speakers in Track A or Track B" is one send, not two.

Campaigns use **All filters (AND)** unless you change it, so each filter you add narrows the audience rather than widening it. Switch to **Any filter (OR)** before adding the second filter when you mean "either of these".

**Also send to specific people (optional)** adds up to 100 people alongside the filtered list — **Add from my team** picks a teammate, **Enter an email** takes anyone else, such as a venue contact or an agency.

**Omit past recipients** skips anyone who already received this campaign, so a re-send only reaches new people. You can scope it to the entire send history, the last send, or a number of days. The send confirmation shows the resolved recipient count — the number the send will actually reach after skipping past recipients — so the number you confirm is the number that goes out.

### One email per session

A Sessions campaign can send **one email per session** instead of one per person. Open **Recipient handling** and turn on **Bundle session participants onto one email** and each session gets a single message: its primary speaker on the **To** line, every other participant of that session on **CC**.

A few things follow from that, and they surprise people:

- **Everyone on the session is reached**, whether they're on To or CC, and each of them gets their own row in History with their own delivery status.
- **Someone on several sessions appears on each session's email.** A moderator across three panels is CC'd on all three, rather than receiving one email about one panel. They are not sent a separate email of their own.
- **The count on the confirm screen is people, not messages.** Three sessions of four participants reads as twelve recipients even though four emails go out. The count already leaves out contacts who have bounced or unsubscribed, the same way the send does, so the number you confirm is the number of people actually reached.
- **Session merge tags resolve per email.** `{{session.title}}` renders that session's title on that session's message.
- **Drafts are skipped.** A participant's unpublished draft session never becomes one of these emails.
- **"Omit past recipients" applies per session.** Someone who already received this campaign for one session is still emailed for their other sessions — otherwise the first session they happened to land in would silence the rest. The run history notes when this happened, so an apparent double-send is explainable.

To send about one specific session instead, set the session on the campaign. Bundling then produces exactly one email, for that session only.

### Step 4: Preview

Review the campaign exactly as recipients will see it, wrapped in your default [email theme](/settings/email-themes). The wizard runs a content review as the step opens and reports **Looks good to send** or lists what is worth a second look — a very short body, a missing sign-off. **Preview as…** lists the recipients; click one to render the message with their values. Switch the **Preview viewport** between **Desktop** and **Mobile**, toggle **Dark**, and click **Send a test** to email it to any address.

![Step 4, Preview: the rendered email with the Send a test button](/images/kb/email-campaign-preview.png)

**Personalize as** decides whose data fills the merge tags in the test:

- Pick a contact or a session and the test email renders with that record's values — the closest thing to what a real recipient receives.
- Leave it on **Don't personalize** and the merge tags stay exactly as written, as literal `{{ }}` text. That is useful for proofreading the template itself, and it means a test can no longer arrive carrying a stranger's session title because Sessionboard guessed a record for you.

### Step 5: Schedule

Choose how it goes out; the **Ready to send** summary on the right shows the **Subject**, the resolved **Recipients** count and when it is **Sending**.

- **Send now:** delivery begins within about a minute.
- **Schedule for later:** pick a **Send date & time**.
- **Recurring:** choose a **Frequency** — Daily, Weekly (with a day of week) or Monthly (with a day of month) — and a **Send time**. The campaign re-evaluates its filters on every run, so a weekly "you have not logged in yet" reminder reaches whoever still matches, and combines naturally with **Omit past recipients**.

![Step 5, Schedule: Send now, Schedule for later and Recurring](/images/kb/email-campaign-schedule.png)

<Tip>
Recurring plus **Omit past recipients** is the pattern for onboarding sequences: a weekly campaign to speakers added to a submission on or after a date, skipping anyone who already received it, welcomes each new cohort once and only once.
</Tip>

## Merge tags reference
Merge tags insert personalized data into your emails. Available tags vary by audience (set in Step 1); click **Merge tags** above the body and search to insert one.

### All audiences
- Event Name — the name of the event (for organization-level campaigns, the organization name)

### People
- First Name
- Last Name
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Job Title
- Company Name
- Portal Link
- Custom contact fields

### Companies
- Company Name
- Point of Contact Name
- Point of Contact Email
- Portal Link

### Sessions
- Session Title
- Session Date & Time
- Session Duration (minutes) — the session's length, worked out from its start and end times
- Session Room / Location
- Session Track
- Speaker Name(s)
- Moderator, chairperson, and custom role name(s) — every role your event uses gets its own tag, listed together under **Session Roles** in the tag picker. Each one has a "with titles" version that adds each person's job title and company.
- Portal Link

<Note>
Changing the audience after composing your message invalidates existing merge tags. Pick the audience before writing the body.
</Note>

## Analytics & History

### Analytics

**Settings → Analytics** shows campaign performance across your event.

![The campaign Analytics page with open, click, bounce and unsubscribe rates](/images/kb/email-campaign-analytics.png)

Key metrics include:
- **Open Rate:** Percentage of recipients who opened the email
- **Click Rate:** Percentage who clicked at least one link
- **Bounce Rate:** Emails that could not be delivered
- **Unsubscribe Rate:** Recipients who opted out
- **Total Sent:** Number of emails delivered

For deeper analysis, build a custom email report in [custom reports](/reporting/custom-reports).

### History

Every campaign run is logged under [History → Emails](/communications/email-sms-history#emails): one row per campaign in **Email Campaigns**, and one row per recipient with a delivery status in **Sent Emails**.

## Unsubscribes

**Settings → Unsubscribes** lists contacts who opted out through the unsubscribe link. They will not receive future campaigns from this event even when they match the recipient filters. This is a separate list from the [suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list), which holds addresses that bounced or failed.

## Permissions & access

Email Campaigns access is controlled at the event level. Go to **Event Team → Permissions**, select a role, and configure under **Emails**:
- Can view email campaigns
- Can create email campaigns
- Can send email campaigns
- Can manage email templates
- Can manage email themes

## FAQ & troubleshooting

### My merge tags are showing as placeholder text instead of real data, what happened?

This usually means the audience was changed after the message was composed. Go back to Step 2, remove the broken merge tags, and reinsert them using the merge tag selector for your current audience type.

### Why do I see fewer recipients than expected in Step 3?

The recipient count only includes contacts matching your active filters who have a valid email address. Check that filters aren't too restrictive and that profiles include an email. Unsubscribed contacts are excluded automatically.

### Can I edit a campaign after I have created it?

That depends on its status. **Draft**, **Scheduled** and **Paused** campaigns are editable — open the campaign from the dashboard, click Edit, make your changes and re-save. Once a send has started, the subject, message and recipients are locked permanently.

| Status | Subject, message and recipients |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Editable. |
| **Scheduled** | Editable — but your changes go out to recipients instead of the version you previewed. Cancel the schedule first if you need time to review, then re-schedule. |
| **Paused** | Editable. Changes take effect the next time you resume the campaign. |
| **Sending**, **Sent**, **Partially sent**, **Failed**, **Cancelled** | Locked. |

A locked campaign still opens, because the wizard is also how you read back what was sent, and its internal title can still be changed. To make a real change, duplicate the campaign from the campaign list and edit the copy. The wizard says so in a banner at the top, so you find out before typing the changes rather than after.

![The campaign wizard on a sent campaign, showing the banner "This campaign has already sent and can no longer be edited" with a link to duplicate it from the campaign list](/images/kb/email-campaign-sent-locked-banner.png)

<Note>
Locking is permanent, not a pause. A campaign that has finished sending does not become editable again once delivery completes.
</Note>

### What's the difference between Recurring and Schedule for later?

Schedule for later sends the campaign once at a specific time. Recurring sends on a repeating schedule — daily, weekly or monthly — and re-runs its recipient filters each time, so it reaches whoever matches on that day.

### Can I pause or cancel a campaign that's already scheduled?

Yes. In the campaign list, open the **⋯** menu on the campaign and choose **Cancel schedule** (a one-time send) or **Cancel series** (a recurring one). A campaign that is mid-send offers **Cancel send**.

### What happens if a recipient is on the unsubscribe list?

Unsubscribed contacts are automatically excluded from all campaigns, regardless of the filters applied in Step 3.

### One contact stopped receiving campaigns while everyone else got them

They are probably on the suppression list, which blocks an address after mail to it fails. Open **Settings → Suppression List** from the campaigns page to see whether the address is there and whether it bounced or was caught by a failed send, then clear the block once the cause is fixed. See [Suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list).

### A sent campaign says delivery analytics will appear once activity is reported

Nothing is wrong with the send. Delivery, open and click figures come from your email provider, which reports them back over the following minutes rather than at the moment of sending, so a campaign you just sent has no figures yet. The card tells you how many recipients it went to and waits for the rest.

Sessionboard deliberately shows that message instead of zeros, because "0 delivered" and "no report has arrived" are very different things and a row of zeros reads like a failed campaign. Refresh after a few minutes. Very old campaigns can keep showing it permanently — providers only retain activity for about a month, so once that window passes the figures are genuinely gone rather than zero.

To send a campaign's content when a session or person field changes — to that record only — use [Automations](/automations/overview).
