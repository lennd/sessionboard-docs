When a message to someone fails, Sessionboard blocks that address so later campaigns skip it. The suppression list shows every blocked address for your event, why each one was blocked, and lets you clear a block once the underlying problem is fixed.

This is the first place to look when one person says they stopped receiving your emails while everyone else on the same send got them.

## Open the suppression list

From **Email Campaigns**, click **Settings**, then **Suppression List**.

## Why an address gets blocked

Every address on the list was blocked for one of two reasons, and the difference matters:

| Reason | What happened | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bounced** | A delivery report came back for this address — a hard bounce, a block, or a spam complaint. The mail server rejected the person specifically. | Check the address for typos and confirm the mailbox still exists before clearing it. |
| **Send failure** | The whole message was rejected before sending, usually an unverified sending domain or sender identity. Nobody on that send received anything, and nothing bounced. | Fix the sending problem, then clear the block. |

A send failure is not the recipient's fault, so those blocks are safe to clear once sending works again. When the list contains any, a banner at the top calls out how many, so you don't mistake a sending-domain problem for a list of bad addresses.

## Read the list

Tabs across the top split the list into **All**, **Send failures**, and **Bounced**, each showing its own count. Use **Search by email…** to find one address.

Each row shows:

- **Address** — the blocked email address, and how many sends it was blocked on
- **Why it's blocked** — a **Bounced** or **Send failure** badge with a plain-language explanation
- **Contacts** — a link to the matching contact record, or **No contact record** if the address was emailed without one
- **Flagged** — when the block was last applied

## Clear a block

1. Find the address and click **Clear block**.
2. Read the confirmation — it tells you whether this address bounced or was caught by a send failure.
3. Optionally type a reason. It's saved with your name so support can see why the block was cleared.
4. Click **Clear block** to confirm.

The address disappears from the list and future campaigns from this event can reach it again.

<Warning>
Clearing a **Bounced** address means emailing a mailbox that already rejected you. Repeatedly sending to addresses that bounce hurts your sending reputation and makes legitimate mail more likely to land in spam. Only clear a bounce when you know the mailbox works now — for example the person confirmed a typo or their mailbox is no longer full.
</Warning>

<Note>
Clearing a block is scoped to the event you're working in, and every clear is recorded with your name, the address, and your reason.
</Note>

## Who can clear a block

Viewing the suppression list needs the same permission as viewing email campaigns. Clearing a block needs permission to edit them, so a team member with read-only access sees the list and the reasons without the **Clear block** button.

## Suppression compared with unsubscribes

They are separate lists and they are cleared in different places:

- **Suppression list** — addresses Sessionboard blocked because mail to them failed.
- **Unsubscribe list** — contacts who chose to opt out. See [Email campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns).

A contact on either list is skipped by future campaigns even when they match your recipient filters.

## Still having issues?

If an address keeps landing back on the list after you clear it, the delivery problem hasn't been solved yet — work through [Why you aren't receiving emails](/faq/why-am-i-not-receiving-emails), which covers typos, corporate spam filters, and allowlisting Sessionboard's sending IPs.

If it still recurs, contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) with:

- Event name
- The affected email address
- The date and time of the send
- Whether the list showed it as **Bounced** or **Send failure**
