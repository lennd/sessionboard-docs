## The notification bell

A bell in the header shows a count of what is waiting. Opening it lists your notifications newest first. You can **Mark all read** from here, or click **See all notifications** to open the full inbox. The settings icon in the top-right opens **Preferences** for the organization or event you are in. On a page that is not inside either, it opens your account settings.

Inside an event or an organization, the bell defaults to showing only that context. A **This event** (or **This organization**) / **All** toggle at the top switches between the two, and a line at the bottom tells you how many notifications are waiting elsewhere.

Inside an event with AI Agents turned on, the bell gains a second tab, **Needs attention**, holding actions your agents have proposed. It opens on whichever tab has work in it, and the count on the bell covers both. Outside an event, or with AI Agents off, there are no tabs — just the inbox.

## The notification centre

Open the full inbox from **See all notifications** at the bottom of the bell. It exists at both levels:

| Scope | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Open the bell from any organization page and choose **See all notifications** |
| Event | Open the bell from any page inside the event and choose **See all notifications** |

The inbox has an **Unread** filter and a **Group** control set to **None**, **Event**, or **Date** — Date bands everything into Today, Yesterday, Earlier this week, Earlier this month, and Older. Grouping by event disappears when you are already looking at a single event, since every row would land in the same group.

The same scope toggle as the bell sits at the top: the current **This event** or **This organization** on one side, **All events & orgs** on the other. Use it to triage one event, then switch to All to clear the rest.

## Choose how you are notified

The **Preferences** tab lists every notification type with a switch per delivery channel. The switches in the column header turn a whole channel on or off at once.

| Channel | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **In-product** | Appears in the bell and the inbox. |
| **Email** | Sends an email, either immediately or in a daily digest. |
| **Slack** | Sends the recipient a direct message from your organization's connected Slack app. |

Types are grouped into **Admin**, **Portal**, **System**, and **Messages** so you can find one quickly, and there is a filter box above the list.

<Tip>
Preferences are per person, not per organization. Changing yours does not change anyone else's.
</Tip>

### Organization and event preferences

You can set preferences at the organization level and then override them for a single event. When you are looking at an event's preferences, Sessionboard notes that **event settings override your organization defaults** — so a busy event can be noisier than your baseline without you having to change everything back afterwards.

### Email frequency

**Email frequency** sits at the top of the preferences you are looking at and covers every notification type at once. It is set per scope, so a single organization or event can run at a different frequency from your account default. The line under the control tells you which is in force — *Following your account default (Daily summary)*, or *This event overrides your account default (Daily summary)*.

![The Email frequency control reading "Following your account default (Daily summary)", with the frequency selector set to Use account default](/images/kb/notifications-email-frequency-scope.png)

- **Immediately** — a notification email as soon as something happens.
- **Daily summary** — one email a day instead of one per notification.
- **Use account default** — removes the override for this organization or event, so it follows your account default again.

<Note>
A frequency you set from an event's portal preference centre applies to that event alone, and it beats your account default there. So if one event quietly stops sending immediate emails, open that event's preferences and look at **Email frequency** — if it shows an override, **Use account default** puts it back. Changing your account default will not do it, because the event's own setting is more specific.
</Note>

Most notification types default to the daily summary. Conversational ones — new messages, mentions, and changes to session data — default to immediate instead, because a batched reply a day later is not much use. Your own choice always wins over the default.

### Slack delivery

Slack notifications arrive as a DM from the Sessionboard app, in the same place as your other app notifications, with a link back to the record.

To use it, connect Slack under **Apps → Slack** first. The preferences screen prompts you when it is not connected. If Slack is unavailable — not connected, or the recipient is not in the workspace — Sessionboard skips the Slack copy quietly and still delivers in-product and email, so nobody misses a notification because of a workspace problem.

## Set an organization-wide policy

Organization admins get a third tab, **Organization policy**, on the org notification centre. It sets the default for everyone in the organization, one notification type at a time:

| Policy | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **On** | Members receive it and can manage it themselves. This is the default. |
| **Off** | Disabled for the whole organization. Nobody receives it. |
| **Required** | Members receive it and cannot turn it off. |

Members see the result as locked rows in their own preferences, labelled **Required by your organization** or **Turned off by your organization**.

The policy also covers evaluators who manage their reviewer emails on a Site (**My Profile → Notification types**). A type set to Off or Required shows there as **Set by your organization** and can't be changed. See [evaluator emails](/evaluations/evaluation-plans).

<Warning>
**Off** is organization-wide and silent. Nobody gets the notification and nobody is told why, so use it for genuine noise rather than as a way to quiet one team.
</Warning>

The tab appears only on the organization notification centre, not the event one, and only for admins who can update organization settings.

## Messages and notes on records

Contact, session, and group records carry two tabs that look similar and behave very differently:

| Tab | Who sees it | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Messages** | The people on the record — a message on a session is delivered to its speakers, one on a contact to that contact, in their portal inbox plus an email. | You want the participant to read and reply. |
| **Notes** | Your team only. Never delivered anywhere. | Context for teammates — call summaries, reminders, caveats. |

The composer says which of the two you are in and who will receive what you write, so a note cannot end up in a speaker's inbox by accident.

### Messages

A session's **Messages** tab is a threaded conversation about that session, with @mentions. Admins see every thread; participants see the ones they are part of, and reviewer identities stay anonymous to the speaker. Recipients need portal access to read and reply — records without a portal user show the thread as visible to your event team only.

Speaker portals show the inbox only when **Enable Messaging** is on for that portal (under the portal's **Configuration**). It is **off by default** on new portals, so turning it on is a deliberate choice to open a reply channel; when it is on, the portal shows a message indicator on a submission when there is something to read.

### Notes

Every contact, session, and group record has a **Notes** tab with the same team-only editor. @mention a teammate in a note and they are notified through whichever channels they have chosen — the notification links straight back to the record. Notes also appear in the record's Activity feed alongside everything else that happened to it.

### The unread dot on a row

In the Sessions, Contacts, Sponsors, Exhibitors, and organization People lists, a small red dot beside a row means there is something on that record you have not seen yet — a new message, a new note, or new activity. It is not an error.

Hover the dot to see what is waiting, for example *2 unread messages, 1 new note*. Click it to open the record straight on the tab that has the unread item: **Messages** first if there are unread messages, otherwise **Notes**, otherwise **Activity**. The dot clears once you have opened that tab. Inside the record, the same dot sits on the tab itself, so you can see at a glance which tab still has something unread.

## Emails to speakers when a submission changes

Separately from the team bell, each portal can email **portal users** when a field they can see on that portal changes, when the session time or room moves, or when they are assigned as a participant. Time and room always notify. Other fields follow **Manage Fields → Submission Fields**. Turn it on per portal under **Configuration**. See [Notify speakers of submission changes](/portals/submission-change-notifications).
