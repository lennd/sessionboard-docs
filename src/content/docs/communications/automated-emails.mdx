The following are emails that are sent by Sessionboard to your event contacts or event users. Some emails can be customized as well as turned on/off.

All emails sent are sent by no-reply@sessionboard.com (unless you have a custom domain).
-  New Team Member Invitation (i.e. Admins & Evaluators)
-  Session Submission Form Confirmation Email
-  Draft Submission Form Reminders
-  Application Confirmation Email
-  Weekly Email Digest
-  Email Notification
-  Evaluation Plan Weekly Reminders
-  Evaluation Plan Open Notification
-  File Request New Message
-  [AI Evaluations] Virtual Evaluations Are Ready

---

### New Team Member Invitation
-  When a new user to added to your event or organization, they are sent an email to inform them that they can now access the event or organization through Sessionboard.
-  These emails can not be turned off/disabled.
  - NOTE: Imported contacts have the option to not be notified upon import.
-  Customization: None

### Draft Submission Reminder Emails

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-23-2026-11-54-54-1231-PM.png)

- Email Cadence: Five days and one day before the submission form closes (this email cadence can not be altered).

### Weekly Digest Email

🗒️ NOTE: Contacts will only receive this email IF they have created an account/accessed their portal previously.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-17-2026-10-40-16-7974-PM.png)

-  Send a weekly email summary of portal actions, upcoming tasks by the due date, as well as new comments.
  -  Emails are sent every Monday at 7 AM UTC. The day of the week and time the email is sent can not be changed.
-  This can be turned on/off for each portal.
-  Customization: None

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/Knowledge%20Base%20Import/Screenshot%2B2023-03-14%2Bat%2B12_45_18%2BPM%2B(1)-1.png)

| --- |

### ​Evaluation Plan Weekly Reminders
-  When enabled, Evaluators who are assigned a specific evaluation plan will receive an email from Sessionboard on Mondays at 7 AM UTC informing them of their plan progress, as well as the number of submissions that have not been reviewed
-  These emails can be turned off/disabled.
-  Customization: None

### File Request New Message
- When a contact/group sends a message through a file request task, all event admins receive an email notifying them that there is a new message that requires their attention.
- Customization: None
