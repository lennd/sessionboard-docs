Emails are sent from wherever the people are. Filter a module down to the records you want — accepted speakers, one track, everyone who has not finished a task — select them, and **Send** opens a composer that fills a template with each recipient's own details and shows you every email before it goes. Sends of any size work the same way; large ones run in the background and report progress under **Send → Campaigns**.

Now the emails. Everything you built in the portal only matters if speakers know it's there, so this chapter is about getting the right message to the right people, and knowing it arrived. Three parts. Templates you write once, the send itself, and the emails the platform sends without you.

Templates live under Settings, Email Templates. Four come with every event, Accept, Decline, and the one-day and five-day reminders for a closing form. You can edit them, you can't delete them. Everything else here is yours, and the list is alphabetical, so a naming habit like "Speaker, dash, something" keeps them together.

Let's make one for the portal link. Add Template, then Add Template again from the menu. A name, this is internal, your speakers never see it. Then the type, and this is the decision that matters. Contacts is for people. Exhibitors and Sponsors is for companies. Sessions is for the session itself, so that's where accept and decline live.

The type decides which merge tags you get. If you change it after you've written the body, the tags you already placed no longer resolve and you'll have to put them back. So pick the type first, write second.

Reply To is where answers land, and it takes one address, so use a shared inbox rather than someone's personal one. Sender Name is what shows in the inbox, your event name is usually right. CC and BCC take up to five each. Then the subject.

The body is where merge tags earn their keep. Merge Tags, top right of the editor. Search for portal, and there's the Portal Login Link, as a hyperlink or the raw address. One click drops it at the cursor, and every speaker gets their own link. First Name works the same way. One template, a personal email for each person. Add Template, and it's on the list.

Sending happens where the people are. In Contacts, the trick is to filter first and select second. Filter to accepted speakers, or one track, or whoever hasn't finished a task, then tick the rows, or the box at the top for the page, and if the filter runs to more pages, select everything that matches. Send, then Send Emails.

The left side is the envelope. Who should receive this email, and whether to copy their additional contacts, that's the assistant or the co-author on their record. Replies sent to, Sender Name, CC and BCC, same as the template. On the right, Template pulls in what we just built, and Merge Tags are here too if you want to tweak one send.

Review, and this is the step I'd never skip. Every recipient is listed. Click a name and you see their actual email, subject, their name, their portal link, filled in. If a tag is empty for someone, you'll see it here rather than hearing about it later. Send Now asks you to confirm the count, then it goes. A small send finishes while you wait; a big one is queued, and you can watch it land under Send, Campaigns. We'll leave the sending for the real thing today.

For sends you repeat, or want to schedule for later, Email Campaigns under Send filter by role, status and custom fields, can leave out people who already got it, and report opens and clicks afterwards. Same templates, same tags.

## Create an email

### Select the recipients

In **Contacts**, **Sessions**, **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors**, filter first and select second. Apply the filters that describe your audience (session status, track, participant role, a task's completion status, any custom field), then tick the rows you want — or the checkbox at the top of the table for the whole page. When a filter matches more than one page, the banner above the table offers **Select all N contacts matching this filter**, so you never have to send in batches.

![The Contacts table filtered to accepted speakers, all rows selected, with the Send menu open](/images/kb/send-emails-menu.png)

In the bulk bar, click **Send → Send Emails**. In Sessions the button is simply **Send Emails**.

### Fill in the envelope

The composer opens with the details on the left and the message on the right.

![The Send Email composer: recipient and reply-to fields on the left, subject and message body on the right](/images/kb/send-email-details.png)

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Who should receive this email?** | Which address on each selected record gets the email. In Contacts that is the contact's email by default, with any email field on the record as an alternative. In Sessions you choose the people — **Everyone**, **Session Participants**, **Session Speakers**, **Primary Speakers**, **Session Chairpersons**, **Session Moderators**, **Session Submitters**, any custom role, or **Select Individual Contacts**. In Sponsors and Exhibitors it is **Primary Contacts** or **Group Users**. |
| **Include additional contacts:** | Contacts and Sessions only. **Include additional contacts of recipients (CC)** copies each recipient's [additional contacts](/contacts/additional-contacts) — an assistant or co-author — **Send only to additional contacts of recipients** emails them instead, and **Do not include additional contacts** leaves them out. |
| **Send one email per session** | Sessions only. Everyone on a session is addressed together in a single email instead of each person receiving their own copy. Personalization uses the primary speaker. |
| **Replies sent to:** | The one address that receives replies. |
| **Sender Name** | The name shown in the inbox. Blank uses your event name. |
| **Send from:** | Only shown when your event has a [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain); the address must be on that domain. Otherwise the email comes from `no-reply@notify.sessionboard.com`. |
| **CC:** / **BCC:** | Up to five addresses each, separated by commas. Invalid addresses are ignored. |

### Write the message

On the right, add a **Subject Line** and the **Message Body**. To start from something you already wrote, click **Template**, pick from your **Email Templates**, and the subject, body, reply-to and sender fields fill in — you can still edit any of them for this send. Only templates whose **Type** matches the module are offered, so a Sessions template will not appear when you send from Contacts.

![The Template picker open above the message body](/images/kb/send-email-template.png)

To personalize, click **Merge Tags** and click a tag to drop it at your cursor. Each tag becomes that recipient's own value when the email is sent, so one send greets everyone by name and gives each speaker their own portal link.

Sending from **Sessions** also gives you tags for the session itself — title, date and time, room, length — and a **Session Roles** group that names the session's speakers, moderators, chairpersons and custom roles, as names alone or with each person's title and company. [Email moderators and chairpersons](/faq/how-to-email-moderators-chairpersons#naming-them-in-the-message-body) lists them.

### Review & Preview

Click **Review**. Every recipient is listed on the left with **Select all** / **Deselect all** and an **Include in send** switch, so you can drop someone without going back to the table. Click a name and the right side shows the email exactly as that person will receive it — their address, the subject, and every merge tag filled in. A tag that is empty for someone shows up here, not in their inbox.

![Review & Preview, one recipient selected and their personalized email shown on the right](/images/kb/send-email-review.png)

### Send

Click **Send Now**. **Confirm Send** tells you how many recipients you are about to email and that the action cannot be undone; click the **Send to … recipients** button.

![The Confirm Send dialog](/images/kb/send-email-confirm.png)

Up to 100 recipients send while you wait and the composer reports **Email Sent Successfully!** Larger sends are queued: the composer reports **Email Queued**, and **View progress** takes you to **Send → Campaigns**, where the send appears with its delivery counts as it runs. Either way, every message is recorded per recipient in [History](/communications/email-sms-history).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What is the email address of the sender?">

By default, emails are sent from `no-reply@notify.sessionboard.com` with your event name as the sender. With a [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain), a **Send from** field appears and you pick an address on your domain.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send a test email to myself?">

Two ways. **Review & Preview** shows the email exactly as each recipient will receive it, merge tags filled in, before you send. And on any [email template](/settings/email-templates#send-a-test), **Send test** mails the template to an address you enter, personalized as a record you choose, without creating a send record.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a limit on how many emails I can send at once?">

No. Select as many records as you need — **Select all N matching this filter** selects across pages. Sends of up to 100 recipients complete while you wait; larger sends are queued and tracked on **Send → Campaigns**. For sends you repeat, or when you want to skip people who already received an email, use [Email Campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns), which filter an audience directly and can **Omit past recipients**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I include attachments?">

Add files to the [portal](/portals/share-files) and direct recipients there to view and download them, or link to the file in the message body.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use more than one address in Replies sent to, or add CC and BCC?">

**Replies sent to** takes one address. **CC** and **BCC** each take up to five addresses, separated by commas. [Additional contacts](/contacts/additional-contacts) are copied when you choose **Include additional contacts of recipients (CC)**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we email only the right people — accepted speakers, one track, or everyone except a few?">

Filter the module first, then select. In Contacts, Speakers, Sessions, Sponsors or Exhibitors, apply filters (session status, track, role, a task's completion status, any custom field), select the matching records and click **Send**. On **Review & Preview**, switch off **Include in send** for anyone who should be excluded. Sending from **Sessions** emails the people on those sessions and adds session merge tags. For recurring or larger sends, [Email Campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns) filter by participant role, submission status, acceptance status and custom fields, combine filters with **All filters (AND)** or **Any filter (OR)**, and can **Omit past recipients**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which emails go out automatically, and which do we have to send?">

Sessionboard sends [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails) for submission confirmations, draft reminders, portal assignments and digests, evaluation invitations and reminders, and account messages. **Acceptance and decline emails are yours to send**: use the built-in **Accept Sessions** and **Decline Sessions** [email templates](/settings/email-templates) after you [update statuses](/sessions/accept-decline), or build an [automation](/automations/overview) that emails the people on a session when its status changes so no one is missed.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do templates and merge tags work?">

Create templates under **Settings → Email Templates**; each has a **Type** (Contacts, Exhibitors/Sponsors, Sessions) that decides which merge tags it can use and where it appears. Pick one with **Template** when writing an email, then personalize with **Merge Tags**, which are replaced per recipient. Sessionboard ships **Accept Sessions**, **Decline Sessions**, **Session Form - One Day Reminder** and **Session Form - Five Days Reminder** templates you can customize. See [email templates](/settings/email-templates) and the [merge tags reference](/communications/email-campaigns#merge-tags-reference).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can emails come from our own domain and carry our logo?">

Yes. A [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain) authenticates your domain so sends come from your address. [Email themes](/settings/email-themes) add your event logo as a header banner and set colors and fonts for campaigns.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A speaker says they never got our email">

Check the delivery **Status** in [History](/communications/email-sms-history) for that send, confirm the address on their contact profile, ask them to check spam, and have their IT team allowlist the sending domain. The step-by-step is in [why are my speakers not receiving emails?](/faq/why-are-my-speakers-sponsors-exhibitors-not-receiving-emails). Anyone on the [suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list) has bounced or unsubscribed and is skipped.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can team members control which notifications they receive?">

Yes. Each admin chooses how alerts reach them (in product, by email, or in Slack) and how often, and an organization can set a policy for everyone. See [notifications and messaging](/communications/notifications).

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
