Sessionboard lets you email event participants directly from the platform.

## Create an email

From the **Contacts**, **Sessions**, **Sponsors**, or **Exhibitors** module, check the records you want to email. In **Contacts**, click **Send → Send Emails**; other modules have a direct **Send Emails** button.

<Warning>
You can send up to **100 emails at once**. Set your module view to 100 records per page. For more than 100 recipients, send in batches.
</Warning>

## Configure the email

In the pop-up, set the left-side fields:

- **Who should receive this email?** — the primary recipient(s), with an option to **include additional contacts**
- **Replies sent to** — where replies arrive (one address only)
- **Send from** — the sending address
- **CC / BCC** (optional) — up to five addresses each, comma-separated

Compose the subject and body on the right, and click **Template** to apply a pre-built template.

## Review & send

Click each contact to preview how the email renders for that recipient, then click **Send Now**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the default sender address?">All emails send from `no-reply@notify.sessionboard.com` unless your event uses a [custom email domain](/communications/create-send-emails).</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a test first?">The Review & Preview page shows how each recipient will see the email before you send.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I attach files?">Upload files to the portal instead, where users can download them directly.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do additional contacts get CC'd?">Only if you select "include additional contacts of recipients (CC)."</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
