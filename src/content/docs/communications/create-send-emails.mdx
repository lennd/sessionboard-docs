<Tip>

</Tip>

## Create an Email
Within the module you intend to send your email from (Contacts, Sessions, Sponsors, or Exhibitors), check the box next to the record(s) you wish to email. In the Contacts module, click Send and choose 'Send Emails' from the dropdown; in other modules, the button is labeled 'Send Emails' directly.

<Note>
**💡 TIP**: To send an email to more than one contact/session/group, select all of the contacts/sessions/groups you would like to email or click the first square to select all within the page.

**Contacts Module**

![1-Jul-07-2026-03-33-15-5191-PM](/images/kb/374bcf32-1-Jul-07-2026-03-33-15-5191-PM.jpg)

**Sessions Module**

![1-Jul-07-2026-03-33-15-5191-PM](/images/kb/6089585a-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-34-56-4585-PM.png)

Increase the number of contacts/sessions/groups you see within your module to show 100 records. When sending emails, only 100 emails can be sent at one time. Contacts/sessions/groups that exceed 100 will need to be sent in groups.

![Increase the number of contacts/sessions/groups you see within your module to show 100 records](/images/kb/5749ff33-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-37-52-6001-PM.png)
</Note>

In the pop-up, fill in the fields on the left side of the screen:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Who should receive this email?** | The email recipients. |
| **Include additional contacts** | Whether additional contacts are included as recipients. |
| **Replies sent to** | The address that receives replies to your email. |
| **Send From** | The address the email is sent from. |
| **CC** *(optional)* | Up to five email addresses, separated by commas. |
| **BCC** *(optional)* | Up to five email addresses, separated by commas. |

![The email composer with the recipient and reply-to fields on the left](/images/kb/6e204f45-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-38-31-4906-PM.png)

On the right side of the window, write the email by adding a subject and message body. To use a template you've already created, click **Template** at the top right.

![The Template button above the message body](/images/kb/866adfa1-undefined-Feb-24-2026-11-44-15-8380-PM.png)

To personalize the message, click **Merge Tags** and click a tag to drop it in at your cursor. Each tag is replaced with that recipient's own values when the email goes out, so a single send can greet everyone by name.

Sending from the **Sessions** module also gives you tags for the session itself — its title, date and time, room, and length in minutes — plus a **Session Roles** group that names the session's speakers, moderators, chairpersons, and any custom role you've created, either as names alone or with each person's job title and company. [Email moderators and chairpersons](/faq/how-to-email-moderators-chairpersons#naming-them-in-the-message-body) shows the full list.

Once the email is built, click each contact to preview how it will look for that recipient.

![Previewing the email as it will appear for an individual recipient](/images/kb/82a11340-image-png-Jul-07-2026-03-37-17-1781-PM.png)

Then click **Send Now** and confirm on the next screen.

![The send confirmation screen](/images/kb/6c3794be-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-44-52-6977-PM.png)

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What is the email address of the sender?">

By default, emails are sent from `no-reply@notify.sessionboard.com` unless your event uses an [event email address on your own domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send a test email to myself?">

Before you send, the **Review & Preview** page shows the email exactly as each recipient will receive it, with their merge tags filled in.

![The Review & Preview page shows the email as each recipient will receive it](/images/kb/d25ede03-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-44-17-9998-PM.png)

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send more than 100 emails at a time?">

Each send is capped at 100 emails. Split a larger audience across several sends, or use [Email Campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns), which filters and sends to a whole segment.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I include attachments?">

Add files to the [portal](/portals/share-files) and direct recipients there to view and download them.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use more than one address in Replies Sent To, or add CC and BCC?">

**Replies Sent To** takes one address. **CC** and **BCC** each take up to five addresses, separated by commas. [Additional Contacts](/contacts/additional-contacts) are CC'd when you choose **include additional contacts of recipients (CC)**.

![Additional Contacts are CC'd when you choose include additional contacts of recipients (CC)](/images/kb/9d31dac7-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-46-03-4406-PM.png)

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we email only the right people — accepted speakers, one track, or everyone except a few?">

Filter the module first, then select. In Contacts, Speakers, Sessions, Sponsors, or Exhibitors, apply filters (session status, track, role, a task's completion status, any custom field), select the matching records, and click **Send**. Untick anyone who should be excluded before you send. Sending from **Sessions** emails the people on those sessions and adds session merge tags (title, time, room, roles). For recurring or larger sends, [Email Campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns) filter by participant role, submission status, acceptance status, and custom fields, combine filters with **Match all** or **Match any**, and can **Omit Past Recipients**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which emails go out automatically, and which do we have to send?">

Sessionboard sends [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails) for submission confirmations, draft reminders, portal assignments and digests, evaluation invitations and reminders, and account messages. **Acceptance and decline emails are yours to send**: use the built-in **Accept** and **Decline** [email templates](/settings/email-templates) after you [update statuses](/sessions/accept-decline), or build an [automation](/automations/overview) that emails the people on a session when its status changes so no one is missed.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do templates and merge tags work?">

Create templates under **Settings > Email Templates**; each has a **Type** (Contacts, Groups, Sessions) that decides which merge tags it can use and where it appears. Pick a template with **Template** when writing an email, then personalize with **Merge Tags**, which are replaced per recipient. Sessionboard ships **Accept**, **Decline**, **One Day Reminder**, and **Five Days Reminder** templates you can customize. See [email templates](/settings/email-templates) and the [merge tags reference](/communications/email-campaigns#merge-tags-reference).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can emails come from our own domain and carry our logo?">

Yes. A [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain) authenticates your domain so sends come from your address. [Email themes](/settings/email-themes) add your event logo as a header banner and set colors and fonts for campaigns.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A speaker says they never got our email">

Check the **Delivery** status in [History](/communications/email-sms-history) for that send, confirm the address on their contact profile, ask them to check spam, and have their IT team allowlist the sending domain. The step-by-step is in [why are my speakers not receiving emails?](/faq/why-are-my-speakers-sponsors-exhibitors-not-receiving-emails). Anyone on the [suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list) has bounced or unsubscribed and is skipped.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can team members control which notifications they receive?">

Yes. Each admin chooses how alerts reach them (in product, by email, or in Slack) and how often, and an organization can set a policy for everyone. See [notifications and messaging](/communications/notifications).

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
