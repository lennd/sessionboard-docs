Navigate to the History module to view a history of all emails and SMS messages sent to event contacts and team members. You can also review integration sync runs, download previous data exports, and view an audit log that shows actions completed by your event contacts or event team.

## Emails

The Emails page within the History module provides a comprehensive log of all email communications created and sent through the platform by event team members, as well as the below system-generated emails.
- New Team Member Invitation
- Session Submission Form Confirmation
- Application Confirmation
- Email Notification (i.e., portal task assignment)
- Weekly Email Digest
- Evaluation Plan Weekly Reminders
- System notification emails such as **New review assigned** and new-message alerts. These are now recorded in **Sent Emails** as well; previously they were delivered but left no trace in History, so a plan could email six reviewers and the ledger would still read zero.

### History (Campaigns & Sent Emails)

![History](/images/kb/82b9d1e7-undefined-Feb-02-2026-09-36-07-3744-PM.png)

- CAMPAIGNS An email campaign is a coordinated series of emails sent to a targeted group of recipients with a specific purpose. Email campaigns might be used to notify speakers of important deadlines, share acceptance updates, or guide them through the submission process. Campaign performance is tracked using metrics such as open rates, click-through rates, and delivery status. Each row represents an individual email campaign with the following columns:
  - **Subject Line:** Title of the email (click to view campaign details).
  - **Module:** What module the campaign was sent from (i.e. Sessions, Contacts, Sponsors).
  - **Total Recipients:** Number of people the email was sent to.
  - **Unique Opens:** Number of recipients who opened the email.
  - **Sent By:** Sender’s name
  - **Sent At:** Date and time the email was sent.

Clicking a campaign subject opens a pop-up model with campaign details:

- **Sent By:** Sender's name.
  - **Sent At:** Date and time sent.
  - **Recipients List:** Link to view recipient(s).
    - Selecting “view recipients” displays:
      - Recipient Name

  - **Total Recipients / Unique Opens / Unique Clicks:** Key engagement metrics.

  - **Sent By:** The name of the user who sent the email.
  - **Sent At:** The date and time the email was sent.

### Email status definitions

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | Handed to our email provider and accepted for delivery. The recipient's mail server still owes us a delivery report. |
| **Processed** | The provider has the message queued for delivery. |
| **Delivered** | The recipient's mail server accepted the message. This does not mean anyone read it. |
| **Opened** | The recipient opened the email. |
| **Clicked** | The recipient clicked at least one link in the email. |
| **Deferred** | The recipient's mail server asked us to try again later. Delivery is still being attempted. |
| **Bounced** | The recipient's mail server rejected the message. Common causes are a full inbox, an address that does not exist, or a security filter. |
| **Blocked** | The recipient's mail server refused the message, usually a reputation or policy rule on their side. |
| **Dropped** | We did not attempt delivery, because this address previously bounced, unsubscribed, or reported spam. |
| **Marked as spam** | The recipient reported the message as spam. This also unsubscribes them. |
| **Unsubscribed** | The recipient opted out through the unsubscribe link. |
| **Failed** | The send itself was rejected before delivery was attempted. Nothing reached the recipient. |
| **No delivery data** | We have no report about this recipient yet. Not a failure — just no information. |

**On "No delivery data".** Providers report delivery for a while, not forever, and some system-generated emails are sent through a path that does not record a per-person outcome. Where we have no report, we say so rather than assume. Previously these rows displayed as **Delivered**, which made an unreported email — including one that failed — look confirmed.

If a recipient tells you they never received something and the row reads **No delivery data**, check the [suppression list](/communications/email-suppression-list) first: a blocked address is the most common cause.

### What we track, and what we don't

Every person **addressed by name** on an email gets their own row in History, with their own status:

- The **To** recipient.
- Each **session participant on the CC line** when the campaign uses "Bundle session participants onto one email" — the session's primary speaker is on To, their co-presenters and moderator are on CC, and all of them appear in History individually.
- Each **additional contact** (assistant, coordinator) CC'd through the "CC the primary contact's additional contacts" option.

Two things are delivered but **not** tracked per person:

- **A campaign-level CC address.** This is one fixed address copied onto every message in the send, usually an internal mailbox. It receives the mail; it does not get History rows or delivery statuses.
- **BCC, in all cases.** BCC is not tracked at all — no History rows, no delivery status, no open or click attribution, and blocked addresses have no effect on it. If you need a delivery record for someone, address them on To or CC rather than BCC.

Both still appear on the sent message itself, so you can always see who was copied by opening the email preview.

The Emails page allows teams to track communication history, verify delivery, and troubleshoot email-related issues more efficiently. You can also search emails by recipient name or address using the search bar at the top of the page.

## SMS

<Note>
Learn more about creating & sending SMS.
</Note>

The SMS tab displays a record of text messages sent through the platform. This allows users to track communication sent for event reminders, updates, or important notifications.

Each SMS entry includes the following information:
- **Recipient:** The name of the person receiving the message.
- **Phone Number:** A masked version of the recipient’s phone number for privacy.
- **Message:** The content of the SMS can be previewed by clicking the message link.

  ![SMS](/images/kb/e0ab0ba7-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-41-21-7968-PM.png)

- **Status:** Displays whether the message was queued, sent, or encountered an error.
- **Sent By:** The name of the user who initiated the message.
- **Sent At:** The date and time the message was sent.

You can search by name or phone number using the search bar at the top of the page.

## Integrations

If your event is connected to a third-party integration (such as Visit, LiveBuzz, Brella, Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo, or Swapcard), the Integrations tab shows a history of every sync run so you can confirm data made it across and troubleshoot failures without contacting support.

Each sync record includes:
- Integration: Which connected integration ran the sync.
- External Event: The mapped event in the partner system (the Visit expo ID, LiveBuzz campaign, and so on).
- Sync ID: A unique identifier for the run (useful when working with support).
- Results: Click **Click to view results** for counts of created, updated, skipped, deleted, and failed records. Skipped rows include the reason (consent not complete, no change since last sync, missing name or email). Failed rows include the vendor error.
- **Status:** Whether the run completed successfully, completed with errors, or failed.
- Started At / Ended At / Duration: When the sync ran and how long it took.

Visit, LiveBuzz, and Brella write the same history rows as the other integrations. The Results overlay is the event-team view of those counts and reasons.

## Exports

The Exports tab keeps a history of data exports generated from your event, so you can re-download a previous export without regenerating it.

Each export entry includes:
- Export File: The file name (click to download).
- Exported At: The date and time the export was generated.
- Size: The file size.
- **Status:** Whether the export completed successfully.

## Audit

The Audit tab provides a chronological activity log that tracks all significant actions taken within the platform. This serves as a transparent history of updates and changes made to contacts, sessions, evaluation plans, and more.

View the audit log for a specific session through the session page.

![View the audit log for a specific session through the session page](/images/kb/b9583876-image-png-Feb-02-2026-09-43-11-8950-PM.png)

Each audit entry displays:
- **Subject:** Displays the name of the item (e.g., a session title or contact name) where the change was made.
- **Type:** Indicates the category of the item being modified (e.g., Session, Contact, Exhibitor, Sponsor).
- **User:** Shows the name and email of the person who performed the action.
- **Action:** Specifies what was done, such as Create, Update, or Delete.
- **Field:** Identifies the specific field within the subject that was changed (e.g., Start Time, Location, Track).
- **New Value:** Displays the updated value that was entered for the field.
- **Occurred At:** Provides the exact date and time when the change took place.

### Changes to your setup, not just your records

The audit log covers two different kinds of change, and it helps to know which one you are looking for.

Most entries are **changes to a record** — someone edited a session's start time, or a contact's company. The Subject is the record, and the Field and New Value tell you what moved.

Other entries are **changes to how your event or organization is set up**. These look the same in the table but the Subject is a piece of configuration rather than a record:

- **Field** — someone renamed a field, changed its type, or added, renamed or removed a dropdown option. This is a change to the field itself, everywhere it appears, as opposed to one record's answer. Deleting a field is recorded too, and the entry keeps the deleted field's name so the history still reads properly afterwards.
- **Org member** — someone was invited to the organization, had their permission role changed, or had their access removed.
- **Permission profile** — a profile was renamed, its permission set was changed, or it was deleted. A permission change records how many permissions the profile held before and after rather than listing each one.
- **API token** — a token was created, edited or revoked. The token value itself is never recorded, here or anywhere else.

Changes made through the public API appear alongside the rest, attributed to the user the API token belongs to.

### Narrowing the log

**Type** and **Action** are grouped, so you can jump to the part of the log you want without reading the whole list. Type separates records from configuration and from access and security; Action separates everyday Create, Update and Delete from access events such as **Invite sent** and **Access revoked**.

Both filters narrow the CSV export as well as what you see on screen, so exporting after filtering gives you just those entries.

<Note>
The filters only list categories your Sessionboard account can actually produce entries for, so the list grows as more of the product is covered. If a category you expect is missing, there is nothing recorded under it yet.
</Note>
