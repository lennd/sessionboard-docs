On event day nobody is reading email. A text reaches a speaker between sessions, a sponsor on the show floor, an exhibitor at their booth. Sessionboard sends SMS from the same composer you use for [email](/communications/create-send-emails): filter the module, select the people, write the message, check the preview, send.

<Note>
SMS is one-way. Recipients cannot reply to the message — put a phone number or a desk location in the text if you need them to reach you. Sessionboard's number is set per region and cannot be changed; recipients can text **STOP** to opt out and **START** to opt back in.
</Note>

## When a text beats an email

- **Speakers, moderators, chairpersons** — "Your session *AI in Healthcare* starts at 10:30 in Room B2; please arrive 15 minutes early for a mic check." A last-minute room change. A deck that still has not been uploaded.
- **Sponsors** — booth setup opens at 7:00; the networking breakfast is in the VIP lounge; attendee traffic peaks at 11:00.
- **Exhibitors** — where the lead-scanner help desk is; the exhibitor lunch moved to 1:00; tear-down starts at 4:00 and not before.

## Send an SMS

SMS is sent from **Contacts**, **Speakers**, **Sponsors** and **Exhibitors** — not from Sessions. Recipients need a number in the standard **Mobile Phone** field (or another phone-type field you choose).

1. Filter the module to the people you want, then tick the rows — or the checkbox at the top for the page, or **Select all N matching this filter** when the filter spans pages.
2. In the bulk bar, click **Send → Send SMS**.

![The Send SMS composer, with the recipient field and the SMS body](/images/kb/send-sms-compose.png)

3. **Who should receive this SMS?** defaults to **Contact Mobile Phone**; any other phone field on the record is offered too. For sponsors and exhibitors you choose between the group's primary contact and all of its users.
4. Write the **SMS Body** — up to 320 characters, and the counter under the box keeps score. Merge tags work here as in email — `{{{first_name}}}` becomes each person's name — and there is no template picker, so keep a few standard texts handy.
5. Click **Review**.

## Review and send

**Review & Preview** lists every recipient with their number; click one and **SMS Preview** shows the exact text they will get, merge tags filled in. Anyone without a usable number is counted at the top — *N have phone issues to resolve* — and is left out of the send; switch off **Include in send** for anyone else you want to drop. Fix a missing number on the contact's record and reopen the composer to include them.

![SMS Preview: recipients on the left, the rendered text for the selected person on the right, with the phone-issue count](/images/kb/send-sms-review.png)

Click **Send Now**, then confirm. Small sends finish while you wait (**SMS Sent Successfully!**); larger ones are queued and report progress under **Send → Campaigns**.

## See what was sent

**History → SMS** lists every text — **Recipient**, a masked **Phone Number**, the **Message**, its **Status**, **Sent By** and **Sent At** — with an **Errors** view for anything undelivered. A single person's texts are also on their contact record under **Activity → Communications**. See [History](/communications/email-sms-history#sms).

<Tip>
A text interrupts, so save them for the time-critical: the day-of reminder, the room change, the file that is due today. Everything else is an [email](/communications/create-send-emails) or a portal [task](/portals/assign-tasks).
</Tip>
