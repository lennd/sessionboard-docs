Exhibitors and sponsors are CRM accounts. With Expo on, an account's profile in the event shows an **Expo** section with its booth, applications and orders.

## Why use it

When an exhibitor calls, you want their booth, their application and their balance in one glance — next to their contacts, tier and agreements rather than in a separate exhibit system.

## Open the Expo section

1. In the event, open **CRM → Exhibitors** or **CRM → Sponsors** and click the company.
2. Open the **CRM** tab of the profile.
3. Under **Expo**:
   - **Booth** — each booth assigned on the floor map, with its package and status.
   - **Applications** — each application, linked to Booth sales, with its status.
   - **Orders** — each order, linked to Registration › Orders, with its status, and the amount outstanding.

**Open Booth sales** goes to [Booth sales](/expo/booth-sales). The account's agreements stay in the same tab, from CRM › Contracts.
