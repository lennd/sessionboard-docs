**Expo → Shop** is the catalog of add-ons exhibitors order from their portal and pay for by card or invoice.

## Why use it

Service orders — a power drop, an extra table, more lead licences — usually arrive by email the week before the show. The shop lets exhibitors order them themselves, charges the late price automatically after your deadline, and puts each order on the exhibitor's account and in Registration › Orders.

## Add a shop item

Click **Shop item**, give it a name, description and price, and under **Shop** set:

- **Category** — **Furniture**, **Electrical**, **Internet**, **Lead retrieval licence**, **Cleaning** or **Other**.
- **Order by** — the order deadline.
- **Late price** — charged after the order-by date.

The **Catalog** groups items by category and shows each one's price, late price and how many were sold. Archiving an item takes it out of the shop; existing orders keep it.

Exhibitors see the shop under **Shop** in their [exhibitor portal](/participants/expo-exhibitor-portal). Their orders show on their account and in [Registration → Orders](/registration/orders-and-invoices).
