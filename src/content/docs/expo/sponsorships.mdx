**Expo → Sponsorships** holds what the Exhibit & sponsor page sells: **Booth packages** that go with a booth, and **Sponsorship inventory** — limited items like the Wi-Fi sponsorship or a session sponsorship.

## Why use it

Sponsorship inventory is limited and often sold by several people at once. Selling it from one live list means the last keynote sponsorship cannot be sold twice, each sponsor's deliverables turn into tasks with due dates in their portal, and the tier they bought lands on their CRM account.

## Booth packages

Click **Booth package** and set its name, description, price and how many can be sold. Under **Booth**:

- **Booth type** — **Any**, **Inline**, **Corner**, **Peninsula** or **Island**.
- **Size** — for example `10x10`.
- **Staff badges** — how many staff badges the package includes.
- **Lead licences** — how many staff can scan leads.

Buyers pick an open booth of this type on the floor map. A booth's own list price on the map overrides the package price.

## Sponsorships

Click **Sponsorship** and set its name, price and **How many to sell** — the live inventory across the public page, the exhibitor portal and staff sales. Under **Sponsorship**:

- **Sponsor tier** — the tier from your sponsor tiers it grants. **Sponsor tiers** at the top opens them.
- **Staff badges** and **Lead licences**.
- **Deliverables** — what the sponsor owes you, such as a logo or ad artwork, each with **Days before** the event it is due. Each deliverable becomes a task in the buyer's exhibitor portal and a row on [Deadlines](/expo/deadlines).

The **Sponsorship inventory** table shows sold against quantity, **sold out** when none are left, and who bought each one. Archiving an item stops it selling; existing orders keep it.

<Note>
Packages and sponsorships are sold through the same checkout and orders as registration, through your organization's own payment gateway.
</Note>
