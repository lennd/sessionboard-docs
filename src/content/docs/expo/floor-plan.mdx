**Expo → Floor plan** opens the event's floor maps (also under **Publish → Floor Maps**). Expo does not have a separate map: booth sales, holds and applications read and write the booths you draw here.

## Why use it

One map means one truth about the hall. When a booth is held, sold or reassigned in Booth sales, the public booth picker, the exhibitor portal and the Attend app all show it at once — no exporting a spreadsheet to a map vendor and no double-sold corner booth.

## Draw the floor

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a map">
Open **Expo → Floor plan** and click **New Floor Map**, or **Import a floor plan** from a link to an existing plan.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the scale and background">
Open **Map settings** to choose **Feet** or **Meters**, the default booth size and status colors, and add a **Hall background** (a PDF page of the venue plan works). **Set scale** by measuring a known segment.
</Step>
<Step title="Add booths">
Draw the **Exhibit floor** region, then use **Add booth** — or **Auto-pack booths** to fill the region — and add **Aisles** and **Facilities**.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe each booth">
Click a booth and set it in the **Inspector**: **Number**, **Status**, **Type** (**Linear**, **Perimeter**, **Endcap**, **Peninsula**, **Island** or **Custom**), **List price**, and the **Exhibitor / sponsor** it is assigned to.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish">
![The floor map designer with a booth selected and the Inspector showing its number, size, status, type, list price and exhibitor](/images/kb/expo-floor-plan-designer.png)

Click **Publish** so the map shows on the Exhibit & sponsor page, the exhibitor portal and the Attend app.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Booth statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** | Open for sale; exhibitors can pick it. |
| **Hold** | Held for someone until a date — for an application, a rebook, or while a buyer checks out. |
| **Reserved** | Taken by an approved application or order, not yet paid in full. |
| **Sold** | Paid. |
| **Blocked** | Not for sale. |

You can change a status on the map, or hold and release booths from **Expo → Booth sales → Booths**. A booth's **List price** overrides its package price.
