**Expo → Floor plan** opens the event's floor maps (also under **Publish → Floor Maps**). The floor plan is part of **Expo Sales** (formerly Floor Maps): booth sales, holds, applications, the exhibitor portal and the attendee map all read and write the booths you draw here.

## Why use it

One map means one truth about the hall. When a booth is held, sold or reassigned in Booth sales, the booth picker on the Exhibit & sponsor page, the exhibitor portal, the attendee map and the Attend app all show it at once — no exporting a spreadsheet to a map vendor and no double-sold corner booth. And because the drawing and the sales are kept apart, you can keep reshaping the hall after you start selling without losing a single hold or sale.

## Start a map

The **Floor Maps** page shows a card per map with a preview of its first hall, its **Published** or **Draft** badge, and its halls, booths and how many are sold.

![Two floor map cards with a preview of each hall, Exhibit Hall marked Published with 95 booths and 16 sold, and Lobby Tabletops marked Draft with 60 booths](/images/kb/expo-floor-maps-list.png)

Click **New Floor Map** and choose what to **Start from**:

| Start from | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Blank hall** | An empty hall. Draw booths yourself, or bring in the venue plan from **Map settings**. |
| **Exhibit hall · ~100 booths** | 10×10 booths in back-to-back rows, 10 ft aisles, an entrance and services. |
| **Large hall · ~250 booths** | 10×10 booths in three blocks, with cross-aisles every seven booths. |
| **Tabletop expo · ~60 tables** | 8×6 tabletop spaces for a conference foyer or ballroom. |
| **Poster hall · ~80 boards** | 8×4 poster boards back to back, numbered for the abstract program. |

![The New Floor Map dialog with the five starters, Exhibit hall · ~100 booths selected, and the map name filled in](/images/kb/expo-floor-maps-new-map.png)

Use a starter as a first draft and reshape it; every booth is an ordinary booth you can move, merge or delete.

### Import an existing plan by link

If last year's show or the venue already has a plan on another platform, click **Import…** (or **Import from URL** on an empty page) and paste a public **ExpoFP**, **eShow** or **Map Your Show** link. The import brings booth shapes, statuses, exhibitors, the venue floor-plan background, zones and featured-exhibitor markers at real scale when the show publishes a public floor plan. A show without one comes in as an exhibitor list laid out on a grid you can rearrange.

Exhibitor names from the import are shown on each booth as **Imported exhibitor**; search in the Inspector to link the matching account.

### Bring in the venue plan

Open **Map settings** (the gear in the designer) and, under **Hall background**:

- **Floor plan image** — a PNG, JPG or SVG of the hall.
- **Import CAD drawing (DXF or SVG)** — an AutoCAD or EXPOCAD export. A DXF that draws booths as closed shapes with a number inside becomes booths on the map, and a drawing with units sets the scale for you. Drawings over 40 MB are refused; export just the exhibit hall. **Map settings** stays open while the drawing is read and uploaded, so nothing on the hall changes under the import.
- **Use a page of a venue PDF** — pick the page with the hall plan.

Then **Calibrate scale (two clicks)**: click both ends of something you know the length of and type it in. Prices by area and walking times on the attendee map depend on the scale.

## Lay out booths

<Steps>
<Step title="Set units and the exhibit floor">
In **Map settings**, choose **Feet** or **Meters**, the grid and the default booth size. With the **Exhibit floor** tool, draw the region booths may go in.
</Step>
<Step title="Add booths">
Press **R** (**Add booth**) and click the floor, draw an odd shape with **Polygon** (**P**), or use **Auto-pack booths** in Map settings to fill the exhibit floor from a size × count table with aisle gaps. Add **Aisles** and **Facilities** — entrances, restrooms, food, stairs, elevators, registration and more.
</Step>
<Step title="Describe each booth">
Click a booth and set it in the **Inspector**: **Number**, **Type** (**Linear**, **Perimeter**, **Endcap**, **Peninsula**, **Island** or **Custom**), size, position, **Rotation (°)**, **List price (USD)**, and the **Exhibitor / sponsor** it is assigned to.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
The designer saves as you work; **Save** (or Cmd/Ctrl + S) saves now.

![The floor map designer with a booth selected and the Inspector showing its number, size, status, type, list price and exhibitor](/images/kb/expo-floor-plan-designer.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

### Work faster with many booths

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Select several booths | Shift-click them, or Cmd/Ctrl + A for every booth on the layer. |
| Move the selection | Drag it, or use the arrow keys (one grid step; hold Shift for a fine step). Locked booths stay put — **L** locks and unlocks a booth. |
| Copy a row | Cmd/Ctrl + C, then Cmd/Ctrl + V. The row drops in below as the next row, numbered after the hall's highest number. Cmd/Ctrl + D duplicates in place. |
| Make an L, T or island booth | Select booths that share an edge and click **Merge _n_ booths** (Cmd/Ctrl + M). Booths must be rectangles at 0° rotation. |
| Edit many at once | With several booths selected, set **Status**, **Type**, price, fill colour or size for all of them. **Select similar booths** picks every booth like the one you clicked. |
| Renumber | Press **N** or click **Renumber by clicking**, set the **First number** and **Step**, then click the booths in order. |
| Find a booth | Type a booth number or exhibitor in **Find booth or exhibitor** (Cmd/Ctrl + F). |
| Undo | Cmd/Ctrl + Z; Shift + Cmd/Ctrl + Z to redo. |

![The designer with four adjoining booths selected and the Inspector showing their combined area and price, shared Status, Type and price fields, and the Merge 4 booths button](/images/kb/expo-floor-plan-merge-booths.png)

### Price booths by area

Under **Pricing** in Map settings, set a **Price per sq ft** (or m²) and a surcharge per booth type for open sides — corner, peninsula and island booths sell above linear. **Price every booth on this hall** writes the list price for all of them; **By area** in the Inspector prices just the selected booths. A booth's **List price** overrides its package price.

## Walkways and accessibility

Directions on the attendee map and **My route** in the Attend app walk along the hall's walkways. On the **Wayfinding** layer, click **Generate walkways** to lay them along the aisles between booths, or **Draw a walkway** (**W**): click along an aisle and press Enter to finish.

Select a walkway and turn off **Step-free (wheelchair accessible)** where it takes stairs. Attendees who ask for a step-free route are kept off those walkways and change levels by elevator. Mark stairs, elevators and escalators as facilities so routes can cross between halls.

![The designer on the Wayfinding layer, with walkways along the aisles and a selected walkway's Step-free (wheelchair accessible) switch, Regenerate walkways and Draw a walkway in the Inspector](/images/kb/expo-floor-plan-walkways.png)

## Sponsor placements on the map

- **Images** layer — **Add a logo or image** for sponsor logos, signage or stage art, and set its **Opacity**. Images sit above the booths on the published map, so you can sell them as placements.
- **Logo in booth (sponsorship)** — on a booth, shows the assigned exhibitor's logo instead of the booth number.
- **Featured on the attendee map** — puts the booth's exhibitor first on the attendee map.
- **Header sponsor logos** — in Map settings, the sponsors whose logos run along the top of the attendee map.

![A sponsor's booth selected in the designer, drawn with its logo instead of the number, and the Inspector with Logo in booth (sponsorship) and Featured on the attendee map turned on](/images/kb/expo-floor-plan-sponsor-placements.png)

## Booth status and booth sales

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** | Open for sale; exhibitors can pick it. |
| **Hold** | Held for someone until a date — for an application, a rebook, or while a buyer checks out. |
| **Reserved** | Taken by an approved application or order, not yet paid in full. |
| **Sold** | Paid. |
| **Blocked** | Not for sale. |

Booth sales owns a booth's status, exhibitor and hold. When you change a booth's **Status** or **Exhibitor / sponsor** in the Inspector, that change is saved straight away, exactly as if you made it in [Booth sales](/expo/booth-sales). Saving or restoring the layout never changes them: you can move, renumber and resize booths while you sell, and every hold, reservation and sale stays with its exhibitor.

Setting a booth to **Available** frees it, as **Release** does in Booth sales: its exhibitor and hold are cleared. A booth assigned to an exhibitor application (and its order) cannot be made available from the designer; the Inspector says so and keeps its status. Release it in **Expo → Booth sales**.

<Warning>
A booth that is on hold, reserved, sold or on a live application cannot be deleted or merged away, and neither can the hall or map it is on. The designer names the booths booth sales is using; release them in **Expo → Booth sales** first. Deleting a hall or a map removes its booths from Booth sales, the Expo overview and the exhibitor's account in the portal.
</Warning>

## Version history

Click the history icon in the designer's toolbar to open **Version history** for the hall. Every save is a checkpoint, with when it was saved, who saved it and how many booths it had; a run of edits by one person within a few minutes is one entry. Click a version to preview it, then **Restore this version**. Restoring brings back the drawing and keeps booth sales: sold and held booths stay with their exhibitors.

![The Version history sheet for a hall, previewing an earlier version above a list of saves with their time, who saved them and their booth count, and the Restore this version button](/images/kb/expo-floor-plan-version-history.png)

## Publish, print and download

Open **Map settings** and click **Publish** so the map shows on the Exhibit & sponsor page, in the exhibitor portal, on the [attendee floor map](/expo/attendee-floor-map) and in the Attend app. Publishing needs at least one booth and no audit errors: the **Audit** in the Inspector lists booths that overlap (by their real shape), sit outside the exhibit floor, have no number or a duplicate number. Booths that only touch, as booths from an imported plan usually do, are not an overlap. **Unpublish** takes the map down.

**Preview** shows the hall as attendees see it. **Print** opens a printable plan of the hall, and the download button saves it as a PNG.
