**Expo → Exhibitor manual** holds the sections exhibitors and sponsors read in their portal — rules and regulations, move-in and move-out, insurance, freight — with documents and an "I have read this" acknowledgement.

## Why use it

A PDF manual gets emailed once and never read. Sections in the portal sit next to the exhibitor's tasks, carry their own due dates, and show you which companies have acknowledged the rules — useful when someone arrives without their insurance certificate.

## Write a section

1. Click **Section**, then select it in **Sections**.
2. Enter a **Title**, choose who it is **Shown to** (**Everyone**, **Exhibitors** or **Sponsors**) and an optional **Due** date.
3. Write the body in the editor.
4. Under **Documents**, click **Add document** and give each a **Name** and **Link**.
5. Turn on **Ask for acknowledgement** if exhibitors must confirm they have read it. It counts toward their deadlines.
6. Click **Save**.

![The exhibitor manual editor with the section list, title, audience, due date, body, a document and Ask for acknowledgement](/images/kb/expo-exhibitor-manual.png)

Use the arrows in **Sections** to change the order exhibitors see them in.

## Track acknowledgements

Each section in the list says how many companies have acknowledged it. Open the section to see **Acknowledged by** — the company, the person who acknowledged and when.

Deleting a section hides it from exhibitors. Acknowledgements already given are kept in History.
