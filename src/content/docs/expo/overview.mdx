**Expo** sells booths and sponsorships and gives exhibitors and sponsors a portal for everything they owe you before the show: agreements, invoices, staff badges, the exhibitor manual, deadlines and service orders. Exhibitors and sponsors are the CRM accounts you already have, and booths are the booths on your event's floor map.

<AddOnNote feature="expo">
{' '}Expo is in Early Access as a Beta: open **Preview** in the event sidebar, find the **Expo** card and choose **Request access**. Expo sells through Registration's orders and checkout, so the event needs Registration too.
</AddOnNote>

## Why use it

Exhibit sales usually run on a spreadsheet, a PDF prospectus and a pile of emailed agreements, while the booth map lives somewhere else. Expo keeps the floor map, the application, the agreement, the invoice and the exhibitor's to-do list on one record — the CRM account — so your sales team sees what is sold, what is signed and what is still owed, and exhibitors stop emailing you for their invoice.

## The Expo pages

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Booths sold, contracted and paid amounts, sponsorship sell-through, applications and deadlines. |
| **Floor plan** | Opens the event's floor map, where booths, prices and holds live. |
| **Booth sales** | Applications, booths, orders, rebooking and the pipeline. |
| **Sponsorships** | Booth packages and limited sponsorship inventory. |
| **Shop** | Add-ons exhibitors order from their portal: furniture, power, lead licences. |
| **Exhibitor portal** | What exhibitor and sponsor contacts see, and where each part is set up. |
| **Exhibitor manual** | Sections, documents and acknowledgements. |
| **Deadlines** | The exhibitor task list across every company. |

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step title="Draw the floor">
Draw halls and booths on the event floor map and give booths a status and list price. See [Lay out booths on the floor plan](/expo/floor-plan).
</Step>
<Step title="Create what you sell">
Add booth packages and sponsorships in **Expo → Sponsorships**, and service items in **Expo → Shop**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set booth-sales defaults">
In **Expo → Booth sales**, click **Settings** to set how long holds last, staff badges per booth, the agreement template, application questions, and whether exhibitors can buy a booth straight from the map.
</Step>
<Step title="Share the Exhibit & sponsor page">
Exhibitors apply or buy on the event Site's Exhibit & sponsor page. Sponsors who do not need a booth use its sponsor-only link.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Read the Overview

- **Booths sold** — sold out of the total, with how many are on hold and available.
- **Contracted** — the value of booth and sponsorship orders, with paid and outstanding amounts.
- **Sponsorships sold** — against your sponsorship inventory.
- **Deadlines** — exhibitor tasks overdue, and how many are due in the next seven days.
- **Booths** — sold, reserved, on hold, available and blocked booths across the event's floor maps, with a button per map.
- **Applications** — waiting, approved and declined. **Review applications** opens Booth sales; **Open Approvals** opens the queue.

Exhibitors and sponsors stay CRM accounts: tiers, contacts, intake forms and agreements are where they always were, and each account's profile shows its booth, orders and applications. See [See an exhibitor's Expo activity](/expo/crm-accounts).
