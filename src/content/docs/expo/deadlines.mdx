**Expo → Deadlines** is the exhibitor task list across every company: logo, insurance certificate, staff names, freight. Each deadline is a task in the portal of every exhibitor or sponsor it applies to.

## Why use it

Exhibitor deadlines slip one company at a time, and you find out at move-in. Deadlines shows, for each task, how many companies have done it and how many are overdue, so you know who to nudge a week out instead of at the loading dock.

## Add a deadline

Click **Deadline** and enter:

- **Task** — for example Upload certificate of insurance.
- **Instructions**.
- **Due** date.
- **For** — **Exhibitors**, **Sponsors** or **Everyone**.
- **Link** — optional: a form or page to complete it.
- **Required**.

Click **Add deadline**. The task is added to every matching portal.

## Read the list

Deadlines are listed by due date, with where each came from (**Portal task**, **Exhibitor manual** or **Sponsorship deliverable**), how many companies completed it out of the total, and how many are overdue.

Sponsorship deliverables are one row per buyer. Tick a deliverable to mark it **Done** when the sponsor delivers, or untick it to reopen it.

**Portal tasks** opens the organization's portal tasks, where these tasks live alongside the rest.
