Every published floor map of the event is also an attendee map on the web, with no login and no app to install. Attendees search for a company or booth, browse exhibitors by category, save the ones they want to visit, and get walking directions — on their phone, on your website, or on a kiosk in the hall.

## Why use it

Most attendees never download the event app, but every one of them has a browser. The web map puts the same live hall in front of them from a link, a QR code on a sign or your own website, so they find the booth they came for and exhibitors get the traffic they paid for. Because it reads the booths you sell in Expo, the map is never out of date: a booth sold this morning shows its exhibitor this afternoon.

## Open the map

The map lives on your event's Site, at the Site address followed by `/map/` and the event ID (the number after `/event/` in the admin URL):

```
https://sites.sessionboard.com/s/<your-slug>/map/<event-id>
```

On a [domain of your own](/site/site-builder#your-own-domain), it is `/map/<event-id>` at the root of that domain. A field event's page has it at `/e/<your-slug>/map`.

The map shows every hall of every **published** floor map of the event. Until a map is published, the page says the event has not published a floor map. Publish from [the floor plan designer](/expo/floor-plan#publish-print-and-download).

## What attendees can do

- **Search** companies, booth numbers and categories from the box at the top.
- Browse **Exhibitors** by category, with **Featured** exhibitors first and header sponsors along the top of the map.
- Tap a booth or exhibitor for its card: logo, description, website, and **Directions**. A zone linked to a session room shows what is on **Now** and **Up next in this room**.
- **Save** exhibitors to a **Saved** list and **Mark visited** as they go. Saves stay in that browser.
- **Share** a booth or exhibitor — the link opens the map on it.
- Switch between halls and levels, zoom, and **Show the whole hall**.

## Directions

**Directions** draws a route from a starting point — a booth, a company or an entrance — to the booth, with turn-by-turn steps and an estimated walking time. Routes follow the walkways on your floor plan and cross between levels by stairs, elevators and escalators. **Accessible route (no stairs)** keeps the route to step-free walkways and elevators, and says so when no step-free route exists.

![The attendee floor map with header sponsor logos, a route drawn along the aisle from the main entrance to Booth 102, and Directions listing the walking time, distance and turn-by-turn steps](/images/kb/expo-attendee-floor-map.png)

<Tip>
Draw or generate walkways before the show (see [Walkways and accessibility](/expo/floor-plan#walkways-and-accessibility)). Without them, the map draws the route as a straight line and labels it approximate.
</Tip>

## Link to a booth, an exhibitor or a spot in the hall

Add these to the map address:

| Add | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `?booth=<number>` | Opens that booth. |
| `?exhibitor=<id>` | Opens that exhibitor. |
| `?hall=<id>` | Starts on that hall or level. |
| `?here=<id>` | Shows **You are here** at a facility or booth, and starts directions from it. |
| `?accessible=1` | Turns on accessible routes. |
| `?kiosk=1` | Kiosk mode. |

Combine them with `&`, for example `…/map/5527?here=<id>&kiosk=1`.

For **You-Are-Here signs**, print a QR code per sign whose link carries `?here=` with the booth number or facility next to that sign. An attendee who scans it sees where they stand and gets directions from there.

## Run it on a kiosk

Open the map with `?kiosk=1` on a touch screen in the hall. After 90 seconds without a touch the map goes back to its starting view, and nothing one visitor saves or marks visited is kept for the next. Add `?here=` so every route starts from the kiosk.

## Embed it on your website

Paste this where the map should appear, with your Site slug and event ID:

```html
<div data-sessionboard-floor-map data-site="your-slug" data-event="5527"></div>
<script async src="https://sites.sessionboard.com/floor-map-embed.js"></script>
```

Optional attributes on the `div`: `data-height` (any CSS height; the default is 75% of the window, at least 480 px), `data-booth`, `data-exhibitor`, `data-hall` and `data-here` (as in the table above), `data-kiosk="1"`, and `data-title` for screen readers. Share links from an embedded map open the full-page map.

## See what attendees look at

The map counts views, searches, booth and exhibitor opens, directions and saves — anonymously, with no sign-in and nothing that identifies the visitor. These feed the same engagement signals as the rest of the event, so exhibitors' booth traffic from the web map counts alongside the app.

<Note>
The attendee map never shows prices, holds or which booths are reserved: a booth is either available or shows its exhibitor. It lists the sponsors and exhibitors the Attend app lists for the event.
</Note>
