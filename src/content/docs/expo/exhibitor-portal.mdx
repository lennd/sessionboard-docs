The exhibitor portal is where exhibitors and sponsors manage their part of the show. **Expo → Exhibitor portal** shows each part of it and links to where you set that part up.

## Why use it

The weeks before a show are full of exhibitors asking for their invoice, their badge count, the move-in times and the freight form. The portal answers all of it for them — their booth, what they bought, what they owe, what is due, and who on their team has a badge — so your exhibit team handles exceptions instead of email.

## Who gets in

Contacts on an exhibitor or sponsor account at this event sign in to the event's portal and see their company's page. Add or remove contacts on the account in CRM. A contact can also ask for a sign-in link by email from the portal's sign-in page; links go only to the event's exhibitor contacts.

Someone who is a contact on more than one company at the event (an agency, or a person exhibiting for two brands) picks the company from **Company** at the top of the portal. **Log out** signs them out of the site, so the next visit asks them to sign in.

Due dates in the portal (My tasks, Key dates, the balance due) are dates in the event's time zone, the same dates you see in Expo, wherever the exhibitor is.

## What the portal shows

| Part | What exhibitors do | Set it up in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Booth & orders** | See their booth under **Where you are** on the published floor plan, what they bought and what they still owe. | [Booth sales](/expo/booth-sales), [Floor plan](/expo/floor-plan) |
| **Staff & badges** | Name booth staff; each draws one staff badge from their allotment and registers through the Exhibitor staff form. | [Registration forms](/registration/forms) |
| **Exhibitor manual** | Read rules, move-in, insurance and freight, and acknowledge sections. | [Exhibitor manual](/expo/exhibitor-manual) |
| **Shop** | Order furniture, power, internet and lead licences. | [Shop](/expo/shop) |
| **My tasks** | Work through deadlines; sponsorship deliverables add their own. | [Deadlines](/expo/deadlines) |
| **Invoices** | See deposits, installments and balances, and pay by card. | [Orders](/registration/orders-and-invoices) |
| **Contracts** | Sign exhibitor and sponsor agreements. | [CRM → Contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts) |
| **Leads** | See badge scans from their booth staff. | [Lead retrieval](/attend/leads) |
| **Company profile** | Edit the listing the directory and event app show. | The CRM account |

![The Exhibitor portal page with Who gets in and a card for each part of the portal](/images/kb/expo-exhibitor-portal-parts.png)

Staff badges come from the package: a booth package or sponsorship sets how many staff badges it includes and how many more the exhibitor can buy at what price, **Booth sales → Settings** sets the default, and rules there can give a sponsor tier or account level more. Extra badges are bought in the portal by card or on the invoice, and count once paid or invoiced. A card purchase that is not finished holds its badges for a day; after that they go back, and if the payment is finished later it goes through only while badges are still available. After the **Staff badge cut-off** the portal closes staff changes. The account's profile in CRM shows staff badges used, included and bought under **Expo**. Staff register through the event's **Exhibitor staff** form; the link from the portal carries the company so each badge counts against the allotment.

**Portal settings** and **Exhibitor accounts** at the top of the page open the event's portals and the exhibitor list.

Booths in the portal and on the Exhibit & sponsor page are drawn from your published floor plan — the hall background, zones and services, with zoom and a tab per hall — so an exhibitor sees exactly where their booth sits. Every hall of every published map can be picked: the hall tabs and the List view cover them all, and with more than one map each hall is named "Map › Hall" beside the booth number. The List view shows each booth's type as set in the designer (Linear, Island…) and its size in square feet; held booths show as **On hold** and blocked ones as unavailable. Until a floor map is published, they see a simple booth grid instead.
