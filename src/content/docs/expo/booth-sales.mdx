**Expo → Booth sales** is where every exhibitor and sponsor application, the booth it lands on and the order it becomes come together. Its tabs are **Applications**, **Map**, **Booths**, **Orders**, **Rebook** and **Pipeline**.

## Why use it

A booth sale has five steps — request, approval, booth, agreement, invoice — and each one usually happens in a different tool. Booth sales walks every application through them on one record, holds the booth while it does, and turns the result into an order your finance team can see in Registration.

## Two ways to sell a booth

| | Applications | Direct purchase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The exhibitor** | Picks booth preferences and a package on the Exhibit & sponsor page and submits an application. | Picks an available booth on the map and pays, or asks for an invoice. |
| **Your team** | Approves, assigns the booth, sends the agreement and creates the order. | Nothing to approve — the order and booth are created at checkout. |
| **Turn it on** | The default. | **Settings → Buy a booth from the floor map**. |

In application mode, the booth an exhibitor picks is a preference, so the Review step shows an **Estimated total**: the order is priced on the booth your team assigns. A direct purchase is charged exactly the total shown.

Sponsors who do not need a booth use the sponsor-only link of the Exhibit & sponsor page. Their requests arrive as applications marked **sponsor only**.

## Work an application

![The Booth sales tabs and the Applications table with company, package, booth, priority and status](/images/kb/expo-booth-sales-applications.png)

<Steps>
<Step title="Open it">
On **Applications**, filter by status (**Submitted**, **Approved**, **Declined**, **Withdrawn**, **Contracted**, **Invoiced**, **Paid**) and click a company. The drawer shows the contact, their answers, booth preferences and priority points.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the package and booth">
Pick the **Package** and the **Booth** (only available booths are offered, with preferred ones marked). Adjust **Priority points** and **Staff badges** if needed.
</Step>
<Step title="1 · Approve">
Click **Approve** or **Decline**, or use **Open in Approvals**. Approving creates the CRM account if needed (with the company name and website from the application), makes the application's contact the account's primary contact (the agreement's signer), and holds the assigned booth.
</Step>
<Step title="2 · Agreement">
Pick a contract **Template** and click **Send**. The agreement goes out through **CRM → Contracts** — Sessionboard e-sign or DocuSign — and the exhibitor signs it in their portal. If it cannot be sent, the reason shows under **Send**.
</Step>
<Step title="3 · Order">
Create the order: **Invoice in full**, **Deposit + balance**, or **Card payment link**. The booth is reserved, and the order appears in **Registration → Orders** and in the exhibitor's portal. A sponsor-only application (sponsorships, no booth) is ordered the same way.

**Deposit + balance** needs a deposit percentage in Registration **Settings → Invoices & deposits**; without one the order is refused and says so. An invoiced order emails its invoice to the purchaser (the **Exhibitor invoice** email), and **Resend invoice** in Registration → Orders sends it again.
</Step>
</Steps>

![An application drawer with package, booth, priority points and staff badges, and the Approve, Agreement and Order steps](/images/kb/expo-application-drawer.png)

Applications are approvals too: when your event has the Approvals Beta, they wait in **Publish → Approvals** under **Exhibitor & sponsor applications**. See [Approve registrations](/registration/approvals).

## Sell from the map

The **Map** tab draws every booth from the floor maps, coloured by status (**Available**, **Hold**, **Reserved**, **Sold**, **Blocked**), with the exhibitor on each assigned booth. Switch halls from **Halls**, zoom with **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Fit**, and type in **Find booth or exhibitor** to jump to one. The search, hall tabs and zoom sit above the plan and the status legend below it, so **Fit** shows every booth.

Click a booth to see its **Type**, **Price**, **Package**, **Exhibitor** and **Held until**, then **Hold 48 h**, **Release**, or **Open application**. It is the view to share with a buyer on a call: they point at a corner, you hold it. Each hall shows how many of its booths are open.

![The Map tab of Booth sales with booths coloured by status and labelled with exhibitors, hall tabs, a status legend with counts, and a held booth's type, price, package and Release button](/images/kb/expo-booth-sales-map.png)

## Hold and release booths

The **Booths** tab lists every booth from the floor map with its status, and filters by **Available**, **Hold**, **Reserved**, **Sold** and **Blocked**. Click **Hold 48 h** on an available booth to set it aside, or **Release** to put a held or blocked booth back on sale. ![The Booths tab listing floor-map booths with their status and Hold 48 h or Release buttons](/images/kb/expo-booth-sales-booths.png)

Changes write the same booth the floor plan, the booth picker, the exhibitor portal and the attendee map show. A status or exhibitor you set on a booth in the floor plan designer is the same change, and saving the designer never undoes one. See [Booth status and booth sales](/expo/floor-plan#booth-status-and-booth-sales).

## Rebook last year's exhibitors

**Rebook** lists exhibitors that bought a booth at another event of your organization, ranked by priority points (one point for each such event), with the booth they had. Tick companies, choose **Hold until**, and click **Hold booths** to keep the same booth numbers for them on this year's map until that date. The confirmation says how many booths were held and names any it skipped, for example a booth number already taken this year.

## Orders and pipeline

**Orders** lists booth and sponsorship orders placed by exhibitor and sponsor accounts. Open one in **Registration → Orders** to record a payment or refund — see [Manage orders, invoices and refunds](/registration/orders-and-invoices).

**Pipeline** links to **CRM → Pipeline**, where prospects, proposals and won deals live with the same stages and owners as the rest of your sales.

## Booth sales settings

Click **Settings** on Booth sales:

- **Hold a booth for (days)** — counted from approval: the application's assigned booth, or the first open booth it prefers, is held this many days. Before approval, a booth an exhibitor picks on the Exhibit & sponsor page is only a preference and stays open.
- **Staff badges per booth** — used when a package does not set its own.
- **Booth staff register on** — the ticket an exhibitor's staff badge uses.
- **Agreement template** — sent through CRM → Contracts after approval.
- **Buy a booth from the floor map** — exhibitors pick an available booth and pay on the Exhibit & sponsor page, with no application or approval. **Hold a picked booth for (hours)** sets how long a booth is held while they check out; it is released if they do not pay.
- **Application questions** — asked on the Exhibit & sponsor page after company and contact: short text, long text, choice or yes / no, each optionally required.
- **Staff badges by tier or level** — rules such as "Platinum: 5 included + 3 more at $100". A rule applies to accounts with that **Sponsor tier** at this event, or with that **account level** in CRM. Set **Included**, **Extra** and **Price each**. When a rule allows more badges than the account's package, the rule wins.
- **Staff badge cut-off** — after this day (end of day in the event's time zone) exhibitors cannot add, remove or buy staff badges in their portal. You can still change staff on the registration.
