You can also surface forms from other events, so it can work as a cross-event hub when you need that.

And it can be more than a hub. With the [Site builder](/site/site-builder) you can design it visually — pick a layout, apply a template and color scheme, add content sections, and turn on public Speakers, Agenda, and Exhibitors pages — so the same URL works as your public event website.

## What the Program Site centralizes

- Call for Papers forms
- Submission forms (abstracts or sessions)
- Awards forms
- Interest and pipeline forms
- Reviewer access (evaluation plans and rounds)
- Public voting ballots (evaluation and awards rounds set to **Public voting**)

## How forms and submissions work

The Program Site does not recreate forms inside the platform. When a user clicks a form, it opens in a new window in its native interface. There is no need to rebuild or duplicate form logic.

All submissions are aggregated into a single unified view, regardless of which form they came from. Users can see submissions across interest forms, awards forms, and session or abstract submission forms, all in one place.

From this view, users can open any submission and be redirected to the original form to view their completed responses.

<Tip>
This is especially useful for events that run multiple submission forms or different workflows in parallel. Instead of sharing multiple links, the Program Site acts as one central hub for all entry points.
</Tip>

<Note>
The Program Site is optional. Teams can still use standalone form links, embed forms on their own website, or keep their existing process. The Program Site is an added layer of convenience, not a replacement.
</Note>

## The Site overview

**Program → Site** opens a status page for this event's Site. The heading shows the Site name with a **Live** or **Draft** dot under it, and two buttons: **Preview Site** opens the public address in a new tab, exactly as a visitor would see it, and **Settings** opens the [Site builder](/site/site-builder).

Four cards summarize what is set, so you can check a Site without opening the builder:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Site URL** | The public address, `https://sites.sessionboard.com/s/[your-slug]`, with its **Live** badge, a copy button, and an open-in-new-tab button. This is the link you share. Once a [domain of your own](/site/site-builder#your-own-domain) is active, this row, the builder's **Open live** button and the vote link copied from a round all use that domain instead. |
| **Landing Page & Login** | What visitors meet before they sign in: how many **Login Options** are enabled, whether **SSO** is on, and whether the public **Logo**, **Custom CSS**, and **Font** are set. |
| **Logged-In Experience** | What participants see after they sign in: the portal **Logo**, **Background Image**, **Primary Color**, and whether the **Events Page** is shown or hidden. |
| **Quick Actions** | **Edit Login Options**, **Customize Branding**, **Manage Pages**, and **Configure SSO** each open the builder with that section already selected. |

## Setting up your Program Site

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the Site overview">

In your event, open **CMS → Site** (the same page is also listed under **Program → Site**). The overview shows the public URL with its **Live** badge, a summary of the login and branding settings, and a **Quick Actions** panel that jumps straight to a section of the builder.

![The Site overview — the public Site URL with a Live badge and copy button, Landing Page & Login and Logged-In Experience summary cards, and the Quick Actions row](/images/kb/site-overview.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Open the Site builder">

Click **Settings** in the top right. This opens the [Site builder](/site/site-builder): a sitemap on the left, a live preview in the middle, and the settings for whatever you selected on the right. Changes save to a draft and go live when you click **Publish**. See [Drafts and publishing](/site/site-builder#drafts-and-publishing).

The sitemap has a **Public** side (what visitors see before they sign in) and a **Portal** side (what participants see after). Each entry shows **Live** or **Off**.

![The sitemap on the Public side, listing the Site, Layout, Login / SSO and Programs / CFP settings, the Landing, Speakers, Agenda and Exhibitors pages, and three custom pages marked Live or Off](/images/kb/site-builder-sitemap.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Set the URL and branding under Site">

Select **Site** under **Settings**.

- **Ready to share** is a four-item checklist — branding, at least one live program or call for proposals, a login method, and an optional privacy notice — so you can see what the public Site still needs.
- **Site URL**: the **Site Name** is an internal label; the **Site Slug** sets your public address, `https://sites.sessionboard.com/s/[your-slug]`. Slugs can be up to 100 characters and must be unique — the builder flags the field if another Site already uses it.
- **Branding** has a **Public landing** group (logo, **Gradient Start Color**, **Gradient End Color**, **Font Family**) and a **Signed-in portal** group (logo, **Background Image**, **Font Family**, **Primary Color**, **Accent Color**, **Text Color**). **Generate** fills these from your website URL as a starting point.

![The Site inspector — the Ready to share checklist, the Site URL fields with the slug counter, and the Branding section with Generate and the public-landing logo and gradient colors](/images/kb/site-builder-site.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Write the landing page headline">

Select **Landing** under **Pages** for the **Title** and **Subtitle** shown at the top of the public page, and the **Advanced** section for custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. On the marketing layout the landing page is built from blocks — see [The landing page](/site/site-builder#the-landing-page).

</Step>

<Step title="Choose how people sign in">

Select **Login / SSO**. At least one method must stay on.

- **Standard Login**: email-based. People sign up and sign in with just their email address.
- **SSO Login**: single sign-on through your organization's identity provider (SAML, OIDC, and others). Contact Sessionboard to enable it.

On the marketing layout you can also hide the sign-in card from the landing page; the **Sign in** menu button then opens it as a dialog. See [Sign-in options](/site/site-builder#sign-in-options).

![The Login / SSO inspector with the Standard Login switch on and the SSO Login switch off](/images/kb/site-builder-login.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Pick the programs to show">

Select **Programs / CFP**. Search across every program in your organization and tick the ones that should appear as cards on the signed-in home page; the preview switches to a submitter's view so you can see them.

Forms that don't require login open straight from the public Site. Forms that do send visitors through sign-in first, then to the form. To link a form from the menu, a hero button, the footer, or a page, choose the **Form** link type. See [Blocks](/site/site-builder#blocks).

![The Programs / CFP inspector — a search box and the Available Programs list with a checkbox for each form](/images/kb/site-builder-programs.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Set up the portal side">

Switch the sitemap to **Portal**.

- **Welcome** is the first page after sign-in. **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** are always collected. **Collect employer** adds an Organization field, used to spot same-organization conflicts of interest in reviews. The **Privacy Notice** is shown beside the standard terms and privacy acceptance — keep it short, for example *"By submitting this form you agree to share your information with [Organization Name], the vendor of this platform."*
- **Home** holds the signed-in **Title** and **Description**, plus the **Portal widgets** switches — **My Submissions** and **My Contracts** in the sidebar, **Notifications** and **My Reviews** on the home panel.
- **Review workspace** is where evaluators land from their magic link; submitters never see it. **My Profile** is the signed-in profile page.

![The Welcome inspector — Required Fields, the Collect employer switch, and the Privacy Notice editor with its example text](/images/kb/site-builder-welcome.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Add custom pages">

Click **+** next to **Custom pages** in the sitemap. Pages appear in the header of the landing page and in the sidebar of the signed-in portal — useful for FAQs, instructions, or any static content participants should reach from the navigation.

Start from a blank page or from one of ten ready-made templates, including Call for papers, Evaluator guide, and Speaker & partner hub. Pages are built from blocks, like the landing page, and each page's URL slug must be unique within the Site — reuse one and the builder adds a number, such as `call-for-posters-2`. You can also copy a page from another event and drag pages to reorder them. See [Custom pages](/site/site-builder#custom-pages).

</Step>

<Step title="Set up reviewer access">

For evaluators, the Program Site becomes their main interface. Reviewers access it via a **magic link** sent from their evaluation plan; no admin account or platform onboarding is required.

Once logged in, reviewers can:

- See all assigned evaluation plans and rounds
- Track pending vs completed reviews
- Access all submissions they need to review

From within each submission, reviewers can:

- View submission details and participant information
- Access uploaded files
- Complete scorecards (ratings, written feedback, and more)

Admins control what metadata is visible to reviewers and which filters are available (for example: track, level, language, tags), ensuring reviewers get the right context without information overload.

<Warning>
Reviewers are granted access through their evaluation plan assignment, not through the Site settings directly. Make sure evaluators are added to their respective plans before sharing the site URL. [Learn how to add evaluators to a plan.](/evaluations/evaluation-plans)
</Warning>

</Step>

<Step title="Publish and share the URL">

Click **Publish** in the builder header, then share the Site URL from the overview with your submitters and reviewers. For a public-voting round, share the copied vote link from that round — it is unlisted, so only people with the URL can open the ballot.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Public voting

When an evaluation or awards round is set to **Public voting**, the ballot lives on this Program Site. Copy the link from the round (**Copy link**) and share it. Voters open the URL and score submissions on that round's scorecard. The round's **Voter sign-in** setting decides whether they sign in with a magic link or SSO first, or vote immediately with no sign-in (IP address only). Rounds can also cap how many submissions each voter votes for (**Votes per voter**).

Voting is open from the round's start date to its end date. The dates are counted in the event's timezone, or in the organization's timezone for Interest Form and awards rounds. Closing the evaluation plan also closes voting, even if the end date is still ahead.

The ballot's heading is the evaluation plan's name — or the awards program's name — and the text under it is the instructions written for the round, formatting included, so voters see what they are voting on and how before they start. A round with no instructions shows a short default line instead.

![Public voting banner with Copy link and Open on an evaluation round](/images/kb/eval-public-voting-copy-link.png)

Public voters are not invited evaluators. They are not assigned a queue under **My Reviews** — they use the vote link only. They join the round when they save a score.

![A public vote submission on the Program Site, with the scorecard and Sign in to vote](/images/kb/public-vote-eval-item.png)

### What voters see once they have voted

- Submissions a voter has already scored carry a **Voted** badge on the ballot, and the ballot header counts them — *3 of 12 voted*. Filters narrow the list to **Voted** or **Not voted**, so a voter can find what is left.
- Inside a submission, **Previous** and **Next** step through the ballot in order and **Back to all submissions** returns to the list, the same way evaluators move through their queue.
- A voted submission shows its saved score. The voter can change their answers and submit again to update it, or click **Undo vote** to take the vote back — the score is removed and, on a round with **Votes per voter**, the vote is returned to their budget. Undo is available only while the round is open for voting.

Pasting the vote URL into Slack, LinkedIn, or similar shows a branded preview of the round — the Site's **Social share image** when one is set, otherwise its logo — and the round name. Opening a single submission in that link shows that item. If the round uses blind review, the preview never includes a name or headshot. Search engines and AI assistants do not index these URLs.

See [Round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations) and [Managing submissions, reviewers & rounds](/awards/managing-submissions-reviewers-rounds) for how to set a round to Public voting and copy the link.

## What each user sees

On the Portal layout, a visitor who opens the Site URL sees the branded header, a tab for each live page (the landing page first, then any custom pages and the public **Speakers**, **Agenda**, and **Exhibitors** pages that are on), and the **Sign in** card. The card asks for an email address only: **Send login link** emails a magic link, and opening it signs the person in.

After sign-in the Site switches to the portal: a sidebar with **Home**, **My Submissions**, the same custom pages, **My Profile**, and **Log Out**. **Home** lists the **Open Programs** you ticked under **Programs / CFP** as cards. Staff accounts are not meant to sign in here; to check the participant view, use **Preview as** in the builder or sign in as a test participant.

| User | Experience on the Program Site |
| --- | --- |
| Submitters | All available submission forms and a unified view of all past submissions across forms |
| Reviewers | All assigned evaluation plans, pending rounds, and submissions to review |
| Public voters | The vote link for that round: sign in (when the round requires it), browse submissions, and complete the scorecard |

<Note>
The Program Site and evaluator experience are available in 5+ languages and are WCAG 2.1+ compliant.
</Note>
