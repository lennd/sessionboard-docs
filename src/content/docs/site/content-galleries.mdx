A content gallery is a hosted page for the content your program produces — session recordings and clips, white papers and slide decks, lessons that award credit, webinar recordings, and the program itself as a browsable catalog. Visitors get a curated **Hub** of collections, an **Explore** page with search and filters, and a **My list** to save things to. You get plays, watch time, saves, downloads and, when the gallery is gated, leads.

Every gallery starts from a template. Templates pre-wire the collections, filters and auto-fill rules for a job, so the gallery fills itself from content you already have and stays current without anyone adding items by hand.

<Note>
Content Galleries is an Early Access **Preview** you can turn on yourself from [Early Access](/get-started/early-access), at either level:

- **Preview** at the bottom of the organization sidebar turns it on for the whole organization. Galleries live under **Publish → Sites** in every event — on the page's **Galleries** tab — and under **Publish → Sites** at the organization level too, which lists every gallery in the organization.
- **Preview** at the bottom of the event sidebar turns it on for that event only. Its galleries live under **Publish → Sites** in that event; other events are not affected.

Until it is on, the Sites page shows what galleries do, and the gallery cards in the create wizard are greyed out with a link to a page where anyone with the **Manage Early Access** permission can turn it on. Everyone else sees a note to ask an admin.
</Note>

Not sure a content gallery is the right surface? Compare it with a Site, an Embed and a Poster Gallery in [Which one do I need?](/site/publishing-surfaces).

## Why use it

Your program produces more content than one event can show: recordings, clips, slides, white papers, lessons. A content gallery gives all of it a home that visitors can browse, search and save from — and **My list** lets them take what they saved to their calendar or inbox, which is the moment a library turns into attendance and a captured email.

## Pick a template

| Template | Build it when… | What it sets up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On-Demand Hub** | The event is over and the recordings deserve a real home | Open to all, dark video-first theme. Featured, Latest, Highlights & clips, Most watched and Keynotes rails. Fills from the Media Hub as recordings land. |
| **Session Catalog** | Attendees should search the program, filter it and build an agenda — before, during and after the event | Open to all, your brand colours, a **Program** page in one of three layouts — **Catalog**, **Timetable** or **Agenda** (see [Program layout](#program-layout)) — plus a **People** directory and a session panel with presentations, speaker bios and **Add to calendar**. Filters for Day, Track, Session type, Level, Topic, Speaker, Location, Language, Credits and Recording available. Sorted in program order. **My agenda** replaces My list. Needs an event. |
| **Learning Catalog** | Courses, webinars and recordings should live in one catalog with credit, format and price filters | Open to all, light reading-first theme, catalog rows with credit chips and Free / Paid. Featured, Continue learning, New this month, Courses with credit, By credit type, Series & bundles and Webinar recordings. Fills from published lessons and imported webinar recordings. |
| **Resource Library** | White papers and decks should capture a lead before download | Email gate, light theme. New this month, White papers, Guides & documents and Articles grids. Fills from files marked visible on sessions. |
| **Webinar Library** | You run a year-round webinar program and want every recording published as it lands | Email gate, your brand colours, landscape cards. Latest webinars, Most watched, Continue watching and By speaker. Fills from recordings imported by a connected webinar platform. |
| **Speaker Spotlight** | You want everything organized by the people who said it | Open to all, light theme. Featured speakers, By speaker and Latest, with a Speaker filter front and centre. |
| **Year-Round Archive** | Members should have one home for every event, every year | Members-only (portal login), your brand colours. Continue watching, Latest additions, By event and Earn credit, with an Event filter. Fills from every event in the organization. |
| **Start from scratch** | You know exactly what you want | An open gallery with one Latest rail and sensible defaults. |

You can change everything after creating the gallery — the template only decides where you start.

## Create a gallery

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Publish → Sites and click New gallery">

The create wizard opens. Pick **Media Gallery** for recordings, clips and resources, or **Session Catalog** for one event's searchable program. **Session Catalog** is marked **Per event**: at the organization level the next step asks which event it shows.

![Step 1 of the create wizard, What are you making?, with cards for Marketing Site, Session Catalog, Media Gallery, ePoster Gallery and Embed above the Purpose, Name, Layout, Access, Brand and Review steps](/images/kb/publish-create-wizard-purpose.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Name it and choose its web address">

The **Name** is what visitors see in the browser tab. The **Web address** shows the host your gallery is served from, followed by the part you type; the wizard checks the address is free as you type.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a layout, who can see it, and a look">

**Pick a layout** offers the templates above, each with a small sketch of how it lays out. **Who can see it?** sets the gallery's access, and **Pick a look** its theme — you can change all three in the builder later. **Review** shows what you chose; click **Create** to open the builder.

</Step>

<Step title="Work through Getting started">

The builder opens on the **Content** tab with a **Getting started** checklist tuned to the template — for example *Sync accepted sessions*, *Pick your keynotes* or *Connect your webinar platform in Integrations*. Each step names what it unlocks. Optional steps are marked.

</Step>

<Step title="Publish">

The gallery is a **Draft** until you click **Publish** in the header. While it is a draft, the public link shows a holding page and the builder's preview shows the real gallery, so you can share the link ahead of time. **Copy public link** and **View live** are in the same header; **Unpublish** takes it down again without losing anything.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
The right-hand preview renders the live gallery as you edit. Switch between the **Hub**, **Explore** and other pages, and between desktop, tablet and phone widths.
</Tip>

## Content: what is in the gallery

The **Content** tab lists every item and where it came from. Drag to set the curated order, star an item to **Feature** it (eligible for the hero and *Featured* collections), or **Hide** it to keep it configured but out of every collection and search.

### Add content by hand

**Add content** offers five sources:

| Source | What it pulls in |
| --- | --- |
| **Recordings & clips** | Videos, clips and podcasts from the Media Hub. |
| **Files & links on sessions** | PDFs, slides and links attached to sessions. Only files marked visible on the session appear. |
| **Lessons** | Published lessons from Learning — the credit statement shows on the card. |
| **Sessions** | Accepted sessions with speakers, time and track. Recordings attach automatically. |
| **External link** | A link to a video, podcast episode or article hosted anywhere. |

You can also add a recording from the Media Hub itself: **Add to gallery** on the recording offers your organization's galleries and the gallery of the recording's own event. It tells you whether the item was **Added**, is already in the gallery, or can't be added there — an event gallery only takes that event's media. See [Media library](/marketing/media-library#work-with-a-recording).

### Auto-fill rules

Rules keep the gallery current: anything new that matches is added the next time the rule syncs. A rule chooses what to **Pull from**, which **Event** (or any event, for organization-level galleries), and narrows it down:

- **Sessions** — **Which sessions**: all, upcoming only or past only.
- **Recordings & clips** — **Include clips cut from recordings**, and **Webinar recordings only** for recordings imported from a connected webinar platform.
- **Lessons** — **Free lessons only** skips lessons with a paid price plan.
- Any source — **Only with tags** and **Published since**.

Each rule shows how many items it last pulled in and when. **Sync now** runs one rule; **Sync all** runs every rule. Turn a rule off with its switch to stop it adding items without deleting the ones it already added.

### Edit an item

Click an item to change its **Title**, **Description**, **Thumbnail**, **Format**, **Published date** and **Tags** (tags power the Tags filter and tag-based rules). **Access** and **Embargo until** override the gallery's settings for this one item; leave **Access** on *Inherit* unless the item needs its own rule.

## Collections: the Hub

Collections are the rows on the Hub page. Each has a **Title**, optional **Subtitle**, a **Cover image**, and a **Layout**:

| Layout | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Rail** — horizontal scroller | Most rows: Latest, Keynotes, By track. |
| **Grid** — every item at once | Document libraries and small, complete sets. |
| **Spotlight** — large featured cards | Two to four things you want everyone to see. |
| **Hero** — full-width banner | One item that leads the page. |

A collection either lists hand-picked items or follows a rule — **With any of these tags**, **Published in the last (days)**, **Featured items only**, an **Order** and an **Item limit**. A **Group label** joins adjacent rows under one heading, for example *By track*. **Access** can be stricter than the gallery's, and **Hidden** keeps a collection configured but off the Hub.

## Design

### Theme and brand

Three presets set the mood: **Dark cinematic** (video-first, deep backgrounds), **Light editorial** (reading-first, white space) and **Your brand**, which keeps a neutral light (or, with a light text colour, dark) canvas and uses your **Primary** colour for the navigation, buttons and selected states, with the **Accent**, **Text colour**, **Font family** and **Logo** you set below it.

### Hero

The banner at the top of the Hub. **Featured item** shows a starred item's art and title with a Play button; **Custom banner** uses your own **Title**, **Subtitle**, **Banner image** and buttons.

### Program layout

Session Catalog galleries only. The Program page (the template's Explore page) can lay the program out three ways; the Hub, My agenda and the session pages are the same whichever you pick, and switching is instant in the preview before you save.

![The Program layout setting in the Design tab, with three radio cards — Catalog, Timetable and Agenda — each showing a small wireframe and a one-line description, and a Show ‘Add to calendar’ on sessions switch below them](/images/kb/content-gallery-program-layout-picker.png)

| Layout | Best for | What visitors get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Catalog** | Large programs people search | A filter rail (Track, Format, Level, Room, Topics — each option with its count) beside session rows grouped by day and start time, with a sticky search bar, active-filter chips and page numbers. |
| **Timetable** | Multi-track days | Rooms across, start times down: each session is a card spanning its slot, colour-coded by track, with a legend, a sticky room header and time column, and a **Grid / List** toggle. Narrow screens and the **TBA** day always show the list. |
| **Agenda** | Single-track programs and phones | Day tabs and one expandable row per session. Open a row for the abstract, the speakers with their bios and the presentations inside it; **Expand all** opens every row. On the day, rows carry **Now**, **Next** and **Ended** chips. |

![The Timetable layout: day tabs across the top, Track, Format, Level, Room and Topics filter menus, a Grid / List toggle, and a grid of session cards laid out by room and start time](/images/kb/content-gallery-program-timetable.png)

![The Agenda layout: day tabs, an Expand all button, and session rows grouped by time with a track-coloured bar, an Ended chip, the session code, speakers and room](/images/kb/content-gallery-program-agenda.png)

![The session panel open beside the program, showing the date, time and room, the session code and title, track, format, level, credits and Recording available chips, the Add to agenda, Add to calendar, Copy link and Full page buttons, the abstract and the people in the session with Bio links](/images/kb/content-gallery-program-session-panel.png)

All three share the same session panel: click any session and it opens beside the program with the date, time, room and time zone, the abstract, the speakers (with **Read more** for bios), the presentations inside a symposium or poster session (searchable, with **Show only presenters**), **Save**, **Copy link** and — when **Show ‘Add to calendar’ on sessions** is on — **Add to calendar** as a calendar file, Google Calendar or Outlook. Links carry the open session, the day and every filter, so a pasted URL lands on the same view. Sessions without a start time collect under a **TBA** day rather than disappearing. Times are shown in the event's time zone.

Add **People** under **Navigation tabs** (below) for a faculty directory: everyone who chairs, speaks or presents, grouped by surname with a role filter and search; each person has a page with their photo, bio and sessions by day.

### Layout and cards

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Card style** | **Poster (2:3)** and **Landscape (16:9)** for video-first galleries, **Compact tiles** for dense libraries, and **Catalog rows** for programs and learning catalogs — each row shows the session code, title, abstract, speakers, time and the chips that matter (track, level, room, language, credits, *Recording available*). On a Session Catalog this applies to the **Catalog** program layout; Timetable and Agenda bring their own cards. |
| **Rail size** | How large the cards in a rail render. |
| **Show format badges on cards**, **Show duration on cards** | The small *Video* / *Document* badge and the running time. |
| **Show session codes** | The program code before the title in catalog rows; the code is searchable too. |
| **Show speaker title and company** | Adds *Title, Company* after each speaker name in catalog rows. |
| **Show credit chips** | Credit designations on lessons and credit amounts on sessions. |
| **Show Free / Paid on lessons** | A price chip on lesson cards. |
| **Hub page** / **Explore page** | Turn either page off — a pure searchable library needs no Hub. |
| **Navigation tabs** | Which of Hub, Explore, My list — and, on a Session Catalog, **People** — appear in the header. |

### Visitor features and copy

Every toggle under **Visitor features** removes a control from the public gallery: **Search**, **My list (saves)**, **Share buttons**, **Downloads**, **Transcripts**, **Related items** and **Continue watching**. Under **Copy**, rename the **Hub title**, **Explore title**, **Explore intro**, the **“My list”** and **“Save”** labels — the Session Catalog template calls them *My agenda* and *Add to agenda* — and the **Empty state** shown when a search finds nothing. The **Call-to-action band** at the foot of every page carries a heading and a button; **Capture emails instead of linking out** swaps the button for an email field whose submissions land in Leads.

On **My list**, visitors can take their saves with them. **Add to calendar** downloads a calendar file with every saved item that has a start time; the button appears once at least one saved item has one. **Email me my list** sends every saved item with its link, and the calendar file attached, to the signed-in member or the address they gave at the email gate; anyone else types an address. Embargoed details stay hidden in the email, and a visitor can send it a few times in a short window before they have to wait.

### Search engines

A published gallery is indexable unless its access mode is **Private**: search engines can crawl the Hub, Explore, every collection and every item a visitor could open without signing in, and those pages are listed in the Site's sitemap. Locked items — behind a gate or a login — are left out, and **My list** is never indexed. **Hide from search engines** adds `noindex` to every page of the gallery and drops it from the sitemap; turn it on for email-gated, members-only and SSO galleries, or for anything you would rather people reach only through your own links.

### Share previews

When someone pastes a gallery link into LinkedIn, Slack, Teams or iMessage, the preview card is built from the gallery: the Hub and Explore use the gallery name, hero subtitle and hero image (or the first item's thumbnail); an item link uses the item's title, description and thumbnail. An embargoed item shares the gallery's card instead, so nothing withheld leaks into a preview, and a private or draft gallery shares a generic Sessionboard card. Previews work even when **Hide from search engines** is on. LinkedIn caches a card for about a week — use its Post Inspector to refresh one after you change a thumbnail.

## Access: who can watch

Set once for the gallery on the **Access** tab; collections and items can be stricter.

| Access mode | Who gets in |
| --- | --- |
| **Open to all** | Anyone with the link can watch, read and download. |
| **Email gate** | Visitors see titles and thumbnails, then enter an email to unlock. Every unlock becomes a lead you can export from Analytics. |
| **Share link only** | Hidden unless the visitor arrives through one of your share links. Each link can have a password, an expiry date and a view cap. |
| **Members (portal login)** | Visitors sign in to the Site (password or SSO) before anything unlocks. |
| **SSO only** | Only your identity provider lets people in — no passwords, no email gate. |
| **Private** | Nobody outside the builder can open it. Use while you curate. |

**Site sign-in** turns **Email and password login** and **Single sign-on (SSO)** on or off for the gallery, so visitors on an open gallery can still sign in to keep their list across devices.

### Embargo

Withhold abstracts and recordings until a session has been presented — scientific programs use this so nothing leaks before the room hears it.

- **Off** — abstracts, players and downloads are available as soon as an item is visible.
- **Until each session's presentation time** — each session lifts on its own schedule. Items without a start time are never embargoed.
- **Fixed date & time** — everything in the gallery lifts at the same moment.

**What to withhold** decides what stays hidden until the lift: **Description (abstract)**, **Player and downloads**, **Speakers** (names and photos on the listing) and **Tags**. With nothing ticked, the gallery withholds the description and the player. Embargoed items still appear in the catalog with their title and an *Available …* chip, so attendees can plan around them — and anything you withhold stays out of the Explore filters, their counts and search until the lift, so a withheld speaker or topic cannot be found by filtering for it.

**Where it applies** decides which places honor the embargo: **Gallery site** (the gallery's own pages, and gallery blocks on your other Sites), **Website embed** (the gallery widget pasted on your own website) and **Mobile app** (the event app reading the gallery feed). With all three ticked, the embargo covers everywhere. Untick a place to show everything there as soon as the item is visible — anyone can read the abstract there before the lift. At least one place stays ticked.

### Explore filters

The filter rail on the Explore page, with a count next to every value. Values come from the items themselves, so a filter with nothing behind it simply does not show. Each filter has a **Label**, a **Display style** (**Chips**, **Dropdown** or **Checkboxes**) and a switch to hide it without deleting it. Choose the **Filter source**:

| Filter source | Values |
| --- | --- |
| **Custom values (set per item)** | Pick from the built-in keys — Topic, Track, Session type, Level, Location / room, Language, Speaker / faculty, Credits offered, Credit type, Format (live / on demand), Price (free / paid), Series / bundle, Webinar platform, Event, Item type — or type a custom key. |
| **Custom session field** | A dropdown or checkbox field from your Sessions module, such as *Audience*, *Industry* or *Product*. Pick the field and its values become a filter; existing items refresh automatically. |
| **Day of the event** | One value per program day, in date order. |
| **Recording available** | A single value that matches sessions with a recording bound to them. |
| **Format**, **Tags**, **Speaker**, **Event**, **Year** | Computed from every item. |

<Tip>
Session facets fill themselves from the session record: Track, Format, Level, Language, Location, Tags, speakers and credits. To add a filter attendees think in — *Audience: Developer, Admin, Executive* — add a dropdown field to the Sessions module, fill it in on your sessions, and publish it as a **Custom session field** filter.
</Tip>

### Share links

Hand a partner, sponsor or the press a tracked link even when the gallery is open. Each link has a name, an optional password, an expiry and a view cap, and shows how many views it has driven.

## Settings

**Gallery name**, **URL slug** and the **Default order in Explore** — **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Title A–Z**, **Most watched**, **Curated order** (your drag order on the Content tab) or **Program order (date & time)**, which lists timed sessions in schedule order. Visitors can change the sort themselves; search results always rank by relevance. **About this gallery** shows the template and event the gallery was created from. **Embed on your website** gives you the code to paste on your own site — under **Show**, pick **All items** or one collection, then **Copy code** (see [Put a gallery on your own website](#put-a-gallery-on-your-own-website)). **Danger zone** deletes the gallery — type the slug to confirm.

## Analytics

Pick a time window and read **Views**, **Unique viewers**, **Plays**, **Watch time**, **Saves**, **Downloads & clicks**, **Searches** and **Gate leads**, with a **Daily views and plays** chart, a **Funnel** from visit to play to save, a breakdown by **Collection**, **Top items** and **Top searches**. Zero-result searches tell you what content to add next. Under **Leads**, every email captured by the gate or the call-to-action band can be exported.

## Put a gallery on a Site

A gallery has its own address, and it can also live inside a regular Site. In the [Site builder](/site/site-builder#media), add a **Content gallery** block, pick the gallery, then choose **Whole gallery** or one collection, a **Rail**, **Grid** or **Spotlight** layout, how many **Items to show**, and whether to show a **See all** link or a search box. The block reads the gallery live, so new items appear on the Site without republishing it. Visitors who click through land in the gallery itself, where its access rules apply. A draft gallery shows nothing until it is published.

## Put a gallery on your own website

The gallery can also sit on a website you already run — the same way an [agenda embed](/sessions/embeds) does. There is no row to create under **Publish → Embeds**. Open the gallery's **Settings** tab and copy the code from **Embed on your website** — it already has your slug and your region. Written by hand, it looks like this, with `acme-library` replaced by the gallery's URL slug:

```html
<script src="https://embeds.sessionboard.com/v0/sessionboard-content-gallery-embed.js"></script>
<sessionboard-embed
    widget-type="content-gallery"
    site-slug="acme-library"
    language="en"
    theme="auto">
</sessionboard-embed>
```

The widget shows search, filters, sort and a page of cards. Opening a card plays or downloads the item when the visitor is allowed to; an email-gated item collects the address in the widget and unlocks it. Members-only and SSO galleries send the visitor to the gallery Site to sign in. A `?share=` on the host page unlocks a share-link gallery.

| Attribute | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `site-slug` | Yes | The gallery Site's slug. |
| `gallery-id` | No | The gallery's id, only needed when one Site hosts more than one gallery. |
| `collection` | No | A collection slug, to show just that rail. |
| `page-size` | No | Cards per page (1–100; default 12). |
| `language` | No | Widget chrome: `en`, `de`, `fr` or `es`. |
| `theme` | No | `light` (default), `dark`, or `auto` to follow the visitor's system. |

Do not load this script on the same page as another Sessionboard widget script — they all define `<sessionboard-embed>`. EU events use `embeds-eu.sessionboard.com` instead of `embeds.sessionboard.com`; the code from **Embed on your website** already points at your region. A draft gallery shows an error in the widget until it is published.

<Note>
This is the Explore view of the gallery — search, filters and cards. The curated Hub, My list and the full Site chrome stay on the gallery's own address.
</Note>

## How content flows in

- **Recordings** — upload to the Media Hub, or connect your webinar platform under **Integrations**. Imported webinar recordings land in the Media Hub and then in every gallery whose rule matches them, so a Webinar Library or Learning Catalog publishes the recording without anyone touching it. Rules with **Webinar recordings only** take just those.
- **Sessions** — accepted sessions arrive with their speakers, time, track, format, level, language, room, tags and credits. When a recording is bound to a session, the session shows a Watch button and matches the **Recording available** filter. When you publish a session's [recap](/marketing/recaps), its item page shows it under the description — only the recap you published, never the unreviewed AI summary — and it stays hidden while the item is embargoed.
- **Lessons** — published lessons arrive with their credit designations, series and price, which power the Credit type, Series / bundle and Price filters.
- **Files** — a file attached to a session appears once it is marked visible on that session.

<Warning>
Deleting a gallery removes its collections, rules, share links and analytics. The recordings, sessions, lessons and files it pointed at are untouched — they live in the Media Hub, the program and Learning.
</Warning>
