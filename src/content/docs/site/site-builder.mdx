The Site builder is the visual editor for your [Program Site](/site/program-site). It has three parts: a **sitemap** on the left listing every setting and page, a **live preview** of your real site in the middle, and an **inspector** on the right with the controls for whatever you select.

Open it from your event under **Program > Site > Settings**, or from your organization portal settings.

![The Site builder with the sitemap on the left, a live preview of the marketing site in the middle, and the Landing inspector on the right](/images/kb/site-builder-overview.png)

## Drafts and publishing

Everything you change is saved to a **draft** as you work, and shows in the preview right away. Nothing reaches your live site until you click **Publish**.

The header tells you where you stand:

- **Saving…** and then **Draft saved** after each edit.
- **Unpublished changes** when the draft differs from your live site.
- **Live** when there is nothing left to publish.

**Publish** makes the whole draft live at once. **Discard changes** deletes the draft and reloads your live site in the builder. Discarding clears your undo history and can't be undone.

**Undo** and **Redo** in the header step through your edits: ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z to undo, ⇧⌘Z / Ctrl+Y to redo. If you try to leave while an edit is still being saved, the builder asks whether to save the draft first.

### Checks before you publish

The builder flags any block that would show up broken, or not at all, on your live site. Examples: an image or video with no link, a button with no label, a link to a page that was deleted or isn't published, a closed form, or a form that needs sign-in on a Site with sign-in turned off.

Flagged blocks carry a warning in the block list and in the preview. When you click **Publish**, the **Check these before you publish** dialog lists them. Click one to jump to it and fix it, or choose **Publish anyway**.

## The sitemap

The sitemap has two tabs.

- **Public**: the pages anyone can open before they sign in.
  - **Settings**: Site, Layout, Navigation, Footer, Login / SSO, and Programs / CFP.
  - **Pages**: Landing, Speakers, Agenda, and Exhibitors.
  - **Custom pages**: pages you add yourself.
- **Portal**: the pages people see after they sign in. These are Welcome, Home, Review workspace, and My Profile, plus Tasks on organization Sites.

Each page shows **Live** or **Off**, so you can see at a glance what visitors can open.

## Site settings

Select **Site** for the basics:

- **Ready to share**: a checklist of what the public Site needs before you send it out. It covers branding (a logo, or use **Generate**), at least one live program or call for proposals, a login method, and a privacy notice.
- **Site URL**: the slug is the public path. The name is the fallback headline when **Title** is empty.
- **Public headline**: shown at the top of the landing page.
- **Branding**: logo, colors, and fonts for the public landing and for the signed-in portal. **Generate** scans your website and fills these in as a starting brand kit.

**Text Color** is the color of the text drawn over your brand colors: the hero banner, the top menu, and the sign-in card. On a dark gradient, use a light color such as white.

## Layouts

The layout decides what visitors see before they sign in. Pick it under **Layout** in the sitemap.

- **Portal**: the classic landing, with a gradient background, sign-in card, program cards, and your custom pages. Every existing Site uses this until you change it.
- **Marketing site**: a public event website with its own top menu, a hero banner, blocks, public program pages, and a footer. Sign-in moves into the page instead of being the page.

<Note>
Switching layouts never deletes anything. Your pages, programs, branding, and login settings carry over, and you can switch back to Portal at any time.
</Note>

## Templates and color schemes

On the marketing layout, the **Layout** inspector offers two independent starting points.

**Templates** set the structure: which hero style leads, which blocks follow, and in what order. Applying one restructures the page and keeps your colors, fonts, logo, copy, and login settings. Like any other edit, it goes into your draft and doesn't go live until you publish.

| Template | Structure |
| --- | --- |
| Conference | Speakers up front: stats, speaker grid, agenda preview |
| Trade show | Partners first: CTA, sponsor wall, exhibitors, then the program |
| Congress | Program first: about, agenda preview, key figures |
| Summit | Editorial and minimal: intro, speakers, one CTA |
| Community | The full story on one page: every section |

![The Layout inspector with Portal and Marketing site layout cards and template wireframe cards for Conference, Trade show, Congress, and Summit](/images/kb/site-builder-templates.png)

**Color schemes** set the palette and font pairing without touching your structure. The schemes are Midnight, Evergreen, Vivid, Slate, Sunset, Ocean, Royal, Aurora, Graphite, Rosewood, and Espresso. Every scheme is checked against WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. **Your brand (saved)** goes back to your saved colors, and **Match my website** pulls colors from your own site.

![Color scheme swatch cards showing each palette's gradient and font pairing, with a check on the active Midnight scheme](/images/kb/site-builder-color-schemes.png)

A check mark shows which template and scheme your site currently matches. Both are starting points, not locks: apply one and then customize freely.

## The landing page

Select **Landing** to edit the public home page: the title and subtitle, the hero banner, and the blocks below it.

![A published marketing-layout landing page with the top menu, hero banner with two buttons, a stats section, and topic cards](/images/kb/site-builder-public-landing.png)

### Hero banner

- **Background**: a brand **Gradient**, a full-width **Image**, or a live **Countdown** to a date you set.
- **Buttons**: up to two. Each button can open sign-in, a page, a form, or an external link.
- **Show submission window**: shows or hides the open and close dates banner under the headline.
- **Add-to-calendar button**: lets visitors download a calendar invite. It appears when your event has dates.

## Blocks

The landing page and custom pages are built from blocks. Click **Add section** in the inspector, or **Add a block here** between blocks in the preview. The **Add a block** picker groups the blocks below and has a search box. Every block starts with sample content you can edit.

![The sections list in the Landing inspector with hide, reorder, and remove controls, and the Add section grid below it](/images/kb/site-builder-add-section.png)

### Text

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Headings, paragraphs, lists, and links |
| FAQ | Questions with expandable answers |

### Media

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Image | One image with alt text, a caption, and an optional link. Width can be **Text width**, **Wide**, or **Full width** |
| Video | A YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, or Wistia video. Paste the link and the builder confirms which service it found |
| Photo gallery | A grid of photos with captions |

Alt text is required on images so people using screen readers know what the image shows.

### Program

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Speakers / agenda view | Speakers or sessions with the full layout options of the public pages |
| Featured speakers | A few featured speakers from your program |
| Agenda preview | The next sessions from your agenda |

**Featured speakers** and **Agenda preview** pull from the public Speakers and Agenda pages, so turn those pages on to show them.

For a **Speakers / agenda view**, choose **Speakers** or **Sessions**, then choose where its settings come from:

- **Use the site's Speakers view** (or Agenda view): shows exactly what that page shows and follows its changes. The page has to be set to **Customize**, because the built-in page can't be placed in a block.
- **Custom view**: gives this block its own layout, fields, filters, and display options. These are the same options as the pages, described under [Public program pages](#public-program-pages).

### Forms

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Form link | A button or card that opens one of your forms. Pick the form, the style (**Button** or **Card**), the button text, and an optional description |
| Form list | Every open form on the Site. Narrow it by type (calls for proposals, awards, session submissions, requests), and turn on **Show closed forms** to list closed forms marked "Closed" instead of hiding them |

Public forms open in a new tab. Forms that need sign-in ask visitors to sign in first. A closed form is hidden on the site until it reopens.

### Layout

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Button | A single call-to-action button that opens sign-in, a page, a form, or an external link, aligned left, center, or right |
| Columns | Two or three columns side by side, each holding its own blocks. On phones, columns stack |
| Divider | A thin line between blocks |
| Spacer | Extra space between blocks, in **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** |

### Marketing

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Stats | Big numbers with labels, such as attendees and speakers |
| Call-to-action banner | A headline, supporting line, and one button |
| Testimonials | Quotes with names, roles, and optional photos |
| Topics & tracks | Topic cards with image overlays for your content pillars |
| Sponsors | Your sponsor wall, grouped by tier, pulled live from your event's sponsors |
| Partner logos | A hand-curated logo wall with optional links, for partners that aren't sponsor records |
| Venue & location | Venue name, address, photo, and a **Get directions** button |

Sponsor and speaker blocks read from your event data, so the site updates itself as your program changes without re-publishing. Every image slot falls back to a clean placeholder, such as initials for people and name tiles for companies, so the page never shows a broken image.

### Apps

Apps installed on your event can add their own blocks, listed under **Apps** in the picker. An app block shows on the live site. The preview shows a placeholder for it. If the app is uninstalled, its blocks are marked unavailable and stop showing on the site until you delete them or reinstall the app.

### Arranging blocks

In the inspector, drag a block to reorder it, use the eye icon to hide it without losing its content, and open **More actions** to duplicate or delete it.

In the preview, hover any block for the same controls: **Edit section**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate**, **Hide from site**, **Edit text**, and **Delete**. **Edit text** lets you type straight into the preview.

<Note>
A landing page holds up to 20 blocks, a custom page up to 50, and each column up to 10. When a list is full, the builder says so. Remove a block to add another.
</Note>

## Public program pages

Marketing-layout sites can turn on dedicated public pages, each with its own menu entry:

- **Speakers**: your speakers. Every speaker also gets a profile page with a shareable URL, their sessions, and a branded share card for social posts.
- **Agenda**: the public session schedule.
- **Exhibitors**: an exhibitor directory with logos. Each exhibitor gets a profile page with their description, website, and share card.

These pages publish only public information. For speakers, that's name, title, company, photo, and bio. For exhibitors, it's company name, logo, website, description, and tier. Emails, phone numbers, and addresses never appear. Each page carries its own title, description, and structured data, so search engines and AI assistants index it correctly. Pasting any page, including a profile, into Slack, LinkedIn, or similar shows a branded preview card.

<Note>
Program pages are off by default. Existing sites never gain public pages until you turn them on.
</Note>

### Page style

Select the **Speakers** or **Agenda** page and choose a **Page style**:

- **Simple (built-in)**: a clean page in your Site's brand. Nothing to set up. The preview uses the live Site's record filters, so changes to those filters show in the preview after you publish.
- **Customize**: choose the layout, fields, filters, and search yourself. These are the same options as a session or speaker embed.
- **Import from an embed**: copy the layout, fields, filters, and display options from an HTML embed you've already built for this event. It's a one-time copy: the embed isn't changed or linked. The page remembers which embed it came from, and **Re-import** copies it again.

### Layouts

| Page | Layout | What visitors see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speakers | List | Rows with photo, title, and bio |
| Speakers | Gallery | Photo tiles in a grid |
| Agenda | Agenda grid | Sessions side by side by time, one column per room |
| Agenda | Session list | One session after another |
| Agenda | Schedule | A day-by-day itinerary |

The **Agenda grid** needs the Location field to build its room columns. If Location is off, every session sits in a single "Unassigned" column. The builder warns you and offers **Show Location** to turn it on in one click.

### Fields and sorting

Choose which fields each card shows, in the **Agenda**, **Speaker**, and **Session** tabs, and the order records appear in. You can give a field a custom label for visitors.

### Which records appear

- **Use site filters**: the same rules as the Site's other public pages.
- **This page only**: rules that apply to this page and nowhere else.

Both Speakers and Agenda start with **Status is Accepted**, so cancelled, declined, and in-review sessions stay off the public site. A speaker whose only session was cancelled drops off the speaker list with it. You can narrow further on any session field, such as track, room, day, or session type, and on the Speakers page, on speaker fields as well. The filter panel shows how many sessions currently match.

The filters cover the whole public surface, not just the list. A speaker's profile page is unreachable while none of their sessions match.

On the Speakers page, **Include contacts without sessions** also lists people with no matching sessions. It needs at least one contact rule under **This page only**, so you can't publish every contact in the event by accident. When speaker acceptance is on for your event, **Participant Status** can limit the page to accepted participants.

### Search, filters, and roles

- **Search and filters for visitors**: turn on **Let visitors search by name**, and choose which filters visitors can use (format, language, location, level, tags, and track).
- **Who is included**: the session roles that appear, such as speaker, chairperson, and moderator.

### Display

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Date format | How dates and times are written |
| Day heading format | The heading above each day. **Default** follows the date format and language. Not shown for the Session list |
| Language | The language the page is shown in, or **Site default** |
| Show times in the visitor's time zone | Converts times to each visitor's own time zone |
| Open speaker details in a pop-up | Clicking a speaker opens their details in a pop-up |
| Show Add to calendar | Adds a calendar button to each session |
| Show session sponsors | Shows sponsors on the sessions they sponsor |
| Hide sessions on speaker cards | Speakers page only: shows the speaker without their session list |
| Sort speakers alphabetically | Speakers page only: orders speakers by last name |

## Navigation and footer

- **Navigation**: the top menu builds itself from your pages, then Speakers and Agenda when they're on. Rename, hide, or reorder items, and add external links or form links. Editing anything switches the menu to custom. New pages are still added at the end until you hide them. **Reset to automatic** returns to the derived menu. The site logo always links back to the home page.
- **Footer**: shown at the bottom of every public page on the marketing site. It holds a short blurb, up to four link columns (site pages, forms, or external URLs), social links, a contact email, and a legal line. Leave everything empty for no footer.

## Sign-in options

Select **Login / SSO** to control how people sign in:

- **Standard Login**: email-based magic links, with no passwords.
- **SSO Login**: single sign-on through your identity provider (SAML, OIDC, and others). Contact Sessionboard to enable it.
- **Sign-in card on landing page**: marketing layout only. Sessionboard isn't your registration platform, so if your site is purely promotional you can hide the sign-in card from the landing page. The **Sign in** button stays in the menu and opens the same sign-in form in a dialog, so speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, submitters, and evaluators can always get to their portal.

<Tip>
Attendee registration stays with your registration provider. People sign in to a Program Site to submit, review, or manage their participation. Pair the hidden sign-in card with the Call for papers and Evaluator guide page templates to route each audience to the right door.
</Tip>

## Programs and calls for proposals

**Programs / CFP** isn't a public page. It lists the programs that show as cards on the signed-in home page, and the preview switches to a submitter's view so you can see them.

## Custom pages

Click **+** under **Custom pages** to add a page. Start from a blank page or from a ready-made template you edit like any other page:

- **Call for papers**: explain your submission process, key dates, and link your forms
- **Evaluator guide**: how reviewing works and where evaluators sign in
- **Speaker & partner hub**: where speakers, exhibitors, and sponsors manage their submissions and tasks
- **Convince your boss**: a ready-to-send justification letter
- **First-timer guide**: orientation for first-time attendees
- **Who should attend**: persona blurbs for your audiences
- **Press & media**: accreditation info for journalists
- **Hotels & travel**: venue, hotels, and the official housing link
- **Contact us**: how to reach your team
- **Scam alert**: warn attendees about registration and housing fraud

![The Add a page dialog with a card grid of the blank page and the ten page templates](/images/kb/site-builder-add-page.png)

Custom pages are built from the same [blocks](#blocks) as the landing page. A page made before blocks existed offers **Switch to blocks**: its current text becomes the first block, unchanged, and you can then add images, videos, buttons, forms, and columns.

Each page has its own URL slug and a **Show this page in the landing header and in the portal sidebar** option. You can also copy a page from another event, and drag pages to reorder them in the menu.

## Editing tools

- **Click to edit**: hover any region in the preview (menu, hero, a block, the sign-in card, the footer) and click **Edit** to jump straight to its controls.
- **Preview as**: see the site as a Visitor, Submitter, Evaluator, or Speaker before sharing it.
- **Device widths**: check desktop, tablet, and phone. Every layout is responsive and meets WCAG 2.1 AA out of the box.
- **Generate**: scan your website to pull a logo, colors, and fonts as a starting brand kit.
- **Open live**: open the published site in a new tab at any time.

## Languages

The public site and the builder's page templates are available in English, Spanish, French, and German. Visitors pick their language from the menu.
