The Site builder is the visual editor for your [Program Site](/site/program-site). It has three parts: a **sitemap** on the left listing every setting and page, a **live preview** of your real site in the middle, and an **inspector** on the right with the controls for whatever you select.

Open it from your event under **Program > Site > Settings**, or from your organization portal settings. Everything you change shows in the preview immediately; nothing reaches your live site until you click **Save Changes**.

## Layouts

The layout decides what visitors see before they sign in. Pick it under **Layout** in the sitemap.

- **Portal** — the classic landing: gradient background, sign-in card, program cards, and your custom pages. Every existing Site uses this until you change it.
- **Marketing site** — a public event website with its own top menu, a hero banner, content sections, public program pages, and a footer. Sign-in moves into the page instead of being the page.

<Note>
Switching layouts never deletes anything. Your pages, programs, branding, and login settings carry over, and you can switch back to Portal at any time.
</Note>

## Templates and color schemes

On the marketing layout, the **Layout** inspector offers two independent starting points:

**Templates** set the structure — which hero style leads, which sections follow, and in what order. Applying one restructures the page without touching your colors.

| Template | Structure |
| --- | --- |
| Conference | Speakers up front: stats, speaker grid, agenda preview |
| Trade show | Partners first: CTA, sponsor wall, exhibitors, then the program |
| Congress | Program first: about, agenda preview, key figures |
| Summit | Editorial and minimal: intro, speakers, one CTA |
| Community | The full story on one page: every section |

**Color schemes** set the palette and font pairing without touching your structure: Midnight, Evergreen, Vivid, Slate, Sunset, Ocean, Royal, Aurora, Graphite, Rosewood, and Espresso. Every scheme is audited against WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, so text stays readable on any of them.

A check mark shows which template and scheme your site currently matches. You can apply either one and then customize freely — they are starting points, not locks.

## The landing page

Select **Landing** to edit the public home page: the title and subtitle, the hero banner, and the content sections below it.

### Hero banner

- **Background**: a brand **Gradient**, a full-width **Image**, or a live **Countdown** to a date you set.
- **Buttons**: up to two. Each button can scroll to sign-in, open one of your pages, or link to any URL.
- **Show submission window**: toggles the open/close dates banner under the headline.
- **Add-to-calendar button**: lets visitors download a calendar invite. It appears when your event has dates.

### Content sections

Add sections from a visual grid, then reorder, hide, or remove them. Each section has its own editor in the inspector, and every image slot falls back to a clean placeholder (initials for people, name tiles for companies) so the page never shows a broken image.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Rich text for about copy, announcements, or anything freeform |
| Stats | Key figures — attendees, speakers, exhibitors — as large numbers |
| Call-to-action banner | A headline, supporting line, and one button |
| FAQ | Expandable question-and-answer pairs |
| Sponsors | Your sponsor wall, grouped by tier, pulled live from your event's sponsors |
| Testimonials | Quotes with names, roles, and optional photos |
| Photo gallery | An image grid with captions |
| Topics & tracks | Topic cards with image overlays for your content pillars |
| Partner logos | A hand-curated logo wall with optional links — for partners that are not sponsor records |
| Venue & location | Venue name, address, photo, and a Get directions button |
| Featured speakers | A live grid of your confirmed speakers |
| Agenda preview | The next sessions from your agenda |

Sponsor and speaker sections read from your event data, so the site updates itself as your program changes — no re-publishing.

## Public program pages

Marketing-layout sites can turn on dedicated public pages, each with its own menu entry:

- **Speakers** — a headshot grid of confirmed speakers. Every speaker also gets their own profile page with a shareable URL, their sessions, and a branded share card for social posts.
- **Agenda** — the public session schedule.
- **Exhibitors** — an exhibitor directory with logos. Each exhibitor gets a profile page with their description, website, and share card.

These pages show only public, PII-safe information, and each page carries its own title, description, and structured data so search engines and AI assistants index it correctly. Pasting any page — including a speaker or exhibitor profile — into Slack, LinkedIn, or similar shows a branded preview card.

<Note>
Program pages are off by default. Existing sites never gain public pages until you turn them on.
</Note>

## Navigation and footer

- **Navigation** — the top menu builds itself from your pages. Open the Navigation inspector to rename items, hide them, reorder them, or add external links. **Reset to automatic** returns to the derived menu at any time. The site logo always links back to the home page.
- **Footer** — a short blurb, link columns (site pages or external URLs), social links, a contact email, and a legal line.

## Sign-in options

Select **Login / SSO** to control how people sign in:

- **Standard Login** — email-based magic links; no passwords.
- **SSO Login** — single sign-on through your identity provider (SAML, OIDC, and others). Contact Sessionboard to enable it.
- **Sign-in card on landing page** — marketing layout only. Sessionboard is not your registration platform, so if your site is purely promotional you can hide the sign-in card from the landing page. The **Sign in** button stays in the menu and opens the same sign-in form in a dialog, so speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, submitters, and evaluators can always get to their portal.

<Tip>
Attendee registration stays with your registration provider. People sign in to a Program Site to submit, review, or manage their participation — pair the hidden sign-in card with the Call for papers and Evaluator guide page templates to route each audience to the right door.
</Tip>

## Custom pages

Click **+** under **Custom pages** to add a page. You can start from a blank page or from a ready-made template you edit like any other page:

- **Call for papers** — explain your submission process, key dates, and link your forms
- **Evaluator guide** — how reviewing works and where evaluators sign in
- **Speaker & partner hub** — where speakers, exhibitors, and sponsors manage their submissions and tasks
- **Convince your boss** — a ready-to-send justification letter
- **First-timer guide** — orientation for first-time attendees
- **Who should attend** — persona blurbs for your audiences
- **Press & media** — accreditation info for journalists
- **Hotels & travel** — venue, hotels, and the official housing link
- **Contact us** — how to reach your team
- **Scam alert** — warn attendees about registration and housing fraud

You can also copy a page from another event, and drag pages to reorder them in the menu.

## Editing tools

- **Click to edit** — hover any region in the preview (menu, hero, a section, the sign-in card, the footer) and click **Edit** to jump straight to its controls.
- **Preview as** — see the site as a Visitor, Submitter, Evaluator, or Speaker before sharing it.
- **Device widths** — check desktop, tablet, and phone. Every layout is responsive and WCAG 2.1 AA compliant out of the box.
- **Unsaved changes guard** — leaving the builder with unsaved edits prompts you to save, discard, or keep editing.
- **Generate** — scan your website to pull a logo, colors, and fonts as a starting brand kit.
- **Open live** — open the published site in a new tab at any time.

## Languages

The public site and the builder's page templates are available in English, Spanish, French, and German. Visitors pick their language from the menu.
