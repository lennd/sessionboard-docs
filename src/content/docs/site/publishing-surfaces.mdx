Everything under **CMS** publishes your program somewhere. The four surfaces do different jobs, and they combine: a Site can carry a Content Gallery block, and an Embed can sit beside it on a website you already run. Start from the goal, not the tool — the table below names the surface and the template for each.

## Start from your goal

| You want to… | Build a… | Start from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Give speakers, submitters and reviewers somewhere to sign in | **Site** | Participant hub |
| Show the agenda on a website you already run | **Embed** | Styled agenda widget |
| Feed sessions into a mobile app or partner system | **Embed** | JSON feed |
| Run a poster session people can browse | **Poster Gallery** | Public poster hall |
| Publish the recordings after the event | **Content Gallery** | On-Demand Hub |
| Let attendees search the program and plan their schedule | **Content Gallery** | Session Catalog |
| Serve or sell courses and webinars from one catalog with credit | **Content Gallery** | Learning Catalog |
| Capture a lead before a white paper or deck downloads | **Content Gallery** | Resource Library |
| Take entries and run judging for an awards program | **Site** | Awards portal |

## The four surfaces side by side

| | Site | Embeds | Poster Gallery | Content Gallery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **In one line** | Your program's own home page and the portal participants sign in to | Live agenda, session and speaker data inside a website you already run | A searchable ePoster hall, every poster viewable inline | An evergreen home for recordings, clips, white papers, lessons and sessions |
| **You get** | Drag-and-drop pages, page starters, and the participant portal behind the same login | Agenda, schedule, session and speaker widgets that update the moment the program changes | Inline viewing of PDF, slides, images, video and audio; filters by track, session and author; kiosk mode; per-poster analytics | Templates with collections, filters and auto-fill rules pre-wired; plays, watch time, saves, downloads and gate leads |
| **Who can see it** | Public pages plus portal login (magic link, password or SSO) | Whatever the host page allows — Sessionboard adds no login | Public, share link, verified email or on-site kiosk | Public, email gate, share link, portal login or SSO — per gallery, collection or item |
| **Not for** | Putting the agenda on a website you already run — that is an Embed | Hosting the page itself — that is a Site. Recordings and files — that is a Content Gallery | Recordings, white papers or the session catalog — that is a Content Gallery | A poster session — that is a Poster Gallery. A widget on your marketing site — that is an Embed |

## Site

Build a Site when your program needs a home of its own: a call for papers page, a participant hub where speakers and sponsors manage submissions and tasks, an evaluators page, or an awards portal. It runs on your domain with your branding, and the portal login (magic link, password or SSO) sits behind the same URL.

Page starters: **Call for papers page**, **Participant hub**, **Evaluators page**, **Awards portal**.

## Embeds

Build an Embed when the website already exists and your marketing team owns it. The embed is a widget or feed that you paste into that site; it refreshes on its own as the program changes, and Sessionboard adds no login of its own.

Starters: **Styled agenda widget**, **Speaker gallery**, **JSON feed**, **iCal feed**.

## Poster Gallery

Build a Poster Gallery for a poster session. It collects the poster files already uploaded to your sessions, renders them inline, and adds the things a poster hall needs: author consent, embargo, a share link per poster, QR labels for the boards, and a touchscreen kiosk mode.

Starters: **Public poster hall**, **On-site kiosk**, **Embargoed preview**, **Verified-email gallery**.

## Content Gallery

Build a Content Gallery for everything else your program produces and keeps producing: session recordings and clips, white papers and decks, lessons with credit, webinar recordings, and the session catalog itself. Each starts from a template that pre-wires collections, filters and auto-fill rules, so new content lands in the right place without anyone adding it by hand.

Templates: **On-Demand Hub**, **Session Catalog**, **Learning Catalog**, **Resource Library**, **Webinar Library**, **Speaker Spotlight**, **Year-Round Archive**, or **Start from scratch**.
