A **skill** is a ready-made workflow for an AI assistant connected to Sessionboard. Ask in your own words, *"Run the Awards review for the Innovation Awards"*, and the skill takes the assistant through the whole job: it reads what is there, does the steps in the right order with Sessionboard's tools, and stops to ask you at every point that changes or sends something.

- **It runs as you.** The assistant signs in as you, so it sees and changes only what your Sessionboard role allows. See [Permissions and control](/skills/permissions).
- **It asks first.** Nothing is created, changed, published or sent until you say yes in the chat, and anything that reaches people outside your team also waits for approval in Sessionboard.
- **It works where you work.** Skills use the open Agent Skills format: install them in Claude and Claude Code, use them in ChatGPT, or paste them into any assistant connected to Sessionboard. See [Install skills](/skills/install).

Each skill's page says what you get, every step, where it stops for you, the tools and permissions it uses, prompts to try, and what it cannot do yet.
