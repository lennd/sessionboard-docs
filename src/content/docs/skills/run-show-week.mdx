## How it works

1. Create the show once: name, cadence, hosts and its podcast feed
2. Plan each episode: topic, guests from your contacts, time and Zoom link
3. Link the recording and draft the podcast episode, clips, newsletter issue, guest kit and page section
4. Publish, schedule and send on your OK, then keep the results on the episode

## Where it stops for you

It stops and asks you in the conversation:

- Before every write: creating or changing a show, podcast or episode, importing a recording, running an episode
- Before sending the guest their kit to review
- Before publishing the podcast episode, scheduling the newsletter or publishing the page

These wait for a person to approve them in Sessionboard, even after you say yes in the chat. The assistant gives you the link and stops:

- Publishing podcast episodes, scheduling the newsletter and sending content kits, only if your organization turned on Assistant Approvals

- Any organization member can read; creating shows and drafting episodes needs an organization admin.

# Run a show week

A recurring show (for example "Event Content Lab with Chris & Josh") lives in
Sessionboard Marketing as a show with numbered episodes. You do the busywork
around each episode; the hosts pick guests, record, approve and post.

## When to use

- "Set up our weekly show." / "Plan next week's episode."
- "Ep 3 is recorded, make everything from it."
- "What's waiting for us on the show this week?"
- "Log the results for last week's episode."

## The flow

1. **Find or make the show.** `list_shows`. No show yet? Create one as a draft
   with `create_show` (name, cadence, usual weekday and time, hosts). It needs
   a podcast feed for episodes: `list_podcasts`, or `create_podcast` for a feed
   of its own, then `update_show { podcast_show_id }`.
2. **Plan the episode.** `get_show` shows every episode and its status
   (idea → booked → recorded → produced → published → shared). Add the next
   one with `create_show_episode` (topic, guests as CRM contacts from
   `find_crm_contacts`, `scheduled_at`, the Zoom link). Booking the guest and
   sending invites happen in the person's calendar and email, after their OK.
3. **Link the recording.** After the call, `list_recordings { available: true }`
   then `import_recording`, and `update_show_episode { recording_media_id }`.
4. **Draft everything.** `run_show_episode` makes, as drafts: the podcast
   episode, a reel, clips, quote cards, a post pack and recap, a newsletter
   issue, a guest kit dry run and the page section. Content renders in the
   background: check `list_content { run_id }`, then run `run_show_episode`
   again to refresh the guest kit (finished steps answer `done`).
5. **Review with the hosts.** Show them what was drafted: the podcast episode
   title, the clips, the newsletter issue, who the guest kit goes to.
6. **Guest review.** On the hosts' OK, `send_content_kits` with confirm sends
   the guest their clips and quotes to review. Sending the kit is a request,
   not the guest's approval: check `list_content` for their decision before
   anything featuring them is published.
7. **Publish on the hosts' OK, one tool each.** `set_podcast_episode_status`
   (published) and `schedule_newsletter_issue`. The episode section for the
   show page is in the run result: add it to the show's Site, check
   `get_site_diff`, then `publish_site`. Mark the episode
   `update_show_episode { status: "published" }` only once those tools report
   it done (not on `needs_approval`, and not before a scheduled send goes
   out), and `shared` once the posts are out.
8. **Results.** A week later, `get_podcast_stats` and `list_content`; keep the
   numbers with `update_show_episode { results }` (they merge).

## Examples

- "Plan episode 4 with Dana from Northwind on hybrid sponsorship, Tuesday 2 pm
  ET" → `find_crm_contacts { q: "Dana" }` → `create_show_episode`.
- "Ep 3 is in Zoom, make everything" → `list_recordings { available: true }` →
  `import_recording` → `update_show_episode` → `run_show_episode`.

## Guardrails

- Ask before every write, and say what it will do: creating or changing a
  show, podcast or episode, importing a recording, and running the episode
  (it spends AI work on clips and posts). Reads need no OK.
- Everything `run_show_episode` makes is a draft. Never publish the podcast
  episode, schedule the newsletter, send guest kits or publish the page
  without the hosts' explicit OK in this conversation. Their assistant's
  permission prompt is the approval; Sessionboard asks again only if the
  organization turned on Assistant Approvals.
- Don't publish anything featuring the guest until the guest has approved
  their kit.
- Never invent results; record only numbers a tool returned or a host gave.
- The show's name is "Event Content Lab"; never shorten it to "Content Lab".
  Don't describe the show as live streamed.
