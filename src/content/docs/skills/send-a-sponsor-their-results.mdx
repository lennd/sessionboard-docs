## How it works

1. Name the sponsor: it finds them and reads their report — session views and attendance, questions, clips, posts, clicks, credited registrations, app placements and booth leads, counts only.
2. It explains what worked and what to offer next time.
3. On your OK it makes a read-only link to the report; no attendee names are on it.
4. If you ask, it counts who opted in to hear from sponsors and asks to make the sponsor a private link to them, which you approve in Sessionboard.
5. It shows which sponsor links were opened and stops one on your OK.

## Where it stops for you

It stops and asks you in the conversation:

- Before adding a sponsor to an event
- Before making a link to the sponsor report
- Before making a private sponsor link to the opted-in people
- Before stopping a sponsor link

These wait for a person to approve them in Sessionboard, even after you say yes in the chat. The assistant gives you the link and stops:

- Making a link to the sponsor report
- Making a private sponsor link to the opted-in people

## What you need

- Reading results and making a results link need an organization Contacts permission. Making or stopping a private sponsor link needs an organization admin.
- The Sessionboard connector, signed in as you. It needs `read:marketing`, `write:marketing`, `write:sponsors`; the write scopes only for the steps that change something. [Install skills](/skills/install) covers connecting.
- Everything runs as you: the assistant sees and changes only what your role allows. See [Permissions and control](/skills/permissions).

# Send a sponsor their results

The person gets a sponsor's report from what Sessionboard already tracks, and a safe way to hand it over: a read-only link to the counts, and, when they choose, a private link to the people who opted in to hear from sponsors. It works for any organization with Sessionboard Marketing, a Marketing workspace or a full-platform account, with the same tools. Plan limits and role permissions are enforced by Sessionboard, not by you.

**Sponsors get aggregates, never attendee names.** The sponsor report is counts only. The only people a sponsor can ever see are those who subscribed to hear from sponsors, through `share_with_sponsor`, and only after a person approves it in Sessionboard.

## When to use

Use this when someone wants to show a sponsor what their sponsorship delivered — pick the sponsor, read their counts-only report (session views and watches, attendance, questions, clips, posts, link clicks, credited registrations, app placement views and taps, booth leads), send them a read-only link to it, give them a private link to the people who opted in to hear from sponsors, see who opened the links, and stop a link working. Triggers on "how did Acme's sponsorship do?", "send Acme their report", "make a sponsor report for Globex", "share the results with our sponsor", "give Acme the opted-in list", "which sponsor links were opened?", "revoke Acme's link", "kill that sponsor link".

## Before you start

- The Sessionboard connector is signed in with OAuth as the person.
- Scopes: `read:marketing` to read reports and links; `write:marketing` to make or revoke a link. Adding a sponsor to an event also needs `write:sponsors`.
- Sessionboard checks that the person belongs to the organization. Reading results and making a results link need an org **Contacts** permission (read, update or manage). Making or revoking a private sponsor link needs an org admin. A connection limited to particular events cannot read an org-wide report.

## Guardrails (read first)

- **Confirm before every write or share.** Before `share_results`, `share_with_sponsor`, `revoke_sponsor_share` or `manage_sponsor`, say exactly what happens: which sponsor, which report or audience, how many people (a count), how long the link works. Get a clear yes in this conversation. Never infer a yes from an earlier answer or a plan. Reads need no confirmation.
- **Some actions wait for a person in Sessionboard.** `share_results` and `share_with_sponsor` from an assistant can answer `needs_approval` with a URL. Tell the person "Waiting for your OK in Sessionboard: <url>" and stop. Do not call it again.
- **Pass `asked`** (the person's request in their own words, one sentence) on `share_results`, `share_with_sponsor` and `revoke_sponsor_share`, so the approval in Sessionboard shows what they asked for.
- **Never put a name in front of a sponsor.** Read the report with `scope: "sponsor"` only. Never paste rows from an event or org report (people, posts by attendees) into anything meant for the sponsor.
- **A link can be opened by anyone who has it.** Say so before making one. A results link works 30 days unless `days` says otherwise.
- **Never invent a number.** Every figure you state is in the report. Credited registrations are shared across touches over 90 days (40% first, 40% last, 20% between), so they can be fractions; say "credited", never "brought".
- Words: **sponsor** (never "partner"), **Advocacy** for sharing, **Surveys** for attendee feedback. Never name the in-product assistant or internal engines.

## The workflow

1. **Pick the sponsor.** `list_report_sponsors { q? }` (up to 50; sponsors on the organization's events and company records). If several match, show the names and ask which. It returns names only: for "our top sponsor", ask the person which one, or read two or three reports (step 2) and compare them before choosing.
   - Not listed because they are not on the event yet: ask, then `manage_sponsor { action: "create", event_id, name, level?, website?, contact_email?, description? }`. Sessions must still be tagged with the sponsor in Sessionboard before the report has numbers.
2. **Read the report.** `get_results { scope: "sponsor", sponsor_id, event_id?, from?, to? }`. `event_id` narrows to one event; `from` / `to` are `YYYY-MM-DD`. Lead with the answer, then the counts: their sessions' views, watches and attendance, questions, clips, posts, link clicks and credited registrations, app placement views and taps, and booth leads. If a list says `truncated`, say the totals still cover everything.
3. **Explain it if asked.** `explain_results { scope: "sponsor", sponsor_id, event_id? }`: use its `headline`, `worked`, `did_not` and `next_moves`. Anything you add must come from its `facts` or the report.
4. **Send the report.**
   **Before you offer a link:** if every count in the report is zero, do not share it. Say so: usually no sessions, clips or placements are tagged with this sponsor yet. Offer to look at what to tag instead.

   **Pause:** "Make a read-only link to Acme's sponsor report (counts only, no attendee names) that anyone with it can open for 30 days?"
   On yes: `share_results { scope: "sponsor", sponsor_id, event_id?, from?, to?, title?, days?, asked }`. Give the `url` (it is returned once) and `expires_at`. If it answers `needs_approval`, say "Waiting for your OK in Sessionboard: <url>" and stop.
5. **Give the sponsor the opted-in list (optional, only when asked).** First count who would be on it, without saving: `build_audience { definition: { roles, eventId?, attended? } }` (roles: speaker, attendee, contact, visitor). Only people who opted in to hear from sponsors appear on the sponsor's list; counts under 10 show as "Fewer than 10" on the sponsor's page.
   **Pause:** "Create a private link for Acme to the attendees of Summit 2027 who opted in to hear from sponsors, for 30 days? They see counts, a profile of the audience and their report totals, and can download only the opted-in people."
   On yes: `share_with_sponsor { sponsor_account_id, definition: { roles?, event_id?, attended? }, audience_name?, days?, asked }`. `sponsor_account_id` is the sponsor's id from `list_report_sponsors`; use `sponsor_name` only for a sponsor with no record. A saved audience can go in as `audience_id` instead of `definition`. It answers `needs_approval`: say "Waiting for your OK in Sessionboard: <url>" and stop.
6. **See what was opened.** `list_sponsor_shares {}`: each link's sponsor, audience, created, expires, active, views and downloads.
7. **Stop a link.**
   **Pause:** "Stop Acme's link now? Views and downloads so far stay on record."
   `revoke_sponsor_share { share_id, asked }`. A results link from `share_results` is revoked by the person in Results in Sessionboard.

## When something goes wrong

- **`needs_approval`**: not an error. "Waiting for your OK in Sessionboard: <url>"; do not call again.
- **403 "This token cannot use Marketing"**: the connection lacks `read:marketing` / `write:marketing`, or the person is not an org admin (for sponsor links). Reports need a connection not limited to particular events.
- **409**: the link or report changed; read it again before retrying.
- **400 / 422 "Invalid request"**: say the message; usually a missing `sponsor_id` with `scope: "sponsor"`, or neither a sponsor nor an audience on `share_with_sponsor`.
- **404 "Not found"**: check the sponsor with `list_report_sponsors` and the link with `list_sponsor_shares`.

## What this skill does not do

- No sponsor report for exhibitors, and no PDF or CSV export of the report from here (the Results page in Sessionboard has Export CSV).
- It cannot list or revoke results links; a person revokes those in Results.
- It cannot email the link to the sponsor; the person sends it.
- It cannot tag sessions with a sponsor, or change an existing event sponsor's details; do those in Sessionboard.
- No revenue share or sponsored-reach packages.

## Tools used

- `list_report_sponsors` — pick the sponsor (read)
- `manage_sponsor` — add a missing sponsor to an event (write)
- `get_results` — the sponsor's counts-only report with `scope: "sponsor"` (read)
- `explain_results` — what worked, in plain words (read)
- `share_results` — a read-only link to the report; may wait for approval (write)
- `build_audience` — count who a sponsor link would cover, counts only; used here without `save`, so nothing is stored (write tool, no change)
- `share_with_sponsor` — a private link to the opted-in people; waits for approval (sends)
- `list_sponsor_shares` — sponsor links with views and downloads (read)
