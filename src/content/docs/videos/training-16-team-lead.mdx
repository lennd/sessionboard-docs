Team Lead. Every screen in Sessionboard has a guide standing next to it, and this is how you open it. Ask, in the top bar, on any page. It slides in on the right and stays there while you work, so you never leave the screen you had a question about.

Before you type anything it reads where you are. The guide names the page the way the sidebar does, the event you're in, and what's already configured here, real counts from your event. Then the questions worth asking about this page. Walk me through it. What's already set up here. What should I set up next. And read the Help Center for this page, which jumps to the matching article. Under that, the neighbouring pages your event already has, only what you can open today, never features to turn on.

Or just ask. Type a question in your own words, which sessions still don't have a room, who hasn't accepted their speaker agreement, and the answer comes from your event's own records, not a description of what the page usually does. It calls pages what the sidebar calls them, and when your event doesn't have something, it says so plainly and points you to your account manager. The microphone is the same conversation out loud, handy when your hands are on a spreadsheet.

It follows you. Move to another page mid-conversation and a card marks the change in the transcript, so the answers that follow are about the new page. New conversation at the top clears the thread and re-reads wherever you're standing.

Create is Team Lead building something for you. Pick a template, a report on review completion by reviewer, say, and it drafts it from your data, ready to adjust. As more templates arrive they show up here.

Learn is the Help Center, filtered to this page. Read is the articles, searchable, with the video tutorials and standard field lists alongside. So when read the Help Center for this page is the prompt you pick, this is where you land.

Watch is the training videos for this page, the same ones as Training in the sidebar. On Sessions that's the platform overview, sessions and the agenda, and review and evaluation, with their running times, and all training videos takes you to the full set.

F A Q collects the questions for this screen, and until there are some, it hands you back to Team Lead. Attention is the one tab that comes to you, anything an agent needs a decision on, a review to approve, a draft to look at. All caught up means exactly that.

If you take one thing from this, it's the habit. Stuck on a page, press Ask before you open a ticket. The Help Center article on the Team Lead page guide has the detail. The Platform overview video is the tour of everything Team Lead can point you to, and every other video in this set is one tab away under Watch.
