Dispatch. Advocacy hands your speakers and sponsors a card and a link and asks them to share. Dispatch points the same idea inward. You package event content into a kit, posts, quote cards, clips, pick the goal, and send it to your own sales, membership or marketing team through Slack and email. In tracked mode every teammate gets a personal link, so you end up with a leaderboard instead of a guess about who posted.

One thing before anything else. Dispatch is in Early Access, and it sits on top of the Marketing module. With Marketing on, the group in the sidebar grows, Dispatch under Promote, Plan, Voices, Posts, Quotes, Clips and Brand under Source and produce. Open Dispatch, and if what you see is the Dispatches are in Early Access card, click Request access and the team will switch it on for your organisation. Once it's on, the card gives way to the real list and a New Dispatch button.

Here it is with the flag on. Marketing, Dispatch. Packaged content sent to your team for this event, tracked, with leaderboards. New Dispatch is the button that starts one. Show guide opens the short version of what you're about to do, package assets per audience, deliver to your own team on Slack and email, one tap share with per person tracking and a leaderboard, and a drip schedule anchored to your outcome's key date.

It helps to hold the two products side by side. Advocacy activates people outside your organisation, speakers, sponsors, exhibitors, and it works on every event with nothing to switch on. Dispatch equips people inside it, and it is the one you request. They look alike, cards, links, a leaderboard, but they point in opposite directions, and most teams will run Advocacy on every event long before they need Dispatch.

What to have ready. Slack connected, if you want kits to land in channels or direct messages. That's an app, under Operations in the marketplace, and email alone works without it. Team members in your organisation, so the recipient picker can find them. And a destination link, if you're running tracked, the page every personal link resolves to.

What goes in a kit comes from the rest of the Marketing group. Quotes is the library of quote cards pulled from your sessions and recordings. Clips is the short video cuts. Posts is the drafted social copy, and Plan is the content plan that lays them out over time. A dispatch picks from these, so the more you've sourced and produced here, the richer the kit your team gets.

The New Dispatch flow asks six things. What's the goal, drive registrations, boost attendance, grow reach and authority, maximise on demand, drive pipeline, or custom, and that sets the default call to action and which metric counts as primary. Who's it for, sales, membership, marketing or custom, plus the call to action and, in tracked mode, the destination link. What goes in, a name and the asset types, LinkedIn post, quote card, clip, audiogram, carousel, blog snippet, talking points, at least one.

Then the posts, with an optional schedule at twenty one, fourteen, seven and one day out, and you edit the copy and the asset on each. How it reaches them, deliver to share, which is tracked, or deliver to use, which is not, then Slack channels, Slack people and email addresses. And create, or send test first so the kit lands in your own inbox before anyone else's.

Once it's out, open the dispatch for its schedule timeline. Send now delivers the next pending item, and each scheduled row lets you nudge the people who haven't shared, mark it sent or edit the copy. Recipients never need a Sessionboard account. They get the kit in Slack or email and share from there.

Tracked dispatches show clicks, shares and conversions with your goal's metric highlighted and a to go count until you hit the target, and Top performers ranks your team by shares. Untracked dispatches show nothing at all, that's the trade off for a simpler kit, and you can switch one to tracked later.

That's Dispatch, your own team as the channel, with the receipts. For the outside version, the one every event already has, see Advocacy, where speakers and sponsors get their branded card and tracked link. And for the assets both of them carry, the Quotes and Clips libraries under Source and produce.
