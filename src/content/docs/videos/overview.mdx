## Training videos

Short chapters recorded on a demo event as an event administrator, with captions and a chapter list. Watch them in any order: each one plays inside the article it was scripted from, so the walkthrough and the written guide sit together.

- **Platform overview** (4:41) — A short tour of Sessionboard before any other chapter. Organisation versus event, the sidebar top to bottom, Program, CRM, Marketing and CMS, the setup rows, pinning and search, and where to start. Watch it in [What is Sessionboard?](/get-started/overview#training-00-platform-overview).
- **Speaker portal & tasks** (5:20) — Where accepted speakers land, what you ask them to do there, and how you see who has done it. Watch it in [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#training-01-speaker-portal-tasks).
- **Communications & emails** (6:39) — Templates with merge tags, sending to the people you filtered, when a scheduled or recurring Email Campaign beats a module send, and the emails the platform sends for you. Watch it in [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails#training-02-communications-emails).
- **Event setup** (5:34) — Event details, the record settings that shape every form, the custom fields your data needs, and getting your team in. Watch it in [Event details & record settings](/events/event-details#training-03-event-setup).
- **Call for papers: the submission form** (6:09) — The program vocabulary the form draws from, who the roles are, and how the form builder turns fields into questions. Watch it in [Session submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms#training-04-call-for-papers-form).
- **Integrations: registration, website and the API** (5:42) — What leaves Sessionboard and when, the App Marketplace, agenda and speaker embeds for your website, and the developer surface. Watch it in [App marketplace](/integrations/overview#training-05-integrations).
- **Sessions, scheduling and the agenda** (4:31) — Creating sessions by hand, the session editor, subsessions, and building the schedule in the agenda views with conflict rules. Watch it in [Build & manage your agenda](/sessions/agenda#training-06-sessions-agenda).
- **Submission review and evaluation: statuses, plans and the evaluator's review** (6:35) — The five status categories and the Status popover, what an evaluation plan is and the settings that shape one, then the evaluator's side end to end on your Site, the sign-in link, My Reviews, the round queue, scoring a submission against the rubric with a comment and Save Review, what done looks like, and how that review lands on the admin's plan, the Evaluators tab, the Review tab and the Evaluation Summary. Watch it in [Create & manage evaluation plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans#training-07-review-evaluation).
- **Users, permissions and seats** (4:39) — Who is on the event team, what each default role can see, custom roles, field and status permissions, portal users versus team members, and organization-level access. Watch it in [Invite & manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members#training-08-users-permissions).
- **Contacts and imports** (5:03) — The Contacts module and its role views, adding a contact without creating a duplicate, columns and saved views, Options > Import (template, upload, mapping, validation), updating existing records by import, removing a contact from one event or all, and merging duplicates. Watch it in [Importing data](/settings/importing-data#training-09-contacts-imports).
- **Reports, table views and exports** (5:54) — The Reports page and the Report Builder, from a gallery example to filters, grouping, saving, scheduling and sharing. Then the Preferences drawer on any module table, saved views, and exporting a module to CSV or Excel. Watch it in [Build a report](/reporting/report-builder#training-10-reports-views-exports).
- **Organization settings** (5:12) — The settings that sit above every event. Organization details and timezone, the shared library of fields, tags and files, email templates and the system emails, the custom sending domain add-on, brand kits, and Preview, the Early Access program. Watch it in [Email templates](/settings/email-templates#training-11-organization-settings).
- **Advocacy: speakers as a sharing channel** (6:38) — Give every speaker, sponsor and exhibitor a branded share card and a tracked link, and see who shared and who drove the traffic. A live campaign with its recipients, activity and analytics, then Quick Launch step by step, the share console your speakers see, the landing page, the notify step nobody should skip, and how the links attribute every click to a person. Watch it in [Advocacy campaigns](/marketing/advocacy#training-12-advocacy).
- **Site and CMS: publishing your program** (6:18) — Everything under CMS puts your program in front of an audience. Which of the four surfaces to build for each goal, the Site overview, the Site builder with its draft-and-publish flow, sitemap, layouts, templates and color schemes, sign-in and program cards, and where Embeds and the galleries fit. Watch it in [Site builder](/site/site-builder#training-13-site-and-cms).
- **Automations** (4:48) — When a session or person field changes, or a new submission arrives, send a branded email or assign reviewers. The gallery recipes, the When and Then cards, re-enroll, test and enable, and the Delivery history that answers "was this person emailed?". Watch it in [Automations](/automations/overview#training-14-automations).
- **Exhibitors and sponsors** (4:41) — Exhibitors and sponsors are groups, companies with contacts attached. The two lists, the group profile and its CRM tab, tiers and what they unlock, portal access for a sponsor's people, the intake form that lets sponsors onboard themselves, and adding a group by hand. Watch it in [Create sponsor groups & contacts](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsors#training-15-exhibitors-sponsors).
- **Team Lead: your guide on every page** (3:07) — Team Lead is the guide in the sidebar. Open it from Ask on any page and it reads where you are, offers the questions to ask about that page, answers from your event's own data and the Help Center, and keeps the matching articles and training videos one tab away. Watch it in [Get around with Team Lead's page guide](/agents/team-lead#training-16-team-lead).
- **Dispatch: your own team as the channel** (4:52) — Dispatch packages event content into a kit and sends it to your sales, membership or marketing team through Slack and email, tracked, with a leaderboard. It is Early Access, so this is what to request, what to have ready, where the assets come from, the six questions the New Dispatch flow asks, and how tracked and untracked kits differ. Watch it in [Dispatch](/marketing/dispatch#training-17-dispatch).
- **Interest forms: a standing speak-with-us page** (7:59) — An organization-level form that collects speaker interest all year, outside any one event, and drops every submission into the Speaker CRM Pipeline as a card with the person saved as a contact. The Interest Forms page and one open form's dashboard, its submissions and speakers, the five-step wizard (details, events, fields, managers, notifications), publishing the form on a Site and what a speaker sees, where submissions land in the Pipeline, and the optional evaluation plan. Watch it in [Speaker CRM: interest forms](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms#training-18-interest-forms).
- **Awards: set up a program** (6:10) — An awards program from the Awards hub through the eight-step wizard, identity and nomenclature, categories, the form builder with its sections, field types, quick add, rules and preview, pricing, rounds, winner tags, managers and notifications, then the program dashboard and publishing the program on a Site. Watch it in [Getting started with awards](/awards/getting-started#training-19-awards-program-setup).
- **Awards: nominate, review and pick winners** (6:48) — One nomination all the way through. The nominee signs in to your Site, picks a category and fills in the form; your team sees the entry on the Submissions tab, adds reviewers and assigns them on the round with the Impact Preview; the scorecard and blind review; then the reviewer in their own portal, the queue, scoring a nomination against the scorecard with a comment and Save Review, what done looks like, and the score arriving on the entry's Reviews tab and the round's Review Progress; then Mark as Winner and the winner notifications. Watch it in [Managing submissions, reviewers & rounds](/awards/managing-submissions-reviewers-rounds#training-20-awards-submit-review).
- **Print: program books, grids and certificates** (6:43) — The Print page and its starters, Word import, AI prompt and legacy documents, certificates per role, the booklet editor with sections, live data blocks, the block inspector, brand kit and page setup, print layout preview, Regenerate with AI, revision history, PDF and Markdown export, the shared snapshot link, and Recompile to keep it current. Watch it in [Print agendas & program books](/marketing/print-agendas#training-21-print-booklets).
