## Training series

Ten chapters that follow the order an onboarding runs in, each recorded on a demo event as an event administrator, with captions and a chapter list. Start with the overview, then take the chapters for the parts of Sessionboard your event uses.

- [00 · Platform overview](/videos/training-00-platform-overview) (3:23) — A three-minute tour of Sessionboard before the nine chapters — organisation versus event, and one screen per module, CRM, Program, Portals, Send, setup and Training — ending with where to start.
- [01 · Speaker portal & tasks](/videos/training-01-speaker-portal-tasks) (5:17) — Where accepted speakers land, what you ask them to do there, and how you see who has done it.
- [02 · Communications & emails](/videos/training-02-communications-emails) (5:07) — Templates with merge tags, sending to the people you filtered, and the emails the platform sends for you.
- [03 · Event setup](/videos/training-03-event-setup) (5:31) — Event details, the record settings that shape every form, the custom fields your data needs, and getting your team in.
- [04 · Call for papers: the submission form](/videos/training-04-call-for-papers-form) (6:10) — The program vocabulary the form draws from, who the roles are, and how the form builder turns fields into questions.
- [05 · Integrations: registration, website and the API](/videos/training-05-integrations) (5:42) — What leaves Sessionboard and when, the App Marketplace, agenda and speaker embeds for your website, and the developer surface.
- [06 · Sessions, scheduling and the agenda](/videos/training-06-sessions-agenda) (4:29) — Creating sessions by hand, the session editor, subsessions, and building the schedule in the agenda views with conflict rules.
- [07 · Submission review and evaluation](/videos/training-07-review-evaluation) (4:45) — The five status categories, changing a status, evaluation plans and what evaluators see, reading the scores and making the committee decision.
- [08 · Users, permissions and seats](/videos/training-08-users-permissions) (4:05) — Who is on the event team, what each default role can see, custom roles and field-level permissions, portal users versus team members, and organization-level access.
- [09 · Contacts and imports](/videos/training-09-contacts-imports) (4:59) — The Contacts module and its role views, adding a contact without creating a duplicate, columns and saved views, Options > Import (template, upload, mapping, validation), updating existing records by import, removing a contact from one event or all, and merging duplicates.
