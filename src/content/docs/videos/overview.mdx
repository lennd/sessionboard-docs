## Training series

Ten chapters that follow the order an onboarding runs in, each recorded on a demo event as an event administrator, with captions and a chapter list. Start with the overview, then take the chapters for the parts of Sessionboard your event uses.

- [00 · Platform overview](/videos/training-00-platform-overview) (4:18) — A short tour of Sessionboard before any other chapter. Organisation versus event, the sidebar top to bottom, Program, CRM, Marketing and CMS, the setup rows, pinning and search, and where to start.
- [01 · Speaker portal & tasks](/videos/training-01-speaker-portal-tasks) (5:20) — Where accepted speakers land, what you ask them to do there, and how you see who has done it.
- [02 · Communications & emails](/videos/training-02-communications-emails) (5:05) — Templates with merge tags, sending to the people you filtered, and the emails the platform sends for you.
- [03 · Event setup](/videos/training-03-event-setup) (5:34) — Event details, the record settings that shape every form, the custom fields your data needs, and getting your team in.
- [04 · Call for papers: the submission form](/videos/training-04-call-for-papers-form) (6:02) — The program vocabulary the form draws from, who the roles are, and how the form builder turns fields into questions.
- [05 · Integrations: registration, website and the API](/videos/training-05-integrations) (5:41) — What leaves Sessionboard and when, the App Marketplace, agenda and speaker embeds for your website, and the developer surface.
- [06 · Sessions, scheduling and the agenda](/videos/training-06-sessions-agenda) (4:31) — Creating sessions by hand, the session editor, subsessions, and building the schedule in the agenda views with conflict rules.
- [07 · Submission review and evaluation](/videos/training-07-review-evaluation) (5:12) — The five status categories, changing a status, evaluation plans and what evaluators see, reading the scores and making the committee decision.
- [08 · Users, permissions and seats](/videos/training-08-users-permissions) (4:05) — Who is on the event team, what each default role can see, custom roles and field-level permissions, portal users versus team members, and organization-level access.
- [09 · Contacts and imports](/videos/training-09-contacts-imports) (5:03) — The Contacts module and its role views, adding a contact without creating a duplicate, columns and saved views, Options > Import (template, upload, mapping, validation), updating existing records by import, removing a contact from one event or all, and merging duplicates.
- [10 · Reports, table views and exports](/videos/training-10-reports-views-exports) (5:54) — The Reports page and the Report Builder, from a gallery example to filters, grouping, saving, scheduling and sharing. Then the Preferences drawer on any module table, saved views, and exporting a module to CSV or Excel.
- [11 · Organization settings](/videos/training-11-organization-settings) (5:09) — The settings that sit above every event. Organization details and timezone, the shared library of fields, tags and files, email templates and the system emails, the custom sending domain add-on, brand kits, and the Early Access program.
- [12 · Marketing: Advocacy and Dispatch](/videos/training-12-marketing-advocacy-dispatch) (5:02) — Turn speakers into a measurable sharing channel. Where Marketing lives in the sidebar, a live Advocacy campaign with its recipients, activity and analytics, Quick Launch for a new one, the notify step nobody should skip, and Dispatch, the same idea pointed at your own team.
- [13 · Site and CMS: publishing your program](/videos/training-13-site-and-cms) (5:59) — Everything under CMS puts your program in front of an audience. Which of the four surfaces to build for each goal, the Site overview, the Site builder with its draft-and-publish flow, sitemap, layouts, templates and color schemes, sign-in and program cards, and where Embeds and the galleries fit.
- [14 · Automations](/videos/training-14-automations) (4:48) — When a session or person field changes, or a new submission arrives, send a branded email or assign reviewers. The gallery recipes, the When and Then cards, re-enroll, test and enable, and the Delivery history that answers "was this person emailed?".
- [15 · Exhibitors and sponsors](/videos/training-15-exhibitors-sponsors) (4:41) — Exhibitors and sponsors are groups, companies with contacts attached. The two lists, the group profile and its CRM tab, tiers and what they unlock, portal access for a sponsor's people, the intake form that lets sponsors onboard themselves, and adding a group by hand.
- [16 · Team Lead: your guide on every page](/videos/training-16-team-lead) (3:07) — Team Lead is the guide in the sidebar. Open it from Ask on any page and it reads where you are, offers the questions to ask about that page, answers from your event's own data and the Help Center, and keeps the matching articles and training videos one tab away.
