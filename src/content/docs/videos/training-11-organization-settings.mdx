Organization settings. Everything in Sessionboard lives in one of two places, inside an event, or above all of them at the organization. This chapter is the organization half, the things you set once and every event inherits, so the team building next year's event isn't rebuilding fields, templates and branding from scratch.

Settings, at the bottom of the organization sidebar. The overview is a map. Organization, your details, the team and their permissions, and single sign-on. Library, the fields, tags, files and session settings shared by every event. Configuration, integrations, API tokens, connected apps and the pipeline. And Communications, email templates. The left rail is the same list, so you can jump straight to any of them.

Details first. Your organization's name and its slug, the short version of the name that stands in for it in links. The timezone matters more than it looks, every organization-wide export writes its dates in this zone. Below it, the export date format and a switch to add separate day, date and time columns to exports, which spreadsheet people love. And your logo and background image, the organization-level branding.

The library is the part that saves the most time. Fields, every attribute on a contact, a group, a session or an event, in one catalogue. Add a field here and it's available in every event, on forms, in tables and in reports. Field Rules format a value, calculate one, or fill in other fields on the same record when a saved value matches. Tags are reusable labels across records. Files is the shared document library, and session settings hold the formats, tracks and statuses that new events start from.

Email Templates. Two tabs. Templates are yours, and Add Template is how you make one. A name, then the type, contacts, groups or sessions, which decides which merge tags you can use and where the template shows up. Reply to, ideally a shared inbox. A sender name. CC and BCC, up to five each. A subject line, and the body, with Merge Tags inserting the contact's name, the session title, the portal link. Send test mails it to you first. Each event has its own Email Templates page too, same dialog.

The System tab is the other half, the emails Sessionboard sends on its own. Organization invites, password resets, two-factor codes, the magic links that sign portal users in. Each row tells you the channel, when it's sent, and what triggers it. You don't write these, but this is where you look when someone asks, what did my speaker get, and when.

One thing you'll notice on every template, there's no from address. By default all of this goes out from no-reply at notify dot sessionboard dot com. The custom email domain add-on changes that to your own address, events at your organization dot org. It's a corporate domain, not Gmail or Outlook, you add a few DNS records we give you, and once they verify, Send From appears on your templates. Allow about a week. Support at sessionboard dot com starts it, and can tell you the domain's authentication status at any time.

Marketing, Brand. A brand kit is your visual identity and your writing voice in one place. Colours, fonts, logos and backgrounds on one side, and on the other a voice, a summary, tone words, vocabulary, things to do and avoid, which Sessionboard can learn from a page you point it at. New kit starts one, your first kit becomes the organization default, and any event can pick a different one. Everything the Marketing module generates, drafts, carousels, clips and share cards, picks the kit up automatically.

Preview, near the bottom of the organization sidebar, is the Early Access program. This is where features that are built and working sit before they're on for everyone. Joining is one click for the whole organization, and it enables nothing by itself, it just unlocks the catalogue. Then every feature is a card. Preview features have a toggle you flip yourself, and turning one off is never destructive. Beta features have Request access, our team reaches out and sets it up with you. Org-wide means every event, per-event means you pick.

Seeing this page needs the Manage Early Access permission, and the rest of these settings need the organization Admin role. Who has which is the Team and permissions chapter. The Help Center articles are Email templates, Brand kits, the custom email domain add-on, and Early Access.
