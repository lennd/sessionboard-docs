Hi. This is a three minute tour of Sessionboard before you go into any of the nine training chapters. Sessionboard runs your speaker and session program end to end, the call for papers, the review, the agenda, the speakers' portal, the emails, and the content that comes out the other side. This is where you land when you sign in, your organisation.

Two levels to keep in your head. The organisation is the container, your events live inside it, and so do the things you share between them, contacts, custom fields, email templates, your team. An event is one edition of one conference. Most of your day is inside an event, so let's open one.

The event dashboard. Header shows which event you're in, with its dates and timezone, and back to organisation is always top left. The left nav is the whole product, grouped, and it only shows what your plan includes and your role allows, so yours may be shorter than mine. Let's walk it top to bottom.

CRM is the people. Contacts is everyone attached to the event, with views by role, speakers, chairpersons, moderators, submitters. Exhibitors and sponsors are groups, companies with their own people and their own portal. Contacts are shared across the organisation, so the same speaker is one record in every event, that's chapter nine.

Program is the content. Sessions, whether they arrived through a submission form or you typed them in, and along the top, forms, the call for papers, evaluation, where your committee scores them, and agenda, where accepted sessions get a room and a time, with conflict checks. Chapters four, six and seven live in here.

Portals is the speaker's side of the product. Each portal is a branded page where a speaker, sponsor or exhibitor signs in, sees their tasks, bio, headshot, agreement, and completes them. You build the tasks here, they see the checklist there. Chapter one.

Send is communications. Campaigns to any group of people, templates with merge fields, the automated emails Sessionboard sends on your behalf, and every message ever sent is in History with its delivery status. Chapter two.

Then the setup rows. Library holds the custom fields and reusable pieces. Reports and automations do what they say. Event team is who can sign in to this event and with which role, chapter eight. And settings, at the bottom, is the event itself, name, dates, branding, program vocabulary, integrations, chapters three and five.

And Training, right under dashboard, is where these videos live inside the product, with a checklist of what's set up and what isn't, so you can watch a chapter and do the step in the same window.

That's the shape of it. People, content, portals, communications, and settings underneath. If you're starting from nothing, go in the onboarding order, event setup first, then team, then the form, and the rest follows. Chapter three is the place to start.
