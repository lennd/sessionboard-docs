Team picks let everyone on your team weigh in on submissions without a formal review. Each teammate gives each submission **Yes**, **Maybe** or **No**, and can add a comment. You still make the decision.

## Give your pick

On **Submissions**, each row has your own **Yes**, **Maybe** and **No**. Select one and it saves straight away. Select your current pick again to clear it.

To add a comment, open the row. Under **Team picks**, write in **Your comment** and select **Save comment**. Your teammates see it. A comment needs a pick first.

You can change your pick or comment at any time.

## See what the team thinks

Each row shows the tally, for example **3 Yes · 1 Maybe · 0 No**. Open a row to see each teammate's pick, when they gave it and their comment. Your own pick is listed first.

To find what needs your attention:

- **Sort** by **Team picks** to put the most Yes first.
- **Filter** by **Team picks**: **Not picked by me**, **No picks yet**, **Mostly Yes** or **Has a No**.
- **Export CSV** adds **Yes**, **Maybe**, **No** and **My pick** columns.

## Picks don't decide

Team picks never change a submission's status. Use **Accept**, **Waitlist** or **Decline** when you're ready, then let speakers know. See [Accept, waitlist or decline submissions](/sessionboard-sessions/submissions/decide).

To invite teammates, select **Invite teammates** in the sidebar. They sign in with a code, no password.
