**Submissions** lists everything that came in through your call for speakers. You decide on each one; there is no review committee or scoring.

## Read a submission

Each row shows the session title, its speakers and when it was sent. Select the row to read the description and see each speaker's name and email.

Use **Show** to switch between **Undecided**, **Accepted**, **Declined** and **All**. The count next to each tells you how many are in it.

## Decide

- Select **Accept** to put the session on your agenda. It appears on **Agenda** under **Not scheduled yet**, and its speakers appear on **Speakers**.
- Select **Decline** to set it aside. It stays in **Declined**.
- Select **Undo** to move a decision back to **Undecided**.

Decisions take effect straight away. Declining an accepted session takes it off the **Agenda** page.

<Note>
Sessions doesn't send acceptance or decline emails. Once you approve the [speaker email plan](/sessionboard-sessions/speaker-emails), the speakers of an accepted session get an invite that asks for their details. Tell declined speakers yourself; their emails are in the submission.
</Note>

## Speakers your plan includes

Your plan includes a number of speakers per event: 75 on Starter and 150 on Growth. A speaker on several accepted sessions counts once. If accepting a session would go past the limit, Sessions doesn't accept it and says so. Choose Growth or talk to us about the platform for a bigger event.

## Review with a committee

Review plans, reviewers, rounds and scorecards are part of the Sessionboard platform. The **Score with your committee** card on Submissions tells you more.
