**Submissions** lists everything that came in through your call for speakers. You decide on each one; there is no review committee or scoring.

## Read a submission

Each row shows the session title, its speakers, when it was sent and the track the speaker chose. Select the row to read the description and see each speaker's name and email.

Use **Show** to switch between **Undecided**, **Accepted**, **Waitlisted**, **Declined** and **All**. The count next to each tells you how many are in it.

## Add a submission yourself

A proposal sometimes reaches you another way, such as an email. Select **Add a submission**, enter the session title, a description and the speaker's name and email, then select **Add submission**. It waits in **Undecided** with the rest.

## Decide

- Select **Accept** to put the session on your agenda. It appears on **Agenda** in **Unscheduled**, and its speakers appear on **Speakers**.
- Select **Waitlist** to keep it in reserve in case a place opens up. It stays in **Waitlisted**.
- Select **Decline** to set it aside. It stays in **Declined**.
- Select **Undo** to move a decision back to **Undecided**.

Decisions take effect straight away. Declining an accepted session takes it off the **Agenda** page.

Deciding never emails anyone by itself. You tell speakers from **Let speakers know**, below.

## Let speakers know

**Let speakers know**, under the list, has one email for each decision. Each one is shown exactly as the speaker gets it, filled in for a real speaker on that decision.

- **Accepted**: accepted speakers get the invite from your [speaker email plan](/sessionboard-sessions/speaker-emails), with links to the details you need from them. It goes out once you approve the plan.
- **Declined** and **Waitlisted**: edit the subject and message. Select a field such as **First name**, **Session title** or **Event dates** to add it where your cursor is; it's filled in for each speaker. The preview changes as you type. **Save** keeps your text; **Use the default** puts ours back.

To send, select **Email N declined speakers** (or waitlisted), then **Send now**. Each speaker gets one email per session, and nobody is emailed twice for the same decision: the count shows who hasn't heard from you yet. Addresses that unsubscribed or bounced are skipped, and Sessions lists them.

<Note>
An assistant connected to Sessions can draft and preview these emails, but sending waits for your OK in Sessions.
</Note>

## Speakers your plan includes

Your plan includes a number of speakers per event: 75 on Starter and 150 on Growth. A speaker on several accepted sessions counts once. If accepting a session would go past the limit, Sessions doesn't accept it and says so. Choose Growth or talk to us about the platform for a bigger event.

## Review with a committee

Review plans, reviewers, rounds and scorecards are part of the Sessionboard platform, and so are statuses of your own, such as Shortlisted or Invited. The **Score with your committee** and **Your own statuses** cards on Submissions tell you more.
