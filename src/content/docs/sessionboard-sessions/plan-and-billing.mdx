Every organization starts with a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, with no card needed. Open **Plan and billing** from the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Plans

|  | Starter | Growth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $99 a month, or $990 a year | $249 a month, or $2,490 a year |
| Events a year | 1 | 3 |
| Speakers per event | Up to 75 | Up to 150 |
| Admin seats | 2 | 5 |

Both plans include the call for speakers, decisions, the agenda, sponsors, the agenda and speaker wall for your website, and CSV import and export. Pay monthly, or yearly and get two months free.

Need more admins? An extra admin seat is $29 a month, or $288 a year on a yearly plan. When you invite a teammate past your plan's seats, **Invite teammates** offers **Add a seat**.

**Events a year** counts the events you created in the last 12 months. **Speakers per event** counts each person on an accepted session once.

## What you use

The top of **Plan and billing** shows your **Events in the last 12 months**, **Speakers per event** and **Admin seats** against your plan.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Plan and billing">
Select the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick monthly or annual">
Use the **Monthly / Annual** toggle. Annual shows the monthly equivalent.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a plan">
Select **Choose Starter** or **Choose Growth** and complete checkout.
</Step>
</Steps>

During the trial you can open your call to the public, decide on submissions and publish your agenda. Your card comes when you choose a plan, and choosing one doesn't end your trial: nothing is charged until it ends. Switching plans during the trial doesn't charge anything either.

## Card, invoices and cancelling

Select **Manage billing** to update your card, download invoices or cancel. After you cancel, **Plan and billing** says when your plan ends, and everything stays as it is until then. Select **Keep** with your plan's name to change your mind.

## When a trial or plan ends

When the trial ends, or a plan is cancelled or unpaid, your organization becomes read-only: you can see and export everything, and nothing you made is deleted. Choose a plan to make changes again.

## The Sessionboard platform

Committee review, speaker portals, more forms, two-way integrations, more events and bigger events, sponsors and exhibitors, Speaker CRM, single sign-on and hosting in the EU or Middle East are part of the Sessionboard platform. Select **Talk to us** on the platform card to start that conversation.

<Note>
If Sessions is on your Sessionboard contract, the billing page says so and there is nothing to pay here.
</Note>
