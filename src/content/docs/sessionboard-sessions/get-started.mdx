Sessionboard Sessions runs your program from the call for speakers to the agenda on your website. Speakers submit through a public link, you accept or decline each submission, and you give each accepted session a time, room and track. There is no speaker portal to manage: you reach speakers by email.

## Sign up

<Steps>
<Step title="Name your event">
Go to the **Start Sessionboard Sessions** page and answer **What's your event called?**
</Step>
<Step title="Say when it starts">
Pick the start date, or choose **Not set yet**. You can set it later.
</Step>
<Step title="Check what we'll set up">
You see your event, a call for speakers ready to open, and an agenda you can put on your website. Nothing is public until you open the call.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm your work email">
Enter your work email. We email you a link and a 6-digit code: select **Continue setting up** in the email, or type the code. Either one works once, for 15 minutes. There is no password. Then name your organization. Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and use your work account.
</Step>
<Step title="Open your workspace">
Select **Open my call for speakers**. Your organization starts a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, with no card needed.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you already have a Sessionboard account with that email, sign in instead.

If your organization already works with Sessionboard on a contract, Sessions doesn't offer a card plan. After you confirm your email, it says so, and your account team gets in touch.
</Note>


## Sign in

There is no password. Go to **Sign in to Sessionboard Sessions**, enter your work email and select **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the email and select **Sign in**, or type the 6-digit code on the page. The link and the code work once, for 15 minutes. If your email opens the link for you, it asks you to select **Continue as** your address first, so the link is still good when you get to it.

Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and pick your work account. The first time you use one with an account that already exists, we email you a 6-digit code to confirm it's you; after that, it signs you straight in. Two-step sign-in still applies when your team uses it.

If you set a password for this account before, it no longer signs you in here: use the emailed link, the code, Google or Microsoft. An account that also works in Sessionboard's platform keeps its password, and an account whose company uses SSO keeps that sign-in.

To change the language of the sign-up and sign-in pages, select the globe at the top right: English, Français, Deutsch or Español. A new account is made in that language, and the sign-in email is written in it.

Two-step sign-in for an account that signs in by email uses an authenticator app or a passkey, not emailed codes, because the sign-in email already goes to that inbox. If you turned on emailed codes before, you keep them.

Creating a workspace means agreeing to Sessionboard's [Terms of Service](https://www.sessionboard.com/legal/terms-of-service/) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.sessionboard.com/legal/privacy-policy/). In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you tick a box to agree, and the [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.sessionboard.com/legal/data-processing-addendum/) is included. A teammate you invite agrees the first time they sign in.

### Speakers sign in the same way

Speakers who answer your call for speakers never make a password. On the form they enter their email and select the link or type the code we send, or use Google or Microsoft. That signs them in to send and edit their session.

## What your first event contains

- **A call for speakers**, closed until you open it. It asks for a session title and description, an optional format and track, and each speaker's first name, last name, email and biography.
- **Rooms and tracks** to rename on the **Agenda**: Room A, Room B and Room C, and Track 1 and Track 2.
- **An agenda** you can publish to your website once sessions have a time.

## The setup checklist

**Home** shows a checklist until each step is done. Each step opens the page that does it:

1. Create your event
2. Open your call for speakers
3. Get your first submission
4. Accept or decline a submission
5. Schedule a session
6. Put the agenda on your website

## Find your way around

The sidebar has **Home**, **Call for speakers**, **Submissions**, **Agenda**, **Speakers** and **Sponsors**. **Home** shows your event's numbers: submissions waiting for a decision, accepted sessions, speakers and how many sessions are scheduled. Select **New event** on Home to add another event; your plan sets how many you can run in a year.

Your plan and its trial days are on the card at the bottom of the sidebar. **Invite teammates** above it adds another admin to your organization.

**Switch apps.** The nine-dot button next to the Sessionboard logo lists every Sessionboard app: Platform, Sessions, Awards, Learning and Marketing. The apps your organization has open in one select. The others open a page about the app, where an admin can add it with a 14-day trial and no card, or book a demo of the platform.
