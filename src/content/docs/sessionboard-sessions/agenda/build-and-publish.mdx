**Agenda** lists every accepted session. Sessions without a time are under **Not scheduled yet**; the rest are grouped by day, in time order.

## Schedule a session

For each session, set:

- **Starts** and **Ends**. Set the start and the end follows: 45 minutes later for a new session, or the same length as before when you move one.
- **Room**, or **No room**.
- **Track**, or **No track**.

Each change saves as you make it. Times are in your event's timezone, and the Agenda says which one.

## Add a session

Not every session comes through the call: a keynote, a panel you invited, a break. Under **Add a session**, enter the **Session title** and, if it has speakers, each speaker's first name, last name and email. Select **Another speaker** for more. Select **Add session**; it's accepted straight away and appears under **Not scheduled yet**.

A speaker who is already on another session, with the same email, is the same person: they appear once on **Speakers**.

## Rooms and tracks

The **Rooms** and **Tracks** cards list what your sessions can use. Rename one by editing its name, add one with **Add**, and remove one with its bin button. You can't remove a room or track that a session still uses; move those sessions first.

## Tag sponsors

Once you have sponsors on **Sponsors**, each session on the Agenda shows **Sponsored by** with a tag for each one. Select a tag to put that sponsor on the session, and select it again to take it off. See [Speakers and sponsors](/sessionboard-sessions/speakers-and-sponsors).

## Put it on your website

<Steps>
<Step title="Publish">
On the **Put it on your website** card, select **Publish the agenda**.
</Step>
<Step title="Copy the snippets">
You get two snippets: **Agenda** and **Speaker wall**. Select **Copy** on each.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste them into your site">
Paste each snippet where it should appear on your page, one snippet per page.
</Step>
</Steps>

After that, your website shows changes as you make them here: a new time, a renamed room, a session you accept. Select **Get the code again** to copy the snippets later.

<Note>
Two-way sync with a registration or event app platform, attendee data and custom mapping are part of the Sessionboard platform.
</Note>
