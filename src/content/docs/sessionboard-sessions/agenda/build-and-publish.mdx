**Agenda** has five tabs: **Schedule**, **Sessions**, **Rooms & tracks**, **Website** and **Connections**. Every accepted session is on it; undecided, waitlisted and declined ones stay in **Submissions**.

## Schedule sessions on the calendar

The **Schedule** tab is a calendar of your event's days, in your event's timezone. Choose **Day**, **Week** or **Month** to change the view. Sessions are coloured by track.

The calendar opens with one column per day. To see a column for each track, room or format, choose it in **Group by** (Day and Week views). Sessionboard remembers your choice. Dragging a session into another column moves it to that track, room or format.

- **Unscheduled** lists accepted sessions without a time. Drag one onto the calendar, or select **Schedule…** to pick its day and start time. Open a session to set its room and track.
- **Move** a session by dragging it, and change its length by dragging its bottom edge.
- **Add a session** by dragging across empty time on the calendar. Enter the title and, if you like, the speaker's name and email. It's accepted straight away.
- **Take a session off the agenda** by dragging it back to **Unscheduled**, or with **Unschedule** on the session. Sessions asks first.

Each change saves as you make it. If a save fails, the session goes back where it was and Sessions says why.

You can do all of this from the keyboard too: **Move to…** on a session sets its day and start time and keeps its length, and **Schedule…** in **Unscheduled** places one.

### Conflicts

Sessions marks a session **Conflict** when one of its speakers is in another session at the same time, or its room is double-booked. The count at the top of the calendar lists every conflict with the reason; select **Show** to jump to it.

## The Sessions tab

**Sessions** lists every accepted session with its time, room and track. Search (accents and capitals don't matter), filter by track, day or scheduled or not, and sort by start time, title, track or room. From here you can:

- Edit a session's time, room and track inline.
- **Import sessions** from a spreadsheet: see [Import or export your program](/sessionboard-sessions/agenda/import-and-export).
- **Export CSV** of the rows on screen.

## Rooms and tracks

On **Rooms & tracks**, rename a room or track by editing its name, add one with **Add**, and remove one with its button. Removing one that sessions still use asks first and takes it off those sessions. Tracks colour the calendar and, with formats, are what speakers pick on your call.

## Tag sponsors

Once you have sponsors on **Sponsors**, open a session to see **Sponsored by** with a tag for each one. Select a tag to put that sponsor on the session, and select it again to take it off. See [Speakers and sponsors](/sessionboard-sessions/speakers-and-sponsors).

## Put it on your website

<Steps>
<Step title="Publish">
On the **Website** tab, select **Publish the agenda**.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview and copy">
You get a snippet for the **Schedule list** and one for the **Speaker wall**. Select **Preview** to see exactly what a snippet shows on a page, then **Copy**.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste into your site">
Paste each snippet where it should appear on your page. To put both on the same page, use the **All on one page** snippet instead.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your website shows accepted sessions only, and shows changes as you make them here: a new time, a renamed room, a session you accept. Select **Get the code again** to copy the snippets later.

<Note>
A room-by-room grid on your website, two-way sync with a registration platform and attendee data are part of the Sessionboard platform. To push your agenda one way into the registration app you already use, see [Push your agenda to a registration app](/sessionboard-sessions/agenda/connections).
</Note>
