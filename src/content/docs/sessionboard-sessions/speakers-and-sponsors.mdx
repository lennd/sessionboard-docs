## Speakers

**Speakers** lists everyone on an accepted session, with their email and their sessions. It also says how many speakers your plan includes for this event, for example **2 of 150 speakers your plan includes for this event**.

A speaker is matched by email across your organization, so the same person on several sessions, or at several of your events, is one person. Select **Export CSV** for a spreadsheet of your speakers.

Speakers don't have a portal in Sessions. You reach them by email: approve the speaker email plan and Sessions asks them for their details and shows who is ready. See [Speaker emails](/sessionboard-sessions/speaker-emails).

## Sponsors

**Sponsors** keeps the sponsor list for your event.

1. Under **Add a sponsor**, enter the sponsor's **Name** and, if you like, their **Website**.
2. Select **Add sponsor**.

Each sponsor shows how many sessions it's on. To put a sponsor on a session, tag it on the **Agenda**: see [Build and publish your agenda](/sessionboard-sessions/agenda/build-and-publish#tag-sponsors). Remove a sponsor with its bin button; that also takes it off its sessions.

<Note>
Sponsor contacts, logos, exhibitor records, forms, tasks and portals are part of the Sessionboard platform.
</Note>
