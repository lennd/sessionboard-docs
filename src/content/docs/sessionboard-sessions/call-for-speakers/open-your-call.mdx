Each event has one call for speakers. It stays closed until you open it, and every submission lands in **Submissions**.

## Write what speakers see

On **Call for speakers**, the **What speakers see** card holds the text above the questions:

- **Title**: the heading of the form. It starts as your event's name.
- **Welcome text**: what you're looking for, who should submit and your key dates. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
- **Closes on**: the date and time the call closes by itself. Leave it empty to close it whenever you like.

Your changes show on the form straight away, whether the call is open or not.

## Open the call

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to Call for speakers">
Select **Call for speakers** in the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Open it">
Select **Open the call**. The card says **Your call is open**, and when it closes if you set a date.
</Step>
<Step title="Share the link">
Select **Copy link** under **Link to share**, or **View** to see the form as a speaker does. Put the link on your website, in your newsletter or wherever speakers will find it.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can open the call during your trial; no card is needed.

## What speakers do

Speakers open the link, sign in or create an account with their email, then fill in two steps:

1. **Their session**: a title and description, and a format and track if they want.
2. **About them**: first name, last name, email and a short biography. They can add co-speakers.

They check everything on a review step and select **Submit**. They see a thank-you page with a link to submit another session, and get a confirmation email.

<Note>
Sessions doesn't send acceptance or decline emails. Once you approve the [speaker email plan](/sessionboard-sessions/speaker-emails), the speakers of an accepted session get an invite that asks for their details. Tell declined speakers yourself; their emails are in the submission.
</Note>

## Close the call

Select **Close the call** at any time. The link then shows the call as closed, and the submissions you already have stay in **Submissions**. You can open it again later.

## One call per event

A Sessions event has one call for speakers with the standard questions. More forms, custom questions, applications and submission fees are part of the Sessionboard platform.
