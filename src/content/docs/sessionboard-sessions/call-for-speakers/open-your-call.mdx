Each event has one call for speakers. It stays closed until you open it, and every submission lands in **Submissions**.

## Write what speakers see

On **Call for speakers**, the **What speakers see** card holds the text above the questions:

- **Title**: the heading of the form. It starts as your event's name.
- **Welcome text**: what you're looking for, who should submit and your key dates. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
- **Closes on**: the last day speakers can submit. The call closes at 11:59 PM that day in your event's timezone. Leave it empty to close it whenever you like.

The preview beside the editor shows the form as speakers will see it while you type. Your changes show on the form straight away, whether the call is open or not.

## Choose the questions speakers answer

Under **Questions speakers answer** there are two lists: **About the session** and **About the speaker**. They use the same fields as the rest of Sessionboard, so an answer lands on the session or the speaker's contact, where you can filter, export and use it.

- **Title**, **Description**, **First name**, **Last name** and **Email** are **Always asked**.
- Turn **Required** on or off for every other question.
- Change the order with **Move up** and **Move down**, or drag a question by its handle.
- Select **Add a session question** or **Add a speaker question**, then choose **From your fields** to pick one you already have (Format, Track, Job title, Company name, Headshot and more), or **A new question** to write one: short text, long text, single choice, multiple choice, yes or no, link or number. A call can have up to 15 questions of your own; the card shows how many you've used.
- **Remove** a question with its button. If speakers already answered it, Sessions says how many; their answers stay stored.

Select **Save questions** to put the changes on the form.

<Note>
Questions that appear only after a certain answer (conditional questions) are part of the Sessionboard platform. Sessions asks every speaker every question.
</Note>

## Open the call

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to Call for speakers">
Select **Call for speakers** in the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Open it">
Select **Open the call**. The card says **Your call is open**, and when it closes if you set a date.
</Step>
<Step title="Share the link">
Select **Copy link** under **Link to share**, or **View** to see the form as a speaker does. Put the link on your website, in your newsletter or wherever speakers will find it.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can open the call during your trial; no card is needed.

## What speakers do

Speakers open the link, sign in or create an account with their email, then fill in two steps:

1. **Their session**: the questions under **About the session**.
2. **About them**: the questions under **About the speaker**. They can add co-speakers.

They check everything on a review step and select **Submit**. They see a thank-you page with a link to submit another session, and get a confirmation email.

<Note>
To tell speakers about your decisions, see [Accept, waitlist or decline submissions](/sessionboard-sessions/submissions/decide). Speakers of an accepted session get the invite from the [speaker email plan](/sessionboard-sessions/speaker-emails).
</Note>

## Close the call

Select **Close the call** at any time. The link then shows the call as closed, and the submissions you already have stay in **Submissions**. You can open it again later.

## One call per event

A Sessions event has one call for speakers. More forms, conditional questions, more questions of your own, applications and submission fees are part of the Sessionboard platform.
