Sessions can hand your speakers something to post, and keep each session's recording once it has happened. Both come from Sessionboard Marketing, and both work right here in Sessions.

## Turn Marketing on

On **Speakers**, a card offers **Start a 14-day Marketing trial**. It needs no card to start, and an org admin can start it. Nothing is sent until you say so, and sends need a card on file. When the trial ends, the card says so and offers **Choose a plan**.

If your organization is on the Sessionboard platform, the card asks you to talk to your account team instead.

## Send share kits

Once Marketing is on, **Speakers** gets a **Share kits** panel. Each accepted speaker gets an email with their kit: a **speaker card**, a **post in their voice** and their own **tracked link**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Speakers">
Select **Speakers** in the sidebar. The panel says how many kits are sent.
</Step>
<Step title="Send">
Select **Send share kits to N speakers**. A preview shows the **Email**, the **Kit page** and the **LinkedIn post**, with a sample speaker. Speakers who already have a kit are left out.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Each speaker gets their own email and link. Select **Open in Marketing** to change the words.
</Step>
</Steps>

If a card isn't on file yet, Sessions opens the card step first. See [Plan and billing](/sessionboard-sessions/plan-and-billing#card-before-you-send).

### See who shared

The panel counts **Kits sent**, **Opened**, **Shared** and **Registrations brought**. Each speaker row has a **Share kit** column with the stage (**Not sent**, **Sent**, **Opened**, **Shared**) and their shares, clicks and registrations. Counts update as speakers open and share.

<Note>
If sends are paused on your Marketing plan, the panel says so. Check **Plan and billing** in Marketing.
</Note>

## Keep a session's recording

On **Agenda**, open the **Sessions** tab and select **Media and transcripts** on a session.

- **Upload a recording**: video or audio, up to 50 GB. It goes straight to secure storage, and you can cancel while it uploads.
- **Transcribe after upload** turns it into a transcript with speakers and timestamps. Transcripts use your monthly caption hours, shown as **hours left this month**. Select **Add 10 caption hours** if you run out. A recording without hours is kept untranscribed; select **Transcribe** once you have hours.
- Each recording shows its status: **Not transcribed**, **Queued**, **Transcribing**, **Transcript ready** or **Transcript failed**. Select **Try again** after a failure.
- Open a transcript and select a time to play from there.
- **Make clips** cuts clips from a ready transcript. You can find and post them from Marketing.

Marketing's Help Center suite covers what you can make from a recording.
