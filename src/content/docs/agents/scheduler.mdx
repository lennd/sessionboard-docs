The Scheduler is the agent that places sessions into rooms and time slots. It works inside an **agenda draft**: you describe the schedule you want — the day's start and end, how long each format runs, which sessions to include, the rules that matter to you — and it proposes a full placement that respects your rooms and speakers. The proposal stays in the draft until you commit it, so the live agenda never changes on its own.

## What the Scheduler does

- Reads a draft agenda's criteria — rules, formats and durations, day start and end — and places sessions into rooms and time slots.
- Enforces two hard constraints every time: a speaker cannot be in two rooms at the same time, and a room hosts one session per time slot.
- Weighs your scheduling rules (keep a track in one room, avoid parallel topics, balance days) to pick the best slot among the valid ones, and scores every placement.
- Explains each placement — the rules it satisfied — and lists any session it could not fit.

The Scheduler never changes the agenda on its own. Every proposed change waits in the draft until you apply it.

## Turn the Scheduler on

1. Open **Agents** in the event sidebar.
2. On the **Scheduler** card, switch the toggle on. The card's chip changes from **Setup** to **On**, and **Active agents** at the top of the page counts it.
3. Click the card to open its panel. **Settings** explains how the Scheduler works and opens the agenda; **Activity** lists the drafts it has scheduled.

Until the Scheduler is on, its button in the agenda's sidebar is disabled with the hint **Enable the Scheduler agent in Agents to use this feature**.

## Settings

The Scheduler has no settings of its own on the Agents page — the draft is the setting. From its panel:

- **Works from your agenda** describes what it reads: a draft agenda's criteria, rules, formats and durations, day start and end.
- **Run Scheduler** → **Open agenda** takes you to the agenda with the Scheduler panel open.

In the agenda, the Scheduler panel is the wand button at the top of the right-hand sidebar, in the **Week** and **Day** views. Its header reads **Scheduling against** the draft you are working in, with a **Generated** or **Not generated** badge, and **Change** or **New draft** to switch drafts. The **Configure** tab holds the draft's criteria:

- **Hard constraints** — the two system rules, always enforced, plus any conflict rule you have set to enforced.
- **Schedule window** — the day's start and end times and the slot interval.
- **Sessions to include** — which session statuses the Scheduler may place.
- **Format durations** — how many minutes each session format runs.
- **Room visibility** — all rooms, or a chosen set.
- **Scheduling behavior** — **Ignore existing times** and **Ignore assigned rooms**, so the Scheduler can start from a blank grid instead of keeping what is already placed.
- **Scheduling rules** — the soft rules, each with a weight from 1 to 15: **Group by Track**, **Avoid Parallel Topics**, **Balance Tracks Across Days**, **Balance Sessions Per Day**, **Workshops in Morning**, **Minimize Wasted Seats** and **Room Variety**. A higher weight means a stronger preference. The footer counts the active rules, criteria and hard constraints.
- **Event criteria** — your agenda's conflict rules, attached to the draft.

## Run the Scheduler

1. In the agenda, open a draft (or create one with **New draft**) and open the Scheduler panel.
2. Check the **Configure** tab, then click **Generate Agenda**. The calendar updates only after generation finishes.
3. The draft's badge changes to **Generated**, the calendar fills with the proposed placements, and the panel switches to **Explain**.

Generating again replaces the previous proposal in the draft, including placements you moved by hand — make your manual adjustments after the last run you intend to do.

## What the Scheduler produces

- **On the calendar** — the draft's Week and Day views show every proposed placement in its room and slot, under the banner **You are viewing a draft of your agenda**. The **Conflicts** tab counts anything in the draft that breaks a conflict rule.
- **In Explain** — **Last Run** shows how many sessions were **Placed**, the proposal's total **Score** and how many were **Skipped**. Below it, each day lists its placements: time, room, title, the placement's score and the rules it satisfied — for example *Groups track together. Balances day load.* A **Could Not Schedule** section lists sessions with no valid slot and why.
- **On the Scheduler's panel** — **Activity** lists each draft with its runs, proposed changes and applied changes.

## Review and override

The proposal is a draft until you say otherwise:

- Move or resize a placement on the draft calendar to override the Scheduler for that session. Your change stays in the draft.
- Adjust the criteria — raise a rule's weight, change a duration, remove a room — and **Generate Agenda** again.
- When the draft looks right, click **View & Commit Changes** in the banner. The **Draft changes** page lists every difference between the draft and your live agenda; keep or remove each one, then **Commit changes**. Only the accepted changes land on the primary agenda and in embeds and integrations.
- **Back to Agenda** leaves the draft where it is and returns you to the live schedule.

## Run history

The **Activity** tab on the Scheduler's panel lists every draft it has worked on, with the number of runs, proposed changes and applied changes. Click a draft to open it in the agenda. In the agenda, the **Explain** tab shows the latest run for the draft you have open.
