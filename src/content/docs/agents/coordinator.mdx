## What the Coordinator does

- Checks every participant it is responsible for against their open tasks and deadlines, on the cadence you choose.
- Drafts one follow-up email per person, in your chosen tone, with a short explanation of why it wrote it and how routine the message is.
- Holds every draft for you on the **Review** tab, tagged **Routine**, **Moderate** or **Novel** so you know where to spend your attention.
- Collects replies on the **Inbox** tab, threaded with the email each one answers, so the conversation stays in one place.
- Records every pass as a run, with how many people it looked at, how many emails it proposed, and how many it deferred.

It never emails anyone without a draft existing first.

## Turn the Coordinator on

1. Open **Agents** in the event sidebar.
2. On the **Coordinator** card, switch the toggle on. The chip changes from **Off** to **On**.
3. Click the card to open its panel. **Settings** holds the cadence, tone and email options; **Activity** lists what it has done.

The Coordinator's own page is **CRM → Coordinator** in the sidebar, with the **Dashboard**, **Review**, **Inbox**, **Sent** and **Settings** tabs. **Enable Coordinator** on that **Settings** tab is the same switch as the card.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab on the Coordinator's panel, and **CRM → Coordinator → Settings**, hold the same options:

- **Participant types to contact** — the session roles the Coordinator writes to. With none selected it contacts speakers.
- **Send frequency** — **Check daily**, **Every 2 days**, **Every 3 days** or **Weekly**; **Agent run at** picks **Morning**, **Evening** or **Night** in the event's **Timezone**.
- **Working hours** — the window in which it sends.
- **Follow-up after (days)** and **Max follow-ups** — how long it waits between reminders to the same person, and when it stops.
- **Tone / Voice** — **Professional**, **Friendly**, **Formal** or **Custom**, plus **Custom instructions** for anything it should always or never say.
- **Reply-to email**, **CC additional contacts** and **Email signature** — where replies go, whether a speaker's additional contacts are copied, and how the emails sign off. The signature supports merge tags.

Click **Save** after changing anything. The **Agent Configuration** card on the dashboard summarises the routine actions the Coordinator handles — first, second and later reminders, thank-you notes, passed deadlines — and the reminder cadence you chose. **Edit** on that card opens the same settings.

## Review drafts

**Review** lists every draft waiting for you, newest first, with the recipient, the subject and the task it is about. Select one to open it:

- The header shows **To**, **CC** and **Subject**. The body is editable — change anything before sending.
- The **AI Reasoning** card on the right explains why the Coordinator drafted this message and tags how routine it is: **Routine**, **Moderate** or **Novel**. Novel drafts deserve a closer read.
- **Approve & Send** sends the email, with your edits. **Decline** drops the draft; nothing is sent.
- The contact panel on the right shows the person's profile and activity so you can check their record without leaving the page.

Use **Filter** to narrow the list by **Priority** or **Participant Type**, and the sort menu to order by **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Priority**, **Participant type** or **Name**.

## What the Coordinator produces

- **Sent** — every email you approved, with the body and when it went out.
- **Inbox** — replies from participants, threaded with the email they answered, with a composer to reply in the thread.
- **Dashboard** — **Pending Review** and **Unread Conversations** counts, **Sent Today**, **Reply rate**, the **Speaker Readiness** breakdown (ready, contacted, not contacted), **Recent Activity**, **Send Volume** and **Recent runs**.
- **Agents page** — the Coordinator card shows **Follow-ups sent**, and **Awaiting your review** at the top of the page counts its pending drafts along with the other agents'.

## Review and override

Nothing leaves without a draft you could have read. To stay in control:

- Read the **AI Reasoning** card before you send. **Routine** is a reminder on the cadence you set; **Novel** means the Coordinator saw something unusual and wants your judgement.
- Edit a draft before **Approve & Send** when the wording is close but not yours. The Coordinator sends your version.
- **Decline** a draft to stop a message. The task stays open and the Coordinator will look at that person again on its next run.
- Switch the agent off from the card or **Enable Coordinator** to pause it entirely; drafts already on **Review** stay there.

## Run history

**Recent runs**, at the bottom of **Dashboard**, lists the last ten passes with a status badge — **Queued**, **Running**, **Completed** or **Failed** — how many actions it proposed and how many speakers it deferred. [Check Coordinator run history](/agents/coordinator-run-history) explains what each status and a deferred count mean. **Run Now**, at the top of the page, starts a pass immediately instead of waiting for the next scheduled slot.
