The Agent window is [Ask](/agents/team-lead) at full size. **Ask** opens in a panel beside the page you are on; the Agent window makes that same conversation the whole screen, with a rail of everything it can open in place of the sidebar. You ask in your own words, the answer comes back set like a document, the records it names open beside the chat, and anything that would change your event waits for your approval. **Back to sidebar** returns you to the page with Ask docked where it was.

## Why use it

Most of a program team's day is questions — which sessions still need a room, who has not replied, what changed this week — followed by the page that answers each one. The Agent window removes the trip. You ask, Ask reads your event's own data, and the answer, the record and the report arrive in the same column. When the answer leads to work — a reminder email, a report, a setting — you ask for that too, and approve it from the same conversation.

## Open the Agent window

1. Click **Ask** in the top bar of any event or organization page. Ask opens in the docked panel.
2. Click **Agent window** in the panel's header. The conversation fills the screen and the sidebar becomes the rail described below.

![The docked Ask panel header with the Agent window button beside the close control](/images/kb/agent-window-button.png)

Click **Back to sidebar** to return. It takes you to the page you left, not to the dashboard, with Ask docked beside it — so a detour into the Agent window costs nothing.

The conversation is one session in both places. Go back to the sidebar in the middle of a thread and it carries on in the docked panel; open the Agent window again and it is still there.

## The rail

In the Agent window the sidebar is replaced by a rail with everything the conversation can open.

![The Agent window rail: the Back to sidebar link, the event name, New conversation, Chats, Dashboards and Reports](/images/kb/agent-window-rail.png)

- **Back to sidebar**, at the top, is the same return link as the header's.
- The name of the event or organization you are in, so you always know the scope of your questions.
- **New conversation** starts a clean thread. The one you were in is kept under **Chats**.
- **Chats** lists your conversations in this event or organization, newest first, each named by its first question. The live one is marked **Current conversation**; click any other to read it. A past conversation opens read-only — start a new one to keep going.
- **Dashboards** and **Reports** list what this event or organization already has. Click one and it opens beside the conversation (see [Open dashboards and reports as canvases](#open-dashboards-and-reports-as-canvases)). The **+** beside each heading puts "Build me a dashboard for" or "Build me a report on" in the question box for you to finish.

## Ask a question

A new conversation opens on the question box.

![The Agent window home: the question box, the Explain, Find, Build and Pressure-test chips, and the suggested questions under Find](/images/kb/agent-window-home.png)

Type what you want to know and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line). Or pick one of the four chips — **Explain**, **Find**, **Build** and **Pressure-test** — and click a suggested question under it; each chip seeds the list with questions of that kind, from "Which sessions still have an unconfirmed speaker?" to "If we cut the last day to a half day, what breaks?". On an event that has a daily brief, **Today's brief** plays under the suggestions with its transcript.

Answers come from your event's own records. The answer reads like a page, not a chat bubble: your question is the heading, one quiet line above the answer says what was checked (**Checked 3 things · 8.7s** — click it to see each step), and the answer is prose with lists and tables.

![A question set as a heading, the Checked 3 things line, an answer with a session chip, and an Open in builder card under it](/images/kb/agent-window-answer.png)

When an answer came from a query, an **Open in builder** card follows it so you can adjust the query in the report builder. When something is drafted for you — a report, a campaign, a submission form — a card links to the draft in the same way.

## Follow up

Under a thread, the question box stays docked at the bottom. When there is a natural next question, it appears greyed in the box: press <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>→</kbd> to put it in the box without sending, then edit it or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

![The docked question box showing a suggested follow-up with Tab or → to accept it](/images/kb/agent-window-follow-up.png)

While an answer is streaming, the send button becomes **Stop answering**. The conversation's title in the header is your first question; **New conversation** there does the same as the rail's.

## Open records beside the chat

When an answer is about a few specific sessions or people, they come back as cards: the record type, its title, its status, and up to four facts in the report's own words. A record mentioned in the prose is a chip you can click.

![A session card under an answer, with Open, Ask about this and Full page](/images/kb/agent-window-record-card.png)

- **Open** on a card, or a click on a chip, opens the record in a panel beside the conversation — the same session or contact flyover as on the Sessions and Contacts pages — so you can read or edit it without leaving the thread.
- **Ask about this** puts the record's name in the question box so your next question is about it.
- **Full page** (or <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click on a chip) opens the record's own page instead.

![A session opened beside the conversation from its card, with the thread still visible on the left](/images/kb/agent-window-record-open.png)

Answers about many records stay a table; cards are for the handful an answer is really about.

## Approve changes

Nothing is sent or changed on its own. Ask to open a form, change a setting or draft reminder emails and the change comes back first as a **Decision needed** card showing exactly what will happen — for a setting, the value before and after, and any warning that applies.

![A Decision needed card in the Agent window with Approve and Reject buttons](/images/kb/agent-window-approval.png)

Click **Approve** to let it through or **Reject** to stop it; you can also just type **yes** or **no**. Nothing changes until you approve, and what you approve is logged like any other change in [History](/communications/email-sms-history).

<Note>
Ask works with the permissions you have. If your role cannot change a setting, the card says so instead of offering to change it.
</Note>

## Open dashboards and reports as canvases

Click a dashboard or a saved report in the rail and it opens as a canvas in the main column, with Ask docked beside it so the conversation that led you there is still in reach.

![A saved report open as a canvas in the Agent window, with Run again and Ask about this at the top and Ask docked on the right](/images/kb/agent-window-report-canvas.png)

A report canvas runs the saved query and shows its rows as a table: **Run again** refreshes it, **Ask about this** hands the report to the conversation, and **Back to the conversation** returns to the thread. Long reports show their first rows with a note of how many there are; ask for the rest or a narrower view. A dashboard canvas is the dashboard itself, with its widgets live.

To build a new one, ask — "Build me a dashboard for speaker readiness", "Build me a report on sessions without a room" — or click the **+** beside **Dashboards** or **Reports** in the rail. What is built for you appears in the rail, and under [Dashboards](/reporting/dashboards) and [Reports](/reporting/report-builder) in the sidebar too.

## Where the sidebar still lives

The Agent window does not replace anything. Every page, setting and list in the sidebar is unchanged, and three things take you back to it:

- **Back to sidebar** in the header and at the top of the rail, both returning to the page you left with Ask docked.
- **Full page** on a record card, which opens that record's own page.
- Anything the rail does not list — forms, evaluation plans, portals, settings — is still where it always was. Ask about it in the Agent window, or go back to the sidebar to work on it directly.

Ask itself is the same in both places: the docked panel you open from **Ask** and the full-screen conversation share one session, one history and one set of approvals. The [Ask page guide](/agents/team-lead) describes what the docked panel adds on each page.

## The Agent window for an organization

**Ask** is in the organization top bar too, and its panel has the same **Agent window** button. The organization's Agent window answers about the organization — its events, speakers, contacts and connected apps — and its rail lists the organization's **Chats**, **Dashboards** and **Reports**. Ask it to list your events and the answer comes with each event's dates, status and format, and a count of upcoming, live, past and draft events; a role scoped to a portfolio sees only that portfolio's events. Event questions are best asked from that event's Agent window, where its sessions, submissions and rooms are in scope.
