Team Lead is the guide to every part of Sessionboard. Open it from **Ask** in the top bar and it reads the page you are on before you type anything: what the page is called, what your event already has configured there, and where to go next. Every answer is grounded in your event's own data, not a generic description of the product.

## The page guide

When you open Team Lead with no conversation running, the **Chat** tab shows a guide for the current page.

![The guide card open on the Forms page, with You're in, What you have here, Ask Team Lead prompts, and More to explore](/images/kb/team-lead-page-guide.png)

- **You're in** — the page's name as it appears in the sidebar (for example **Program → Forms**), the event you are working in, and a badge when the organization is in onboarding mode, a sandbox, or a trial.
- **What you have here** — counts and settings already configured on this page: how many forms are open, how many have notifications or closing dates, and so on. When nothing is configured yet, the card says so rather than showing an empty list.
- **Next** — the next open setup step for this event, with how many are done, when there is one. Click it to have Team Lead help with that step.
- **Ask Team Lead** — prompts that start a conversation about this page: **Walk me through …**, **What's already set up here?**, and **What should I set up next here?**. **Read the Help Center for this page** opens the **Learn** tab on the matching article.
- **More to explore** — pages near this one that your event already has, from the same part of the sidebar. Each one is a link. Team Lead only lists pages you can open today; it never lists features that are not turned on for your event.

<Note>
Which features an event has is set by your account manager. Team Lead does not turn features on and will not offer to. If you ask about something your event does not have, it says so plainly and points you to your account manager.
</Note>

## Moving between pages

Team Lead follows you. When you change pages during a conversation, a card marks the change in the transcript so the answers that follow are about the new page.

![The card Team Lead adds to a conversation when you move to a new page, showing You're now in Dashboard with that page's counts and two prompts](/images/kb/team-lead-page-change-card.png)

The card shows the new page's name, its configured counts, and the same **Walk me through …** and **What's already set up here?** prompts as the guide. Ask a question with no page named and Team Lead answers about the page you are on now.

## Starting over

Click **New conversation** at the top of the panel to clear the transcript. Team Lead re-reads the current page and shows its guide again.

## How Team Lead names things

Team Lead calls pages what the sidebar calls them — **Advocacy**, **Print**, **Program → Forms** — and describes what you have in terms of your own records: "you have 2 open forms, neither with a closing date". It does not describe what a page "typically" contains, and it does not mention Early Access, previews, betas, or plan tiers unless you ask about one directly.
