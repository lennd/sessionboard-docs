## What the Reviewer does

- Reads each submission assigned to it on an evaluation plan round and answers the round's scorecard — the same rating scales and comment fields your evaluators see.
- Reviews as each persona on the round, so one submission can carry a cautious reading and an enthusiastic one side by side.
- Writes an overall opinion and an overall ranking for each session it reviews, which you can open from the session's **Reviews** tab.
- Keeps a run log, so you can see which sessions it has looked at and when.

It never changes a submission's status. Accepting or declining stays with your committee.

## Turn the Reviewer on

1. Open **Agents** in the event sidebar.
2. On the **Reviewer** card, switch the toggle on. The card's chip changes from **Off** to **On**, and **Active agents** at the top of the page counts it.
3. Click the card to open its panel. The **Personas** tab is where you choose who reviews; the **Activity** tab lists what it has done.

The Reviewer only scores what you assign to it on a plan round, so turning it on changes nothing until you add personas to a round and run them.

## Personas

Personas are the Reviewer's settings: they decide who does the reviewing and how. Each one has an identity (name, title, biography), a review style (tone, what it likes, what it dislikes) and up to six custom questions it answers about every session.

- **Active Personas** — the panel of up to three personas available to review sessions in this event. The strip under the heading shows how many of the three slots are used.
- **Persona Library** — every persona you can use. **Default Personas** ship with Sessionboard. **Custom Personas** are yours; create one with **Create New**, and edit it later from the pencil icon.
- To put a persona on the panel, open it from the library and click **Add to Event**. When all three slots are used, the button reads **Panel Full** until you remove one with **Remove from panel**.

<Note>
Personas are shared across the evaluation plans in an event. A persona you add to a plan round is the same persona, with the same style and questions, that you see on the Active Personas panel.
</Note>

## Add personas to a plan round

The Reviewer scores inside your evaluation plans. On a plan:

1. Open the plan and go to the **Evaluators** tab, then the **Personas** sub-tab. The **Persona Evaluators** table lists every persona enrolled on the round.
2. Click **Add personas** and pick the personas for this round. They answer the round's scorecard and roll up alongside your human reviewers.
3. In a persona's **⋯** menu, choose **Edit assignments** to pick which submissions it reviews. A persona with no assignments has nothing to score.
4. Click **Run personas**. Each persona reviews its assigned submissions in the background; the row shows **Running** while it works and the **Progress** column fills in as reviews complete.

To take a persona off the round, use **Remove from round** in the same menu. Its finished reviews stay on the submissions it already scored.

## What the Reviewer produces

- **On the plan** — expand a persona's row in **Persona Evaluators** to see each assigned submission with its score and a **Completed** badge. **View feedback** opens the submission's **Reviews** tab.
- **On the submission** — the **Reviews** tab lists the plan and round with the persona's answers next to the human evaluators' answers, and a **Reviewer agent results** card with each persona's overall opinion and overall ranking from its latest run.
- **On the Agents page** — the Reviewer card shows how many sessions it has reviewed and the average score it gave, and **Awaiting your review** at the top counts anything an agent has queued for you.

## Review and override

Persona scores are advice, weighted like any other evaluator's on the round. To act on them:

- Open the plan's **Review** tab to compare submissions by average score, then open a submission to read each evaluator's answers — personas and people together.
- Disagree with a persona? Leave its review where it is and let the committee's own scores carry the decision, or **Remove from round** to drop its scores from the round's average.
- The decision itself — accept, decline, waitlist — is made on the session, by a person. The Reviewer never changes a status.

## Run history

The **Activity** tab on the Reviewer's panel is the run log: one line per session reviewed, with the average of the personas' overall rankings and the date, newest first. **View all runs** expands it. The **Reviewer agent results** card on a session shows that session's runs, newest first, with each persona's answers from each run.
