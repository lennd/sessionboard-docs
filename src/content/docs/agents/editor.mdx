## What the Editor does

- Reads the fields you let it review and drafts a revised version where the copy can be clearer, more consistent or more on-brand. Clean copy comes back without a suggestion.
- Explains each rewrite, so you can see why it changed what it changed before you decide.
- Shows its suggestions as **insights** on the session or speaker profile they apply to, where **Accept** applies the new text to the record.
- Keeps a run log, so you can see what has been published and what is still waiting.

The Editor changes a field only when you click **Accept**. Nothing in the program moves until a person does.

## Turn the Editor on

1. Open **Agents** in the event sidebar.
2. On the **Editor** card, switch the toggle on. The card's chip changes from **Off** to **On**, and **Active agents** at the top of the page counts it.
3. Click the card to open its panel. The **Settings** tab is where you set its voice and scope; the **Activity** tab lists what it has published.

The Editor card shows two numbers: **Pending approval** is how many suggestions are waiting for a decision, and **Published** is how many you have accepted.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab controls how the Editor writes and what it is allowed to touch.

- **Tone of voice** — **Friendly**, **Professional** or **Concise**. This is how the Editor writes and speaks on your behalf.
- **Brand voice (global)** — a free-text description of your brand's tone, style and writing guidelines. It applies to everything the Editor reviews in this event.
- **What Editor reviews** — one tile per module: **Contacts** (speakers, reviewers, staff profiles), **Exhibitors**, **Sponsors** and **Submissions** (abstracts and sessions). Each tile shows how many fields are reviewed, or **Off**. Click a tile to open it; the switch at the top turns that module on or off.
- **Specific instructions** — notes that apply to the selected module only, on top of the global brand voice. Rules like "keep titles under ten words" belong here.
- **Fields** — the checkboxes for the selected module. For submissions they are **Title**, **Description**, **Tags** and **Track**; for contacts the speaker bio; for exhibitors and sponsors the description. The Editor proposes changes only to fields that are checked.

Changes save as you make them.

## Run the Editor

The Editor runs on the records you choose:

1. Open **Sessions** and select one or more sessions in the list.
2. In the selection bar, open **More** and choose **Run Editor Agent**. The same action is available on the agenda's list view.
3. Team Lead opens with a loading card for each session while the Editor reads it. As each run finishes, the card becomes the proposed change, and the suggestion appears on the session itself.

A session whose copy already fits your settings comes back with no suggestion.

## What the Editor produces

- **On the session** — open the session and look above the details for **N insights**. Expand it to see the Editor's card: the reason for the change, the field it applies to and the proposed text. When there are several, arrows and dots step through them.
- **On a speaker** — the same card appears on the contact's profile for bio suggestions.
- **On the Agents page** — **Pending approval** on the Editor card counts suggestions still waiting, and **Awaiting your review** at the top of the page counts anything an agent has queued for you.

## Review and override

Each suggestion is yours to decide, from the record it belongs to:

- **Accept** applies the proposed text to the field right away, and the record shows **Update applied**. The accepted suggestion moves to the Editor's **Activity** tab and counts toward **Published**.
- **Dismiss** hides the card and leaves the field exactly as it was.
- Prefer a different wording? Dismiss the suggestion and edit the field yourself, or tighten the module's **Specific instructions** so the next run comes back closer to what you want.

## Run history

The **Activity** tab on the Editor's panel lists every accepted suggestion, newest first: the record and module, the field that was updated, the new text and the text it replaced. Click a session entry to open that session. Suggestions you have not decided on yet are not in Activity — they stay on their records until you accept or dismiss them.
