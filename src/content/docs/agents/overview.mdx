Agents are supervised AI teammates that act on your event graph — sourcing speakers, drafting evaluations, packaging content, and more. They run at a tunable level of autonomy, with every action logged and reversible.

<Note>
Agents build on the same data and permissions as the rest of Sessionboard. What an agent can see and do is governed by its scopes and your [Event Team permissions](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members).
</Note>

## Autonomy & trust

Agents operate at supervised autonomy levels — they propose and act within scope, but you stay in control:

- **Activity logging** — every agent action appears in the relevant record's Activity feed, distinct from **User**, **System**, and **Rule** events.
- **Scoped access** — agents inherit token scopes and PII settings; see [API tokens](/integrations/api-tokens).
- **Reversible** — agent changes are auditable in [History](/reporting/insights-ai) and can be rolled back.

## Connect your own AI assistant
Any MCP-compatible assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) can act as an agent against your event data via the Sessionboard MCP server. See [API tokens → Connect AI assistants](/integrations/api-tokens).
