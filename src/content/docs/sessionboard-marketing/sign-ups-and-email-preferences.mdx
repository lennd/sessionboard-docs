A sign-up form on your event page collects emails for your newsletter. Nothing to set up: it uses your brand, and every new subscriber confirms their email first.

## Add a sign-up form

- After you publish an event page, select **Add a sign-up form** on the **Collect emails from visitors?** card, then **Publish changes**.
- Or ask: "Add a sign-up form to the Boston dinner page". Ask adds it to the event you name and asks you to publish.

Visitors enter their email and select **Sign up**. Sharing their details with your sponsors is a separate box, off unless they tick it. If registration for the event isn't open yet, the page's Register card points visitors to the form.

## Confirmation

Each new subscriber gets an email with **Confirm my email**. Until they select it they show as **Waiting to confirm**, and they get nothing else from you. The link works for a few days.

## See who signed up

Go to **People** and select **Signed up**. Filter by status (**Subscribed**, **Waiting to confirm**, **Unsubscribed**, **Bounced**, **Marked as spam**) or by where they signed up, search, and select **Export CSV**. Or ask: "How many people signed up this week?"

## Email preferences

Every newsletter and automatic email has a link to the subscriber's own preferences page. There they can:

- **Pause for 30 days** or **Pause for 90 days**, and **Start again now**
- **Unsubscribe** from your newsletter, or **Unsubscribe from everything**
- Change their name and company, then **Save my details**

Changes show in **People** straight away, and each one is kept in the person's consent history.

<Note>
Your newsletter and automatic emails need your organization's mailing address in their footer. Sessionboard asks for it the first time you send or switch on an automatic email.
</Note>
