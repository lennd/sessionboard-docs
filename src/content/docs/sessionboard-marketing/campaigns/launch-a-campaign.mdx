A campaign puts your speakers, guests, sponsors, exhibitors, team or attendees to work for an event: sharing it, posting, recording a short video, or following up. You get the whole campaign on one page, check it, then launch.

## Why use it

Your speakers, sponsors and attendees each reach people you can't. Each one gets a card, a post and their own tracked link, so you see who brought in each registration. You can email them their kits, or skip the emails and share a link or an embed on your own site.

## Kinds of campaign

![The Start a campaign box: a "What do you want to happen?" field with Draft it, above eight kinds of campaign, each with an example](/images/suites/marketing/marketing-campaigns-start-a-campaign.png)

- **Speakers and guests share**
- **Your team shares**
- **Collect videos**
- **Follow up with everyone who came**
- **Attendees share they're going**
- **Email until they register**
- **Win back last year's people**
- **Keep everyone engaged**

**Your team shares** appears once **Your team sharing** is in your Marketing, and **Collect videos** once **Video testimonials** is. Add them from [Add to Marketing](/sessionboard-marketing/add-to-marketing); both are on every plan.

## Draft and launch

<Steps>
<Step title="Say what you want">
Go to **Campaigns**. Under **Start a campaign**, type what you want to happen and select **Draft it**, or pick one of the kinds above.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the page">
The draft shows **Which event**, **Send to**, **What** (subject and message), **Link**, **When** and an optional **Reward**: a personal promo code, a prize for the top sharers, a reward for everyone who brings a number of people (for example "Bring 3 people: we'll save you a front-row seat"), or a leaderboard. Change anything, then select **Save changes**.
</Step>
<Step title="Launch">
Select **Launch**, then **Yes, launch it**. You stay on the campaign, and a line tells you what to do next.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can't launch with unsaved changes. You also can't launch with nobody to send to, unless you chose to share it yourself (below).

## Email the kits, or share it yourself

**Speakers and guests share** and **Attendees share they're going** ask how the campaign goes out, under **Who**:

- **Email each one their kit**: each person gets their card, post and tracked link in one email.
- **I'll share a link or embed**: every kit and tracked link is made, but nobody is emailed. You send the link yourself or put it on your site.

With **I'll share a link or embed** you can launch with nobody in **Who** yet. After launch the campaign says "Nobody was emailed: share the link or embed below." For **Attendees share they're going**, everyone who registers from then on still gets the "I'm attending" kit in their confirmation email.

### Share it yourself

A launched share campaign has a **Share it yourself** card, whichever way it went out:

- **Your link** (attendee campaigns you share yourself): anyone with it can make their "I'm attending" card, registered or not. Select **Copy link**, or **Download QR code** to print it.
- **On your site**: a snippet to paste on your website. For speakers, sponsors and exhibitors it is an "Email me my link" form: they type their email and get their kit. Only people in the campaign get a link. For attendees it is a sign-up form for your registration confirmation page.
- **Opens in**: **New window** or **Popover**. With **Popover** you can also tick **Open automatically when the page loads**.
- **Preview** shows the real embed with sample values. Nothing you do there is saved.
- **More options** (speaker campaigns) has a snippet for one person's own card.

## Sponsors and exhibitors

In **Speakers and guests share**, you can pick **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors** under **Who** instead of speakers. Their cards read "Proud sponsor of" or "We're exhibiting at" your event, with **Sponsor** or **Exhibitor** under the company name.

## What each person gets

Each speaker's kit has a post in their voice, their tracked link, their card and an animated card. Their kit page also lets them:

- **Make it yours**: add a name prefix (like "Dr.") or credentials after their name (like "MD, PhD"), frame their photo, and pick a tone for the post: **Excited**, **Professional** or **Short**.
- Download their card in three sizes: **Square**, **Link preview** and **Stories**.
- Post to LinkedIn, or share another way, for example X, WhatsApp, email, a QR code or **Email a colleague**.
- See their own results: how many people their link brought, their clicks and registrations, and their place on the leaderboard if the campaign has one.

The kit page is also available in Arabic.

## Archive or delete

- A **draft** you don't want: select **Archive**. To bring it back, open **Archived drafts** from the **archived** link in **Campaigns** and select **Restore**.
- A **launched** campaign: select **Delete**, then **Delete campaign**. Its tracked links stop working and its results leave **Campaigns**. This can't be undone. To keep the results, select **Pause links** instead.

## While it runs

A running campaign shows a live feed, a **Leaderboard** and its results. A campaign nobody was emailed shows **Shares**, **Clicks** and **Registrations**. With a reward for bringing people, each speaker's page shows how many they have brought so far ("1 of 3 so far"), it is met on its own when their link brings that many registrations, and the campaign shows how many have made it. Select **Pause links** to stop the links working for a while, and **Resume links** to start them again. A video campaign also offers a **Venue kiosk** link so people can record at the event.

Campaign statuses are **Draft**, **Running**, **Paused** and **Ended**.

## Your team on Slack

If Slack is connected, your team's kit arrives by Slack direct message. Anyone not on Slack gets the same kit by email.

<Note>
During the trial, without a card on file, a campaign reaches at most 500 people a day.
</Note>
