**Collect videos** asks people to answer a few short questions on video, in the browser or on a phone. You approve every video before it is used anywhere. It is a kind of campaign, and it appears once **Video testimonials** is in your Marketing; add it from [Add to Marketing](/sessionboard-marketing/add-to-marketing), free on every plan.

## Why use it

Short, real answers from your speakers and customers are the content people trust most. You don't need to book a studio or edit anything: people record on their own, and you keep the best.

## Draft the request

<Steps>
<Step title="Start the campaign">
On **Campaigns**, choose **Collect videos**, or tell Ask, for example "Collect customer videos about our spring launch". When an event has speakers, Home can also offer **Collect videos from the speakers of** that event.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the questions">
The questions are written from your event or topic. Up to five, asked one at a time; edit any of them, set how many seconds each answer can run, add teleprompter lines, or select **Add a question**. Select **Save questions**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who is asked">
Pick **Speakers**, **Sponsors**, **Exhibitors**, **Attendees**, **Your subscribers**, **People you list** (paste their email addresses, one per line), or **Anyone with the link or QR code**. With the open link, nobody is emailed.
</Step>
<Step title="Launch">
Check the draft and launch. People are shown what they agree to before they record, can take the video back at any time, and nothing is used until you approve it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Share the link or QR code

While the campaign runs, **Open link and QR code** shows a link anyone can answer from. Put it in a newsletter or a post, or select **Download the QR code** to print on a poster or a badge. Videos can also come in from a kiosk.

## Review the videos

Under **Videos**, switch between **To review**, **Approved**, **Not used** and **Withdrawn**, and filter by question. For each video you can:

- **Approve** it (or **Approve all** for the question), so it can go in galleries and reels.
- **Don't use** it. Add a note only your team sees. It stays under **Not used** if you change your mind.
- **Mark as one of the best**, so it goes first in reels.
- **Copy share link**.

The quote and captions appear once the transcript is ready. A person who takes their consent back moves to **Withdrawn**, and that video is never shown.

## Make a reel

Under **Reel**, choose how many of the best approved answers and the frame (**Vertical 9:16**, **Square 1:1**, **Wide 16:9** or **Portrait 4:5**), then select **Make a reel**. It is cut to each answer's best moment, with your brand, names and captions, and lands in **Content** for you to approve. You can also ask: "Make a reel from the best five videos".

## See how it's going

**Results** shows answers, completion, approved, plays and shares, question by question, and which channel answers came from: email, link, QR code or kiosk. A person's card in [People](/sessionboard-marketing/people) shows whether they were asked, opened it, sent a video, or took it back.
