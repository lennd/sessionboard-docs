Every feature is on every plan, and events and webinars are never counted. Plans differ in seats, contacts, caption hours and AI credits. Your organization starts with a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, no card needed. Open **Plan and billing** from the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Plans

|  | Starter | Growth | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $99 | $299 | $599 |
| Yearly | $948 | $2,988 | $5,988 |
| Seats | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| Contacts | 1,000 | 10,000 | 50,000 |
| Caption hours a month | 10 | 30 | 100 |
| AI credits a month | 500 | 2,000 | 6,000 |

Extra seats are $29 a month or $288 a year.

## Top-ups

Under **Usage this month** you can add **Caption hours +10** ($150), **AI credits +1,000** ($100), or a **Conference boost** ($299: 40 more caption hours and 2,000 more AI credits until the end of the event's month). Top-ups last 12 months.

## During the trial

Until a card is on file, sends reach at most 500 people a day and ads stay off. Adding a card early doesn't shorten your trial.

## Take a break

On a monthly plan, **Taking a break between events?** pauses billing for 1, 2 or 3 months at no charge.

## Card, invoices, plan changes and cancelling

Select **Manage card, invoices or plan** to update your card, download invoices, change plans or cancel. A cancelled plan runs to the end of its term. After that the organization is read-only and your data is kept for 12 months.

## If a payment fails

You have 14 days to update your card. After that, sends and ads pause until the payment goes through. A live caption room is never cut off, and incoming contacts are never dropped.

<Note>
If your organization is billed by contract, the billing page says so and there is nothing to pay here.
</Note>
