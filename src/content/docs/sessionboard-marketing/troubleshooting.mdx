import { Accordion } from '@compat';

<Accordion title='"Save your changes before launching."'>
Select **Save changes** on the campaign, then **Launch** again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title='"Nobody to send this to yet."'>
The campaign's **Send to** list is empty. Add speakers, guests or people to the event, or pick another audience.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My campaign stopped at 500 people today">
Without a card on file, a trial sends to at most 500 people a day. Add a card on **Plan and billing**; your trial keeps going.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An app needs a quick setup from our team">
Some apps need us to turn them on for your workspace. Use **Wish** to ask, and we'll be in touch.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My custom domain says Waiting for DNS">
Add the DNS record shown under **Your own domain** at your domain provider. Changes can take a while to spread.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="You can't open this workspace">
The workspace doesn't exist, or your account isn't a member. Ask someone on the team to invite you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My sign-in code didn't arrive">
Codes are good for 10 minutes. Check your spam folder, then select **Send a new code**.
</Accordion>
