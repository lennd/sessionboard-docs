A recording page is a page on your own site for one webinar recording: the video, the speakers, a transcript, and an email form people fill in to watch. Your assistant builds one template and Sessionboard makes a page for every recording in your [on-demand library](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/content-gallery).

You do this from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant connected to Sessionboard.

## Why use it

Each recording becomes a page that search engines and AI answers can read in full, and every viewer who unlocks it is a sign-up with a known source. When you add a recording to the library, its page exists.

## Before you start

- Choose what the library holds, in [Choose what your library holds](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/choose-library-items).
- Publish the library. A preview of your site can read an unpublished library, but a live page needs it published.
- Have your [Brand Kit](/sessionboard-marketing/brand) filled in, so the page uses your colors, fonts and logo.

## Build the pages

<Steps>
<Step title="Ask for the pages">
Ask: *"Build a recording page for every recording in the Sessionboard webinars library, at /webinars, in my brand, with an email gate before the video plays."* Your assistant reads the recording page kit (`get_page_kit` with `kind` `webinar_recording`), then builds the site (`make_site`).
</Step>
<Step title="Preview a recording">
Ask for a preview link. Open one page and play the video, check the form, the chapters and the transcript.
</Step>
<Step title="Add your tags">
Ask your assistant to turn on analytics for the site. See [Track conversions](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/track-conversions).
</Step>
<Step title="Publish">
Ask your assistant to publish. It shows you what changes and waits for your OK. Publishing a site is always yours to approve.
</Step>
</Steps>

Every recording the library shows publicly gets its own address, `/webinars/<recording-slug>`. Items that are hidden, held back until a date, or limited to members never get a page. Your site's sitemap lists every recording page.

## What visitors see

- **A gated player.** Before they watch, visitors see the poster and your sign-up form with its questions and consent sentence. Any valid email unlocks the video. People join your list only if they tick the box. The form checks that a person, not a bot, is filling it in.
- **One email per page view.** Visitors don't sign in, so the page remembers their email only while it stays open. A refresh or a later visit shows the form again. The same email also unlocks any other gated video and the [webinar takeaways](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/webinar-takeaways) on the page.
- **A gate partway through.** For a gate at a set time (for example five minutes in), the video plays up to that second, pauses and shows the form.
- **An open player.** With no gate, the video plays straight away. A recording you imported from YouTube or Vimeo plays in its own player, in privacy mode.
- **Captions and chapters.** Captions come from the recording's transcript. Chapters are buttons that jump to a moment, and a link ending in `#t=90` or `#t=1m30s` opens the video at that point.
- **A button and "Watch next".** Add a button from a set second or at the end, and the next recordings in the library are linked to their own pages.
- **A transcript.** The full transcript is on the page in a section visitors can open.

Keyboard and screen reader users get the same page: after the form is sent, focus moves to the video and the unlock is announced.

## Set up the gate

Ask for a gate when you build the page, or later: *"Ask for an email before every recording plays."* The gate is the same one described in [Add an email gate, a button and what to watch next](/sessionboard-marketing/content/around-the-video). To gate recordings automatically as each webinar ends, use the [after-webinar plan](/sessionboard-marketing/events/after-webinar-plan).

## What search engines and AI answers read

Live recording pages (not previews) carry structured data that Sessionboard writes for you:

- **Video details:** title, description, thumbnail, upload date, length, transcript and the page address. Chapters become key moments.
- **Breadcrumbs:** your home page, the page at the parent address, then the recording.
- **FAQ:** any question you add to a page as an open-and-close block becomes a question and answer in search.

You don't paste any of it.

## For people who write their own page

If your assistant writes the page's HTML, the player is one element: `<sb-portal kind="video">`. Point it at a recording with `media`, at a library item with `item`, or leave both out on a recording page to show that page's recording. Add `transcript="off"` when the page prints the transcript itself. A page can hold several video players. To offer the slides and other files under the video, add `<sb-portal kind="assets">` (see [Webinar takeaways](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/webinar-takeaways)); one email unlocks the video and the files together. When you publish, Sessionboard checks that every id belongs to your organization.

<Note>
Ask your assistant to read the recording page kit before it writes the page. It has a worked example that already follows these rules.
</Note>

## Questions

**Why is a recording missing a page?** It may be hidden, held back, or limited to members, or the library may not be published. Ask your assistant to list the library's items.

**Can people watch without giving an email?** Only if the recording has no gate. Gated recordings need a valid email.

**Do I need a developer?** No. Your assistant builds and publishes the pages, and you approve the publish.
