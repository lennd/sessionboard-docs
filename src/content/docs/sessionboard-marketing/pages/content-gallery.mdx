The on-demand library is one page that holds your recordings, clips and slide decks. Visitors can search it, and they can ask it a question and get an answer drawn from what was said. The add-on is called **Content Gallery**; on the page itself, the card is called **On-demand library**.

## Where to find it

Open **Pages** and look for the **On-demand library** card. It appears once you have added **Content Gallery**, or once a library already exists. If you haven't added it, see [Add to Marketing](/sessionboard-marketing/add-to-marketing).

## Make a library

<Steps>
<Step title="Name it">
In **Library name**, keep **On-demand library** or type your own name.
</Step>
<Step title="Make it">
Select **Make the library**. Sessionboard fills it from your recordings, clips and slide decks. It starts as a **Draft**, so only your team can see it.
</Step>
<Step title="Check what is in it">
The row shows how many items the library holds. Select **Open** to see it at its address.
</Step>
</Steps>

To make another one, use **Make another library**.

## Publish it

<Steps>
<Step title="Select Publish">
On the library's row, select **Publish**.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
The dialog asks **Publish the library?** It becomes public at its address, in search engines and in embeds. Select **Publish** again, or **Not yet** to wait.
</Step>
</Steps>

The status changes from **Draft** to **Live**. Gated or embargoed items keep their own rules, so publishing the library does not open them.

## Ask this library

Type a question into **Ask this library** and select **Ask**. You get an answer with numbered sources. Select a source to open that item.

The answer only draws on items a visitor could open without signing in. A line under it says how many items it read and, when some were left out, how many gated or embargoed items were not read.

## Put it on your own website

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the library">
Under **On your own website**, choose the library in the **Library** list.
</Step>
<Step title="Get the snippets">
Select **Get the snippets**, then **Copy** the one you want, such as **On-demand library**.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste it on your page">
Use one snippet per web page. To show several widgets on one page, copy **All on one page** instead.
</Step>
</Steps>

A draft library shows nothing in the widget until you publish it. More on snippets is in [Publish an event page](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/publish-an-event-page#on-your-own-website).

## Take it down

- **Unpublish** makes the library a draft again. Its address and widgets stop showing it, and nothing in it is deleted. Select **Publish** to bring it back.
- **Delete** removes the library. Its address stops working and its widgets show nothing. The recordings stay in **Content**.

Both ask you to confirm first.

## Questions

**Do I add recordings by hand?**
No. The library is filled from your recordings, clips and slide decks when you make it.

**Can I collect an email before someone watches?**
Yes, per recording. See [Around the video](/sessionboard-marketing/content/around-the-video).

**What does a visitor see of a gated recording?**
Only what its own rule allows. The library's answers never use the description or transcript of a gated or embargoed item.
