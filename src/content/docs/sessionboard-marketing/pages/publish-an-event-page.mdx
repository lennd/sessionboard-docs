**Pages** holds your event pages, tour pages, on-demand library and snippets for your website, all in your brand. Nothing goes live until you publish it.

## Make and publish an event page

<Steps>
<Step title="Describe the event">
Under **Event and webinar pages**, say what the event is about and who it is for, then select **Make the page in your brand**.
</Step>
<Step title="Change it in plain words">
Under **Change the page**, describe what to change and select **Change it**. Use **Pick a brand kit** or **Refresh from the brand kit** to restyle it.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish">
Select **Publish**, then **Publish** again to confirm. The page goes live at its public address and registration opens.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Collect emails

The **Collect emails** card adds a sign-up form to your event page. People who sign up and confirm become subscribers for your newsletter.

## Launch an on-demand library

Name the library and select **Make the library**. Visitors can ask the library questions about its sessions. Publish it when it is ready.

## Use your own domain

Under **Your own domain**, connect an address such as `events.yourcompany.com` once, add the DNS record it shows you, then pick it for the page under **Which page uses which domain**. See [Use your own domain](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/use-your-own-domain).

## On your own website

Select **Get the snippets** to put your event and library on your own site. To see who from your audience visits your website, use **Track visitors on your own website** and paste the one line it gives you into your site.
