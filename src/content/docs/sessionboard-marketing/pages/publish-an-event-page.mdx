**Pages** holds your event pages, tour pages, on-demand library and snippets for your website, all in your brand. Nothing goes live until you publish it.

## Make and publish an event page

<Steps>
<Step title="Describe the event">
Under **Event and webinar pages**, say what the event is about and who it is for, then select **Make the page in your brand**.
</Step>
<Step title="Change it in plain words">
Under **Change the page**, describe what to change and select **Change it**. Use **Pick a brand kit** or **Refresh from the brand kit** to restyle it.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish">
Select **Publish**, then **Publish** again to confirm. The page goes live at its public address.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Where people register

- **In-person events and roadshows** take registrations on the page itself: people save a seat, and you approve them under the event's **Guests**.
- **Events that register somewhere else** (Cvent, Eventbrite and the like): give Ask the registration link during setup, under **Where do people register?** The page's **Register** button then opens it.
- **No registration yet?** The page says registration opens soon. Add a sign-up form (below) and the Register card points visitors to it, so you still collect their emails.

## Collect emails

After you publish, the **Collect emails from visitors?** card adds a sign-up form to your event page; select **Publish changes** to put it live. You can also ask: "Add a sign-up form to the Boston dinner page". People who sign up and confirm their email become subscribers for your newsletter. See [Sign-ups and email preferences](/sessionboard-marketing/sign-ups-and-email-preferences).

## Launch an on-demand library

Name the library and select **Make the library**. Visitors can ask the library questions about its sessions. Publish it when it is ready.

## Use your own domain

Every Marketing plan includes your own domain. Under **Your own domain**, enter an address such as `events.yourcompany.com` and select **Connect**. Add the DNS record it shows you; the status reads **Waiting for DNS** until it is in place.

## On your own website

Select **Get the snippets** to put your event and library on your own site. To see who from your audience visits your website, use **Track visitors on your own website** and paste the one line it gives you into your site.
