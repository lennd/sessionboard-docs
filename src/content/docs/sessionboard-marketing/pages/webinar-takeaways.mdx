Takeaways are the files and links that go with a webinar: the slides, a PDF guide, a worksheet, a link to a template. You attach them to the webinar, and its page lists them with a download button each. Visitors give their email once to get all of them, and every download is recorded on that person.

You do this from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant connected to Sessionboard.

## Why use it

A takeaway is a reason to sign up that people want after the webinar ends. One email unlocks everything on the page, so nobody fills in a form twice. Each download shows up on the person's timeline, so sales and marketing can see what they took.

## Attach takeaways

<Steps>
<Step title="Give your assistant the file">
Ask: *"Attach the slides at https://example.com/content-graph-slides.pdf to the Content Graph webinar."* Your assistant copies the file into Sessionboard (`attach_webinar_asset`).

You can also attach a file you have on your computer. Your assistant asks Sessionboard for an upload link (`request_webinar_asset_upload`), uploads the file and attaches it. A link works too: *"Add a takeaway that links to our planning template."*
</Step>
<Step title="Check the list">
Ask: *"Which takeaways does the Content Graph webinar have?"* Your assistant lists them in page order with their type, size and download counts (`list_webinar_assets`).
</Step>
<Step title="Put them on the page">
Ask: *"Add a Takeaways section to the webinar's page."* Your assistant adds the takeaways list to the page draft. Preview it, then approve the publish.
</Step>
</Steps>

Files can be PDFs, slides, Word documents, spreadsheets, or PNG or JPG images, up to 100 MB each. A webinar holds up to 50 takeaways. Every file is checked for viruses and file type before it is attached.

To change a title, description, cover image or the order, ask your assistant (`update_webinar_asset`). To take one off the page, ask it to remove the takeaway (`remove_webinar_asset`). The people who already downloaded it keep that on their timeline.

## What visitors see

- **One email for the whole page.** The first time a visitor clicks a gated takeaway, a sign-up form opens with the same consent sentence as your gated recordings. After they send it, the file they clicked is ready to download.
- **Every gate on the page opens at once.** That one email also unlocks the other takeaways and any gated recording on the same page. Unlocking a recording first works the same way: the takeaways then download without the form.
- **No email again while they stay.** Every further download on the page goes straight through and is recorded against the same email.
- **A refresh shows the gates again.** Visitors don't sign in, so the page remembers their email only while it stays open. Reloading the page, or opening it later, asks again. Gated recordings work the same way.
- **Open takeaways.** A takeaway you mark as not gated downloads for anyone, with no form, and nothing is recorded.

Keyboard and screen reader users get the same page: the form opens in a dialog with focus on the email field, and closing it returns focus to the link they used.

## See who downloaded

- **On the person.** Each download is a *Downloaded* activity on the contact's timeline, with the webinar and the takeaway's title.
- **Per takeaway.** `list_webinar_assets` shows clicks and the number of people for each takeaway.
- **In your engagement tools.** Downloads count as engagement for the webinar, so the people who downloaded show up when you ask who engaged.
- **Sign-ups.** People who sign up through the takeaways form show with the source *asset_gate* in your sign-ups.

Try: *"Who downloaded the Content Graph slides?"* or *"Which takeaway got the most downloads?"*

## For people who write their own page

If your assistant writes the page's HTML, takeaways come in two forms:

- `<sb-portal kind="assets">` is a ready-made list: title, type and size, description and a Download (or Open) button. Add `event="…"` to show another event's takeaways, or `asset-kind="slides"` to show one kind.
- `<sb-data source="assets">` gives the rows (title, description, type, size, image, gated, url) for your own layout. Link each one with `sb-attr:href="url"`.

Either way, the page adds the email form for you. You don't write a gate. Sessionboard adds one form per page whenever the page shows a gated takeaway.

<Note>
Ask your assistant to read the webinar page kit (`get_page_kit`) before it writes the page. It has a worked example with a Takeaways section.
</Note>

## Questions

**Does a visitor have to give their email again on another page?** Yes. The email is kept for one page while it stays open.

**Can I attach a takeaway that isn't a file?** Yes. Attach a link. It opens in a new tab and the click is recorded like a download.

**Can I gate some takeaways and not others?** Yes. Each takeaway is gated unless you ask for it to be open.

**Is there an API?** Yes. See the [Marketing API reference](/sessionboard-marketing/api) and the [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools).
