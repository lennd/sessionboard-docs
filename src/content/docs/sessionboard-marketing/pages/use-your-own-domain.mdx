Your event pages and on-demand libraries live at a Sessionboard address until you give them your own, such as `events.yourcompany.com`. You connect a domain once, then choose which page or library uses it. A domain serves one page or library at a time.

Everything is in **Pages**, under **Your own domain**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect the domain">
Enter the address under **Domain** and select **Connect domain**. A subdomain such as `events.yourcompany.com` works best. A bare domain such as `yourcompany.com` only works if your domain provider supports CNAME flattening, and the page warns you when you enter one.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the DNS record">
The domain opens with a **CNAME** record, and sometimes a **TXT** record, each with a copy button. Add them at your domain provider. If someone else looks after your domain, use **Copy instructions** or **Email to IT contact** to send them the records.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the page">
Under **Which page uses which domain**, each event page and library lists its Sessionboard address and your domains, with each domain's status and the page that uses it. Pick the domain. If another page uses it, confirm the move: that page goes back to its Sessionboard address.
</Step>
<Step title="Wait for it to go live">
The status moves from **Waiting for DNS** to **Issuing certificate** to **Active**. Select **Check now** to check right away. Until the domain is active, the page stays on its Sessionboard address.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Stop using a domain

- To move a page back to its Sessionboard address, pick **Sessionboard address** for it. The domain stays connected for another page.
- To remove the domain altogether, open it under **Your own domain** and select **Remove domain**. The page that used it goes back to its Sessionboard address.

<Note>
Your own domain is not on for every workspace. If **Your own domain** says it is not switched on, make a wish with the wand on the right and we will turn it on.
</Note>
