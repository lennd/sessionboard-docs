The website tag is one line you paste on every page of your own website. When someone signs up or opens your newsletter, you see the pages they read before.

## Why use it

You learn which pages bring people in, and a person's card in [People](/sessionboard-marketing/people) shows what they read, not only what they signed up for.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Pages">
The **Track visitors on your own website** card appears on **Pages** once one of your event pages is live.
</Step>
<Step title="Copy the line">
Select **Copy**, and paste it on every page of your website. Select **Send to a developer** to email them the instructions.
</Step>
<Step title="Check it works">
Open your website. The card reads **Waiting for the first page view** until it sees you, then **We see your site** with the last page view and the visitors in the last days.
</Step>
</Steps>

Someone who can edit Marketing turns this on. If you can't, the card tells you to ask them to open **Pages**.

## Privacy

The tag uses no third-party cookies, and visitors with Do Not Track on are never recorded. To wait for consent first, set `window.sbTag = { consent: false }` on your site.
