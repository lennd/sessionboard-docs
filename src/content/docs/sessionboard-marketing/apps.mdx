**Apps** connects the platforms your events and webinars already run on, and brings their sessions, speakers, attendees and recordings in. Every app is on every plan.

| Group | Apps |
| --- | --- |
| Webinar platforms | Zoom, Goldcast, ON24 |
| Registration platforms | Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo, RainFocus, Eventbrite, Swapcard, Visit |
| Event listings | Luma, LinkedIn Events |
| CRM | HubSpot, Salesforce |
| Association management | iMIS, Nimble |
| Design | Canva |
| Your team | Slack, Microsoft Teams |
| Ad accounts | Meta Ads |

## Connect an app and bring people in

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect">
Select **Connect** on the app. Some apps take a key in the card; others open a window to sign in and allow access.
</Step>
<Step title="Bring in">
Select **Bring in**, choose the Sessionboard event to bring it into, then **Bring them in**. A CRM also has **Sync now**.
</Step>
</Steps>

To stop, select **Disconnect** and confirm. If an app needs a quick setup from our team, its card says so.

## AI assistants

Go to **Apps** > **AI assistants** to connect Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant: add the Sessionboard connector, sign in, or copy the connector address. Under **What assistants may do**, assistants read and draft freely; sends, posts and spends need you. The activity log shows everything an assistant did.

## Waiting for your OK

When a connected assistant wants to launch a campaign, send kits, publish, approve spend or start captions, the request waits under **Waiting for your OK** on Home and on the matching section. Nothing has gone out yet: **Approve** runs it as you, **Decline** drops it.
