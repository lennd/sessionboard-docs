**Found by AI** is on an event's goal page, under **Goals and ads**. Each week Sessionboard asks AI assistants the questions a buyer would ask, such as "What are the best data conferences in Austin in 2027?", and shows whether the answers name your event, its speakers or its sessions, and whether they cite your own pages.

Beside those checks you can add **ChatGPT's own numbers**: what ChatGPT reports for your business in its Business Hub.

## Read the checks

- The headline says how many answers named you, against the check before, for example "Mentioned in 3 of 16 AI answers, up from 1".
- **What to do next** suggests one move, such as publishing your event page or asking speakers to share.
- **The answers** can be searched, filtered by assistant or by whether you were mentioned, sorted, and exported as CSV.
- **Mentions you created** counts what your speakers, sponsors, attendees and team shared.

The weekly check runs on Mondays. Select **Check now** to run it today; the cost is shown before you do. Select **Edit the questions** to change what is asked, or **Turn off the weekly check**.

## Claim your ChatGPT profile

OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT gives a claimed business a Business Hub. It shows **mentions** (ChatGPT answers that cite your website), **website clicks** and the **discovery topics** people find you for, over a rolling 28 days.

<Steps>
<Step title="Claim your business">
Sign in at [chatgpt.com/business-hub](https://chatgpt.com/business-hub) and claim your organization.
</Step>
<Step title="Verify your domain">
Add the DNS TXT record Business Hub gives you to your website's domain.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish on your own domain">
Clicks count only for the domain that claims them. Put your event pages on your own domain so they get the credit.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Enter ChatGPT's numbers

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the form">
On the goal page, under **ChatGPT's own numbers**, select **Enter Business Hub numbers**.
</Step>
<Step title="Copy the numbers">
Fill in **Mentions**, **Website clicks** and **Last day of the 28 days**. Paste the **Top discovery topics** one per line, in Business Hub's order, and add your **Claimed domain**. Leave a box empty if Business Hub shows nothing.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Select **Save numbers**. Found by AI then reads, for example, "ChatGPT reports 42 mentions and 9 clicks in the 28 days to Nov 6, 2026", with the reading before.
</Step>
</Steps>

The numbers belong to your organization, so every event's Found by AI shows the same reading. Enter new numbers whenever Business Hub updates; each reading is kept, so you can see the trend. Entering the same period again replaces it.

Each topic shows how many of your sessions cover it. When ChatGPT sends people to you for a topic you have no session on, Found by AI says so, and Content Gaps can use it to suggest what to make next.

<Note>
ChatGPT has no API for Business Hub, so Sessionboard never reads it for you. The numbers are the ones you enter. You can also ask Ask, or an assistant connected through the MCP tools, to record numbers you give it.
</Note>
