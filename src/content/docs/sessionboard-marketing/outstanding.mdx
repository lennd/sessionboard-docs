Your assistant keeps a to-do list for your whole Marketing workspace. Ask it what needs doing and it reads everything outstanding, puts the most important first, asks you one question at a time, and runs the steps for the choice you make.

## Why use it

With several events and webinars running, the next thing to do is rarely obvious: a draft waiting for approval, a library nobody published, recordings with no email gate, a webinar with no host. This list finds them all in one read, so you don't open each event.

## Ask

Say: *"What do I need to do?"* or *"What's outstanding for the December launch webinar?"* Your assistant calls `get_outstanding`. It starts with the most important item, asks its question in plain words with your choices, and carries out the choice you pick.

Things on the list include:

- content drafts waiting for your approval, and presenter kits not yet sent;
- event pages and libraries with changes not published, and libraries never published;
- recordings of past events with no email gate;
- missing dates, hosts, agendas or webinar tools, and registrations waiting for approval;
- speaker links and photos still needed;
- the website tag when it is off;
- suggestions after a webinar;
- approvals waiting for you.

Ask for **all products** and your Platform tasks due in the next seven days join the list.

## What each item tells you

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Title and why | What it is, and in one sentence why it matters |
| Urgency | Now, soon or later |
| Question and choices | What your assistant asks you, for example publish, review first, or skip |
| Steps | What your assistant does for each choice, in order |
| Needs your OK | Whether the step reaches people or the public. If so, your assistant waits for a clear yes |

Your assistant never publishes or sends from this list without your OK. If something can't be done through your assistant, it says so plainly.

## One event or all

Name an event or webinar to see only its items. A list shows up to 50 items, most important first, and tells you how many more there are. Ask for more, or narrow it to one event.

## Through the API

`GET /v1/marketing/outstanding` returns the same list. Add `event_id` for one event, `product=all` for Platform tasks, and `limit` for fewer items. It needs the `read:marketing` scope and an organization-wide token. See [Marketing MCP tools](/sessionboard-marketing/api/mcp-tools#whats-outstanding).

<Note>
Workspaces that turned on Assistant Approvals see their approvals in Sessionboard's own approvals list, not in this one.
</Note>
