Sessionboard Marketing fills your events and webinars and turns them into content. You say what you want to happen; Sessionboard drafts the page, the campaign or the clips, and you check it before anything goes out.

## Sign up

<Steps>
<Step title="Say who it's for">
On **Start Sessionboard Marketing**, type your company or organization, or paste your website, and confirm the match. No website, or it isn't found? Select **We don't have a website** and carry on with the name you typed.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick your role">
Choose the role closest to yours, such as **Event marketing** or **Membership**.
</Step>
<Step title="Tell us about your next event">
Paste its page or Zoom link, or describe it, for example "Data Summit, March 12, in Boston". A city means it's in person, so you aren't asked again. You see a preview of what Sessionboard would make for it.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm your work email">
Enter your work email and the 6-digit code we send you. The code is good for 10 minutes. Personal email addresses are not accepted. Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and use your work account instead of a code.
</Step>
<Step title="Say what you want done">
Pick as many goals as you like, such as **Get speakers sharing**, **Hit a registration number** or **Turn talks into clips**, then the systems you already use. You can invite the people on stage now or skip it.
</Step>
<Step title="Start your trial">
Select **Open your workspace**. You start a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, with no card needed.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in

Go to **Sign in to Sessionboard Marketing**, enter your work email and select **Email me a code**. There is no password.

### Sign in with Google or Microsoft

On the sign-in page, select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and pick your work account. The first time you use one with an account that already exists, we email you a 6-digit code to confirm it's you; after that, it signs you straight in. Two-step sign-in still applies when your team uses it. An account that signs in with a password or your company's SSO keeps that sign-in.

## Set up with Ask

**Home** is a conversation with Ask. The first time, it asks a few quick questions about your next event: what kinds of events you run, how many people come, and **Where do people register?** If people register on another platform, such as Cvent or Eventbrite, paste its registration link: your event page's **Register** button opens it.

Then Ask suggests the tools for that event and walks you through them one card at a time: speaker sharing, the event page, recordings, team posting, attendee follow-up, goals, results and your apps. Each card has one button that does the job, and **Skip for now** if you'd rather come back to it. When you are done, select **Take me to my workspace**.

Last, and optional: give the three robots who did the work a name, a hat and an outfit, or keep them as they are. Your whole team sees them.

After setup, Home asks **What do you want to happen?** Type it in your own words, paste a link, or start from one of your events.

## Invite your team

Select **Invite teammates** at the bottom of the sidebar and enter their emails. They sign in with a code, no password. Once a teammate joins, everyone on the team sets up two-step sign-in.

## Find your way around

The sidebar has **Home**, **Events**, **Campaigns**, **Goals**, **Content**, **Pages**, **Brand**, **People**, **Results** and **Apps**. Every section can be run from Ask. The rail on the right has **Ask**, **Learn**, **Help** and **Wish**.

**Switch apps.** The nine-dot button next to the Sessionboard logo lists every Sessionboard app: Platform, Sessions, Awards, Learning and Marketing. The apps your organization has open in one select. The others open a page about the app, where an admin can add it with a 14-day trial and no card, or book a demo of the platform.
