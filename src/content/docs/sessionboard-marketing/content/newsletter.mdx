After each event we draft an issue from its recaps, clips and podcast episodes. You read it, send yourself a test and schedule it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the newsletter">
On **Content**, select **Newsletter**.
</Step>
<Step title="Draft an issue">
Select **Draft the newsletter**, or **Write one by hand**.
</Step>
<Step title="Test it">
Select **Send test to me** and check the email.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it">
Select **Send it**, then **Schedule** or **Send now**. **Send now** asks once (**Yes, send now**), because a sent issue can't be unsent.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Layout and pictures

Each issue has a layout: **One column** (everything in order, pictures full width), **Feature** (a lead story with a large picture, then the rest as a list) or **Digest** (short items with small pictures). Pick one above the blocks; the preview shows the email as it will be sent.

To add a picture, select **Image** under **Add**, then **Upload a picture** (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF). Say what it shows, for people who can't see it; if it is only decoration, tick **It's only decoration** instead. A caption and a link are optional.

## Settings

Under **More options** you can set the newsletter's name, where replies go, your mailing address, and whether an issue is drafted **After each event** or **Only when I ask**.

<Note>
A newsletter needs your mailing address in its footer, and it goes only to subscribers who confirmed. Collect subscribers with a sign-up form on your event page: see [Publish an event page](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/publish-an-event-page#collect-emails).
</Note>
