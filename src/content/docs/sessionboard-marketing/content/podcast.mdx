import { Steps, Step } from '@compat';

Your sessions can become a podcast, one episode per session.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the podcast">
On **Content**, select **Podcast**, then **Turn this event into a podcast** and **Make the podcast**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the episodes">
Each episode starts as a draft. Select **Publish** on the ones that are ready, or **Add episodes** from more sessions.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a cover">
Pick a cover, select **Try other styles**, or **Upload your own**.
</Step>
<Step title="Get it into podcast apps">
Copy the **Feed URL** and submit it to the podcast apps you want to be in.
</Step>
</Steps>

Turn on **Email me these numbers every Monday** to get the podcast's listening numbers each week.

The same Monday email goes to workspace admins whenever your content was busy last week, with or without a podcast: gallery views, plays and shares, clips shared, registrations and how many of those came from shares. Turn it off from the link at the foot of the email.
