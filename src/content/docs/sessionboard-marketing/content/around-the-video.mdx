**Around the video** sets what a viewer sees before, during and after a recording plays. Open a recording in **Content** and find it under its video.

## Why use it

A recording on your event page or in a gallery can do more than play: it can collect the email of everyone who watches, point them to your next event, and suggest the next video.

## Ask for an email

Under **Ask for an email** choose **Never**, **Before it plays** or **Partway through** (enter the time as minutes:seconds, such as 5:00; it can't be after the end). Any email unlocks it. People join your newsletter only if they tick the box. Select **Copy the link to share**: visitors enter their email, then it plays, and they show in [People](/sessionboard-marketing/people).

## Add a button

Under **Button**, choose one of three options. **Register for your next event** is followed by the name of your next scheduled event in brackets, or by **(none scheduled yet)** when there isn't one. **My own button** lets you enter the **Button text** and **Link**. **No button** turns it off. Choose when the button appears in **Show from (minutes:seconds)**, for example `1:30`; leave it blank (the box reads **At the end**) to show it when the video finishes.

## Suggest what to watch next

Turn on **Suggest what to watch next when it ends** to show an end card.

Select **Save**. Viewers see the changes the next time the video loads. **More options** and **Fewer options** show and hide the settings; a one-line summary stays visible, such as "Asks for an email before it plays".

## Premiere it

A premiere plays the recording at a set time for everyone on the event page, with questions and polls beside it. People can ask for a reminder.

<Steps>
<Step title="Link the recording to an event">
A recording that isn't linked to an event shows **Link this recording to an event to premiere it on the event page.**
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a time">
Choose a date and time under **Starts**, then select **Put it on the event page**.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish the page">
The premiere is added to your event page draft. Publish the page when it looks right; see [Publish an event page](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/publish-an-event-page).
</Step>
</Steps>

The status reads **Premieres** with the time, **Playing now for everyone**, or **Premiered**, and the recording is on demand afterwards. Select **Change the time** to move it.
