**Content** is one library of every reel, clip, quote card, post and recap made from your recordings, which you can search and share.

## Why use it

Sessionboard Marketing turns your session recordings into searchable, editable transcripts so you can pull quotes, create clips and publish polished content without leaving the platform. The full transcript experience — including speaker labels, keyword search, editing and download in TXT, DOCX, SRT and VTT — is part of the Sessionboard platform. The upgrade module lets you see exactly what you get before you make the move.

## Bring in a recording

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Add a recording">
Go to **Content** and select **Add a recording**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose where it is">
**Paste a link** to YouTube or Vimeo (and confirm you own it or have permission), **Upload a file**, or bring it in **From your webinars**: Zoom, Goldcast or ON24.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the details">
Say which event it is from and list any words to spell right, such as names and product terms. Tick **Medical or clinical content** if it applies.
</Step>
<Step title="Bring it in">
Select **Bring it in**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Make everything

Your first recording is made into everything on its own: a captioned reel, clips, quote cards, a post pack and a recap. The library opens on them as they are made.

For later recordings, open **Sources** and select **Make everything** on the recording. If a piece stops partway (for example during maintenance), it starts again by itself; if it still can't finish, it shows **Try again**.

## Find and use what you made

Filter by type, status, event or speaker, and sort by **Best performing**. Open an item to copy or download it, add it to a campaign, mark it as posted, or check its rights and approval.

## Send speakers their clips

Select items and choose **Send to the speakers**. Each speaker gets a link to approve or ask for changes. Nothing is posted.

## Live captions

Select **Live captions** to caption a session in the room. Attendees scan a QR code and read along on their phone, in their language. Each language you add is translated with AI credits, and caption hours come from your plan. A room that is already live is never cut off.
