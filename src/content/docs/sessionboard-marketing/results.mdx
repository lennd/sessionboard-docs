**Results** shows which people and which posts drove each registration, so you know what to do again.

1. Under **Which results**, choose **Latest webinar**, **This event**, **All events** or **Sponsor reports**.
2. Select **What worked?** for a summary of what worked, what did not, and what to do next time.
3. Select **Share** to make a read-only link that works for 30 days without signing in, or **Export CSV**.

## How credit is counted

Each registration's credit is split across the touches that led to it: 40% to the first touch, 40% to the last, and 20% shared among the ones in between.
