## Why use it

Sessionboard Marketing connects your event content to your audience growth in one place. Use it to turn every session into a podcast episode, newsletter issue, or short video, then distribute through automated journeys and measure what drives registrations. Sponsors and exhibitors get their own audience links so you can demonstrate value without exporting data.
