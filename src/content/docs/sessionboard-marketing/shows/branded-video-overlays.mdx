A **branded overlay** turns a plain recording into a produced-looking video. Sessionboard reads the recording's transcript and chapters and draws the overlay on top in your [brand](/sessionboard-marketing/brand) colours and fonts. The result is a new video; your original recording isn't changed.

You add overlays from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant connected to Sessionboard, or through the API.

## What an overlay can show

| Element | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Rundown | The episode's chapters, with the current one highlighted. |
| Countdown | Time left in the whole video, or in the current chapter. |
| Name plates | A host's or guest's name and title when they start talking. |
| Topic banner | The current chapter, or a fixed line you choose. |
| Ticker | A scrolling line along the bottom, such as the next episode or your show's link. |
| Captions | Captions with the word being said highlighted. |

## Templates

Every brand kit has three templates to start from:

- **Broadcast** (the default): rundown on the right, a large countdown, banner, name plates, captions and ticker.
- **Lower third**: the video fills the frame, with a chapter label, banner, name plates and captions.
- **Podcast**: captions first, for audio-led shows.

Ask *"Which overlay templates do we have?"* to see them, along with any you've made.

## Add an overlay

<Steps>
<Step title="Preview a frame">
Ask, for example: *"Preview the broadcast overlay on episode 4's recording."* You get a still frame from about a third of the way in, plus a check that every text colour is readable against its background (WCAG 2.2 AA). Ask for a 15-second sample if you want to see it move.
</Step>
<Step title="Fix names and lines">
If a speaker's name plate is wrong, tell your assistant who is speaking, such as *"Speaker 1 is Jane Smith, Director of Programs."* Add ticker lines or a fixed banner for this video if you want them.
</Step>
<Step title="Render it">
Ask your assistant to apply the overlay to the whole video, or to part of it (up to three hours). Choose the frame: 16:9, 9:16 for vertical video, 1:1 or 4:5. When the render is ready, your assistant gives you the download link.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Name plates come from the recording's tagged speakers. If speakers aren't tagged to contacts, tell your assistant who each speaker is before you render.
</Note>

## Make your own template

Ask your assistant to create a template from one of the three built-ins and change what you need: which elements show, the countdown label and whether it counts the episode or the chapter, a fixed banner, ticker lines and colours. Colours come from your brand's roles; text on them is kept readable automatically. You can make your template the default for your brand kit, change it later, or delete it. The built-in templates can't be changed, but any of them can be the default.

## Use it with a show

On a [weekly show](/sessionboard-marketing/shows/run-a-weekly-show), add an overlay to each episode's recording before you share it. Put the next episode's date and your show page in the ticker.
