A **guest booking link** lets a show guest, speaker or interviewee pick their own time. It checks every host's Google Calendar together, so the guest only sees times when all of you are free.

You set it up from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant connected to Sessionboard, or through the API.

## Why use it

Finding a time with two hosts and a guest usually takes a handful of emails. With a booking link the guest picks a time in their own time zone, everyone gets the calendar invite, and the guest's record in [People](/sessionboard-marketing/people) shows they said yes.

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step title="Invite your co-hosts">
Every host must be a teammate in your workspace. Invite them from **Invite teammates** at the bottom of the sidebar. They sign in with their email and a 6-digit code.
</Step>
<Step title="Create the link">
Ask, for example: *"Make a guest booking link for our show with me and Chris as hosts, 45 minutes, Tuesday and Thursday afternoons, with Google Meet."*
</Step>
<Step title="Connect each host's Google Calendar">
Each host asks their own assistant *"Connect my calendar to the scheduler"* and opens the link it gives them. The link works for 30 minutes; ask again if it expires. Sessionboard reads free and busy times, creates the booking events and reads your calendar's time zone.
</Step>
<Step title="Share the link">
Ask *"Send me our guest booking link"* and paste it into your invitation. The link's address is `/book/` followed by its name.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The link takes no bookings until every host has connected a calendar. Ask *"Is our booking link ready?"* to see who still needs to connect.
</Note>

## What the guest sees

The booking page shows the meeting's title, who it's with and how long it is. Times are shown in the guest's own time zone, and they can switch to another. They pick a day and a time, add their name and email, and they're booked. Host email addresses are never shown.

A time is offered only when every host is free (with any buffer before and after kept clear), it falls in the link's working hours, and it's after the minimum notice and within the booking window.

## What happens when a guest books

- **One calendar event for everyone.** It goes on the first host's calendar, with the other hosts and the guest invited.
- **A meeting link.** Google Meet, or a Zoom meeting through your workspace's connected Zoom app.
- **Reschedule and cancel links.** The invite has both. They work without signing in, and a change updates every calendar.
- **People.** The guest is added to [People](/sessionboard-marketing/people), or matched by email, with a note saying when they booked.

Ask *"Who's booked this week?"* to see upcoming bookings. Your assistant can also move or cancel a booking for you.

## Settings

Ask your assistant to change any of these. Existing bookings keep their time.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hosts | Who must be free. The first host's calendar holds the event. |
| Time zone | The zone for working hours. By default, the first host's calendar time zone. |
| Duration and start times | How long the meeting is, and how often a start time is offered. |
| Buffers | Free time kept before and after each meeting. |
| Working hours | The days and times guests can book. By default, weekdays 9 to 5. |
| Minimum notice | How soon a guest can book. By default, 4 hours. |
| Booking window | How far ahead a guest can book. By default, 30 days. |
| Meeting | Google Meet, Zoom or none. |

To stop taking bookings, ask your assistant to turn the link off. You can turn it back on later.
