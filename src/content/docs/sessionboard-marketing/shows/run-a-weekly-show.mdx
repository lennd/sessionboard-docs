A **show** is a recurring program you record, such as a weekly video show or a podcast series with guests. Sessionboard keeps the show's schedule, hosts and episodes together, and turns each recording into everything the episode needs.

You run a show from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant connected to Sessionboard, or through the API.

## Why use it

A weekly show is only as good as its follow-up: the podcast episode, the clips, the newsletter and the note to the guest. Doing that by hand each week is where most shows stall. With a show, one recording becomes every draft in one step, and nothing goes out until you say so.

## Set up the show

<Steps>
<Step title="Create it">
Ask, for example: *"Create a weekly show called Event Content Lab with Chris and me as hosts, Tuesdays at 2 pm Eastern, an hour long."* The show starts as a draft.
</Step>
<Step title="Link what it feeds">
Ask your assistant to link the show's [podcast](/sessionboard-marketing/content/podcast) and [newsletter](/sessionboard-marketing/content/newsletter). The newsletter defaults to your workspace newsletter. Link the podcast before your first recording so episodes are drafted into it.
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it on">
When you're ready, ask your assistant to set the show to **active**. You can **pause** it later, or archive it.
</Step>
</Steps>

Every host needs to be a teammate in your workspace. Invite them from **Invite teammates** at the bottom of the sidebar first.

## Plan episodes

Ask *"Add an episode about reusing what speakers say, with Jane Smith as the guest, next Tuesday at 2 pm."* Episodes are numbered for you. Guests are contacts from [People](/sessionboard-marketing/people); if the guest isn't there yet, ask your assistant to add them first.

Each episode moves through these stages:

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Idea | A topic you might record. Never shown on your show page. |
| Booked | It has a recording time. |
| Recorded | The recording is in **Content**. |
| Produced | Its drafts are made. |
| Published | You published the podcast episode and the rest. |
| Shared | The newsletter and guest kit went out. |

Add the meeting link to an episode so everyone knows where to join. Use a [guest booking link](/sessionboard-marketing/shows/guest-booking-link) to let guests pick their own time.

## Turn a recording into the episode

Once you've recorded, bring the recording into **Content** (upload it, or import it from Zoom) and ask your assistant to link it to the episode. Then ask *"Run episode 4."* One run makes:

- a draft podcast episode in the show's podcast;
- a reel, clips, quote cards, social posts and a recap;
- a draft newsletter issue;
- a guest kit dry run: who would get what, before anything is sent;
- the episode's section for your show page.

<Note>
Everything is a draft. Nothing is published or sent until you tell your assistant to publish the podcast episode, schedule the newsletter issue and send the guest kits. A run is safe to repeat: finished steps stay as they are, and you can ask to remake just the podcast, the content or the newsletter.
</Note>

Add a [branded overlay](/sessionboard-marketing/shows/branded-video-overlays) to the recording for a broadcast-style version with chapters, name plates and captions.

## Give the show a page

Ask your assistant to build a page for the show. A show page reads the show directly: the next booked episode with a countdown, past episodes and the guests who've been on, so it stays current as you add episodes. Add a sign-up form so visitors get each episode by email. Your assistant shows you a preview first and publishes only when you say so. Until it's published, visitors to its address see a holding page in your brand, never an error.

## Keep score

Ask *"How did episode 4 do?"* Your assistant can record each episode's views, downloads, shares, registrations and pipeline on the episode, adding to what's there without wiping earlier numbers.

## Stop a show

Archive a show when it ends. Removing an episode or archiving a show keeps the recordings and everything drafted from them in **Content**, **Podcast** and **Newsletter**.
