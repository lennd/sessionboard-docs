Open a webinar under **Events** and select the **Sharing** tab. Everything there is in your brand, and every link is tracked, so you see who brought people in.

## Why use it

People sign up when someone they know invites them. Sharing turns your team, your speakers and your attendees into that invitation without anyone writing a post from scratch.

## Share cards

Sessionboard makes six cards for the webinar, three with your speakers' faces and three without, each with the title, date, time and a QR code to register.

- Select **Show cards** to see them, and the download button on a card to save it.
- Switch on **On share pages** for the cards your speakers and attendees can post from their share pages. The cards come off those pages when the webinar starts.
- Select **Regenerate** after you change the title, date, speakers or brand.
- Select **Copy attendee share link** and send it to people who registered: each one makes their own card.

## Email signatures

A small banner and one line your team adds to their Gmail, Outlook or HubSpot signature. It says "Save your seat" before the webinar and switches to "Watch the recording" by itself once it starts, so signatures already pasted never go stale.

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick your teammates">
Select **Send to the team**, pick the people, and select **Email links**. Each person gets an email with their own signature and the steps for their email tool. **Add without emailing** puts them on the list without sending.
</Step>
<Step title="Or send a link yourself">
Under **Team**, select **Copy link** next to a teammate and send it to them however you like.
</Step>
<Step title="Watch who it brings in">
Each teammate's row shows their clicks and registrations.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also copy a version for yourself with **Copy signature**, or **Copy HTML** for tools that take signature code.

## Speaker links

Select **Turn on** under **Speaker links**. From then on:

- every confirmation email carries the attendee's own share link, and
- each speaker and co-presenter gets their own link. Select **Copy link** to send it, or **Share card** to copy a link that signs them in to their own share card.

When you add a speaker later, select **Give speakers links**.

Publishing a webinar's page turns this on for you.

## Your team sharing

Under **Share cards**, select **Send to the team**. Sessionboard drafts a message for your team with the cards and posts ready to share. Review it, choose who gets it and send it; nothing goes out until you do.

<Note>
Sharing is for webinars. In-person events have their own guest list and emails under **Events**.
</Note>
