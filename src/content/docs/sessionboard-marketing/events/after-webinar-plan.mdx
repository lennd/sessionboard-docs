An after-webinar plan is what you want to happen once a webinar ends. You set it before the webinar, approve it once, and Sessionboard runs it when your webinar tool says the webinar is over and the recording is ready.

You set it from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant connected to Sessionboard, or through the API.

## Why use it

The hours after a webinar are when people are most interested, and when your team is most tired. A plan does the follow-up for you, with one email for people who came, a different one for people who didn't, and the recording on your site, gated, the same day.

## What a plan does

When the webinar ends and the recording is in, Sessionboard runs these steps in order:

1. **Import the recording.** It waits for the recording from your connected webinar tool, such as Zoom, Goldcast or ON24.
2. **Make content from it.** Choose from everything, a reel, clips, quote cards, a post pack, a recap, a recap deck, key takeaways or a newsletter issue.
3. **Gate the recording.** Ask for an email before it plays, partway through at a second you choose, or not at all.
4. **Add it to your library.** It joins your [on-demand library](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/content-gallery), and appears on your [recording pages](/sessionboard-marketing/pages/recording-pages).
5. **Send presenters their kit.** Each presenter gets their clips to approve. Your team gets the team kit as a draft.
6. **Email people.** One email for people who attended and one for no-shows, each with a button to the recording, your library or a link you choose.
7. **Send a summary** to the event owner, the hosts and the team emails you name.

Each step runs once. If the recording isn't ready, Sessionboard checks again every 15 minutes until it is, or until the wait you set is over.

## Set the plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Ask for the plan">
Ask: *"Set the after-webinar plan for the December launch webinar: bring in the recording, make clips and a recap, add it to the Sessionboard webinars library, gate it before it plays, and email attendees and no-shows."* Your assistant calls `set_after_webinar_plan`. It merges onto your organization's defaults, so you only name what changes.
</Step>
<Step title="Review the emails">
Ask to see the plan. Your assistant shows both email drafts with their links, and who gets presenter kits and the summary. Edit the words until you like them.
</Step>
<Step title="Approve it">
Tell your assistant it is OK. It approves the plan (`approve: true`). That OK covers every email and presenter kit the plan sends later.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Write the emails

The attendee and no-show emails each have a subject, a message, a button label and a link. The link can be the recording, your library, or any https address. Use these fields in the subject or message:

| Field | What it fills in |
| --- | --- |
| `{first_name}` | The person's first name |
| `{name}` | The person's full name |
| `{event}` | The webinar's name |
| `{link}` | The button's link |
| `{recording_link}` | The recording |
| `{library_link}` | Your library |

Leave one audience empty and that audience gets no email. Choose how many hours after the recording is ready the emails and the kits go out.

## Approval is the OK

Emails and presenter kits send by themselves only when:

- you approved the plan **before the webinar started**, and
- the plan hasn't changed since. Any edit clears the approval, so approve again.

Otherwise everything else still runs, but the emails and kits stay drafts for your hosts, and the summary tells them so. A workspace with no card on file also gets drafts. When a plan sends its own emails, the general "Recording ready" email isn't sent, so nobody gets two.

If your organization turned on **Assistant Approvals**, an approval waits in Sessionboard, and your assistant gives you the link instead.

## Set the same plan for every webinar

Ask: *"Make this our default after-webinar plan."* Your assistant calls `set_after_webinar_defaults`. Defaults never send anything by themselves. Each webinar's plan is approved on its own.

## See what happened

Ask: *"How did the after-webinar plan do for the launch webinar?"* Your assistant reads `get_after_webinar_plan`. You get:

- which steps ran;
- emails sent, opened and clicked, for attendees and no-shows;
- how many people unlocked the recording, and how many watched.

A click on an email also lands on the person's record in [People](/sessionboard-marketing/people), with the webinar's topics, so you see what they care about. Clicks from email security scanners are not counted.

<Note>
You can run the after-event emails by hand too. For a webinar on any Marketing account, your assistant can plan and send them with `plan_after_event` and `send_after_event`. For a webinar, attended means attended in your webinar tool.
</Note>

## Questions

**Can I change the plan after approving it?** Yes, but any change clears the approval, so approve again before the webinar starts.

**What if I approve after the webinar started?** The plan still runs, but emails and kits stay drafts.

**Does it replace the [results email](/sessionboard-marketing/events/results-email-after-your-webinar)?** No. That email goes to your team and keeps its own timing.
