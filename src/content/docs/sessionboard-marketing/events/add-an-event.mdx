**Events** holds your in-person events, tours and webinars, each with its page, guests, campaigns and results. Events never count against your plan.

## Import an event from its link

<Steps>
<Step title="Paste the link">
Under **Add an event**, choose **Import from a link** and paste the **Event page link**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check it">
Sessionboard reads the page. Select **Add this event**, or **Not this one** if it picked the wrong one.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Create a webinar or a series

<Steps>
<Step title="Start a webinar">
Under **Add an event**, choose **Webinar**.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill it in">
Set the date, time and timezone, and pick the webinar platform: Zoom, Goldcast, ON24 or **No platform yet**.
</Step>
<Step title="Make it a series (optional)">
Turn on **Make it a series** and choose the number of episodes, from 2 to 26.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Select **Create webinar**. It starts as a draft; nothing is created on the webinar platform until you publish.
</Step>
</Steps>

To bring in a series you already run, use **Bring in a webinar series**. Past episodes bring their recording and attendance, and upcoming ones bring registrants as they sign up.

## Create an in-person event

Choose **In-person event** and fill in the name, the date and time at the venue, the city and venue, and the time zone. You can also set:

- **Seats**: leave it at no limit, or set one; a waitlist opens when it is full.
- **Who can register**: **Anyone can ask, you approve**, or **Only people you invite**.
- **Plus-ones**, **Hosts** and a **Tour** if it is one stop of several.

Select **Create the event**. It starts as a draft: nobody is invited and its page is not live until you say so. See [Run an in-person event](/sessionboard-marketing/events/run-an-in-person-event).

## The event's page in Sessionboard Marketing

Select an event to open its profile: **At a glance** numbers (registrations, speakers, sessions, shares, content made and page views), registration and attendance goals, and **Tools for this event**, each with **Set it up** or **Open**.

## Follow-ups that run by themselves

On an event, switch on the follow-ups you want: no-shows get the recording, attendees get the recap, new subscribers get a welcome, and invited guests get a reminder. Each asks you to confirm before it turns on.
