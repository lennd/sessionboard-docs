**Journeys** are four emails that are already written and previewed. You don't build anything: switch one on and it sends to each person once, at the right time. Anyone who unsubscribed is left out.

| Journey | Who gets it | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New subscribers get a welcome** | Everyone who confirms a newsletter subscription from now on (for the whole workspace) | Right away |
| **No-shows get the recording** | People who registered and didn't attend | 1 day after the recording is ready |
| **Attendees get the recap** | People who attended | 1 day after the event |
| **Invited guests get a reminder** | People on the guest list who haven't registered | 3 days before the event |

## Switch one on

1. Open the event in **Events**. Journeys appear on the event once someone qualifies for one (for example after attendance comes in), or open them with `?tab=journeys`.
2. Select **Preview** to read the email exactly as it sends, footer included.
3. Select **Edit the email** to change the subject, the words, the button or the delay.
4. Switch it on. Each card shows how many people will get it.

If your organization's mailing address isn't set yet, the Journeys section asks for it right there: the law in most countries requires a postal address in every marketing email. Add it once and every newsletter and automatic email uses it.

You can also ask: "Send no-shows the recording".

<Note>
Each person gets each journey once, even if they qualify again. Goals counts these emails under **Automatic emails**.
</Note>
