An in-person event has tabs for each part of the job: **Guests**, **Accounts**, **Emails**, **Event page**, **Check-in**, **Follow-up**, **Listings** and **Settings**.

## Build the guest list

On **Guests**, add people or select **Upload a CSV**. When people ask to come, select **Approve**, **Waitlist** or **Decline**.

### Decide by organization

Select **By organization** to see requests grouped by company, with the mix of organization types in the room. Select **Approve all**, **Waitlist all** or **Decline all** to decide a whole team at once. To keep the room balanced, set **Seats per organization**: approving a team stops at that number and leaves the rest waiting.

### Approve from Slack

On **Settings**, under **Slack approvals**, connect the Sessionboard Slack app if it isn't connected yet, then pick a channel. Check **All stops of this tour** to use the same channel for every stop, and add **Approvers** to limit who can decide. Each new request is posted there with **Approve**, **Waitlist** and **Decline** buttons. Select **Post waiting requests** to post the ones already waiting.

A decision in Slack works like one on **Guests**: the guest gets the usual email, and the Slack message shows who decided and when. The person must have a Sessionboard account in your organization with the same email as in Slack. If your Slack workspace is already connected to another Sessionboard organization, you can still connect it here for seat requests: Slack commands and mentions keep answering for that other organization.

## Send the invitations

<Steps>
<Step title="Check the email">
On **Emails**, read the invitation. Select **Rewrite this email** to have it redrafted, or **Keep these words**.
</Step>
<Step title="Send">
Select **Send invitations** on **Guests**, or **Send this email** on **Emails**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Invitations go out as soon as you send them and can't be recalled.
</Warning>

## Know before you go

On **Settings**, under **Know before you go**, upload the event's packet (a PDF) and fill in **Getting there**: transit, parking, rideshare and accessibility. Select **Copy from the event page** to reuse the page's own Getting there section. The confirmation, reminder and details-updated emails include the packet link and the Getting there lines.

If you changed one of those emails yourself, add the packet and Getting there to it on **Emails**: Sessionboard tells you which ones are missing them.

## Know where sign-ups come from

On **Follow-up**, under **Sources**, make a sign-up link for each channel (LinkedIn, newsletter, email) and for each person sharing it. Every sign-up through a link counts toward its channel and person, so you can see sign-ups per stop by source.

## Moments worth acting on

Sessionboard emails the event's hosts, and posts to the Slack approvals channel, when something happens that is worth acting on: the date or venue changed and guests weren't told yet, 80% of the seats are taken, or the event ended. Each message suggests next steps, such as telling guests what changed, a social post, a newsletter item or a mention on your show. Nothing goes out until someone says yes. Turn these off, or choose who gets them, under **Moments**.

## After the event

Under **After the event**, see how many came and how many didn't, and what people said they hoped to get out of it, counted without names. Send a thank-you with your Survey link to the people who came, and a "we missed you" to no-shows. Each person gets it once. Then make the photo gallery and share cards.

## Check people in

On **Check-in**, use **Find a guest** to mark people as they arrive. Select **Open on another phone** to check in from a second device at the door.

## List the event

On **Listings**, post the event to LinkedIn Events or Luma, and confirm with **Yes, post it**.

## Tours

If the event is one stop of a tour, you can add another stop like it and send invitations for every stop at once from Ask.

Ask can also invite people near each stop from your CRM. Each person goes to the soonest stop in their city, or to every stop there if a city has two dates. Anyone already signed up for a stop is skipped. They are added to the guest lists, and nobody is emailed until you send the invitations.
