About an hour after your webinar ends, you get one email, its subject your webinar's title followed by **your results and follow-ups are ready**. It says who came, which records we suggest you update, what to do next and who owes what. Nothing in it changes your records until you apply it.

<Note>
This email is rolling out to Marketing workspaces. If your webinars don't send it yet, the numbers are still on the webinar's results.
</Note>

## Why use it

The results email arrives after each webinar with a summary of registrations, attendance and engagement so you can act on the numbers without opening the platform. Granular opt-out controls let each team member choose which digest emails are useful to them, keeping inboxes tidy while ensuring the people who want the data still get it automatically.

## When it arrives

The email waits until the webinar's clips and its questions are ready, so the suggestions are complete. That's usually within an hour of the webinar ending, and never more than 4 hours after.

No email is sent for a webinar with fewer than 5 attendees.

## Who gets it

The webinar's owner and its hosts who are members of your workspace. Each person gets their own copy. Panelists and speakers from outside your workspace never get it.

## What's in it

- **Three numbers**: how many people registered, how many came (and what share of registrants that is), and how many questions they asked.
- **Suggested updates**, up to three, the ones that affect the most people first. For example:
  - Tag the people who look ready to talk as **Hot**: they asked for a demo, clicked your offer, or stayed past three quarters of the webinar and asked a question.
  - Add contacts from your target accounts to your pipeline, if your workspace has a CRM.
  - Mark open questions for follow-up, one task per question, assigned to you.
  - For associations: invite engaged nonmembers to join, or remind members who are due to renew.
- **Next step**: one sentence with a date, such as answering the open questions and sending the recap.
- **Action items**, each with an owner and a date: you answer the open questions, each presenter approves their suggested clips, the CRM owner of the target-account contacts calls them, and you send the recap.

The email uses your workspace's own words for people: customers, members or exhibitors.

The numbers describe the people who came. They don't claim the webinar caused a deal, a renewal or a contract. Results show who was there, not what gets the credit.

## Act on it

1. Select **Review results and follow-ups**. The webinar opens in Sessionboard with its follow-ups at the top.
2. Apply or skip each suggested update. An applied update shows who applied it and when.
3. To draft the follow-up, select **Make clips and a recap email** (or **Draft a recap email** when there are no clips). It drafts clips and a recap for attendees and for people who registered but didn't come.

Nothing is sent until you approve it. Suggested updates you don't apply expire after 14 days.

## Ask your AI assistant

If you've connected an AI assistant under **Apps** > **AI assistants**, ask it "What should I do after yesterday's webinar?". It reads the same results, suggestions and next step as the email, so the two always agree. An update the assistant applies for you follows your assistant's approval rules, and pending updates appear under **Waiting for your OK**.

## Turn it off

The foot of each email says why you get it and how to turn these emails off. Turning them off stops them for you only; your hosts still get theirs.

<Note>
These emails are for Sessionboard Marketing workspaces. Notices about your account, like your trial ending, still arrive when you turn this off.
</Note>
