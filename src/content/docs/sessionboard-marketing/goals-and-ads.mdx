A goal is an outcome, such as a number of registrations. Sessionboard drafts a plan across your speakers sharing, email and ads on your own accounts, you approve it, and then you follow the pace and what each channel brought.

## Plan a goal

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick a starter">
Go to **Goals and ads** and pick **Fill my event**, **Bring back last year's people** or **Run ads for my event**. Or describe it under **Or say it in your own words** and select **Draft it**.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the facts">
We fill in the event, how many registrations, the date and an ad budget, which can be **No ads**. Change anything that's off, then select **Draft the plan**.
</Step>
<Step title="Approve it">
Read **Your plan · waiting for your OK** and select **Approve the plan**, or **Not now**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Once it runs, the goal shows its progress and pace and **What brought registrations**, credited across every touch.

## Ads on your own accounts

Ads run on your own Meta account. Select **Draft the ads**, then **Approve the spend** for the amount shown, or **Not yet**. Sessionboard does not create the campaign in Meta yet, so approved ads wait in the goal and spend nothing.

## Audiences and sponsors

Under **More**:

- **Audiences from your people**: count who matches, save the audience, and download it as a hashed file for your ad account.
- **Sponsored reach**: send a sponsor a link to the audience they reached, valid for 30 days.
