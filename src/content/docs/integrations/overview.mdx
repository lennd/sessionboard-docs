## Where to find it

- **Organization → Settings → Integrations** lists every connector your organization can switch on, with a **Connect** button and a **View Documentation** link on each card. Connectors that are set up once for the whole organization — LiveBuzz, Visit, Brella and Maritz RTS — open their App Marketplace page from here.
- **Event → Settings → Integrations** holds the two event-level developer connectors, **Webhooks** and **Zapier**, and links back to the organization page for everything else.
- The **App Marketplace** itself is the catalog behind those cards. **Connect** links around the product — in Media → Discover, in the Canva picker, in Webinars, and in the onboarding import step — open it directly.

## The App Marketplace

The Marketplace is organized by what you are trying to do. Use the category rail on the left, or search by name.

| Category | Apps |
| --- | --- |
| **Content & Marketing** | Canva, Adobe Express |
| **Media** | Zoom Webinars, ON24, Goldcast, BigMarker, Riverside, Descript, Vimeo, YouTube |
| **Engagement** | Gleanin, Swapcard, Grip, NoteAffect, Snöball, InGo, Brella |
| **Program & Scheduling** | Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo, ExpoPlatform, ASP Events, Stova, Xtag, LiveBuzz, Maritz RTS, Visit |
| **Speakers & CRM** | iMIS |
| **Operations** | Slack, Microsoft Teams |
| **Automation & Developer** | Webhooks, Zapier |

Each card carries a badge for where the app connects — **Org-level** apps connect once for the whole organization; **Event** apps connect per event — and for what it contributes: **Discover**, **Webinars**, **Clips** and **Send to** capabilities, or **signals** to the graph. Apps with a **Soon** badge are listed in the catalog but cannot be connected. **Featured** at the top collects the most-used apps.

![The App Marketplace with the category rail and the Featured row](/images/kb/apps-marketplace.png)

Open a card and the app page starts with what syncs and in which direction, then the setup steps down the side — for a registration platform, that is Connections, Map events, Configure, Map session fields and Map speaker fields. The steps stay locked until you have connected, and a **Read the setup guide** link sits above the Connect button. [What syncs](/integrations/what-syncs) has the per-app table.

![The Cvent app page: what syncs, the setup steps, and Read the setup guide](/images/kb/apps-app-page.png)

The **Skills** tab lists the skills your connected apps give to Team Lead and the other agents.

## Field mapping

Every connector that pushes people or sessions has a field map: a table that pairs a Sessionboard field on the left with the destination's field on the right. The app's **Map Speaker Fields** and **Map Session Fields** tabs show it once an event is mapped. Each connector ships with a default map covering the fields the destination requires; those rows are starred and cannot be removed. Add a row for anything else the destination should receive — a field with no row is simply not sent — and the pickers only offer pairs whose types can sync. The full explanation, including why a mapped field can still arrive empty, is in [Before you connect](/integrations/integration-readiness#how-field-mapping-works).
