A custom dashboard is a board of live widgets you lay out yourself — KPIs, charts and tables built on your event's data, or on every event in the organization. Custom dashboards sit as tabs next to the built-in **Today** dashboard on an event and next to **Overview** on the organization Dashboard, and they run on the same reporting engine as the [Report builder](/reporting/report-builder), so anything you can report on, you can put on a dashboard.

## Where dashboards live

- Event level — open an event's **Dashboard**. Every custom dashboard is a tab after **Today**, and **Add Dashboard** on the right starts a new one. Widgets are scoped to that event.
- Organization level — open **Dashboard** in the organization sidebar. Custom dashboards are tabs after **Overview**; the same **Add Dashboard** button is on the right. Widgets here read across all of the organization's events, so this is where you compare events, follow a pipeline over the year or watch a speaker pool.

The selected tab is kept in the page URL, so a link to a dashboard opens on that dashboard.

Above the widgets, a **Custom dashboard** label and the description you gave it sit on the left, and **Add Widget** and **Settings** on the right.

## Create a dashboard

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add Dashboard**. The **New Dashboard** dialog opens with three ways to start: **Gallery**, **AI prompt** and **Build manually**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a starting point:

- **Gallery** — pre-built dashboards, one tile each, with a preview, a short description, the area it covers and how many widgets it holds. Click a tile and the dashboard is created and opened with its widgets already in place.
- **AI prompt** — describe what you want to track in **What do you want to track?**, or pick one of the example prompts, then click **Generate dashboard**. Sessionboard writes the widgets for you.
- **Build manually** — give the dashboard a **Dashboard name** and an optional **Description**, click **Create dashboard**, and start from an empty board.
</Step>
<Step>
Add, edit and rearrange widgets. Drag a widget by its handle to move it, and drag the corner to resize it. The layout saves as you go.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Templates in the gallery

The gallery depends on where you are and on which modules your event or organization uses — a sponsor template only appears when Sponsors is on, for example.

| Scope | Templates |
| --- | --- |
| Event | **Event Overview**, **Submissions Pipeline**, **Speaker Tracking**, **Review Progress**, **Evaluation Plans by Tracks**, **Schedule Health**, **Sponsor Performance**, **Exhibitor Performance** |
| Organization | **Portfolio Overview**, **Cross-Event Pipeline**, **Speaker Portfolio**, **Review Across Events**, **Programs Overview**, **Partner Performance** |

Each template creates four or five widgets — a few KPI numbers and a couple of charts or tables. Nothing about a template is fixed once it is on the board: rename, re-query or delete any widget, and add your own.

## Add a widget

Click **Add Widget**. The **Add Widget** dialog has three tabs.

### Gallery

A catalog of ready-made widgets, each with a preview, a one-line description, the module it draws on and its visualization. Filter by area with the chips under the search box — **Sessions**, **Speakers**, **Submissions**, **Evaluation**, **Agenda**, **Tasks & Compliance**, **Sponsors** and **Exhibitors** on an event; **Sessions**, **Speakers**, **Submissions**, **Evaluation**, **Programs** and **Portfolio** on the organization — or search by name. Click a widget to add it.

### From report

Turn a saved custom report into a widget that stays linked to it.

1. Choose the report under **Report** — every custom report saved in this event (or, on an organization dashboard, at the organization level) is listed, and the search box narrows it.
2. Pick a **Visualization type**.
3. Click **Add linked widget**.

A linked widget runs the report's query, so when you edit and save the report, the widget follows. In the widget's editor the query is shown as **Linked to report** and cannot be changed; **Open report** takes you to it. **Edit query anyway** unlinks the widget so it keeps its own copy of the query — the report itself is not affected.

The same link can be made from the other direction: on the Reports page, a custom report's **Actions** menu has **Add to dashboard**, which asks for a **Widget name**, a **Visualization type** and the **Dashboard** to put it on. Inside the Report builder, the **Widgets** tab does the same for the report you have open.

<Note>
**From report** lists custom reports only — the ones you have built and saved. A pre-built report from the Reports gallery has to be opened and saved first before it can back a widget.
</Note>

### Build custom

Write the widget yourself. Give it a **Widget name**, choose a **Visualization type**, then build the query in one of three ways — the same three the Report builder offers:

- **Describe it** — type what you want in plain English, or pick one of the suggested questions, and click **Generate Report**. You can attach a sample spreadsheet with **Attach sample report (CSV or XLSX)** to show the shape you are after.
- **Build** — pick the record type, columns, filters and grouping visually.
- **SbQL** — write the query directly. **Run** previews the results; **Schema** opens the field reference.

**PII Mode** masks personal details in the widget's output, the same way **Hide personal details** does on a report.

## Visualization types

Every widget has a visualization, chosen when you add it and changeable from **Edit widget**:

| Visualization | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Table** | Data in rows and columns |
| **Number / KPI** | A single metric |
| **Bar Chart**, **Stacked Bar** | Comparing categories, with or without segments |
| **Pie Chart**, **Donut Chart** | A proportional breakdown |
| **Line Chart**, **Area Chart**, **Stacked Area** | Trends over time |
| **Radar** | Comparing several measures at once |
| **Treemap** | Nested proportions |
| **Funnel** | Stage-by-stage flow, such as a submission pipeline |
| **Scatter Plot** | Correlation between two values |
| **Radial Bar**, **Progress Bars** | Progress against a total |
| **List** | A simple ranked list |
| **Heatmap** | A color-coded grid |

A dashboard holds up to 20 widgets. At the cap, adding is disabled with the message **Maximum of 20 widgets per dashboard** until you remove one. To track more, [create another dashboard](#create-a-dashboard) for the rest. Module tables have a separate column limit, covered in [Module table views](/reporting/dashboard-views).

## Work with a widget

Hover a widget for its toolbar:

- **Export** — download the widget's rows as **Export .CSV** or **Export .XLSX**.
- **Run as report** — open the widget's query in the Report builder to reshape, save, schedule or share it as a full report. On a linked widget this is **Run linked report**.
- **Edit widget** — rename it, change the visualization or rework the query.
- **Delete widget** — remove it from the board.

Widgets compute when the dashboard opens. **Settings > Refresh** re-runs every widget on the board.

## Share a dashboard

**Settings > Share** opens **Share** for the dashboard. **Create New Link** makes a public link that anyone can open without a Sessionboard login — give it a **Link name**, and optionally **Require password**, **Set expiration** or **Limit views**. Existing links can be copied or revoked from the same dialog, and revoked links stay listed under **Revoked links**. Shared dashboards show live data.

<Warning>
A share link shows whatever the widgets return. Turn on **PII Mode** on any widget that carries personal details before you share the board.
</Warning>

## Delete a dashboard

**Settings > Delete dashboard** removes the dashboard and its widgets. Reports that widgets were linked to are not deleted.

## Permissions

Organization members can open, build and edit dashboards at both levels. On an event, users who are not organization members need the event role's **Reports** permissions — view reports to open dashboards, create reports to add and edit widgets. The dashboard rows on a role — **Can view dashboards**, **Can add dashboards**, **Can update dashboards** and **Can delete dashboards** — are under **Data & Insights**. See [Invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members).
