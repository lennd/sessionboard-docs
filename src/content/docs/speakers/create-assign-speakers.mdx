Most speakers create themselves: when someone completes your submission form, Sessionboard creates their contact and attaches them to the session they submitted. This article covers the other case — a keynote you booked directly, a panelist added after acceptance, a co-presenter the submitter forgot — where an admin creates the speaker and assigns them to a session by hand.

A speaker is an ordinary contact attached to a session in the **Speaker** role. **CRM → Speakers** is a filtered view of your contacts, so a person only appears there once they are on at least one session.

## 1. Create the contact

If the person is already in your event, skip to [step 2](#2-assign-them-to-a-session) — the session's **Add** search finds them.

1. Go to **CRM → All Contacts** and click **Add Contact** at the top right.
2. Search by name or email first. Add Contact checks your organization before it creates anyone, so a speaker from last year's event is attached rather than duplicated.
3. If there is no match, click **Add new contact** and fill in **First name**, **Last name** and **Email** (required), plus phone and biography if you have them.
4. Click **Create Contact**.

![New Contact Details: required first name, last name and email, then mobile phone and biography](/images/kb/contacts-add-new.png)

The full flow, including what happens when the search finds someone, is in [Add or delete a contact](/contacts/add-delete-a-contact).

## 2. Assign them to a session

1. Go to **Program → Sessions** and click **Edit** on the session.
2. Open the **Participants** tab. There is a block for each role your program uses — **Speakers**, **Chairpersons**, **Moderators**, the **Submitter**, and **Sponsors** and **Exhibitors** where those are enabled.
3. Click **Add** on the **Speakers** block and type at least two characters of the person's name or email.

![The Speakers block with the Add search open, showing a matching contact plus Create new contact and Browse full list](/images/kb/session-participants-add.png)

4. Pick the contact from the results. Only contacts in this event appear, so if the person is missing:
   - **Create new contact** opens the Add Contact panel without leaving the session and links the new contact as a speaker when you save it.
   - **Browse full list** opens the A–Z directory, where you can tick several people at once.
5. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the panel. Nothing is written to the session until you do.

![The Participants tab with two speakers listed, each with a drag handle, eye icon and remove button](/images/kb/session-editor-participants.png)

<Note>
You can add speakers while creating a session too: **Add Session** has the same **Participants** tab.
</Note>

## 3. Order and visibility

Each row in the **Speakers** block has three controls:

- **Drag handle** — drag a speaker up or down to set the order. Agendas, speaker pages and embeds list speakers in this order.
- **Eye icon** — every speaker is public by default. Click it (**Make private**) to hide the speaker from embeds, the CMS site and the public API; click again (**Make public**) to show them. Hiding a speaker changes nothing about their portal, tasks or emails.
- **Remove** (×) — takes the speaker off this session. Their contact stays in the event.

Speakers on a session also carry a status — **Pending**, **Accepted** or **Declined** — which drives the acceptance flow. See [Speaker acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance).

Click **Save Changes** after reordering or toggling visibility.

<Tip>
In the [public API](/integrations/api-tokens), each session participant carries `is_public`. A speaker you made private returns `false`, so an agenda you build outside Sessionboard can respect the same choice.
</Tip>
