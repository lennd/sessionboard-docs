Session Speaker Acceptance lets speakers accept or decline their participation for each accepted session directly through their portal.

<Note>
If this feature isn't available in your **Record Settings**, contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) to enable it for your event.
</Note>

## Enable the feature
In **Settings → Record Settings**:

- **Enable Speaker Acceptance** — lets speakers accept or decline participation.
- **Allow Session Withdrawal** — lets speakers withdraw a submission from the portal acceptance task. Turn off to hide the **Withdraw** button.

## Set a speaker's status (as an admin)
<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the session">In **Sessions → Submissions** (or **Agenda**), click the pencil icon next to a session.</Step>
  <Step title="Go to Participants">Open the **Participants** page to see the speaker's role.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">Use the **Status** dropdown to set *Accepted*, *Pending*, or *Declined*.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Click **Save Changes**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm participation (as a speaker)

<Note>Speakers can only confirm participation for **Accepted** sessions.</Note>

In the **My Sessions** widget, a **Confirm** button appears for each accepted session. Selecting it opens a modal to accept or decline, then **Save**. Speakers can also **Withdraw** a session entirely (if withdrawal is enabled).

When a session is withdrawn, admins can see it in **Sessions → Submissions**. Opening the session shows who withdrew it and why; you can delete the session, set it to **Declined**, or undo the withdrawal at the bottom of the page.

## View speaker status
Status indicators: 🟨 Pending · 🟩 Accept · 🟧 Decline

- **From Contacts** — add the **Sessions** field to your module view.
- **From Sessions** — add the **Speakers** field to your module view.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can you create custom speaker statuses?">No, custom speaker statuses aren't available at this time.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can you assign a status to a moderator or chairperson?">No, statuses are supported for speakers only.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the Withdraw button be removed from the portal?">No, it can't be removed from the portal view.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can speakers edit co-presenters?">Yes, if the portal setting **Manage Related Sessions & Participants** is enabled (disabled by default). It also lets speakers edit session details.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do only accepted speakers sync to native integrations?">Yes — only speakers with an *Accepted* status on an accepted session sync to native [integration partners](/integrations/overview).</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
