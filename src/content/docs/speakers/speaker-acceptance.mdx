Participant acceptance lets people confirm or decline their part in a session from their portal, rather than by email. Each participant answers for every role they hold on a submission, so someone who is both a speaker and a moderator confirms each one separately.

<Note>
Participant acceptance is part of the Pro tier. If the **Participant Acceptance** section is missing from **Record Settings**, contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) to enable it for your event.
</Note>

## Settings

In **Settings → Record Settings**, under **Participant Acceptance**:

![The Participant Acceptance section of Record Settings with acceptance, withdrawal and portal invitation switches and the Portal Status Verbiage fields](/images/kb/acceptance-record-settings.png)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable Participant Acceptance** | Lets participants accept or decline each of their roles on a submission. Everything below appears only once this is on. |
| **Enable Participant Acceptance for Subsessions** | Shows acceptance on subsessions and stand-alone submissions only, not on parent submissions that have children. |
| **Allow Submission Withdrawal** | Lets participants withdraw a submission from the portal acceptance task. Turn it off to hide the **Withdraw** button. |
| **Portal Status Verbiage** | Overrides the status text participants read in the portal. |

### Portal Status Verbiage

Two labels can be reworded, each up to 60 characters. Leave either blank to keep the default.

| Field | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Invited (awaiting confirmation) | Confirmation Needed |
| Submitted (in review) | Under Review |

<Tip>
Use this when your programme's language differs from ours — for example, changing "Confirmation Needed" to "Please confirm by 1 March" so the deadline travels with the status.
</Tip>

## Set a participant's status as an admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the session">In **Program → Sessions**, click **Edit** on the session.</Step>
  <Step title="Go to Participants">Open the **Participants** tab. Each person's row shows their current status as a chip.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">Click the chip and choose **Pending**, **Accepted** or **Declined**.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the panel.</Step>
</Steps>

![The Participants tab of a session with a speaker's Pending chip open, offering Pending, Accepted and Declined](/images/kb/acceptance-admin-status.png)

A participant with no answer yet reads as **Pending**. You can also set statuses for many people at once from the sessions grid and its bulk actions.

## Confirm participation as a participant

<Note>Participants can only confirm for **Accepted** sessions.</Note>

In the portal, sessions waiting on the participant sit under **Invited Sessions** with the status **Confirmation Needed** (or your Portal Status Verbiage) and a **Confirm** button.

![The Invited Sessions group in the portal with two sessions marked Confirmation Needed, each with a Confirm button](/images/kb/acceptance-portal-invited.png)

**Confirm** opens the **Confirm Acceptance** dialog, which lists every participant on the submission and their role. The participant sets their own row to **Confirm** or **Decline** and clicks **Save**; if the portal lets them manage related participants, they can answer for the others too. If withdrawal is enabled, **Withdraw Submission** pulls the whole submission and asks for a reason.

![The Confirm Acceptance dialog listing three participants with their roles, a status control on the participant's own row, and Withdraw Submission](/images/kb/acceptance-portal-confirm.png)

Once answered, the session moves to **Confirmed Participation** (or shows **Declined**). A submission whose speakers have all declined is withdrawn automatically.

When a submission is withdrawn, it appears under the **Withdrawn** view in **Program → Sessions**. Opening it shows who withdrew it and why, and you can delete the session, set it to **Declined**, or undo the withdrawal.

## Control who sees invitations

With acceptance on, two more switches appear under **Participant Acceptance** in **Settings → Record Settings**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show Acceptance Invitations in Portals** | When off, no portal user sees a Confirm / invitation. Your team still sets Accepted, Pending, or Declined from the session Participants page, grids, and bulk actions. Turning it off also clears any invitation filters. |
| **Limit Invitations to Matching Submissions** | When on, only submissions that match the filters you add (for example Track = AI) show an invitation in the portal. Non-matching submissions never show one; your team can still manage their statuses from the admin app. Subsessions inherit their parent submission's match. A live count tells you how many submissions currently match. |

Events that have never touched these switches keep the original behavior: invitations visible, unfiltered.

## How the portal groups sessions

When acceptance is on, the portal splits a participant's sessions into sections. You control the grouping and the headings per portal — see [portal participation sections](/portals/portals-101#participation-sections).

## View participant status

- **Dashboard** — the Speakers section counts **Accepted**, **Pending response**, **Declined** and **Withdrawn**; each count links to the matching list.
- **CRM → All Contacts** — filter on **[Session] Session Status** to list everyone who is accepted, pending or declined on a session. See [Filter contacts by session status](/faq/how-to-filter-contacts-by-session-status).
- **Program → Sessions** — the **Speakers** column lists each session's participants; open a session's **Participants** tab for the status chip on each row.
- **Communications** — the campaign recipients step has a **Participant acceptance** quick filter, so you can remind only the people who have not answered.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can you create custom participant statuses?">No. The statuses themselves are fixed, but you can reword what the portal calls a pending status using **Portal Status Verbiage**.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can you assign a status to a moderator or chairperson?">Yes. Acceptance now covers every role a participant holds on a submission, and each role is confirmed separately.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the Withdraw button be removed from the portal?">Yes. Turn off **Allow Submission Withdrawal** in Record Settings.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can participants edit co-presenters?">Yes, if the portal setting **Manage Related Submissions and Participants** is enabled (disabled by default). It also lets them edit session details and answer for the other participants in the Confirm Acceptance dialog.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do only accepted participants sync to native integrations?">For scheduled and full syncs, yes — only participants with an *Accepted* status on an accepted session sync to native [integration partners](/integrations/overview). An admin can still push specific people ahead of acceptance by selecting them and using the integration's **Sync** bulk action — see [Visit](/apps/visit-integration) for an example.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
