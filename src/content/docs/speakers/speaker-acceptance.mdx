## Why use it

A session is not settled when you accept it — it is settled when the people on it say they will be there. Participant acceptance gives you that answer per person and per role, in one place, instead of chasing replies by email. Send each participant a formal invitation, give them a date to answer by, and let Sessionboard remind them and flag anyone who misses it, so your team spends its time on the few who need a call rather than on everyone. Conditions keep invitations from going out before a session is ready, and presenting authors of placed abstracts join the list without anyone adding them by hand.

## Settings

In **Settings → Record Settings**, under **Participant Acceptance**:

![The Participant Acceptance section of Record Settings with acceptance, withdrawal and portal invitation switches and the Portal Status Verbiage fields](/images/kb/acceptance-record-settings.png)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable Participant Acceptance** | Lets participants accept or decline each of their roles on a submission. Everything below appears only once this is on. |
| **Enable Participant Acceptance for Subsessions** | Shows acceptance on subsessions and stand-alone submissions only, not on parent submissions that have children. |
| **Allow Submission Withdrawal** | Lets participants withdraw a submission from the portal acceptance task. Turn it off to hide the **Withdraw** button. |
| **Portal Status Verbiage** | Overrides the status text participants read in the portal. |

### Portal Status Verbiage

Two labels can be reworded, each up to 60 characters. Leave either blank to keep the default.

| Field | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Invited (awaiting confirmation) | Confirmation Needed |
| Submitted (in review) | Under Review |

<Tip>
Use this when your programme's language differs from ours — for example, changing "Confirmation Needed" to "Please confirm by 1 March" so the deadline travels with the status.
</Tip>

## Invitations and deadlines

Once **Enable Participant Acceptance** is saved on, an **Invitations and deadlines** panel appears under **Participant Acceptance** in **Settings → Record Settings**. It controls when participants are asked, by when they must answer, and who gets reminded. The panel saves on its own with **Save invitation settings**, separately from the page's main **Save**. If you have just switched acceptance on, save the page first; the panel says so until you do.

Nothing changes for an event until you use it: invitations go out only when you send them, reminders are off, and with no accept-by rules there is no deadline.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask participants to accept** | Where a participant on a sub-session is asked. **On each session they are on** (the default) asks on the sub-session and mirrors the answer to the parent. **On sub-sessions only (not the parent session)** asks only on the sub-session; the parent shows them as context, with nothing to answer. **On the parent and each sub-session** asks at both levels, and each answer stands on its own. Someone assigned directly to the parent, such as a moderator, is always asked on the parent. |
| **Send reminders** | Emails participants who have not answered before their **Accept by** date. **Days before Accept by** takes whole numbers from 1 to 365, separated by commas — `7, 2` sends one reminder a week out and another two days out. Each reminder goes once, and never on the day the invitation was sent. |
| **When overdue, set participant status to** | Moves an overdue participant to one of your custom participant statuses. Leave it on **Leave status unchanged** to keep their status as it is. |
| **View invitations** | Opens the list of every participant who needs to accept, across the event. |

### Accept-by rules

**Accept-by rules** set the deadline. Click **Add rule** and choose:

- **Session format** and **Participant role** — leave either on **Any format** / **Any role**. A rule with both on "any" is the **Event default**.
- **Accept by** — **A fixed date** (in the event timezone), or **Days after the invitation is sent** for a rolling deadline.
- **Grace days** — 0 to 90. Participants can still respond this many days after **Accept by**; they show as **Overdue** meanwhile.
- **Send only when** — conditions that must all be true before an invitation can be sent (see [Hold invitations until a session is ready](#hold-invitations-until-a-session-is-ready)).

The most specific rule wins: format and role, then format, then role, then the event default. You can have only one rule per format and role. A session that matches no rule has no deadline. A rolling rule has no date until the invitation is sent.

### Hold invitations until a session is ready

Conditions under **Send only when** stop an invitation from going out before the session is ready for it — for example, only once the session is **Accepted**. You can check **Session status**, **Session custom status**, **Session format**, **Session date** (is set or is empty), **Parent session status**, and **Another participant in role** (has or has not accepted — useful for inviting a co-presenter only after the lead speaker has confirmed).

A participant whose conditions are not met shows as **Blocked**, with the reason. Sending to a mix sends the eligible rows and lists the blocked ones; send again once the session is ready.

## Send invitations

Invitations are sent from two places, both with a table of participants and their **Invitation** state and **Accept by** date:

- **One session** — open the session from **Program → Sessions** and expand **Acceptance invitations** in its side panel or on its profile page.
- **Several sessions** — select them in **Program → Sessions** and click **Send invitations** in the bulk action bar (up to 150 sessions at a time). The dialog lists only the participants on those sessions.

Filter the table with **All**, **Awaiting response**, **Not sent**, **Sent**, **Overdue** and **Blocked**. Each row has a **Send invitation** or **Resend invitation** button; tick rows and use **Send / resend** for a batch, or, with nothing ticked, **Send invitations** for everyone on the listed sessions. After a send, a summary counts what was sent, resent, already sent and blocked, and lists each skipped row with its reason. Someone already invited is not emailed again unless you choose **Resend invitation**.

A participant gets one email per session, even if they hold several roles on it. Sending needs permission to send emails.

### Extend a deadline

Click the calendar icon (**Extend deadline**) on a row — or tick several rows and use the one beside **Send / resend** — pick a date and click **Apply**. The date becomes the end of that day in the event timezone, and the row shows **Extended**. **Remove extension** goes back to the rule's date. You can only extend an invitation that has been sent, and you need permission to edit sessions.

### Reminders and overdue invitations

With **Send reminders** on, Sessionboard checks every hour. Reminders go at your **Days before Accept by** offsets. When **Accept by** passes without an answer, the participant gets one overdue email, your event admins get one notification listing overdue participants by session format (it opens the **Overdue** list), and the participant moves to your overdue status if you set one. Reminders stop as soon as the participant answers.

Overdue rows show in red under **Overdue**. Once a participant is past their **Accept by** date plus grace days, the portal no longer lets them change their answer; extend their deadline if they still need to respond.

### Invitation emails

Four emails are sent for invitations. Reword them under the System tab of your event emails — see [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails#sessions--submission-forms):

| Email | When it's sent |
| --- | --- |
| Participant invitation | You choose **Send invitation** or **Resend invitation**. |
| Participant invitation reminder | At each reminder offset before **Accept by**. |
| Participant invitation overdue | Once, after **Accept by** passes without an answer. |
| Abstract placed into a session | The first invitation to the presenting author of an abstract placed into a session. |

They share merge tags for the session title and date, parent session title, the participant's roles, the accept-by date, days left to respond, and the portal link. To show the deadline in your own emails and campaigns, use the **Accept By Date** session merge tag (`{{accept_by_date}}`) — it shows the recipient's earliest open deadline for that session in the event timezone, and is empty once they have answered or when there is no deadline.

### Presenting authors of placed abstracts

When you place an abstract into a session — for example with **Create a subsession** on the [Abstracts](/sessions/abstracts#create-subsessions-from-abstracts) list — its presenting author is added as a participant waiting to accept, at the level you chose. Co-authors are not invited. Their first invitation uses the **Abstract placed into a session** email, and the row reads **Placed from abstract**. The abstract keeps its own review status.

## Set a participant's status as an admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the session">In **Program → Sessions**, click **Edit** on the session.</Step>
  <Step title="Go to Participants">Open the **Participants** tab. Each person's row shows their current status as a chip.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">Click the chip and choose **Pending**, **Accepted** or **Declined**.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the panel.</Step>
</Steps>

![The Participants tab of a session with a speaker's Pending chip open, offering Pending, Accepted and Declined](/images/kb/acceptance-admin-status.png)

A participant with no answer yet reads as **Pending**. You can also set statuses for many people at once from the sessions grid and its bulk actions.

## Confirm participation as a participant

<Note>Participants can only confirm for **Accepted** sessions.</Note>

In the portal, sessions waiting on the participant sit under **Invited Sessions** with the status **Confirmation Needed** (or your Portal Status Verbiage) and a **Confirm** button.

![The Invited Sessions group in the portal with two sessions marked Confirmation Needed, each with a Confirm button](/images/kb/acceptance-portal-invited.png)

**Confirm** opens the **Confirm Acceptance** dialog, which lists every participant on the submission and their role. The participant sets their own row to **Confirm** or **Decline** and clicks **Save**; if the portal lets them manage related participants, they can answer for the others too. If withdrawal is enabled, **Withdraw Submission** pulls the whole submission and asks for a reason.

![The Confirm Acceptance dialog listing three participants with their roles, a status control on the participant's own row, and Withdraw Submission](/images/kb/acceptance-portal-confirm.png)

Once answered, the session moves to **Confirmed Participation** (or shows **Declined**). A submission whose speakers have all declined is withdrawn automatically.

### Deadlines in the portal

When an [accept-by rule](#accept-by-rules) applies, the participant sees **Respond by** and the date under the **Confirm** button, and the dialog opens with **Please respond by** and their earliest open deadline, in the event timezone. A role with a different deadline shows its own date.

After the deadline plus any grace days, the dialog reads "The deadline to respond has passed. Please contact the event team to change your response." and that role can no longer be changed. Extend the participant's deadline to let them answer. With **On sub-sessions only (not the parent session)**, roles that belong to a sub-session are locked on the parent with "Respond to this role on the sub-session".

On events that use accept-by rules or ask at a level other than **On each session they are on**, a submitter who also presents on an accepted session stays under **Invited Sessions** until they confirm, rather than being confirmed automatically.

When a submission is withdrawn, it appears under the **Withdrawn** view in **Program → Sessions**. Opening it shows who withdrew it and why, and you can delete the session, set it to **Declined**, or undo the withdrawal.

## Control who sees invitations

With acceptance on, two more switches appear under **Participant Acceptance** in **Settings → Record Settings**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show Acceptance Invitations in Portals** | When off, no portal user sees a Confirm / invitation. Your team still sets Accepted, Pending, or Declined from the session Participants page, grids, and bulk actions. Turning it off also clears any invitation filters. |
| **Limit Invitations to Matching Submissions** | When on, only submissions that match the filters you add (for example Track = AI) show an invitation in the portal. Non-matching submissions never show one; your team can still manage their statuses from the admin app. Subsessions inherit their parent submission's match. A live count tells you how many submissions currently match. |

Events that have never touched these switches keep the original behavior: invitations visible, unfiltered.

## How the portal groups sessions

When acceptance is on, the portal splits a participant's sessions into sections. You control the grouping and the headings per portal — see [portal participation sections](/portals/portals-101#participation-sections).

## View participant status

- **Dashboard** — the Speakers section counts **Accepted**, **Pending response**, **Declined** and **Withdrawn**; each count links to the matching list.
- **CRM → All Contacts** — filter on **[Session] Session Status** to list everyone who is accepted, pending or declined on a session. See [Filter contacts by session status](/faq/how-to-filter-contacts-by-session-status).
- **Program → Sessions** — the **Speakers** column lists each session's participants; open a session's **Participants** tab for the status chip on each row.
- **Communications** — the campaign recipients step has a **Participant acceptance** quick filter, so you can remind only the people who have not answered.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can you create custom participant statuses?">No. The statuses themselves are fixed, but you can reword what the portal calls a pending status using **Portal Status Verbiage**.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can you assign a status to a moderator or chairperson?">Yes. Acceptance now covers every role a participant holds on a submission, and each role is confirmed separately.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the Withdraw button be removed from the portal?">Yes. Turn off **Allow Submission Withdrawal** in Record Settings.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I withdraw a submission on a speaker's behalf?">No. Withdrawing is the participant's own action, from the Confirm Acceptance dialog in their portal. To pull a submission yourself, set its status to Declined or delete it. If one was withdrawn by mistake, open it and click **Undo Withdrawal** on the banner.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can participants edit co-presenters?">Yes, if the portal setting **Manage Related Submissions and Participants** is enabled (disabled by default). It also lets them edit session details and answer for the other participants in the Confirm Acceptance dialog.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do only accepted participants sync to native integrations?">For scheduled and full syncs, yes — only participants with an *Accepted* status on an accepted session sync to native [integration partners](/integrations/overview). An admin can still push specific people ahead of acceptance by selecting them and using the integration's **Sync** bulk action — see [Visit](/apps/visit-integration) for an example.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
