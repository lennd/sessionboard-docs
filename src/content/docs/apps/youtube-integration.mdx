Connect your YouTube channel once and publish a webinar's recording to it from the webinar's **After** tab: the title, a description with chapter timestamps, tags from the recording's topics, captions from the transcript, the privacy you choose and a playlist. The YouTube video can then be the **Watch on demand** link in recaps, the team share kit and the Content Gallery. The same connection lists your channel's videos in **Media → Discover**, so you can bring them into Media to transcribe and clip.

## Why use it

Your webinar recordings belong where your audience already watches: your own channel, with your branding and subscribers. Sessionboard does the production work on the way out (chapters, captions, a written description) and keeps the link in your follow-up pointing at the video people can find and share.

## Connect YouTube

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the app">
Open **Apps** in the organization sidebar, go to the **Media** category and click **YouTube**.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in with Google">
Click **Connect** and sign in with the Google account that manages your channel. If the channel is a Brand Account, pick the channel, not your personal account, when Google asks which one to use.
</Step>
<Step title="Allow access">
Approve viewing your channel and uploading and managing its videos. Sessionboard uploads videos, adds captions and adds videos to a playlist; it does not delete or edit your existing videos.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you connected YouTube before publishing was available, the webinar's **After** tab says **Reconnect to allow uploads**. Click it, then **Reconnect** in Apps, and approve the new access.
</Note>

## Publish a webinar recording

On the webinar's **After** tab, under **Publish the recording**, click **Publish to YouTube** once the recording is in Media. The form is filled in from the recording:

- **Title**: the webinar's title, up to 100 characters.
- **Description**: the webinar's description followed by a **Chapters** list (`0:00 Welcome`, `6:40 Trial results`, …). YouTube turns the list into chapters on the video when it has at least three timestamps, the first at 0:00.
- **Tags**: the topics found in the recording.
- **Privacy**: **Unlisted**, **Public** or **Private**.
- **Playlist**: any playlist on the channel, or none.
- **Upload captions from the transcript**: adds a caption track built from the recording's transcript.
- **Use this video as the on-demand link**: see below.

<Warning>
YouTube is still reviewing Sessionboard's access to upload videos. Until that review is done, YouTube keeps every video Sessionboard uploads **private**, even when you choose **Unlisted** or **Public**, and only a small number of uploads a day are allowed across all customers. This page will say when the review is complete.
</Warning>

Click **Publish to YouTube**. The upload runs in the background, so you can leave the page; the row shows its progress, then **Open on YouTube** when it is done. See [Follow up after a webinar](/webinars/after#publish-the-recording-to-youtube-or-vimeo) for the rest of the After tab.

## Use the YouTube video as the on-demand link

Turn on **Use the YouTube video as the on-demand link** on a published video and the recaps, the CSV export, the team share kit's **Watch on-demand** button and the Content Gallery link to it instead of the recording page. A private video cannot be the on-demand link, because the people you send it to could not open it. While YouTube keeps Sessionboard's uploads private (see the note above), use [Vimeo](/apps/vimeo-integration) or the recording page as the on-demand link instead.

## Publish automatically

Turn on **Publish automatically when the recording arrives** on the After tab and pick YouTube and a privacy. When the provider's recording has come into Media and been transcribed, it is published with the filled-in title, description, tags and captions.

## Bring your channel's videos into Media

Your channel's uploads are listed under **Media → Discover**. Import a video to transcribe it, find its chapters and clip it. On the YouTube app page you can limit Discover to certain playlists.

## Troubleshooting

- **YouTube's daily upload quota for Sessionboard is used up**: YouTube limits how many uploads one app makes per day. Click **Try again** the next day.
- **This YouTube channel has reached its daily upload limit**: YouTube limits uploads per channel per day. Try again the next day.
- **The account refused our sign-in** or **Sign-in expired**: reconnect YouTube in **Apps**.
- **Captions did not go through**: the video is up; add the caption file in YouTube Studio, or publish again.
- **The upload stopped**: click **Try again**. The upload continues from where it stopped.
