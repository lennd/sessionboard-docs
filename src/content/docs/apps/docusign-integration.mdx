Docusign is the e-signature service behind **Contracts** in Sessionboard. Connect your organization's Docusign account once, and your team can send Docusign templates from the people and program records they already manage, pre-filled with contact, session, and event data. Docusign sends the envelope and captures the signature; Sessionboard tracks the status, files the signed PDF on the record, and can move the speaker's pipeline entry forward when the agreement is signed.

<Note>
Contracts is an Early Access feature. Turn it on from **Org → Early Access**, or contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com). See [Early Access](/get-started/early-access).
</Note>

## Before you start

- **A Docusign eSignature account.** Envelopes are sent from your account, so your Docusign plan's limits and branding apply. Don't have one? You can [start a free trial](#start-a-docusign-free-trial) from inside Sessionboard.
- **At least one Docusign template.** Sessionboard sends templates, not free-form documents. Templates define the document, the signer role, and the fields the signer completes. See [Prepare a template](#prepare-a-docusign-template).
- **Permission to manage app connections** on your Sessionboard organization. Ask an organization admin if the **Connect Docusign** button is missing.

## Connect Docusign

Connections are per user. Each teammate who will send contracts connects their own Docusign login, and Sessionboard sends envelopes as that person.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Docusign integration">
Go to **Organization Settings > Integrations** and click **Settings** on the **Docusign** card. The Docusign panel opens on the right.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Connect Docusign">
A Docusign sign-in window opens. Sign in with your Docusign credentials and allow Sessionboard to send envelopes on your behalf. Sessionboard receives only an access token; it never sees your Docusign password.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm the connection">
The window closes and your name, email, connection date, and Docusign account appear under **Connections**. The Docusign card now shows **Connected**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If your browser blocks the pop-up, Sessionboard opens Docusign in the current tab instead and brings you back when you are done. Allow pop-ups for Sessionboard for the smoother experience.
</Tip>

## Start a Docusign free trial

If your organization has no Docusign account yet, click **Don't have a Docusign account? Start a free trial** at the top of the Docusign panel. Docusign opens its sign-up in a pop-up. When the trial account is active, Docusign returns you to Sessionboard and the new account is connected in the same step, so you can send your first contract right away.

## Manage connections

The **Connections** list shows every teammate who has connected Docusign. Click the trash icon next to a connection and confirm **Disconnect Docusign?** to revoke it, for example when a teammate leaves your organization.

Disconnecting stops new sends from that connection. Contracts that were already sent stay in Docusign and keep their status in Sessionboard.

## Auto-advance the pipeline

Under **Auto-advance on contract completion**, choose a [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline) stage. When a contract linked to a pipeline entry is signed, Sessionboard moves that entry to the stage you chose, so a signed speaker agreement can land a prospect in **Confirmed** without anyone dragging a card. Leave it at **No auto-advance (off)** to keep stage changes manual.

## Prepare a Docusign template

Sessionboard pre-fills a template by matching each Docusign text field to a Sessionboard variable:

1. In Docusign, create or edit a template. Add the document and a signer role.
2. Drag a **Text** field onto the page wherever Sessionboard should fill a value.
3. Set the field's **Data label** to a Sessionboard variable, for example `contact.full_name`, `event.name`, or `contact.custom.hotel`.
4. Add the **Signature** and **Date Signed** fields the signer completes, then save the template.

The **Variable reference for Docusign templates** section of the Docusign panel lists every variable available to your organization, including your custom contact and account properties. Use the search box to find one and the copy icon to copy its exact id. The full list is in [Docusign template variables](/speaker-crm/docusign-template-variables).

## Send and track contracts

Once connected, a **Contracts** tab appears on contact, account, group, and pipeline entry records, and **CRM > Contracts** lists every contract in your organization. Sending, statuses, reminders, voiding, and portal signing are covered in [Send and track contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts).

## How status updates work

Every envelope Sessionboard sends asks Docusign to notify Sessionboard when it changes. When the signer signs, declines, or the envelope is voided, the contract's status in Sessionboard updates within moments and the signed PDF is stored on the record. Nothing needs to be configured in your Docusign account for this to work.

## Security

- Sessionboard connects with OAuth 2.0. Your Docusign password is never shared with Sessionboard.
- Access tokens are stored encrypted and refreshed automatically.
- Signed PDFs are stored in Sessionboard's encrypted file storage in your data region.
