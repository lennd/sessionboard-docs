Connect your Vimeo account once and publish a webinar's recording to it from the webinar's **After** tab: the title, a description with chapter timestamps, chapters in the Vimeo player, captions from the transcript, tags from the recording's topics, the privacy you choose and a folder. The Vimeo video can then be the **Watch on demand** link in recaps, the Dispatch share kit and the Content Gallery. The same connection lists your Vimeo videos in **Media → Discover**, so you can bring them into Media to transcribe and clip.

## Why use it

If your organization hosts its videos on Vimeo, your webinar recordings should land there too, with the player, privacy and embed settings you already use. Sessionboard adds the chapters and captions on the way out and keeps your follow-up pointing at that video.

## Connect Vimeo

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the app">
Open **Apps** in the organization sidebar, go to the **Media** category and click **Vimeo**.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in with Vimeo">
Click **Connect** and sign in with the Vimeo account your organization's videos live in.
</Step>
<Step title="Allow access">
Approve the access Vimeo lists: viewing your videos, uploading, editing the videos Sessionboard uploads and adding them to folders.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you connected Vimeo before publishing was available, the webinar's **After** tab says **Reconnect to allow uploads**. Click it, then **Reconnect** in Apps, and approve the new access.
</Note>

## Publish a webinar recording

On the webinar's **After** tab, under **Publish the recording**, click **Publish to Vimeo** once the recording is in Media. The form is filled in from the recording:

- **Title** and **Description**: the webinar's title, and its description followed by a **Chapters** list of timestamps.
- **Tags**: the topics found in the recording.
- **Privacy**: **Unlisted**, **Public** or **Private** (Vimeo calls private **Only me**). Unlisted needs a paid Vimeo plan.
- **Folder**: any folder in the account, or none.
- **Upload captions from the transcript**: adds a caption track built from the recording's transcript.
- **Add the chapters to the Vimeo player**: creates one Vimeo chapter per chapter of the recording.
- **Use this video as the on-demand link**: see below.

Click **Publish to Vimeo**. The upload runs in the background, so you can leave the page; the row shows its progress, then **Open on Vimeo** when it is done. See [Follow up after a webinar](/webinars/after#publish-the-recording-to-youtube-or-vimeo).

## Use the Vimeo video as the on-demand link

Turn on **Use the Vimeo video as the on-demand link** on a published video and the recaps, the CSV export, the Dispatch share kit's **Watch on-demand** button and the Content Gallery link to it instead of the recording page. A private video cannot be the on-demand link, because the people you send it to could not open it.

## Publish automatically

Turn on **Publish automatically when the recording arrives** on the After tab and pick Vimeo and a privacy. When the provider's recording has come into Media and been transcribed, it is published with the filled-in title, description, chapters, tags and captions.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Vimeo account has no upload space left**: free space in Vimeo or upgrade the plan, then click **Try again**.
- **The video host refused the upload**: the message says why Vimeo refused it, for example a privacy setting the plan does not include. Change it and publish again.
- **The account refused our sign-in**: reconnect Vimeo in **Apps**.
- **Chapters** or **Captions did not go through**: the video is up; add them in Vimeo, or publish again.
- **The upload stopped**: click **Try again**. The upload continues from where it stopped.
