## Where to find Sites

Sites are the public-facing portals where submitters sign in, complete entries, and track them. Open **Sites** in your organization's left sidebar.

The page lists your organization's sites in two tabs: **Global Sites**, which belong to the organization and can host awards programs and interest forms, and **Event Sites**, which belong to a single event. Each card shows the site's URL, how many programs are published on it, how many pages it has, and when it was last updated. **Which one do I need?** opens a comparison of Sites, Embeds, Poster Galleries, and Content Galleries if you are not sure which surface fits.

![The Sites page with the Global Sites and Event Sites tabs and a site card showing its URL, programs, and pages](/images/kb/awards-sites-list.png)

<Tip>
A single global site can host awards programs and interest forms together, so you may not need a separate site for each. See [Speaker CRM: interest forms](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms) for publishing interest forms.
</Tip>

## Creating a new site

Click **Add Site**. Give the site a **Site Name** (an internal label, shown to your admin team only) and a **Site Slug**, the last part of its public URL: `https://sites.sessionboard.com/s/your-slug`. Slugs can be up to 100 characters; keep them short and easy to type, because they become the link you share.

When you already have a site you like, choose it under **Copy configuration from**. The copy brings over the site settings, branding, and pages — but not the slug, and not the programs published on the source site, so the new site starts empty and you link the right programs yourself. The source site is not changed.

![The Add Site dialog with Site Name, Site Slug, and Copy configuration from](/images/kb/awards-add-site.png)

Click **Create Site** to open the site's overview.

## The site overview

Each site has an overview page with its status, **Preview Site** and **Settings** buttons, and three cards: **Site URL** (with copy and open buttons), **Landing Page & Login** (login options, SSO, logo, custom CSS, font), and **Logged-In Experience** (portal logo, background image, primary color, and whether the Events page is shown). The **Quick Actions** row — **Edit Login Options**, **Customize Branding**, **Manage Pages**, **Configure SSO** — jumps to the matching part of the builder.

![A site's overview page with the Site URL, Landing Page & Login, and Logged-In Experience cards and the Quick Actions row](/images/kb/awards-site-overview.png)

## The Site builder

**Settings** opens the Site builder: a sitemap on the left, a live preview in the middle, and an inspector on the right for whatever you select. Edits show up in the preview as you type; nothing reaches the public site until you click **Publish**. **Open live** opens the published site in a new tab, and the **Preview as** menu above the canvas switches the preview between **Visitor**, **Submitter**, **Evaluator**, and **Speaker** so you can check each experience.

![The Site builder with the sitemap, the live preview of the landing page, and the Landing inspector](/images/kb/awards-site-builder.png)

The sitemap has two sides:

- **Public** — pages anyone can open before signing in. Under **Settings**: **Site**, **Layout**, **Login / SSO**, and **Programs / CFP**. Under **Pages**: **Landing** and **Events**. **Custom pages** are yours to add.
- **Portal** — the signed-in experience: **Welcome**, **Home**, **Review workspace**, **Tasks**, and **My Profile**.

![The sitemap with Public selected, listing Settings, Pages, and Custom pages with their Live and Off status](/images/kb/awards-site-builder-sitemap.png)

Each entry shows **Live** or **Off**. Off pages stay hidden from visitors while you draft them.

## Site URL & branding

Select **Site** in the sitemap. The inspector opens with a **Ready to share** checklist — branding, at least one live program, a login method, and an optional privacy notice — with a running count of what is done.

![The Site inspector: the Ready to share checklist, Site URL, and Branding with the Generate button and logo](/images/kb/awards-site-builder-site.png)

**Site URL** holds the **Site Name** and **Site Slug** from when you created the site; the name doubles as the public headline when the landing Title is empty.

**Branding** has two groups:

- **Public landing** — **Logo** (PNG or SVG, 400×160px, up to about 500KB), **Gradient Start Color** and **Gradient End Color** for the landing background, and **Font Family** (any Google Font; the default is Inter).
- **Signed-in portal** — a separate **Logo**, an optional **Background Image**, **Font Family**, and **Primary Color**, **Accent Color**, and **Text Color**.

Primary Color is used for section backgrounds such as the sign-in card and the portal navigation, Accent Color for buttons and links, and Text Color for the title, subtitle, and paragraph text drawn over those surfaces. Text Color is white until you set it, and Sessionboard applies it exactly as entered — check that it stays readable against your gradient and Primary Color. Evaluators see the same colors on the evaluator site.

### Generate branding

Instead of setting colors and logos by hand, click **Generate**, enter your organization's website — a bare domain like `example.com` works — and Sessionboard extracts colors, logo, and text from it to fill the branding fields. Fine-tune afterward. If the site can't be scanned (a typo in the domain, or a site that blocks automated requests), the dialog says what went wrong.

## Landing page

Select **Landing** under Pages. Set the **Title** (the main heading on the public page) and **Subtitle** (shown beneath it) to say what your program is and invite people in.

**Advanced** holds custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the public page. This is intended for developers: unscoped code can break styles and existing functionality, so use it only with developer help.

## Login methods

Select **Login / SSO**. At least one method must be on:

- **Standard Login** — Submitters enter their email address and receive a sign-in link (a magic link). The link is valid for 30 minutes; there is no password. This is on by default.
- **SSO Login** — Single Sign-On through your organization's identity provider (SAML, OIDC, and others). Contact Sessionboard support to set SSO up for your organization.

![The Login / SSO inspector with Standard Login checked and the SSO Login option](/images/kb/site-builder-login.png)

## Programs / CFP

Select **Programs / CFP**. This is not a public page: the programs you select here appear as cards on the signed-in home, and the preview switches to a submitter view so you can see them.

- **Selected Programs** lists what is published on this site, each with a type badge (Awards, Sessions + Speakers, Request, Event) and its end date when one is set. Drag to set the display order; click **×** to remove one.
- **Available Programs** lists everything else you can publish. Tick a program to add it.

![The Programs / CFP inspector with Selected Programs in display order and the Available Programs list](/images/kb/awards-site-builder-programs.png)

<Note>
Publishing an awards program on a Site is what opens it to submitters. A program that is not on any Site stays in **Draft**, whatever its rounds say. See [Program statuses](/awards/getting-started#program-statuses--lifecycle).
</Note>

## The signed-in portal

Switch the sitemap to **Portal** to configure what submitters see after they sign in.

### Welcome

The **Welcome** page is the first-time registration screen. **Required Fields** are First Name, Last Name, and Email — fixed, and email is pre-filled from the sign-in link. Under **Optional Fields**, **Collect employer** adds an organization field. The **Privacy Notice** editor adds your own consent language alongside the standard terms and privacy acceptance; keep it concise.

![The Welcome inspector: Required Fields, the Collect employer switch, and the Privacy Notice editor](/images/kb/awards-site-builder-welcome.png)

### Home

**Home** is the portal's landing page after sign-in. Give it a **Title** and a **Description** (rich text for instructions or announcements), then choose the **Portal widgets**:

- **Sidebar** — **My Events**, **My Submissions**, and **My Contracts**.
- **Main panel** — **Notifications** (cart, reviews, tasks) and **My Reviews**.

![The Home inspector with Title, Description, and the Portal widgets switches](/images/kb/awards-site-builder-home.png)

Turn on **My Events** when your organization uses Sessionboard for events as well as awards, so people can see the events they are part of from the same portal.

### Events page

Under **Pages** on the Public side, **Events** is a public list of your organization's events, separate from the signed-in My Events item. Switch on **Show Events page**, then choose whether to filter by time window or hand-pick the events, and where each card links — the event's Sessionboard Site or an external page.

## Custom pages

Click **+** next to **Custom pages** to add content pages — FAQs, eligibility guidelines, sponsor information. Each has a title, a slug, and rich-text content, and shows as **Live** or **Off** in the sitemap. Live custom pages appear in the header navigation of the public landing page and in the signed-in portal.

## Publishing

When the preview looks right, click **Publish**. Until then, your edits are a draft that only the builder shows. Use **Open live** to check the published site, and share the Site URL with submitters.

## Public voting

When an awards round is set to **Public voting**, the ballot uses this same Site. Copy the link from the round (**Copy link**) and share it. Voters sign in with a magic link or SSO, then score submissions on that round's scorecard. See [Managing submissions, reviewers & rounds](/awards/managing-submissions-reviewers-rounds).

![Awards vote cards in grid view, with a submission photo as the card thumbnail](/images/kb/public-vote-awards-grid.png)

Pasting the vote URL into Slack, LinkedIn, or similar shows a branded preview of the round or the individual submission. Search engines and AI assistants do not index these URLs.

## What submitters see

### Landing page

Visitors see your branded landing page with the gradient, logo, title, and subtitle you configured, a **Sign in** card where they enter their email to receive a login link, and your live custom pages in the navigation.

### First-time registration

The first time someone opens their sign-in link, the Welcome screen asks for the required fields (email pre-filled and locked) and the terms and privacy acceptance, plus your privacy notice if you added one. After that they land on the portal home.

### Signed-in portal

The portal home shows your title and description, then **Open Programs** — one card per published program with its submission window, status, and a button to start an entry (labeled with your program's action label, such as **Apply Now** or **Nominate**). The sidebar carries the widgets you switched on, so submitters can return to **My Submissions** to continue a draft or check the status of a completed entry, and reviewers see their queue under **My Reviews**.

![The Site builder previewing the signed-in portal as a Submitter, with the Open Programs cards](/images/kb/awards-site-preview-submitter.png)

Because one portal can host awards programs and interest forms together, all of a person's submissions appear in the same list.
