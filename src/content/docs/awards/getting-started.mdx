## Where to find Awards

In the left sidebar of your organization, click **Program → Awards** to open the Awards hub. It lists every program as a card, with high-level stats across all of them: **Completed Submissions**, **Total Fees**, **Active Reviewers**, and **Pending Decisions**.

The hub also has four views across the top — **Programs**, **Submissions**, **Reviewers**, and **Invoices** — so you can work across all programs without opening each one.

![The Awards hub, with the four views across the top, the stats row, and a program card showing submissions, pending count, current round, and reviewer completion](/images/kb/awards-hub.png)

## Creating a new program

Click **New Program** in the top-right corner of the Awards hub. This opens the guided setup wizard, which walks you through your program configuration in eight steps. Each **Continue** saves the step you are on, so you can leave and come back — the program exists from the first step onward.

<Tip>
You can run several programs from the same organization. Each program has its own form, categories, pricing, reviewers, and notifications, so an annual awards program and a separate grants program can run side by side.
</Tip>

## The 8-step setup wizard

The **Program Setup** sidebar on the left lists the steps and shows where you are: Identity, Categories, Form Builder, Pricing, Rounds, Winners, Managers, and Notifications. Click a completed step in the sidebar to go back to it; **Continue** saves and moves forward.

### Step 1: Identity & Nomenclature

Define what your program is and how it is labeled throughout the product.

![Step 1 of the wizard: Program Name, Program Type cards, Program Description, and the Dynamic Labels Preview](/images/kb/awards-wizard-identity.png)

**Program Name** — Appears on the public portal and throughout the admin interface. Choose something descriptive, such as "2026 Excellence in Innovation Awards".

**Program Type** — Pick the type that best matches your use case. It sets the default terminology used across the platform:

- **Awards** — Recognition programs with nominations. Uses "Nominator," "Nomination," and "Nominate."
- **Grants** — Funding applications with proposals. Uses "Applicant," "Application," and "Apply."
- **Scholarships** — Educational funding applications. Uses "Applicant," "Application," and "Apply."

**Program Description** — Describe the purpose and eligibility criteria. This is internal-facing and helps your team keep track of what each program is for.

**Dynamic Labels Preview** — After you pick a type, this panel shows the three labels the type sets. Each one is editable:

- **Person submitting** — for example "Nominator" (up to 20 characters)
- **Single entry** — for example "Nomination" (up to 20 characters)
- **Action button** — for example "Nominate" (up to 15 characters)

**Submitter editing after submit** — The **Editing policy** decides whether a submitter can change an entry after sending it in. The default is **Never allow edits after submit**. Turn on **Notify admins on submitter edit** if you allow edits and want an email each time one happens. You can override the policy for a single entry later from its detail sheet.

**Default status for new submissions** — The status every entry lands in once it is submitted, or once payment clears when fees are due on submit. **Submitted** is the default; choose **Under review**, **Accepted**, or **Rejected** to have entries arrive already in that state — for example **Under review** when every entry goes straight to your panel.

<Tip>
Dynamic labels make the platform feel native to your program. If your organization calls submissions "entries" or "applications," change the labels here and the terminology updates everywhere — forms, portal, emails, and admin screens.
</Tip>

### Step 2: Categories

Categories are the taxonomy for your program. They organize submissions and can be linked to pricing rules and reviewer assignments later.

Click **Add Category** to create your first category. Each one has a **Category Name** and a **Description/Instructions** field for category-specific guidance to submitters.

Categories support up to **two levels of nesting**: Parent → Child → Grandchild. Use the **Sub** button on a category to add a sub-category beneath it, and drag or use the arrows to reorder.

![Step 2 of the wizard, listing the program's categories with Sub, reorder, and edit controls](/images/kb/awards-wizard-categories.png)

<Note>
Each category shows a count of linked **Pricing Rules** and **Reviewer Rules**. Those links are configured in the Pricing step and in round assignments — but settling your category structure now makes both easier.
</Note>

### Step 3: Form Builder

The Form Builder is where you design the submission form that applicants or nominators fill out. It has its own guide — see [Building your awards submission form](/awards/awards-submission-form) — but in short you will configure:

- **Sections** — Category Selection, Submitter Information, Nomination Details, and Form Questions, each with its own fields and instructions.
- **Standard fields** — A Quick Add panel offers pre-built fields (nominee name, email, title, organization, nomination statement, supporting documents, and so on). Required fields are flagged if missing.
- **Custom fields** — Add fields using types such as text, long text, dropdown, checkbox, date, email, and file upload.
- **Field options** — Custom label, placeholder, help text, required or optional, and conditional question rules.
- **Form Rules** — Combined character or word limits across several text fields.

### Step 4: Pricing

If your program charges a fee, this is where you configure it. Pricing has its own guide — [Awards pricing, payments & invoices](/awards/awards-pricing-payments-invoices) — and the wizard step covers:

- **When to Collect Payment** — **Do Not Collect Payment** (free submissions) or **Upon Submission** (payment is collected when the submitter completes the entry).
- **Select Payment Gateway** — Pick a gateway connected under your organization's payment settings. If none is connected, click **Manage Payment Settings** to add one.
- **Base Fee** — The default fee for every submission, in USD.
- **Date & Time Tiers** — Early-bird, regular, and late prices that switch on a schedule.
- **Pricing Rules** — Change the fee when a condition is met, for example a specific category.
- **VAT Rules** — Tax applied to the total in the submitter's cart.
- **Promo Codes** — Discount codes applicants enter at checkout.

![Step 4 of the wizard with Upon Submission selected, showing the gateway picker, Base Fee, Date & Time Tiers, Pricing Rules, VAT Rules, and Promo Codes](/images/kb/awards-wizard-pricing.png)

<Tip>
If your program is free, keep **Do Not Collect Payment** and move on. You can turn payments on later by editing the program.
</Tip>

### Step 5: Rounds

Rounds define the evaluation timeline. A default round called **Submissions and Review** is created for you. This step shows your rounds and links to **Program Dashboard → Rounds**, which is where you add, edit, and schedule them.

![Step 5 of the wizard, listing the Evaluation Rounds with a link to the Program Dashboard](/images/kb/awards-wizard-rounds.png)

There are two round types:

- **Submission + Review** — Accepts new submissions and lets reviewers evaluate them in the same round.
- **Review Only** — No new submissions; reviewers evaluate entries that are already in (a finalist round, for example).

Each round has a name, submission and review periods, reviewer instructions, voting settings, and a scorecard. See [Managing submissions, reviewers & rounds](/awards/managing-submissions-reviewers-rounds).

### Step 6: Winners

Winner Tags are the kinds of recognition your program hands out. After review, you apply them to winning submissions.

The **Quick Add** panel adds common presets in one click: Award Recipient, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Honorable Mention, Finalist, First Place, and Runner Up. **Add Winner Tag** creates a custom tag with a name and an optional description.

![Step 6 of the wizard, with the Add Winner Tag button and the Quick Add presets](/images/kb/awards-wizard-winners.png)

<Tip>
You can add or edit winner tags after launch. There is no need to settle every tier now.
</Tip>

### Step 7: Managers

Program Managers are co-administrators. Unlike reviewers, who evaluate submissions, managers have **full administrative access** to the program — submissions, settings, and the review process.

- **Add Existing Admin** — Choose from people who are already admins in your organization and click **Add**.
- **Invite New User** — Send an invite to someone who is not an admin yet; they get access to this program when they accept.

![Step 7 of the wizard, showing Current Managers, the Add Existing Admin list, and the Invite New User form](/images/kb/awards-wizard-managers.png)

### Step 8: Notifications

The last step lists the emails the program sends automatically, in three groups: **Submitter Notifications**, **Reviewer Notifications**, and **Admin Notifications**. Each has a switch to turn it on or off and a **Customize** button that opens the template. Most start on; Deadline Reminder, Non-Selection Notification, Submission Updated Alert, and New Submission Alert start off.

![Step 8 of the wizard, listing each notification with its switch and Customize button, and the Launch Program button](/images/kb/awards-wizard-notifications.png)

Notifications are covered in [Awards notifications & email templates](/awards/awards-notifications-email-templates).

Click **Launch Program** to save the final step and go to your Program Dashboard. Launching does not open the program to submitters by itself — see [Program statuses](#program-statuses--lifecycle) below.

## Your program dashboard

The **Program Dashboard** is the operational hub for one program. The header shows the program name, its current status with the completed submission count, and two buttons: **Settings** (Duplicate, Archive, Delete) and **Edit**, which reopens the wizard. Below the header, tabs switch between **Dashboard**, **Submissions**, **Rounds**, **Reviewers**, **Reviewer Tags**, and **Invoices**.

![The Program Dashboard: status line, Settings and Edit buttons, the tab row, Quick Views, the Submission Funnel, and Review Progress](/images/kb/awards-program-dashboard.png)

The Dashboard tab itself shows:

- **Quick Views** — Submissions by Category, Submissions by Round, and Unassigned Submissions.
- **Submission Funnel** — Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, and Rejected counts, with **View All**.
- **Review Progress** — Completion by round.
- **Category Breakdown** — Submissions per category.

### Getting Started checklist

A **Getting Started** card tracks what is left to set up: configure categories, customize the submission form, configure evaluation rounds, add reviewers, and add the form to a portal. Each item links to the right screen.

## Program statuses & lifecycle

A program's status is worked out from its setup, rather than switched by hand:

- **Draft** — Not published on a Site yet, no active period, and no submissions. Use this time to finish the form, pricing, notifications, and Site.
- **Open** — Published on a Site, and a round's submission period is running (or no submission dates are set). Submitters can reach the form through the Site.
- **Reviewing** — Submissions are in and a review period is running, or the program is between periods.
- **Closed** — Submissions are in and every configured submission and review period has ended.
- **Archived** — You archived it from **Settings**. Data is kept for reference; nothing new comes in.

<Note>
To open a program to submitters, publish it on a Site (**Programs / CFP** in the Site builder) and give its first round a submission period. See [Setting up your Sessionboard site](/awards/setting-up-your-sessionboard-site).
</Note>

## Managing existing programs

From the Awards hub, search your programs or filter by year and status, then click a card to open its Program Dashboard. There, the **Settings** button offers:

- **Duplicate** — Copy the program. Useful for running the same awards annually: duplicate last year's configuration and update the details.
- **Archive** — Keep the data but stop new submissions.
- **Delete** — Permanently remove the program and everything in it.

**Edit**, next to it, reopens the setup wizard so you can change any step.

![The Settings menu on a Program Dashboard, with Duplicate, Archive, and Delete](/images/kb/awards-program-settings-menu.png)

## Next steps

With your program created, here is the recommended order for getting fully set up:

1. [Building your awards submission form](/awards/awards-submission-form) — Design the form your submitters fill out, including fields, sections, conditional logic, and validation rules.
2. [Awards pricing, payments & invoices](/awards/awards-pricing-payments-invoices) — Configure submission fees, payment gateways, VAT rules, promo codes, and invoice tracking (skip if your program is free).
3. [Awards notifications & email templates](/awards/awards-notifications-email-templates) — Customize the automated emails for confirmations, reviewer invites, winner announcements, and more.
4. [Managing submissions, reviewers & rounds](/awards/managing-submissions-reviewers-rounds) — Set up evaluation rounds, add reviewers, configure scorecards and assignments, and move submissions through review.
5. [Setting up your Sessionboard site](/awards/setting-up-your-sessionboard-site) — Publish a branded portal where submitters sign in, complete entries, and track their status.

<Tip>
Finish your form and pricing before notifications and the Site, so confirmation emails and portal pages reference the right details and fees.
</Tip>
