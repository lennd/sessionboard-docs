## What the Form Builder is for

**Form Builder** is the third step of the program setup wizard. It is where you decide what a nominee or applicant is asked, in what order, and under which conditions. Open it from the program dashboard with **Edit**, then **Form Builder** in the step list on the left, or continue into it from **Categories** while creating a new program.

With the Form Builder you can:
- Organize questions into sections, each with its own title and introduction
- Add pre-built fields from **Quick Add**, or your own fields of fourteen types
- Set a custom label, placeholder, help text, character limit and required state per question
- Show or require a question based on the category or an earlier answer with **Use question rules**
- Keep some fields for staff only with **Mark as admin field**
- Cap the combined length of several written answers on the **Rules** tab

Changes are saved when you click **Continue** at the bottom of the step, or when you switch to another step in the list on the left.

## Sections

**Category Selection** always sits at the top of the form and is always required. Submitters pick one of the categories you configured in the **Categories** step, and that choice is what most question rules key off.

Below it, the form is a list of sections. Every new program starts with two:
- **Submitter Information** — who is filling in the form
- **Nomination Details** — who or what is being put forward

Each section has:
- A title, edited in place (**Section title...**)
- A description, written in a rich-text editor with bold, italic, underline, superscript and subscript, links, lists and indentation, alignment, images, clear formatting and a **Source code** view; the **...** button holds whatever the toolbar width cannot show. This is where instructions, eligibility notes or examples go.
- Its own **Form Questions** list, split into **Required Fields** and **Additional Fields**

Drag the handle on the left of any additional field to reorder it within its section.

---

## Standard fields and Quick Add

The **Quick Add** panel on the right lists pre-configured fields grouped by section. Click a field to add it to the matching section; the counter on each group shows how many of its fields are already on the form.

**Submitter Information**: Your First Name, Your Last Name, Your Email, Your Title/Position, Your Organization, Your Phone, Relationship to Nominee.

**Nomination Details**: Nominee First Name, Nominee Last Name, Nominee Email, Nominee Title/Position, Nominee Organization, Nominee Department, Nominee Phone, Nomination Statement, Impact & Achievements, Nominee Headshot, Supporting Documents, CV/Resume, Website/Portfolio.

Six of these are locked as **Required Fields** and cannot be removed: the submitter's first name, last name and email, and the nominee's first name, last name and email. They are what the platform uses to identify the submitter and to match the nominee across entries. If one is missing from the form, a warning at the top of the step lists what to add from **Quick Add** before you can continue.

**Nominee Headshot** is a single-image upload (JPG, PNG or GIF) that the platform recognizes as a headshot rather than a generic file, so it shows up as a photo in review and exports.

## Custom fields and section elements

**Add Field** in a section's **Form Questions** header opens the field picker. Search by name, or pick a type:

- **Short Text**, **Long Text**, **Rich Text**
- **Email**, **URL**, **Phone Number**, **Number**
- **Dropdown**, **Multiple Select**, **Checkbox**
- **File Upload**, **Date**, **Signature**
- **Co-Authors** — lets the submitter name additional people on the entry

**Add Section Element** at the top of the same picker inserts layout rather than a question: a **Section Header**, a **Rich Text** block for instructions between questions, or a **Divider**.

![The Add Field picker with Add Section Element, the search box and field types](/images/kb/awards-form-add-field.png)

## Per-question options

Every additional field has a **Required** switch and a **...** menu:

- **Customize question** — label, placeholder, help text and limits (below)
- **Use question rules** — show this question only for certain categories or answers. Open it on a later field to set when it appears, or on a dropdown or checkbox to choose every later field that answer should show. To write other field values when the submission is saved, use an Assignment [Field Rule](/settings/field-rules) instead.
- **Mark as admin field** — hide the question from submitters. Staff fill it in from the submission in the admin, and it is listed on the **Internal fields** tab of the Form Builder.
- **Remove from form**

The locked required fields have the same menu without **Remove from form**.

![A question's menu: Customize question, Use question rules, Mark as admin field, Remove from form](/images/kb/awards-form-field-menu.png)

### Customize Question

- **Field name** — the program-specific name shown in the builder and to submitters. Standard fields carry a **Standard** badge.
- **Custom Label** — override the name for submitters only, so the builder keeps the recognizable field name
- **Placeholder** — example text shown inside the empty field
- **Help Text** — rich text shown under the field
- **Max Characters** — a per-field limit; leave empty for no limit. For a limit shared across several answers, use the **Rules** tab.
- **Required Field**

![The Customize Question dialog for Relationship to Nominee](/images/kb/awards-form-customize-question.png)

## Form Rules

The **Rules** tab holds one **Combined Text Limit** that applies across several written answers, for example "3,000 words across the nomination statement and the impact section". **Add Rule** creates it; the tab badge shows a red dot until the rule has both a limit and at least one field.

- **Limit Type** — **Characters** or **Words**
- **Max Characters** / **Max Words**
- **Allowed Overage** — how far over the limit a submitter can go before the form blocks submission
- **Fields included in this limit** — tick the short text, long text and rich text fields that count toward it

![A Combined Text Limit rule with two fields selected](/images/kb/awards-form-rules.png)

## Preview

**Preview** at the top right of the step renders the form as a submitter will see it, category picker first, so you can check section copy, required marks and question rules before the program goes live. **Exit preview** returns to the builder.

**Download Form**, next to **Preview**, saves a blank copy of the form as a PDF or Word document, with every section and question, including the ones that a category or a form rule shows. See [Download a blank copy of a form](/concepts/download-a-blank-form).

<Tip>
Walk the whole form once as a submitter before you launch: pick each category in turn and confirm the questions that appear are the ones you expect.
</Tip>
