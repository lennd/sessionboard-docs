A **Call for reviewers** on an awards program recruits judges the same way Sessions does: invite a named list, import people you already know, or publish an interest form. Recruits pick from this program's catalogs — categories, category expertise tags, program reviewer tags, and nomination fields marked reviewer-searchable. Accepted people enroll into the program rounds you name.

Open the program, then the **Call for reviewers** tab beside **Reviewers** and **Reviewer Tags**.

The **Training · Feature spotlight** on this tab walks these steps.

<Gallery />

## Why use it

Awards already adds reviewers one by one or from the org pool. A call adds the accept step, the expertise questionnaire, and a public application — and those picks become the **Match expertise** rule on the assign wizard, so a judge who picked a category is a candidate for nominations in that category.

## Create a call

![The awards program Call for reviewers tab with the call selected, Invite people, Import reviewers, Settings, and the public application link](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-awards-tab.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Open the program and click **Call for reviewers**. If you have not created one yet, click **Create call**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set the window">
    **Call name** is required (up to 255 characters). **Opens** and **Closes** are optional. Dates use the program timezone shown under the fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how people join">
    Click **Settings**. Under **Intake**:

    - **Require approval** — people who accept or apply wait as **Pending approval** until you approve them. Turn this off to enroll them automatically when they finish.
    - **Interest form** — publish a public application link.
    - **Require sign-in** — applicants must sign in before they can respond.

    The Settings sheet matches Sessions. Awards catalogs replace session tracks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick expertise lists and enrollment">
    Under **Expertise applicants can select**, turn on **Categories**, **Category expertise tags**, **Reviewer tags**, and any nomination fields flagged reviewer-searchable. Session tracks, tags, and taxonomy topics do not appear here.

    Under **Enroll accepted reviewers into**, pick the program rounds they should join. Create a review round first if the list is empty. Public-voting rounds do not take assigned judges — pick a reviewer-scoped round.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the portal copy">
    **Title**, **Introduction**, **Success message**, and **Closed message** appear on the public call page. Leave a field empty to use the default wording.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can run more than one call per program. Use the **Call** selector to switch.

## Invite, import, and the public link

The three intakes match Sessions. Labels and skipped-row reasons are the same.

- **Invite people** — paste emails or upload a CSV. Each person is **Invited** and is not on the program roster until they finish (or you approve them, if approval is on). **Resend invitation** issues a new seven-day magic link to that person only. A repeated email is skipped.
- **Import reviewers** — enrolls them on the program rounds immediately with an **Onboarding incomplete** badge on the **Reviewers** tab. They can still be selected when you assign. Re-importing the same email does not create a second roster row. They receive the call's onboarding email, not the standard **Review Invitation**.
- **Public link** — shown when **Interest form** is on. Anyone with the link can apply. A closed call rejects new submissions; you can still approve someone who applied before it closed. Submitting twice with the same email updates the pending application.

The invitation and onboarding emails use the program's Site. If the **Public link** card says there is no portal site yet, set one up first.

![The public application form with About you fields and Your expertise](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-portal-apply.png)

<Note>
The public form does not create a nomination. It only creates a person on this call.
</Note>

## The people list

Search by name or email. Filter by **Status** or **Source** (Invited, Imported, Applied).

![The people table with Invited, Applied, and Imported rows and their statuses](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-people-table.png)

**Conflicts** uses the same detector the rounds use against this program's nominations — submitter, co-authors, shared organization, and anything they declared. Flags are informational. Approving or enrolling is never blocked.

**Approve** enrolls a pending applicant and keeps the expertise they submitted. **Decline** leaves the roster unchanged and can send a decline email. **Remove** takes them off the call; reviewers already enrolled keep their assignments.

## Match expertise when assigning

On a round's **Assign** tab, under **How should they be distributed?**, choose **Match expertise**. A judge is paired with a nomination only when, on every list the call asks about and the nomination has values for, at least one of their picks matches. A judge who picked one category is a candidate for nominations in that category, and is not paired with nominations in other categories. Judges with no picks are not assigned by this rule.

![Match expertise on the awards Assign tab](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-awards-match-expertise.png)

**All to All**, **By Reviewer Tag**, and **Individual Reviewer** do not start requiring expertise. The round's conflict-of-interest rules still drop pairs after matching.

A public-voting round hides **Assign**. Use a reviewer-scoped round.

See [Managing submissions, reviewers and rounds](/awards/managing-submissions-reviewers-rounds#2-how-should-they-be-distributed).

## What the reviewer sees

The Program Site shows the same accept / onboarding / apply pages as Sessions: **Your expertise**, **Organization** when the Site collects affiliation, and an optional conflict note. Completing the page writes the picks onto this program's reviewer tags and category assignments, so later edits update those rows.
