Sessionboard supports over 100 payment gateways. When adding a new gateway, you can choose from the following featured providers:
- Stripe — Cards, wallets, and bank transfers.
- Braintree — PayPal, Venmo, and cards.
- Authorize.net — Cards and eChecks.
- PayPal Commerce — PayPal, Pay Later, and Venmo.
- BluePay — Cards and ACH (Fiserv).

These descriptions are what each provider supports, not what your submitters can pay with — see [What can submitters pay with?](#what-can-submitters-pay-with) below. Any other gateway on the full list is connected by the Sessionboard team; the **Add Gateway** screen says *Don't see yours? Reach out to us*.

Connect a gateway under your organization's **Settings → Payment Settings → Add Gateway**. The steps are in [Awards pricing, payments & invoices](/awards/awards-pricing-payments-invoices#adding-a-payment-gateway).

## Why use it

Check this list before you set up paid submissions, so you can keep using the payment processor your finance team already works with. It also tells you what submitters can pay with at checkout, and the extra settings BluePay needs before it takes a real payment.

## BluePay (Fiserv) setup

BluePay asks for your 12-digit **Account ID** and 32-character **Secret Key**. In the BluePay Gateway Manager, open **Administration → Accounts → List**, open the account, and copy both values.

<Note>
On the same account, click **Edit** and set **Hash Type in APIs** to **MD5** before you take payments. Sessionboard signs every BluePay request with MD5. An account left on HMAC_SHA512 passes **Test Connection** (which only checks that BluePay accepts the credentials) but then rejects every charge with `SECURITY ERROR`, and your submitters see a payment failure. If you have more than one BluePay account, set it on the account whose Account ID you entered.
</Note>

After saving the gateway, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Send $1.00 test charge**. Sessionboard authorizes $1.00 and voids it immediately, so nothing settles. This is the only check that reaches the processor, and it catches account settings such as the hash type that **Test Connection** cannot. On a live gateway, use a real card you control; some banks show the voided hold as pending for a few days.

BluePay has no separate sandbox account. **Test Mode** runs your existing account in BluePay's TEST mode, where no money moves: BluePay approves odd-dollar amounts (for example $1.00 or $65.00) and declines even-dollar amounts (for example $2.00), so use an odd amount to see a successful test payment. Turn Test Mode off to take real payments.

## What can submitters pay with?

Checkout takes **credit and debit cards** only, whichever gateway you connect. The payment form asks for a card number, expiry and security code; Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Venmo and bank transfers (ACH or eCheck) are not offered at checkout, even when your gateway supports them.

When a promo code brings the total to $0, checkout completes without asking for a card.
