## Where to find notification settings

Notifications is the last step of the program setup wizard, after Identity, Categories, Form Builder, Pricing, Rounds, Winners, and Managers. To come back to it later, click **Edit** on the Program Dashboard and choose **Notifications** in the **Program Setup** sidebar.

![The Notifications step, with Submitter, Reviewer, and Admin groups, a switch and Customize button on each notification, and the Launch Program button](/images/kb/awards-wizard-notifications.png)

<Tip>
You can change notifications at any point — during setup or after your program is live. Review them before publishing the program on your Site so submitters and reviewers get the right emails from the start.
</Tip>

## How notifications work

Sessionboard Awards sends emails automatically when specific things happen in your program — an entry is submitted, a reviewer is assigned, a winner is tagged. Each notification has:

- A **switch** to turn it on or off. When off, the email is not sent even if the trigger happens.
- A **Customize** button that opens the template, where you change the from name, subject, and body.

Notifications are organized in three groups: **Submitter Notifications** (people who submit entries), **Reviewer Notifications** (people who evaluate them), and **Admin Notifications** (program managers).

## Submitter notifications

### Submission Confirmation

Sent when a submitter completes and submits an entry — their receipt that it arrived. When your program collects payment on submission, this goes out once payment is processed. On by default.

### Invoice / Receipt

Sent when a payment completes, with the amount charged and the entry it belongs to. Relevant only to programs that charge fees. On by default.

### Deadline Reminder

Sent to submitters who started an entry but have not completed it, five days and one day before the submission deadline. Completed submissions are not reminded, and people who never started an entry are not emailed.

<Note>
This is the same cadence as [session submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms) draft reminders. The days cannot be changed — you can only turn the notification on or off and customize the copy. The switch starts off, so turn it on before you need the nudges.
</Note>

### Winner Notification

Sent to submitters whose entries you tag as winners. Customize it with next steps, ceremony details, or anything the winner needs to do. On by default.

### Non-Selection Notification

Sent to submitters whose entries were not selected. Use it to communicate the outcome, thank participants, and encourage them to apply again. Off by default.

<Note>
Winner and Non-Selection notifications are not sent the moment you tag a winner. The Mark as Winner confirmation offers to send the Winner Notification right away; otherwise, you choose when to send.
</Note>

## Reviewer notifications

### Review Invitation

Sent when you add a reviewer with the **Send email invite** switch on in the Add Reviewer dialog. It gives the reviewer a link to the review portal along with the program and round details. On by default.

![The Add Reviewer dialog, with the Send email invite switch that uses the Review Invitation template](/images/kb/awards-add-reviewer.png)

### Reviewer Deadline Reminder

Sent to reviewers with outstanding reviews five days and one day before the round's review period ends. The days cannot be changed — you can only turn the notification on or off and customize the copy. On by default.

<Tip>
Edit the Review Invitation template before adding your first reviewer, so the welcome message reads the way you want from the start.
</Tip>

## Admin notifications

Admin notifications go to the program's managers.

### New Submission Alert

Sent when a new submission comes in, so admins know without checking the dashboard. Off by default — turn it on for lower-volume programs where each entry matters.

### Submission Updated Alert

Sent when a submitter edits an entry after submitting it. This only applies when the program's editing policy, or a per-entry override, allows edits after submit. Off by default.

### Round Complete Alert

Sent when every review in a round is complete — the signal that you can advance finalists, select winners, or close the round. On by default.

## Customizing email templates

Click **Customize** next to any notification to open its template. You can change:

- **From Name** — Who the email appears to be from. The default is the program name.
- **Subject** — The subject line.
- **Body** — The email content, with a formatting toolbar for bold, italics, lists, alignment, and links.

The **To** field is fixed by the notification type — for example, the submitter's email or the program's administrators. The **Merge Tags** button next to Subject and Body inserts placeholders such as the program name, the submitter's name, or the round's review deadline, which are filled in when the email is sent. **Reset to Default** restores the original template.

![Editing the Submission Confirmation template, with From Name, the fixed To field, Subject and Body with Merge Tags, and Save Changes](/images/kb/awards-notification-customize.png)

<Note>
Customizations apply to the current program only. A new program — including one made with **Duplicate** — starts from the default templates, so customize them again for each program.
</Note>

### Who the emails come from

If your organization has a system email address configured, each notification also has a **From** field. Leave it blank — that is the default — and the notification sends from your organization's system email, the same address your event-level emails use. Fill it in only when a particular notification should come from somewhere else.

An address you type has to be on a sending domain your organization has authorized. Anything else is refused when you save, with a message naming the domain mismatch. If your organization has no system email at all, awards notifications send from a Sessionboard no-reply address.

<Tip>
Setting up a custom sending domain, or changing which address your organization sends from, is covered in [Add-on: custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain).
</Tip>

### Turning notifications on and off

Flip a notification's switch off to stop it sending, even when the trigger happens, and back on at any time. This is useful for pausing communications — for example, holding Winner Notifications until you are ready to announce publicly.

## Best practices

- **Review templates before launching:** Customize Submission Confirmation and Review Invitation before you publish the program. They are the first emails participants receive.
- **Coordinate winner announcements:** Keep Winner and Non-Selection notifications off during judging. Turn them on when you are ready to announce.
- **Use deadline reminders:** Turn on the submitter Deadline Reminder and keep the Reviewer Deadline Reminder on. Automated nudges reduce incomplete entries and late reviews.
- **Keep admin email manageable:** Leave New Submission Alert off for high-volume programs and watch the Program Dashboard instead.
- **Test before going live:** Submit a test entry to your own program to see the emails as submitters will.
