**Repurpose run** is the page where an event's recordings become content for your pathways. Open your event and go to **Marketing → After the event → Repurpose run**. It appears when Pathways is turned on for your organization.

The run starts the day after the event and runs again whenever a new recording lands. You don't start it. You watch it, pause it, and decide what it keeps.

## The five stages

The page shows each stage with a count, so you can see where a recording is.

1. **Read.** The recording is transcribed and split into chapters. Each chapter gets a topic, a level and a length.
2. **Cut.** Chapters that stand alone are kept, within your length range. Intros, Q&A housekeeping and breaks are dropped.
3. **Draft.** Clips, short reads, quote cards and check questions are written from the kept chapters. Each is cited back to the chapter it came from and written in your brand voice.
4. **Check.** Anything that needs a person is sent to Review. That includes items that need a speaker's OK, continuing-education (CE) items that need a reviewer, and items with a claim to confirm. The stage says when nothing is waiting.
5. **Slot.** Approved items go into your pathways as options on the steps they fit. Fit picks decide who gets which one.

Nothing reaches a pathway without passing **Check** first. Items already in Review stay there when you pause the run.

## Change the rules

Select **Settings** on the page to open **Rules for this run**. Changes apply only to recordings that arrive after you save.

| Rule | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Chapter length** | **Shortest chapter (seconds)** and **Longest chapter (minutes)**. The shortest is at least 10 seconds, the longest at most 30 minutes, and the longest must be longer than the shortest. |
| **Voice** | The brand voice the drafts are written in. |
| **Skip speakers who said no** | Keeps the parts of speakers who declined out of every item. |
| **CE items** | Sends CE items to any reviewer, or always to one named person. |

## Pause and resume

**Pause** holds new recordings until you choose **Resume**. The page shows **This run is paused** while it is held. Use it when you need to fix a rule before more content is drafted.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it publish anything on its own?**
No. Drafts go to Review, and only approved items go into pathways.

**Why is a recording missing?**
A recording is picked up when it lands on the event. If the page says **No recordings** yet, add the recording to the event first.

**Can I run it for an event that already happened?**
It starts the day after the event and then follows new recordings. Recordings added later are picked up the same way.
