Pathways sits right under **CRM** in the sidebar and answers one question for every program decision: who is this for? It brings registrations and check-ins together across the registration, event-app and membership platforms you already use, rolls them up by topic and speaker, and feeds the result back into the agenda, the Speaker Score and your reports.

<Note>
Pathways is an Early Access feature. If you do not see it under CRM, request it from **Preview** at the bottom of the organization sidebar or contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com).
</Note>

## At the organization

Open **Pathways** from the organization sidebar for the view across every event.

- **Audience** — **Attendees**, **Members** and **Accounts**: every person and company you have seen, with the events, sessions and topics they showed up for.
- **Program** — **Topics**: your taxonomy with attendance and fill rolled up per topic, so you can see which subjects fill rooms and which do not.
- **Outreach** — **Personalize**: what you send each audience, shaped by what they attended.
- **Data** — **HubSpot** and **Membership & pipeline**: connect your marketing stack and load membership or pipeline value so every room, topic and speaker can be read against it.

## On an event

Open **Pathways** on an event for that event's audience.

- **Audience › Attendees** — registrations and check-ins per session, with capacity and fill. Import them from a spreadsheet or let a connected platform sync them: [Track session attendance and room fit](/sessions/session-attendance-and-room-fit).
- **Feedback › Pre-event quiz** — a short quiz attendees take before the event that tells you which topics they want and gives each of them session picks.
- **Feedback › Surveys** — attendee scoring during the event: [Surveys](/evaluations/surveys).
- **Results › Program report** — total check-ins, median fill, full sessions and ratings in one place after the event.

## What it changes elsewhere

- **Agenda** — the **Room fit** panel forecasts which sessions will overflow and suggests a larger room: [Get room-fit suggestions for your agenda](/sessions/session-attendance-and-room-fit#get-room-fit-suggestions-for-your-agenda).
- **Speaker Score** — room draw becomes a weighted lane in the blend: [Weigh room draw in the Speaker Score](/sessions/session-attendance-and-room-fit#weigh-room-draw-in-the-speaker-score).
