An evaluation plan is a set of submissions, a set of evaluators, and a way of scoring — how a committee's judgement gets into Sessionboard so you can read it next to each session before you [accept or decline](/sessions/accept-decline). This article is the tour: where plans live, the wizard, and what a running plan shows you. The full reference for rounds, scorecards, reviewer views, and assignment strategies is [Setting up round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations).

## Why use it

A committee's scores land next to each session, so the accept and decline decisions rest on the rubric rather than on memory. Every evaluator sees only the submissions they're assigned, and a track chair whose role has a session filter sees, promotes and exports only their own track's submissions.

## Where evaluation lives

Open **Program → Evaluation**. The **Summary** tab is the dashboard — total evaluations, submissions scored, plans, evaluators, completion, average scores, top submissions — and is covered in [Evaluation Summary](/evaluations/evaluation-summary).

**Call for Reviewers** (Sessions 2.0) is the tab beside **Evaluators** and **Evaluator Tags**. Use it to invite a list, import last year's committee, or publish an interest form so people pick expertise before you assign. See [Open a call for reviewers](/evaluations/call-for-reviewers).

![The Evaluation Summary tab with totals, reviewer activity, completion status, and average scores by plan](/images/kb/evaluation-summary.png)

**Evaluation Plans** lists every plan as a card: open or closed, how many rounds, evaluations done, submissions, and the due date. Click a card to open the plan; the ellipsis (⋯) holds the same actions as the plan's toolbar.

![The Evaluation Plans tab with a card for each plan showing its status, progress, and due date](/images/kb/evaluation-plans-list.png)

Each card also carries a **v1** or **v2** badge, and the **Version** filter shows only one or the other; a **v1 plans** count appears while any remain. A **v1** badge marks a plan built before Sessions 2.0. Reviews already in progress keep working, but if your event starts more than 60 days from now a v1 plan can only be viewed: a banner says so, and editing and sending wait until you choose **Convert to V2…** from the card's menu. Converting creates a new v2 copy with one round and leaves the original in place. If you need to keep editing a v1 plan without converting it, ask your Customer Success Manager; they can unlock v1 editing for the event, and the list then shows **v1 editing is unlocked for this event**.

### Convert a v1 plan to v2

**Convert to V2…** on a v1 plan's card menu opens **Convert to Evaluation Plan v2**. Click **Create v2 copy**. Sessionboard creates a new, closed plan named **Copy:** plus the original name, and opens nothing and emails nobody. The v1 plan is not changed or locked by the conversion: it keeps its reviews and scores, and stays open if it was open.

| Carried over to the v2 copy | Not carried over |
| --- | --- |
| Every v1 evaluator, enrolled in one round named **Round 1** | Reviews and scores. They stay on the v1 plan. |
| The rubric and evaluation fields, as the Round 1 scorecard. Question types v2 can't show exactly are converted to the nearest scale. | Assignments. The copy has none, so it shows 0 submissions until you assign. |
| The fields evaluators see, instructions, anonymized review, weekly reminders, uploaded files, subsessions, other reviewers' scores, and hiding empty fields | Filter rules on a field that was deleted or belongs to another event or organization. They are listed on the copy (see **Plan scope** below). |
| What the plan evaluates (parent submissions or subsessions) | Conflict moderation, which starts off |
| The plan's scope: its filter rules, or its tag, status, format, track, language and level scope turned into filter rules | |
| The due date, as Round 1's **Review closes**, unless it has passed. A past date is cleared. | |
| Evaluators' event-team tags, as **Evaluator Tags** | |

A plan whose evaluators are AI personas can't be converted. Create a v2 plan and add the personas to its rounds instead.

Before you convert, run **Export reports** on the v1 plan if you need its scores outside Sessionboard. Converting doesn't move or delete them.

After you convert:

1. **Edit** the copy and check the Round 1 scorecard and the fields evaluators see.
2. On the **Assignments** step, check **Plan scope**, then assign. See [assignments](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#step-4-assignments).
3. Close the v1 plan so evaluators don't keep scoring both. On an event whose v1 plans are view-only, closing it also needs v1 editing unlocked, so ask your Customer Success Manager.
4. Open the copy with **Open plan**, setting the round dates, and notify evaluators.

#### Plan scope

A plan made by **Convert to V2…** or by **Add plan ▸ Copy from…** keeps its source plan's scope: it only covers the submissions that match the source's filters. The **Plan scope** card at the top of the **Assignments** step shows those filters. Change a rule and click **Save scope**, or click **Remove scope** to cover every submission. Assignment rules, including **All submissions in plan scope** and **By submission filters**, choose from this set.

Rules the copy could not keep are listed under **Not carried over from** on the same card, with the reason: the field was deleted, it belongs to another event, or (for **Copy from…**) an option such as a track or tag has no match of the same name in this event. Add them again in **Plan scope** if you still need them, then click **Dismiss** to clear the list. **Copy from…** also names the affected plans in a message when the import finishes.

## Create a plan

Click **Add Plan**. The wizard has five steps down the left — **Type**, **Overview**, **Rounds**, **Evaluators**, **Assignments** — and you can come back to any of them later with **Edit**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type — what to evaluate">
    **Parent Submissions** (the abstracts or sessions themselves) or **Subsessions** (each subsession scored on its own). This is set when the plan is created and can't be changed, so choose it with the review in mind.

    ![The Type step asking what you would like to evaluate — parent submissions or subsessions](/images/kb/evaluation-plan-target.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Overview — name and basics">
    - **Name** — “First Round Reviews”, “Track A Evaluations”.
    - **Set Plan as Open** and a **Due Date** — or leave it closed while you set up. Closed plans are view-only for evaluators.
    - **Scoring method** — **Percentage based** (rubric weights normalize to 100%) or **Points based** (the raw sum of points; weights are hidden). Applies to every round in the plan.
    - **Allow messaging with submitters** — lets evaluators send a message to a submission's authors through the portal. Submitters read and reply on their portal's **Messages** page, so their portal also needs **Enable messaging** on (portal **Configuration**). If it is off, evaluators see earlier messages only and cannot send new ones, and submitters get no message emails.
    - **Instructions** (optional) — shown to evaluators when they start; these belong to your default round.
    - **Conflict moderation** and **Abstaining** — how conflicts of interest are handled and whether evaluators can set a submission aside instead of scoring it, with or without a reason.

    ![The Overview step with the plan name, open switch, scoring method, messaging, conflict moderation, and abstaining settings](/images/kb/evaluation-plan-settings.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rounds">
    Every plan has at least one round; add more when the same pool goes through stages. **Funnel** carries only promoted submissions forward; **Parallel** runs rounds side by side. Each round has its own dates, blind-review setting, scorecard, and reviewer view. See [rounds](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#step-2-rounds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluators">
    Choose who evaluates. Pick people who are already users on the event, or click **Add evaluator** to invite someone new by name and email. Either way they're enrolled in the rounds you pick. **Send invite email to new evaluators** controls whether they're emailed on the spot. An evaluator sees only their queue: no sessions module, contacts, emails, or settings. They sign in on **this event's Program Site** — not the organization awards Site. If the event has no program site yet, set one up under **Program → Site** before you invite; otherwise they will see **No evaluations found**. See [what access do evaluators have?](/faq/will-evaluators-have-the-same-access-to-my-event-that-i-do-as-an-admin)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assignments — who evaluates what">
    Pick the submissions (**All submissions in plan scope**, **By track**, **By submission filters**, or **Individual submissions**), choose how they're distributed across evaluators, preview the result, and **Save assignments**. Conflicts of interest are flagged or excluded in the preview according to the round's setting. See [assignments](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#step-4-assignments).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Scorecard questions, rubric weights, and the fields evaluators see are configured per round. Settle the scorecard before you open the plan — once evaluations exist, changing questions changes what's being compared. See [configuring scorecards](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#configuring-scorecards) and [reviewer view configuration](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#reviewer-view-configuration).
</Tip>

## Open the plan

A plan created closed is opened from its toolbar with **Open plan** (or **Open** on the card's ellipsis menu). The dialog sets **Review opens** and **Review closes** for each round and the **Plan due date**, then asks whether to notify evaluators. Choose **Notify all evaluators** or **Notify new evaluators** — you can send either later from **Plan Options → Notify evaluators** — or open without sending and tell them yourself.

Once open, evaluators can grade; a closed plan shows them their submissions in view-only mode.

To reopen a plan you closed, use **Open plan** again. Closing a plan sets its due date to the moment you closed it, so the dialog needs a later date before **Open** works:

- **v2 plans:** the dialog shows the round's saved **Review opens** and **Review closes**. If **Review closes** has passed, it reads **Review end must be in the future**; pick a later date. A plan with several rounds has a round picker at the top; set the dates for the round you are reopening.
- **v1 plans:** **Plan due date** shows when you closed the plan and reads **Due date must be in the future**; pick a new date.

## A plan in progress

Open a plan and the tabs across the top follow the work.

![An open evaluation plan on the Submissions tab, with each submission's evaluators, progress, and average score](/images/kb/evaluation-plan-monitor.png)

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Submissions** | Each submission in the round with its evaluators, progress, average score, and status. Select rows to **Promote to next round**, **Demote to previous round**, or **Move round**. To put submissions into a round (or take them out) without assigning evaluators, select them in the **Submissions**, **Abstracts** or **Sessions** list and choose **More → Add to evaluation round…** or **Remove from evaluation round…**; the dialog previews what will be added, skipped or moved before you apply. Removing keeps their reviews. A team member whose role has a session filter sees only the submissions it matches, here and in the export. Each row also counts its reviews across every plan; see [which submissions have every review in](#see-which-submissions-have-every-review-in). |
| **Rounds** | The rounds, their dates, and their settings. |
| **Evaluators** | Each evaluator's assignments, progress, and status. Resend invites, remove someone from a round, or **Preview as evaluator in portal**. |
| **Review** | The results. Pick a submission on the left to read its average score, how many rated or abstained, and each evaluator's scorecard answers and comments. **Filters** above the list offers the same filters your evaluators have (for example track, tags or language), with a count per value — values in one filter widen the list, filters together narrow it. **Showing X of Y submissions** and the filter chips show what is applied, **Clear filters** resets it, and the filters stay applied when you open a submission and come back. The filters come from the round's reviewer view settings, or from the plan's other rounds when this round has none. |
| **Builds** | Reviewer session builds, when the plan uses them. |

### See which submissions have every review in

When a submission is reviewed in more than one plan — its own category's plan, plus a co-review round in another category's plan — the **Submissions** tab counts every review it has, in every plan of the event. Under each title, **12 of 17 reviews** says how many are done; a review counts as done when the evaluator has answered every required question or abstained. A green **Reviews complete** badge marks the submissions whose evaluators have all finished, in every plan.

Use the **Reviews** filter above the list to narrow it:

- **All submissions**: everything in the plan (the default).
- **Reviews complete**: only submissions with every review in, across every plan.
- **Still reviewing**: submissions still waiting on at least one evaluator.

![The Submissions tab of an Evaluation Plan 2.0 plan with the Reviews filter open on All submissions, Reviews complete and Still reviewing, and each title showing a count such as 0 of 3 reviews](/images/kb/eval-plan-submissions-reviews-filter.png)

The filter is part of the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view, and **Plan Options → Export** exports what the filter shows. The numbers are the same as the **Reviews complete** and **All reviews in** columns of the **Score Summary Report**. Scouting rounds and public-voting rounds are not counted, nor are AI personas, and a submission moved out of a round no longer counts that round's evaluators.

### Get told when all reviews are in

When the last outstanding review of a submission comes in, in whichever plan it sits, the event's **Admins** and **Admin Lites** get one **All reviews in** notification: *All reviews are in for SESS-214 — title*. Its button opens the plan's **Submissions** tab filtered to **Reviews complete**. It counts reviews the same way as the badge above, so a submission reviewed in its own category's plan and in a co-review round elsewhere is announced once, when the last of them is done, whether that is a score, an abstention, or an admin removing the last evaluator who had not started.

- It is sent once per submission. Editing a finished review sends nothing. If you add an evaluator afterwards, the submission is announced again when that evaluator finishes.
- Scouting rounds, public-voting rounds and AI personas neither hold it back nor trigger it.
- Nothing is sent from changes made while a plan is closed.
- It is on by default and follows each admin's [notification preferences](/communications/notifications): the bell straight away, and the email in the daily summary unless the admin chose an email for each alert. An admin who does not want it turns off **All Reviews In** on the **Preferences** tab.

![The Evaluators tab listing each evaluator with their organization, status, and progress](/images/kb/evaluation-plan-evaluators.png)

![The Review tab with a submission's evaluation summary, description, submission details, and speakers](/images/kb/evaluation-plan-review.png)

![Plan options open on Export, listing Individual grade report, Cumulative grades report and Score summary report, with Score summary report offering Export .XLSX and Export .CSV](/images/kb/eval-plan-export-score-summary-report.png)

The toolbar holds **Open plan** / **Close plan**, **Edit**, and **Plan Options**:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Export reports** | **Individual Grade Report** — every evaluator's answers per submission, with comments and criteria scores. On events with more than one language, a **Review language** column says which language each review was written in, once any review has been tagged. **Cumulative Grades Report** — one row per submission with its aggregated scores and submission details. **Score Summary Report** (Evaluation Plan 2.0) — one row per submission, lowest average first: each reviewer's score (abstentions left out), the average, the standard deviation (Excel STDEV.P), every comment question joined with " \| ", participants by role such as Presenting Author, and the submission's dropdown fields. An abstract later converted into a session keeps its scores, and one merged into a session names the session it went into. Every row also has **Reviews complete** (for example *12 of 17*, across every plan, abstentions counted as done) and **All reviews in** (Yes or No). When some of the plan's submissions are also reviewed in another plan — a co-review round in a second category, say — the report adds **Scores in other plans** (labelled *plan · round*), the other plans' comments, and **Combined Scores (all plans)**, **Combined Average** and **Combined Standard Deviation**; scores only combine when both plans score on the same scale. The plan's own scores, average and deviation are unchanged. |
| **Notify evaluators** | Email all evaluators, or only the newly added ones, that they can log in and grade. |
| **Duplicate plan** | A closed copy with the same rounds (review dates included), evaluators, assignments, scorecards, and filters — no scores. |

<Warning>
What a plan evaluates (parent submissions or subsessions) is fixed when it's created. Evaluators can be added, enrolled in more rounds or removed whether the plan is open or closed; someone added to an open plan can start once they have assignments and the round's review period has begun.
</Warning>

## Manage evaluators

**Program → Evaluation → Evaluators** lists everyone enrolled on the event's Evaluation Plan 2.0 plans, one row per person. Tag them for routing and reporting, and fix their details here.

### Tag many evaluators at once

Create tags on the **Evaluator Tags** tab first. Then:

1. Check the evaluators you want, or check the header box to select the whole page. Search first to narrow the list, for example to everyone from one institution.
2. To go past the current page, click **Select all … evaluators matching this filter** in the bar under the selection. Every evaluator matching your search is selected, not just the rows on screen, and they are added to anyone you had already checked. Your picks stay checked while you search again or change the plan filter — only switching events clears them — and when some of them are hidden by the current search, the bar says **N not in this list** so the count is clear before you act.
3. Click **Edit tags**, choose what happens to the tags they already have, check the tags, and save.

![The selection bar showing 25 evaluators selected with Edit tags, and the option to select all 47 evaluators matching this filter](/images/kb/evaluators-select-all-matching.png)

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Add to existing tags** | Checked tags are added. Tags they already have stay. |
| **Replace existing tags** | The checked tags become the only tags on each selected evaluator. Saving with nothing checked removes every tag from them. |
| **Remove these tags** | Checked tags are removed. Other tags stay. |

![The Edit tags sheet for 47 selected evaluators, with Add to existing tags chosen and two tags checked](/images/kb/evaluators-bulk-edit-tags.png)

Everyone in the list can be tagged, including evaluators who joined through a portal or a round and are not on the event team. If the selection includes someone who is not an evaluator on this event, the rest are still tagged and the message says how many were skipped (**Tags updated for N evaluators. M skipped**). Editing tags and affiliations needs the permission to update evaluation plans.

To tag one person, open the **⋯** menu on their row and choose **Edit tags**.

### Fix an evaluator's name or email

Open the row's **⋯** menu and choose **Edit details**. Change the first name, last name or email and click **Save**. Their rounds, assignments and scores stay as they are, and the new name and email also update their contact record in your organization.

The email can be changed only while the evaluator hasn't taken ownership of the address. Once they've signed in or reviewed, or they belong to another organization, the email field is read-only and says **This person manages their own sign-in email**. You can still fix their name. To move their work to a different address, add that person as a new evaluator.

If the new email already belongs to someone else, Sessionboard doesn't save it. It names that person and offers **Use … instead**:

![The Edit evaluator details dialog saying the email already belongs to Priya Raman, with a Use Priya Raman instead button](/images/kb/evaluator-edit-details-email-in-use.png)

Confirm with **Move to …** and the existing person takes over this evaluator's rounds, assignments and scores on every Evaluation Plan 2.0 plan in the event. The record you were editing no longer evaluates there. Use this when the same reviewer was invited twice under two addresses. If both records are already on the same round or assigned the same submission, the move is blocked and Sessionboard says how many overlap; remove the duplicates from one of them, then try again.

## Evaluation fields

Scorecard questions you create in a plan are saved at the event level, so the same question can be reused on other plans without recreating it. Manage them from **Content → Fields** alongside your session and contact fields.

<Note>
Evaluators see the field names you gave your session questions. Name them the way you'd want a reviewer to read them.
</Note>

## After the review

Scores inform the decision; they don't make it. When the committee has decided, [update each submission's status](/sessions/accept-decline) and [email the speakers](/communications/create-send-emails) — neither happens on its own.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Do ratings and comments automatically get sent to speakers?">

No. Ratings and comments are visible only to event admins with access to plan results. To share feedback, copy the results into an [email](/communications/create-send-emails) to the session's speakers.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can evaluators see other evaluators' comments and ratings?">

Not unless you turn on **Show Scores From Other Evaluators** in the plan's additional settings. With it off (the default), each evaluator sees only their own review and only admins with access to results see everything.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can evaluators communicate with session speakers?">

No. Evaluators have view-only access to the submissions assigned to them. See [what access do evaluators have?](/faq/will-evaluators-have-the-same-access-to-my-event-that-i-do-as-an-admin) for the full comparison with admin access.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How should we structure our plan — one plan, several plans, or rounds?">

One plan per decision. Use **rounds** inside a plan when the same pool of submissions goes through stages (a first pass, then a committee shortlist): **Funnel** carries only promoted submissions forward, **Parallel** lets rounds run side by side. Use separate plans when different submission types are judged by different committees on different criteria. Scope a plan with submission filters (form, track, format, status) so each plan sees only its submissions. See [round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#round-mode-funnel-vs-parallel).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do evaluators see only the submissions and fields that concern them?">

Assignments decide *which* submissions each evaluator reviews; **Reviewer View Configuration** decides *what they see* on each one. Hide fields with **Visible Submission Fields to Reviewers**, choose the dropdowns they can filter by with **Filterable Fields for Reviewers**, and turn on **Enable Anonymized Review** to remove speaker names. Evaluators then work from a filtered list in their review portal. See [reviewer view configuration](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#reviewer-view-configuration).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we add evaluators and assign submissions to them?">

Add them from evaluation, not from the event team. Open **Program → Evaluation → Evaluators** and click **Add Evaluator**, choose the plan and rounds, then pick an **Existing user** or enter a **New evaluator**'s name and email. You can also do it inside the plan, on its **Evaluators** step, with **Add evaluator**. On Sessions 2.0 events the event team invite doesn't offer the Evaluator role, so evaluators can't be invited from there.

To put someone who is already on the plan into another round, use that round's tab on the **Evaluators** step. See [add an evaluator who is already on the plan to another round](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#add-an-evaluator-who-is-already-on-the-plan-to-another-round).

The **Assignments** wizard picks the submissions (all, by filter, or individually), then distributes them **All to All** or to **Individual Reviewers**, with optional caps on **Reviewers per submission** and **Max submissions per evaluator**. Route by track or topic with a submission filter per rule. To keep evaluators tagged for several tracks from collecting reviews from all of them, set a plan-wide cap and run saved assignments in priority order — see [cap each evaluator's workload across the plan](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#cap-each-evaluators-workload-across-the-plan). See [assignments](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#step-4-assignments).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What emails do evaluators receive, and how do we stop them?">

An invitation when you add them (controlled by **Send invite email to new evaluators**, resendable from the Evaluators tab), an email when the plan opens (if you choose **Notify evaluators** when opening it), an email for each new assignment while the plan is open, a weekly reminder every Monday for pending sessions while **Enable Weekly Reminders** is on (until 7 days after the event ends), and a reminder 7 days and 1 day before the deadline to anyone with reviews still pending. The deadline is the plan's due date or a round's **Review closes** date (for rounds with weekly reminders on), and the days are counted in the event's time zone, so a round closing on the 24th reminds on the 23rd there. These due reminders go out even after the event's own dates have passed. Assignments made while the plan is still closed send nothing, so you can stage a committee's workload before launch; the plan-opened email tells evaluators what is waiting for them. Wording for each is in [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails). For anything else, [email your evaluators](/faq/will-evaluators-have-the-same-access-to-my-event-that-i-do-as-an-admin#emailing-your-evaluators) through the Contacts module — add them as contacts first.

Evaluators who review on a Site can turn these off for themselves. On the Site, **Notifications** in the sidebar (also **My Profile → Reviewer Notifications**, and where the **Manage notification preferences** link in their emails lands) lists **Evaluation plan opened**, **Evaluation reminder**, **Evaluation reminder summary**, **Evaluator Due Reminder**, **Evaluator Assigned**, **Session File Uploaded** (a participant uploaded or replaced a file on a submission they review, in a round that shows uploaded files), **Returned for changes** (an admin returned their session build, with the reason) and, for awards reviewers, **Reviewer deadline reminder**; the review invitation itself can't be turned off because it is how they get in. **Email me** chooses between an email for each alert and one daily summary. On an event's Site the choice applies to that event; on an organization-wide Site it applies to every event. A type your organization's policy sets to Off or Required shows as **Set by your organization** and can't be changed — see [notifications](/communications/notifications#set-an-organization-wide-policy).

To stop an email for your evaluators yourself:

- Invitation: turn off **Send invite email to new evaluators** before you add them.
- Plan opened: open the plan without notifying, and send nothing until you choose **Plan Options → Notify evaluators**.
- One email per assignment: assign while the plan is closed; those assignments send nothing. To stop it for every plan, an organization admin sets **Evaluator assigned** to **Off** on the **Organization policy** tab of the organization notification centre.
- Weekly reminder: turn off **Enable Weekly Reminders**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Admins get an email every time a review is submitted. How do we turn that off?">

**Review submitted** and **Evaluator abstained** are admin notification types, not plan settings, so there is no switch on the plan. Both are **off by default**: an admin who wants them turns them on for themselves on the **Preferences** tab of the notification centre — at the event, or at the organization for every event. Anyone who had already switched them on keeps them. If an admin is still receiving them, that is their own choice to undo on the same tab (the **Manage notification preferences** link in the email lands there). To decide for everyone at once, an organization admin opens the **Organization policy** tab on the organization notification centre and sets the type to **Off** or **Required**. See [notifications](/communications/notifications#set-an-organization-wide-policy).

These two alerts go to every **Admin** and **Admin Lite** on the event, not to evaluators. If your evaluators are receiving them, they hold an admin role on the event — check their role on **Event Team** and change it to **Evaluator**. The usual cause is **Add ▸ Copy from…** on the Evaluators tab with the role picker left on Admin User; see the next question.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="We copied last year's evaluators and they came in as admins. What happened?">

**Add ▸ Copy from…** on the Evaluators tab (and **Add From…** on Event Team) brings people over from another of your organization's events. It applies **one** role to everyone in the batch, chosen in the role picker at the bottom of the sheet; the role shown next to each name in the list is the one they held on the *source* event and is only there to help you pick. The picker starts on **Keep each person's role from their source event**, which gives last year's evaluators the Evaluator role again, and opening it from the Evaluators tab preselects **Evaluator**. If you pick Admin User or Admin Lite and anyone in the batch has an email address outside your organization, Sessionboard asks you to confirm before it grants them admin access.

To fix people who already came in with the wrong role, open **Event Team**, find each person and change their role to **Evaluator**. Their assignments and reviews are unaffected.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can reviews be blind, and how are conflicts of interest handled?">

**Enable Anonymized Review** hides submitter and participant identity from evaluators. For conflicts, leave **Allow reviewers to abstain** on so a reviewer can step aside from a submission, and turn on **Require a reason when abstaining** with a fixed list of reasons if you need to report how many abstentions were conflicts. See [abstaining](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#abstaining).

Evaluators have two conflict controls, and they do different jobs:

- **Abstain**, on a submission, sets aside one submission already in their queue.
- **Declare a conflict**, on the round page, names a person (by email) or an organization they can't review impartially. It applies across the event's plans, and the round's **Conflict moderation** setting decides whether matching submissions are flagged or excluded when you assign. Abstaining for a conflict of interest is kept on record there too, and the evaluator can withdraw a declaration from the same dialog.

**Declare a conflict** is always on the evaluator's round page; there's no setting to hide it.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the difference between the plan due date and a round's close date?">

The round's **Review closes** date is the real deadline. It's the deadline evaluators see on **My Reviews**, saving a review stops at that moment, and the round then drops off their list. The plan's **Due Date** is the date your team sees on the plan card. To give evaluators an earlier target than the cutoff, write it in the round's instructions and leave **Review closes** at the date you'll actually stop accepting reviews.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An evaluator sees “No evaluations found”. What's wrong?">

Sessionboard can't find a round for them in this event. Check, in order:

1. **They're on the right Site.** Evaluators review on this event's Program Site. Opening their evaluator link on any other Site shows **This is not the program site for this event**. If the event has no Program Site yet, set one up under **Program → Site**.
2. **They signed in with the email you enrolled.** Open the plan's **Evaluators** tab and confirm they're listed in a round.
3. **The round is still open.** A round whose **Review closes** date has passed disappears from the evaluator's list. A duplicated plan keeps the original plan's round dates, so set new dates when you open the copy.

**No Open Programs** on the Site's home page is about submission forms that are accepting submissions, not about reviews. Evaluators find their work under **My Reviews**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can evaluators use a Site that shows only their reviews?">

No. Evaluators review on the event's Program Site, the same Site your submitters and speakers sign in to, and an organization-wide Site can't host session reviews. After sign-in, the reviews link in the Site's sidebar appears only for evaluators, but the home page and the submissions link show for everyone signed in, evaluators included. There is no Site that shows evaluators their review queue alone. The evaluator emails link straight to the Program Site, and evaluators find their queue under **My Reviews**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do scores turn into accept and decline decisions?">

Read scores on the **Summary** tab and in the plan's **Review** tab, then [update submission statuses](/sessions/accept-decline) from Submissions or the session profile. **Reviewer recommendations** let evaluators mark Accept, Decline, or Alternate alongside scores, and admins can apply those recommendations to statuses in bulk. In a funnel plan, **Promote** moves a submission to the next round; who may promote is a [permission](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members#custom-roles). See [applying recommendations to submission statuses](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#applying-recommendations-to-submission-statuses).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we report on scores, or reuse a plan next year?">

The [Report Builder](/reporting/report-builder) has Evaluation examples and a pre-built **Sessions with Evaluation Ratings** report, and you can group and filter by track, format, or status from there. [Cloning an event](/faq/clone-an-event) copies evaluation plan structure into the new event.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can AI help review submissions?">

Yes, with the [Reviewer agent](/agents/reviewer). Switch it on from the event's **Agents** page, then enroll its personas on a plan round from **Evaluators → Personas**. Each persona answers the round's scorecard, and its scores sit next to your human evaluators' scores. Your committee still makes every decision. If **Agents** isn't in your event menu, ask your Customer Success Manager.

</Accordion>
