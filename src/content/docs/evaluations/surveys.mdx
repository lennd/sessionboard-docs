Surveys collect ratings and comments from attendees **while the event runs and right after it**: attendees scanning a QR code on their way out of a talk, opening a link from your event app, or a committee member scoring sessions from an assigned room. Every response lands on the session, speaker, poster or subsession it is about, and the results become a packet you can print or export.

Surveys are in **Program → Collect & Review → Surveys** on every event. There is nothing to turn on.

## Why use it

Attendee feedback usually lives in a separate survey tool, keyed by session titles that never quite match your agenda, and someone spends a week after the event matching rows to sessions. Surveys asks the questions against your own agenda, so every answer is already tied to the right session and speaker. Attendees answer from one link, a QR code at the room door, or a page inside the event app they already use, and the results are ready per session the moment the session ends.

## Surveys and review plans

Both live under **Program → Collect & Review**, but they answer different questions:

| | Review plans | Surveys |
|---|---|---|
| When | Before the event, reviewing **submissions** | During or after the event, about the **live program** |
| Who | Assigned reviewers signed into the review portal | Attendees with the link, or named people you add |
| About | Submitted sessions | Sessions, speakers, posters or subsessions on the agenda |

If you are deciding what makes the program, you want [review plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans). If attendees are telling you how it went, you want Surveys.

## Create a survey

<Steps>

<Step title="Name it and pick what attendees rate">

Click **New survey**, name it, and choose what each response is about:

- **Sessions**: one card per session.
- **Sessions + speakers**: session comments, then a card for each speaker on that session.
- **Speakers only**: one card per speaker, across every session they appear on.
- **Posters**: one card per poster (sessions with attached poster files).
- **Sessions + subsessions**: parent session comments, then a card for each subsession.

For the nested modes you also pick a **Headline rating** recipe: the session's own rating only, an equal blend of session and speaker or subsession scores, or a weighted blend you control.

Identity fields decide what attendees are asked before they start. Leave them all optional for anonymous feedback; make any of them required to collect names.

</Step>

<Step title="Build the questions">

Add questions from the builder. Scored types roll up into each card's score; the rest collect context:

| Type | Scored | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 / 1–5 / 1–10 scale | Yes | Quick tap-to-rate scoring |
| Numeric | Yes | Custom min/max ranges |
| Dropdown | Yes | Custom options, each with its own point value |
| Checkboxes | No | "Pick all that apply": topics covered, formats used |
| Yes / No | No | One-tap questions, such as "Was commercial bias present?" |
| Free text | No | The comments speakers and committees actually read |
| File upload | No | Photos or documents |
| Separator | n/a | Section headings inside the form |

Scored questions carry a **weight** (in percentage mode) so a headline question can count more than a nice-to-have. In nested modes there are two question lists: one about the session, one about each speaker or subsession.

You can also **reuse questions from another survey**: pick the source survey and its questions are appended as an independent copy.

</Step>

<Step title="Show a question only when it applies (optional)">

Click the branch icon on any question to show it only when an earlier answer matches. For example, ask **"Describe the bias"** only when **"Was commercial bias present?"** is **Yes**.

Conditions key off a **dropdown, checkboxes or yes/no** question. A required question hidden by its condition never blocks anyone from finishing; it only becomes required when it appears.

</Step>

<Step title="Add people (optional)">

Most attendee surveys skip this step: the survey link and QR codes work without it. For committee or floor-review work, add named people one at a time or with **Import people** (CSV). Each person gets their own link tied to their name.

A person can also be given a **station**: a room plus an AM/PM slot and a role (floor reviewer, moderator or attendee). They open their link and see exactly the sessions running in their room during their slot, and the list follows late room changes.

</Step>

<Step title="Pick the agenda">

Choose which sessions the survey covers, or **sync from the agenda** to pull everything in. Speaker, poster and subsession cards are made from the sessions you pick.

</Step>

<Step title="Open it">

A new survey is a **Draft**. Set its status to **Open** in the Basics step, or click **Open survey now** on the Share step. Until then, anyone who opens a link sees "This survey isn't open yet". Add an opening and closing time if you want the links to start and stop on their own.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Share a survey

The **Share** step has everything you need to put the survey in front of attendees.

### Survey link and QR code

The **Survey link** opens the whole survey. Attendees see every session on it, grouped by day, with search, a room filter and a choice of sorting by time or room, and they pick the sessions they attended. Copy it into emails, slides or your event app.

The QR code under it opens the same link. Click **Download QR code (PNG)** for a print-ready file for signage, badges or the last slide of each talk.

To show the survey in another language, add `?language=fr`, `?language=de` or `?language=es` to the link.

### Embed in your event app or website

**Embed in your app or website** gives you a snippet to paste wherever your event app or site accepts HTML: a custom page, a web view or an iframe block. The survey fits the space it is given and grows with its content, so attendees don't scroll inside a small box.

If the app only accepts a web address (many mobile event apps have a "custom link" or "web page" field rather than HTML), use **Copy embed link** instead. It opens the same survey without the full-page frame, so it looks at home inside the app.

<Tip>
In mobile event apps such as Cvent, Swapcard or Grip, add the survey as a custom link or web-view page in the app menu, and put each session's own link (below) on that session's page. Attendees then rate a session from the session itself.
</Tip>

### Per-session links

Every session on the survey has its own link that opens the survey for **that session only**. Use it:

- on the session's page in your event app, so "Rate this session" goes straight to the right questions;
- as a QR code printed at the room door or on the last slide (**Download QR code** next to each session);
- in a follow-up email to that session's attendees.

**Download all links (CSV)** gives you a spreadsheet with every session's ID, title, room and start time, its link, its embed link, and the survey link with the session filled in. Import it into your event app, or use it for a mail merge.

Some tools fill in a session ID for you when you build a link. Use the survey link with `?session=` and the Sessionboard session ID, for example `https://…/surveys/<token>?session=<session id>`, and the survey opens on that session.

### Named links

People you added in the **People** step each have their own link, tied to their name and, if set, their room and time slot.

## What attendees see

The survey works on a phone and on a desktop:

1. If you asked for names or an email, attendees fill them in first.
2. On the survey link they see their list of sessions, with a check mark on each one they have finished. On a session link they go straight to that session's questions.
3. **Save draft** keeps partial answers; **Mark done** submits. If a required question is missing, the page says which one.
4. After a session link they see **Thank you!**, with a way to change their answers. On the survey link the next unfinished session opens, and once every session is done they are thanked at the top of the list.

Answers resume on the same device if they come back later. If a link can't be opened, attendees see why instead of an error: the survey **isn't open yet** (with the opening time, when you set one), it **has closed**, or the **link isn't valid**.

## Results

The **Results** step updates as responses arrive:

- **Scores**: every session with its response count, its **Own score** (mean), and in nested modes the speaker or subsession mean plus the headline blend you chose.
- **By question**: each scored question's mean and response count; dropdown, checkbox and yes/no questions show how often each option was picked.
- **Share the results, no login needed**: comments grouped by session, then by speaker, with the respondent's name, role and room on each line. **Print packet** turns it into a PDF hand-out.
- **Export CSV**: one row per response, with every answer in its own column.

<Tip>
Scores are a percentage of the maximum by default. If your committee thinks in raw points, switch the survey to **Points** scoring in the Basics step: weights are hidden and answers are summed as they are.
</Tip>

### Feedback from another survey tool

If you ran your survey in another tool, import its file under **Participant portal → Feedback → Import**. The imported ratings, comments, audience questions and polls show on each session's **Survey feedback** tab and in Reports. See [Participant Feedback](/portals/speaker-feedback#see-imported-feedback-in-the-admin).

### Sharing results with speakers

Speakers can see the feedback on their own sessions in the speaker portal with [Participant Feedback](/portals/speaker-feedback), which you control: when it is released, which sessions, and the minimum number of ratings before anything shows.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Do I need to turn Surveys on or request access?">

No. Surveys is on for every event, under **Program → Collect & Review → Surveys**. You need permission to view review plans to see it.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I put the survey inside our mobile event app?">

Yes. Paste the embed code where the app accepts HTML, or use the embed link where it accepts a web address. For session-level feedback, put each session's own link on that session's page in the app; **Download all links (CSV)** has them all.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do checkboxes and yes/no questions affect the score?">

No. Scores come only from scales, numeric questions and dropdowns with point values. Checkbox and yes/no answers are reported as option counts in the results.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can the same person rate more than one session?">

Yes, that is the default. The survey link and named links show the whole list (or the stationed room's list), and each session is rated on its own card.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do anonymous surveys work?">

Leave every identity field optional in the Basics step. Attendees go straight to the session list, and their rows show as **Anonymous** in the results. Make fields required when you need to know who said what.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An attendee says the link shows &quot;This survey isn't open yet&quot;.">

The survey is still a draft, or its opening time hasn't come. Open it in the Basics step or with **Open survey now** on the Share step.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
