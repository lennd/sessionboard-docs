Most reviewer tickets come down to four things: the reviewer can't sign in, they sign in and see nothing, their email changed, or you need to remove reviews. This page covers each. Reviews were called evaluations until October 2026, and some screens and emails still say so.

## The reviewer didn't get a sign-in link

1. **Check the address.** On the plan's **Reviewers** tab, find the reviewer. The email you enrolled is the one they must use. A typo here is the usual cause. Open the row's **⋯** menu, choose **Edit details**, and correct it ([how email changes work](#the-reviewer-needs-a-different-email-address)).
2. **Check spam and junk.** Ask them to search for the sender and the event name.
3. **Resend.** Use the resend-invite option in the reviewer's **⋯** menu, or have the reviewer return to the Program Site sign-in page and ask for a new sign-in link.
4. **Check you invited them.** If you imported reviewers with **Send Invite Email** off, or left invitations off while you set up, nothing was sent. Invitations can be resent from **Reviewers** when you're ready.
5. **See what they see.** From the **⋯** menu, choose **Preview as reviewer in portal**. It opens the Program Site signed in as that reviewer for two hours. You need permission to view the portal as other users.

See [How reviewers sign in](/evaluations/review-plan-reviewers#how-reviewers-sign-in) for the Program Site requirement, and use the link in each reviewer's own email rather than the **Evaluator Login Link** merge tag, which opens the general sign-in page.

## They signed in but My Reviews is empty

The two messages mean different things.

| What the reviewer sees | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This is not the program site for this event** | They opened a Site that isn't the event's Program Site. | Send them the Program Site address, or use the link in their invitation. |
| **No reviews found** | Nothing is open and assigned to them yet. | Check the plan is open, the round is active, and the reviewer has assignments. |

For **No reviews found**, work through this list:

- **Is the plan open?** A closed plan shows nothing. Assignments made to a closed plan send no email, and reviewers hear about the whole batch when it opens.
- **Is the reviewer on the right round?** Reviewers only see rounds they belong to.
- **Do they have assignments?** Open the reviewer's **⋯** menu and choose **View Assignments**. If it's empty, assign submissions on the plan's [Assignments step](/evaluations/review-plan-assignments).
- **Are they signed in as someone else?** A reviewer with two email addresses may have signed in with one you didn't enroll. **Preview as reviewer in portal** shows what the enrolled address sees.

## The reviewer needs a different email address

Open the reviewer's **⋯** menu and choose **Edit details**.

- **Before they have signed in:** change the email and click **Save**. Their rounds, assignments and scores stay as they are.
- **After they have signed in or reviewed:** the email is the address they sign in with, so it can't be edited. The dialog shows **Signs in as** with their current address. To move their reviewer role to a new address, choose **Review the move**; saved scores move with the role. If the new address already reviews the same rounds or submissions, the move is blocked so two people's scores don't mix. Remove one of them from the shared rounds first.

The full steps are in [Fix a reviewer's name or email](/evaluations/evaluation-plans#fix-a-reviewers-name-or-email).

## One review form for every category

A scorecard belongs to a **round**, so every submission in a round is scored on the same questions. To score categories differently:

- **Use one plan per category.** Give each plan its own scorecard and limit it to that category with **Plan scope** (a rule on the track, format or other field, then **Save scope**). One plan per decision is how Sessionboard expects you to organize reviews.
- **Or keep one form and use question weights.** Every scoring question has a **Weight**, so a question that matters less for one category can count for less. See [the scorecard](/evaluations/review-plan-scorecard#scoring-questions).

Questions that don't apply to some submissions can be left optional so reviewers aren't forced to score them. See [Optional questions left unscored](/evaluations/review-plan-scorecard#optional-questions-left-unscored).

## Remove reviews

There is no single delete button for one review. These are the removals the product offers, and each is permanent for the scores involved:

| To remove | Do this | What happens to reviews |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everything one reviewer did | **Remove Reviewer** (one program) or **Remove from all plans** (every plan on the event), from their **⋯** menu | Their reviews for the rounds they're part of are removed (in this program, or on every plan). |
| A whole round | Open the round, expand **Dangerous**, click **Delete round** | The round's reviews and assignments drop out of reports. You can restore it from **Event History → Trash**. |
| A reviewer's assignments only | See [Replace not-yet-reviewed](/evaluations/review-plan-assignments#review-and-apply) | Assignments with no review activity are removed. Started and abstained reviews stay. |

Reviewers can change a review they submitted while the round is open, so for a mistaken score the simplest fix is to ask them to edit it. If you need one saved review reset and none of the above fits, contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com).

<Warning>
**Remove Reviewer** and **Remove from all plans** remove that person's scores from the results. Export the review summary first if you may need them.
</Warning>
