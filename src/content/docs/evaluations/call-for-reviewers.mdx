A **Call for Reviewers** is how you recruit evaluators for Sessions 2.0. You create the call on the event, choose which expertise lists applicants pick from, and enroll accepted people into the evaluation-plan rounds you name. The same call can invite a named list, import people you already know as reviewers, and take public applications.

The tab sits next to **Reviewers** and **Evaluator Tags** on **Program → Evaluation**. It appears when Sessions 2.0 is on for the event, or when the event already has a 2.0 evaluation plan.

The **Training · Feature spotlight** on this tab walks these steps.

<Gallery />

## Why use it

Today a reviewer is someone you already have an email for. Societies also need a public call, an accept step, and an expertise questionnaire that assignment can use. A call collects that once, flags co-author and affiliation conflicts before you assign work, and writes the picks onto the same reviewer-tag rows the assignment wizard already filters on.

## Create a call

![The Call for Reviewers card with the open call selected, Invite people, Import reviewers, Settings, and people counts](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-sessions-tab.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Open **Program → Evaluation**, then **Call for Reviewers**. If you have not created one yet, the empty state offers **Create call**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set the window">
    **Call name** is required (up to 255 characters), for example "2027 Program Committee reviewers". **Opens** and **Closes** are optional. Dates use the event timezone shown under the fields. An explicit **Close call** later overrides the dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how people join">
    Click **Settings**. Under **Intake**:

    - **Require approval** — people who accept or apply wait as **Pending approval** until you approve them. Turn this off to enroll them automatically when they finish.
    - **Interest form** — publish a public application link so people can apply without an invitation.
    - **Require sign-in** — applicants must sign in before they can respond. Turn this off to allow anonymous apply.

    ![Call settings with Require approval, Interest form, and Require sign-in](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-settings.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick expertise lists and enrollment">
    Under **Expertise applicants can select**, turn on the catalogs this call should offer. Sessions can offer **Tracks**, **Tags**, **Topics**, **Reviewer tags**, and any custom session field of the **Dropdown** type. Each dropdown field appears under its own name, and applicants pick from its options. Other field types (text, number, date, and so on) and contact fields are not offered, because there is no list of options to match on. Awards catalogs do not appear here.

    On the public form, sub-topics are listed under their parent topic. Picking a sub-topic is a narrower pick than picking the parent.

    Under **Enroll accepted reviewers into**, pick the evaluation-plan rounds they should join. Create a plan with rounds first if the list is empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the portal copy">
    **Title**, **Introduction**, **Success message**, and **Closed message** appear on the public call page. Leave a field empty to use the default wording.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can run more than one call per event. Use the **Call** selector at the top of the tab to switch.

## Invite people

**Invite people** is for a named list you do not want on the roster until they accept.

![Invite people, Import reviewers, Settings, Close call, and Delete call](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-toolbar.png)

1. Click **Invite people**.
2. **Paste a list** — one person per line, or separate addresses with commas or semicolons. Accepted formats: a bare email, `Name <email>`, or `email, First, Last, Affiliation`.
3. Or **Upload CSV** with a header row. Recognized columns: email, first name, last name (or a single name column), and affiliation or organization.
4. Leave **Send invitation emails now** on, or turn it off and **Resend invitation** later from the people list.
5. Click **Send invitations**.

![The Invite people dialog with a pasted list and Send invitation emails now turned on](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-invite-dialog.png)

Each person lands as **Invited**. They are not on the evaluation-plan roster yet. A repeated email is reported as skipped and does not send a second invite. Up to 2000 people per upload.

The invitation email uses a seven-day magic link to the event's Program Site. The event needs an organization-level Site for that link to be minted — if the **Public link** card says there is no portal site yet, set one up first.

<Note>
**Invite people** and **Import reviewers** are turned off while the call is closed, and the toolbar says why:

- **"This call is closed. Reopen it to invite or import people."** — you clicked **Close call**. Click **Reopen call**.
- **"This call is past its closing date. Move the closing date later in Settings to invite or import people."** — the **Closes** date has passed. Open **Settings** and move **Closes** later, or clear it.
</Note>

## Import reviewers

**Import reviewers** is for people you already know as reviewers — last year's committee, a society list. They are enrolled immediately, then asked to finish onboarding.

1. Click **Import reviewers**.
2. Paste or upload the same formats as invite.
3. Leave **Send onboarding emails now** on, or turn it off.
4. Click **Add reviewers**.

Each person lands as **Onboarding**. They appear on the **Reviewers** tab with an **Onboarding incomplete** badge and can still be selected when you assign. Re-importing the same email does not create a second roster row. They get the call's onboarding email, not the standard "you have reviews" invitation.

## Publish the interest form

Turn **Interest form** on in **Settings**. The **Public link** card shows the application URL. Anyone with the link can apply. With **Require approval** on they wait as **Pending approval**; with it off they enroll as soon as they complete the form.

The form collects name, email, the expertise catalogs you turned on, **Organization** when the Site asks reviewers for affiliation, and an optional conflict note. Submitting twice with the same email updates the pending application instead of creating another.

![The public application form with About you fields and Your expertise](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-portal-apply.png)

A closed call rejects new submissions and shows **Applications closed**. You can still approve someone who applied before it closed.

<Note>
The public form is not a Call for Papers and does not create a session. It only creates a person on this call.
</Note>

## The people list

The table lists **Name**, **Email**, **Source** (Invited, Imported, or Applied), **Status**, **Affiliation**, **Expertise**, **Conflicts**, and **Added**. Search by name or email. Filter by status or source.

![The people table with Invited, Applied, and Imported rows and their statuses](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-people-table.png)

Row actions, when you can manage the call:

- **Resend invitation** — issues a new magic link to that person only.
- **Approve** — enrolls a pending applicant and keeps the expertise they submitted. Also shown on someone *you* declined, so you can change your mind.
- **Decline** — leaves the roster unchanged. Optionally send a decline email.
- **Remove** — takes them off the call. Reviewers already enrolled in rounds keep their assignments.

**Approving someone who declined.** If an organizer declined the person, **Approve** is still in the row menu and enrolls them with the expertise they picked. If the person declined the invitation themselves, they cannot be approved — they said no, so enrolling them would put them on rounds they turned down. The menu says "Declined their own invitation, so they can't be approved. To invite them again, remove them first." **Remove** them, then **Invite people** again so they get a fresh invitation they can accept.

**Conflicts** uses the same detector the rounds use. A flag such as a co-author on sessions in this event, a shared organization, or a declared note is informational — approving or enrolling is never blocked. Round-time conflict rules still enforce flag vs exclude when you assign.

Statuses you will see: **Invited**, **Accepted**, **Pending approval**, **Approved**, **Declined**, **Onboarding**.

## Close, reopen, or delete

**Close call** stops new applications and acceptances, and turns off **Invite people** and **Import reviewers**. You can still approve, decline, and remove people already on the call. **Reopen call** turns intake back on. Once the **Closes** date has passed, **Reopen call** is not offered — move the date later in **Settings** instead. **Delete call** removes the call and everyone on it. Reviewers already enrolled in rounds keep their assignments. That cannot be undone.

## Match expertise when assigning

Expertise collected on the call is what assignment uses.

On a plan's **Assignments** step, turn on **Match expertise**. An evaluator is paired with a session only when, on every list the call asks about and the session has values for, at least one of their picks matches. A session with no track, for example, is not judged on tracks. An evaluator who skipped a list the session has values for is not paired with it. Picking a topic also covers its sub-topics. Evaluators with no picks are not assigned by this rule. **All to All** and hand-picked assignment do not start requiring expertise. Existing conflict-of-interest exclusion still drops pairs after matching.

![Match expertise on the Assignments step](/images/kb/call-for-reviewers-match-expertise.png)

See [Setting up round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#stage-2-how-should-they-be-distributed).

## What the reviewer sees

Invited and imported people open a magic link, sign in, and land on a **Reviewer invitation** or **Finish setting up your reviewer profile** page. They confirm their name, enter **Organization** when the Site collects it, pick expertise from the catalogs you turned on, and can leave a conflict note. **Accept invitation** or **Finish setup** enrolls them when approval is off, or leaves them pending when it is on.

Applicants on the public form see **Call for reviewers**. After submit they see **Thank you** — either "We've received your application" (pending) or "You're in" (auto-enrolled). A closed or upcoming call says so with the date when you set one.
