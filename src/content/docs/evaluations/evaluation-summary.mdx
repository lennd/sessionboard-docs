The **Summary** tab is the dashboard for everything reviewers have done in your event. Use it to see how far evaluation has progressed, which submissions are scoring best, where reviewers disagree, and whether anyone has stalled — without opening each plan.

## Open the Summary

Go to **Program → Evaluation**. **Summary** is the first tab.

![The Summary tab for all plans with four KPI cards, Reviewer activity, Completion Status, Average Submission Score By Plan, Top 10 and Thought Provoking Submissions](/images/kb/evaluation-summary.png)

The **Plan** picker at the top defaults to **All plans**. Choose one plan to scope every number to it; the page then adds a **Plan Summary** tab and one tab per round, so you can look at a single round on its own.

![The Summary scoped to one plan, with Plan Summary and Round 1 tabs under the Plan picker](/images/kb/evaluation-summary-plan.png)

<Tip>
Click a submission in any list to open it in a new tab.
</Tip>

## What each panel shows

| Panel | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Total Evaluations** | Reviews submitted across the plans in scope. |
| **Evaluated Submissions** | Submissions with at least one submitted review. |
| **Evaluation Plans** | Plans configured for the event (or 1 when scoped to a plan). |
| **Evaluators** | Distinct people assigned to review across the plans in scope. |
| **Reviewer activity** | Two ledgers — **Assignments** (per submission: Started, In progress, Complete) and **Evaluators** (per reviewer: Started, In progress, Complete). An abstained assignment counts as started and complete, never as in progress. |
| **Completion Status** | Evaluations submitted as a share of the total required across the plans in scope. |
| **Average Submission Score By Plan** | Average reviewer score per plan, across all completed reviews in that plan. |
| **Highest Score** / **Lowest Score** | The single best- and worst-scoring submission by average reviewer score. Submissions with no scored review are excluded from Lowest. |
| **Top 10 Submissions** | Highest average reviewer scores. A submission needs at least one completed, scored review to appear. |
| **Thought Provoking Submissions** | Where reviewers disagreed most, ranked by the spread between the highest and lowest individual ratings. The headline is the average; **Range** shows the lowest–highest individual rating. |

## Reading it during a review cycle

- **Reviewer activity → Evaluators → In progress** is how many reviewers have started but not finished. The **Evaluators** tab lists them by name.
- Scope the picker to a plan and open a **Round** tab to check one round's completion before you open the next.
- **Thought Provoking Submissions** are the ones worth a committee conversation — a high average with a wide range means at least one reviewer saw something the others did not.
