## Why use it

A reviewer only sees the submissions assigned to them. The **Assignments** step turns "the oncology committee reads the oncology abstracts, three reviewers each, nobody more than fifteen" into the exact list of who reviews what, and shows you that list before anything is saved.

You build an assignment in three parts, top to bottom:

1. [**Which submissions?**](#which-submissions): the submissions this assignment hands out.
2. [**How should they be distributed?**](#how-submissions-are-distributed): which reviewers take part, and how much each one gets.
3. [**Review and apply**](#review-and-apply): check the preview, choose what happens to assignments already in the round, and click **Assign**.

Rarely needed settings (matching on reviewer expertise, a limit across the whole plan, and saved assignments that run in order) are under [**Advanced settings**](#advanced-settings), collapsed until you need them.

Every setting has an **ⓘ** beside it. Hover or click it for a short explanation with an example, and **Learn more** opens the matching section of this article.

![The help card for Reviewers per submission, opened from its ⓘ, with an example and a Learn more link](/images/kb/review-assignments-help-popover.png)

![The Assignments step of a review plan with the round banner, Current state counts, the Which submissions? choices, and the Impact preview on the right](/images/kb/review-assignments-overview.png)

## Before you start

- **Assignments are per round.** The banner at the top right, **Configuring assignments for**, names the round. When a plan has several rounds, tabs above the form switch between them. Public voting rounds are not listed, because they have no reviewers; choose what voters see on the [Ballot step](/evaluations/review-plan-ballot).
- **Only enrolled reviewers can be assigned.** A reviewer must be on the plan and enrolled in this round on the [Reviewers step](/evaluations/review-plan-reviewers).
- **Assigning is a one-off action.** Clicking **Assign** creates the reviews right away. It does not run again when new submissions arrive. Run it again, or use an [automation](/automations/overview) to assign new submissions as they come in.
- **Conflicts of interest follow the plan's [Conflict moderation](/evaluations/review-plan-overview#conflict-moderation) setting.** Conflicted pairs are either flagged in the preview and still assigned, or kept out of the assignment. When a pair is excluded, the submission gets the next available reviewer, and the preview warns you if that leaves it short.

The **Current state** strip shows the round as it is right now:

| Count | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Submissions in round** | Submissions currently in this round. In a funnel plan, later rounds only contain what was promoted into them. |

### Plan scope

A plan copied with **Add plan ▸ Copy from…** keeps its source plan's scope: it covers only the submissions that match the source's filters. The **Plan scope** card above the form shows those filters. Change a rule and click **Save scope**, or click **Remove scope** to cover every submission. Everything on this step chooses from that set.

Rules the copy could not keep are listed under **Not carried over from**, with the reason: the field was deleted, it belongs to another event, or a track or tag has no match of the same name here. Add them again if you still need them, then click **Dismiss**.

## Which submissions?

Choose the submissions this assignment hands out. Build one assignment per group that needs different reviewers, for example one per track, and run them one after another.

![The three choices under Which submissions?, All submissions in plan scope, By submission filters and Individual submissions](/images/kb/review-assignments-which-submissions.png)

### All submissions in plan scope

Every submission the plan covers.

- **Use it when** one committee reads everything, or with [Reviewers per submission](#reviewers-per-submission) to spread a whole program evenly across all reviewers.
- **Watch out:** without a plan scope this includes submissions from every submission form. To take one form's submissions, use [By submission filters](#by-submission-filters) with a **Source** filter.

### By submission filters

{/* training-clip:start 75-review-plan-assignments#by-submission-filters */}
<TrainingVideo id="training-75-review-plan-assignments-by-submission-filters" compact title={"By submission filters"} src="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/75-review-plan-assignments.mp4" poster="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/poster.jpg" captions="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/75-review-plan-assignments.vtt" start={81.2} end={101.9} />
{/* training-clip:end */}

Only submissions whose fields match the filters you add: standard fields (status, format, track, tags, language, level, source) and your custom submission fields. Add up to six filters.

- **Use it when** different groups of submissions need different reviewers, for example **Track is Oncology** to the oncology committee and **Format is Poster** to the poster judges.
- **Statuses:** a filter on one of the five standard statuses also matches the custom statuses under it. **Status is Pending** includes submissions in **In Review**, for example. A filter on a custom status matches only that status.
- **Example:** a summit with four tracks runs four assignments, one per track, each with **Track is …** and that track's reviewers under [Individual reviewers](#individual-reviewers).

![By submission filters selected, with a filter reading Track, Is, Research & Best Practices and the Add filter link](/images/kb/review-assignments-filters.png)

### Individual submissions

Pick submissions one by one. Search by title or ID (for example `SESS-123`, or `SESS-123-2` for a sub-session), tick them, and page through the list. Your picks are kept when you search again, so you can build a set across several searches. **Select all … in plan scope** ticks every submission at once.

- **Use it when** a few late submissions need reviewers, a submission needs a second opinion, or one needs a particular expert.

Choose which reviewers take part, then optionally limit how much each one gets.

![How should they be distributed? with All to All selected, Individual Reviewer below it, and the Workload constraints box](/images/kb/review-assignments-distribution.png)

### All to All

Every reviewer enrolled in this round can be given the submissions above.

- With no workload limits, **every reviewer gets every submission**. That suits a small committee that reads everything.
- With [Reviewers per submission](#reviewers-per-submission) on, the submissions are shared out across all of them instead.

### Individual reviewers

Only the reviewers you tick. Search by name or email, or filter the list by reviewer tag. Only reviewers enrolled in the current round are listed; the note above the list says how many that is out of everyone on the plan. AI reviewer personas are marked **Persona**.

- **Use it when** a track goes to its own subcommittee, or to bring in a specialist for a handful of submissions.
- **Example:** tag your oncology reviewers **Oncology** on the Reviewers step, then filter by that tag here and tick them all.

The bar above the list works on the reviewers the search and tag filter show: **Select all *N* shown**, **Invert selection** (flips their ticks) and **Clear** (unticks them). Ticks on reviewers the filter hides stay as they are, so you can tick one tag's reviewers, switch to another tag and add those too; the count says how many selected reviewers are hidden by filters.

#### Remove reviewers from these submissions

**Bulk action** next to the list switches between **Assign reviewers** and **Remove reviewers**. Remove takes the ticked reviewers off the submissions chosen under [Which submissions?](#which-submissions), in the round selected at the top, without assigning anything:

1. Choose the submissions, then **Individual reviewers**, and tick the reviewers to take off. A tag filter is a quick way to pick a whole group.
2. Set **Bulk action** to **Remove reviewers**. In place of the impact preview, the **Removal preview** counts the **Open assignments to remove**, the **Submissions affected**, and the **Completed reviews kept** and **Reviews in progress kept**.
3. Click **Remove *N* reviewers** and confirm.

Only assignments nobody has started are removed. Completed reviews, reviews in progress and declines stay with the reviewer, and the reviewers stay on the plan; to take people off the plan itself, see [remove reviewers from a plan in bulk](/evaluations/evaluation-plans#remove-reviewers-from-a-plan-in-bulk).

### Workload limits for this assignment

{/* training-clip:start 75-review-plan-assignments#workload-limits-for-this-assignment */}
<TrainingVideo id="training-75-review-plan-assignments-workload-limits-for-this-assignment" compact title={"Workload limits"} src="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/75-review-plan-assignments.mp4" poster="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/poster.jpg" captions="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/75-review-plan-assignments.vtt" start={118.9} end={143.7} />
{/* training-clip:end */}

**Workload constraints (optional)** holds two limits. They apply to **this assignment only**. With both off, every chosen reviewer gets every chosen submission. To limit a reviewer across every assignment and round, use the [plan-wide limit](#max-submissions-per-reviewer-whole-plan).

When a limit is on, a line under the boxes spells out what the rule will do, and the preview warns you if the limits can't all be met.

![Workload constraints with Reviewers per submission set to 3 and Max submissions per reviewer set to 5, and the line summarising about 30 assignment pairs](/images/kb/review-assignments-workload.png)

### Reviewers per submission

How many reviewers each submission gets. It can't be more than the reviewers you are drawing from.

- **How it shares out the work:** Sessionboard rotates through the reviewers, so in one run nobody gets more than one review more than anyone else.
- **Topping up:** with **Add to existing**, reviewers a submission already has count toward the number. Running the same rule again fills in submissions that are short and leaves full ones alone.
- **Use it when** every submission must be read by the same number of people, which is the most common setup.
- **Example:** 40 submissions, 10 reviewers and **3** per submission make 120 reviews, 12 for each reviewer.

### Max submissions per reviewer

The most submissions one reviewer gets from this assignment.

- **On its own,** each submission gets **one** reviewer, shared out evenly, and nobody gets more than the number.
- **With Reviewers per submission,** it caps that. If the cap is too low for every submission to get its reviewers, some submissions get fewer, and the preview says how many before you assign.
- **Example:** 3 reviewers each for 40 submissions is 120 reviews. With 10 reviewers that needs a cap of at least 12; a cap of 10 leaves 20 reviews unassigned.

<Tip>
This limit counts this run only. Three runs with a limit of 20 can give someone 60. To stop that, set the [plan-wide limit](#max-submissions-per-reviewer-whole-plan).
</Tip>

### When applying

{/* training-clip:end */}

What happens to assignments that already exist in this round:

| Option | What it does | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add to existing** | Adds new pairs only and never removes anything. Safe to run again. | Almost always: first assignment, topping up, adding late submissions. |
| **Replace not-yet-reviewed** | This rule becomes the round. Removes every assignment in the round that doesn't match the rule and has no review activity, including assignments on submissions that no longer match the filter. Started and abstained reviews stay. | A submission's track or category was corrected and the wrong reviewers still have it. |
| **Replace all (including reviewed)** | Same, but also removes reviews already started. | Starting the round over. Rarely right once reviewing has begun. |

With either **Replace** option, a submission that no longer matches and is left with no reviewers is removed from the round, so **Submissions in round** drops to what the rule selects. In a funnel plan, Round 2 and later only remove assignments; submissions promoted into the round stay in it.

Before a **Replace** runs, a confirmation lists **New assignments to create**, **Existing assignments to remove**, **Submissions removed from the round** and what is left unchanged. Scores already entered are never deleted: if the same reviewer is assigned the same submission again later, their earlier answers reappear.

![The Assign 22 reviews button above When applying, with Replace not-yet-reviewed selected and the line Will add 22, remove 4, keep 8 unchanged](/images/kb/review-assignments-when-applying.png)

<Warning>
The **Replace** options act on the whole round, not only on the submissions this rule selects. Read the counts in the confirmation before you confirm.
</Warning>

### Assign

The **Assign** button counts reviews, one per reviewer per submission. **Assign 15 reviews** with "5 submissions · 3 reviewers" underneath means each of 5 submissions goes to 3 reviewers. A confirmation appears once the reviews are created, with **Assign more submissions** to start the next assignment.

To leave without creating anything, click **Close without assigning**. If the form would create reviews, Sessionboard asks first, **You haven't assigned these submissions yet**, with **Assign now**, **Leave without assigning** and **Cancel**. The same question appears when you change step or round. Leaving through the main sidebar or the browser's **Back** button does not ask.

### Match expertise

{/* training-clip:start 75-review-plan-assignments#match-expertise */}
<TrainingVideo id="training-75-review-plan-assignments-match-expertise" compact title={"Match expertise"} src="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/75-review-plan-assignments.mp4" poster="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/poster.jpg" captions="https://static.sessionboard.com/training/videos/75-review-plan-assignments/v2026-11-03-3b9c5cc5/75-review-plan-assignments.vtt" start={227.2} end={242.4} />

Pairs a reviewer only with submissions whose track, tags, topics or custom fields overlap what that reviewer picked when they answered your [Call for Reviewers](/evaluations/call-for-reviewers).

- **Reviewers who picked nothing get nothing** from this assignment.
- **Use it when** reviewers chose their own areas of expertise and you want each submission read by people who know the field.
- **Combine it** with [Reviewers per submission](#reviewers-per-submission): a reviewer whose expertise doesn't match is replaced by the next matching one. If a submission matches nobody, the preview says how many submissions would get no reviewer.
- It applies only to the assignment you're building. **All to All** and **Individual Reviewer** assignments without it are unaffected.

### Max submissions per reviewer (whole plan)

Under **Reviewer workload across the plan**, one limit for every assignment and round in the plan. Enter a number and click **Save cap**; leave it blank for no limit.

- **What counts:** completed and in-progress reviews, in every assignment and round. Declined and recused reviews don't.
- **Who gets the work:** with a plan-wide limit, new reviews go to the reviewers with the fewest reviews across the whole plan first.
- **Lowering it removes nothing.** Reviewers already over it are listed as over the cap and get no new assignments. Unassign by hand to rebalance.
- **It wins over the assignment limit.** If an assignment's own [Max submissions per reviewer](#max-submissions-per-reviewer) is higher, the plan limit applies.
- It also limits saved assignments and **Assign reviewers** automations.
- **Use it when** you run one assignment per track and the same reviewers are in several tracks, so per-assignment limits can't stop them collecting work from every track.
- **Example:** with **12**, no reviewer gets a 13th submission anywhere in the plan. The line beside **Save cap** shows the spread today, for example "18 reviewers with reviews · fewest 4 · median 9 · most 15".

### Saved assignments, in run order

Save the assignment you have set up, give it a name, and run all saved assignments together, top to bottom.

<Steps>
<Step title="Save each assignment">
Set up an assignment in the form, enter a name such as the track it covers, and click **Save current setup as an assignment**. Repeat for each track.
</Step>
<Step title="Put them in order">
Use the arrows under **Saved assignments, in run order**. With a plan-wide limit, earlier assignments use reviewer capacity first, so put the track with the fewest qualified reviewers, or the one that matters most, at the top.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview">
Click **Preview run all**. Nothing is saved. You see the reviews each assignment would add, each reviewer's total **Now** and **After run** (anyone **Over cap** is flagged), assignments that need more reviews than the reviewers have room for, the submissions that would end up short, and reviewers left out because they are no longer on the round.
</Step>
<Step title="Run">
Click **Run all in order**. Each assignment gives its new reviews to the reviewers with the fewest reviews across the whole plan first.
</Step>
</Steps>

- **Use it when** a program has several tracks sharing reviewers and you want a repeatable, ordered run instead of assigning track by track by hand.

![Three saved assignments named after tracks with up and down arrows, Preview run all and Run all in order, and a preview adding 2 reviews with each reviewer's Now and After run totals](/images/kb/review-assignments-saved-assignments.png)

<Tip>
If the preview shows submissions short of reviewers, raise the plan-wide limit slightly or move the short track higher. A limit set to the exact even split often leaves a thinly staffed track under-covered.
</Tip>

## Import assignments from a spreadsheet

When you already know who reviews what (a committee chair's spreadsheet, or dozens of subtracks), import the pairs instead of building an assignment for each. Open the plan, choose **Plan options → Import assignments**, click **Generate Template** for a file pre-filled with this plan, fill it in, then **Upload File**.

![The Plan options menu on a review plan, with Export, Duplicate plan, Import reviewers, Import assignments highlighted, and Delete plan](/images/kb/eval-plan-options-import-assignments.png)

Each row assigns one reviewer to one or more submissions:

| Column | Required | What goes in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Email` | Yes | The reviewer's email. Someone who isn't a user yet is invited. |
| `First Name`, `Last Name` | For new users | The reviewer's name. |
| `Evaluation Plan Name` | Yes | The plan, matched without regard to capitals. One file can cover several plans. |
| `Round Name` | No | The round. Leave it blank for the plan's first round. |
| `Submission ID` | Yes | The submission's ID as shown in the grid, for example `SESS-123`, or `SESS-123-2` for a sub-session. Separate several IDs with commas, semicolons or pipes: `SESS-101, SESS-102, SESS-123-2`. |
| `Send Invite Email` | No | `true` (the default) emails reviewers the import newly enrolls; `false` sends nothing. |

If any ID in a row is wrong, the whole row is skipped and the error names every such ID, so nothing from it is half-applied. Fix the ID and import the row again.

Reviewers who aren't on the event, plan or round yet are enrolled automatically. An import only adds: existing pairs are left alone and nothing is removed. It doesn't check the plan-wide limit or the round's conflict settings, so check the plan's **Submissions** and **Reviewers** tabs afterwards. To try it out, import a few rows into a closed plan.

## Recipes

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Three reviewers per abstract, spread evenly">

1. **Which submissions?** → **All submissions in plan scope**.
2. **How should they be distributed?** → **All to All**.
3. Tick **Reviewers per submission** and enter **3**.
4. **When applying** → **Add to existing**, then **Assign**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Each track to its own committee">

1. On the [Reviewers step](/evaluations/review-plan-reviewers), tag each reviewer with their track.
2. **By submission filters** → **Track is Oncology**. **Individual Reviewer** → filter by the **Oncology** tag, tick them all. **Reviewers per submission** → 3. **Assign**.
3. Click **Assign more submissions** and repeat for each track.

If reviewers sit on several committees, set a [plan-wide limit](#max-submissions-per-reviewer-whole-plan) first, and use [saved assignments](#saved-assignments-in-run-order) so the order is deliberate.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reviewers chose their own topics on the Call for Reviewers">

**All submissions in plan scope** → **All to All** → **Reviewers per submission** 2 → open **Advanced settings** and tick **Match expertise**. The preview warns about any submission no one picked; assign those with **Individual submissions**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A few submissions arrived after the deadline">

**Individual submissions** → search and tick them → **All to All** with **Reviewers per submission** → **Add to existing**. Existing assignments are untouched.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Reviewers got more submissions than the maximum I set. Why?">

**Max submissions per reviewer** limits only the one assignment you're running, and only while its box is ticked. Every **Assign** run, every saved assignment and every **Assign reviewers** automation counts on its own, so three runs of 20 can give someone 60. An **Assign reviewers** automation gives each session to every reviewer it names.

To bring workloads back down:

1. Under **Advanced settings → Reviewer workload across the plan**, enter **Max submissions per reviewer (whole plan)** and click **Save cap**. Reviewers at or over it get no new assignments from the wizard, saved assignments or automations. The cap removes nothing.
2. Remove the extras. On the plan's **Reviewers** tab, find the submissions under that reviewer and choose **Remove assignment** from the ⋯ menu of the ones they haven't started.
3. Make further changes while the plan is closed. Assignments made to a closed plan send no email; reviewers hear about the whole batch when you open the plan and notify them.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I moved submissions to another track or category. How do I refresh assignments?">

Assignments don't follow a track change on their own. An **Assign reviewers** automation adds the submission to the new track's plan, but it never takes it out of the plan or round it was in before.

For each plan the submissions left, open the **Assignments** step and run its rule again with **Replace not-yet-reviewed**. Assignments that no longer match and haven't been started are removed, and submissions left with no reviewers leave the round. Assignments a reviewer has already started stay; remove those by hand with **Remove assignment** on the **Reviewers** tab if they should go too.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does the preview show more than my number of submissions?">

It's counting reviews, not submissions: one per reviewer per submission, so 5 submissions and 3 reviewers reads **Assign 15 reviews**.

If **Filtered submissions** itself is higher than you expect, **All submissions in plan scope** includes submissions from every submission form unless the plan's scope is limited. To assign one form's submissions only, choose **By submission filters** and add a **Source** filter for that form.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will new submissions be assigned automatically?">

No. **Assign** runs once. Run the assignment again with **Add to existing** to pick up new submissions, or set up an **Assign reviewers** [automation](/automations/overview) that assigns a submission when, for example, its status becomes **Accepted**.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
