This is the evaluator's guide for **classic evaluation plans** — plans that score inside the admin app rather than in the reviewer workspace. If your invitation email or the plan card sends you to a Program Site or a **My Reviews** page, you are on a Sessions 2.0 plan: follow [Evaluate sessions (Sessions 2.0)](/evaluations/how-to-evaluate-sessions) instead.

<Note>
Event admins: the setup side — criteria, comments, abstaining, blind review, close dates — is in [Evaluation plans](/evaluations/evaluation-plans). Which guide an evaluator needs depends on how the plan was created, and the plan's card tells you.
</Note>

## 1. Accept the invitation

You receive an email with the subject **[Event name] Evaluator Invitation**. It comes from `no-reply@sessionboard.com`, or from the event's own address when the organizer has set one up. Click **View Event** and sign in.

## 2. Find your plans

After signing in, open **Program → Evaluation** and click the **My Evaluations** tab. This is **My Evaluation Plans**: one card per plan assigned to you, with its status (**Open** or **Closed**), the **Due** date and your **Progress**. Search by plan name, or switch between **Card view** and **Table view**.

Click a plan to start reviewing.

## 3. Read the instructions

The review page opens with the organizer's **Instructions** and the plan's **Closes** date at the top. Read them first — they say how to interpret the criteria and whether comments are wanted. **Open Instructions** brings them back at any time.

If the plan requires a minimum number of reviews, the page tells you how many evaluations to complete before the due date.

## 4. Review each submission

The left column lists the submissions assigned to you, each marked **Reviewed** or **Not Reviewed**. Search by title to jump to one. Selecting a submission shows:

- **Submission Details** — title, description, format, tags and any files the submitter uploaded. If the organizer enabled blind review, speaker names and profiles are hidden.
- **Speakers** — the people on the submission (when not blinded).
- **Grading** — the scoring panel described next.

## 5. Grade it

Under **Grading**:

1. Give a **Rating** for each criterion the organizer set up (or a single overall rating when the plan has no criteria).
2. Add **External Comments** if the plan allows them — the organizer can pass these back to the speaker — and **Internal Comments** for the committee only.
3. Answer any extra **Questions** the organizer added.
4. If you have a conflict of interest, click **Abstain from Grading**. The button changes to **You are Abstaining from Grading** and no rating is recorded for that submission.

Then click **Save & Next** to record the review and move to the next submission. **Previous** and **Next** move through the list; **Save** records without moving on. If you try to leave with an unsaved review, the page warns you.

<Tip>
Every rating has to be filled in before a review saves — the page says **Please fill out all ratings** if one is missing.
</Tip>

## 6. Finish before the close date

Your progress meter on the plan card and at the top of the review page climbs as you save reviews. When every assigned submission is marked **Reviewed** the page reads **All sessions reviewed!**. Finish before the **Closes** date: once a plan closes you can still open submissions, but the grading controls are read-only.
