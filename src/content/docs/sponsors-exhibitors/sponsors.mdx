Exhibitors and sponsors. Speakers are people. Exhibitors and sponsors are companies, and a company brings a booth, a logo, a contract, and usually more than one person who needs to log in. So Sessionboard treats them as groups. A group is the company record, and contacts attach to it. Both live in the CRM section of the sidebar, exhibitors and sponsors each with their own list and their own intake form.

All exhibitors. One row per company. Name, the primary contact, which portal the group has been assigned, then the profile fields, website, address, phone, description, logo, and social links. Same grid as everywhere else, so saved views, columns, sort and filter all work the way you'd expect. The second tab is the useful one, groups with no primary contact yet, which is your list of companies nobody can log in for.

Sponsors is the same list with one extra column, tier. Platinum, gold, silver, whatever your prospectus calls them. Filter by tier and save the view, and you've got all gold sponsors as a one-click list you can email, text, or assign tasks to. The pencil at the start of a row opens the profile.

The sponsor profile. Logo, name, and which portal they're on across the top, and six tabs. Profile is the company itself, tier, description, contact information, social links, and any custom fields you've added, here a booth number and whether the contract is signed. Every value is editable in place. Notes and activity are your team's history with them, messages is what's been sent, and program is the events they're part of.

CRM is the people. This company has one contact so far, marked primary, and add attaches more, either someone already in your contacts or a new person. Everyone attached shares the group. Each contact gets their own login, and they all see the same tasks, files and information, so a marketing manager and a booth lead can split the work without sharing a password.

Actions is where you act on the group. Send an email or a text to its people, copy the portal link, view the portal as they'd see it, and manage portal access. That dialog lists everyone attached who can't log in yet, and give portal access sends them their invitation. When a sponsor writes to say a colleague needs to get in, this is the whole job, add the contact, give access, done.

Tiers. Rename these to match your levels or add new ones, and the count shows how many sponsors sit in each. Tiers matter beyond the label. A portal can be filtered to a tier, so gold sponsors see a task to upload their sponsored session slides and silver sponsors never do. Emails can go to one tier. And if levels change after a sale, an import updates a hundred sponsors at once.

The intake form is how sponsors put themselves in. Four steps to set up. Welcome and terms is what they see first. Group information is the company fields you want filled in. Contact information is the person filling it in, who becomes the primary contact. And notifications decides who on your team hears about it and what the confirmation email says. Copy link gives you the address to put in your sponsor contract or confirmation email. When a sponsor completes it, the group and the contact are created and they're in the portal.

You can always add a group yourself. Add sponsor searches first, in case the company already exists in your organization from another event, so you attach it rather than duplicating it. Add new sponsor opens the company fields, and those fields are the group layout from record settings, so what your team fills in is up to you.

If you take one thing from this, it's the shape. Company first, people attached, and everything else, tier, portal, tasks, hangs off the company. ## Add Sponsor Group
1. Within the Sponsor module, select the orange "Add" button at the top right-hand corner of the screen.
    

   ![Within the Sponsor module, select the orange "Add" button at the top right-hand corner of the screen](/images/kb/9274d136-Screen-Shot-2023-02-09-at-2_48_58-PM-1.png)

2. Select the "Add New Group" button in the pop-up window.
    

   ![Select the "Add New Group" button in the pop-up window](/images/kb/e00e15ce-Screen-Shot-2023-02-09-at-2_49_38-PM-1.png)

3. Add all of the Sponsoring organization's information (i.e. name, phone number, address).

4. Click "Save" once complete

   1. If you would like to add contacts to the group, click the button "Save & Add People" instead.

<Tip>
The fields shown in the *Add New Group* modal can be customized per event in **Settings → Record Settings → Layouts →** **Group Fields**
</Tip>

## Add Sponsor Contact
1. Once a group is created, click into the field within the column "Primary Contact"

   1. If the group has no contacts, the cell will say "+ Add Contact". If there are contacts already attached to the sponsor group and you want to add additional ones, click the cell that says the current contact(s).
        

      ![If the group has no contacts, the cell will say "+ Add Contact"](/images/kb/9f47bc41-Screen-Shot-2023-02-09-at-2_51_28-PM-1.png)

2. Within the pop-up window, click "+ Create a new contact".

   ![Within the pop-up window, click "+ Create a new contact"](/images/kb/5603b0ce-Screen-Shot-2023-02-09-at-10_28_18-AM-3.png)

3. Add all of the contact's information. Ensure to list the Record Type as "Sponsors".

   1. If the contact is marked as a **primary contact**, they will be the person to receive any communications the event admin sends from Sessionboard.

   2. If the contact is marked to **give them login access to the portal**, they will not be sent an email from Sessionboard. Once they are given access, the sponsor will be able to log in to the portal using the portal link.
        

      ![If the contact is marked to give them login access to the portal](/images/kb/b8a5d72a-Screen-Shot-2023-02-09-at-2_50_32-PM-1.png)

4. Once complete, click the orange "Add" button at the bottom of the pop-up window.
