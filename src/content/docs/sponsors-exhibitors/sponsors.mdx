---
title: "Create sponsor groups & contacts"
description: "Add sponsor groups and their contacts, with or without an intake form."
---

import { Note, Tip, Steps, Step } from '@compat';

<Note>This applies to events with the **Sponsor module** enabled. To enable it, contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com).</Note>

When a sponsor completes the intake form, a new sponsor contact and group are created automatically. Admins can also add groups and contacts manually.

## Add a sponsor group
<Steps>
  <Step title="Add">In the **Sponsor** module, click the **Add** button in the top right.</Step>
  <Step title="Add new group">Select **Add New Group** in the pop-up.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">Add the sponsoring organization's information (name, phone, address).</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Click **Save**, or **Save & Add People** to add contacts right away.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Customize the group fields per event in **Settings → Record Settings → Layouts → Group Fields**.</Tip>

## Add a sponsor contact
<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Primary Contact cell">
    In the group's **Primary Contact** column, click **+ Add Contact** (or the existing contact to add more).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new contact">Click **+ Create a new contact** in the pop-up.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Add the contact's information and set **Record Type** to **Sponsors**.
    - Mark as **primary contact** to make them the recipient of admin communications.
    - Mark to **give portal access** so they can log in via the portal link (no email is sent automatically).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add">Click **Add** at the bottom of the pop-up.</Step>
</Steps>
