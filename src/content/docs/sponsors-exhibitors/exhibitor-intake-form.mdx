### Create Intake Form

Navigate to the Exhibitors module. Select Intake Form:

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-23-2026-11-58-25-6822-PM.png)

The Intake Form has three pages that will need to be updated with your event-specific information:
1. Welcome & Terms - The page portal users see when they navigate to the intake form.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-23-2026-11-58-56-5132-PM.png)

2. Group Information - Information about the exhibiting organization/company

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-23-2026-11-59-36-2948-PM.png)

3. Contact Information - Information about the point of contact of the exhibiting organization.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-23-2026-11-59-49-8124-PM.png)

### Share Intake Form

When it is time to now accept exhibitors and invite them to the portal, you will send them the link to your Exhibitor Intake Form. This link can be found at the top right section of the form, by clicking "Copy Link". You can send this link to all of your exhibitor contacts.

![](https://657654.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/657654/image-png-Feb-24-2026-12-01-15-3086-AM.png)
