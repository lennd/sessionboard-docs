An exhibitor in Sessionboard is a **group** — the company record, with its logo, website, booth details and description — and the **contacts** attached to it, each with their own portal login. Most exhibitors arrive through the [exhibitor intake form](/sponsors-exhibitors/exhibitor-intake-form), which creates the group and its first contact in one go. This article covers doing it yourself: adding a group, attaching people, and letting them in.

<Note>
Exhibitors is part of the Sponsors & Exhibitors module. If **CRM → Exhibitors** is missing from your sidebar, ask [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) to enable it for your event.
</Note>

## The exhibitor list

**CRM → Exhibitors** shows one row per company: **Name**, **Primary Contact**, **Assigned Portal**, then the profile fields — website, address, phone, description, logo and social links. It is the same grid as your other lists, so **Saved Views**, **Columns**, **Sort** and **Filter** all apply, and selecting rows opens the bar with **Edit**, **Send**, **Assign**, **More** and **Delete**. The **No Primary Contacts** tab lists the companies nobody can log in for yet.

![The Exhibitors list with Name, Primary Contact, Assigned Portal and profile columns, and the All Exhibitors and No Primary Contacts tabs](/images/kb/exhibitors-list.png)

## Add an exhibitor group

The flow is the same as for sponsors, with exhibitor labels; the panels below are the sponsor versions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Exhibitor">
    Click **Add Exhibitor** at the top right of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search first">
    The panel starts with a search across your organization, so a company that already exhibited at another event is attached rather than duplicated. Type the name; matches are grouped into exhibitors already in this event and exhibitors elsewhere in the organization. Tick one or more and click **Add to Event**.

    ![The Add group panel with its organization-wide search and the Add new button](/images/kb/sponsors-add-search.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add new exhibitor">
    If it is a new company, click **Add new exhibitor**. **New Exhibitor Details** asks for the **Name**, **Website** and **Description**, plus a logo. Which fields appear here is the **Group Fields** layout under **Settings → Record Settings → Layouts** — add a booth number or contract field there and it shows up in this panel and on every exhibitor's profile.

    ![The New group details panel with logo, Name, Website and Description fields](/images/kb/sponsors-add-new.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create Exhibitor** to save the group, or **Create & Add Contacts** to save it and go straight on to attaching people.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a contact to an exhibitor

Open the exhibitor with the pencil on its row and select the **CRM** tab. **Contacts** lists everyone attached, with the **Primary** badge on the person who receives the group's communications.

1. Click **Add**. The search lists contacts already in the event — pick one to attach them.
2. For someone new, click **Create new contact**, then **Add new contact** on the **Add Contacts to …** panel.
3. Fill in **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** (mobile phone and biography are optional), tick **Set as primary contact for …** if this person should receive the group's emails, and click **Create Contact**.

![The New Contact Details panel opened from a group's CRM tab, with the Set as primary contact checkbox](/images/kb/sponsor-add-contact.png)

The contact is created as an exhibitor contact, attached to the group, and appears in **CRM → All Contacts** like anyone else. Every contact attached to a group shares its tasks, files and information in the portal, so the booth lead and the person shipping the crates can each log in without sharing a password.

## Give the contacts portal access

Creating a contact does not send anything. When they should be able to sign in, open the exhibitor, click **Actions → Manage Portal Access**. **People without access** lists the attached contacts who cannot log in yet; select them and click **Give Portal Access**, then choose **Send Email** — with an optional personal message — or **Get Link** to copy the invitation link and send it yourself.

![The Manage Portal Access dialog for a group, listing people without access and the Give Portal Access button](/images/kb/sponsor-portal-access.png)

The same **Actions** menu has **Send Email**, **Send SMS**, **Copy Portal Link** and **View Portal**, which opens the portal as the exhibitor sees it. Which portal that is shows under the group's name as **Portal:** and in the **Assigned Portal** column — see [Creating custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals) to give exhibitors a portal of their own, with booth-specific tasks and files.

<Tip>
For contacts you already have in the event, [Invite people to the event portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal) covers granting access in bulk from **All Contacts**.
</Tip>
