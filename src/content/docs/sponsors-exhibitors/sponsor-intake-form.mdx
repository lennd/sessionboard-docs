## Create intake form
Navigate to the Sponsors module. Select Intake Form:

![Create Intake Form](/images/kb/e778ef16-image-png-Feb-24-2026-12-04-09-4124-AM.png)

The Intake Form has three pages that will need to be updated with your event-specific information:
1. Welcome & Terms- The page portal users see when they navigate to the intake form.

   ![Welcome & Terms- The page portal users see when they navigate to the intake form](/images/kb/d66c8e7f-image-png-Feb-24-2026-12-04-34-0011-AM.png)

2. Group Information - Information about the sponsoring organization/company

   ![Group Information - Information about the sponsoring organization/company](/images/kb/9fb71db9-image-png-Feb-24-2026-12-04-48-0212-AM.png)

3. Contact Information - Information about the point of contact of the sponsoring organization. ​

![Contact Information - Information about the point of contact of the sponsoring organization](/images/kb/5b5ff710-image-png-Feb-24-2026-12-04-54-4542-AM.png)

## Share intake form
When it is time to now accept sponsors and invite them to the portal, you will send them the link to your Sponsor Intake Form. This link can be found at the top right section of the form, by clicking "Copy Link". You can send this link to all of your sponsor contacts.

![Share Intake Form](/images/kb/26632738-image-png-Feb-24-2026-12-01-15-3086-AM.png)
