The intake form is how sponsors put themselves into Sessionboard. A sponsor opens the link, fills in their company and their own details, and on submit the **group** and its **primary contact** are created in your event and the contact is in the portal. You set it up once under **CRM → Sponsors → Intake Form**.

<Note>
Sponsors is part of the Sponsors & Exhibitors module. If **CRM → Sponsors** is missing from your sidebar, ask [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) to enable it for your event.
</Note>

## Why use it

Intake forms let sponsors and exhibitors submit their own information — contacts, booth details, branding assets — directly through a branded form without requiring organizer data entry. Question rules show or hide fields based on earlier answers, keeping the form focused for each submitter. Open and close dates, custom success pages, and multi-language support give organizers control over the full submission experience from a single editor.

## Set up the form

Go to **CRM → Sponsors → Intake Form**. The builder has six steps in the left rail; move through them with **Next** and click **Save** at the top right when you are done — nothing is published until you save.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome & Terms">
    What a sponsor sees first. Set the **Welcome Title**, switch the **Welcome Description** on and write the short explanation under it, and switch **Terms & Conditions** on to require acceptance of your sponsor terms before the form opens.

    ![The Sponsors Intake Form builder on Welcome & Terms, with the Welcome Title, Welcome Description and Terms & Conditions controls](/images/kb/intake-welcome.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Group information">
    The company. Give the section a **Section Title** and **Description & Instructions**, then build the **Form Questions**. **Name** is always required; **Add Field** offers every group field on the event — **Tier**, **Website**, **Booth Number**, address, social links and any custom group field you have created — plus section headers to break the page up. Drag fields to reorder them and use the **Required** switch on each.

    The builder works like a [submission form](/sessions/submission-forms#use-question-rules): add **question rules** so a question, a rich-text block or a divider shows only when an earlier answer matches (**Name**, **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** are always shown), **lock** a field so submitters see it but cannot change it, set per-form character, word and selection limits, and rename the step as it appears in the form's progress bar.

    ![The Group information step with its section title, instructions and the Form Questions list](/images/kb/intake-group-fields.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact information">
    The person filling the form in, who becomes the group's **primary contact**. **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** are always required; add **Job Title**, **Mobile Phone** or any other contact field the same way.

    To collect more people than the submitter, such as a billing or onsite contact, turn on **Let submitters add more contacts**. See [Let submitters add more contacts](#let-submitters-add-more-contacts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Form settings">
    **Open date** and **Close date**: before the open date the form says it is not open yet, and from the close date it says it is closed and takes no new submissions. Leave either empty to open now or stay open. **Success page message** replaces the standard thank-you page. **Flag over-limit text instead of cutting it off** keeps text pasted past a question's limit, marks it in red and blocks **Next** until it fits. **Enable multi-language submissions** adds a language selector for the languages set up for the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pricing & payment">
    Leave it on **Do Not Collect Payment** for a free form, or choose **Upon Submission** to charge a fee. See [Charge a fee](#charge-a-fee).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notifications">
    Who on your team hears about a new sponsor, and what the sponsor receives. Pick the admins under **What admins should be notified when a new group is submitted?**, then review the two templates: **Submission Confirmation** goes to the submitter (switch it off or **Customize** the wording) and the admin alert goes to the people you picked.

    ![The Notifications step with the admin picker and the Submission Confirmation template](/images/kb/intake-notifications.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Let submitters add more contacts

On the **Contact information** step, turn on **Let submitters add more contacts**. The public form then shows **Add another contact** under the contact questions, where the submitter gives each person's first name, last name, email and, if you offer roles, their role.

- **Most contacts a submitter can add**: 5 by default, up to 20.
- **Group roles submitters can choose**: starts with Group User, Billing Contact and Onsite Contact. Remove any of them or add your own with **Add role**. With no roles, the form does not ask for one.

Each added contact needs a first name, last name and a valid email that is not already on the form, including the submitter's own. When the form is completed, each one is matched by email to a contact your organization already has, or created, then added to the event and linked to the group with the chosen role. The submitter stays the group's only primary contact and the only one with portal access; give others access from the group afterwards.

## Charge a fee

On the **Pricing & payment** step, choose **Upon Submission** and set the fee the same way as on a [submission form](/sessions/submission-forms#payments--fees): a **Payment Gateway** from your organization's payment settings, a **Base Fee**, **Pricing Rules** on any group or contact question (for example, a **Tier** of Gold costs more), **VAT Rules** and **Promo Codes**.

When a sponsor completes a form with a fee, the form moves to a **Payment** step that shows the order summary, takes a promo code and the card. The price is fixed when they complete the form, so later changes to the fee do not change what they owe. Until they pay, the invoice is **Pending payment** and no confirmation is sent; the **Submission Confirmation** and the receipt each go out once, after payment. A promo code that brings the total to $0 completes the form without a card.

Intake invoices are on the event's **Invoices** page under **Sponsor & exhibitor intake forms**, with the group each one belongs to. Forms left on **Do Not Collect Payment** work as before.

## Share the form

Click **Copy Link** at the top of the builder and put the address in your sponsor contract, confirmation email or prospectus page. **View Form** opens it exactly as a sponsor will see it. Each sponsor who completes it creates one group and one contact — you never key in a confirmed sponsor by hand.

## After a sponsor submits

- The group appears on **CRM → Sponsors** with the answers from **Group information** on its **Profile** tab, and the submitter on its **CRM** tab marked **Primary**.
- Any contacts the submitter added are on the group's **CRM** tab with their roles.
- If the form charges a fee, the group's invoice is on the **Invoices** page, **Pending payment** until they pay.
- The submitter already has portal access. To add colleagues, open the group and use **CRM → Add**, then **Actions → Manage Portal Access** — see [Create sponsor groups & contacts](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsors).
- If **Tier** is on the form, the sponsor's choice is set on the group; otherwise set it yourself — see [Sponsor tiers](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsor-settings).

<Tip>
Keep **Group information** to what you need before the event starts. Booth graphics, logos and session details are easier to collect afterwards as portal tasks, where you can chase them one at a time — see [Assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks).
</Tip>
