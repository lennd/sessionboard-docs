The intake form is how sponsors put themselves into Sessionboard. A sponsor opens the link, fills in their company and their own details, and on submit the **group** and its **primary contact** are created in your event and the contact is in the portal. You set it up once under **CRM → Sponsors → Intake Form**.

<Note>
Sponsors is part of the Sponsors & Exhibitors module. If **CRM → Sponsors** is missing from your sidebar, ask [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com) to enable it for your event.
</Note>

## Set up the form

Go to **CRM → Sponsors → Intake Form**. The builder has four steps in the left rail; move through them with **Next** and click **Save** at the top right when you are done — nothing is published until you save.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome & Terms">
    What a sponsor sees first. Set the **Welcome Title**, switch the **Welcome Description** on and write the short explanation under it, and switch **Terms & Conditions** on to require acceptance of your sponsor terms before the form opens.

    ![The Sponsors Intake Form builder on Welcome & Terms, with the Welcome Title, Welcome Description and Terms & Conditions controls](/images/kb/intake-welcome.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Group information">
    The company. Give the section a **Section Title** and **Description & Instructions**, then build the **Form Questions**. **Name** is always required; **Add Field** offers every group field on the event — **Tier**, **Website**, **Booth Number**, address, social links and any custom group field you have created — plus section headers to break the page up. Drag fields to reorder them and use the **Required** switch on each.

    ![The Group information step with its section title, instructions and the Form Questions list](/images/kb/intake-group-fields.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact information">
    The person filling the form in, who becomes the group's **primary contact**. **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** are always required; add **Job Title**, **Mobile Phone** or any other contact field the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notifications">
    Who on your team hears about a new sponsor, and what the sponsor receives. Pick the admins under **What admins should be notified when a new group is submitted?**, then review the two templates: **Submission Confirmation** goes to the submitter (switch it off or **Customize** the wording) and the admin alert goes to the people you picked.

    ![The Notifications step with the admin picker and the Submission Confirmation template](/images/kb/intake-notifications.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share the form

Click **Copy Link** at the top of the builder and put the address in your sponsor contract, confirmation email or prospectus page. **View Form** opens it exactly as a sponsor will see it. Each sponsor who completes it creates one group and one contact — you never key in a confirmed sponsor by hand.

## After a sponsor submits

- The group appears on **CRM → Sponsors** with the answers from **Group information** on its **Profile** tab, and the submitter on its **CRM** tab marked **Primary**.
- The submitter already has portal access. To add colleagues, open the group and use **CRM → Add**, then **Actions → Manage Portal Access** — see [Create sponsor groups & contacts](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsors).
- If **Tier** is on the form, the sponsor's choice is set on the group; otherwise set it yourself — see [Sponsor tiers](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsor-settings).

<Tip>
Keep **Group information** to what you need before the event starts. Booth graphics, logos and session details are easier to collect afterwards as portal tasks, where you can chase them one at a time — see [Assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks).
</Tip>
