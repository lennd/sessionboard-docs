**Tier** is a standard field on every sponsor group. The levels it offers — Gold, Silver, Bronze, or whatever your prospectus calls them — are set per event under **CRM → Sponsors → Tiers**, and once a sponsor has a tier you can filter, email, assign and build portals by it.

## Manage the tiers

Go to **CRM → Sponsors → Tiers**. Every event starts with **Gold**, **Silver** and **Bronze**; the table shows each tier's **Name**, its display **Order**, and how many **Sponsors** currently hold it.

![The Tiers page listing Platinum, Gold and Silver with their order and sponsor counts](/images/kb/sponsor-tiers.png)

- **Add → Add Tier** creates a level: give it a **Name** and a **Display Order** (lower numbers sort first).
- **Add → Copy from…** brings the tiers over from another of your events.
- **Actions → Edit** renames a tier or changes its order; sponsors keep the tier under its new name. **Actions → Delete** removes it.

## Assign a tier to a sponsor

- **One sponsor:** open it from **CRM → Sponsors** and set **Tier** on the **Profile** tab, or choose it under **Tier** when you [add the sponsor by hand](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsors).
- **Several at once:** select the rows on the Sponsors list and use **Edit** in the action bar to set **Tier** for all of them — see [Bulk edit fields](/settings/bulk-edit-fields).
- **From a spreadsheet:** when levels change after the sales cycle, an [import](/settings/importing-data) that includes a **Tier** column updates every sponsor in one pass.
- **From the intake form:** add the **Tier** field to the **Group information** step of the [sponsor intake form](/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsor-intake-form) if you want sponsors to pick their own level.

The **Tier** column sits next to **Name** on the Sponsors list, so the level is visible at a glance.

![The Sponsors list with the Tier column showing Platinum, Gold and Silver next to each company](/images/kb/sponsors-list.png)

## What tiers unlock

- **Filtered views.** **Filter** the Sponsors list by **Tier**, then **Saved Views** to keep "All Gold Sponsors" or "Needs assets – Bronze" one click away for the whole team.
- **Targeted outreach.** Select the rows in a tier and **Send** an email or SMS — logo specs to Silver and Bronze, sponsored-session details to Gold only.
- **Fulfilment by tier.** From a filtered view, **Assign** tasks, files or forms so the deliverables each level owes are tracked in one place.
- **Tier-specific portals.** Build a portal whose filter is **Tier = Gold** and give it the tasks and resources that level gets — "Upload sponsored session slides" for Gold, never shown to Bronze. See [Creating custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals).
- **Integrations.** Tier is a standard field, so it is available to connected apps that read sponsor groups — see the article for your app under **Apps**.

<Tip>
Tiers are per event. If you run the same sponsorship packages every year, set them up once and use **Add → Copy from…** on the next event instead of retyping them.
</Tip>
