Sessionboard CRM bills for the people you work with, not the people you store. Keep everyone you know, for free. Your plan counts **active contacts**, and up to 1,000 is free.

## What makes a contact active

A person is an active contact when, in the last 12 months, you:

- emailed them,
- invited them,
- sent them a contract or a form,
- scheduled them in a session,

or they registered for one of your events.

## A rolling 12 months

The window moves every day. Someone you last reached 13 months ago is no longer active, without you doing anything. Reach them again and they count again.

## Each person counts once

A person is counted once, by email, across Sessionboard CRM and Sessionboard Marketing. A speaker you invited in CRM and emailed from Marketing is one active contact, not two. Marketing and CRM count active contacts the same way.

## What doesn't make a contact active

- Adding or importing them, from a spreadsheet or a connected system.
- Finding them in your CRM, AMS or event platform.
- Tagging them, putting them in a group, writing notes, or exporting them.

## Where to see it

**Home** and **Plan and billing** show your active contacts against your plan. Filter **People** by active to see who counts.

<Note>
Going past your plan's active contacts never removes, hides or blocks anyone. **Plan and billing** suggests the plan that fits. See [Plans and billing](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing).
</Note>
