**Companies** lists the organizations you work with: your sponsors and exhibitors, and any other company your people work at. Each company has its tags, its website and logo, and the people who work there.

## Why use it

A sponsor is a company, not one person: your contact there changes, and several people may sign, exhibit or speak. Keeping the company with its people means you always know who to call, and see every agreement and pipeline entry with that company in one place.

## Filter with tag chips

Chips for **Sponsor**, **Exhibitor** and **Untagged** show how many companies have each. Select one or both to filter. You can also search by name, sort by name, number of people or when added, and export the list as a CSV file.

Each row shows the company's tags and how many people, contracts and pipeline entries it has.

## Add a company

On **Companies**, add a company with its name and, if you like, its website and tags. Sessionboard CRM tells you if the name is already here.

## Open a company

Select a company to see its people, its contracts and its pipeline entries. Change its name, website or tags here.

## Add the people who work there

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the company">
On the company's page, choose to add people.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who">
Pick people already in **People**, or add someone new with their email, name and job title.
</Step>
</Steps>

The people stay in **People**, linked to the company, and can go in the pipeline and get contracts as the company's sponsor or exhibitor contacts. A person can work at more than one company. To remove someone from a company, unlink them: they stay in **People**.

## Delete companies

Select the companies and choose delete. Sessionboard CRM asks you to confirm first. The people who worked there stay in **People**.

<Note>
Companies keep standard fields only: name, website, logo and tags. Custom fields are part of the Sessionboard platform. See [Custom fields](/sessionboard-crm/upgrade/custom-fields).
</Note>
