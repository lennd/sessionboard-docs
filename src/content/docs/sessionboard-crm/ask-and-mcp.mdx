## Ask

Ask is on **Home** and behind **Ask** on the right-hand rail. Type what you want in your own words, or start from one of the suggestions. Ask answers questions about your contacts and can take you through the setup checklist one step at a time.

## Connect your own AI assistant

Sessionboard CRM works with AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT through the Sessionboard connector (MCP). Add the Sessionboard connector in your assistant and sign in with your Sessionboard account. Then ask in your own words. An assistant can:

| Ask for | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "How many active contacts do we have?" | Reads **Home**: contacts by type, active contacts against your plan, the setup checklist. |
| "Find speakers at Acme" | Searches and filters your contacts, and opens one with their history and notes. |
| "Add Dr. Ana Duarte as an SME" | Adds a contact, changes one, or adds a note. An email already in CRM isn't added twice. |
| "Tag these 30 people Keynote shortlist" | Adds or removes types and tags on many contacts at once, and creates, renames or deletes tags. |
| "Import this spreadsheet" | Previews the import first and shows you the counts; imports once you agree, 250 rows at a time. |
| "Who in our CRM isn't a contact yet?" | Reads a connected system and says what it found ("we found 40 SMEs, 12 of them new") for you to review before anything is imported. |
| "Export our sponsor contacts" | Gives you the filtered list as CSV, up to 1,000 rows. Export bigger lists from **Contacts**. |
| "Delete these contacts" | Deletes them, after asking you first. |

## What an assistant never does

- **It never connects a system.** Connecting takes your keys, so you do it on **Connections**.
- **It never emails anyone.** Nothing in Sessionboard CRM's tools sends a message.
- **It never writes to your CRM, AMS or event platform.** Finding people there only reads.

<Note>
An assistant needs the CRM permissions (`read:crm` to read, `write:crm` to make changes) on its connection. Developers: the [CRM API](/sessionboard-crm/api) and [CRM MCP tools](/sessionboard-crm/api/mcp-tools) list every operation.
</Note>
