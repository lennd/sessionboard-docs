Every organization starts with a 14-day trial of the Starter plan, with no card needed. Open **Plan and billing** from the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Plans

Every plan has every feature of Sessionboard CRM, including the pipeline and contracts, and unlimited seats. Plans differ only by how many **active contacts** they include.

| Plan | Active contacts | Billed yearly | Billed monthly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Up to 500 | $3,750 a year ($313 a month) | $375 a month |
| Up to 1,000 | 501 to 1,000 | $6,750 a year ($563 a month) | $675 a month |
| Up to 2,000 | 1,001 to 2,000 | $12,000 a year ($1,000 a month) | Yearly only |
| Up to 5,000 | 2,001 to 5,000 | $26,250 a year ($2,188 a month) | Yearly only |
| Up to 10,000 | 5,001 to 10,000 | $45,000 a year ($3,750 a month) | Yearly only |
| Enterprise CRM | More than 10,000 | Talk to us | |

## Yearly or monthly

A year costs the same as 10 months, so yearly billing saves you about 17% compared with paying monthly for 12 months. The two smallest plans, Starter and Up to 1,000, can be billed either way. Plans from 1,001 active contacts are billed yearly.

You store as many contacts as you like for free. An active contact is someone you reached in the last 12 months, counted once across Sessionboard CRM and Marketing. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).

## What you use

The top of **Plan and billing** shows your active contacts against your plan, and how many contacts you store. When your active contacts grow past your plan, it suggests the plan that fits. Going over never removes, hides or blocks a contact.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Plan and billing">
Select the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose yearly or monthly">
**Plan and billing** shows yearly prices first. On Starter and Up to 1,000, switch to monthly to see the monthly price.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a plan">
Choose the plan for your active contacts and complete checkout.
</Step>
</Steps>

Your card comes when you choose a plan, and choosing one doesn't end your trial: nothing is charged until it ends.

## Card, invoices and cancelling

Select **Manage billing** to update your card, download invoices or cancel. After you cancel, **Plan and billing** says when your plan ends, and everything stays as it is until then.

## When a trial or plan ends

When the trial ends, or a plan is cancelled or unpaid, your organization becomes read-only: you can still see your contacts, and nothing you made is deleted. **Plan and billing** says how long your data is kept. Choose a plan to make changes again.

## More than 10,000 active contacts, or the platform

Enterprise CRM is for more than 10,000 active contacts. Interest forms, Scout, Pathways and Content Gaps are part of the Sessionboard platform, not Sessionboard CRM. Each has its own entry at the bottom of the sidebar, where you can book a demo or upgrade. See [Interest forms and Scout](/sessionboard-crm/upgrade/interest-forms-and-scout).

<Note>
If your organization works with Sessionboard on a contract, the billing page says so and there is nothing to pay here.
</Note>
