Sessionboard CRM is free for up to 1,000 active contacts. There's no card to add and no trial clock. Open **Plan and billing** from the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Plans

Every plan has every feature of Sessionboard CRM, including the pipeline and contracts, and unlimited seats. Plans differ only by how many **active contacts** they include.

| Plan | Active contacts | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Up to 1,000 | Free |
| Up to 2,000 | 1,001 to 2,000 | $12,000 a year |
| Up to 5,000 | 2,001 to 5,000 | $26,250 a year |
| Up to 10,000 | 5,001 to 10,000 | $45,000 a year |
| Enterprise CRM | More than 10,000 | Talk to us |

Paid plans are billed yearly. You store as many people and groups as you like for free: an active contact is someone you reached in the last 12 months, counted once across Sessionboard CRM and Marketing. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).

## What you use

**Home** and **Plan and billing** show your active contacts against your plan, such as "812 of 1,000 free".

- **Near your limit** (80% or more): Sessionboard CRM suggests the plan that fits.
- **Over your limit**: it asks you to choose a plan. Nothing stops working while you decide: no person, group or feature is removed, hidden or blocked.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Plan and billing">
Select the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a plan">
Choose the plan for your active contacts and complete checkout with a card. It's billed yearly.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Terms

**Plan and billing** shows who accepted Sessionboard's [Terms of Service](https://www.sessionboard.com/legal/terms-of-service/) for this workspace, and when. Sessionboard keeps a record of every acceptance: who, which version, when, and how.

## Card, invoices and cancelling

Select **Manage billing** to update your card, download invoices or cancel. After you cancel, **Plan and billing** says when your plan ends, and everything stays as it is until then.

## More than 10,000 active contacts, or the platform

Enterprise CRM is for more than 10,000 active contacts. Interest forms, Scout, Pathways, Content Gaps and custom fields are part of the Sessionboard platform, not Sessionboard CRM. Each has its own entry at the bottom of the sidebar, where you can book a demo or upgrade. See [Interest forms and Scout](/sessionboard-crm/upgrade/interest-forms-and-scout) and [Custom fields](/sessionboard-crm/upgrade/custom-fields).

<Note>
If your organization works with Sessionboard on a contract, the billing page says so and there is nothing to pay here.
</Note>
