Sessionboard CRM keeps the same standard fields for every organization. Fields of your own, such as a member number, a dietary need or a booth size, are custom fields, and they're part of the **Sessionboard platform**. They aren't in Sessionboard CRM, on any CRM plan.

## Standard fields

| Record | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| A person | Email, first name, last name, job title, company, tags, companies. |
| A company | Name, website, logo, tags. |

Notes, pipeline stages and contracts sit alongside these, on every plan.

## Add a field

**Add a field** on a person or a company opens a page about custom fields. On that page:

- **Talk to us** books a demo with our team.
- **Upgrade** starts moving your organization to the Sessionboard platform.

Your people, companies, tags, pipeline and contracts are the platform's own, so everything you have in Sessionboard CRM comes with you.

<Note>
Until then, a note on the person or the company is a good place for details that don't fit a standard field.
</Note>
