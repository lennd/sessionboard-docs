Interest forms and Scout are part of the **Sessionboard platform**. They aren't in Sessionboard CRM, on any CRM plan. The bottom of the CRM sidebar lists each one, and each opens a page about it.

## Interest forms

Interest forms let speakers, sponsors and exhibitors raise their hand on your website. Whoever fills one in lands in your pipeline, with their answers, ready for your team to review.

## Scout

Scout finds new speakers and experts for the topics of your events, and adds them to your pipeline as people to consider.

## How to get them

On the page for either one in Sessionboard CRM:

- **Talk to us** books a demo with our team.
- **Upgrade** starts moving your organization to the Sessionboard platform.

Your contacts, tags, pipeline and contracts are the platform's own, so everything you have in Sessionboard CRM comes with you.

<Note>
Pathways and Content Gaps are part of the platform too. For more than 10,000 active contacts, see Enterprise CRM in [Plans and billing](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing#more-than-10000-active-contacts-or-the-platform).
</Note>
