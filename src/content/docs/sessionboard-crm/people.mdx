**People** lists everyone your organization knows, one row per email: speakers, members, customers, prospects, influencers and attendees, and the people who work at your companies. It's the same list Sessionboard Marketing sees, so the two never disagree about who someone is.

## Why use it

Your speakers are in a spreadsheet, your attendees in your event platform, your members in your AMS and your prospects in your CRM. **People** puts them in one list you can slice in a click: every speaker who is also a member, every prospect who came to three webinars, everyone at one sponsor.

## Filter with tag chips

Above the list, a chip for each tag shows how many people have it: **Speaker**, **Member**, **Customer**, **Prospect**, **Influencer**, **Attendee**, and **Untagged**. Select a chip to filter; select more to widen the list to anyone with any of them. Turn on **Match all** to show only people with every tag you chose, such as speakers who are also members.

You can also:

- **Search** a name, an email or a company.
- **Filter** by company, in the pipeline or not, attended or not, event, and active or not.
- **Sort** by name, company, events attended, webinars attended or when they were last seen.
- **Export** what's on screen as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets.

## What each row shows

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Tags | Speaker, member, customer, prospect, influencer, attendee. A person can have any number. |
| Companies | Where they work, from **Companies**. |
| Pipeline | Their pipeline stage, if they're in it. |
| Attended | How many of your events and webinars they came to. |
| Last seen | Their latest sign of interest, such as registering, attending or watching. |

## Open a person

Select a person to see their details, tags and companies, their pipeline stage and contracts, and every sign of interest in time order: what they registered for, came to, watched and opened, and when they became a member or a customer.

## Add a person

<Steps>
<Step title="Add">
On **People**, add a person with their email and, if you like, their name, job title, company and tags.
</Step>
<Step title="Link a company">
Choose one of your companies to link them to it, or add a company first. See [Companies](/sessionboard-crm/companies).
</Step>
</Steps>

An email already here isn't added twice. Adding someone emails no one. To add many at once, see [Add and import people and companies](/sessionboard-crm/import).

## Tag or untag many at once

Select people in the list, then add or remove a tag on all of them. Their other tags stay as they are. Some tags do more than label: tagging someone **Speaker** lets you put them in the pipeline and send them a contract. See [Tags](/sessionboard-crm/people/tags).

## Delete people

Select the people and choose delete. Sessionboard CRM asks you to confirm first. Their pipeline entries and contracts stay. You don't need to delete anyone to keep your bill down: people you haven't reached in 12 months stop counting as active on their own. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).

## Warm opportunities

**Warm opportunities** is a saved filter for people who:

- came to 5 or more of your webinars or events,
- aren't in your pipeline, and
- aren't tagged **Customer**.

They know you well, and nobody is working with them yet. The page shows these thresholds next to the list.

<Note>
Sessionboard CRM keeps standard fields only: name, email, job title, company, tags and companies. Custom fields are part of the Sessionboard platform. See [Custom fields](/sessionboard-crm/upgrade/custom-fields).
</Note>
