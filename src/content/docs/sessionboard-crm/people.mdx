**People** lists everyone your organization knows, one row per email: your contacts, the attendees of your events and webinars, your members, your prospects and customers, and your team. It's the same list Sessionboard Marketing sees, so the two never disagree about who someone is.

## Why use it

Your speakers are in one place, your attendees in your event platform, your members in your AMS and your prospects in your CRM. **People** puts them side by side, so you can see who came to five of your webinars, who's a member, and who nobody on your team is talking to yet.

## Contacts and people

A **contact** is someone you work with: a speaker, a subject-matter expert (SME), an influencer, or an exhibitor or sponsor contact. Contacts have types and tags, notes, a pipeline stage and contracts.

A **person** is anyone your organization knows. Every contact is a person too, but most people aren't contacts: an attendee or a member is someone you know about, not someone you're working with.

That's why attendees and members don't become contacts on their own. Your contacts stay the people your team works with, and you choose who joins them. When you add a person to your contacts, Sessionboard CRM creates the contact and links the two, so each one's history shows on the other.

## What each row shows

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Roles | Speaker, sponsor, attendee, member, or added by your team. |
| Contact | Whether they're one of your contacts, and their types. |
| Member | Whether your AMS says they're a member. |
| Lifecycle | Customer, prospect or lead, as your CRM last said. |
| CRM owner | Who owns them in your CRM. |
| Pipeline | Their pipeline stage, if they're in it. |
| Attended | How many of your events and webinars they came to. |
| Last seen | Their latest sign of interest, such as registering, attending or watching. |

## Search and filter

- **Search** finds a name, an email or a company.
- **Filters** narrow the list by role, type, member or not, lifecycle stage, in the pipeline or not, attended or not, event, and active or not.
- **Sort** by name, company, events attended, webinars attended or when they were last seen.
- **Export** downloads what's on screen as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets.

Open a person to see every sign of interest in time order: what they registered for, came to, watched and opened, and when their membership or lifecycle stage changed.

## Warm opportunities

**Warm opportunities** is a saved filter for people who:

- came to 5 or more of your webinars or events,
- aren't in your pipeline, and
- aren't customers, as your CRM says.

They know you well, and nobody is working with them yet. The page shows these thresholds next to the list.

## Add people to your contacts

<Steps>
<Step title="Select people">
Select the people on **People**, up to 200 at a time. Warm opportunities are a good place to start.
</Step>
<Step title="Add them to your contacts">
Choose their types, such as SME. Anyone whose email is already a contact is linked to that contact instead of being added twice.
</Step>
</Steps>

Adding someone to your contacts doesn't email them, and it doesn't make them an active contact: reaching them does. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).

<Note>
Nothing on **People** writes to your CRM, AMS or event platform. To bring more people in, see [Bring in attendees and members](/sessionboard-crm/connections/attendees-and-members). To invite people to a webinar or nominate them for an award, see [Hand people to other Sessionboard apps](/sessionboard-crm/people/hand-off).
</Note>
