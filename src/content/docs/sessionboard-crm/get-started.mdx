Sessionboard CRM keeps everyone your events depend on in one place. **People** are your speakers, members, customers, prospects, influencers and attendees. **Companies** are your sponsors and exhibitors, with the people who work there. Bring them in from a spreadsheet or from your CRM, association management system (AMS) or event platform, then track who you're asking in the pipeline and send agreements for e-signature.

## Sign up

Sessionboard CRM is free for up to 1,000 active contacts, with no card and no time limit.

<Steps>
<Step title="Start Sessionboard CRM">
Open the Sessionboard CRM sign-up page and enter your work email.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm your email">
We email you a link and a 6-digit code: select the link in the email, or type the code. Either one works once, for 15 minutes. There is no password. Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and use your work account.
</Step>
<Step title="Name your organization">
Your organization starts on the Free plan.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you already use Sessionboard Marketing, Learning, Awards or Sessions, add CRM to the same organization from the app switcher instead of signing up again: your people are already there.

If your organization works with Sessionboard on a contract, its people and companies are in the Sessionboard platform, and CRM doesn't offer a card plan. Your account team can help.
</Note>

## Sign in

Go to the Sessionboard sign-in page, enter your work email and select the emailed link, or type the 6-digit code. The link and the code work once, for 15 minutes. **Continue with Google** and **Sign in with Microsoft** work too, when you see them. If CRM is your organization's only Sessionboard app, signing in opens CRM.

To change the language of the sign-up and sign-in pages, select the globe at the top right: English, Français, Deutsch or Español. A new account is made in that language, and the sign-in email is written in it.

Two-step sign-in for an account that signs in by email uses an authenticator app or a passkey, not emailed codes, because the sign-in email already goes to that inbox. If you turned on emailed codes before, you keep them.

## Find your way around

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your active contacts against your plan, the setup checklist and your most recent people. |
| **People** | Everyone you know. Filter by tag (speaker, member, customer, prospect, influencer, attendee) in one click. |
| **Companies** | Your sponsors and exhibitors, and the people who work at each. |
| **Pipeline** | Who you have in mind, who you've asked and who said yes, stage by stage. |
| **Contracts** | Agreements sent for e-signature, and who has signed. |
| **Import** | Bring people or companies in from a CSV or Excel file. |
| **Connections** | Read people from your CRM, AMS or event platform and choose who to bring in. |

Below them, the sidebar lists what the Sessionboard platform adds (Interest forms, Scout, Pathways, Content Gaps and custom fields) and Enterprise CRM, for more than 10,000 active contacts. Each opens a page about it.

## The setup checklist

**Home** shows a checklist until each step is done. Each step opens the page that does it:

- Connect your CRM, AMS or event system
- Import a spreadsheet
- Add a contact
- Mark your speakers, SMEs and sponsors
- Put your speakers in a pipeline
- Send your first agreement
- Invite a teammate

## Your plan

You store as many people and companies as you like. Your plan counts **active contacts**: the people you email, invite, send a contract or a form to, schedule in a session, or who register, in the last 12 months. Up to 1,000 is free. Above that, plans are billed yearly, and nothing stops working while you decide. See [Plans and billing](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing).

## Switch apps

The nine-dot button next to the Sessionboard logo lists every Sessionboard app: Platform, Sessions, Awards, Learning, Marketing and CRM. The apps your organization has open in one select. The others open a page about the app, where an admin can add it, or book a demo of the platform. People are shared: a speaker in CRM is the same person in Marketing and Sessions.
