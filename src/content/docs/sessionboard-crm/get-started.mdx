Sessionboard CRM keeps the people your events depend on in one list: speakers, subject-matter experts (SMEs), influencers, and your exhibitor and sponsor contacts. Each person is a **Contact**, with their history across your events, your team's notes, and the types and tags you give them. Bring them in from a spreadsheet, or read them from your CRM, association management system (AMS) or event platform, and review them before anything is imported.

## Sign up

<Steps>
<Step title="Start Sessionboard CRM">
Open the Sessionboard CRM sign-up page and enter your work email.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm your email">
We email you a link and a 6-digit code: select the link in the email, or type the code. Either one works once, for 15 minutes. There is no password. Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and use your work account.
</Step>
<Step title="Name your organization">
Your organization starts a 14-day trial of the Starter plan, with no card needed.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you already use Sessionboard Marketing, Learning, Awards or Sessions, add CRM to the same organization from the app switcher instead of signing up again: your contacts are already there.

If your organization works with Sessionboard on a contract, its contacts are in the Sessionboard platform, and CRM doesn't offer a card plan. Your account team can help.
</Note>

## Sign in

Go to the Sessionboard sign-in page, enter your work email and select the emailed link, or type the 6-digit code. The link and the code work once, for 15 minutes. **Continue with Google** and **Sign in with Microsoft** work too, when you see them. If CRM is your organization's only Sessionboard app, signing in opens CRM.

## Find your way around

The sidebar has seven sections:

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | How many contacts you have of each type, your active contacts against your plan, the setup checklist and your most recent contacts. |
| **Contacts** | Search, filter, sort and export your contacts, and open one to see their history and notes. |
| **Pipeline** | Track who you have in mind, who you've asked and who said yes, stage by stage. |
| **Contracts** | Send agreements for e-signature and see who has signed. |
| **Tags** | Your own lists, such as a keynote shortlist, a program committee or a region. |
| **Import** | Bring contacts in from a CSV or Excel file. |
| **Connections** | Read people from your CRM, AMS or event platform and choose who to import. |

Below them, the sidebar lists what the Sessionboard platform adds (Interest forms, Scout, Pathways and Content Gaps) and Enterprise CRM, for more than 10,000 active contacts. Each opens a page about it. See [Interest forms and Scout](/sessionboard-crm/upgrade/interest-forms-and-scout).

## The setup checklist

**Home** shows a checklist until each step is done. Each step opens the page that does it:

- Connect your CRM, AMS or event platform
- Import a spreadsheet
- Add a contact
- Mark your speakers
- Put your speakers in a pipeline
- Send your first agreement
- Invite a teammate

## Your plan

You store as many contacts as you like for free. Your plan counts **active contacts**: the people you email, invite, send a contract or a form to, schedule in a session, or who register, in the last 12 months. Every plan has every feature and unlimited seats. The two smallest plans can be billed monthly or yearly; the others are billed yearly.

## Switch apps

The nine-dot button next to the Sessionboard logo lists every Sessionboard app: Platform, Sessions, Awards, Learning, Marketing and CRM. The apps your organization has open in one select. The others open a page about the app, where an admin can add it with a 14-day trial, or book a demo of the platform. Contacts are shared: a speaker you add in CRM is the same contact in Marketing and Sessions.
