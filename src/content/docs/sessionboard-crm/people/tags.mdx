Tags say who someone is to you. A person or a group can have any number of them, and each one is a chip you can filter by on the **People** and **Groups** tabs of **People**.

## Why use it

One person is often several things to you: a speaker this year, a member, and a prospect for your next event. Tags keep all of that on one record, so you can find them from any angle without keeping separate lists.

## People tags

| Tag | Use it for | What it also does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Speaker** | People who speak, or might, at your events. | Lets you put them in the pipeline and send them a contract. |
| **Member** | Members of your organization. | Records them as members, so Marketing and Learning see the same. |
| **Customer** | People who buy from you. | Records the change, and keeps them out of warm opportunities. |
| **Prospect** | People you'd like to win. | Records the change. |
| **Influencer** | People who spread the word about your events. | |
| **Attendee** | People who come to your events. | Your event platform also adds it when you bring attendees in. |

## Group tags

| Tag | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Exhibitor** | Groups that exhibit at your events. |
| **Sponsor** | Groups that sponsor your events. |

A group can be both.

## Filter with chips

Each tag is a chip with a count, after **All**. Select one chip, or several to see anything with any of them. With two or more selected, turn on **Match all** to see only records with every tag you chose.

## Manage tags

**Manage tags**, at the end of the chip row on **People**, lists the six relationship tags, which are the same for every organization, and your own tags. Your own tags are lists you make, for example to hand people to another Sessionboard app. Add, rename or delete them there. Deleting a tag asks you to confirm, and never deletes anyone.

## Tag many at once

Select people or groups in the list, then add or remove a tag. Their other tags stay as they are. To change one record's tags, open it.

## Taking a tag off

Removing a tag takes the tag off and undoes what it recorded where that can be undone, such as membership. Pipeline entries and contracts stay as they are.

<Note>
Tagging emails no one, and doesn't make anyone an active contact. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).
</Note>
