Sessionboard CRM is where your people live. The other Sessionboard apps are where you do something with them. From a list in CRM, hand a selection to the app that does the next step.

## Why use it

You found the right people in CRM: SMEs on a topic, your warm opportunities, last year's speakers. Handing them off takes them to the next step in one go, with no export, no spreadsheet and no copying names.

## What you can do

| Action | Opens | What happens there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invite to a webinar** | Sessionboard Marketing | Pick the webinar and launch the invitation. |
| **Put on a call for speakers** | Sessionboard Sessions | Invite them to submit to your call for speakers. |
| **Turn their talks into a course** | Sessionboard Learning | Start a course from their sessions. |
| **Nominate** | Sessionboard Awards | Nominate them in an awards program. |

## Hand people off

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose who">
On **People** or **Contacts**, select people, or filter the list to everyone you want, such as your warm opportunities.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the action">
Sessionboard CRM saves your selection as a new tag named for where it came from and the date, such as "From CRM · Warm opportunities · Nov 9". It then opens the app with that tag.
</Step>
<Step title="Carry on in the app">
The app picks the tag as the audience, or shows it in its people picker. Nothing is sent until you send it there.
</Step>
</Steps>

The tag stays in CRM under **Tags**, so you can see later who you handed off and when.

## When you don't have the app yet

If your organization doesn't use that app yet, the action opens the app's page instead, where an admin can start a 14-day trial. Your tag is waiting when you open the app.

<Note>
Handing people off doesn't email anyone, and contacts and people stay the same records in every Sessionboard app: a speaker in CRM is the same person in Marketing, Sessions, Learning and Awards.
</Note>
