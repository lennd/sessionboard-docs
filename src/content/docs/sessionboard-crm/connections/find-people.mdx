**Connections** lists the systems Sessionboard CRM can read people from. Finding people there is read-only. Sessionboard CRM looks, tells you what it found and compares it with your people and companies, and nothing is saved in CRM, or changed in the other system, until you choose who to import.

## What each system brings

| System | Who you can find |
| --- | --- |
| HubSpot, Salesforce | Your contacts there, as people, and the companies they work at. |
| iMIS | Your members, as people tagged **Member**. |
| Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo, ON24, Goldcast | The speakers of one event you choose, as people tagged **Speaker**. |

An event's attendees come in through the event platform's own sync: see [Bring in attendees and members](/sessionboard-crm/connections/attendees-and-members). Using Sessionize, Airtable, Fonteva, Nimble AMS or Swapcard? Export a spreadsheet and import it instead: see [Add and import people and companies](/sessionboard-crm/import#bring-people-in-from-another-system).

## Connect a system

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Connections">
Each system shows whether it's connected and, once it is, whether the connection is healthy.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect">
An admin chooses the system and enters what it asks for, such as an API key or your account's address. It's the same connection Sessionboard Marketing and Sessions use, so a system you connected there is already connected here.
</Step>
<Step title="Test it">
Test the connection to check that Sessionboard CRM can read from the system.
</Step>
</Steps>

To stop using a system, an admin disconnects it. Sessionboard CRM asks you to confirm first. People and companies you already brought in from it stay in CRM.

## Find people

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose what to look for">
For a CRM or AMS, pick the kinds of people you want. For an event platform, choose the event whose speakers you want. You can also choose the tag found people get when the system doesn't say, such as **Speaker**.
</Step>
<Step title="See what was found">
Sessionboard CRM reads the system and tells you how many people it found by tag, how many are new to you, how many you already have and how many can't be imported (for example, without an email). If the system doesn't share a kind of person, it says why. A very large result is cut short, and says so.
</Step>
<Step title="Review">
Go through the list, deselect anyone you don't want, and give the rest tags. Where the system holds organizations, choose whether they come in as companies.
</Step>
<Step title="Import the ones you chose">
Import them the same way as a spreadsheet: check the preview, then import. Each person remembers the system they came from.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Finding people never writes to your CRM, AMS or event platform, and never emails anyone. Only the people you import are added to Sessionboard CRM.
</Note>

## Active contacts

Importing people from another system costs nothing by itself: they count as active contacts only once you reach them. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).
