**Connections** lists the systems Sessionboard CRM can read people from: CRMs, association management systems (AMSs) and event platforms. Finding people there is read-only. Sessionboard CRM looks, tells you what it found and compares it with your contacts, and nothing is saved in CRM, or changed in the other system, until you choose who to import.

## Connect a system

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Connections">
Each system shows whether it's connected and, once it is, whether the connection is healthy.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect">
An admin chooses the system and enters what it asks for, such as an API key or your account's address. It's the same connection Sessionboard Marketing and Sessions use, so a system you connected there is already connected here.
</Step>
<Step title="Test it">
Test the connection to check that Sessionboard CRM can read from the system.
</Step>
</Steps>

To stop using a system, an admin disconnects it. Sessionboard CRM asks you to confirm first. Contacts you already imported from it stay in CRM.

## Find people

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose what to look for">
Pick the kinds of people the system holds that you want, such as contacts or speakers. For an event platform, also choose which of its events to look in.
</Step>
<Step title="See what was found">
Sessionboard CRM reads the system and tells you how many people it found by type, how many are new to you, how many are already your contacts and how many can't be imported (for example, without an email). If the system doesn't share a kind of person, it says why. A very large result is cut short, and says so.
</Step>
<Step title="Review">
Go through the list, deselect anyone you don't want, and give the rest types and tags.
</Step>
<Step title="Import the ones you chose">
Import them the same way as a spreadsheet: check the preview, then import. Each contact remembers the system they came from, so you can filter **Contacts** by source later.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Finding people never writes to your CRM, AMS or event platform, and never emails anyone. Only the people you import are added to Sessionboard CRM.
</Note>

## Speakers from a Sessionize event

Sessionize doesn't need a connection. Paste the public Sessionize API link to the event's speakers, which the event's organizer sets up in Sessionize, or the event's Sessionize ID. Sessionboard CRM reads the speakers, tells you who is new, and you review and import them like any other system.

## Active contacts

Importing people from another system costs nothing by itself: they count as active contacts only once you reach them. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).
