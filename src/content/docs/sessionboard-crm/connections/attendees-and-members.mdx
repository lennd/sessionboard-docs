Your speakers come in as contacts. Everyone else, such as the attendees of your events, your members and your prospects, comes into **People**, where you can see them beside your contacts and add the ones you'll work with. See [People: everyone in one place](/sessionboard-crm/people).

## What each system brings

| System | Who | Where they land |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot | Contacts, with their lifecycle stage and owner | **People**, as prospects or customers, or **Contacts**: you choose when you review them |
| Salesforce | Contacts | **People** or **Contacts**: you choose when you review them |
| iMIS | Members | **People**, as members |
| Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo | One event's speakers | **Contacts** |
| Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo | One event's attendees | **People**, as attendees of that event |
| ON24, Goldcast | One webinar's speakers | **Contacts** |

Using another system? Export a spreadsheet and import it. See [Add and import contacts](/sessionboard-crm/contacts/add-and-import#bring-people-in-from-another-system).

## Bring in members and prospects

<Steps>
<Step title="Find them">
On **Connections**, choose the system and what to look for. Sessionboard CRM reads the system and tells you how many it found, how many are new to you and how many you already know.
</Step>
<Step title="Review">
Deselect anyone you don't want. For HubSpot and Salesforce, choose whether they come in as people or as contacts.
</Step>
<Step title="Bring them in">
Check the preview, then bring them in. Members arrive with their membership; HubSpot prospects and customers with their lifecycle stage and owner.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Bring in an event's attendees

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the event">
On **Connections**, choose your event platform and the event. Sessionboard CRM counts its attendees first, without bringing anyone in.
</Step>
<Step title="Start the sync">
Choose the Sessionboard event they belong to, if there is one, and start. The event platform's own attendee sync brings them in, the same one Sessionboard Marketing uses. A big event takes a few minutes.
</Step>
</Steps>

Attendees land in **People** with what they registered for and came to, never in your contacts. Run it again after the event to bring in who checked in. Each run brings the attendees up to date.

## Keep a source in sync

**Keep in sync** brings in new people from a connected system every night, the same way you first brought them in: the same kinds of people, landing as contacts or people. Turn it off at any time; the people already brought in stay.

If you don't see **Keep in sync**, it isn't turned on for your organization. Ask us to turn it on, or bring people in again when you need to.

<Note>
Bringing people in only reads: nothing is written to your CRM, AMS or event platform, and nobody is emailed. People you bring in count as active contacts only once you reach them. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).
</Note>
