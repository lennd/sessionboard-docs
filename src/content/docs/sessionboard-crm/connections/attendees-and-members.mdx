Everyone comes into **People**, tagged for who they are to you: speakers, members, prospects, customers and attendees. Organizations come into **Groups**. See [People](/sessionboard-crm/people).

## What each system brings

| System | Who | Where they land |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot | Contacts, with their lifecycle stage and owner, and their companies | **People**, tagged **Prospect** or **Customer**, and **Groups** |
| Salesforce | Contacts and their companies | **People** and **Groups** |
| iMIS | Members | **People**, tagged **Member** |
| Fonteva, Nimble AMS, Protech | Members, with their member type, status and renewal date, and the registrations the AMS keeps | **People**, tagged **Member** while their membership is current, and **Companies** |
| Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo | One event's speakers | **People**, tagged **Speaker** |
| Cvent, Swoogo, Bizzabo | One event's attendees | **People**, tagged **Attendee**, with that event |
| ON24, Goldcast | One webinar's speakers | **People**, tagged **Speaker** |

Using another system? Export a spreadsheet and import it. See [Add and import people and groups](/sessionboard-crm/import#bring-people-in-from-another-system).

## Bring in members and prospects

<Steps>
<Step title="Find them">
On **Connections**, choose the system and what to look for. Sessionboard CRM reads the system and tells you how many it found, how many are new to you and how many you already know.
</Step>
<Step title="Review">
Deselect anyone you don't want, and check the tags they'll get. For HubSpot and Salesforce, choose whether their companies come in too.
</Step>
<Step title="Bring them in">
Check the preview, then bring them in. Members arrive with their membership; HubSpot prospects and customers with their lifecycle stage and owner.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Bring in an event's attendees

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the event">
On **Connections**, choose your event platform and the event. Sessionboard CRM counts its attendees first, without bringing anyone in.
</Step>
<Step title="Start the sync">
Choose the Sessionboard event they belong to, if there is one, and start. The event platform's own attendee sync brings them in, the same one Sessionboard Marketing uses. A big event takes a few minutes.
</Step>
</Steps>

Attendees land in **People**, tagged **Attendee**, with what they registered for and came to. Run it again after the event to bring in who checked in. Each run brings the attendees up to date.

## Keep a source in sync

**Keep in sync** brings in new people from a connected system every night, the same way you first brought them in: the same kinds of people, with the same tags. Turn it off at any time; the people already brought in stay.

For Fonteva, Nimble AMS and Protech, the nightly read also updates membership terms and takes the **Member** tag off people who lapsed: see [Keep members in sync nightly](/sessionboard-crm/ams/nightly-sync).

If you don't see **Keep in sync**, it isn't turned on for your organization. Ask us to turn it on, or bring people in again when you need to.

<Note>
Bringing people in only reads: nothing is written to your CRM, AMS or event platform, and nobody is emailed. People you bring in count as active contacts only once you reach them. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).
</Note>
