The **guest scheduler** gives you a booking link for speakers, podcast guests and interviewees. It checks every host's Google Calendar together, so the guest only sees times when all of you are free.

## Why use it

Finding a time with two hosts and a guest usually takes a handful of emails. With a booking link the guest picks a time in their own time zone, everyone gets the calendar invite, and the guest's record in your CRM shows they said yes.

## What the guest sees

The booking page shows the meeting's title, who it's with and how long it is. Times are shown in the guest's own time zone, taken from their browser, and they can switch to another zone. They pick a day and a time, add their name and email, and they're booked.

A time is offered only when:

- every host is free on their Google Calendar, with any buffer before and after kept clear;
- it falls in the link's working hours, in the link's time zone;
- it's later than the minimum notice and within the booking window.

## What happens when a guest books

- **One calendar event for everyone.** The event goes on the first host's calendar, with the other hosts and the guest invited, so it shows on every calendar.
- **A meeting link.** Google Meet, or a Zoom meeting through your organization's connected Zoom app.
- **Reschedule and cancel links.** The invite has both. They work without signing in, and a change updates every calendar.
- **Your pipeline.** The guest becomes a Contact (or is matched to one by email) and moves to **Confirmed** in your [pipeline](/sessionboard-crm/pipeline), with a note saying when they booked.
- **Hand-offs.** If the link has a release template, the release waits for you to send it from [Contracts](/sessionboard-crm/contracts). Sessionboard never sends an agreement on its own.

## Set it up with your assistant

Set up the scheduler from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant connected to Sessionboard, or through the API.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Create the link.** Ask, for example: *"Make a guest booking link for our show with me and Chris as hosts, 45 minutes, Tuesday and Thursday afternoons, with Zoom."*
</Step>
<Step>
**Connect each host's calendar.** Each host asks the assistant for their calendar link (or the assistant offers it) and opens it to connect their Google Calendar. Until every host is connected, the link isn't taking bookings.
</Step>
<Step>
**Share the link.** Ask *"Send me our guest booking link"* and paste it into your invitation.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hosts | Who must be free. The first host's calendar holds the event. |
| Time zone | The zone for working hours. By default, the first host's calendar time zone. |
| Duration and start times | How long the meeting is, and how often a start time is offered. |
| Buffers | Free time kept before and after each meeting. |
| Working hours | The windows on each day of the week when guests may book. |
| Minimum notice and window | How soon and how far ahead a guest may book. |
| Meeting link | Zoom, Google Meet or none. |
| Pipeline stage | The stage a booked guest moves to. Confirmed by default. |
| Release and prep brief | The release template to send after booking, and whether to ask for a prep brief. |

<Note>
Rescheduling or cancelling from the assistant asks you first, because Google emails every attendee about the change.
</Note>
