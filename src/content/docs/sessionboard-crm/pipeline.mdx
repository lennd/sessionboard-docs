**Pipeline** shows where each person stands on the way to your events: who you have in mind, who you've reached out to, who said yes and who said no. Each person in it is a contact from **Contacts**, so their details, history and notes stay in one place.

## Why use it

A shortlist in a spreadsheet goes stale the moment someone replies. With the pipeline, the team sees one list: who to ask next, who hasn't answered, and who has confirmed. Every move is kept with who made it and why, so the next person picks up where the last one left off.

## Stages

Your pipeline starts with eight stages. You don't set anything up: they're there the first time you open **Pipeline**.

| Stage | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Identified | Someone you'd like to work with. |
| Researching | You're checking their fit. |
| Approved | The team agreed to ask them. |
| Contacted | You've reached out. |
| Interested | They want to hear more. |
| Confirmed | They said yes. |
| Declined | They said no. |
| Future fit | Not this time, but worth asking again. |

Confirmed counts as won, declined as lost, and future fit as people to keep in touch with. The others are open.

## The board and the list

Switch between two views of the same people:

- **The board** has a column for each stage, with a card for each person and a count at the top of each column.
- **The list** shows one row per person with their stage, the event they're in the pipeline for, when they entered their stage, their last activity and how many contracts they have.

In either view:

- **Search** finds a name, an email or a company.
- **Filters** narrow it by stage, type (speaker, SME, influencer, exhibitor contact, sponsor contact), tag or event.
- **Sort** the list by name, company, stage, when they entered their stage, last activity, or when they were added.
- **Export** downloads what's on screen as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets.

## Add people from Contacts

<Steps>
<Step title="Select contacts">
On **Contacts**, select the people you want to track. Filter first to pick, say, every SME tagged with a topic.
</Step>
<Step title="Add them to the pipeline">
Choose the stage they start in (the first stage, unless you pick another) and, if they're for one event, the event.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can add up to 200 people at once. Anyone already in the pipeline is skipped and listed, so nobody is in it twice. You can also add one person from their contact page. Adding someone to the pipeline doesn't email them.

## Move someone to another stage

On the board, drag their card to another column. In the list, or on their contact page, choose a new stage. Add a note about why with the move, such as "Said yes on the call, sending the agreement." Moving someone doesn't email them.

## See a person's history

Open a person in the pipeline to see every move: the stage they came from and went to, who moved them, when, and the note. Their contact page shows their stage too, and **Contacts** has a stage filter and column.

## Take someone off the pipeline

Choose **Archive**. Sessionboard CRM asks you to confirm first. The contact stays in **Contacts** with their history, notes and contracts. To record a no, moving them to **Declined** or **Future fit** keeps them in view for next time.

<Note>
Putting someone in the pipeline doesn't make them an active contact. Emailing them, inviting them or sending them a contract does. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).
</Note>
