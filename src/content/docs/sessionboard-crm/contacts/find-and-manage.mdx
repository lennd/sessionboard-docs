**Contacts** lists everyone in Sessionboard CRM, 50 to a page. Each row shows the contact's name, email, company, job title, types and tags, how many of your events and sessions they've been part of, their last activity, and whether they're an active contact.

## Search and filter

- **Search** finds a name, an email or a company.
- **Filters** narrow the list by type (speaker, SME, influencer, exhibitor contact, sponsor contact), tag, source (where the contact came from, such as an import or a connected app), event, and active or not.
- Next to each filter value, a count shows how many contacts in the current search have it.

## Sort

Sort by name, company, last activity, number of events, or the date the contact was added, in either direction.

## Open a contact

Select a contact to see:

- **Their details**: name, email, company, job title, types and tags. Change any of them here.
- **Their history**: each time they spoke, sponsored, exhibited or attended one of your events, the campaigns that reached them, and the contracts and forms they were sent, with the event and session.
- **Notes**: what your team wrote about them. Add a note for the next person who works with them.

## Change many contacts at once

Select contacts in the list to add or remove a type or a tag on all of them. Types and tags you don't touch stay as they are.

## Export to a spreadsheet

Export downloads the contacts on screen as a CSV file, with the search, filters and sort you chose, up to 25,000 contacts. The file opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets.

## Delete contacts

Select the contacts and choose delete. Sessionboard CRM asks you to confirm before it removes them from your list.

<Note>
You don't need to delete a contact to keep your bill down: stored contacts are free, and someone you haven't reached in 12 months stops counting as active on their own. See [Active contacts](/sessionboard-crm/plan-and-billing/active-contacts).
</Note>
