Types say what a contact does for your events. Tags are your own lists. Both filter **Contacts**, both can be set on many contacts at once, and both can be given to everyone in an import.

## Types

A contact can have any of these five types, or several:

| Type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Speaker** | People who speak, or could speak, at your events. |
| **Subject-matter expert (SME)** | Experts you invite to review, advise or present in their field. |
| **Influencer** | People whose audience helps you promote an event. |
| **Exhibitor contact** | The people you work with at exhibiting companies. |
| **Sponsor contact** | The people you work with at sponsors. |

**Home** counts your contacts by type. Set types on a contact's page, on many contacts at once from **Contacts**, or for every row of an import.

<Note>
Types are saved on the contact as the **Contact type** field. If your organization moves to the Sessionboard platform, every contact keeps its types.
</Note>

## Tags

Tags group contacts however your team works: a keynote shortlist, a program committee, past speakers, a region.

- **Create a tag** on **Tags**, or while you import.
- **Tag contacts** on a contact's page, on many contacts at once from **Contacts**, or for every row of an import.
- **Rename a tag** on **Tags**. Every contact that has it keeps it.
- **Delete a tag** on **Tags**. Sessionboard CRM asks you to confirm. The contacts that had it stay in CRM; only the tag goes.

**Tags** shows how many contacts have each tag. To see them, filter **Contacts** by the tag.
