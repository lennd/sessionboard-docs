Every field event has one registration form. You edit it in the field event's **Settings**, under **Registration**: what it asks, who skips approval, and what happens after someone registers. The registration section saves on its own with **Save registration**.

## Why use it

A dinner invitation needs three questions, not a conference registration form. Keeping a field event to one short form means guests register in seconds, and you still get their dietary needs, who they are bringing and whether they want to hear from you — on the same record as the rest of what they do with your organization.

## Questions

Name, work email and organization are always asked. Add questions from the attendee fields — for example dietary needs or which session they are interested in — mark them required, or show one only after a certain answer.

## Who can register

**Who can register**, in the field event settings, decides who gets in:

- **Open** — anyone with the link.
- **Invite only** — only people on the invite list can register. Everyone else sees the page but not the form.
- **Invite, then approve** — anyone with the link can ask for a seat and you approve them. People on the invite list get a seat without approval.

Add people to the invite list from **People → Invite list**: one email per line, with optional columns `first_name, last_name, company, registrant_type`. Click **Add**, or **Upload CSV** for a longer list; **Replace the unused part of the list** swaps out invitees who have not registered yet. **People on the invite list skip approval** controls whether invitees are confirmed straight away.

## Registration types

![The field event registration form's questions and the Registration types section with Add a type](/images/kb/field-events-registration-types.png)

Everyone registers as a guest by default. Click **Add a type** for partners or sponsors, and set:

- the type's name,
- an **Invitation code** (optional) that unlocks it,
- how many **seats** one registration of this type covers,
- whether it **Skips approval** or **Needs approval**.

## Consent lines

Two consent checkboxes appear on the form — marketing and sharing with your account team. Both are unticked by default. Enter your own wording, or leave a line blank to use the standard wording. Each registration records which ones the guest ticked.

## After someone registers

- **Waitlist when it is full** — once the capacity is reached, people join the waitlist instead of being turned away. Needs a capacity in the field event settings.
- **Fill freed seats from the waitlist** — when someone cancels, the first person waiting is confirmed and emailed.
- **Guests** — people can bring someone on their registration. Set **At most** how many guests each; a registration type's seats can cap it.
- **Reminder the day before** — at 9:00 in the event's time zone, with the calendar invite and check-in code.
- **Reminder an hour before** — a short note with the address and the room.

![The After someone registers section with invite list, waitlist, guests and reminder switches](/images/kb/field-events-after-someone-registers.png)

Guests change or cancel from the link in their confirmation email.

## Registration page

Set the **Headline**, **Introduction**, **Header image URL** and **Accent** color people see above the form.

Click **Save registration** when you are done.
