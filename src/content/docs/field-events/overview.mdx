A **field event** is a small, single-room event your organization runs around the conference: a customer dinner, a hands-on lab, a workshop, a chapter meeting. You create and run it from **Program → Field Events** at the organization level, with registration, check-in, a page and follow-up built in.

<Note>
Field Events is in Early Access as a Beta. To ask for it, open **Early Access** in the organization sidebar, find the **Field Events** card and choose **Request access**. Our team sets it up with you.
</Note>

## Why use it

Field events are where relationships get built, but they usually run on a spreadsheet and a calendar invite. Running them in Sessionboard puts every guest on the same CRM record as their conference registration and speaking history, so you can see who came to the dinner, who they met and what they did next — without a separate tool for each one.

## Field events, events and webinars

| | Field event | Webinar | Event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **For** | Dinners, labs, workshops, chapter meetings — one room, one afternoon | An online session on your webinar platform | A conference with a call for papers, evaluations and an agenda |
| **Where you run it** | **Program → Field Events** | **Program → Webinars** | **Program → Events** |
| **Registration** | One registration form | One registration form | As many forms as you need, with Registration |
| **People** | Guests | Registrants | Contacts, speakers, registrants |

A field event can grow into a full event: **Settings → Convert** turns it into a Standard event with CFP, evaluations and the agenda builder, and everything you set up comes along.

## Create a field event

<Steps>
<Step title="Start it">
Open **Program → Field Events** and click **New field event**. You can also click **Add Program** and pick the **Field event** card.

![The Conference, Awards, Webinar and Field event cards with Field event selected](/images/kb/field-events-new-kind-cards.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the basics">
Give it a **Name**, when it **Starts** and **Ends**, and its **Time zone**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set where and how many">
Under **Where**, enter the **Venue**, **Address** and **Room**. Under **Capacity**, enter the number of seats, or leave it blank for no cap, and choose whether people join a waitlist when it is full.
</Step>
<Step title="Add hosts">
Under **Hosts**, add the names or emails of the people running the room. They get the host view on the day.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who can register">
Under **Who can register**, choose **Open** (anyone with the link), **Invite only** (only people you invite) or **Invite, then approve** (anyone with the link can ask; you approve, and invitees from your list skip approval).
</Step>
<Step title="Create it">
Click **Create and get the invite link**. The field event opens on its **Overview** as a **Draft**; its **Needs you** list shows what is left, such as naming a host and publishing the page. Click **Publish** at the top when the page is ready to share.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The field event settings for venue, address, room, capacity with a waitlist, hosts and who can register](/images/kb/field-events-new-where-who.png)

You can also choose **Recording** (off, some sessions or every session — recorded talks land in Media), and **Pathways · enroll everyone who registers**.

## Inside a field event

![A field event's header with its date and venue, the Overview, People, Program, Pages and After tabs, and the Needs you list](/images/kb/field-events-shell-overview.png)

Each field event has the tabs **Overview**, **People**, **Program** (the agenda), **Pages** and **After**, with **Settings** behind the gear.

- **People** lists every guest with their status — **Confirmed**, **To approve**, **Waitlist**, **Checked in**, **Cancelled** — and lets you approve, waitlist, check in or cancel several at once. **Invite list** holds the people you invited.
- **Settings** holds the basics, the field event settings, the [registration form](/field-events/registration-form) and [check-in links](/field-events/checkin).

![The Field Events page with Upcoming, Past and All tabs and a field event card](/images/kb/field-events-list.png)

**Program → Field Events** lists every field event as a card, with **Upcoming**, **Past** and **All** tabs and a search box.
