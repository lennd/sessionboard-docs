Field event check-in runs on staff phones, with no app to install. Staff open a door link, enter its PIN and check guests in; hosts get a live view of who has arrived.

## Why use it

At a dinner for forty, the host wants to know who is still on their way, not to hold a clipboard. A door link turns any phone into the guest list, and every arrival is recorded on the guest's CRM record as it happens.

## Create a door link

<Steps>
<Step title="Open check-in">
In the field event, open **Settings → Check-in**, or click **Check-in** on the **People** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Make a link">
Under **At the door**, give the link a label and click **New link**. The link, a QR code for it and the PIN show once — copy them now.
</Step>
<Step title="Share it">
Send the link to the person at the door, and the PIN in a separate message. They open the link on their phone, enter the PIN and check guests in. It works with no signal, and the QR code in each confirmation email scans too.
</Step>
</Steps>

To lock a phone out, revoke the link. The refresh button revokes it and makes a new link with a new PIN.

## Send hosts the live view

Under **Host view**, click **Send to hosts** to email the link and PIN to every host with an email, or **Link for me** to get one yourself. The host view shows who is in the room, live, by organization type, with no check-in buttons. Add hosts in the field event settings.

## Check guests in from People

On the **People** tab, tick guests and click **Check in** (or **Undo check-in**). Filter by **Checked in** to see who came.

See [Run the web check-in desk](/onsite/web-desk) for everything the door link can do, including walk-ins.
