A field event's form can take on features from Registration one at a time: paid tickets, promo codes, invoices, the Approvals queue and badges. Each is its own Early Access option, so a free dinner stays simple and a paid workshop gets exactly what it needs.

## Why use it

Some field events cost money — a hands-on lab with a fee, a members' workshop with a discount code, a partner summit billed to a company. These options let a field event charge, discount, invoice and badge its guests through the same checkout and orders as your conference registration, without turning it into a full event.

## Turn them on

All of these build on **Field Events on Registration**, an organization option that runs each field event's form on Registration. Ask for it from **Preview** in the organization sidebar.

Then each option is turned on per field event:

| Option (in Preview) | What it adds to the field event's form |
| --- | --- |
| **Field Events: paid tickets** | A **Tickets** section with the seat's price (**Edit price**), the **Payment gateway** it charges, and **How people pay** — card through your gateway. |
| **Field Events: promo codes** | A **Promo codes** section: add a code that takes a percentage off at checkout, and see how often each code was used. |
| **Field Events: invoices** | **Pay later by invoice** and **Deposit now, balance later** under **How people pay**. |
| **Approvals** | Requests that need approval also wait in the event's Approvals queue. Approving or declining them on **People** closes them there too. |
| **Field Events: badges & desk** | Printed badges at the door: the check-in desk links print them. |

Payment gateways are connected in **Organization settings → Payments**. With one gateway it is used automatically; with several, pick one. Gateways in test mode are marked **(test)**. Money goes straight to your gateway — Sessionboard is not in the flow of funds.

## Where things show up

- Paid field event orders appear with their payments, invoices and refunds like any registration order. See [Manage orders, invoices and refunds](/registration/orders-and-invoices).
- Badges print at the desk from the field event's check-in links. See [Run the web check-in desk](/onsite/web-desk).

<Note>
Without these options, a field event's registration is free and its form works as described in [Set up registration for a field event](/field-events/registration-form).
</Note>
