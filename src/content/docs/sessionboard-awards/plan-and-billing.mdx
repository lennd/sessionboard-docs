Every workspace starts with a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, with no card needed. Open **Plan and billing** from the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Plans

|  | Starter | Growth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $99 a month, or $948 a year | $299 a month, or $2,988 a year |
| Submitted nominations a year | 250 | 1,000 |
| Admin seats | 3 | 10 |
| Support | Email and Ask | Chat 9–6 ET and a setup call |

Both plans include unlimited programs, reviewers and categories. Only submitted nominations count toward the limit; drafts don't. Nominations are never refused mid-season: if you go over, we count them and let you know.

## Add a card and choose a plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Plan and billing">
Select the plan card at the bottom of the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick monthly or annual">
Use the **Monthly / Annual** toggle. Annual shows the monthly equivalent.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a plan">
Select **Choose** on the plan you want and complete checkout.
</Step>
</Steps>

During the trial you can build programs privately without a card. You need a card on file to open nominations to the public, and adding one doesn't end your trial.

## Card, invoices and cancelling

Select **Manage card and invoices** to update your card, download invoices or cancel.

## When a trial or plan ends

- In the last three days of a trial with no card, a banner reminds you to pick a plan.
- When the trial ends, or a plan is cancelled or unpaid, the workspace becomes read-only. Everything you made is kept.
- If a payment fails, the workspace keeps working for 14 days, then sending pauses until the payment goes through.

## Enterprise Awards

Review rounds and review plans, submission fees and payments, single sign-on, a named CSM with an SLA, and a connection to your events and Speaker CRM are part of Enterprise Awards on the Sessionboard platform. Select **Talk to us** on the Enterprise card to start that conversation.

<Note>
If Awards is on your Sessionboard contract, the billing page says so and there is nothing to pay here.
</Note>
