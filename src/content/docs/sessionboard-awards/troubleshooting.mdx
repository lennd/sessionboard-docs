import { Accordion } from '@compat';

<Accordion title='"Add at least one category before opening nominations."'>
Open the program, add a category on the **Categories** card, select **Save categories**, then open nominations again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title='"Add a card to open nominations to the public."'>
Go to **Plan and billing** and choose a plan. Your trial keeps going; the card is only charged when it ends.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't save winners">
Every submitted nomination needs a complete scorecard first. Go to **Review** to see who hasn't scored, assign more reviewers if needed, or have Ask remind them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My organization is read-only">
Your trial or plan has ended. Choose a plan on **Plan and billing**. Everything you made is still there.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A tile says Enterprise">
Review rounds, submission fees, single sign-on and a named CSM are part of Enterprise Awards. The tile opens the Enterprise page, where you can talk to us.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My sign-in code didn't arrive">
Codes are good for 10 minutes. Check your spam folder, then select **Send a new code**. Sign-up needs a work email address.
</Accordion>
