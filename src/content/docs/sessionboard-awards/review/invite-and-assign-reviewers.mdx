Reviewers score the nominations assigned to them on the program's scorecard, once each. You can invite and assign them while nominations are still open. They can start scoring when nominations close.

## Invite reviewers

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Review">
Go to **Review**. If you have more than one program, choose it from **Program**.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste names and emails">
Under **Invite reviewers**, paste one reviewer per line: a name and an email, for example `Grace Liu <grace@example.org>`. You can invite up to 50 at a time.
</Step>
<Step title="Send the invitations">
Select **Invite**. Each reviewer gets an email invitation.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Assign nominations

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose how many reviewers per nomination">
Under **Assign nominations**, pick **Reviewers per nomination**, from 1 to 5.
</Step>
<Step title="Assign">
Select **Assign**. Sessionboard gives each nomination to the reviewers with the fewest assignments first and tells you how many new assignments it made.
</Step>
</Steps>

Assigning again later keeps every existing assignment and only adds what is missing, so you can assign again after more nominations arrive.

## Follow progress

The tiles show **Reviewers**, **Assigned**, **Scored** and **Not started**. The reviewers table shows how many each person has scored. **Nominations by average score** ranks every nomination with its category, number of scorecards and average.

To nudge reviewers who haven't finished, ask: "Remind reviewers who haven't finished." Ask shows who it will email before it sends. See [Get things done with Ask](/sessionboard-awards/ask).

## What reviewers see

Reviewers sign in to your public awards page with a link sent by email and open **My Reviews**. Before nominations close, the review shows as upcoming and not yet open. Once it opens, they pick a nomination and fill in the scorecard.

<Note>
Self-serve programs have one review pass. More rounds are part of Enterprise Awards.
</Note>
