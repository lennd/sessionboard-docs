**Results** shows how every program is doing in one place.

- **Totals**: Programs, Nominations, Finished (the share of nominations that were completed), Scorecards and Winners.
- **One row per program**: its status, nominations, unfinished nominations, reviewers, scorecards and winners.

For a quick summary, ask "Show me this season's results."

**Home** also shows **This season**: nominations, reviewers, scorecards and winners across your programs, with the items **Ready for your review**.
