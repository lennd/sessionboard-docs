Your organization, and everything in it (your programs, nominations, reviews and winners), is stored in one region: the **United States** or the **European Union**. You choose it once, when you sign up.

## Choose a region when you sign up

Under your work email, the sign-up says where your data will be stored: **Your data is stored in the United States · Change**. Select **Change** to switch to the European Union, and select it again to switch back.

We start with the region that fits where you are. If you're in the EU, the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, it starts on the European Union. Otherwise, when your email's address ends in one of those countries' domains (such as `.de`, `.fr` or `.co.uk`), it starts on the European Union. Everyone else starts on the United States. Your own choice always wins.

Your email, code and answers go straight to the region you chose; they never pass through the other region. After you enter the 6-digit code, your organization is created there. If that region isn't the one the page opened on, you go straight to that region's address and carry on where you were. You don't sign up twice.

<Note>
You choose the region once. Moving a organization to the other region later is an Enterprise service: talk to us at [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com).
</Note>

## Sign in from anywhere

Use **Sign in** with your work email as usual. We ask the region you last signed in to first, so your email goes only to your own region, then send your code from there and open your organization at its own address:

| Region | Address |
| --- | --- |
| United States | `appv2.sessionboard.com` |
| European Union | `appv2-eu.sessionboard.com` |

If you've set a password, or your company signs in with SSO, you're sent to the sign-in page for your region.

## See where your data is stored

Ask the assistant: **Where is our data stored?** It answers with your organization's region.
