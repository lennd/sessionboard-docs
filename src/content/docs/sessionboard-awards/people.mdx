**People** lists everyone in your awards across every program, one row per person.

| Role | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **Nominee** | Someone a nomination is about. A nominee without an email is listed by name, once per nomination. |
| **Nominator** | Someone who sent or started a nomination. |
| **Reviewer** | Someone you invited to review. |
| **Winner** | A nominee you picked as a winner. Finalists stay nominees. |
| **Team** | People on your organization's team. |
| **Added** | People you added by hand or from a CSV. |

A person can have several roles, for example a reviewer who also nominated someone.

## Find someone

- Select a role to show only those people. Each role shows how many people have it.
- Search by name, email or organization.
- Select a person to see their details.

## Add, import and export

- **Add person** adds one person by email.
- **Import CSV** adds several. You see what will be added before anything is saved.
- **Export CSV** downloads the list as it is filtered.

<Note>
People in your awards don't count toward the contacts on a Sessionboard Marketing plan, even when your organization has both.
</Note>
