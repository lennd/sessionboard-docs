**Apps** lists how people get in, what your own tools can reach, and which assistants are connected.

## Why use it

Run your programs from Claude or ChatGPT, read results in your own tools, and keep winners on each contact in your CRM, without anyone setting up passwords.

## What's here

- **Sign-in.** **A link by email**: no passwords, for nominators and reviewers.
- **AI assistants.** Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants connected over MCP. Select **Manage AI assistants** to see what each may do and what it did. Opening nominations, inviting reviewers and announcing always wait for a person to approve.
- **Your own tools.** The **Public API** reads programs, Review progress and results, with `read:awards` and `write:awards` keys: select **Create a key**, or **API docs**. **HubSpot and Salesforce** put winners and finalists on each contact.

You can also tell Ask, for example "Connect Claude".
