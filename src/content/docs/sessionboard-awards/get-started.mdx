Sessionboard Awards runs a nominations program from start to finish. People nominate, your reviewers score the nominations on a scorecard, and you pick and announce the winners. Sessionboard drafts the first program for you, so you start by reviewing a draft rather than filling in a blank form.

## Sign up

<Steps>
<Step title="Start your trial">
Go to the **Start Sessionboard Awards** page and answer the first question: **First, who are these awards for?** Choose **Members**, **Customers and partners**, **An industry** or **Employees**. Your answer picks three starting categories and whether people nominate someone else or themselves.
</Step>
<Step title="Say what you are awarding">
Type your categories separated by commas, or choose **Use the usual ones**. Then pick when nominations close, or choose **Not set yet** to close them 30 days after you open them.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the draft">
You see the program Sessionboard will draft: your categories, a nomination form, a scorecard for your reviewers with one review pass, and the **Winner** and **Finalist** recognitions. Nothing is public until you open nominations.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm your work email">
Enter your work email. We email you a link and a 6-digit code: select **Continue setting up** in the email, or type the code. Either one works once, for 15 minutes. There is no password. Personal email addresses are not accepted. Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and use your work account.
</Step>
<Step title="Open your program">
Select **Open my program**. Your organization starts a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, with no card needed.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you already have a Sessionboard account with that email, sign in instead.
</Note>


## Sign in

There is no password. Go to **Sign in to Sessionboard Awards**, enter your work email and select **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the email and select **Sign in**, or type the 6-digit code on the page. The link and the code work once, for 15 minutes. If your email opens the link for you, it asks you to select **Continue as** your address first, so the link is still good when you get to it.

Or select **Continue with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**, when you see them, and pick your work account. The first time you use one with an account that already exists, we email you a 6-digit code to confirm it's you; after that, it signs you straight in. Two-step sign-in still applies when your team uses it.

If you set a password for this account before, it no longer signs you in here: use the emailed link, the code, Google or Microsoft. An account that also works in Sessionboard's platform keeps its password, and an account whose company uses SSO keeps that sign-in.

To change the language of the sign-up and sign-in pages, select the globe at the top right: English, Français, Deutsch or Español. A new account is made in that language, and the sign-in email is written in it.

Two-step sign-in for an account that signs in by email uses an authenticator app or a passkey, not emailed codes, because the sign-in email already goes to that inbox. If you turned on emailed codes before, you keep them.

## What your first program contains

- **Categories**: the ones you named, up to 50.
- **Nomination form**: Nominee name, Nominee organization, Summary of achievement and Evidence of impact.
- **Scorecard**: Impact, Originality and Evidence, each scored 1 to 5, plus a comment box.
- **One review pass**, named Review. It runs for 21 days after nominations close.
- **Winner** and **Finalist**, for you to award from the scores.

You can change the categories and the closing date. The nomination form and the scorecard are shown on the program page for you to check.

## The Getting started checklist

**Home** shows a checklist under **Setting up** your organization. Each step opens the screen that does it, and nothing goes out until you say so:

1. Say who the awards are for
2. Draft your first program
3. Open nominations
4. Invite reviewers
5. Assign nominations to reviewers
6. Pick winners from the scores

## Find your way around

The sidebar has **Home**, **Programs**, **Review**, **Winners**, **Results**, **People**, **Brand** and **Apps**. The rail on the right has **Ask**, **Learn**, **Help** and **Wish**. Your plan and its usage are on the card at the bottom of the sidebar.

**Switch apps.** The nine-dot button next to the Sessionboard logo lists every Sessionboard app: Platform, Sessions, Awards, Learning and Marketing. The apps your organization has open in one select. The others open a page about the app, where an admin can add it with a 14-day trial and no card, or book a demo of the platform.
