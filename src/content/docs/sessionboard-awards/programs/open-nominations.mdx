Opening nominations puts the program on your organization's public awards page and starts taking nominations. Closing them starts the review pass.

## Before you open

- The program needs at least one category.
- No card is needed during the trial.

## During your trial

During the trial your programs take up to 50 nominations in all. Past that, nominations are still accepted, so nobody is turned away and no one loses what they wrote. The program page then says **Choose a plan to keep receiving nominations**, and announcing the winners waits until you choose one. Choosing a plan ends the limit at once, and your trial keeps going until its last day.

## Open nominations

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the program">
Go to **Programs** and select the program.
</Step>
<Step title="Open nominations">
Select **Open nominations**. The status changes to **Nominations open**.
</Step>
<Step title="Share the link">
Select **Copy link**, or **Public page** to see what nominators see.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Change the closing date

On the **Nominations** card, enter a **New closing date** and select **Save date**. The card shows when nominations opened and close and when review runs.

## What nominators see

Your public page asks people to enter their email for a sign-in link. The link expires in 30 minutes. After signing in, they choose an open program and a category, fill in the nomination form and submit. They can attach supporting files from their computer, up to three per nomination and 250 MB each.

After they submit, they see **Nomination received!** with a confirmation number and can download their nomination as a PDF. **My Submissions** shows what they have sent and lets them finish a nomination they started.

Nominating is free. Self-serve programs do not charge a submission fee.

## Close nominations

Select **Close nominations** on the program page. The status changes to **In review**, and reviewers can start scoring.

<Warning>
Closing early starts review now. Nominators can no longer submit or finish a nomination.
</Warning>

## Remind people who haven't finished

While nominations are open, Home lists unfinished nominations in each program. Ask can email those nominators a reminder. You see who it will email before anything is sent, and nobody gets the same reminder twice in 24 hours. See [Get things done with Ask](/sessionboard-awards/ask).
