Each awards program has its own categories, nomination form, scorecard and nomination window. A new program starts as a **Draft**, and only you and your team can see it until you open nominations.

## Create a program

![The Programs page with the New program button and one program, its status and its numbers](/images/suites/awards/awards-programs-list.png)

<Steps>
<Step title="Start a new program">
Go to **Programs** and select **New program**.
</Step>
<Step title="Name it">
Enter a **Program name**. You can also set **Nominations close** now. Past dates are not accepted, and you can change the date later.
</Step>
<Step title="Draft it">
Select **Draft it**. The program opens with starting categories, a nomination form and a scorecard.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also ask for it from Home: select **Ask** and describe the program you want.

## Edit the categories

On the program page, the **Categories** card has one row per category.

1. Edit a name in place, select ✕ to remove a category, or select **Add a category**.
2. Select **Save categories**.

A program keeps at least one category, and it can have up to 50.

## Check the form and the scorecard

- **Nomination form** lists the questions nominators answer, grouped by section. Required questions are marked with an asterisk. The form works as it comes; select **Edit form** to add questions, change them or preview the form. See [Edit the nomination form](/sessionboard-awards/programs/nomination-form).
- **Review pass and scorecard** lists what each reviewer scores. Every reviewer scores their assigned nominations once, on this scorecard.

Who can nominate is shown on the **Nominations** card: **People nominate themselves.** or **People nominate someone else.** It comes from who the awards are for.

## Program statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Only your team can see it. |
| **Nominations open** | It is on your public page and taking nominations. |
| **In review** | Nominations are closed and reviewers are scoring. |
| **Closed** | Review is over. |
| **Archived** | Kept for your records. |

<Note>
Several review rounds, submission fees and single sign-on are part of Enterprise Awards. Your program shows them as locked tiles that open the Enterprise page.
</Note>
