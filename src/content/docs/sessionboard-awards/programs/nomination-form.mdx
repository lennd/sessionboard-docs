Every new program comes with a nomination form that works without changes. Edit it only when you want to ask something else.

## The form you start with

| Section | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| **About you** | Your first name, last name and email (required) |
| **Your nomination** | Nominee first name and last name (required), nominee email, nominee organization, **Summary of achievement** (required), **Evidence of impact**, **Supporting file** |

**Nominee email** is optional, so someone who doesn't know it can still nominate. **Supporting file** takes up to three files: documents, slides, spreadsheets, images, video or audio, up to 250 MB each.

The program page lists the questions under **Nomination form**, grouped by section.

## Edit the form

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the form">
Go to **Programs**, select the program, and select **Edit form** on the **Nomination form** card.
</Step>
<Step title="Add or change questions">
Select **Add Field** in a section to add a question, or pick one from **Quick Add** on the right, for example **Nominee Headshot** or **CV/Resume**. Select a question to change its label, help text, whether it's required, or its options. Drag questions to reorder them.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview it">
Select **Preview** to see the form the way a nominator does, with the categories they can choose.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Select **Save form**. Nothing changes on your public page until you save.
</Step>
</Steps>

The names and emails of the nominator and the nominee are always on the form, so every nomination can be reported and announced. You can rename them, but you can't remove them.

## Question types

Short answer, long answer, formatted text, email, link, phone, number, choice, multiple choice, checkboxes, date, file upload, signature and co-authors.

## More options

- **Rules** sets limits that apply across several questions.
- **Internal fields** are questions only your team sees and fills in.
- **Success page** is the message nominators see after they submit.
- **Download Form** gives you a blank copy of the form to share before nominations open.
- A question can depend on the category or on another answer: open the question and add a condition.

## What the reviewers see

Reviewers see the nominator's answers on each nomination, with the nominee's name as its title. They score on the program's scorecard, which you set on the program page. See [Draft an awards program](/sessionboard-awards/programs/draft-a-program#edit-the-scorecard) and [Invite and assign reviewers](/sessionboard-awards/review/invite-and-assign-reviewers).
