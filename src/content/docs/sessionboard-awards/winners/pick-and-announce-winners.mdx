import { Note, Steps, Step, Warning } from '@compat';

You pick the winners from the scores. Nothing is sent until you announce.

## Pick the winners

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Winners">
Go to **Winners** and choose the program.
</Step>
<Step title="Set each recognition">
For each nominee, set **Recognition** to **Winner**, **Finalist** or **None**. The table shows each nomination's category and average score.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Select **Save**. You can save once every submitted nomination has a complete scorecard. Until then, Sessionboard tells you how many still need one.
</Step>
</Steps>

Ask can show you the scores, but it never picks winners. Only a person does.

## Announce the winners

During a trial, a program with more than 50 nominations announces once you choose a plan. See [Plan and billing](/sessionboard-awards/plan-and-billing).

<Steps>
<Step title="Add a note">
In the **Announce** card, add **A line from you (optional)**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who hears">
Leave **Thank everyone else who was nominated** on to also thank the people whose nominees weren't picked.
</Step>
<Step title="Send">
Select **Announce the winners**. The card shows the emails as they go out.
</Step>
</Steps>

The emails go to the people who submitted each nomination. A winner's nominator hears "Congratulations. *Nominee* is a Winner in *Program*." The others get a thank-you for their nomination.

Winners and finalists also hear it themselves when the nomination gave their email: "Congratulations, *Nominee*. You are a Winner in *Program*." Nominees who weren't picked are not emailed. When someone nominated themselves, they get one email.

<Warning>
The announcement is sent once and can't be unsent. If some emails don't go through, the button changes to retry just those.
</Warning>

## Let the public vote: People's Choice

On **Winners**, **People's Choice** opens a public vote on your awards page. Anyone who signs in with their email can vote once in each category.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the ballot">
Pick **Every nomination**, or **Finalists only** once you've marked finalists above.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the closing date">
Voting runs until the end of that day.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the vote">
Select **Open the public vote**. Copy **The ballot** link to share it.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Public votes** then shows beside each nomination's average score, with how many people have voted. Select **Close the vote now** to end it early.

- Voters see the nominee and the nomination. They never see who nominated whom, or any email address or phone number.
- Votes never change reviewers' scores. Pick the People's Choice winner on Winners like any other.
- People's Choice is included in the Growth plan and during a trial. Weighted voter groups and voter lists are part of Enterprise Awards.

## Help finalists share the news

Select **Give every finalist a share kit with Marketing** to open Sessionboard Marketing, which makes share kits for each finalist.
