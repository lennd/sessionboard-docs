---
title: "Sessionboard standard fields"
description: "When working within the Sessionboard platform, understanding the structure and classification of fields is key to successfully organizing, collecting, and utilizing data across your events."
---

Standard fields are the fields every Sessionboard event starts with, before you add any custom ones. Each belongs to one of three types — Contact, Session, or Group — and that type determines which forms, views, and imports it is available in.

Use this page to look up a field's type and where it applies when you are building a submission form, a portal form, or an import template.

---
