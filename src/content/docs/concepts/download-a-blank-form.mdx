**Download Form** gives you an offline copy of a form, as a PDF or a Word document. It is the whole form in the order a submitter sees it: every question, section heading, divider, text block, and description. Questions that only appear after a particular answer are included too, each with a note saying when it shows.

Use it to send the questions to a committee for sign-off, to let speakers draft long answers before they open the form, or to answer a submitter who asks for a printable version.

## Download a form

Open the ⋯ menu on the form, choose **Download Form**, and pick **PDF** or **Word (.docx)**.

![The ⋯ menu on a submission form with Download Form open, offering PDF and Word (.docx)](/images/kb/download-form-menu.png)

The file is built in the background. It shows up in the export tracker at the bottom of the screen and opens in a new tab when it's ready, usually within a few seconds.

| Form | Where to find **Download Form** |
| --- | --- |
| Submission form | ⋯ on the form in **Program > Forms**, or the **Download Form** button in the form editor |
| Application form | ⋯ on the form in **Program > Applications**, or the button in the form editor |
| Portal form | ⋯ on the form in **Program > Portals > Forms**, or the button in the form editor |
| Intake form | The **Download Form** button in the sponsor or exhibitor intake form editor |
| Interest form | **Settings** on the interest form's dashboard in **CRM > Interest Forms** |
| Awards submission form | The **Download Form** button next to **Preview** in the program's form builder |

<Note>
The download is built from the last saved version of the form. Save your changes in the editor first if you want them in the copy.
</Note>

## What the copy includes

- **Every question, in form order**, with its description and a space for the answer: a line for short text, a box for long text, and a placeholder for file uploads, dates, and signatures.
- **Choices for dropdowns, radio buttons, and checkboxes**, marked **Choose one.** or **Choose all that apply.**
- **Required questions** marked with a red asterisk, and any limits you set: minimum and maximum words or characters, the number of selections allowed, and the maximum file size.
- **Section headings, dividers, and text blocks** where you placed them.
- **Conditional questions**, each with a line such as *Shown if "Session format" is "Workshop".* Questions that depend on a participant's role say which roles see them.

Submission forms come out in the same steps as the online form: submitter, session, sub-sessions, and participants. Repeating steps say so, for example *Complete once for each participant.*

Awards forms open with a **Category** question listing the categories a nominator can pick, so questions shown only for certain categories make sense on paper.

Questions marked admin-only on awards and interest forms aren't in the copy, because submitters never see them.

<Tip>
Choose **Word (.docx)** when you want to edit the copy, such as adding instructions for a committee or deleting sections that don't apply. Choose **PDF** to send it out as it is.
</Tip>

## Collect answers online

The copy is a blank reference. Answers typed into it don't reach Sessionboard. To capture a submission, send people the form's link. See [Which form should I use?](/concepts/which-form-should-i-use) for where each form's link lives.
