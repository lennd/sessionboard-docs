Portals hand a task to everyone who matches their filters. When a handful of people need something extra — a visa letter, a second agreement — assign it to those records directly from the **Contacts**, **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors** table. Nothing changes for anyone else, and anything assigned this way can be taken back.

<Note>
Direct assignment works for contact tasks and group tasks. Submission tasks are assigned through a portal's **Assign By Filter** setting — see [Create & assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks#assign-the-task-to-a-portal).
</Note>

## Assign to one record

<Steps>
<Step title="Show the task column">
Open the table and make sure the task is a column. If it is not, open **Columns**, search for the task, and tick it — see [Dashboard views](/reporting/dashboard-views).

![A task column in the contacts table](/images/kb/contacts-task-column.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Click the grey plus">
In that column, the grey plus on a row means **Not Assigned**. Click it and confirm with **Assign**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set a due date if you need one">
Click the icon again and choose **Options**. **Assigned Task Options** has the same controls as a portal assignment: **Due Date**, **Extended Due Date**, **Required** and **Make Completed Tasks View-Only**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Assign to several records at once

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the records">
Tick the checkbox on each row (or select all matching). The bulk action bar appears above the table.
</Step>
<Step title="Assign → Tasks, Forms or File Requests">
Click **Assign** and pick **Tasks**, **Forms** or **File Requests**.

![The bulk action bar with Assign open](/images/kb/contacts-bulk-assign.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the items, then the settings">
Tick the items you want and click **Next**. The second screen sets the due date, whether the assignment is required, and whether it closes once complete. Click **Assign**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Unassign

Click the task icon on the record and choose **Unassign Task**. Only tasks that were **Manually Assigned** (amber clipboard) can be removed this way. A task that shows as **Assigned Via Portal** (blue circle) came from a portal's filters — to take it away, remove it from that portal or change the portal's criteria.

## Reading the column

| Icon | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green check | **Complete** | Done. |
| Blue circle | **Assigned Via Portal** | Reached them through a portal; still open. |
| Amber clipboard | **Manually Assigned** | Assigned directly to this record; still open. |
| Grey plus | **Not Assigned** | Click to assign. |

File requests add a yellow clock for **Pending Approval** and red for **Rejected**; forms show **Submitted** in place of Complete.
