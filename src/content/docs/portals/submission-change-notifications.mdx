Turn on **Submission change notifications** when speakers need to hear about last-minute program edits without you sending each email by hand. Sessionboard emails the portal users on that submission, and shows the same alert in their portal notification centre.

<Note>
**Notifications & Messaging** must be on for the organization. Anyone with **Manage Early Access** can switch it on under **Org → Early Access**. Until it is on, the portal toggle is hidden. See [Early Access](/get-started/early-access) and [Notifications and messaging](/communications/notifications).
</Note>

## What speakers are told about

Sessionboard emails speakers when:

- A field **this portal shows** changes. The list is **Manage Fields → Submission Fields** (the same list as **Which fields?** on the preview card, plus time and room).
- **Start time**, **end time**, or **location / room** changes — including agenda drag-and-drop. Those three always notify, even when they are hidden on the portal.
- They are **assigned as a participant** on the submission, when **Participants** is on that portal (it is on by default).

Custom session fields follow the Manage Fields rule: visible on the portal, they notify; hidden, they do not.

| Change | Speakers emailed? |
| --- | --- |
| A field this portal shows (title, description, track, a custom field, and so on) | Yes |
| The same field, hidden on this portal | No — the session still updates |
| Start time, end time, or location / room | Yes — even if those fields are hidden |
| The person who saved the change | Never |
| Another speaker on the same submission | Yes, when the field notifies |

<Warning>
Hiding Title, Track, or a custom field still suppresses that email. Time and room do not need to be on **Manage Fields** for agenda moves to email speakers.
</Warning>

## Who receives it

On a given submission, Sessionboard emails portal users who:

- Can see the submission in **My Submissions** (a participant role, the submitter, or a sponsor/exhibitor contact on that session)
- Belong to a portal — including the **Default People Portal**, not only custom filtered portals
- Have **Submission change notifications** turned on for **that** portal

The person who saved the change is not emailed. Other speakers on the same submission are.

Someone newly assigned as a participant gets a separate “added to a submission” email when **Participants** is visible on their portal. They do not need to have logged in yet.

<Tip>
Turn the toggle on for every portal that should notify, including the default catch-all. Contacts who do not match a custom portal’s filters live on the default portal — see [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101).
</Tip>

## How to turn it on

<Steps>

<Step title="Turn on Notifications & Messaging">
At the organization, open **Early Access** and enable **Notifications & Messaging**.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the portal’s Configuration step">
Go to **Portals**, open the portal, and go to **Configuration**.
</Step>

<Step title="Enable Submission change notifications">
Turn on **Submission change notifications**. Click **Save and Next** — the setting only persists when you save.
</Step>

<Step title="Check which fields notify">
On the preview card, open **Which fields?**. Title, description, track, and other fields follow **Manage Fields → Submission Fields**. Start time, end time, and location always notify.
</Step>

<Step title="Preview or send a test">
Use **Preview as** to see **Submission field updated** or **Assigned as a participant**. Send a test to yourself before going live.
</Step>

</Steps>

Repeat for each portal that should notify (default and any custom portals).

## What the email and in-product alert look like

Speakers get both:

- An **in-product** notification in the portal (and in the header bell when Notifications & Messaging is on)
- An **email**, usually within a minute or two after you stop editing

Rapid edits to the same session within about a minute are combined into one notification that lists the fields that changed, for example Title, Start time, Location.

Emails use the same layout as portal assignment emails (event name and logo when the event has a logo). Portal users who chose a **daily summary** in their notification preferences receive these in that digest instead of immediately; everyone else defaults to immediate.

Event team members who watch a session still get the usual in-product **session updated** alert. That is separate from this speaker email.

## What does not send an email

These do not produce a submission-change email, by design:

- A field that is **not** on **Manage Fields → Submission Fields** for that portal — except start time, end time, and location, which always notify
- The person who made the edit (other speakers on the session still can be emailed)
- Imports, integrations, and agent writes
- Submission form and follow-up form saves (those have their own emails)
- A portal with the toggle off, or an organization without Notifications & Messaging

## If speakers did not get an email

Work through these in order:

1. Confirm the toggle is on **and saved** (**Save and Next**) on the portal the contact actually belongs to — including the **Default People Portal**.
2. Confirm **Notifications & Messaging** is on for the organization.
3. Confirm the recipient is a **different** portal user than the person who saved.
4. Wait about two minutes. Rapid edits to the same session are combined into one email.
5. Check **History > Emails**. If nothing was sent, the notification did not fire. If it was sent, see [Why you aren't receiving emails](/faq/why-am-i-not-receiving-emails).
