A resource page lives inside the portal. Unlike a [file](/portals/share-files), it is a page you write and edit here — hotel and travel details, brand guidelines, an FAQ for sponsors — and every portal it is assigned to shows the current version the next time someone opens it. Pages can pull in the reader's own details with merge tags, so one "Your booth" page reads differently for every exhibitor.

## Create a page

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Resources">
Go to **Program → Portals → Resources** and click **Add → Add Page**.

![The Resources library under Portals](/images/kb/portal-resources-library.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Write it">
![The page editor with Title, Subtitle, Type and the content area](/images/kb/portal-page-editor.png)

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Shown on the portal home. |
| **Subtitle** | One line under the title on the portal home. |
| **Type** | **General** for content that is the same for everyone. **Contacts** or **Groups** unlocks that record's merge tags — the reader's name, company, booth number — and limits the page to portals of that kind. |
| Content | Rich text: headings, lists, images, links. **Insert Merge Tag** drops in a field placeholder. |

Click **Save**. On a **Contacts** or **Groups** page, **Preview as** in the header then reads the saved page as a specific contact or group, with the merge tags filled in from their record. **General** pages do not support merge tags, so the preview is not offered there.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Changing **Type** after you have inserted merge tags can leave tags that no longer resolve — the editor warns you; review the content after switching.
</Note>

## Assign the page to a portal

Open the **⋯** menu on the portal card and choose **Edit Tasks**. In the **Resources** section of the **Assign Items** step click **Add**, tick the page, and click **Add Selected**. The pencil under **Actions** lets you give it an **Alias** for that portal.

![The Assign Items step, with its content sections](/images/kb/portal-editor-assign-items.png)

A page can sit on as many portals as you like. Readers find it under the portal's resources; check with **Preview as…** on the portal editor ([Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#see-it-as-a-speaker)).
