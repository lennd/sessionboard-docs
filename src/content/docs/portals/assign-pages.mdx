---

Before you can assign a page to a portal, you first need to create the page.

## Create a resource
Navigate to the Portals module and select "Resources" at the top of the page.

![Create A Resource](/images/kb/2eb4719b-image-png-Feb-25-2026-02-46-51-2694-PM.png)

Select the blue "Add Page" button and complete the following fields:

![Create A Resource (2)](/images/kb/02232d5f-undefined-Feb-25-2026-02-48-22-5333-PM.png)

1. Title: This is visible from the portal home page.
2. Subtitle: This is visible from the portal home page listed below the title.
3. Page Content: This will be the body of your page. Add text, images, and links to share additional information with your portal users. Once complete, click the "Save Page" button at the bottom of the pop-up window.

## Assign a resource page
Now that you have created your page, it is time to assign it to the respective portal.

<Note>
Pages can be assigned to more than one portal.
</Note>

Within the Portals module, select the ellipses to the right of the portal you would like to assign the wiki page to and select Edit Tasks.

![Assign A Resource Page](/images/kb/829167ab-image-png-Feb-25-2026-02-49-31-1707-PM.png)
 Within the Pages widget, select the Add button to open a pop-up module. This will show any resources you have created to select and assign to your portal.

![Assign A Resource Page (2)](/images/kb/782739cd-image-png-Feb-25-2026-02-50-09-6397-PM.png)
 From the list of pages you have created, select the page(s) you would like to assign to this portal. Once done, click the "Add Selected" button at the bottom right-hand corner of the pop-up module.
