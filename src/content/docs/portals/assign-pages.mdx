A resource page lives inside the portal. Unlike a [file](/portals/share-files), it is a page you write and edit here — hotel and travel details, brand guidelines, an FAQ for sponsors — and every portal it is assigned to shows the current version the next time someone opens it. Pages can pull in the reader's own details with merge tags, so one "Your booth" page reads differently for every exhibitor.

## Why use it

Speakers and sponsors ask the same logistics questions every year. A resource page answers them once, in the portal they already use, and stays current when the hotel or the deadline changes — no re-sending PDFs. Assign a page to a whole portal for shared details, or to individual people for the ones only they should see.

## Create a page

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Resources">
Go to **Program → Portals → Resources** and click **Add → Add Page**.

![The Resources library under Portals](/images/kb/portal-resources-library.png)

The library's order is the order portal users see. Drag a page by its handle, or use **Move up** and **Move down**, and the new order saves at once and applies to every portal of the event — put the onboarding checklist first and the venue map last. Reordering pauses while a search filters the list.
</Step>
<Step title="Write it">
![The page editor with Title, Subtitle, Type and the content area](/images/kb/portal-page-editor.png)

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Shown on the portal home. |
| **Subtitle** | One line under the title on the portal home. |
| **Type** | **General** for content that is the same for everyone. **Contacts** or **Groups** unlocks that record's merge tags — the reader's name, company, booth number — and limits the page to portals of that kind. |
| Content | Rich text: headings, lists, images, links. **Insert Merge Tag** drops in a field placeholder. |

Click **Save**. On a **Contacts** or **Groups** page, **Preview as** in the header then reads the saved page as a specific contact or group, with the merge tags filled in from their record. **General** pages do not support merge tags, so the preview is not offered there.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Changing **Type** after you have inserted merge tags can leave tags that no longer resolve — the editor warns you; review the content after switching.
</Note>

## Assign the page to a portal

Open the **⋯** menu on the portal card and choose **Edit Tasks**. In the **Resources** section of the **Assign Items** step click **Add**, tick the page, and click **Add Selected**. The pencil under **Actions** opens **Edit Settings**, where **Assign By Filter** shows the page only to portal users with at least one submission matching the **Submission Filters**, for example *Format is Poster*. The filter works only when **Control Submission Visibility** is on in the portal's **Configuration**. Leave it off and everyone in the portal sees the page.

![The Assign Items step, with its content sections](/images/kb/portal-editor-assign-items.png)

A page can sit on as many portals as you like. Readers find it under the portal's resources; check with **Preview as…** on the portal editor ([Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#see-it-as-a-speaker)).

## Assign pages to contacts, sessions or groups

You can also assign straight from the library. Each page in **Program → Portals → Resources** carries a badge: **Everyone** when it is not assigned to anyone individually, so it shows to everyone in the portals it is on, or **N assigned** when it is.

![Three pages in Resources, two with an Everyone badge and one with a 2 assigned badge, with one page ticked and the Assign to contacts, sessions or groups button in the selection bar](/images/kb/portal-resources-assignment-badges.png)

1. Tick the pages in **Resources**, then click **Assign to contacts, sessions or groups** in the bar that appears.
2. In the dialog, choose a tab: **Contacts** for specific people, **Sessions** for every participant of a session, or **Groups** for every member of a group. Search, tick the targets (you can tick on more than one tab), and click **Assign**.

![The Assign 1 resource dialog on its Contacts tab with two contacts ticked, the Sessions and Groups tabs beside it, and the Assign button](/images/kb/portal-resources-assign-dialog.png)

An assignment adds to what a portal shows: the people you pick see the page in their portal in addition to the resources the portal shows everyone, just as a manually assigned task does. Selecting and assigning needs permission to update portal tasks; everyone who can open Resources sees the badges. Files work the same way, see [Upload & share portal files](/portals/share-files#assign-a-file-to-contacts-sessions-or-groups).

## Assign a page to individual contacts

When only a few people need a page — a visa letter, a personal itinerary — assign it to those records instead of to a whole portal.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the people">
In **Contacts** (or **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors**), tick the records.
</Step>
<Step title="Assign → Resources">
Click **Assign** in the bulk action bar and pick **Resources**. The list offers **General** pages and pages of that list's type. Tick the pages and click **Assign**. Resources have no due date, so there is no second screen.

![The Assign Resources panel for 2 selected records, with Speaker FAQs and Day of Speaker Checklist ticked and the Assign button](/images/kb/contacts-assign-resources-dialog.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

The page then appears in each person's portal with the portal's own resources. A page that is on their portal as well is listed once.

Once a page is assigned to anyone individually, only those people — and people whose portal already lists it — can open it; anyone else following the link gets "not found". A page with no individual assignments works exactly as before.

To see or undo individual assignments, open the page's **⋯** menu in **Resources** and choose **Assigned to…**. The **Assigned to** sheet lists each contact, session and group the page is assigned to; **Remove** takes one assignment away, or tick several and use **Remove selected**. Assigning the same page to the same person twice does nothing. Removing an individual assignment does not affect a portal the page is on.
