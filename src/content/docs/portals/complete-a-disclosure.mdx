Some events — accredited continuing-education programs in particular — ask every participant to disclose financial relationships that could relate to their role. When an event asks you, the request appears in your [portal](/portals/portals-101) as a task you complete in a couple of minutes without leaving the page. This article is for the participant doing it; organizers set it up in [Disclosures](/contacts/disclosures).

## Why use it

You already sign in to the portal to confirm your session, upload slides or answer a form. The disclosure is one more task in the same place, so there is no separate email to find, no link to keep, and nothing to print and scan. You see it only when an event actually needs one from you, and a disclosure you complete once covers every event the organizer runs until it is due for renewal. Completing it in the portal also clears the organizer's reminder, so you are not asked again by email for the same thing.

## Why it is asked

Accreditors require the people presenting, reviewing or judging credit-bearing content to state their relationships with companies whose products could be affected, so the organizer can show the content was handled fairly. A disclosure is a dated statement about a period — usually the past 24 months — and once it is on file it is good for that period across every event the organization runs. You will not be asked again until it is about to lapse.

## What you see

**On Home.** A banner at the top of the portal reads **A disclosure is needed** (or **Your disclosure is about to expire**, with the date it is valid until) and a **Complete disclosure** or **Renew disclosure** button. **Hide for now** puts the banner away for this visit; it comes back next time until the disclosure is in.

**On Tasks.** A **Disclosure** task sits with your other tasks. Open it the same way.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the task">
Click **Complete disclosure** on the banner, or the **Disclosure** task. The form opens in a panel: the organizer's introduction, then the questions, then the attestation.
</Step>
<Step title="Answer the questions">
Most forms ask whether you have any relationships to disclose. If you do, list each one — the company, the nature of the relationship (consultant, speakers bureau, research funding, employment, ownership, royalties, honoraria), and where the form asks, an amount range and dates. If you have none, tick **I have no financial relationships to disclose** where the form offers it.

Follow-up questions appear as you answer; a question about off-label discussion, for example, shows only when the form is built that way.
</Step>
<Step title="Attest and submit">
Tick the attestation — the statement that your answers are complete and accurate — and click **Submit disclosure**. The panel confirms **Thank you — your disclosure is on file**, with the date it is valid until, and the task clears.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The form never shows what you said last time. A renewal is a fresh statement, which is what accreditors expect, and it means nothing on file is ever displayed in a browser window someone else might see.
</Note>

## When a disclosure expires

A disclosure is valid for the period the organizer set — 24 months on the standard form, 12 on the shorter conflict-of-interest forms. Inside the last 60 days the banner changes to **Your disclosure is about to expire** and the task reappears; the organizer may also email you a private link to renew. Either route works, and completing one clears the other.

## If something goes wrong

- **The form was updated** — the organizer changed the form while you had it open. The panel reloads the questions; check your answers and submit again.
- **The panel says no disclosure form is set up** — the organizer has not published one yet. Nothing is needed from you until the task shows the form.
- You cannot submit — if you are looking at the portal through an organizer's **View portal as**, the form is read-only. Only the participant can submit their own disclosure.

<Tip>
You can also be asked by email: the message carries a private, single-use link to the same form, which you complete without signing in. The link and the portal task record the same disclosure — do whichever is in front of you.
</Tip>
