Participant Feedback adds a **Feedback** tab to each session in the speaker portal. Everyone on a session sees the same anonymous numbers: the attendee rating, comments, questions and polls, and, if you share them, the score your reviewers gave the submission. You decide which sources count, what speakers see, which sessions qualify, and when it goes live, and you can preview the exact speaker view before anything is released.

<Note>
Participant Feedback is a Beta in the Early Access program. Open **Early Access** at the bottom of the sidebar, join Early Access if your organization has not yet, and choose **Request access** on the Participant Feedback card. Our team turns it on for your organization and walks you through the first release.
</Note>

## Why use it

Speakers ask how their session went, and the answer usually lives in a survey export and a reviewer spreadsheet nobody sends them. Participant Feedback puts it in the portal they already use, with the same numbers for co-presenters, so you are not answering one email at a time. Sharing reviewer scores closes the loop with people who submitted, and the anonymity rules keep a single reviewer or a thin sample from being exposed.

## Where the settings are

Open the event, then **Settings** → **Record Settings** → **Participant portal**. The Participant Feedback section has four blocks: **Sources**, **What speakers see**, **Who sees it**, and **When**. **Preview as speaker** sits at the top of the section.

![Participant Feedback settings with the Preview as speaker button, the portal switch, and Sources listing a survey file and two evaluation plans](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-settings-sources.png)

## Sources

Ratings collected in Sessionboard (session surveys) are always included. You can add two more sources.

### Import a survey file

Use the file you already have from your survey tool or event app, Excel or CSV, in any column layout.

<Steps>

1. Under **Survey file**, choose **Import** and pick the file.
2. Check what each sheet is: **Ratings**, **Comments**, **Questions**, **Polls**, or **Don't import**. Change a column if the guess is wrong.
3. Choose **Review matches**. Each row is matched to a session in this event, and the counts show how many sessions, ratings, comments, questions and polls will import.
4. Rows the file cannot place on its own come back for you, one card per title (a card that stands for several rows says so). Under **Fit more than one session**, pick the session a title belongs to, choose **All N sessions** to apply it to every run, or choose **Don't import this row**. Under **Didn't match a session**, search this event's sessions by title or speaker and pick one, or several when the file groups a series under one label. Rows you leave alone are not imported. Choose **Continue**.
5. Choose **Import**.

</Steps>

![The survey file importer with a Ratings, Comments, Questions and Polls sheet, each column mapped to a field](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-import-map.png)

![Review matches showing 12 sessions, 12 ratings, 9 comments, 4 questions and 4 polls ready to import](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-import-review.png)

Rows are matched by a session ID column first: the Sessionboard session ID, or the session's own code if your sessions have one. Without an ID, rows match by title. When several sessions share a title, a **Location** or **Room** column, the speaker names, and a trailing number in the title ("HubSpot Lab 3" is the third of that title by start time) narrow it down.

<Note>
Importing replaces the previous file. To correct something, fix the spreadsheet and import it again.
</Note>

<Tip>
A session that runs several times often appears once in a survey export, with one combined rating. **All N sessions** gives every run that rating, so each speaker on any of the runs sees it.
</Tip>

### Evaluation reviews

Under **Evaluation reviews**, choose the evaluation plans whose scores speakers may see. Each plan shows whether it is scored by **People** or **AI reviewers**, how many scored reviews it has, and how many sessions they cover. Reviewers stay anonymous, and internal notes are never shared.

![Evaluation reviews with an AI reviewers plan and a People plan selected, each showing its scored reviews and sessions](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-evaluation-reviews.png)

Scores come from the overall rating on each review. For Evaluation Plans 2.0, which score through round rubrics, each session's score comes from the last round in which it has a completed review: the rubric score as a percentage, shown out of 5. Reviews by AI reviewers on a 2.0 plan show as their own block, as for other plans. Abstentions, incomplete and unscored reviews do not count, and 2.0 plans share no reviewer notes.

## What speakers see

- **Ratings**, the **Rating breakdown** (session score against speaker score, when both exist), **Comments**, **Questions** and **Polls** each turn on or off.
- **How comments appear**: **Individual comments**, **Team summary only**, or **Summary and individual**.
- **Minimum ratings** hides the score until that many ratings arrive. Comments, questions and polls can still show.
- **Reviewer score** is the average from the plans you chose, out of 5, with the number of reviewers.
- **Minimum reviewers** hides the reviewer score until that many people have scored the session, so no single reviewer can be singled out. The default is 2.
- **AI reviews**: **Show as a separate AI panel score**, or **Do not show AI reviews**. AI scores are never averaged into the reviewer score.
- **Reviewer notes** shares only the notes reviewers wrote for the submitter.

![Switches for ratings, rating breakdown, comments, questions, polls and reviewer score, with AI reviews shown as a separate panel and minimum reviewers set to 2](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-what-speakers-see.png)

## Who sees it

Only people on a session can open its feedback: its speakers, chairs, moderators, other participants, and the submitter. Nobody else on the event can read another session's feedback.

- **Everyone on the session** shows feedback on every session that has some.
- **Only accepted sessions** hides it on any session that is not accepted.

![Who sees it set to Only accepted sessions](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-who-sees-it.png)

## When

Turn on **Show feedback in the participant portal**, then set **Release at**. Speakers see nothing before that time. Leave it empty to release as soon as you save.

![Release at set to a date and time after the event](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-when.png)

When feedback is released, the speakers and other participants on this event's sessions who have a portal login get one email telling them it is ready. It goes out once per event.

<Warning>
Turning feedback on with an empty **Release at** releases it, and sends the email, the moment you save. Set a release time first if you are not ready.
</Warning>

## Preview as speaker

**Preview as speaker** opens the Feedback tab a speaker on a session would see with your current settings, saved or not. Search for a session; sessions with imported ratings or reviews are listed first. The preview tells you when a session would show nothing and why: it is not accepted while feedback is limited to accepted sessions, or it has fewer ratings than your minimum. The on/off switch and release time only decide when feedback appears, so you can preview before you turn it on.

![Preview as speaker showing a session's average rating, reviewer score, AI review panel and reviewer notes](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-preview-as-speaker.png)

## What speakers see in the portal

Once released, the speaker portal shows:

- A banner on the portal home when feedback is ready, and **Feedback ready** on each session that has some.
- A **Feedback** page listing every session the speaker is on, with its score.
- A **Feedback** tab on each session with the average rating and count, then the reviewer score, the AI review panel (if shown), reviewer notes (if shared), comments, questions and polls.

![A session's Feedback tab with an average rating from 118 attendees, reviewer score, AI review panel, reviewer notes, comments, questions and a poll](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-speaker-view.png)

Before the release time, the tab says feedback will appear when the organizers release it. Below the minimum, it says there are not enough responses yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do speakers see who rated them?**
No. Attendees and reviewers are anonymous, and everyone on a session sees the same numbers.

**Can I share reviewer scores without the attendee survey?**
Yes. Choose evaluation plans and leave the survey file empty. Ratings collected in Sessionboard still show if the session has any; turn **Ratings** off if you only want the reviewer score.

**A row in my file matched the wrong session.**
Add a session ID column, or a room column if the titles repeat, and import the file again. Rows that still fit several sessions come back for you to place before importing.

**My file's titles are cut short or spelled differently from my sessions.**
A shortened title still matches when it is the start of the session's title. Anything else comes back under **Didn't match a session**, where you search the session out once per title; the pick covers every row with that title.

**Is this the same as Pathways?**
No. Participant Feedback is separate and works whether Pathways is on or off.
