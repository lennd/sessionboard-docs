Participant Feedback adds a **Feedback** tab to each session in the speaker portal. Everyone on a session sees the same anonymous numbers: the attendee rating, comments, questions and polls, and, if you share them, the score your reviewers gave the submission. You decide which sources count, what speakers see, which sessions qualify, and when it goes live, and you can preview the exact speaker view before anything is released.

<Note>
Participant Feedback is a Beta in the Early Access program. Open **Early Access** at the bottom of the sidebar, join Early Access if your organization has not yet, and choose **Request access** on the Participant Feedback card. Our team turns it on for your organization and walks you through the first release.
</Note>

## Why use it

Speakers ask how their session went, and the answer usually lives in a survey export and a reviewer spreadsheet nobody sends them. Participant Feedback puts it in the portal they already use, with the same numbers for co-presenters, so you are not answering one email at a time. Sharing reviewer scores closes the loop with people who submitted, and the anonymity rules keep a single reviewer or a thin sample from being exposed.

## Where the settings are

Open the event, then **Settings** → **Record Settings** → **Participant portal**. The Participant Feedback section has four blocks: **Sources**, **What participants see**, **Who sees it**, and **When**. **Preview as participant** sits at the top of the section. The section says *participant* rather than *speaker* because feedback reaches everyone on a session, in any role.

![Participant Feedback settings with the Preview as participant button, the portal switch, and Sources listing a survey file and two evaluation plans](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-settings-sources.png)

## Sources

Ratings collected in Sessionboard (session surveys) are always included. You can add two more sources.

### Import a survey file

Use the file you already have from your survey tool or event app, Excel or CSV, in any column layout, or start from the template.

Feedback belongs to sessions, not to people. Every row needs a **Session ID** or the session title, and each kind of feedback goes on its own sheet.

<Steps>

1. Under **Survey file**, choose **Import**. To start from the template, choose **Download template** first, fill it in, and come back. Otherwise pick your file.
2. Check what each sheet is: **Ratings**, **Comments**, **Questions**, **Polls**, or **Don't import**. Change a column if the guess is wrong. A sheet that is missing a column it needs says so, for example *Choose a column for: Session ID or Session title*, and **Review matches** waits until you choose one.
3. Choose **Review matches**. Each row is matched to a session in this event, and the counts show how many sessions, ratings, comments, questions and polls will import. Rows that cannot import are listed with their sheet, row number, column and the reason, such as a rating that is not a number from 0 to 10. Choose **Download list** to fix them in your file, or import without them. Rows with no answer are left out and counted.
4. Rows the file cannot place on its own come back for you, one card per title (a card that stands for several rows says so). Under **Fit more than one session**, pick the session a title belongs to, choose **All N sessions** to apply it to every run, or choose **Don't import this row**. Under **Didn't match a session**, search this event's sessions by title or speaker and pick one, or several when the file groups a series under one label. Rows you leave alone are not imported. Choose **Continue**.
5. Choose **Import**.

</Steps>

![The survey file importer with a Ratings, Comments, Questions and Polls sheet, each column mapped to a field](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-import-map.png)

![Review matches showing 12 sessions, 12 ratings, 9 comments, 4 questions and 4 polls ready to import](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-import-review.png)

Rows are matched by a **Session ID** column first. It can hold the session code shown on the session (for example `SESS-12`, or `SESS-12-2` for a sub-session), the Sessionboard ID, or your Client Session ID. When a Client Session ID looks like a code, the Client Session ID wins. Without an ID, rows match by title. When several sessions share a title, a **Location** or **Room** column, the speaker names, and a trailing number in the title ("HubSpot Lab 3" is the third of that title by start time) narrow it down.

<Note>
Importing replaces the previous file. To correct something, choose **Download** next to **Import** to get what is imported now, fix it, and import it again.
</Note>

### The template

**Download template** gives you a workbook built for this event:

| Sheet | What goes in it | Columns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ratings** | One row per response, rated 0 to 10 | Session ID, Session title, Rating (one response) |
| **Comments** | One row per comment | Session ID, Session title, Comment, Survey question (optional, groups comments by question) |
| **Questions** | One row per audience question | Session ID, Session title, Audience question |
| **Polls** | One row per answer given | Session ID, Session title, Poll question, Poll answer |
| **Sessions** | Every session on this event, to copy from | Session ID, Sessionboard ID, Client Session ID, Session title, Room, Starts at, Speakers |

Fill in either the Session ID or the title on each row. Leave a sheet empty if you have none of that feedback. If your survey tool gives totals instead of responses, use the columns **Average rating** and **Number of ratings**, one row per session. **Read me** and **Sessions** are not imported.

<Tip>
A session that runs several times often appears once in a survey export, with one combined rating. **All N sessions** gives every run that rating, so each speaker on any of the runs sees it.
</Tip>

### Evaluation reviews

Under **Evaluation reviews**, choose the evaluation plans whose scores participants may see. Each plan shows whether it is scored by **People** or **AI reviewers**, how many scored reviews it has, and how many sessions they cover. Reviewers stay anonymous, and internal notes are never shared.

![Evaluation reviews with an AI reviewers plan and a People plan selected, each showing its scored reviews and sessions](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-evaluation-reviews.png)

Scores come from the overall rating on each review. For review plans, which score through round rubrics, each session's score comes from the last round in which it has a completed review: the rubric score as a percentage, shown out of 5. Reviews by AI reviewers on a review plan show as their own block, as for other plans. Abstentions, incomplete and unscored reviews do not count, and 2.0 plans share no reviewer notes.

## What participants see

- **Ratings**, the **Rating breakdown** (session score against participant score, when both exist), **Comments**, **Questions** and **Polls** each turn on or off.
- **How comments appear**: **Individual comments**, **Team summary only**, or **Summary and individual**.
- **Minimum ratings** hides the score until that many ratings arrive. Comments, questions and polls can still show.
- **Reviewer score** is the average from the plans you chose, out of 5, with the number of reviewers.
- **Minimum reviewers** hides the reviewer score until that many people have scored the session, so no single reviewer can be singled out. The default is 2.
- **AI reviews**: **Show as a separate AI panel score**, or **Do not show AI reviews**. AI scores are never averaged into the reviewer score.
- **Reviewer notes** shares only the notes reviewers wrote for the submitter.

![Switches for ratings, rating breakdown, comments, questions, polls and reviewer score, with AI reviews shown as a separate panel and minimum reviewers set to 2](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-what-speakers-see.png)

## Who sees it

Only people on a session can open its feedback: its speakers, chairs, moderators, other participants, and the submitter. Nobody else on the event can read another session's feedback.

- **Everyone on the session** shows feedback on every session that has some.
- **Only accepted sessions** hides it on any session that is not accepted.

![Who sees it set to Only accepted sessions](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-who-sees-it.png)

## When

Turn on **Show feedback in the participant portal**, then set **Release at**. Speakers see nothing before that time. Leave it empty to release as soon as you save.

![Release at set to a date and time after the event](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-when.png)

When feedback is released, the speakers and other participants on this event's sessions who have a portal login get one email telling them it is ready. It goes out once per event.

<Warning>
Turning feedback on with an empty **Release at** releases it, and sends the email, the moment you save. Set a release time first if you are not ready.
</Warning>

## Preview as participant

**Preview as participant** opens the Feedback tab a participant on a session would see with your current settings, saved or not. Search for a session; sessions with imported ratings or reviews are listed first. The preview tells you when a session would show nothing and why: it is not accepted while feedback is limited to accepted sessions, or it has fewer ratings than your minimum. The on/off switch and release time only decide when feedback appears, so you can preview before you turn it on.

![Preview as participant showing a session's average rating, reviewer score, AI review panel and reviewer notes](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-preview-as-speaker.png)

## See imported feedback in the admin

Your event team can read a survey file's results without previewing as a participant, and whether or not you have released feedback.

- **On the session:** open a session, from its page or the flyover in the sessions list, and choose the **Survey feedback** tab (after **Reviews**). It shows the average rating and the number of ratings, comments grouped by survey question, audience questions, and each poll's answers with counts and percentages. **Imported** gives the date of the file it comes from and links to these settings. A session with no rows in the file says so.
- **In reports:** in the [Report Builder](/reporting/report-builder), sessions have six fields from the import: **Imported Survey Rating (Avg)**, **Imported Survey Rating Count**, **Imported Survey Comment Count**, **Imported Audience Question Count**, **Imported Poll Count** and **Imported Survey Comments** (one comment per line, with its survey question). Add them to compare sessions, sort by rating or download the feedback.

Anyone who can view a session sees its imported feedback; a role limited to some sessions sees only those. The tab and the fields appear only for organizations with Participant Feedback.

## What speakers see in the portal

Once released, the speaker portal shows:

- A banner on the portal home when feedback is ready, and **Feedback ready** on each session that has some.
- A **Feedback** page listing every session the speaker is on, with its score.
- A **Feedback** tab on each session with the average rating and count, then the reviewer score, the AI review panel (if shown), reviewer notes (if shared), comments, questions and polls.

![A session's Feedback tab with an average rating from 118 attendees, reviewer score, AI review panel, reviewer notes, comments, questions and a poll](/images/kb/speaker-feedback-speaker-view.png)

Before the release time, the tab says feedback will appear when the organizers release it. Below the minimum, it says there are not enough responses yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do speakers see who rated them?**
No. Attendees and reviewers are anonymous, and everyone on a session sees the same numbers.

**Can I share reviewer scores without the attendee survey?**
Yes. Choose evaluation plans and leave the survey file empty. Ratings collected in Sessionboard still show if the session has any; turn **Ratings** off if you only want the reviewer score.

**A row in my file matched the wrong session.**
Add a Session ID column (copy the IDs from the **Sessions** sheet of the template), or a room column if the titles repeat, and import the file again. Rows that still fit several sessions come back for you to place before importing.

**My file's titles are cut short or spelled differently from my sessions.**
A shortened title still matches when it is the start of the session's title. Anything else comes back under **Didn't match a session**, where you search the session out once per title; the pick covers every row with that title.

**Is this the same as Pathways?**
No. Participant Feedback is separate and works whether Pathways is on or off.
