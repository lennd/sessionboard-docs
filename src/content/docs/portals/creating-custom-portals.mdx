Every event has a default portal for people and, if sponsors and exhibitors are enabled, one for groups. A custom portal is one you add on top with **filters**: only contacts or groups that match land in it, and everything on it — tasks, forms, pages, appearance — is yours to shape for that audience. Keynote speakers get a slides deadline that breakout speakers never see; Gold sponsors get the tasks that come with their package.

<Warning>
A contact or group is in **one portal at a time**. When someone matches several portals, they land in the **first** one in the list. Put the most specific portals at the top — see [Order portals](#order-portals-by-priority).
</Warning>

## Create the portal

<Steps>
<Step title="Create Portal">
Go to **Program → Portals** and click **Create Portal**. Give it an internal **Name** and pick the **Portal Type**:

- **Contacts** — people: speakers, chairs, moderators, submitters, and the individual contacts of sponsors and exhibitors.
- **Groups** — sponsor and exhibitor companies. Only available when those modules are on.

![The Add Portal panel](/images/kb/portal-create.png)

Sponsor and exhibitor contacts also have a *contact* portal of their own, so you can hand a task to one person at the company (share your details) as well as to the company (upload your logo).
</Step>
<Step title="Set the filters">
The editor opens on **Select Participants**. Under **Portal Filters** click **Add filter**, build the criteria, then **Save Filters**. **Portal Selection Criteria** on the right fills in with everyone who matches, so you can check the audience before you go further.

![The Select Participants step with filters on the left and matching people on the right](/images/kb/portal-editor-criteria.png)

| Portal type | What you can filter on |
| --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | Contact fields (name, email, job title, custom fields), plus session fields on the sessions they are attached to — **[Session] Tags**, **Tracks**, **Levels**, **Languages**, **Formats**, **Session Names**, **Source** and **Session Status** — and **Role**. |
| **Groups** | Group fields (name, custom fields) and **[Account] Tiers**. |

Two filters worth knowing about:

- **[Session] Source** — which form brought the contact in: any submission or application form on the event, or **Manual** for people you added by hand. Use it to give one call for papers its own portal.
- **[Session] Session Status** — the status of the contact's session, including custom statuses. This is how you build a portal for accepted speakers only.

Filters are live. As a session's status changes or a track is reassigned, people move between portals automatically; anyone who stops matching every custom portal falls back to the default.
</Step>
<Step title="Finish the editor">
**Save and Next** through **Assign Items**, **Configuration**, **Appearance** and **Manage Fields** — the same steps as any portal. [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#create-a-portal) covers each one.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Anyone who matches none of your custom portals lands in the **Default People Portal** (or the default group portal). Set that one up too — it is the safety net.
</Note>

## Order portals by priority

On **Program → Portals**, click the pencil next to **Create Portal** to enter edit mode, drag the cards into order, and click **Save Changes**. Sessionboard asks you to confirm, because reordering changes which portal people who match more than one will see.

![Portals in edit mode, ready to drag](/images/kb/portals-reorder.png)

<Tip>
Add the **Assigned Portal** column to a **Contacts**, **Speakers**, **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors** view to see which portal each record has landed in — see [Dashboard views](/reporting/dashboard-views).
</Tip>

## Duplicate, resync and delete

From the **⋯** menu on a portal card:

![The portal card menu](/images/kb/portals-card-menu.png)

- **Duplicate** copies the portal — filters, content, configuration and appearance — so a Day 2 portal starts from Day 1.
- **Delete** removes it; the people in it fall through to the next portal they match. Confirm in the dialog.

**Resync Portal Assignments** (in the **⋯** menu next to **Create Portal**) recomputes who belongs where across every portal. Use it after a large import or if a card's **Assigned to** count looks stale.

## Ideas that work

| Audience | Filter |
| --- | --- |
| Keynote vs breakout speakers | **[Session] Formats** |
| Day 1 vs Day 2 speakers | **[Session] Session Names** or a custom contact field |
| Speakers vs moderators vs chairs | **Role** |
| Accepted speakers only | **[Session] Session Status** is Accepted |
| One call for papers | **[Session] Source** |
| Gold / Silver / Bronze sponsors | **[Account] Tiers** |
