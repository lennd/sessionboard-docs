Every portal on the event shares one login link — `https://app.sessionboard.com/portal-login/<event-slug>/…`. Whoever signs in with it lands in the portal their filters put them in, so you never need a different link per portal. Copy it from **Program → Portals**: open the **⋯** menu on any portal card and choose **Copy Link**.

![The portal card menu, with Copy Link at the top](/images/kb/portals-card-menu.png)

There are two ways to get it to people.

## Send an email with the link

Write the email from **Contacts**, **Speakers**, **Sponsors**, **Exhibitors** or **Sessions** — see [Create & send emails](/communications/create-send-emails). Rather than pasting the URL, insert the **Portal Login Link (Hyperlink)** merge tag (or **Portal Login Link (Raw URL)** if you want the address spelled out). Group emails carry the same tags.

<Tip>
Point people at [Access your portal](/participants/access-portal) — it walks a first-time user through signing in.
</Tip>

## Grant access and share login details

Use this for a new contact who has never signed in, or for someone who has lost the link.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the people">
In **Contacts**, **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors**, tick the records. In the bulk action bar choose **More → Manage Portal Access**.

![Manage Portal Access, listing people with and without access](/images/kb/manage-portal-access.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Give access to anyone who lacks it">
The panel splits your selection into **People without access** and **People with access**. Click **Give Portal Access** for the first group; they become portal users on the spot. Anyone with an invalid email address is listed separately so you can fix the record first.
</Step>
<Step title="Share Login Details">
Click **Share Login Details**, then either **Send Email** — a portal invitation carrying the login link, with an optional personal message — or **Get Link** to copy the invitation link and send it yourself.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Access is per person, not per portal. Which portal they see is decided by the portal filters — see [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#select-participants).
</Note>
