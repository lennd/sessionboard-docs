Portals give contacts (speakers, moderators, chairpersons) and groups (sponsors, exhibitors) a centralized hub to access event information and complete tasks.

## Four steps to launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create portals & assign filters">
    Every event includes default portals: **Default People**, **Default Exhibitor**, and **Default Sponsor**. Create **custom portals** to tailor the experience by criteria such as contact role, session type, or sponsor tier. To show tasks only to accepted speakers, disable **Always Show Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create & assign tasks & resources">
    **Task types:**
    - **Task** — a general action item with a deadline (e.g., "Register for the event")
    - **File request** — requires an upload (e.g., "Upload contract")
    - **Form** — collects structured responses (e.g., "Provide headshot & bio")

    **Resources:**
    - **Files** — static, downloadable documents (e.g., a slide template)
    - **Wiki pages** — dynamic, editable pages (e.g., a "Know Before You Go" guide)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings">
    Control session/task/contact visibility and reminder emails — including **Always Show Tasks**, **Extend Task Deadlines** (set a **Final Deadline** — the number of days after the original due date before tasks are locked; defaults to 7), **Manage Profile**, **Manage Related Sessions & Participants**, and **Send Weekly Digest Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize appearance">
    Set a welcome message, accent color, logo, and background image per portal. Control which **contact** and **session** fields users can view or edit via **Show/Hide Fields** (lock to make view-only, or hide entirely). Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share your portal

Once configured, [invite users to the event portal](/portals/portals-101) to give them access.
