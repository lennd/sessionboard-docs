Portals give contacts (speakers, moderators, chairpersons) and groups (sponsors, exhibitors) a centralized hub to access event information and complete tasks. There are four steps to work through before you share a portal with your event contacts and groups.

![The Portals module listing every portal in an event](/images/kb/acdcad06-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-2.46.26-PM.png)

## Step one: create portals and assign filters

<Note>
Portals are not only for speakers. All event contacts and groups are assigned to a portal, and you control which one by using filters.
</Note>

Every event starts with a **default portal** for each module — Default People Portal, Default Exhibitor Portal, and Default Sponsor Portal. Each one is a catch-all for users and groups who do not match the filters on any custom portal you create.

![The default People, Exhibitor, and Sponsor portals in a new event](/images/kb/3feb7155-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-43-25-5472-PM.png)

- **Default People Portal** — every user in the **Contacts** module
- **Default Exhibitor Portal** — every group in the **Exhibitors** module, if you have access to it
- **Default Sponsor Portal** — every group in the **Sponsors** module, if you have access to it

<Tip>
Stay on the default portals if all your contacts and groups have the same tasks to complete and resources to view. To show tasks only to accepted speakers, turn off **Always Show Tasks** in [step three](#step-three-configure-settings).
</Tip>

### Custom portals

**Custom portals** tailor the experience for specific users and groups. You create them with **filters** that control who gets assigned, so the portal adapts to criteria such as contact role (speaker or moderator), session type (breakout or keynote), or sponsor tier (gold or silver). Everyone then sees only what is relevant to them.

See [creating custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals) for the full walkthrough.

## Step two: create and assign tasks and resources

Next, create the tasks and resources your contacts and groups will see in their portal. There are three types of task and two ways to share a resource.

### Task types

![The three task types available in a portal](/images/kb/ef751adc-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-2.47.11-PM.png)

- [**Task**](/portals/assign-tasks) — a general action item to complete by a deadline. For example, register for the event.
- [**File request**](/portals/collect-documents) — a task that requires uploading a document, image, or other file directly in the portal. For example, upload a contract.
- [**Form**](/portals/create-assign-forms) — a structured way to collect detailed responses through text inputs, dropdowns, checkboxes, and file uploads. For example, provide a headshot and biography.

### Share resources

![The two resource types available in a portal](/images/kb/7d704b49-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-2.47.19-PM.png)

- [**Files**](/portals/share-files) — a static document users can download and view, such as a slide template.
- [**Wiki pages**](/portals/assign-pages) — a dynamic, editable page hosted in the portal that updates in real time, such as a "Know Before You Go" guide.

<Tip>
To change these later, select the ellipsis to the right of a portal and click **Edit Tasks**.

![The Edit Tasks option in a portal's ellipsis menu](/images/kb/305e286a-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-51-32-4867-PM.png)
</Tip>

## Step three: configure settings

Portal settings control session, task, and contact visibility, along with reminder emails.

![The settings panel for a portal](/images/kb/65220754-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-52-05-4719-PM.png)

![The remaining portal settings, covering profile access and reminder emails](/images/kb/9c7c6691-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-52-18-3466-PM.png)

**Control Session Visibility** — display sessions to portal users. When unchecked, portal users do not see the sessions they are associated with.

![The Control Session Visibility setting](/images/kb/0a28fda9-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.28.12-AM.png)

**View Session Submission Form from Portal** — let users open their submission through the submission form. If you are not using the session submission form to collect proposals, consider turning this off.

![The View Session Submission Form from Portal setting](/images/kb/4617d4cb-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.30.23-AM.png)

**Always Show Tasks** — display portal tasks to all users.

<Warning>
When **Always Show Tasks** is off, tasks and resources appear only to speakers with accepted sessions. Contacts and groups outside that criteria — moderators, or gold-level sponsors — see no tasks or resources in their portal.
</Warning>

**Extend Task Deadlines** — let contacts and groups complete past-due tasks for a set window. Set a **Final Deadline**, the number of days after the original due date before tasks lock, which defaults to 7. The original due date stays visible to the portal user and the task keeps an **Open** status until the final deadline passes. This applies to every task in the portal; to change one task, edit its due date directly.

**Manage Profile** — let portal users view and edit their own profile: name, headshot, biography, address, and social media links.

![The Manage Profile setting](/images/kb/7b109a43-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.32.11-AM.png)

**Manage Related Sessions & Participants** — let portal users edit related sessions and participant information.

**Add Participants to Submissions** — let portal users add new speakers or contacts to their submissions and assign them a role. Available when Manage Related Sessions & Participants is on.

**Selectable Participant Roles** — limit which roles portal users can assign when adding a participant. Pick the allowed roles per portal; leave the selection empty to allow every role in the event. Participants already saved with a role you later exclude are unaffected — the filter only shapes what can be newly assigned.

![The Add Participants to Submissions toggle with the Selectable Participant Roles picker limited to Speaker](/images/kb/portal-selectable-participant-roles.png)

**Send Weekly Digest Email** — send a weekly summary of portal activity and upcoming tasks by due date.

**Email Notifications** — email everyone assigned to this portal when a task, form, file request, or wiki page is added. Recipients see the item name and due date. This is not limited to primary contacts: every member of the portal is emailed.

**Submission change notifications** — email portal users when a field they can see on this portal changes, when the session time or room moves, or when they are assigned as a participant. Time and room always notify. See [Notify speakers of submission changes](/portals/submission-change-notifications).

Assignment, weekly digest, and submission-change emails are listed in [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails).

<Tip>
To change these later, select the ellipsis to the right of a portal and click **Edit Settings**.

![The Edit Settings option in a portal's ellipsis menu](/images/kb/2e7054c6-image-png-Feb-18-2026-09-10-10-4126-PM.png)
</Tip>

## Step four: customize appearance

Portal appearance controls how the portal looks and which session and contact fields are visible.

<Tip>
To change these later, select the ellipsis to the right of a portal and click **Edit Appearance**.

![The Edit Appearance option in a portal's ellipsis menu](/images/kb/aa817580-image-png-Feb-18-2026-09-10-36-6652-PM.png)
</Tip>

### General

- Set a welcome message that everyone assigned to this portal sees.
- Use an accent color to customize the widget header.
- Add a logo and background image specific to this portal. To make the background full-width, use [portal settings](/settings/portal-settings).

![The general appearance settings for a portal](/images/kb/114542bf-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-9.28.37-AM.png)

<Warning>
Click **Save** once you have finished the general appearance settings.

![The Save button on the portal appearance settings](/images/kb/1b2e8365-image-png-Feb-18-2026-09-11-02-5894-PM.png)
</Warning>

### Manage fields

For each portal you decide which fields portal users can see and edit, separately for their contact information and their session information. Use the **Show/Hide Fields** button on the right of the screen, then select the ellipsis beside a field to either:

- **Lock** — the field is view-only and cannot be edited.
- **Hide** — the field is not accessible to the portal user at all.

![The Show/Hide Fields panel controlling which fields portal users can see and edit](/images/kb/89efe731-image-png-Feb-23-2026-11-56-25-9405-PM.png)

### Add help text to a field

A field name on its own rarely says what you actually want. Add help text to tell portal users how to fill a field in — a word count for a biography, an example of the format you want, or what a term means at your event.

In **Manage Fields**, each visible field has a **Help Text** button beside it. Click it to open a rich-text box under that field, type your guidance, and save with the rest of Manage Fields. The text appears below that field in the portal, exactly where the person filling it in is looking.

Practical notes:

- Help text is set **per portal**, so the same field can carry different guidance to speakers and to sponsors.
- It works on profile, submission, and participant fields.
- Leave the box empty for no help text — nothing is rendered, and the portal's spacing is unchanged.
- Up to 5,000 characters of text. Basic formatting is kept; anything else is stripped.
- Hiding a field clears its help text, since nobody fills in a hidden field.
- The button is highlighted on fields that already have help text, so you can see at a glance which fields are documented.

## Participation sections

When [participant acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) is on, the portal splits each person's sessions into sections. You control the grouping and the wording per portal, in **Portals → [portal] → Configuration**, under **Participation sections**. The controls appear only when acceptance is enabled and the portal is set to show sessions.

By default a participant sees three sections:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Invited Sessions** | Sessions they have been invited to but not yet answered |
| **My Submissions** | Submissions they made |
| **Confirmed Participation** | Everything they have accepted |

### Show a separate Confirmed Participation section

On by default. Turn it off to collapse to two sections — accepted submissions stay in **My Submissions**, and answered invitations stay in **Invited Sessions**. Useful when a third section is more structure than your participants need.

### Show subsessions in My Submissions

On by default. Turn it off and **My Submissions** lists the parent submission only, with subsessions visible once the submitter opens the record. Worth doing when a single submission carries many subsessions and the list becomes hard to scan.

**Invited Sessions** always shows the specific subsession a participant is on, whichever way this is set.

### Section titles

Rename any of the three headings, up to 100 characters each. Leave a field blank to keep the default. If your portal has multi-language enabled, custom titles are auto-translated along with the rest of the portal header copy.

The **Confirmed** title field is hidden when you have turned that section off, since there is nothing to name.

## Dates and times in the portal

Every date and time a participant sees in their portal is rendered in the **event timezone** from [Event Details](/events/event-details) — not in their own timezone, and not in yours. Each one names the zone it belongs to, so a speaker in London reading a New York event's schedule can tell which clock the time is on.

| What a participant is looking at | How it reads |
| --- | --- |
| Session **Starts At** and **Ends At** | The event timezone with its abbreviation: `January 20, 2026 9:00 AM EST` |
| A custom **Date & Time** field, on a session or on their profile | The same, in whatever format the field is set to use |
| A custom **Date** field or **Time** field | Exactly as it was entered, with no zone |

Daylight saving is worked out per value rather than per event, so on a New York event a January session reads `EST` and a July one reads `EDT`.

If **Timezone** is blank in Event Details, these times fall back to UTC and carry no zone at all — set it before you share a portal.

<Note>
**Date** and **Time** fields store a wall-clock value with no date-and-time instant attached, so there is nothing to convert and nothing to label. Use a **Date & Time** field for any deadline a participant in another country has to act on.
</Note>

Once configured, [invite users to the event portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal) to give them access.

## Common questions

The questions event teams ask most while setting up their first speaker portal, with the section or article that answers each.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Can tasks show only to accepted speakers, or depend on role, session type, or status?">

Yes, in two layers. Portal **filters** decide who is assigned to a portal (by role, session type, sponsor tier, or any contact or session field), so a "Keynote speakers" portal carries a different task list from the default one. Inside a portal, turn off **Always Show Tasks** to show tasks only to speakers on accepted sessions, and use **Assign By Filter** on a session task to show it only for sessions that match up to three filters, such as *Track is Leadership*. See [create custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals), [step three](#step-three-configure-settings), and [create and assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks). The two recommended set-ups for an accepted-speakers portal are compared in [create a portal for accepted speakers](/faq/how-to-create-a-portal-for-accepted-speakers).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a task, a form, and a file request?">

A **task** is an action item with a due date (register, book travel). A **file request** requires an upload, and admins can approve or decline what arrives (contract, presentation). A **form** collects structured answers through fields (bio, headshot, dietary needs, disclosures). All three live in [step two](#step-two-create-and-assign-tasks-and-resources). Use a form when you need the answers as data on the contact record; use a file request when you need the document itself.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What can speakers see and edit in their portal?">

You decide per portal. **Manage Fields** ([step four](#step-four-customize-appearance)) sets each contact and session field to editable, locked (view-only), or hidden. **Manage Profile** lets speakers edit their own name, headshot, biography, address, and social links; **Manage Related Sessions & Participants** lets them edit session details and co-presenters, and **Add Participants to Submissions** lets them add people. Sessions appear only when **Control Session Visibility** is on, and the submission form opens from the portal only when **View Session Submission Form from Portal** is on. Whether a speaker sees a pending session's custom status name follows that status's **Show custom status name to external users** setting in [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#statuses).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we collect bios, headshots, disclosures, and consent?">

Add a **form** to the portal with the fields you need, and mark fields required. Questions that map to contact, group, or session fields (First Name, Company Name, Session Title, and your custom fields) show the existing value for the speaker to review and update, so a biography collected here is the biography your agenda and integrations use. For a signed document, use a **file request**; for e-signature, use [Docusign contracts](/apps/docusign-integration). See [create and assign portal forms](/portals/create-assign-forms) and [collect documents](/portals/collect-documents).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do speaker agreements need Docusign, or can Sessionboard handle them?">

Either. A **file request** collects a signed PDF and lets you approve or decline it; a **form** with a consent checkbox records agreement as data; and [Docusign](/apps/docusign-integration) sends a pre-filled template from the contact record and tracks the signature back onto it. Choose Docusign when you need a legally executed envelope; choose a form or file request when a recorded acknowledgement is enough.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do due dates and reminders work?">

Each task, form, and file request carries a **Due Date**. Once it passes, portal users can no longer complete the item unless you set an **Extended Due Date** (per item) or **Extend Task Deadlines** (portal-wide, in [step three](#step-three-configure-settings)). Reminders are automatic: the **Weekly Digest Email** summarizes upcoming and overdue items, **Email Notifications** announce newly added items, and [submission change notifications](/portals/submission-change-notifications) tell speakers when a visible field, time, or room changes. To chase a specific group, [send a targeted email](/communications/create-send-emails) filtered by task status.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we see who has finished what, and complete a task on someone's behalf?">

Add the task reporting columns to the Contacts, Speakers, Sponsors, Exhibitors, or Sessions view and filter by status; each task shows a status icon. From a contact's profile, the **Portal Tasks** section lets an admin complete a task for them. See [track task completion](/faq/how-to-track-task-completion-in-the-event-portal) and [mark tasks complete for portal users](/faq/mark-tasks-complete-for-portal-users).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do we need separate portals for keynotes, panelists, sponsors, faculty, or external speakers?">

Only when the tasks or resources differ. Everyone is assigned to exactly one portal per module, chosen by filters, and the default portal catches anyone who matches no custom portal. Sponsors and exhibitors are groups and use the sponsor and exhibitor portals; people use the People portal. When several custom portals could match the same person, set the portal priority ([create custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals)).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do invitations, acceptance, and portal access fit together?">

Speakers get portal access when they submit through a form, when you grant it from **Contacts → More → Manage Portal Access**, or automatically on creation when **Automatically provision contact portal access** is on in Record Settings. Every portal shares one login link, sent with the portal-login merge tag ([inviting users to the event portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal)). Turn on [participant acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) and each speaker confirms or declines every role they hold, with the portal splitting their sessions into Invited, My Submissions, and Confirmed sections.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A speaker says their portal is empty or they cannot log in">

Check the role's **Portal Access** toggle, confirm they are using the **Portal Username** on their contact profile (it is separate from the communication email), then reset access from **Manage Portal Access**. The full checklist is in [why can't a portal user see any tasks assigned to them?](/faq/why-can-t-a-portal-user-see-any-tasks-assigned-to-them) and [common speaker portal issues](/faq/common-speaker-portal-issues-and-how-to-fix-them).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we preview the portal as a speaker before we share it?">

Yes. See [how can I view a portal as an admin?](/faq/how-can-i-view-a-portal-as-an-admin). Do this after every change to tasks or Manage Fields; what you see is exactly what that person sees.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
