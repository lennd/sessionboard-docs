## Why use it

A portal gives every speaker, sponsor or reviewer one place to see what you need from them and do it — upload the deck, confirm the bio, sign the form — without an email thread per person. You decide which fields they see and which are locked, so the data your team set stays as you set it, and the status column tells you at a glance who is done.

## Where portals live

Open **Program → Portals**. The page lists **Contact Portals** (people — speakers, moderators, submitters) and **Group Portals** (sponsors and exhibitors), each as a card with its filters, who it is assigned to, and an ellipsis (⋯) menu.

![The Portals page with the Contact Portals and Group Portals lists](/images/kb/portals-list.png)

Every event starts with a default portal per module — **Default People Portal**, **Default Exhibitor Portal**, **Default Sponsor Portal**. A default portal is the catch-all: it takes everyone who matches no custom portal, and it cannot carry filters of its own.

The card menu jumps straight into one step of the editor — **Edit Criteria**, **Edit Tasks**, **Edit Settings**, **Edit Appearance**, **Manage Fields** — and holds **Copy Link**, **Duplicate**, and **Delete**.

![A portal card's menu with Edit Criteria, Edit Tasks, Edit Settings, Edit Appearance, Manage Fields, Duplicate and Delete](/images/kb/portals-card-menu.png)

<Tip>
Stay on the default portals if everyone has the same tasks to complete and resources to read. Create a custom portal only when a group of people needs a different list — see [creating custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals).
</Tip>

## Build the content once

Tasks, forms, file requests, pages and files are built in their own libraries under **Portals**, then assigned to as many portals as need them. Build first, assign second.

| Library | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [**Tasks**](/portals/assign-tasks) | An action item with a due date — register, book travel, confirm your session. Three types: **Contacts**, **Groups**, **Submissions**. |
| [**File Requests**](/portals/collect-documents) | An upload you review — a signed agreement, a headshot, the slides — with **Pending**, **In Review**, **Approved** and **Denied** states. |
| [**Forms**](/portals/create-assign-forms) | Structured answers through fields — biography, dietary needs, disclosures — that can write straight onto the contact, group or submission. |
| [**Resources**](/portals/assign-pages) | Pages you write in the portal — a Know Before You Go guide — updated live, with merge tags for personalized pages. |
| [**Files**](/portals/share-files) | A document or link people download — a slide template, the venue map. |

![The Tasks library with one card per task and its type](/images/kb/portal-tasks-library.png)

Every library's **Add** button has two entries: **Add …** creates a new item, and **Copy from…** brings items over from another event in your organization, so a recurring event reuses last year's tasks, forms and pages instead of rebuilding them.

## Create a portal

Click **Create Portal**, give it a **Name**, and choose the **Portal Type**: **Contacts** for speakers, presenters and individual participants, or **Groups** for sponsors, exhibitors and organization accounts. The editor then walks left to right through five steps.

![The Add Portal panel with the portal name and the Contacts or Groups portal type](/images/kb/portal-create.png)

### Select Participants

**Portal Filters** decide who is assigned. Click **Add filter** and build the criteria — contact role, session status, sponsor tier, any contact or submission field — then **Save Filters**. The table below previews who matches. Contacts who already match a custom portal higher in the list are not included; to take them, create the portal, then move it to the top of the Portals page (see [reorder portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals#order-portals-by-priority)).

![The Select Participants step with the portal filters and the matching records](/images/kb/portal-editor-criteria.png)

### Assign Items

**Assign portal content** lists the portal's **Tasks**, **Forms**, **File Requests**, **Resources**, **Files**, and **Advocacy campaigns**. Click **Add** in a section to pick from the library, or **Manage** to open it. Each row shows **Name**, **Alias**, **Due Date**, **Extended Due Date** and **Required**; drag rows to set the **Portal Tasks order**, or leave the **Smart (recommended)** ordering — required and incomplete items first.

![The Assign Items step with tasks, forms and file requests assigned to the portal](/images/kb/portal-editor-assign-items.png)

The pencil on a row opens **Edit Settings** for that item on this portal only: an **Alias** to display a different title, the **Due Date** and **Extended Due Date**, **Due date relative to assignment** on tasks (N days after the person is assigned or after the assignment email is sent), **Required**, **Allow edits** once a form is submitted, **Make Completed Tasks View-Only**, and for submission tasks **Assign By Filter** — show the task only for submissions that match **Submission Filters** or carry certain **Participant Roles**.

![The Edit Settings dialog for an assigned item with alias, due dates, required and assign-by-filter](/images/kb/portal-item-settings.png)

### Configuration step

What portal users can see and do. The full list is [below](#configuration).

![The Configuration step with the submission visibility, tasks, profile and notification settings](/images/kb/portal-editor-configuration.png)

### Appearance

A **Portal Title** (the default is "Home"), a **Welcome Message**, a **Logo Image** (100 × 100), a **Background Image** (1920 × 200), an **Accent Color**, and **Advanced CSS** for anything the controls do not cover. To make the background full-width, use [portal settings](/settings/portal-settings).

![The Appearance step with the portal title, welcome message, images and accent colour](/images/kb/portal-editor-appearance.png)

### Manage Fields

Which fields portal users see and edit, in tabs for **Submission Fields**, **Contact Fields** (or **Group Fields**), **Profile Fields**, and the **Contact Participants** / **Group Participants** shown on co-presenter records. Each row has a **Locked** switch — locked fields are view-only. **Show/Hide Fields** opens the picker where you choose the visible fields, drag them into order, and add **Help Text** under any of them. **Hide Empty Submission and Participant Fields** tidies records that have blanks.

![The Manage Fields step with the field tabs and the Locked column](/images/kb/portal-editor-manage-fields.png)

![The Show/Hide Fields panel with selected fields, drag handles and a Help Text button](/images/kb/portal-show-hide-fields.png)

Each step ends with **Next** — it reads **Save and Next** once you have changed something on the step — and the last with **Done**. The toolbar at the top holds **Preview as…** (open the portal as any assigned participant), **Copy Link**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**, and you can click the portal name to rename it.

## Configuration

The **Settings** section:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Control Submission Visibility** | Show portal users their assigned submissions. Off, and speakers and submitters see no sessions at all. |
| **Session list order** | **Chronological** (agenda order, unscheduled last) or **Alphabetical**. |
| **Participation sections** | How submissions and invitations are grouped when [participant acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) is on — see [below](#participation-sections). |
| **Add to Calendar** | An Add to Calendar option on accepted submissions with start and end dates; the calendar text is set in [Program Settings → Agenda](/sessions/program-settings#agenda). |
| **View Submission Form** | Let portal users open their submission in the submission form. Turn it off if you did not collect proposals through a form. |
| **Always Show Tasks** | Show the task section even when there are no tasks assigned. Off, and tasks appear only to speakers with accepted submissions. |
| **Show Submission Tasks on Subsessions** / **Show Tasks on Parent Submission** | Where submission-level tasks appear when the submission has subsessions. |
| **Extend Task Deadlines** | Keep tasks open after their due date until a **Final Deadline** — a number of days after the original, 7 by default. The original date stays visible and the task stays **Open**. |
| **Manage Profile** | Let portal users view and edit their own profile. |
| **Manage Related Submissions and Participants** | Let portal users edit their submissions and the people on them. |
| **Add Participants to Submissions** | Let them add new speakers or contacts to a submission and give them a role; **Selectable Participant Roles** limits which roles. Needs Sessions 2.0 on the event. |
| **Add Contacts to Group** | Group portals: let a sponsor or exhibitor add contacts to their group and give them portal access. |
| **Use Submission Client ID** | Show your client ID instead of the internal submission ID. |

<Warning>
When **Always Show Tasks** is off, tasks and resources appear only to speakers with accepted sessions. Moderators, chairpersons, and anyone without an accepted session sees an empty task list.
</Warning>

The **Portal Notifications** section:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly Digest Email** | A weekly summary of portal actions and upcoming tasks by due date. |
| **Email Notifications** | Email everyone on the portal when a task, form, file request or page is added — the item name and its due date. |
| **Enable messaging** | Let portal users message the organizing team. Off, and the portal hides the Messages tab and session message threads. |
| **Submission change notifications** | Email portal users when a field they can see changes, when the start time, end time or location moves (those three always notify), or when they are assigned as a participant. See [Notify speakers of submission changes](/portals/submission-change-notifications). |
| **Multi-language** | **Enable multi-language portal** shows a language picker and auto-translates the headers, labels, placeholders and confirmations you tick. |

![The Portal Notifications settings — weekly digest, email notifications, messaging and submission change notifications](/images/kb/portal-editor-notifications.png)

Assignment, weekly digest, and submission-change emails are listed in [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails).

## Participation sections

When [participant acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) is on, the portal splits each person's sessions into sections. You control the grouping and the wording per portal under **Participation sections** in Configuration. The controls appear only when acceptance is enabled and the portal shows submissions.

By default a participant sees three sections:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Invited Sessions** | Sessions they have been invited to but not yet answered |
| **My Submissions** | Submissions they made |
| **Confirmed Participation** | Everything they have accepted |

- **Show a separate Confirmed Participation section** — on by default. Off, and accepted submissions stay in **My Submissions** while answered invitations stay in **Invited Sessions**.
- **Show subsessions in My Submissions** — on by default. Off, and **My Submissions** lists the parent submission only; **Invited Sessions** always shows the specific subsession a participant is on.
- **Section titles** — rename any of the three headings, up to 100 characters each. Blank keeps the default, and multi-language portals translate custom titles with the rest of the header copy.

## Dates and times in the portal

Every date and time a participant sees is rendered in the **event timezone** from [Event Details](/events/event-details) — not their own, and not yours — and names the zone it belongs to.

| What a participant is looking at | How it reads |
| --- | --- |
| Session **Starts At** and **Ends At** | The event timezone with its abbreviation: `January 20, 2026 9:00 AM EST` |
| A custom **Date & Time** field | The same, in the field's format |
| A custom **Date** or **Time** field | Exactly as entered, with no zone |

Daylight saving is worked out per value, so on a New York event a January session reads `EST` and a July one `EDT`. If **Timezone** is blank in Event Details, times fall back to UTC with no zone — set it before you share a portal.

## See it as a speaker

Click **Preview as…** in the editor toolbar and pick anyone assigned to the portal. Their portal opens in a new tab exactly as they see it. Do this after every change to assigned items or Manage Fields.

The same view is one click away from any event page: **View Portal** at the top right opens **View my portal** and **View portal as…**, where you search for a contact (or a sponsor or exhibitor) and open their portal. **View portal as…** needs the impersonate-portal permission; team members without it see the entry disabled.

![The Preview portal as dialog listing participants assigned to the portal](/images/kb/portal-preview-as.png)

## Share your portal

Once configured, [invite users to the event portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal). Every portal shares one login link; the portal-login merge tag puts it in any email.

To nudge people with open items, select them in **Contacts** and choose **Send → Send Portal Summary**. Each person gets the [weekly portal summary](/communications/automated-emails) email right away — what is due this week, overdue, completed and awaiting approval, with a link to the portal — instead of waiting for the Monday digest.

## Common questions

The questions event teams ask most while setting up their first speaker portal, with the section or article that answers each.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Can tasks show only to accepted speakers, or depend on role, session type, or status?">

Yes, in two layers. Portal **filters** decide who is assigned to a portal (by role, session type, sponsor tier, or any contact or session field), so a "Keynote speakers" portal carries a different task list from the default one. Inside a portal, turn off **Always Show Tasks** to show tasks only to speakers on accepted sessions, and use **Assign By Filter** on a session task to show it only for sessions that match up to three filters, such as *Track is Leadership*. See [create custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals), [Configuration](#configuration), and [create and assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks). The two recommended set-ups for an accepted-speakers portal are compared in [create a portal for accepted speakers](/faq/how-to-create-a-portal-for-accepted-speakers).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a task, a form, and a file request?">

A **task** is an action item with a due date (register, book travel). A **file request** requires an upload, and admins can approve or decline what arrives (contract, presentation). A **form** collects structured answers through fields (bio, headshot, dietary needs, disclosures). All three are built in the [libraries](#build-the-content-once) and assigned in [Assign Items](#assign-items). Use a form when you need the answers as data on the contact record; use a file request when you need the document itself.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What can speakers see and edit in their portal?">

You decide per portal. **Manage Fields** ([step five](#manage-fields)) sets each contact and session field to editable, locked (view-only), or hidden. **Manage Profile** lets speakers edit their own name, headshot, biography, address, and social links; **Manage Related Submissions and Participants** lets them edit submission details and co-presenters, and **Add Participants to Submissions** lets them add people. Sessions appear only when **Control Submission Visibility** is on, and the submission form opens from the portal only when **View Session Submission Form from Portal** is on. Whether a speaker sees a pending session's custom status name follows that status's **Show custom status name to external users** setting in [program settings](/sessions/program-settings#statuses).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we collect bios, headshots, disclosures, and consent?">

Add a **form** to the portal with the fields you need, and mark fields required. Questions that map to contact, group, or session fields (First Name, Company Name, Session Title, and your custom fields) show the existing value for the speaker to review and update, so a biography collected here is the biography your agenda and integrations use. For a signed document, use a **file request**; for e-signature, use [Docusign contracts](/apps/docusign-integration). See [create and assign portal forms](/portals/create-assign-forms) and [collect documents](/portals/collect-documents).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do speaker agreements need Docusign, or can Sessionboard handle them?">

Either. A **file request** collects a signed PDF and lets you approve or decline it; a **form** with a consent checkbox records agreement as data; and [Docusign](/apps/docusign-integration) sends a pre-filled template from the contact record and tracks the signature back onto it. Choose Docusign when you need a legally executed envelope; choose a form or file request when a recorded acknowledgement is enough.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do due dates and reminders work?">

Each task, form, and file request carries a **Due Date**. Once it passes, portal users can no longer complete the item unless you set an **Extended Due Date** (per item) or **Extend Task Deadlines** (portal-wide, in [Configuration](#configuration)). An Extended Due Date replaces the date portal users see, in the task list and in the message shown when an item has closed. The Extend Task Deadlines window does not change the date shown. Reminders are automatic: the **Weekly Digest Email** summarizes upcoming and overdue items, **Email Notifications** announce newly added items, and [submission change notifications](/portals/submission-change-notifications) tell speakers when a visible field, time, or room changes. To chase a specific group, [send a targeted email](/communications/create-send-emails) filtered by task status.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do we see who has finished what, and complete a task on someone's behalf?">

Add the task reporting columns to the Contacts, Speakers, Sponsors, Exhibitors, or Sessions view and filter by status; each task shows a status icon. From a contact's profile, the **Portal Tasks** section lets an admin complete a task for them. See [track task completion](/faq/how-to-track-task-completion-in-the-event-portal) and [mark tasks complete for portal users](/faq/mark-tasks-complete-for-portal-users).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do we need separate portals for keynotes, panelists, sponsors, faculty, or external speakers?">

Only when the tasks or resources differ. Everyone is assigned to exactly one portal per module, chosen by filters, and the default portal catches anyone who matches no custom portal. Sponsors and exhibitors are groups and use the sponsor and exhibitor portals; people use the People portal. When several custom portals could match the same person, set the portal priority ([create custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals)).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do invitations, acceptance, and portal access fit together?">

Speakers get portal access when they submit through a form, when you grant it from **Contacts → More → Manage Portal Access**, or automatically on creation when **Automatically provision contact portal access** is on in Record Settings. Every portal shares one login link, sent with the portal-login merge tag ([inviting users to the event portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal)). Turn on [participant acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) and each speaker confirms or declines every role they hold, with the portal splitting their sessions into Invited, My Submissions, and Confirmed sections.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A speaker says their portal is empty or they cannot log in">

Check the role's **Portal Access** toggle, confirm they are using the **Portal Username** on their contact profile (it is separate from the communication email), then reset access from **Manage Portal Access**. The full checklist is in [why can't a portal user see any tasks assigned to them?](/faq/why-can-t-a-portal-user-see-any-tasks-assigned-to-them) and [common speaker portal issues](/faq/common-speaker-portal-issues-and-how-to-fix-them).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we preview the portal as a speaker before we share it?">

Yes. See [how can I view a portal as an admin?](/faq/how-can-i-view-a-portal-as-an-admin). Do this after every change to tasks or Manage Fields; what you see is exactly what that person sees.

</Accordion>
