Portals give contacts (speakers, moderators, chairpersons) and groups (sponsors, exhibitors) a centralized hub to access event information and complete tasks. There are four steps to work through before you share a portal with your event contacts and groups.

![The Portals module listing every portal in an event](/images/kb/acdcad06-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-2.46.26-PM.png)

## Step one: create portals and assign filters

<Note>
Portals are not only for speakers. All event contacts and groups are assigned to a portal, and you control which one by using filters.
</Note>

Every event starts with a **default portal** for each module — Default People Portal, Default Exhibitor Portal, and Default Sponsor Portal. Each one is a catch-all for users and groups who do not match the filters on any custom portal you create.

![The default People, Exhibitor, and Sponsor portals in a new event](/images/kb/3feb7155-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-43-25-5472-PM.png)

- **Default People Portal** — every user in the **Contacts** module
- **Default Exhibitor Portal** — every group in the **Exhibitors** module, if you have access to it
- **Default Sponsor Portal** — every group in the **Sponsors** module, if you have access to it

<Tip>
Stay on the default portals if all your contacts and groups have the same tasks to complete and resources to view. To show tasks only to accepted speakers, turn off **Always Show Tasks** in [step three](#step-three-configure-settings).
</Tip>

### Custom portals

**Custom portals** tailor the experience for specific users and groups. You create them with **filters** that control who gets assigned, so the portal adapts to criteria such as contact role (speaker or moderator), session type (breakout or keynote), or sponsor tier (gold or silver). Everyone then sees only what is relevant to them.

See [creating custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals) for the full walkthrough.

## Step two: create and assign tasks and resources

Next, create the tasks and resources your contacts and groups will see in their portal. There are three types of task and two ways to share a resource.

### Task types

![The three task types available in a portal](/images/kb/ef751adc-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-2.47.11-PM.png)

- [**Task**](/portals/assign-tasks) — a general action item to complete by a deadline. For example, register for the event.
- [**File request**](/portals/collect-documents) — a task that requires uploading a document, image, or other file directly in the portal. For example, upload a contract.
- [**Form**](/portals/create-assign-forms) — a structured way to collect detailed responses through text inputs, dropdowns, checkboxes, and file uploads. For example, provide a headshot and biography.

### Share resources

![The two resource types available in a portal](/images/kb/7d704b49-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-2.47.19-PM.png)

- [**Files**](/portals/share-files) — a static document users can download and view, such as a slide template.
- [**Wiki pages**](/portals/assign-pages) — a dynamic, editable page hosted in the portal that updates in real time, such as a "Know Before You Go" guide.

<Tip>
To change these later, select the ellipsis to the right of a portal and click **Edit Tasks**.

![The Edit Tasks option in a portal's ellipsis menu](/images/kb/305e286a-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-51-32-4867-PM.png)
</Tip>

## Step three: configure settings

Portal settings control session, task, and contact visibility, along with reminder emails.

![The settings panel for a portal](/images/kb/65220754-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-52-05-4719-PM.png)

![The remaining portal settings, covering profile access and reminder emails](/images/kb/9c7c6691-image-png-Feb-18-2026-08-52-18-3466-PM.png)

**Control Session Visibility** — display sessions to portal users. When unchecked, portal users do not see the sessions they are associated with.

![The Control Session Visibility setting](/images/kb/0a28fda9-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.28.12-AM.png)

**View Session Submission Form from Portal** — let users open their submission through the submission form. If you are not using the session submission form to collect proposals, consider turning this off.

![The View Session Submission Form from Portal setting](/images/kb/4617d4cb-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.30.23-AM.png)

**Always Show Tasks** — display portal tasks to all users.

<Warning>
When **Always Show Tasks** is off, tasks and resources appear only to speakers with accepted sessions. Contacts and groups outside that criteria — moderators, or gold-level sponsors — see no tasks or resources in their portal.
</Warning>

**Extend Task Deadlines** — let contacts and groups complete past-due tasks for a set window. Set a **Final Deadline**, the number of days after the original due date before tasks lock, which defaults to 7. The original due date stays visible to the portal user and the task keeps an **Open** status until the final deadline passes. This applies to every task in the portal; to change one task, edit its due date directly.

**Manage Profile** — let portal users view and edit their own profile: name, headshot, biography, address, and social media links.

![The Manage Profile setting](/images/kb/7b109a43-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.32.11-AM.png)

**Manage Related Sessions & Participants** — let portal users edit related sessions and participant information.

**Send Weekly Digest Email** — send a weekly summary of portal activity and upcoming tasks by due date.

**Email Notifications** — email primary contacts when tasks are assigned to this portal.

Both emails are shown in [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails).

<Tip>
To change these later, select the ellipsis to the right of a portal and click **Edit Settings**.

![The Edit Settings option in a portal's ellipsis menu](/images/kb/2e7054c6-image-png-Feb-18-2026-09-10-10-4126-PM.png)
</Tip>

## Step four: customize appearance

Portal appearance controls how the portal looks and which session and contact fields are visible.

<Tip>
To change these later, select the ellipsis to the right of a portal and click **Edit Appearance**.

![The Edit Appearance option in a portal's ellipsis menu](/images/kb/aa817580-image-png-Feb-18-2026-09-10-36-6652-PM.png)
</Tip>

### General

- Set a welcome message that everyone assigned to this portal sees.
- Use an accent color to customize the widget header.
- Add a logo and background image specific to this portal. To make the background full-width, use [portal settings](/settings/portal-settings).

![The general appearance settings for a portal](/images/kb/114542bf-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-9.28.37-AM.png)

<Warning>
Click **Save** once you have finished the general appearance settings.

![The Save button on the portal appearance settings](/images/kb/1b2e8365-image-png-Feb-18-2026-09-11-02-5894-PM.png)
</Warning>

### Manage fields

For each portal you decide which fields portal users can see and edit, separately for their contact information and their session information. Use the **Show/Hide Fields** button on the right of the screen, then select the ellipsis beside a field to either:

- **Lock** — the field is view-only and cannot be edited.
- **Hide** — the field is not accessible to the portal user at all.

![The Show/Hide Fields panel controlling which fields portal users can see and edit](/images/kb/89efe731-image-png-Feb-23-2026-11-56-25-9405-PM.png)

## Participation sections

When [participant acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) is on, the portal splits each person's sessions into sections. You control the grouping and the wording per portal, in **Portals → [portal] → Configuration**, under **Participation sections**. The controls appear only when acceptance is enabled and the portal is set to show sessions.

By default a participant sees three sections:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Invited Sessions** | Sessions they have been invited to but not yet answered |
| **My Submissions** | Submissions they made |
| **Confirmed Participation** | Everything they have accepted |

### Show a separate Confirmed Participation section

On by default. Turn it off to collapse to two sections — accepted submissions stay in **My Submissions**, and answered invitations stay in **Invited Sessions**. Useful when a third section is more structure than your participants need.

### Show subsessions in My Submissions

On by default. Turn it off and **My Submissions** lists the parent submission only, with subsessions visible once the submitter opens the record. Worth doing when a single submission carries many subsessions and the list becomes hard to scan.

**Invited Sessions** always shows the specific subsession a participant is on, whichever way this is set.

### Section titles

Rename any of the three headings, up to 100 characters each. Leave a field blank to keep the default. If your portal has multi-language enabled, custom titles are auto-translated along with the rest of the portal header copy.

The **Confirmed** title field is hidden when you have turned that section off, since there is nothing to name.

## Share your portal

Once configured, [invite users to the event portal](/portals/inviting-users-to-the-event-portal) to give them access.
