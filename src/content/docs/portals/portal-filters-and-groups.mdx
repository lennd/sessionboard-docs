Every person who can sign in to a portal lands in exactly one custom portal at a time. Which one is decided by the portal's filters and by the order of the portals on **Program → Portals**. This page explains that rule and the questions it raises. To build a portal from scratch, start with [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101).

## How a contact is matched to a portal

1. Each custom portal has **Portal Filters**: conditions on the contact's role, session status, sponsor tier, or any contact or submission field.
2. Sessionboard checks the portals **from the top of the list down**. A contact lands in the first portal whose filters they match.

3. A contact who matches no custom portal stays in the default portal for their module (the catch-all).

So a person who matches two portals sees the higher one, and a new portal placed at the bottom takes nobody who an earlier portal already holds. The table under the filters in the editor previews who matches, but it leaves out contacts already held by a portal higher up.

<Warning>
If a filter on the new portal shows nobody, check the portals above it first. Another portal probably matches those people already.
</Warning>

### Move people into a different portal

- **Reorder the portals.** On **Program → Portals**, click the pencil next to **Create Portal** to enter edit mode, drag the portal up, and click **Save Changes**. Confirm the prompt: reordering changes where everyone who matches more than one portal lands.
- **Narrow the portal above.** Add a condition to the higher portal's filters so it stops matching these people.
- **Check the result.** Add the **Assigned Portal** column to a **Contacts**, **Speakers**, **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors** view to see where each record has landed. See [Dashboard views](/reporting/dashboard-views).

The default portal is the catch-all, so a group can't be "kept out" of it by a setting. To move a group elsewhere, put a portal whose filters match that group above it, or narrow the filters of the portal above.

## Filters that match too many or too few people

- **Every condition must match.** A filter on a role plus a status shows only people who have both.
- **Read the preview.** Open **Select Participants** and check the preview table row by row before you save, especially for a filter on a checkbox or custom field.
- **Recompute after big changes.** After a large import, or if a card's **Assigned to** count looks stale, use **Resync Portal Assignments** in the **⋯** menu next to **Create Portal**. See [creating custom portals](/portals/creating-custom-portals#duplicate-resync-and-delete).
- **Visibility is separate.** The portal's **Visibility filter** decides which of a person's submissions they see, not who is in the portal. See [Show only some submissions](/portals/portals-101#show-only-some-submissions).

## The Submitter filter

**Submitter** is a checkbox filter. A person counts as checked when they are the **submitter** of at least one session or abstract on this event, meaning the person whose name is on the submission, not a co-author, speaker or other participant. A person who has not submitted anything is unchecked.

- **Drafts and deleted records don't count.** Someone who only started a form and never submitted it is unchecked. So is someone whose only submission was deleted.
- **It is per event.** A submission on another event doesn't make someone a submitter here.
- **Checked and unchecked are opposites.** Filter **Submitter** on checked to reach everyone who submitted, and on unchecked to reach everyone else, such as speakers added by the team. A person is never in both.
- **To reach speakers on accepted submissions**, combine the filters rather than relying on **Submitter** alone: for example **Submitter** checked plus the session status you want. Every condition must match.

If the preview table shows people who clearly submitted nothing, or leaves out people who did, read the **Submitter** column in the preview and tell support which event and portal it is. Fixing the filter is a product change, not something to work around in the filter itself.

## Contacts portals and Groups portals

When you create a portal you choose a **Portal Type**:

| Type | For | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | Speakers, presenters and individual participants. | Nothing extra. |
| **Groups** | Sponsors, exhibitors and other organization accounts. | **Sponsors Pro** or **Exhibitors Pro** on the event. Without either, the choice is locked and the panel says why. |

A sponsor who also presents can still reach a **Contacts** portal through their submission. In a **Groups** portal, **Add Contacts to Group** lets a sponsor or exhibitor add their own colleagues and give them portal access.

## Relative due dates

On a portal's **Assign Items** step, click the pencil on a task, form or file request to open **Edit Settings** for that item on this portal only. Besides a fixed **Due Date**, you can set a **Due date relative to assignment**: a number of days after either:

- the person is assigned to the portal, or
- the assignment email is sent.

Every person then gets their own deadline. Use a fixed date when everyone must finish together. **Extended Due Date** and **Extend Task Deadlines** (a **Final Deadline** a number of days after the original) work with either kind. See [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#assign-items).
