## What the Checklist Includes
The onboarding checklist is organized by **Sessionboard modules and setup phases** so you can follow along as your event configuration progresses.

Sections include:

- **Event Details Setup: F**oundational event configuration, such as:

  - Event name

  - Event slug

  - Timezone

  - Event dates

  - Event logo

  - Portal background images

  - Record settings

***These settings are important because many areas of Sessionboard automatically pull information from this page.***

- **Module Setup Guides:** Depending on the modules included in your Sessionboard plan, the checklist may include sections for:

  - Speaker Management

  - Session Submission Forms

  - Agenda Building

  - Sponsor Management

  - Exhibitor Management

  - Evaluations

  - Portal Configuration

Each step includes:

- A **task to complete**

- A **knowledge base article**

- A **training video**

This allows your team to quickly find the resources needed to complete each step.
