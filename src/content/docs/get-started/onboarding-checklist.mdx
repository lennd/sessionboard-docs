## Welcome to Sessionboard! 🎉

This page walks through the essential steps for configuring your event and preparing your team to manage sessions, speakers, sponsors, and more. It follows the order our onboarding team uses in kickoff and training calls, so you can work through it on your own between sessions or use it as the agenda for those calls.

Each step links to the article that covers it. Skip any area your plan does not include.

## Your Onboarding Sandbox

When your team completes the welcome survey, Sessionboard creates an **Onboarding Sandbox** event: a practice event filled with sample sessions and speakers that match your kind of event, so your team can learn Sessionboard before building your real events. A banner across the top marks it as a sandbox, and work there may not carry over to your official events.

While your organization is in guided onboarding, four things are locked until your Customer Success Manager unlocks them at your kickoff call: deleting events, sending email campaigns, publishing a public site, and changing integration credentials. Want to try a change on a real event safely later on? Ask support to [clone it into a test event](/faq/clone-an-event).

## Step 1: Event details

Many areas of Sessionboard pull from this page, so complete it first.

- Set the event name, slug, timezone, dates, logo, and portal background in [Event details](/events/event-details).
- Define your program vocabulary — tracks, formats, rooms, levels, tags, session roles, and statuses — in [Program settings](/sessions/program-settings).
- [Create the custom fields](/faq/how-to-create-and-delete-custom-fields) you need on sessions and contacts before you import or build forms.
- Recurring event? Ask about [cloning last year's event](/faq/clone-an-event) before you rebuild. Need an empty event first? [Request it with Add Event](/faq/clone-an-event#start-a-new-event-from-scratch).

## Step 2: Users and permissions

- [Invite your event team](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members) and pick a role for each person; use a filter on Session Manager roles to scope people to a track or form.
- Decide who reviews, who accepts and declines, and who only views — [custom roles and field permissions](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members#custom-roles) cover the rest.
- Set how speakers will sign in to their portal: [portal login methods](/settings/portal-login-methods).

## Step 3: Session submission forms

If you collect abstracts or session proposals, build the form before anything that depends on it.

- Start with [which form should I use?](/concepts/which-form-should-i-use), then [build the submission form](/sessions/submission-forms).
- Set the [open and close dates](/sessions/submission-forms#deadlines) and confirmation email, and match your brand in [submission form settings](/settings/submission-form-settings).
- [Share and test the form](/faq/how-do-i-share-my-submission-form) with a real submission before you publish the link.

## Step 4: Integrations

Bring up integrations early so mapping and testing happen before the program is final.

- See [what each integration syncs](/integrations/what-syncs) and the [integrations overview](/integrations/overview); event-platform differences are in the [comparison](/apps/event-platform-comparison).
- Publish sessions and speakers to your website with [embeds](/sessions/embeds) or [publishing surfaces](/site/publishing-surfaces).
- Single sign-on for speakers is in [SSO](/integrations/sso); developers can use [API tokens](/integrations/api-tokens) and [webhooks](/integrations/webhooks).

## Step 5: Evaluation and acceptance

- Create an [evaluation plan](/evaluations/evaluation-plans), add evaluators, and assign submissions.
- Understand [what evaluators see](/faq/will-evaluators-have-the-same-access-to-my-event-that-i-do-as-an-admin) and how scores turn into decisions.
- [Accept and decline sessions](/sessions/accept-decline) and send the decision emails with the built-in templates.

## Step 6: Agenda

- Choose which statuses can be scheduled and set up rooms in [agenda settings](/sessions/program-settings#agenda).
- Build and adjust the schedule in the [agenda](/sessions/agenda) and clear the **Conflicts** list before publishing.

## Step 7: Speaker portal and tasks

- Read [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101), then create a portal for each speaker type you need.
- [Create and assign forms](/portals/create-assign-forms) and [tasks](/portals/task-assignment) — bios, headshots, disclosures, agreements — with due dates.
- [Preview the portal as an admin](/faq/how-can-i-view-a-portal-as-an-admin) before speakers are invited.

## Step 8: Communications

- Send from your own domain with a [custom email domain](/communications/add-on-custom-email-domain).
- Review the [automated emails](/communications/automated-emails) Sessionboard sends and turn on the ones you want.
- Build [email templates](/settings/email-templates), then send from the module or with [email campaigns](/communications/email-campaigns). Keep the [History](/communications/email-sms-history) open for delivery status.

## Step 9: Contacts and imports

- [Import](/settings/importing-data) contacts first, then sessions, then sponsors and exhibitors; use the update flow rather than re-importing.
- Clean up with [merge duplicate contacts](/contacts/merge-duplicates).
- Year-round speaker data lives in [Speaker CRM](/speaker-crm/overview).
