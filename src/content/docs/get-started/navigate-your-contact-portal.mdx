## Why use it

Participants who know where things are in the portal finish their tasks without emailing you. Send them this page with the invitation, so they can find their sessions, confirm their participation and upload what you need on their own.

## Signing in

The invitation email carries a personal link. Opening it signs the person straight in; if they come back later without the link, they sign in with the same email address on the portal's login page. Someone who belongs to more than one portal or event can move between them with **Switch Portals** and **Switch Events** in the header menu.

## The pages

| Page | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Cards for **My Participation**, **My Profile** and **Tasks**, a **Calendar** of the person's upcoming sessions, and any files you have shared. Each card has **View All**. |
| **My Participation** | Every session, abstract and application the person is on, grouped by status, with search — see [below](#my-participation). |
| **Profile** | The person's contact fields — bio, headshot, title, company and whatever else you have made visible. They can edit anything you have not locked. |
| **Tasks** | Everything they owe you, split into **My Tasks** (about them) and **Submission Tasks** (about a session they are on, which any participant on that session can complete). **Incomplete** and **Due This Week** filters narrow the list. |
| **Resources** | The pages you published to the portal — travel details, speaker guidelines, FAQs. |
| **Files** | Files you shared with the portal, to download. |

Clicking a submission on **Home** or under **Tasks** opens it in a panel with **Details**, **Tasks**, **Files** and **Messages** tabs — that panel is where they confirm participation, edit session details, [upload a presentation](/get-started/upload-a-file-to-a-session) and complete anything you asked for on that session.

<Note>
Which fields show, which are editable, and which submissions appear are portal settings. If a participant cannot see something, check the portal's **Manage Fields**, **Control Submission Visibility** and **Visibility filter** first. See [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101#configuration).
</Note>

<Tip>
Point participants at this article from a portal task or resource page, so the instructions sit next to the thing they are being asked to do.
</Tip>

## My Participation

The **My Participation** card on Home, and the **My Participation** tab, list everything the person is on. Chips across the top — **All**, **Abstracts**, **Sessions**, **Applications** — narrow the list; only the types they have appear, and **All** shows when there are two or more.

Rows are grouped, and each group can be collapsed:

| Group | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending Participant Confirmation** | Accepted sessions — and abstracts, when the event asks for them — waiting for the person to confirm, each with a **Confirm** button. Split into **Sessions** and **Abstracts** when both are present. Shown only when [participant acceptance](/speakers/speaker-acceptance) is on. |
| **Pending**, **Declined** and **Accepted Sessions** | Sessions by the status your team set. Custom statuses that are still in progress sit under **Pending**. |
| **Pending**, **Declined** and **Accepted Abstracts** | The same for abstracts. |
| **Pending**, **Declined** and **Accepted Applications** | The same for applications. |
| **Withdrawn Sessions** / **Withdrawn Abstracts** | Withdrawn submissions, collapsed by default. |

Each row shows the status name your team uses, including custom statuses. A row waiting on a confirmation is listed once, in the confirmation group. Subsessions stay with their parent: when only a subsession is in a group, its parent appears above it as a muted heading.

Once the person has confirmed or declined, the row's button reads **Participant Confirmation Completed**. It opens their answers, read-only; to change one, they contact the event team.
