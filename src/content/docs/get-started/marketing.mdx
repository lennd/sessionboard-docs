## Why use it

Sessionboard Marketing and Learning bring event promotion, audience engagement, and continuing education into one workspace. Marketing lets you run field events and webinars, score every contact's engagement across all your channels, and turn recordings into shareable content — without stitching together separate tools. Learning adds structured plans, credits, certifications, and learner transcripts so your audience can earn and prove credit directly from your event content.
