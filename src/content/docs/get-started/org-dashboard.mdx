The **Dashboard** is the watching page for your whole organization. It answers "how are we doing across every event?" in one screen, and every number on it opens the list behind it. For the things you need to act on today, use [Organization Home](/get-started/organization-home).

## Where to find it

Open **Dashboard** from the organization navigation. The page title reads "*Your organization* at a glance". The **Overview** tab is always there.

## What the Overview shows

Cards appear top to bottom. Some are hidden when you do not have access to the area behind them.

| Card | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs you across events** | One line with how many things need you, and the most urgent one (for example, an event with reviews past the deadline). **Open Home** takes you to the full list, most urgent first. |
| **Headline numbers** | **People**, **On a pathway**, **Accepted speakers** and **Returning speakers**, each with how much it changed. Cards for areas you cannot open are left out. |
| **Upcoming events** | Your next events with dates, days to go, the program size (speakers and sessions) and a health note such as late reviews or a call for speakers that is about to close. **All events** opens the events list. Shown to people who have access to Events. |
| **Your year** | A timeline of programs and deadlines across all events (see below). |
| **Pathways** and **Topics people want** | Live pathways, how many reached their goal, the step losing the most people, and the top topics with their coverage (**Under-served**, **No session yet** or **Covered**). Shown to people who have access to Pathways. |

The Overview can also show your Disclosures tile when disclosures are turned on for your organization.

Compare ranges: where a card shows change, the **Compare** menu sets the window: **Last 30 days vs 30 before**, **Last 90 days vs 90 before** or **Last 12 months vs 12 before**.

## The Your year timeline

The **Your year** card plots every program and deadline across your events on one timeline, so you can spot clashes and gaps. Events plot from your calendar. Calls for speakers, reviews, interest forms, awards and webinars join once their dates sync.

Click **Expand** to open the full page, **Dashboard > Your year**. There you can:

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose what to show">

Under **Show**, switch each row on or off: **Events**, **Calls for speakers**, **Reviews**, **Interest forms**, **Awards**, **Webinars**. At least one row stays on. You only see the rows you have access to: Events, Calls for speakers and Reviews need access to Events; Interest forms, Awards and Webinars need access to those areas.

</Step>

<Step title="Set the zoom">

Open **Zoom** and choose **Close up**, **Months** or **Whole year**. The timeline scrolls sideways.

</Step>

<Step title="Open an item">

Hover a bar for details and click it to open the event, form or program.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the Coming up table">

Below the timeline, **Coming up** lists every dated moment ahead, soonest first, with **Date**, **What**, **Program**, **Runs** and **Status**. A **Late** status means a deadline has passed; **Closing soon** means one is near.

</Step>

</Steps>

If nothing is dated yet, the card offers shortcuts such as **Add an event** and **Open a call for speakers**.

## Your own dashboards

If your role can view or create reports, a tab bar appears above the Overview with each saved dashboard as its own tab.

<Steps>

<Step title="Add a dashboard">

Click **Add Dashboard** and follow the dialog.

</Step>

<Step title="Switch between them">

Click a tab. **Overview** is always first.

</Step>

</Steps>

An organization can have up to 10 dashboards. At 10, **Add Dashboard** is disabled with the tooltip "Maximum of 10 dashboards reached".

<Note>
Without report permissions you see the Overview only, with no tab bar and no **Add Dashboard** button.
</Note>

## FAQ

**A card is missing from my Overview.** Cards depend on your access. People without access to Events do not see **Upcoming events**; people without access to Pathways do not see **Pathways** or **Topics people want**.

**Where did Data health go?** The chores that used to sit on the Dashboard, such as cleanup items, are now listed under **Needs you across events** on Home.

**Why is an event missing from the timeline?** Events plot from your calendar. Other items appear after their dates sync, and your own row toggles under **Show** also hide items.
