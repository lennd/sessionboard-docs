**Home** is the first row in the organization sidebar, above **Training**. It is where everyone in your organization lands, whether they run one event or all of them. It answers two questions: *where is the thing I am looking for?* and *what needs me today?*

<Note>
Home is cut to you. You only see records and links you have permission to open, and a person who works on one event sees that event's work, not the whole portfolio. Two people in the same organization can see different Home pages. That is expected.
</Note>

## Find or ask

The box at the top has two modes, **Find** and **Ask**. Use the **Mode** switch to pick one. 

- **Find** searches people, sessions, topics, content and accounts in one pass. Try a topic or a name.
- **Ask** answers a question from your own data, for example *Which topics have demand and no recent session?* The answer shows what it was worked out from, and **Copy as a table** puts it on your clipboard so you can paste it into a sheet or doc.

If your organization does not have Advanced Search yet, the box does not appear.

### Working with results

Results are grouped by type: **People**, **Sessions**, **Topics**, **Content** and **Accounts**. Pick a tab to see only one type, or use the filters for **Type**, **Topic**, **Event**, **Relationship** and **Account**. The counts update as you pick, and the line under the box says how many results match.

Select a result to see a preview on the right: who a speaker spoke with, which events and topics they connect to, and buttons such as **Open profile**, **Open session** and **Explore graph**. **Save as segment** keeps the search so you can use it again.

<Note>
If a search does not answer, your words and filters are kept. Try again, or drop a filter.
</Note>

## Needs you across events

This list gathers what is waiting on you in every event you can see. Each row has one button that opens the place to fix it.

| Row says | Button |
|---|---|
| Reviews are past the deadline at an event | **Nudge reviewers** |
| A call for speakers closes soon, with drafts not submitted | **Remind drafters** |
| Likely duplicate people are waiting in Match review | **Review matches** |
| People have no email | **Open people** |
| Tags that look like tests | **Clean up** |
| Pathway items waiting in Review, or disclosures still needed | **Review** |

Choose **All** and the count to see everything, or **Show fewer** to fold the list back up. Rows disappear when the underlying problem is fixed.

## Recently updated

Events, sessions and people your team edited most recently. Use it to pick up where a teammate left off.

## Home or Dashboard

Home is for the whole organization. The **Dashboard** is for one event: its countdown, stat cards and attention list. Open an event, then **Dashboard**, to see it. See [Finding your way around the sidebar](/get-started/sidebar/).

## Troubleshooting

- **I do not see the search box.** Your organization does not have Advanced Search turned on. Ask your Customer Success contact.
- **A row I expected is missing.** Home hides anything you cannot open. Check your role under **Event Team** and your portfolio scope with an organization admin.
- **Part of Home did not load.** You will see a message with **Retry**. Search keeps working while the rest loads.
