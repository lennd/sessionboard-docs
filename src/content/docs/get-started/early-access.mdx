## Preview and Beta

Every feature in the catalogue is one of two stages, and the stage decides whether you can help yourself.

| Stage | How you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Preview** | Toggle it on and off yourself, immediately. |
| **Beta** | Request access. Our team reaches out to set it up with you. |

Beta features usually need configuration, data, or a conversation about fit — which is why they are not a switch. Preview features are self-serve because the worst case is that you turn them back off.

<Tip>
Turning a Preview off is not destructive. Anything you configured is still there if you turn it back on.
</Tip>

## Where to find it

**Preview** is the last row but one in the sidebar footer, at both levels, and a feature appears at whichever level it applies to:

| Scope | Where | What lives there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization | **Preview** in the organization sidebar | Features that apply across every event, with an **All** / **Org-wide** / **Per-event** filter |
| Event | **Preview** in the event sidebar | Features you enable for one event, plus the org-wide ones for reference |

You need the **Manage Early Access** permission to see the page; the Admin and Admin Lite roles have it. Without it, Sessionboard shows a permission message instead. Ask an admin on your team if you need it added.

## Join the program

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Preview">

Click **Preview** at the bottom of the sidebar. Before you join, the page describes the program, counts the features available, and shows every card with a **Join Early Access to enable** (or **to request**) badge in place of its control.

</Step>

<Step title="Join">

Click **Join Early Access**. The dialog spells out what joining means — features can change, bugs happen, feedback is expected — and asks you to acknowledge it. Joining does not enable anything on its own; it only unlocks the controls on the cards. One opt-in covers the whole organization.

</Step>

</Steps>

Each card opens a fuller description of what the feature does and what it gives you, so you can decide before switching anything on.

## Turn a Preview on or off

Preview cards carry a toggle. Flip it and the card reads **Active**, or **Active org-wide** for a feature that covers the whole organization. Flip it again to turn the feature off; the card goes back to **Per-event — toggle to enable** or **Org-level — toggle to enable**.

Turning on an org-wide Preview from an event asks you to confirm first, because it reaches every event in the organization. From the organization page, a per-event Preview opens an event picker instead of a plain switch, so you choose which events get it.

## Request a Beta

Beta cards have a **Request access** button instead of a toggle. The request asks what you want to use the feature for and when you hope to use it, and our team reaches out to set it up with you. Once you have asked, the card reads **On the list — we'll reach out** and the toggle stays greyed out until the feature is on. A counter at the top of the page tracks how many you are **On the waitlist** for.

When our team turns a Beta on for you, its card reads **Active** with a switch you cannot flip yourself — Betas are managed with us, not self-serve.

## Organization or event

Every card carries an **Org-wide** or **Per-event** chip, and the chip tells you how far a switch reaches.

- **Org-wide** features are one setting that every event inherits. Turn one on from an event and Sessionboard confirms you mean the whole organization.
- **Per-event** features are enabled for one event at a time. Open **Preview** inside the event to flip them, or use the event picker on the organization page.

The organization page lists both kinds and filters them with **All**, **Org-wide** and **Per-event**. The event page lists what you can decide for that event, and shows org-wide features for reference.

## What changes in the sidebar

A feature that is off but available to you shows in the sidebar as a row with a small lock icon. Turning the Preview on removes the lock, and the row opens the feature. Turning it off puts the lock back. Nothing else moves, so a Preview never rearranges the pages you already use.

If you cannot find a row you expect after turning a feature on, open its module in the sidebar — modules open one at a time, and the new row sits with the rest of that module.

## Send feedback

Preview and Beta features change based on what you tell us, and the place to say it is **Community**, the row just above **Preview** in the sidebar footer. **Ideas** is where you ask for a change or vote for one someone else already raised, **Roadmap** shows what is being built, and **What's New** announces what has shipped. See [Community: ideas, roadmap and what's new](/help/community). Beta onboarding also includes direct feedback with our team as you use the feature.

## When a feature graduates

Features leave the catalogue when they are ready for everyone. A graduated feature is on by default for every organization, its card disappears from **Preview**, and nothing you configured while it was a Preview changes. **What's New** in **Community** is where graduations are announced.

<Note>
The September 2026 release graduated a whole wave this way — AI Event Agents, Email Campaigns, Automations, Print Agendas, Notifications & Messaging, Live Transcribe and Advocacy are now on for every organization and no longer appear in the catalogue. New Navigation rolled out to every organization at the same time.
</Note>

## Leave the program

**Leave program** sits on the enrolled card at the top of the page. Leaving keeps every Preview you already turned on, and any Beta access you have been granted continues; you can still turn those off one by one. What stops is new opt-ins — you cannot enable further Previews or request Betas until you rejoin, which is the same one click.

## What is in the catalogue

The list changes as features arrive and graduate, so treat this as a snapshot rather than a promise. Today the catalogue holds one Preview and a set of Betas:

- **Content Galleries** (Preview) — hosted, searchable galleries of recordings, clips, transcripts and documents, as one org-wide switch or per event. See [Content galleries](/site/content-galleries).
- Organization Betas — **Marketing**, **Dispatches**, **Requests**, **Contracts / Docusign**, **Scouting Reports & Scout Agent**, **Learning (Lessons & Credits)** and **Portfolios**. See [Portfolios](/events/portfolios) and [Send and track contracts](/speaker-crm/contracts).
- Event Betas — **Poster Gallery**, **Surveys**, **Floor Maps** and **Event Pipeline**. See [Poster galleries](/marketing/poster-gallery) and [Surveys](/evaluations/surveys).

Each feature's own article tracks what it does today.

## Things worth knowing

**Some features need another one first.** Dispatches sits inside the Marketing module, so it needs Marketing too; the card tells you so and stays disabled until it is on. Features that use AI carry an **AI** badge and need **AI features** switched on for the organization first; their cards say so.

**Preview features move quickly.** They ship on a faster cadence than the rest of Sessionboard, so labels and layouts can shift between releases.
