The sidebar is grouped into modules — a handful of rows that each open to reveal the pages inside. Instead of one long list, you open the part of Sessionboard you are working in and see only that.

You never have to remember which module owns a page. Search finds any page you have access to, so the fastest route to something you use rarely is to type its name.

## How it is laid out

Every sidebar, at the organization level and inside an event, has the same five zones from top to bottom.

| Zone | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Context header | Which organization or event you are in. Click it to switch to another one, or jump back to the organization. |
| **Dashboard** | Always the first row, above everything else. |
| Pinned | The pages you chose to keep at the top. Empty until you pin something. |
| Modules | The grouped rows — Program, CRM, Marketing and the rest. One opens at a time. |
| Footer | The things you reach occasionally: reporting, history, setup and settings. |

Only one module stays open at a time. Opening a second closes the first, which is what keeps the sidebar short no matter how much of Sessionboard you use.

<Note>
You will not see every module and every link listed on this page. The sidebar only shows what your organization has enabled and what your permissions allow, so your sidebar is a subset of what follows.
</Note>

## The organization sidebar

The organization sidebar is what you see outside a specific event — across your whole programme.

![The organization sidebar with the Program module open, showing Overview, Events, and Awards, with CRM, Marketing, Reports, and History below](/images/kb/sidebar-org-modules.png)

### Program

Everything that is a thing you run.

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Programs | **Events**, **Awards**, **Webinars** |
| Build | **Briefs**, **Sites** |

### CRM

Your people and the pipeline of who might join them.

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Contacts | **Contacts**, **Exhibitors**, **Sponsors** |
| Pipeline | **Pipeline**, **Interest Forms**, **Segments**, **Portal Tasks** |

### Marketing

Content and promotion across every event. See [Marketing](/marketing/what-is-sessionboard-marketing).

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Promote | **Advocacy** |

Organizations in the Marketing beta see more here — the full content engine of media, voices, posts, and brand surfaces. Their pages describe themselves; see [Early Access](/get-started/early-access).

### Below the modules

**Reports** and **History** sit under the modules, and the footer holds **Setup**, **Community**, **Preview** — the [Early Access](/get-started/early-access) catalogue — and **Settings**.

## The event sidebar

Inside an event the modules cover the same ground, scoped to that event.

![The event sidebar with the Program module open, showing the Submissions, Collect & Review, Portals, and Configure sections](/images/kb/sidebar-event-modules.png)

### Program

Sessions, speakers, submissions and everything you do to them.

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Submissions | **View All**, **Abstracts**, **Sessions**, **My Evaluations**, **Files** |
| Collect & Review | **Forms**, **Send**, **Evaluation**, **Surveys**, **Agenda**, **Invoices**, **Applications**, **Site**, **Print** |
| Portals | **Portals** — one row per portal you have built |
| Configure | **Settings** |

### CRM

The people attached to this event, grouped by the role they play.

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| People | **Contacts** — one row per participant role you use |
| Exhibitors | **Exhibitors** |
| Sponsors | **Sponsors** |
| Configure | **Coordinator** |

### Marketing

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Promote | **Advocacy** |
| Source & produce | **Transcribe** |

As at the organization level, the Marketing beta adds the rest of the content engine here.

### CMS

Where the event shows up in public: **Overview**, **Embeds** and **Site**.

### Below the modules

**Automations**, **Reports**, **Workflows**, **Agents** and **History** sit under the modules. The footer holds **Event Team**, **Setup**, **Community**, **Preview** and **Settings**.

## Pin the pages you use every day

Pinned pages sit above the modules, so the handful you open constantly are always one click away.

<Steps>

<Step title="Hover the page you want">

Open the module the page lives in and hover its row. A pin icon appears on the right.

</Step>

<Step title="Click the pin">

The page moves into the Pinned section at the top. Click the pin again — from either place — to remove it.

</Step>

<Step title="Drag to reorder">

Grab a pinned row and drag it up or down.

</Step>

</Steps>

Pins are saved to your account, not your browser, so they follow you to any computer you sign in on. They are also scoped: your organization pins and each event's pins are separate lists, because the pages you need for one event are rarely the ones you need for the next.

<Tip>
Pins are per person. Yours do not change anyone else's sidebar, so pin freely.
</Tip>

## Search instead of hunting

Search sits at the top of every page, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> on Windows). It finds pages as well as records, and only ever returns what your permissions allow — so it is the quickest way to reach anything you use too rarely to remember the location of.

## Fields, tags and personas

These live under **Settings → Library**, at both the organization and event level, alongside your files and — inside an event — your evaluation criteria.

Submission uploads are the exception: they stay with the work, under **Program → Submissions → Files**.

<Note>
Old links and bookmarks still work. A saved URL from before the sidebar was regrouped redirects itself to the current location, so there is nothing to go and update.
</Note>

## Make the sidebar smaller

Collapse the sidebar to a narrow icon rail using the control at the bottom of it — useful on a laptop, or on a page with a wide table. Modules still open as flyouts when you hover them, and the choice is remembered the next time you sign in.
