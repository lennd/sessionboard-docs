You never have to remember which module owns a page. Search finds any page you have access to, so the fastest route to something you use rarely is to type its name.

## How it is laid out

Every sidebar, at the organization level and inside an event, has the same five zones from top to bottom.

| Zone | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Context header | Which organization or event you are in. Click it to switch to another one, or jump back to the organization. |
| **Dashboard** and **Training** | Always the first two rows, above everything else. Training holds the short video chapters and the setup path for the organization or event you are in, shown for the parts of Sessionboard you can use — see [Video tutorials](/videos/overview). |
| Pinned | The pages you chose to keep at the top. The zone is hidden until you pin something. |
| Modules | The grouped rows — Program, CRM, Marketing and the rest. One opens at a time. |
| Footer | The rows you reach occasionally: reporting, history, setup and settings. The control that collapses the sidebar sits at the very bottom. |

Only one module stays open at a time. Opening a second closes the first, which is what keeps the sidebar short no matter how much of Sessionboard you use.

<Note>
You will not see every module and every link listed on this page. The sidebar only shows what your organization has enabled and what your permissions allow, so your sidebar is a subset of what follows. Rows marked *optional* below appear only when the feature behind them is on.
</Note>

A row with a small lock icon is a feature your organization can add — through [Early Access](/get-started/early-access) or as an add-on. Click it to read what it does and to request or turn it on.

## The organization sidebar

The organization sidebar is what you see outside a specific event — across your whole programme.

![The organization sidebar with the Program module open — Overview, then Events and Awards under Programs, then Sites and a locked Content Galleries row under Build, with CRM, Marketing and Learning below](/images/kb/sidebar-org-modules.png)

### Program

Everything that is a thing you run.

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Programs | **Events**, **Awards**, **Webinars** *(optional — with Media Gallery)* |
| Build | **Briefs** *(optional)*, **Sites**, **Content Galleries** *(optional)* |

### CRM

Your people and the pipeline of who might join them.

![The organization sidebar with the CRM module open — Overview, then Contacts, Exhibitors and Sponsors under Contacts, then Pipeline, Interest Forms, Segments and Portal Tasks under Pipeline](/images/kb/sidebar-org-crm.png)

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Contacts | **Contacts**, **Exhibitors**, **Sponsors** |
| Pipeline | **Pipeline**, **Interest Forms**, **Segments**, **Portal Tasks**, plus **Scout** and **Contracts** *(optional)* |

### Marketing

Content and promotion across every event. See [Marketing](/marketing/what-is-sessionboard-marketing).

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Promote | **Advocacy** |
| Source & produce | **Media**, **Create** |

Organizations in the Marketing beta see more here — **Dispatch**, **Plan**, **Creators**, **Voices**, **Posts**, **Quotes**, **Clips**, **Brand** and an **Intelligence** section. Their pages describe themselves; see [Early Access](/get-started/early-access).

### Learning

**Learning** is a module row like the others, but it stays visible even before your organization has it. Locked, it opens a short introduction to Lessons & Credits with a button to request the beta; unlocked, it holds **Lessons**, **Series**, **Catalogs**, **Credits**, **Credit Types**, **Destinations** and **Credit Settings**.

### Below the modules

**Reports** and **History** sit under the modules. The footer holds **Community**, **Preview** — the [Early Access](/get-started/early-access) catalogue — and **Settings**, with a **Setup** row above them for organizations that have the Setup Center.

## The event sidebar

Inside an event the modules cover the same ground, scoped to that event.

![The event sidebar with the Program module open, showing the Submissions and Collect & Review sections and the start of the Portals section](/images/kb/sidebar-event-modules.png)

### Program

Sessions, speakers, submissions and everything you do to them.

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Submissions | **View All**, **Abstracts**, **Sessions**, **Files**, plus **My Evaluations** for reviewers who can see their assignments but not the sessions list |
| Collect & Review | **Forms**, **Send**, **Evaluation**, **Agenda**, **Site**, **Print**, plus **Surveys**, **Invoices** and **Applications** *(optional)* |
| Portals | **Portals**, **Tasks**, **Forms**, **File Requests**, **Resources**, **Files** — the things you build once and assign across your portals |
| Configure | **Settings** |

### CRM

The people attached to this event, grouped by the role they play.

![The event sidebar with the CRM module open — All Contacts and one row per participant role under People, All Exhibitors and Intake Form under Exhibitors, All Sponsors, Tiers and Intake Form under Sponsors](/images/kb/sidebar-event-crm.png)

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| People | **All Contacts**, then one row per participant role you use — **Speakers**, **Chairpersons**, **Moderators**, **Submitters** and so on — and **Additional Contacts** |
| Exhibitors | **All Exhibitors**, **Intake Form** |
| Sponsors | **All Sponsors**, **Tiers**, **Intake Form** |
| Configure | **Coordinator** |

### Marketing

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| — | **Overview** |
| Promote | **Advocacy** |
| Source & produce | **Transcribe**, **Media**, **Create**, **Slides** |

As at the organization level, the Marketing beta adds the rest of the content engine here.

### CMS

Where the event shows up in public: **Overview**, **Embeds** and **Site**. **Poster Gallery**, **Content Galleries** and **Floor Maps** sit between them with a lock until they are on for the event.

### Learning

The same locked-until-you-have-it row as at the organization level. Inside an event it unlocks when the organization has Lessons & Credits or the event uses Surveys.

### Below the modules

**Automations**, **Reports**, **Agents** and **History** sit under the modules, with **Workflows** between them for events that use approval workflows. The footer holds **Event Team**, **Community**, **Preview** and **Settings**, plus **Setup** for organizations with the Setup Center.

## Pin the pages you use every day

Pinned pages sit above the modules, so the handful you open constantly are always one click away.

<Steps>

<Step title="Hover the page you want">

Open the module the page lives in and hover its row. A pin icon appears on the right of the row.

</Step>

<Step title="Click the pin">

The page moves into a **Pinned** zone at the top of the sidebar. Click the pin again — from either place — to remove it.

![The Pinned zone at the top of the sidebar holding the Evaluation page](/images/kb/sidebar-pinned.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Drag to reorder">

Grab a pinned row and drag it up or down.

</Step>

</Steps>

Pins are saved to your account, not your browser, so they follow you to any computer you sign in on. They are also scoped: your organization pins and each event's pins are separate lists, because the pages you need for one event are rarely the ones you need for the next.

<Tip>
Pins are per person. Yours do not change anyone else's sidebar, so pin freely.
</Tip>

## Search instead of hunting

The **Find or ask** box sits at the top of every page; click it or press <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> on Windows). Start typing and results appear as you go, grouped under **All**, **Pages**, **Contacts**, **Sessions** and **Files** — each page result shows the module path it lives under, so you learn where it is at the same time. The first row is always **Ask Team Lead**: press it to hand the same words to Team Lead as a question about this event instead. Search only ever returns what your permissions allow, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

![The Find or ask overlay with "Agenda" typed, showing an Ask Team Lead row, the All / Pages / Contacts / Sessions / Files tabs, and page results with their module path](/images/kb/sidebar-search.png)

## Fields, tags and personas

These live under **Settings → Library**, at both the organization and event level, alongside your files and — inside an event — your evaluation criteria.

Submission uploads are the exception: they stay with the work, under **Program → Submissions → Files**.

<Note>
Old links and bookmarks still work. A saved URL from before the sidebar was regrouped redirects itself to the current location, so there is nothing to go and update.
</Note>

## Make the sidebar smaller

Collapse the sidebar to a narrow icon rail using the arrow at the bottom of it — useful on a laptop, or on a page with a wide table. Modules still open as flyouts when you hover their icons, pinned pages gather under a pin icon, and the choice is remembered the next time you sign in. The same arrow expands it again.

![The sidebar collapsed to an icon rail](/images/kb/sidebar-collapsed.png)
