Your **Onboarding Sandbox** is a practice event. You can build in it freely, but you don't move the sandbox itself to live. You **clone** it into a new event, choose which parts come across, and that new event is your live event. This page covers what to bring, what to leave, and what to check afterward.

{/* label-ok: Onboarding Sandbox */}

## Before you clone

- **Decide what you built that you want to keep.** Forms, review plans, portals, tasks, email templates and settings are worth cloning. The sample sessions, speakers and contacts the sandbox was filled with are not.
- **Ask your Customer Success Manager first if you are still in guided onboarding.** Cloning is available when **Clone Events** is switched on for your organization. If you don't see **Clone** on the **Events** page, ask your CSM or [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com). Support can run the clone for you.
- **Pick the organization.** To clone into a different organization you own, say which one when you ask support. Both organizations must be in the same data region.

## Clone the sandbox

<Steps>
<Step>
Open your organization's **Events** page, open the sandbox event's menu, and choose **Clone**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter an **Event Name** for the live event, then the **New Start Date** and **New End Date**. The sandbox's dates are shown for reference.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Select copy options**, tick what you want to bring. Leave **Contacts**, **Sessions** and **Sponsors** unticked unless you built real records there, because the sandbox's are sample data. The full list and what each option does is in [What gets copied](/faq/clone-an-event#what-gets-copied).
</Step>
<Step>
Decide whether the new event keeps the sandbox's branding (**Start with this event's own branding** is the alternative), then start the clone. It can take a few minutes, and you are taken to the new event when it finishes.
</Step>
</Steps>

{/* label-ok: Start with this event's own branding */}

<Note>
Every clone is a new event, so nothing is merged into an event you already built. If your live event already exists, copy pieces into it by hand, or ask support which parts they can carry across.
</Note>

## What to check in the live event

Some things are copied in a safe state on purpose, and some never come across.

- **Review plans** are copied **closed**. Set a new due date to reopen each one.
- **Email campaigns** are copied as **Draft** with no schedule or history. Send stays locked until your kickoff call.
- **Sites** get a placeholder slug ending in "-copy". Set the real slug in Site Settings, and publish the new event's programs yourself.
- **Submission forms** copy their questions, not their submissions. Check the **Open Date** and **Close Date**, and the participant role limits.
- **Integration credentials** are not something you can change yourself until your kickoff call. Your CSM connects them with you.
- **Anything the sandbox banner warned about.** The banner says work in the sandbox may not fully transfer, so open each form, portal and plan in the live event once before you announce it.

## Starting the live event clean instead

If you would rather not bring anything across, ask for an empty event with **Add Event**, as described in [start a new event from scratch](/faq/clone-an-event#start-a-new-event-from-scratch), and rebuild with the [onboarding checklist](/get-started/onboarding-checklist) as your order of work.
