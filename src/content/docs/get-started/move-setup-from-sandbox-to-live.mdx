Your **Onboarding Sandbox** is a practice event. You can build in it freely, but you don't move the sandbox itself to live. Your live event is a **copy** of the sandbox: you ask for a new event that starts from the sandbox, choose which parts come across, and our Customer Success team creates it for you. This page covers how to ask, what to bring, what to leave, and what to check afterward.

{/* label-ok: Onboarding Sandbox */}

## Before you ask

- **Decide what you built that you want to keep.** Forms, review plans, portals, tasks, email templates and settings are worth copying. The sample sessions, speakers and contacts the sandbox was filled with are not.
- **Know who can ask.** Org admins see **Add Program**. New conferences aren't created on the spot. Your submission goes to Customer Success as a request, and they create the event.
- **Pick the organization.** To copy into a different organization you own, say which one in the request's note. Both organizations must be in the same data region.

## Request your live event as a copy of the sandbox

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add Program**, either on your organization's **Events** page or under the **+** button in the header, and pick **Conference**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Start from**, choose **A copy of an existing event**, then pick your sandbox as the **Event to copy**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **What to bring over**, tick what you want to copy. Leave contacts, sessions and sponsors unticked unless you built real records there, because the sandbox's are sample data. Tick **Reset branding** if the live event should start with its own logo and background instead of the sandbox's. Each option is explained in [What gets copied](/faq/clone-an-event#what-gets-copied).
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **The basics** for the live event: its name, start and end dates, and time zone. Answer **Help us set it up** if you can. Rough answers are fine and every question is optional.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Send request**. You'll see **Request sent**. Customer Success creates the live event as a copy of your sandbox with the options you picked, adds you to its team, and emails you when it's ready. Until then, the request is listed on the **Add Program** page.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Some organizations have **Clone Events** switched on. If yours does and you're an admin on the sandbox, the button reads **Clone event** instead of **Send request**, and the copy is created straight away with no request. In that case you'll also find **Clone** in the sandbox's menu on the **Events** page. See [Clone it yourself](/faq/clone-an-event#clone-it-yourself).
</Tip>

<Note>
Every copy is a new event, so nothing is merged into an event you already built. If your live event already exists, copy pieces into it by hand, or say in the request which parts you want carried across.
</Note>

## What to check in the live event

Some things are copied in a safe state on purpose, and some never come across.

- **Review plans** are copied **closed**. Set a new due date to reopen each one.
- **Email campaigns** are copied as **Draft** with no schedule or history. Sending stays locked until your kickoff call.
- **Sites** get a placeholder slug ending in "-copy". Set the real slug in Site Settings, and publish the new event's programs yourself.
- **Submission forms** copy their questions, not their submissions. Check the **Open Date** and **Close Date**, and the participant role limits.
- **Integration credentials** aren't something you can change yourself until your kickoff call. Your CSM connects them with you.
- **The sandbox banner stays behind.** The copy is a normal event, not a sandbox, so it doesn't show the sandbox banner.
- **Anything the sandbox banner warned about.** The banner says work in the sandbox may not fully transfer, so open each form, portal and plan in the live event once before you announce it.

## Starting the live event clean instead

If you'd rather not bring anything across, choose **A blank event** under **Start from** and send the request the same way, as described in [Start a new event from scratch](/faq/clone-an-event#start-a-new-event-from-scratch). Then rebuild using the [onboarding checklist](/get-started/onboarding-checklist) as your order of work.
