Sessionboard runs your speaker and session program end to end: the call for papers, the review, the agenda, the speaker portal, the emails, and what you publish. New here? Follow the six steps below in order — each one links to the article that covers it. Already set up? Jump to your module.

<Tip>
Our onboarding team sets up a new event in the same order. The long form, with every sub-step, is the [New client onboarding checklist](/get-started/onboarding-checklist). Prefer watching? Every step has a training chapter — start with the [Platform overview](/get-started/overview).
</Tip>
