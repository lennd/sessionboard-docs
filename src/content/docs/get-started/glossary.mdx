Sessionboard uses a handful of words in a specific way. Each term here gets a sentence or two and a link to the article that goes deeper. If a word you hear from our team is missing, tell us at [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com).

## Where things live

**Organization** — the container for everything your company or association runs on Sessionboard. Events live inside it, and so do the things you share between them: contacts, global custom fields, email templates, your team, the Speaker CRM and Awards. [Find your way around the sidebar](/get-started/sidebar)

**Event** — one conference, program or awards cycle, with its own dates, forms, sessions, speakers, agenda and portals. Most of your day-to-day work happens inside an event. [Event details](/events/event-details)

**Module** — a group in the left sidebar: Program, CRM, Marketing, CMS, Awards, Portals, Communications, Automations, Reporting, Agents, Event Team, Settings and Apps. The Help Center's sidebar mirrors them. [Get started by module](/get-started#get-started-by-module)

**Dashboard** — the first page of an event: what needs attention, upcoming deadlines, recent activity, and pinned shortcuts. [Event dashboard](/events/event-dashboard)

## The program

**Submission** — what arrives through a submission form: an abstract or session proposal with the people on it, and the files that came with it. A submission becomes a session when you accept it, or it can be a session from day one. [Submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms)

**Session** — a talk, panel, workshop, poster or any other slot in the program, with its participants, fields, files and a place on the agenda. [Create a session](/sessions/create-a-session)

**Subsession** — a session nested inside another, for a symposium with several talks or a track block. [Create a subsession](/sessions/create-a-subsession)

**Participant roles** — the roles a person can hold on a session: **Submitter** (who sent it in), **Speaker**, **Chairperson** and **Moderator**. One person can hold several; each role has its own fields and portal view. [Session participant roles](/concepts/participant-roles)

**Contact** — a person on the event, in any role. The same person is one contact across every event in the organization, so a bio or headshot updated once follows them. [Contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile)

**Program settings** — your event's vocabulary: tracks, formats, rooms, levels, tags, session statuses and roles. Set it before you build forms, because every form and filter uses it. [Program settings](/sessions/program-settings)

**Agenda** — the schedule: sessions placed in rooms and time slots, with conflict alerts, drag-and-drop and a public view. [Agenda](/sessions/agenda)

## Forms

**Submission form** — the call for papers: how proposals and the people on them come into an event. [Submission forms](/sessions/submission-forms)

**Application form** — a form a person fills in about themselves rather than about a session — speaker, volunteer or scholarship applications. [Create applications](/applications/create-applications)

**Portal form** — a form you assign inside a portal to collect something later: a headshot, an agreement, AV needs. [Create and assign forms](/portals/create-assign-forms)

**Interest form** — a standing "speak with us" page at the organization level; submissions land in the CRM pipeline rather than on an event. Part of Speaker CRM. [Interest forms](/speaker-crm/speaker-crm-interest-forms)

**Which form should I use?** — the one-page comparison of every form kind. [Which form should I use?](/concepts/which-form-should-i-use)

## Fields

**Standard field** — a field Sessionboard provides on every record: title, description, name, email, company. [Sessionboard standard fields](/concepts/sessionboard-standard-fields)

**Custom field** — a field you add: CE credits, dietary needs, a LinkedIn URL. Each has a type (text, dropdown, file, URL, formula…) that decides what it can hold. [Create and delete custom fields](/faq/how-to-create-and-delete-custom-fields) · [Custom field type reference](/concepts/custom-field-type-reference)

**Event field vs. global field** — an event field exists only on that event; a global field's data follows the record across every event in the organization. Organization-level fields are always global. [Field types: session, people & group](/concepts/field-types)

**Field rule** — formats text, calculates into an editable field, or sets fields when values match. [Create a field rule](/settings/field-rules)

## Review and selection

**Evaluation plan** — the rules for reviewing a round of submissions: who reviews what, how many each reviewer takes, what they score. Written once, applied to the whole round. [Set up round-based evaluations](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations)

**Round** — one pass of review. A program can run several rounds — a first cut, then a committee round — with different reviewers and criteria. [Rounds](/evaluations/setting-up-round-based-evaluations#step-2-rounds)

**Reviewer / evaluator** — a person invited to score submissions. They work in their own evaluator view, not the admin. [Navigate evaluation plans](/get-started/navigate-evaluation-plans)

**Accept / decline** — the decision on a submission, with the emails and status changes that follow. [Accept and decline](/sessions/accept-decline)

**Status** — where a submission or session is in its life (Pending, Accepted, Declined and the custom statuses you add under them). Filters on a standard status include its custom ones. [Program settings](/sessions/program-settings)

## Portals and communications

**Portal** — the branded page where a speaker, sponsor or exhibitor signs in to see their tasks, update their bio and headshot, upload files and sign agreements. Each audience can have its own portal. [Portals 101](/portals/portals-101)

**Task** — something you ask a participant to do in their portal by a date: upload slides, confirm travel, sign an agreement. [Assign tasks](/portals/assign-tasks)

**Email template** — a reusable message with merge tags, shared across the organization. [Email templates](/settings/email-templates)

**Sending domain** — the address your emails come from (`speakers@yourconference.org` rather than a Sessionboard address). [Send email from your own domain](/settings/domains)

**Automation** — a recipe: when a field changes or a form comes in, send an email, assign reviewers or write a field. [Automations](/automations/overview)

## Beyond the event

**Speaker CRM** — the organization-level module for speakers you have not booked yet: interest forms, a pipeline board, speaker profiles across events. An add-on. [What is Speaker CRM?](/speaker-crm/overview)

**Pipeline** — the CRM's kanban board: prospects as cards, moved through stages, assigned to an event when confirmed. [Pipeline](/speaker-crm/pipeline)

**Awards** — the add-on for awards, grants and scholarships: application forms, judging rounds, results. [Getting started with Awards](/awards/getting-started)

**Program site / embeds / content gallery** — the three ways the program appears in public: a hosted site, live embeds on your own website, and a browsable library of recordings and resources. [Which surface do I need?](/site/publishing-surfaces)

**Agents** — AI teammates inside Sessionboard. **Team Lead** answers questions about your event and the product; **Coordinator** chases tasks and reminders. [Agents overview](/agents/overview)

## Access and availability

**Event team** — the people who can sign in to an event, each with a role that decides what they can see and change. [Invite and manage event team members](/event-team/invite-manage-event-team-members)

**Permission** — one thing a role may do (read sessions, update settings, manage users). Articles list the permission a step needs in the box under their title.

**Preview (Early Access)** — the catalogue of features you can switch on for your organization before they are on for everyone. *Preview* features are self-serve; *Beta* features you request and our team enables. [Preview](/get-started/early-access)

**Add-on** — a product sold separately (Speaker CRM, Awards, SSO, Custom Sending Domain). Your Customer Success Manager turns it on. The box under an article's title says whether it applies.
