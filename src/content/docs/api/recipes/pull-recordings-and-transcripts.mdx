---
title: "Pull recordings and transcripts"
description: "Fetch a session's audio recordings, its transcriptions and the composed content pack."
---

Pull what Sessionboard made from your sessions into a video platform, an LMS or an archive. You need a token with `read:transcriptions`.

## Everything for one session in one call

[`GET /v1/event/{eventId}/sessions/{sessionId}/content`](/api/reference/transcriptions/get-composed-content-for-a-session) returns the session's composed transcription, preferred summary, insights, podcast and summary links. Start here for an app or partner integration.

## Transcriptions across the event

[`GET /v1/event/{eventId}/transcriptions`](/api/reference/transcriptions/list-transcriptions-for-an-event) is paginated. Filter by `session_id`, by `language_code`, or by `type` (`fragment`, `summary`, `insight`, `translation`).

## Audio recordings

[`GET /v1/event/{eventId}/sessions/{sessionId}/recordings`](/api/reference/session-recordings/list-session-audio-recordings) lists a session's recordings. Get one with [`GET …/recordings/{recordingId}`](/api/reference/session-recordings/get-a-session-audio-recording).

To go the other way and send media in for transcription, see [Media & Transcriptions](/api/guides/uploading-media-and-transcriptions).
