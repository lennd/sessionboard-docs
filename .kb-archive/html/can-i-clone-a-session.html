How to Clone a Session

  1. From the Sessions Dashboard, select the session(s) you would like to clone by clicking the checkbox found on the left of your view. 
  2. A sub-menu will appear. Here, click on 'More' and select 'Duplicate Sessions'. 
    1. A confirmation pop-up will appear, giving admins an option to 'Duplicate' or 'Cancel' the request.
  3. Your session has been cloned. 

What is included in my cloned session?

When cloning a session, all information is included except

  • The status of the cloned session will be pending
  • Uploaded files will not be included
