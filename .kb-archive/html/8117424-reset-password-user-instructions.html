Forgot your password?

  1. Go to https://app.sessionboard.com/forgot or select Forgot password? on the sign-in page.

  2. Enter the email address of the user who forgot their password.

  3. Click Send Reset Link. The user will receive an email with instructions on resetting their password. The email will come from "no-reply@sessionboard.com".
