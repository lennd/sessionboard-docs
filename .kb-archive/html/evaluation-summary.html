The Evaluation Summary Page is a powerful new dashboard that provides a high-level overview of all session evaluations within your event. This page is designed to help event teams quickly assess evaluation progress, identify top-performing sessions, and track evaluator engagement in real-time.

How to Access the Summary Page

In the left-hand menu, select Evaluation under the Sessions module and click on the page Summary at the top of your page.   

The Summary page includes the following key metrics and visualizations:

💡TIP: Click the session to view its full details in a new tab.


Top-Level Stats

  • # of Evaluations: Total number of evaluations submitted.

  • # of Evaluated Sessions: Total sessions that have received at least one evaluation.

  • # of Evaluation Plans: Total active evaluation plans in the event (includes both AI & standard Evaluation Plans).

  • # of Evaluators: Number of unique evaluators who are assigned to an evaluation plan.

Highest and Lowest Scoring Sessions

  • View the session with the highest average score and the one with the lowest. Helpful for identifying your strongest and weakest submissions.

Completion Status Chart

  • A visual breakdown of the evaluation process showing the percentage of completed and incomplete evaluation plans.

Average Session Score by Plan

  • A bar graph comparing average scores across different evaluation plans.

Top 10 Sessions

  • A ranked list of the sessions with the highest average scores. 

“Thought-Provoking” Sessions

  • Sessions that received a wide range of evaluator feedback from the highest and the lowest score. This highlights submissions that may be more subjective or spark discussion.
