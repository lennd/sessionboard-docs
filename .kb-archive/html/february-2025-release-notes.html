Release Date: February 14, 2025

Welcome to this month’s product updates! Below is a summary of new features, enhancements, and bug fixes released during this cycle. These updates are designed to improve your overall experience and streamline your workflows within Sessionboard.

✨ Enhancements

All enhancements mentioned below are available through Portals (Pro).

  1. Edit Profiles Directly – Speakers can now update their own profiles year-round without back-and-forth emails.
  2. Create & Assign Session Tasks – Organizers can create session tasks that can be seen by all session participants (i.e. co-speakers).

  3. Fully Mobile-Friendly – A seamless experience across all devices, making it easier than ever for contacts and groups to stay up-to-date.
