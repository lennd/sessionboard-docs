Release Date: April 10, 2025

Welcome to this month’s product updates! Below is a summary of new features, enhancements, and bug fixes released during this cycle. These updates are designed to improve your overall experience and streamline your workflows within Sessionboard.

✅ New Features

  1. Assign Tasks Directly to Contacts, Groups, or Sessions: Admins can assign tasks to individual records directly from the Contacts, Speakers, Sessions, Sponsors, or Exhibitors modules in just a few clicks.
  2. Merge Duplicate Contacts in Your CRM: Maintain accurate contact data to ensure smooth communication and efficient speaker management. Easily merge up to three duplicate contacts at once for a streamlined, organized database. Admins can now merge up to three contacts at a time.

✨ Enhancements

  1. Increased visibility in sent emails: After sending an email through Sessionboard, Admins can monitor whether the email has been delivered, opened, or clicked. 
  2. List Speakers in their Assigned Order: Customize and update your speaker order to best reflect your event's priorities. This revised order will show in the Portal along with the Contact and Session profiles. 

Thank you for choosing Sessionboard! We are committed to continuously enhancing your experience in future releases. Your feedback is crucial to us.
