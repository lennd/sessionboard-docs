Once you've been set up as an event team member (i.e. admin or evaluator), you'll be able to log into Sessionboard to work on your events.

An email will be sent to you from no-reply@sessionboard.com stating that someone from your team has invited you to join Sessionboard. You can access Sessionboard by clicking the button "View My Events" or "Join your event team".

Sample Invite Email

Subject Line: <User Name> has invited you to join Sessionboard

 


If this is your first time accessing Sessionboard, you’ll be prompted to create a password to use for future logins.
 

Next, you’ll be redirected to your Sessionboard dashboard, where you’ll see all events you have access to. Click on an event name to enter it.

 

In the future when you wish to log back into Sessionboard, use the URL app.sessionboard.com.
