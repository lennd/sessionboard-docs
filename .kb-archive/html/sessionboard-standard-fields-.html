Understanding Standard Fields in Sessionboard

When working within the Sessionboard platform, understanding the structure and classification of fields is key to successfully organizing, collecting, and utilizing data across your events. Sessionboard provides a flexible system of standard fields that are designed to capture essential information, whether it pertains to individual contacts, specific sessions, or broader groups.

In this article, we’ll break down the standard fields available in Sessionboard, categorize each by its designated field type (such as text, dropdown, or multi-select), and clarify whether it belongs to a Contact Field, Session Field, or Group Field. This guide will help you quickly identify where to find and how to use these fields, enabling a more streamlined and efficient event management workflow.

Whether you're setting up forms for speaker submissions, session details, or group categories, this reference will serve as a practical companion for navigating Sessionboard’s field system.
