What is Sessionboard Marketing?

Sessionboard Marketing is the content orchestration layer that connects every moment of the event (before, during, and after) into a continuous content operation that generates value all year long.

It is not a content distribution tool. It is a native capability within the Sessionboard platform that connects speaker preparation, live session capture, and multi-channel distribution into a single, always-on content operation.

The core problem it solves: Event teams typically rely on multiple fragmented tools across the event lifecycle: speaker management, session capture, content creation, and distribution. The result is lost content, constant rework, and value that never gets activated. Sessionboard replaces those fragmented workflows with a single content layer that transforms live-event moments into structured, reusable content.

Key positioning: "Turn every event into a year-round content engine — before, during, and after."

The Event Content Lifecycle

Sessionboard Marketing operates across all three phases of your event.

 

Before the Event: Preparation & Coordination

Coordinate speakers, collect materials, and give AV teams everything they need before the first session starts.

  • Speaker portal for asset and bio collection
  • Content collection and review workflows
  • Ready Room for AV teams with real-time access to assets, schedules, and agendas

During the Event: Live Capture

Capture every session as it happens and turn it into ready-to-use content instantly.

  • Live transcription directly from the soundboard
  • AI-generated summaries per session
  • Automatic theme and quote extraction
  • Content is ready for distribution within minutes of a session ending

After the Event: Amplification & Reuse

Activate the content your event generated for weeks, months, and years after.

  • Speaker amplification campaigns (Amplify)
  • Searchable Media Library of all session content
  • Clips, summaries, and branded assets
  • Campaign tracking & analytics

Key Features

Speaker CRM

The Speaker CRM is the connective layer for all Sessionboard Marketing features. Every speaker builds a profile that includes their bio, session history, transcripts, shared content, and campaign performance across every event your organization has run.

Over time, the CRM becomes your organization's institutional memory of subject matter expertise: searchable, attributable, and connected to your full event history.

Key capabilities:

  • Engagement tracking: who shared what, how it performed, who your top advocates are
  • Subject matter expert discovery by topic across your entire speaker network
  • Cross-event content attribution per speaker

Speaker Ready Room

The Speaker Ready Room is a live operational view for AV teams with instant access to speaker assets, schedules, and transcription controls, all in one place on event day.

No accounts or onboarding required. AV teams access the Ready Room via a magic link.

Key capabilities:

  • Magic-link access — no login or onboarding required for AV teams
  • Filter sessions by room, track, or time slot
  • Download speaker files directly from the portal
  • Start and stop live transcription with a single click

 

Live Transcription & Real-Time Content

Live Transcription captures audio directly from the soundboard and transforms every session into speaker-attributed transcripts, AI summaries, key themes, and reusable content (all connected to the Speaker CRM).

Browser-based > no apps, hardware, or software installation required.

Key capabilities:

  • Full content history tied to each speaker's CRM profile
  • AI-generated summaries, quotes, and themes per session
  • Content ready for immediate distribution after each session ends
  • Session-level controls for "Do Not Record" and "Licensed Content — No Transcription"

Note: Transcription is disabled by default and must be manually started from the Ready Room. Speakers can configure consent preferences through the Speaker Portal.

 

Content Campaigns & Distribution (Amplify)

Amplify is a branded, video-first campaign system that turns every speaker, sponsor, and exhibitor into a personalized distribution channel, with full brand control maintained by the organizer.

Key capabilities:

  • Video-first campaign templates
  • Personalized UTM tracking and promo codes per speaker
  • Analytics for shares, clicks, and registrations per speaker
  • Admin approval workflows to protect brand voice
  • Distribution across LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp, and more
  • AI-generated social copy in multiple languages

Tip: Amplify is connected to the Speaker CRM and session data — it's not a standalone distribution tool. Campaigns are generated using speaker history and session content already in the platform.

Media Library & Content Reuse

The Media Library is a searchable, speaker-attributed archive of every session from every event. Generate highlight reels, extract clips, create branded summaries, and build promotional packages, all from content you've already captured.

Key capabilities:

  • Ingest video from any source: AV uploads, Goldcast exports, Zoom recordings, and other webinar platforms
  • Search by topic, speaker, or keyword
  • Use past event content to promote future events
  • Generate clips, summaries, and branded assets


AI Reports & Event Analytics

AI Reports turns natural language questions into dashboards and insights in seconds — no SQL, no BI tools, no data team required.

Key capabilities:

  • Natural language queries (e.g., "Which sessions drove the most engagement?")
  • AI-generated dashboards from a single prompt
  • Scheduled reports delivered to any email address
  • Share via protected links — no login required for stakeholders
  • Real-time org and event data

 

Common Use Cases

Post-Event Content Activation

Scenario: "Our event ended 3 weeks ago and we still haven't published anything because we have to edit everything manually."

Transcripts and AI summaries for each session are ready within 15 minutes of it ending. The Media Library has all clips indexed. Amplify launches speaker campaigns in days, not weeks.

Features involved: Live Transcription + Media Library + Amplify

Day-of AV Coordination Without Chaos

Scenario: "On event day, our AV team doesn't have access to speaker files and has to ask for materials 10 minutes before each session."

The Ready Room gives the AV team real-time access to all assets, schedules, and transcription controls (magic link access, no onboarding, one click to start or stop recording).

Features involved: Speaker Ready Room + Speaker Portal

Year-Round Content Engine

Scenario: "We run 4 events a year but after each one, the content ends up in a Dropbox folder that nobody revisits."

The Media Library centralizes all content from all events, searchable by topic, speaker, and keyword. Past content promotes future events. The CRM builds speaker expertise history over time.

Features involved: Media Library + Speaker CRM + Amplify

Speakers as a Distribution Channel

Scenario: "We want speakers to share content but we have to manually send assets to each one and never know if they actually share."

Amplify creates personalized campaigns per speaker with branded assets, AI-generated copy, and UTM tracking. One-click activation, and the organizer sees shares, clicks, and registrations per speaker in real time.

Features involved: Amplify + Speaker CRM + Analytics

Proving Event ROI to Stakeholders

Scenario: "Our CMO wants to know which sessions drove the most engagement and how it compares to last year. We have the data in 3 different tools."

AI Reports answers in plain English. Report generated in seconds, shared via a protected link. No exports, no SQL, no waiting on the data team.

Features involved: AI Reports + Analytics

Integrations

Sessionboard Marketing doesn't need to be the only platform in your stack. It's the central content layer: it doesn't replace your marketing tools; it makes them work better by giving them clean, attributed, ready-to-activate content.

Key Integrations

Integration Type Notes
Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini MCP / AI Direct query to Sessionboard data from your preferred AI tool. No manual exports required.
Gleanin & Snöball Distribution Available for organizations with existing contracts. Amplify replaces these long-term.
Cvent Event Management Event data connected and continuously enriched inside Sessionboard.
Goldcast, Zoom, and other webinar platforms Video Ingest Import video and transcripts to centralize all content in the Media Library.

MCP Integration

The MCP integration turns Sessionboard into the centralized content source for all AI tools your team uses. Teams can ask questions in Claude or ChatGPT about Sessionboard data without leaving those tools:

  • "Which session topics drove the most engagement across our last 3 events?"
  • "Draft a sponsor report based on this track's performance data."
  • "Who should anchor next year's program based on historical speaker scores?"

Read-only, token-scoped. PII is obfuscated by default. 90-day audit log.
