Evaluation Plans gather feedback on submitted sessions from evaluators using rating scales. Navigate to **Sessions → Evaluation → Evaluation Plans** and click **Add Plan**.

<Note>
Want AI to score submissions instead of (or alongside) people? See [AI Evaluations](/evaluations/ai-evaluations).
</Note>

## Build a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type">Select **Assign Evaluators**.</Step>
  <Step title="Configuration">
    - **Name** and **Instructions** (criteria and scoring guidance)
    - **Set the plan as open** — enable to set a due date now, or open later
    - **Enable anonymized review** — hides speaker names
    - **Enable weekly reminders** — automated Monday emails to evaluators
    - **Include uploaded files** — give evaluators access to session files
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluators">
    Assign Evaluators, Evaluator Session Managers, and Admin Users.
    <Note>Evaluators must first be added as an Evaluator to the event, and the plan must be **closed** to assign them.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Session filters">Filter by standard and custom session fields to assign specific submissions.</Step>
  <Step title="Display fields">Choose which session and speaker fields evaluators see, and create custom evaluation questions (e.g., a "Would you recommend this session?" dropdown, external/internal comment boxes).</Step>
  <Step title="Grading options">
    <Warning>Grading options **cannot** be edited once the plan is created.</Warning>
    - **Rating icon** — faces/numbers (1–5) or stars/hearts (1–20)
    - **Enable rubric** — weighted criteria totaling 100% (max 255 characters per criterion)
    - **Evaluation limits** — max evaluations per submission
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage & open plans

- Manage reusable fields under **Content → Fields**.
- To open a closed plan, click the ellipsis and choose **Open and notify evaluators** or **Open and do not send notifications**.
- Other actions: **Review** (progress and ratings), **Edit**, **Export** (Individual or Cumulative Grades Report), **Delete** (permanent), **Duplicate**, and **Notify New Evaluators**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do ratings automatically reach speakers?">No. Ratings and comments are only accessible to event admins with access to plan results.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can evaluators see other evaluators' comments?">No — only admins can access evaluation results.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can evaluators communicate with speakers?">No, evaluators have view-only access through their assigned plans.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
